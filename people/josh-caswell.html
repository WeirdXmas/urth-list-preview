  • 2010-06-11 Wall of NessusArgues that the Wall of Nessus in The Book of the New Sun could simply be a relic of earlier generations, a link to a past whose exact function nobody in the present understands.   • 2010-06-11 Wall of NessusSuggests that the Wall of Nessus may be a metaphor for the separation of Nessus, and by extension Severian, from the rest of Urth, much as the scattered places are cut off from one another.
