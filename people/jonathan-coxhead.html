  • 1997-07-02 Shape of gammadionProposes that the gammadion Severian's religion uses is a swastika-like evil mockery of Christianity, drawn as a square shape with a void centre. Grounds this in the swastika's early Christian history, Wolfe's habit of neutral scene descriptions that creep up on readers, and the Matachin Tower library picture of Neil Armstrong on the Moon.
  • 1997-07-02 KalevalaCorrects an earlier post's description of the Kalevala, saying it is a modern 1835 compilation but rooted in real Finnish folklore, and names Elias Lönnrot as its compiler. Adds that Ian Watson wrote two books inspired by it, then jokingly asks why the subject came up at all.
