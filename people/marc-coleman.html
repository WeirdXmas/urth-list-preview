  • 1998-07-08 Severian and the White FountainAgrees with the quoted point that Severian's torturer training shaped his later acceptance of the New Sun, adding that his walk down the hallway before his examination, where he sees his clients from their side, is a key moment. Also reads his killing of the old Autarch and absorption of the Autarch's personalities as symbolic of his later willingness to do what must be done.
  • 1998-07-07 A Solar LabyrinthAgrees with Nutria's reading that A Solar Labyrinth is about religion, saying a recent reread confirmed it, and then shifts to praising the story On the Train and quoting its line about telephone poles reeling like drunks. Responds to Nutria's post directly, without developing an argument of its own about the religious content.
