Their messages (3 with summaries)

  • 2000-08-19 Inhumi secret and horn/silk/inhumi related?Proposes that the inhumi secret is that offering one's blood or essence to an inhumu lets one defeat it, and that Silk gave himself over to an inhumu so his essence now sits in that body, with Horn's soul bolstering it. Grounds this in Horn being transported to a body whose spirit was dying, and suggests the result might be a 'New Inhumu' mixing human and inhumu essences, though the poster admits dissatisfaction.
  • 2000-08-16 Clute's revelationArgues that Horn/Silk may be an inhumu, a possibility the poster reached while reading In Green's Jungles and that John Clute had already raised in SF Weekly. Grounds this on Oreb still liking him and Horn's remark near the end about his too-great appetite, and floats the idea that willing human sacrifice would stop inhumu from killing.
  • 2000-07-08 OBW: Yet another Inhumi secret theoryProposes a new theory of the Inhumi secret: that the Inhumi would neither kill nor drink the sentience of people who willingly give themselves over to them, linking this to giving as a theme. Grounds the idea in Krait's refusal to kill Horn once Horn has properly given his word, and admits the theory may be too simplistic.
