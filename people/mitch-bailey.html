  • 1999-12-22 A Little Dotty? M*MILLION artifact of character setExplains that the poster meant the plate's dot as a mid-height bullet, which Number Five realizes is a multiplication sign rather than a period, so the plate reads M*MILLION. Responds to Dan's suggestion that it reads M. Million, and notes the asterisk stands in for the bullet because email text cannot show one; also apologizes for an HTML mishap.
  • 1999-12-20 Isid Iooo IoooE , duh!Corrects a previous post's arithmetic on the hex-number decoding of the Isid Iooo IoooE puzzle, saying the numbers should be two 32-bit and one 48-bit rather than two 64-bit and one 128-bit. Quotes the earlier suggestion and jokingly recalls a Wolfe character who read "M*MILLION" as Monsieur Million, which is a self-deprecating aside rather than a substantive reading.
  • 1999-12-18 Isid Iooo IoooE ("Hoo-oo-WEE!")Proposes that the name Isid Iooo IoooE is a code, suggesting that capital I's and lower-case o's be read as 1s and 0s, giving ISID 1000 1000E, and possibly a machine model number. Responds to Mr. Ansley's letter about the name's observation, and offers a further reading that treats the s as a 5 and the parts as hexadecimal numbers such as 151D, 1000, 1000E, while admitting their significance is unclear.
