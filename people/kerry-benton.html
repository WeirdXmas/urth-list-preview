  • 2008-10-24 Fuligin in illustrationArgues that the text never mentions a red or other coloured lining for Severian's cloak, and that the Orb Shadow & Claw cover's varicolored lining is inaccurate.   • 2008-05-14 Other SF writers?Recommends Frank Herbert's Dune to a newcomer and ranks its sequels, placing Children and Messiah as decent and God Emperor, Heretics and Chapterhouse as readable while dismissing later books by another author. Grounds the praise in the sense of a complete, well-constructed universe glimpsed in small details, compared to a well-made meal and to Tolkien's world-building.
  • 2008-05-13 Steampunk booksExplains steampunk to a newcomer as a homage to Jules Verne, describing it as anachronistic fiction where steam power and clockwork drive science-fictional machines such as giant walking weapons platforms. Responds to Chris Laybourn's request to be filled in on the term, and gives a quick informal synopsis with no Wolfe-specific evidence.
  • 2008-05-13 Steampunk booksSeconds Nicholas Gevers's recommendation of Jay Lake's steampunk novel MAINSPRING for Wolfe readers, saying the poster enjoyed Lake's style and twisting story despite not being a steampunk fan. Responds to Gevers's announcement of the Extraordinary Engines anthology and its suggestion that Lake's book has Wolfean echoes.
  • 2007-12-15 Cumaean???Argues that the Moon in the Book of the Short Sun probably only looks larger rather than being closer, since an atmosphere's light scattering would enlarge its apparent disc. Responds to Pedro Pereira's idea of solar black-hole gravity by citing the Roche limit, roughly 9500 km for Earth and Moon, against an atmosphere about 40 km deep.
