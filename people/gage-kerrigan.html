  • 2002-04-14 opinion of pandoraAsks why Pandora Box, a Wolfe novel the poster disliked, was underappreciated and what made it unenjoyable. Hypothesizes that the mismatch between its style and plot, and from Wolfe's other work, was intentional to characterize narrator Holly Hollander as an amateur writer, and asks others about their own reasons for disliking it.
  • 2002-03-24 Uncollected Wolfe Bibliography, brush-bushArgues that the disputed 'bush' versus 'brush' wording in The Shadow of the Torturer is deliberate, since Wolfe would have used a pronoun for the same kind of object, and apologizes for an earlier retyping. Asks the list for a complete bibliography of Wolfe's uncollected short stories, and also asks what a brush is.
  • 2002-03-21 Evacuation plansProposes that in The Book of the New Sun humanity's spacefaring elite may have left Earth before a predicted calamity, keeping the warning from those who could not leave. Grounds it in hypothetical questions about leaving Earth and a dull lifeless future, and clarifies he is not denying humanity reached the stars.
  • 2002-03-20 lacerations?Replies to a prior post's claim that humanity did not make it into space, arguing that his point was only that the space program died while humanity would still leave Urth later. He asks whether The Book of the New Sun gives evidence of lacerations shredding flesh from victims or whether that is simply assumed.
  • 2002-03-19 "a right good thrashing..."Reports that the poster found a McCaffery interview in which Wolfe says the Urth of The Book of the New Sun depicts a world that followed a do-nothing course on resources and space exploration, and presents this as vindication of the poster's earlier theory. Quotes Wolfe's reply at length, then mocks the list's earlier rejection of the claim that humanity will be stuck on a worsening Earth.
  • 2002-03-19 Sev's Dream QUOTATIONQuotes Severian's dream passage from The Shadow of the Torturer, in which he imagines time stopping and the sun winking out and a miraculous light animating living things. Supplies the requested text, which an earlier poster had said hints at a Green or Blue outcome for Urth, citing page 15, chapter II.
  • 2002-03-12 Thoughts on pollution <MANTIS> esp.Asks why humanity would abandon Earth and what caused the decline from interstellar technology to medieval conditions in the New Sun setting, rather than the economic pollution the poster first raised. Proposes air pollution, a dimming sun, ice caps, and a link between ecosystem and society as possible causes, citing the Book of the New Sun's history and its lack of outside contact.
  • 2002-03-11 Follow up: I need helpAsks the list for evidence of Earth's pollution beyond the poisoned water supply and for information on the history of Urthian society before the New Sun. Admits the request is impatient and impertinent, and gives no textual passage or prior reading as grounds.
  • 2002-03-08 Appian from 'the cat', need help with reportProposes a dating argument that Appian was Autarch sixty-two years before Severian took power, using Odilo's twenty years of service, Sancha's fifty-year absence and the Cat's timeline. Grounds the claim in those intervals and asks readers to check the math before quoting it, then asks for chapter-referenced material on societal decline in the Urth books for a school report.
