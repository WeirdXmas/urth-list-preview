Their messages (5 with summaries)

  • 1999-08-31 Geography question?Argues that the Urth continent's location in the southern hemisphere cannot settle which continent the Long Sun characters inhabit, because plate tectonics over long time spans let any continent drift there. Responds to alga's geography remark by rejecting using animal species names as evidence, since animals and birds migrate between continents, citing horses, lions, opossums and humanity.
  • 1998-05-15 Whatever happened to... ?Asks whether Gene Wolfe will ever finish the Soldier of... series, noting that a third book was announced years earlier but nothing has appeared since. Admits the question may have been answered before and is not about The Book of the New Sun.
  • 1998-05-13 Suzanne Delage:Quilts & smallpoxArgues that the smallpox-in-quilts idea is historically plausible, citing accounts of European colonists and soldiers giving clothing and bedding from smallpox victims to Native Americans in hopes they would catch the disease. Responds to the thread's question of whether Suzanne Delage's quilts could really spread smallpox, grounding the claim in several historical accounts the poster says he has read.
  • 1998-05-07 Book covers, Palaemon and ReadingArgues that Palaemon's remark that Severian would forget his advice is not literal, but means the advice would pass unheeded, as young men often ignore their elders. Responds to the quoted observation about Palaemon's odd words, which noted Severian's famously perfect memory, and thanks list members for help with book covers.
