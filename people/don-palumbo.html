Their messages (11 with summaries)

  • 2003-09-07 Duh! Part 2.Argues that Typhon and Baldanders are the same person thematically, as both embody the Ogre-Tyrant of the status quo that the hero must overthrow. Grounds this in Campbell's monomyth from The Hero with a Thousand Faces, presenting The Book of the New Sun and Urth of the New Sun as Wolfe's treatment of that pattern, and responds to James Wynn's question about whether Typhon and Baldanders are the same.
  • 2003-08-08 Thecla's fourth bookProposes that the fourth book Severian fetches for Thecla may itself be contained within The Book of the New Sun, as Dr. Talos' play is inside Claw. Grounds this in the tetralogy's nested tales and fractal structure, based on Campbell's monomyth, and asks for textual evidence and the Planet Engineering essay reference.
  • 2003-08-08 What becomes of THE WONDERS OF URTH AND SKYThanks Mantis for pointing to the passage in The Citadel of the Autarch, chapter 34, where Severian still carries the book of Wonders of Urth and Sky on his return to the Citadel. Responds to an earlier post asking what becomes of the book, confirming the answer with the chapter reference and noting Severian's intention to keep it.
  • 2003-08-07 What becomes of THE WONDERS OF URTH AND SKYAsks where the Book of the Wonders of Urth and Sky ends up in The Book of the New Sun, whether Severian still has it when he returns to Nessus as Autarch or loses it earlier, perhaps near Lake Diuturna. Offers no answer or evidence of its own, only a specific question about the book's fate across the tetralogy.
  • 2003-07-10 Severian, memory, alzaboRecounts Wolfe's offhand willingness to tell Don Palumbo the ending of the series, and Palumbo's refusal, while agreeing with James Jordan that the Autarchs do not use the alzabo drug to pass on memory. Supports the point by recalling the Autarch's own statement about his vial drug being rarer and stronger.
  • 2003-07-09 Severian, memory, alzaboPoints out that the Autarchs do not pass on memories through the alzabo drug, citing the Autarch's own statement that the vial around his neck comes from a rarer and more powerful source. Responds to Craig Brewer's proposal that Severian's perfect memory arises from a recursive alzabo effect, offering a textual correction to that idea.
  • 2003-07-09 stealing apu punchauProposes that Severian succeeds in bringing the New Sun because he is, or still thinks like, a professional torturer, since ending the Old Urth requires a torturer's equanimity. Responds to James Jordan's point about Severian's resurrection powers and the Claw as a sacrament, and asks whether others have made the torturer argument.
  • 2003-07-09 sev's skullArgues that Severian's death-and-rebirth episodes in The Book of the New Sun should be read symbolically through Campbell's monomyth rather than as literal deaths and replacements. Responds to Marc Aramini's claim that the narrator is a copy and that his skull is evidence, saying the monomyth already explains the repeated death imagery and that no literal replacement is needed.
  • 2003-07-08 Sev's skull and spring constellationsArgues that The Book of the New Sun borrows Joseph Campbell's monomyth so heavily that it becomes one of the most elaborate reworkings of that formula in science fiction, and announces a planned long scholarly article on it. Responds to Alice K. Turner's claim that Wolfe does not copy, by conceding the borrowing while stressing Wolfe's twists and comparing it with Dune, The Stars My Destination and Flowers for Algernon.
  • 2003-06-30 Crowley, Wolfe, BrunoArgues that Severian in The Book of the New Sun repeatedly recovers overlooked details from his own memories and combines them with new information to reach conclusions. Gives as an example his realization in The Claw of the Conciliator that Terminus Est lies behind the broken door to the mirror-image storeroom, and replies to Craig Brewer's question about Wolfe and Bruno only indirectly.
  • 2003-06-29 Who are Robert and MarieAsks the list to identify Robert and Marie, the husband and wife met in the jungle tree hut in Chapter 21 of The Shadow of the Torturer, noting Robert's Paris art-student past and apparent malaria. Grounds the question in the book's text and says that most of BOTNS's other allusions are easily identified, so these should be too.
