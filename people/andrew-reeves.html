Their messages (8 with summaries)

  • 2002-09-06 Was Jonas a Chem?Proposes that Jonas in The Book of the Long Sun may be a chem partly repaired with human parts, like Marble, citing his odd personality and its resemblance to Marble's merging with Rose. Grounds the idea in personality oddities rather than textual detail, and excuses technological gaps in the far future via Clarke's Law.
  • 2002-06-17 Thoughts on Undines, and other ramblingsAsks how the undines relate to Erebus, Abaia and Typhon's daughter in the Long Sun and Short Sun books, and who the girl in Jonas's story is and what the beans are. Grounds the question in Jonas's tale and events of Short Sun, which the poster says suggest something links Typhon's daughter with the great monsters in the sea.
  • 2002-06-15 Thoughts on Undines, and other ramblingsRaises questions about the Undines of the Briah universe: why Severian and Silkhorn treat undine representatives so casually, why the Commonwealth never moved against them, and whether Urth under Typhon had faster-than-light travel. Grounds these on the Juturna and Seawrack encounters, Scylla's arrival from Blue, and the Green Man's indifference to Abaia and Erebus.
  • 2002-06-11 War With The Ascians as a Proxy War?Clarifies that a proposed analogy between the Ascian war and a real proxy war is not a one-to-one mapping, and suggests Wolfe's own war experience might shape how he portrays such conflict. Compares the tone of North Korean state radio to the propaganda-like language of Loyal to the Group of Seven in The Book of the New Sun.
  • 2002-06-10 Some problems if the Pancreator/Outsider is theHoly TrinityQuestions whether James Jordan's claim that Urth is an earlier universe-cycle, with no promise to a prior Noah, comes from an authoritative Wolfe interview or elsewhere outside the text. Points to the Apollo moon landing picture, Severian's box with the book surviving a singularity, and the 20th-century window in the botanical gardens as problems for that reading.
  • 2002-06-08 Some problems if the Pancreator/Outsider is the Holy TrinityArgues that if the Outsider is the Holy Trinity, the Long Sun and Short Sun books raise theological problems: Silk's vision of the crucified Christ, the long absence of the Church and parousia, and God apparently breaking his covenant never to destroy the world by water. Grounds these in the Urth of the New Sun flood and the Hierodules rebuilding Severian's body, which implies the Increate caused it.
  • 2002-06-08 War With The Ascians as a Proxy War?Proposes that the Ascian War is better read as a counterinsurgency against an occupying totalitarian force, comparing the Ascians to Communist revolutionaries and Erebus to Kim Il Sung. Responds to Crush's proxy-war reading and a second reply, suggesting the giants won over humans with techno-toys, and that bad Autarchs, like bad clergy, do not discredit a just cause.
  • 2002-06-06 War With The Ascians as a Proxy War?Proposes that the Commonwealth's war with the Ascians in The Book of the New Sun is a proxy war between alien powers, with the autarch as a pawn, and asks whether Wolfe intends the angelic beings to be benevolent. Also questions why an angel captains spaceships given Wolfe's Catholic theology, citing the Long Sun and Short Sun books and Silk's enlightenment.
