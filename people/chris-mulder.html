  • 2007-01-21 Pas's gammadion and the voided crossJokingly suggests that the voided cross Wolfe had in mind might look like an arrangement of Allen wrenches, a playful picture of the symbol's shape. Responds to Matthew King's request for a photo and his question about voiding a cross by pointing to several archive posts, including a Wolfe Q&A session, where the symbol was discussed.
  • 2007-01-21 Pas's gammadion and the voided crossExplains that the voided cross and the gammadion are the same figure, citing the Litany of the Long Sun passage where Silk scratches a sign of addition with the corner of one of four gammadions. Works out geometrically that voiding a Greek cross through its ends yields four gammas, so any such symbol may be called a gammadion.
  • 2006-11-02 The littlest apprenticesArgues that the torturers' children who know no breast are those seized as small children with their mothers, weaned before capture, so there is no contradiction about breastfeeding in Severian's recovered memory. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's quoted reasoning, restating the three groups of children and why the memory of nursing and the deduction that the woman is his mother both hold.
  • 2006-10-27 The Katharine maidArgues that Severian's memory of suckling must be of his mother, since children taken by the torturers know no breasts and so the nursing must predate his taking. Reads the sentence as meaning a time before the Guild, offering a timeline of Catherine's imprisonment and death to show no contradiction between the passages.
  • 2006-09-08 Close Reading: Torturer Chapter 1Argues that the scene with Thecla in the first chapter of The Shadow of the Torturer was a loyalty test set by the guild for Severian, which he failed. Agrees with Daniel D Jones's reading of the chapter and reasons that the guild would want to learn early whether an apprentice could be trusted.
