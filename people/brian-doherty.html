Says that same lack of plot clarity now ruins Wolfe's recent standalones for them, though they cannot explain why BOTNS survived it.
  • 2015-04-29 Wolfe media: unreleased Death of Dr Island short filmInforms the list that a short film of Wolfe's "Death of Dr. Island", starring Daniel Browning Smith, was shot years earlier but apparently never released or finished in visual effects and editing. Points readers to the film's surviving promotional website as the only remaining access.
  • 2014-04-28 Urth Digest, Vol 116, Issue 28Explains what it means for a publisher to accept a manuscript and estimates that the forthcoming Wolfe book is probably about a year from release, perhaps spring 2015. Draws on the poster's own experience as a nonfiction author with a major New York house, where even a finished manuscript took at least four months to reach stores.
  • 2013-08-20 More HOME FIRES QsAsks how readers know that Skip ordered or was responsible for Z man's death and how they know the object sought from Sims and Chelle was a weapon or secret. Also praises the "fragility of tenacity" as a theme in Home Fires, calling it the best choice yet; no textual evidence is cited.
  • 2013-08-20 HOME FIRES finished thoughts (spoilers)Asks several questions about Home Fires: whether Chelle's discharge or death follows from fragging a commander, whether the body after the Sims meld is the original Chelle's, what secret the spies wanted, and what the novel's theme is. Responds to nothing earlier in the thread beyond the quoted Heinlein comparison, offering no answer of its own.
  • 2013-08-20 HOME FIRES finished thoughts (spoilers)Argues that Home Fires reads as Wolfe's take on a late-Heinlein style novel, with Skip and Chelle as Heinlein-type figures, and that the ending moved the poster despite its puzzling elements. Raises three questions: whether Charles Blue is an Os, whether other characters are brain scans in new bodies, and whether the Blue and Chelle diner scene reflects lost identity, asking for discussion sources.
  • 2013-08-19 Urth Digest, Vol 108, Issue 19Remarks that the poster was unaware of any significant critical backlash against The Book of the New Sun, which he thought was a classic from the start, and says he prefers The Book of the Short Sun from memory. He adds that he loved the first series while reading it without knowing key facts revealed later in commentary.
  • 2013-08-19 Gonna keep up w/ random comments on HOME FIRES in progressAsks whether the 'suicide club' element in Home Fires arrives abruptly or is properly set up in the text, and whether it fits the novel's world, requesting a clever commenter explain it. Also questions whether the search for 'Jerry's room' is drawn out unnecessarily, noting the reader has reached the scene where shots ring out.
  • 2013-08-17 New Wolfe novel on Amazon!Announces the forthcoming novel The Land Across and reports that the poster is struggling with Home Fires, finding its talky secrets and repeated boat-hijacking plot dull. Says it is his least favorite Wolfe so far, speculating that memory-wiping talk hides clues he is missing.
  • 2013-08-11 Home Fires: Wolfe doing Heinlein?Asks whether Home Fires, read about 130 pages in, feels like Wolfe's version of Heinlein's characters, scenarios and tone. Frames the comparison tentatively as a possibly overdone or absurd observation, offering no specific passages or evidence.
  • 2013-08-06 Quick thoughts on GATE OF HORN...Argues that the reference guide to The Book of the Long Sun is too Wolfean to be useful, with terse entries that omit who characters like Incanto are and list Latin plant names instead. Says the guide drained the poster's enjoyment of the Long Sun and Short Sun books, and invites other opinions on the reference work.
  • 2013-08-05 Some possibly old hat Qs from someone who has been offlist for nearly a decadeAsks whether Wolfe is working on or has announced new fiction, and whether he has retired, since the poster is nearly finished with Home Fires and will soon run out of new novels. Also asks whether Gate of Horn has had long discussion on the list, so he can check earlier posts before adding his own comments.
