  • 2003-07-08 never the Twain shall meet Lafferty nor DaviesProposes a link between Robertson Davies and Wolfe, arguing that the angel Lesser Tzadkiel, who records a person's whole life in Davies's Cornish Trilogy, parallels Severian's total memory in The Book of the New Sun. Offers the connection tentatively, noting it may be coincidence, and builds on Mantis's remark about Davies's Fifth Business.
  • 2003-06-19 >>On Encompassing the Universe<<Reports that the McAffery interview with Wolfe, titled On Encompassing the Universe, appeared earlier in Across the Wounded Galaxies, which a Science Fiction Studies review dates to 1986 rather than 1988. Replies to James Wynn's thread quoting Sam Keyes by noting the review omits any earlier publication, and links an excerpt and suggests interlibrary loan.
  • 2003-04-09 New Wolfe storiesInforms readers that Wolfe has a new story in the forthcoming anthology 13 Horrors, released at a horror convention, and that RevolutionSF.com will post a story called Easter Sunday billed as a classic reprint. Notes that Easter Sunday seems uncollected and absent from standard bibliographies, which the poster finds curious.
  • 2002-05-20 PANDORA written in the SeventiesArgues that the foreword to Pandora by Holly Hollander was written later than the novel, based on the author's letter dating its completion to 1979. Questions whether the book was originally published in France, as the narrator's foreword claims, noting that searches found no evidence and suggesting the narrator may be untruthful.
  • 2002-05-12 VanderMeer's City of Saints / Story SuitesNotes that Claude Lalumière's review of VanderMeer's City of Saints & Madmen proposes the term "mosaic" for linked story collections, filling the same gap Le Guin addressed with "story suite." Wonders what it means that two competing terms appeared within weeks of each other for a form that is not new.
  • 2002-05-10 New Wolfe non-fiction piecesInforms readers of a short Wolfe commentary in the 2002 Nebula Awards Showcase, framed as a response to Damon Knight's remark about golden age SF writers. Also reports an appreciation of R.A. Lafferty Wolfe wrote for the May Locus, noting it is not online while other tributes by Gaiman and Bisson are.
  • 2002-03-22 Another uncollected Wolfe storyInforms readers of an uncollected Wolfe story, "Pocketsful of Diamonds", appearing in the anthology Strange Attraction edited by Edward Kramer.
