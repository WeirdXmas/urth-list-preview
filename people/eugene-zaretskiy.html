  • 2010-07-05 The mystery of the image of an astronaut cleaned by RudesindArgues that the Autarch's appearance at House Azure is a purposeful mask that changes the meaning of ruling the Commonwealth and Severian's trip, and that Inire being Rudesind would add nothing. Rejects the mask-hunting behind the claim, saying the monkey description of Inire is a metaphor that makes him seem non-human to common folk, and asks why Inire would be Rudesind.
  • 2010-06-21 Is Agia a Robot?Answers David Stockhoff's question about which collection a story appears in, saying it is in the recent The Best of Gene Wolfe. Praises that collection for including Wolfe's own commentary on each story, which the poster finds funny in places and insightful elsewhere.
  • 2010-06-14 'Gene Wolfe’s Soldier of the Mist decoded: Asopus/Plataea'Quotes a passage from a classical source addressing the River Asopos as a horned one, supplying the reference that the replied-to poster said he could not find. The post cites a website collecting myth about the river, answering James Wynn's remark that he had found no mention of horns.
  • 2010-06-07 Travelling North aka miscellaneous thoughts on WolfeArgues that dismissing the faster-than-light travel method in The Book of the New Sun as unfeasible is presumptuous, since future technology may overturn current scientific limits. Responds to Gerry Quinn's claim by noting that people once declared modern inventions impossible and that the ships are millennia removed from us.
  • 2010-06-02 traveling northQuestions Lee Berman's claim that Urth lacks Christlike spiritual events, arguing instead that the Conciliator is the Jesus-like figure while Severian is decidedly non-divine. Grounds this in Severian's dark origins, his rise to saviour status, and his early respect for the Increate and the sun, which he suggests shows where the spiritual path leads.
  • 2010-04-30 Ted = Goldwurm?Asks whether any confirmed factual or textual errors exist in Wolfe's books, noting a typo in The Book of the New Sun and asking whether new editions fixed errors that affect understanding of a scene. Responds to Craig Brewer's remark that a waitress calling Winker Chinese may be a mistake in list-making or a deliberate fabrication.
  • 2010-04-23 TSH: Ted's identityProposes that Bax made up the whole story of The Sorcerer's House, since its mixed mythologies are what a PhD-trained narrator would draw on. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's view of Faerie as a catch-all, citing references from many mythologies to Dickens' The Old Curiosity Shop that Bax's doctorate would make him expert in.
  • 2010-04-21 KikiArgues that the name Ted in Wolfe's work links to Theodore, meaning divine gift, and that Amborsius derives from Greek ambrotos, meaning divine or immortal, so the two names connect. Responds to Adam Thornton's and Fred Kiesche's pop-culture guesses about Griffin by defending etymological readings and noting that Kiki from Delivery Service is a witch.
  • 2010-04-20 The Sorcerer's House Questions (*Major Spoilers*)Argues that Bax from The Sorcerer's House planned from the first letter to replace his brother George and woo Millie, so his early letters read as false. Supports this by pointing to the letters' calculated kindness toward George and Millie's later reply, and reads the last letter as partly for appearances, while noting the con-man reading also fits.
  • 2010-04-19 The Sorcerer's House Questions (*Major Spoilers*)Argues that Wolfe may intend The Sorcerer's House to allow both a reading where Bax invented the fantastic events and a puzzle-box reading, rather than forcing a choice. Responds to Craig Brewer's point that Bax's con-man career makes fabrication plausible, and notes the long-running debate between those who accept and reject the fabrication theory.
  • 2010-04-12 more questions on Sorcerer's House (spoilers)Argues that Bax compares the dwarf Quist to Quilp from Dickens's The Old Curiosity Shop as a metaphor, citing Quilp's moneylending against a jeweler who buys jewelry, and suggests the final letter may be forged by Bax. Also proposes Lupine as Doris's twin and asks whether the ring was a gift, reasoning from the book's many twins.
