Their messages (7 with summaries)

  • 2000-02-23 Discussions of "Ascian"Reports that the Portuguese dictionary lists the word "áscio" for a person living in the torrid zone where the noon sun casts no shadows, tracing it to Latin "ascios" as a possible source for Wolfe's Ascian. Offers this as a find from a separate thread on the name's origin, supplying a dictionary definition rather than an interpretation of the books.
  • 1999-11-06 Baptism vs. ResurrectionConcedes that the earlier baptism reading was a guess and now argues that baptism and resurrection symbolism are both entangled in Severian's submersion in the Gyoll, since his public career begins after it as Jesus's does. Offers a quoted passage from Clark Ashton Smith's "Nostalgia of the Unknown" as an added echo, without further argument.
  • 1999-11-05 Severian the LiarCorrects the earlier poster's naming, noting the river is the Gyoll rather than the Nessus, and proposes that Severian's emergence from the water parallels Christ's baptism by John rather than the resurrection. Responds to the quoted claim that Severian's rescue from the Nessus marks his rebirth as a Christ figure, offering a different Christian parallel.
  • 1999-10-29 Inca Head of Day RE: postmodernism, OTism, continent-i smConfirms that the mate herb is South American and that the metal straw, or bomba, used with chimarrão is characteristic of southern Brazil, northern Argentina and some Andean countries. Supports the earlier post's claim that the mate-drinking custom through a metal straw points to Latin American, Quechua-related origins for the autochthons in the Inca Head of Day story.
  • 1999-10-29 postmodernism, OTism, continent-ismArgues that the Apu Punchau and Pampa names in The Book of the New Sun come from Quechua, not Southeast Asian sources, and that the Typhon mountains resemble the Andes, so the Commonwealth is South American. Supports this with Quechua words like p'unchau and apuc, the name Machu Picchu, and Tupac Amaru, answering Martinho's wife's blank response to the Apu words.
  • 1999-05-01 Digest urth.v026.n008Argues that the Conciliator and New Sun myths in The Book of the New Sun may be a warped memory of Christianity, with Dr Talos's play read as a distorted Gospel, and asks whether Christianity is a mythic reading of science events or the reverse. Responds to a poster's remark about the invisible church by raising this chicken-and-egg question and suggesting Wolfe leaves such questions open on purpose.
  • 1999-04-23 Deluge, Hierodules, MaterialismArgues that materialist and supernaturalist categories do not fit TBONS or UOTNS, since the people there see scientific facts as the Increate's magic. Reads the Increate as Spinoza's Substance and suggests Tzadikiel could be both a high-tech android and the archangel Gabriel, since angels are tools of the Increate.
