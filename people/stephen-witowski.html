  • 1999-01-05 An Instance of the FingerpostRecommends Wilkie Collins's novel The Moonstone to readers of Wolfe, praising its complex plot, characters such as Sergeant Cuff, and sense of place.   • 1998-10-13 Third Rock from the New SunDisputes Robert Borski's genealogical reading of The Book of the New Sun, arguing that his family-relationship theories rest on thin evidence and wild speculation.
