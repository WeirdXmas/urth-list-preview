Their messages (9 with summaries)

  • 2000-03-28 Of Flagging Talent and FairiesAsks the list about Andrew Lang's fairy books, after saying they dissatisfied them in Strange Travelers, "The Ziggurat," but liked the "Koschei the Deathless" adaptation. Requests guidance on whether the Lang series is good, which volume to read first, and whether a boxed set exists.
  • 2000-02-10 PosthumousArgues that the posthumous-life idea in Wolfe's new collection Strange Travelers derives from Swedenborg's system of afterlife, and that Wolfe likely drew on Borges's writing about Swedenborg. Points to Blake and M. Night Shyamalan's The Sixth Sense as later uses of the same idea, and poses whether the characters are already dead.
  • 2000-01-12 The Detestable Thor Power Tools ScandalShares a link to Alibris, a rare book search engine, suggesting list members use it to find out-of-print titles and noting it can locate almost any book by edition and price. The post mentions Bibliomen as an example of a hard-to-find book, with no discussion of Wolfe's text.
  • 1999-12-13 TBOTNS FAQArgues that a list FAQ should focus on questions that the group's combined learning can actually answer, such as Terminus Est's Latin roots and Universal Death in the Lexicon Urthus, rather than trivia. Proposes that Will Ansley draft the first set of questions, citing the sword's Roman boundary-god name and Borges' Universal History as examples of useful sourcing.
  • 1999-12-12 TBOTNS FAQArgues that the TBOTNS FAQ should first introduce readers to Gene Wolfe himself, his background, interviews, essays and recurring literary sources, before speculating on puzzles like Severian's parentage. Proposes Christy volunteer as chair and suggests the FAQ avoid drawn-out debates such as the continent question.
  • 1999-10-28 TalesReports that Extraordinary Tales is similar to but not the same as the anthology under discussion, noting its shorter stories and shared Borges-inspired selections. Answers the earlier question by relaying that the two books share a mission statement and several pieces but differ in length, and states a preference for Extraordinary Tales.
  • 1999-10-28 TalesProposes Borges and Bioy Casares' Extraordinary Tales as the most recent Brown Book, rejecting Nicholas Gevers' point that the Brown Book and the Book of Gold are the same. Grounds the candidate in the book's out-of-print 1971 Herder and Herder translation, its featureless brown cloth binding, and its fit with Severian's exposition and the Brown Book's subtitle.
  • 1999-03-01 UbermenschesAsks why Wolfe's protagonists, from the two tetralogies to the Soldier series, Silhouette and Seven American Nights, seem handsome, strong and intelligent beyond ordinary people, like ubermensch figures. Suggests the epic may explain this but the traits seem more like Wolfe's perception than autobiography, responding to Reverend Jack's remark that Typhon is an Uebermensch.
  • 1999-01-25 The Wolfe LibraryProposes a list of essential reference and influential books for understanding Wolfe, naming Borges, Vance, Chesterton, Nabokov and Budrys along with dictionaries, encyclopedias and a saints' lives. Asks the list what authority such an essential library could have and jokingly nominates the poster as chairman of an approval committee.
