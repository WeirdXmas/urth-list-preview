  • 1998-06-27 Little Severian, Things in Jars, and MartiansArgues that the Little Severian sequence in The Book of the New Sun uses shock rather than foreshadowing, so rereading it knowing his death felt flat. Also asks for information on a homunculus and cites Stanley G. Weinbaum's Valley of Dreams, where Tweel chases a ratlike creature from a library, as a parallel.
  • 1998-06-22 Hints about Conciliator from volume namesArgues that the Book of the New Sun volume titles imply Severian is the Conciliator, since his title fits the pattern of torturer, lictor and autarch while Claw does not. Builds the case on the title format itself, reasoning that Severian may always have been the Conciliator, and responds to an earlier post by adam louis stephanides that cites Severian's healing powers as clues.
  • 1998-06-22 Little Severian and the Thing in the JarAsks why Little Severian occupies so much of The Sword of the Lictor if he serves only as a plot function, and what the homunculus scene in The Citadel of the Autarch means. Notes the Encyclopaedia of Science Fiction reading that the homunculus is Severian's sibling, and says Merryn seems more likely based on earlier list discussion, as a newcomer's self-introduction.
