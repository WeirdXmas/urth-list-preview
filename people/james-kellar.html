  • 2001-02-27 Horn DiesAgrees with Nutria that Catholicism gives a writer a rich store of symbolism to draw on, applying this to Wolfe's own faith. Follows a quoted message recalling that Wolfe called himself a writer who is a Christian rather than an evangelist, and ends with a reminder that the list is for Book of the Long Sun discussion.
  • 2001-02-27 Horn DiesAsks whether Wolfe's fiction is meant to make readers think more favourably of Catholicism, not necessarily to convert them. Responds to a list question about who is Christian or Catholic, replying to the Catholic side of the discussion with a Blattid-style aside.
  • 2001-02-23 spam? also godlingAsks whether the list was spammed from Germany and raises a complaint about an illustration in Return to the Whorl that misreads the faceless man with a claw-like hat as the godling's finger. Also agrees that Pig is a godling, citing the matching description of his claws, and notes others have already mentioned the illustration.
  • 2001-02-12 pig's dialectProposes that the Hoof/Hide passages' author may have misread Horn/Silk's description of Pig's Scottish-sounding accent, and argues Pig had been blind for a while before attacking Silk. Responds to an earlier post questioning Pig's extra H sounds, and grounds the blindness claim in Pig's skill at navigating in the dark.
  • 2001-02-12 pig's dialectArgues that Pig's dialect in The Book of the Long Sun is meant to be Scottish rather than Irish or Welsh, citing 'dinna fash' and a passage where Pig says 'wind' so it sounds like 'fiend'. Asks which part of Scotland Pig comes from and floats the idea that Pig is a godling, reading the chapter title as bearing on more than the last paragraph.
