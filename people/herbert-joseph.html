Their messages (2 with summaries)

  • 2009-11-03 The spiny orange - we used to throw the fruit at each other as kids and call them stink bombs.Identifies the spiny orange of Able's bow in The Fifth Head of Cerberus as Osage orange, and notes Wolfe owns a walking stick of that wood carved by Joe Mayhew. Supports this by pasting a Wikipedia entry on Maclura pomifera describing the fruit, its latex, its use for bows, and its colloquial names.
  • 2009-10-30 (no subject)Proposes that Wolfe intends readers to imitate his boy-turned-man hero, as Don Quixote imitates his knightly heroes, and asks whether 'Arthur Ormsby' is another name for King Arthur. Grounds this in a quoted passage on John Ormsby's 1885 Don Quixote translation, which calls the novel a satire of chivalry books, and in the poster's boyhood reading of knight stories.
