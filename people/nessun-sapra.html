  • 2015-01-05 Long Sun and the Ancient MediterraneanArgues that the poster's earlier point about Euhemerus gains force because Euhemerus was called a "Bergaios", a deliberate liar, in antiquity. Adds this classical detail to Marcos Bidoli Gouvêa's argument that Wolfe uses Euhemerism in the Long Sun books, where Scylla resembles a sea goddess.
  • 2014-08-06 Severian forgets a few things ...Argues that Severian's repeated claims to have forgotten things in The Book of the New Sun form a running gag rather than a single incident, listing many quoted instances of him failing to recall details. Responds to Marc Aramini's post, which questioned whether Severian's supposed perfect recall holds, citing the coin and Thecla's meal scenes in The Shadow of the Torturer.
  • 2014-08-06 Severian forgets a few things ...Collects many instances of Severian saying he forgot or could not recall something in The Book of the New Sun, quoting lines about failed recollection. Responds to Marc Aramini's opening quotes, noting the tension with Severian's claimed perfect recall and asking whether his memory lapses are triggered by distraction.

  • 2014-08-01 short story project so farAsks Marc Aramini's compiled list of Gene Wolfe short stories to correct its dates and contents, noting missing titles such as "Kid Sister", "A visit from his confidant" and "Dumpster World". Responds to Gerry Quinn's reply to a question about whether Delany admired Wolfe, which had suggested only shared interests; the post offers a textual reference rather than an argument.
  • 2013-04-16 Flaubert's _The Temptation of Saint Anthony_ as influence on _New Sun_?Reports that a reading group found Flaubert's The Temptation of Saint Anthony shares much archaic vocabulary with The Book of the New Sun, and asks whether Flaubert influenced Wolfe. Grounds this in a 1981 Joan Gordon interview listing Flaubert among Wolfe's favorite authors and an academic book claiming the Temptation and Borges's Book of Imaginary Beings were sources.
  • 2012-03-21 The Books in THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUNInforms Fred Kiesche that Gene Wolfe's essay "The Books in The Book of the New Sun" first appeared in a 1984 volume after Castle of the Otter, and was later reprinted in Peter Wright's Shadow of the New Sun. Gives the reprint's page range as the source, answering Kiesche's question about where else the essay appeared.
