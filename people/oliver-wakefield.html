Their messages (9 with summaries)

  • 2008-05-14 Urth of The New SunReports that the poster finds The Urth of the New Sun harder than The Book of the New Sun because it moves into the metaphysics of the Sun universe, though its prose is easier to follow. Argues it is vital for understanding the earlier four books, yet works as a coda, and asks the list what the general consensus is on the book.
  • 2008-05-13 Other SF writers?Replies to Pedro Pereira's thread on other SF writers by complaining that much fantasy merely copies Tolkien's orcs, elves and dwarves and lacks imagination, which is why he moved to science fiction. Also asks whether anyone recommends Dune, which he plans to read next.
  • 2008-05-13 Whos met or written to WolfeJokes that Wolfe rarely answers fan letters and imagines a mock autoreply claiming Severian is Erebus' son and Abaia's nephew and that the New Sun was a dream. Playfully riffs on the wish to contact Wolfe, with no argument, and the parody itself is the only evidence offered.
  • 2008-05-13 Other SF writers? (Was: How list works andquestionsAsks what readers disliked in China Miéville's Iron Council, calls its socialist message laboured, and asks for steampunk recommendations after finding The Difference Engine weak at the end. Lists favourite SF novels and notes that The Wastelands includes a Wolfe short story, so the post is a general SF discussion rather than a reading of Wolfe.
  • 2008-05-12 How list works and questions to mailersComments on the poor online presence of Gene Wolfe, praising a fan site, and criticizes the UK cover designs of Wolfe's books as misleading to intellectual readers. Notes few newspaper reviews online yet favourable press quotes on the covers, and asks how members travel to conventions.
  • 2008-05-11 Whos met or written to WolfeAsks whether other list members have met Gene Wolfe or written to him, and whether writing a fan letter through his agent is a bad idea. Explains the poster cannot attend conventions from abroad and wants a short appreciative letter, with no discussion of the books' interpretation.
  • 2008-05-11 How list works and questions to mailersThanks list members for earlier replies, reflects on why Wolfe seems little known and reads as a curiosity, and says a friend could not get past the opening pages of The Shadow of the Torturer. Blames the UK cover's pulpy epic fantasy look for misrepresenting the book, then introduces himself as a war studies student with a list of favourite authors.
  • 2008-05-11 How list works and questions to mailersAsks how to keep text within the window when posting to the list and asks who the contributors are, whether they are science fiction fans or general readers, and where they live. Also asks whether Wolfe is more popular in the US, noting he has met only one reader of The Book of the New Sun in the UK.
  • 2008-05-10 Gene Wolfe PopularityAsks whether Gene Wolfe had a larger public profile in the past, such as the 1980s or early 1990s, given his critical acclaim and apparent obscurity. Newcomer wonders why such an admired author has stayed relatively unknown and does not cite any specific book or passage.
