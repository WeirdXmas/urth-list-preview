  • 2014-09-08 VanderMeer's Southern Reach trilogyOff-topic social reply saying the poster liked the first Southern Reach book and has preordered the other two, plus praise for VanderMeer's City, Dradin in Love and Shriek. Responds to Fred Kiesche's note that he had read the first volume and planned to read the rest; it names no Wolfe material and offers no argument or evidence.
  • 2014-08-27 Polish translation of Long Sun.Reports that a Polish translation of The Citadel of the Autarch rendered a dial as "zegar", a word that means clock, so the room Severian finds full of dials reads as a room of clocks. Responds to a thread on the Polish translation of the Long Sun books, drawing on the poster's own memory of reading the Polish and English texts side by side.
  • 2014-08-27 Polish translation of Long Sun.Asks whether translating the name Silk as Jedwab, the Polish word for silk, in the Polish edition of the Long Sun books can be justified. Describes being outraged that the translator who handled The Book of the New Sun well changed a character's name, and says the poster has not yet read the Long Sun books.
  • 2014-08-11 The Land Across Special EditionReports that the poster will not buy the limited Subterranean Press special edition of The Land Across because shipping to Poland costs about $28, and prefers the Tor cover. Replies to Fred Kiesche, who had shared the Subterranean Press announcement and asked whether readers had $70 to spare for it.
  • 2014-08-01 short story project so farAsks Marc Aramini whether he has compiled a complete list of Wolfe's short stories so the poster can track which ones he has read, and offers to buy a copy if Marc ships internationally. Responds to Marc's quoted note about reading his way through the published stories, including The Eyeflash Miracles and Seven American Nights.

  • 2014-05-29 KingProposes that the author Wolfe meant Isaac Singer, not Stephen King, in the self-interview 'Lone Wolfe' quoted from Castle of Days. Notes that Singer was still alive in 1983, when that piece appeared, to support the identification.

Responds to António Pedro Marques's question about whether Wolfe's endorsement of King has been analyzed, offering a specific textual example rather than an interpretation.
