Their messages (8 with summaries)

  • 2009-02-10 mystery of the guildProposes that the Guild's "mystery" in The Book of the New Sun echoes the Greek word mysterion, meaning unknowable or initiates-only knowledge, so the secret is how to torture a person while keeping them alive. Grounds this in the word's sense as used in mystery religions, offering it as a guess about Wolfe's intent rather than citing a passage.
  • 2009-02-05 Wolfean theologiesArgues that the thread has drifted away from Gene Wolfe, and declines to continue a personal exchange about Catholics and insults so the list can return to interpreting Wolfe's work. Responds to Chris P.'s quoted complaint that the digressions about priests, family and Satanists are irrelevant and amount to trolling, addressing him directly and ironically.
  • 2009-02-04 Wolfean theologiesObjects to Son of Witz's claim that the filioque clause caused later Catholic abuses, including priest rapists, and asks the list to stop the attacks on Catholics. Points to the discussion's drift away from Wolfe, saying the earlier conversation was interesting until the anti-Catholic remarks appeared.
  • 2008-12-29 Sleeping BaldandersProposes combining Son of Witz's observation about a sleeping Baldanders with Bruce's idea that hormones affect Baldanders' personality and Dave's idea that he only pretends to sleep, to explain a puzzling scene. Responds to Son of Witz's reading of the quoted exchange in which Baldanders apparently knows Severian is the New Sun.
  • 2008-12-29 Sleeping BaldandersAsks what is really happening in the short sleep-talk exchange in Chapter XV of The Book of the New Sun, where Baldanders utters four fragments and Severian answers as if half awake. Argues the scene must accomplish more than recapping Severian's expulsion from the guild and journey toward Thrax, and invites readers' ideas on its purpose.
  • 2008-11-13 Shadow, Chapter XAsks what the enigmatic opening of Shadow Chapter X means, where Severian and Roche leave the House Azure and a white-haired man produces a vial shaped like a phallus that Severian first takes for an icon. Suggests it may foreshadow Severian becoming Autarch, but says it should make sense within the scene and invites guesses about it.
  • 2008-09-29 a lurker asks a questionAsks what in-story plot function explains a Citadel scene, setting aside the Borges allusion and Wolfe's Hitchcock-like cameo as likely non-reasons. Replies to Adam Thornton's post, which had cited the archive search, the Nessus-as-Buenos-Aires equation and the blind librarian as Borges, and says he is still stumped.
  • 2008-09-29 a lurker asks a questionAsks what Chapter VI, The Master of the Curators, contributes to The Book of the New Sun beyond Severian fetching books for Thecla, noting its details of Citadel life and the Curators' guild. Says he has re-read the book and searched the Urth archives without finding an answer, so he brings the question to the list.
