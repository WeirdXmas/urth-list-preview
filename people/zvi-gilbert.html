  • 2008-12-17 Crowley and mysteryArgues that John Crowley's Engine Summer and Little, Big are not confusing and are not like Wolfe's New Sun, and says a discussion of them would turn into praise. Proposes instead discussing Crowley's four-novel Aegypt series, whose characters such as John Dee and Giordano Bruno are drawn from history, and asks for an erudite reader to explain the historical sources.
  • 2005-03-22 OT: watchmen on trialArgues that Moore's Watchmen plays with superhero conventions to show how real superhero types would deform the world, with Dr Manhattan's interventions causing the war. Grounds this in the Vietnam, Russian deterrence and Nixon details, and compares the book to Disch's The Genocides, Russ's We Who Are About To and Wolfe's The Wizard Knight. Responds to James Wynn's claim that Watchmen embraces superhero fascism.
  • 2004-12-07 AI soulsProposes that Wolfe's tongueless Apheta and her kind are literalizing the idea of the tongue as evil speech, so their purity is a matter of having no 'bad tongue'. Supports this by citing Roy Lackey's point about metonymy and the Hebrew phrase lashon ha-rah, and by quoting a sermon on the evil tongue from a religious website.
