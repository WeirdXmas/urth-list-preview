  • 1999-02-15 New/Long Sun; Derivation of "talus"Argues that the Long Sun word "talus" derives from Talus, the justice-enforcing demigod in Spenser's Faerie Queene, and that the earlier archive derivation omitted this key source. Grounds the claim in Talus's invincibility, stupidity, and slaughter of a town, which parallel the Long Sun taluses, and also asks whether New Sun and Long Sun are proven to share a universe.
