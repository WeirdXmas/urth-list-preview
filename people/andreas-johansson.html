  • 2015-04-29 Adaptations?Asks whether any adaptations of Wolfe's works exist, such as graphic novels, theatre, short films or a videogame set in Severian's Urth, and imagines a Moebius-style comic or 3D world of Nessus or Viron. Closes with an unrelated question about which Lafferty book to start with.
  • 2014-11-03 Wolfe and the Ancient worldAsks how accurate the claims about ancient Egypt are in the unsigned foreword to Soldier of Sidon, particularly regarding Egyptian and Nubian armies and weaponry. Notes the foreword is credited to G.W. in the Latro in the Mist omnibus but not in Sidon, and adds an aside on reading Wolfe as an atheist and absorbing the list's discussion of Catholicism.
  • 2014-04-30 Urth Digest, Vol 116, Issue 28Reports that the poster's digest mail lands in a Gmail promotions-style folder while other lists go to Forum or spam, speculating that mail-filter arbitrariness explains the lost posts. Also jokes that the rumoured title "Labyrinth in the Library" for a new Wolfe book sounds like a thesis on Borges.
  • 2014-03-13 Urth Digest, Vol 115, Issue 7Recommends Karin Tidbeck's story collection Jagannath and her novel Amatka, then reports starting Peace again and reviews Alastair Reynolds' On the Steel Breeze and Peter F. Hamilton's The Reality Dysfunction. Contrasts the hard SF explanations of those books with the lighter approach the poster admires in Wolfe and Philip K. Dick.
  • 2013-10-11 Urth Digest, Vol 110, Issue 10Proposes that the Whorl's link to our solar system is symbolic, and that Typhon sent it to colonize a planet resembling a pristine Urth. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's idea that the Ring of Glass could be Father Inire's Atropaic Gardens if Urth is Blue, and raises time dilation as an answer to the Whorl's chronology.
  • 2013-10-09 Short Sun notes: RemoraProposes that the green world of the Short Sun books is really Lune, with Blue being Urth after the flood of The Urth of the New Sun, and that Typhon-Pas sent the Whorl beyond Severian's re-creation. Admits the idea has no mechanism yet, noting the Neighbours and inhumi, and rests it on the blue-Earth and green-neighbour pairing and a Typhon-to-Peace name joke.
