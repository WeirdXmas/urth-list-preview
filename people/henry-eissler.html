Their messages (14 with summaries)

  • 2009-04-19 PF as YAAgrees that Pirate Freedom could suit a young audience, citing the Treasure Island echo, and disputes the idea that Chris's choices are plainly bad. Argues his plan to break his celibacy vow is not disturbing since his wife is the woman involved, and that his actions seem contextually appropriate to his character.
  • 2009-04-17 PF as YAArgues that Pirate Freedom is about situational morals: Chris knows his pirate deeds are horrible, yet he takes up his role under Bram Burt as a God-given situation and tries to guide his crew. Grounds this in Chris's reluctant turn to piracy and his later efforts to make his men more devout, and rejects calling the novel Young Adult, unlike The Wizard Knight.
  • 2009-03-27 STFUProposes that the ice cream shop girl in An Evil Guest is also part of the hidden charade surrounding Cassie, and asks why she is so eager to tell Cassie she hates her. Responds to Don Doggett's quoted remark about the cop's profane 'Shut the fuck up' line in the shop scene, which he found jarring and funny.
  • 2008-12-18 AEG clones...send them inProposes that Cassie in An Evil Guest may contain two people, with the banshee spirit from the Washer at the Ford living alongside her, citing the ending where she calls for Wally as proof that the original Cassie survives. Responds to Dave Tallman's observation that the changed names at the end suggest two different people, extending it with the idea that Gideon infused her with the banshee's spirit.
