  • 2007-04-17 Bringin' it back to Wolfe by the long roadArgues that Gandalf, Frodo and Aragorn are Christ-like in the same loose way as Able, Severian and Silk, so Tolkien's world has Christ figures too. Responds to Don Doggett's claim that Tolkien lacks an overtly Christian template, citing Gandalf's return from death, Frodo's sacrifice, and Aragorn's harrowing of hell.
  • 2007-04-05 MemorareArgues that Memorare is a commentary on surface appearance versus deeper truth, tying it to the relationships of March and Kit and March and his ex-wife. Cites Kit spotting the robot in the tomb by its too-perfect body and the asteroid's illusory perfection, replying to Roy C. Lackey on male/female relationships.
  • 2006-11-30 Essential Background Reading for Soldier SeriesArgues that no prior reading is required to make sense of Soldier of Sidon, which is more straightforward than Soldier of Arete, though some Egyptian mythology, geography, the Egyptian Book of the Dead, King Solomon's Mines and the Burton/Speke Nile search would help. Responds to don doggett's list of classical background reading (Iliad, Odyssey, Herodotus, Rome, Graves' Greek Myths, Frank Miller's 300) by respectfully disagreeing with it.
  • 2006-06-15 objective measure of "good"Argues that good writing is objectively real despite disagreement over particular works, using Tom Stoppard's play The Real Thing and its cricket-bat analogy to make the point. Quotes the exchange between Henry and Annie about Brodie's writing, drawing on the play's claim that quality is 'better because it's better' rather than a matter of consensus or convention.
  • 2005-06-18 BOTNS :Sev's DadArgues that Ouen is Severian's father, citing a line from The Citadel of the Autarch in which Ouen admits he can read and write despite being a dull-seeming figure. Offers this textual clue as support for voting Ouen in the poll on who fathered Severian.
  • 2005-03-27 TWK: Mag & Stuff I Don't Understand About DragonsArgues that Setr stopped using women as sailor-bait on Glas because he trapped Mag in the Tower, and that he planned for Art to find her remains later. Agrees with Andrew that Mag is not Art's mother, and answers thalassocrat's Stuff I Don't Understand questions by citing Setr's disguise as Garsceg and Art's failure to defeat Kulili.
  • 2005-03-22 In defense of Watchman, SeverianArgues that Watchman and Severian should not be judged fascist merely because their stories let fascism or a messianic choice succeed, since authors need not endorse their characters' victories. Defends Severian's choice as shaped by others and notes his guilt and sympathy as signs of humanity, while responding to Dan'l's claim that Dune is fascist.
  • 2005-01-13 Ending of The KnightArgues that The Knight and The Wizard Knight are two books in one series rather than self-standing novels, because Able changes from a neophyte before Skai to an insider after it. Supports this with Arthurian comparisons (Parsifal, Lancelot/Arthur), the recurring "just a kid" complaint read as ironic, and the shift to narration outside Able's point of view.
  • 2004-12-06 Thecla/SeverianDisputes the view that Thecla's presence in Severian is merely mechanical memory transfer, arguing instead that her soul is present in him and is dormant until he is weak. Cites the antechamber girl sensing a lady, the Cumaean's ritual awareness, Thecla taking control to confront the Autarch, and the Claw bringing her back, and suggests Tzadkiel relocated her in the final book.
  • 2004-11-30 OT: PoughkeepsieExplains the phrase "That'll play in Poughkeepsie" as a mainstream-appeal saying tied to Poughkeepsie's distance north of New York City, comparing it to Sleepy Hollow's remoteness in Burton's film. Admits the post is off topic and apologizes, noting the poster works there, so it is a social digression rather than a reading of Wolfe.
