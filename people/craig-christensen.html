  • 1998-06-25 ForlesenSummarizes the plot of the novella Forlesen for a reader who has forgotten it: a man wakes with no memory, is hurried through a drab workday by his wife and coworkers, and slowly recovers fragments of his past. Responds to Michael's request for a brief reminder, offering a plot recap, a reading of the name as forlorn, and a comparison to the film Brazil.
  • 1998-06-05 Wolfe's LamarkismAsks Mark Millman for specific passages where Wolfe discusses or defends Lamarckism, distinguishing it from Lysenkoism and mentioning the mice story. Also recommends Loren Eiseley's Darwin's Century and praises Eiseley's essay collections.
