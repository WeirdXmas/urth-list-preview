  • 2000-11-22 Amazon has RTW cover upNotes that the giant on the Amazon cover of Return to the Whorl resembles Gene Wolfe in a photo online, and worries that a metafictional intrusion into the Whorl storyline would be unwelcome. Expresses hope the artist meant it as a tip of the hat to Wolfe and asks what the giant's odd headgear signifies.
  • 2000-11-22 Amazon has RTW cover upAsks who the giant on the Return to the Whorl cover is and whether the small figure in his hand is humanoid, describing a finger and arm he sees and a light-obscured head. Notes that the figure is very hard to make out and that Oreb is the one certain identification on the cover.
  • 2000-08-16 Horn/Silk as inhumuProposes that Horn and Silk are inhumu, arguing that a willing giving of self to the inhumu lets one take them over, which would explain Horn's lack of appetite, his adopting of inhumi, and the Babbie identity switch. Raises timeline problems with the Krait and Sinew episodes and the Silk body-switch in In Green's Jungles, and admits the idea remains tempting despite them.
