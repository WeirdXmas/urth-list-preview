Their messages (3 with summaries)

  • 1997-10-11 Still more physicsArgues that the Long Sun floater's weightless hovering requires full cancellation of the Whorl's rotation, since partial cancellation would only explain the height, not the floating. Supports this with the quoted Calde passages and calculations on centrifugal force, and extends it by noting gravity falls off linearly with distance from the center.
  • 1997-10-07 The Physics and Dimensions of the WhorlWorks through physics calculations to pin down the radius and circumference of the Whorl, using Earth gravity and speed estimates from the floater trips in Nightside the Long Sun and Calde of the Long Sun. Concludes that the floater speeds implied give an unrealistically small Whorl, and that the mantis radius estimate implies speeds too high for the floater, so Wolfe took significant artistic license.
  • 1997-10-06 Blue/Green == Urth/Moon???Asks whether the Blue and Green of the Long Sun books could be Urth and the Moon, recalling that the Moon in The Book of the New Sun was planted with forests, and whether Typhon launched the Whorl to repopulate or retake Urth. Also asks whether the Whorl is a hollowed asteroid and what the term Short Sun signifies, given it names both the prior world and Wolfe's next series.
