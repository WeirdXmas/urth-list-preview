  • 1997-06-25 Digest whorl.v006.n011Proposes that Severian's death and resurrection, especially the Apu Punchau sequence at the end of the New Sun books, bear on whether he is a Christ figure. Argues that Horn's Long Sun narrative, reconstructed after the fact, parallels the Gospels, and that the New Sun's first-person narration is written from the Messiah's point of view, answering Rostrum's criteria.
  • 1997-06-21 Digest whorl.v006.n008Argues that Severian is a Christ figure without being a Christian, citing his healing the sick, raising the dead, and restarting the red sun, and contrasts Silk as a penitent figure closer to Paul. Responds to Alga's objection by defending the Christ-figure reading and noting a snake-in-Garden image in the inhumi and a question about Satanic imagery.
  • 1997-06-17 Digest whorl.v006.n005Proposes that the journeys in the New Sun and Long Sun books begin on false pretenses yet prove worthwhile, citing Vodalus's false coin for Severian and Silk's mistaken pagan gods. Presents Silk as a virtuous pagan, a transition from Latro's world-view to Severian's Christian one, and closes with a frivolous question about Typhon and Piaton's Simpsons parallel.
