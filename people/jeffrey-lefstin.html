Their messages (10 with summaries)

  • 2006-07-30 Urth geochronologyArgues that Urth must be far older than the "perhaps a million years" figure Jeff Wilson quoted, because the planet's interior has solidified and volcanic activity has stopped. Grounds this in Severian's repeated mentions of the lack of volcanism and a geologist's rough sense of cooling timescales, while conceding Wolfe is not a hard SF writer.
  • 2006-07-26 5HC QuestionClarifies that the poster did not mean to define or limit literature, and asks for a word for works rich in layered symbolism. Also notes that the overmonitor in Silhouette, which survives by distributing itself into appliances, recycles the idea of Pas in The Book of the Short Sun.
  • 2006-07-25 5HC questionArgues against the puzzle-based reading that the Short Sun's shapeshifting inhumu are secretly linked to Fifth Head of Cerberus, favoring the view that Wolfe merely reused a conceit. Supports this by citing Wolfe's recycling of the ship in "Silhouette" and the duel in "Tracking Song", and urges focusing on symbolic meaning instead of hidden identities.
  • 2006-05-24 Speaking of Hierodules...Argues that Wolfe misdirects readers when he glosses "hierodule" as "holy slave", since the word means temple prostitute and ties to hieros gamos. Grounds this in a Wikipedia account of hierogamy and reads Severian's union with Apheta as the fertility rite restoring Urth, noting Apheta is a larval hierogrammate.
  • 2006-01-30 Ascians' Thematic PurpposeAsks whether Ascian is meant to evoke Asian, arguing that the Ascians seem to come from North America while the Commonwealth sits in South America. Grounds the question in the 'waist of the world' geography, the Lexicon Urthus discussion of explicit Asian references, and a recollection that Wolfe claimed a classical origin for the word.
  • 2005-08-23 5HC (again)Recalls a line Wolfe wrote, which the poster calls among his most beautiful: the wolf is God's dog, half-mad with hunger and the memory of His hands upon her head. Supports the earlier thread's link between the surname Wolfe, the French word for dog, and the wolf by citing this line as the essay on dogs being tame wolves.
  • 2005-04-20 Abaia and the ClawQuotes the Claw of the Conciliator passage where Idas admits searching Severian's stateroom for the letter from the aquastors and where Severian says she was looking for something else too. Supports the earlier point that Idas was searching for Inire's letter, adding that she sought another hidden item as well.
  • 2005-04-18 Abaia and the ClawProposes that Abaia enlists Severian because Abaia foresees he will wield the Claw, pointing to the dream's altered words AZURE, SAND-GARDEN and TALON and to Idas searching the cabin for the thorn. Grounds this in Severian's remarks at the start of The Claw of the Conciliator and end of The Citadel of the Autarch, and in Apheta's line that the thorn was saturated with his blood.
  • 2005-01-11 urth-urth.net Digest, Vol 5, Issue 16Disputes the claim that political views inserted into a character's mouth were jarring because of readers' liberal leanings, arguing that any modern political debate would be equally out of place. Responds to Crush's quoted post on pro-gun and supply-side remarks by imagining Silk voicing reproductive rights or welfare positions, and says such topical content does little for a novel unless it is allegory.
  • 2005-01-11 urth-urth.net Digest, Vol 5, Issue 15Remarks that the political passages praising supply-side economics and universal gun ownership in Exodus from the Long Sun felt clumsily inserted, leaving the poster unsure whether they were satire or Wolfe's own views. Responds to a thread about Orwell's attack on Chesterton and whether a poster's insult toward Wolfe's religious views was fair, and the poster now assumes the passages reflect Wolfe's own opinions.
