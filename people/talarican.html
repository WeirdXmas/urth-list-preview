  • 2001-09-04 Voyage of the Whorl: Calculations of Thrust, Distance and Peak VelocityCalculates how fast the starcrosser Whorl must accelerate and decelerate under a half-and-half ramjet-style profile to cover the distance to its destination in the Long Sun books within a 550-year Urth time. Argues that the required acceleration of about one fiftieth gee and the peak speed near 0.996c show the ramjet alone cannot fit the textual evidence, and suggests the ship later switched to matter-antimatter propulsion. The argument rests on relativistic formulas, CRC handbook constants and the stated trip times, and is posted as a proposal to the list.
