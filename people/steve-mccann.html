Their messages (8 with summaries)

  • 2009-01-27 Inhuma continuedExpresses agreement with Matthew Groves that vampires traveling through space to reach victims is appealing, arguing that since the story is science fantasy there is no problem with it. Responds directly to Groves's quoted post about Inhuma's vampires, offering only a brief endorsement without textual evidence or detailed reasoning.
  • 2009-01-22 Silk corrupted?Asks whether Silk in Book 4 of The Book of the Long Sun recognizes his own corruption during the trip to the mainframe, and whether this causes his depression. Lists parallel corruptions in the series (Muccor, Blood, Hyacinth, the councilors) as support for a corruption theme. Responds to a thread about Tom Waits and a reply by Adam Thornton, but the question itself is a sincere query about the book.
  • 2009-01-22 Silk corrupted?Asks whether Silk was corrupted by Typhon during the Mainframe trip in The Book of the Long Sun, proposing that Silk realized Typhon's presence in himself and that this drives his near-suicidal moment on the blimp. Grounds the question in Horn's narration growing more distant from Silk's thoughts and in Silk's later state in The Book of the Short Sun.
  • 2009-01-09 Long Sun LaughsConnects Son of Witz's remark about Silk seeing the stars and the real sun to Wolfe's view of fantasy. Quotes Wolfe's Nebula Awards interview, where fantasy says things could be very different just over the hill, and links it.
  • 2008-12-20 Abaia and the undinesShares a link to an artwork by Adriane Fleet, saying it reminded the poster of the Abaia thread about undines. The post reports no argument, and it offers no reading of the books beyond the loose association with the thread.
  • 2008-10-01 a lurker asks a questionProposes that the word "graven" matters only if the alzabo was engineered to carry past lives from one person to the next. Responds to Adam Thornton's claim that the key lies in the holographic memory storage and the analeptic alzabo's transfer of memories by eating brain tissue.
  • 2008-10-01 a lurker asks a questionAsks where a recoiling-from-a-picture citation appears in The Shadow of the Torturer, then reports a passage in The Master of the Curators about a book of graven pictures and suggests the chapter's function is to put Severian's becoming Autarch in play. Grounds the guess in Ultan's account of waiting for his master to die and in the eucharist-like echo of the word chrisos, which he could not find in the OED.
  • 2008-07-30 The Old Pen CaseShares a personal reaction to the scene in The Book of the Short Sun where Horn's death is revealed through Remora and Silk, describing how the reader kept hoping for rescue despite knowing Horn was dead. Responds to Dave Tallman's quoted passage about Seawrack and Nettle by reflecting on Wolfe's habit of revealing events to attentive readers and on whether such a game can produce strong feeling.
