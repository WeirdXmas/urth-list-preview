Their messages (10 with summaries)

  • 1997-06-16 The Next WhorlJokingly proposes that the publisher reissue the first two Latro books with covers featuring half-naked women so they will sell. Responds to a quoted news post reporting that Latro's memory failure is a stumbling block for new readers and that the Soldier books are unlikely to continue.
  • 1997-06-14 Silk's ReligionObserves that Wolfe seems to disparage doctors, citing Kichesippos in Soldier of the Mist, who calls Latro's visions of gods occasional hallucinations. Quotes the exchange where Latro reports his only symptom and Io says gods appear to him, extending the thread's point about Crane's brain-seizure explanation of Silk's experiences.
  • 1997-06-09 Silk loves!Argues that Silk loved Hyacinth as a woman rather than as the embodiment of a principle or goddess, and that Silk would not stop loving her over Horn's view that she was unworthy. Grounds the claim in a comparison to the scene in Shadow where Paelamon gives Severian Terminus Est, and in Silk's defense of the Trigivantes.
  • 1997-06-09 Welcome RostrumArgues that the flash Silk saw is a restart of the Long Sun and that Kypris is a cybercopy of Typhon's courtesan rather than a goddess of love. Supports the claim by contrasting the real Kypris's confidence with Mint's lack of it, and by crediting Mint's own courage and the public endorsement of a goddess for her effectiveness as a general.
  • 1997-06-05 Silk StalkingArgues that the Outsider had abandoned Silk by the end of Exodus from the Long Sun, so Silk could not have survived the next twenty years without the Outsider's active help. Responds to a quoted post about the Short Sun announcement, and describes the Long Sun ending as disturbing because Silk seems betrayed by his beliefs with only Oreb left.
  • 1997-06-05 Digest whorl.v005.n023Corrects the earlier claim that Incus fixes Hammerstone with a gammadion, arguing instead that Quetzal uses an optisynapter on him. Cites the point that Quetzal could repair Maytera Marbles' arm while Silk could not, to show the two healers' tools differ.
  • 1997-05-31 gammadionProposes that the gammadion in The Book of the Long Sun echoes the T of Huxley's Brave New World, where the material becomes divine. Grounds this in the gammadion being joined screwdrivers and in Comely Kypris saying the Chapter parodies Urth's state religion, qualifying an earlier post on its Greek Orthodox link.
  • 1997-05-16 Typhon and PasStates that the poster, a new list member, does not see why Typhon and Pas would be the same figure, since Pas shows some benevolence while Typhon cared only for himself. Responds to an earlier list discussion he missed, offering a contrast of character rather than a textual argument.
  • 1997-05-15 Typhon and PasQuestions the earlier claim that Pas and Typhon are the same figure, arguing that Pas shows some benevolence while Typhon cares only for himself. Says the poster is new to the list and missed the earlier discussion, so responds with a personal disagreement rather than textual evidence.
  • 1997-05-12 Ask Gene WolfeArgues that Gene Wolfe often builds scenes around pretenses that turn out to be true, citing Patera Silk's pretended sabotage of the Trigivante dirigible and the staged dinner for Maytera Mint in The Book of the Long Sun. Proposes asking Wolfe why he is drawn to such situations, and frames the question as part of a round-table interview from the list.
