  • 2005-03-22 Long Sun/Short SunAsks readers which of the Long Sun and Short Sun series they prefer and why, and how these two series relate to The Book of the New Sun. The poster has read the New Sun books but not the later series and is requesting opinions from those who have read them.
  • 2005-03-22 OT:HyperionRemarks that film adaptations of Simmons's Hyperion quartet would be hubristic, given a poor adaptation of Ender's Game, and doubts that Scorsese will direct it. Ends by suggesting Gene Wolfe's novel Peace would make a good strange film; it is an off-topic aside with no argument about Wolfe's text.
  • 2005-03-22 OT:HyperionRiffs on a Hyperion film adaptation, suggesting Scorsese might only executive-produce it, and argues that Wolfe's work is so bookish it resists filming unlike SF written with a movie in mind. Offers no textual evidence; the claim rests on the poster's general sense of which SF translates to screen.
  • 2005-03-22 OT: watchmen on trialArgues that Herbert's Dune and Moore's Watchmen critique messiah figures and the fascist logic of supermen rather than endorse them, and that Wolfe's books share a touch of this material but explore an older tradition instead of glorifying superior people. Responds to zvi's point about space-opera tropes, offering a general account of how power fantasies get read as liberal in post-Tolkien fantasy.
