  • 2010-09-16 Oldest altarArgues that the knife used in Orthodox liturgy, where it is ritually inserted before a formal cutting motion, could point to a eucharistic ceremony in Wolfe's books, since Wolfe has said the state follows a Byzantine pattern.   • 2010-08-16 Standard Wolfean RiddleArgues that if Urth echoes Earth, Urth's fall can be only an echo of the Fall, so the mechanics of salvation remain intact even without a literal Christ on Urth. Responds to Lee Berman's claim about Urth lacking Christ, offering a theological reconciliation of Severian's role with Christian salvation.
  • 2010-08-13 Imputing motivesDenies being a recently removed member that Lee suggested, explaining that his simple quoting comes from posting on an iPhone. Points to his earlier post asking whether An Evil Guest is in the Book of the New Sun universe and proposing that Severian is the Storm God.
  • 2010-08-13 Limited Good ChoicesDisputes the claim that perfect temporal choices are limited, arguing that choosing an apple or a pear can both count as good at a given time. Argues that a Christian God outside time can grant free choice because every choice happens instantaneously from his perspective.
  • 2010-08-12 Limited Good ChoicesDisputes the claim in an archived post that choices become limited as beings move up toward Yesod, calling it unsupported. Argues that neither the text nor Catholic theology supports the idea, and complains that the list does not trim its sources.
  • 2010-08-12 Limited Good ChoicesDisputes a previous poster's claim that choice is limited by good, arguing that mainstream Christian theology sees good as infinite and sin as mere corruption. Grounds this in traditional doctrine and suggests Wolfe has Gnostic and Manichean leanings but does not treat evil as liberating.
  • 2010-08-06 Alien MonstersDisputes the claim that Severian is a rule-breaker, arguing instead that he lives by rules and breaks them only under severe duress, such as Thecla's torture and the execution of his aunt. Grounds this in Severian's guilt over both events and his developing higher loyalties, then jokingly asks to return to the Brunians discussion.
  • 2010-07-21 On Pedophillia and HomosexualityArgues that Baldanders' sex with a modified child cannot be judged by modern categories, since preference was not fixed in classical antiquity and the child's nature is not clearly human and male. Disputes an unnamed poster's claim that the child is not a man, contrasting Greek views with Christian ethics; suggests Baldanders' flirtations with Severian reflect their rough equality.
  • 2010-07-21 On Pedophilia and HomosexualityArgues that Wolfe draws on classical Christian and Greco-Roman views of nature, so that in The Book of the New Sun the Badlanders' interest in men and pederasty is not sharply separated from homosexuality. Grounds this in Severian's rejection of his own attraction to men, his acceptance of fatherhood with Little Severian, and a comparison with Marcus Aurelius.
  • 2010-07-02 AlbiaProposes that Abia, the powerful being Severian encounters, is Severian himself, and that Abia has fought Erebus before. Grounds this in Severian's remarks about the Storm God and the priest's correction, his linking of Abia with the God of the Lake, and Abia's single failed attempt to kill him through a child, which drives him toward the woman who later sways his trial.

Asks whether others skipped Dorcas when summarizing the plot to a grandmother and whether the Increate blocks the hierarchy idea.
