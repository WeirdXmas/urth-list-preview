Their messages (5 with summaries)

  • 2009-02-12 Morgan/WolfeAsks whether a reviewer's comparison of Richard K. Morgan's work with Gene Wolfe's Book of the New Sun is accurate, noting that Morgan is clearly mining Wolfe territory. Says the Kovacs novels did not strike him as Wolfean, while describing Wolfe's blend of gritty realism and remote future fantasy as the subgenre's model.
  • 2007-06-07 Good advice from Alan MooreRecommends that list members take to heart Alan Moore's remark that not everything in Watchmen means much, and notes a misquoted Zappa lyric from Daniel's post. Asks whether Wolfe's readers are more religious in general than typical science fiction readers, linking this to the poster's own Catholic upbringing.
  • 2006-10-20 urth Digest, Vol 26, Issue 23Supports Adam Thornton's claim that chivalry is biology and Hmph's view on children, arguing that sex differences are exaggerated by people's wish to belong to defined roles. Argues chivalry suited a time of needing population but not 6.5 billion people facing mass extinction, and cites Sturgeon's Venus Plus X.
  • 2006-05-14 Wiki draft proposalSuggests naming the proposed wiki "WolfeWiki" or "GeneWolfeWiki" rather than an obscure name, so that people interested in Gene Wolfe can find it more easily. Responds to an earlier remark, which the post quotes, about who else would find an obscure name, with a joking reference to the BTK killer.
  • 2005-06-12 urth-urth.net Digest, Vol 10, Issue 12Asks the list to define "filk" and "filksinging," saying the poster has read science fiction for forty years and attended conventions but still does not know what the term means. The question is a request for a definition of a fan term, not a reading of any Wolfe text, and it follows a digest that quotes an earlier discussion of cthulhu mythos and The Book of the New Sun.
