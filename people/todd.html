  • 2007-12-24 The Tomb of the Unknown SeverianArgues that Severian exists as a single self whose time-walking produces two divergent Urths, with Sev1's judged, fountain-empowered life reshaping the world Sev2 inhabits.   • 2007-12-14 Cumaean???Argues that the star Severian brings as the New Sun cannot be Fomalhaut as described, since Fomalhaut's age was only determined in 1998 and Wolfe would have seen it as a middle-aged A-class star rather than a red giant. Responds to Pedro Pereira's suggestion by noting that a red giant status could have been plausible had Wolfe assumed the star near its end, and that an intelligence in the system offers other explanations.
