  • 2007-12-17 Prior Race On Blue And GreenDisputes Pedro Pereira's idea that Blue and Green are future Earth and Lune or Venus, arguing Typhon would not recolonize a dying sun system. Grounds this in the Whorl's 300-year voyage, SilkHorn's dream travel to Urth, and the Inhumi burning, then offers a rival scenario of solar mass drained by a warp and Typhon stranded.
  • 2007-12-16 Cumaean???Argues that the Cumaeans, the prior race on Blue and Green, left because of the Inhumi and sound nothing like humans, and that Pas would not have recolonized Earth. Responds to Michael Dale's question about how close together two worlds are, reasoning that Pas would only return if he expected the New Sun, which he did not believe in.
  • 2007-12-16 Cumaean???Argues that if Pas wanted to recolonize Earth because the star was being harmed, he must have expected the New Sun, which he did not believe in, so the recolonization would not have happened. Responds to a thread on the Cumaean stars and the Earth-Lune-Venus question by offering a plot-logic argument about Pas's motives and beliefs.
  • 2007-12-16 Cumaean???Argues that the Whorl was built for colonizing another star system rather than Earth or Lune, that Blue and Green lie in a different system, and that the Whorl's power source may have caused the sun's black hole. Also proposes that Typhon withheld FTL technology deliberately, and that Auk was meant to build a terminal for Pas on the colony planet.
  • 2007-12-15 Dying SunProposes that Typhon caused the Sun's decline and black hole by stripping its energy for the Whorl, a speculation the poster admits he cannot source. Grounds it in the energy needs of ships like the Whorl and in Typhon's scientists underestimating the rate of the Sun's diminishment during his rule.
  • 2007-05-03 Severian raising the the uhlan, ZamaProposes that the cyborg Jonas, after a ship crash, took biological parts from the dead crewmate Miles, so Jonas absorbed part of Miles rather than the reverse. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's discussion of Severian's resurrections of Zama and the uhlan, suggesting Severian's interactions with Miles may shape Jonas's behaviour and that the confusion stems from resurrection shock.
  • 2006-11-29 Confussion about Sev/Pas/Silk/Typhon/AbleAsks a series of questions and proposes theories about the parentage of Severian, Silk's genetic origin, the timeline of the Whorl's launch, and Able's dreams in The Wizard Knight. Also argues that Typhon built the Whorl with slower-than-light technology, offering three explanations, and asks why no chems or android bodies appear in the later books. The post is a long list of loose speculation rather than a single argued case, and it names no critical source for its claims.
