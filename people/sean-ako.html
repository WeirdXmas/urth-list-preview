  • 2010-04-28 "TRACKING SONG" animal/human analogsProposes that the animal/human analogs in Tracking Song are engineered organisms made by people of the Great Sleigh, and that the whole planet is artificial.   • 2010-04-20 Cutthroat "identity" from "TRACKING SONG"Proposes that Cutthroat in "Tracking Song" is an anthropologist doing fieldwork among the beast-human tribes, citing his daily logs, his questioning of other groups, and the tape recorder, shaver, knife and firestarter he falls with. Grounds the idea in his amnesia being a fieldwork protocol and in Wolfe's recurring scientist-observers, naming the anthropologists in The Shadow of the Torturer and the astronauts in "Alien Stones".
  • 2010-04-09 Cim Glowing as platypus/human analog(?)Proposes that Cim Glowing in "Tracking Song" is a platypus-human analog, since all the human figures Cutthroat meets are animal-human hybrids. Grounds the claim in her short hair and inexpressive face, the endieva wand resembling a platypus hindspur and flipper, and her lack of breasts, which might fit platypus milk grooves.
