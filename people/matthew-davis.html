Their messages (11 with summaries)

  • 2002-05-07 Seven American NightsConcedes that the poster miscounted the nights in Seven American Nights, having taken a dream of mouldy bread as a break between days. Rereading, the poster finds the bread passage is an interjection during Nadan's journey north in Washington, not a day break.
  • 2002-05-07 Seven American NightsArgues that Nadan's story contains seven American nights, not six, by counting an overlooked first night aboard the ship before he wakes in the hotel. Responds to a question about reconciling the title with the six nights in Nadan's journal, citing the textual markers between the lines "It's his country bleeding to death" and "Here it is evening again".
  • 2002-05-01 A Fish storyArgues that Wolfe's short story "A Fish Story" is about Rab's denial of the truth, especially his refusal to learn whether there is an afterlife, and that evasion cannot stop the truth from returning. Grounds this in the opening line about embarrassment with the truth, the friends' lie that proves true, the meaning of a fish story as a false yarn, and Rab's encounter with his aunt at the hospital.
