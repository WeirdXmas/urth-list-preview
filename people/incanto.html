  • 2018-09-04 Typhon's concessionsAgrees with Marc Aramini that the Typhon scene should not be read as carrying the same theology as Christ's triumph over temptation, and argues Wolfe used Catholic material as a template for a new cosmology rather than a copy. Responds directly to Aramini's point about the scene's theological implications and the Solar Cycle's relation to Christianity.
  • 2018-09-04 Typhon's concessionsAsks whether Typhon's refusals of physical need reflect Christ's control over desire, or a state of being so fixed in divine presence that personal advantage has no pull, and whether Severian shares that status. Responds to Marc Aramini's allegorical reading by saying it remains valid and that Wolfe's Christ-saturated novels are widely accepted.
  • 2018-09-03 Typhon's concessionsAsks why Typhon first demands the claw from Severian and then lets him display it in his own hand, and why Typhon must follow any form at all. Quotes Typhon's line 'It is no more than a form we must follow' to ask whether he acknowledges a higher power to whom obeisance is owed.
  • 2017-10-13 bridge of swords and the seven chained chestProposes that the scenario in the thread cannot fit any ordinary framework and suggests instead a living, non-framework tissue of realms and dimensions, where passageways are hard to trace and identity keeps morphing without being fully defined. Offers this as a loose analogy to our own identities, with no textual passage or specific Wolfe detail cited to support it.
  • 2017-10-13 WK; Arnthor's feastProposes that Morcaine's transformation into her brother's face in the feast scene is a glimpse of her sorcery, with her intercepting a communication meant for Arnthor.
