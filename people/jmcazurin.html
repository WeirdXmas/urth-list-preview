  • 1999-10-30 postmodernism, OTism, continent-ismProposes that Wolfe's name Apu-Punchau, glossed as Head of Day, shares a root with a Tagalog word for leader, and builds a geographic theory that Nessus lies south of the poster's region with the Ascians suggesting Asia. Grounds the idea on the translation and on the reasoning that receded oceans would leave Southeast Asia landlocked, and asks whether South American languages hold a similar word.
