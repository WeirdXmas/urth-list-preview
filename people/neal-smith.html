Their messages (4 with summaries)

  • 1998-06-17 Severian's reticenceArgues that Severian's claim of perfect recall is undercut by his deliberate omissions, which make him seem like a different narrator who adds details later. Attributes the gaps mainly to narrative style, since Severian records only what affects his actions and leaves out emotion and side matters, citing his Dorcas conversation and Adam's remark about the Thecla tryst.
  • 1998-06-17 More 5HC questionsAsks whether the addictive substance used by the Shadow Children in the story "A Story" is linked to the loss of tool use, and whether the abos' inability to use tools is environmental rather than innate. Also asks whether Roy T. is a drunkard or intoxicated on something else, and notes that tool-use arguments about abos and half-abos depend on this.
  • 1998-06-11 Digest urth.v013.n003Argues that the Whorl's natives became spacey and New Agey because the original colonists lost acquired knowledge, and suggests an addictive, possibly hallucinogenic drug shown in "A Story" as another cause. Offers the colonist-inbreeding explanation as a bridge between Alga's and Borski's views, and cites the shadow children's addiction in "A Story" as evidence.
  • 1998-06-09 Fifth head times 2 brothersArgues that the Annese race and humanity in The Fifth Head of Cerberus were once one people, using the middle novella's twin brothers Eastwind and Sandwalker as a metaphor for two separated races. Grounds this in the textual clues already discussed and the parallel of one brother's death leaving the other uncertain of his identity, while admitting the migration's timing is unclear.
