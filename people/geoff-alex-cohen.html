Their messages (6 with summaries)

  • 1999-05-19 Wolfe appearance in _Shadow_Proposes that Gene Wolfe makes a cameo in The Shadow of the Torturer, with the assistant Cyby in the Library chapter standing in for Wolfe himself as Ultan's apprentice. Grounds the claim on Cyby's physical description, said to match Wolfe around 1980, and on the symbolism of an apprentice serving a figure read as Borges.
  • 1997-06-30 Severian's familyProposes that Severian's sister is Merryn, the Cumaean's companion at the end of The Claw of the Conciliator, reasoning that Severian and Severa are twin names and that female children of prisoners go to the witches. Grounds the identification of his mother in Ouen's naming of Katharine at the end of The Citadel of the Autarch, and cites a Katharine the Guild holds.
  • 1997-06-12 Digest urth.v002.n002Reports Wolfe's hesitant answer in a People Online chat about whether the narrator of The Book of the New Sun killed other characters, and suggests that since someone killed them, the real question is who did it. The post recounts the exchange's timing and Wolfe's pauses, leaving the puzzle of the killer open.
  • 1997-05-23 Digest urth.v001.n020Reports recollections of a live chat interview with Wolfe, including his remark that Merryn is almost certainly Severian's sister, the New Sun set 1000 to 2000 years ahead, and the Short Sun set twenty years after the Long Sun. Responds to a request for the transcript by recounting what the poster remembers from the chat.
  • 1997-02-01 New Sun storiesLists additional New Sun short stories beyond those Michael Bowman named, adding "The Cat" set in the House Absolute during Severian's Autarchy and noting its anthology appearance.   • 1996-12-09 The Whorl and Operation ARESArgues that Wolfe's early novel Operation ARES and Exodus from the Long Sun share a plot pattern: a teacher drawn unwillingly into rebellion, and outside invaders who end up ruling. Points to a near-identical speech on gun rights given by Silk and by John, and suggests this may reveal Wolfe's politics.
