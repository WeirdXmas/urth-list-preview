  • 2007-04-16 ot-my mini review of Children of HurinArgues that Tolkien's books are not a different genre from earlier and later fantasy but simply better examples of it, and that Wolfe shows Tolkien is far from the end of fantasy. Answers Nathan Spears's claim that Tolkien founded modern high fantasy by pointing to William Morris, Le Guin and Guy Gavriel Kay, whose stories share Tolkien's structure.
  • 2007-04-16 ot-my mini review of Children of HurinReplies to don doggett's claim that Children of Hurin is indistinguishable from modern epic fantasy, arguing that Tolkien was playing a different game in writing in the tradition of myth and epic romance. Contends that the book is better defined by what it tries to be than by its place among later genre fantasy, and points to the earlier models of William Morris, Lord Dunsany and E. R. Eddison.
