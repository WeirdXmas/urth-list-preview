  • 2005-06-21 posting conventions (was GW and CP)Apologizes for an earlier mistaken claim about top- versus bottom-posting, urging interleaved bottom posting instead, and raises a side question about whether "chastise" and "chaste" share a root. Cites the Random House Unabridged Dictionary's archaic senses of "chastise" meaning to restrain, refine, or purify as the only hint of a connection.
  • 2005-06-16 gene wolfe and cyberpunkAsks that posters spell out abbreviations such as DADoES, GitS and AS rather than assuming readers know earlier thread context. Responds to Dan's post on cyberpunk and anime by pointing out that its unexplained shorthand excludes readers who did not follow the earlier messages.
  • 2005-03-31 Previous universe, was Re: Increate on trialArgues that Severian's adventures could be set in the far future of our own manvantara rather than in a previous universe, since the cycle may contain parallel universes existing simultaneously. Responds to Roy C. Lackey by citing Urth's and Citadel's statements about the Hierogrammates and Yesod, and by questioning what the word manvantara means.
  • 2005-03-31 Increate on trialArgues that Wolfe likes the idea of the Increate-era story set in a vast future past, and that his texts often undercut reasonable conclusions, with the Bang-Gnab cosmology in The Book of the New Sun as the test case. Grounds this in the list's past discussions and in modern cosmology, noting that Brian Greene's 2004 book assumes a steady universe rather than continued expansion, so Wolfe's translated theory may be outdated. Responds to James Wynn's post about the futuristic-novel reading.
  • 2005-03-31 Previous universe, was Re: Increate on trialDisputes the idea that the Sun cycle takes place in a previous universe, arguing that the text outweighs Wolfe's later statements about it. Cites the 'A Note on the Translation' appendix at the end of Shadow, which says the Urth Cycle chronicles the future, in reply to Adam Stephanides, who said he accepts Wolfe's remark because Wolfe made it.
  • 2005-03-11 TestReports that the list archives stop at the end of February, so the poster's suspected mailing problems starting around March 1 could not be checked there.
