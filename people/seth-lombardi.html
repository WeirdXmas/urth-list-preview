  • 2005-10-23 Marcus v. Franzen = Unexpected Wolfe sightingArgues that Able in The Wizard Knight is deliberately portrayed as flawed, ending in a sub-human world with a semi-imaginary girlfriend, and reads this as Wolfe's commentary on the limits of pagan, pre-Christian heroism and morality. Responds to Adam Stephanides's point about Able's portrayal and also comments on Rich's blog critique of sexual weaknesses in the text, saying he would not resort to name-calling.
  • 2005-06-17 Severian's skin colorArgues that Severian's appearance cannot be fixed to any real-world ethnic type, since the continental-physical-type theory would require long isolation, unchanged diet and environment, and the sun has dimmed. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's claim that the generic SFF hero is assumed white by noting that Severian is tall, possibly has exultant blood, and that exultants may not be Urth natives.

The post is brief and adds no textual citation from the novels themselves.
