Their messages (10 with summaries)

  • 2001-03-26 truculent commentsDisputes Blattid's claim that Tzadkiel's ship only has Urthish speakers because it is Star Trek-like, arguing instead that the ship houses different alien races kept in separate sections. Cites Gunnie's explanation to Sev about similar races being placed near each other and hints of differing atmospheres in the ship's far reaches, and adds a light note on Wolfe having written a Star Trek episode. Also asks where to get a copy of the Clute review.
  • 2001-02-10 Digest whorl.v012.n041Works through several open questions about the Long Sun books, arguing that the eye transfer to Pig likely went Pig to glass to Horn/Silk rather than directly. Raises whether Severian is a dark son of the royal family buried in Scylla's mausoleum, and asks whether Seawrack and Silk's link is explained and why the farmer turned hostile.
  • 2001-02-06 RTTW (SPOILERS)Asks for an explanation of the final Remora scene in The Return to the Whorl, especially the Hyacinth passage, and then collects others' readings. Argues that the 'It' is the azoth and that Horn's and Silk's memories are mixing, so Nettle may have sailed on to Pajorocou; also suggests Severian takes Horn/Silk's spirit for Malrubius' ghost.
  • 2000-09-03 Why Sinew isn't KraitArgues that Sinew and Krait in The Book of the Short Sun are not the same person, and that the narrator Horn is deluded or misleading about them, citing page-by-page evidence. Responds to Dennis's idea that Sinew and Krait are one person, by listing passages from On Blue's Waters where the two seem to be separate, such as Sinew using Horn's needler and Sinew staying behind with Seawrack.
  • 2000-08-20 time framesArgues that the dream-transfer scenes in In Green's Jungles may project into the future, so that the Green village events fall about two and a half years after Sinew's defection and the Citadel scene of The Book of the New Sun. Grounds this on the timing of Sinew's defection and the claim that Sinew has a three-year-old son by Bala, and admits the reasoning is tentative.
  • 2000-08-15 Digest urth.v029.n047Gives a technical tip about sending list messages as plain text in Outlook Express to avoid formatting problems. Responds to a poster named Marc who was having trouble with his messages, with no discussion of Wolfe's fiction.
  • 2000-08-08 Digest whorl.v011.n011Proposes that in On Blue's Waters and In Green's Jungles the narrator is neither Silk nor Horn in body but a spirit split between them, occupying a third unknown body. Grounds this in spirit-logic from Oreb and the inhumi, the narrator's unrecognized body, and how Auk and Colonel Abanja recognize him only during the dream shift to Green.
  • 2000-08-05 Digest urth.v029.n039Informs readers that a website is taking pre-orders for a one-volume paperback edition of The Book of the New Sun, due later in the year. Notes that a review posted on the site has been cut up and is largely taken out of context.
  • 2000-08-04 Blue not urth.......? (IGJ spoilers)Asks whether the Blue-is-Urth, Green-is-Lune theory for the Long Sun books has been abandoned, citing the red sun chapter and Mantis's moon phase. Also floats the idea that the Mother in In Green's Jungles is Typhon's daughter Scylla, and speculates on the inhumi secret, prompted by a post from Robert.
  • 2000-07-30 Digest whorl.v011.n002Asks why Horn's self-loathing in Book of the Short Sun's first volume, On Blue's Waters, vanishes by In Green's Jungles, given the short time gap between them. Proposes the mysterious forest encounter at the end of On Blue's Waters, where Horn is comforted in the dark, as a possible cause.
