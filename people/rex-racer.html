Their messages (8 with summaries)

  • 2006-05-06 Ship CitadelArgues that the Citadel is made of several separate landers converted into towers rather than one ship with other ships attached, disagreeing with b sharp's reading of the hulks passage. Grounds this on the wording of the supporting passages, which describe individual landers at rest at a spaceport, and on the idea of a collection of towers and walls.
  • 2006-05-06 Eyes of TheclaArgues that "forebearance" with an e is simply a variant spelling of "forbearance" with no link to parentage or forebears, so reading a hidden meaning into it is overreach. Supports the claim by citing common usage, several dictionaries, and the poster's own business-letter use of the word, while rejecting the claim that it is an old-fashioned correspondence usage.
  • 2006-05-05 Eyes of TheclaDisputes the claim that the spelling 'forebearance' in a Chapter VI passage of Shadow is a typo, arguing that the e-variant is an accepted form of 'forbearance'. Responds to b sharp's clue by citing the poster's letter-writing habits and how the spelling seems more serious, as with grey versus gray.
  • 2006-05-05 HethorArgues that the Whorl was built in orbit rather than on the ground, citing Mamelta's talk with Silk about landers as textual evidence and noting the energy cost of lowering a hollowed asteroid. Also suggests the Wall around Nessus is probably just a wall, cautioning against reading too much into ordinary details in Wolfe.
  • 2006-05-05 HethorArgues that the Citadel of the Autarch is a spaceport whose towers are old landers and spaceships repurposed and built over, not one giant ship. Responds to Macronaut's question about textual support by recalling the Book of the New Sun's descriptions of the towers and noting nothing in the books suggests the whole Citadel is a ship.
  • 2006-04-26 Severian's miraculous eclipseArgues that the eclipse Severian witnesses at Apu Punchau marks the approach of the New Sun's white fountain toward Urth, not the insertion of a black hole, because the affirmation of life fits the Christian symbolism better. Responds to Jeff Wilson's quoted post by agreeing with and extending Rex Racer's earlier point that redemption precedes the need for it, while rejecting the idea of punishment sown before the crime.
  • 2006-04-26 Severian's miraculous eclipseDisputes the idea that the black hole's punishment was planted before Typhon's offense, arguing that the redemption of Urth coming first makes sense but punishment should follow the offense under the Monarch Typhon. Grounds this in Catholic notions of wrong and punishment, Severian's name Head of Dawn, and the hopeful tone of the Apu-Punyi section; also argues Severian's Triskele timing error is an intentional lie, not Wolfe's nod.
  • 2006-04-26 Severian's miraculous eclipseProposes that a black hole was inserted into the Sun as punishment under Typhon, and that Tzadkiel's ship caused Severian's eclipse, linking this to the New Sun's approach. Argues from the Whorl's calendar and Silk's phrase "many centuries" at Nessus that roughly 3000-4000 years passed, citing time dilation from the Whorl's flight.
