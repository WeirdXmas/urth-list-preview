Responds to Kevin Maroney's report of being surprised by the reveal, saying the poster doubts Wolfe would have switched narrators merely to launch a new series.
  • 1998-12-14 Horn the NarratorAsks whether readers missed clues that the narrator of the Long Sun series would be Horn rather than Silk, questioning how the story moves from Silk's epiphany to Horn narrating. Responds to Kevin Maroney's remark about being surprised to find Horn was the narrator, and says the poster doubts Wolfe would have switched narrators merely to launch a new series.
  • 1998-04-09 Severian's womenAsks whether Severian truly loves Dorcas and observes that he measures every woman he meets against Agia. Responds to Tony's remark on Severian's temptation in the wilderness, moving the thread toward his relationships with women.
  • 1998-04-07 Severian's HumanityAsks whether anyone has discussed Severian's emotional, inner journey in The Book of the New Sun and when he finds his humanity. Traces his development from caring for Triskele through his cruelty at Saltus and toward Dorcas, Jolenta and Cyriaca, and treats his wandering in the mountains after Typhon kills little Severian as a turning point.
  • 1998-04-06 Severian's EmotionsArgues that Thecla lives on inside Severian and that some of his memories, such as the painful visit to the whorehouse, are really Thecla's, since women feel pain in first sex and men do not. Supports this with the point that Severian had no need to return to the brothel because he had his trysts with Thecla in her cell.
  • 1998-01-06 Mother, brother and sisterAsks for the reasoning behind the claim that Katherine is Severian's mother and Merryn his sister, challenging it on textual grounds. Points to Severian's description of Katherine's face as a pool reflecting the beholder and to the Feast of Holy Katherine observed for over 300 years, arguing these conflict with that identification.
