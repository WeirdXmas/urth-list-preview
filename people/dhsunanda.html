  • 1999-09-04 Zoroastrianism? Buddhism?Argues that Wolfe quotes a Buddhist text fairly straight while the Ascians use its teaching to enforce mindless conformity, making the book a parody of the Ascian use rather than of Buddhism itself. Grounds this in the idea that memorised texts replace acting on their spirit, and points to a remark in The Shadow of the Torturer about concepts being weaker than the words that bind them, plus a recurring tradition-versus-experience theme across the Urth books.
  • 1999-09-03 Zoroastrianism? Buddhism?Argues that the Ascian's speech in the quoted passage parodies Buddhist texts rather than only Communist ones, identifying its question and four causes of disagreement with a Buddhist source. Grounds this on the Buddha's answer to King Ajatasattu about the Vajjians' state and the traditional four speech precepts, which the poster says the Ascian's list rearranges.
