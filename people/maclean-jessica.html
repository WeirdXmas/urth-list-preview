Cites the scene atop Apu Punchau's building with the Cumean and Merryn, and wonders whether Wolfe deliberately kept Merryn's sibling relation vague to preserve mystery for readers.
  • 2007-07-05 Madame Serpentina's computer code- Free Live FreeAsks whether anyone has decoded the code Madame Serpentina enters in Free Live Free, reporting a claimed Rembrandt Faust source and an anagram of ADAM ETMERADAG and Tetragram Graal Satan. Proposes that Serpentina is a Faust figure, likely the redeemed Goethe version rather than Marlowe's doomed one, based on the source and her ending.

  • 2007-05-07 5HOC - no. 5, Marsch, Eastwind and SandwalkerArgues that Marsch is either Sandwalker or Eastwind, and that Maitre may be a clone and possibly the officer Marsch who reads the notes. Grounds the case in the cat bite, Marsch's clumsiness with tools, the lone survivor of the trip, and Marsch's written story, and raises who killed Maitre.
  • 2007-04-23 Severian raising the the uhlan, ZamaProposes that Severian's resurrections recreate the dead person in his own mind, so Zama and the uhlan know little of themselves while Miles carries Jonas in. Also argues that the title "Eschatology and Genesis" in Talos's play suggests the Bible might run Revelation before Genesis in a cycle, and that Wolfe's mixed myths may be versions of one event. Grounds these in the resurrection scenes and the Bible's order of books.
