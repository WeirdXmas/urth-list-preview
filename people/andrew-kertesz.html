  • 2005-06-18 Severian's skin colorProposes that the common people of Severian's far-future world are somewhat Neanderthal-like, citing Wolfe's references to high foreheads and thick brow-bones. Answers Seth Lombardi's claim about continental physical types by offering a different textual impression, while admitting uncertainty about the recollection.
  • 2005-04-06 the EpitomeArgues that the Hierogrammates could be wise and just while working against the Increate's will, since Melito's angel admits it can only guess that will and all beings are infinitely far from it. Grounds this in the exchange of places between Hierogrammates and humans, and claims the Book of the New Sun is more about personal redemption and discovery than about biblical floods and scourges.
  • 2005-04-05 TEOTNSProposes that the ship in The Book of the New Sun moves backward through time because faster-than-light travel incurs a relativistic time debt, so it arrives before departing. Suggests Wolfe may have framed the book as found, like Eco's opening in The Name of the Rose, and asks whether Yesod exists apart from Briah's time continuum.
  • 2005-04-03 Back, after the Urth of the New Sun (2nd attepmt)Reports finishing The Urth of the New Sun and reflects that its added events enriched the series while making it harder to decipher. Withdraws an Agilus theory after finding Ossipago is a machine, not a cacogen, and floats alternatives: Agilus as Abaia's child or a sorcerer, and a far-fetched idea that Agilus is Severian on a time journey.
  • 2005-03-14 Newbie - a theory and some questions.Proposes that Agilus belongs to a different race of Cacogen whose masks are layered inversely to the Heirodules, with an inhuman mask beneath a human one. Cites the Sword of the Lictor passage on Ossipago, Barbatus and Famulimus, then asks whether the Gyoll boatman is Jonas, who Erebus is, and whether Abia is alien.
