  • 2003-07-29 wright's bookQuestions whether Peter Wright's demythologizing reading of Wolfe's Urth and Severian, from his God-games articles and forthcoming book, is consistent with its own deconstructive method. Argues that removing one layer of mythic metaphor only substitutes Wright's own mythology, citing Master Ash's remark on religion and science.
  • 2003-06-25 How would you rate Wolfe as a short story writer?Argues that Wolfe's apparent weakness in short stories may be inevitable, since the short form is less suited to the grand-scale questions and games Wolfe favors than the multi-volume novel. Responds to Anthony's earlier view, quoted in the post, that Wolfe's stories fall short of his novels, and notes the poster has read few of the stories, citing Island of Doctor Death and Wolfe's own remarks on big projects.
  • 2003-06-19 >>On Encompassing the Universe<<Answers a newcomer's request for an online copy of the paper "On Encompassing the Universe" by saying it is probably the Larry McAffery interview, published in Science Fiction Studies in November 1988, and that it is not online. Notes that the poster has not yet read the Latro books and is following general Wolfe discussion instead.
  • 2003-06-13 Urthian Society RepostAdvises a newcomer that Urthian society's history cannot be summarized briefly and should be pieced together by continuing through the Long Sun books and reading Wolfe's posthistory. Recommends Castle of the Otter, the Lexicon Urthus and the list archives, then offers a short sketch of Urth as a world where civilization simply waits until resources run out.
