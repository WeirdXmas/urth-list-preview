  • 2000-04-27 Little, BigArgues that Little, Big's Smokey is a saint and a Tam Lin figure caught in a fairy spell, with the fairies using him to father their children and manipulating the Drinkwater clan. Responds to an earlier poster who called the novel a cautionary tale and a clever plot, rejecting that reading and citing Gnostic themes, the Victorian fairy tradition and American Gothic as context.
