  • 2012-04-15 Science Fiction and Fantasy QuestionArgues that the spaceship in The Book of the New Sun and the Whorl are one vehicle that transformed over time, from a lowly Earth vessel into a self-sacrificing higher being, and suggests the ship in Silhouette may be a stage of that development. Responds to Sergei Soloviev's objection that the ship is angelic and unlike a sailed craft, citing other references to The Ship and to people meeting earlier versions of themselves aboard.
  • 2012-04-15 Science Fiction and Fantasy QuestionArgues that the Whorl in The Book of the Long Sun could be converted and used as a module of the Ship, since its interior descriptions do not match the Ship's symmetric decks and sails. Replies to Nathan C. Tresch's question about whether the Whorl is the Universal Transport in the Solar Cycle, offering a reconciling possibility rather than settling the matter.
  • 2012-04-15 Science Fiction and Fantasy QuestionArgues that the Whorl could be an early stage of the Ship from Wolfe's Solar Cycle, answering Gerry Quinn's objection that the Whorl is a hollowed asteroid with a limited lifespan. Offers as grounds that the Ship has symmetric decks and sails, that interior descriptions differ, and that the Whorl may have left for repairs and transformation.
