Their messages (7 with summaries)

  • 1999-05-09 Brain backOffers a list of Vironese criminal cant from Short Sun words, matching each term to senses in a 1998 slang dictionary and the OED, such as 'brick', 'buy him rope', 'card in a cart' and 'shook'. Builds on Mantis's booklet LS-2 and suggests the glossary might feed into a later reference work, with the Short Sun book still forthcoming.
  • 1998-11-23 Marble ArchReports that Marble Arch was designed by John Nash and built in 1828 as a monument to Nelson before moving to Hyde Park, and that Wellington Arch by Decimus Burton carries a copy of the Parthenon frieze. Responds to Mantis's search for a Latin inscription by admitting no inscription turned up online and suggesting a Greek one, with a London reader checking in person.
  • 1998-05-16 Another pip of the scavyReports on a glossary of Short Sun slang from the booklet LS-2, Languages of the Short Sun Whorl, checking each term against Partridge's slang dictionary. Works through terms such as "card in a cart," "dog's right," "nanny nipper," "pip of the scavy" and "scrape out," asking the list for ideas on unresolved ones.
  • 1998-05-09 Eloquent profanityOffers a long list of glosses for slang and Cant terms in Michael's draft booklet LS-2 on the languages of The Book of the Long Sun, such as 'bit', 'beggar's root', 'lay' and 'flue', drawing on British usage and dictionaries. Grounds each gloss in the usage in the text with volume and page references and in Concise Oxford and Shorter Oxford definitions, and offers corrections where Michael's sources seem off.
  • 1998-05-09 Pennington coversReports that Wolfe called Bruce Pennington's Shadow cover his finest jacket art and that it inspired a passage in The Urth of the New Sun where Severian strides in a billowing cloak. Cites Wolfe's confirmation of the link and points readers to a Pennington art collection.
  • 1997-11-25 Mystery Play?Argues that the play-within-the-novel in The Book of the New Sun is unfinished, that its final scene with the New Sun and Urth's rebirth has not been shown, and that the work recalls a medieval Mystery Play. Grounds this in the dramatis personae order, Severian's remark that the scene was meant to end rather than the play, and the morality-play style introduction by Talos.
  • 1997-10-25 Lilies, quits and ballsIntroduces a new reader who discusses cant and rhyming slang in the Long Sun books, such as 'lily' and 'quits', and asks whether the ball game in which Silk receives enlightenment is basketball or something like Aztec or Maya ball games. He also recommends Darrell Schweitzer's The Mask of the Sorcerer and asks how to find a Soldier of the Mist audiobook and a comic adaptation. He cites Doug Eigsti's earlier post and Michael's booklets as his entry points.
