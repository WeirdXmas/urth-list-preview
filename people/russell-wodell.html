Responds to James Wynn's point that Herodotus, not Wolfe, records Demeter striking Latro, and asks whether Wolfe has hinted at a fourth novel.

  • 2008-07-08 Wolfe and NoahQuestions whether the flood ending of The Urth of the New Sun, in which the New Sun melts Urth's glaciers and Urth is washed away, is a strategic win for Abaia and the undines over Erebus.
