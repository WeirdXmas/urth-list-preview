  • 2015-04-28 Pirate Freedom: Is Chris Blackbeard?Proposes that Chris, the narrator of Pirate Freedom, is Blackbeard, who returns to the past after the book's events and lives out a pirate life under the name Edward Teach. Grounds this on a supposed 1717 death date fitting Blackbeard's death, the glossary entry naming Edward Teach as a possible misdirection, Blackbeard's minimal-killing style, and Wolfe's habit of weaving history with fiction as in The Devil in a Forest, while admitting weak evidence. Responds to Robert Pirkola, who argued Chris cannot be Blackbeard because the book's late 1670s setting predates Teach's birth around 1680.
