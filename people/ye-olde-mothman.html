  • 1998-10-28 Julian Jaynes' Bicameral BrainAsks whether Julian Jaynes's bicameral-mind theory has been discussed in relation to Soldier of the Mist, since the protagonist hallucinates gods and lacks self-consciousness. Grounds the question in Jaynes's claim that pre-500 B.C. internal voices were attributed to gods, citing the Iliad's speaking gods heard only by heroes.
  • 1998-06-17 Re. Severian' reticenceArgues that Severian's delayed disclosures, such as his concealed sexual relationship with Thecla, are best understood as narrative effect rather than hidden motive. Responds to a quoted post asking what explains Severian's suppression of Thecla, suggesting late revelations feel weightier, as if confided.
  • 1998-05-15 Re. Last SeinfeldArgues that the final Seinfeld episode's jail-cell ending recalls Sartre's No Exit and its line 'Hell is other people', rather than Severian's examination before his past. Responds to an earlier post that had noted the Urth examination scene and expected White Fountain references, offering a different literary parallel for the same ending.
  • 1998-03-28 Key to the UniverseAsks whether a passage in The Citadel of the Autarch, where Severian recounts Master Malrubius' account of the universe's cyclical nature, has been discussed, noting that its first three paragraphs repeat on the next page before veering off. Wonders whether this is a typo or deliberate inconsistency by Wolfe, and says the poster is new to the list.
