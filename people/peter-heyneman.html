  • 2007-04-10 The SoundtrackImagines The Book of the Long Sun as a television mini-series with an offbeat soundtrack of electronic and experimental artists, arguing it suits adaptation because it relies less on narrator ambiguity and has fight scenes and a love story. Responds to Daniel D Jones's reply to Mark Lewin's claim that no Wolfe book would make a good film, playfully treating the question as a joke.
  • 2006-10-16 The Death of Doctor Island MovieArgues that Wolfe's short stories from that era, such as The Death of Doctor Island, translate better to film than The Wizard Knight, whose shifting narration and withheld detail would become a dry sword-and-sorcery tale on screen. Responds to Kurt Schwind's question whether the stories were more accessible than The Wizard Knight, agreeing that the Wizard Knight suits teen readers.
  • 2006-10-16 The Death of Doctor Island MovieWelcomes news of a short film adaptation of Wolfe's story The Death of Doctor Island and suggests the story and its related works would make a strong set of short films.
