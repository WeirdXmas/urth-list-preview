Their messages (7 with summaries)

  • 2010-06-19 Is Agia a Robot?Proposes that Merryn, not Agia, could be Hethor's missing paracoitae, citing Severian's descriptions of her as an ivory mask and a black-clad doll that reveal no life beneath. Grounds the idea in the Witches' Tower scene where Merryn and the Cumaean's true forms are revealed, and in the tone of Severian's descriptions.
  • 2010-01-13 Severians Later Appearance (Spoilers)Proposes that in the Short Sun, Silk or Horn is stopped from contacting Severian, suggesting the Mother figure or Sea Wrack intervenes, and that an earlier Severian iteration was shaped by the Outsider to produce the later Severian. Responds to Dave Tallman's claim that Severian is an earlier iteration of himself, rejecting the idea that Silk's enlightenment is needed for the New Sun plot.
  • 2010-01-12 Severians Later Appearance (Spoilers)Argues that the Severian met by Silkhorn in The Book of the Short Sun cannot be the same iteration as the New Sun narrator, since the New Sun never mentions the meeting. Responds to Jerry Friedman's criticism of the astral-projection encounter, citing Severian's claimed perfect memory and Merryn's line 'He is my brother' as evidence of misdirection.
  • 2010-01-12 Severians Later Appearance (Spoilers)Asks why Wolfe chose to bring Severian into the Short Sun books and argues that the appearance contradicts the New Sun text, rejecting the idea that Wolfe simply forgot to mention it. Responds to brunians, who claimed Wolfe had not planned a Short Sun when writing the New Sun and asked whether the Short Sun figure is meant to be Severian.
  • 2010-01-12 Severians Later Appearance (Spoilers)Asks why Wolfe included Severian in the Short Sun books and whether this is the New Sun Severian, arguing that his silence about Silk and Horn and his failure to mention Merryn seem inconsistent. Requests theories that would make the appearance feasible, even if Severian simply omits Silk and Horn.
  • 2010-01-11 Babbie, a Destrier?Argues that the Destrier and Babbie the Shear Bear-like creature in the Long Sun books are not the same animal, contrary to Tom Hopkins's suggestion that Babbie is a Destrier. Quotes the Appendix to The Claw of the Conciliator, where Wolfe says he hesitated to call destriers horses and that they are swifter and more enduring than known animals, suggesting they are genetically modified horses rather than eight-legged hus.
  • 2010-01-10 Babbie, a Destrier?Proposes that Babbie, the Long Sun creature, is a Destrier from The Book of the New Sun or is related to one, citing shared traits such as tusks or long teeth, red eyes, and claws called hooves. Grounds this in Horn's description of Babbie's claws, while raising the objection that a Hus has eight legs and asking why the creatures are so similar.
