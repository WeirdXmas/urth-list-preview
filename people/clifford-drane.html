Their messages (12 with summaries)

  • 1999-12-11 Tracking Song haunts meProposes that "The Tracking Song" is an origin myth of an aboriginal people, narrated from inside a world that still lives in myth, with magic not reduced to science. Grounds this in Mailer's Ancient Evenings and the animated Watership Down prelude, which tell myth as real, and dismisses American Indian parallels as a red herring.
  • 1999-11-09 the points of it allArgues that the storytelling contest in The Book of the New Sun is cut short by the attack, so chance or fate, not the characters' investment, decides the outcome. Responds to Cliff Judge's claim that Severian's promise to record the four stories grants immortality, agreeing the two readings coexist while noting the series itself is storytelling and that Severian, an executioner, would have judged the contest.
  • 1999-11-08 the point of it allArgues that the storytelling contest in the Urth books has no ending, and that this lack of resolution is the books' central point, with life's meaning and riddles never fully grasped. Disputes Daniel's view that all four embedded stories are worthy, instead citing the absence of any happy ending and the contest's lack of end.
  • 1999-06-24 Eyeflash - the Land of Earth_OzProposes that the Oz world in Eyeflash is an alternate reality Little Tib can visit, with Tib as a psychic ruler of Oz whose future self pulls Oz characters into the story. Answers William's questions about setting, the parents and the blinding, and rejects William's idea that Tib draws on his father's subconscious, arguing the father is too peripheral to explain the Oz figures.
  • 1999-06-16 Eyeflash/SugarlandArgues that the Eyeflash and Sugarland stories are linked in ways that need explaining, offering two possibilities for the Oz references: Oz as a real alternate reality reached through Little Tib, or spirits taking Oz characters' forms. Claims Sugarland is both heaven and a real place near Houston, citing the Houston experiments on the children and nearby train tracks that he thinks run to Sugar Land.
  • 1999-06-14 searchable archives/Eyeflash MiraclesAsks whether a searchable archive exists and requests immediate reactions to the story The Eyeflash Miracles. Notes that the characters' ending trip to Sugarland matches a real town near Houston where the poster lives, and suggests Wolfe meant that Sugarland, citing its sugar refineries.
  • 1999-04-13 Sev's geneologyAsks for a narrative explanation of a proposed family tree in which Severian fathers Catherine, and whether Dorcas or an unknown woman is Catherine's mother. Responds to a quoted claim that Charon is Severian's paternal grandfather and Severian is his own maternal grandfather, saying the poster needs proof before accepting it.
  • 1999-04-07 DorcasArgues that Dorcas may be dead or in stasis rather than alive, using Charon's remark that the water pickles the bodies, the popping eyes, and the weighted, glued corpses to suggest she was drowned or dumped by Charon. Also notes Severian grasping the sword and Dorcas's hand together as a possible Lady of the Lake parallel, and a friend's observation that Dorcas follows Severian like a dog, paralleling Triskele.
  • 1999-04-07 Dorcas: 'Buried' dead or alive?Proposes that Dorcas was not truly dead but in stasis, her eyes opening as she sank, preserved by the water for forty years until Severian's touch woke her. Also asks whether Dorcas has been read as a parallel to the Lady of the Lake, citing Severian grasping her hand and the sword together.
  • 1999-04-01 TBOTNS ComicsInforms readers that comic adaptations of The Shadow of the Torturer are for sale on eBay at a low price, and asks whether anyone owns them and can recommend them. Says the poster will not bid because he expects the adaptation to be abridged and summarized, with material omitted.
  • 1999-03-19 Greetings and a thoughtIntroduces the poster, a returning reader of The Book of the New Sun, and argues that Wolfe's mythmaking devices, names and places mentioned in passing, carry real substance unlike Tolkien's. Grounds this in the contrast with a critic's complaint about The Lord of the Rings and asks for the list's perspective on it.
  • 1999-03-19 Greetings and a thoughtIntroduces the poster and then argues that Wolfe, like Tolkien, uses names and places mentioned only in passing, but in The Book of the New Sun these devices carry real substance that can be researched and debated. Grounds the claim in a comparison with an unnamed critic's objection to Tolkien's mythmaking devices.
