  • 2010-01-15 Severians Later Appearance (Spoilers)Rejects Tom Hopkins's theory that the Rajan, time-traveling through Viron, fathered Hyacinth, calling the idea too squicky to contemplate. Objects that the theory would make Silk/Horn the father of the woman he married, and that his sex-changing and selling of his son Hyacinthus would be horrific parenting, incompatible with the enlightened Silk.
  • 2009-11-24 AEG againSummarizes the long-running Alien Evil Guest discussion by listing eight speculative readings of Cassie's role, including Margaret as a time-travelling Cassie, werewolves, and multiple Reis copies. Argues that Reis planted the gold to lure the Navy, and that the storm did not turn off after the sacrifice but died down only when bat-men carried Cassie away.
  • 2009-09-16 Cassie as the Ambassador's wifeProposes a step-by-step reconstruction of Cassie's time travel in Wolfe's Sun Times novel, having her return to Earth to marry Bill Reis, bear Rian, and later loop back as Margaret. Grounds the sequence in the book's ages and the picture on page 301, and reads the loops as a self-repeating cycle that Cassie herself cannot break.
  • 2009-09-15 Cassie as the Ambassador's wifeProposes that Cassie is the Ambassador's wife, arguing that Madame Pavlatos is Cassie's older self who time-travelled to Woldercan to rescue her younger self and recreate a marriage. Grounds this in the coincidence of her arrival, Rian's sixteen-year age, Chase possibly recognizing Cassie, and Margaret's hidden wealth, while replying to Dave/Charles's earlier post.
  • 2009-09-14 Wolfe at Dragon*Con (AEG)Argues that Cassie in An Evil Guest is not meant to find Bill Reis alive on Woldercan in the present, and offers two explanations: she journeys to the past to become the ambassador's wife, or she goes to Woldercan to change the past and save Bill's life. Supports the second by linking it to his earlier theory that Margaret Briggs is future Cassie.
