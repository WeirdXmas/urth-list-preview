  • 2006-09-22 Wolfe being clear on 5HoCArgues that art draws on subconscious currents of experience that the artist never consciously controls, so readers' interpretations deserve equal weight with the author's intent. Responds to Nathan Spears's quoted claim, drawn from an essay, that conscious control cannot account for a complex creative work, and points him to Jung's essay.
  • 2006-09-21 Wolfe being clear on 5HoCArgues against the view that an author's own account of a work should outweigh readers' interpretations, recommending Jung's essay on analytical psychology and poetry. Grounds this in the claim that the writer is a conduit for universal and cultural experience and cannot consciously control every dimension of a complex work, which responds to Nathan Spears's claim that interpretations exceeding authorial intent are diversionary.
