Their messages (7 with summaries)

  • 2010-12-19 Gene Wolfe is for SocialistsArgues that a blog's claim that Gene Wolfe is a socialist writer is wishful thinking, since The Fifth Head of Cerberus is postcolonial but its colonizer-native identities are too vague to support a socialist purpose. Adds that if VRT is a despicable figure, as Borski reads him, the political reading becomes even more muddled, and credits the author with missing the identity difficulties.
  • 2009-03-23 BSG SpoilerArgues that the narrative problems in the Battlestar Galactica series come from writer oversight by RDM, not hidden design like Wolfe's, citing the Temple of the Five and the lack of connection between Earth and the 13th tribe. Responds to John Watkins's defence of Cavil's suicide and Starbuck's mystery by calling such elaborate explanations tenuous fanboy searching.
  • 2009-03-22 BSG SpoilerJokingly suggests that the head angels in Battlestar Galactica might be inter-dimensional insects, a quip riding on a quoted exchange about Chris P.'s more complex Wolfe-style reading. The post is social banter with mostly email header clutter; it offers no argument, evidence, or textual grounds.
  • 2009-03-22 BSG SpoilerArgues that the surviving humans would have a harder time farming on Earth than on New Caprica, and that a dispersed population would not build a civilised city-state, so the finale's premise is implausible. Replies to Jeff Wilson's point about subsistence farming and seed stock, and says the plot conclusion seems a stretch by the writers despite liking the character arcs.
  • 2009-03-22 BSG SpoilerArgues that the survivors of the Battlestar Galactica fleet would have faced a grim existence had they scattered as subsistence hunter-gatherers rather than founding a settlement. Responds to Jeff Wilson's claim that settling would have been more than splitting up, pointing to lost seed stock, no shared farming skills, no physical capital, and disease.
  • 2009-03-22 BSG SpoilerCritiques the Battlestar Galactica finale as having strong character moments but a weak plot, mocking Cavil's irreligious behaviour and arguing a settlement would have been more efficient than splitting up. Replies to Jeff Wilson's complaint about the ending; the post is off-topic for Wolfe and does not discuss his fiction.
  • 2009-03-22 BSG SpoilerCriticizes the Battlestar Galactica finale as a lazy, incoherent ending that relies on divine intervention, magic and a rushed split of the fleet. Responds to Jeff Wilson's satisfaction with the answers by listing plot holes such as Kara's coordinates and praising Babylon 5 and Carnivale for better planning.
