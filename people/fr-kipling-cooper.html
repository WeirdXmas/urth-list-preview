  • 2002-11-08 TBotNS - Pinakotheken solved!Reports the etymology and usage of the word pinakothek, citing an 1911 encyclopaedia entry to argue that the plural pinakotheken could denote many picture-gallery chambers off one great hallway in the Book of the New Sun. Supports the earlier questioner's suggestion that the term means separate rooms rather than a single gallery, grounding this in Greek, Roman and Munich usage.
