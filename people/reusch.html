  • 1997-04-16 let it be, let it beProposes widening discussion beyond the heavily debated TBoTNS to obscure works such as Castleview and Peace, and asks what readers are missing in them.   • 1997-04-13 Lost in SpaceQuestions why Pike in Lost Sun-era Long Sun prayed to the Outsider and what he feared, proposing Pike worked with or against Quetzal, and suggests Rose's personality survives in Marble's parts. Also asks whether Horn could know Rose-as-Echidna's scene, questions Silk's explanation of the bird's death, and asks about feathers and his pseudonym Vance, while thanking the list for restored mail and mentioning aquastors.
  • 1997-04-11 thank you, my sonReusch supports Mantis's Pike-Quetzal explanation of what Silk saw and floats the aquastor theory, suggesting that mainframe's visions of dead loved ones might be aquastors. He replies to Mantis's earlier response to his initial questions, saying he is not sure what Silk saw and is not yet convinced of the aquastor idea, and mentions awaiting Mantis's Q&D Guide.
  • 1997-04-11 identity crisisAsks whether Pike was really what he seemed, questioning why a holy man would pray to the Outsider for tax relief and whether he knew Quetzal's nature. Builds on a reading of Silk's vision and on Rose's corpse-like state, and offers a speculative theory that Silk's bird-killing reflects genetic super-hero abilities.
  • 1997-04-08 Pike/Quetzal, and other ?sAsks whether the Pike's ghost scene in Silk is really Quetzal flying out the window with Silk then seeing Pike, arguing that Wolfe links the two deliberately and asking why. Also raises several reread questions: whether Rose has a daughter (citing 1:69hc), how Silk killed the white-headed one, and how Horn could know of the Urus and Trivigaunti officer meeting.
