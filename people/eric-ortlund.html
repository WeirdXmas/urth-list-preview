  • 2008-12-28 Random Semi-Literary Thoughts on AEGArgues that AEG's ending is deliberately unresolved, with Squiddie still present and Gideon ambiguous, and asks whether Gideon's remark that going up is harder than down signals moral growth. Responds to Dave Tallman's earlier remarks by contrasting Gideon with Reis, linking Hanga's conduct in The Tree is My Hat, and noting recurring despair in Wolfe's protagonists across the books.
  • 2008-10-22 At risk of getting in over my head . . .Proposes that the Neighbors transport inhumi physically by spaceship from Green to the Whorl and their new world, with a low domed building on Green's mountain as the ship and a Neighbor-naut as the source of SilkHorn's information. Grounds this in the Neighbors' breeding process and in SilkHorn's account in Return to the Whorl, then questions Mr Thalassocrat's claim that inhumi never fly from Green to Blue by citing their arrival in waves every few years.
  • 2008-10-21 At risk of getting in over my head . . .Asks how Patera Quetzal got onto the Whorl, given Wolfe's interview remark that he was a late arrival and that no major Book of the New Sun characters were inhumi. Considers whether Quetzal was on Urth unnoticed, notes that the inhumi barely survive the Blue-to-Green crossing, and asks whether any textual clues exist.
  • 2008-06-23 Sev's silence about CatherineProposes, though admittedly tentatively, that Catherine and the Katherine feast are the same woman, suggesting Severian himself starts the ritual he later joins as a young man. Grounds this on the shared name and on Typhon's speech about a past troublemaker, which Eric reads as Severian returning as the new Sun, and replies to b sharp's post.
  • 2008-06-10 The Apu-Punchau EclipseArgues that the Apu-Punchau eclipse was not a natural event but was caused by Severian's own body blocking the sun, since he is the new sun. Responds to Roy Lackey's point that the eclipse was staged by the Ship by offering this simpler explanation, citing the way Wolfe hints at it in his postscript to the book.
  • 2008-06-10 The Apu-Punchau EclipseProposes that Severian himself causes the Apu-Punchau eclipse, because as the new sun his body stands between the sun and the natives and casts the shadow. Responds to Matthew Groves's reference to the scene, citing the natives' bowing to Severian as evidence that he is the cause.
  • 2008-06-05 What's So Great About Ushas?Disputes the moral inversion reading of Ushas that Dave Tallman defends, arguing the trial cannot be fair and Severian knows it. Cites characters in The Urth of the New Sun saying the trial is not fair and Severian's play on his torturer identity in the fifth book, and suggests that a refusal by Severian would draw complaints too.
  • 2008-05-12 Whos met or written to WolfeAsks who Wolfe's agent is and how one can reach him, admitting the question may be naive. Responds to Andy Robertson's post asking whether anyone has met Wolfe or written to him, and adds a related question about contact.
  • 2008-05-05 This Craft of Verse: gift idea for Gene Wolfe?Asks fellow list members for a postal address for Gene Wolfe so the poster can write to him, responding to patri10629's question about sending Wolfe a gift of Borges's lectures. The post is a personal request with no discussion of the fiction, offering only a promise not to abuse the address.
  • 2008-03-18 First sight of *An Evil Guest*Asks whether the latest Latro book, set in Egypt, is the final volume of the series, and whether to wait for another. Follows up on aaron's remark about Wolfe getting old, admitting the poster has not yet read the Egypt book.
  • 2008-01-04 Two (hopefully) quick questionsAsks why Severian stresses that Dr. Talos's coin matches the one Vodalus gave him, suggesting an implied epiphany the reader is not catching, and considers several candidate explanations. Also asks how Valeria's details in The Book of the New Sun, such as her ageless face, her family awaiting the stars, and the high walls of the Atrium of Time, could form a slingshot ending tied to the final ringing of the bells.
  • 2007-12-14 Cumaean???Asks whether Merryn and Severian are really twins and whether the Cumaean is the heavenly being Severian sleeps with on Yesod in the fifth volume. Cites Severian's vision of a reptilian Cumaean as a reason to consider Tzadkiel, and notes a parallel between the last chapter's no-magic remark and Shadow's opening on symbols.
  • 2007-11-27 Questions . . .Argues that Severian's names (Death, Life) are deliberately ambiguous and that Severian narrates in a way that deflects attention from his own wrongs, citing the garden description after the Jolenta and Dorcas scenes and the Saltus execution of Morwenna. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's earlier answers on coincidence and the quercine penetralia phrase, and notes recurring character instability across Wolfe's books.
  • 2007-11-27 Questions . . .Suggests that the Pelagic Argosy phrase in The Book of the New Sun hints at two readings: a ship of the deeps tied to Abaia and chaos, or the ships Severian sails on to Yesod and after the flood on Urth. Grounds this in the parallel between the dream-fleshed man Jonas and Severian, both of whom change form and sail on ships, and in Vodalus's league with the sea powers.
  • 2007-11-27 Questions . . .Thanks a fellow poster for an answer about the inset tale Severian reads to Jonas, and says they never saw it that way. Argues that Wolfe must have planned the books in detail, pointing to the idea of humans sailing into space and getting lost in time, which is only explicitly raised when Gunnie speaks of it in The Urth of the New Sun, written years later.
  • 2007-11-27 Questions . . .Asks how old Inire must be, since the Autarch says he has been building clever contraptions since the first Autarch, and notes that Inire is apparently a hierodule. Raises this as an unrelated side note after thanking Roy C. Lackey for an answer about Rudesind's confusion, and says it may already have been discussed.
  • 2007-11-26 Questions . . .Asks three questions about The Book of the New Sun: whether the tale Severian tells Jonas about the man fleshed from dreams carries meaning beyond an interlude, whether Rudesind's confusion about his location is intentional, and whether the ship phrase Vodalus and the Autarch use hides a deeper significance. Raises these as open questions to the list without offering an answer.
  • 2007-11-14 Questions about BNSAsks the list to explain five puzzling details in The Book of the New Sun: Agilus's corpse-like mask, the Jungle Garden fish-woman tale, the palace vision near Nessus, the gate soldiers, and the Gyoll drowning. Cites the Deuteronomy 34 reading and the boatman's Malrubius remark, suspecting Severian withholds what he knows about the gate.
