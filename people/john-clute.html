  • 2000-08-24 Pretty impressive, AlexAsks about the list's chronology and says the Moses image for Silk was his own, first offered in a 1996 NYRSF review of Exodus from the Long Sun. He says he did not credit any earlier pattern of thought behind it and would have if he had known.
  • 2000-08-23 Neighbor + Inhumu + One?Argues that the emotional tone of Wolfe's major works, including In Blue's Waters and later stories, is not Christian but deeply melancholic, and suggests Gnosticism as a better frame, with Return to the Whorl possibly shifting tonal change. Responds to Nutria's broad claim about Christian structure, admitting the topic is premature but a route all decipherments must follow.

  • 2000-08-21 Clute's Non-RevelationClarifies that the author's earlier reading of In Green's Jungles was a general first-response sketch, not a revealed secret, and agrees Oreb may be a Holy Spirit figure.
