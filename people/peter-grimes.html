  • 1999-03-15 Reference to new sun books?Asks whether an odd image in Exodus from the Long Sun, a madman among tombs howling that the sun would die, is a deliberate echo of The Book of the New Sun and perhaps refers to Severian on Urth. Grounds the suggestion in the passage on page 255 where Silk sees it during an out-of-body experience after his wedding, and notes the Whorl's sun may be switched off.
  • 1998-08-27 Eco's ticArgues that Eco's Foucault's Pendulum does not fail by ending with Foucault killing the conspiracy, since the book is about how obsession with conspiracy creates the conspiracies it sees. Responds to Peter Grimes's quoted charge that Eco cannot write endings, and attacks Illuminatus! as poorly written with a misogynistic cast, while praising Wolfe's and Eco's prose.
  • 1998-06-19 Book and Urth; A & M; black holeArgues that Severian may be the Conciliator, citing the healing powers that come from himself rather than the Claw and the Conciliator's link to the New Sun. Responds to adam louis stephanides, who said the first four books give no hint of this, and concedes that the reading may be read into the text after The Urth of the New Sun.
  • 1998-05-14 Claw Power in BOTNSArgues that Severian survives in the hills without food for ordinary reasons, since humans can last weeks without eating when they have water and Severian describes growing weaker. Adds that the power of the New Sun he already carries, which began travelling toward Urth long before, also explains it; no mystery is needed.
  • 1998-05-01 The Two SeveriansArgues that Odilo, Pega and Thais are dead rather than long-lived, and that the bowers in Urth of the New Sun are their tombs. Bases this on the Sleeper's priest saying he is the grandchild of the first arrivals at Ushas, calculating generations to show that Odilo would be about 90 to over 100 years old.
