  • 2009-09-23 Silk for calde blog: Wolfe thesisClarifies that his thesis covers only SF works with a priest as central character whose faith is an important part of the story, citing Clarke's "The Star" as a negative case where faith is shaken. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's objection that no SF text fits, by saying his earlier examples were extremes and deferring a clearer topic to his blog.
  • 2009-09-22 Silk for calde blog: Wolfe thesisIntroduces the poster as an undergraduate writing a thesis on Gene Wolfe's priest figures in Silk and The Book of the Long Sun, and proposes that Wolfe occupies a middle ground between SF stories where priests' faith is debunked and those where it is reaffirmed. Responds to Jerry Friedman's and Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's skeptical remarks by restating the thesis as provisional and apologising for the rebuke.
