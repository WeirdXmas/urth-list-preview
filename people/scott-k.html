Their messages (5 with summaries)

  • 2007-06-19 Film Version of New Sun?Proposes that an animated, not-for-kids adaptation or a TV miniseries would best convey the atmosphere of The Book of the New Sun, rather than a cinema film. Supports the idea by pointing to the Ghormenghast TV miniseries as an example of atmospheric adaptation, and doubts a cinema audience exists.
  • 2006-11-04 Jolenta the beautiful via TalosProposes that Jolenta's beauty owes more to a glamour or fast biochemical enhancement than to surgery, and wonders whether Talos could use the lighter machine technology that made Jonas's mech body. Raises doubts about the metal wires Severian sees under her flesh, citing Severian's remark that she was heavy and asking why Talos would bother with implants at all.
  • 2006-10-30 Jolenta the beautiful via TalosArgues that Talos's glamour, not surgery, made Jolenta beautiful, and that the glamour was a costly illusion that faded and wore her body out. Grounds this in Talos's own words about casting the glamour and in the faerie and Zeus senses of the term, and suggests Baldanders' laboratory experiments as the source.
  • 2006-10-20 Are old questions OK?Asks whether old questions are acceptable and then asks the list for its general reception of Borsky's book Solar Labyrinth, disputing its claim that the Navigator was JFK and its tendency to find blood ties everywhere. Notes that the poster has just finished Return to the Whorl and is considering Peace and There Are Doors.
  • 2006-09-23 urth - collectiblesReports that the poster owns the first three issues of a comic adaptation of Wolfe's New Sun story, judging it a good attempt at a nearly unadaptable work. Mentions the artwork page showing the finding of Triskele and names Ultan's library and the giant undine as favorite sequences.
