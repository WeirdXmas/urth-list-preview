  • 2006-10-01 urth Digest, Vol 25, Issue 25Argues that Severian's claim in the closing lines of The Citadel of the Autarch that it is the last year of the old sun may simply be wrong, since narrators are not to be trusted.   • 2006-09-10 A very interesting interview indeed (Daniel D Jones)Argues that Severian is a Christian figure rather than a Christ figure, since Christians are expected to act as flawed mini-messiahs serving a higher good. Grounds this in Severian's rape of Jolenta, killings, torture and lies before his hierophany, and contrasts him with Silk, who moves from admirable to flawed.
  • 2005-06-11 urth-urth.net Digest, Vol 10, Issue 11Proposes that the clone #5 and his father may be bound simulators as well as clones, suggesting the father's experiments installed a bound personality that explains #5's increasing amnesia.   • 2005-06-08 urth-urth.net Digest, Vol 10, Issue 8Argues that Japanese fiction about souls in dolls and robots, tracing back to bunraku puppet theater, offers useful parallels to Maytera Marble, and says Rah Xephon is a better-developed version of Evangelion. Responds to a post that compared Maytera Marble to Kusanagi Motoko, Lang's Metropolis gynoid, and Hadaly, and that called Evangelion gnostic. Mostly an aside that drifts into anime, with a brief apology and return to topic.
  • 2005-06-06 OT:GoitS2: InnocenceArgues that the anime film Ghost in the Shell 2: Innocence feels disjointed because hard-boiled detective fiction is naturally hard to follow, then recommends the anime series Rah Xephon for its Wolfe-like execution and messianic themes. Responds to Maru Dubshinki's remark about the film's choppy, cut-off feel, offering a genre explanation and a related recommendation rather than a textual analysis of Wolfe's books.
  • 2005-06-05 OT:GoitS2: InnocenceArgues that hardboiled detective stories, including Ghost in the Shell II: Innocence, are deliberately disjointed and hard to follow, and recommends the anime Rah Xephon for its Wolfean execution and messianic struggle between competing futures. Responds to a thread about Ghost in the Shell II by drawing a genre comparison and offering a personal anime recommendation, without close reference to any Wolfe text.
  • 2005-05-02 Jonas, Ship, and shipsArgues that the Ship is a singular, all-powerful vessel distinct from ordinary ships, comparing it to God versus gods, and suggests the different names (Fortunate Cloud, Quasar) may be the same Ship called by different names in different areas. Responds to Josh Young's earlier post noting Hethor's ship name and the idea of a single ship in The Urth of the New Sun, offering the naming explanation as a tentative reconciliation.
