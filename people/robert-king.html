  • 2002-06-12 Loyal to the Group of SeventeenArgues that the Ascians' restricted speech in The Book of the New Sun echoes Newspeak in Orwell's 1984, but Wolfe rejects Orwell's pessimism about totalitarianism, since Loyal to the Group of 17 can still tell his story. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's Newspeak claim, quoting Hartshorn's reply that Ascian speech refers to Mao quotations instead.
  • 2002-06-12 Loyal to the Group of SeventeenArgues that Wolfe's Ascian speech in The Book of the New Sun rejects Orwell's pessimism about totalitarian thought control, since Loyal to the Group of Seventeen can still tell his own story within restricted phrases. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's claim that the Ascians derive from Newspeak in Nineteen Eighty-Four, and also relays a reply that the Ascian speech instead alludes to Mao quotations.
  • 2000-07-05 QuotehuntRecalls the line Alex Groce is searching for as coming near the end of The Claw of the Conciliator, in the scene where Jolenta dies, and offers a corrected wording.   • 1999-10-27 Pynchon (was: One last modernist yap)Argues that Pynchon is intensely postmodern, a blender of past genres and styles, and contrasts him with the more rigorous modernists like Joyce, asking whether modernists believed in art's progress. Praises Mason & Dixon's embedded tale "The Captive's Tale" as a trick almost worthy of Wolfe, while admitting uncertainty about the modernism question.

  • 1998-05-02 NapoleonArgues that the schoolboy Weer's remark about Napoleon's hand in his waistcoat was probably innocent, since children often make innocent remarks that offend adults. Responds to William H. Ansley's view that the remark was not innocent by questioning whether it would really offend everyone and praising Wolfe's withholding of what Napoleon was doing.
  • 1998-04-30 NapoleonJokingly suggests that Weer's theory about Napoleon's hidden hand in portraits is that he was fondling himself under his clothes. Adds a Seinfeld quip and notes the hand would need to sit much lower than portraits show, then jokes that boys would relish such an explanation to offend older relatives.
