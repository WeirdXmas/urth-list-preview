Their messages (8 with summaries)

  • 2001-05-07 copperheadProposes that the dialect words 'neffer' and 'grawnt' in the story Copperhead are 'never' and 'grunt', reading the line as Jane Doe refusing the President sex, and guesses that 'groose' means 'gross'. Also asks whether the inhumi's breeding method, described by Jahlee or Fava, bears on the Short Sun plot and on Horn or Silk as possible inhumi options.
  • 2000-08-21 GurloesProposes that Gurloes and the Autarch are linked, since Severian's descriptions of both share the same mannerisms and bright eyes, and Severian sees the Autarch's mannerisms without placing them. Grounds this in the passage describing Gurloes and the later scene at the house Azure, and asks whether Gurloes or Palaemon uses the prosthetic phallus.
  • 2000-08-17 Sev's memoryArgues that Severian's claim of a near-perfect memory in The Claw of the Conciliator is self-contradictory, since recalling how a remembered scene differed from the original memory concedes imperfect recall. Grounds this in the quoted passage about the library and the child's table, then asks whether the memory flaws come from Hierarch-era alterations of reality or from the narrator simply omitting things, such as Thecla, and whether the manuscript itself is a faulty palimpsest.
  • 2000-08-16 Another tangentAsks what Gene Wolfe's political stance is, whether he is right, republican or democrat, and why The Book of the New Sun presents Vodalus's plan to send man to the stars negatively. Raises the original-sin and quest-for-balance readings and asks how the rejection of Vodalus's aims fits the rest of the work.
  • 2000-08-10 A walk on the wild sideAsks list members how to interpret the passage in The Sword of Cyriaca about the starfaring people leaving their wild half behind to thinking machines, and what might have caused that separation. Writes that the question is for a symposium paper and invites opinions, citing the lost-archive story in the Sword of Cyriaca.
  • 2000-07-11 Digest urth.v029.n032Argues that Vodalus and Jolenta are both assigned roles, since both are deposed and replaced, so their selfhood is fixed by a formal structure set by the Increate, the Autarch or the author. Replies to a question about their connection and closes by pointing readers to M. John Harrison's Viriconium books as similar to the New Sun, though bleaker.
  • 2000-06-12 Dredging up the pastProposes that the single starship in The Urth of the New Sun has a non-linear internal history, so Jonas may have served on it and his crash-landing could be the end of Tzadkiel's ship. Builds the argument from Jonas stepping into Father Inire's mirrors and promising to return when he is sane and whole, reading that as a return to his own past aboard the ship.
  • 2000-06-07 Rumour ControlAsks where the rumour that Severian will appear in the Short Sun books originated and what its details are. Says the poster searched the list archives and found no confirmation of the claim.
