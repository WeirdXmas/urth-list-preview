  • 2003-03-12 Typhon, Sev, SilkArgues that Severian can be messianic and Typhon satanic in one figure without collapsing the categories, and that the Graves clone thesis should not be dismissed on principle.   • 2003-02-11 TIDDAOSAOSArgues that the mystery ship's construction (hatches, guide wires instead of bunks) suggests it was built for humans or human-like beings rather than AIs, and speculates it may be a ghost ship whose original crew was eliminated by the AIs. Builds on Daw's observations and Michael Straight's and Rostrum's earlier points about the starcharts and passageways, reasoning from design needs to conclusions about the ship's origin.
