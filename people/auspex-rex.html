Their messages (4 with summaries)

  • 2014-09-16 The Land AcrossAgrees with Frederick Hudson Norwood that the conveyor belts in The Land Across are a clue, and proposes that they signal shifting parallel time tracks. Cites Grafton noticing the train has changed from diesel to steam once on the first belt, and the observation car already differing before that.
  • 2014-09-14 The Third Policeman and The Land AcrossAsks whether Flann O'Brien's The Third Policeman could help interpret Wolfe's The Land Across, comparing the two books' third figures. Points to the third border-guard, sometimes invisible and resembling both the leader and Grafton's father, and to missing third persons, citing Hotel Sacher and The Third Man.
  • 2013-12-18 The Land Across Discussion (Spoilers!)Lays out a series of puzzles about The Land Across, including whether Leader is visible only to Grafton, whether the house named Nuts is possessed, and whether the severed hand belongs to Yelena. Supports these with quoted passages about the invisible driver's passenger, Grafton's sense of being inside the house, and the hand's apparent friendliness. Also proposes that multiple overlapping versions of the same people, such as Leader as an older Grafton, may explain the clues.
  • 2013-12-11 The Land Across (Spoilers!)Introduces a newcomer who proposes a string of tentative readings of The Land Across: a hand hidden in Martya's hatbox, stairs carrying Grafton between times or realities, and links between mirrors, Vodou and the leader's identity. Grounds these in passages about The Willows, Volitain's logic speech, the conveyor belts, dates, and the photographs.
