  • 2002-10-26 optical connectionsArgues that Whorl technology, including the cards and data transmission in Horn's world, is photonic rather than electronic, and admits the earlier claim was stated too confidently. Responds to a quoted reply that called the cards electronic by noting that optical fibers and waveguides are used in both optical and electronic systems, citing a Scientific American article series and a post by David.
  • 2002-10-24 Optical connectionsArgues that Wolfe's Short Sun books use recurring optical and photonic imagery, such as the wave guide coupler on Green and the reconnection of the Whorl's optical backbone, to echo the theme of letting light pass. Grounds this in Horn's repair episode, Silk's eye given to Pig, and the Whorl's photonic technology, and also asks what a wabe is, citing a Jabberwocky glossary.
  • 2002-07-20 inhuma in brazilJoe Eull, newly delurked, reports a Peter Fleming passage naming the inhuma, a bird of central Brazil, and asks whether Wolfe's use of it is a clue. He also proposes that Tzadkiel's probability remark makes time a decision tree tied to the dice-playing god, and that Olivine's repeated speech is an incompletely configured voice generator, then asks who Remora and Incus were based on.
