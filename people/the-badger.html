Their messages (10 with summaries)

  • 2012-11-27 MaxentiusArgues that the Maxentius of The Book of the New Sun may be Saint Maxentius of France rather than only the Roman emperor who persecuted Saint Katherine, and that the historical identity remains open. Points to the saint's legends, including a drought relief and a soldier whose raised arm froze, linking them to Severian's difficulty wielding the fake sword in the ritual.
  • 2012-11-26 Claw = Fang?Argues that serpent and clawed images in Wolfe's New Sun cycle carry both angelic and demonic meaning, since seraphim were serpentine and Christ and Satan are both imaged as lions. Grounds this in a theologian's view of seraphim as winged dragons and in the claim that the Conciliator's claw becomes a figure of healing rather than tearing.
  • 2012-11-24 The Secret of the TorturersArgues that the 'secret' Severian withholds in The Sword of the Lictor is more likely obedience, an all-pervasive guild principle, than a discrete hidden doctrine. Still hopes there is a separate esoteric secret tied to Katherine the Weal or the Autarch, citing the passage where Severian says he will reveal one secret.
  • 2012-11-24 The Secret of the TorturersAsks whether the reader ever learns the guild secret that Master Gurloes reveals to Severian after his elevation to journeyman in Shadow. Points out that Severian says he promised silence and later broke that promise, and questions whether the book ever states what the secret is.
  • 2012-11-23 Claw = Fang?Adds a minor observation to the Claw plant-or-animal question: in The Shadow of the Torturer, the torturers' wheel at the Feast of Holy Katherine seems covered in serpents that turn into roses. Notes that the effect comes from colored paper, as Severian himself observes, and responds to Lee Berman's thread about whether the Claw is animal or plant.
  • 2012-11-23 What symbols mean for WolfeProposes that the thread on symbology should start from the passage in The Shadow of the Torturer where Severian describes three levels of meaning embedded in every event. Responds to Bill Burgess's request for an inquiry into symbology itself, and asks someone to supply the page reference.
  • 2012-11-23 Claw = Fang?Proposes that the Hebrew letter gimel might visually resemble a claw, suggesting a link between the Claw of the Conciliator title and a Hebrew letter. Builds on Bailey Swartz's idea by noting gimel's association with reward and punishment, which connects to the torturers' justice theme.
  • 2012-11-22 Claw = Fang?Asks whether there is any evidence that the Claw of the Conciliator is of extraterrestrial origin, whether an artifact, natural object or animal part, or whether it could have been made on Urth in an earlier technological age. Responds to Bill Burgess's claw-fang identification thread and frames the question as a request for evidence from the text.
  • 2012-11-21 Claw = Fang?Argues that the Claw's healing contrasts with a serpent's poison and that it may be a stylized cross, then offers a forwarded argument that serpent and thorn imagery in Genesis and Exodus makes curse also cure. Replies to Bill Burgess by adding that Claw heals as serpent fangs poison, citing Eden, Moses' bronze serpent and Paul's thorn.
  • 2012-11-01 Claw = Fang?Proposes that the Claw of the Conciliator resembles a serpent's fang, suggesting the Satan-as-serpent and Garden of Eden imagery. Admits this seems not to fit the book's theology or symbolism and asks whether other readers have pursued the idea.
