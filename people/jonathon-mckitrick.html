  • 2006-08-25 What meaning does the family tree lend to the story itself?Asks what additional meaning can be drawn from understanding Severian's entire family tree once one grasps that he becomes the New Sun and saves Urth. Notes that the tree seems impossible to resolve without Wolfe's own confirmation, so its true meaning may never be known.
  • 2006-08-25 Mistakes/editing errors in "Solar Labyrinth"??Asks whether two apparent errors in the Solar Labyrinth reference book are real, pointing to a crash in the Cathedral placed in The Claw of the Conciliator rather than early in The Shadow of the Torturer, and a claim in the Catherine chapter linking lame characters to Severian the Lame that he cannot find. Also asks whether an errata page exists for the book.
  • 2006-08-19 Satyricon and SoldierSuggests that Fellini's 1969 film of the Satyricon may have influenced Wolfe's narrative gaps, and floats whether it shaped the future-influencing-the-past structure of The Book of the New Sun, while admitting this is a joke. Also offers Harryhausen's Jason and the Argonauts and Back to the Future as film comparisons for Latro.
  • 2006-08-18 BOTNS is a classicAsks whether The Knight is a true magic book, science fiction in disguise, or neither, after saying McKitrick found Wolfe's Solar books less engaging than The Book of the New Sun. Grounds the question in his experience reviewing The Knight for the NYRSF and finding it hard to stop reading and rereading.
  • 2006-08-18 BOTNS is a classicResponds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's claim that The Wizard Knight is his favorite living-author book by welcoming the agreement and admitting he has little interest in fantasy. Notes that he took a long time to give The Book of the New Sun a chance because it did not look obviously like science fiction.
  • 2006-08-15 Re-reading, hoping for some discussion ;-)Chats about whether a Book of the New Sun film could capture the books' tone, comparing it to the Dune adaptations that seemed empty despite some merit. Responds to b sharp's mixed feelings about a film version and mentions the poster's own reading of Solar Labyrinth, offering no interpretation of Wolfe's text.
  • 2006-08-14 Re-reading, hoping for some discussion ;-)Asks whether Gene Wolfe planned the many details of Severian's lineage in The Book of the New Sun deliberately, or whether he will take the final explanation to his grave. Written as a returning poster's request for patient help sorting out the questions after reading recent posts on the topic.
  • 2004-09-30 Finally... UOTNS has arrivedComplains that the list's archives are hard to search, so newcomers struggle to find earlier discussions Christopher Culver pointed them to. Responds to Culver's suggestion that a question was already covered in the archives and in Wolfe interviews, saying browsing by subject line does not help.
  • 2004-09-30 Finally... UOTNS has arrivedAsks Andrew Bollen what he thinks evidence shows about Wolfe changing the back-story of The Book of the New Sun in The Urth of the New Sun. Questions whether the tied-up loose ends reveal a different story from Wolfe's original plan, and says the first book could not stand alone given how much it leaves unexplained.
