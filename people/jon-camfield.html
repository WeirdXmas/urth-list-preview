Their messages (20 with summaries)

  • 1999-12-01 Sev's parentsProposes that Severian's perfect memory is too perfect to be literal, arguing that as he writes he gets lost in memories of boyhood and so omits facts he knows, which explains contradictions. Responds to a Roy Lackey post quoting the torturers'-apprentice passage about never knowing parents, and suggests checking whether Severian's meta-comments thin out where he errs.
  • 1999-10-17 Aw Hell (Giants)Argues that giant undines like Abaia are physically possible despite the square-cube limits on oxygen uptake, citing whales and dinosaurs as proof that large animals exist. Responds to a quoted objection about lung capacity by suggesting alternative respiratory methods, such as smaller alveoli or larger lungs or gills, and asks whether Abaia breathes at all.
  • 1999-10-04 Dear DorcasProposes that Dorcas was in a coma rather than dead when drowned, that she awoke, vomited some shot to become light enough to move, and then died, citing her dream of being poled across the lake and the little shot in her stomach. Speculates about her past as a possible former Virgin of Nessus who married and was scorned, and suggests an unsanitary abortion may have caused the coma.
  • 1998-12-03 Thus Spoke Severian? (Nietzsche links)Proposes parallels between Nietzsche's essay on history and The Book of the New Sun, suggesting Severian as a culture's memory living unhistorically and Terminus Est as the line dividing light from dark. Grounds this in quoted passages on remembering and forgetting and in Zarathustra's Last Man and Overman; asks whether others have seen such links.
  • 1998-10-09 Granny? DorcasProposes that Dorcas may not be the true mind inhabiting her body, suggesting Severian's imagination filled in her memories and that she could be Severian's sister resembling their mother. Grounds this in the boatkeeper's account of Cas, Dorcas following Severian around dazed as Miles does later, and the Ouen comment about resemblance, while admitting the idea is far-fetched.
  • 1998-10-07 Digest urth.v019.n012Speculates that Severian's sister was born to Catherine in the Obuilette and asks where the infant went, proposing that Catherine gave Severian to the Seekers and the daughter to the healers, Pelerines, or the Echopraxia. Responds to Dan Parmenter's remarks on the torturers' healers and Gurloes's hints, noting that torturers do not accept very young children and that the metal phallus scene was meant as mirroring.
  • 1998-10-05 Digest urth.v019.n010Argues that Severian may have a twin sister hidden among the witches or in the Healer's Tower, and that the witches' true-name practice may be moot since magic is very limited in Urth. Responds to Mark Millman's idea that witches hide true names by reasoning from Catherine's infant placement, Merryn's plot function, and Gurloes's hints about a sister.
  • 1998-10-04 "Severus, bishop of Milevis"Proposes that Augustine's City of God anecdote about Bathanarius and a lodestone trick parallels Severian's struggles with Baldanders, with the diamond disabling the technology. Quotes the passage from City of God XXI and points to the diamond-related failure of machines and the burning-in-water image as matches with the Diuturna bullets of power.
  • 1998-10-03 WF travel timeArgues that the Whorl Fragment (WF) was already prepared and moving toward Urth before Severian's trial, so its transit time needn't be a puzzle, and asks whether its travel length can be estimated from Typhon's place in time relative to Severian's reign. Also asks whether the distances in Brook Madregot are logarithmic and whether Madregot appears outside the kabbalistic references; grounds the claim in the WF's motion during Typhon's time but not Apu-Punchau's.
  • 1998-10-03 Eclipse?Argues that Sev's acceptance into the Stone Town strengthens the Conciliator myth, which lets him succeed in Typhon's time and pass through Os. Questions what the witches sought during Sev's meeting with his duplicate there, then proposes that Tzadkiel, seeing a causal hole, made Sev Apu Punchau, needing the miracle without the White Fountain's power.
  • 1998-09-30 SabretacheProposes that Severian's sabretache is really a version of Heward's Handy Haversack, the magic bag from old Dungeons & Dragons material. Lists the items the pouch held, such as the Claw, a whetstone, the letter for the Archon, and flint and steel, while admitting he cannot recall the rest.
  • 1998-09-21 Sev and Thecla in childhoodProposes that Dorcas is Severian's paternal grandmother and that Thecla is his half-sister, so the 'father's mounted guard' detail in Claw points to Ouen and an affair with an exultant. Grounds the theory in the passage about pebbles and dodging hooves, the memory-mixing after the House Absolute performance, and the missing-sister problem.
  • 1998-09-04 Digest urth.v018.n014Argues that the 'descendants' clause in Severian's remark about his priest's forbears is conditional, so it may not identify the Adam and Eve pair as the sailors. Tests Tony Ellis's reading against the text, noting Severian never mentions children and Apheta is not human, and floats a non-literal sense of descendants; it ends unconvinced by either option.
  • 1998-09-02 Relative & AbsoluteArgues that Baldanders and Foila (B&F) live backward in time like Merlin, born in the future and dying in the past, yet still interact normally with people. Responds to John Gersten's question about how they meet Severian, drawing a Kantian comparison about causality and citing Mantis's view that the meetings can be mapped in The Book of the New Sun.
  • 1998-09-02 Relative & AbsoluteProposes that Blue and Father Francis (B&F) live backward in time like Merlin, born in the future and dying in the past, yet interact forward with Severian's people. Offers a Kantian view that causality is imposed on the universe rather than inherent, and defers to Mantis's point after noting he lacks the book to map the meetings.

Floats several motives for such a placement, such as cooling Earth, making Venus habitable, or a hierodule scheme to help humanity develop, offering these as questions rather than settled answers.
  • 1998-08-04 Black HolesProposes that the black hole in the sun, unlike a mass-increasing one, would have consumed solar mass and cooled the sun quickly before stabilizing, and suggests this could estimate the hole's size. Grounds the argument in Typhon's report of a 0.002 drop in Earth's temperature and Sean Walen's wormhole argument, while noting Severian sees black and white holes as gateways between universes.
