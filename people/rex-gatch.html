Their messages (5 with summaries)

  • 2003-07-29 My thoughtsIntroduces a long-time lurker who praises the list's informed discussions, recalls reading The Claw of the Conciliator after a White Dwarf review, and links Wolfe's religious themes to attending Orthodox churches. Also reacts with discouragement to a mention of a Wolfe writing class, saying he would never want Wolfe's opinion of his own writing.
  • 2003-06-23 Latro and Memory lossReports a television program about a head-injured patient who kept old memories but could not form new ones, yet learned table tennis, suggesting motor skills survive memory loss. Offered as possible help for understanding Latro's memory loss in the Latro books, with a reference to the film Memento.
  • 2003-06-05 Dinner & the Long SunAsks whether Wolfe's use of familiar genre devices like Needlers, flying vampires and 19th-century foundry-made taluses in the Long Sun books is meant as deliberate clues or simply accepted as written. Reflects on how a reader's age and early reading shape the sense of thrill from genre-breaking works, citing Neuromancer and older classics, and touches on the Horn/Nettle authorship question.
  • 2003-06-03 Dinner & the Long SunProposes that Blood, the drug in Tim Powers's Dinner at Deviant's Palace, made from degenerate cultists' blood, may connect to Rust in The Long Sun. Grounds this in the shared idea that blood is memory and in Rust's likeness to dried blood, admitting he lacks a quote, and invites Powers readers' reactions.
  • 2003-05-31 The LS series and ' Dinner at Deviants Palace'Asks whether other list members have noticed links between the Long Sun series and Tim Powers's novel Dinner at Deviants Palace, and offers to expand a thesis if others agree. Gives no specific parallels, only a general claim of resemblance and a note about posting correctly.
