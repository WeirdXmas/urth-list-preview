  • 2000-11-26 Digest whorl.v012.n008Lists five candidates for the giant figure on the cover of Return to the Whorl (the Outsider, Silk, Pas/Silk, Gene Wolfe, or a mixture) and rejects each in turn.   • 2000-09-09 Endymion9's IGJ QuestionsArgues that the inhuma Fava could have learned composition through long practice, since she is much older than she looks, answering the question of how an inhuma writes. Also argues that the dragon Oreb has his own personality rather than being wholly a god's vessel, citing two occasions in In Green's Jungles where Horn asks Oreb to find something and he does.
  • 2000-08-31 Inhumu feeding/breeding cycleProposes that inhumu do not cross between the green and blue planets as often as earlier posters speculated, because the crossing is dangerous. Grounds this on Horn's remark, cited from memory without a reference, that many inhumu die on the trip even at conjunction, and infers they must get blood and breed on both worlds.
