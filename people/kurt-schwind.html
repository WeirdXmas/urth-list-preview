  • 2006-10-16 The Death of Doctor Island MovieArgues that the Death of Doctor Island stories share the shifty narration found in Wolfe's other work, and that The Wizard Knight is more straightforward than Latro, so a film adaptation would lose what makes Wolfe's writing valuable. Responds to Peter Heyneman's claim that Wolfe's unreliable voice makes his books nearly impossible to film, agreeing that Hollywood would strip out those qualities.
