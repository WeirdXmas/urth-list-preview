  • 2004-12-03 BotNS in GermanAsks whether the translator Hmpf MacSlow has written to Gene Wolfe for permission before translating The Book of the New Sun into German, stating that translation needs the copyright holder's consent even without profit. Responds to MacSlow's quoted remark about teaching himself literary translation by working on Wolfe's text, raising a legal point rather than a reading of the books.
  • 2004-10-05 Silkhorn's mission after BotSSProposes that Silk's departure at the end of The Book of the Short Sun is an optimistic finale, with Silk as a galactic bringer of the Outsider's good news rather than a cruel abandonment of Blue. Grounds this in a Cambridge Companion to Science Fiction essay calling Silk a fisher of men, and in the idea that the Holy Spirit's work continues.
  • 2004-10-02 What does Horn look like?Argues that Horn in On Blue's Waters could not have had white hair at about thirty-five, so balding is more likely, and that Silk's long hair would also make him look old too early. Grounds the view in the ages of Horn and Silk and the stated length of Silk's hair in The Book of the Long Sun, replying to James Wynn's question about how the two look.
  • 2004-09-30 Finally... UOTNS has arrivedArgues that Wolfe meant The Book of the New Sun to stand alone and wrote The Urth of the New Sun only because of editorial pressure. Replies to Jonathon McKitrick's doubt that the first four books could stand on their own, pointing to archive discussions and Wolfe interviews and urging newcomers to read those before asking.
