Their messages (5 with summaries)

  • 2008-05-15 Women read Wolfe too & Other SF writers & questions to mailersIntroduces herself as a 27-year-old woman from the Chicago area who first encountered Wolfe at an event with Neil Gaiman, and names her favorite Wolfe books and other authors she enjoys. Replies to P Konopka's earlier post, which asked whether any other women read Wolfe, by welcoming her and mentioning Steve Brust's Vlad in the Jhereg series as resembling Latro.
  • 2008-04-23 PEACE: Gift ExchangeReplies to Dave Tallman's reading of the Christmas gift exchange in Peace, agreeing it is a good explanation and sharing the reader's first reaction to the toilet-water gift as a terrible present. Responds to Dave's argument that Della, not Mr. Elliot, sent the gift as a malicious message to Mab, and wryly notes the pleasure of that interpretation.
  • 2008-04-21 PEACE: One DevilAnswers Michael Straight's question about the name of the Tang-like drink Weer drinks in Peace by identifying it as Tang, the real-world brand. Reasons that Wolfe would not use the real-life product name, and jokingly compares the missed clue to failing to see how Dorcas relates to Severian.
  • 2008-04-18 PEACE: One DevilArgues that Alden Weer is a psychopath, that the coldroom victim may be a son of Mrs. Green, and that Weer and his father are becoming "one devil." Responds to Dave Tallman's One Devil idea by citing the harem room, the axes, Lois's gun, and Aunt Olivia's use of "Den" instead of "Alden."
  • 2008-02-11 "The Eleventh City"Admits being puzzled by The Eleventh City but finds it more thought-provoking than puzzling, and poses questions about the title, Jacinta's stoning of Sam Cooper, and the postscript. Replying to JBarach, suggests the title extends the Ten Cities metaphor for a once-small world now whole, and asks whether the postscript's photograph drives Cooper mad.
