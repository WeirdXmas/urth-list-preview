Grounds the claim on the Janus Quadrifons temple on the Forum of Nerva and the Bifrons epithet, noting the parallel between two faces and four faces.

Also asks what a confusing passage about Pig's embryo and the Red Sun Whorl means, and proposes that Silk let Juganu drink his blood to free Horn.
