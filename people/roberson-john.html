Their messages (5 with summaries)

  • 2000-08-24 Digest whorl.v011.n027Proposes that the inhumi's secret is that humans offering themselves willingly would instill selflessness, so inhumi feeding only on animals would lose sentience and revert to croc-like vermin. Answers Spectacled Bear's question about Horn's claim that the inhumi could be reduced to beasts, arguing this conflicts with Incanto's view that their developing souls must not be killed.
  • 2000-08-22 Horn as Inhumi and Silk as suicide?Argues that the postscript describing Silk was written by Horn and Nettle after landing on Blue, not by the Rajan, and that Silk would not commit suicide given his earlier rejection of it on the airship, though self-sacrifice for Hy fits him. Offers several speculative readings: Jahlee as an inhumu absorbing Incanto's traits, and Horn's identity confusion with Babbie as psychic bonding linked to Mucor.
  • 2000-01-21 Inhumu's secretProposes that the Rajan's threat to the inhumu, and their secret, involves a chameleon-like theory in which they lose intelligence and human shape after feeding on cow blood once starved. Grounds this in the Rajan offering Jahlee a cow after hibernation and in whether she seems dumber after drinking, while admitting little textual support.
  • 1999-11-16 Horn as Silk?Proposes that Horn's identity confusion after returning from the Long Sun Whorl comes from a Pas-Silk amalgam program possessing him, as it did Maytera Mint. Grounds this on Silk's apparent scanning into Mainframe and a possible surgeon's graft, and also asks whether the inhumi mimic men's mutual hatred, which would explain Quetzal's guilt-ridden benevolence.
  • 1999-11-05 Digest whorl.v010.n043Proposes that Silk's program has possessed Horn, transferring part of Silk's identity into him so Silk can lead New Viron and Blue without leaving the Long Sun Whorl. Grounds this on Horn's Silk-like attitudes, his augur's robes, Oreb's mistaking him for Silk, and a surgeon's remark about Pas-silk in The Book of the Long Sun.
