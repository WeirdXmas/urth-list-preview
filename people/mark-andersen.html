Their messages (6 with summaries)

  • 2000-06-21 Digest whorl.v010.n125Proposes that Quetzal, not Crane, was behind Silk's rise to calde in the Long Sun books, while suggesting the Outsider may have manipulated both Crane and Quetzal for its own ends. Responds to Michael Straight's quoted argument, which in turn answers Alice Turner, that Trivigaunte's Crane chose Silk as a puppet for a Viron coup.
  • 2000-04-04 SorryArgues that Quetzal instigated the rebellion aboard the ship to move the Cargo out of the hold and onto Green, where his kind could use it. Builds the case from Quetzal's position, his motive of securing food back home, and Silk's role as calde, which fits Silk's commitment to the Plan of Pas.
  • 2000-04-04 Digest whorl.v010.n097Argues that the hidden manipulator of the Long Sun plot must know Silk, control money and aides, and have access to the Ayuntamiento, and suggests Quetzal instigated the rebellion to move the Cargo to Green. Responds to Alice Turner's remark that one person sets the whole structure in motion, offering a method of working backward from motives and opportunities.
  • 1998-07-14 Urth condemned, the Autarch's memoriesProposes that Typhon's self-worship as a god caused Urth's condemnation, and asks whether the Long Sun and New Sun parallels between true and false gods are substantive. Responds to adam louis stephanides's quote on the Autarchy's origin, citing the dying autarchs' cry "Find somebody else" and suggesting they want new bodies to live in.
  • 1998-06-16 Moths and DarwinArgues that natural selection follows logically from heritable variation among individuals, and that the noisy machine in the Saltus mine should be read as a mechanical guardian, sketching a timeline of the treasury's founding and rediscovery. Responds to Dan Parmenter's remarks on the Peppered Moths and Dollo's law, and uses Darwin's Beagle observations and Popper's critique as grounds before offering the Saltus timeline.
  • 1998-05-13 Casting callReacting to a casting thread, the poster suggests the actress Cindy Crawford, Anna Nicole Smith and Phoebe Cates for Jolenta and Valeria and proposes Brian Dennehy as Hildegrin and John Gielgud as Master Palaemon. The post adds to a list of film-star casting ideas for The Book of the New Sun, with no textual evidence offered and only a quip about the actresses' acting ability.
