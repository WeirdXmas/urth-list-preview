  • 2000-01-25 Haunted Boardinghouse and a 1 - 2 - 3Argues that the substance Jo An takes in "1 - 2 - 3" is heroin, so her death was an accidental poisoning rather than a suicide, and questions whether the earlier civilization used the phone and drug for suicide. Responds to Jim Jordan's request for Latin translations in "The Haunted Boardinghouse" and draws on the colour, effects and death described in the story; also links it to John Crowley's "Little Big" and doubts a mass-suicide reading.

  • 1999-11-09 (blue) Notes from a 2nd reading [SPOILERS]Proposes that Horn's pit vision, a tall long-nosed man touching his forehead, may be a hallucinated Babbie, and links it to Severian's habit of holding the claw to people's foreheads to bring them back. Grounds this in the parallel recovery pattern of bewilderment and amnesia seen in others Severian restored, while admitting the idea seems implausible.
