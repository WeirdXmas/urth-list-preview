1999-10-26 Paul C Duggan #

Asks whether anyone knows the contents of the forthcoming Strange Travelers collection of Wolfe stories, since the poster's emails to the publisher Tor and to David Hartwell went unanswered. Seeks inside information about which stories will be anthologized; the post includes no analysis of the fiction.
questions

Does anyone have a list of, or even partial knowledge of, the stories that will be anthologized in the new collection Strange Travelers?

I tried emailing Tor and David Hartwell, but no response was forthcoming. I figure somebody here might have the "inside info". Thanks.

1999-11-06 Kevin J. Maroney #

Lists the table of contents of the forthcoming Strange Travelers anthology, taken from a galley, in reply to a request for the story list. Adds a remark that it is the first book seen with a 2000 copyright.
informs

From the galley:

Blueberry Jam
One-Two-Three for Me
Counting Cats in Zanzibar
The Death of Koshchei the Deathless
No Planets Strike
Bed and Breakfast
To the Seventh
Queen of the Night
And When They Appear
Flash Company
The Haunted Boardinghouse
Useful Phrases
The Man in the Peppermill
The Ziggurat
Ain't You Most Done?

It's the first book I've seen with a 2000 copyright. Cool.

Wombat, a.k.a. Kevin Maroney [email]
Kitchen Staff Supervisor, New York Review of Science Fiction http://ebbs.english.vt.edu/olp/nyrsf/nyrsf.html
