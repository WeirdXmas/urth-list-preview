1997-09-09 Alice Turner #

Mocks a poster's concern about copyright by arguing that the reply-sender offers faster same-day service than the other side can manage, and demands that any copyright fee be paid out of a debt owed to them. Responds to Wombat's remark that the works are copyrighted, with the poster's own jokes about delays and a long-owed $25, and no engagement with Wolfe's texts.
chats

Oh, get real, Wombat! It would take you guys three months to get around to mailing the thing (I know whereof I speak), whereas I offer same-day service with a free copy of Playboy thrown in (you have to ask specially for that). Besides, I've only had four requests. If you insist on your nano-ounce of flesh, take it out of the $25 you guys have owed me for more than an year and a half.

-alga-
