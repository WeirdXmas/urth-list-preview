Replies to Marc Aramini's wish for a literal solution to the Green-is-Urth puzzle, saying he himself finds religious meaning in the metaphor he rejects. Lists six similarities between Blue and Ushas, including a flooded world, separated islands, refugees from a spaceship, and mermaids.

I hear you, Marc, and feel your pain. I consider the literal solution to be a possibility and I hope you'll find resolution at some point. What I meant in the last post is the irony that I am not religious yet I find religious significance to the metaphor you reject but which would provide you the salvation you seek.

FWIW and to anyone who is not Marc reading this thread I'll sum up by mentioning what I think are the similarities of Blue to Ushas:

1. Flooded world with sunken ruins
2. No large continents but instead smaller, separated islands (we only see one on Ushas though)
3. Yellow sun, temperate climate
4. Populated by refugees from a spaceship
5. Elevation and worship of gods co-opted from a previous world 6. Presence of mermaids (and demons?)
