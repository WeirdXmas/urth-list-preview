Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

2010-05-17 Adam Thornton #

Argues that the Book of the New Sun's subtext does not show a vast empty gap between humanity and God, but a gap that begins only above humanity, with the hierarchy of beings in between. Grounds this in the Cock and the Eagle episode and questions whether Wolfe's phrase about God's message refers to God or to the Hierogrammates.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: Gap between humanity and God begins above humanity

My interpretation of the subtext of the Sun series is this- There is not a vast, empty gap between humanity and God (the Increate/Outsider etc.).

Based on the Cock and the Eagle, I'd have to say there is such a gap.

It just doesn't start immediately above humanity.

Adam

2010-05-18 Jeff Wilson replying to Adam Thornton #

Argues that the angel's claim of a gulf between humanity and the Increate in "The Cock and the Eagle" is just that character's opinion, since the angel is wrong about the story's moral. Replies to Adam Thornton on whether "God's message" means God or the Hierogrammates, noting the angel spoke after shedding his angel-nature, placing him further from the Increate.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: The angel in The Cock and the Eagle is wrong about the moral; The angel's claim of a divine gulf is only his opinion

If you man the cock and the angel, that's the angel's opinion. However, the angel turns out to be wrong about the fundamental moral of the story, and IIRC the angel makes his statement about the Increate's separation while he is without his angel-nature; having removed that, he has placed himself that much further from the Increate.

It's ambiguous, I'm jus sayin'
