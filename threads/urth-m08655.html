Jonas/Jonah definition

2009-07-27 O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) #

Proposes that Jonas in The Book of the New Sun may be named after Jonah, citing a Wiktionary entry that defines Jonah as a jinx or bringer of bad luck on a ship. Suggests the name fits because a crash landing after centuries adrift in time and space could count as bad enough luck to qualify.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Jonas is named after Jonah as a jinx

Hi all,

Not sure if this has been posted before; I couldn't find it in the archives.

Check out the third definition of Jonah (from which Jonas is derived) as given below:

http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Jonah
Jonah
A male given name </wiki/given_name>.
(Biblical) A book of the Old Testament </wiki/Old_Testament> and the Hebrew </wiki/Hebrew> Tanakh </wiki/Tanakh>. (nautical, slang </wiki/Appendix:Glossary>) A person who brings a ship bad </wiki/bad> luck </wiki/luck>. (slang </wiki/Appendix:Glossary>, by extension of the nautical sense) Any person or object which is deemed to cause bad luck; a jinx </wiki/jinx>.

I wonder if crash landing a space ship after centuries adrift in time and space could be considered bad enough luck to qualify!
