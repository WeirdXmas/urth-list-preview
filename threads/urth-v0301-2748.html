Green's intelligence

Urth list · 1 messages · 2003-01-21 to 2003-01-21

2003urthAdam Stephanides

2003-01-21 Adam Stephanides #

Disputes Wolfe's own description of Green in There Are Doors as unintelligent, citing the confrontation with Lara in chapter 31 as showing Green is no less intelligent than other protagonists. Adds that Wolfe's male leads in later novels seem largely the same person, excepting Horn in On Blue's Waters and possibly Latro.
disputes · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft

I have to admit, when I last read TAD (several years ago, at least) it never struck me that Green was lacking in intelligence (or courage, or enterprise). And rereading the confrontation scene with Lara (chap. 31), Green seems no less intelligent than Wolfe's other protagonists.

Actually, now that I think about it, Wolfe's main male characters in his novels written after TBOTNS all seem to me to be more or less the same person, except for Horn in OBW (and possibly Latro in the Soldier books, my memory of which is sketchy).

--Adam
