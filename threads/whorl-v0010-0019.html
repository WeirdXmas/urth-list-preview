1999-09-14 Alice Turner #

Reports a Publishers Weekly review of On Blue's Waters, summarizing its plot: civilization decaying on Blue, Horn sent to find a lander, his self-summary en route, and vampires wanting humans as cattle. Quotes the review's verdict that the book is slow but sets up mysteries for later volumes.
informs

PW has a (non-starred) review this week: a summary. Don't read it if you really don't want to know.

Civilization is decaying on Blue, and Horn is sent to find Silk, i.e. a lander capable of returning to the Whorl. He gives us a summary of his own life en route (rather ditheringly), and battles vampires who want to take humans to Green as cattle. The book is slow, but mysteries are established for later volumes. (severely truncated summary)
