Scipioun's Drem

2000-05-09 Alice Turner #

Agrees with Nigel that Chaucer's "Tullyus of the Drem of Scipioun" points to Macrobius' commentary on Scipio's dream, and says the dream-vision trope was long taken as truth. Responds to Nigel's correction by noting that after Dante the dream-vision became a popular literary device, and that from about 400 to 1400 many readers accepted such accounts as fact.
supports · source-and-allusion · theories: Chaucer's Scipio dream refers to Macrobius commentary

Yes, I did, sorry to be foggy. But you are once again right: post-Dante it beome a very popular literary trope. During the thousand years that stretched from about 400 to 1400, it was related as *truth*, however, by a surprising number of people. That's why I made the UFO reference.
