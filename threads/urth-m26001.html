2014-10-15 Marc Aramini replying to Piotr Szczęsny #

Reports that the poster owns the Kindle edition of Jay Lake's Last Plane to Heaven and praises Wolfe's foreword's metaphor contrasting short stories with novels. Recounts meeting Lake's daughter at the 2013 Nebula dinner, where Lake kept stopping by the table, and describes the family as very nice.
chats

I have it on kindle. The metaphor for short stories vs novels Wolfe uses is colorful, sweating in the dark with no bed against a more romantic consummation. At the Nebula Dinner in 2013 I sat next to Jay Lake's daughter at dinner, with Silverberg's agent and Connie Willis' family right across from us, so Jay kept coming up to the table. Seemed like a very nice family.
