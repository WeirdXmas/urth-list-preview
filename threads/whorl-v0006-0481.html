followup on lander's destination--who decided?

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-06-27 to 1997-06-27

1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunRon Crown

1997-06-27 Ron Crown #

Argues that Wolfe's PEOPLE interview answer about who decided the lander's destination in Exodus from the Long Sun contradicts the novel's text, which says the colonists chose Green and the monitor overruled them. Cites the passage where the colonists learn three days later that the monitor had overruled Remora's advice, and notes Wolfe's answer blamed the colonists.
disputes · close-textual-reading · author-and-biography

After Kevin McGuire's post on his question similar to mine during the PEOPLE interview, I checked the transcript for Wolfe's answer which is given as follows: (emphasis added)

"Quetzal, on his deathbed so to speak, advises them to land on Green. When they discover that he's an alien, _they_ reverse his decision and land on Blue, and I thought that was clear."

I guess this is an example of how even an author can lose track of the details of his text because the text of that section in Exodus makes it quite clear that the _colonists_, following Remora's advice, decided to continue on course to Green and only discovered three days later "that the _monitor_ had overruled him [i.e. Remora]. No one questions _its_ decision now." (emphasis added).

So Wolfe's online answer appears to make things even murkier!

Details and memory! (two important desiderata for Wolfe and his readers, I should have thought)
