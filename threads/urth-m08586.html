Update on _The Death of Doctor Island_

Urth list · 2 messages · 2009-01-30 to 2009-02-05

2009-01-30 Gwern Branwen #

Asks whether the film project The Death of Doctor Island is dead, noting that a web page said the first vignette was scheduled for completion in early 2008, which is now a year past. Quotes the project's own description of shooting on location and locked edits as the basis for the question.
questions

Has anyone heard anything recently about the film project?

I read on http://www.doctorisland.com/secure/about/about2.htm that

"Doctor Island was shot on 35mm film, on location in Costa Rica and Los Angeles. The edit for each of the seven vignettes has been locked. Sound design is nearly complete. The first vignette is scheduled for completion in early 2008. Five years in the making thus far, the final result will be an integrated, yet disturbing world through which one may explore, but from which one cannot escape."

Early 2008 will soon be a full year ago. Is the project dead?

2009-02-05 Nigel Price replying to Gwern Branwen #

Reports that the poster bought a still frame from the proposed film of The Death of Doctor Island and framed it so the nude cast would not embarrass future grandchildren. Replies to Gwern Branwen's earlier question about whether he had bought a still, answering with a joking account of the purchase.
chats

Concerning buying a still frame from the forthcoming (we hope!) movie version of "The Death of Doctor Island", Gwern Branwen asked, a while back...

Yes. The still frame was a striking image and nicely printed.

As I planned to display the photo in my study, my main difficulty in choosing a frame from all those available was finding one in which the cast's utter nakedness was not going to cause bewildered or sly amusement among any future grandchildren I might have. To prevent snickering behind Granddad's back, I eventually managed to find one in which the hero is shown sufficiently deeply immersed in water to hide his...er...embarrassment!

Nigel
