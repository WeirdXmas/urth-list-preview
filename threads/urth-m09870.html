2009-10-29 Gwern Branwen replying to Allan Anderson #

Jokingly argues that the joke about the pantry in the Wizard Knight Companion's foreword is a pun, reading 'pantry' as 'pan treat' and so a cookie. Responds to Allan Anderson's remark that he enjoys the book's Forward, playfully pretending to have been fooled by Wolfe's wordplay.
chats

Yeah. To tell you the truth, I was convinced that they were in the pantry, because you see, the linguistic evidence is obvious: 'pantry' is pronounced as 'pantree', or 'pan-tree', or pronounced in a guttural giant voice, 'pan treat', a treat cooked in a pan - and what might that be but a cookie? Classic Wolfe!

But I guess he fooled me again. :(
