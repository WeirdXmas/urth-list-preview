The Cat and significance to BOTNS

Urth list · 5 messages · 2009-02-03 to 2009-02-04

2009urthThe Book of the New SunO'Donnell, Tim (BOSI)Jeff Wilson
The thread: Tim O'Donnell proposed that the phantom cat from "The Cat" is Jonas, who left Father Inire's mirrors and later bonded with Severian and possessed Miles. Jeff Wilson disputed that the Endangered Species cat is the same creature, suggesting Inire supplied a catlike creature to distract the girl. Wilson then argued from Jonas's notules that creatures are summoned, and O'Donnell built on this with speculation that Hethor sends ordinary animals through mirrors and that summoned creatures take on expected properties. O'Donnell repeatedly admitted the ideas lacked textual evidence, and the thread ended with the speculation openly acknowledged rather than resolved.
  • Tim O'Donnell: the phantom cat is Jonas returning from the mirrors, with speculative mechanisms throughout
  • Jeff Wilson: the cat in Endangered Species is a catlike creature Inire supplied, not the mirror-tossed cat
  • Jeff Wilson: creatures are summoned, not conjured, as shown by Jonas's notules
  • Tim O'Donnell: Hethor makes creatures by sending animals through mirrors, admittedly without textual support

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Doubles, twins and clones, Robots, androids and artificial life

2009-02-03 O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) #

Proposes that Jonas, after leaving Father Inire's mirrors as an invisible entity like the phantom cat in "The Cat", bonded with Severian and later possessed Miles when Severian revived Miles's body. Grounds the idea in the cat's invisibility, its traces, and its ability to invade Leocadia's dreams, treating these as a mode of return from the mirrors.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Jonas returned from the mirrors as an invisible entity; Jonas possessed Miles after Severian revived the body

Hi all,

I was just thinking on the story of The Cat in Endangered Species. Set in the same universe as BOTNS. Specifically, the following occurred to me:
The titular moggie is tossed carelessly through Father Inire's mirrors by Sancha and comes back as a phantom cat. The cat cannot be seen by anyone though it does leave evidence of its existence. The cat also has the power to invade the dreams of Leocadia.

This provides us with some evidence of at least one mode of return from the mirrors. As some sort of invisible entity that can (to some extent) occupy the thoughts of others. You see where I am going with this...does this tie in with Jonas as Miles? Could Jonas have simply popped out of the mirrors as an invisible entity (with no need for a body). He may have bonded to Severian in much the same way as the cat bonded to Sancha. Then, when Severian re-animated the body of Miles spirit-Jonas saw an opportunity to occupy a new body and possessed Miles.

F

2009-02-03 Jeff Wilson replying to O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) #

Disputes O'Donnell's idea that the cat in Endangered Species is the same cat tossed through Father Inire's mirrors and returned as a phantom. Suggests instead that Inire supplied a catlike creature to distract the girl, which bonds with her and has pookalike qualities.
disputes · puzzle-solving · theories: The cat in Endangered Species is not the transformed original cat; Inire supplied a catlike creature to distract the girl

I'm not convinced this is the same cat transformed; IIRC it could more easily be a catlike creature Inire supplied to distract the girl from her loss with novelty. Bonding ensues between the girl and the feline that turns out to have pookalike qualities.

2009-02-04 Jeff Wilson replying to O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) #

Argues that Jonas's recognition of the notules from his travels to other planets shows the creatures are summoned rather than conjured or transmogrified. Builds on Tim O'Donnell's speculation that Hethor makes creatures through mirrors, suggesting an exchange of creatures may be needed, which could explain Hethor's paracoita and the cat-thing.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Hethor summons creatures rather than transmogrifying them; Creatures are exchanged to summon new beasts through mirrors

Jonas' recognition of the notules from his travels to other planets is textev that the creatures are summoned, rather than conjured or transmogrified.

The possibility remains that some kind of exchange of creatures might be necessary or at least facilitate the process; that might explain what happened to Hethor's paracoita as well as the cat thing.

2009-02-04 O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) replying to O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) #

Argues that Inire summoned the cat back through the mirrors, tying this to Miles/Jonas and the mirrors' role in the story, and speculates that Hethor creates his creatures by sending ordinary animals through mirrors. Responds to Jeff Wilson's suggestion that the cat is a catlike creature Inire supplied to comfort the girl, and admits the speculation has no textual evidence.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Inire summoned the cat back through the mirrors; Hethor creates creatures by passing animals through mirrors

Yeah, well, that is fair, it is not clear what happens, this is Wolfe after all! Whether Inire conjures up some kind of cat or whether Inire summons the cat back through the mirrors is unclear. Me, I favour the theory that Inire summoned the cat back through the mirrors and this has a bearing on Miles/Jonas. I think this story tells us more about the mysterious mirrors and that is (one of) its purpose(s).

Also, I wonder if Hethor doesn't just call up his creatures with his mirrors, what if he creates them in this way as well? By putting an innocent slug through the mirrors he could get a snuffling beast that leaves a slimy trail, the salamander could originally have been a newt and so on. All this is wild speculation of course with no textual evidence... :)
