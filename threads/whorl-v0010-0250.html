2000-01-30 David Langford #

Corrects a claim that Patera Pike's ghost is Quetzal by citing Wolfe's 1997 answer to a list question denying it, and notes Alex David Groce's point that Covenant's ring is his wedding ring. Responds to Alga's post and the Covenant-Lena ring passage, grounding the correction in the author's own written reply rather than fresh argument.
disputes · author-and-biography · source-and-allusion · theories: Patera Pike's ghost is Quetzal

When members of this very list posed a number of questions to Wolfe back in 1997, Q27 and Wolfe's answer went as follows:

Is Pike's ghost really Quetzal?
No.

Of course it might still have been some other inhumu! I ran this material as part of an APA contribution, and it's still on my website at:

www.ansible.demon.co.uk/cc/cc77.html#wolfe

Also Alex David Groce wrote, in the context of Horn's rape of Seawrack and the ring:

Um, no: Covenant's ring is his wedding ring, from this world and from before his leprosy.

Clearly I'm feeling picky today!

Dave
