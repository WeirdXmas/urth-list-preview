Gormenghast: The Rock Opera

Urth list · 2 messages · 2001-07-02 to 2001-07-02

2001urthDan ParmenterAdam Stephanides

2001-07-02 Dan Parmenter #

Alerts readers to a rock opera adaptation of Mervyn Peake's Gormenghast composed by Irmin Schmidt, a founder of the band CAN, and points to a website with extracts. Notes that the poster has not yet read the novel and found the sample tracks unappealing, so the post is informational rather than analytical.
informs

Disclaimer:

I haven't yet read Gormenghast, though it's "on the list".

I didn't watch the TV show yet because I prefer to read things before I see any film/TV adaptation and I figure it won't be too hard to track down a copy of the film when I do finally read the book.

But I wanted to alert the truly curious to another adaptation of Gormenghast, namely the "rock opera" version. The sample tracks I downloaded didn't do a thing for me, although the composer, Irmin Schmidt, is a musician I very much admire as one of the founders of the seventies-era German rock band CAN, which this stuff sounds nothing like at all!

Go to:

http://www.spoonrecords.com/

And follow the Gormenghast links for information and downloadable extracts.

Dan

2001-07-02 Adam Stephanides #

Recalls a 1960s psychedelic rock track by a band named Steerpike, which released a concept album claiming that Steerpike is the true hero of the Gormenghast books. Notes the poster has no memory of what the track sounded like, offering a light aside rather than an argument.
chats

Somewhere in storage I have an anthology of rare psychedelic rock from the 60s which includes a track by a group called, iirc, Steerpike, which released a concept album whose concept was that Steerpike was the true hero of the Gormenghast books. I have no recollection whatever of what the track sounded like.

--Adam
