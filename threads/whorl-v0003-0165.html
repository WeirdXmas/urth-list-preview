1997whorlThe Book of the New SunJoshua A. Solomon

1997-02-22 Joshua A. Solomon #

Asks whether anyone has compiled a bibliography of Wolfe criticism, then proposes real-world locations for the New Sun rivers Nessus and Gyoll, suggesting the Parana and Uruguay rivers in Argentina. Grounds this on remembered clues: Nessus sits on a delta, the sea lies downstream, and mountains and warmer climate lie upstream, plus the town of Salto as a possible match for Saltus.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Gyoll is the Parana river; Nessus lies near Buenos Aires; Gyoll might be the Uruguay river

Advance apologies for lack of LONG SUN content.
Previously, someone mentioned literary criticism of Wolfe. Has anyone ever compiled a bibliography of Wolfe criticism? One puzzle from the NEW SUN that intrigued me was geography. Assuming that Urth resembles Earth, where are Nessus, Gyoll, etc. today? I cant remember all of the relevant clues (Nessus is on a delta; downstream is the sea; upstream is mountains and a warmer climate), but I was led to believe that Gyoll might be the Parna river in Argentina and Nessus might be in the vicinity of Buenos Aires. Alternatively, Gyoll might be the Uruguay (Argentinas border with Uruguay). Theres a town on the Uruguay called Salto which might correspond to NEW SUNs Saltus.
