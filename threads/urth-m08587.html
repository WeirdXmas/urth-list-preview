Update on _The Death of Doctor Island_

Urth list · 3 messages · 2009-01-30 to 2009-02-01

2009urthGwern BranwenNigel PriceKerry Benton
The thread: Readers asked about the status of a fan film adaptation of Wolfe's The Death of Doctor Island. Nigel Price reported that CGI fundraising had stalled and the project was probably paused rather than abandoned, and encouraged buying film frames to help. Gwern Branwen doubted that emailing the producers would yield much beyond official non-answers and wanted unofficial news. Kerry Benton mainly described buying a framed still and enjoying it whether or not the film is finished. The thread ended without new production information, drifting into a personal purchase.

2009-01-30 Nigel Price #

Reports the latest status of a film adaptation of Wolfe's story The Death of Doctor Island, saying fundraising for CGI effects is stalled and the project probably paused rather than abandoned. Answers Gwern Branwen's question about the launch date from what the poster last heard, adds that the producers can be e-mailed via the website, and encourages readers to buy film frames to help.
answers

Gwern Branwen asks concerning the movie version of "The Death of Doctor Island" and it's supposed launch date...

Good question. Last I heard, they were still trying to raise money for the CGI effects and were selling frames from the film on the website in an attempt to generate cash. The current economic climate is not exactly propitious, so my guess is that the project is still stalled, though not abandoned.

You can e-mail the producers if you're interested and ask how it's going. There's a contact address on the website.

I bought a frame from the movie, by the way. If any of you want this film to see the light of day...one day...I would encourage you to do the same.

Nigel
