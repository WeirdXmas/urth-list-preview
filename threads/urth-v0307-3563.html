doubling ideas ...

2003-07-05 Marc Aramini #

Reports that Marc Aramini found a web post repeating his own idea that Urth is doubled and his Miracle of Apu-Punchau reading, which he had written in the December archives and forgotten. Asks whether other list members have had the same experience of rediscovering an idea they thought was new, joking that nothing is new under the New Sun.
chats · theories: Urth is doubled; Miracle of Apu-Punchau reflects a doubled Urth

Boy, this is weird. Have you ever felt like you had an original idea, thought about it a little, got excited, and then realized that you had already posited the same idea long ago and dismissed it, but forgot all about it?

I was googling "Urth is doubled" and "Miracle of Apu-Punchau", and I found a fascinating post that was almost word for word what I wrote two days ago. However, when I looked at it, I realized that I had written it last December in the archives and completely forgotten about it. I knew that I had briefly entertained the notion of the doubing Urth, but I thought the Miracle of Apu-Punchau thing was a new spin on it, but jeez ... it's all right there in the December archives - in my ponderous and vague prose, too. Nothing is new under the New Sun, I guess.

Has this ever happened to anyone else on the list? Genuinely thinking you had a new idea only to discover that a much younger version of your self had gone through with far more alacrity than you are now capable of sustaining?

Marc Aramini
