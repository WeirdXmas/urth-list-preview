1998-08-19 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Informs a newcomer, Jonathan Laidlow, about books on The Book of the New Sun, identifying Wolfe's The Castle of the Otter and its Tor reprint Castle of Days alongside Lexicon Urthus. Notes that Joan Gordon's Gene Wolfe bibliography omits Lexicon Urthus and lists few books on the tetralogy besides the Bible and reviews.
informs · source-and-allusion

Hello Jonathan Laidlow.

Re: book(s) listed in Joan Gordon's GENE WOLFE; pertaining to THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN; specifically (?) Gene Wolfe writing about how he wrote TBOTNS (?).

The book in question would be THE CASTLE OF THE OTTER: A BOOK ABOUT THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN (1982). It has been reprinted by Tor in an omnibus edition with GENE WOLFE'S BOOK OF DAYS (1981) as CASTLE OF DAYS (1992).

The only other book about TBOTNS (that I'm aware of) is LEXICON URTHUS (1994), but it is not listed in Joan Gordon's GENE WOLFE (1986), nor does it have articles by Wolfe about the writing of TBOTNS.

Otherwise, the book(s) listed in Joan Gordon's GW in the chapter about TBOTNS: only THE BIBLE (the other citations are reviews of TBOTNS, an interview with Gene Wolfe, and an article for writers by Gene Wolfe).

I hope this helps.

=mantis=

1998-08-19 Stephen Witowski #

Recommends Castle of Days, which includes The Book of Days stories, new essays, and The Castle of the Otter, a book about The Book of the New Sun, and points the newcomer to UK bookshops. Responds to Jonathan Laidlow's request for information on Wolfe's writing of The Book of the New Sun, offering the poster's own edition and sources.
answers

Jonathan,

Another English fan here. You could do worse than picking up a copy of Wolfes non-fiction/fiction book "Castle of Days" which contains "The Book of Days" an excellent short story collection, some new essays and "The Castle of the Otter" which is described as a book about The Book of the New Sun. Unfortunatly I don't think it is in print anymore my edition is a US hardback from Tor. Try the Waterstones or Dillons websites for in print details. If you want more info E-mail me privately and we'll take it from there.

Good Hunting
