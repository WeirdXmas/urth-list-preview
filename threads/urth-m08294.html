Fantasy Casting Thread

Urth list · 30 messages · 2009-01-14 to 2009-01-16

2009urthThe Book of the New SunThe Book of the Long SunJohn WatkinsJeffrey Brent McBethDave LeblingAdam ThorntonJosh GellerGwern BranwenJames WynnJames CrossleySon of WitzStuart HammDan'l Danehy-OakesJohn Smith
The thread: Readers on the Urth list spent a long fantasy casting thread proposing actors for characters in The Book of the New Sun and Long Sun, starting with Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's list and Dave Lebling's Dorcas debate. Most posts were playful casting picks such as Johnny Depp for Severian, Brando for Baldanders, and Jimmy Stewart for Silk, while James Crossley disputed the Dorcas choice and Son of Witz and Adam Thornton argued over whether John Hurt suits the Autarch. Son of Witz pushed the topic furthest, offering Michael Trucco for Severian and linking Battlestar Galactica to Wolfe's themes and to Clive Barker's Imagica. The thread drifted into off-topic film chat and a minor correction about a sequel, ending without any textual consensus.
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: proposed a broad cast list and rejected Keanu Reeves as Severian
  • Son of Witz: most active proposer, offering Christian Bale, Michael Trucco and other picks with physical-description reasoning
  • Adam Thornton: argued John Hurt can play the effeminate Autarch
  • James Crossley: disputed Anne Hathaway as Dorcas and proposed Samantha Morton
  • John Smith: cast lesser-known actors by their past roles
  • Gwern Branwen: offered a single playful Brando suggestion

Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Robots, androids and artificial life, Books, libraries and writing

2009-01-14 Dave Lebling replying to James Crossley #

Proposes actor casting choices for Wolfe's minor characters, naming Erland van Lidth de Jeude for Baldanders, Marilyn Monroe for Jolenta, Natalie Wood for Dorcas and Anne Hathaway as a contemporary Dorcas. Offers a playful grounding: van Lidth de Jeude's role as a naviscaput in Time Bandits, and a claim that Wolfe said Monroe 'was' Jolenta. Responds to James Crossley's and Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's Dorcas casting debate, which proposed Samantha Morton.
chats

The late Erland van Lidth de Jeude would have been a perfect Baldanders. He actually played a naviscaput in "Time Bandits," and we all know that's how Baldanders would have ended up in time.

Marilyn Monroe was the only person who could have played Jolenta. I think Wolfe said explicitly that she "was" Jolenta.

The young Natalie Wood would have been perfect for Dorcas. Anne Hathaway isn't such a bad choice among contemporary actors though.

-- Dave Lebling, aka vizcacha
