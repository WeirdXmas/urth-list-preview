Malrubius's ghost

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-03-29 to 2001-03-29

2001whorlThe Book of the New SunNicholas Gevers

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2001-03-29 Nicholas Gevers #

Disputes the idea that Horn's astral self is the ghost of Malrubius, arguing it contradicts what The Book of the New Sun says about the aquastor Malrubius. Argues instead that Wolfe's SFBC note implies that accepting paranormal phenomena means accepting real ghosts, comparing it to Pike's ghost.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Horn's astral self is Malrubius's ghost; Malrubius's ghost is the deceased Master Malrubius

Re Wolfe's remarks in that SFBC catalogue: beware
Genes bearing gifts. The idea that Horn/Silk's astral
self is the ghost of Malrubius is very creaky, in
flagrant contradiction of everything imparted
concerning this aquastor or eidolon in THE BOOK OF THE
NEW SUN. Wolfe's SFBC Note actually means in all
likelihood that by accepting the existence of the
paranormal--astral projection and the like--we also
accept the literal reality of ghosts of the dead. Just
as the ghost of Pike was indeed Pike's spirit, so the
ghost of Malrubius is indeed the deceased Master
Malrubius. Nothing more complicated than that...

--Nick Gevers.
