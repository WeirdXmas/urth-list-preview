1997-02-23 Patrick O'Leary #

Informs readers that a Joshua Solomon reference may be the 1991 essay "Self-Referentiality in Gene Wolfe's 'Seven American Nights'" in the Journal of the Fantastic in the Arts, giving the volume, issue and editor. Replies to a list post by Mantis and offers the bibliographic details as a pointer for someone seeking the source.
informs · source-and-allusion

Joshua Solomon may be referring to:

"Self-Referentiality in Gene Wolfe's 'Seven American Nights'", Journal of the Fantastic in the Arts, Vol. 3, No. 2, Whole No. 10, 1991-- Journal of the Fantastic in the Arts, 1157 Temple Trail, Stow, OH 44224. Editor was: Carl B. Yoke.

Thanks for the list, Mantis.
