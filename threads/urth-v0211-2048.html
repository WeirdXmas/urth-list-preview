Themes: Christ figures and messiahs

2002-11-07 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Proposes that Typhon in SWORD is a Satan image, much as Severian is a Christ image, since Typhon undergoes a desert temptation. Ties the curtain scene with Piaton's body to the Oz line about the man behind the curtain, and mentions Roy C. Lackey's writing on the subject.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · source-and-allusion · theories: Typhon is a Satan image paralleling Severian as Christ; The curtain scene alludes to The Wizard of Oz

Okay, I assume everyone is agreed that Typhon, in SWORD, is a Satan image in much the same way & extent that Severian is a Christ(ian) image, right? I mean, he goes through the whole tempting in a desert place routine and all that, so anyway...

Roy C. Lackey wrote (among other stuff)...

It never occurred to me before that there was an Oz reference here. I mean, how are you supposed to deal with temptation? Ignore it until it leaves you alone.

In other words:

Pay no attention to the man behind the curtain ...

--Blattid
