Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

2012-03-16 Andrew Mason #

Argues that the word 'misinterpreted' is clear enough, and asks whether the correct reading of a passage means 'universe' in the sense that Severian's universe differs from ours, or the whole created order. Responds to Gerry Quinn's view that a playful theological speculation need not be a joke, raising the question of what the right interpretation would be.
questions · theology-and-religion

No, it doesn't have to be, but 'misinterpreted' seems fairly clear. (The question then is what would be the correct interpretation - does it mean 'universe', in the sense in which Severian's universe and ours are different, or does it mean the whole created order? This is a question that might not arise outside a science-fictional context, of course.)
