Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Memory and forgetting

2004-12-05 Dan Rabin #

Traces the Greek root of 'arch' in 'archive' to a word for town hall, arguing the Citadel's library holds the House Absolute's government records, including The Book of the New Sun. Asks why the Hypogeum Apotropaic, Underground Hall of Protection from Evil, is linked to Father Inire, and whether the Hypogeum Amaranthine means Severian's memory or the Autarchs' memories.
questions · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Hypogeum Amaranthine refers to Severian's memory; Hypogeum Amaranthine refers to Autarchs' transmitted memories

After my last message on vocabulary, I decided to look up some works in the _American Heritage Dictionary_, third edition.

It turns out that the "arch" in archive is derived from the same Greek word meaning "to rule" as "archon": it comes by way of a word meaning "town hall".

So it is entirely appropriate that the library/archive of the Citadel should also be that of the House Absolute--it's the set of records kept in the seat of government. And this particular archive is also the repository containing _The Book of the New Sun_, both Canog's and Severian's.

+++++

Thinking of the Greek words in TBotNS got me thinking about "Hypogeum Apotropaic". Has anyone figured out why the Underground Hall of Protection from Evil should be associated with Father Inire? There's also a "Hypogeum Amaranthine", (unfading, never withering). Severian's memory? The transmitted memories of the Autarchs?

-- Dan Rabin
