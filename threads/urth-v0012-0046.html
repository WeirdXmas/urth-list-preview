Shares film scores the poster listens to while reading The Book of the New Sun, such as Trevor Jones' Dark Crystal and Jerry Goldsmith's Legend score. Pairs specific scenes with music, linking the Skeksis emperor's funeral, the Vodalus sequences and the House Absolute to Alex North and Phillip Glass pieces, without making an argument.

Howdy folks,
I've been quite enjoying the list so far, and thought I'd jump in with a comment or two on the music angle.
My music tastes when reading the BotNS was to put on some film sound scores, particularly Trevor Jones' Dark Crystal (a longtime favorite), majestic, forlorn and moody as hell. The Funeral music for the Skeksis emperor fits the mood of the piece especially well. Other good sounds are Jerry Goldsmith's unused score for the Ridley Scott film Legend. Alex North's Cleopatra and Spartacus scores go well with the Vodalus sequences and Phillip Glass's Mishima is perfect for the House Absolute. Mike
