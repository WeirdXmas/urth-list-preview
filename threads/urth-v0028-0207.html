Question about Ossipago

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-10-17 to 1999-10-17

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1999-10-17 Daniel Fusch #

Asks whether readers can deduce from The Book of the New Sun alone that Ossipago is a robot, or whether the revelation in The Urth of the New Sun is an unfair withholding. Raises the detail that Ossipago's joints differ from a human's as a possible hint, and asks whether that implies mechanical nature.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Ossipago is a robot

Hello!

I also have a question about Ossipago, come to think of it. Can the reader intuit from reading The Book of the New Sun that Ossipago is a robot? If not, then it seems very unlike Gene Wolfe to throw that tidbit of information out there (in "Urth"), where the reader need not reach for it. That takes away half the fun!

On the other hand, it may be that the reader IS able to deduce it from hints dropped in "Book," but I missed the hints. I remember reading that Ossipago's joints did not match those of a human--but does this imply that he is mechanical in nature?

Any thoughts?

Daniel Fusch
