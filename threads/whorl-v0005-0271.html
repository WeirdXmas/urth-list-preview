1997-05-14 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Recounts sightings of Gene Wolfe's moustache at conventions in 1995 and 1996, noting it changed from a straight, Fu Manchu-like style to feathery ends. Also answers a question about the word "Cyphlothorax" by proposing "Cyphellothorax" as a coinage, meaning hollow of the ear breastplate, judged less euphonious than cephalothorax.

Re: Gene Wolfe's whiskers. I first saw 'em at World Fantasy Con in Baltimore, November 1995. At that time he told me they were still "under construction." Kind of straight--like early Fu Manchu.

I saw him next about six months later at Neb Award weekend in Long Beach. The ends were feathery at that time. Far more whimsical, no longer on the Manchu track.

That's what I know--others can chip in.
