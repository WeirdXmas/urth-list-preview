addendum on "Silhouette"

Urth list · 1 messages · 1997-07-13 to 1997-07-13

1997urthRon Crown

1997-07-13 Ron Crown #

Proposes that the planet name Neuerddraht in the story "Silhouette" is German, parsing it as neu (new), Erd (earth or world) and Draht (wire or cable), giving a rough sense of 'new world cable'. Adds a note that the ship's captain is female, a supplementary detail to his earlier post on the story.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Neuerddraht is a German compound meaning new world cable

I forgot to mention the name the crew of the ship gave to the planet-- Neuerddraht--German, obviously, neu is new, erd I take to be short for Erde, i.e., earth or world, and Draht means wire or cable. New world cable????

Oh, and the captain is a she, of course.
