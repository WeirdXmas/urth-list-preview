1999-09-15 Steve Taylor #

Asks where to buy Lexicon Urthus, noting Amazon's three-week backorder and requesting a mail-order source from Australia. Suggests the author, who he believes is on the list, might have unsold copies, so the post is a purchasing question with a light joke.
questions

The time has finally come for me to join the community of civilised readers and buy a copy of Lexicon Urthus - or at least persuade my girlfriend to buy me one for my birthday. Amazon has it listed as 'three week back order', which is Amazon speak for 'damned if we know how to get a copy, but we'll gladly take your credit card number'.

Is there any other (mail order from Australia) source for the book? I believe the author is on this mailing list. Perhaps he has a stack of unsold copies in his basement?

Steve
