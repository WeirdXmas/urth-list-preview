I'd say that we should definitely wait until we've heard from Wolfe on the first barrage of questions. Who knows what he'll say that will either be so oblique that we need to ask around it again, or what revelation might throw us on our collective ear. Also, we should see what is said on the on-line interview. After that, It's a good idea to give priority to those who didn't get a question in for the first round. This kills me say, because I REALLY want to ask the Pas-Typhon question. I'll just have to bide my time...

BTW, mantis, could you post the fifteen questions that made it into the hopper?
