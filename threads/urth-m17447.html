2011-01-04 Lee Berman #

Mocks Tony Ellis's stance on the Marsch replacement theory in The Fifth Head of Cerberus and asks him to say which of the two Shadow Children theories is stronger and why. Responds to Ellis's quoted claim that none of the replacement theories were good and that evidence supports two Shadow Children theories, questioning whether he is refusing to commit to a position.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: Dr. Marsch was replaced by a double; Shadow Children are human replacements

LMAO! Funny. So this is a roundabout way of saying....what? People who thought there was human replacement were not good Wolfe readers? Wolfe is not a good Wolfe reader? Wolfe lied or was confused in his interview?

Or are you now saying that even the theory of Marsch not being replaced is no good. So 5HoC is not a good book? Or it is a good book filled with nothing but bad evidence? Surely you can admit that what satifies you as a good theory is not going to be the same thing which satifies others as a good theory, yes?

Tony, you have a long history of refusing to pin yourself down to anything and subject yourself to criticism. But I admit that even recognizing this, you have confused me here on your stance or lack of one. Is there any hope?

So let's try this one.

Which of the two Shadow Children theories is much stronger and why?
