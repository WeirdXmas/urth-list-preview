The thread: Antonin Scriabin announced that Tor was running a March project pairing short stories with John Palencar paintings, and that the first Gene Wolfe story, Dormanna, was out, sharing enthusiasm without analysis. Fred Kiesche guessed the stories were posted individually rather than as one ebook, and Scriabin corrected him: the remaining stories appear weekly on the Tor site, while the full set is already in the ebook. Daniel Petersen praised the ending as awe-inspiring but said the illustration spoiled it, and Scriabin replied that reading the story before seeing the picture was the better order. The thread stayed light, with no real dispute and no substantive reading of the text.

  • Fred Kiesche: guesses the stories are posted individually rather than as a package
