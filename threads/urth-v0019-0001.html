1998-09-21 Marc Coleman #

Recommends Free Live Free and describes it as a film-noir comedy mystery about a man letting people stay free in a house slated for demolition, with a witch, a prostitute and a private detective. Responds to a request for a review and plot outline by giving a brief, recollected overview and calling it a lighter Wolfe book that still unsettles the reader.
informs

Been a while, but I remember it as a film-noir-comedy-mystery. Some guy named Free is letting people stay at his house free even tho it's going to be demolished in a few days. A witch, a prostitute, a private detective, you get the idea. I loved it, not New Sun but one of his weird sorta mainstream but Wolfe messes with yr head anyways books. I'd get it. marc of Serotonia

1998-09-22 Matthew Malthouse #

Reports that the poster finished Free Live Free on a first reading and felt unsatisfied, finding no clues or curiosities that sparked and not believing the denouement. Responds to a prior post asking for a review and plot summary, adding a personal verdict instead of answering those questions.
informs

I finished it (my first reading) last week and found myself rather unsatisfied.

Being Wolfe I was trying to keep my brain receptive to clues and curios but found nothing that really sparked. The denoument was particularly troubling. Frankly I just didn't believe it.

Is it me, or is the book?

Matthew
