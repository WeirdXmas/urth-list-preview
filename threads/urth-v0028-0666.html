GURPS New Sun

Urth list · 3 messages · 2000-01-02 to 2000-01-03

2000urthMichael BowmanRon Hale-EvansMichael Andre-Driussi
The thread: Readers thanked the author of the GURPS New Sun role-playing book, and Michael Andre-Driussi redirected gaming questions and errata to SJGames venues while encouraging Urth scenario and Pyramid submissions. Ron Hale-Evans argued that gaming talk should be anecdotes from campaigns or playtesters rather than rules questions, noting the game had over a year of playtesting. Michael Bowman, who had not read the book, reported receiving no errata and gave his contact address for corrections. The thread was brief and ended with no real disagreement, only a mild steer toward keeping game matters off the list.
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: gaming questions and errata belong on SJGames pages; readers should write Urth material for Pyramid
  • Ron Hale-Evans: share campaign anecdotes rather than gaming questions, given the year-long playtest
  • Michael Bowman: no errata received, has not read the book, supplied correct contact address

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2000-01-02 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Thanks fellow posters for kind words about the GURPS New Sun game and directs gaming questions and corrections to other venues, including SJGames pages and an errata contact. Encourages readers to write new Urth scenarios, rules additions or other material for submission to SJGames' Pyramid magazine.
informs

Ron Hale-Evans and Jim Henley,

Thank you both for your kind words.

We don't want to clutter up these places with gaming questions and comments, but I'm sure there are other places (especially over at the SJGames pages) where discussion would be welcome.

And of course corrections for GURPS New Sun should be sent to Michael Bowman <[email]>

(I don't know if they have posted an errata sheet yet.)

Having said that, I don't have much else to say, other than I hope you (Ron and Jim) and other people who get GURPS New Sun will take this opportunity to dabble in the world of Urth, writing new Wolfean scenarios, adventures, rules additions, or anything else, and sending it to SJGames for possible publication in their well-paying online magazine "Pyramid."

That would make me happy.

=mantis=

2000-01-02 Ron Hale-Evans #

Suggests that the poster share anecdotes from New Sun role-playing campaigns, either their own or playtesters', rather than adding gaming questions to the list. Responds to mantis's remark that gaming discussion belongs on the SJGames pages, noting GURPS New Sun was in playtest for over a year.
proposes

I also don't think it's a good idea to clutter the list with GURPS stuff, but maybe it wouldn't be amiss if you passed on a couple of anecdotes from New Sun campaigns that have already happened, either yours or those of playtesters. As I recall, _GURPS New Sun_ was in playtest for a year or more.

Ron
