Hethor's mirrors

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-07-02 to 1998-07-02

1998urthThe Book of the New Sunmary whalen

Themes: Mirrors, pictures and art

1998-07-02 mary whalen #

Proposes that Hethor's small hand mirrors could summon large creatures if arranged in a triangle so their light converges, since beings could be small originally or fold their bodies. Grounds this in the Book of the New Sun's description of the mirrors and in Jonas standing between the Inire's mirrors at the point of confluence to be sent away.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Hethor's small mirrors summon large creatures via converging light

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

A while ago I think there was some question about how big Hethor's mirrors would have to be for him to get all those creatures through them. It's possible that all the creatures had a small original form or that they can fold their bodies enough to make it through. That would take care of how Hethor could carry small mirrors around with him that bring out large beings.

However, the mirrors (that's what they're always called in the story, so I'll assume he has more than one) could summon large beings withought being large at all. With three small handmirrors arranged on the ground in a triangle with each bent back at an angle, in the area where light from the three combine, a fairly large creature could appear. Remember how Jonas never goes through the Inire's mirrors, he just stands between them at the point of confluence and is sent away.

prion
