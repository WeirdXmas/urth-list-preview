Cards as currency

Themes: Power, rulers and politics

1998-07-12 Brownes3 #

Argues Silk's cards are not interchangeable, since Malmeta needs a specific kind and tells them apart by shape, and that Pas spread them through the ship so no faction could control the landers. Disputes the idea that cards are interchangeable, citing the Silk and Malmeta conversation and recalling the cards' differing shapes from memory.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Cards are not interchangeable currency; Pas distributed cards to prevent faction control

They aren't interchangable. Look at the conversation between between Silk and Malmeta. He has lots of cards, but they are the wrong kind. She can't use all the ones he has and needs the right kind. Further, if memory serves, the cards are shaped differently and that's how Malmeta tells them apart.

I do agree with the notion of Pas' plan to keep everyone onboard. It's also likely that he distributed the different cards throughout the ship so no one faction could control the landers and therefore the colonies on the short sun world.

I'm glad someone else has just finished TBotLS, too. I've a few questions and observations to make also.

Rich Browne

1998-07-12 Brownes3 #

Argues that a proposed card-currency explanation is unlikely because it leaves unexplained what happened to the original currency, since no other money is mentioned even by nostalgic characters. Reasons from the absence of any mention of earlier money in the text, concluding cards must have been the only currency in the Whorl.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Cards were the only currency in the Whorl

Sensible, but not likely because of what it doesn't explain: what happened to the original currency, then? There's no mention of other currency, even by "good ole days" types of characters. Cards had to have been the only currency know in the Whorl.
