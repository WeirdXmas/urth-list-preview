2001-06-25 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Recommends watching the British television adaptation of the first two New Sun books if it is the series aired a couple of years earlier, noting its faithful plot and set design. Responds to Alga's question about the PBS two-parter by praising the look of the show and the eerie casting of characters such as Swelter and Flay.
informs

I don't know about PBS, but if this is the British series of a couple years ago, see it. Modulo some simplification, they got the plot of the first two books right (they only do the first two), the look is good, and several of the characters are downright eerie, notably Swelter and Flay.

--Dan'l
