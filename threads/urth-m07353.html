The thread: Brian Lovely asked what the ruined glass enclosure with odd trees that Horn and Seawrack find in The Book of the Long Sun is, suggesting it might be where the Green Man's race was made or linked to the treelike Neighbors, while admitting he was working from memory. Pedro Pereira replied that the enclosure was one of the last Neighbor sites before they left and asked whether Blue/Green is Urth's future system. He later rejected identifying Blue/Green with Urth or Lune, keeping an open mind, and then proposed that some Other Lords were once humans, such as Great Scylla transformed from Cilicia, citing the Lexicon Urthus. The thread ended without consensus, with the Blue/Green identification left unresolved and the human-origin idea tentative.

  • Pedro Pereira: the enclosure was a late Neighbor site and he asks whether Blue/Green is Urth's future
  • Pedro Pereira: rejects Blue/Green as Urth, Lune, or a combination, keeping an open mind
  • Pedro Pereira: some Other Lords were once human, e.g. Great Scylla from Cilicia, per the Lexicon, admitted uncertain

Asks what the ruined glass enclosure that Horn and Seawrack find on an island in The Book of the Long Sun is, suggesting it may be a greenhouse where the Green Man's race was made or linked to the treelike Neighbors. Admits working from memory without the books, so the description of the glass circle, its directional light and the odd trees is tentative.
questions · theories: Ruined glass circle is a greenhouse where the Green Man's race was made

The glass intensifies light in one direction. There are some kind of large or unusual trees or bushes in the area (I think I'm remembering this correctly.

What the heck is this? It sounds like a greenhouse to me. Somewhere where the Green Man's race was made? Something to do with the treelike Neighbors?

2008-09-18 Pedro Pereira replying to Brian Lovely #

Asks whether the ruined glass-walled enclosure with unusual trees that Horn and Seawrack find in The Book of the Long Sun is a place where the Green Man's race was made, and why the Neighbors are called treelike. Replies to Brian Lovely by recalling the enclosure as one of the last Neighbor sites before they departed and asking whether Blue/Green is Urth's future system where the Green Man originated.
questions · source-and-allusion

I think it was what was suggested in the books: one of the last spots of the Neighbors before they departed. I don't remember anything unusual about the trees.

By the way, why "treelike" Neighbors? As for the Green Man's race, I guess you are assuming that Blue/Green is Urth's system in the future and that is where the green man originated?

Pedro

2008-09-19 Pedro Pereira replying to Pedro Pereira #

Proposes that some of the Other Lords were once humans, suggesting Great Scylla may have been transformed from Cilicia, citing Andre's Lexicon Urthus entry on Typhon's rebellious family. Grounds this in the Lexicon's note that Typhon was called Father of Monsters, while admitting he cannot recall where the idea came from and would need to reread the books.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Great Scylla was Cilicia transformed into an Other Lord; Some Other Lords were once humans

By the way, I was doing some bathroom reading of LEXICON URTHUS and under the entry Typhon Andre says that some of Typhon's rebelious family members possibly turned into Other Lords and so he came to be also known as "the Father of Monsters". This seems to give some substance to my previous assumption that Great Scylla could have been Cilicia (or whatever her name was) "transformed" into one of the monstrous Lords. Although that doesn't seem to be the case with Cilicia herself (because of Short Sun) it shows that my idea that some of the Lords had been humans before maybe was not all that wrong after all. The problem remains that I don't remember where I got that idea from. I would have to re-read the Book of the New Sun as I'm sure it was there or on Urth of the New Sun.

Pedro
