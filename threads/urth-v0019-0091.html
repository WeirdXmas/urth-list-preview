Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

1998-10-11 mary whalen #

Asks whether the man living in the house in "In Looking Glass Castle" could be a figure who lives a double life, dressing as a woman and going out, so that he might be mentioned yet disguised and could have boarded the boat at the end. Grounds the idea in how such a disguise would make his survival easier than the hallucinatory reading suggests, and invites others to name who he could be.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: the house man is disguised and boards the boat

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

I recently read "In Looking Glass Castle" and wondered if the man living in the house might seem less like a hallucinatory problem if he actually dressed like a woman and had a life outside of the house. It would make it easier for him to survive. If he is mentioned in the book but is in disguise, it might be that he could have gotten on the boat at the end of the book. Does anyone have any ideas about who he could be?

prion
