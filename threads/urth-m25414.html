the stag at bay and the deer treaty skin

Urth list · 1 messages · 2014-01-01 to 2014-01-01

2014urthMarc Aramini

2014-01-01 Marc Aramini #

Argues that the stag at bay and John Weer taking the dog boy hunting echo the deerskin treaty imagery. Points to Olivia's joking "chief White Fawn" remark and Joe's gun and wooden dog tied to Bobby Black's death, then asks why John takes the dog boy hunting.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Stag at bay echoes deerskin treaty imagery

Well, now that I consider it, the stag at bay and weer's father taking the dog boy to hunt resonates with the imagery of the deer skin that the women write the treaty for the land on in imitation of the Indian agreement which becomes accepted as the real document, shot by John Weer, jokingly called chief White Fawn by Olivia in that scene. )His long dead brother Joe has a gun and a wooden dog in the picture that leads to Bobby Black's death.) but still ... Why would John take the dog boy hunting?
