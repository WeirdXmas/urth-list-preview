1997-02-15 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Jokingly reports that reading a whole Wolfe short story collection in a day or two gives the poster a headache, like eating something too cold too fast, so they ration the stories over a week. Replies to Dr. Gouvea, whose earlier post is quoted in the auto-reply header, and treats the stories as too concentrated to binge.

Dr. Gouvea,

I love Wolfe's short stories, but I confess that on those occassions when I have read an entire collection through in a day or two, I tend to get the kind of headache we normally associate with the result of eating something very cold (like an Orange Julius drink, or a milk shake) too fast. Personal weakness. So I have to limit myself on those short stories, make the book last at least a week--they are just too concentrated!
