Novel 3: Devil in a Forest

Themes: Myth and folklore sources

2013-06-23 Marc Aramini #

Argues that The Devil in a Forest is set in a fictionalized version of Central Europe rather than England, pointing to a Slavic-seeming setting despite the English names. Grounds this in a St. Agnes well or fountain said to be a tourist stop near Prague and in hints that Baba Yaga's chicken-footed hut echoes Mother Cloot's hut.
proposes · source-and-allusion · genre-and-craft · theories: The Devil in a Forest is set in fictionalized Central Europe

Perhaps it is England. Yet the presence of st. Agnes well/fountain which is a tourist stop near Prague and the hints that the tales of Baba Yaga chicken footed hut refer to mother Cloot's hut are a bit more Slavic in their character, despite the names of the characters. It would have to be a fictionalized tourist stop, then.
