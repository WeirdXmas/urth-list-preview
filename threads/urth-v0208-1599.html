2002-08-23 Marc Aramini #

Proposes that the victim of the coldhouse prank in Peace was Sherry Gold's brother, who may have confronted Weer about his sister after taking a job there, giving Weer a motive to stage it. Grounds this in recalled details of the brother's strained family ties and the job-seeking, and notes that Gold's and Weer's motives fit this reading while admitting the poster needs to reread the novel.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Sherry Gold's brother was the coldhouse prank victim; Weer staged the prank to eliminate Gold's brother

I had always assumed that the victim in the coldhouse prank was Sherry Gold's brother, who was not on the best of terms with his parents and wanted to move out (as I recall from a readiing a few years ago - or was he already living in a boardinghouse?) Perhaps he confronted Weer about his sister after getting a job there (he was looking for a job, right?) and then Weer took the opportunity to play a convenient prank on him. Of course, I need to read Peace again. Just my two cents. At least Gold has motive to get close to Weer, and Weer has reason to get rid of Sherry's brother. Marc Aramini

2002-08-23 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that the coldhouse prank happened in 1938, before Sherry Gold was born, so the prank victim could not be Sherry's brother as Aramini assumed. Supports the point with date arithmetic: Weer met Sherry around 1954, so she was only just born at the time of the prank.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic

The coldhouse prank was in 1938.

Weer met Sherry around 1954.

Sherry was only just born at the time of the prank (1954 - 16 = 1938). Which is rather weird, come to think of it.
