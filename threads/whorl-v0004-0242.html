1997-03-16 Alice Turner #

Disputes the idea that Orchid's manse in The Book of the New Sun is a Chicago location tied to the Playboy Mansion, noting the mansion's real-world home is in Los Angeles. Argues instead that Wolfe used a brothel to bring women into his lowlife cast, pointing to the similar establishment on Sainte Croix as evidence against the Chicago reading.
disputes · source-and-allusion · author-and-biography · theories: Orchid's manse is a Chicago Playboy Mansion

Oh, yes, though it's surely only a local joke, I hope that no one is going to pursue the idea of Orchid's manse as part of Chicago geography. For one thing, the Playboy Mansion as hub of the universe has for the past quarter-century been located in LA (Holmby Hills, to get technical). (The old Chicago building houses Art Institute grad students now.) For another, what about the similar, though more upscale establishment on Sainte Croix--would you try to implicate that too? It's much more likely that Wolfe wanted to get women into his lowlife dramatis personae (everyone we meet in Viron except for the religious figures is pretty low on the social-educational scale) and using a brothel was the easiest way to do it. Hollywood thinking, I'd say.
