2001-06-21 Alice Turner #

Expresses doubt that the forthcoming book Aelfrice will be good, calling it a likely dreadful medieval sword-and-sorcery work and finding its name trite. Responds to Robert Borski's wish to dream of the book's wonders, offering a skeptical reaction based only on the title and premise rather than any reading of the text.
disputes

I hope this book is better than it sounds. It sounds like one more dreadful "medieval" s&s mess that will have to clamber out of a swamp of competition. The very name "Aelfrice" strikes a dauntingly trite note.

-alga
