Proposes that the mother and brides of Abaia in The Book of the Long Sun may connect to the megatherians, possibly as vanished gods, and asks for input. Grounds the idea in earlier list speculation that Pas's family are megatherians and in Borski's Solar Labyrinth remark that Typhon was a neighbor.

Proposes that the beans in Jonas's story are cells from which the Vanished Gods grew bodies to download into, making the megatherians the same kind of grown vessels used to create humans on the Whorl. Offers this as a speculative link between the Long Sun story and the Vanished Gods, asking the list for their views.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Megatherians are bodies grown by the Vanished Gods

So maybe the "beans" in Jonas story are a reference to cells from which the Vanished Gods grew bodies to download into becoming the megatherians in the same way they grew humans on the whorl. Any thoughts?
