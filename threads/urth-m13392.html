Dorcas (was: The mystery of the image of an astronaut cleaned by Rudesind)

Urth list · 23 messages · 2010-07-09 to 2010-07-10

2010urthThe Book of the New SunRyan DunnJosh GellerAntónio Pedro MarquesJames WynnTony EllisDan'l Danehy-OakesRoy C. Lackey
The thread: The thread asked whether the Dorcas-as-Severian's-grandmother puzzle (linked to the astronaut image cleaned by Rudesind) was a neglected or late-recognized clue. James Wynn argued the late-1990s list debate and simian hints made it visible, but was challenged on whether the theory was convincingly textual and whether it was ever really hidden. Tony Ellis and Ryan Dunn argued the text itself makes the grandmother reading clear and that readers solved it in the 1980s, citing a 1991 Vector article. António Pedro Marques repeatedly questioned Wynn's claims about how he came to see the signals. Heated exchanges led to a mutual apology on July 10, and the thread ended in personal reconciliation without resolving the textual question.
  • James Wynn: the Dorcas grandmother reading was debated in the late 1990s and simian hints signal it, though it was never convincing and may be a feint
  • Tony Ellis: the text makes Dorcas as grandmother clear, so the theory was never neglected and readers solved it in the 1980s
  • Ryan Dunn: Dorcas is Severian's grandmother who with the boatman produced Ouen, who fathered Severian through Catherine, all supported by the text
  • António Pedro Marques: challenged Wynn to explain what made the signals obvious and how his awareness arose
  • Roy C. Lackey: cited the Lexicon Urthus entry naming Dorcas as paternal grandmother and redirected quotations to other threads

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Fathers, mothers and children, Names and their meanings

Argues that the Lexicon Urthus entry naming Dorcas as Severian's paternal grandmother was placed in the commentary, where Mantis tended to put speculation, so it may have been a feint. disputes · puzzle-solving · author-and-biography · theories: Dorcas is Severian's grandmother

This is an excellent example of what I'm talking about.

In the definition for Dorcas, Mantis places her status as Severian's grandmother in the commentary...a location that, in the 1st edition, he seems to have favored for speculation and spoilers. Far less often for self-evident points of fact. And as Tony Ellis IIRC referenced, many people viewed it as a feint. And why wouldn't they? Sev's opinion about Ouen's and Catharine's connection is rather peculiar: "A woman you loved or perhaps only one who loved you".

I'm not going to disparage those who had alternate readings in this case. Suffice that Wolfe has instilled extreme paranoia in his readership and inclined them to straining at ants. I don't say he has no one to blame but himself, but he bears a large culpability.

2010-07-09 Ryan Dunn replying to James Wynn #

Argues that Dorcas is Severian's grandmother, that she and the boatman produced Ouen, and that Ouen fathered Severian through Catherine, claiming the text itself supplies these explanations. Grounds the case on the birth exposition at the Matachin Tower and on Severian sharing Ouen's described profile, replying to James Wynn's claim about when the idea appeared.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Dorcas is Severian's grandmother; Dorcas and the boatman parented Ouen; Ouen fathered Severian through Catherine

I've read BotNS twice through, and am on a third scouring, non-linear, clue hunting, note-taking. I came to the grandma Dorcas without much effort, which is saying alot if you've read my previous argument starters (aka mailing list threads).

But I think the point was, it wasn't explained in the late 90's, but rather in the mid-80's when the books were published, because the explanations are in the text, quite obviously in my opinion.

Dorcas and the boatman birthed 'You-Are-My-Mother-Come-Again' Ouen.

Ouen knocked up Catherine, who was handed to the torturers where she birthed Severian (plenty exposition going to great lengths to explain what happens when a woman gives birth while a client of the Matachin Tower.

And finally, Severian clearly described as sharing Ouen's profile.

Done and done, as far as I can deduce.

Now, about those monkeys........

...ryan

Argues that Dorcas being Severian's grandmother is made transparently clear in the text itself, not just in list criticism, so the theory was never neglected.
