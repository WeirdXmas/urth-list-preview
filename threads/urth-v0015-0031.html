1998-07-02 mary whalen #

Relays a half-joking theory that the Cumaean is an inhumus, then asks why Father Inire, Ossipago, Barbatus and Famulimus, who call her a hierodule in The Urth of the New Sun, would not know this. Grounds the question in the explicit text on page 357 of that book, which contradicts the proposed identity.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: The Cumaean is an inhumus

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Alga has a (half-joking) theory that the Cumaean is an inhumus. However, why don't Father Inire, Ossipago, Barbatus, and Famulimus know this? They all say she is a Hierodule (The Urth of the New Sun, p. 357).

prion
