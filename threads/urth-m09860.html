AEG: Any clues from Wolfe?

Urth list · 3 messages · 2009-10-07 to 2009-10-07

2009urthDave TallmanMatthew KeeleyKieran Mullen
The thread: Kieran Mullen asked whether Gene Wolfe has given any clues confirming speculative readings of An Evil Guest, such as Cassie being Pavlatos, multiple Reises, or Margret as a werewolf, and worried the discussion had drifted beyond textual or authorial support. Matthew Keeley answered that at Readercon Wolfe spoke of enjoying writing the novel but gave no real plot hints beyond calling Reis a villain who wanted to rule the world. Dave Tallman then asked the same question again, quoting Kieran's report of a clue that Cassie loved Bill Reis and went to Woldercan, which Kieran said had fueled time-travel and doppelganger theories. The thread stayed a short question-and-answer exchange with no real resolution, ending with the consensus that Wolfe offered little concrete help.

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Time loops and causality
