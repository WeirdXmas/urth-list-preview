Space Monkeys?

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1999-11-16 William H. Ansley #

Jokingly proposes that the inhumi of the Long Sun books are essentially giant Sea Monkeys, since they survive mummified, depend on humans and take on human traits. Responds to Alice Turner's quoted post about the secret of the inhumi, dismissing her conjectures and offering the comparison as a light-hearted theory.
proposes · theories: The inhumi are giant Sea Monkeys

Alga,

Wait a minute! The inhumi can survive "mummified" for long periods of time, they depend on humans for sustenance and they take on human characteristics*.

These guys aren't evil, they're just really big Sea Monkeys!

(For those of you who don't have any idea what I'm talking about, please ignore me. I'll probably stop if you ignore me.)

William Ansley

*I'm basing this on the picture on the Sea Monkey box.
