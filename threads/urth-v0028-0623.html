Themes: Stories within stories

1999-12-13 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Corrects a credit by naming Julia Ecklar as the writer and recorder of two songs, and lists story titles from the stories-within-stories material. Suggests "The Legend of Xi Cygnus" may be a brown book tale without Urth or Skuld, and that "The God and His Man" is almost certainly one, citing modern touches shared with "The Old Woman Whose Rolling Pin Is the Sun." Responds to no one in particular.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: The Legend of Xi Cygnus is a brown book tale

Right--Julia Ecklar (sp?) (Forgive me, I'm still operating on fritzy brain alone) wrote and recorded the two songs.

Another note: the other story title is "The Sailor Who Sailed After the Sun," and it is on Earth.

Still more: "The Legend of Xi Cygnus" just might be a brown book story, but no Urth or Skuld; and a few more modern touches (to be fair, the tribal story "The Old Woman Whose Rolling Pin Is the Sun" has many of the same modern bits). "The God and His Man" almost certainly is a brown book tale (but maybe not).

=mantis=
