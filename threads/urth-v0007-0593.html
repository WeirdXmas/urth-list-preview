BotNS Copyright Concerns.

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-03-04 to 1998-03-04

1998urthThe Book of the New SunC R Culver

1998-03-04 C R Culver #

Asks whether Tor and Wolfe would object to a t-shirt printed with the Urth of the New Sun title page and to a project typing the full Book of the New Sun text for private study. Raises copyright concerns about both, noting that a public web page would be infringement while private use by owners might not be.
questions

I've been wondering for some time now just how sensitive Tor and Wolfe would be to the use of BotNS text and pictures. I seek two things that could be taken as copyright infringement:

1) I want to go up to the local mall and get a t-shirt made with the title page of UotNS on the front (you know, the handsome calligraphy like on the _Soldier of Arete_ cover) and would not like to be confronted by a lawyer the second I put it on.

2) It'd be nice if someone or someones could begin a project of typing in the entire BotNS, including the UotNS, so that it would be handy for citation and quoting when a person if authoring e-mail relating to it. This would most certainly be seen as infringement if it was placed on an easily-accessible web page, but I want it for the private use of those who already own the BotNS and are just studying it.

Christopher R. Culver <[email]>
http://members.aol.com/crculver/index.html
