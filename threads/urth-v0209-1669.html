Reports that Locus for September announces Gene Wolfe has sold and delivered a new, untitled story collection to Tor. Also points out a Locus interview with Wolfe conducted by Neil Gaiman, his collaborator on A Walking Tour of the Shambles, and notes the interview offers interesting insights.
