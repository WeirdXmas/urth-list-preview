1998-11-18 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Announces three new booklets of notes and synopses on Wolfe's books, listing their contents, prices and postage. Describes Additions, Errata, &cetera volume 3, NS-1 synopsis with appendices, and LS-2 on Long Sun languages, with topics such as the three universe model and Urth's power grids.
informs

This seems like a good time to mention that there are three new booklets available right now:

"Additions, Errata, &cetera: volume 3" (1998) is the final booklet of notes, weighing in at 11,000 words. This volume's celebrity contributor is David Langford. Topics include: the three universe model, mechanics of mirror magic, Valeria's Reign, the black hole in the dying sun, notes on Eschatology & Genesis, astereoengineering, Kardashev levels of interstellar civilization, Urth's power grids, and much more. Price: $3 (plus postage).

"NS-1: a synopsis of Severian's narrative" (1998). A chapter by chapter summary of The Book of the New Sun and The Urth of the New Sun. Appendices for "Sailor Talk" (listing all the Wellerisms used by the sailors); "Authorized Texts" (the stock phrases used by Ascians); a newly revised and expanded Timeline of Posthistory; and a "Stream of Symbols." Price: $5 (plus postage).

"LS-2: Languages of the Long Sun Whorl" (1998) covers all the slang, cant, jargon, and foreign language of the Long Sun series. Price: $5 (plus postage).

(Other booklets available.)

Postage cost: domestic US, $.60 per booklet. International Airmail: $2 for one booklet, $3.50 for two, $5 for three.
