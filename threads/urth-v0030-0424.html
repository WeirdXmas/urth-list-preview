2001-05-17 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Disagrees with Mantis's reading that the national crisis in Wolfe's novel is a replay of the Eden story, arguing the signs of crisis are ambiguous and that the NP alone may be in trouble. Compares Wolfe's method to the Narnia books, suggesting he writes symbolically rather than allegorically, with patterns recurring across different worlds.
disputes · allegory-and-typology · genre-and-craft · theories: National crisis is a replay of Eden

Don't like it, but have to agree. (Side note -- Lilith is often identified with the Greek snake-women, lamiae, which adds flavor to this.)

Right.

Realistically, _none_ of this makes sense...

I can't help thinking you're seeing what you want to see there -- indications of crisis are ambiguous at best; I suspect that, if there's a crisis going on, it's only a crisis for the NP, not for the whole country.

I certainly think you're reading-into the conversation with the security guy.

I think a good analogy here is to the Narnia books -- it isn't a "replay," but, if God made multiple worlds, how things might play themselves out, differently but according to the old patterns, in those worlds. Like those, I don't think Wolfe writes to be taken allegorically, but perhaps symbolically.

--Blattid

2001-05-18 Michael Straight #

Suggests that C.S. Lewis's Perelandra offers a better comparison than Narnia, with a fallen human acting as agent of a second fall on an Edenic world. Builds on Danehy-Oakes's Eden-replay question by proposing that the New Pilgrim, a fallen human, would threaten an unfallen Eden in Wolfe's Lilith story.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: The New Pilgrim as agent of a second fall

Better yet, Lewis's Perelandra, in which a human from Earth becomes the agent of evil attempting to orchestrate a second fall on the Edenic Venus.

I like the idea that, disasterous as Lilith is running amok in our world, the NP, a fallen human, would be a worse threat to unfallen Eden. (But it only works if Lilith is not talking about Cain and Abel after all.)

-Rostrum
