Silk in Corridors of Time?

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-04-18 to 1997-04-18

1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunDave Lebling

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Time loops and causality

1997-04-18 Dave Lebling #

Argues that the corridor episode in The Book of the Long Sun (chapter 8) is a dream, citing the blue tunic Silk takes from the house where he is held prisoner by Oosik and the fading of "of Pas" in the words he writes. Responds to mantis and Xury, who speculated whether Silk was in the corridors of time at that point.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Silk's corridor episode is a dream

mantis and xury speculate whether Silk may have been in the corridors in III, 290 (ch. 8).

My guess is that this is a dream -- the same blue tunic that Silk takes from the house where he is held prisoner by Oosik figures in the episode. It has a definite dream-like quality; the "of Pas" fading in the words Silk writes, and so on.

-vizcacha
