1998-11-06 Michael Straight #

Recommends a literary website that has few Wolfe pages but strong sections on Borges, Eco, Pynchon, Joyce, Kafka and García Márquez, giving its archive address. Responds to Alex David Groce, who had mentioned a Wolfe-Borges page on the site quoting a McCaffrey interview, and describes the site as one of the best literary websites seen.
informs

That whole website might be of interest to Wolfe fans. He has very little Wolfe stuff as yet, but he's got some wonderful pages about Borges, Eco, Pynchon, Joyce, Kafka, and Gabriel Garcia Marquez. It's one of the best "literary" websites I've seen.

Start here:

http://www.rpg.net/quail/libyrinth/archives.html

-Rostrum
