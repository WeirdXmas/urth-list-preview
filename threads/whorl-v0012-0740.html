Political Inhumi

Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Power, rulers and politics

2001-04-26 James Jordan #

Argues that the inhumi's control over other peoples is learned from humans, drawing on Jesus's teaching that rulers lord it over others while disciples must be servants of all. Responds to Adam's quoted warning that inhumi could come to control the humans on Blue, saying the inhumi will cease to be inhumi once humans stop being inhuman.
disputes · theology-and-religion · theories: Inhumi learn domination from inhuman humans

Interesting observation. This is something they learn from inhuman humans. As Jesus said, and Wolfe no doubt agrees, the kings of the earth love to lord it over others. The disciples, however, must be "servants of all." When humans stop being inhumans, the humi will stop being inhumi.
