2001-02-05 David DiGiacomo #

Answers Tim Boolos's questions on Return to the Whorl, proposing that Dr. Crane entered Pig to save him after Pig killed the augur, and that Crane's ghost may be only a memory. Grounds this in Crane watching through the window, and notes that Horn's statement does not imply Nettle is dead, while speculating on Horn's motives and lander parts.
answers · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Dr. Crane entered Pig to save him; Crane's ghost was only a memory

I certainly don't qualify, but I won't let that stop me.

He was observing through the window as usual, and saw Pig kill the augur. It seems in character for him to enter Pig to understand/save him.

A manifestation of the Neighbors? (OK, not enough limbs.)

Perhaps just a memory rather than a ghost.

That confused me also, but in fact he didn't "use it", so the statement doesn't imply that Nettle is dead.

To another star, to continue the plan of Passilk. We don't know how many landers are left, where they are, or if it's possible to construct new ones. Sequel alert :-)

Perhaps Horn killed the Inhumu on Green.

After the wedding, he claims that he didn't intend the battle - that he wished to kill only himself. Of course, he did invite Gyrfalcon.

Horn led his bandits against Sinew's village to try to find a waveguide coupling to repair the lander on Green. (But why wouldn't Sinew give his father whatever waveguide accessories he had? Just out of spite?)

We can hope that Horn arranged to free them on Green.

I think the Mother gave it to Scylla, as she had given Seawrack the silver ring.

Horn bound Silk's wounds with Hyacinth's hanky, and received first aid from the corn farmer's wife and Tansy. We know that Silk's body has unusual recuperative powers.
