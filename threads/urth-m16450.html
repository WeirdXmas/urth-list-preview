Severa

The thread: The thread asked whether Severian had a twin sister named Severa and, if so, which character she might be, with Jolenta and Merryn the main candidates. Lee Berman argued Jolenta is the ex-witch Severa and that the cast staged a test of Severian, while Gerry Quinn disputed conspiracy readings and suggested Merryn at most, with the witches naming the twin and the torturers naming Severian. David Stockhoff argued Severa is a placeholder and not a twin's name, then floated the maid as Severa, while Andrew Mason and andrew.mason53 pressed the twin reading from the name. The debate ended in impasse, drifting into side arguments over the inhumi secret and in some chatty posts.
  • Lee Berman: Jolenta may be Severa, the ex-witch, in a staged test of Severian; later dismisses the 'no sister' view and calls the inhumi reveal anticlimactic
  • Gerry Quinn: Severian's name implies a twin; torturers named the boy, witches named a sister Severa; rejects Jolenta and conspiracy readings
  • David Stockhoff: Severa is only a placeholder with no established name; Severian is a plain Latinate name; maid may be Severa
  • Larry Miller: Jolenta's red-gold hair links her to the Pelerines and possibly Catherine; leg injuries are too coincidental
  • Andrew Mason: the inhumi secret was meant to be obvious to readers, and Merryn's ignorance is no real puzzle
  • andrew.mason53: Severian's brother-sister name shows a sister was present, so the twin is real

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Fathers, mothers and children, Names and their meanings
