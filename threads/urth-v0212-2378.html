2002-12-10 Marc Aramini #

Reports that a recent message posted to the Urth list was an identity scam from Nigeria, and asks whether others received the same message in their individual inboxes. Says the poster got a similar scam message privately from a different sender the week before.
informs

I scanned the recent message on the Urth archive with some chagrin - what has the world come to when a Wolfe mailing list is used to pull off an identity scam in Nigeria? It's kind of fitting. I had a similar message in my individual inbox last week, from a different sender. Anyone else get this message individually, or was it just me? Take care.
Marc Aramini
