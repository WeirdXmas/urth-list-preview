Dog-headed ape

Themes: Myth and folklore sources

2003-08-01 Sikozu Sta.-Ana #

Explains that the cynocephalus, or dog-headed ape, was the baboon in the ancient world, a symbol of barbarian hordes and wild lands, and that St. Christopher was a baboon converted to Christianity. Offers a reference book on the dog motif in Eurasian myth, MYTHS OF THE DOG-MEN, and says it can be cited later.
informs · source-and-allusion · myth-and-structure

"Dog-headed ape" (CYNOCEPHALUS) is the baboon, known in the ancient world as a symbol of the barbarian hordes and the wild lands. St. Christopher, defender of the Faith, was a baboon who was converted to Christianity.

No, I'm not making this up. Look it up yourself if you doubt it.

Anyway, there's a whole book on the motif of the dog in Eurasian myth, from Asia to India to Europe, called MYTHS OF THE DOG-MEN. Anyone wants the cite I can pass it on (right now in the wrong place to do so).
