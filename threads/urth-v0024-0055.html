1999-03-11 William H. Ansley #

Reports that the poster has not finished the next installment of an Oz essay series and has been rereading Baum's Oz books, noting that The Tin Woodman of Oz is gruesome because its characters cannot die even when chopped apart. Gives no Wolfe content; the post is an update on the poster's own research schedule and a passing remark about the Oz books.
informs

I will not have the next installment ready to post today. I am continuing to do research as I go along and have re-read _The Lost Princess of Oz_ and _The Tin Woodman of Oz_, the eleventh and twelfth in the series. There is very little in these two books that has a bearing on my essays, but I will comment in passing that _Tin Woodman_ is quite gruesome. It would be hard to make a wholesome family movie out of this book and stay true to it. You see, everyone who lives in Oz is immortal and they don't die, even if chopped to bits.

William Ansley
