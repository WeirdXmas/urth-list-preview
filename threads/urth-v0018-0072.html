1998-09-01 Jonathan Laidlow and Rachel Williams #

Informs readers that Lake of the Long Sun remains in print in the UK and offers to supply details of British internet booksellers. Also notes that a Birmingham bookshop will mail science fiction anywhere in the world on credit card payment.
informs

Lake of the Long Sun is still in print in the UK - I can provide details of Uk internet booksellers who will send copies out if you like. Also, Andromeda Books here in Birmingham, England, will send SF out to anywhere in the world if you have a credit card.
