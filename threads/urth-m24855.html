Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

2013-10-10 Jensen Webb #

Proposes that Blue, the planet in the Long Sun and Short Sun books, is actually the ship Yesod arriving in Briah to monitor the shaping of Urth and Green. Grounds this on thematic parallels, noting Yesod has no moon as Urth's moon is gone, and on Blue's lack of nutrients, while admitting no textual evidence is at hand.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Blue is the ship Yesod

Hello,my first posting here. I have lurked for a while now, and I have great interest in how ALL of Wolfe's stories are connected together, thematically and literally.

As far as Blue's identity goes, could it be the ship planet Yesod coming into Briah to directly monitor their molding of earth/urth/ushas/green? I don't have any textual evidence at hand, but there might be some thematic similarities (yesod has no moon, and urth's moon is gone ). The lack of nutrients on Blue also makes me think it could be a ship pretending to be a planet. This creates a flood of problems that probably can't be fixed, but I thought to throw it out there.

Apologies in advance if my post adds nothing to the discussion.
