Inhuma on Urth?

Urth list · 1 messages · 2008-12-02 to 2008-12-02

2008urthThe Book of the New SunO'Donnell, Tim (BOSI)

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2008-12-02 O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) #

Asks whether the inhuma, a creature from Wolfe's Book of the New Sun, appears on Urth, suggesting that the bite Jolenta receives in the night and the reptile Severian sees wrapped around a stick at the witches' ritual could be one. Grounds the question in those episodes after the play at House Absolute, admitting the evidence is thin.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: The old woman is an inhuma

Another question that struck me recently - are there Inhuma on Urth? When Baldanders/Talos and Severian/Jolenta/Dorcas part in the woods after the staging of the play at House Absolute Jolenta is bitten in the night by some creature. Shortly afterwards the group meet the witches and participate in the ritual with the necromancer/vivimancer. During this incident Severian sees the old woman as a reptile wrapped around a stick. Could this be an inhuma?

Very little to go on I know...
