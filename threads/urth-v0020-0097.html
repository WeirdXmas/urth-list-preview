Wolfe's Swords

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-10-22 to 1998-10-22

1998urthThe Book of the Long SunMichael Straight

1998-10-22 Michael Straight #

Lists the magical and notable swords in Wolfe's fiction, including Terminus Est, Silk's Azoth, Latro's magic sword, Weer's penknife, and Excalibur in Castleview, and asks whether Wat has a special sword in The Devil in a Forest. Responds to Mark Millman's remark on swords as symbolic freight by offering a catalogue of examples rather than an argument.
questions

Also, Wolfe just loves magic or otherwise nifty swords. Can we name them all?

Terminus Est
Silk's Azoth
Silk's walking stick
Latro's magic sword
Weer's penknife (as Chesterton says, a penknife is more romantic than a sword, because it is a secret sword)
Excalibur in Castleview
Doesn't Wat have some kind of special sword in "A Devil in the Forest"?

-Rostrum
