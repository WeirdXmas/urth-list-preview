Brother North-wind's Secret REVEALED!

Themes: Stories within stories

2000-05-15 William H. Ansley #

Reports that the Book Two chapter of The Book of the Long Sun titled Brother North-wind's Secret opens with schoolchildren reading a children's story of that name, whose secret is withheld until Smoky Barnable recalls it pages later. Compares this direct reveal with what Wolfe would have done, and notes that Lupine's book still has many ambiguities and mysteries.
informs · close-textual-reading

Now that I have paused a couple days to keep you all in suspense, here is more detail. Book Two of LB is called "Brother North-wind's Secret". Chapter II of this Book starts off with several school children taking turns reading a children's story of the same name. In the story, a Meadow Mouse, who has never seen Winter is questioning animals to find out how to prepare for it. A Crow tells him that one thing he needs to know is Brother North-wind's secret, which is the answer to the question, "What is the one good thing about Winter?" But the crow won't tell and neither will Brother North-Wind.

Before the last child can finish the story, just before the secret is revealed, the teacher (Smoky Barnable) interrupts him. So far, so Lupine. But, only a little over 3 pages later, Smoky recalls the answer (he read the story before): "If Winter comes, Spring can't be far behind."

My expectation, if this was a Wolfe story, is that the answer would be revealed less directly or at a greater remove from the question or not [email] isn't to say that Crowley doesn't have his
secrets, there are plenty of ambiguities and mysterious goings-on in LB.

William Ansley
