Themes: Colonization and the native

1999-10-29 Greene, Carlton #

Argues that the autochthons of the Long Sun-era Whorl speak original Quechua, citing the title Apu-Punchau as an authentic Inca term for the sun deity. Supports this by listing further evidence: the autochthons' dress, their reed boats, and the custom of drinking mate through a metal straw, which the poster links to Peru and Bolivia. The post replies to Mitchell Bailey, who had questioned whether the poster could claim to know Quechua.
