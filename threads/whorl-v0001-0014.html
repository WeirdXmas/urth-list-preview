Void Cross

Whorl list · 3 messages · 1996-12-16 to 1996-12-19

The thread: Doug Eigsti opened by arguing that the void cross Silk wears is a gammadion, an X-shaped void made of four gammas around a plus sign, not a swastika, and tied it to the cross symbolism of the story. Jim Jordan answered that the symbol could be a swastika, a voided Greek cross, or a deliberately ambiguous gammadion given Pas's perversion of religion, and supplied dictionary and pre-Hitler Christian symbol history. Eigsti then pushed a negation reading, casting Pas as the devil opposed to the Christian cross and the Outsider as God, citing Nightside passages. The thread ended without resolution, with the disagreement centering on whether the symbol's meaning is fixed or ambiguous.
  • Doug Eigsti: void cross is a gammadion, an X-shaped void, fitting Christian cross symbolism
  • Jim Jordan: the symbol is deliberately ambiguous, possibly swastika or voided Greek cross
  • Doug Eigsti: the cross is a negation symbol making Pas the devil and the Outsider God

1996-12-16 Doug Eigsti #

Argues that the void cross Silk wears, which he calls a gammadion, is not a swastika but an X-shaped void formed by four gamma shapes arranged around a central plus sign. Supports this with the Nightside page references and a geometric construction of a 5 by 5 grid, offering it as fitting the cross symbolism of the story.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Void cross is a plus-sign void, not a swastika

Whorl Wolfe's;

What is the Void Cross that Silk wears? Silk refers to it as a gammadion. Some have said that it is a swastika but this does not quite fit the descriptions: NIGHTSIDE pages (hardback); 43, 112, 170, 217, 239, 291. It clearly must have an "X" shaped cross or plus sign void in the center of the four gammas. A swastika does not fit the bill. One configuration that does fit follows: Imagine a 5 x 5 square with a cross, or plus sign, occupying the five center squares. Now arrange four "gammas" around the edges consisting of four squares in line and then one square next to the last one but to the right. This is like an elongated Knight's move. Each gamma on Silk's necklace can be separated and used as a screwdriver for checking the electrical connections on the Sacred windows. When connected in this manner the four gammas do form a void cross in the center. It does not have the mistique of a swastika but is more appropriate for the cross symbolism of Wolfe's story.

=Doug Eigsti=

1996-12-18 James Jordan #

Argues that Silk's void cross could be a swastika, a voided Greek cross, or a gammadion whose meaning is deliberately ambiguous, given Pas's perversion of the religion. Responds to Doug E.'s question by offering the dictionary sense of gammadion, the voided Greek cross reading, and the pre-Hitler Christian symbol history.
answers · source-and-allusion · theology-and-religion · theories: Void Cross is a voided Greek cross; Gene intends ambiguous religious associations for the cross

Not sure. My dictionary gives "swastika" as equivalent of "gammadion." Silk's cross has corners that he can us to carve with.

If I read you correctly, this is not one of the traditional meanings of "gammadion." The other is "voided Greek cross." The Greek cross has the crossbar in the center of the vertical bar, and of equal length, looking exactly like a plus sign +. A voided Greek cross evidently has two lines in each directly, closed at the ends. Thus, one line is straight, while the other is a gamma -- in each of the four directions.

Only Gene could answer which is in view, but Meyers is certainly right that a cross emptied of Christ is partly in view -- since Pas has tried to pervert the traditional religion of the people. But since this is Pas's cross (as we are told), it may be a swastika.
And don't put it past Gene to be doing something sophisticated. Remember that before Hitler the swastika was a perfectly good Christian symbol. This story is set in the equivalent of the far future but in a universe preceding ours -- so the Hitler-association would be long gone, if ever it existed.
I suspect Gene intends a variety of associations, corresponding the ambiguity of the religious Silk serves.

Jim Jordan

1996-12-19 Doug Eigsti #

Argues that the Void Cross Silk wears is a negation symbol, making Pas the devil opposed to the Christian cross, with the Outsider as the Christian God. Replies to Jim J's account of the four gammas forming a plus-sign void by citing Nightside passages saying the Outsider was worshipped before the Nine and is everywhere outside the Whorl.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: Void Cross is a negation of the Christian cross; Pas is the equivalent of the Devil; The Outsider is the Christian God

Jim J,

I can see how your Voided Greek Cross, which does indeed look like a plus-sign, can be made out of four gammas and four straight lines but to my knowledge Wolfe never mentions the Voided Cross of Pas as consisting of anything more than four gamma shaped pieces that Silk uses to tighten the connections of the Sacred Windows. Somehow these four gammas must fit together leaving a plus-sign shaped void in the center.
If the Outsider is the God of the Christian Bible and Pas and his cohorts are considered to be mere pretenders to the throne of men's souls then the Void Cross would be a negation symbol indicating Pas' antagonizm to everything the Christian Cross stands for: i.e. he is the equivalent of the Devil. Early in the text of NIGHTSIDE Wolfe indicates that the Outsider was worshiped in The Short Sun Whorl before the Nine. The Nine are limited to inside the Whorl. The Outsider is everywhere on the outside, just as the Christian God is omnipresent.

=Doug=
