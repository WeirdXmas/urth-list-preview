Wolfe's new book

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-03-29 to 2002-03-29

2002urthNicholas Gevers

2002-03-29 Nicholas Gevers #

Reports that Wolfe's new collaboration with Neil Gaiman, A Walking Tour of the Shambles, is a light, joky illustrated chapbook from a small press, and compares it to Bibliomen. Describes its quasi-tourist-guide account of an apocryphal Chicago district with haunted houses, Captain Hook's crocodile and a museum of living clocks, and recommends it as enjoyable.
informs

I've now read Gene's latest book, co-authored with
Neil Gaiman: A WALKING TOUR OF THE SHAMBLES. It's an
illustrated chapbook, published soon by the small
press American Fantasy. It's best compared with
BIBLIOMEN: light, joky, a great writer on holiday,
really. The book describes, in quasi-tourist-guide
fashion, an apocryphal district of Chicago filled with
sinister attractions: haunted houses, a lake hosting
Captain Hook's crocodile, a museum of living clocks,
etc. There are even some gross recipes from a ghoulish
restaurant. Under "Other Books by Gene Wolfe" at the
front, there is mention of such gems as HANDSAW:
ESCAPE FROM WOODEN CAGES. Highly enjoyable tour, or at
least I thought so.

--Nick Gevers.
