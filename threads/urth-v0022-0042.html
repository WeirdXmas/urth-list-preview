Letters Home & Bibliome

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-12-17 to 1998-12-17

1998urthMichael Andre-Driussi

1998-12-17 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Informs readers that Letters Home collects Gene Wolfe's Korean War letters to his mother, and contrasts the Cheap Street and Bryan Cholfin editions of Bibliomen. Describes Bibliomen as a collection of character sketches and short-shorts, some previously published in magazines, with a few extra characters in the Cholfin version.
informs

LETTERS HOME is about Gene Wolfe's experience in the Korean War, made up of the letters he wrote to his mother.

BIBLIOMEN is another case of Cheap Street (expensive) versus Bryan Cholfin (not expensive and a few more characters added as well). It is basically a collection of short-shorts, character sketches; "characters in search of a book" ("biblio-men," see?). A few of them have been published in magazines.
