Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Hidden identities and parentage

1998-01-21 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Summarizes Clute's essay claiming Severian's mother Catherine is also the Old Autarch, and adds minor points about Thecla and Cyriaca as deep and shallow kin. Also proposes a kabbalistic ordering of universes (Briah, Yesod, Malkuth) and suggests Urth may lie in our future or in another universe, citing the appendix noting Wolfe's visit to Urth's extant buildings.

Ori,

The Main Point of Clute's seminal Wolfe essay (1986) is that Severian's mother is Catherine, who is also <drum roll> the Old Autarch, hence "Catherine the Weal."

Along the way are a number of interesting minor points: contrary to what I posted before, Thecla is referred to as a "deep sister" (rather than the deep aunt I misremembered); it is Cyriaca who is termed a "shallow aunt."

Christopher,

By kabbalah (and these terms are from kabbalah), the chain of universes should go:

Briah--Yesod--Malkuth (our universe)

OTOH the appendix to book one (re the translation of the manuscript) mentions that Gene Wolfe has examined artifacts from Urth, and in fact has been allowed to visit and photograph the era's few extant buildings. Suggesting at first that Urth is within our universe, just within our future; or at second, that something more wild and wooly is at work--a brief guided jaunt to another universe?

=mantis=
