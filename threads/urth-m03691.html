2007-02-16 James Machin #

Reports that the poster saw a snowboard in an Austrian shop whose design copies the Bruce Pennington cover of The Shadow of the Torturer, and jokes that Severian could have used it to slide down the Typhon statue. Shares a link to the shop and a catalogue page showing front and back views of the board.
informs

I was snowboarding in Austria recently when this board in a display outside a shop caught my eye:

http://www.snowboard-shop.cz/info_delphic_stuf.php

Back and front view on page 7 here:

http://www.sport2000.at/backend/_data/aktionsprospekt/2/ap2_winter_05.pdf

It's an exact (?) facsimile of the Bruce Pennington cover to The Shadow Of The Torturer. Perhaps Sev could've used one to scoot down the side of the mountain statue of Typhon.
