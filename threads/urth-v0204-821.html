Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

2002-04-28 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that the idea of prehistoric human migrations from Earth to other worlds in The Fifth Head of Cerberus may derive partly from Jack Vance novels of the 1960s. Cites The Star King, Space Opera and the Tschai series, noting Vance used the concept repeatedly despite its implausibility.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Wolfe's prehistoric migration idea derives from Vance novels

As Jack Vance is and was a major unfluence on GW, the
idea in 5HC of prehistoric human migrations from Earth
to other worlds may have been picked up, in part, from
certain Vance novels of the Sixties: THE STAR KING,
SPACE OPERA, and the Tschai series. However
implausible the concept, Vance used it repeatedly.

--Nick Gevers.
