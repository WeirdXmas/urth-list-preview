2003-08-08 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Answers Don Palumbo's question about where to find the essay on the fourth book of The Book of the New Sun by giving its source: a 1984 NESFA Press volume, Planet Engineering, whose first essay is 'Books in The Book of the New Sun'. Responds directly to Palumbo's request for the citation, supplying bibliographic information rather than argument about the text.
answers

I will answer the last part: PLAN[E]T ENGINEERING is a book published by NESFA Press in 1984. The first essay in this book is "Books in THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN." That is the essay I referred to.

2003-08-08 Don Palumbo #

Proposes that the fourth book Severian fetches for Thecla may itself be contained within The Book of the New Sun, as Dr. Talos' play is inside Claw. Grounds this in the tetralogy's nested tales and fractal structure, based on Campbell's monomyth, and asks for textual evidence and the Planet Engineering essay reference.
proposes · myth-and-structure · source-and-allusion · theories: Thecla's fourth book is contained within The Book of the New Sun

Andrew,

That's really interesting, especially as the content of THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN is already inside The Book of the New Sun in that we get the text of Dr. Talos' play, which is explicitly based on it, for about twenty pages at the end of CLAW, so it would be especially neat if this book itself was also inside the book. The dozens of other tales within tales in BNS are the best examples of numerous other instances of the nod to fractal structure that Wolfe is giving, here, in basing BNS on Campbell's monomyth (that Severian's companion through most of CLAW--which is the "Belly of the Whale" episode in the monomyth--is named Jonas is another big-but-not-so-subtle nod), which also has a remarkably thorough fractal structure (but not a whole lot of people know it).

So, is there any evidence in the text of the tetralogy itself that this 4th book in THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN?

And where exactly can I find this essay in PLAN[E]T ENGINEERING?

--Don Palumbo
