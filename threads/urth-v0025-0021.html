Dorcas drowned

Themes: Water, the sea and drowning

1999-04-07 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Thanks a fellow poster for praise of the Lexicon and offers a name for the unnamed boatman, suggesting readers call him Charon or number him by rank. Says he has covered the Lady of the Lake elements and the question of whether Dorcas was accidentally murdered by her husband in a 1996 essay, and thinks she was, citing her memory of being dropped into water.
supports · myth-and-structure · puzzle-solving · theories: Dorcas was accidentally murdered by her husband; Boatman is a figure of Charon

Cliff Drane,

Thank you for your kind words about the Lexicon. It doesn't give the name of the boatman, I don't think, but Robert Borski and I are in the habit of calling him one version or another of "Charon," since he is so obviously linked to the ferryman of the dead.

I suppose when we all grow up and get serious we will call him "Boatman I, XXII" ("boatman eye-twenty-two") in contrast to "Boatman I, II."

I'm delighted by your observations and speculations since I've had them myself! My essay "Gene Wolfe at the Lake of Birds" (in "Foundation" No. 66, 1996) covered some of the Lady of the Lake elements as well as the "was Dorcas accidentally murdered by her husband?" mystery (I think it quite likely that she was--especially since =she= also seems to remember being dropped into the water!--but maintain the ambiguity).

=mantis=
