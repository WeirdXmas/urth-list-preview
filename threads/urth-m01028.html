2005-01-19 Jim Raylor #

Responds to a thread about possible hidden sources by saying he sews seeds, checks Google results carefully, and plans a website on his ideas. Argues that Wolfe's city references in the Fifth Head of Cerberus are symbolic, citing Geneva, Rome, Tower Bridge, Whistler, Swann and Dublin, and wagers that Wolfe would confirm Gene's Ancient Gods influence.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: European city names in Fifth Head of Cerberus are symbolic

Not pearls before swine... I sew seeds or beans.
Google often misleads... which is why I approach it
cautiously and check and cross-check.
Inspired by you and Borski and many others here, I do
intend to fashion a web-site to pursue these matters.
I lurked a long time here to read what others had said
and many of the ideas have been developing within me
for many years, but I did not want to reveal what was
obvious to others.
I try to indicate which associations I feel are just
fortuitous... but if anyone here dare ask GW how much
AoGG influenced 5HOC, I would bet $100 dollars that
his answer would surprise you.

Geneva I do believe is a very symbolic city to Gene,
but as soon as he mentions it, he also makes reference
to all the major European cities and a few more
besides. Rome was a no-brainer, but the reference to
Tower Bridge made me grin. (John Wolfe-Barry)

La Musee D'Orsay is also worth a visit. Whistler...
Swann ... what is the french for Foxfire?
Dublin has been mentioned too many times already and
Vienna (probably) and Prague.
