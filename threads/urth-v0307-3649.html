2003-07-10 Seth Lombardi #

Asks where Severian's thousands of Autarch personalities come from, given the Lexicon Urthus lists far fewer Autarchs, and suggests Thecla's previous meals. Then asks why Severian denies having a sister despite a twin's name in BOTNS, proposing that the child Silk meets is an alternate version.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Autarch personalities come from Thecla's previous meals; Child Severian Silk meets is an alternate version

Two questions that might be sort of remedial:

First, if I'm not mistaken, Severian makes reference to having _thousands_ of personalities to draw from after becoming Autarch. Now, seeing as how my Lexicon Urthus tells me there are have been far less than a thousand known Autarchs, where does this legion come from? Are they somehow the result of Thecla's previous meals, and I just missed it?

Second, and I KNOW this has been debated before so just feel free to ignore it if it's redundant, what the HECK is going on with Merryn at the end of the Book of the Short Sun? Even ignoring the reference to her being other than human, why does Severian seemingly deny knowledge of a sister when confronted with the fact that he has the name of a twin in BOTNS, but acknowledges to SilkHorn that she is his sister at the end of BOSS? Could it be that the lil' Severian that SilkHorn meets is actually one of the alternate unsuccessful versions? Or perhaps a further refinement? Seth Lombardi

2003-07-11 Andrew Bollen #

Disputes Seth's claim that Severian denies knowing a sister in the Book of the Short Sun, arguing that the kinship line belongs to Merryn, who speaks it ironically. Grounds the reading in Merryn's line in Chapter 13 and asks what Seth means by her being other than human.
disputes · close-textual-reading

Unless I've missed it, I don't think Sev makes this claim. It's Merryn who says: "He's my brother ... We're brothers & sisters, we witches & torturers" (Ch 13). I don't think there's any claim of real kinship in that, just some smart-ass irony.

What's the reference to her being other than human?
