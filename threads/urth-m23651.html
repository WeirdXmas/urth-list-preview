2012-10-02 Daniel Petersen replying to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Congratulates Allan Anderson on finding a copy of Lafferty's Apocalypses and says Lafferty is a favourite author of both the poster and Gene Wolfe. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's remark that it is his favourite Lafferty book, noting that many fans are dubious about the first novella, 'Where Have You Been, Sandaliotis?', which the poster finds amazing. Social exchange rather than analysis of Wolfe.
chats

Congrats, Allan! Thanks for sharing! Always glad to hear about my favourite author (and one of Gene Wolfe's favourites).

Dan'l, that's very interesting that it's your favourite book by Laff. I love it too, but I find a lot of fans more dubious, especially about the first novella, 'Where Have You Been, Sandaliotis?'. I thinks it's amazing.

(I wish there was an 'urthlist' equivalent for discussion of Lafferty's works!)

-DOJP
