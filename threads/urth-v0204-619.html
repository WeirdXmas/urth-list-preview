Borski notice picking up

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-04-15 to 2002-04-15

2002urthMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

2002-04-15 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Reports that David Hartwell's introduction to Gene Wolfe's story "The Walking Sticks" in Year's Best Fantasy names Robert Borski's essay on the wolf hidden in Wolfe's fiction. Quotes the intro's line and celebrates the mention, adding a note about Sirius Fiction booklets on Wolfe and Crowley.
informs

I don't recall if this has been noted already or not, but our own Robert Borski is mentioned by name in the David Hartwell intro to Gene Wolfe's "The Walking Sticks" in David Hartwell's YEAR'S BEST FANTASY (published July 2001).

"Writer Robert Borski recently published an essay on the thesis that Wolfe often puts a wolf into his fiction, thus inviting the reader to 'find the wolf.'"

Well allll right!

Kudos to Robert Borski! That's like being mentioned in the dispatches, or so it seems to me.
