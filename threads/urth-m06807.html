The thread: Readers debated Borski's Solar Labyrinth theory that Thecla is the Autarch's daughter. The thread ended with the paternity question unresolved and the textual evidence still disputed.
  • Son of Witz: Thecla is the Autarch's daughter, supported by the cat-headed servants' recognition

  • Dave Tallman: Thecla was destined for the throne before the unmanning, supported by the house-absolute passage
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Autarch had no children, relaying Borski's theory for others to check

Presents Borski's theory from Solar Labyrinth that Thecla is the Autarch's daughter, arguing that the Autarch's cat-headed servants recognizing her through Severian makes the idea compelling. Replies to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's claim that the Autarch has no children, asking for text denying it and questioning when Appian was unmanned and Thecla's age.
supports · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Thecla is the Autarch's daughter

It's not my theory, it's Borski's, from Solar Labyrinth, and the text seems to support it. He's talking about before his unmanning. The text says she was born in the house absolute and destined to sit on the throne. 2008-11-19 Roy C. Lackey replying to Son of Witz #

Argues that the Autarch cannot be Thecla's or Thea's biological father because Thecla is an exultant and the Old Autarch is not, citing the Claw appendix on exultant stock. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's relay of Borski's theory that the Autarch had no children, asking for text showing he had none, and estimates Thecla's age at 23-25 from the timeline of Appian's unmanning in The Citadel.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: The Autarch is not Thecla's biological father; Thecla is an exultant of exultant stock; Thecla is a little older than Severian

seems to support it. He's talking about before his unmanning. The text says she was born in the house absolute and destined to sit on the throne. The Autarch's personal cat headed servants recognize her through Severian, why would that be if she's just some noble. Can you find any text that supports that the Autarch did NOT have children.

As for the issue of the Old Autarch being Thecla's father -- he cannot be her or Thea's biological father, as I've said before, and recently. It's this simple: Thecla is an exultant; the Old Autarch is decidedly not. He is a little guy, scarcely taller than Cyriaca. One must be *born* an exultant, of exultant stock. See the first appendix to CLAW.

One born of the mating of an exultant and common person is taller than the average commoner, but notably shorter than an exultant. A person of such mixture is regarded as a bastard, such as the fat guy outside Agilus' cell.

older is Thecla? How long back was Appian unmanned?<<

He was unmanned not too long before Severian was born, then opened the whorehouse about the same year Sev was born. See chapter XXIV, CITADEL.

The best determination I can make of Thecla's age is that she is a little older than Sev, but not a lot older. Maybe 23-25.

-Roy

2008-11-19 Dave Tallman replying to Son of Witz #

Reports a theory from Borski's Solar Labyrinth that Thecla was destined for the throne before the autarch's unmanning, and argues the text supports it. Cites the passage saying she was born in the house absolute, and notes that in The Urth of the New Sun the old autarch was unmanned so no child would block Severian's path.
supports · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Thecla was destined for the throne before unmanning; Thecla sits on the throne in Severian

It's not my theory, it's Borski's, from Solar Labyrinth, and the text seems to support it. He's talking about before his unmanning. The text says she was born in the house absolute and destined to sit on the throne.

<snip>

In UotNS, it says that the old autarch was unmanned specifically so that no child would stand in Sev's way to the throne. But in this version Thecla would already be standing in the way.

I note that Thecla's destiny did come true, in an ironic sort of way. She did sit on the throne -- in Severian.
