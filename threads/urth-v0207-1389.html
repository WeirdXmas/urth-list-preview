Silk-23 and the Silkicide mystery

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-07-16 to 2002-07-16

2002urthMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2002-07-16 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that placing Silk-23 inside Horn's body makes the twenty years of unknown events, including the suicidal episode before the first switch, unknown to Horn just as they are to Silk-23. Grounds this in the premise that Silk-23 was not present for those events, so either personality in control of the Narrator would be equally ignorant, and that Silk-23 would avoid rulership having seen Silk-43's ruin second-hand.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk-23 in Horn's body explains unknown years; Silk-23 avoids rulership after seeing Silk-43 destroyed

Just in case it is slipping past unnoticed, and at the grave risk of belaboring the obvious: the beauty of putting Silk-23 in to the Horn-in-Silk-body is that the past 20 years are as unknown to Horn as they are to Silk-23, including the mysterious suicidal events immediately prior to the first switcharoo.

Horn isn't being evasive, he really doesn't know -- this we grant easily. But the same would hold true for Silk-23 -- he wasn't there, either. So no matter which personality is in control of the Narrator, the result would be the same.

It would also mean that the Narrator would be walking away from rulership roles on Blue, not because of the crippling first-hand experiences of Silk-43, but because Silk-23 has seen second-hand that such a path will destroy him (i.e., as Silk-43 was destroyed, spiritually if not suicidally).
