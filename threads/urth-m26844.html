2019-05-24 Fernando Q. Gouvea #

Reports the arrival of a Folio Society limited edition of The Book of the New Sun and wishes aloud for a Library of America edition of Wolfe's Solar Cycle, first volume to include The Urth of the New Sun and related stories, text established by Marc Aramini. Grounds the wish in the Folio edition's signed, numbered format and the marketing email's note that Wolfe had recently died.
informs

Got this today from Folio. Expensive, for sure, but it's good to see Wolfe getting published by them.

Of course, what I really want is to see a Library of America <https://loa.org/> edition of the oeuvre. I can even imagine the first volume, with the Book of the New Sun and The Urth of the New Sun and related short stories. Text established by Marc Aramini. To be followed by the rest of the Solar Cycle...

Fernando
