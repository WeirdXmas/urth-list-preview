Marble on NPR

Urth list · 4 messages · 2002-11-20 to 2002-11-20

2002urthThe Book of the Long SunDan'l Danehy-OakesJames WynnChris PJoe Cilluffo
The thread: A post relaying an NPR interview about kidney recipients taking on donor traits prompted a loose link to Wolfe's Marble and Rose. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argued Marble's inherited memories from Rose work like a cut-up hologram, so Rose's personality persists in Marble's parts, citing Delany and possible Gibson and Short Sun echoes. Chris countered that the simplest reading is that Rose's personality came over with the transplanted parts, but a 'reclaim' passage and a hint that Marble was speaking beforehand complicate it. cilluff1 added a light joke about a Simpsons hair transplant. The thread ended with no resolution, only a shift from the NPR anecdote to a contested reading of the Marble and Rose passages.
  • James Wynn: relays the NPR kidney-donor interview as a loose thematic link to Marble and Rose
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Marble's inherited memories act like a cut-up hologram, so Rose persists in her parts
  • Chris: simplest reading is personality transferred with the parts, but the 'reclaim' passage and Marble speaking beforehand disturb it
  • cilluff1: jokes about a Simpsons Treehouse of Horror hair transplant and offers no reading

Themes: Memory and forgetting, Doubles, twins and clones, Robots, androids and artificial life

2002-11-20 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues that Marble's inherited memories from Rose work like a cut-up hologram, so her parts still carry Rose's personality and memory into Marble. Grounds this in a holographic memory model, cites Samuel R. Delany's stories as parallels, and connects the confusion to the narrator's state in Short Sun and a possible nod to William Gibson.

2002-11-20 Chris P #

Argues that the simplest reading of Marble and Rose is that Rose's personality came over with her transplanted parts, but a passage where Rose seems present in Marble beforehand disturbs this. Grounds the doubt in the 'reclaim' passage, where Rose explains taking back parts from her old body, and in a theory that Marble was speaking.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Rose's personality transferred with her donated parts; Marble was speaking when Rose said reclaim

You know, since you mention it I have to bring up something that's been nagging at me persistently since I first read it.

The easiest and most coherent explanation of what happens with Marble and Rose is exactly as you describe, that some of the attributes/personality of Rose came over with her parts when Marble scavenged them. This is what I intuited, at any event, even before Silk realizes what's going on. *But* then I got thrown a loop which I find disturbing. There's a passage where Rose/Marble is speaking as Rose, which basically implies that she was present in Marble *before* the parts came over. I this is the same passage where she uses the phrase "reclaim" (in reference to the prosthetics) which has led some people here to theorize that it was Marble speaking, and that Rose had originally taken those parts from Marble long ago. However, the sense of it was that Rose was explaining why she had to take back the parts from her old body, and it makes no sense for her to go through this thought process (even as a rationalization) unless she was present in Marble just beforehand.

Of course, Rose/Marble could just be very confused by what she's gone through. I am uncomfortable dismissing it so easily, though.

Chris
