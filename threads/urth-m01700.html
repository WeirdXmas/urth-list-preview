Answers a newcomer's question about how Earth became Urth by saying the books never explain the change. Cites Wolfe's outside comments that the future is a do-nothing Earth without a real process, and calls that evasive.
answers · author-and-biography

It doesn't.

In some external comments, Wolfe has stated that there is no real process: that this is the "do-nothing" future of Earth where we neither really solve any of our big problems nor quite ever succumb to them. This is (like so many Lupine extratextual statements) a bit evasive, as it does not explain a great deal...

--Dan'l
