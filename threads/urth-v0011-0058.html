Erebus, Abaia, Scylla etc.

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-16 to 1998-05-16

1998urthThe Book of the New SunAlice Turner

Themes: Power, rulers and politics, Water, the sea and drowning

1998-05-16 Alice Turner #

Argues that the sea-powers Erebus, Abaia and Scylla have inherited Ushas after the flood and that their interest in Severian served that end, making him a servant of evil. Builds the case from the flooded world giving them more water and the undines' visible boldness, and invites comment from the list.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Erebus, Abaia and Scylla inherited Ushas; Severian serves the sea-powers' ends

Speaking of these major players, as mantis was doing if only en passant, it seems to me that Wolfe has left a distinctly loose thread in their regard. The New Sun, the great flood et al would seem to me to be just dandy for them. True they're lost most of their human allies, but they probably retain a proportional number of survivors. They have much more water to wallow about in. The undines are feeling feisty, as we actually see. It could be said that E, A & S have inherited Ushas. And that their interest in Severian was to that end. And that therefore he is a servant of evil, certainly an enemy of the Commonwealth, willingly or not. Any comment?

-alga-
