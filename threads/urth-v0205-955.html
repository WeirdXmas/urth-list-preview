2002-05-10 David DiGiacomo #

Asks where a reader can find a copy of Letters Home at a reader's price rather than a collector's price, and whether anyone can describe its contents. Notes that Operation Ares and the Kai Lung books are easy to locate through the used-book search site addall.com.
questions

OPERATION ARES and the Kai Lung books seem to be readily available (my favorite search site is http://www.addall.com/used), but how about LETTERS HOME? Is it available anywhere at a reader's, rather than collector's, price? And would any reader care to describe the contents?

Thanks...

2002-05-10 Mark Millman #

Describes Letters Home as a collection of surviving letters and fragments from Wolfe's Korean War service, noting Wolfe's introduction that most novel readers will find little of interest. Answers David DiGiacomo's question about the contents and availability by quoting the introduction, though admits no information on price or where to buy it.
answers

Mr. DiGiacomo,

_Letters Home_ is a collection of "all the letters,
and fragments of letters, that survive from" Wolfe's
service in Korea. He also says in his introduction
that "[m]ost readers of my novels will find little
of interest in these letters," but that he thinks
they ought to be preserved.

I've taken him at his word, and haven't gotten
around to reading it. I'm afraid I also don't have
any information on its availability or price.

Mark Millman
(Nacre)
