1997-11-26 Charles Dye #

Informs readers that Orb has released The Urth of the New Sun as a trade paperback, matching the size and colour of the two-volume Book of the New Sun and priced reasonably. Gives the ISBN so readers can locate the edition; no reading or argument about the text is offered.
informs

For them what has not yet seen it, Orb has (finally!) released The Urth of the New Sun in a trade paperback. Size and color are compatible with their two-volume "Book", and the price is not exorbitant. ISBN 0-312-86394-2.
