2000-11-28 Kevin J. Maroney #

Replies to Nick Gevers's questions about Return to the Whorl by saying it completes the trilogy well, while declining to say more. Also reports that Tor is not yet sending out general review copies of the book.
answers

Well, yeah. :-)

As to the first: I think it completes the trilogy very well. I really don't want to comment further, for a complex of reasons.

As to the second: I don't think they're sending out general review copies yet.
