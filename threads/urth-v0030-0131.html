PEACE: fuzzy new president

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-11-18 to 2000-11-18

2000urthMichael Andre-Driussi

2000-11-18 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that a roadhouse scene in Peace, where Charlie Scudder drives Den home, belongs to the early presidency period and contrasts with a later vision of the ruined roadhouse. Grounds this on the workers not recognizing Den, labor tensions, and their vagueness about his appearance, suggesting the episode predates his fiftieth birthday.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Roadhouse scene predates Den's fiftieth birthday

Roy and Adam,

While I'm thinking of it, here's another fuzzy factoid/episode from the "early president" period: that roadhouse where the workers go (152 for Roy; 162 for Adam).

Charlie Scudder is giving Den a ride (as a stop along the way home or will they go back to the plant for Den to get his car? Must be along the way home. Scudder is taking him to his apartment?); Den is not entirely recognized as being the president (they are thinking of Julius); labor/management tensions are high (Den wonders if Scudder's tires will be slashed); and then there is that vision of the roadhouse in ruin, which must belong to "later president" period.

This one episode seems to show a contrast between the early and the later periods, and suggests a span of several years.

Because the workers are so vague as to what Den looks like, maybe this episode happens before Den's 50th birthday, when there is the (photographed) company event at which Dan French gives him the box.

=mantis=
