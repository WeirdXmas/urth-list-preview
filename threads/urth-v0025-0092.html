Pronunciation?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-04-14 to 1999-04-14

1999urthYann Oehl

1999-04-14 Yann Oehl #

Asks whether readers of The Book of the New Sun have reached a consensus on how the main character names are pronounced, listing several competing pronunciations for Severian and Hethor. Notes that friends who read the book each arrived at their own version, so the question is about pronunciation rather than interpretation.
questions

I've got a silly question...
Everyone that I know who has read TBotNS has come up their own interpretation of how the characters names are pronounced. Is there a consensus?
For instance:
Severian- sev'ear'ian / sever'ian / sev'rea'an / sev'rin Hethor- heh'ther / hea'thoar / heth'oar

Just wonderin.
y.
