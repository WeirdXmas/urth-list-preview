Recalls a line Wolfe wrote, which the poster calls among his most beautiful: the wolf is God's dog, half-mad with hunger and the memory of His hands upon her head. Supports the earlier thread's link between the surname Wolfe, the French word for dog, and the wolf by citing this line as the essay on dogs being tame wolves.

2005-08-23 Don Doggett replying to Jeffrey Lefstin #

Argues that the surname clue in The Fifth Head of Cerberus is a subtle pun resting on canis, the Latin word shared by dog and wolf, rather than the obvious French loup. Replies to Jeffrey Lefstin's question about the chien-and-wolf puzzle, calling loup a ham-handed giveaway that fits Wolfe's style better and sparks debate.
