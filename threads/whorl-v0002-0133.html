1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunAlice Turner

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1997-02-11 Alice Turner #

Argues that Tartaros, using a Pas puppet displayed on screens and manteions, is the figure who spread word of escape across the Whorl, and that the Outsider is unlikely to be involved. Responds to Dave's questions about who got people from other cities to Blue, citing Silk's reading of the Outsider's message and the Fliers Tartaros uses.
answers · puzzle-solving · theology-and-religion · theories: Tartaros used a Pas puppet to spread word across the Whorl; Tartaros is a god who may be working for the inhumi

Here are my answers to Dave's questions, last first. Tartaros picked Auk, I think, simply because he could, given his limitations. After he created the Pas puppet to speak for him, he could have it appear on screens and manteions throughout the Whorl, and that's just what I think he did, which answers the earlier questions. I find it sort of unlikely that the Outsider is involved since the Outsider's message, in neither case, seemed to involve actually leaving (at least Silk didn't see it that way, though in the interesting discussion above, it's been established that Silk is sometimes wrong). Tartaros has the Fliers working for him to spread the word too. Tartaros is an intriguing guy/god and it remains to be seen if he's good (working for mankind) or evil (working for the inhumi to set up a great big picnic). The real Pas, I'm convinced, was a nogoodnik, and it's debatable whether the Whorl is a legitimate colony ship.

-algae- (I concede the "alga" question, but it looks too odd; algae seems humorous)
