Themes: Myth and folklore sources, Water, the sea and drowning

2009-04-06 b sharp #

Proposes that Abaia, the mythological giant eel said to cause floods, may be an ally rather than an enemy of the New Sun, suggesting the undine Idas's failed attempts to kill Severian are a set piece to bring a flood. Grounds this on a newly learned fact from an external Wikipedia article about Abaia and on Idas's unsuccessful attempt on Severian, responding to a comment by Bruinian about divine intent.
proposes · source-and-allusion · myth-and-structure · theories: Abaia's floods are part of a set piece; Idas's failed attempts deliberately bring a flood

for some reason, Bruinian's comment:

reminded me of a new fact I encountered recently. That Abaia is the name for a mythological giant eel has been mentioned numerous times in the past. But I'd never before heard that part of his myth is that he causes floods. Seems like the concensus has been that he is the enemy of the New Sun but this bit makes me wonder. We even have the child undine, Idas, trying to kill Severian to halt the New Sun from coming but...is it all part of a set piece to bring a flood, using deliberately unsuccessful attempts to impede Severian?

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abaia
