1998-10-30 Gap #

Introduces the poster to the list and argues that Jack Vance's The Languages of Pao shows language used to unite cultures, suggesting this as a tool in SF alongside Orwell's 1984 and Keyes's Flowers for Algernon. Responds to a thread on Lewis's Out of the Silent Planet and linguistics in SF, mainly by offering examples and a personal remark about learning the language in Montreal.
