Themes: Unreliable narration

1997-05-13 Kieran Cleary #

Proposes asking Wolfe whether Typhon launched the Whorl to reach the Blue/Green system, joking that the question may count as two. Responds to Michael Richman by citing Severian's line "Have I said that time turns our lies into truth?" and the disguise episodes it refers to.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Blue/Green system was Typhon's intended destination

This is like the three wishes problem ("I wish for infinite wishes"). I guess I'd have to ask "What was Typhon's purpose in launching the Whorl and was the Blue/Green system the intended destination?" Somebody will have to arbitrate as to whether that's two questions, but I can't help feeling that Mr. Wolfe will be tricksy in his answers.

BTW, Michael Richman: somewhere in tBotNS, Severian says "Have I said that time turns our lies into truth?" referring to a number of similar situations (his armiger disguise which made him seem "a pilgrim bound for some vague northern shrine" amongst them).

Lasrach
