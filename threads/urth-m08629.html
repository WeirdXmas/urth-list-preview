2009-07-22 Milton Jackson #

Asks whether Wolfe would ever write all the stories slated for The Wonders of Urth and Sky, a planned companion volume to The Book of the New Sun. Cites The Boy Who Hooked the Sun and Empires of Foliage and Flower as known outtakes from that book and suggests a thematic collection would be an ideal companion to the Severian tetralogy.
questions

I just had a wild thought while readin Starwater Strains: Does anyone know if Wolfe would write all the stories that are supposed to appear in The Wonders of Urth and Sky? I know The Boy Who Hooked the Sun and Empires of Foliage and Flower are supposed to be outtakes from the book, and there may be others although I'm not nearly done with all Wolfe's short fiction, so I can't be sure. To me if Wolfe were to do a thematic book like that and commit to paper all the stories that appear in the book that helps shape Severian, that would be the perfect companion piece to The Book of the New Sun.
