Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-08-03 mary whalen #

Lists the meanings of the name Mattak (also spelled maktak, maktuk, muktuk, muntuk), an Inuit word for whale skin or whale meat, and identifies Mattak as a soldier, Oosik's son, and fed on by Quetzal. Offers a glossary-style entry with no reply to another poster, tying the word's real-world Inuit origin to the character's name and family relations.
informs · source-and-allusion

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Mattak. Also maktak, maktuk, muktuk, muntuk. Inuit (Eskimo) word meaning whale skin, whale meat, or whale meat with skin on it, or the dried variety of any of the other definitions. Eaten as food. Soldier. Oosik's son. Fed on by Quetzal.
