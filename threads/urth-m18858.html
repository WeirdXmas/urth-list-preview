How *we* can get a special new "retro" edition of BotNS

Urth list · 8 messages · 2011-05-02 to 2011-05-02

2011urthThe Book of the New SunDerek BeyerGerry QuinnJames WynnSon of WitzDan'l Danehy-OakesDaniel Petersen
The thread: Derek Beyer urged list members to vote in Gollancz's 50th-anniversary retro-edition poll for The Book of the New Sun, noting it was filed under fantasy rather than science fiction. James Wynn argued Wolfe, Vance and Lewis are genre-busters deserving their own category, while Gerry Quinn disputed the fantasy placement, pointing to a Vance novel and Hyperion in other categories. Daniel Petersen jokingly scolded members for not voting and said BotNS might belong in science fiction. The thread stayed on the poll and genre labeling rather than the books themselves, and it ended without any resolution about where the novel belongs.
  • Derek Beyer: urges voting for BotNS in the Gollancz poll and notes it is miscategorized as fantasy
  • James Wynn: Wolfe, Vance and Lewis are genre-busters needing their own category
  • Gerry Quinn: BotNS is misplaced in the fantasy category
  • Daniel Petersen: jokingly calls non-voters traitors and suggests BotNS belongs in science fiction

Themes: Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both
