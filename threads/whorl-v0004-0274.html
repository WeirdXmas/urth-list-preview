Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1997-03-24 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Responds to a poster's Long Sun analysis by floating theories about Hy's access to surveillance monitors and Kypris, suggesting a motive for her airship encounter, and by commenting on Tick's gender and a Sphinx connection. Replies to vizcacha's post, praising the work and asking where the Tick-as-female claim came from.
supports · puzzle-solving · theories: Hy was a spy holding out on Crane; Kypris watched through monitors via Hy

vizcacha,

Great work! (All those child sacrifice threads find a very nice grounding in Jerboa-as-one-of-the-old-timers-who-did-that-before-it was-illegal, btw. But you already knew that.)

Re: "statutory warnings that Silk is more Moses than Jesus," <g>. I keep promising myself to find the time to write something about that . . . maybe this week?

Re: Hy seeing sub-surface areas. Good points. If she was a spy and saw these things then she was holding out on Crane, since he was poking around at Blood's looking for a way into the tunnels. If it is not so much her being a spy as her being possessed 'round the clock by a goddess, then I can speculate that Kypris heard the story and went to check it out via monitors--but in this case we have to ask when the last time Hy had access to a monitor was. Another motive for playing tribadic/Trivigauntic patty-cake on the airship--to get to a monitor? (I can't recall where the glass was on the airship.)

Re: Tick talk. Neat, complete. (Hey, I never thought of Tick as a female critter! I believe you, but where did you pick that up?) And another point for the Sphigx connexion--interesting!

Keep up the good work!

=mantis=
