Applause for Urth essays

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-07-17 to 1999-07-17

1999urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

1999-07-17 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Praises published essays by Mitchell A. Bailey on the power behind the Phoenix Throne and by Roy C. Lackey on the flowers of Dorcas, and mentions an upcoming Robert Borski essay. Offers no argument about the books; it is a social message of appreciation for the authors' scholarly labor.
chats

I usually appreciate the discussion and debate that goes on around here (well, obviously--otherwise I wouldn't be here).

In addition to that, it is very exciting to see fully developed pieces like Mitchell A. Bailey's "The Power Behind the Phoenix Throne" and Roy C. Lackey's recent piece on the flowers of Dorcas. (I'll be seeing Robert Borski's latest "NYRSF" essay when my subscription copy arrives--soon. First it must battle its way through the walls of snail flesh between yon and hither . . . )

There is little reward for such labors (unless you happen to be an academic, at which point you've got the gun to your head <g>). And so I salute Messrs. Lackey and Bailey and applaud their essays.

Yay!

=mantis=
