The thread: Posters discussed how Lesage in The Wizard Knight-adjacent Urth-style plot always seems to know events in advance and profits from them. Dan argued Lesage is a time traveler and Chris's son returning to secure his own birth, citing timing, the buccaneers' laughter, and the visitor receiving the manuscript. stoneox17 proposed Ignacio feeds Lesage future information so events repeat exactly, and Ashley Crill and thalassocrat both argued Ignacio struck a deal with Lesage for Valentin's cave, Burt's caravan, and a share of the loot. The thread ends with competing guesses and no resolution, with several posters admitting they need to reread the text.
  • Dan: Lesage is a time-traveling son of Chris who returns to secure his own birth
  • stoneox17: Ignacio feeds Lesage future knowledge so events recur exactly
  • Ashley Crill: Ignacio made a deal with Lesage, trading cave and caravan details for something
  • thalassocrat: Ignacio was secretly in charge and took a large share of the loot, with Lesage fronting

Themes: Time loops and causality, Hidden identities and parentage, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

2008-03-13 Dan #

Argues that Lesage is a time traveler who knows events in advance, and that he is Chris's son who returns to ensure his own birth, killing Valentin and betraying everyone at the end. Grounds this in Lesage's uncanny timing, the buccaneers' laughter at Chris's question, Chris's wariness, and the visitor who receives Chris's manuscript at the book's start.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Lesage is Chris's son returning through time; Lesage is the visitor who receives Chris's manuscript; Monastery students begin time travel as novices

The buccaneers were laughing at Chris when he asked if they'd seen Valentin, because Lesage had recently offered them a reward for Valentin and they thought that was why Chris was looking for him too. It's clear Lesage found him first.

Lesage seemed to have the uncanny knack of being able to be in the right place at the right time, show up when it was profitable and be delayed when things went awry. In fact, the characters even discussed this at one point and were at a loss to figure it out. In a story where time travel is possible, this rises above the point of coincidence and is evidence that someone has pre-knowledge of events. Chris seems very wary of Lesage but says little about him, almost as if he doesn't have to or doesn't want to. I believe that Lesage was the visitor at the beginning, for whom Chris wrote the book. Chris was very paranoid about not messing with the timeline as Ignacio and realized that for things to turn out right for him, Lesage would have to go back as well. With Chris' manuscript, he would know what to do and where and when to show up or stay away.

Perhaps Lesage is Chris' son, doing the time travel thing to make sure things turn out the same to make sure he'll be born! That includes killing Valentin and betraying everyone at the end.

I don't believe there is any, other than what I speculated on above.

I don't think there was a large seperation in age between Chris and Lesage not to see a resemblence.

At the point when the students become novices, they begin to travel back in time. I think this was a property of the Monastery. If you recall, other people also left abruptly, so they may have been doing the same thing.

I believe that was after his second raping when he suffered severe head trauma and was wishing death on his tormentors. An homage to Latro, maybe?
