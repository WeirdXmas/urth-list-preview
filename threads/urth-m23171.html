Question about scanning (short story project)

Urth list · 3 messages · 2012-03-24 to 2012-03-24

2012urthDavid StockhoffMarc Aramini

2012-03-24 Marc Aramini #

Asks the list whether he may scan Wolfe's uncollected early stories, including those in Young Wolfe, and send them privately to members of a chronological discussion. He states he would not post them online, would skip stories in widely published anthologies, and wants to know if private non-profit sharing is legal.
questions

For the stories in Young Wolfe and the shorter uncollected stories that are not online, I would be willing to scan these and send them to off list requests for our continuing chronological discussion IF THIS WAS LEGAL and for private, non-profit use for the purpose of our discussion. I would not post these online, nor would I send them to anyone who did not request it privately from my email. Nor would I scan any that were included in widely published Wolfe anthologies like Island of Doctor Death, etc., only the uncollected ones.

My question to the list is - is this legal? if it is private correspondence between colleagues and is not released for any sort of profit?

2012-03-24 Marc Aramini replying to David Stockhoff #

Announces a plan to scan out-of-print Wolfe short stories for discussion, sending them on request with an opt-in and opt-out arrangement, and excluding stories in Tor collections. Responds to David Stockhoff's reply that the scanning seems fair use under a Cornell checklist, saying he agrees and will reassess when stories still in print are reached.
informs

Excellent. I think so too, especially for the works that are long out of print and generally unavailable. when (or if, but I fancy when) we get to 1999 then I will perhaps stop and reevaluate, since those stories are at least sometimes still in print. So the way I would like to handle this is that I will scan a few at a time before discussion, and send them to those who request it. We will make it an opt in/ opt out, after you request it I will continue to send it to you until you say, no more scans, please. Send requests to be included to my private e-mail, [email]. I will try to get on this ASAP.

No stories collected in any of the Tor collections will be included or reproduced; you are all on your own for those ones.

I appreciate everyone's input so far - it will get much more exciting when we reach 1970 and Wolfe suddenly became a super talented, fully developed adult writer of short stories!
