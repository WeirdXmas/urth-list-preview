The missing chems

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-08-06 to 2000-08-06

2000whorlThe Book of the Long SunDan Rabin

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Ghosts, dreams and visions

2000-08-06 Dan Rabin #

Asks whether chems have spirits as humans do in Green's, and whether the unresolved matter of Horn bringing Olivine's eye to Marble might tie chems into the spiritual scheme. Also asks how inhumi adopting human souls squares with Mucor calling Quetzal "the man who isn't there" and whether Mucor sees people differently from Horn's astral projections.
questions · theology-and-religion · close-textual-reading · theories: Chems may be tied into the spiritual scheme

As we discuss the respective humanity of humans and inhumi, it occurs to me that _Short Sun_ has dealt little with chems so far. They were certainly accepted as people in the Long Sun Whorl (although sometimes the subject of discrimination). Do they have spirits in the sense of _Green's_?

We know that there's an unresolved plot element concerning Horn's bringing Olivine's eye to Marble. Perhaps chems will yet be tied into the spiritual scheme of things.

On a related topic: how are we to square the adopting of human souls by inhumi with Mucor's characterization of Quetzal as "the man who isn't there"? Does Mucor perceive people distinctly from the way they appear in Horn's astral projections?

-- Dan Rabin
