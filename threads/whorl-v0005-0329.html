Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1997-06-05 Michael Richman #

Argues that the Outsider had abandoned Silk by the end of Exodus from the Long Sun, so Silk could not have survived the next twenty years without the Outsider's active help. Responds to a quoted post about the Short Sun announcement, and describes the Long Sun ending as disturbing because Silk seems betrayed by his beliefs with only Oreb left.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Outsider abandoned Silk at the end of Exodus

I was rather surprised with this announcement because I got the impression at the end of Exodus that the Outsider had abandoned Silk. I don't think Silk would have survived to the end of the series without the active help of the Outsider, and I pictured him dying a few minutes after Horn last saw him.

I fould the ending to Long Sun very disturbing (even more than Urth) in that it seemed that Silk had been betrayed by everything he'd believed in, and that all he had left was Oreb.
