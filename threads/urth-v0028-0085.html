Geography question?

Themes: Deep time and ruins

1999-08-31 Michael Doolittle #

Argues that the Urth continent's location in the southern hemisphere cannot settle which continent the Long Sun characters inhabit, because plate tectonics over long time spans let any continent drift there. Responds to alga's geography remark by rejecting using animal species names as evidence, since animals and birds migrate between continents, citing horses, lions, opossums and humanity.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Urth's continent is not South America

Hello (delurking for a moment),

I tend to agree with you. They are in the southern hemisphere, but discussion of which continent they are on is meaningless without an idea of the time scale. Given sufficient time, ANY continent could be in the southern hemisphere. Using the names of animal species as evidence of locale is also meaningless, since animals & birds have an annoying habit of migrating from one continent to another (vis. horses, lions, opussums, the Galapagos islands, and humanity itself).

(lurking again)

Mike
