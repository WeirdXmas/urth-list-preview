2002-01-31 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Reports that the list's digest now arrives as attachments, which may be awkward to read, and promises to look for a fix. Also says the archives will return within weeks and asks that administrative and technical comments be sent privately to the list administrator rather than posted to the list.
informs

As many of you have remarked, the new Urth list's digest format appears as a series of attachments rather than
a single long message. Depending on your mail software, this may be a particularly inconvenient or just an ugly way to read the digest. I'm stuck with the mailing list
software that's available to me, but I'll see if there's a way to fix the digests. Please put up with it for now, or switch to individual messages, and maybe a solution will present itself.

As for the archives, they should be available and current again within a few weeks.

To keep the list discussion focused on matters Lupine,
please send administrative and technical comments and
questions directly to me at [email].

- ranjit
