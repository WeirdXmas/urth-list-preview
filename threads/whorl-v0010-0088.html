Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1999-10-12 Raymond Dann #

Proposes that the Sun, Ship and Rose symbols on Severian's burial plaque gain meaning because Severian will appear in Return to the Whorl, and suggests these symbols link the Urth, Long Sun and Short Sun books as a trilogy. The poster admits they may be overthinking it and offers no textual evidence beyond the plaque's symbols and the planned appearance.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Plaque symbols link the three sun series as a trilogy

I suppose that I am overthinking this but Severian's burial plaque with the Sun, the Ship, and the Rose will have more meaning now that he will make in appearence in _Return to the Whorl_. Let's see...We have Urth, Long Sun, and Short Sun. A kind of trilogy in itself.

Xury
