2000-06-12 Jonathan Laidlow #

Asks that discussion of In Green's Jungles be delayed until July rather than starting two months early, because digest spoilers would be hard to avoid. Suggests advance copies could be provided to all readers so the discussion can begin fairly.
questions

I'm afraid I would be against starting the discussion of IGJ two months early - simply because the digests make it difficult to avoid spoilers, and I doubt I'd have the time to look back through old discussions once I do have a copy in my hands. But hey, what do I know?

Can we save it until July at least? (Or provide advance copies for all?)

Jonathan
