Silk Insane?

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-04-10 to 2001-04-10

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunMichael Straight

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Unreliable narration

2001-04-10 Michael Straight #

Asks whether Horn in Blue's Waters is deluded or whether his outward search for Silk is a subconscious call for Silk's persona to resurface, citing the Rajan's remarks on searching and on the man who could not see the inhumi. Grounds this in the Rajan's dream of killing Silk and the narrator's apparent sanity, and also doubts Hyacinth's death could drive Silk insane.
questions · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Horn is deluded about Silk's existence; Silk's persona is split from Horn subconsciously; Silk was insane before Hyacinth's death

Rereading OBW, I was trying to make sense of the Rajan's musings that Silk might be on Blue somewhere, or have died or Green. Could Horn be that deluded even after the confrontation with Mint? Might this outward search for Silk be a sort of subconscious goad to Silk's persona--if Horn calls out "Silk? Silk?" enough times, he might eventually pop up and say "Here I am!"? The Rajan writes: "Searching does not neccessarily imply movement" (p. 18).

But then I read this comment after the Rajan has made reference to slavery in New Viron: "On Green I met a man who could not see the inhumi. They were there, but his mind would not accept them...In just the same way, my own interior sight refuses to focus upon matters I find agonizing. In Ermine's I dreamed that I had killed Silk..." (p. 74).

I can accept that such a person as he describes, unable to see inhumi, could exist, but I would not consider him sane. If the Silk(Horn?) is unable to accept who he is "in just the same way" then he is insane and I think my attempt to find a coherent explanation of what Horn and/or Silk knows about himself is futile.

The problem is, the Narrator doesn't otherwise seem insane. I guess Horn is a sufficient crutch until Silk's mind can heal. The bigger problem I have is accepting that Silk could be driven insane by Hyacinth's death. I guess that Horn (and we readers of The Book of Silk) never knew how screwed up Silk really was.

(Maybe his brain was somehow adversely affected by the upload to Mainframe? Making him too-dependent on (Kypris-posessed?) Hyacinth?)

(And if the Urbasecundus copy of Starcrosser's Landfall were being made today, don't you think 'Mainframe' would be translated 'Network' instead? I guess were lucky it wasn't rendered 'MultivAC.')

-Rostrum
