The weapon too heavy to wield

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-04-28 to 2001-04-28

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunAlan Phillips & Ling Xiang

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2001-04-28 Alan Phillips & Ling Xiang #

Proposes that the inhumi secret is a soul-link: inhumi inherit intelligence and a psychic connection to their human victims, so killing bitten humans would reduce inhumi to animal intelligence. Grounds this in the book's oath-bound silence and fear of witch-hunts, while admitting the idea is unverified against the text.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theology-and-religion · theories: Inhumi intelligence depends on a soul-link to victims

I just had an idle thought on what the nature of the inhumi secret may be. This may not be supported by the books, but what if the inhumi inherit not only human intelligence from the blood of thier parents but also inherit a link to part of the human spirit. The inhumi would gain sentience and a "soul" of sorts which is connected (astrally? psychic?) to the victims of their parents. If the human victims of inhumi bites die, then the inhumi children might "loose their souls" i.e. die or cease to be intelligent.

Here is where we get the "weapon too heavy to wield". There are 2 ways to reduce the inhumi to mindlessness, one improbable and one possible:

1) For all victims of inhumi bites to become so altruistic that the inhumi who share their spirits stop drinking blood and die out. This, however, is so unlikely that even the most paranoid inhumi need not fear it.

2) Kill all humans who have been bitten by inhumi. This would reduce all inhumi to animal intelligence. This may be what Horn/Silk fears. If word of this got out there could be a mass witch-hunt to slaughter all the inhumi victims. There are probably many on Blue (and Green) who would gladly attempt this if they though it would work. This may be what the inhumi fear. Horn/Silk would also be reluctant to reveal this--less so because of his oath than to prevent the murder of innocent people.

Again, I haven't re-read the books to try to back this up and you have to swallow my soul-link theory to make it work but it sounded somewhat plausible.

--Alan
