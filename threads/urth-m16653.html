Quotes Wolfe's interview remark that Ares is the soldier god rather than the war god and that Aphrodite is the sex goddess, using it to rebut Marc Aramini's idea that Latro is an avatar of Ares trapped in human form. The post answers Aramini by citing Wolfe's own words on the classical gods rather than arguing from the text directly.

Gene Wolfe: Pas is certainly not Zeus. (No doubt he would like to be.) The

classical gods are, in general, not as people today imagine them. Ares is

the *soldier god*, not the war god, for example. Aphrodite is the sex goddess,

not the love goddess; the love god is Eros, her son.
