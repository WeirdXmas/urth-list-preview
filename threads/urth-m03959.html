Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

2007-06-12 Nigel Price #

Asks Matthew Groves to explain the connection between Gene Wolfe's story Tracking Song and Jack London's The Call of the Wild, which the poster has read but cannot see the link to. Replies to Groves's earlier suggestion that the two works correspond, expressing interest and admitting feeling stupid about missing it.
questions

I'm feeling very stupid at the moment. I've read "The Call of the Wild", and even read it within the last couple of years, but at the moment its connection to "Tracking Song" entirely eludes me, even though "Tracking Song" is one of my favourite Lupine tales. Far from being bored, I should be deeply fascinated, Matthew, if you would expound upon the correspondences.

Please, I should really like to hear about this!

Nigel
