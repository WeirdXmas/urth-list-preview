2009-03-11 Nicholas Gevers replying to JBarach #

Announces the anthology Other Earths, edited by Nicholas Gevers and Jay Lake, which includes a new Gene Wolfe story, "Donovan Sent Us", about a spy sent from Nazi America to German-occupied Britain. Describes the story as sinister and notes other Wolfean tales in the book by Paul Park and Theodora Goss plus a Lucius Shepard novella.
informs

As John Barach says, the anthology *Other Earths*, edted by yours truly and Jay Lake, due out on April 9 as a mass market paperback from DAW Books, contains a new story by Gene Wolfe: "Donovan Sent Us". OE is an alternate-history antho, and Gene's contribution is splendidly sinister. Nazi America sends a spy to German-occupied Britain, and said spy must act German--or perhaps *become German*...

Other rather Wolfean tales in the book are by Paul Park and Theodora Goss. Lucius Shepard fans should note his original novella, "Dog-Eared Paperback of My Life".

--Nick Gevers.
