Virus alert!

Urth list · 3 messages · 2002-12-23 to 2002-12-24

The thread: Members warned each other about a Klez-family email worm disguised as a 'Happy Christmas' message apparently sent from Stone Ox. Roy C. 2002-12-23 Roy C. Lackey #

Warns list members that an email apparently sent from Stone Ox, subject line Happy Christmas, carries the Klez virus and should not be opened. Reports that it crashed the poster's Outlook Express and suggests others check their hard drives for infection.
informs

All,

I received an e-mail this afternoon at 3:03 central time, subject line "Happy Christmas" from list-member Stone Ox. It crashed Outlook Express when I highlighted it (fortunately!), so I couldn't tell if it was sent to me as an individual or to all list members, so beware. It is infected with the Klez virus. Don't open the attachments and check your hard drives for any infections. I have never corresponded with Stone Ox, so I shouldn't have been in his address book, which is how the virus is spread, I think. So, if I got the e-mail, you probably all did. Take care.

-Roy

2002-12-24 Alice Turner #

Warns list members about a rampant Klez-family email virus disguised as Christmas greetings, describing how it spread and what it did to the poster's machine. Responds to Alice K. Turner's earlier report of an infected 'Happy Christmas' message by advising antivirus updates and caution with unrecognized attachments.
informs

This thing is rampant. I've received it three times today as an attachment from [email] (whose messages I have now blocked; you might consider doing that in advance). It's morphed from an earlier Klez virus that Norton now can cope with. Don't let your Norton lapse--it always catches up eventually! Don't open attached Xmas greetings if you don't recognize the sender! This is not a nasty virus, one of those evil worm things, just prankish. In the earlier version, which I was also lucky enough to get, it (a) made me unable to get the Norton quarantine notice off my desktop (that is fixed); (b) cricket sounds, not unpleasant, turn up at irregular intervals, even when I'm playing music (that has just started up again, though I opened none of the attachments).

Merry Christmas, every one,
-alga

2002-12-24 Fernando Q. Gouvea #

Informs the list that the Klez email worm forges the From line of the messages it sends, so the apparent sender may not be the real one. Gives a general warning to members, with a seasonal greeting appended; the post has no connection to Wolfe's fiction.
informs

Hi, folks. You should note that Klez will fake the "from" line in the email it sends, so that the apparent sender is not always the real sender.

Merry Christmas to all!

Fernando
