Supplies dictionary-style etymologies for the words lynx, schola and tessera, with the Whorl's own uses of each, such as Mucor's explanation of why lynxes are so named. Draws the words from a glossary-like list of terms in the Long Sun books, and gives their Latin and Greek roots.
informs · source-and-allusion

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Lynx. Any species of genus Lynx, especially the common lynx (Lynx lynx). In the Whorl Mucor says they are called lynxes because they hunt during the daytime. The name lynx is related to lux (Latin for light) and leukos (Greek for white).

Schola. Plural scholae or scholas. From Greek skhole. A Roman school, private room, lecture hal, meeting hall, association of people with the same profession. In the Whorl this is the place Paterae go to be inducted into their profession.

Tessera. Plural tesserae. From the Greek for four (tesseres, tessares, tettares). A square tablet used for identification or a password with the same purpose.
