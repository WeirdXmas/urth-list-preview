Ultan's Library

1999-04-08 Jonathan Laidlow #

Announces a new personal website called Ultan's Library, with a Gene Wolfe section offering a working bibliography of secondary criticism and an unfinished catalogue of reviews, and asks list members to send corrections and missing post-1990 material. Describes the planned year-by-year list of Wolfe's unpublished short fiction and notes overlap with Phil Stephensen-Payne's Urth-Man Extraordinaire, which the site will supplement.
informs

Hello folks,

Just delurking again for a minute.

I've been meaning to tell you about this for some time, and ask for your help. I've recently set up my own webpage (to alleviate work related boredom) on my research interests, and have decided to set up a Gene Wolfe section, known as Ultan's Library. I'm using this website to provide scholarly resources for my research (I'm completing my PhD on 18th century literature this summer).

At the moment the Library contains a working bibliography of secondary criticism relating to Wolfe, and an unfinished (very!) catalogue of reviews of Wolfe's fiction.

There is also a tentative plan to use the website as a springboard to some kind of printed journal/newsletter containing some kind of scholarly or serious discussion of Wolfe's fiction. This is obviously a long way off, as the time required for such a project will only be available once my PhD is finished (soon! soon!)

Anyway, if you'd all like to come along and have a look at the site, I'd appreciate your input on the many things that I have missed.

I'm aware of the similar work by Phil Stephensen-Payne in 'Urth-Man Extraordinaire', so the first stage of the project is to cover material post 1990 that isn't covered by him.

In a similar vein I'd also like to include a page which provides a year by year list of Wolfe's unpublished short fiction post 1990. This is not a task I'm well equipped for - its very hard to get all of the short fiction magazines here in the UK - so this is where you come in...

I'd be extremely grateful if you could send me any information that would help expand the website, to this address:
[email], marking your emails ULTAN in the header so
that I can keep them separate from work-related stuff.

The website can be found at:
http://members.tripod.co.uk/laidlow/index.htm

As its hosted by Tripod, you'll get a pop-up ad on the index-page. Either disable your java, or close it down. it will only appear on the first page you load in.

I'll post on the list whenever major updates are done to the site.

Thanks again,

Jonathan
