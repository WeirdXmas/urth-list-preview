Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2002-11-12 Dan Rabin #

Corrects Dan'l Danehy-Oakes by arguing that the paternal line is the one the books make clear, not the maternal. Points to the passages showing Dorcas and the elderly boatman in the Botanic Garden as Ouen's parents, while Severian's mother Catherine, an implied ex-Pelerine, is the obscure side.
disputes · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Dorcas and the boatman are Ouen's parents

Um, I think this is reversed. _New Sun_ makes it fairly clear that Dorcas and the elderly boatman in the Botanic Garden are the parents of Severian's father Ouen. It's Severian's mother and her parentage that we know little about, save that she was an ex-Pelerine (only implied, actually--she had "run off from some order of monials") named Catherine.

-- Dan Rabin
