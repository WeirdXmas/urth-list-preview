2000-06-11 David Langford #

Announces that David Langford has written a review of In Green's Jungles, based on a Tor proof, to appear in Vector, and will post it here with spoiler space. Answers Yves Menard's doubts by noting the book resolves many questions, including how Horn leaves Green and reaches the Whorl in a body the list had already suspected.
informs

I reviewed it from a Tor proof a couple of months back -- the review is due out in the British SF Association's =Vector= but I'll post it here with lots of (mostly unnecessary, I hope) spoiler space if that's OK with the list members.

Many answers are provided by the book, including the surprising way in which Horn leaves Green and reaches the Whorl in a body whose likely identity has already been suspected in discussion here.

Dave
