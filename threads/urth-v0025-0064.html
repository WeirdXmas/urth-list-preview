Humble retractions

1999urthNicholas Gevers

1999-04-13 Nicholas Gevers #

Retracts two earlier claims: that a literary feud existed between Wolfe and Brian Aldiss, deferring to Damien Broderick, and that the Whorl mailing list had gone inactive. Also asks whether anyone has read Elizabeth Hand's Winterlong, which the poster calls an early homage to The Book of the New Sun.
informs · theories: Helliconia was Aldiss's substitute for a Malacia sequel; The Malacia Tapestry may have inspired Wolfe

I must apologize for two rash utterances:
1) Any literary feud between Wolfe and Aldiss: Damien Broderick is on the inside track with Brian Aldiss, and his testimony to the contrary should be respected. The closest I've come to meeting Aldiss was when, at the age of one, I lived next door to his mother... But concerning HELLICONIA: this was, I suppose, the best way Aldiss could continue in a baroque idiom after any Malacia sequel was nixed. HELLICONIA simply isn't as good, a fact Dr. Broderick makes abundantly clear in READING BY STARLIGHT. I think it is still eminently possible that THE MALACIA TAPESTRY inspired Wolfe.
2) My statement that WHORL had become inactive: this is indeed nonsense. I thought I'd subscribed to WHORL, and that the lack of any Digests sent to me meant it had fallen silent...
One further note on Wolfean authors: has anyone else read Elizabeth Hand's WINTERLONG and its sequels? They're a clear, and initially very good, homage to TBotNS.
//--------------------------------------
Nicholas Gevers
