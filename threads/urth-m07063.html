Diffusion of Lupine influence in popular culture

2008-10-10 Nigel Price #

Asks whether the upcoming film The Mutant Chronicles contains other references to The Book of the New Sun beyond a sword-wielding character named Severian. Notes the name appears in preview material and wonders whether Wolfe's influence is reaching popular culture.
questions

I gather that there's a new SCiFi action/adventure/horror/nonsense film coming out in the UK soon called "The Mutant Chronicles". I see from the preview sites that one of the characters, who is decribed as "sword wielding", is called Severian.

Anyone seen this movie yet? Are there any other nods to TBotNS or is it just the name of the character?

I don't know whether it's a good thing or not, but Wolfe's influence seems finally to be penetrating the popular culture!

Nigel
