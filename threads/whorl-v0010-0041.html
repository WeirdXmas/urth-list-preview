Mucor creepy

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-09-29 to 1999-09-29

1999whorlThe Book of the Long SunDan Rabin

1999-09-29 Dan Rabin #

Argues that Mucor, the creature in the Long Sun books, has become treated in a cute, familiar way by the later volumes, unlike the frightening demon exorcised from Orchid's. Clarifies a earlier remark made in response to Alex David Grace's comment that Mucor is still creepy in Blue's Waters, citing Maytera Rose calling him her granddaughter as the evidence of this change.
disputes · close-textual-reading

Alex David Grace remarks (in commenting on my original comment) that Mucor is still creepy in _Blue_.

To clarify, my original remark was intended to refer to the way in which in the course of _Long Sun_ Mucor becomes treated familiarly. In particular, by the time Maytera Rose is calling he "my granddaughter" the whole thing seems very cutesy to me. Not at all the frightening demon who has to be exorcised from Orchid's.

-- Dan Rabin
