Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-06-11 adam louis stephanides #

Argues that the eoliths V.R.T. searches for in Free Live Free are not the Annese artifacts Roy sold, since V.R.T. hates his father and has assumed the identity of Marsch. Reasons that he would not risk returning to Frnechmen's Landing to pass along fake arrowheads and could use Marsch's funds instead, and suggests he wants the eoliths for his own use.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Annese artifacts are not the eoliths; V.R.T. searches for eoliths to use himself

Even if the implements Roy and V.R.T. sold were eoliths, I don't think that's the reason why V.R.T. is looking for them now. He hates his father, and he's assumed the identity of Marsch. Is it really likely that he would plan on returning to Frnechmen's Landing, where he would run the risk of being recognized as V.R.T., to pass along a few fake arrowheads? If he in fact had wanted to help his father, wouldn't he have access as Marsch to funds which he could just give to him?

As for why V.R.T. is searching for eoliths, it's probably to use them himself, since he can't make implements of his own. The Free People didn't use them, but V.R.T. may not have been taught all the skills they had. Also, he's been spoiled by "civilization": slightly before the mention of eoliths, he wishes he could shoot a large animal, presumably to eat.

--Adam
