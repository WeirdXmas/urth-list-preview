Isid 1000 1000e and the spectrum

2014-08-06 Marc Aramini #

Asks whether anyone knows of any light or microwave science behind the name Isid 1000 1000e, given that much of the technology in The God and His Man is wavelength based. Notes that E is a term for wavelength and frequency but doubts Isid relates to it, reluctantly suspecting the name is only a serial number.
questions · source-and-allusion

Since most of the technology seems to be wavelength based in The God and His Man, I was wondering if anybody knew anything in light or microwave science that corresponded to that kind of name - isid 1000 1000e. E is a term used in describing wavelengths and frequency, but I don't think isid has anything to do with that. I hate to say it's just a serial number.

2014-08-06 Gerry Quinn replying to Marc Aramini #

Speculates that the name Isid 1000 1000e might be verbalised binary code, while admitting having no firm idea for it. Responds to Marc Aramini's question about whether the name corresponds to real wavelength or microwave science, and adds that the name is probably unimportant to interpreting the book.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Isid 1000 1000e is verbalised binary code

I ofteh wondered about that name myself, but I don't have any ideas for it. Could it be verbalised binary code? In the end, I doubt it is important to the interpretation anyway.

- Gerry Quinn
