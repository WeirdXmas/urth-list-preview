2001-05-01 Adam Stephanides #

Informs readers that a five-page London Review of Books article on Edward Kelley, John Dee's partner, is available, covering his years with Dee and later career. Notes the poster only skimmed it and is unsure how relevant it is to the Aegypt books, and that it is not yet online.
informs · source-and-allusion

For those who are interested in the historical background to the Aegypt books, there is a five-page article in the April 19, 2001 issue of the LONDON REVIEW OF BOOKS on Edward Kelley, John Dee's partner, dealing both with his years with Dee and his subsequent career. I just skimmed the article (and I don't remember that much about Kelley from the books, to be honest) so I can't say how relevant it is to the books.

The LRB has a website (www.lrb.co.uk), but the article isn't online, at least not yet.

--Adam
