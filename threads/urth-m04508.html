Notes that the new cover art for An Evil Guest looks retro and appealing, and asks whether Wolfe's title connects to J.S. Le Fanu's story The Evil Guest, pointing to Le Fanu's Carmilla and a Project Gutenberg link. Admits he has not read the advance copy, so he cannot judge how well the art fits the book.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: An Evil Guest echoes Le Fanu's The Evil Guest

There was a post about the cover art for Wolfe's new book some time back, but it seems to have changed. I think the new art looks pretty great, nice retro feel, though not being one of the lucky few advance readers, I can't say how well it fits the book.

Also - I wonder if there's a connection with J.S. Le Fanu's story The Evil Guest? Le Fanu isn't THAT obscure, and his Carmilla is still fairly well-known, one of the key vampire novels, etc.

Here's the Le Fanu story at Project Gutenberg:
http://www.gutenberg.org/etext/10377

-Matt
