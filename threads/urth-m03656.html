STL vs FTL

Urth list · 4 messages · 2007-12-22 to 2007-12-22

2007urthJeff Wilsonthalassocrat
The thread: Thalassocrat asked how a ruler of many worlds could govern without faster-than-light travel, pressing on Jeff Wilson's suggestion that slower travel might explain Typhon's far-flung journeys in Wolfe's fiction. Wilson answered twice, arguing that an absentee ruler could hold distant worlds through stationary vassals, a technological edge, ties to subordinates and bribes, citing historical empires along with Dune and the Foundation series as models. The thread ends with the question left open rather than resolved, and the posts show no clear point of agreement beyond Wilson's defence of slower-than-light rule.

2007-12-22 Jeff Wilson replying to thalassocrat #

Argues that a far-flung empire like the one in Wolfe's Long Sun or New Sun setting could be governed without faster-than-light travel, by ruling through stationary vassals in each territory. Responds to thalassocrat's question about how a ruler of many worlds could manage without FTL, offering historical examples from Alexander to Victoria as the model.
answers · myth-and-structure

The way it was done by rules from Alexander to Victoria: by proxy through stationary vassals, the individual territories garrisoned with loyal forces superior to any the locals are likely to raise.

2007-12-22 Jeff Wilson replying to thalassocrat #

Argues that when travel takes centuries, an absentee ruler can hold distant garrisons only through a technological edge over the locals, strong ties to vassals, and bribes. Answers thalassocrat's question about travel times of hundreds of years by citing Dune and the Foundation series as examples of such rule.
answers · theories: Distant rule requires technological edge and strong vassal ties

The more infrequent the contact the more important the technological edge the garrison has over the locals, and the stronger the ties between the vassal and the absentee ruler must be. Bribes help, too. DUNE and the Foundation series have some examples.
