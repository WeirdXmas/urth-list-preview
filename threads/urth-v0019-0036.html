1998-10-02 Alex David Groce #

Gives a brief opinion that The Devil in a Forest is a good book though not Wolfe's best, calling it a well-written juvenile that is charming in a limited way. Replies to Alex David Groce, who asked whether anyone had read it before he started, and says it is quick to read and needs less puzzling than most Wolfe.
supports

It's a good book; not Wolfe's best--in some ways a well-written juvenile. Certainly worth reading, and won't take as long to read (or to puzzle over) as most Wolfe. Charming, in a limited way.
