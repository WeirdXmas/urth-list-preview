Conjunction Rules Out Green=Lune?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-04-01 to 1998-04-01

1998urthThe Book of the New SunKieran Cleary

Themes: Time loops and causality

1998-04-01 Kieran Cleary #

Concludes that Green is probably not Lune, since the evidence is lacking, while floating a speculative idea that the New Sun's arrival shifts Lune into an elliptic orbit of Urth. Also offers a playful theory that Typhon sends his downloaded persona and an army by relativistic time machine to arrive after the New Sun, responding to the earlier claim that Blue and Green are independent planets not Urth-Lune.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Green is Lune shifted into elliptic orbit by the New Sun; Typhon sends downloaded persona to future by relativistic time machine

well, <grasping at straws here> maybe the cataclysmic arrival of the New Sun shifts Lune into an eliptic orbit of Urth? Baldanders speaks of the waves of gravitation which cause the earthquakes at the close of UotNS....

All in all though, given the current lack of evidence it seems ever more the reasonable attitude that Green is not Lune. Nice thought though: megalomaniacal Typhon, in a Rex quondam Rexque Futurus gambit, sends his downloaded persona, a standing army and loyal subjects trained to believe that Typhon is a god, in a relativistic time machine to the future (timed to arrive after the coming of the New Sun, when Typhon will claim Ushas). ok ok, but it was a nice thought.
