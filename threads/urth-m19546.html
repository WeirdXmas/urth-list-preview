Reports that the poster read Paul McAuley's Confluence trilogy about a decade ago and judges it decent but weaker than Wolfe's work. Argues that McAuley uses circular time and concealed identities but is less mystical in worldview, making it poorer than the Sun series.
answers · genre-and-craft

I read it about a decade or go I think. It was all right, fantasy motiffs become increasingly science fictional as it progresses, but as an author while McAuley uses circular time and concealed identities he is a bit less mystical in his world view, and in my opinion it makes it a much poorer work than anything in the Sun series.

I might read it again someday, but not until I have finished everything on my shelf and there are no more new books I am interested in coming out. My remembrance of it is that it's decent but not great like Wolfe.
