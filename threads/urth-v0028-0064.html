1999-08-17 Jonathan Laidlow #

Notes that the film The Iron Giant is based on Ted Hughes' children's book The Iron Man, and jokes that the retitling was likely due to a Marvel superhero. Includes a signature promoting a personal Gene Wolfe web resource, Ultan's Library.
chats

'The Iron Giant' is of course based on former UK poet laureate Ted Hughes' superb children's book 'The Iron Man'. No idea why they changed the title, except maybe because of that dumb Marvel superhero. Eejits.

Jonathan

1999-08-17 Stephen Witowski #

Reports that Ted Hughes' children's book The Iron Man is also published under the title The Iron Giant in the United States, correcting the earlier post's implication about the title change. Offers this as a fact from a librarian, responding to Jonathan Laidlow's remark about the film's title and the book it is based on.
informs

Apparently the book is also known as "The Iron Giant" in the Us as well. (Another useless fact brought to you by a librarian!)
