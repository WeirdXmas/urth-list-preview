Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

2000-11-14 Tony Ellis #

Offers a secular reading of The Fifth Head of Cerberus, arguing that the final line shows Number 5 has become his own father, so the unique young man has ceased to exist and become the thing he hates. Grounds this in the preceding paragraphs, which the poster says already hint at the lost self, and frames loss of the self as the ultimate horror.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Number 5 has become his own father

Or if you want a more secular reading, from someone who doesn’t believe 5HC is intended as a Christian allegory in the first place… :-)

…If I was to call the last line “disturbing”, it would be because it confirms what the previous paragraphs have already hinted at: that Number 5 has become his own father. The unique, intelligent young man, whose life we have for a little while lived, has ceased to exist. Worse still, he has become the thing he hates. Loss of the Self is, perhaps, the ultimate horror.
