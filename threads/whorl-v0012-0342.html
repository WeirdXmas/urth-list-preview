How They Make Spam

2001-02-23 Dave Lebling #

Explains that spammers collect addresses by harvesting email addresses from any available source, including web pages, and that the list's public web archives make subscribers' addresses accessible to such address miners. Responds to a quoted question asking whether the list was spammed by someone who got the addresses directly, answering it with a general account of how spam addresses are gathered. Off-topic to Wolfe.
answers

Spammers get addresses by extracting email addresses from any available source, including web pages. Since the lists are archived on the web, they are accessible to address miners.
