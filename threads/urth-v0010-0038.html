Venus in the RAW

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-01 to 1998-05-01

1998urthMichael Andre-Driussi

1998-05-01 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Recounts that the Venus de Milo was a modern forgery whose arms were broken off, and that Robert Anton Wilson's Illuminatus! trilogy identifies her as Eris holding the golden apple of discord. Notes that Eris, Aphrodite and Eve are confused through the apple, and that Peace also appeared in 1975, suggesting shared influences on both novels.

Dan,

FWIW, I first encountered the Venus de Milo story (modern forgery, arms were actually broken off to increase saleability yet left in the cave, etc.) in something by Robert Anton Wilson--probably the ILLUMINATUS! trilogy (1975). RAW says she ain't Venus but Eris, if I remember rightly . . . in any event, one hand of the missing arms holds the golden apple of discord, causing some iconic confusion between Eris (giver of apple), Aphrodite (winner of apple, triggering Trojan War), and Eve (eater of apple, triggering Fall of Man).

As PEACE was also first published in 1975, I concluded that something was in the air during the generative periods of these novels . . . happy hunting!

=mantis=
