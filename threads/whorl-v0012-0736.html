2001-04-26 Nicholas Gevers #

Announces that a new Gene Wolfe story, "Copperhead," has been posted online at the SCI FICTION website, noting that the poster had earlier urged editor Ellen Datlow to publish one. Shares the link so list members can read it, with no analysis of the story itself.
informs

and it can be found here:

2001-05-04 Dennis G. Berdanis #

Replies to David DiGiacomo's dislike of a new online Wolfe story by saying the poster liked it, found the dialect easier to follow than usual, and could not understand the final paragraph. Contrasts the story's clarity with other Wolfe works where the poster did not know what a character was saying.
disputes · close-textual-reading

I liked it. Didn't understand the last paragraph but everything else seemed fairly straight forward. At least I could figure out the phonetic dialect this time. Many times I didn't know what Pig was saying.
