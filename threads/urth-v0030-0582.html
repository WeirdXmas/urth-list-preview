Proposes several children's and fantasy novels as contenders for the best juvenile or fantasy of the twentieth century, including The Magic Faraway Tree, A Wizard of Earthsea, and The Weirdstone of Brisingamen. Replies to alga's suggestion, noting that Wolfe's The Devil in a Forest should be mentioned though it would not rank in the poster's top ten.
