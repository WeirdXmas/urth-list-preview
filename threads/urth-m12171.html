2010-12-18 Lee Berman #

Reports that the poster has shifted from seeing Yesod as the next universe after Briah to seeing it as a higher universe above the manvantaras, with Kabbalistic meaning as a factor. Grounds this in the reading that the creations would be universes rather than galaxies, arguing that a timeless super-universe would not build additions to its own heaven.
proposes · theology-and-religion · close-textual-reading · theories: Yesod is a universe above the manvantaras; Creations refer to universes not galaxies

I used to think Yesod was the next universe in succession after Briah. And that they would eventually get an Earth and a Jesus there. In this scenario, the "creations" as galaxies would make sense. I haven't fully given up this view.

But last summer's discussion swayed my interpretation toward those of others who see Yesod as a universe above the others, at some higher plane than the manvantaras Banging and Gnabbing below. Considering the meaning of Yesod in a Kabbalich sense was part of the shift. In this view it makes more sense for the creations to refer to universes, not galaxies.

I mean if there is a super-universe which exists both in the cosmic future and past of Briah then it has a rather eternal essence. If they are building stuff it wouldn't be for their own eternal, heavenly space. Surely God doesn't build garages or other additions to heaven.

2010-12-19 Jeff Wilson replying to Lee Berman #

Retracts an earlier claim that Yesod is the next universe after Briah, after rechecking the text and finding that the Yesodis use the word galaxy only in its literal sense. Argues instead that the Yesodis watch over many galaxies within the same incarnation of Briah, moving through Briah-time over their lifetimes, so the Yesodis are not a separate universe.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: Yesod is a higher plane above the manvantaras; Yesodis watch many galaxies within one Briah

I believe I must retract the above statement, it is in error. I have re-checked, and I see only galaxy used to mean galaxy by the Yesodis.

It's clear that the Yesodis watch over many galaxies at once, and back and forth through Briah-time over the lifetime of those galaxies, but these are all within the same incarnation of Briah.

Argues that the Increate's garages, if any existed, would sit in a still higher universe, and that the ordinary words for cosmic structure are inadequate for describing Wolfe's cosmology. Responds to Lee Berman's objection that God would not build garages or additions to heaven, offering a light, self-aware remark rather than textual evidence, and appends a spam-like web link.
disputes · theology-and-religion

Surely the Increate's garages would be in a yet higher "universe."

I might note that, to some degree, all out terms are inadequate for what we are describing. People simply don't discuss other universes in their daily conversation! So we have to make do with specific religious coinology or else use ordinary words to suggest slightly expanded meanings.
