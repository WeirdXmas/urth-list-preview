Accepts a suggested name, Durra, for the Green Man and asks about its pronunciation. Then asks whether Severian must personally act to bring Ushas into bloom or to introduce Anthroposophy so the symbiotic algae potential remains, citing the four grassy hills he finds after his time as Apu Punchau.
questions · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Green Man named after durra grass

Hey, I'll take it! Good thinking, and very appropriate. Any hints on pronunciation?

I recall you recently mentioned having yet to ponder how Severian might go about bringing Ushas into full bloom, but isn't that something that would follow without his help, once the New Sun has come? The four grassy hills he returns to find after his stint as Apu Punchau seem to be good evidence his work is done.

Or since we are talking about the Green Man/Men, does he need to do something personally to introduce Anthroposophy so that the potential to engineer their symbiotic algae remains?
