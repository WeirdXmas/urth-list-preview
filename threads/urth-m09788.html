2009-11-16 James Wynn replying to PMorris33 #

Reports that Wolfe said at WindyCon that he would not mind another Latro sequel but doubts he will get to it given his age. Adds that the poster was surprised Soldier of Sidon let Latro return home, which removes an important motivation for the character.
informs · author-and-biography

I think someone probably asks Wolfe about this at every panel, but I recall it was asked last year at WindyCon. Wolfe said he would not mind doing another sequel but he's getting old, the clock is running, and he doubts he'll get around to it.

Personally, I was surprised regarding "Sidon" that Wolfe allowed Latro to return home. That's struck me as an important motivation for his character that was taken off the table.

J.
