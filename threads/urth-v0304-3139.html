2003-04-10 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Explains that a poster's earlier silence on the list was caused by a mundane email address change at his employer, which blocked his posts as coming from a non-member until the list administrator fixed it. Responds to Crush's joking image of electric arcs from Blattid's keyboard, correcting the jest with a self-deprecating account of the technical problem.
informs

If you please, we blattidae hiss or crackle. We do not yelp.

Alas, the real explanation was much more mundane and kind of dumb. Back in February, my employer changed the format for eddresses (so that mine, for example, went from "[email]" to
"Dan'[email]"). Mail to the old eddress continued to
be delivered, so I could read Urth; but mail from the new eddress was rejected as not coming from a listmember. The obvious solution -- unsubscribe and resubscribe -- wasn't possible because I couldn't send an email from the old eddress to unsubscribe. I could have just resubbed from the new eddress, or this one, but I didn't want to deal with two copies of each message.

Well, Ranjit's fixed it and I'm a happy bug.

--Blattid
