Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

2000-01-14 Joel Priddy #

Speculates that if Green is Venus, the Long Sun Whorl would be the moon, riddled with underground kingdoms, and raises questions about the inhumi's use or avoidance of technology, citing Krait's rifle and boat rigging. Also recalls expecting Horn not to narrate the later books and guesses Sinew would write Green, asking whether others had the same impression.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Sinew narrates Green; Long Sun Whorl is the moon

If Green is Venus on the basis that steamy dinosaury jungle planets are always Venus, then the Long Sun Whorl is the moon, which is riddled with underground kingdoms in the same sorts of stories.

As far as the inhumu secret: the other inhumi mystery is the use (or lack thereof) of technology. I haven't grasped what the inhumi can or can't do with technology, or how far imitation can take them (Krait and the rifle versus Krait and the boat's rigging). But, how might this fit into the secret vulnerability? It can't actually be the secret, as Krait was pretty blithe about mentioning his capabilities with both the rifle and the rigging, but it surely ties into the nature of inhumi intelligence.

Also, I can't remember what made me think this, but when reading Blue, I kept thinking that Horn wouldn't be the narrator of the next two books. I think I decided that Sinew would write Green. I'll eventually get around to rereading and see if this was just ramapnt second-geussing on my part, or if I was actually picking it up from the text. Did anyone else have this impression?

cephalothorax
