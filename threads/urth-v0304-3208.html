Themes: Free will and choice

2003-04-17 Malcolm McGugan #

Supports ArchD'Ikon Zibethicus's view that Lanark's failure at the Provan council assembly was avoidable, arguing the text allows an alternative in which Lanark's actions could change Unthank. Echoes Nastler's quoted remark about the 'incredible amount of freedom' in the flying machine's 'is...if' voices, links Lanark to Severian as a figure pushed by larger forces, and agrees Sludden planned the humiliation.
supports · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft · theories: Lanark's failure at the assembly was avoidable; Sludden deliberately sent Lanark to be humiliated
