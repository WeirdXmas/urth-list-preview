Argues that an interviewer, Nick Gevers, obtained a statement from the poster before permission was granted, then confronted Wolfe with it and mocked it, which the poster calls unethical. Rejects the interview question and Wolfe's answer as an attempt to trap Wolfe in a puzzle he deliberately built, so they carry no validity.
