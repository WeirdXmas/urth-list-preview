2003-08-14 Marc Aramini #

Reports a personal meeting with Gene Wolfe, relaying his remarks that The Wizard Knight is in a sixth draft and that work on Soldier of Sidon is slow. Notes his mention of Xerxes' brother as a historical figure and asks whether list attachments work for sharing photos.
informs

I've been having a problem looking up the Urth archives today, so forgive me if any of this is repetitive. I just got back from a trip - ran into Mr. Wolfe. He told me that he thought he was done with Wizard (book II of Wizard Knight) after the fifth draft, but that he is going through a sixth draft now. So I guess it's pretty close to being sent off. He said that work on Soldier of Sidon was slow, and when I asked if he was using any historical figures he said Xerxes' brother. I think. I could be wrong. Latro et al are sailing down the Nile.

I have a few pictures of me with Wolfe that I wanted to post to the list, but I'm not sure if attachments work. Does anybody know if they do? If not, no big deal.

Marc Aramini

2003-08-15 Josh Geller #

Corrects the travel route in Soldier of Sidon by noting the Nile flows north, so a voyage upstream would begin in Uganda or Ethiopia, and wonders whether the characters will use a canal. Responds to maa32's report on Wolfe's work, and anticipates Wolfe's take on Phoenician religion.
informs · chronology-and-plot-logic

Not unless they started in Uganda (or Ethiopia); the Nile flows from South to North. They are sailing up the Nile. I wonder if they are going to use the canal.

Sidon's a good place to be from if you want to do some serious world travelling in the the fifth century BC. Can't wait to see GW's take on Phoenician religion.

.
