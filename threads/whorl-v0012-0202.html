Pig, Godling

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-02-13 to 2001-02-13

2001whorlKieran Cleary

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

2001-02-13 Kieran Cleary #

Argues that the godling's encounter with Pig in The Book of the Long Sun resembles Severian's meetings with Juturna and the hierodules, since the godling kneels and recognises Silk as the 'holy one'. Responds to Alga, agreeing with her point and joking about Pig's struggle to decipher the dialect.
supports · close-textual-reading · allegory-and-typology · theories: the godling is a debased hierodule

Alga,

Couldn't agree more re Pig. Did he like deciphering that dialect? He did nae!

The encounter with the godling struck me as very similar to one of Sev's meetings with Juturna ('I would save you'), or with the hierodules, where they kneel before him. In a sense, the godling is a debased version of a hierodule, and recognises Silk as the 'holy one'.

Kieran C.
