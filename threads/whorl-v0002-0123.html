Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1997-02-07 Alice Turner #

Argues that Mucor is Orchid's child rather than Musk's, and that Silk's "mantis" and "young girl" remarks are metaphorical, disputing an earlier reading. Supports this with Orchid's admitted earlier lost child, Mucor's possible age given brain damage, and the name Mucor combining Musk and Orchid.
disputes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Orchid carried Mucor to term; Mucor's name combines Musk and Orchid

Checked those refs re Mucor's parentage, mantis, and you are wrong, wrong, wrong. Silk is speaking metaphorically in each case. In the first he has just met her and is thinking of her a young girl; in the second he refers to the fact that she has already been responsible for two sad deaths. Two hard knuckle-raps with Maytera Rose's ruler for mantis!

I don't think we will ever know who Silk's or Mucor's parents are, nor does it really matter. But it may interest some to know that both mantis and I think that Orchid carried Mucor to term. Who else, after all, would do it? Orchid admits to Silk that she had "lost" a child before Orpine. At first I thought that Mucor wasn't old enough to be older than Orpine---but Orpine may have been pretty young, and Mucor, like other brain-damaged people, may be somewhat older than she seems. There is also a possible clue in her name (it means "mould") which combines Musk and Orchid. (Of course it's a bit far-fetched to think that Musk, who is fairly young himself, was already Blood's lover when Mucor was born.)

-algae-
