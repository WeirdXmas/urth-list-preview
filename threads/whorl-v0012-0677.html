The eyes have it

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-04-20 to 2001-04-20

2001whorlRobert Borski

2001-04-20 Robert Borski #

Defends the size figures in the Long Sun books by arguing that godlings have characteristically small eyes, quoting Horn's remark to a godling that its eyes are small only in proportion to its face. Responds to Kevin M.'s objection that a 22-foot Pig could not have eyes fitting Silk or Horn's sockets, treating the objection as a question of physical scale.
disputes · close-textual-reading

The sharp-sighted Kevin M. writing:

<If Pig is 22 feet tall, it's hard to imagine that one of Silk|Horn's eyes would fit into his eye sockets.>

I think 22 feet is a little tall myself, but in defense of Matthew's figures, isn't one of the features of the godlings that they have characteristically small eyes? E.g., Horn says to the godling who picks him up: "Your eyes seem very small to me, but that's only because they are small in proportion to your face." (RTTW, 139)

Robert Borski
