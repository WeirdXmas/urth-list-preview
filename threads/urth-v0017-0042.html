Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-07-19 Alex David Groce #

Asks whether the name Eleanor Bold in Peace is a coincidence or a deliberate allusion to Trollope's Barchester novels, and what it might suggest. Grounds the question in a recalled Wolfe remark about Trollope and a job outside writing, reasoning that Wolfe has likely read Barchester Towers.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: Eleanor Bold name alludes to Trollope's Barchester Towers

I've often wondered if the name "Eleanor Bold" in PEACE is simply a coincidence, or is intended to mean something. I've seen Wolfe refer to Trollope before, particularly in an essay or interview when he was discussing the value to a writer of having a job other than writing (I think this is in CASTLE OF DAYS). So, it seems reasonable to assume Wolfe's read BARCHESTER TOWERS, and possibly all of the Barchester books, and it is an intentional effect. But what exactly is it supposed to suggest? Any ideas?
