Speculates that Valeria in Wolfe's fiction may be modeled on Rosemary Wolfe, citing shared physical traits, and wonders whether Wolfe's family reads his work given personal family material in the stories. Responds to a quoted post saying Rosemary Wolfe would not want to be put in the story, offering a reply that the artist's needs excuse such use.
proposes · author-and-biography · theories: Valeria is modeled on Rosemary Wolfe

Maybe. Valeria shares some of the physical descriptions. If not, the violet eye color and Gene Wolfe's age make a pretty good case for Elizabeth Taylor.

I sometimes wonder how much Gene Wolfe's family reads his work. I'm pretty sure he has included individual and family problems of rather personal nature in his stories. If they do read his work they just have to understand the needs of an artist, I guess.

The family problems of many other artists are much less disguised and more widely and luridly known.
