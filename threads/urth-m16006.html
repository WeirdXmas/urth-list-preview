Vodalus and Severian continued

Urth list · 6 messages · 2010-09-27 to 2010-09-29

2010urthThe Book of the New SunRyan DunnJeff WilsonLee BermanAndrew Mason
The thread: Readers debated why Hildegrin visited the Garden of Endless Sleep and what the bog there was for. Andrew Mason argued he went to find another victim, a regular practice, and later that the bog is a hiding place for recently dead bodies that might keep memories intact. Ryan Dunn backed the victim-hunting idea and pointed to the old boatman as a sign of regular visits. Jeff Wilson offered the rival view that Hildegrin was stashing a dead sympathizer, and he doubted the bog could preserve memories for long. Lee Berman proposed that Vodalus's followers are being steered to ingest important dead people, and he tied the boatman to Father Inire. The thread ended without consensus, with the bog-stash reading gaining ground but no one settling the matter.
  • Andrew Mason: Hildegrin visits the Garden to find victims, and the bog hides recently dead bodies, likely with memories intact
  • Ryan Dunn: Hildegrin visits the Garden to find victims and maybe Dorcas, based on the boatman's hint
  • Jeff Wilson: Hildegrin stashes a recently dead sympathizer in the bog; the bog cannot keep memories long
  • Lee Berman: Vodalus's followers are being led to ingest dead people important to Severian, such as the boatman linked to Father Inire

Themes: Memory and forgetting, Ghosts, dreams and visions, Death and resurrection

2010-09-27 Lee Berman #

Argues that the maid playing Holy Katharine, not the corpse, has the oval face and dark complexion, and links the Contessa Carina's maids to a woman on the Path of Air and to the dead woman's lividity from Allowin's Necklace. Responds to Jane Delawney's quoted description of the corpse's face by adding that the Path of Air woman matches all the features and that Cyriaca's strangulation may echo Severian's mother's fate.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Holy Katharine's maid is the oval-faced woman; Path of Air woman links Carina's maids; Severian's mother was strangled for adultery

It is the maid playing Holy Katharine who has the oval face and darker complexion. There are some missing connectors. In Dr. Talos' play there is a character named Contessa Carina (form of Catherine) who has three maids with beheaded saint names.

Eventually, I think even Gene Wolfe had to admit to himself that this wasn't enough of a clue. So he gave us a detailed peek at one final connector in UotNS, the woman on the Path Of Air who matches the Contessa Carina. This woman has all the characteristics combined, dark hair, oval face, olive complexion and pale gown. I find the attention Wolfe gives to her in this scene, without any other explanation, to be compelling and insistent that we connect her to other parts of the story. why would this woman's face tear at Severian's heart?

As Catherine may have been a Pelerine, I would not discount anything that might imply she had witchy connections. I think there is something connecting these two groups of women.

My own theory is that the lividity come from her manner of death- Allowin's Necklace, a device which gets an otherwise rather inexplicably detailed description. Ancillary to this is the sentence given to that other dark haired, oval faced ex-Pelerine Cyriaca- strangulation. Her crime was adultery and given the Ouen episode, I consider that may have been the crime of Severian's mother also.

But I am happy to incorporate witchiness also, especially given the missing Severa. Thanks for that Jane!

There is some vampiric imagery associated with a character or two in BotNS but we'll save that for some other thread.

2010-09-28 Lee Berman replying to Lee Berman #

Proposes that Vodalus and his followers are being directed to ingest dead people important to Severian, suggesting the old boatman in the Garden of Endless Sleep is such a person. Grounds this in the boatman's resemblance to Father Inire, Hildegrin's and the Cumaean's links to Inire, and a parallel with the Stone Town fishing scene.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Vodalus's followers ingest dead people important to Severian; Old boatman resembles Father Inire

It might. I think my thoughts have drifted in that direction. However it might also support my current hypothesis that Vodalus and his followers are being directed to ingest dead people who are important to Severian. Obviously the lake contains such a one.

Hildegrin and the old boatman know each other. It may be that their purpose is more intertwined than is apparent from their casual mention of each other. I say this considering that the boatman bears a resemblance to Father Inire, Hildegrin is associated with the Cumaean and she and Inire are associated, similar beings.

The lake scene with the boatman might also connect to the Stone Town scene. There we find the Cumaean, Merryn and again Hildegrin fishing for and hoping to restore life to someone important to Severian.

2010-09-28 Jeff Wilson replying to Andrew Mason #

Proposes that Hildegrin visited the Garden of Endless Sleep to stash a recently dead sympathizer rather than to seek a victim, responding to Jane Delawney's idea. Doubts the bog could keep memories intact for long, and speculates that Thecla's cooked meat may be pork spiked with her retentive tissues.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Hildegrin stashed a dead sympathizer in the Garden; Thecla's meat is pork spiked with her retentive tissues

Not to look for a victim/donor, thought it might be a place to stash a recently deceased known sympathizer, in a pickle-barrel sort of way.

I'm a bit skeptical that the bog would keep the memories intact for very long, but if they survive being roasted they ought to survive the bog. Though there is the possibility that in Thecla's case, the cooked meat is really pork spiked with her uncooked "retentive tissues" to maximize viability and minimize the gaminess that could gather even in a snow cave after so long.
