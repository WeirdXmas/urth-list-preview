Michael Richman,

Yes, the restarting of the long sun was at maximum power, which is what set the fig tree on fire . . . or so they reason. But I thought we were talking about the flash of light that Silk sees while admiring the starscape in the lander at the bottom of the shrine of Pas? Which so frightens him that he runs away.

Re: Mint as general. Somewhere she picked up some real military terms, military strategy, and military ability. Not likely to have come from religious school, nor from "Kypris" (not-her-real-name) the mortal courtesan. If you mean to say that Mint is a late blooming natural military genius, well sure, there's always the possibility that she is another cold one suddenly activated, just like we know Silk was.
