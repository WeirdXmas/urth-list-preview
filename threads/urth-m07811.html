2009-04-29 Ryan Buller #

Announces the poster's presence on Facebook and Twitter, noting Gene Wolfe groups there and a weekly practice of posting random Wolfe links under hashtags to attract new readers. Shares personal profile and website links, inviting other fans to connect on social networks.
informs

I'm on Facebook as Ryan Buller. There are a couple of Gene Wolfe groups there too.

Also, I'm on Twitter as mraf and have met a few other Wolfe fans through that social network.

http://www.twitter.com/mraf

Every Wednesday I post a random Gene Wolfe link under the hash tags #genewolfe and #wolfewednesday. I'm trying to get a trend going for the fans out there and get new people interested in Wolfe's work.
