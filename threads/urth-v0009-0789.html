1998-04-29 William H. Ansley #

Asks whether anyone knows what the hand-in-waistcoat detail refers to in Peace, where Weer mentions a Napoleon biography, and whether a biography of Napoleon by a man named Ludwig actually exists. Poses a factual question about the book's opening page, citing the Orb edition, with no argument offered.
questions · source-and-allusion

I reread PEACE recently and have a question. (Well, I've got a lot of questions, of course, but one in particluar.)

On page 2 of the Orb edition Weer mentions knowing why Napoleon had one hand thrust into his waistcoat, having read it, possibly in "Ludwig's biography of him." But when he told people he found that "the innocent remark invariably offended."

Does anyone in this group know what Wolfe is referring to here? And is there really biography of Napoleon by a man named Ludwig?

Thanks for any help.
William Ansley
