2008-11-05 Rob Thornton replying to Stuart Hamm #

Advises a reader who found Lady of Mazes slow that the novel needs close reading and may not suit them, and recommends Schroeder's Ventus or Permanence as blends of action and ideas. Responds to Stuart Hamm's question about whether the book picks up after three chapters, and mentions the Queen of Virga series without endorsing it.
answers

Not really. I think "Lady of Mazes" requires very close reading for it to work well and if you are not into it soon, it might not work for you at all. If you want to read a better blend of action and ideas from Schroeder, I would suggest Ventus or Permanence. His Queen of Virga series is good but I'm not turned on by the book's world-building.
