1997-06-04 Alice Turner #

Answers Alice Turner's question about who carries the gammadion in The Book of the Long Sun by saying all priests carry it and are trained to repair Windows, with richer ornament for higher rank. Cites Quetzal's jeweled gammadion, Incus fixing Hammerstone with one, and black mechanics who may use them, and endorses a looser Swiss Army knife analogy.
answers · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion

All priests carry them and have been taught to use them to fix Windows. They get fancier with rank---Quetzal's is jeweled. Incus fixes Hammerstone with one, and his non-priestly black mechanic pals doubtless wield them too, possibly illegally or at least heretically.

I don't think any of us thinks of a simple equivalent (gammadion = screwdriver), but it doesn't hurt to use an analogy. Mantis once came up with gammadion = Swiss Army knife, and I rather like that.

-alga-

1997-06-05 Michael Richman #

Corrects the earlier claim that Incus fixes Hammerstone with a gammadion, arguing instead that Quetzal uses an optisynapter on him. Cites the point that Quetzal could repair Maytera Marbles' arm while Silk could not, to show the two healers' tools differ.
disputes · close-textual-reading

see below

Quetzal doesn't fix Hammerstone with a gammadion but with something called an "optisynapter" IIRC. The Quetzal was able to repair Maytera Marbles arm while Silk was not.
