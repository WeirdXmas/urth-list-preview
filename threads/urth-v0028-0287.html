1999-10-27 Alice Turner #

Replies to Ratso's argument about the Commonwealth's Byzantine-Mediterranean geography by suggesting Spain as a cultural source, and notes underworld allusions in the book's gallows humor. Grounds this in Nessus, the centaur who bloodies the cloak of death, and Gyoll, the river of Hel, and adds a joke about server problems.
