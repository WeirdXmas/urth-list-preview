Themes: Names and their meanings

2005-02-19 Maru Dubshinki #

Asks why the talking cat in The Wizard Knight is named after Mani, the Persian founder of Manichaeism, and what that name could have to do with Mani. Halfway through the book, it links an everything2 entry on Mani and offers no answer yet.
questions · source-and-allusion

So, I finally bullied my library into buying a copy of the Knight, since I'm a cheap bastard.

Half-way through, I find myself wondering,
why on earth is a talking cat named after the Persian founder of Manichism?
What could that have to do with Mani?

~Maru
