Thecla as transvestite

Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Women, sex and gender

2006-05-26 b sharp #

Notes a Severian dream in which he becomes Thecla and is pleased to wear men's clothing, and links it to the historical Thecla who travelled with Paul disguised as a man. Grounds the connection in the saint's reported hair-cutting and removal of jewelry, and her earlier listening to Paul's preaching from a window.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Severian's dream echoes the historical Thecla's disguise

I was always amused and puzzled by a scene, in a Severian dream, where he turns into Thecla and she is pleased to find herself in men's clothing.

A recent web surf showed me that the original Thecla travelled around with Paul, with her hair cut and jewelry removed, so she could pass as a man and not be molested (by rapists or suitors).

Before that, there is a lot of imagery of her sitting in a window (trays and a rood?) to listen to Paul's preaching when she wasn't allowed to go out.
