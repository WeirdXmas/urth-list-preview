Chrasmological Writings

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-04-03 to 1997-04-03

1997whorlJames Jordan

1997-04-03 James Jordan #

Explains the Greek root of "Chrasmological," arguing it derives from khresmos (oracle) and logos (word), so the phrase means oracular writings. Responds to alga's request for a root by confirming an option Mantis listed in his glossary and detailing vowel and ending changes in compounds.
answers · source-and-allusion

Oh, well good ole Mantis has it as one option in his Q&DGTTLSW. I just want to assure him that this is certainly the correct option.

Khresmos - oracle. The "e" is the long "e", the "eta," which has a bright "ay" sound and can become the broad "a" sound as in "cat, bat, rat." The final "s" drops off in compounds, as in khresmodoteo, "give an oracular response." BTW, a related word, "khrisma," means "anointing," and is the root for "Christos," the Anointed One. But here it is khresmo-, "oracular."

Logos - word, speech.

Khresmologia - oracular word.

Chrasmologic Writings would really just mean Oracular Writings, or more complexly, Writings of Oracular Messages.

Nutria, the Big, Big Rat
