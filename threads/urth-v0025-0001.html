1999-04-05 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Marks the second anniversary of the Urth mailing list, noting its roughly 7 megabytes of messages and about 2000 pages written since it opened. Offers thanks to the members for the long, fascinating conversation, with no argument about any of Wolfe's books.
chats

Today is the second anniversary of the opening of the Urth list on April 5, 1997.

Since that day we've written almost 7 megabytes or about 2000 pages.

To put this into perspective, the stream of words would
reach to Alpha Centauri and back, if each letter were seven million miles long. Well, maybe that doesn't really put things into perspective at all. Anyway, thanks for the
long, fascinating conversation.

-- ranjit
