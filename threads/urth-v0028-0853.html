Admits the poster misread which edition of The Fifth Head of Cerberus came first, and says a textual variant in the sentence about all the worlds being born puzzled them for years until they read the Scribner's version. Responds to Damien Broderick's acknowledgement of his own misreading of the variant texts, treating the difference as a simple typographical error rather than a deliberate change.
