Severian as Jonah

Urth list · 12 messages · 2012-04-16 to 2012-04-17

2012urthThe Book of the New SunMatthew KnightLee BermanGerry QuinnDavid StockhoffMarc AraminiDan'l Danehy-OakesAntonin Scriabin
The thread: Participants debated whether Severian in The Book of the New Sun can be read as Jonah. Marc Aramini and Antonin Scriabin rejected the parallel, arguing Jonah's task of saving Nineveh from moral corruption differs from Severian's mundane bringing of the New Sun, and that Jesus' willing sacrifice contrasts with Jonah's flight. Matthew Knight and Lee Berman defended the typology, citing Matthew 12:40 and the recurring fish-mouth, cave and belly motifs. David Stockhoff granted the analogy while noting it breaks down, since Severian is not swallowed by a whale and Urth is doomed regardless. Aramini later partly supported the reading, and Berman shifted to a Noah's Flood cleansing reading, so the thread drifted rather than resolving.
  • Marc Aramini: Jonah parallel fails; Severian's task is mundane and Jesus' sacrifice contrasts with Jonah's flight, though he later notes the city-sign echo
  • David Stockhoff: Comparison holds with qualifications; the whale and doom parallels break down
  • Matthew Knight: Jonah reading is historically grounded in Matthew 12:40 and defended against moral objections; also sees Jesus' sea voyages as a parallel
  • Lee Berman: Severian is a Jonah-like precursor tied to fish-mouth and cave motifs; later reads him as a Noah-style cleansing of Urth
  • Antonin Scriabin: Different salvation types; Severian's mundane task is not Jonah's moral mission

Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Catholic faith and sacraments, Ghosts, dreams and visions

2012-04-16 Lee Berman #

Proposes that Severian in The Book of the New Sun is a Jonah-like precursor, citing a recurring fish-mouth and cave motif in the book and the Jonah-Jesus typology. Grounds this in the three-day belly-and-cave parallel, the Jurupari, Ouroboros, Abaia, man-ape cave and Fomalhaut references, and the idea of a gnostic Urth awaiting Christ.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: Severian is a Jonah-like precursor to Christ

I was watching a TV show on the "Secrets of Christianity" and there was a segment on a sea voyage Jesus took. The interesting part (for this list) was a mention that Jesus probably took this trip as a deliberate attempt at recreating Jonah's voyage. There are a number of parallels to be found between Jonah and Jesus (http://www.agapebiblestudy.com/charts/The%20Typology%20of%20Jonah%20and%20Jesus.htm)

The part I found most pertinent to BotNS was the parallel of Jonah spending 3 days in the belly of the great fish, then being expelled, parallelling Jesus' 3 days in the cave then being resurrected.

Previously I had posted about a recurring theme regarding fish mouths and caves that I saw in BotNS including the Jurupari reference, the Ouroboros story, Abaia and undines, the man-ape cave and the Fomalhaut reference. Though I saw this theme, I really didn't understand its significance. Perhaps now I do.

Not everyone agrees but I see Urth (and The Whorl) as a "gnostic" world which, despite being more technologically advanced (and declined) than modern earth, is spiritually retarded; i.e. they have not yet received Christ. If this is the case then perhaps the fish mouth/cave theme is meant to invoke Severian as a Jonah-like figure. A precursor who helps prepare the world for a future coming of Christ.

FWIW, various translations of the Jonah story list him as swallowed by a whale, a great fish and even *a great beast*.

2012-04-17 Lee Berman replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that Severian's bringing of the New Sun works as a cleansing of Urth like Noah's Flood, purging corruption from fallen-angel and human-animal matings rather than letting humanity evacuate. Grounds this in Dr. Talos' play narrated by Gabriel, which mentions Nephilim, demons and a new creation, and in the cleansing of the Vincula and Horn's sewer cleansing in IGJ.
proposes · theology-and-religion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Severian's New Sun is a Flood-like cleansing; Urth's humanity contains fallen-angel matings; Dr. Talos' play foreshadows Noah parallels

To me they are quite similar but it bears some explanation. Master Ash tells us that if Urth took the path of ice, most of the humanity there would be evacuated to other planets before the freeze. So what is the purpose of bringing the New Sun? (and, as I once asked, What's So Great About Ushas?)

Superficially we know that the New Sun and Ushas somehow allow for the creation of the Hiero-types. This facilitation of creation of a higher race of (human-derived) beings could be considered a form of salvation. But I think there is more to it. The answer is found mostly in Dr. Talos' play.

The Bible does not clearly explain what wickedness was occurring on earth which necessitated Noah's Flood. But in context it would seem to have something to do with the Nephilim, giants or "men of reknown" walking on earth who were the product of fallen angel/demon matings with human women. James Jordan (as a modern Christian theologian) once vehemently disagreed with me on this interpretation but I think, at least in the context of BotNS, it is how Wolfe is viewing things. (elaboration on the demons, giants and Nephilim can be found in the non-canonical Book of Enoch and the Dead Sea Scrolls)

Dr. Talos' play is narrated by Gabriel, who is a herald of great Biblical events (in the Book of Mormon, "Gabriel is believed to have lived a mortal life as the prophet Noah. The two are regarded as the same individual; Noah being his mortal name and Gabriel being his heavenly name."). Dr. Talos (who plays Gabriel) mentions many of the pre- Flood elements in this play including Nephilim, demons, demon-human mating and the dawn of a new creation of humanity. Elements from other, similar Flood myths are also included (e.g. the names Meschia and Meschianne).

I think in bringing the New Sun, Severian is performing a similar cleansing of Urth as Noah's Flood brought to earth. I think there has been alien (fallen angel) human matings on Urth (not to mention human-animal matings) and this is part of the corruption which must be swept away and must not be perpetuated by an evacuation. (hence the prevailing isolation/quarantine of Urth's humanity). The height of exultants and the size of Baldanders (a potential ruler) suggests the nephilim/giant aspect from Genesis.

As has been discussed before, the cleansing of the Vincula is a symbolic representation of Severian's purifying role. Horn's cleansing of the sewer in IGJ performs a similar function. Both hearken back to the cleansing of the Augean Stables by Hercules, who, like Jonas and Dionysus, can be seen as a precursor to Christ.

dawn of a new creation of humanity. Elements from other, similar Flood myths are also 
included (e.g. the names Meschia and Meschianne).
 
I think in bringing the New Sun, Severian is performing a similar cleansing of Urth
as Noah's Flood brought to earth. I think there has been alien (fallen angel) human
matings on Urth (not to mention human-animal matings) and this is part of the
corruption which must be swept away and must not be perpetuated by an evacuation.
(hence the prevailing isolation/quarantine of Urth's humanity).  The height of exultants 
and the size of Baldanders (a potential ruler) suggests the nephilim/giant aspect from 
Genesis.
 
As has been discussed before, the cleansing of the Vincula is a symbolic representation
of Severian's purifying role. Horn's cleansing of the sewer in IGJ performs a similar
function. Both hearken back to the cleansing of the Augean Stables by Hercules, who,
like Jonas and Dionysus, can be seen as a precursor to Christ.
