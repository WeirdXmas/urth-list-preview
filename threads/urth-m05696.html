Creating the Claw

2008-07-07 Dave Tallman #

Asks how the rose thorn that Severian left in the past could end up at the center of a sapphire without being destroyed, citing synthetic corundum methods such as the Verneuil process and hydrothermal synthesis. Frames the question as a follow-on to the discussion of how Terminus Est was forged, and raises the problem of centering the thorn in the gem.
questions · source-and-allusion

All the interesting discussion on how to forge Terminus Est leads me to think of another question. Sev dropped off his plain rose thorn in the past. Centuries later, it shows up in the center of a sapphire. I know there are techniques for creating synthetic corundum crystals, like the Vereuil process and hydrothermal synthesis, but they seem to involve high heats and pressures. How could the gem be created without burning up the thorn? Also, how could it be centered?

2008-07-07 Lane Haygood replying to Dave Tallman #

Proposes that the rose thorn Severian left in the past could survive being grown inside a synthetic sapphire if coated in a heat-resistant material, such as an advanced plastic. Offers this as a layperson's speculation, noting that a society able to build solar sails might have such materials, in reply to Dave Tallman's question about how the gem could form without burning the thorn.
proposes · genre-and-craft · theories: Thorn coated in heat-resistant material survives synthesis

I'm no geologist, but I did have an interest in the subject in my younger days, so I'll take a stab at this one. I'd say that as long as the thorn was coated in some sort of heat-resistant material, like an advanced type of plastic, it could survive the processes used to create such a gem. Then again, in a society that can build solar sails...

Lane
