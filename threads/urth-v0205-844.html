Urth list · 3 messages · 2002-05-01 to 2002-05-02

2002-05-01 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Informs the list that Lexicon Urthus, the reference book on Wolfe's terms, is now officially out of print and no longer sold directly by Sirius Fiction. Notes that some copies may still be filled through distributors and Amazon, and closes by advertising Sirius Fiction booklets and a timeline for Peace.
informs

Ladies and Gentlemen,

Late breaking news--LEXICON URTHUS is now officially out of print.

Some are still in the distributor system, so orders via Amazon.com might still be filled for a while yet.

But direct purchase from Sirius Fiction is no longer possible.

Thank you very much, everyone, and Good Night.
