1999-10-07 Dave Lebling #

Asks whether the Innovation Comics graphic novel adaptation of The Shadow of the Torturer published two or three of its six proposed issues, and requests hard evidence such as a copy of a third issue. Reports owning the first two issues from Mile High Comics and praises their quality, noting a third issue is mentioned by sources like Paul Duggan's Gene Wolfe page.
questions

I'm curious as to the facts concerning the Innovation Comics _Shadow of the Torturer_ graphic novel. There seems to be no consensus on whether two or three of the six proposed issues were ever published.

I was able to get the first two issues from Mile High Comics ( http://www.milehighcomics.com), less expensively than the eBay offering a few months ago -- less than $10 including shipping for two mint condition issues. It's really quite well done, capturing the Wolfean flavor better than I expected it would. A must for Wolfe completists.

Mile High only lists two issues in their online catalog, but I've heard of a third issue from various usually reliable sources, such as Paul Duggan's Gene Wolfe page.

So, is it two issues or three? Does anyone have hard evidence, such as a copy of the third issue?

-- Dave Lebling
(aka vizcacha)
