Five Dog Knight, aka Casting Couch

Urth list · 3 messages · 1998-05-14 to 1998-05-14

1998urthThe Book of the New SunThe Book of the Long SunAlice TurnerPeter StephensonPeter Westlake
The thread: Readers playfully cast actors from the Five Dog Knight thread as characters in Wolfe's books, with Alice Turner proposing Sir John as Palaemon, Sir Alec as Malrubius, and similar picks for other roles. Peter Westlake and Peter Stephenson joined in with jokes, Westlake agreeing that Nicolas Cage as Silk and Sissy Spacek as Dorcas would be bad choices and Stephenson offering Leslie Nielsen as Malrubius and ALF as Triskele. The thread stayed lighthearted and offered no textual argument. It ended without resolution as the casting game simply wound down, and Westlake said he would take a related idea to the Whorl list because the list had been quiet.
  • Alice Turner: proposes British actors for main roles and rejects Nicolas Cage as Silk
  • Peter Westlake: agrees Cage is wrong for Silk and says Spacek would be wrong for Dorcas
  • Peter Stephenson: offers comic casting picks such as Leslie Nielsen as Malrubius and ALF as Triskele

Themes: Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both, Humour in Wolfe

1998-05-14 Alice Turner #

Casts actors from the Five Dog Knight thread as Wolfe characters: Sir John as Palaemon, Sir Alec as Malrubius, Dame Maggie as Old Valeria, Sir Ralph as Gurloes, Sir Derek as the Autarch. Rejects Nicholas Cage as Silk and backs Leo, a joking reply to an earlier suggestion, with no textual argument.
chats

Okay, there seems to be a consensus on Sir John as Palaemon. Can we then give Malrubius to Sir Alec and Old Valeria to Dame Maggie? And is Sir Ralph still with us?--he could be memorable as the somewhat slimy Gurloes. And Sir Derek IS the Autarch--I could kick myself for overlooking him even as a momentary lapse.

(The Brightest Stars in the Aristocratic Firmament Set This Epic Adventure Ablaze.)

But fie on the foul felon who proposed Nicholas Cage as Silk. That's a terrible idea. Everybody loves Silk. Does everybody love Nick Cage? Does *anybody* love Nick Cage? I'm sure O'Leary will back me up when I put myself 100% behind Leo, Leo, Leo.

-alga-

1998-05-14 Peter Westlake #

Jokingly agrees that casting Nicolas Cage as Silk is a bad idea and adds that Sissy Spacek as Dorcas would also be wrong. Responds to alga's casting joke and mentions a Hitchhiker's Guide idea, then says he will post it to the Whorl list instead as the list has been quiet.
chats

It is, isn't it? Mind you, Sissy Spacek as Dorcas was a bit off, too :-) Mucor, I grant you.

As for the Hitchhiker's Guide - but that's a Long Sun idea, and a very silly one too. I shall post it to Whorl instead. It's been rather quiet lately.

-- Spectacled bear.

Peter Westlake, Harlequin Ltd, Barrington Hall, Barrington, Cambs CB2 5RG.

1998-05-14 Peter Stephenson #

Jokingly proposes casting Malrubius and other roles for a film version of Wolfe's books, suggesting Leslie Nielsen for Malrubius, ALF for Triskele, a cameo by the late Larry Olivier-type actor as the Pancreator, and Chatsworth as the House Absolute. Responds to Alice Turner's casting remark about Sir Ralph and Gurloes, playing along with the lighthearted casting game.
chats

Alas not, so he'd better be Malrubius :-)

That reminds me, I missed out two from my anti-list

Malrubius: Leslie Nielsen
Triskele: ALF

Well, more sort of `knightly' rather than `aristocratic'. If Larry were still around, he could do a cameo of the Pancreator, say. Maybe you could hire a stately home for the House Absolute. Chatsworth would do.
