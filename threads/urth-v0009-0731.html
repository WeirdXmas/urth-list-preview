Thank you all very much

1998-04-14 William H. Ansley #

Thanks the list for an enjoyable introduction after reading its Urth and Whorl archives, praising the members' learning and the Q&A sessions with Gene Wolfe. Says a question and comment will follow in a separate message, and laments that participation seems to be declining.
chats

Hello,

I discovered this mailing list a few weeks ago and have just finished reading all of the archives of both the Urth and Whorl lists and boy is my brain tired! It is wonderful to read the opinions of people discussing intelligently one of my favorite authors. The members of this group possess an awe-inspiring amount of erudition, but I suppose that is to be expected in GW fans. I am sorry I didn't chance upon the lists sooner. I don't know that I would have had anything to contribute or would have contributed it if I did, but I like to think I would have. Now it seems as if participation in both lists (but especially Whorl) is dying down. I do have a question and comment or two, which I will post in a separate message, in any case.

I want to thank all of the contributors to both lists for providing me with a most entertaining and educational experience. Getting to read the Q&A sessions with Gene Wolfe in the Whorl list was alone more than worth the price of admission.

Gratefully yours,
William Ansley
