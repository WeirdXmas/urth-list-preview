Mistakes/editing errors in "Solar Labyrinth"??

Urth list · 2 messages · 2006-08-25 to 2006-08-27

2006urthThe Book of the New SunJonathon McKitrickRoy C. Lackey

2006-08-25 Jonathon McKitrick #

Asks whether two apparent errors in the Solar Labyrinth reference book are real, pointing to a crash in the Cathedral placed in The Claw of the Conciliator rather than early in The Shadow of the Torturer, and a claim in the Catherine chapter linking lame characters to Severian the Lame that he cannot find. Also asks whether an errata page exists for the book.
questions · close-textual-reading

I'm on my second reading of BotNS, and I just got "Solar Labyrinth" in the mail. And I was surprised that only 12 pages into the book, I might have found 2 errors. I'm probably way off on this, but here goes:

1. A reference to the crash in the Cathedral as having occurred in Claw, when it was obviously the early half of Shadow.

2. The author says in the chapter "Catherine" that anyone lame or limping, he associates with Severian the Lame. I looked back at the chapter, and saw no such statement.

Am I missing something huge here? Is there an errata page for this book?

Jonathon McKitrick

2006-08-27 Roy C. Lackey replying to Jonathon McKitrick #

Corrects Jonathon McKitrick's claim that the Catherine chapter of Solar Labyrinth says anyone lame associates with Severian the Lame, stating the limping reference appears in the chapter's Note 4 rather than the chapter text. Answers the errata question with a flat no, locating the passage in the notes section.
answers · close-textual-reading

Yeah, that's a mistake.

You are right about his not having made such a reference directly in the text of the "Catherine" chapter. Rather, he made the limping reference in Note #4 for the "Catherine" chapter.

No, and no.

-Roy
