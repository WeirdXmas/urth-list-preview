Old Sun

2002urthThe Book of the New SunJames Jordan

Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, The dying sun and the cosmos

2002-03-05 James Jordan #

Proposes that the Old Sun is the "old Adam" and the black hole symbolizes human depravity needing regeneration by the White Fountain, while the New Sun Severian is a "new Christ." Responds to Roy's quoted claim that agents of the Increate wounded the sun as retribution, granting that the black hole is more than a symbol but pointing to redemption.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: Severian is a new Christ figure; Old Sun is the old Adam; Black hole symbolizes human depravity needing redemption

If Sev. is the New Sun, and a kind of "new Christ," then the Old Sun is the "old Adam." The black hole is a symbol of the depravity in the heart of man, which needs regeneration by the White Fountain of the Holy Spirit. Of course, it's not just a symbol. But I think that however the black hole got there, it is intended as a pointer to the need for redemption.

Nutria
