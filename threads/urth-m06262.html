size of erebus, et al.

Urth list · 6 messages · 2008-06-19 to 2008-06-21

2008urthThe Book of the New SunJeff WilsonDave LeblingStanisław Bocianb sharp
The thread: Thread asked how large Erebus, the naviscaput monster in The Fifth Head of Cerberus, really is. b sharp argued it is mountain-sized with a body reaching the ocean floor, and that the New Sun will end such monsters. Jeff Wilson disputed this, suggesting the giant's scale owes more to the submerged Monitor of Hampton Roads, then offered a possible echo of Hampton Roads in the canal network. b sharp then argued that megatherians like Tzadkiel are defined by their ability to make smaller agents rather than by true size. Dave Lebling argued the megatherians are fallen angels like devils, sharing angelic capabilities, and urged setting size debates aside. The thread drifted from size toward the nature of the megatherians without clear resolution.
  • b sharp: Erebus is mountain-sized, reaching the ocean floor, and megatherians matter for making smaller agents rather than true size
  • Jeff Wilson: Erebus's scale draws on the submerged Monitor at Hampton Roads, and the canal network may echo that name
  • Dave Lebling: megatherians are fallen angels like devils, sharing angelic powers, so size debates can be dropped

Themes: War and soldiers, Gods, angels and the Increate, The dying sun and the cosmos

2008-06-19 b sharp #

Proposes that Erebus, the naviscaput monster, is far larger than the ship at its head, its body reaching the ocean floor and its size akin to a mountain. Cites hints in the text, including Severian's talk with Master Ash about Ascian military haste, to suggest the New Sun will rid Urth of such monsters.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Erebus's body extends to the ocean floor; Ascians hurry because the new autarch is near; New Sun will destroy monstrosities on Urth

Though it is a mangled legend, I agree, keeping in mind that it is a naviscaput= ship head monster, the big ship being only the tip of the "iceberg". I always assumed the rest of his body extended down to the ocean floor, his "feet" roiling the benthos as he walked. Seems plausible his entire size could be somewhat akin to a mountain's.

I also find numerous hints in the text that the coming of the new sun will, in fact, rid Urth/Ushas of such monstrosities/megatherians. I just found another subtle one during a recent re-reading:

In his conversation with Master Ash, Severian is made to understand that the Ascians are currently engaged in a desperate, frantic attempt to gain a quick military advantage. The reason for the desperate need is left sounding important but unexplained. I think it may be that Erebus and Abaia are somehow aware that the autarchy is about to be passed on to a likely New Sun bringer and they hope to stop it.

2008-06-19 Jeff Wilson replying to b sharp #

Argues that the giant Erebus in The Fifth Head of Cerberus owes more to the Battle of Hampton Roads, with the Monitor's low, mostly submerged bulk and small exposed turret, than to a mountain-sized ship-head monster. Responds to b sharp's quoted remark that Erebus is a naviscaput whose body reaches the ocean floor and is roughly mountain-sized, and reframes the giant's scale around the Monitor's submerged profile.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Erebus is modelled on the USS Monitor

The giant keeps to channels on the island most of the time, doesn't he?

I think he owes more to the other ur-story, the Battle of Hampton Roads, where the Monitor's considerable but not mountainous bulk remains at or below the waterline with only a 10 x 20 foot turret exposed.

Responds to b sharp's thread on Erebus's size, admitting an earlier confusion about the naval name and offering this as a possible explanation, citing no passage.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Naviscaput's canals reflect the name Hampton Roads

You're welcome. It confused me for the longest time how there could be a *naval* battle at a place called Hampton Roads, but I guess I 'm not the only one and perhaps it is reflected in the network of canals, watery "roads" that allow the naviscaput to be on the island but still in the water.

2008-06-20 b sharp replying to b sharp #

Argues that the megatherians, like Tzadkiel, are god-like beings whose true size matters less than their ability to create smaller agents of themselves. Grounds this in Tzadkiel pinching off a finger to make a human-sized agent, a fairy-sized Tzadkiel later, and the Naviscaput's fingertip, while replying to b sharp's Monitor comparison.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: Megatherians are peers of Tzadkiel; Tzadkiel pinched-off agents create smaller agents; Giant Tzadkiel may be a pinched-off version

Jeff Wilson says:

Yes, me calling the channels "ocean" could be a notable error since we are discussing size. Battle of Hampton Roads! I hadn't heard the Monitor and Merrimac battle called that before; thanks! Anyway, the Naviscaput has a fingertip so I extrapolate that he has a somewhat human body, not just a boat shape.

However, I think all debates on the "true" size of megatherians might be moot. I consider them to be in some way the peers of Tzadkiel: god-like creatures. And I think the scene where Tzadkiel pinches off a finger to create a human sized agent is very important. We see a fairy-sized Tzadkiel later. Could the giant Tzadkiel that Severian talks to be a pinched off version also? How big is the Tzadkiel Severian sees in the old Autarch's book? Planet-sized? Sun sized? Galaxy-sized? That may seem ridiculous but the Hierogrammates are capable of creating as many suns as they deem necessary; they might be very very big.

Anyway, I think the megatherians might able to create smaller agents of themselves in similar fashion, enabling them to mate with Juturna and her sisters or interact directly with humans, take other shapes, etc.

2008-06-20 Dave Lebling replying to b sharp #

Argues that the megatherians such as Abaia are like devils, just as Tzadkiel is like an angel, so as fallen angels they would share angelic capabilities and appearance. Responds to b sharp's quoted remark by Jeff Wilson on the size of the Naviscaput and the megatherians, offering this as a reason to set aside size debates.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: Megatherians are fallen angels like devils

Just as Tzadkiel is like an angel, Abaia and the other megatherians are like devils; as fallen "angels" is it surprising that they would have many of the same capabilities and similar appearance?

-- Dave Lebling, aka vizcacha
