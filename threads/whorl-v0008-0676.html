Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-07-28 mary whalen #

Continues a list of names from The Book of the Long Sun with glosses on Babirousa, Bass, Bellflower, Betel and Bison, linking each to a character. Suggests Babirousa echoes Incus' circle, Betel may hint at Maytera Betel's past, and Bison fits Mint's fighting, with real-world origins given for each.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Maytera Betel was once a prostitute

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Continuing the name list:

Babirousa. Also babirusa or babirussa. (Babyrousa babyrussa is the scientific name). Malay, from baabii (hog) and ruusa (deer). A hairless porcine creature. The male's teeth stick out beyond the lip. Likes to hide. He is a member of Incus' circle of black mechanics, and the teeth sticking out remind us of Incus.

Bass. The giant sea bass (Stereolepis gigas) most closely resembles the bouncer at Orchid's.

Bellflower. A plant with bell-shaped flowers. Usually of the genus Campanula or a related genus.

Betel. The betel pepper (Piper betle) or the betel palm (Areca catechu). The nut of the palm is chewed with the leaves of the pepper as a stimulant by southern Asian cultures. This may show that Maytera Betel was once a prostitute (compare many with drug names) and that is why she's simpathetic to Rose's pregnancy.

Bison. A species in the genus Bison. Because all of the animals used as names in BLS come from the Americas, this is probably the bison (Bison bison). He probably has this name because bisons fight for cows, like he fights for Mint, though not in exactly the same way.

prion
