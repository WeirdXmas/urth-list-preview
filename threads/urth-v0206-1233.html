2002-06-16 Mark Millman #

Reports that the poster has read Gene Wolfe's new story "Shields of Mars" in the anthology Mars Probes and liked it. Describes the story as more a vignette than a short story and as straightforward and simple, though offers no specific passage as evidence.
supports

Yes, I've seen it and like it. It struck me as more
of a vignette than a short story, despite its eleven-
page length, and as pretty straightforward, even
simple. Regrettably, I'm having trouble coming up
with other stories that give me a similar impression.

Mark Millman
(Nacre)
