1999-09-29 Tony Ellis #

Disputes Carlton Greene's link between the word 'strike' on Dorcas's vomited lead shot and Greek ostracism, arguing it instead comes from Greek slingers who inscribed such shot. Grounds this in the slingers' practice of scratching 'strike' on their shot in hope of its effect, and tentatively suggests a Latro book alludes to the same practice.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Dorcas's 'strike' shot echoes Greek slingers' practice

Right culture, wrong tradition. The ancient Greek slingers scratched "strike" on their lead shot in the hope that it would do just that. I may be wrong, but I think there's also a reference to this practice in one of the Latro books too.
