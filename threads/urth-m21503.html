Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2011-10-22 Lee Berman #

Argues that Inire may be both Ossipago and Palaemon, citing Borski's Solar Labyrinth and the Latin roots of the name Inire. Supports the Palaemon link with evidence from Terminus Est, fashioned by Jovinian, and ties Inire to Faunus and Inuus, responding to larry miller's question.
supports · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Inire is Palaemon; Inire is Ossipago; Inire connects to Faunus and Inuus

In Solar Labyrinth, Borski makes the case for Inire as Ossipago and Palaemon.

I have seen the Boatman, Fechin and Hethor connections and find these two to

be less certain candidates but still very much worth considering.

In regard to Palaemon, there is the rather compelling evidence of Terminus Est,

which was fashioned by Jovinian. The Latin connection to the name Inire seems

worthy of some thought by itself. The Roman mythological reference to Jove only

adds greater depth to the connection of the subtext which includes The Cumaean,

Rhea Silvia, Fauna, and others (of course including Faunus/Inuus/Inire).
