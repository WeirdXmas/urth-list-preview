2000-07-14 Adam Stephanides #

Reports that the poster has received a copy of In Green's Jungles at a Manhattan comics shop, is partway through it and finds it absorbing, and dislikes its cover compared with On Blue's Waters. Asks whether they are the first on the list to announce the book's release, with no spoilers given.
informs

I got my copy at Midtown Comics (40th St. just west of 7th Ave, Manhattan). I'm up to page 104 and so far it's thoroughly absorbing; I doubt I'll be able to go to bed until I've finished it. Ugly, ugly, ugly cover, though. I don't have OBW in front of me, but from my recollection IGJ's cover is much worse.

Am I actually the first to post that IGJ is out?

--Adam
