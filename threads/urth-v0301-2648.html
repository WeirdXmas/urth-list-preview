2003-01-11 Ron Crown #

Recommends the Wolfe story "From the Cradle" from Greg Ketter's Shelf Life anthology, calling it solid Wolfe with a pun that doubles as a clue. Points to its stories within a story, all drawn from a brown book of tales of wonder, as another instance of Wolfe's favourite device.
supports

I just read this story from Greg Ketter's Shelf Life anthology (rare book lovers, N.B. the title). Good, solid Wolfe, with an amusing pun which is also a clue. It's also another example of GW's love for telling stories within the story; what's more, the stories are all from a brown book (!) containing "tales of wonder!" Recommended.

Ron Crown
