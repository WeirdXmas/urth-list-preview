1997-04-15 Alice Turner #

Agrees with Mantis that Keef works as a Vironese male name, noting it is how she refers to Mr. Richards, and rejects Ma'at as too witty for the purpose. Proposes alternative feather-part names such as Vane, Plume, Tuft and Fluff, since Quill is already taken, as a name for a character.
supports · source-and-allusion

I concur with mantis that Keef in its Malaysian form works fine as a Vironese male name. It's the way I invariably refer to the Hon. Mr. Richards. (So does he!)

I don't think Ma'at would be right, though, witty as its derivation is. The three parts of a feather are the pin, shaft and vane. Vane might be interesting, though almost too close. Quill is taken. Plume? Tuft? Fluff?

-alga-
