1999-01-29 Dave Lebling #

Informs list members that AltaVista Discovery, a free desktop indexing program, could make the mailing list archives searchable if split into one file per message. Gives an example query for finding messages containing several Wolfe-specific words and notes that a beta worked well a year earlier.
informs

Altavista Discovery is a product that allows you to index the text on your system. If you burst the archives into separate files (one for each message), it would allow you to access them using their search engine.

You can do queries like "mantis agia claw" to find files that had those three words in them.

I tried a Beta version of this about a year ago and it worked pretty well. I don't know what its current state is, but navigate to " http://discovery.altavista.com" to find out more. It's a free download.

-- Dave Lebling
(aka vizcacha)
