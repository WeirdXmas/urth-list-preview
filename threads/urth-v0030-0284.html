Quotes a parody verse about the Shrike from Dan Simmons's Hyperion, rewriting the Wizard of Oz song with punning lines about spikes, shrikes and time warps. Responds to an earlier post that mentioned the Shrike, offering the joke rather than any reading of Wolfe's text, and closes with a link to a fiction catalog.
chats

"We're off to see the Shrike,
the Wonderful Shrike of Hyperion!
It really is a spike of a shrike,
The Wonderful Shrike of Hyperion!

If ever a swindlous spindle there hikes,
Or time warp respites,
Or sado delights,
It's all because, because, because, because!
Because of the wonderful beings it spikes!"
