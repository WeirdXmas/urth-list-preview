Will there ever be a Book of the Old Sun?

Urth list · 9 messages · 2003-01-19 to 2003-01-21

2003urthThe Book of the New SunRyan ThistlethwaiteJosh GellerDan'l Danehy-OakesAnthony FerraraJames JordanMichael Straight
The thread: Anthony Ferrara asked whether Wolfe would ever write a Book of the Old Sun, reasoning from the Long Sun and Short Sun titles, and then asked how the Latro Soldier books compare with Wolfe's other work. Josh Geller predicted Latro could rank with The Book of the New Sun, and Dan'l Danehy-Oakes welcomed the idea of Apollo's role in a Latro-based Old Sun. James Jordan argued Latro's amnesia makes him thematically suited to such a book, and suggested reading Latro alongside the Severian quintet and knowing Greek myth and Zoroastrianism. Ryan Thistlethwaite replied with a joking remark that left the question unresolved, so the thread ended in speculation rather than a settled answer.
  • Anthony Ferrara: asks whether a Book of the Old Sun will come, since Long Sun and Short Sun suggest a companion series
  • James Jordan: Latro's amnesia fits an Old Sun book set early in time, when memory is short
  • Josh Geller: Latro books may rank with The Book of the New Sun once finished
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: finds the Latro-as-Old-Sun idea interesting and wants to see Apollo's importance
  • Ryan Thistlethwaite: jokes about the question and a possible Lucas link, offering no evidence

Themes: Memory and forgetting, Time loops and causality, Deep time and ruins

2003-01-20 James Jordan #

Argues that Latro, the amnesiac narrator of the Soldier books, is thematically what a proposed Book of the Old Sun would look for, since he lives early in time when memory is short, unlike Severian near the end of time. Suggests reading the Soldier books alongside the Severian quintet to find parallels, responding to a poster who proposed an Old Sun series set before the New Sun.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Latro lives before the theoanthropos arrives; Soldier books parallel the Severian quintet

Latro, man. Latro lives before the Ultimate New Sun arrived in history (the theoanthropos). He lives at the beginning of time, when memory is short, unlike Severian, who lives toward the end, when memory is almost infallible. Thematically, anyway, Latro is what you are looking for. Let's hope he lives to finish it the way he planned.
It would be interesting to read the Soldier books with an eye on the Severian quintet, and see how many things are thematically or narratively parallel. I suspect there's a lot.

Nutria
