Themes: Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies

2002-10-28 Andy Robertson #

Argues that Lindsay's A Voyage to Arcturus is the most tremendous modern fiction based on Gnosticism, though Wolfe drew little from its savage pessimism. Replies to James Wynn's quotation of Tom that Gnostics were euphoric, by contrasting Lindsay's deeply pessimistic book and recommending it as worth reading.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theology-and-religion · theories: Wolfe owes little to Lindsay's Gnosticism

The most tremendous modern fictional work based on Gnosticism is A VOYAGE TO ARCTURUS - and I *don't* think Wolfe derived much from this incredibly savage and *deeply* pessimistic work.

However, it is richly worth a look in itself, and is online somewhere IIRC
