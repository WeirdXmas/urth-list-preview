Themes: Light, darkness and colour

1998-02-10 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists a series of green elements across Wolfe's books, including the green face in House Absolute's antechamber, the green-haired undines, the green megathere, the schiovani flag and the Domnicellae's green eyes. Offers no argument; it is a catalogue of colour occurrences, with a passing mention of William Green in There Are Doors.

Green Empire (aka Vert). The giant green face that appears in the antechamber of House Absolute. Green hair for undines. The green megathere at House Absolute (probably in the Green Room). The green and black flag of the schiovani (military group Severian joined). The green eyes of the Domnicellae at the Destruction of the Altar (v I). Green Lune.

William Green (in THERE ARE DOORS).
