Some are opaque---we -still- don't know about Pike's ghost!

And some are ambiguously helpful. Yes, it was a legitimate expedition. But -why- would Q like "a thriving colony of human beings on Green"??? Lunch??? The Kypris answer to Mint is nice, and Pike as Blood's father is excellent!
