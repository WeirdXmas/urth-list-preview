Black Sun, White Fountain

Urth list · 3 messages · 1998-02-12 to 1998-02-12

1998urthThe Book of the New SunKieran ClearyJames JordanMichael Straight
The thread: Kieran Cleary proposed that the black hole consuming the Old Sun and the White Fountain are two mouths of one wormhole, so the Fountain could regenerate the Old Sun and carry dualist implications for Severian. Michael Straight objected with jokes about plugging holes and argued the idea cannot explain the New Sun, since a separate new universe would leave Urth without one and Ushas would not need it. Jim Jordan offered a theological reading in which the black hole is original sin and the Fountain is redemption and the Holy Spirit, with a possible Adam-and-Christ parallel to Revelation 22. The thread ends with the wormhole idea challenged and the theological reading left as a separate proposal, with no resolution.
  • Kieran Cleary: black hole and White Fountain are two mouths of one wormhole, Fountain regenerates the Sun
  • Michael Straight: wormhole theory fails because a separate universe leaves Urth without a New Sun
  • Jim Jordan: black hole is original sin, Fountain is redemption and the Holy Spirit, drawing on Revelation 22

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Death and resurrection, Catholic faith and sacraments

1998-02-12 Michael Straight #

Argues against Kieran Cleary's idea that the Old Sun's black hole and the White Fountain are two mouths of one wormhole, using jokes about plugging holes and draining bathtubs. Notes that if the new world is a separate universe, Urth never gets a New Sun and Ushas needs none, so the wormhole idea cannot explain the New Sun.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Old Sun's black hole and White Fountain are one wormhole; New Sun heals the Wounded Sun rather than replacing it

Kind of like plugging a hole by putting the other end of the hole in the original hole? Or maybe a bathtub drain that leads back to the bathtub, refilling it?

If so, it might be more accurate to speak of Healing the Wounded Sun rather than replacing the Old Sun with the New Sun.

Except I still obviously don't understand anything because if the world Severian returns to is a whole new universe, then the Urth of the first four books never gets a New Sun, it just dies with the old one. And the new world, Ushas, shouldn't need a New Sun.

Time to reread.

-Rostrum

1998-02-12 James Jordan #

Proposes that the black hole in the old sun symbolizes original sin and the white fountain symbolizes redemption and the Holy Spirit, with a possible old and new Severian paralleling Adam and Christ. Grounds this in a parallel to the river from Yesodh to Beriach in Revelation 22 and the book's theological subtexts, and responds to Christopher Culver's question about what happens when the White Fountain meets the Old Sun.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: Black hole symbolizes original sin; White fountain symbolizes redemption and Holy Spirit; Old Severian as Adam, new Severian as Christ

I'm partial to Alga's suggestion that the black hole, the worm in the heart of the sun, relates to original sin symbolically. The white fountain relates symbolically to redemption and the outpouring of the Holy Spirit (parallel the river flowing from Yesodh to Beriach, Revelation 22:1ff.). This seems very Wolfean to me, especially given the theological subtexts in the book.
Now, IF there is an old Severian and a new Severian, then this might be like the old Adam and the New Adam (Jesus). In that case, it MIGHT be the case that the old Severian is guilty of introducing the black hole (original sin), while the new Severian comes to undo that sin and restore the sun.
I'm still not sure about the two Severians, though.
Maybe Wolfe needs to write a "Book of the Old Sun" for us, about the old Severian?
Any thoughts on this?

Nutria

1998-02-12 Kieran Cleary #

Proposes that the black hole destroying the Old Sun and the White Fountain are the two mouths of a single wormhole, so matter leaving the black hole emerges from the Fountain. Responds to Christopher Culver's earlier argument by working out how the White Fountain could regenerate the Old Sun, and notes dualist implications for Severian.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Black hole and White Fountain are two mouths of one wormhole

Christopher Culver says:

er, that's kind of what I was getting at in a previous post.

While I've been aware that there was a black hole in the Old Sun, and that the catalyst for the conversion of the Old Sun to New Sun is a White,I've never been able to imagine what exactly is supposed to happen when White Fountain meets Old Sun and why this event creates a young regenerated sun. It's a bit like the two scientists at the blackboard in Larson's cartoon: "and then a miracle happens".

So to be explicit: It seems plausible that the black hole in the Old Sun is *directly* connected to the White Fountain. Energy/matter exiting through the black hole in the Old Sun appears from the mouth of the White Fountain. Which means that its not a Black Hole complete with singularity but instead both phenomena represent the two mouths of the same wormhole. This has some nice cyclical resonances if we can get over the dualist implications it has for Severian's character and nature, and also gives us a way to think about the rejuvination of the Old Sun.
