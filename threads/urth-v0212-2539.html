2002-12-26 Roy C. Lackey #

Informs readers that a new Gene Wolfe story, "From the Cradle", appears in the limited-edition bookstore anthology SHELF LIFE, edited by Greg Ketter with an introduction by Neil Gaiman. Gives publication details such as the 1,000 numbered signed copies and the publisher Dreamhaven Books, with a link, and says the poster has not yet read the story.
informs

For Christmas I received a copy of SHELF LIFE, a collection of stories about bookstores. Edited by Greg Ketter, introduction by Neil Gaiman. It contains the new story "From the Cradle" by Gene Wolfe, but I haven't read it yet. The book is a limited edition (1,000 copies). Each is numbered and signed by each contributor. The publisher is Dreamhaven Books. You can read more about it and view the cover here:

< http://www.dreamhavenbooks.com/ >

-Roy
