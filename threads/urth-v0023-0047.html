1999-02-04 Alice Turner #

Supplies a note on the colour-coded regions of Oz, placing the Munchkins east, the Winkies west, the Gillikens north and the Quadlings south, and noting that an early map reversed east and west. It replies to William Ansley's earlier Oz-Eyeflash material and offers further contributions, without engaging any Wolfe passage.
informs

William Ansley,

Thanks for your Oz-Eyeflash material, which I would have helped you with except that I've been unable to post. Here's another try. The Munchkins (blue) are usually to the east of Oz, while the Winkies (yellow) are to the west. I say "usually" because one of the earliest maps reversed them, and confusion followed. But the Gillikens (purple) are always to the north and the Quadlings (red) to the south.

If this goes through, I'll send some more.

-alga
