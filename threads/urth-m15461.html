Gene Wolfe Reading Tonight

The thread: Joe Bushong-Taylor posted a local notice that a Palatine, Illinois bookshop might host a Gene Wolfe reading of a short story that evening, passed along via Wolfe's daughter. Ryan Dunn replied with a recollection of having once lived near Wolfe in the Chicago area and regret at being unable to attend. Rebecca Bushong-Taylor then reported that Wolfe had mentioned Ryan in a phone call, offering reassurance. The thread is brief and friendly, ending on a warm note rather than any debate.
