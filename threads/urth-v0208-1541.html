2002-08-06 Craig Brewer #

Asks Michael Andre-Driussi where to obtain out-of-print copies of "Vance Space" and the "Quick and Dirty Guide" to the Long Sun books, and whether digital versions could be ordered through Sirius. Addresses the question directly to Andre-Driussi, presumably as the publisher's contact, and assumes the texts exist as electronic files.

Do you have any advice on where I could obtain copies

Thanks in advance for any help,
Craig Brewer

2002-08-09 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Advises Craig Brewer on where to find the out-of-print guides Vance Space and the Quick and Dirty Guide to Long Sun, suggesting used-book search engines and eBay auctions. Responds to Brewer's question about digital versions by saying none exist or are planned, and that the poster would announce any change.
answers

Tricky. "Vance Space" might show up if you used all the different used book search engines (there were a few dealers who bought quantities, iirc). "Q&D," wow, unlikely (no sales to dealers), but then again it could happen (an owner decides to auction it on eBay or something).

No digital versions yet, nor any plans for that. I would announce it.

=mantis=
