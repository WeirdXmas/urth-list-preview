2010-12-04 Andrew Mason #

Asks whether the maid who plays Catherine in Severian's memory is literally his mother, or whether the symbolically beheaded Saint Katharine is merely identified with her. Responds to a post proposing that the beheaded Katherine represents Severian's mother, calling the idea a brilliant explanation for why his mother is named Catherine but questioning its mundane logic.
questions · allegory-and-typology · puzzle-solving · theories: Katherine beheaded in memory represents Severian's mother

Brilliant idea; it explains, what has always puzzled me, why Severian's mother is called Catherine. But I'm not sure it solves the problem of who is who at the mundane level. Does it mean that the woman who plays the part, the maid, actually is his mother? (If 'maid' is understood in its traditional sense, of course, she can't be.) Or does it just mean that the woman he is symbolically killing, Holy Katharine, is somehow identified with his mother?

2010-12-06 Son of Witz replying to Andrew Mason #

Argues that Severian's mother Catherine and the maid Katherine, a symbolic prisoner, may be the same person, with the name link working symbolically even if the maid is not literally his mother. Responds to Andrew Mason's question about who is who at the mundane level, citing the book's rule that symbols shape us whether or not we recognize them.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · puzzle-solving · theories: Catherine and Katherine are the same person; maid Katherine is a symbolic prisoner; Severian's mother works as a symbolic mother figure

Thanks.

I think it works symbolically as his mother via the names, even if the maid isn't his mother. Catherine was his mother, a prisoner of the torturers who might have died in their dungeon. The maid is a symbolic prisoner named Katherine. Rule 1 in this book is that "Symbols Invent Us even if we are unaware of their import at the time." So it doesn't matter id Sev makes the connection.

However, I do think that Catherine the mother and Katherine the forever unchanged maid are probably the same person.

~Witz
