2000-08-04 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs the list that Wolfe's contribution to the limited-edition anthology Taps & Sighs is an original story titled "The Walking Sticks", answering a newcomer's question about whether the collection is new. Also lists Wolfe's other recent small-press appearances, "A Traveler in Desert Lands" in The Last Continent and a new story in Strange Attraction.
informs

As far as I know, Wolfe's contribution to TAPS AND
SIGHS (a very expensive limited edition small-press
anthology) is an original story titled "The Walking
Sticks". Wolfe also has a new story in Ed Kramer's
anthology STRANGE ATTRACTION, published by Bereshith /
Shadowlands Press; his story "A Traveler in Desert
Lands" appeared in another Shadowlands anthology, John
Pelan's THE LAST CONTINENT: NEW TALES OF ZOTHIQUE, in
1999.
