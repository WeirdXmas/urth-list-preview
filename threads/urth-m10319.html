The thread: Ranjit Bhatnagar, as list administrator, asked members to slow down after the discussion became noisy and reported that some users had been asked to take a break. Brunians responded tersely about the break and hinted at further consequences, and a poster announced leaving the list for Facebook, which drew friendly replies. Fred Kiesche then asked why the same threads kept splitting under identical subject lines, making the list look noisier. Roy C. Lackey explained the splits as caused by posters replying with new subject lines and by a mail server problem, and Jane Delawney confirmed the Gmail-based mail service was the cause. The thread drifted from moderation into a technical explanation of the splitting, with no real reading of Wolfe's fiction.

2010-08-09 Roy C. Lackey replying to Fred Kiesche #

Explains that split message threads on the list happen because some posters, including Lee Berman, send replies as new threads with new subject lines, and suggests Jane Delawney's mail server causes similar problems. Responds to Fred Kiesche's question about why the same threads keep splitting, describing his own workaround of copying his replies into his list folder.
answers

It is because some people's posts (Lee Berman), even when responding to an existing thread, are sent to the List as if they were new threads with a new subject line. That's why Lee's posts don't have the usual "Re:" at the beginning of the subject line. I don't know why he does that. Maybe he has the same problem that Jane Delawney has, that her mail server will not send her a copy of her own posts unless she starts a new thread. I have had the same problem she has since my mail server, though my email address does not reflect it, became a bastardized form of gmail two years ago. I get around the problem by just copying a copy of of my response post to the folder in which I keep the other List posts, in the place where it would be if things worked right.

-Roy
