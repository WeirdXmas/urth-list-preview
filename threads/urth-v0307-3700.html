2003-07-19 Sikozu Sta.-Ana #

Asks the list for a compiled list of conclusions about the proper names and references in the Short Sun books, after noting earlier etymology lists for the Long Sun's Arab-Indian names. Says the poster admires the Blue-Green stories but struggles to keep up with the names despite knowing languages.
questions · source-and-allusion

Hello. New to the list, hoping someone here could point me or mail me a list of ruminations/conclusions on the references to proper names from the Short Sun. I know there have been lists discussed & compared on this list of etymologies (for example, of the Arab-Indian names featured in the Long Sun) & was hoping to get a better grasp of the Short Sun.

To be honest, I really adore the Blue-Green stories but am at a loss to keep up with the names despite an excellent ear for languages.

Gaonically,
Sikozu
