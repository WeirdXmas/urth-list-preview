1999-08-14 Alice Turner #

Argues that the Spading Moon in Wolfe's work corresponds to March, citing the Farmer's Almanac table Roy had posted as the most inclusive list of moon names, where that month's moon is called Plow Moon. Responds to a prior post by Roy, offering the almanac table as near-conclusive evidence for the identification.
supports · source-and-allusion · theories: Spading Moon is March

Apologies for being inattentive---I simply hadn't registered that Roy had already posted the Farmer's Almanac table, the most inclusive of all these lists. But isn't that virtually proof positive that the Spading Moon is in March, called there (among other things) Plow Moon?
