Oz dreams

1999urthAlice Turner

1999-06-25 Alice Turner #

Argues that only the film version of Dorothy's journey to Oz is a dream, while Wolfe drew on Baum's Oz books, where Tip and the transformed Ozma appear. Responds to Clifford's claim by citing Peter Stephenson's note that the Baum books are public domain and online, and recommends the reprint editions.
disputes · source-and-allusion

Clifford,

Emphatically, only in the movie was Dorothy's trip to Oz a dream. She was not dreaming!!!! Wolfe was operating from the Oz *books* (where Tip, the transformed Ozma, appears in -The Land of Oz-); as Peter Stephenson notes, all of the Baum books (I do not think Wolfe refers to any except the Baum books, though several other authors took up the series after Baum died) are in public domain and are now on line. They are fun to read; any of you with kids should definitely seek them out. Books of Wonder has nice reprint editions with the Denslow and Neil illustrations.

-alga
