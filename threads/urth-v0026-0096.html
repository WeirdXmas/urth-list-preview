1999-05-08 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes a jokey casting of Urth list members as characters in a Book of the New Sun adaptation, pairing Severian with Michael Andre-Driussi and Tzadkiel with Gene Wolfe. Opens by congratulating Nigel Price on his religious comments and urges the list to drop the iron men thread, mocking the Carry On casting talk with a playful invitation for additions.
chats

Congratulations to Nigel Price: very interesting and even graceful input on the religious issue. A welcome change from this increasingly rusty topic of the iron men; shouldn't we move on to other threads, like whether Severian's guild cloak is made from tussah, chenille, or silk?
On casting for THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN: Carry On is all very well, but why not look for talent closer to home? With hope that I avoid inflicting any personal injury, I'd like to suggest the following casting, based on known critical attributes of the individuals concerned:
SEVERIAN = Michael Andre-Driussi
THECLA = Joan Gordon
JUTURNA = Alice K. Turner
MASTER ULTAN = Jonathan Laidlow
ODILO = Nigel Price (because of the "dull video")
BALDANDERS = Ori Kowarsky
DR. TALOS = Robert Borski
FATHER INIRE = Damien Broderick (lurking)
CANOG = Patrick O'Leary
MASTER ASH = Nick Gevers
EREBUS = Peter Wright
TZADKIEL = Gene Wolfe
Any additions?
//--------------------------------------
Nicholas Gevers

1999-05-08 William H. Ansley #

Jokes that the poster, a list member, will not comment on the suggested casting of Urth list members as characters, then proposes himself as Hethor or Patera Remora from the Wolfe books. Responds to the earlier casting post by playfully claiming the babbling-fool roles rather than offering any reading of the text.
chats

I won't comment on any of the suggestions above; I don't know anyone well enough to know who's going to be pleased or disgruntled.

Now who could I be? Hmmmm... Hethor. Babbling fool, deadly assasin. Well, I've got the first part down cold, maybe I can fake the second part. No, hold on. I'll have to wait for the casting of _The Book of the Long Sun_. Patera Remora is just a babbling fool; I can be him!

William Ansley

1999-05-10 Peter Stephenson #

Playfully asks to join a critics' game by casting himself as Hildegrin, and wonders whether Kenneth Grahame had Mr Badger from The Wind in the Willows in mind when writing the Lake of Eternal Sleep scene. Responds to William H. Ansley's joke about choosing the name Hethor, with a light aside about sports journalists.
chats

kn i play 2?

I'd like to be Hildegrin, because Mr Badger in Wind in the Willows is one of my favourite fictional creations. I've been wondering if it had it in his mind when he wrote the Lake of Eternal Sleep scene --- lots of rushes and backwaters as well as badgers.

(There's another for sports journalists, desperately trying to fill pre-superbowl slots with idiotic questions.)

corncrake
