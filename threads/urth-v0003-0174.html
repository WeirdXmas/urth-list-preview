Argues that Wolfe's stories "The Changeling" and Peace share a mythical modern town, so their links run both ways, while Castleview may sit in the same landscape without hard connections. Responds to Ron Crown by reporting the poster's first reading of Castleview, which gave only a general sense of the same mythical midwestern setting.

Ron Crown,

The connections between PEACE and "The Changeling" are definitely two-way; both are set in the same imaginary town and PEACE refers to Crazy Pete.

There may be similar hard-links between CASTLEVIEW and "The Changeling," but I haven't found them yet. When I read CASTLEVIEW the first time, I immediately placed the town in the same mythical modern landscape as PEACE, yet even with this anticipation I didn't find any linkage beyond that general sense of "mythical, modern, midwest."

FWIW.

=mantis=
