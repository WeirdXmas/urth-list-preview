Palaemon

The thread: Thalassocrat opened the thread asking how Palaemon came to own Terminus Est and inviting backstory speculation, including whether he is the silent traveller at the Stone Town or related to Severian. Thalassocrat then argued Palaemon was near the Pelerines when he punished Winnoc about ten years before Severian's birth, placing the events at the river port Liti and linking Catherine to Ouen's sister. Dave Tallman proposed instead that Palaemon was demoted to journeyman exile and later restored, perhaps as the autarch's agent in the torturers' guild, based on guild members' dates and ages. The thread ends with three posts and no recorded reply, so the competing theories were left standing without resolution.
  • thalassocrat: Palaemon punished Winnoc near the Pelerines around Severian's birth, at Liti, and is tied to Catherine and Severian's family
  • Dave Tallman: Palaemon was demoted to journeyman exile and restored, possibly as the autarch's agent in the guild

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Fathers, mothers and children

2008-07-03 thalassocrat #

Asks how Palaemon came to own the valuable antique sword Terminus Est and invites readers to invent backstory for him, including whether he is the silent traveller at the Stone Town, linked to the Pelerine garment, or related to Severian. Lists specific clues such as his exile length, Winnoc's remarks, and Casdoe's father resembling him, as grounds for speculation.
questions · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Palaemon is the silent traveller at the Stone Town; Palaemon was given Terminus Est for a service

The Terminus Est discussion raises a tangential issue which has bothered me: how does Palaemon come to have such a valuable antique in his personal possession?

Not by inheritance, of course.

Did he save up his torturer salary and buy it? Unlikely? I think probably somebody gave it to him, as he gave it to Sev. If in return for a service, I imagine it must have been a very valuable service. And he must like Sev *a lot*, to give it to him.

Anyway, I think Palaemon deserves more story-making-up than he's been given. Where did he get his sword? Where did he get his eyes? Why was he temporarily exiled? Can we connect him to Sev, family- wise?

Was he the silent traveller who spent an evening with the old woman and her son in the Stone Town, sleeping beside his staff? (Recalling that Sev believes many must have mistaken Terminus Est for a staff during his travels.) There aren't many other candidates, if the traveller is somebody we know from elsewhere in the story.

And if he was the traveller, does he have anything to do with the Pelerine garment Sev finds himself lying upon after his encounter with Apu Paunchu? It would be nice if the garment were related to Catherine, somehow. Did he also encounter Apu Paunchu?

Palaemon was in exile about 30 years (maybe a little less) before Sev meets Winnoc in the Pelerine's lazarette. About 10 years before Sev's birth, by Sev's reckoning. IIRC, Winnoc gives the impression that Palaemon's exile was for a fixed, not very lengthy term.

Casdoe's father reminds Sev of Palaemon (except for the eyes).

Ummmm ... dunno. Anybody care to make up a story from all this?

2008-07-05 thalassocrat replying to thalassocrat #

Proposes that Palaemon was near the Pelerines when he punished Winnoc about ten years before Severian's birth, and that the place was Liti, the southern river port. Builds on the Catherine-as-Ouen's-sister guess, placing a thirteen-year-old Catherine as a postulant, and cites Eata's UOTNS account of caravels, Winnoc's ship plans and Palaemon's slip about Severian's southern destination.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Palaemon punished Winnoc near the Pelerines; Catherine is Ouen's sister; Catherine was a Pelerine postulant at thirteen; Liti is where Winnoc might sail

Trying to make up a Palaemon story is like squeezing blood from a stone, but I guess there's a couple more pieces of data which might be relevant.

We can infer that he was in the same general neighborhood as the Pelerines at the time he punished Winnoc, around ten years before Sev's birth. After the punishment, Winnoc searches for a group to belong to, and soon ends up with the Pelerines.

At that time, it's plausible that Catherine would have been a Pelerine postulant. Eg: If my Catherine = Ouen's sister story is right, she would have been around 13 years old then (born ~41 years before Sev meets old boatman, when Sev is ~18). If I'm wrong, a ~13 year old Catherine is still plausible.

We know from Cyriaca that Pelerine postulants could be about this age.

I think it's a fair guess that the place was Liti, down south along the river. Eata's story in UOTNS tells us that foreign-sailing caravels departed from there; Winnoc was thinking of signing on a ship like that; Palaemon little glitch about Sev's destination, thinking for a moment that it was to the south.

Liti of course was Burgundofora's destination with her captain lover, after betraying Sev.

Dunno what, if anything, all this means ...

2008-07-05 Dave Tallman replying to thalassocrat #

Proposes that Palaemon, Master Gurloes's trainer, was demoted to journeyman exile and later restored, perhaps as the autarch's agent in the torturers' guild. Grounds this in the dates and ages of the guild members and the old autarch's remark about an agent in the guild, and suggests Severian's own training may explain Palaemon's restored favor.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Palaemon is the autarch's agent in the guild; Palaemon's restoration tied to Severian's training

Another detail you can work with is that Palaemon trained Master Gurloes. The dates and ages seem to imply that Palaemon was a master of apprentices, then a journeyman exile (demoted from a Master?), then back to a Master and teacher again in Sev's time.

The old autarch says that he has an agent in the guild (in Citadel), who could well be Palaemon. Perhaps Paleamon's restoration to favor and his valuable sword are both a result of his autarch connection. Could the reason for this be Sev's training itself? Possibly, since we don't know for sure who trained Sev1.
