2002urthThe Book of the New SunAndrew Bollen

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Water, the sea and drowning

2002-12-19 Andrew Bollen #

Asks a series of questions about several loose points in Wolfe's fiction: the unnamed woman Severian sleeps with before his trial, where Silk and Horn go at the end of Return to the Whorl, and the undines and Abaia in the flood. Frames the first point as a question about Wolfe's narrative style of leaving blanks for readers to fill, and the rest as open puzzles about the undines' origins, aims and the Mother's identity.
questions · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving

Just a collection of things on my mind, while I am thinking about Wolfe. = I know some of the questions have been discussed, but I haven't seen any = proposals which I ind convincing; perhaps Marcs revelation will help!=20

1. The demiurge Severian sleeps with before his trial (can't remember = her name), and her mode of "talking" - shaping blank spaces in the = background noise. This is kind of Wolfe's narrative style, isn't it - = leaving blank spaces in a background of narrative, for readers to fill = in for themselves.

2. Where does SilkHorn go when he leaves on the Whorl at the end of = RTTW? Is it back to Urth, now Ushas, to repopulate the world? "Good = fishing" on Ushas, I would think. Perhaps for my hypothetical melding = with Severian?=20

3. Undines and Abaia. What happens to them in the flood? Why is this = something they would resist? What happens to them, with the making of = Ushas? What are they, and where do they come from? What are their aims & = goals? Some of the tales in New Sun hint that they were brought to Urth = by men; how did they arrive on Blue? Why does the Mother give Seawrack = to Horn? Is the Mother an Abaia (I would think so), or a Vanished People = undine? Did the Vanished People worship the Mother, and did this = contribute to their ruin?
