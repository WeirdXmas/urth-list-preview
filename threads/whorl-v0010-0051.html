Buried Alive

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1999-10-03 Alice Turner #

Argues that Horn learned from Krait how to appear to kill inhumi while secretly reviving them, using Occam's razor to explain how he could plan a covert military force of buried inhumi. Responds to Robert and Alex, defending the idea that Krait's secret explains Horn's strategy over Alex's intelligent-blood theory.
disputes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Krait taught Horn how not to kill inhumi; Horn secretly revived buried inhumi for military force

Robert and Alex,

No, we're somewhat at cross purposes here. Horn knows perfectly well how to kill an inhumu--he was present at Quetzal's death, after all. The secret he has learned from Krait is how *not* to kill an inhumu while giving the appearance of doing so. Let's use Occam's razor here. Horn as political leader has
presided over the live burial of (let's assume) 25 inhumi. Horn as military leader covertly digs up and coerces or bribes 18 or these (more to come) into
operating in his side in a highly original military maneuver (I applaud whoever it was who remarked that Wolfe has put some new, well, maybe not life--undeath <g>?--into the overworked vampire genre). How would he possibly know how to do this, and to plan it so far in advance, without help? That is why I think this knowledge (and possibly advice too) is Krait's secret.

Alex's "intelligent blood" theory remains strictly theoretical. But you need a rational explanation for Horn's strategy, which is why I invoke Occam's razor.

-alga
