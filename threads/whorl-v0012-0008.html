IGJ and Lune's orbit

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-10-30 to 2000-10-30

2000whorlThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

2000-10-30 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that the visitors to Urth in In Green's Jungles give an objective outside view of Nessus and the dying Old Sun, and that their observation of the bloated sun explains why Lune orbits so close to Urth. Grounds this in the visitors' new figures on Nessus's ruinification and their remark that Lune must be close enough to fully eclipse the bloated sun.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Lune is close to Urth to eclipse the bloated Old Sun

One of the neat things about the visit to Urth is the way that the visitors give us a different, objective view of Nessus and the dying Old Sun. They give new figures on the ruinification of Nessus, the size, timeframe, and all that--they really are our stand-ins, doing exactly what we Wolfe readers would do if given the chance.

And when they notice how bloated the Old Sun is, we get the inference of another piece of the Lune's orbit puzzle (in fact, it might be the main reason why Lune is so much closer to Urth than the Moon is to Earth): it has to be, in order to fully eclipse the bloated Old Sun!

=mantis=
