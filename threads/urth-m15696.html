2010-09-13 Roy C. Lackey replying to James Wynn #

Clarifies that his earlier weight point was not about Hyacinth being unusually light but that chems, male and female, weigh more than humans. Supports this with two passages from Calde and Exodus: Silk struggling to lift Marble after Rose's funeral, and Mint trying to lift Marble.
informs · close-textual-reading · theories: Chems weigh more than humans

I don't presume to know what you were thinking then or now, but since it was I who brought up the weight issue back then, I do know what I was thinking and what I said. It had nothing to do with Hy's weight being unusually low; it was that chems, particularly the males (you were saying that Hy was a male chem), weigh much more than humans. Even a fem chem weighs more than human women.

This is indicated a couple times. One is in the wake of Rose's funeral. Marble had killed Musk and overheated in the fire and collapsed. Later, Silk helped her to stand up but had to heave "with all his strength" to do it. He helped her to the arbor near the manse and the Rose in her started talking about giving birth to Blood. At one point he said "I would have carried you, Maytera, if I could; but I knew you'd be too heavy for me to lift." (CALDE, chap. 4, 131)

Another place is when Mint (shortly after getting out of the tunnels) and Marble were reunited near the Grand Manteion. "Maytera Mint was laughing and hugging her and trying to lift her off the ground, which was so ridiculous that Maytera Marble exclaimed, 'Stop, sib, before you hurt yourself!'" (EXODUS, chap. 11, 215)

-Roy
