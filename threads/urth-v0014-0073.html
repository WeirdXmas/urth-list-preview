1998-06-22 Alice Turner #

Argues that Marsch and the boy are not human, citing their bright green eyes, which the poster calls a non-human shade. Responds to Sgt. Rock's defense of Marsch's humanity and villainy by noting the boy's pale skin and dark hair, and by pointing out that Marsch's own word is the only support for his human appearance.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Marsch and the boy are not human

Sgt. Rock,

Nice to see you again, sir!

You make a good argument wrt Marsch's humanity and villainy, but there's strong evidence against it, and that is his bright green eyes--the boy and his mother both have bright green eyes, and that is not a human shade. The boy also has pale skin and dark hair, as does the Marsch we see.

You could argue against that, of course, by saying that we only have Marsch's word for that--but then we start going round in circles.

-alga-
