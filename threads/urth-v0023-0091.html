1999-02-22 Peter Stephenson #

Jokingly suggests that Dickinson's lines about stopping for Death are a jingle from the London department store Dickens 'n' Jones, offering invented shop slogans as the supposed source. Responds to Charles Dye's earlier joke that the verse comes from a Coca-Cola theme song, and playfully asks whether the running joke has been exhausted.

Isn't it associated with the London department store Dickens 'n' Jones? It goes with their famous advertising lines:

My shop closed twice before it closed;
It yet remains to see
If opening hours unveil
A Sale Event for me.

Have we milked this one dry yet?
