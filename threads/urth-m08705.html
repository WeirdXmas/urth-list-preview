Recommends the Dixon Gardens and Gallery in Memphis as a place to see early German porcelain and pewter, alongside its American Impressionist paintings. If you're interested in early German porcelain I highly recommend the Dixon Gardens & Gallery in Memphis. They have a great collection of porcelain and pewter from Germany as well as a beautiful collection of American Impressionist paintings.
