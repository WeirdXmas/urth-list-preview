2000-02-10 Nicholas Gevers #

Asks Nutria how Aldo fits into a purgatory reading of the traffic jam, and disputes Nutria's view of "The Ziggurat" by arguing Wolfe meant the Maximum Delusion theory to stand out. Grounds this on the story's many hints of delusion and abuse, which the poster sees as deliberate.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Maximum Delusion theory in The Ziggurat

Thanks to Nutria for his several comments and
responses on ST. But to Nutria and others: please call
me Nick, or Nick Gevers; being called "Gevers" makes
me uneasy...

I think the view of the traffic jam as purgatory is
interesting; but Nutria, how does Aldo fit into this
scheme? Could you elaborate?

On "The Ziggurat": I don't agree with you here,
Nutria; I think Wolfe himself intended the Maximum
Delusion theory to be prominent in people's minds: why
else does he leave so many suggestions of delusion and
abuse?

2000-02-11 James Jordan #

Nutria reflects on whether the traffic jam in Peace is purgatory and wonders how Aldo fits, floating puzzles about the name Mary, Marta and Mar as clues to a city and a train line. The post also admits uncertainty, says it will re-read, and suggests Wolfe was devoutly Catholic and linked to Vance's Integral Vance.
questions · puzzle-solving · allegory-and-typology · theories: Traffic jam in Peace is purgatory; Aldo's love Marta is a Mary-Martha double reference

Hmmm. Good question. I was just bringing in a global view of Wolfe as devout RC and the purgatory theme in *peace* along with what seems to be the drift of the Sandman story. I need to re-read, and think about the people who have "died" in the jam (which would mean they've moved on to heaven), or who have disappeared. Isn't Aldo's love named Mary? That would be a clew, too. No. It's Marta isn't it. Hmm. Double reference? Marta as the train line into the city. What city. THE City? She's called just "Mar," which can be either Mary or Marta. Marta also as Martha, sister of another Mary. (Gee, once you're on this track, where does it stop?) And the end of the first story? It sounds like revolt and revolution. But maybe it points to the end of history, when purgatory will no longer exist. Dunno. Gotta reread.

Good question. I think I'll just have to leave the Zig for another day, though.
BTW, if you have not mentioned the Integral Vance to this list, you should. Wolfeans should love Vance; Wolfe does. Does Wolfe know about it?

http://www.massmedia.com/~mikeb/vie/

Nutria
