Who are Robert and Marie

Urth list · 1 messages · 2003-06-29 to 2003-06-29

2003urthThe Book of the New SunDon Palumbo

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2003-06-29 Don Palumbo #

Asks the list to identify Robert and Marie, the husband and wife met in the jungle tree hut in Chapter 21 of The Shadow of the Torturer, noting Robert's Paris art-student past and apparent malaria. Grounds the question in the book's text and says that most of BOTNS's other allusions are easily identified, so these should be too.
questions · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving

<html>
List,<br>
<br>
I know you have had an inconclusive running discussion regarding who these characters may be.  Since just about all of the many apparent allusions in BOTNS are rather easily identifiable, I'm hoping that someone can identify these characters, too:<br>
<br>
in Chapter 21 of SHADOW OF THE TORTURER, in the Jungle Garden of the Botanic Garden, Severian and Agia enter a jungle tree hut containing two apparently early-twentieth-century characters and a native named Insangoma.  The other characters are a husband and wife named Robert and Marie.  Robert is also a former art student in Paris and appears to have malaria.  (In reference to your earlier musings, the malaria may account for Robert's ability to see S & A.)  <u>But who are these people?<br>
<br>
</u>--Don Palumbo</html>
