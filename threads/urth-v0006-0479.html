1998-01-31 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that the Old Sun is not dying of natural stellar evolution but has had a black hole placed in it by someone, and that this is established in the text. Responds to a newcomer named Dave's question about the sun's death, presenting the claim as textual fact and linking it to a collapsing-scale pattern in the books.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: the Old Sun was killed by a black hole

Dave,

Hello there and welcome aboard.

The Old Sun is not dying of natural causes.

I repeat, the Old Sun is =not= dying of natural causes (i.e., stellar evolution).

Somebody put a black hole in the Old Sun at some point (for purposes unknown, but sure and I've got a few theories) and it is now dying from that.

This is established in the text. And another example of the "collapsing scale" used so often in TBOTNS.

1998-01-31 C R Culver #

Proposes two theories for how a black hole got into the dying Old Sun: that scientists created one that escaped and fell into the sun, as in David Brin's Earth, or that a naturally wandering black hole passed into the sun. Responds to Mr. Driussi's remark that someone put a black hole in the Old Sun, and invites other list members' opinions.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: a black hole fell into the Old Sun; scientists created a black hole that escaped into the sun

unknown, but sure and I've got a few theories) and it is now dying from that.<

I've often wondered how it got there myself. I have established 2 theories:

1) As in David Brin's novel _Earth_, some tinkering scientists create a black hole and it gets loose, ultimately falling into the sun.

2) Maybe a black hole happened to be floating around in our area of space and passed into the sun then.

I'd be most interested to hear you opinions, Mantis, as well as those of the rest of this motley group.
