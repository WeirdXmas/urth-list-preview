Reminds subscribers that the Urth and Whorl list archives are publicly available on the web, so anyone can quote the list without subscribing. Suggests adding a stern copyright warning to the archive boilerplate and asks that comments be sent privately to him rather than to the list.
informs

I'd like to remind everyone that the archives of the Urth and Whorl mailing lists are available on the web at

http://www.urth.net/urth/archives
and http://www.moonmilk.com/whorl/archives

Besides being a helpful resource, these archives are by
their nature available to anyone on the internet. If
someone wants to quote -- or misquote -- from the list, they don't have to be a subscriber to do so.

It probably wouldn't hurt to add a stern copyright warning to the archive boilerplate. Please send comments on this matter directly to me ([email]) and not to the
mailing list.
