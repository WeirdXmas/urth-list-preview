Themes: Fathers, mothers and children

2004-12-07 Marc Aramini #

Reports biographical facts about Gene Wolfe's parents: that his mother outlived the Korean War period, as shown by the letters she saved, and that his father probably died first. Cites the non-fiction piece "Kid Sister", where Wolfe describes a cross-country drive with his mother, and notes he was an only child.
informs · author-and-biography

Certainly Gene's parents survived until after the Korean war, as indicated by Letters Home, which are letters Gene's mother saved. His father probably died before his mother. There is a non-fiction story called "Kid Sister" in which Gene talks about driving with his mother across country, so she definitely survived into his early middle-age. He is an only child.

Marc
