2000-12-15 Jeremy Crampton #

Corrects a joke about the word Amazon, arguing that the folk etymology from 'without a breast' is not attested in ancient sources despite some Greek acceptance. Supports the point by citing Strabo and Herodotus, and notes Herodotus' Scythian passage as the source Wolfe used for the character Oior.
informs · source-and-allusion

Actually (though I get your joke) the derivation of "Amazon" from a-maza or without the (right) breast, although popularly accepted by some Ancient Greeks (eg., see Strabo 11.5.1) is not attested. Herodotus mentions them in the same passage Wolfe used to name his Scythian character Oior ("Man"); see Hdt. 4.110.

Jeremy
