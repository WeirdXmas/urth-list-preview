Confirms that the star name in the text is Xi Cygnus rather than the grammatically expected Xi Cygni, calling it a deliberate technical aside rather than an error. Responds to Jerry Friedman's remark that the form should be Xi Cygni, and compares it to the Folia's Story magazine title that matches the tale Foila tells in The Citadel of the Autarch.
