2002urthThe Book of the Long SunJames Wynn

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies

2002-10-10 James Wynn #

Argues that the Outsider is God and that Pas and the Whorl are blasphemous inversions of God's creation, rather than Pas being God, as in the proposed Garden of Eden mapping. Grounds this in the black mass practice of turning religious objects upside-down, and in the Mithraic allusions in The Book of the Long Sun that the poster sees as the intended reference to Genesis 2.
disputes · theology-and-religion · source-and-allusion · theories: Outsider is God, Pas a blasphemous imitation; Whorl is a blasphemous imitation of creation; Pas parodies creation as a black mass inversion

Tom adds:
Even the cylinder shape with the people inside is a nice inversion of the real world.

Crush interjects:
I consider it a mistake to try to incorporate the Outsider into the system of Pas' creation story.

I believe the proper equations are:
Outsider=God,
Pas=blasphemous imitation of God,
Whorl=blasphemous imitation of God's creation

Pas like Horn parodying Silk for the *amusement* of his friends.

One characterizations of black masses is to use actual religious objects and rituals but turn them upside-down or apply the opposite meanings to them. This is what Pas has done. He's taken creation and *turned it inside-out*.

I realize there are lots of deliberate flags to Mithraism (which is not the same thing as Gnosticism) in tBotLS, but I believe this the intended understanding of the references to Genesis 2.

--Crush
