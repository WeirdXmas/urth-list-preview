Jonas = Quixote?

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-09-26 to 2002-09-26

2002urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Straight

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2002-09-26 Michael Straight #

Proposes that Jonas's feeling for Jolenta is not lust but love for an idealized fantasy woman, a Dulcinea figure in the Quixote sense, though the text offers little support. Responds to Roy Lackey's claim that Jonas and Jolenta had no relationship beyond his lust, granting it as well argued while suggesting the fantasy may be unknown to Jonas himself.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Jonas loves an idealized fantasy of Jolenta as Dulcinea

Well argued. I think you're almost certainly right.

But it is possible (although the text doesn't do much to support it) that Jolenta is not, for Jonas, a Siren but rather Dulcinea. That, rather than being in lust for Jolenta, he is in love with his own fantasy, his ideal woman. The fantasy may have been sparked by Jolenta and given her name because of lust, but Jonas may be unaware of that. He may continue to see himself as a robot with some icky spare parts, and may imagine himself returning from the unreachable star all fixed up, ready to love Jolenta purely and chastely, if not from afar.

-Rostrum
