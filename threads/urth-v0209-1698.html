_Annotated Hobbit_, second edition

Reports that Douglas Anderson's second edition of The Annotated Hobbit has greatly expanded its note on the word "warg," which cites a Tolkien letter to Gene Wolfe. Notes that the new text adds an appreciation of Wolfe's work and of his interest in words related to his surname, printed on pp. 146-147.
informs · source-and-allusion

Douglas Anderson's _The Annotated Hobbit_ was reissued last month in a second edition. In this new edition, Anderson has greatly expanded the note on "warg" that cites a letter of J.R.R. Tolkien to Gene Wolfe; the new text contains a brief appreciation of Wolfe's work and of the significance of Wolfe's interest in words related to his surname.

The note is printed on pp. 146-147 of the new edition.

-- Dan Rabin
