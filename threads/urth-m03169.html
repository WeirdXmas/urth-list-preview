Reports where the characters Auk and Chenille appear in In Green's Jungles: they land on Green, survive, and are held prisoner in Sinew's home in chapters 24-25. Cites chapter 4, where Horn or Silk's story mentions Auk, and chapter 17, where Auk is mentioned again near the end at the Juzgado.
informs · close-textual-reading

Auk and Chenille landed on Green and survived.
Sinew stayed there too. Auk and Chenille were
prisoners in Sinew's home.
Chapters 24-25 in of "In Green's Jungles".

In Chapter 4 Horn/Silk relates his story and Auk is
mentioned.
Ch 17 near the end Auk is mentioned again (at the
Juzgado).
