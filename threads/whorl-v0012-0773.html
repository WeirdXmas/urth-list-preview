2001-05-14 Nigel Price #

Offers to e-mail a photo of attendees at a Gene Wolfe symposium held in Birmingham the previous year, naming list contributors Jonathan Laidlow and James Russell. Shares no interpretation of Wolfe's books; the post is a social announcement with an offer to send an image file.
chats

Can't imagine who might be interested, but I have a photo of the attendees at the Gene Wolfe symposium held in Birmingham, UK, last year. Some of them contribute to this list, so if your curiosity overcomes your good taste and common sense, drop me an e-mail and I'll send you a JPEG. The current version is 230K, but I can make it smaller if you wish. Jonathan Laidlow, James Russell and the old Spectacled Bear himself are all in the picture, although I am safely out of shot, operating the camera.

Nigel Price
Minety, Wiltshire
England
