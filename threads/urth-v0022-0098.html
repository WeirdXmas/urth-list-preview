1999-01-19 Craig Christensen #

Corrects an earlier claim that Soldier of the Mist exists as an audiotape, explaining that an Amazon search returned it but the listing was not an audiotape. Apologizes for the misinformation, acknowledging that the poster had reported it without checking the entry.
informs

My apologies. As far as I know, there is no audiotape of 'Soldier of the Mists'. When I searched on Amazon.com under audiotape by 'gene wolfe' the Soldier story was pulled in, too. (I was pretty excited - I listen to a lot of books on tape.) After posting my misleading message, I returned and noticed that the entry was not an audiotape. Sorry for the misinformation.
