Themes: Time loops and causality

1999-06-24 Dan Parmenter #

Recounts a convention hospitality-suite conversation in which an unnamed fan argued that the film Bill and Ted's Excellent Adventure is essentially the same story as The Book of the New Sun, comparing its time-tripping protagonists to Severian's bootstrap-style loops. Offered as a light, fannish aside continuing a thread on comic strip parallels, with no textual evidence given.
chats

While we're at this rather "fannish" practice of finding parallels between comic strip characters and the cast of BotNS, I'm reminded of the fact that at the last SF convention I attended (Arisia in Boston last winter) a fellow (whose name I never caught, one of those people one sees at these sorts of things without necessarily registering names) in the con hospitality suite held forth on the idea that the movie "Bill and Ted's Excellent Adventure" with its "differently clued" protaganists and haphazard time tripping could be seen as essentially the same story as BotNS. There were several amusing specifics, but I can't remember them. I suppose the movie is really more "By His Bootstraps" but so is BotNS at one level, eh?

Lex
