2003-04-07 Stephen Case #

Shares three favorite Wolfe quotations from Citadel, The Woman who Loved the Centaur Pholus, and On Blue's Waters, saying that such lines make Wolfe his favorite author. Offers no analysis of the passages; the post is a simple list of quoted lines and a statement of admiration.
informs

This is a multi-part message in MIME format.

------=_NextPart_000_0001_01C2FD4A.AD768C70
Content-Type: text/plain;
charset="us-ascii"
Content-Transfer-Encoding: 7bit

"We are as solid as most truly false things are-a dance of particles in space. Only the things no one can touch are true, as you should know by now."
-Citadel

"The second-best thing was to fight and win. But the first-best thing was to fight the fight worth fighting. Where would he be, if not here?" -The Woman who Loved the Centaur Pholus

"If I need more courage than I have to live, I will pretend to have it and live anyway. I did that on the battlefield . . . I acted the part of a hero. That is to say, I acted as it seemed to me I would have if I had actually possessed dauntless courage."
-On Blue's Waters

. . . I have dozens more. Quotes like these are what make him my favorite author.

-Steve
