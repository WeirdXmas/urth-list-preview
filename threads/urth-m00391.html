2005-04-12 Daniel D Jones #

Asks the list for the location of a passage in The Book of the New Sun where Severian justifies the Torturers' Guild by citing the costs of long-term imprisonment and work programs that would take jobs from the poor. Notes the poster cannot recall which book contains it and requests a pointer or quotation.
questions

There's a passage in the BOTNS where Severian gives a justification for the Torturers Guild. He talks about long term imprisonment being a drain on society, work programs taking jobs from the indigent, etc. Unlike Severian, I forget things and I can't recall which book it's in. A memory jog or pointer to the quote would be greatly appreciated.

2005-04-12 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to Daniel D Jones #

Answers Daniel D Jones's request for the passage where Severian justifies the Torturers' Guild, pointing to The Sword of the Lictor and the scene where Dorcas lies ill at the inn at Thrax. Gives a locating memory rather than quoting the justification itself, admitting uncertainty about whether it comes on Severian's first visit or a later one.
answers · close-textual-reading

It's in "Sword of the Lictor," when Dorcas is lying ill in the inn at Thrax. I don't recall if it's when he first brings her there or on his next visit.

--Dan'l
