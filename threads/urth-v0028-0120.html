1999-09-16 Therese Littleton #

Informs readers that Orb's Litany of the Long Sun, the first half of The Book of the Long Sun, is slated for April 2000 per the Tor/Forge catalog, and that Strange Travelers, a new Wolfe collection, arrives in January. Replies to Tony A. West's question about a Long Sun reissue, offering the catalog listing as the answer.
informs

Hi Tony and all;
Sorry for the whorlish post, but thought you would like to know that LITANY OF THE LONG SUN, Orb's release of the first half of The Book of the Long Sun, is scheduled for publication in April 2000, according to the Tor/Forge catalog. Also, STRANGE TRAVELERS, a new Wolfe collection, is coming out in January.
--Therese Littleton (fellow lurker)
