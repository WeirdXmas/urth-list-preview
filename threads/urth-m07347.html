2008-09-18 Craig Brewer replying to TGoulding05 #

Reports on a small reading by Wolfe in Palatine, sharing gossip about his views on China Mieville, his collaboration with Damon Knight, and his research habits. Mentions that he read chapter 11 of An Evil Guest aloud and that The Wizard Knight sells well in Europe; the post is a reply thanking Teri Goulding for the announcement.
informs

Thanks for posting this! I got the email after work, turned around and headed back to the city. Worth the 1.5 hour drive. Very small group: only about 10 people or so, but also quite fun. He was very chatty.

Just some tidbits and gossip that the list might appreciate:

He understands why people think China Mieville is good, but he doesn't personally. He and Damon Knight used to try to write stories by taking various genre "premises" for stories, use one for the beginning of a story and the other for the end, but they never quite got it to work. He never researches formally before jumping into a work. Instead, he waits until he needs some information. (His example: What did ancient Greeks eat for breakfast? Look it up. Turns out they didn't eat breakfast at all. And on with the work...) Wizard Knight is making him tons of money in Europe.
He seemed to have an incredibly good time reading chapter 11 of _An Evil Guest_ for us.

I'm sure there was more, but I'm quite tired after the drive.

But thank you again to Teri for posting this to the list!

Craig
