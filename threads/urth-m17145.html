Inhumi in the whorl

Proposes that the Neighbour's reference to the Western stars means the Neighbour Whorl is a physical place reachable by travel, and that the departed people may have gone to Lune. Grounds this in the Neighbour's wording and the regional split between Western and Eastern usage of 'Neighbours' and 'Vanished People', answering David Stockhoff's view that the other world is only like Faerie.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: The Neighbour Whorl is Lune

The Neighbour looks towards the Western stars when he says this, which suggests to me that he is thinking of an actual place you can travel to by physical means.

That no one else in the series uses the term 'Neighbour Whorl' is not too surprising from an in-story point of view; the people who would use the term would be those of the Western continent, who say 'Neighbours', and have more to do with them, while most of the action takes place on the Eastern continent, where they say 'Vanished People'.

Still, the way it's said sounds a bit like a a clue to me. Is it possible that they have gone to Lune? That's a neighbour whorl in a very obvious sense.
