2003-01-29 Josh Geller #

Proposes that Baldanders in The Book of the New Sun might represent Samuel R. Delany, extending the idea that other writers are encoded in Wolfe's characters. Grounds the suggestion in Wolfe's earlier remark that Master Ultan stands for Borges, and responds to Michael Andre-Driussi's joke about Green's name decoding to William Dhalgren.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · author-and-biography · theories: Baldanders represents Samuel R. Delany; Master Ultan represents Borges

That reminds me.

Gene Wolfe told me once that Master Ultan was or represented Borges.

I have been wondering for a while now if there might not be some other writers encoded in there, and specifically if Baldanders might not be or represent Chip Delany. The notion just won't go away.
