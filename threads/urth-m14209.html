Proposes that Severian's reluctance to leave the Sand Garden hints at a desert Gene Wolfe might have in mind, suggesting Father Inire may have met Jesus during a forty-day period in the wilderness. Offers no textual citation, grounding the idea only in the parallel to the gospel forty days and in the poster's sense of Wolfe's habits.
