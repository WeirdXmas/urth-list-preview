Floating Islands: Urth Isn't Magnetic

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1999-10-01 Mitchell A. Bailey #

Argues that Urth has little or no magnetic field because its core has nearly solidified, so compasses would not work and Severian never uses one. Responds to Carlton Greene's suggestion that reversed magnetic poles explain Severian's northward travel from the stone town to Lake Titicaca, citing the text's silence on compasses as weak support.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Urth has no magnetic field

...

Relevance? Severian doesn't use a magnetic compass. Nor does anyone else.

As I pointed out previously, Urth probably has little or no magnetic field because her core is said to have solidified or nearly solidified. And lack of any mention of magnetic compass use in the text is (admittedly weak) empirical support for that proposition.
