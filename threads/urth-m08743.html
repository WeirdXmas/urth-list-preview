2009-03-16 Jeff Wilson replying to Son of Witz #

Explains that a sword sheath can have extra covering parts that protect the blade from tarnish and weather, so it may extend over the guard, comparing it to a military pistol holster flap that snaps down over the weapon. Responds to Son of Witz's question about how Terminus Est's sheath could cover its guard, a point of swordsmanship raised in the Wolfe mailing list discussion.
answers · genre-and-craft

A sheath can have extra bits to protect the weapon from tarnish and the elements, esp if it isn't drawn that often. Similar to the way military style pistol holsters have a wide flap over the top that snaps down, almost completely covering the weapon.
