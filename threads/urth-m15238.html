Proposes that a teatime detail in Chapter 36 of The Magician, where cups, saucers, cream and sugar are set out, secretly echoes the Tarot suits of cups, staffs, coins and swords laid before the magician of the first trump. Asks whether the resemblance could be mere coincidence, offering the Tarot card layout as the basis for the comparison.
