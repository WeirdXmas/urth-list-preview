The Books in THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN

Urth list · 4 messages · 2012-03-21 to 2012-03-21

2012urthThe Book of the New SunFred KiescheNessun Saprà
The thread: Fred Kiesche asked whether Gene Wolfe's essay "The Books in The Book of the New Sun" had been published anywhere besides PLAN(ET) ENGINEERING, noting it was absent from CASTLE OF THE OTTER. Nessun Saprà answered that the essay first appeared in a 1984 volume and was later reprinted in Peter Wright's Shadow of the New Sun, giving the page range. Kiesche thanked them and said he would check his own copy of that collection. The thread is a short publication-history exchange that ended with the question answered and no disagreement.
  • Fred Kiesche: asked where else the essay was published and expressed surprise it was missing from Castle of the Otter
  • Nessun Saprà: reported the essay's 1984 first appearance and its reprint in Peter Wright's Shadow of the New Sun

Themes: Books, libraries and writing
