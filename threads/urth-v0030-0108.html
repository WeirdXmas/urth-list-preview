Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2000-11-10 Roy C. Lackey #

Proposes that Miss Birkhead in Peace is really Sherry Gold in disguise, citing the parallel deaths of Sherry and Birkhead followed by Den's strokes. Grounds the idea in textual gaps: Birkhead is never described, Sherry's fate is unexplained, and Birkhead was Smart's secretary before Den, while noting the death notice's marriage and maiden name as counterevidence.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Miss Birkhead is Sherry Gold

About Miss Birkhead: I've had to talk myself out of the idea--more than once--that she and Sherry Gold were the same person. Sherry dies; Den has a stroke. Birkhead dies; Den has a stroke/heart attack/whatever and dies. It's a bit much for coincidence. Birkhead is never described, mention is never made of what, exactly, became of Sherry, and the Sherry is 16 _now_ comment seems to indicate that he knew her when she was past that age. Yes, Birkhead was secretary to Smart before Den (for how long?); yes, she told Den later about how the department heads could tell when Smart was going to make an inspection (how long ago was that?); yes, the death notice said she was married with children (does that make it true?), and her maiden name would seem to be Birkhead, yes, but still . . . still, the notion has its appeal. It can't be true--can it?

And a birk is a birch. What can be made of that?

Roy
