Themes: Names and their meanings

2000-07-22 Alice Turner #

Relays that Chris Culver has translated the names of characters in The Book of the Long Sun into English using a dictionary, and asks the list whether the translation list should be posted. Responds to an earlier post about Wolfe's use of Italian in character names, and offers the list as a time-saving and sometimes amusing reference.
questions · source-and-allusion

From Chris Culver:

I translated most of the characters' names for mantis. Would the list like this to be posted? (It saves time with a dictionary, and is, in a few cases, amusing.) You could email me, but, if there are compelling negatives, perhaps posting is better.

-alga
