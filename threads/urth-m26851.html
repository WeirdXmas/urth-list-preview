Goodreads Book Giveaway for Michael Andre-Druissi's Newest

Urth list · 5 messages · 2019-10-02 to 2019-10-03

2019urthRereading WolfeMarc AraminiCharles GillinghamPaul Rydeen
The thread: A Goodreads giveaway announced 100 advance Kindle copies of Michael Andre-Driussi's chapter guide to The Book of the New Sun, pitched as spoiler-free for both first readers and rereaders. Marc Aramini questioned how many Wolfe fans would pay for such a guide and why a publisher would give away so many copies. Charles Gillingham answered with a personal anecdote about his own buying habits rather than a market argument. Aramini then reported that the author's next book, The Land Across, is still in editing with no release date, and the thread ended as a short social exchange without real resolution.
  • Marc Aramini: doubts many Wolfe fans would pay for a chapter guide and questions the giveaway's logic
  • Charles Gillingham: says he buys physical copies of books by authors whose free digital copies he owns, offering an anecdote on the market
  • Rereading Wolfe announcement: the guide avoids spoilers and suits first readers and rereaders alike

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2019-10-02 Rereading Wolfe #

Announces a Goodreads giveaway of 100 advance Kindle copies of Michael Andre-Driussi's chapter guide to The Book of the New Sun, running October 5 to 13. Notes the guide promises to avoid spoilers and address details only as they occur, so it suits both first readers and rereaders.
informs

"Gene Wolfe's The Book of the New Sun: A Chapter Guide"

Coming up this Saturday 10/5 through Sunday 10/13:
Gene Wolfe's The Book of the New Sun: A Chapter Guide
by Michael "Mantis" Andre-Driussi.
Giveaway: 100 advance copies of the Kindle edition

It promises not to be spoilery during the reading and only address details as they occur, so I think it will work for 1st reads as well as rereads.

If you don't get one of the 100, no problem, you'll only have to wait a month for the general release.

https://www.goodreads.com/giveaway/show/298421?fbclid=IwAR2x47LarTErBTBjNIMKMzXdWEjgCxbC_a_FbhohW6XqwS3zQ8o8vROr60k

~ James
The Rereading Wolfe Podcast
