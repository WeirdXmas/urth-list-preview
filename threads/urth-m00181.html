Next Wolfe collection: date and contents

Urth list · 5 messages · 2004-11-30 to 2004-11-30

2004urthNicholas GeversMaruCharles ReedJohn BarachDan'l Danehy-Oakes
The thread: Readers on the Urth list shared news about the next Gene Wolfe collection, Starwater Strains. Nicholas Gevers announced Tor's August 2005 schedule at 130,000 words and listed the contents, including Viewpoint, Rattler and The Boy Who Hooked the Sun. Maru asked whether the listed story Empires of Foliage and Flower matches a tale from the Book of the Wonders of Urth and Sky that they had long sought. Charles Reed replied that the story already appears in The Best of Crank, so readers need not wait until August. The exchange was a simple factual one with no real disagreement.

STRAINS, has been announced for August 2005 by Tor.

the contents list:

> "Viewpoint"
> "Rattler"
> "In Glory Like Their Star"

> "Of Soil and Climate"
> "The Dog of the Drops"
> "Mute"
> "Petting Zoo"
> "Castaway"
> "The Fat Magician"
> "Hunter Lake"
> "The Boy Who Hooked the Sun"
> "Try and Kill It"
> "Empires of Foliage and Flower"
> "Game in the Pope's Head"
> "The Arimaspian Legacy"
> "The Seraph from Its Sepulcher"
> "Lord of the Land"
> "Golden City Far"
