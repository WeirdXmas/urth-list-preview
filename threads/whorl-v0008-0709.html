Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-08-03 mary whalen #

Proposes that the name Murtagon in Wolfe's work derives from the martagon lily, noting its alternative spellings, its red-cap nickname, and the Spanish word martagon meaning cunning or artful. Adds that the red cap resembles an artist's beret, linking the name to the artist sense, and offers these as a possible naming source for the character.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Murtagon named after the martagon lily

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Murtagon. All I could find fitting this is martagon, also mortegon, martagan, and martigon. With all those alternative spellings, it's probable that murtagon exists, too. It's also called the red-cap lily, turk's-cap lily, and the turk's cap (Lilium martagon or Lilium superbum). Martagon is also Spanish for cunning, artful, clever. Murtagon is a famous artist. The red cap reminds me of the stereotypical artist's beret.
