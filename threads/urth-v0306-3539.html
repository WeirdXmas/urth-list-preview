Themes: Stories within stories

2003-06-26 David Duffy #

Questions whether Wolfe can be a lesser short story writer, given how strong and central the embedded stories in his novels are, citing Peace as an example. Also offers Philip José Farmer's Sail On, Sail On as a memorable SF short story with world building.
questions

If Wolfe is a lesser short story writer, then why are the embedded stories (eg Peace) so good and so central to the novels ;)

A most memorable SF/fantastic short with world building has to be Philip Jose Farmer's "Sail On, Sail On".
