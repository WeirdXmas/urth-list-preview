AEG: Cursing/Not Cursing

Urth list · 1 messages · 2010-08-06 to 2010-08-06

2010urthBrian Lovely

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones

2010-08-06 Brian Lovely #

Asks which characters in An Evil Guest avoid cursing and what links them, noting Cassie and Margaret's substitute oaths and a character who swears heavily at a waitress. Suggests that if only Cassie and Margaret avoided cursing it would hint they are related or the same person, but the broader pattern makes it more interesting.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Cassie and Margaret are related or the same person

I don't remember exactly who, but some characters pretty obviously avoid cursing, and some are fairly obvious about it. I think it's Cassie and Margaret who use substitute words like "ladydog" and "bullhockey" (or something). At least one character really tears into somebody (Ries into a waitress?).

If it was just Cassie and Margaret avoiding cursing, I'd say it was just an indicator that they are somehow related (or the same person), but the boolean aspect makes it more interesting. What connects the characters for whom cursing seems to be a "thing"?
