1998-05-09 William H. Ansley #

States a preference for Bruce Pennington's cover paintings for Wolfe's books over the Maitz versions, finding them old-fashioned and dated-looking. Thanks Tony Ellis for posting the scans and compares Pennington's style to Gray Morrow's, while admitting that comparison may not hold up side by side.
chats

Aaaaarrrgh! I should have known! There are no easy questions involving Wolfe. 8->

Thanks very much to Tony Ellis for putting up the Bruce Pennington cover paintings. I like them better than the Maitz versions. They strike me as "old fashioned" somehow. (This is not necessarily a bad thing.) Perhaps it is because they are scans of worn covers, but I would have guessed that the books were published at least 25 years ago, just judging from the cover art. They remind me slightly of Gray Morrow's work, although I am not sure this would hold up after a side-by-side comparison.

William Ansley
