3 waves of colonists

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-02 to 1998-06-02

1998urthMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Colonization and the native, Doubles, twins and clones

1998-06-02 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that Sainte Anne was settled in several waves of ancient colonists, matching fairy-tale races to real mythic homelands, and suggests David was designed as a tanist twin for Number Five to kill in place of Maitre. Grounds the twin idea in the knife wound to David's thigh during the attack on the mantis slave and in the brother-kill-brother pattern of a Wolfe story.
proposes · source-and-allusion · allegory-and-typology · theories: David was designed as Maitre's tanist twin; Sainte Anne had several waves of colonists

There could be more than one wave of ancient colonists to Sainte Anne. Arbitrarily:

Gondwanaland: the Shadow Children (goblins)
Etruscan Italy: the Wetlanders (trolls)
Homeric Greece: the Free People (elves and dryads)
Poictsme: ?
Country of Friends: ?

A note about the lethal twins and David:

I wonder if David was designed by Maitre to be his own tanist--that is, a twin for Number Five to kill in rivalry instead of killing Maitre in usurpation?

It nearly happens, when the attack on the "mantis" slave goes awry and Number Five's knife goes into David's thigh (with shades of the brother-kill-brother in "A Story"). As Maitre patches David's leg up without comment, he must be realizing that the ploy didn't work.

=mantis=
