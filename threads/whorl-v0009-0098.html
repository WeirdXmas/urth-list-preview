A correction to Ultan's Library

Whorl list · 3 messages · 1999-04-09 to 1999-04-09

1999whorlThe Book of the Long SunJonathan LaidlowAlastair ReynoldsDouglas, Wesley
The thread: A brief correction thread on the Urth list. Jonathan Laidlow clarified that his earlier request on the Ultan's Library site was for a list of Wolfe's uncollected short fiction, not unpublished fiction, and apologised for the wording. Wesley Douglas asked to be removed from the list, reacting irritably to the correction, and mentioned a forthcoming Wolfe short story collection called Strange Cargo reported in Locus. Alastair Reynolds noted the announced collection without a date, addressed the bibliography wording, and reminded the list it is not the Long Sun discussion list. No textual argument developed; the thread ended with the correction and the Strange Cargo news.
  • Jonathan Laidlow: wanted uncollected short fiction listed, not unpublished fiction; apologised for the wording
  • Wesley Douglas: asked to be removed from the list; reported Strange Cargo from Locus without a date
  • Alastair Reynolds: noted Strange Cargo announcement; clarified the bibliography wording; reminded list of its scope

1999-04-09 Jonathan Laidlow #

Corrects an earlier request on the Ultan's Library website post, clarifying that he wanted a list of Wolfe's uncollected short fiction rather than unpublished fiction. Apologises for the wording error; it is a brief correction with no textual argument.
informs

A correction folks...

In my post about the Ultan's Library Website and bibliography I asked for help in producing a list of Wolfe's unpublished short fiction. I meant, of course, uncollected short fiction.

I do apologise.

Jonathan
