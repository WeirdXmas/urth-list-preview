The *Ten* Heads of Cerberus

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-01 to 1998-06-01

1998urthRobert Borski

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

1998-06-01 Robert Borski #

Argues that The Fifth Head of Cerberus pairs everything, so the head count yields two multiples of five: a vertical sequence of Wolfes counting down from Number Five and a horizontal one including Maitre, Aunt Jeannine, David and Phaedria. Grounds this in the sequence of named characters and in assigning the virgin role to Mr. Million and to Phaedria, responding to Adam Louis Stephanides's head-counting post.
proposes · puzzle-solving · myth-and-structure · theories: Fifth Head of Cerberus counts multiples of five; Mr. Million is the virgin; Phaedria is the virgin in the horizontal configuration; Gene Wolfe 0 is the founder's clone-numbering

To Adam Louis Stephanides, who's joined the head counting debate:

I'm now arguing there are two multiples of five because *everything* in FIFTH HEAD is paired.

The vertical configuration counts backwards from Number Five through time, although its impetus is forward:

5. Number Five
4. Maitre
3. Mr. Million
2. Gene Wolfe II (first cloned progeny)
1. Gene Wolfe (founder, ur-patriarch)

In this group, to incorporate David's maidenhead pun, Mr. Million is the virgin.

As for our Gene Wolfe, the one you number 1, he would be Gene Wolfe 0.

The horizontal configuration includes the current batch of Wolfes, the one Aunt Jeannine can't quite total to five (because she's unaware of who Number Five's sister is).

1. Maitre
2. Aunt Jeannine
3. Number Five
4. David
5. Phaedria

In the above configuration Phaedria is the virgin, because I refuse to believe that given the casual attitude about sex and the licentiousness of the Maison du Chien that Aunt Jeannine is still hymenally intact.

Robert Borski
