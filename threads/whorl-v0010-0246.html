Love thy Neighbour

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-01-29 to 2000-01-29

2000whorlThe Book of the Short SunCharis Wakelin

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

2000-01-29 Charis Wakelin #

Proposes that the Neighbours' arrival on Green turned the inhumi from chameleon-like beings into sentient ones, and that a pact with Horn linked the Neighbours to protecting humans. Grounds this in Krait's account of inhumi history and in the scene where Horn's manacle returns Jahlee to her natural state, suggesting it may reverse the change.
proposes · myth-and-structure · close-textual-reading · theories: Neighbours transformed the inhumi into sentient beings; Horn's pact made Neighbours protectors of humans; Iron manacle reverts Jahlee to inhumi form

I'm de-lurking to throw an idea into the pot. What if the love thy neighbour angle means exactly that in OBW? The Neighbours went to Green and it was then that the inhumi changed from being lizard like chameleon beings to sentient ones. Also Krait talks about the history of the inhumi before he dies. I think the Neighbours and the inhumi are intertwined somehow and the Neighbour Horn met agreed that they would help protect humans from the inhumi if Horn agreed on behalf of mankind to let the Neighbours come in peace etc. I'm sure all this is there for a reason. What do you all think? Another thing I noticed was that when Horn put the manacle on Jahlee she started to return to her natural state, where the iron was touching her. Could this be a way to send them back to the way the inhumi were?

Going back into lurk mode.

Charis
