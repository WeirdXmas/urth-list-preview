1998-06-15 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Reports that Amazon lists Endangered Species as out of stock but Planet Engineering as out of print, and describes a walking drag-mining machine seen on cable TV. Responds to Marty Light's question about whether the two books are still in print, with a joking sign-off that also replies to a list-moderation tone remark by Dan Parmenter.
informs

Amazon.com lists Endangered Species as "out of stock" and Planet Engineering as "out of print." The implication is that the former might be reprinted soon but the latter
won't.

Also:

There's a "drag-mining" machine that stands a few dozen
stories tall and walks on gargantuan pneumatic feet. Saw it on cable teevee last night. It looks more like an oil rig than a tower.

Finally, Dan Parmenter writes

To which I concur. Stay cool and you won't feel my WRATH.

r.
URTH den mother

1998-06-16 Michael Bowman #

Reports that the poster found a cheap copy of Planet Engineering through Bibliofind, a database of rare and used bookstores, and describes how its search and want-list services work. Responds to Ranjit Bhatnagar's quoted question about whether Endangered Species and Planet Engineering are still in print, offering a practical way to locate out-of-print copies.
answers

I found a copy of Plan[e]t Engineering via Bibliofind a few months ago, and it was fairly cheap. If you're not familiar with Bibliofind, it is a database of many rare and used book stores. When you search you're searching, and ordering online from, many stores. They also take want lists.
