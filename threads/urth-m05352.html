[AEG] Gideon Chase's identity

Urth list · 5 messages · 2008-12-21 to 2008-12-22

2008urthDave TallmanRoy C. LackeyDavid DuffyBlake Hyde
The thread: Readers debated whether Gideon Chase in An Evil Guest is a hybrid of a Wolder and a human, possibly the child of Chase's wife rather than of the man who raised him. Blake Hyde proposed the hybrid idea from Wolfe's remarks on Wolder hybridization, Gideon's eyes, and Klauser's comments to Cassie, and later argued Gideon was cuckolded and that the bat-people are a separate shy race. Dave Tallman offered a restated theory that Gideon's biological father is a Woldercan dream-walker who inspired Lovecraft's Randolph Carter. Roy C. Lackey pointed out that the hybrid idea had been raised earlier without reply, and David Duffy supported Hyde while extending it to the bat-people. The thread ended with supporters adding to the idea rather than any settled reading.
  • Blake Hyde: Gideon is a Wolder-human hybrid, possibly via the elder Chase's wife, and the bat-people are a separate race
  • Dave Tallman: Gideon's biological father is a Woldercan dream-walking wizard who inspired Randolph Carter; Robert Chase engineered the birth
  • Roy C. Lackey: the hybrid idea was already raised earlier without follow-up, so repeated observations are normal
  • David Duffy: Gideon is a Wolder-human hybrid because only male Wolders alter their DNA; bat-people may also be Wolder-animal hybrids

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Beasts and the animal in us, Doubles, twins and clones

2008-12-21 Blake Hyde #

Proposes that Gideon Chase in An Evil Guest is a hybrid of a Wolder and possibly the elder Chase's wife. Grounds the idea in Wolfe's remarks that Wolders can hybridize with lower forms, an explanation for Gideon's eyes, and Klauser's late statement to Cassie that Wolders look human until closely examined.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Gideon Chase is a Wolder-human hybrid

Sorry if this is a common theory; I wasn't able to find anything like this in the archives. It seems to me that Gideon Chase is likely to be a hybrid of a Wolder and perhaps the elder Chase's wife. Wolfe mentions several times that Wolders can hybridize with "lower forms," and it would explain his eyes. Near the end of the novel, Klauser tells Cassie that Wolders look just like humans until you look closely.

Hmm.

2008-12-21 Dave Tallman replying to Roy C. Lackey #

Proposes a restated five-point theory that Gideon Chase's biological father is a Woldercan native and dream-walking wizard who inspired Lovecraft's Randolph Carter. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's complaint that an earlier post went unanswered, linking the earlier version and adding that Robert Chase engineered the birth and trained his son in Earth arcana.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Gid's biological father is a Woldercan native; Gid's father inspired Lovecraft's Randolph Carter; Robert Chase had his wife impregnated by a Woldercan; Chuck Finney descends from Circus of Dr. Lao's author

I brought it up a couple of months ago, along with some additional twists. Here is my reference:
http://lists.urth.net/htdig.cgi/urth-urth.net/2008-October/010311.html

In brief, my theory is that:
1) Gid's biological father is a Woldercan native.
2) His father is a "walker of dreams" (wizard) who inspired Lovecraft's tales of Randolph Carter.
3) Working from arcane knowledge he possessed, Robert Chase worked with Chuck Finney to get Woldercan discovered. Finney is a descendant of the man who wrote "The Circus of Dr. Lao" and may have had some secret notes from the time his forefather met that alien being.
4) Robert Chase probably had his wife impregnated by a Woldercan deliberately, in order to produce a powerful child.
5) Robert Chase trained his son in Earth arcana.

2008-12-21 Blake Hyde replying to David Duffy #

Argues that Gideon Chase's "father" was cuckolded, so Chase may be the child of a Wolder rather than of the man who raised him. Supports this with Klauser's remark to Cassie that Wolders sometimes try to seduce human women, and separately argues the bat-people are a different shy race, not Wolder-animal hybrids.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Gideon Chase's father was cuckolded by a Wolder; Bat-people are a shy race, not Wolder hybrids

Yes, this is why I think Chase's "father" was being cuckolded. This would also explain why Klauser tells Cassie that the Wolders sometimes try to seduce human women.

I think the bat-people are something else entirely, one of the shyer races who live "like mice in [the Storm King's] barn."
