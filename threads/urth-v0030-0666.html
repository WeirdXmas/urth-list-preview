2001-08-24 Nigel Price #

Relays Ron Crown's question about Peter Wright's essay collection "Attending Dedalus", which draws on Wright's doctoral thesis and published essays on Wolfe. Asks whether anyone recalls the earlier title Wright mentioned at the Birmingham Wolfe Symposium, addressing Jonathan, Joshua and Spectacled Bear directly.
questions

Ron crown asks:

Peter Wright told us about his book "Attending Dedalus" at the Wolfe Symposium at Birmingham University last year. This is, to the best of my recollection, a book of his own essays about Wolfe, and includes material taken from his doctoral thesis and published essays. It's certainly one that I want to get hold of.

Don't remember if he mentioned the first title here, though I've got a feeling he might have done. Anyone remember? Jonathan? Joshua? Spectacled Bear? Again, looks very interesting.

Nigel

2001-08-24 Peter Westlake #

Replies that the poster does not recall whether Peter Wright mentioned his essay book, and notes that Wright once wanted to join the Urth and Whorl lists but did not know how to subscribe. Responds to Nigel Price's quoted question, which asks about Wright's Wolfe essay collection 'Attending Dedalus' discussed at a Birmingham symposium, and asks whether Wright ever joined.
chats

Sorry, I don't recall. One thing I do remember is that Peter was interested in the urth and whorl lists, but didn't know how to subscribe. He doesn't seem to be here now; did anything happen about that?

Spectacled Bear.
