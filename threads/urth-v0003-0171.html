Themes: Unreliable narration

1997-07-20 Matthew Freestone #

Argues that the hermit Crazy Pete's dating in Peace should be anchored to Weer's imagined visits to Dr Van Ness rather than to the picnic. Supports this by citing Weer's remark about consulting Dr Black as a boy and Dr Van Ness as a man, and Van Ness's statement that Weer is about sixty, which he says fits the dates in Changeling better.
