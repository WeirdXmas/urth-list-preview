WF travel time

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-10-03 to 1998-10-03

1998urthThe Book of the New SunJon Camfield

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-10-03 Jon Camfield #

Argues that the Whorl Fragment (WF) was already prepared and moving toward Urth before Severian's trial, so its transit time needn't be a puzzle, and asks whether its travel length can be estimated from Typhon's place in time relative to Severian's reign. Also asks whether the distances in Brook Madregot are logarithmic and whether Madregot appears outside the kabbalistic references; grounds the claim in the WF's motion during Typhon's time but not Apu-Punchau's.
questions · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: The Whorl Fragment was pre-made and en route before Severian's trial

In one of the AEs, (Aes??) -mantis- had a paragraph about the transit-time of the WF-- I don't understand there being a question here. We know that the WF was pre-made and already in route to Urth, even before Sev's trial (heck, the WF was already moving in Typhon's time, but not in Apu-Punchau's, b/c of Sev's link to its power).

Don't we (I must admit, I have with me neither the books nor the Lexicon, so can't reference this) have a general knowledge of how far back Typhon was in comparison to Sev's reign, and can give an estimation of how long the WF has existed and travelled for?

Related question: are the distances in the Brook Madregot logarithmic? Have we ever come up with further references to Madregot than the kabbalistic ones? (remember, I'm new to the list and haven't completely read the archives, so if it's there, tell me to go RTFM and I'll nicely shut up and go do some reading)
