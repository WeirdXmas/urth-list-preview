Wolfe-inspired short story collection

Urth list · 9 messages · 2013-05-13 to 2013-05-16

2013urthThe Book of the New SunStephen PalmerStephen CaseAndy RobertsonAntónio Pedro MarquesMarc AraminiTony EllisDan'l Danehy-OakesDaniel Petersen
The thread: Stephen Case announced his Kindle short story collection TREES AND OTHER WONDERS, which he credits Wolfe as inspiring, and asked list readers for opinions. The thread quickly became a roll call of list members who write or have published Wolfe-influenced fiction, including Marc Aramini's forthcoming anthology story, Daniel Petersen's aspirations, and Andy Robertson's Nightland stories. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes and Tony Ellis listed their own publications, with Ellis declining to name a magazine. Little critical discussion of Wolfe's books occurred; the thread drifted into social chat and self-promotion and ended without any real debate.
  • Stephen Case: promotes his Wolfe-inspired collection and seeks reader feedback
  • Marc Aramini: recalls former list writers and critics, chats about Notre Dame
  • Daniel Petersen: aspires to write Wolfe-influenced fiction and asks who else does
  • Andy Robertson: notes Wolfe-like elements in his Nightland stories
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: lists his own published stories, none Wolfean
  • Tony Ellis: admits one Wolfe-inspired story was published but won't name it

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both, Stories within stories

2013-05-13 Stephen Case #

Announces a new Kindle short story collection, TREES AND OTHER WONDERS, by the poster, who says Wolfe has been a primary inspiration and has given feedback on his stories. Asks list readers for their opinions, pitching the collection to fans of Wolfe's sense of wonder.
informs

Please pardon this rather self-serving note to the list and feel free to ignore if uninterested. I've recently published a collection of short stories, TREES AND OTHER WONDERS, that is now available on the Kindle Store:
http://www.amazon.com/Trees-Other-Wonders-Collected-ebook/dp/B00CPS3C3C/

A. Lafferty, and Jorge Luis Borges," which is why I trouble you with it at all.

Wolfe has long been a primary inspiration for my own writing. We have corresponded for a number of years, and he has given me very helpful feedback on some of my own stories. This collection includes ten previously-published works (first appearing in a number of pro and semi-pro zines) as well as two appearing here for the first time. I'm certainly very far from being anywhere near Gene's league, but I hope that fans of Wolfe might find glimmers of that sense of wonder he does so well in my own work.

Plus, I'd be very interested to hear what you think-- because fans of Wolfe are the sort of readers I'm looking for.

-Steve
