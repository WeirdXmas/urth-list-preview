Witches and Things in Jars

1998urthThe Book of the New SunPeter Westlake

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Hidden identities and parentage

1998-06-22 Peter Westlake #

Argues that the homunculus in the Citadel is not Severian's sister, since the room it came from held very old objects, and that Merryn may be far older than Severian. Responds to Adam and John by noting that the Witches' Tower meeting with a Merryn-like figure and the hints of a twin sister weaken the sibling theory, citing Wolfe's own uncertainty in interviews.
disputes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Merryn is not Severian's sister; Merryn is much older than Severian

Adam - thanks for explaining A & M.

The extraterrestrial mind contacted by the Cumaean I took to be just some ancient Fomalhautian of no other particular significance. I did wonder if the Cumaean was from Fomalhaut herself and knew it from back home, but there's no strong evidence for that.

John:

I wondered about the homunculus being Severian's sister for a moment (when it called him "brother"), but then I remembered where he had found it, among the effects of former Autarchs. I'll have to check when I get home, but I'm firmly under the impression that all the stuff in that room was very old, that the room hadn't been used for centuries.

Unfortunately, when Severian went on the errand to the Witches' Tower as a lad, he met someone who looked exactly like Merryn. So either that was someone else, or she's a lot older than him too. All the hints about him having a sister suggest a twin sister, which rather weakens the case for me.

Then there are interviews in which Wolfe himself says that he isn't sure whether Merryn is Severian's sister!

I *do* like this passage. One of the most amazing things about BotNS is that Wolfe manages to keep the whole intricate edifice together and still has time for new insights into the eternal verities. A genius!

Spectacled Bear.
