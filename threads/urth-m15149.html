Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2010-05-04 Mo Holkar / UKG #

Proposes that the mysterious third attender at Zwart's funeral in The Sorcerer's House, a figure seen as Skotos, alludes to the character Harry Lime in the film The Third Man. Grounds the claim in the shared shady nature of the two figures and notes that the book never says exactly what rackets Skotos ran.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: The third attender at Zwart's funeral alludes to Harry Lime

Hi all,

Just a throwaway point, but I guess people have noticed that Zwart's presence as the mysterious third attender at his own (as Skotos) funeral (242, 259) is surely a reference to The Third Man. We don't know exactly what rackets Skotos was mixed up in, but he has his shady nature in common with Harry Lime.

Mo
