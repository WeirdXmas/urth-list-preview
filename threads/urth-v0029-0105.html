2000-07-06 William H. Ansley #

Corrects Robert King's recollection of a line from the end of The Claw of the Conciliator, supplying the wording that begins 'In the final reckoning' rather than 'In the end'. Playfully presents the correction as a quiz-show answer, and offers the correct passage in place of the quoted version.
informs · close-textual-reading

Is that your final answer? Sorry. Ooooh, you were so close!

The correct answer is:

"In the final reckoning there is only love, only that divinity. That we are capable only of being what we are remains our unforgivable sin."

But you still get some lovely parting gifts. <g>

William Ansley
