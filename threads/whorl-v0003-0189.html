Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1997-02-26 xury #

Asks how Quetzal knows so much, including Pas's plans and Pas's death, given that he is an inhumi and the Prolocutor and that there have been no theophanies for thirty years. Also asks how Quetzal could hear the god's instructions during Pas's theophany if he is only a stowaway, and requests opinions on these points.
questions · theology-and-religion

Another question one must ask, is that of the matter explaining, how does Quetzal know so much? He is inhumi and the Prolocutor, but does this explain why he knew Pas' plans or even that Pas was dead, especially since there have been no theophanies for 30 years. Also when Pas' theophany occured he was able to hear the instrucions that the god gave also, so he too is able to communicate with the gods even though he is a stowaway? I would like to know some opinions on these matters, thanks. Xury
