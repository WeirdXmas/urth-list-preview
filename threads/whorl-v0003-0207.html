1997-03-07 Doug Eigsti #

Offers a more complete list of place names from the Gazetteer for Wolfe's Urth books, including Viron, Urbs, Trivagaunte, Sedes, Palustria, Limna and street names, with page and chapter references. Responds to earlier contributions by Ranjit Bhatnagar and vizcacha by compiling their city names into a fuller index and asks for further additions and corrections.
informs · close-textual-reading

Ranjit Bhatnagar and vizcacha have submitted names of cities. After a more thorough search I am able to offer a more complete list:

Viron
Ur Vol. II, Ch. 6, P. 171.4 (Hardback)
Urbs ditto
Trivagaunte ditto
Sedes ditto
Palustria ditto
Gens II,5,131.2
Wick II,9,228.6

River:

Limna

The Mtns. that look at Mtns. IV,3,70.2

Streets of Viron:

Market
