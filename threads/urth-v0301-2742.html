2003-01-20 Dan Rabin #

Recounts that the poster reread the Latro books after reading Herodotus, finding the history and geography easier to follow and enjoying the reread. Offers this as an answer to the thread's question of what to read alongside Latro, with no argument beyond personal experience.
informs · source-and-allusion

I've read the Latro books twice. The second time was after I read Herodotus. That helped a lot with the history and geography, besides being a lot of fun in itself.

-- Dan Rabin

Shares that the poster rereads Herodotus and Bernal Diaz del Castillo's history each year and counts them among favourite books. Replies to Dan Rabin's comment that reading Herodotus helped with the history and geography of the Latro books, agreeing with his recommendation.
