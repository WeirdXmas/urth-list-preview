Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1999-04-13 Roy C. Lackey #

Lays out a short family tree in which Charon and Dorcas have a son Ouen, and Dorcas and Severian have a daughter Catherine, and Ouen and Catherine have Severian. Gives the lineage as a plain sequence of begettings, with no supporting argument, and notes that the formatting looked broken on the list.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Severian is the child of Ouen and Catherine

I don't know why it looks so bad on the list, though it looks okay in the digest. I'll try the hard way.

Charon + Dorcas begat Ouen. Dorcas + Severian begat Catherine.

Ouen + Catherine begat Severian.

Roy
