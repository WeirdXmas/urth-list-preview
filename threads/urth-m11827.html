Suzanne Delage: another view

Proposes that Wolfe's story 'Suzanne Delage' may be influenced by Chekhov's short story 'The Beauties', noting the sparse plot and dreamlike tone the two share. Grounds the suggestion in the plot summary of 'The Beauties' (an unnamed narrator meets two very beautiful girls) and in Wolfe's likely familiarity with Chekhov's work.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Suzanne Delage draws on Chekhov's The Beauties

Hi all,

Coincidentally, just after the recent talk about 'Suzanne Delage', I came across the Chekhov short story 'The Beauties', which I wasn't previously familiar with. The text is here:
http://www.ibiblio.org/eldritch/ac/beauties.htm

In summary: the unnamed narrator encounters a very beautiful girl; some time later, he encounters another very beautiful girl. The story is almost completely devoid of incident, and (to me) has the same slightly dreamlike tone as 'Suzanne Delage'.

I don't know if much has been said about any influence of Chekhov on Wolfe: I couldn't find anything on this list's archives, anyway. But it seems likely that, Wolfe being Wolfe, he would be well versed in the writing of the master of the modern short story.

best wishes,

Mo
