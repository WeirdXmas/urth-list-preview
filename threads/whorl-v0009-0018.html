1998-10-31 Kieran Mullen #

Jokingly argues that a historical figure like Neil Armstrong does appear in The Book of the New Sun, in one of Rudesind's paintings, answering Ranjit Bhatnagar's worry that naming French and Latin undermines the idea of an invented Whorl language. The reply is brief and playful, offering the painting as a counterexample to Bhatnagar's claim that real-world names would be hard to explain away.
chats · close-textual-reading

Of course Neil (or someone like him) *does* appear in TBotNS in one of Rudesind's paintings. :-)

Kieran Mullen

1998-11-01 Jack Lyons #

Jokingly proposes that Neil Armstrong appearing in one of Rudesind's paintings in The Book of the New Sun is another Trinity allusion, with Neil as Christ, Ultan as Father and Rudesind as Holy Spirit. Responds to Ranjit Bhatnagar's worry that French and Latin named in the books are real-world references, playing on the idea of a real historical name appearing in the text.
