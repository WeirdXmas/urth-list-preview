Mucor's parentage; Chenille

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-02-06 to 1997-02-06

1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunDave Lebling

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1997-02-06 Dave Lebling #

Disputes that Mucor is Molpe's literal child, arguing that Silk's phrase 'child of Molpe's' only means she is insane, since Molpe is patroness of the insane. Answers mantis's two cited references by likening their phrasing to 'Jerry's Kids'; also agrees with algae that Chenille is Tussah's normal daughter, citing identical facial descriptions.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Mucor is insane, not Molpe's literal child; Chenille is Tussah's normal daughter

[Mucor]

I've checked out the two references that mantis gave on Mucor's parentage, and sorry, I don't buy it. Both are in exactly the form that implies a caring or proprietary relationship, similar to referring to children with disabilities as "Jerry's Kids," due to Jerry Lewis's sponsorship of his telethon.

When Silk says Mucor is a child of Molpe's, he means that she is insane. Molpe is the patroness of the insane (being subject to insanity herself). The reference to Heirax is similar.

[Chenille]

I agree with algae (actually isn't one an _alga_?) that Chenille is the normal, straightforward daughter of Tussah. There's a passage soon after Silk gets confirmation that he is Tussah's adopted son in which Chenille's face is described in exactly the words used to describe Tussah's: "coarse features, broad flat cheeks" (more or less).
