How Does Q Know?

1997-02-27 Dave Lebling #

Proposes that Quetzal knows so much because he is in contact with other cities and runs his own spy network, and argues that the Whorl has been in Blue and Green for about a century, so Quetzal may have been aboard for most of that time. Responds to Xury's question about Quetzal's knowledge and disputes alga's view that Quetzal was aboard from the start, citing a page reference from an earlier post and a prior theory about demons.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Quetzal runs a spy network through contact with other cities; The Whorl has been in Blue and Green for about a century; Quetzal was aboard the Whorl for most of that time

Xury,

Another explanation for Quetzal knowing so much is that he's in contact with other cities, and presumably has a spy network of his own. See one of my previous posts for a page cite. Also keep in mind that alga's theory that Quetzal has been on board from the start is, uhm, controversial. I for one think the _Whorl_ has been in Blue/Green for about a hundred years, and Quetzal may have been on board for most of that time. Recall Pike's theory that demons had invaded the whorl.
