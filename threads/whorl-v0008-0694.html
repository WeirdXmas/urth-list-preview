Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-07-29 mary whalen #

Offers a list of glosses for the word and name "Nettle" from a dictionary-style run through Wolfe's names, including the plant genus Urtica, Horn's wife, the coauthor of the Book of Silk, and a newt derived from "ewte". Provides no argument; it simply lists the name's meanings and character associations as a wordplay exercise.
informs · source-and-allusion

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Nettle. Any species of prickly or stinging plants of genus Urtica or family Urticaceae. Horn's wife. Coauthor of the Book of Silk.

Newt. A small amphibious salamander, usually of genus Triturus. The name formed because of an incorrect merging of an ewte (a newte). Ewte was the animal's original English name, and it remains in the word eft. New member of the Ayuntamiento.

Nizam. Trivigaunti private who takes in Tick for a while.
