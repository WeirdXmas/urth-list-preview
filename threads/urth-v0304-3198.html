I'll second the recommendations for _Lolita_, which is a fine book. My personal favorite Nabokov novel, however, is _Pnin_. It is not the tour de force that _Pale Fire_ is, but has its own trickiness.

I've probably suggested it on the list before, but I think most Wolfe fans would also greatly enjoy Robert Irwin's _The Arabian Nightmare_.

2003-04-16 Richard Horton #

Lists Richard Horton's favourite Nabokov novels, including Pnin, Pale Fire, Ada, and The Defense, and notes that Ada and King, Queen, Knave are science fiction of an odd kind. Responds to a previous poster's recommendation of Pnin and Robert Irwin's The Arabian Nightmare by simply ranking titles and admitting he has not read The Gift.
chats

My favorite Nabokov books are _Pnin_ and _Pale Fire_, order depending on mood.

_Ada_ is another outstanding book, though, and SF (if of a decidedly odd nature). (His early Russian novel _King, Queen, Knave_ is also SF.) _Invitation to the Beheading_, _Bend Sinister_, _The Real Life of Sebastian Knight_, and _The Defense_ are also all excellent. Of his Russian novels (which include _The Defense_ and _Invitation to the Beheading_) _The Gift_ seems most highly regarded, but I haven't read it yet.
