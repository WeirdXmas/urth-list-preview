1998-02-07 C R Culver #

Reports that the passage about polychrome in The Book of the New Sun is located in The Shadow of the Torturer, in the chapter "Baldanders" or the one following it, at paragraph 14. Responds to earlier list talk that had wondered where the polychrome passage could be found, offering a locating reference rather than an interpretation.
informs

Friends,

There has been recent talk of the part of the BotNS concerning polychrome, though many have wondered where it is. I have found in in _Shadow_, the chapter called "Baldanders" or the one right after it, paragraph 14.
