Tunnels beneath the city

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-07-07 to 2002-07-07

2002urthThe Book of the Long SunDan Rabin

2002-07-07 Dan Rabin #

Reports that Chicago has abandoned tunnels under its central business district and compares them with the tunnels beneath Viron in Wolfe's Long Sun books, noting shared features like ash haulage, water, and concrete lining. Supports the comparison with links and a 1982 railroad publication, and argues Wolfe must have known of the tunnels since the 1992 flood came too late to have influenced the writing.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Wolfe drew Viron's tunnels from Chicago's abandoned tunnels

I was recently reminded that Chicago has abandoned tunnels that underly most of the central business district (read about it at <http://www.ameritech.net/users/chicagotunnel/tunnel1.html>, <http://www.irm.org/railwire/rw162a1.html>). I was struck by the similarity to Viron's tunnels, and especially by the fact that the Chicago system was used to haul away coal ash, and the Viron system for ash from sacrifices. The Chicago tunnels also have water in them to varying depths, have access to the city hall, and are lined with concrete ("shiprock"). On the other hand, the tunnels were *not* built mainly for the purpose of cooling Chicago :-).

The existence of these tunnels was (it is alleged) largely forgotten after their abandonment after World War II, but a railroad-enthusiast publication by Bruce Moffatt in 1982
<http://catalog.loc.gov/cgi-bin/Pwebrecon.cgi?v1=1&ti=1,1&Search_Arg=F orty+Feet+Below&Search_Code=TALL&PID=5424&CNT=25&SEQ=20020708002451&SI D=1> brought them to the attention of at least that audience, and they made news in 1992 when the Chicago River breached the system owing to a construction accident and flooded many basements in the Loop.

The latter incident was too late to have influenced the writing of _Long Sun_; if Wolfe had these tunnels as a model, he must have known of them beforehand.

Yours railroad-enthusiastically,

-- Dan Rabin
