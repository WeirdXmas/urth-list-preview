Typhon Era Fliers

Urth list · 3 messages · 1999-09-23 to 1999-09-24

1999urthThe Book of the New SunMitchell A. BaileyMacronaut
The thread: Readers asked whether the locust-shaped fliers of Typhon's era could be attack helicopters, given their blunt cylindrical bodies and thrumming sound. Macronaut first proposed the helicopter idea, and Mitchell A. Bailey backed it, citing Severian's locust comparison and a possible echo of Revelation's locusts from the Pit. Macronaut then offered a metaphorical reading that treats the fliers as a plague on Earth's resources, leaving the helicopter question and the Revelation allusion unresolved, with no final agreement.
  • Macronaut: the fliers could be attack helicopters, given their blunt bodies resembling locusts or grasshoppers
  • Macronaut: the locust fliers fit a human empire, represented by Typhon, as a plague ravaging Earth
  • Mitchell A. Bailey: the fliers may be helicopter-like machines, since Severian's thrumming locust comparison fits military copters
  • Mitchell A. Bailey: a tangential allusion to Revelation's locusts from the Pit may be intended, raised as a question

Themes: War and soldiers, Deep time and ruins, Myth and folklore sources

1999-09-23 Macronaut #

Proposes that the locust-shaped fliers of Typhon's era could be attack helicopters, since their blunt cylindrical bodies might resemble locusts or grasshoppers. Raises this as a first post, noting it responds to an earlier list remark contrasting those fliers with the cherry-leaf fliers of the Autarchy's time.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Locust-shaped fliers are attack helicopters

My first post, and I hope one that does not repeat an
earlier discussion!

An earlier post remarked on the difference between the
locust-like fliers in the era of Typhon and the cherry
leaf fliers in use at the time of the Autarchy. Could
the locust-shaped fliers be attack helicoptors of some
sort? -- it seems to me that the blunt cylindrical
bodies of such craft might resemble a locust or
grasshoper.

1999-09-24 Mitchell A. Bailey #

Proposes that the locust-like fliers of the Typhon era might be helicopter-like machines, citing the thrumming sound Severian compares to locusts and the poster's own experience of grasshoppers and military copters. Suggests a tangential allusion to the locusts rising from the Pit in Revelation, offered as a question about whether Wolfe intended it.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Typhon-era fliers may be attack helicopters; Wolfe alludes to locusts of Revelation

Macronaut wrote:...

I seem to recall that Sev compared their "thrumming" sound as well as looks to that of locusts. That thought occurred to me during a period when we had an unusual amount of military copter traffic around here AND the usual early autumn grasshopper invasion. The hoppers make a racket very like little 'copters.

Another thought floated up unbidden like a denizen of the Lake of Birds, who spat up its lead slingstones, even as I write: one of the final plagues in Revelation involved "locusts" which ascend from the Pit and attack people. Did Wolfe want to evoke that reference, tangentially? Make of that what you will.
