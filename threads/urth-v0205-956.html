2002-05-10 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Passes along Ursula K. Le Guin's term "story suites" for books like The Fifth Head of Cerberus and Kim Stanley Robinson's Icehenge, works that are more than story collections or fixups but not quite novels. Reports this from an online interview with Nick Gevers and says the term fits well, responding to a quoted remark that the Fifth Head of Cerberus may not be considered a novel.
