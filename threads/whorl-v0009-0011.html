Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-10-29 Charles Dye #

Lists Severian's wounds, including the leg injury, chest scar and torn cheek, and suggests a facial injury may be the key to Silk's identity. Responds to Peter Westlake's quoted remark about a razor scar and a Greg Neyman suggestion, then notes that the traffic is unusual for the Whorl list and proposes merging it into Urth.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Silk is Severian with a facial injury

Severian's wounds: lame (leg injury from battle of Orithyia); scar on chest (Agilus's avern leaf); torn cheek (Agia's brass knucks.) Hmmmm. I think some kind of facial injury for Silk is very likely.

This much traffic is very unusual for the Whorl list. Perhaps we should consider folding it into Urth?
