SS: 1000 years to Typhon

Themes: Time loops and causality

2003-02-20 Roy C. Lackey #

Argues that Silkhorn's spirit travel in the Long Sun books was spatial rather than temporal, citing a passage where Tartaros says a thousand years have passed since the ship launched. Grounds the claim in the gap between the Whorl's year 332 in Calde and the thousand years Tartaros mentions, matching the span from Typhon to Severian.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Silkhorn's spirit travel was spatial, not temporal

Apropos of nothing going on at the moment, I just wanted to shoot another hole in the bullet-riddled notion that Silkhorn's spirit travel was temporal, rather than spatial.

In chapter 5 of CALDE we learn that the year in the _Whorl_ is 332. In the following chapter Auk encounters Tartaros, who tells him, "The faces we had as mortals have rotted to dust, and the voices we once possessed have been still for a thousand years."

The computer personality of the god in Mainframe _knows_ that 1,000 years, not 332, have passed outside the ship since it was launched. I,000 years, roughly, was the span of time between Typhon and Severian. Silkhorn's spirit travel to Urth brought him to Sev's era, not Typhon's. Q.E.D., Silkhorn _did not_ travel through time.

-Roy
