2000urthThe Book of the New SunJim Russell

2000-07-11 Jim Russell #

Argues that Vodalus and Jolenta are both assigned roles, since both are deposed and replaced, so their selfhood is fixed by a formal structure set by the Increate, the Autarch or the author. Replies to a question about their connection and closes by pointing readers to M. John Harrison's Viriconium books as similar to the New Sun, though bleaker.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Vodalus and Jolenta are alloted roles within a formal structure

Both Vodalus and Jolenta are playing alloted roles, at
least as I see it. That they are both deposed (In one sense or another), and replaced, indicates that "what they are" to paraphrase Sev, is locked within a certain formal
structure which dictates their selfhood. This could be the work of the increate, the autarch, or the author, depending on your viewpoint.

On a seperate matter, has anybody encountered M. John
Harrison's 'Viriconium' books, which have recently been
republished on the masterworks list in the UK? They contain certain similarities to the New Sun Books, although they are far bleaker, and might be of interest to some of you dudes.

Ta ta...

Jim Russell
University of Exeter
