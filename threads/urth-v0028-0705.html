Proposes that Talos's sarcastic 'Where's your friend?' alludes to Severian's sword Terminus Est, implying that Severian offers life while carrying death. Offers a second reading linking the line to Jonas, and judges that alternative a weaker fit for the scene.
proposes · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Talos's 'Where's your friend?' refers to Terminus Est

Could this be a reference to _Terminus Est_? Is Talos saying "If you're going to give him life, why do you have your sword with you?" Another possibility, if he really is referring to Jonas, is "You brought death upon Jonas, in spite of your intentions, and you will do the same to Baldanders." This alternative doesn't seem to me to fit as well.

Dave Lebling
