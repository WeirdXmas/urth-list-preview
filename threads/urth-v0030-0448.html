Ram's Horns Blowing in the Wind

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-05-25 to 2001-05-25

2001urthMartin Light

2001-05-25 Martin Light #

Jokingly proposes that the closing phrase of a Wolfe story, where a mountain ram 'winded its horns,' refers to a Dodge Ram pickup's horn rather than the animal. Responds to a poster who objected that no animal can blow a horn, playing on the Texas and Aggie references in that objection.
