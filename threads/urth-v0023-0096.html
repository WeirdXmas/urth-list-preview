1999-02-25 Kieran Cleary #

Argues that the 'Green = Luna' hypothesis is at an impasse and bets that Green is Lune, the moon, within the Green/Blue system. Responds to Dan Parmenter's question on its status by arguing that Merryn and Sev can only appear in that system if Green is Lune, and calling the Pas=Typhon link arbitrary by itself.
supports · puzzle-solving · myth-and-structure · theories: Green is Luna; Green is Lune, the moon; Merryn and Sev appear in the Green/Blue system

One word: impasse. I've always been fond of that particular hypothesis, but some recalcitrants on the list just won't have it. And I'm going to push the boat out a little: I betya Green=Lune. For one thing, not to impose my lack of imagination on Wolfe, but I can't see how Merryn and Sev are going to do a cameo in the Green/Blue system otherwise. And link to the BotNS (Pas=Typhon, so what?) would be slightly arbitrary if that's all there was to it.

We'll just have to wait and see.
