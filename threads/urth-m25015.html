The thread: The thread asked what Remora's Prochain Ami is and who the Neighbours and Inhumi are, moving from Andrew Mason's idea that Remora is a Neighbour in a human body toward wider questions about the Whorl's origins. Lee Berman and Gerry Quinn disputed whether Neighbours came from Green or Blue, and Marc Aramini pressed a series of increasingly sweeping proposals: vanished gods as trees, a biological cycle of hybridization, and Green as Urth itself. Andrew Mason pushed back on several of these, and David Stockhoff asked what Blue is, which drew a brief reply from António Pedro Marques and an unusual Green-is-Lune theory from Andreas Johansson. The thread ended without consensus, with most ideas left unsupported by detailed textual evidence.
  • Andrew Mason: Remora may be a Neighbour in a human body; Neighbours originated on Blue and settled Green
  • Lee Berman: Neighbours came aboard infected from Green and may mingle with humans; Inhumi could evolve toward angelic beings
  • Gerry Quinn: Neighbours may have contacted MainFrame or chosen individuals; the launch planet may be the industrial one
  • Marc Aramini: Trees are vanished gods; hybrids form by being eaten by trees; Green is Urth; Silk imagines a pre-corruption past
  • Andreas Johansson: Green world is really Lune and Blue is Urth after the flood
  • António Pedro Marques: Insists the line is 'Urth is Green' and that word order is significant

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Beasts and the animal in us, Myth and folklore sources

2013-09-27 Andrew Mason #

Proposes that Remora, the Prochain Ami in Short Sun's Remora, may be a Neighbour possessing a human body, and that this would mean the Neighbours support Quetzal's decision to send humans to Green. Grounds the idea in Windcloud's trial remark about Neighbours who knew many humans, Horn's suspicion that Remora knows more than he admitted, Remora's position watching Quetzal, and the odd title 'Prochain Ami' meaning 'neighbour'.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Remora is a Neighbour possessing a human body; Neighbours confirm Quetzal's decision to send humans to Green

OK, this one is very speculative, and may seem round the bend. In the trial scene in Dorp, Windcloud says ?I was one of those who boarded your whorl when it neared our sun. In the Whorl, I made the acquaintance of many of your race.? This has given rise to speculation that some people we know from _Long Sun_ may be Neighbours.

There doesn?t seem to be any suggestion that Neighbours can change shape, like inhumi, so if they are manifesting as humans it is probably by projecting their spirits into humans in a way akin to possession. We know that they were able to convey Horn?s spirit into a new body, so it is likely that they are able to do the same with their own spirits.

This got me wondering about Remora. There are three interesting facts about him.

a. He is well placed to observe Quetzal, and one of the reasons Neighbours are on the Whorl is to observe humanoid inhumi.

b. Horn says, when talking to the Neighbours, that he suspects Remora knows more about them than he had previously realised.

c. When Remora is first introduced he is referred to by a strange title ? the Prochain Ami ? which even becomes the title of a chapter. This is said to refer to his status in relation to the Prolocutor, but it is not used again. And why is anyone in Viron, whose heritage seems to be Latin American, using French? But one possible translation of ?prochain? is ?neighbour?.

So, might Remora be a Neighbour (without ceasing to be human)? One interesting consequence of this would be that we should see the Neighbours as confirming Quetzal?s decision that humans should go to Green (which means that there must be more to it than his just wanting humans to be eaten).

2013-10-04 Lee Berman replying to Andrew Mason #

Argues that Remora is unlikely to be a straightforward Inhumu like Quetzal, and that Windcloud's remark about boarding the Whorl points to Neighbors who came aboard already infected from Green. Builds on Andrew Mason's observations, citing Horn's sighting of the multi-limbed Neighbors and the idea that Neighbors rank above humans while Inhumi rank below.
disputes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Remora is not a straight Inhumu like Quetzal; Neighbors boarded the Whorl already infected from Green

Andrew, I think you make good observations and arguments against Remora being a straight-up Inhumu, like Quetzal.

Regarding Windcloud's statement, I think we can be pretty sure the Neighbors weren't galloping around the Whorl in their multi-limbed original form, as seen by Horn. So I always took Windcloud's statement as being related in someway to the mystery regarding the Inhumi on the Whorl and how they got there.

We aren't given the details, but surely any Neighbors who got onto the Whorl did so from Green, not Blue, meaning they were Neighbors who had already been infected. Or intertwined (as in trees and vines).

I think we are supposed to get the sense that Neighbors are a higher order of being than humans. And Inhumi, despite their cunning, are a lower order. So perhaps a combination of the two is a good blend to walk among the middle ground of humanity.

2013-10-04 Gerry Quinn replying to Lee Berman #

Questions whether the Neighbors reaching the Whorl must have come from Green rather than Blue, and whether the Inhumi could be said to infect them at all. Responds to Lee Berman's argument by suggesting the Neighbors may have contacted MainFrame or selected individuals, and that the launch planet could be the one with the larger industrial base.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Neighbors contacted MainFrame or selected individuals; Neighbors launched from planet with greater industry

From: Lee Berman

It's possible that they were in contact with elements of MainFrame. Or that they lurked in the tunnels and contacted only selected individuals.

Where are we told that anyone becomes 'infected' by Inhumi?

As for where they launched from, it's seems reasonable to hypothesise that they collected immature Inhumi from Green, but I don't know why that would imply that they could not have come originally from Blue. Why not just assume that the expedition was most likely launched from whichever planet had the greater industrial complex?

- Gerry Quinn

Argues that the Neighbours in the Short Sun books originated on Blue and had travelled to Green long before the events of the story, with Green's city built by Neighbours. Grounds this in Windcloud's own account that he lived in Inclito's house on Blue before the Rajan came to stay there, and the house's core being an old Neighbour structure.
supports · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Neighbours originated on Blue and travelled to Green

That seems to be the story accepted by the characters: The Neighbours originated on Blue, and are indeed akin to other creatures there. But they had travelled to Green, and met the inhumi there, long before the events of the story. I believe the city on Green was built by Neighbours.

Of Windcloud, however, we do know that he had previously lived on Blue, since he reveals that he was the original occupant of Inclito's house - he says that the Rajan came to stay in his house, being invited by a man who was living there (and we are indeed told that the core of Inclito's house was an old Neighbour structure).

2013-10-07 Lee Berman replying to Andrew Mason #

Argues that the Neighbors, like the Inhumi, may have mingled with humans on the Whorl, and speculates that Inhumi-types could evolve or hybridize into angelic beings, linking Tzadkiel, Barbatus and Famulimas to aquatic beings. Supports this by citing Windcloud's remark about meeting many of her race, and by contrasting the Neighbors' keeping to themselves with the Inhumi's dispersal, then conceding the author's own view of higher and lower beings.
proposes · close-textual-reading · allegory-and-typology · theories: Neighbors wandered the Whorl like the Inhumi; Inhumi-types could hybridize into angelic beings; Humans are baser animals resisting Inhumi infection

What Windcloud says is: "In the Whorl, I made the acquaintance of *many* of your race.?" This would not seem to be the way one would describe electronically wandering around Mainframe or lurking in the tunnels. I think it implies Windcloud wandered around places like Viron, connecting with the regular sorts of Whorl residents we know best.

Moreover, we know that Inhumi have infiltrated the Whorl and walked among its residents incognito, I expect the revelation that Neighbors were also on the Whorl to follow a similar pattern rather than have it be a random and purposeless event.

I agree. Such dispersion is to be expected from a species whose niche is to imitate other species and hitchhike. Like weeds on earth, their very essence is to be able to disperse everywhere and grow anywhere.

So who/what/where on Urth do we find shapeshifters.....? This has been hotly debated on this board in the past. There are hints of shapeshifters on Urth to be found with Mother Pyrexia, the angel in Melito's story, the Armiger's Daughter/lark story, and perhaps others. But even the most stringent skeptics agree that Tzadkiel is a shape-shifting example which cannot be denied.

So, is it possible Inhumi-types could somehow evolve (or hybridize?) into mighty angelic beings? The hints of an aquatic origin for Tzadkiel and Barbatus and Famulimas seem to hint at a connection to such beings as Abaia, Erebus, Scylla, the Mother, etc. Is Apheta's insectoid nature somehow related?

My interpretation is that humans are baser animals than Neigbhors and are thus more equipped to resist infection by Inhumi. SilkHorn beats Jahlee to death. Other groups bury the Inhumi alive etc. Perhaps The Neighbors were unable to perpetrate such violent atrocities against their parasitic enemies and were forced to escape to another plane of existence. They were unable to deal with the Inhumi here in the material world, as humans are at least partially able to do.

Good point. But is there any indication that Neighbors have a lifespan? If he is more or less immortal, couldn't Windcloud have originally lived in Inclito's place then gone to Green and become entwined with the parasitic Inhumi? Getting onto the Whorl and being able to walk among and interact with humans seems so completely Inhumi-like. My sense is that the original Neighbors prefer to keep to themselves. Horn has to go to their camp, not vice versa. (Remaining above vs. mixing it up with the humans and their lives. I think there is a God/Demiurge/Devil analogy in there somewhere).

Well, I think that is a good, objective, agnostic sort of analysis that I would tend to share, personally.

But in his writing and also in interviews, Gene Wolfe seems to express a personal belief in higher and lower beings. so I have to interpret his work in light of these apparent intentions.

Proposes a full biological scheme for the inhuma, vanished people, liana vines and tree-gods on the Whorl, in which creatures are hybridized by being eaten by trees. Builds the case from the doubled limbs of bluvian life, the sailor shot into the sea, the blood-drinking inhuma, and Horn falling into a pit that may be a giant herb's maw.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Trees are vanished gods that hybridize creatures by eating them; Inhuma and vanished people arise through consumption convergence; Horn is made after falling into a giant herb's maw

Just wanted to clarify my biological scheme for inhuma and neighbors one more time in light of the tree on the whorl being called a "parent".

The trees are vanished gods, different from vanished people in that they haven't gone anywhere but they "create " all multilimbed creatures by "eating" creatures and spitting out stronger hybrids, symbolized by the doubled limbs of bluvian life, which was once not doubled and had half the chromosomes.

Thus the vanished people are all that was left of humanity after they were eaten and hybridized, and they left.

Liana vines parasitized the mystical life of the god-trees but failed to attain it fully. They drink blood but revert to their primitive form. Reptilian victims breed reptilian generations of inhuma, human victims breed sentient inhuma.

The multilimbed creature rising up from the sea might not be ancient. A sailor is shot and falls into the ocean. Is that genetic material enough to create a new hybrid with the giant herbs that are actually the islands on blue? However, perhaps it is ancient, with all the accumulation of detritus and rot, unless that is a feature of the submerged god.

It is a morlock-Eloi scheme- both inhuma and vanished people are simultaneously convergent and divergent evolution through consumption. Both are vegetable and human to achieve sentience, but the inhuma are a more sterile and imitative combination, not a true hybrid, who must always drink of the blood, but do not have life within them eternal.

The neighbor Horn is only made after Horn falls into the pit, which can be inferred to be the Maw of one of the giant herbs that make up the islands on blue.

Mechanism is missing. I was looking for a way that different time could pass between rigoglio leaving urth and showing up midstream at sev's time, but it wasnt there because wolfe pulled the bait and switch. Blue is colder than Urth. Green is hotter. When silk shows up on urth, he was imagining a time before green's corruption; that is the mechanism. The city of the inhumi is Nessus. Wolfe meant to obfuscate who, what, when, why, and where.
