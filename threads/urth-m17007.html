Neighbours in the Whorl

Urth list · 3 messages · 2011-02-09 to 2011-02-09

2011urthThe Book of the Long SunAshley CrillJames WynnAndrew Mason
The thread: Thread asks how the Neighbours observe and act on the Whorl, with the question of whether they need ships or physical presence. Andrew Mason proposes they are disguised as humans or fused with human hosts, able to move spirits between bodies, grounded in their interest in observing humanoid inhumi. James Wynn supports this, citing their advance knowledge of Silk's body becoming available. Ashley Crill offers an alternative: they watch through the Sacred Windows and Glasses, which explains how they saw Silk mourning and dying, and asks whether this is astral projection or needs a physical presence. The thread ends with three competing mechanisms and no resolution.
  • Andrew Mason: Neighbours are disguised as humans or fused with hosts, moving spirits between bodies
  • James Wynn: Neighbours are actively present on the Whorl without ships or breaking the airlock, supporting Mason
  • Ashley Crill: Neighbours watch through the Sacred Windows and Glasses, possibly via Mucor's travel between whorls

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Ghosts, dreams and visions, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

2011-02-09 Andrew Mason #

Argues that the Neighbours in the Whorl are disguised as humans or fused with human hosts, perhaps like possession, and could move their own spirits into new bodies. Grounds this in the Neighbours' aim of observing humanoid inhumi, suggesting one should be in Viron watching Quetzal, and in the Whorl's ability to transfer spirits between bodies.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Neighbours disguise themselves as humans in the Whorl; Neighbours can fuse with or possess human hosts; Neighbours can transfer their own spirits into new bodies

One of the aims of the Neighbours was to observe humanoid inhumi, so you would expect them to be where the inhumi were; there ought to be at least one of them in Viron to keep watch on Quetzal.

It seems likely to me that the Neigbours in the Whorl are disguised as humans (or indeed fused with humans, in a way akin to possession). If they can transport someone else's spirit into a new body, it's not improbable that they can also transport their own spirits into new bodies.

2011-02-09 Ashley Crill replying to Andrew Mason #

Proposes that the Neighbors watch the Whorl through the Sacred Windows and Glasses, which would explain how they saw Silk mourning and dying inside the manteion. Grounds this on Mucor's apparent ability to travel between whorls, and wonders whether the watching is astral projection or requires a physical Neighbor present.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Neighbors watch the Whorl through Sacred Windows and Glasses

I always thought that the Neighbors spy on the whorl through use of the Sacred Windows and Glasses. This would explain how they saw Silk mourning and dieing inside the manteion. I'm not sure if they can do this through 'digital-astral projection' or if an actual Neighbor would to be present on the Whorl. Mucor seems able to travel between whorls in this fashion so i assume the Neighbors can also do so.
