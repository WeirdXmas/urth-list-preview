Themes: Free will and choice, Hidden identities and parentage

2000-04-20 Nicholas Gevers #

Adds to the thread on Swallow as a god by pointing out that Chenille is a fifth visitor alongside the others, and contrasts her with them. Argues she was designed to be the Calde's heir yet escapes that destiny because she is a woman unaware of her father's identity, so she lacks any programming to follow.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Chenille was designed as the Calde's heir

I should have said in my previous post that Swallow
has FIVE visitors--Chenille is also present, of
course. And she's unlike the others in being a person
who was naturally "designed" to be the Calde's heir,
but free from any such burden of destiny because she
was a woman and ignorant of her father's identity. A
perhaps significant contrast--she has no programming
to follow.
