2002-11-17 Adam Stephanides #

Asks whether list members know anything about John Crowley's reported sale of a book titled Lucifer: Lord Byron's Undiscovered Novel to William Morrow, noting it does not sound like the fourth Aegypt book. Gives no textual argument; the post cites only a report in the most recent Locus magazine as its source.
questions

I read in the most recent LOCUS that Crowley has sold a book entitled LUCIFER: LORD BYRON'S UNDISCOVERED NOVEL to William Morrow. Anybody know anything about this? It doesn't sound like the fourth book in the Aegypt series.

--Adam

2002-11-17 Alice Turner #

Relays John Crowley's own news that his fourth Aegypt volume is titled Endless Things and that a Byron novel is planned after a publisher's check arrives, asking readers to keep it quiet. Quotes Crowley's message directly and mentions a story in Conjunctions, with no discussion of Wolfe's fiction.
informs

From Crowley. And please honor his "don't tell," those of you connected with the press etc.:

My news is that the fourht volume of Aegypt will be done by year's end. It has a title now: it's called Endless Things.

Congratulations on that, and I look forward! But rumor has it that you have also completed something on Byron.

Nope. When I get a check from Morrow I will begin on something about Byron. A novel. It's called at the moment Lord Byron's Novel, which it will contain: all of that wonderful funny romantic Gothic wild ironic adventure he never wrote but should have. Don't tell yet though. What's new for me is a story called The Girlhood of Shakespeare's Heroines, in a magzien called Conjunctions, guest-edited by Peter Straub -- but you probably know all this.

-alga
