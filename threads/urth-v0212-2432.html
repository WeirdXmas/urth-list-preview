I recant. but I wasn't REALLY wrong

Urth list · 5 messages · 2002-12-16 to 2002-12-17

2002urthThe Book of the New SunWilliam H. AnsleyNigel PriceMarc AraminiTami WhiteheadDon Doggett
The thread: Participants revisited an earlier claim that Urth is not Blue in the Short Sun books, after reports that Gene Wolfe had weighed in. Maa32 recanted but noted a valid reason for Urth not being Blue, and mocked Marc Aramini's stance with teasing jokes. Don Doggett also recanted yet insisted the original position was not wholly wrong, proposing Blue is Lune and Green is Urth while admitting he could not justify or explain it. Tami Whitehead playfully suggested Marc's distress came from the green man in The Shadow of the Torturer, and Nigel Price pleaded for Wolfe's private messages about the Short Sun books. The thread was light and jokey, ending without a settled answer on the color question.

2002-12-16 Marc Aramini #

Recants an earlier claim about whether Urth is Blue in the Short Sun books, saying Wolfe has weighed in and that a valid reason for Urth not being Blue exists. Reports that Urth in The Book of the New Sun has sentient, possibly cannibal trees, while mocking Marc Aramini's earlier stance with a jokey, teasing tone.
informs · puzzle-solving · theories: Urth is not Blue in Short Sun

Mr. Wolfe has weighed in. I don't know if I can even post about short sun anymore.
I know the truth now :(

Gene Wolfe is far trickier than any of us could ever possibly imagine. Ever. No one was right - all your reasons for Urth not being Blue AREN'T valid ones. There IS one valid reason it probably isn't Blue. I don't know what to do.

In regards to Urth in the old New Sun book, there ARE sentient trees on it! :) Maybe even cannibal trees. That's all I'm going to say. Good fishing. Holy crap. How could one man be so damned brilliant?!!! Merry Christmas, Marc Aramini. Be careful what you ask for - you might get it. :( Just remember that I wasn't really, really wrong. Just kind of. But not really. I don't know what to tell you. If anyone else comes up with the "solution" of the text independently, I will start posting again about Short Sun.
Marc Aramini

proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Blue is Lune and Green is Urth
