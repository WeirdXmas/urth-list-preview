Jokingly argues that uncondensed gas clouds deserve the same 'fortunate' status as stars, playfully suggesting Jonas's ship be called the Gravitationally Disadvantaged Cloud. Then offers a factual aside that quasars are compacted chunks of galaxies, super-compact yet solar-system sized, responding to Rostrum's question about star formation in a Long Sun-related thread.
