What meaning does the family tree lend to the story itself?

Urth list · 3 messages · 2006-08-25 to 2006-08-27

2006urthThe Book of the New SunJeff WilsonJonathon McKitrickBob Miller
The thread: Jonathon McKitrick asked what extra meaning Severian's family tree adds once it is clear he becomes the New Sun, and doubted its true meaning could be known without Wolfe's confirmation. Jeff Wilson answered that a hidden relation might someday make the tree's meaning obvious in retrospect, though he offered only speculation. Bob Miller argued the tree's fog is deliberate design rather than a puzzle to be solved, and repeated the question of what it adds. The thread ended without resolution, with the debate left open on whether the tree's ambiguity is meaningful or unanswerable.
  • Jonathon McKitrick: the family tree's meaning may be unknowable without Wolfe's confirmation
  • Jeff Wilson: a hidden relation could be found and its meaning would seem obvious in retrospect
  • Bob Miller: the tree's inscrutable details are deliberate design, not flaws to solve

Themes: Fathers, mothers and children, Names and their meanings

2006-08-25 Jonathon McKitrick #

Asks what additional meaning can be drawn from understanding Severian's entire family tree once one grasps that he becomes the New Sun and saves Urth. Notes that the tree seems impossible to resolve without Wolfe's own confirmation, so its true meaning may never be known.
questions · puzzle-solving

I'm still at the point of figuring out the time traveling, the multiple Sev's and so on, but once you understand that Sev becomes the "New Sun" and saves Urth, what additional meaning is there to be extracted by understanding his entire family tree? And since that really cannot be done, apparently, without confirmation from Mr. Wolfe himself, how can the true meaning of an inscrutable family tree ever be known?

Jonathon McKitrick

2006-08-26 Jeff Wilson replying to Jonathon McKitrick #

Argues that the family tree of Severian's line may not be inscrutable and that some new meaning could emerge once a hidden relation is found, which would be obvious only in retrospect. Responds to Jonathon McKitrick's question about how the true meaning of an unclear family tree can be known without Wolfe's confirmation, offering a brief, speculative reply rather than specific evidence.
answers · puzzle-solving

It may not be inscrutable; hopefully, some new meaning emerges that is obvious in retrospect if X and Y are related by Z, but wouldn't otherwise.

2006-08-27 Bob Miller replying to Jonathon McKitrick #

Proposes that the foggy, inscrutable details of Sev's family tree are part of Wolfe's meaningful design rather than flaws to be solved. Responds to Jonathon McKitrick by asking what further meaning the tree adds once Sev is known to become the New Sun, and how its true meaning could be known without Wolfe's confirmation.
proposes · theories: Inscrutable family tree is part of the design

While we all have fun niggling at the detailsand trying to read the mind of someone we, frequently, have not met, it often appears that part of the meaning IS in its being inscrutable, in the fact that this "fogginess"?, lack of clarity in and of themselves give us points of reference on the "map" of the author's meaningful design. Possibly, because similar issues--the exact date of death, exact spelling of grandfather's name, which county born in--have embroiled many academics for thousands of pages and their readers for thousands of doses of dramamine.
