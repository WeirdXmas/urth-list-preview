Notes that the Rajan in On Blue's Waters says year length varies across cities' calendars, which complicates mantis's calculations of when Quetzal reached the Whorl. Adds this caveat to mantis's quoted post on the Whorl's flight time of about 200 years, offering no new figure of its own.
supports · chronology-and-plot-logic

The Rajan also mentions that the length of year varies across different cities' calendars, making his (and mantis's) calculations slightly harder.
