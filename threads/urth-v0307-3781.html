Where to get your Crowley...

2003-07-28 James Wynn #

Recommends AbeBooks as a source for buying used books at reasonable prices, citing copies of John Crowley's Little, Big in paperback and first edition and his Three Novels. Gives price ranges and notes that shipping is excluded, with a sign-off disclaiming any connection to the site or to Crowley's work.
informs

Best place I've found to buy books a reasonable (or better) price: http://www.abebooks.com

Here one can buy copy of Little, Big in pb (very good condition) for $3.75 or a first edition hardback with dust jacket for $600, and 186 choices between.

Or you can buy his "Three Novels" for $16 in Very Fine 6x9" pb or pay as little as $5 in 7x9 pb.
(all stated costs do not include shipping)

Very rarely can I not find a book I'm looking for at this site.

-- Crush (who is not employed by abebooks.com, nor has he read anything by Crowley)
