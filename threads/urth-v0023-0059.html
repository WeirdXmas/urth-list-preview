1999-02-10 Kieran Mullen #

Proposes that the story's rule of three and its stops for passing vehicles recall the Prodigal Son, with the cattle and garbage offers echoing the son's hunger among the pigs. Grounds this in the quoted passage's sequence of cattle truck, garbage truck and old bus, and notes that the Good Samaritan's three passers-by do not match the pattern.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Little Tib's sequence echoes the Prodigal Son

Well, it's got the standard storytelling structure-of-three. The first thing it made me think of was the Prodigal Son, who was willing to eat the garbage, the food of the ... oops. Not cattle, but pigs. It doesn't fit the Good Samaritan story either (three people passing by).

Kieran Mullen
