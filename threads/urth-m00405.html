2005-04-17 Jeff Wilson #

Reports that Borges and his story "Tlon, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius" were mentioned on an episode of the television drama The L Word, where a demanding teacher assigns students to create a sequel in another medium. Gives the air date and network, offering the mention as an example of Borges appearing in popular media rather than as a comment on Wolfe.

Borges and his "Tlon, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius" were mentioned on last Sunday's (4/10/05) "The L Word" television drama on Showtime in the US, in particular as part of a curriculum where a notoriously demanding teacher assignes the creation of a sequel to it in another medium, IIRC.
