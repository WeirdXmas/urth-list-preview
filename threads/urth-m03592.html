2007-12-17 Ashley Crill #

Argues that Typhon used the Whorl as a resurrection scheme and meant to download himself into Silk's embryo, which would explain Silk's strength, charisma and rapid healing. Doubts that Typhon planned a return to Earth, suggesting instead a new galactic empire founded on a suitable planet. Builds on the earlier poster's account of Typhon's recolonization plan.
supports · myth-and-structure · theories: Silk's embryo was Typhon's chosen body; Typhon planned a new galactic empire, not Earth return

This is basically how I understand the Whorl. Typhon was hedging his bets, and using the Whorl as a resurrection scheme. He did intend to be downloaded into a human body at some point and the Silk embryo was his chosen body, hence Silk's great strength, charisma, and rapid healing powers. I'm not too sure that he intended a return to Earth however, perhaps he anticipated starting a new galactic empire from a suitable planet.
