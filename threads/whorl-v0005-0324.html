1997-06-04 Alice Turner #

Proposes that the Short Sun trilogy covers the twenty years after Book of the Long Sun ends, beginning when Horn is asked to return to the Whorl to bring Silk to Blue. Reads the timeline as continuous rather than starting twenty years later, suggesting Silk presumably stays aboard the Whorl or that the colonists believe he does.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Short Sun covers the twenty years after Long Sun

Just read the transcript, and it makes me fret for the answers to our questions! But I did want to pick out one reply for clarification

[paste, re Short Sun trilogy]
It will deal with the colonists, and will take up about twenty years after the ending of "Book of the Long Sun," when
Horn is asked to return to the Whorl to bring Silk to the colonized world of Blue.

I read that not as starting 20 years later but as the story of the next 20 years, during which Silk presumably stays aboard the Whorl, or at least the colonists think he does.

-alga-
