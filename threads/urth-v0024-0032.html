1999-03-02 Tony Ellis #

Argues that the couple seen in the jungle greenhouse cannot be Robert Louis Stevenson and Marie, since the overhead plane they see postdates Stevenson's 1894 death. Responds to John Bishop's suggestion by pointing to that anachronism, while wondering whether Robert stands for some other real person and endorsing Tony's theory.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · source-and-allusion · theories: Robert in the greenhouse is Robert Louis Stevenson

Great idea, but... the couple see a plane (or what they interpret as a plane, it could be a flyer) passing over head. RLS died in 1894, long before the days of powered flight.

But you've got me wondering if Robert is supposed to be some other real person.

Seconded. I prefer Tony's theory too <g>.
