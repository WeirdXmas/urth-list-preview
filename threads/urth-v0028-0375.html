Planaria and the consumption of brains

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-11-04 to 1999-11-04

1999urthDavid Duffy

Themes: Memory and forgetting

1999-11-04 David Duffy #

Argues that eating brains could be a plausible scientific fiction for transferring memory, citing reported new neurons in the human brain and earlier planarian memory-transfer experiments. Adds science fiction antecedents for the alzabo's brain-eating habit, naming a giant amoeba story and Greg Bear's Blood Music.
supports · source-and-allusion · theories: Eating brains transfers memories, as in the alzabo

You shouldn't be completely dismissive of this. It was replicated up as high as cats (trooly rooly), but the effects were never really big. Just recently they've found there are a few hundred new neurons appearing in the human brain every day (maybe via the diet;)): it's been suggested they might represent laying down of new memories. So it can definitely be used as a scientific fiction to explain how eating your enemies' or relatives' brains works after all.

WRT SF antecedents, Damon Knight has a giant amoeba with a similar habit to the alzabo. Riffing on race memory, Greg Bear has DNA, (or was it microtubule based computers?), storing all one's ancestors' experiences in _Blood Music_.

David Duffy.
