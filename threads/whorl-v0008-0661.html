Who is Auk's mother?

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1998-07-12 to 1998-07-12

1998whorlThe Book of the Long SunBrownes3

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-07-12 Brownes3 #

Proposes that Auk in The Book of the Long Sun is an embryo like Chenille and Silk, and asks other readers for further clues to his parentage. Grounds the theory in Auk's staying power when injured, his engineering theophany, and his leadership of others toward Blue, and cites his memory of a mother named Lilly and the secret of his birth noted near Nightside the Long Sun page 35.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Auk is an embryo like Chenille and Silk; Blood might be Auk's father; Auk's mother is named Lilly

As I re-read BotLS, I tried to pick out references to Auk's parentage. Around page 35 or so of Nightside, the sibs are talking about Auk and mention his parentage is a secret, much like Blood's. I originally thought Blood might be Auk's father, but there were no further clues along that line, and the secret of Auk's birth never comes again.

While he's with Tartaros, he recalls his mother and remembers her name is "Lilly" -- Truth. but that's about all I could find regarding clues.

My theory is he's a an embryo like Chenille and Silk. He seems to have their "staying power" when injured and he's picked out for the "engineering" theophany that lets him repair equipment.

Also he's a born leader, able to get others to follow him to Blue.

Any other thoughts?

Rich Browne
