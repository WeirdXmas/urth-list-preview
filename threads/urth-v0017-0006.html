Asks for a volunteer to act as guest moderator for the Urth and Whorl lists during a two-week vacation, mainly to unsubscribe accidental floods. Also notes the web account is nearly full and that heated arguments could fill the archives before he returns.

I need someone to take care of the Urth and Whorl mailing lists while I'm on vacation for two weeks. The only real responsibility is to unsubscribe anyone who accidentally floods the mailing list due to misconfigured email software. Any volunteers?

Also, I have just about one megabyte left in my web account. You folks have been writing at the rate of about a third of a megabyte every week lately, so things should be ok while I'm away. Just don't let the arguments get too heated, or the web archives will fill up before I get back.

Ranjit
