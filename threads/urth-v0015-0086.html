Severian as the Conciliator

1998-07-03 J. Schultz #

Argues that Severian is the Conciliator because he is the New Sun, and that the New Sun is the Conciliator returning to Urth. Builds the case from the Undines, Ash's disappearance, Severian's identity as Apu-Punchau, and Urth theology in The Book of the New Sun, plus Severian's musing on the Green Man's time travel.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theology-and-religion · theories: Severian is the Conciliator; Severian is the New Sun; The New Sun is the Conciliator returning to Urth

Addressing the hints that Sev. is the Conciliator found in the BotNS

Apology in advance, I am writing this quickly late at night....

While their arn't very many hints that Sev is the Con, their are several that he is the New Sun. I will not go into all of them, as most have been addressed in previous discussions. The invovement of the Undines, the disapearance of Ash, his identity as Apu-Paunchu, etc, all point to the fact that Sev IS the New Sun.
Fact Two: The New Sun is the Conciliator returning to Urth. In the BofNS the text makes it clear that according to Urth theology the Conciliator came to Urth to bring about a conciliation between humans and the powers that be. Having done this he will later return to Urth to bring about its renewal. Few people, including Sev, think that the New Sun is ACTUALLY a sun. Although it turns out that it is both a man and a star.
Using logic we deduce that Sev is both the New Sun and Conciliator. How could this be possible? Sev. tells us while pondering the time travel of the Green Man that the Concilliator (who is so powerful) must also have time at his command. Sev deduces that this is how the New Sun is really the Conciliator come again. And it turns out he's right.

FunkMonkey
