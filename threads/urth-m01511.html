Solar Labyrinth

Urth list · 5 messages · 2005-11-04 to 2005-11-06

2005urthThe Book of the New SunStanisław BocianLos Arboles Del FriopthwndxrclzpBroken Fiction
The thread: Posters debated whether Solar Labyrinth's text can be accessed through Google Print's new online library, a question raised after Los Arboles Del Frio announced the listing and noted an authors' association lawsuit against Google. Pthwndxrclzp and Broken Fiction both corrected the earlier claim, saying only the foreword and covers are shown in full, but that keyword searches expose individual matching pages. Los Arboles Del Frio and Stanis³aw Bocian described a workaround in which searching a term such as 'fechin' opens pages, with only about five readable per search. The thread ended as a practical how-to exchange rather than an interpretive discussion.
  • Pthwndxrclzp: only the foreword and covers of Solar Labyrinth are visible on Google Print
  • Broken Fiction: keyword searches make the whole text reachable page by page
  • Los Arboles Del Frio: searching inside the book reveals pages, a few at a time
  • Stanis³aw Bocian: search a term like 'fechin' or a page number to read about five pages per search

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2005-11-04 Broken Fiction replying to pthwndxrclzp #

Corrects a earlier claim by explaining that Google Print lets users search every keyword in a book even though full text cannot be read cover to cover. Gives the example of searching for "Catherine" to show how each matching page is reachable, so the whole text is effectively available in searchable form.
informs

You can *search* on any keyword in the book through Google Print. The full text is not available to be read cover to cover, but if you search on the word "Catherine", for instance, you have access to all of the pages with that keyword. In this way the whole book is posted, but only searchable.

2005-11-04 Los Arboles Del Frio replying to pthwndxrclzp #

Reports that a Google Print search inside Solar Labyrinth can reveal individual pages of the book despite the limited preview, though only a few pages can be read before another search is needed. Responds to Aq's claim that the book's text is not available through Google Print, offering a search term ('fechin') as the working example.
disputes

Not really. If you search within the book you can get to the rest of it. For instance, with the search 'fechin' I get this results page: http://print.google.com/print?q=fechin&ie=UTF-8&prev=http%3A%2F%2Fprint.google.com%2Fprint%3Fq%3Dgene%2Bwolfe&id=vFMA1iULux0C And from there you can access the different pages.
The problem is you can only read five pages before you need to do another search (for the following page, hopefully).
LAdF
