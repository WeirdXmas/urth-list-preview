The thread: Marc Aramini raised the opening line 'someone comes to town, someone leaves town' as a possible key to the mountain-and-laundress passage in An Evil Guest, while admitting he had not read the book. Dave Tallman argued the passage echoes Cory Doctorow's novel of the same title, said this settles the laundress mystery, and then proposed that Margaret is a werewolf and spy. Gwern Branwen agreed the Doctorow echo was real, saying readers recognised it before any rumbling was mentioned, and noted that Aramini's list of matching details was not needed to make the connection. The thread ended in agreement on the Doctorow link, and these posts contain no reply to the Margaret proposal.

2008-10-20 Dave Tallman replying to Marc Aramini #

Argues that Alan's washing-machine mother and mountain father in Wolfe's Evil Guest blurb point to Cory Doctorow's novel Someone Comes to Town, Someone Leaves Town, and that this settles the laundress mystery. Then proposes that Margaret is a werewolf and spy, citing her loping gait, grey colouring, the initials M.B., her misreported name Ladydog, and her role in the killings.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Alan's parents come from Doctorow's novel; Margaret is a werewolf and spy

I think you've nailed it with this reference. I took a quick look at the online html version of this book:
http://craphound.com/someone/Cory_Doctorow_-_Someone_Comes_to_Town_Someone_Leaves_Town.htm and it's a perfect fit.

1) The mountain quakes when it's angry.
2) There are gnomes and golems in its caves.
3) The setting is near Toronto, Canada. (The protagonist goes to Willowdale, a suburb of Toronto, at one point).
4) The nearest large mountain to Toronto is Blue Mountain. There is a trek to the summit which takes about 2.5 hours on foot.
5) The mother of Alan and his siblings is a literal washing machine. His father is a mountain.

With this mystery settled, there is no longer any real connection to Margaret as a laundress. However, I do think she is a werewolf and a spy.

1) The initials M.B. match Mariah Brownlea, and as Cassie remarks Mariah believes in God but Cassie does not. The matching initials suggest to me that Margaret's religion is likewise an act.
2) There's the clue of the werewolf's loping gait -- does any other character have a unique walk described? (Margaret bobs along). 3) She is gray and colorless.
4) Something happened to Alexa right before Margaret started working for Cassie. ("I see you know.")
5) She avoids an obvious word when misreporting the name of Sharon Bench as "Ladydog." As a werewolf female she might be sensitive to this word. 6) Her kidnapping might be the work of another faction to get her out of the way. One of the kidnappers seems to wear werewolf colors. 7) The fingering of the upstairs neighbor for the Squid God's killers could well be her work. How else would the assassin know that Cassie even knew his name?
8) She had the opportunity to go after Jimmy and scare him to death when she was sent for the stage manager. She seems to pick up on Jimmy's death too quickly.
9) Cassie doesn't call her from the South Seas, unlike her other friends. Could Chase's warnings of the marks of a werewolf have caused her to suspect Margaret?
