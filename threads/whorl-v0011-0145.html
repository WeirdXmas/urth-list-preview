2000-08-22 Nicholas Gevers #

Asks whether Wolfe intends readers to equate the City of the Inhumi with Nessus, the two cities seemingly linked by both being accursed. Poses the question to the list without offering a supporting passage, inviting others to share or test the impression.

congruent in being accursed?
