2011-11-06 Larry Miller #

Asks whether Silk or Horn appears in The Book of the New Sun as a ghost or astral projection, specifically as the ghost of Malrubius, citing Wolfe's Gevers interview answer "Yes but not a leading part." Also asks whether the idea that Malrubius is an aquastor still holds given that interview.
questions · author-and-biography · theories: Silk/Horn appears as the ghost of Malrubius

Looking over the Gevers interview (Magus of many suns) earlier and intrigued by one of Wolfes comments. He is asked "Does Silk/Horn take an active part via astral projection in the events of BotNS? Specifically as the "ghost" of Malrubius?" Wolfe replies "Yes but not a leading part." What does everyone make of this? Does anyone think that Silk/Horn makes appearances in the New Sun books? What about Malrubius being an Aquastor? Is that still valid?
