Fliers' names

Themes: Names and their meanings

1997-02-25 Kieran Cleary #

Reports that the fliers' names in Wolfe's Long Sun books are Irish words, listing Grian (sun), Aer (air), Mear (finger) and Sciathan (wing) from memory. Notes that Sumaire would need to be checked, and frames the post as a follow-up to an earlier archive question about the names.
informs · source-and-allusion

On another etymological note, I've been re-reading the mailing list archives, and someone wonders about the fliers' names. In fact they're Irish. Off the top of my head:

Grian: sun
Aer: air
Mear: finger
Sciathan: wing

Otherwise I'll have to check Sumaire.
