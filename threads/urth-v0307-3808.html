2003-07-29 Jonathan Laidlow #

Offers a free copy of John Crowley's Little Big to a UK reader for the cost of postage, saying the poster did not like the novel and gave up after two attempts. Also announces Peter Wright's forthcoming book-length study of Gene Wolfe, Attending Daedalus, with pre-order links via Ultan's Library.
informs

If anyone in the UK wants a free, albeit a bit
battered from going on holiday with me twice, copy of
the Fantasy Masterworks edition of Crowley's "Little
Big", they are welcome to it for the price of postage.

No - I didn't like it, and two attempts is quite
enough....

In other news, Peter Wright's book-length study of GW,
Attending Daedalus, is due out in a few months, and
I've put up pre-order links through Ultan's Library's
association with Amazon [email]....

Jonathan
