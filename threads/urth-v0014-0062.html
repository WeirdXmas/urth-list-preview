Argues that V.R.T.'s guess that #5 is Maitre's clone is too implausible given the information he was explicitly given, while allowing he might have seen Maitre in person. Responds to William Ansley by conceding a sleep-deprived misreading, citing V.R.T.'s prior contacts with Maitre and Aunt Jeannine, and noting the first meeting with #5 was V.R.T.'s first direct contact.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: V.R.T. guessed #5 is Maitre's clone from appearance

You said so clearly; the misapprehension was my fault. When I wrote that post I was in a sleep-deprived state, so my brain was not at its best.

Likewise, apologies for not recalling this.

You may well be right, which is why I thought it perhaps more likely that V.R.T. had seen a photograph of Maitre. OTOH, #5 has been seen in public; presumably so was Maitre at his age, since Maitre wants #5's upbringing as well as his heredity to be as close to his own as possible. It's not impossible that someone old enough to have seen them both could note the identical appearances and draw the correct conclusion, or even make a guess based on comparing #5's appearance with Maitre's current appearance.

I still feel that it's too implausible for V.R.T. to have guessed this based on just the information we are explicitly told he had (and unlike #5 in the last interview, he is presumably not trying to be offensive).

But that's the kind of exchange I like best!
