Themes: Names and their meanings

2011-10-26 Andrew Mason #

Proposes that the name Hethor in The Book of the Long Sun points to a Catholic saint, one of the martyrs of Chertsey, rather than the demonic magistrate Lee Berman had found. Grounds the claim in the idea that Wolfe's Hethor is trying to pass as a Commonwealth citizen, so the saintly name fits the disguise.
