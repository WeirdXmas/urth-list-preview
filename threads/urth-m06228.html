The thread: Marc Aramini argued that the jungle hut scene with its isangoma proselytes and a picture of Buzz Aldrin on an airless moon show that Christ existed in Urth's past, offering these images as evidence without close citation of the text. Jeff Wilson countered that the Aldrin picture may be a Wolfe error, a painting by an astronaut-artist, or evidence that Urth and Earth have diverged, noting the list had found no photograph matching the described composition. Adam Thornton joked that the picture might come from a later Moon visit, such as a Chinese landing in 2041, rather than Apollo 1969. The thread stayed a brief exchange of speculation with no resolution.
