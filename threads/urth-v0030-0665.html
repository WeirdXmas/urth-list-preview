2 books new from Liverpool U.

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-08-24 to 2001-08-24

2001urthRon Crown

2001-08-24 Ron Crown #

Reports two forthcoming Liverpool University Press books listed on the Locus website, a Wolfe essay collection and a Peter Wright collection of essays on Wolfe, and asks whether list members know their contents. Notes that he expects some list members contributed to the second book, and that the press's website has no information.
informs

The following items are listed in the "Forthcoming books" section of the Locus website. Do any list members know details of the contents? I imagine at least one or two of you are contributors to the second item. (There's nothing at the press's website.)

Wolfe, Gene. Shadows of the New Sun: Essays (Liverpool University Press, nf, hc)
Wright, Peter. Attending Dedalus: Essays on Gene Wolfe (Liverpool University Press, nf, hc)

Ron Crown
Reference Librarian
