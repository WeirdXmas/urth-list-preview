2002-09-04 Alice Turner #

Asks newcomers to turn off rich text or HTML formatting when replying to Urth list messages, because the list program cannot handle it and digests become hard to read. Explains that the poster is making a general request rather than contacting individuals privately, and asks that replies be sent as plain text only.
questions

Please, please, newbies, turn off your "rich text" or html option when you reply to an urth message. Ranjit's program can't support it. If there were only one of you, I, or someone else, would ask you to do this privately, but there's an epidemic in the last few days, which makes the diigest horrible to read, and I think a general plea might be more effective. What you want to send is "plain text." Only. Your address book will show you how to do it.

-alga
