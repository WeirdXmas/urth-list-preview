1999-01-23 Rich Horton #

Asks which Damien Broderick book to read next, noting that the poster has only read Sorcerer's World and found it a fair Vancean Dying Earth novel. Describes the book's cutesy names that become jokes when spelled backward, and mentions owning The White Abacus and The Sea's Furthest End and having just acquired Door Number Three.
questions

Confession time: despite owning copies of _The White Abacus_ and _The Sea's Furthest End_, the only Damien Broderick book I've read is _Sorcerer's World_. Which I suspect wouldn't be the book Damien would choose as his main representative to the world, though it's not bad, in the Vancean Dying Earth tradition. Though replete with cutesy names which spelled backward are jokes.

So which should I read next, Damien? TWA or TSFE? Though to be sure, I also just picked up _Door Number Three_, so I have lots of listmember work to anticipate. <g>
