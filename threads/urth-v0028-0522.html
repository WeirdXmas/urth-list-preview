Themes: Christ figures and messiahs

1999-11-23 Redhaven1 #

Speculates that Wolfe may have written a messiah story like Herbert's Dune, suggesting Severian may be revered as a messiah or cursed as a devil in the Short Sun books. Compares Paul Atreides, a mortal man who founded a religion and became a god-like figure, and says the poster has not yet read the first Short Sun book.
proposes · genre-and-craft · theories: Severian becomes a messiah on Urth's future

Maybe Gene just wanted to write a "messiah" book kinda like Herbert's Dune. Paul Atreides was just a man but he changed the course of his entire world, established a sort of religion, and was remembered as a god-like figure all throughout the rest of the books. Maybe in the coming Short Sun books [i haven't read the first one yet so don't give anything away!] Severian's role in his planet's past will be explained more. I know he wasn't mentioned in Long Sun, but I heard he makes an appearance in this next series. I wonder, will he be revered as a messiah or a cursed as a devil?

-Steve
