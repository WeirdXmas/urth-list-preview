1997-11-11 Joshua A. Solomon #

Argues that the recurring phrase "God's fingers" in Wolfe's recent stories, including "The Eyeflash Miracles", points to divine agency behind Little Tib's visions, which he claims are revelations rather than sight. Grounds this in the theologist's "hand of God" remark and in revelations driving plots in the Soldier stories and Long Sun series, while admitting the reading may not fit the other stories and asking for more references.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: God's hand causes Little Tib's visions as revelations

A while back I noticed that "God's fingers" are mentioned in two of Gene's latest stories. Re-reading "The Eyeflash Miracles" last week, I noticed that they crop up there too. Originally, I guessed that "God's fingers" were responsible for the circumstances around which the plot revolves. I still think that I'm right, but only indirectly.

SPOILER WARNING FOR "THE EYEFLASH MIRACLES"

The eponymous miracles are perfromed by our genetically enhanced hero, Little Tib. One of the supporting characters is, among other things, a theologist. He sees "the hand of God" in these miracles and he wants to know what Little Tib sees when he is performing them. Another character derisively suggests that the theologist is trying to take "God's fingerprints." Little Tib says that he sees angels.

In this story anyway, the hand of God is responsible for what Little Tib sees. I.e. his revelations. (He can't *really* see anything; he is blind.) As revelations are the circumstances around wich the plot revolves in other Wolfe works (particularly the Soldier stories and the Long Sun series), my initial guess was somewhat correct. I am not sure if this interpretation is consistent with "God's fingers" as rendered in the recent stories. (Sorry, I've forgotten their titles. Wolfe published 3 in Asimov's and F&SF in the last year or so. The two that I've been able to track down both mentioned "God's fingers.")
Anybody else know of any other references to God's fingers?

1997-11-15 James Jordan #

Asks Joshua to remind the list of other stories where God's fingers or hand appear, listing the Ten Commandments, the writing on the wall at Belshazzar's feast in Daniel 5, and Jesus writing on the ground in John 8. Raises the question of possible connections between these biblical passages and Wolfe's use of the divine hand in the books.
questions · source-and-allusion · theology-and-religion

Joshua,

Remind us of the other stories where God's fingers are mentioned.

Biblically:

1. God wrote the 10 Commandments with His own hand.
2. At Belshazzar's Feast (Daniel 5), God's fingers wrote judgement on the wall.
3. Jesus wrote with His hand on the ground in John 8, in the story of the woman taken in adultery.

Possible connexions?

Nutria
