5HOC - Prisoner next door

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2008-07-11 O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) #

Argues that the prisoner in the room next door in the final story of The Fifth Head of Cerberus is the prisoner's mother, citing the mad cellmate's furious tapping as a clue linked to the talking drums of the Abos in the first story. Responds to a poster who said there is only a single clue for this, promising text citations later.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: The prisoner next door is the prisoner's mother

I am really sorry, I don't have a copy of the book with me (I think I am going to have to post on this forum only when I am at home) so I can't give you text references but: In the first story, it is mentioned that the Abos had a form of percussion they used to communicate "talking drums". In the last story, the prisoner talks about their mad cellmate and mentions them 4 or 5 times. At one point (I can't remember the exact text I am afraid) the prisoner notes that their cell mate tapped furiously throughout the night until at last I thought I could discern my own name. I will get text citations this evening and post them.

Greg Bates
