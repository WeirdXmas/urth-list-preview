Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-07-13 mary whalen #

Proposes that the Autarch Appian's name is a homophone of "apian" (bee-like), asking whether this links him to the Honey Steward. Adds that worker bees die when they lose their stingers while queens do not, hinting at a parallel with the Autarch's role.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Autarch Appian's name links him to bees

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Has anyone noticed that the Autarch Appian's name is a homophone for apian (bee-like)? Any connection with the Honey Steward? Also, most bees are sterile workers. However, when a bee loses it's stinger, it dies. Unless it's the Queen Bee, of course, which doesn't lose it's stinger.
