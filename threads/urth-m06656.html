Another Latro in the Works

2008-11-04 Joe Bushong-Taylor #

Expresses hope for another Latro book and reports that Wolfe's World Fantasy Convention acceptance speech in 2007 suggested he would respond to reader demand for more. Grounds this in the speech, which said Soldier of Sidon was written largely because of reader requests and that more requests were already arriving.
informs · author-and-biography

I find myself rereading the Latro books for the sheer joy of the images and characters in them. I certainly hope we see another Latro book. I do not know of any comments from Wolfe that indicate that he has yet started work on another one, but one would seem to be a possibility in the future. In his acceptance speech at the World Fantasy Convention in Saratoga Springs, in 2007, when *Soldier of Sidon* won Best Novel, he said that *SoS* was written largely because of requests from readers for more about Latro, and, that these requests were already being made again post-*SoS*. He indicated that he would not ignore demand for one. At least that is what my ears heard...

2008-11-04 John Watkins replying to Joe Bushong-Taylor #

Argues that a fourth Latro book would be preferable to further novels in Wolfe's other worlds, because Latro has never received a satisfactory farewell, unlike Severian, Silk and Abel. Responds to Joe Bushong-Taylor's rereading of the Latro books and hope for another one, citing Wolfe's age and two announced stand-alone novels as reasons for uncertainty.
supports

Gene Wolfe is 77. He does not have infinite additional books. I know he has two upcoming, stand-alone novels.

That said, it will be a minor tragedy if he does not write a fourth Latro book. I prefer a fourth Latro book to an additional book set in any of Wolfe's other worlds, because Latro--unlike Severian, unlike Silk, unlike Abel--hasn't had a satisfactory farewell.
