Themes: Power, rulers and politics

1999-11-16 Alice Turner #

Proposes that Horn's appointment by the Vanished People to speak for humanity echoes Severian's earlier role as representative of Urth, and predicts that something bad will follow. Grounds this in Horn's honourable nature and in glimpses of troubling conditions in the Whorl, such as the chained clerk and the harem.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · close-textual-reading · theories: Horn's role as spokesman echoes Severian's

One thing that I don't think anyone has yet mentioned: The echo of NS5 in Horn's being asked by the Vanished People to speak for his race (humanity) just as Sev had to represent the whole of Urth. And of course he is honorable and generous and respectful. Something bad or sad is going to come of this, I think. We have been shown glimpses of some pretty distressing goings-on in this brave new world---the clerk in chains, for instance. The harem.

-alga
