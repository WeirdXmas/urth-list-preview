2002-05-09 Mark Millman #

Corrects the blurb calling Peace Wolfe's first novel, noting that Operation ARES appeared earlier as a paperback original and The Fifth Head of Cerberus was published before Peace. Speculates that Wolfe may have started Peace early and shelved it, suggesting heavy rewriting, while admitting this is unknown. Replies to Darryl Swett's question and draws on Christopher Culver's earlier message about Operation ARES.
answers · author-and-biography · theories: Wolfe began Peace early and shelved it

Darryl,

No, it's not true. _Operation ARES_, for which
Christopher Culver is looking (it's because of
his message that we're discussing this), is
Wolfe's first published novel; Berkley did so
in 1970, releasing it as a paperback original.
_Peace_ is from 1975. _The Fifth Head of Cer-
berus_ is older as well, since it was published
by Scribner's in 1972, though not everyone may
wish to consider it a novel.

What I don't know, of course, is whether Mr.
Wolfe started _Peace_ first, and then left it
in a drawer for several years before taking it
up once again. Certainly the book we have is
much more accomplished than _Operation ARES_,
and clearly the product of a more mature writer
--if what I've suggested above is the case,
there must have been very extensive rewriting.

Mark Millman
(Nacre)
