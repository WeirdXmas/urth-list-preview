Redbeard and st. Gildas/conomor origin for Bluebeard

Urth list · 1 messages · 2014-08-08 to 2014-08-08

2014urthMarc Aramini

Themes: Myth and folklore sources

2014-08-08 Marc Aramini #

Argues that Wolfe knew the origin of the Bluebeard tale in the legend of Conomor, a werewolf-like lord who beheads his wife, and Saint Gildas, who restores her to life, and that this shows in The Shadow of the Torturer. Grounds the claim on a passage where a woman playing Saint Katherine is recalled from Gildas's time as captain of apprentices, and on the timing of the Redbeard release after The Book of the New Sun.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Wolfe knew the Conomor and Gildas origin of Bluebeard

Well, in my write up I speculated whether Wolfe would be aware of the origin of the Bluebeard story with Conomor the supposed werewolf and st. Gildas, who restores his wife to life even though she is beheaded by Conomor. As I was reading shadow of the torturer, the first talk of the woman who plays st. Katherine is a memory of when Gildas was captain of apprentices. So gene knew about Gildas and the survived beheading with the werewolf Conomor, as Redbeard was released just after New Sun. What an erudite fellow.
