Forelesen

1998-09-28 Patrick O'Leary #

Offers a plain reading of Forelesen as a portrait of a contemporary working man, then proposes that the dog's head in a red triangle on the factory front is Cerberus, making the factory a dogfood plant. Responds to Mr. Borski's take on the story and asks whether a real canned dogfood logo resembles the emblem.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: The dog's head logo on the factory is Cerberus; The factory is a dogfood plant

Fascinating take on Forelesen, Mr. Borski.

I have no idea what this magnificent story means. I took it as a fairly accurate portrait of a contemporary working man. Hell, in other words.

But this one bit seems obvious to me...
<<
On the front of the factory into which the mustangs are being herded, Forlesen sees a picture of "a dog's head in a red triangle on a field of black." Could this be my old friend Cerberus?

Dogfood factory.

Isn't there a canned dogfood that has a similar logo? Not sure.

best

Patrick O'Leary
