Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

1999-10-05 Alice Turner #

Retracts a earlier theory about Krait's secret and speculates that Horn's mind may be in Silk's body, while admitting the reveal is far off. Raises why Seawrack lacks the double limbs seen in the Neighbors, asking whether it proves she is not native, and reaffirms the idea that Mom is an undine.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Horn's mind is in Silk's body; Seawrack is not native because she lacks four arms; Mom is an undine

Okay, I'm going to disagree with myself as well. Krait's secret probably isn't the inhumation (thanks, Dave). Though I still marvel at the ingenuity of Horn's strategy and wonder how he could have figured it out. The secret is one of those things that Wolfe isn't going to let us know for quite some time, I would guess now.

Horn's mind in Silk's body---hmmm. That's distinctly possible, and a real leap. I'm sure we're not supposed to suspect that yet!

I hadn't picked up that the Neighbors also have four arms; I'd seen them as centaurs. But now I'm just starting over again. Wrt double limbs, how come Seawrack hasn't got 'em? Some sort of proof that she's not native? I still like Mom as an undine.

-alga
