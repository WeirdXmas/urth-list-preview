2005-01-18 Jim Raylor #

Proposes that The Fifth Head of Cerberus has the shape of a Möbius band, arguing that its words written on a strip of paper and twisted before the ends are joined make the book's form. Offers this as a playful image of the novella's structure rather than citing specific passages or evidence from the text.
