2001-01-08 The Laughing Skull #

Proposes that several names in Wolfe's books come from Spanish words, including Triskle from triste (sad), Calde from alcalde (mayor), and Ayuntamiento (copulation) as a hint about Silk. Grounds these readings in the poster's recent study of Spanish cross-referenced with the works read, and invites other readers to add more examples.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Triskle's name echoes Spanish triste; Calde derives from Spanish alcalde; Ayuntamiento hints at Silk's trouble with sex

Hey all,
I've recently picked up a fair amount of the spanish language, and when cross referenced with all of the Wolfe works I've read, I've noticed a few interesting things.

1) Severian's 3 legged dog is named Triskle. The spanish word for sad is 'Triste', very close. If that dog's story isn't sad, I don't know what is 2) Probably the most recognized is the term Calde, coming from Alcalde, meaning mayor
3) The word 'Ayuntamiento' in Spanish means "copulation." Seeing as how the cause lots of problems for Silk, could this mean that they're "The Fuckers?"

I'm sure there are loads more that I've missed, but I'd just like to see what the rest of you thought
