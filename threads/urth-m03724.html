2007-01-29 Jonathan Laidlow #

Announces that the first issue of the Book of Gold is now available on the Ultan site, thanking David DiGiacomo for it. Also states that the last quarter of Soldier of Arete is disappointing and hard to follow, speculating that Wolfe wrapped up threads because sales did not support a third volume.
informs

Thanks to David DiGiacomo, we now have the first issue of the Book of Gold on Ultan.

www.ultan.org.uk

Thanks David!

And on Soldier of Arete. I too must confess that in my recent rereading of the Soldier novels I found the last quarter of Arete to be supremely disappointing, and very difficult to follow. I can only speculate that Wolfe was told at this point that sales didn't warrant his writing a 3rd volume, so he tried to wrap up narrative threads that would have continued in another volume.

But don't worry - Sidon doesn't really require comprehension of the ending of Arete - it makes very little reference to the earlier novels.

J

> However, the gammadions could very well bisect a point that would resemble a slash through the cross.  Uggh.

I seriously doubt Wolfe intended the voided cross to be interpreted as 
one with a slash through it.  See:

http://www.seiyaku.com/customs/crosses/voided.html

which says: [The Voided Cross] is simply a heraldic term for when only 
the edge of the cross is traced. It can be any shape or form of cross.

So, a voided cross is just the outline of a cross, which can be 
approximated quite closely with four gammas.
