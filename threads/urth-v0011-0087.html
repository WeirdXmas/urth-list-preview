R=Robert/Roncevaux

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-21 to 1998-05-21

1998urthRobert Borski

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-05-21 Robert Borski #

Proposes that the two Robert Culots in VRT are grandfather and grandson, paralleling the Roy Emersons, and reads R=Robert as a minor echo. Also offers a simple A=1 letter code on the Rm E2S14 Seat 18 numbers, suggesting the letters spell out 'SeNioR' and 'SoN' for two Roy Emersons.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Two Robert Culots are grandfather and grandson; Rm E2S14 numbers encode 2 Roy Emersons via A=1 code

To Peter Stephenson:

Your idea of R=Robert is interesting in a slightly different context. While there are no Roberts listed as family members of GW, in VRT there are _two_ Robert Culots, and their relationship is the same as Roy Emersons I and II: i.e., grandfather and grandson. The Proustian de Loup tie-in is lagniappe. Thanks for bringing it to my attention.

To Tony Ellis:

I like your idea that little Victor may have thought his room and seat number were as important as his name. It's also possible he was simply playing dumb, as his father taught him to do (Marsh, in fact, initially thinks him retarded).

R=Roncevaux (french for thorn) has not nearly the thematic resonance for me as Roy. Also try saying the name Victor Roncevaux Trenchard fast 3X.

I'd also like to suggest something else, Tony. When I originally looked at the numbers in Rm E2S14 Seat 18, I thought they might have signaled a date of significance--2/14/(19)18--maybe a birth or a death or an anniversary, but fruitlessly failed to find corroborative dates. Then just after I sent my first VRT post, I wondered what would happen if I utilized the simplest of all possible codes, where A=1, B=2, etc..
This would give us an N and R to work with, and if we ignore the lower case m/e/a/t, we're left with R E 2 S N R. Could this possibly signify 2 Roy Emersons, SoN/SeNioR? If not, it's a strange, if admittedly forced coincidence.

To Tania Ruiz:

Your constellation info was very interesting. Any chance it reveals which star the two planets Ste. Anne--Ste.Croix might orbit? mantis and I might want to go there some day, maybe hunt a few alzabos.

Robert Borski
