Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2001-02-24 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Recounts the poster's earlier mistake of reading a possessing goddess with a whip in the Long Sun books as a war goddess, Scylla or Spingx, and reports that Wolfe confirmed she is Love. Responds to Bob Musser's caution about Seawrack's return by offering this as a cautionary parallel and pointing readers to the archives.
informs · author-and-biography

Well, =maybe= . . . I mean, I recall too well my own similar reaction to a possession in the Long Sun series, thinking that it must have been a goddess of war (Scylla or Spingx) rather than a goddess of love, since She had a Big Whip and was cracking it in fury . . . turns out, when we asked Wolfe about it, that it really was Love. He doesn't want Love presented as some sort of milquetoast, lisping, etc. sort of thing. She can really kick bootie, no kidding.

All details in the archives.
