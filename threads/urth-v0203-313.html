2002-03-07 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists seven early Wolfe stories by their one-line plot features, including a Chinese screen art object, a lake-side rape and murder mystery, and a man's relationship with a computer. Replies to Adam Stephanides' request by quoting Joan Gordon's description of Wolfe's first published science-fiction story and adding the poster's own impressions, such as a Macbeth-like spaceship murder.
informs

Adam Stephanides asked about some stories. I'll quote Joan Gordon by way of reply, adding a little:

"Wolfe's first published science-fiction story, this is an initiation story showing his characteristic interest in myth."

Hmmm. It has something of the feel of "`A Story' by John V. Marsch." It is set on a terraformed Mars, iirc.

"A non-science-fiction mystery story based on false assumptions."

It is a Chinese screen. Art object, not motion-picture initiation.

"A straight mystery story"

A lake in the woods. Rape, murder, guns. And a Volkswagon, of course.

"Crossed communications and the value of humanity."

Hmmm. A "Hollywood bowl" type dingus writes out a message from the stars, fragment by fragment.

"Who is real and who is not, in the sexual arena."

"A spaceship murder with allusions to _Macbeth_."

"A personal relationship between a man and a computer."

The computer is deep underground.

=mantis=
