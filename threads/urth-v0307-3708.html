Wolfe's novel schedule

Urth list · 1 messages · 2003-07-21 to 2003-07-21

2003urthThe Book of the New SunJames Wynn

2003-07-21 James Wynn #

Reports Wolfe's stated timeline for forthcoming work, saying the next Latro novel is in its very early stages and Wizard is expected early next year, followed by Knight and a short story collection. Responds to a reader's question about publication dates for the next Latro volume, noting Wolfe's advancing age and the difficulty of the research for the first two books.
