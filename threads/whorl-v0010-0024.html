Where are the 'bots?

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1999-09-18 Redhaven1 #

Asks what happened to the soldiers, Sand and Hammerstone, when the Cargo left the Whorl, and whether the chems would still be needed after settlement on Blue. Also questions why the chems have such human personalities if they are machines, with no reply yet to any earlier poster.
questions · chronology-and-plot-logic

Hey, what ever happened to Sand, Hammerstone, and the rest of the soldiers when the 'Cargo' left the whorl? Was this just meant to be left hanging, or had they served their purpose and their time was finished? But that doesn't quite fit, because Moly left the whorl with the humans. Would they still need chems after they had settled on Blue? I'm also unclear on why the chems have such human personalities if they're just machines.

-Steve
