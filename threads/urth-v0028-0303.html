PKD a hack?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-10-28 to 1999-10-28

1999urthMichael Straight

1999-10-28 Michael Straight #

Argues that Philip K. Dick's The Man in the High Castle contains genuinely beautiful prose, contrary to the view that Dick was a hack. Recounts the poster's surprise on reading it after Little, Big, which changed his sense of Dick as a merely wacky writer; it responds to a quoted call to read Timequake and The Three Stigmata of Palmer Eldritch.
supports

Or read the MAN IN THE HIGH CASTLE. There is some really beautiful prose in that novel. I read it right after _Little, Big_, so I'm not just talking relatively beautiful prose, or "pretty good for a hack" prose.

It was my first Dick novel and I was astounded. I said to myself, "I thought Dick was just a mess of wacky drug-induced paranoia...no one ever told me he *wrote* so well!"

-Rostrum
