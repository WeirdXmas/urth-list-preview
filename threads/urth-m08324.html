Calde of the Long Sun

Urth list · 6 messages · 2009-01-15 to 2009-01-16

2009urthThe Book of the Long SunDavid StockhoffJohn WatkinsPaul BDan'l Danehy-Oakes
The thread: Posters debated whether Silk in Calde of the Long Sun was Typhon's intended physical body. David Stockhoff suggested Silk may be one of many embryos made for Typhon, so several Silks could have served different roles, and quoted Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's view that Silk was meant as Pas's body. Paul B. objected that the valuable embryo for Typhon's digital copy would have been guarded far more tightly, citing Typhon's vanity, weak guarding of the lander embryos, and Horn's search in Short Sun against many physical Silks. He also argued that the dead Pas still guarded the golden ring in The Book of the New Sun, as a parallel to guarding the embryo, against Danehy-Oakes's claim that a dead Pas could not place anyone in charge. Stockhoff briefly agreed a physical Typhon was possible, and the thread ended without resolution.
  • David Stockhoff: Silk may be one of many embryos made for Typhon, so several Silks could have existed
  • Paul B: Silk cannot be Typhon's body because the valuable embryo would have been guarded more tightly
  • Paul B: the dead Pas still guards things, as with the golden ring in The Book of the New Sun
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Silk was meant to be Pas's body (quoted, not posting)
  • David Stockhoff: agrees a physical Typhon is possible

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Robots, androids and artificial life, Hidden identities and parentage

2009-01-15 Paul B replying to David Stockhoff #

Argues that Silk cannot be Typhon's intended body, since the valuable embryo that could hold Typhon's digital copy would have been guarded far more tightly than one that ended up with the cargo. Grounds this in Typhon's vanity as shown in the first book, the weak guarding of the lander embryos that show special powers, and the details of Horn's search in Short Sun against the idea of many physical Silks.
disputes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Silk was not Typhon's intended body; Typhon's digital copy was meant for a guarded embryo

Knowing Typhon (booby-trapped giant golden ring on a giant mountain carved in his likeness) he'd keep a close watch on the one single embryo his digital facsimile was intended to inhabit someday, placing it under tighter security than most of the stuff in Mainframe. Typhon seems like a pretty vain guy in BotNS. Those who would suggest that Silk was his intended body should figure out a way to account for how this embryo, likely the most treasured object on the Whorl, fell into the grubby hands of the cargo.

Conversely, the embryos in the landers also seem to possess special powers (Mucor, Pig, Mora) at least in some cases, but aren't particularly well guarded.

As for the existence of other physical Silks, that seems implausible given the details of Horn's search for one in Short Sun.

Paul
