Confirms James Wynn's guess that the name Babbie is a nickname for Babirusa, citing chapter 5 of Calde of the Long Sun where General Mint asks soldiers for their names and one answers Babirousa, an Indonesian wild pig. Supports the earlier suggestion by pointing to this passage as textual evidence for the name's origin.
