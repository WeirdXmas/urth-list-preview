Themes: Women, sex and gender

2001-01-17 David DiGiacomo #

Reports that the poster's copy of Return to the Whorl has shipped from bn.com with a discount code, then asks the list for views on the rape of Mora in Wolfe's Long Sun books. Asks whether Wolfe included the brief scene as a provocation, saying the poster finds it more disturbing than the Seawrack incident on rereading.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Wolfe included the Mora rape as provocation

I know this is not very interesting, but my RttW has been shipped from bn.com, so it really is available. There is a coupon code "CISCOEF" which is good for $10 off on a $40 order, so everyone should order two copies.

On a more substantive note... any thoughts on the rape of Mora? On rereading, I found this brief scene even more disturbing than the Seawrack incident. Is it possible that Wolfe included it as a provocation? I am having trouble finding another explanation.
