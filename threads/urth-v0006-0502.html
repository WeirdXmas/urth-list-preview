Re. (urth) Black Sun, White Fountain

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-02-02 to 1998-02-02

1998urthThe Book of the New SunKieran Cleary

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-02-02 Kieran Cleary #

Proposes that the black hole devouring the Old Sun is the necessary complement of the White Fountain that brought the New Sun, so both are opposite sides of one coin, Severian. Responds to -viz's question on natural causes versus murder, citing the Conciliator's 'Black Sun' name and the Fountain as the opposite of a black hole.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Black hole eating the Old Sun complements the White Fountain; Black hole and White Fountain are two sides of Severian

-viz wonders:

How about this then:

Item: The Conciliator is sometimes referred to as "Dark Sun" (or "Black = Sun"
can't remember which.)
Item: The New Sun itself is brought by the White Fountain, which is = described in the text as being the opposite of a black hole, i.e. an = erruption of matter from one
(lower) universe to another (higher) universe.
Item: There is a black hole in the Old Sun
Item: The White Fountain that brought the New Sun into being was not = born in the same universe as Urth. (shaky here, but I thought that was = mentioned in tUotNS)

So, speculation follows: the black hole that is destroying the Old Sun = is
merely the necessary complement to the White Fountain. One has caused = the other, and are both different sides of the same coin that is = Severian.
