Newt's main referent

Urth list · 6 messages · 2002-09-01 to 2002-09-03

2002urthNicholas GeversAlice TurnerDan'l Danehy-OakesMichael Straight
The thread: Nicholas Gevers proposed that Councillor Newt in Exodus from the Long Sun is modeled on Newt Gingrich, pointing to the 1994 timing and the character's resemblance to the politician. Alice K. Turner backed this with a vague memory of a convention banquet speech by Gingrich that some attendees walked out on, and later suggested checking convention dates against the novels' publication dates. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes reported that Worldcon records list an Atlanta convention in 1986 that fits that memory and noted Gingrich's SF novel appeared in 1995. Turner then accepted the 1986 date, said Wolfe and his circle would not have attended that banquet, and credited Gevers and Blattid with the solution. The thread ended in apparent agreement on a real SF figure as the referent, though the sources are anecdotal.

2002-09-01 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that Councillor Newt in Exodus from the Long Sun is modeled on Newt Gingrich, who became Speaker of the US House after the 1994 Republican victories, the same years the book was written. Grounds this on the timing of composition and on the claimed close resemblance between the character as shown in the book and the politician.
proposes · author-and-biography · theories: Councillor Newt is modeled on Newt Gingrich

Considering when EXODUS FROM THE LONG SUN was being
written (1994-5), Councillor Newt has a pretty obvious
real-life referent: Newt Gingrich, elected Speaker of
the US House of Representatives after the unexpected
Republican congressional election victories of
November 1994. From what we see of Newt, the
resemblance is pretty profound...

--Nick Gevers.

2002-09-01 Alice Turner #

Recounts attending a 1980s convention, likely WorldCon in Atlanta, where Newt Gingrich gave the banquet speech and some attendees walked out, supporting Nick Gevers's identification of Councillor Newt with Gingrich. Offers this as a personal memory, admitting vagueness about the event's details and whether it was Atlanta.
supports · author-and-biography · theories: Councillor Newt is modeled on Newt Gingrich

Good call, Nick. Some major convention (WorldCon, I guess) was held in, I think, Atlanta in the 80s and Newt, for reasons that I think had to do with Bruce Sterling, was the banquet speaker. Though I'm not much of a conventioneer, I attended this (my parents lived in Atlanta) as a friend of Lucius Shepard, and, though I never met him there, I'm pretty sure Wolfe was on hand. I'd only barely heard of Gingrich at that point. But, among the more politicially minded, there was much furor, and a contingent, including Lucius (but not me), ostentatiously walked out of the banquet as Gingrich was introduced. Can't remember much about the speech or the evening otherwise except that both were boring--but that you expect. Gingrich seemed more menacing later, but that was the explicit SF connection. (Sorry to be so vague on particulars, but I've sort of accidentally attended a few conventions or parts of them; the only ones I remember well are those I'd planned in advance, and this was a last-minute thing--if it wasn't Atlanta I apologize, but it was somewhere else I just happened to be for other reasons.)

-alga
