Embedded stories in SHORT SUN?

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-11-15 to 1999-11-15

1999whorlThe Book of the Short SunNicholas Gevers

Themes: Stories within stories

1999-11-15 Nicholas Gevers #

Speculates that Wolfe may have held back two Brown Book stories, "Empires of Foliage and Flower" and "The Boy Who Hooked the Sun", to embed them in a Severian-narrated part of a Short Sun book. Grounds the idea in the omission of these stories from the Strange Travelers collection, while admitting it is a guess.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Brown Book stories embedded in a Severian-narrated Short Sun section

Just a far-fetched thought stemming from the indicated
contents of STRANGE TRAVELERS: the items omitted from
that collection include "Empires of Foliage and
Flower" and "The Boy Who Hooked the Sun", the two
"Brown Book" stories not included in the text of
BOTNS. Could GW have kept those stories back in order
to embed them in a Severian-narrated portion of THE
BOOK OF THE SHORT SUN? It's an appealing idea, but a
complete stab in the dark...
