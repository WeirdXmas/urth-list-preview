1999-09-30 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks when Gene Wolfe stopped working at Plant Engineering and requests the title of a Wolfe short story set in a five-sided house with a background Mexican invasion of the Midwest. Gives the magazine headline he recalls and says the story appeared in a 1990s magazine, possibly F&SF, but offers no answer or evidence beyond memory.
questions

Does anybody recall what year it was when Gene Wolfe quit working at "Plant Engineering"? I vividly recall the magazine news section headline: "(Genre/SF) Gains Full-Time Wolfe!" (Okay, so maybe not quite =vividly=.) I have a sense that it was in the 90s . . .

And while we're poking around in what I can't remember, what was the title of that Wolfe short story with a five sided house (ala LITTLE, BIG) and had a background detail about an invasion of the US by Mexico that had gobbled up much of the Midwest? It was published in a magazine (F&SF?) in the 90s.

Thanks in advance!

=mantis=

1999-09-30 Nicholas Gevers #

Answers Michael Andre-Driussi's trivia questions by giving the year Wolfe left Plant Engineering magazine and naming the short story with the five-sided house and Mexican invasion detail. Supplies the answers directly, citing the anthology where the story appeared and calling it one of Wolfe's best stories.
answers

Answers to the below:
Wolfe quit PLANT ENGINEERING in 1984. And the story
including the interesting architecture and the Mexican
invasion was "The Haunted Boardinghouse", in WALLS OF
FEAR (ed. Kathryn Cramer), Morrow 1990, one of Wolfe's
best stories, incidentally.
