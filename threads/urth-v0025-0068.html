When Did You Last See Your Father?

Urth list · 3 messages · 1999-04-13 to 1999-04-13

1999urthThe Book of the New SunRoy C. LackeyKieran ClearyPeter Westlake
The thread: Readers debated who the child in the basket at Oldgate is, centering on whether it is Severian's mother Catherine or his child by Dorcas. Roy C. Lackey pushed the Catherine idea, making Severian his own father via a time loop, building on Robert Borski and Peter Westlake's mutual-recognition point. Peter Westlake argued Dorcas might be older than assumed, perhaps born at the start of Severian's reign, so the basket child could be hers. Kieran Cleary questioned the Catherine theory on grounds of its time-travel logic and the family genealogy it implies. The thread ended with no resolution, just competing proposals and objections.
  • Roy C. Lackey: the basket child is Catherine, so Severian fathered his own mother
  • Peter Westlake: Dorcas may be older and born early in Severian's reign, not the basket child's mother
  • Kieran Cleary: skeptical of the Catherine theory, citing the need for time travel and the genealogy problem

Themes: Time loops and causality, Fathers, mothers and children, Memory and forgetting

1999-04-13 Roy C. Lackey #

Proposes that Severian's mother Catherine is the child in the basket, so Severian fathered his own mother, combining this with Robert Borski's idea that Catherine is his mother. Builds on Peter Westlake's point about the wistful mutual recognition between Severian and the woman, rejecting the Severian's-reign timing as incompatible with that recognition.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Catherine is the child in the basket; Severian fathered his own mother; Catherine is Severian's mother, per Robert Borski

very start of Severian's reign, for instance?<<

Believe me, that occurred to me, but I couldn't reconcile it with the wistful, mutual recognition. But if you put it together with Robert Borski's idea that she is Catherine, his mother, then it all comes full circle. The child in the basket was his mother. Sev fathered his own mother on his grandmother! He's his own grandpa! Too much!

Roy

1999-04-13 Peter Westlake #

Proposes that Dorcas may be older than assumed, perhaps born at the very start of Severian's reign, rather than being the mother of the basket's baby in Oldgate. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's quoted post, which relays Robert Borski's suggestion that the basket in Oldgate holds Severian's child by Dorcas, citing the timing of Severian's last sight of her.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Dorcas was born at the start of Severian's reign

Maybe she's a little older than that; could she have been born at the very start of Severian's reign, for instance?

1999-04-13 Kieran Cleary #

Questions a theory that the child in the basket is Severian's mother Catherine, asking why Catherine would need to be sent back through time to meet Oeuen and conceive Severian. Also asks how Severian's mother could be an exultant Pelerine if her mother was Dorcas and her father Severian himself.
questions · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Catherine is the child in the basket

child in the basket was his mother.<

I like that as a possibility, but for this to work, Catherine needs to get whipped back through the corridoors of time in order to meet Oeuen (sp?) so that she can conceive Sev. What's the rationale for this time travelling?

Also, Sev's mother was supposed to be an exultant Pelerine? Whence the exultant genes if her mother was Dorcas and father Sev himself?
