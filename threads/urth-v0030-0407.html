2001-05-11 Kevin J. Maroney #

Reports a secondhand claim that Wolfe's next project is a single-volume fantasy novel titled The Wizard Knight, with Soldier of Sidon queued after it, and adds a wish for an Oz novel. Relays the information from Robert Borski and notes the author has not yet delivered the manuscript to the publisher.
informs

According to Robert Borski, Wolfe's next project is a
complete-in-one-volume Big Fat Fantasy Novel (around 1100 pages) with the title _The Wizard Knight_. That is literally all I know about it. I don't believe he has delivered it to Hartwell yet; David usually mentions such things to me just to see me writhe in pain.

_Soldier in Sidon_ is apparently next in the queue after that. I want Wolfe to write an Oz novel.
