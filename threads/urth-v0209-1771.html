2002-09-25 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Notes that the announced table of contents for the Wolfe collection lacks the story "Talk of Mandrakes", which Wolfe reportedly wrote and sold to a magazine but never published. Recalls Wolfe's remark that the title is neither unwritten nor unsold, only unpublished, and signs off with a mantis emoticon.

2002-09-25 Nicholas Gevers #

Shares the provisional contents list of Gene Wolfe's planned sixth collection, Wraiths, Waifs, and Wolves, expected from Tor in Spring 2004. Lists its stories, described as emphasizing fantasy and horror, and notes that the closing 11,000-word story first appears in Harry Turtledove's anthology First Heroes in December 2003.
informs

From Gene: the provisional contents list for his sixth
major collection, WRAITHS, WAIFS, AND WOLVES, likely
to appear from Tor in Spring 2004. Emphasis is on
fantasy and horror...

The Tree Is My Hat
The Old Woman Whose Rolling Pin is the Sun
The Friendship Light
Slow Children at Play
Under Hill
The Monday Man
The Waif
The Legend of Xi Cygnus
The Sailor Who Sailed After the Sun
How the Bishop Sailed to Inniskeen
Houston, 1943
A Fish Story
Wolfer
The Eleventh City
The Night Chough
The Wrapper
A Traveler in Desert Lands
The Walking Sticks
Queen
Pocketsful of Diamonds
Copperhead
The Lost Pilgrim

That last story is an 11,000-worder due to appear
first in a Harry Turtledove anthology, FIRST HEROES,
in December 2003.

--Nick Gevers.
