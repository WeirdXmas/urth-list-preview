Winnoc

1998-07-31 mary whalen #

Reports that a saint named Winnoc (also Winoc, Wenoc, Gwinnoc and other Breton forms) is a candidate source for the name Winnoc, noting he died in 717 and is patron of millers. Gives the list of spelling variants and says St. Wenoc may have existed though nothing is known of him, offering the name as a find rather than an argument.
informs · source-and-allusion

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

I've also found St. Winnoc (or Winoc) of Wormhoult. He died in 717. He is the patron of millers. He has a Breton name, too, and it is also found in forms Wenoc, Wennog, Winneg, Gwinnoc, Gweneg, Gwezheneg, and about anything else like this. St. Wenoc might have existed, too, but there is nothing known about him.
