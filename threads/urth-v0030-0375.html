Copperhead (spoilers, with luck)

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

2001-05-04 Peter Westlake #

Proposes that the Changer swaps things with cognates, so the creature under the leaves is a Copperhead snake exchanged for a Serpent from another universe, and the man swapped at the end is the supreme leader. Responds to Blattid's bafflement by offering this reading and listing open questions: whether Boone and General Martens are the same person, who is murdered and why, and what the dialect words "groose" and "grawnt" mean.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Changer swaps Copperhead snake with Serpent; Swapped man is the supreme leader

The Changer swaps things with cognates, i.e. rough equivalents. The thing moving under the leaves is surely a Copperhead snake, which has been swapped with a Serpent from another universe. That also accounts for the identity of the man swapped for the president - supreme leader, big boss - at the end, and of course he's annoyed.

Things that confused me:

1. Are Boone and General Martens the same person?

2. Who gets murdered and why?

3. The dialect gets impenetrable at the worst possible moment - what do "groose" and "grawnt" mean?

Spectacled Bear.
