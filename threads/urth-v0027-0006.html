1999-05-19 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Clarifies that a dealer only offered the first two issues of a Phantom Menace comic adaptation for sale and never claimed to have seen the later issues. Responds to vizcacha by pointing to the dealer's exact wording and noting that his mentions of inventory and expertise do not imply he had seen the phantom issues.
disputes · close-textual-reading

vizcacha,

I thought I was careful in saying that he was selling issues one and two, and that he =said= the series went on to six. Then there was a lot of stuff about comic book inventory and/or expertise, which, by design or by accident, makes each phrase significantly more weighty than a toss off comment based upon "original plans" or hearsay--it makes it seem as if he has at least seen them.

But no, he does not offer the phantom issues for sale; nor does he claim to have seen them, or even know someone whose brother has one (that wonderful tag for so many urban legends); nor, for that matter, does he say that he is just repeating the original plan from circa 1991.

=mantis=
