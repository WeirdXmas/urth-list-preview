Nature of the Claw

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-02-01 to 1998-02-01

1998urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Straight

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1998-02-01 Michael Straight #

Proposes that the Claw's power came from the Increate, manifested through thorns like the Claw, channeled by Severian from the New Sun, and asks whether this holds. Responds to Michael Straight's post about Severian and the Claw, citing the thorn bushes near the end of Citadel and the glowing thorn Severian found.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: The Claw's power comes from the Increate through thorns; Severian channels New Sun power through the Claw

Maybe this would be a good place to ask this question. Near the end of Citadel Severian finds the bushes with thorns identical to the Claw. My take on this is that he realizes that the "claw" he found after the gem was destroyed was just another one of these thorns (not necessarily one that had been inside the shattered gem as Severian thought) and that the actual power came from the Increate who is able to manifest his power through any part of creation he chooses (thus the whole world is "holy ground").

Then, having read Urth, I'm inclined to say that the power of the claw came from the Increate via the New Sun via Severian himself, but still the same principle. I'm reminded of the end of Donaldson's Thomas Covenant series.

Does this reading make sense, or are there other reasons to believe the Claw had some intrinsic power apart from Severian?

(Looking back, I see the thorn/claw was glowing when Severian found it. Perhaps he unconsciously needed something to focus the power he was tapping into from the New Sun?)

This list has made me realize how much I need to re-read these books. I'm amazed at how much I missed.

-Rostrum
