Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-08-30 mary whalen #

Proposes that the delayed dawn in The Book of the New Sun was caused by Tzadkiel's ship lying between the earth and the sun, and that Ossipago, Barbatus and Famulimus may have informed Tzadkiel so he could help Severian. Notes that Wolfe's afterword allows the delay might be imagined by the natives, influenced by Severian's narrative.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Tzadkiel's ship caused the delayed dawn; Ossipago, Barbatus and Famulimus informed Tzadkiel

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

The delay of dawn was most likely caused by the ship of Tzadkiel being between the earth and the sun. Ossipago, Barbatus, and Famulimus could have given Tzadkiel the information that Severian needed some (subtle) help. Tzadkiel might have been able to learn this without the three's help, too.

In the back of the book, Gene's afterward section also says that it's possible that the natives just imagined that it was taking a long time to dawn because of being caught up in the force of Severian's narrative, or for other reasons.

prion
