A fourth "Sun" series?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-12-02 to 1999-12-02

1999urthRon Hale-Evans

1999-12-02 Ron Hale-Evans #

Speculates that Wolfe may complete a fourth sun-titled series, The Book of the Old Sun, after the New, Long and Short Sun books. Grounds the guess in the symmetrical wordplay of the titles of his stories "The Island of Doctor Death" and its permutations, and admits he has no inside knowledge.
proposes · author-and-biography · theories: Wolfe will write The Book of the Old Sun

Sorry to get your hopes up. I don't know any more than you do, but in light of Wolfe's almost Oulipean symmetrical language play in the titles of "The Island of Doctor Death", "The Death of Doctor Island", "The Doctor of Death Island", and "The Death of the Island Doctor", can we expect the following sequence to be completed sometime next millennium?

THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN
THE BOOK OF THE LONG SUN
THE BOOK OF THE SHORT SUN

and finally

THE BOOK OF THE OLD SUN?

Cheers...

Ron H-E
