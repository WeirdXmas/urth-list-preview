Informs readers of a Yahoo! Clubs mailing list devoted to John Crowley, called johncrowleyreaders, and gives how to view its archives and join. Points out that it was first mentioned by William Ansley in April and notes a pointer to the Crowley papers index at University of Texas-Austin; this is an off-topic announcement with no discussion of Wolfe.
informs

I had missed William Ansley's mention of this back in April, and I thought that other people who might find it of interest had missed it, too.

There is a Yahoo! Clubs mailing list dedicated to John Crowley. The name is the utterly prosaic "johncrowleyreaders", though it does bear "The Invisible College" as a subtitle. Traffic is low, currently, but there have been a few interesting posts, including a pointer to the index of the Crowley papers at University of Texas-Austin.

You can view the archives (such as they are) at
<http://clubs.yahoo.com/clubs/johncrowleyreaders>, and can join for free by first registering with Yahoo! Clubs at <http://clubs.yahoo.com>.
