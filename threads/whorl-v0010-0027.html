1999-09-22 Alice Turner #

Disputes the theory that Typhon knew in advance what the Inhumi were, so that the human cargo was sent as cattle. Responds to Hi Hat by citing Wolfe's explicit denial, given when a list member asked him directly, and noting that the cattle idea recurred in earlier discussions.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · author-and-biography · theories: Typhon knew the Inhumi's nature beforehand; Human cargo sent as cattle for Inhumi

snip

Hi Hat,

Well remembered. But the trouble is--and you can check this out in the archives--Wolfe has explicitly denied Typhon's having prior knowledge of the nature of the inhumi and quite probably even *of* the inhumi. Variations on the idea of the cargo as "cattle" for the inhumi turned up several times when this group was younger (!). It was a pet of mine for a while, but someone, I forget who, asked him flat out about it and he denied it.

-alga
