1997-03-13 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Proposes splitting the list into separate mailing lists for The Book of the New Sun, general Wolfe discussion, and the Long Sun books, so the Whorl list can stay focused. Asks readers for suggestions and says the new lists will open in April, with an added note on whether a list about Wolfe lists is needed.
proposes

OK, I've been restraining myself, but perhaps the time has come to act. Do we need...

A mailing list for discussing THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN

A mailing list for GENE WOLFE stuff in general

I'd be glad to set them up if there's demand, but I'm not sure what the right way to do it is. I'd prefer to keep the Whorl list focused mostly on Long Sun. If the New Sun list is similarly focused, then we do need at least three lists to cover Urth, Whorl, and The Rest. On the other
hand, if everyone in the world who's interested in one of these topics is equally interested in the other two, then it may be foolish of me to try to keep them separated.

Let me know what you think; I'll open up the new list or lists in April. Please send your suggestions directly to me ([email]) and not to the Whorl list. Perhaps we
need a mailing list for discussion of wolfe-related mailing lists?

ranjit
whorl-list's owner

1997-03-13 Joshua A. Solomon #

Argues for keeping a single mailing list rather than splitting into several, and suggests it need not focus only on the Long Sun books. Cites the lack of replies to his earlier question about Nessus's current location as evidence that members hold back from other topics, and mentions reading Joyce's Ulysses.
proposes

I vote for a single list, not necessarily LONG SUN-centric. I am afraid that several folks on this list have been
restraining themselves when it comes to discussion of
other topics. Why else would no one have replied to my
query re. Nessus' location today?
Maybe it's discussed in one of the critical essays.
I'll have to dig those up.
