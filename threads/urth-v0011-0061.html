The thread: Alice Turner asked why the Autarch and later Severian, with Father Inire's complicity, keep subversively encouraging the rebels and arranged Agia's succession after Vodalus's death, when the movement seems small and the rebels distract from the Ascian war. M.driussi proposed that the Phoenix Throne covertly backs Vodalus and the Brotherhood in a 1984-style controlled rebellion, perhaps with Vodalus as a throne candidate like Baldanders, citing the real war with Ascia and Thecla's arrest. 1998-05-17 Alice Turner #

Asks why the Autarch and later Severian, with Father Inire's complicity, keep subversively encouraging the rebels and arrange Agia's succession after Vodalus's death when the movement seems small. Notes the rebels cause damage and draw attention that could otherwise go to fighting the Ascians, so the poster is seeking an explanation of the rulers' strategy.
questions · close-textual-reading

And another thing I've never quite understood. Why do the Autarch, and after him Severian, with the complicity of Father Inire, continue to subversively encourage the rebels, taking care to set up Agia after Vodalus's death? This movement doesn't seem to be particularly large, but it does a lot of damage and it requires attention that might be better put to fighting the Ascians.

-alga-

1998-05-17 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the Phoenix Throne covertly backs Vodalus and the Brotherhood in a 1984-style scheme of controlled rebellion, with Vodalus perhaps another candidate for the throne like Baldanders. Grounds this on the Commonwealth's real war with Ascia and on Thecla's arrest, which may have been ordered by a faction worried about Vodalus rather than by the autarch.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Phoenix Throne covertly backs Vodalus as 1984-style control; Vodalus is a patsy cultivated by the autarch; Thecla's arrest ordered by a rival faction

alga,

I've always taken the covert support by the Phoenix Throne of Vodalus/Agia as a "1984-ish" trick--Big Brother (gov't) controls the Brotherhood (anti-gov't underground). With a dash of Machiavelli, since the Commonwealth really is at war with Ascia (rather than the seeming puppetshow wars of 1984--the Brotherhood would appear to be a domestic affair rather than an international terrorist cell); Vodalus is working both sides to gain the throne for himself; Agia just likes to play Xena.

I was going to write that if Vodalus knew with certainty that he was being supported by the autarch himself rather than a shadowy faction within the House Absolute, he would break the link. But I'm not so sure . . . Vodalus might be another patsy, another candidate for the throne being cultivated by the autarch, in the same way that Baldanders is being cultivated for the throne by O, B, & F. (For example, Thecla was not arrested by the autarch's order, but by some faction apparently a bit more concerned with stopping Vodalus.)

An espionage game of "toying with the devil you know" rather than letting unknown alien agents run around in the countryside.

=mantis=

1998-05-17 Alice Turner #

Argues that Vodalus is an ersatz, false figure in The Book of the New Sun, pointing to the false coin and his betrayal of the Autarch, and finds the Vodalus connection to the other ruling-faction threads loosely built. Responds to a prior post's idea that the Phoenix Throne covertly controls Vodalus and the Brotherhood as a 1984-style trick, citing the Thecla arrest as evidence.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Vodalus is an ersatz candidate for the throne cultivated by the Autarch

That is a depressing thought. Vodalus is ersatz to the bone, as symbolized by the false coin. His only redeeming feature is a certain courtliness toward Thea--but look how he betrays his "servant" (the Autarch) in the crunch. The "1984" analogy is a good one (that's hard to follow too!), but I must say that together with the E, A & S (and Baldanders too) role under the New Sun, the Vodalus connection seems to me among the less than tightly hammered together links of the series.

-alga-
