1998-05-12 Craig Christensen #

Asks where Wolfe's recent stories were published, listing Try and Kill It, Counting Cats in Zanzibar and The Ziggurat, and whether The Ziggurat first appeared in a magazine. Requests pointers to other recent stories the poster could find in used SF bookstores.
questions

Can anyone tell me where Wolfe's most recent stories have been published? I found a reference to Try And Kill It , Isaac Asimov's magazine 10/96 and 11/96. I read Counting Cats In Zanzibar in a "best of" anthology. I have heard that The Ziggurat is in another anthology (Full Spectrum, 5?), which I haven't been able to locate. Was it published in a magazine first? Are there other recent stories anyone can point me at? I can probably locate the past issues at local used SF bookstores.
