2006-05-29 thalassocrat #

Points readers to a New York Review article on the Gospel of Judas and quotes Jesus' final words to Judas about the race of Adam and a guiding star. Notes the resonance of that passage with themes in The Book of the New Sun, without developing a detailed reading of Wolfe's text.
informs · source-and-allusion

There's a nice piece about it in the latest NY Review:
http://www.nybooks.com/articles/19031

Anybody interested in this stuff has probably noted the resonance of Jesus' last words to Judas with BOTNS themes:

"And the image of the great race of Adam shall be lifted up, because before heaven and earth and the angels, that race existed throughout the eternal realms. Behold, you have been told everything. Lift up your eyes and see the cloud and the light within it, and the stars surrounding it. And the star that leads the way, that is your star."
