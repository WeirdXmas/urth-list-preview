Black Holes, Black Chasms, and Einstein-Rosenburg Bridges

Urth list · 2 messages · 1999-12-01 to 1999-12-01

1999urthGreene, CarltonMichael Straight

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1999-12-01 Greene, Carlton #

Argues that the 'black chasm' at the sun's heart in The Book of the New Sun is effectively a black hole, and that the White Fountain's source fits that concept. Offers the Einstein-Rosenberg bridge as a possible model for a black hole with a ring singularity leading elsewhere, and cites the old theory that quasars are black holes' outpourings from other universes. Responds to Rostrum's question about whether anyone calls the sun's far end a black hole.
answers · source-and-allusion · theories: The black chasm is a black hole; White Fountain comes from a black hole in another universe; Einstein-Rosenberg bridge model fits Wolfe's cosmology

<<Does Apheta or anyone else use the words "black hole" to describe the other end of the White Fountain or the thing eating away at the sun?

Perhaps there is some other phenomenon, unknown to 20th century astronomers because it doesn't emit jets of x-rays, that is not a black hole but is a rift between universes where energy is sucked out of one universe and emerges as a White Fountain in the other.>>

Although I do not have my books at hand, I recall that someone refers to the "black chasm" at the heart of the sun, and that there were other, similar references. Sounds pretty close to "black hole" to me.

One possibility is an Einstein-Rosenburg bridge. These scientists theorized that a black hole with a ring (rather than point) singularity could have at its center a tube of bent space time that would pass "elsewhere." Still, all of Wolfe's imagery goes along with the black hole concept. In particular, there was a popular theory (which may well have been discredited by now) that quasars, with their enourmous energy output and unknown genesis, were the outpourings of black holes in other universes. This theory seems to fit well with Wolfe's conception.

Carlton

1999-12-01 Michael Straight #

Argues that the dying sun in the New Sun books is being sabotaged, and that the far end of a white fountain would be a wormhole sending energy into another universe rather than a black hole. Responds to Carlton Greene's question about whether Apheta or anyone uses the term black hole, offering the distinction as a clarification of his earlier point.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: White fountain's far end is an inter-universe wormhole; Dying sun is prematurely sabotaged

Well sure, but my point is that, just as the dying sun initially looks like it's dying of old age but is actually being prematurely sabotaged, this opposite end of a white fountain would suck everything up very much like a black hole, but instead of the energy being emitted in this universe as X-rays, it is actually a wormhole spewing energy into another universe. It might be confused with black holes, but would be a different astronmical phenomenon (as would be appararent to any astronomer looking for X-rays).

-Rostrum
