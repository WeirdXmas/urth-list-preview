Rigoglio and Kim Il Sung

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-08-22 to 2000-08-22

2000whorlThe Book of the Short SunNicholas Gevers

Themes: War and soldiers

2000-08-22 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that the Soldo/Blanko war in The Book of the Long Sun mirrors the Korean War, with Rigoglio cast as a Typhon imitator like Kim Il Sung as a Stalin imitator. Grounds the comparison on the North-South division and on the protagonist being an outsider who intervenes like a GI, noting Wolfe's own service in Korea.
proposes · author-and-biography · source-and-allusion · theories: Soldo/Blanko war mirrors the Korean War; Rigoglio is a Typhon imitator like Kim Il Sung
