Omnibus Editions

Urth list · 7 messages · 2011-03-22 to 2011-03-22

2011urthThe Book of the New SunAntónio Pedro MarquesPedro PereiraJack SmithAntonin Scriabin
The thread: Newcomer Antonin Scriabin asked whether omnibus editions gathering Wolfe's Long Sun and Short Sun novels existed, and the thread became a practical exchange about which editions to buy. Jack Smith and Scriabin traded notes on SFBC omnibus volumes already owned or wished for, while António Pedro Marques advised against Long Sun and Short Sun omnibuses as unwieldy and suggested a single omnibus of the whole solar series. Pedro Pereira supplied links to the Short Sun omnibus and the two-volume Long Sun softcover and hardcover editions, and reminded the newcomer to add Urth of the New Sun. The thread ended with the question answered and no interpretive debate.
  • Antonin Scriabin: asks about omnibus editions and wants the SFBC Long Sun omnibus to complete the set
  • António Pedro Marques: advises against Long Sun and Short Sun omnibuses as too unwieldy, prefers one omnibus of the whole solar series
  • Pedro Pereira: lists Short Sun omnibus and two-volume Long Sun editions with links, recommends Urth of the New Sun
  • Jack Smith: reports owning SFBC omnibuses of the Book of the New Sun and Book of the Short Sun

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2011-03-22 Pedro Pereira replying to Antonin Scriabin #

Informs a newcomer that omnibus editions exist for the Short Sun books and that the Long Sun novels appear in two-volume softcover and hardcover editions, and recommends The Urth of the New Sun as a sequel. Responds to a new subscriber who asked whether omnibus editions of the Long Sun and Short Sun novels were available, giving Amazon links as the sources.
informs

There is an omnibus edition of Short Sun: http://www.amazon.com/book-short-sun-waters-jungles/dp/0739416898/ref=sr_1_8?s=books&ie=UTF8&qid=1300814848&sr=1-8

There are editions of the Long Sun books but in two books, both in Softcover: http://www.amazon.com/Litany-Long-Sun-Nightside-Books/dp/0312872917/ref=sr_1_4?s=books&ie=UTF8&qid=1300814914&sr=1-4

http://www.amazon.com/Epiphany-Long-Sun-Calde-Exodus/dp/0312860722/ref=pd_bxgy_b_text_b

and Hardcover:

http://www.amazon.com/Litany-Long-Sun-Gene-Wolfe/dp/B0015U6PVM/ref=sr_1_3?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1300814983&sr=1-3

http://www.amazon.com/Epiphany-Long-Calde-Exodus-Book/dp/1568652755/ref=sr_1_3?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1300815013&sr=1-3

Also don't forget to get Urth of the New Sun, a sequel (coda if you like) to the Book of the New Sun.
