Matachin Tower birth

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-11-09 to 1999-11-09

1999urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Suffering and sacrifice

1999-11-09 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that Severian's birth in the Matachin Tower was not a quick event but followed a long imprisonment of Cathy, with the wait itself serving as psychological processing. Builds the case from Ouen's account of his affair with Cathy before she was disappeared, and from the timing of the pregnancy and the lactation that would make a wetnurse unnecessary.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Severian is the child of Cathy and Ouen; Cathy was held for months before giving birth

My impression of the Matachin Tower is that it is not a "one day" service place, like a dry cleaner's shop. To the contrary, clients wait months, seasons, even years before being physically "processed" (the wait itself being a form of psychological "processing").

So Cathy the naughty monial is arrested on whatever trumped-up charges or official pretext for disappeared persons (that is, why should the city/state/government even give a fig about her; let alone put her in the maximum security prison--aye, there's a big hairy mystery).

We understand from Ouen that she and he trysted for a while before she was disappeared. If we are to believe that Severian is a product of that union, then nine months or three seasons later, Severian (and twin sister) are born.

Why hire a wetnurse when you've got this woman lactating for free? Who knows, it could be done--depends upon the application; the larger point that the assayist may be trying for.

And so on.

Born in a locked tower, to a fallen professional virgin who is probably of exultant stock, of a mysterious union with an almost unknown father (no jokes about "showers of gold," please).

=mantis=
