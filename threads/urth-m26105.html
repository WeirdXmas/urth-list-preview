Themes: Colonization and the native

2014-09-03 Lee Berman #

Praises Richard Simon's analysis comparing the Ste. Anne and Ste. Croix aboriginals in The Fifth Head of Cerberus to colonized peoples in Latin and North America, and says the debate turns on whether Wolfe asks readers to take leaps of faith. Responds directly to Simon's quoted comparison of colonial assimilation in the two Americas, offering agreement without adding new evidence.
