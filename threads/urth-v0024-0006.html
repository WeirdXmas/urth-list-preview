The thread: Kevin J. Maroney and Jack Lyons debated whether Typhon, the creator of the Whorl and Mainframe, should be read as a satanic or Demiurge-like figure, or as an Übermensch and despot akin to Hitler and Stalin. Maroney argued Pas and Typhon carry Satanic evil, linking Typhon's meeting with Severian to the temptation in the desert, while Lyons countered that the satanic reading does not fit Typhon's role in building the Whorl. The exchange ended with Maroney refining his view to combine the Demiurge and despot readings, and no agreement appears in the cards.
  • Kevin J. Maroney: Typhon is a Satan or Demiurge figure, and Pas inherits that satanic evil
  • Jack Lyons: Typhon is an Übermensch and despot like Hitler or Stalin, not Satan, since he built the Whorl and Mainframe
  • Kieran: dismissed the link between Pas and Typhon as arbitrary (as reported by Maroney)

Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies, Gods, angels and the Increate

1999-02-25 Kevin J. Maroney #

Argues that identifying Pas with Typhon reveals the moral character of the man who created the Whorl and Mainframe, and that this makes the evil behind Pas central to the struggle Silk faces. Responds to Kieran's dismissal of the BotNS link as arbitrary, grounding the point in Pas's characterization as a Satan figure rather than in Pas's own mentality, which the Long Sun books rarely show.
disputes · allegory-and-typology · theories: Pas is Typhon

Telling us that Pas was Typhon tells us a great deal about exactly what kind of man created the Whorl and Mainframe. We don't get a lot of insight into Pas's mentality throughout _Long Sun_, but we don't need to--we've met him already, and he's metaphorically Satan.

Knowing of the evil behind Pas tells us a great deal about the world against which Silk is striving.

Disputes Kevin Maroney's claim that Pas is Typhon's successor by arguing Typhon is better read as an Übermensch figure than as Satan, comparing him to Hitler and Stalin. Grounds the case in Typhon's responsibility for the creation and progress of the Whorl and Mainframe, which makes a satanic reading unfit.

1999-02-25 Kevin J. Maroney #

Argues that Typhon in The Book of the Long Sun resembles the Demiurge, the false god of this world, and is close to Satan, while also being a despot like Stalin. Grounds this by comparing Severian's meeting with Typhon in The Book of the New Sun to Christ's temptation in the desert from Luke and Matthew, and by noting Satan as Lord of Pride. Responds to Kevin's earlier post calling Typhon an Uebermensch.
disputes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: Typhon is a Demiurge figure close to Satan; Severian's meeting with Typhon echoes Christ's temptation

This should be moved over to Whorl probably...

In _The Book of the New Sun_, Severian's meeting with Typhon deliberately echos Christ's temptation in the desert (from Luke and Matthew), complete with the three temptations. Severian, being more hands-on than Jesus, does more than order Typhon to get behind him, but it's clear what Typhon is.

In _The Book of the Long Sun_, the builder of the _Whorl_ is very much like the Demiurge, the false God of This World who tries to distract us from worshiping the true God. The Demiurge isn't *exactly* Satan, but he's very close.

None of what you say is false: Typhon the man is very much like Stalin or any other despot, moved to the ultimate degree--remaking mountains in his image, building his own universe of worshippers. But remember that Satan is the Lord of Pride, which Typhon has in abundance.
