2001-02-19 William H. Ansley #

Thanks Robert for his elaborate interpretation of Wolfe's Long Sun books while saying he disagrees with nearly every part of it. Says he feels Wolfe has reached new heights of obscurity in the Short Sun series and despairs of understanding the books, though he admires the imagination and work behind the theories.
chats

Robert,

They say every reader brings something of his own to every book he reads. You seem to bring more than most, in fact you seem to bring an entirely different book. At least it seems this way to me.

But I applaud your efforts and marvel at your imagination, ingenuity and the amount of work you put into laying out your theories.

And I want to thank you. It is a great comfort to me that even though I feel that Wolfe has reached new heights of obscurity in the SS series and I despair at ever grasping what is going on in the books I still disagree with almost every aspect of your interpretation of them. 8->

William Ansley
