Reports a review by John Clute of Paul J. McAuley's Confluence trilogy that compares it systematically with Wolfe's books, and says the poster agrees with Clute's analysis. Recommends the series, calling its final volume a redemptive parody of The Urth of the New Sun while noting McAuley downplays the homage.
informs · genre-and-craft · theories: Shrine of Stars is a redemptive parody of The Urth of the New Sun

In his review of Paul J. McAuley's CONFLUENCE series,
just posted on SF WEEKLY, John Clute does a systematic
comparison of the trilogy with THE BOOKS by Wolfe. I
agree with Clute's analysis; but McAuley himself
downplays the homage element. In any event, I strongly
recommend CONFLUENCE to everyone; SHRINE OF STARS, the
final volume, is very like a redemptive parody of THE
URTH OF THE NEW SUN.
