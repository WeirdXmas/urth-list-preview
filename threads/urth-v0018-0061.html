Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Gods, angels and the Increate

1998-08-30 Tony A. West #

Asks what large opaque object caused the eclipse over the stone town near the end of The Urth of the New Sun, and whether it was external or tied to Severian/Apu-Punchau. Floats the idea that the three Hierodules, after hearing Severian's story in the House of Day, contrived the eclipse; also says the answer to the three Hierodules was already presented on rereading.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Hierodules contrived the eclipse after hearing Severian's story
