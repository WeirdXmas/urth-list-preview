Cro-Magnons and Neanderthals in Tracking Song?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-07-02 to 1998-07-02

1998urthmary whalen

Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

1998-07-02 mary whalen #

Proposes that the conflict and possible eventual union between Cro-Magnons and Neanderthals parallels events or characters in Tracking Song, and asks whether that fits the story. Grounds the idea in the etymology of Neanderthal, noting that Neander means new man in Greek and that the valley was named for Mr. Neumann.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Tracking Song parallels Cro-Magnon and Neanderthal conflict

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

I just had the thought that perhaps the struggle (and possible eventual union) between the Cro-Magnons and Neanderthals is parallelled by action or characters in Tracking Song in some way.

By the way, in spite of their antiquity, the Neander of Neanderthal (however way you wish to pronounce it) means new man in Greek. Relevant to the story? The reason is that they were first found in the valley (thal) that belonged to Mr. Neumann (Neander).
