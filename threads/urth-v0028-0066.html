Ancients of Days

1999-08-17 Kieran Cleary #

Reports having finished Paul J. McAuley's Ancients of Days, the second Confluence book, and argues the series lives up to expectations set by Greg Bear's short story Recording Angel in New Legends. Asks other list members for their views on the series and mentions an Amazon related-authors list that suggests Wolfe readers also buy Dan Simmons and Michael Swanwick rather than spy novelists.
chats

Just finished reading Paul J. McAuley's second Confluence book, Ancients of Days, and contrary to the faint praise of a previous posting of mine, I think that the series is living up to the expectations created (for me) by the related short story Recording Angel in Greg Bear's New Legends (to which I am also indebted for introducing me to Greg Egan).

Anyway, I'd be interested to hear coments from other Urthlings about the series.

Vizcacha, regarding the related-authors list from Amazon,

Frederick Forsyth
John Le Carre
Daniel Silva
Joseph Kanon
William Safire"<<

I recently saw the following on Amazon (after another despairing, craven visit to the "On Blue's Waters" section):

"Customers who bought titles by Gene Wolfe also bought titles by these authors:

Dan Simmons
Michael Swanwick
Patricia A. McKillip
Alfred Bester
Theodore Sturgeon "

which would seem to be *slightly* more on the mark.
