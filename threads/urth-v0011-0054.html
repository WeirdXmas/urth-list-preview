1998urthYe Olde Mothman

1998-05-15 Ye Olde Mothman #

Argues that the final Seinfeld episode's jail-cell ending recalls Sartre's No Exit and its line 'Hell is other people', rather than Severian's examination before his past. Responds to an earlier post that had noted the Urth examination scene and expected White Fountain references, offering a different literary parallel for the same ending.
