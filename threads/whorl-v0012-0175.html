Horn's spirit

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-02-11 to 2001-02-11

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunJacob Corbin

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Ghosts, dreams and visions

2001-02-11 Jacob Corbin #

Asks when Horn's spirit left Silk's body, doubting that Remora's passage alone explains it, and whether Silk's astral form still resembled Horn aboard the ship on Ocean. Also floats a separate idea that Jahlee's attack on Nettle was prompted by her drinking blood from the amoral people of New Viron.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Horn's spirit left Silk at a time other than Remora's passage; Jahlee's attack was precipitated by drinking New Viron blood

I agree with the posters who've pointed out that there was more of Horn in Silk than a set of memories. I wonder, though, if we can pin down a time when Horn's spirit left Silk's body -- somehow I doubt that Remora's passage was all that was involved. I don't have my copy to hand, so does anyone remember if Horn/Silk's astral self still bore a resemblance to Horn when the ship was on Ocean? If so, perhaps the final disposition of Horn's spirit was one of the subjects discussed in the meeting with the Urthly Scylla.

On a completely different topic altogether, did anyone else think that perhaps Jahlee's murderous attack on Nettle was perhaps precipitated, at least in part, by her drinking the blood of the amoral denizens of New Viron?

Jacob G Corbin
