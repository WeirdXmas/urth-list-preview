Severian among the Ascians

Urth list · 11 messages · 2008-12-11 to 2008-12-12

2008urthThe Book of the New SunJeffrey Brent McBethAdam ThorntonJosh GellerDavid StockhoffSon of WitzDave TallmanJohn Smith
The thread: Posters asked when Severian spent a year among the Ascians, a question prompted by a Lexicon Urthus entry. Dave Tallman and David Stockhoff proposed the year fell after he became Autarch and before going to Briah, probably after a battle capture paralleling the old Autarch's unrecognized capture. John Smith questioned whether the text states this at all, and Son of Witz and Stockhoff disputed the entry's speculation lacking textual support. Tallman replied that Urth chapter 51 cites the year without saying slavery, and Stockhoff added a 1982 Wolfe remark about slavery while noting the interview does not say so. Adam Thornton's Mormon and fairy-tale analogies drew side debates about Jesus's missing years and whether Polynesian legends could link to America, which drifted away from Wolfe. The thread ended without resolving whether the text supports the claim.
  • Dave Tallman: Ascian year falls after Autarchy and before Briah, likely after a battle capture; chapter 51 supports the year but not slavery
  • David Stockhoff: Same post-Autarch timing, with capture and enslavement; 1982 Wolfe remark predates Urth of the New Sun
  • John Smith: Doubts the text states the Ascian year; sees it as an inference from clever reasoning
  • Son of Witz: Enjoys the Lexicon's speculation but wants it backed by passages from the text
  • Adam Thornton: Offers analogies (Mormon missing years, year-and-a-day trope) rather than textual evidence
  • brunians: Speculates a boat could carry travelers to America and cites Polynesian stories, saying they are probably unconnected to Mormon belief

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Memory and forgetting, Slavery and service

2008-12-11 Dave Tallman replying to David Stockhoff #

Argues that Severian's year among the Ascians falls after he became Autarch and before he left for Briah, most likely after he was captured while leading a battle against them. Builds the case on the timeline (Autarch in 1 S.R., Briah in 10 S.R.) and on the parallel with his earlier capture alongside the old Autarch, when he was not recognized. Responds to David Stockhoff's question about the Lexicon Urthus entry.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Severian was captured by Ascians after becoming Autarch

It must be after he became Autarch (1 S.R.) and before leaving for Briah (10 S.R.). The most likely scenario is that he was captured while leading a battle against the Ascians. Just like the first time he and the old Autarch were captured, he wouldn't have been recognized as Autarch. He was enslaved by them for about a year.

2008-12-12 David Stockhoff replying to David Stockhoff #

Points out that Wolfe, in a 1982 remark, mentioned Severian's year as a slave of the Ascians, and argues that this predates The Urth of the New Sun, where the assassin episode appears. Notes that Lexicon Urthus cites Urth chapter 51 and a Wolfe interview for the Ascian year, though the interview does not say Severian was a slave.
informs · author-and-biography · source-and-allusion

The text is here:

Again I endured my imprisonment in the jungle ziggurat by Vodalus, the year I had spent among the Ascians, my flight from the white wolves in the Secret House; and a thousand similiar terrors, until at last it seemed to me that a demon desired that I surrender my miserable existence to Apu-Punchau, and that the demon was myself.

Wolfe is here:

Wolfe: "There was the time when Severian encountered assassins in the Secret House who had come to kill Ymar, an autarch a chiliad dead. I may write about that sometime. And the year he spent as a slave of the Ascians. But I doubt that either will make it into print."

Since this was in 1982, it was before he wrote TUotNS, in which Severian does indeed meet a corpse of an assassin who comes to life as he watches, then presumably stabs Valeria.
