2011-06-08 Jeff Wilson replying to Son of Witz #

Explains how to stop LinkedIn invitation emails from reaching a personal inbox by having them pile up on the site instead, with the site's reminders being easier to filter. Responds to Son of Witz's post, which quoted a complaint about repeated unwanted LinkedIn emails from a former business partner. Off-topic social advice with no connection to Wolfe's fiction.
answers

If you join the collective, instead of e-mailing you directly, the invites pile up in your inbox at LinkedIn instead of coming straight to your e-mail address. LinkedIn itself will remind you this is happening once in a awhile, but the reminders aren't spoofed and can be more easily filtered out.
