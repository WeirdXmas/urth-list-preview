Wolfe v Blish ?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-06-02 to 1999-06-02

1999urthDavid Duffy

1999-06-02 David Duffy #

Compares Wolfe with James Blish, noting Wolfe's Catholic faith against Blish's atheism and their different literary tastes, such as Borges and Chesterton versus Joyce and Cabell. Suggests the ambiguous natural-versus-supernatural ending of Blish's City of Cosmic Chaos resembles Wolfe's open endings, and asks what ending the Mantis would have preferred for Theron Ware.
proposes · author-and-biography

A difference is that Wolfe actually is Catholic, but Blish IIRC was an atheist; and while Wolfe likes Borges and Chesterton, Blish liked Joyce and Cabell (and Star Trek) ;). The ending of CoC has been discussed a bit (maybe in Foundation). It might be reminiscent of Wolfe in that there are natural and supernatural interpretations, with no definitive answer one way or another.

David Duffy, wondering what ending Mantis would have preferred for Theron Ware?
