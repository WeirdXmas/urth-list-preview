Proposes that Fava's soul-self may have come from Silk rather than another source, such as Patera Pike, arguing from her yellow hair. Cites Silk's yellow hair, Fava's kindness compared to Jahlee, her schooling, and the Rajan saving her childhood, noting that time-loops need starters. Responds to James Wynn.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Fava's soul came from Silk; Fava's soul came from Patera Pike; Fava's soul-self has yellow hair

If we establish yellow hair for Fava's soul-self, that opens the possibility of a different Fava/Silk connection. Silk has yellow hair, and that color seems to be fairly rare in Viron. Could Fava's soul have come from Silk? She's nicer than many inhuma (compare her to Jahlee). Like Silk, she did well at school both in both academic subjects and athletics. The Rajan was drawn to save her life in her early childhood on Green. (Wolfean time-loops require starters -- if she never survived childhood the Rajan could never have heard her story and inserted himself into it).

Another possible soul source is Patera Pike, if the clone theory is correct. Pike knew something about the inhumi and how to ward them off, knowledge he passed on to Silk.
