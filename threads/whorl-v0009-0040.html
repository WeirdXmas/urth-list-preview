Themes: Memory and forgetting

1998-11-03 Kevin J. Maroney #

Points out that the opening lines of Wolfe's story "The Night Chough" read "Silk was gone. It had to remember that," suggesting a possible echo for the question of whether Silk has truly vanished from the Long Sun narrative. Offers the quotation as a textual parallel to Alex's doubt about Silk's return, citing the story's first sentences as the evidence.
informs · source-and-allusion

"Silk was gone. It had to remember that."

(These are the first sentences of Wolfe's "The Night Chough" in the anthology _The Crow: Shattered Lives and Broken Dreams_, ed. James O'Barr and Ed Kramer.)
