Offers an Eastern Orthodox perspective on the list's discussion of resurrected souls and bodies, arguing that Horn, Silk, Pas and Pig would each see their creator at the Universal Resurrection and carry traces of their merger into transfigured life. Responds to the earlier joking exchange about out-referencing the barroom and Borges, praising the references so far.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: Horn and Silk see creator at Universal Resurrection

I am enjoying watching the multiple Catholic
viewpoints and just wanted to put in that you have an
Eastern Orthodox viewer.

To that end I find the point of Resurrected
souls/bodies discussion an outstanding one. In
Orthodoxy both Horn and Silk (and Pas and Pig for that
matter) would "...see my creator ... with my own
eyes..." for the Universal Resurrection. Each would
presumably carry the residue of the merger into his
transfigured life.

As far as Alga saying:

I wouldn't take that bet. The references have been
pretty damn good so far.
