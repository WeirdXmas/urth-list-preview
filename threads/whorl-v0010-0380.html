OBW: Yet another Inhumi secret theory

Whorl list · 3 messages · 2000-07-08 to 2000-07-08

2000whorlThe Book of the Short SunNicholas GeversDavid Lomax
The thread: David Lomax proposed that the Inhumi neither kill nor drink the sentience of people who willingly give themselves to them, grounding this in Krait sparing Horn once Horn had properly given his word, while admitting the idea might be too simplistic. Nicholas Gevers replied twice with a variant: that willing donors would make the absorbed sentience benign and self-sacrificial, and so make the Inhumi themselves more benign. He offered it as more likely than an earlier theory, without citing textual evidence. The posts show a short exchange on the Inhumi secret with no recorded rebuttal or resolution.
  • David Lomax: Inhumi spare and do not drink the sentience of those who willingly give themselves, tied to giving as a theme, grounded in Krait sparing Horn
  • Nicholas Gevers: willing donors make the absorbed sentience benign and self-sacrificial, so the Inhumi become more benign

Themes: Suffering and sacrifice, Christ figures and messiahs

2000-07-08 David Lomax #

Proposes a new theory of the Inhumi secret: that the Inhumi would neither kill nor drink the sentience of people who willingly give themselves over to them, linking this to giving as a theme. Grounds the idea in Krait's refusal to kill Horn once Horn has properly given his word, and admits the theory may be too simplistic.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Inhumi spare those who willingly give themselves over

All,

Pardon for going back to a dead horse, stick in hand, but I just got around to reading OBW (in preparation for the next which I believe is coming out soon). Immediately, I went and read a whole bunch of old posts from this list which I had been saving for the occasion. I'm fascinated with the quest for the Inhumi secret (which I acknowledge may be unknowable given our present information). Like most of you folks, I had rejected the "can't we all just get along" hypothesis and many of the other likely candidates. One I haven't seen suggested (perhaps because I haven't read _all_ the old posts) has to do with giving.

Giving, people giving themselves to others, seems an important theme for Wolfe. What if the secret went something like this: if people were to willingly give themselves over to the Inhumi, then (1) the Inhumi wouldn't be able to kill the people (much as Krait doesn't kill Horn or Horn's people once Horn has properly given his word) and (2) the Inhumi would, for some reason, no longer drink sentience along with the blood.

This is not a satisfying theory, mostly because it seems too simplistic, but I thought it might spark of someone else's ideas. If it's a repetition of stuff that's already been said, my apologies.

David Lomax

2000-07-08 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that humans giving themselves to the Inhumi would make the sentience the Inhumi absorb benign and self-sacrificial, which would make the Inhumi themselves more benign. Offered as the more likely alternative to an earlier Inhumi theory in reply to David Lomax in the On Blue's Waters thread, with no textual evidence cited.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Humans' self-sacrifice makes the Inhumi benign

More likely, by giving themselves to the inhumi,
humans would see to it that the sentience the inhumi
imbibed was of a benign, self-sacrificial flavour--a
means of rendering the inhumi themselves more benign.
