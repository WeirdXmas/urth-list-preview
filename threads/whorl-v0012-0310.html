Did Pig Kill Silk?

Whorl list · 2 messages · 2001-02-19 to 2001-02-19

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunJames Jordan

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2001-02-19 James Jordan #

Questions whether Pig could have believed he killed Silk when Silk is plainly alive, since Pig claims only to have killed an augur. Argues that Pig's shallow arm cuts make a poor killing job, and notes this fits badly with the thesis that Silk can revive.
questions · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk died and revived via Tartaros

Well, Borski has sure done a job for us. But I have one question: Pig says he killed an augur. Okay. But "Silk" is very much alive, as he knows as well as anyone. Thus, it does not appear to me that he could believe that he had killed Silk. I should think he would say, "I tried to kill an augur." I guess it also seems to me that Pig would have done a more thorough job. A bunch of cuts on the arms is not a good killing job. A beheading, or a thrust through the heart would be better. Of course, on your thesis, Silk had to be able to "revive," so "Tartaros" had to kill him in a rather "indirect" way. But my first question still stands.
Untie this knot for me.

Patera Nutria
