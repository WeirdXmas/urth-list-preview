1999-04-09 Roy C. Lackey #

Supports Robert Borski's idea that Dorcas is pregnant and that the basket Severian sees in Oldgate holds a baby. Notes that he had also read Dorcas's vomiting scenes and the basket as irrelevant before, and now connects them with Borski's reading.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Dorcas is pregnant with Severian's child; The basket in Oldgate contains a baby

sickness? Severian and Dorcas have been having sex long enough for her to be entering this stage of pregnancy, and it's interesting to note that nine or so months later, when Severian sees Dorcas for the last time in Oldgate, "on the floor near her was a basket, not small yet not large either." Could this basket perhaps contain a baby?<<

Damn! I just reread the series, noted and wondered about the basket (seemingly irrelevant to what was going on; she wasn't on a picnic), then forgot it. I think you're right. The idea of morning sickness had also occured to me when I read the vomiting scenes. I should have put them together!

Roy
