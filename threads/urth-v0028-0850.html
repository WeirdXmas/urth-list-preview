Themes: Power, rulers and politics

2000-03-15 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Quotes Typhon's speech from the Urth of the New Sun section in which he explains that he made Urth his capital and was besieged there after his followers fled the stars. The post offers the passage without commentary, letting the quoted words speak for the claim that Typhon was trapped on Urth.
informs · close-textual-reading

Indeed, verily.

"This world, the most ancient of all, I made my capital. That was an error, because I lingered too long when disaster came. By the time I would have escaped, escape was no longer open to me--those to whom I had given control of such ships as could reach the stars had fled in them, and I was besieged on this mountain. I shall not make that mistake again. My capital will be elsewhere, and I will give this world to you, to rule as my steward" (III, ch. 26, p. 213).
