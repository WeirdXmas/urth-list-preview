Whos met or written to Wolfe

Urth list · 8 messages · 2008-05-11 to 2008-05-13

2008urthThe Book of the New SunOliver WakefieldJosh GellerAdam ThorntonAndy RobertsonEric Ortlund
The thread: Members asked whether anyone had met Gene Wolfe or written to him, and how to reach his agent for a fan letter. Adam Thornton and brunians shared convention encounters with Wolfe, describing him as a very nice man, while brunians reported that letters sent through his publisher went unanswered. Oliver Wakefield, who cannot attend conventions from abroad, asked about writing through the agent and later joked that Wolfe rarely answers fan mail. The thread stayed personal and light, with no debate about the books and no clear resolution beyond the shared sense that Wolfe is hard to reach.
  • Adam Thornton: met Wolfe at Archon after asking him a question at ReaderCon 1999, and felt awkward there
  • Oliver Wakefield: wants to send a short appreciative fan letter through the agent since he cannot attend conventions
  • Eric Ortlund: asks how to reach Wolfe's agent, admitting the question may be naive
  • brunians: met Wolfe twice at conventions decades apart, but letters through the publisher went unanswered

2008-05-11 Oliver Wakefield #

Asks whether other list members have met Gene Wolfe or written to him, and whether writing a fan letter through his agent is a bad idea. Explains the poster cannot attend conventions from abroad and wants a short appreciative letter, with no discussion of the books' interpretation.
questions

Hi

Jus wondering if anyones met Wolfe? Scanning the net ive discovered the sci-fi convention and sadly due to my location will probably never go to one. It saddens me that I'll probably never meet Wolfe. I don't have the funds to travel to the US yet.

As that in the case I'm considering writing a letter to Wolfe. I must say that such things I have always thought of as obssessive and a bit odd but as Wolfe is a relatively low profile author there is always the tantalising chance of a reply to show the grandchildren. I must say that reading Wolfe has been probably the most enriching experiences in my life and my girlfriend has to put up with my rants of his greatness.

Have any of you guys attempted this. I know you can contact him through his agent. If you think this a really bad idea please tell me. I am aware that the answers are in the book its more a 'your great, keep up the good work' sort of letter.

2008-05-12 Josh Geller replying to Eric Ortlund #

Describes the poster's attempts to contact Gene Wolfe through his publisher without reply, and their two meetings with him at science fiction conventions decades apart. Reflects that although the poster feels they know Wolfe from his writing, Wolfe does not know them, and says he seems a very nice man; it responds to Eric Ortlund's question about how to reach Wolfe's agent.
informs

Normally, one writes an author in care of his publisher. I have done this with Wolfe and received no reply even after years, which hurt my feelings a bit until I reflected that he is a busy guy and certainly had no obligation to write me back. The problem, of course, is that having read as much of his stuff as I have I feel that I know him a bit, and maybe I do, but he does not (of course) know me at all.

I've also met him twice, like fifteen or twenty years apart, at the only two SF conventions that I have attended. He does indeed seem a very nice man.

.

chats

Dear Reader

Thanks for praise of my books. Severian is acctually erebus' son and abaia's his uncle and he didn't really save the New Sun. It was all a dream. Now leave me alone.

Gene Wolfe
