Themes: Myth and folklore sources

2005-06-06 Stanisław Bocian #

Reports finding the word Akhkharu in William Burroughs' Cities of Red Night, described there as blood-sucking beings who desire to become men, and says the poster could not learn more about them. Asks for information on these supposed Sumerian or Babylonian vampires, placing the question in a Wolfe discussion thread about the word.
questions · source-and-allusion

In a book by William Burroughs, "Cities of Red Night" I found the following quotation "Akhkharu, who suck the blood of men since they desire to become men".

Akhkharu are apparently Sumerian or Babylonian vampires, but I was unable to learn anything else about them.
