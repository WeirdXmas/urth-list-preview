2001-03-09 Adam Stephanides #

Asks what Father means in saying he feels sorry for Beroep when speaking with Hoof about Scylla in New Viron, a passage the poster says he did not understand. Quotes the line from page 339 and says he is also puzzled by the reference to Beroep.
questions · close-textual-reading

This may just be something I missed, but in New Viron "Father" is talking to Hoof about Scylla, "and I asked him if there had ever been a real Scylla. 'Yes,' he said. 'Yes, that's the terrible part.' Then he said something I did not understand at all: 'I feel sorry for Beroep.'" (339) I'm also mystified by the reference to Beroep.

--Adam

2001-03-09 Dave Lebling #

Asks readers to solve two mysteries Wolfe leaves unstated in In Green's Jungles (why Inclito's brother was called Incanto) and Return to the Whorl (Silk's last, troubling explanation for astral travel). Notes Wolfe's habit of having Silk or Horn claim to know an answer and then withholding it, and invites more examples.
questions · puzzle-solving

Wolfe has a habit of introducing a mystery, having Silk/Horn state that he knows the answer, and then not saying what it is. Does anyone here know the answers to:

1) IGJ. Silk/Horn asks Mora(?) if she knows why her father's brother was called Incanto, then declares that he knows why, but he never tells us the answer. Why was Inclito's brother called Incanto?

2) RttW. Silk/Horn gives several explanations for how he can perform astral travel without an inhumu present. He lists several explicit ones (hidden inhumi, etc.) then a final one which worries him, but doesn't say what it is. What is it?

Feel free to add more to this list...

Dave Lebling
aka vizcacha
