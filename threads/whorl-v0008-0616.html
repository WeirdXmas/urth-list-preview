Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1998-03-03 G R Hail #

Informs readers that Gina Kolata's 1998 book Clone mentions Gene Wolfe's The Fifth Head of Cerberus as a work shaped by late-1960s and early-1970s discussion of cloning. Reports that the book gives a plot summary of the novel and relates it to public fears and hopes about cloning, citing page 114.
informs

Gene Wolfe's the _Fifth Head of Cerberus_ is mentioned as a work influenced by the discussion of cloning in the later 1960s and early 1970s in _Clone_ by Gina Kolata (William & Morrow, 1998) on page 114. A plot summary is offered and the work is related to the fears and hopes people have about cloning.
