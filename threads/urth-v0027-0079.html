Compares the staging and dialogue of Blish's A Case of Conscience to the blunt declamatory style of Cordwainer Smith's Scanners Live in Vain, as a contrast to Smith's Alpha Ralpha Boulevard. Responds to Nigel Price's earlier remark about Shaw-like dialectic, and says the comparison was something he meant to raise.
