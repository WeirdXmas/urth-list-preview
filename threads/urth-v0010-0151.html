1998-05-12 Tony Ellis #

Disputes Mantis's claim that the crowd shootings are ancient practice, arguing that the roads were closed only in Palaemon's lifetime and the mounted soldiers are his patrols. Cites Palaemon's account in Shadow XIII of uhlans ordered to kill travellers, and Dr Talos's remark that watchers seeking one man would sally out and seize him rather than fire indiscriminately.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Gate soldiers are Palaemon's patrols, not a redoubt; Roads closed only in Palaemon's lifetime

12/05/98
11:07 Meanwhile, back at the gate

Wrong. This _is_ standard operating procedure, as Gene Wolfe is careful to make Master Palaemon tell us: "...there is a redoubt, so I've heard, every fifty leagues... [the roads] are patrolled by uhlans under orders to kill anyone found on them, and since they have permission to loot the bodies of those they slay, they are not much inclined to ask excuses." Shadow, XIII

And no-one is suggesting that firing on the crowd has been going on for "chiliads" as you say. We are specifically told that the roads have only been closed in Master Palaemon's lifetime. Since the soldiers are mounted they are clearly one of Palaemon's patrols rather than a redoubt, and it is simply the travellers' misfortune to encounter them here.

I find far sillier the idea that

These watchers spend a lifetime watching for a familiar face, and yet when they see one they don't block-off the gate, or infiltrate the crowd, but station soldiers _outside_ the gate with orders to fire indiscriminately? Hardly. As Dr Talos says "if they were to see the one they seek, they would sally out and lay hold of him."
