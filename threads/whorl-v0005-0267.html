Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1997-05-14 Dave Lebling #

Expresses delight that Gene Wolfe is available to answer questions, and lists a question about whether Silk's manteion sighting was Pike's ghost or an aquastor. Also asks Wolfe about his moustache and chats about Patricia Anthony novels and a compilation of mysteries.
chats

This is like being a kid in a candy store, but with only one quarter to spend!

"Was what Silk saw in the manteion Pike's ghost, or an aquastor, or what?"

and "What about..." No, no, I'll restrain myself. I can do it... I'll save the other 800 questions for another time.

This could be a lot of fun, and it's very kind of Mr. Wolfe to do it.

-vizcacha
([email])

p.s. to Patrick: Agree wholeheartedly about _There Are Birds_. Have you tried _Humpty Dumpty: An Oval_? I'll have to check out _God's Fires_, as I loved _Cold Allies_ and _Brother Termite_, but haven't read any other Patricia Anthony since.

p.p.s: Bonus question for Gene Wolfe: "How long have you been wearing a moustache?" (Saw a picture of him with same in SF Chronicle; he hasn't always had one, has he?) Try to be oracular about that one!

p.p.p.s: mantis, if you run out of questions (hah!), send him my compilation of mysteries, literary or whorlographic.
