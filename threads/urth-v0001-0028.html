Proposes that the French film The City of Lost Children draws heavily on Gene Wolfe stories, naming The Island of Doctor Death and Other Stories and The Fifth Head of Cerberus as its sources. Offers no close analysis; simply recommends the film to list readers as an uncanny resemblance to Wolfe's fiction.

Anybody else out there see this great French science fantasy movie in the cinema a year or two ago, or recently see it on video, and think: "Wow, what a great movie made out of a bunch of Gene Wolfe stories!"?

No, =really=! "The Island of Doctor Death and Other Stories" plus "The Fifth Head of Cerberus," for starters.

Uncanny. Highly recommended. "The City of Lost Children."
