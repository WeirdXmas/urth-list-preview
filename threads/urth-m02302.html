2006-06-16 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs the list that David Herter is a Wolfean writer whose novel Ceres Storm recalls Wolfe, and that PS Publishing will issue his novellas On the Overgrown Path and The Luminous Depths. Describes Overgrown Path as Wolfean in technique, involving composer Leos Janacek and a chthonic mystery in Slovakian mountains; no argument is developed.
informs

David Herter is a good Wolfean writer: his novel CERES
STORM in particular recalls GW. The small press for
which I work, PS Publishing, will be issuing two of
his novellas, ON THE OVERGROWN PATH and THE LUMINOUS
DEPTHS, the first later this year. OTOP is distinctly
Lupine in technique, involving the composer Leos
Janacek and a strange chthonic mystery in the
mountains of Slovakia.

--Nick Gevers.
