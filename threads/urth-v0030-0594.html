Delany, Splendor and Misery...

2001-08-02 Jonathan Laidlow #

Informs the list that an extract from the second part of Samuel Delany's diptych was published in The Review of Contemporary Fiction in 1996, and how to obtain an electronic copy. Also mentions another Delany extract, 'Phallos', that appeared in Callaloo, offering to supply details later.
informs

The day I decide I have 10 seconds to actually read an
Urth digest I find I can contribute again!

An extract from the second part of the diptych *was*
published in a literary journal. I purchased an
electronic version of the article from
www.northernlight.com (search term: delany, splendor,
then click on 'special collections documents' on the
left hand side)

It was originally published in *The Review of
Contemporary Fiction* Fall/1996, (v16 n3) Start Page:
p103 ISSN: 0276-0045.

I recommend it, but it is tantalising. Delany has
actually published a few things of interest in
literary journals, but I can't remember the reference
- one was called 'extract from Phallos!' in Callaloo,
but you'll have to remind me to look for the full
details if you want them.

Sorry for lack of communication folks! New job is fun,
rewarding, and busy..

Jonathan
www.english.bham.ac.uk/ultan
