Themes: Fathers, mothers and children

2015-04-30 Marc Aramini replying to Lee #

Disputes the biographical reading that ties Gene Wolfe's family troubles to Horn and his sons in the Short Sun books, saying the poster is not interested in that kind of exegesis. Corrects the replied-to poster by noting the difficulties involved a younger son, not the oldest, and points to the autobiographical background in How I Got 3 Zip Codes.
disputes · author-and-biography · theories: Horn's troubles mirror Wolfe's difficulties with his son

Yes, certainly Short Sun has that autobiographical synchronicity ... the difficulties with one in particular were quite pronounced, though I do not think it was his oldest son.

In How I Got 3 Zip Codes I felt the background bio information was worth it since it is basically Gene on the post office (or the real story behind Car Sinister)... but I am not certain the Korean war influence on New Sun or Wolfe's parental struggles on Horn and his family are the type of exegesis I am interested in, as Wolfe seems particularly against the excavations of the biographer... perhaps when enough time has passed, when the things like the disintegration of the relationship between melville and Hawthorne can be speculated upon without injury to either, I would feel more comfortable exploring stuff like that as pertinent to the literature ...

2015-04-30 Lee Berman #

Proposes that Wolfe's real difficulty with his older son is reflected in Horn's troubled relationship with his oldest son Sinew and the monstrous surrogate son Krait in the Long Sun books. Responds to Marc Aramini's remark that Wolfe has two sons, offering the parallel as a personal reading rather than citing evidence from the text.
proposes · author-and-biography · theories: Horn's troubles with Sinew mirror Wolfe's difficulty with his older son

I am pretty sure some of the (published) difficulty Wolfe had with his older son is reflected

in the fictional difficulty Horn has with his oldest son, Sinew, and his monstrous surrogate

son, Krait.
