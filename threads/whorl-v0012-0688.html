The thread: Readers debated whether Horn in The Book of the Long Sun can travel astrally, and where his Neighborly ability comes from. Robert Borski argued Horn's Neighborliness comes from Passilk ancestry and noted he never astral-travels despite owning a ring and an inhumu. Fernando Gouvea countered that Horn's sudden return to the island with Nettle, while he was in the pit, might be an involuntary astral trip, and suggested the inhumu Krait may be nearby. Michael Straight then disputed the wider theory that Pas or Passilk is a Neighbor who possessed Silk or Horn, granting only that Silk|Horn shares Silk's nature. The thread ends without resolution, with the astral-travel question left open.
  • Robert Borski: Horn's Neighborliness comes from Passilk ancestry, and he never astral-travels despite owning a ring and inhumu
  • Fernando Q. Gouvea: Horn's sudden return to the island may be his first involuntary astral trip; Krait may be nearby
  • Michael Straight: no evidence that Pas or Passilk is a Neighbor possessing Silk or Horn; Silk|Horn shares Silk's nature but has no Typhon

Themes: Time loops and causality, Doubles, twins and clones, Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies

2001-04-21 Robert Borski #

Questions whether the Narrator alone can astral-travel in The Book of the Long Sun, challenging the claim that Horn's Neighborly ability stems from remolding by Neighbor technomagic. Argues Horn's Neighborliness comes from Passilk ancestry, and notes that Horn owns a ring from Seawrack yet never astral-travels despite access to an inhumu.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Horn's Neighborliness comes from Passilk ancestry; Horn's astral travel is not caused by the ring

The water rat, Dan'l D'Tree and Kevin M. all contributing to the following thread:

not just any human, because of his Neighborliness.>

<I wondered about that. Do Horn's sons ever initiate travel on their own? If not, then I've already given a reason why the Narrator would have a Neighborly ability that no other human has--his life was completely remolded by the Neighbors' magic/science in a way that no other human's was.>

<Say... doesn't the astral travelling only begin after Oreb brings the Narr back the ring?>

My own explanation for Horn's Neighborliness, of course, is that he's part Passilk, and Pas is selon moi a Neighbor--not that Horn's life has been reconstructed/reconstituted by the technomagic of the Neighbors. The net result is still the same.

Also: isn't the ring Oreb brings back a different ring? And as far as this being the instigator of Horn's astral travels, he's given the original ring early on by Seawrack, but despite access to an inhumu (Krait), never does any astral traveling. If you wish to argue this is because he has not been transformed by the Neighbors yet, why given him the ring in the first place--since it's unlikely the Neighbors know Horn will eventually be uploaded in Silk's body?

Robert Borski

2001-04-21 Fernando Q. Gouvea #

Argues that Horn's sudden return to the island with Nettle, while he is in the pit, may be his first involuntary astral trip, which would undercut the claim that he never travels astrally. Responds to Robert Borski's point that Horn owns a ring and an inhumu yet never uses them for astral travel, and suggests the inhumu Krait may be nearby.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Horn's pit return was involuntary astral travel

I don't have the book here, but if I'm remembering right that looks like a first astral travel incident, completely involuntary on Horn's part. Presumably, Krait is nearby at that point.

Fernando

2001-04-22 Michael Straight #

Disputes the theory that Pas or Passilk is a Neighbor who possessed Silk, Horn or Silk|Horn, arguing there is no evidence of such possession. Responds to Robert Borski's claim that Horn is part Passilk and Pas a Neighbor, granting only that Silk|Horn shares Silk's nature but denying any Typhon in it.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon is a Neighbor who possessed Silk or Horn

Never mind the wacky Typhon-is-a-neighbor theory, I don't think there's any evidence that Pas or Passilk ever posessed Silk, Horn, or Silk|Horn. Silk|Horn is Passilk inasmuch as there's some Silk in both of them, but there's no Typhon in Silk|Horn.

-Rostrum
