Summa Contra Hieros

Urth list · 3 messages · 2008-07-14 to 2008-07-14

2008urthThe Book of the New SunDan'l Danehy-Oakesb sharpPaul B
The thread: Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argued that the Hiero-Foos manipulating cosmic cycles mirrors Mr. Million's warping of his children, with the Line of Millions in The Fifth Head of Cerberus as grounds. b sharp disputed this, saying the Hiero-Foos are superhuman and foresee outcomes while Million was human and could not, partly playing devil's advocate. Paul B then challenged the omniscience assumption, pointing to Tzadkiel's admission that his guess about Severian was only probabilistic and asking for textual support for such claims. The thread ended without resolution, with the omniscience question left open.
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Hiero-Foos' cosmic meddling is the same act as Mr. Million's, on a larger scale
  • b sharp: Hiero-Foos differ because they are superhuman and foresee results, unlike human Million
  • Paul B: Hierogrammates are not omniscient; Tzadkiel only guessed probabilistically about Severian

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, The dying sun and the cosmos, Free will and choice

2008-07-14 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues that the Hiero-Foos' manipulation of entire cosmic cycles is the same act as Mr. Million's warping of his own children to ensure his repetition, only on a cosmic scale. Grounds the claim on the Line of Millions in The Fifth Head of Cerberus, concluding that if the Millions are wrong the Hiero-Foos are far more so.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Hiero-Foos and Mr. Million act alike

When you come right down to it.

Isn't what the Hiero-Foos are doing,
the same thing Mr. Million/Maitre/Number 5 do,
only on a cosmic scale?

Rather than warping their own children to ensure their own repetition they are warping entire cosmic cycles to do so.

If what the Line of Millions do in "Cerberus" is wrong,
then what the Hiero-Foos do is insanely wrong.

2008-07-14 b sharp replying to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues that the Hiero-Foos differ from Mr. Million because they are superhuman and know the results of their tampering, while Million was human and could not foresee his experiments' outcome. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's claim that the Hiero-Foos do the same as Million on a cosmic scale, playfully taking the opposing side and citing Million's mean offspring.

2008-07-14 Paul B replying to b sharp #

Disputes a reading that Hierogrammates are omniscient, arguing that Tzadkiel admits he only made a probabilistic guess about Severian that happened to be right, so even a Hierogrammate's foresight is imperfect. Asks that unusual claims about Hierogrammates' angelhood or omniscience be backed with textual references rather than assumed.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Hierogrammates' foresight is not perfect

You persist in making unwarranted assumptions.
Tzadkiel says, and I doubt you'll claim he's lying in this case, that he made a probabilistic guess about Severian, which did indeed turn out right. Ergo, even a Hierogrammate's foresight is not perfect.
If you intend on making unusual claims about various properties of the Hierogrammates, be it angel-hood or omniscience, please substantiate them with some references.

Paul
