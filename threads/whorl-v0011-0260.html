Reports that the poster asked editor David Hartwell whether Return to the Whorl definitively concludes the Urth/Whorl cycle, and quotes Hartwell's reply that another Urth book or story will someday appear. Gives no analysis; the post simply passes along the editor's answer as a factual report of what he said.

I recently asked David Hartwell whether RETURN TO THE

that there will someday be another Urth book or story."
