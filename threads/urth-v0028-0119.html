Themes: Books, libraries and writing

1999-09-16 Paul C Duggan #

Argues that the framing Rorschach's Journal in Watchmen exposes a sordid plot and that the journal represents the triumph of the Word over deception. Supports this with Dr. Manhattan's line that nothing ever ends and a quotation from Eugen Rosenstock-Huessy about transmitting mental experiences.
chats · theories: Rorschach's Journal triumphs over deception

What Nutria would say: ;-)

Watchmen begins and ends with Rorshach's Journal. The journal reveals the sordid plot to us (readers of Watchmen), but it also about to be revealed to the New American editors. The lame cover story will be undercut, and the world will continue to careen towards holocaust. Dr. manhattan says "nothing ever ends".

Rorshach's Journal represents the triumph of the Word over deception.
