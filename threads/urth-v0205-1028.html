Themes: Unreliable narration

2002-05-20 Jeff Prucher #

Argues that the foreword to Pandora by Holly Hollander was written later than the novel, based on the author's letter dating its completion to 1979. Questions whether the book was originally published in France, as the narrator's foreword claims, noting that searches found no evidence and suggesting the narrator may be untruthful.
questions · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading

This suggests to me that the forward to PbHH was written later than the rest of the novel. In it, Wolfe writes (as Pandora): "This just one that took a long time to sell. (Except in France, so _vive la France!_....)" Was Pandora published originally in France, in any form? I couldn't find any evidence in the usual sources, and I didn't find anything from a quick google search. It seems an odd thing to write if it's not true, but it wouldn't be the first time one of Wolfe's narrator's was less than truthful.

-Jeff Prucher
