Themes: Myth and folklore sources

1998-02-02 Alice Turner #

Gives a key matching names in The Play to figures of the Persian creation myth: Nod to Gayomard, Jahi to Jeh, and M&M to Mashya and Mashyane. Offers the mapping as a time-saver for readers who want to work through the myth, and says the poster does not think readers are dim.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Names in The Play map onto Persian creation figures

Oh, in case some of you do want to slog through the Persian creation myth, I should add that Nod=Gayomard, Jahi=Jeh, M&M=Mashya and Mashyane. Not that I think you're dim in any way, but it might save a puzzled minute or two.

-alga-
