Asks whether readers know more about a reported story that a getaway train waited at a Moscow station for Stalin during the German invasion of Russia, which the poster says could have toppled the country. The post is off-topic, a historical aside prompted by Michael Andre-Driussi's remark about the stupidity of invading Russia, with no reference to Wolfe's fiction.
questions

I heard recently that not only did far more of the German high command agree with this beforehand than would admit to it afterwards, but that it nearly succeeded. A getaway train was prepared and waiting for Stalin at Moscow station, and if he had turned up and escaped on it, Russia would have fallen. Connoisseurs of pivotal moments might like to add this one to their collection.

Anyone know anything more about it?

Spectacled Bear.
