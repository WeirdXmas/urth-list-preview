Kafka and Wolfe

1998-04-27 adam louis stephanides #

Argues that Kafka's novel The Castle is a clear source for There Are Doors, pointing to the character Klamm as taken directly from it and to parallels between the two protagonists' situations. Responds to alga's nomination of Kafka's In the Penal Colony and claim that The Castle relates to Peace, The Fifth Head of Cerberus, and Seven American Nights.
supports · source-and-allusion · theories: There Are Doors draws on Kafka's The Castle

I hadn't thought about those works, but THE CASTLE is an obvious source for THERE ARE DOORS. Klamm, of course, is directly from THE CASTLE, and there are parallels between the situations of the protagonists.

--Adam
