5HC: star located

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-03 to 1998-06-03

1998urthMichael Andre-Driussi

1998-06-03 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the star of The Fifth Head of Cerberus is Wolf 294, a red dwarf about 19.4 light years away in Gemini. Grounds the identification in the text's red or pink light with no blue output, the star being a Wolf star that is not a flare star, and its distance being within the stated twenty light years.
proposes · source-and-allusion · puzzle-solving · theories: The Fifth Head of Cerberus star is Wolf 294

The star has been located (until somebody finds one better):

=Wolf 294=
Location: RA: 6 hr 51m 35s. Dec.: 33, 20.
Constellation Location: Gemini
Distance from Sol: 19.4 light years
Spectral Class: dM4
(dwarf red star with no emission lines, i.e., not a flare star--they erupt, which would probably cause problems at habitable planets)

The arguments in favor:
--red star as per text re: pink light, no blue output from star. --a Wolf star (that isn't a flare star).
--less than or equal to 20 light years from Sol.
--(special bonus) located in the constellation of the Twins.

Your Copernican Correspondent turned crispy critter,

=mantis=
