2000-02-08 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks alga to place several later Wolfe works, including the stories "Forlesen", "The Island of Doctor Death and Other Stories" and "Beech Hill", on the scale of alga's stated dislike of Wolfe's modern fiction. Responds to alga's remark that he dislikes nearly all the modern material except "The Ziggurat" by probing where those other titles fall.
questions

Now alga, granted you don't like PEACE, nor FREE LIVE FREE, nor THERE ARE DOORS, nor PANDORA, nor CASTLEVIEW. But for the purposes of our calibrating your tastes, where does such a modern tale as "Forlesen" fall? Do you dislike it as much? Or is it below the radar (i.e., completely forgotten)?

And how about "The Island of Doctor Death and Other Stories"? "Beech Hill"? I mean, I can guess that you dislike "The Woman Who Loved the Centaur Pholus," but these other ones I don't know.

=mantis=
