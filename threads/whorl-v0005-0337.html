Silk lives!

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-06-09 to 1997-06-09

1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunPeter Cash

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1997-06-09 Peter Cash #

Argues that Silk did not lose the Outsider's favor at the end of Exodus from the Long Sun, but instead had to recognize how badly he had misunderstood the Outsider, and that Silk survives. Responds to Michael Richman's claim that Silk was abandoned and would have died, and asks how Silk will react to his world breaking apart and whether he will grasp the Outsider's spiritual nature and Love.
disputes · theology-and-religion · theories: Silk was forced to recognize his misunderstanding of the Outsider

I don't think the Outsider abandoned Silk at all--I think Silk was forced to realize how badly he had misunderstood the Outsider. Nor do I think that Silk died; he's a survivor, and he's likely to live through even the worst disaster--he can't help it.

Rest assured that I found it equally disturbing! (And I wasn't so sure about Oreb...) Now that I've had time to think about it, though, I'm really interested to see what Silk does next. How does he react to his world crashing into pieces? Does he come to understand that the Outsider is a spiritual God, and not at all like the programs in Mainframe? Does he come to a better understanding of Love (and that Hyacinth isn't its embodiment?)

Rock
