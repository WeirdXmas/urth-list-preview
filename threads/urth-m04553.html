PEACE: Typo or clue?

2008urthRoy C. Lackey

2008-04-27 Roy C. Lackey #

Reports that the apparent Scrudder/Scudder name difference in Peace is a typo, not a clue, because the 1985 Chatto British hardcover spells it Scudder in both places. Responds to Dave Tallman's question about whether the name error appears in all editions, answering it with a specific edition comparison.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Scrudder spelling is a typo, not a clue

I believe Scrudder is a typo. In the 1985 Chatto (British) hb edition the pagination is different but the name is Scudder in both places you cite in the Berkley pb.

-Roy
