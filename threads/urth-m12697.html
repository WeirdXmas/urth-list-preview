2010-01-12 John Watkins replying to Gwern Branwen #

Points Gwern Branwen to a likely source for the Wolfe-Tolkien correspondence: Wolfe's own fan letter to Tolkien and Tolkien's reply. Cites Wolfe's essay "The Best Introduction to the Mountains", where Wolfe describes the exchange and transcribes Tolkien's response, and notes the letter would survive only in Tolkien's papers.
answers · source-and-allusion

Possibly that just refers to the fan letter Wolfe sent Tolkien and the reply he received? Wolfe describes both, and transcribes Tolkien's response, in "The Best Introduction to the Mountains."

Unless Wolfe preserved a copy of his letter, it must survive, if at all, in Tolkien's papers.
