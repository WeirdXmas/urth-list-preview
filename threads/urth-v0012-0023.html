Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Names and their meanings

1998-05-31 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Playfully parodies a phone call to a numerology department, in which a ship named Nine-Eight-Six is linked to 'Christos' via a Greek letter-number equivalence (st=6) meaning Christ. Its grounds are the jokey dialogue itself, which references the ship's name and the Greek versus Hebrew question, and it points readers to Jim Jordan for Hebrew.
chats · puzzle-solving · theories: Nine-Eight-Six is a Greek numerological reference to Christos

<ring, ring>

"Numerology Department, can you please hold?"

"Yeah, su--"

<muzak plays for a few seconds>

"Numerology Department, how can I be of service."

"Gotta number, colony ship from Earth bound for Sainte Anne, name is number Nine-Eight-Six, looking for a possible reference for Nine-Eight-Six?"

"One moment please. Hmmm. Very little for Nine-Eight-Six, just one in fact: `Christos' (with the `st' equalling 6), meaning Christ, useful, serviceable."

"Interesting. So that's Greek?"

"Yes, that's right."

"Nothing in Hebrew?"

"Sorry, we have very little Hebrew on hand. You might ask Jim Jordan."
