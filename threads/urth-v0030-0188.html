Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2000-12-09 Paul C Duggan #

Argues that Wolfe appears inside The Book of the New Sun as the translator of Severian's manuscript, and asks whether the Long Sun and Short Sun books will explain how that manuscript reached Wolfe for publication. Points to the archaic Urth-style diction in In Green's Jungles as further evidence of this metafictional link between the series.
proposes · author-and-biography · close-textual-reading · theories: Wolfe is the translator of Severian's manuscript

I also think it is unlikely that wolfe would put himself in the Short Sun series as the outsider or some such.

BUT: Wolfe is "in" the New Sun, as the "translator" of the manuscript that severain sent back through the corridors of time. Is there, or will there be any hint as to how the Long and Short sun books got into Wolfe's hands to publish for us?

There is also the illumiating reversion to Urth-diction that we find in IGJ.

Paul
