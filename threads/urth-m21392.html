2011-10-19 David Stockhoff #

Answers Sergei Soloviev's question about where a troopship appears in The Book of the New Sun by placing it in Severian's approach to the Citadel as Autarch, with mist on the Gyoll and undines' voices. Gives the grounds as a recollection of a ship coming upriver packed with pale spearmen, offered tentatively.
