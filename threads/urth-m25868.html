2014-10-07 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to Marc Aramini #

Argues that in quantum mechanics a particle moving forward in time is equivalent to its antiparticle moving backward, so time's arrow does not apply at the particle level. Responds to Marc Aramini's note that Wolfe makes this claim in Seven American Nights, saying the interview source is still to be checked, and adds that decoherence supplies the entropy that gives time a single direction.
supports · genre-and-craft

Wherever the interview is, Wolfe is quite correct; in quantum mechanics a particle travelling in one direction in time is the same thing as its antiparticle travelling in the other direction in time. In the QM universe, time's arrow seems not to apply...any given event can happen in either direction. But decoherence gives us a meaningful entropy and so a unidirectional time...
