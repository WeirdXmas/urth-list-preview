2011-02-09 Lee Berman #

Argues that the blue glass ring and younger shrubbery around a crater Horn finds mark where an Inhumi ship took off, so the Inhumi left only about twenty years ago. Supports Andrew Mason's point that the departure could be recent, using the growth evidence at the crater and asking how long a burnt crater takes to regrow shrubs.
supports · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Inhumi ship departed from the crater Horn finds

I think the evidence in at the crater Horn finds. It is ringed with a circle of blue glass and the plant growth in circle is newer growth shrubbery compared to the surrounding mature trees. I think that's where the ship took off. So how long does it take a barren, burnt blast crater to grow shrubbery? I guess 20 years might work though perhaps somewhat fewer in Blue years?

2011-02-10 Gerry Quinn replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that the Neighbours' departure from the Whorl was probably not by ordinary spacecraft, suggesting instead they went to another dimension. Responds to Lee Berman's proposal that a crater ringed with blue glass and newer shrubbery marks a blast pit where a ship took off, noting that such a pit would have side vent chambers that were not mentioned.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: The Neighbours left the Whorl for another dimension

From: "Lee Berman" <[email]>

Indeed, it had also occurred to me that the pit might well be a blast pit for one of the Neighbours' space vehicles. However I'd be inclined to expect a blast pit to have vent chambers to the sides which weren't mentioned, so I didn't put it forward as a suggestion.

I'm not sure they went to their new place in ordinary space vessels, though... It's almost as if they went to another dimension...

- Gerry Quinn
