1998-06-01 Michael Straight #

Argues that TALUS is probably not an acronym, reasoning that Horn writes it in lowercase rather than capitals, so any origin as an acronym would have become invisible like radar or scuba. Responds to a newcomer's joke expansion of TALUS by calling it possible but unprovable from the text.
disputes · source-and-allusion

Well, Horn doesn't write it in all caps, so if it originally was an acronym, it's probably one like 'radar' or 'scuba' where most people don't know what it stands for or even that it ever was an acronym.

So I'd put that under 'possible, but I doubt we'll ever get any evidence for it.'

-Rostrum
