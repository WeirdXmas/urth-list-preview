1998-05-21 adam louis stephanides #

Supports a proposed collage of the list's discussion of a short story, saying the variety of interpretations would make a good case study of interpretation. Suggests someone could write a close microanalysis of the story and the discussion, modeled on Barthes's S/Z, and asks how much of the original posts would be cut.
supports · close-textual-reading

I think it's a great idea (though I'd like to know how much of the original posts will be cut). It was indeed fascinating to see how many divergent interpretations there were of such a seemingly simple story. It could even make a very informative case study of the process of interpretation. (Maybe somebody will write "S/D," microanalyzing the original story and the discussion in the same way that Barthes analyzed a Balzac story in his book S/Z.)

--Adam

1998-05-23 adam louis stephanides #

Clarifies that the poster was not worried about being misquoted but about conclusions being published without their supporting reasoning, which could make them seem more speculative. Responds to Damien Broderick's remark about the Suzanne collage suggestion and encourages him to proceed with the project.
supports

I wasn't worried about being misquoted. What did concern me slightly was the possibility that my conclusions might be given without the reasoning behind them, making them appear more speculative than they are.. (After all, I don't know how much space the editors at NYRSF will give you.) But to repeat, I think it's a great idea, and I hope whatever "worries" I or others may have expressed won't stop you from going ahead with it.

--Adam
