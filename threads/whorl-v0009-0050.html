1998-11-18 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Announces that a new booklet, LS-2: Languages of the Long Sun Whorl, is available, covering the slang, cant, jargon and foreign language of the Long Sun series. Gives the price, postage rates for domestic and international orders, and notes other booklets are available.
informs

This seems like a good time to mention that the new "LS" booklet is available right now:

"LS-2: Languages of the Long Sun Whorl" (1998) covers all the slang, cant, jargon, and foreign language of the Long Sun series. Price: $5 (plus postage).

Other booklets available.

Postage cost: domestic US, $.60 per booklet. International Airmail: $2 for one booklet, $3.50 for two, $5 for three.
