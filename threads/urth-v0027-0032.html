1999-05-29 C R Culver #

Asks whether anyone has read Candas Jane Dorsey's novel Black Wine, which the poster is halfway through and was told resembles Wolfe's style. Also asks for online resources for discussing its mysteries and for concrete similarities to Wolfe beyond prose style.
questions

Friends,

With such wonderful activity, there is no need for an off-topic message to have some signs of life, but perhaps it may be excused.

I am presently about 50 pages into BLACK WINE, by Candas Jane Dorsey. I picked it up after reading on the back that the style is similar to Gene Wolfe, and I so liked the make of the book (an imprinted cover and the pages are scented) that I bought it. Has anyone on the list read this? And are there any Internet resources for discussing its mysteries? Also, are there actual Wolfe similarities beyond the writing style?

Thanks,

Christopher R. Culver
