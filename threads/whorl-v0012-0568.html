Themes: Catholic faith and sacraments

2001-04-11 Nicholas Gevers #

Suggests that Wolfe is ironically commenting on how hard it is to apply Christ's message in the real world, proposing that only small vigilant communities like Sinew's have a real chance. Grounds the view in the practical difficulties of living by the Secret and in the fact that the attacks have stopped, responding to the earlier thread on dealing with the inhumi.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: Wolfe ironically critiques applying Christ's message in the real world

I see the practical problems in applying the Secret,
believe me I do. I wonder whether GW isn't commenting
ironically on the difficulty of applying Christ's
message in the "real world". Perhaps only small
vigilant communities like Sinew's have a real chance;
after all, no more massed attacks...

--Nick Gevers.
