The Ziggurat and the "petite" lie

Urth list · 2 messages · 2008-08-28 to 2008-08-29

2008urthDave Tallman

Themes: Unreliable narration

2008-08-28 Dave Tallman #

Asks why Emery's offhand lie to the police, that Tamar is an exchange student and that his wife calls her "petite," is so careless when his wife is an adversary in a divorce. Raises the possibility that Emery knows his wife and children are dead and proposes reviving an older Maximum Delusion theory from the archives.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Emery knows his wife and children are dead; Maximum Delusion theory of The Ziggurat

There's something very strange at the end of "The Ziggurat." Emery is trying to lie his way out of trouble and he tells the police that "Tamar" is an exchange student who is staying with them. A little later he says:

"My wife calls her petite." The lie came easily, unbidden.

What's going on here? He's telling a lie his wife can contradict. That might make sense if they had a loving relationship and could collude beforehand, but they are adversaries in a divorce case. Another possible explanation is that he knows his wife and kids are dead. So do we need to dust off the Maximum Delusion theory I've seen in the archives?

2008-08-29 Dave Tallman replying to Dave Tallman #

Argues that the man in 'The Ziggurat' is not lying to Tamar but is simply confused, since he does not believe his wife is dead. Responds to Dave Tallman's claim that the lie implies knowledge of his family's deaths by citing his whispered remark to the sleeping Tamar that his wife will not want a divorce.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Protagonist does not believe his wife is dead; Protagonist is confused rather than lying

It looks like he just isn't thinking clearly, which is understandable. He doesn't believe his wife is dead, because he whispers to Tamar that his wife will not want a divorce after she learns of the new company. Tamar is asleep and doesn't understand everything he says, so there is no reason for him to lie to her.
