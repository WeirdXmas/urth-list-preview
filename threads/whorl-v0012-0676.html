A Point about Astral Travel (was re: The Secret: a new theory)

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2001-04-20 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Proposes that the Narrator can astral travel only after his whole-body transplant, offering two explanations: Silk had the power unknowingly, or the Narrator is less firmly attached to Silk's body than most people. Responds to the quoted claim that inhumi are surprised by astral travel, making it unlikely Krait told Horn about it.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Narrator's astral travel results from weak attachment to Silk's body; Silk had astral travel power unknowingly all along

...which caused me to note something that hadn't occurred to me before: The Narr is capable of astral travelling only after he's had his whole-body transplant. The dumb explanation would be "it's a power Silk has had all along but never knew about because he never slept around any sleeping inhumi," which is possible but not very plausible. A somewhat better explanation: it's somehow due to the fact that the Narr is not attached as firmly to the body of Silk as most people are to their bodies.

--Blattid
