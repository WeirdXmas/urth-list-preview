2002-12-31 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs the list that the two volumes of The Wizard Knight will be published by Tor, with The Knight due in September 2003 and The Wizard about a year later. Reports that a new collection, Wraiths, Waifs, and Wolves, is expected in spring 2004, answering Anthony Ferrara's earlier question about publication dates.
informs

Anthony Ferrara asked about WIZARD KNIGHT publication
dates. Volume One, THE KNIGHT, will be out Sept 2003
from Tor in hardcover, Volume Two, THE WIZARD, most
likely a year or so later. In between (likely Spring
2004), the new collection, WRAITHS, WAIFS, AND WOLVES,
will appear from the same publisher.

--Nick Gevers.
