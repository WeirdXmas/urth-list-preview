2000-03-28 Dennis G. Berdanis #

Argues that the Beautiful Ones' planet in "No Planets Strike" was a sleepy backwater that outsiders never left, so its rulers kept their conduct hidden and the wider authorities would not bother to intervene. Responds to a poster's complaint about the galactic authority's weakness by reasoning from the story's rule that no one was allowed to leave and would not be seen again.
