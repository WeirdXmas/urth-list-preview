Pitched battles

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-04-22 to 2001-04-22

2001whorlNicholas Gevers

Themes: War and soldiers

2001-04-22 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that the only way to lure the inhumi's fugitive fighters into a pitched battle is to attack a place they consider crucial or holy. Grounds this in the City of the Inhumi and the wedding, where the inhumi lose pitched battles easily; attacking their birthing pools would force a fatal mass defence.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Attacking the inhumi birthing pools forces a fatal pitched battle

A further point about defeating the inhumi militarily:
the one way to force fugitive guerilla-type fighters
into a pitched battle is to attack a location they
consider strategically crucial or holy. In the City of
the Inhumi and at the wedding, the inhumi are easily
defeated in pitched battles; attack their birthing
pools--the strategic outcome of the Secret--and they
will be forced to fight en masse in defense of the
pools, a step fatal for them.

--Nick Gevers.
