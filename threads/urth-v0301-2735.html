DOORS: malicapata and multiple Earths

Urth list · 1 messages · 2003-01-20 to 2003-01-20

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

2003-01-20 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the protagonist Green may be a persona or one personality of a man with multiple personalities, so the doctor is addressing the wrong one, and offers this as an early reading of DOORS. Grounds this in the chapter sequence, noting the other-Earth possibility only arrives in chapter 22, and tests the word Malicapata by splitting it into possible roots.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Green is a persona of a man with multiple personalities; Malicapata is a disease name built from Latin-like roots

I understand. Another possible spin at that early stage is that "Green" is the name of a persona (of an unhinged person who thinks he is someone else) or a personality of a man with multiple-personalities (so the doctor is struggling to address the correct one of many).

The possibility of another version of Earth doesn't come until chapter 22 (when backstory is filled in), and even then it is only a possibility.

I don't know. My research has turned up nothing so far. I find it Graveseanly significant that the "boy" died 8 years ago, since 8 year cycles figure prominantly in the Graves plan.

I'm not sure how to parse the word. "Mali-" can be "evil" or "apple"; "-capata," I don't know . . . looks like past tense? "He took the apple"? "Evil got him"?
