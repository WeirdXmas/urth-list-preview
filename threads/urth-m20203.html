Argues the Yesodi plan molds humans into the Hieros, who will unite with the Yesodis and God, answering Larry Miller's question about Horn and Silk. Says everyone encouraging spiritual growth contributes, including good and evil doers, and compares the scheme to Teilhard de Chardin's plan laid out as a spiral rather than a closed time loop.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: Yesodi plan molds humans into Hieros; Good and evil both advance spiritual growth; Plan resembles Teilhard de Chardin's spiral

I think it's the Yesodi's plans to mold the humans into the Hieros, who will ultimately unite with the Yesodis and with God. Everyone who can encourage others to spiritually grow toward that state of conciliation contributes in a meaningful way that can be built upon in future time and successive manvantaras. This includes doers of both good and evil acts, though a little evil goes a long way.

It's very much like the Teilhard De Chardin plan laid out in a spiral rather than a closed timeloop.
