2014-10-01 Marc Aramini #

Argues that the numerical string in The Seal of Pas is probably a traditional cipher rather than coordinates, since coordinates make little narrative sense for the Whorl. Responds to Jeff Wilson's quoted reply to Antonio Marques by dismissing the coordinate reading and citing the story of Eratosthenes' well as a favourite.
disputes · puzzle-solving · theories: The Seal of Pas numbers are a traditional cipher

If it has any meaning, I think it might be a more traditional cipher, but I don't have the patience. Coordinates make little narrative sense (the whorl never went much of anywhere, anyways, or if it did, in a nice big Noah's Ark style circle. [Eratosthenes' well is one of my favorite stories].)
