Argues that Reis wrote the play for Cassie as a trap, and that it was a message meant for Chase warning him to join Reis's side. Grounds this in Reis's remark that he is financing the show as a vehicle for Cassie, quoted from page 144, and reads that remark as a threat aimed at Chase.
supports · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Reis wrote the play as a trap for Cassie; The play was a message to Chase

I had the impression he wrote it for her (as part of a trap, initially). Reis seems to admit it here: "I'm financing the show you're in as a vehicle for you, Cassie." (p. 144). I believe it was meant to send a message to Chase: "You think you can recruit someone to spy on me? Look how quickly I can entrap and destroy her. You'd better join my side."
