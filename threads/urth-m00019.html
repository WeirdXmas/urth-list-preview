2004-12-02 Nicholas Gevers #

Lists the post-1980 Gene Wolfe stories that will remain uncollected once STARWATER STRAINS is published, and notes that Lexicon Urthus may appear as a print-on-demand title. Speculates that pre-1980 uncollected stories were likely repudiated by Wolfe, since he would otherwise have included them.
informs

As far as I can see, the publication of STARWATER
STRAINS will leave only the following post-1980 Wolfe
stories uncollected (presumably the uncollected ones
from before 1980 are ones GW has repudiated, as they
surely would have been used by now otherwise):

At the Point of Capricorn
The Tale of the Four Accused
Talk of Mandrakes
How I Got Three Zip Codes
Prize Crew
Comber
The Little Stranger
Monster

Another point: Michael Andre-Driussi confirms LEXICON
URTHUS will probably appear as a Print On Demand title
in the next "year or three".

--Nick Gevers.
