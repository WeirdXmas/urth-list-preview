Themes: Names and their meanings

1997-07-10 Alice Turner #

Lists Wolfe's stories that use the names Kyle, Hyle and Crane, noting the Lt. Kyle of a robot-war story, a fragment titled The Death of Hyle, Kyle in Hues, and Dr. Charles Crane in The Last Thrilling Wonder Story. Gives publication years for each, offering the names as a record of recurring names across the short fiction.
informs · source-and-allusion

Just for the record, Lt. Kyle is the human leader of the robotic soldiers of "the HORARS of War" (1970). Then there's "The Death of Hyle" (1974), certainly a peculiar fragment. "Hues," with its Kyle, is 1987.

And---whadyaknow---the generic pulp biochemist with the beautiful daughter in "The Last Thrilling Wonder Story" (1982) is named Dr. (Charles) Crane!

-alga-
