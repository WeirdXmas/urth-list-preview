2003-01-07 Matthew Malthouse #

Informs readers that the BBC is broadcasting a radio adaptation of Philip Pullman's His Dark Materials trilogy in its Saturday afternoon slot, with schedule and online access details. Responds to a request from Alice K. Turner for Pullman readers, noting the series is off-topic for a Wolfe list and offering only a brief personal opinion that the adaptation is good.
informs

As an aside the BBC are broadcasting an adaptation of the series in it's Saturday Afternoon Play slot 14:30 - 17:00 GMT. I heard much of it and thought it rather good.

The first aired last Saturday and can be heard at
http://www.bbc.co.uk/radio4/arts/hisdarkmaterials/index.shtml There is additional material on the site.

"The Subtle Knife" will be broadcast on Saturday 11 January and "The Amber Spyglass" will be broadcast on Saturday 18 January. Presumably they will subsequently be available at the the same site.

Matthew
