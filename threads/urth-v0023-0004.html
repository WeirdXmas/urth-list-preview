1999-01-21 Kevin J. Maroney #

Informs the list that a second 1856 British Guiana one-penny stamp has reportedly been discovered, and suggests it is the stamp that serves as the central pivot of John Crowley's novel The Great Work of Time. Offers no detailed argument; the claim rests only on the poster's recollection of the novel and the news report.
informs

The Daily Telegraph reports today that a second 1856 British Guiana 1-penny stamp has been discovered.

1999-01-21 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Jokingly speculates about a newly reported second 1856 penny stamp, asking its colour, cancellation initials and discoverer, and suggests a discoverer named Caspar Last would mean things turn weird. Responds to Wombat in a playful, conspiracy-tinged tone, with no textual argument about any Wolfe book.
chats

Wombat,

Ah, what colour is the stamp?(!)

Well, if they are reporting that a second 1856 1-penny stamp has been found, it must be magenta. Wow! Spooky! What kind of cancellation does it have--that is, whose initials?! (Since they were hand-canceled with personal initials.) Where was it "found"?

If the discoverer's name is "Caspar Last," grab tight your chairs, ladies and jellybeans, because things are going to get very weird--retroactively and forevermore.

(Of course, you, Wombat, are the Man Who Knew Too Much. You will have to be eliminated. Watch the shadows--trust no one.)

=mantis=
