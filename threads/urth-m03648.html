2007-12-21 Josh Young #

Questions whether Typhon's era in the Long Sun books had faster-than-light travel, suggesting FTL belonged to a distant machine empire that predates Typhon's post-machine age. Admits having missed earlier threads on Typhon and the Whorl, so the question rests on the poster's reading of the setting rather than on a cited passage.
questions · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: FTL travel belonged to the distant machine empire

2007-12-21 Jeff Wilson replying to Josh Young #

Argues that Typhon's dry dialogue suggests he knows some suspended animation, allowing slower-than-light travel between stars, but that the time needed for news and doctors to arrive makes the timeline tight. Responds to Josh Young's claim that Typhon's era lacks FTL travel by reasoning from the timing of his medical treatment and his likely lifespan.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Typhon's era uses slower-than-light travel with suspended animation

Possible - Typhon's "dry" dialogue indicates he is familiar with some form of suspended animation that might allow STL travel between multiple stars by a particular person, but there still needs to be time for the news of Typhon's need to travel to many worlds and the various doctors from each of them to travel back with enough time left to perform the procedure. Typhon's natural lifetime could have been extended by divers means, but given his face orientation, it seems unlikely he would have hibernated while on Urth, waiting for the physicians to arrive.
