Argues that a nurse accomplice's motive matters less than whether North would use her, since patients' gossip already exposes Gloria. Responds to Roy Lackey's reply by considering the 'William' name as a puzzle clue, with its meaning and possible historical Williams, and floats an alphabet-sequence idea for the names.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Gloria's exposure makes her unsuitable for North's plan; William names belong to an alphabet game of Visitors
