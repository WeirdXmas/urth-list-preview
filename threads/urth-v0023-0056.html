Themes: Myth and folklore sources

1999-02-09 William H. Ansley #

Asks whether Little Tib's prayer in The Eyeflash Miracles, where he offers to ride a cattle truck and then a garbage truck before a school bus stops, alludes to a known source. Quotes the passage and says he feels it refers to something he should recognize but cannot identify.
questions · source-and-allusion

Before I go on with more Oz material I would like to ask for some help from the other list members.

At one point in _The Eyeflash Miracles_, Little Tib, Nitty and Mr. Parker are hitchhiking. No one stops to pick them up and it seems as if no one is ever going to. Finally, Little Tib prays for someone to stop and his prayer seems to be answered, in the following passage:

For a long time more no cars stopped. Little Tib thought about a cattle truck stopping and told God he would ride with the cattle. He thought about a garbage truck stopping, and told God he would ride on top of the garbage. Then he heard something old coming down the road. It rattled, and the engine made a strange, high-pitched noise an engine should not make. "Looks like a old school bus," Nitty said. "But look at those pictures on the side."
"It's stopping," Mr. Parker said, and then Little Tib could hear the sound the doors made opening.

This is what I want help with: I am sure that Little Tib thinking about riding on a cattle truck and then a garbage truck and then having a vehicle stop for him is a reference to something that I should know, but I can't bring anything definite to mind.

If anyone can enlighten me here, I would be grateful.

William Ansley

1999-02-09 Tony Ellis #

Proposes that Little Tib's truck ride in The Wonderful Eyeflash of Oz and Ymar's episode in The Book of the New Sun both allude to one original story behind them. Grounds this in the parallel of waiting under a tree, a passing merchant, a beautiful woman, and following a dog, and responds to William H. Ansley's request for a reference. Also links the message of humility to Christ's teachings and to Taoist or Buddhist parables.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theology-and-religion · theories: Little Tib and Ymar allude to one original story

It reminds me immediately of the story of Ymar in BOTNS, where he waits under a tree
while the merchant goes by, and the beautiful woman, and finally follows the dog.
But that suggests to me in turn that both incidents are allusions to another,
original, story which stands behind both, and maybe that's the one that you're
thinking of.

The message of humility makes me think of Christ's teachings, but it also has the
feel of a Taoist or Buddist parable. Do any of our resident religious scholars
hear bells ringing?
