Did Horn Have "Help" With BLS?

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-10-14 to 1999-10-14

1999whorlJames Jordan

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

1999-10-14 James Jordan #

Argues that Horn and Nettle could not have written The Book of the Long Sun without supernatural help, doubting that learned material and inventive dialogue explain its knowledge. Responds to Vizcacha's claim that Horn's wife aided the writing, and asks whether anyone has re-read the Quartet through Horn's eyes and whether that approach works.
disputes · author-and-biography · theories: Horn and Nettle needed supernatural help to write the Long Sun books

How dare you imply that I'd forgotten Nettle's help? Don't forget that Nutria's eat Vizcachas alive bite by bite (whatever Vizcachas are)! Hyoomr aside, I still find it hard to understand how either of them could have known many, many of the things in TBLS. Inventing "typical" dialogue is a long way from the large stretches of material they had to learn from other people, and I'm having trouble thinking of how they might have learned all this stuff, and from whom. But what I really need to do is re-read the Quartet through Horn's eyes, and see if it is really possible that he and Nettle could have written it without "supernatural" help. Has anyone done this?
It's a bit like going to see "The Sixth Sense" a second time, and noticing how everything is completely different the second time through. Does it work to re-read TBLS this way?
Sorry to bring this up if it has already been discussed on this list. I've got all the old posts filed but I've been too busy to stay active for many months, and I've not had time to read them all.

Enraged Nutria
