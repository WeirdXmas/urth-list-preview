2001-02-01 james.kellar #

Reports that the poster finished Return to the Whorl overnight and found the reading experience rewarding, citing the investment in the previous eleven books. Adds a personal aside about lost sleep and his wife's dislike of the covers, and says he looks forward to sharing questions and opinions.
informs

good morning all

I finished RTTW last night (well, this morning, strictly speaking!), which was a truly amazing experience for me - the commitment and investment of reading the previous 11 books in the sequence has been repaid many times over, I feel.

There were many times when you, the reader, find yourself astonished by the sheer audaciity of the ideas Wolfe is trying to get across.
I'm a little drained - or that could be because I only ended up getting about 2 hours' sleep, much to my poor wife's dismay. (I can't get her to try Wolfe at all, as she thinks, correctly in my view, that the covers are dreadful!).

looking forward to sharing some questions and opinions

gerbil
