1999-04-09 Jonathan Laidlow #

Announces that Jonathan Laidlow has compiled a short list of uncollected Gene Wolfe story titles with locations and reprint details, and asks readers to supply page numbers the sources lack. Offers to share a web page once it is up and to email material to readers without web access.
informs

Well thanks to Mantis and Craig Christensen I have managed to put together a short list of uncollected story titles - not on the website yet. Thanks to the internet speculative fiction database and Locus we also have locations and reprint locations for these stories. So what I will need from you avid readers, is something that these fantastic resources don't include: page numbers!

I'll let you know when the page is up, so you can have a look. Anyone without web access can contact me, and I may be able to email bits to you in some form or another.

Jonathan
