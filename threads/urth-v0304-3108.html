Proposes that the Latin title Terminus Est signals Severian's role in a rift between humanity and the Pancreator, linking it to Revelation's beast and to an Abaia sea-monster reading. Cites Revelation 13 and mentions Asimov's Terminus planet, a Kay-style weave idea, and a question about Yesod, Briah and Madegrot.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · source-and-allusion · theories: Terminus Est is a two-bladed sword fulfilling Revelation; Severian is judged on whether humanity rejoins the Universe; Severian is a central weave like Kay's Fionavar
