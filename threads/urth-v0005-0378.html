1997-11-03 Peter Cash #

Proposes that the French word "saltimbanque" has an English equivalent in "mountebank" and notes the connotations of quackery or larceny. Adds that the word can also mean a table dancer or entertainer, a sense the poster ties to a question about the word's use in Wolfe's text.
informs · source-and-allusion

The english analogue to "saltimbanque" would be "mountebank". As Cristina (?) has pointed out, there's a connotation of quackery or larceny. However, I believe that "saltimbanque" can also refer to a table dancer--an entertainer.

Sgt. Rock
