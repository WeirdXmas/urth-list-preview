Asks whose face Able sees in Grengarm's maw and proposes that Setr ate Garsecg and took his face, the Sea Aelf king, so Setr could assume his form. Grounds this in Art's account in The Wizard Knight of screaming sacrificed faces, but notes the theory lacks support and Garsecg seems older than his supposed siblings.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Setr ate Garsecg and took his face

Art says somewhere in Wizard that he saw the screaming faces of those who had been sacrificed to Grengarm by the Easterlings at ummm the Mountain of Fire or whatever it's called.

How that fits with Garseg's face in Setr, I'm not sure. After reading Knight, I was convinced that Setr had eaten Garsecg, who I thought must have been the original king of the Sea Aelf, and therefore Setr had his face & coud assume his form. That would have

fitted with the faces in Grengarm, but it doesn't seem to get supported by much else.

On the other hand, Garsecg appears to be much older than Arnthor and Morcaine, his supposed siblings ...

2005-05-05 thoms #

Asks which face Able sees inside Grengarm's maw as the dragon opens its mouth during the fall in The Wizard Knight, and whether it could be Arnthor or Morcaine. Grounds the question in Able's line that what was hidden was revealed and in Garsecg's face appearing in the mouths of both his alligator and dragon forms, while noting the siblings appear alive after Grengarm dies.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: The face in Grengarm's maw is Arnthor or Morcaine

I'm new to the list, and I have a burning question, having just finished reading both the Knight and the Wizard:
Whose face does Able see as he looks into Grengarm's maw? At the end of TK, after Able stabs Grengarm and the two begin falling, the dragon's head turns toward him and opens. At this moment Able writes something to the effect of "that which was hidden was revealed."
Wolfe established earlier that no matter what form Garsecg took, be it the alligator-like creature on the steps of the Tower of Glas, or his true dragon-form, Setr, Garsecg's face appears in the mouths of both. At first I thought it would turn out to be Arnthor or Morcaine, since they were siblings to Setr/Garsecg, but their appearance after Grengarm is killed would seem to rule that out.
Anyone else have any thoughts about this?
Thanks, and I have enjoyed reading through the previous posts. Scott
