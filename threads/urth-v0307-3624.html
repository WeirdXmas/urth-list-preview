The thread: Lisa Schaffer-Doggett proposed that the name Horn in The Book of the Long Sun derives from Arabic and Hebrew roots meaning to scratch, gore, fish with a hook, and wait, linking it to the bull Al Heeka and to Taurus. She supported this with a dictionary entry and Kabbalistic associations of Taurus with Aleph and the Juggler card. Josh Geller disputed the Taurus-Aleph-Juggler link, arguing that Taurus corresponds to Vav and the Hierophant in some qabalistic systems and that aleph means ox and is assigned to Air. The thread ended with Geller's two near-identical corrections and no reply recorded, so it stopped without resolution.
  • Lisa Schaffer-Doggett: Horn's name derives from Semitic roots tied to Taurus, Aleph and the Juggler
  • Josh Geller: Taurus corresponds to Vav and the Hierophant; aleph is ox and Air, and qabalah has many schools

2003-07-10 Josh Geller #

Disputes Lisa Schaffer-Doggett's claim that Taurus corresponds to the letter Aleph and the Juggler trump, arguing instead that Taurus is linked to Vav and the Hierophant in some qabalistic systems. Grounds this in the meaning of aleph as 'ox' and the traditional elemental association of aleph with Air, based on decades of qabalah study.
disputes · source-and-allusion

By some qabalists, perhaps, I haven't run across it in thirty years of doing qabalah, but I don't know everything, and there are lots of different schools of qabalah. The word 'aleph' means 'an ox', as others have pointed out here before. However, the letter is generally associated with the element of Air. Taurus, in these systems, is associated with the letter Vav and trump V, 'The Pope' or 'The Hierophant'.
