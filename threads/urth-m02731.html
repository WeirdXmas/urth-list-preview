2006-11-10 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Asks for a successor to take over managing the Urth mailing list, since the host has neglected it for ten years. Requests volunteers with skills to improve the web site and a commitment to keep the list running, and invites interested readers to get in touch.
questions

I've been hosting the urth mailing list (formerly whorl) for ten years now (!), and I'm afraid that for most of that time, I haven't been able to give it the attention it deserves. The archives are clunky and the wiki project is still just a dream. So I'd like to find a successor to take over management of the list -- preferably someone with the skills and interest to improve the web site, and the commitment to keep the list going for another ten years at least. If you're interested, please contact me.

- ranjit

2006-11-15 Ranjit Bhatnagar replying to Matthew King #

Responds to Matthew King's offer to take over the list by asking whether he would tire of running the mailing list and website after a year or two. Proposes handing over administrative access to the listserv and site on the current host, with later options to move the list or take over the hosting account.
answers

And you don't think you'd get sick of taking care of the mailing list and web site after a year or two? :) Admittedly, it hasn't been much work for me lately, but that's partly because I've ignored the calls for a wiki and complaints about the poor search facilities and the disjoint between older and newer archives!

In the short term, if you take over, I think it would be easiest if I arrange for you to get administrative access to the listserv and web site on the current host (dreamhost.com). Eventually, if you wanted, you could move it to your own host or take ownership of the account on dreamhost.

- Ranjit
