Where to buy a sabertache

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-05-07 to 2001-05-07

2001urthChristopher Culver

2001-05-07 Christopher Culver #

Asks where a modern reader could buy a sabertache, the satchel Severian uses in the Book of the New Sun, suggesting a maker of replica British uniform items. Jokingly says he wants one to carry a CD player on walks, so the post is social and not a reading of the text.
questions

Hello all,

We've all seen how useful Severian's sabertache is. Does anyone know where one could buy a sabertache in this modern world? Surely there is at least a company that makes replicas of older British uniform items somewhere.

In case anyone wants to know why I want such an anachronism, I often go for long walks with my CD player and think a sabertache would be the perfect thing to store the CD player in when I enter a store or whatever.

Christopher CULVER <[email]>
