BoTNS makes the Guardian's Top 1000

Urth list · 3 messages · 2009-01-22 to 2009-01-22

2009urthTim WaltersMatthew Keeley
The thread: Tim Walters reported that The Book of the New Sun appears in the Guardian's list of 1000 books everyone should read, placed in its science fiction section, while noting the list is Anglocentric and seems to omit Disch, Crowley and Le Guin. He then questioned whether titles in Matthew Keeley's remarks are part of the main list or extras highlighted separately, noting the list was still incomplete. Matthew Keeley answered that Disch appears on Moorcock's dystopia list and Le Guin in Susanna Clarke's imagined-worlds section, linking both. The thread is short and ends with the omissions corrected rather than any real dispute.

Reports that The Book of the New Sun appears in the Guardian's list of 1000 books everyone should read, placed in its science fiction section. Notes the list is Anglocentric and omits writers like Disch, Crowley and Le Guin, though most listed books are good.
informs

The Guardian (UK) has published a list of 1000 "books that everyone should read," and BoTNS made the SF section:

http://tinyurl.com/dfk9dz

The list is a bit Anglocentric, and (perhaps for that reason) leaves off some writers (Disch, Crowley, Le Guin) that I would consider to be no-brainers; but at least most of the books on it are, in fact, quite good, which isn't always the case with such lists.
