2005-11-08 Cliff Judge #

Observes that The Wizard Knight lacks the extended scenes of groping through darkness found in Wolfe's other series, and asks readers to point to any such passage. Suggests the absence may relate to Able being a true hero, the least tainted good guy among Wolfe's major characters.
proposes · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft · theories: Able's heroism explains the lack of darkness scenes

I kept waiting for something through the two books and, in retrospect, I find it interesting that it never arrived.

There were no scenes involving groping around in the darkness, were there? If anybody can remind me of one please remind me.

There were plenty of blind men in Knight and Wizard, of course. There was one generally invisible monster, and I think when Arnthor had Able in chains it was pretty dark. But no impossibly lush descriptions of plodding along in complete darkness tlike the ones in every other Wolfe series I have read.

I wonder if this has to do with Able's stature as a true hero, probably the least tainted "good guy" of any of Wolfe's major characters.
