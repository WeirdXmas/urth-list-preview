Themes: Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both, Memory and forgetting

2000-11-02 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Proposes that the sepia-toned photograph in Wolfe's Lord Ascian Dragon-related story maps onto a Wizard of Oz device, with sepia for Kansas memory and colour for Oz, so that the sepia photo serves as a souvenir of the narrator's past life. Floats the idea tentatively, asking whether it works or only through forced fitting, and mentions wanting to see a fifth part set more in the office.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: sepia photo reflects Kansas-like memory versus Oz present

And Mantis replied:

Just a weird thought... We know that one of Wolfe's favorite films (used heavily in at least two other stories) is THE WIZARD OF OZ; and one of the "devices" in the film is the use of sepiatoning for the "Kansas" segments and color for the main "Oz" part of the film. I don't rightly know how (if at all) this relates to the sepiatone photo.

But... Perhaps his life is "Kansas" and his LAD "Oz?" Idunno. That doesn't seem to work too well. I sort of want to see Part 5, which takes place more in the LAD office and less in his memory/world as "Ozzish" and the sepia photo as a reminder (souvenir?!?) of the "Kansasish" memory/world. But is it workable? And if it is, is it workable only by forcefitting?

--Dan'l
