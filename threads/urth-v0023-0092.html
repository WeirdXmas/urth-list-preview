Gilligan's Rock

Riffs playfully on a novelty parody that sets Gilligan's Island lyrics to Led Zeppelin's Stairway to Heaven, linking the thread back to its Beatles origins. Offers no argument about Wolfe, instead spinning jokes on Biblical allusion, Ouroboros, and a mock book title about Led Zeppelin as a crypto-Christian band, signed off with 'Hail Eris'.
chats

To bring the "Gilligan" thread back to the "Beatles" thread that spawned it ("Get Back," as it were--or close enough for relaxed fit stadards), to close the circle like Ouroboros "eating his own young" ala Ouranos, I cannot be the only one who has heard the recorded novelty song wherein the words to "Gilligan" are set to the tune of Led Zeppelin's "Stairway to Heaven"? I'll leave you with the end and let you work out the rest:

LED | GILLIGAN
"And she's | "And they're
"By-high-ing | "Ah-all here
"A Sta-hair-way-hey | "On Gill-i-ga-han's
"To Heaven" | "Is-land."

Which neatly ties in with Biblical allusion, popular tv shows, rock and roll music. Doorways open upon vast vistas of Led Zeppelin as a "crypto-Chrisitan" rock band; one can envision revelations with titles like "The Chthulu Mask of Jesus: Rock 'n' Roll of the Ages."

Hail Eris, to Infinity and Beyond!

=mantis=
