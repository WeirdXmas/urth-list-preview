2003-08-14 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Recounts that the poster's small press magazine Aberrations is credited in the original publication notes of Jeffrey Ford's collection, and notes that another Ford story he published, "The White Man," is missing from that list. Replies to Allan Lloyd's first impression that Ford's stories look Wolfe-like, offering a personal anecdote rather than comparing the stories to Wolfe's work.
informs

One of the few residual pleasures from my sentence (objectively short/subjectively long) as a small press magazine editor/co-owner was granted to me just a few weeks ago when I took up that book and turned to the page where original publication venues are noted. There I saw the name of my wretched little magazine, "Aberrations." Touching.

I was somewhat surprised that Jeffrey Ford did not include at least one of the others I bought from him and published -- collectors take note! I'm thinking of "The White Man."

=mantis=
