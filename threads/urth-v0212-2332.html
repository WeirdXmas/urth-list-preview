2002-12-02 James Wynn #

Offers a dictionary-style definition of 'diegesis' as the world a narrative's characters inhabit, in reply to Jerry F's use of the term 'deictic'. Posts the definition with a note that the concept could apply to relativity explanations and thanks Jerry for the term.
informs

diegesis
[NL., fr. Gr. ?, fr. ? to narrate; dia` through + ? to lead.] A narrative or history; a recital or relation.
The world in which the narrative is set. In other words, the world fictional characters inhabit.

diegetic space
The physical world in which the narrative action of the television program takes place.

diegetic sound
Dialogue, music and sound effects that occur in the diegetic space of the television program. I.e., sound that is part of the characters' world.

I can, of course, see how this is applicable in Relativity explanations. Thanks Jerry.

-- Crush
