The Tin Man's Organ

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-02 to 1998-06-02

1998urthMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-06-02 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks whether Robert has worked out the Tin Man's organ, linking the bronze object Marsch/V.R.T. sees at Sainte Croix to M. Million's heart and to the book's south-of-equator setting. Then proposes a list of characters sorted by eye color (blue, brown, gray, green, red), inviting others to add names to each group.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: the Tin Man's organ is M. Million's heart; the bronze object at Sainte Croix relates to the organ; eye colour groups clone and character identities

Robert,

I was asking if you'd figured it because we thought it was connected with the bronze dingus that Marsch/V.R.T. sees at Sainte Croix.

You divine that it is M.Million's organ, and say this is his heart.

But after all, this =is= 5HC, and we do think said organ relates to the bronze dingus, so you know that the organ in question is located south of the equator.

There's something so deeply ancient, Egyptian or Sumerian, about M.Million leaving his organ as an offering at the Threshold.

THE EYES HAVE IT. (Help us out here)

Blue--David, C. Etienne, Phaedria, R.T., (others?)

Brown--Number One to Number Five, clones of same still human (others?)

Gray--Dr. Marsch? (what color are my eyes mother? look gray right now but they're green, the color of eyes)

Green--Cedar Branches Waving (by implication?), Mary Pink Butterflies, Sandwalker, V.R.T., (others?)

Red--the mantis, clones of Wolf(e) no longer human, (others?)
