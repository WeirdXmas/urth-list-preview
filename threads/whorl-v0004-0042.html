1997-04-12 Doug Eigsti #

Argues that Severian is a mere mortal whose immortality and supernatural traits come from the Increate's intervention rather than innate qualities, making him the Increate's chosen instrument just as Silk is the Outsider's. Responds to Talon's quoted claim that Severian's progressive supernatural traits, such as miraculous healings, being tested as a god, and saving Urth, make him more than mortal and that the Outsider is the God of gods.
disputes · theology-and-religion · theories: Severian is a mortal chosen by the Increate; Silk is the Outsider's chosen instrument

I see Severian as a mere mortal that has put on immortality, and other supernatural characteristics, through the workings of the Increate/Pancreator, not through any innate qualities he may possess. His elevated station is the result of divine intervention. He is the Increate's chosen instrument, just as Silk is the Outsider's. (I had to work in some Whorl content for Ranjit).
