Stay away from Quechup.com!

2007urthRanjit Bhatnagar

2007-09-03 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Warns list members to avoid the Quechup social networking site, explaining that it automatically sends invitation spam to everyone in a new registrant's address book. Defends Bryan Alexander, whose invitation prompted the warning, by noting he likely did not know this when he signed up, and links to a blog post describing the problem.
informs

Let this be a lesson - beware of Quechup! It's not Bryan's fault that this message went out; he probably didn't know when he signed up that Quechup automatically spams everyone in a registrant's address book. Here's more information:

http://www.kith.org/journals/jed/2007/09/02/4021.html

- Ranjit
