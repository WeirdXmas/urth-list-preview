Themes: Unreliable narration

1997-04-16 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Poses a list of puzzles about Peace, asking whether the narrator actively murdered many characters or did so passively, what the town's name is given conflicting versions, whether Sherri Gold is pregnant and what happens to any baby, and what the Book of Gold is. Raises these as open questions for readers to solve, with no evidence offered beyond specific scenes like Bobby Black and the warehouse ghost.

Mystery no. 2: Given the case of Bobby Black and also the ghost of the warehouse, did narrator actively murder many (most/all?) of the characters in PEACE, or was it mostly accidental/passive? <g>

Mystery no. 3: What is the name of the town? (I've seen three versions, I think.)

Mystery no. 4: Is Sherri Gold pregnant at her visit to the doctor? If so, does she have the baby or suffer an . . . accident?

Mystery no. 5: In PEACE, what is the "Book of Gold"? (Give at least two answers. Three would be better. More than that is gravy.)
