Themes: Myth and folklore sources

1997-11-20 Paul C Duggan #

Proposes two explanations for the stones thrown by Dr. Talos' survivors: Jesus' warning about raising sons of Abraham from stones, or Seth's godly line intermarrying with the Cainites. Responds to Tony Ellis's note on the Greek flood myth, recalling a Koran source from the Urth Dictionary and admitting he has not checked it.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theology-and-religion · theories: Stones allude to Jesus' sons of Abraham saying; Stones are Seth's line intermarrying with Cainites

I had originally thought that that was a reference to Jesus words about being able to raise up stones as sons of abraham as a warning to those who trusted in their heritage to save them. I don't know if that fits the reference very well without looking.

I remember from the Urth Dictionary that they were from the (persian) Koran?

Huh? Part of the Bible but not the "christian universe"? I guess most assume they perished in the flood (except for grendels mother :-).
