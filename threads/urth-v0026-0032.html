Wolfe's Short Fiction -- 1999

1999-04-26 Robert Borski #

Lists new Gene Wolfe short stories expected in 1999, including titles, anthologies and publishers, noting the year as a rich one after a lean previous year. Gives no interpretation of the stories; the post is an announcement of publications with bibliographic details and a publisher's website link.
informs

After a very lean year wherein only one new piece of GW's short fiction was published ("The Night Chough" in THE CROW, which takes place on the post-EXODUS Whorl,) 1999 is shaping up to be a real feast, with six or seven new stories slated for publication. In no particular order--and where information is known--they are:

"The Tree Is My Hat," 999, edited by Al Sarrantonio, AVON.

"A Traveler in Desert Lands," THE LAST CONTINENT: NEW TALES OF ZOTHIQUE, edited by John Pelan, Shadowlands Press. (This is the Clark Ashton Smith homage anthology.)

"Pocketsful of Diamonds," STRANGE ATTRACTION, edited by Edward Kramar, Shadowlands Press.

[For information about Shadowlands Press, check out their website at http://www.bereshith.com/shadow.htm]

"Has Anybody Seen Junie Moon?" MOON SHOTS, edited by Peter Crowther, DAW.

"Fish Story," The Magazine of Fantasy & Science Fiction.

"The Walking Sticks," TAPPINGS AND SIGHS.

"The Eleventh City," GROTESQUES (still tentative, pending acception of story).

Robert Borski
