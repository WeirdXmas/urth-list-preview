2003-06-06 Nigel Price #

Lists the twelve volumes of Wolfe's Urth/Sun cycle in the order Nigel Price considers best to read them, from The Shadow of the Torturer through Return to the Whorl. Adds a list of related short stories, including "The Map", "The Cat", "Empires of Foliage and Flower", "The Night Chough" and "These are the Jokes", answering Tyreal's welcome question about where to start.
informs

Tyreal (Welcome!) asked:

OK! There are (currently!) four main works in the Urth/Sun cycle, and together they take up 12 volumes.

These "four main works" are _The Book of the New Sun_ (4 vols), _Urth of the New Sun_ (1 vol), _The Book of the Long Sun_ (4 vols) and _The Book of the Short Sun_ (3 vols). That's...

The Book of the New Sun, comprising
1 The Shadow of the Torturer
2 The Claw of the Conciliator
3 The Sword of the Lictor
4 The Citadel of the Autarch

5 Urth of the New Sun

The Book of the Long Sun, comprising
6 Nightside the Long Sun
7 Lake of the Long Sun
8 Calde of the Long Sun
9 Exodus from the Long Sun

The Book of the Short Sun, comprising
10 On Blue's Waters
11 In Green's Jungles
12 Return to the Whorl

That's the order in which they were writen and, for my money, by far the best order in which to read them too.

If you're *really* hooked, there are also several short stories set in the same universe. Off the top of my head, there's a couple in the collection _Endangered Species_ ("The Map" and "The Cat"), one in "The Best of Crank" (the utterly wonderful "Empires of Foliage and Flower"...or is it "...Flower and Foliage"?), one called "The Night Chough" that's collected somewhere or other... plus one or two others... um..."The Old Woman whose Rolling Pin is the Sun" or something... Plus the amusing "These are the Jokes" in _Castle of the Otter_.

Anyway, there's plenty of reading there to keep you busy!

Nigel
