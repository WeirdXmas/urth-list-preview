The Cumaean's True Name

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-10-10 to 1998-10-10

1998urthThe Book of the New SunRobert Borski

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Time loops and causality

1998-10-10 Robert Borski #

Argues that the Cumaean's true name is Camoena and that, like the hierodules Famulimus and Barbatus, she may experience time backwards, which would explain her later appearance with Merryn in the Short Sun trilogy. Supports this by pointing to the New Sun's many temporal paradoxes and comparing them to Heinlein's time-loop stories "By His Bootstraps" and "All You Zombies".
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: The Cumaean's true name is Camoena; Hierodules may experience time backwards; Cumaean and Merryn reappear in the Short Sun

It's Camoena, and like her fellow Hierodules, Famulimus and Barbatus, she may experience time backwards (which is why I expect the Cumaean and her acolyte Merryn to turn up in the Short Sun trilogy).

Just another example of the myriad temporal paradoxes in New Sun (it's as if GW has undertaken to rewrite Heinlein's "By His Bootstraps" and "All You Zombies").

Robert Borski
