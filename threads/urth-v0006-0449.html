Themes: Water, the sea and drowning

1998-01-27 Alice Turner #

Sets aside the free will versus predestination question to argue that the undines survive the New Sun and are seen cavorting in the flood waves. Responds to Xury's question about why the undine saved Severian as a child by citing the near-final act, in which Severian makes a sentimental sacrifice to Juturna.
informs · close-textual-reading

Xury,

Apart from larger questions of free will v. predestination----the undines survived the New Sun splendidly, and are seen cavorting in the flood waves. Practically the last act Severian does in the series is to make a sentimental sacrifice to Juturna.

-alga-
