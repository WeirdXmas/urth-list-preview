2003-09-04 James Jordan #

Argues that Silk fits the role of the Long Sun because he is tall and a clone of the Whorl's builder who departs with the Whorl, so the Long Sun narrative runs from Pas through Silk. Responds to Blattid's question about who the Short Sun and the narrator are, offering only a guess that the narrator is short in time because of his brief time on the planets.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theories: Silk is the Long Sun; narrator is the Short Sun because brief time on planets

Well, Silk is tall, and more importantly, I guess, he is, we assume, a clone of the builder of the LS Whorl, and he goes off with the Whorl. What begins with Pas ends and continues beyond ending with Silk. So, that seems to fit.

Narrator? I haven't a clew, unless his brief time on the planets makes him short in time.

Nutria
