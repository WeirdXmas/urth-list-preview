Wolfe media: unreleased Death of Dr Island short film

Urth list · 2 messages · 2015-04-29 to 2015-04-29

2015urthaaronBrian Doherty

2015-04-29 Brian Doherty #

Informs the list that a short film of Wolfe's "Death of Dr. Island", starring Daniel Browning Smith, was shot years earlier but apparently never released or finished in visual effects and editing. Points readers to the film's surviving promotional website as the only remaining access.
informs

While it never was released to the world in any commercial venue as far as I know, a short film, starring a good friend and screenwriting partner of mine named Daniel Browning Smith, was shot of "Death of Dr. Island" about 7 years or so ago? As far as I know the makers never finished the visual effects or got it to finished edited form.

But for those curious, their promo web site of it is still up: http://www.doctorisland.com/

Brian Doherty

2015-04-29 aaron replying to Brian Doherty #

Reports that a filmmaker told the poster a year earlier that the short film of Death of Dr Island is still hoped to be completed, though the visual effects shots lack funding. Replies to Brian Doherty's earlier post about the unreleased short starring Daniel Browning Smith, adding an update on its status and expressing hope it will be released.
informs

I spoke to one of the filmmakers about a year ago, asking about the release. He told me that they are still hopeful that it will be completed one day and that they have not given up on it. They lack the funds to complete the effects shots as far as I can tell. Here's hoping that it will see the light of day.
