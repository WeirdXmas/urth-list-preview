Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-07-02 mary whalen #

Argues against a suggested theory that Cutthroat's neck scar marks a head transplant onto a different body, which would explain his memory of a heavier body. Points out that the Sleighers healed Cim's mate quickly, so they should be able to remove a scar from their own work if it came from them.
disputes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Cutthroat's head was attached to a different body

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

There hasn't been much talk recently about Tracking Song, so I thought I'd bring up something mentioned but that I don't recall being responded to.

Someone said that maybe Cutthroat's neck scar is the wound from his head being attatched to a different body.

I think this might explain why Cutthroat says he remembers having a heavier body. When reading, I assumed he meant he was used to greater gravity, but this could be a way to throw us off the track.

However, as evidence against this is the fact that the Sleighers could heal Cim's mate in very little time. Shouldn't they then be able to get rid of a scar very quickly if it resulted from their work?

prion
