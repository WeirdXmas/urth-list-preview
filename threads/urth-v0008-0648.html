Disclave is Cancelled

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-03-19 to 1998-03-19

1998urthAlice Turner

1998-03-19 Alice Turner #

Informs the list that the Disclave convention has been cancelled because the hotel evicted it, and advises members who booked to cancel their reservations. Relays a suggestion from Terry B. about seeking money back from the hotel if plane tickets cannot be fully refunded, while saying the poster knows nothing more.
informs

Attention! Disclave has been canceled; apparently the hotel threw the convention out. I had made reservations and will cancel, as should anyone else who has done so. MA-D, Terry B. thinks you can get some money out of them if you can't get a complete plane-ticket refund. I don't know any more about it than this.

-alga-
