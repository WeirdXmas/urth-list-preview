Praises a quoted teaching remark by Alice K. Turner about women writers avoiding first-person present and men avoiding weapons in genre classes, and asks permission to forward it to colleagues. The post is social and does not discuss any Wolfe book or reading; it ends with a link and an unrelated Martial epigram.
chats

Alice K. Turner wrote: "In my classes, especially genre classes, I used a cheap throwaway that nevertheless has some truth. I would say that none of the women writers in the class should use first-person-present for at least a year, where as none of the men should use weapons..."

That is a fabulous quote. I'm going to forward that to a few friends in the biz, if u don't mind.
