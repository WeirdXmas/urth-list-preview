2000-07-28 Jonathan Laidlow #

Announces that three Symposium abstracts will be posted later, reports a pending paper on The Fifth Head of Cerberus and postcolonialism, and outlines plans for publishing papers in Ultan's Library or as a small book. Also floats a 'Notes & Queries' section and a full commentary on The Book of the New Sun, asking list members for thoughts.
informs

Hi folks,

I will post the three abstracts of Symposium papers on the list next week. I'm still waiting for Peter Wright's proposal, although I do have an idea of what he'll be doing (its on Fifth Head and post- colonialism).

As for publishing them - there is obviously a place for them in the Winter edition of Ultan's Library, depending on any contributions we receive from other writers, but I'm exploring avenues for publishing the papers as a small book. Obviously the latter course is more exciting!

I do hope that we can muster enough support for a substantial second edition of the journal. I'm thinking about including a short 'Notes & Queries' section, in which specific points (possibly explored on Urth/Whorl) can be discussed. Nigel has also suggested that a full commentary on the Book of the New Sun would be a desirable project (and a long one!) which could perhaps evolve as a feature of the journal.

Any thoughts, anyone?

Jonathan
