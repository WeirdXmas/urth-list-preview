Valeria and the House Azure

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-10-09 to 1998-10-09

1998urthThe Book of the New SunC R Culver

1998-10-09 C R Culver #

Proposes that Valeria in The Book of the New Sun is modeled on Valeria Messalina, third wife of Claudius, and suggests she may have a link to the House Azure brothel because the classical figure worked in a brothel. Grounds the idea in a classical mythology dictionary entry on Valeria and in the brothel's location near Valeria's house in the text.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Valeria is based on Valeria Messalina; Valeria has a tie to the House Azure

It's been suggested that Valeria in the BotNS is based upon Valeria Mescalina, er, Messalina, who was the third wife of the Emperor Claudius. Going through a dictionary of classical mythology today, I was reading the entry on Valeria and discovered that Valeria, due to madness or just plain out of spite to her husband, often worked in a brothel. We have a brothel in the BotNS, not too far from Valeria's house, so is it possible she has a tie to the House Azure?
