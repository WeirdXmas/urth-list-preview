PEACE vs. ENGINE SUMMER

2000urthMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Unreliable narration

2000-10-26 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that Den in Peace may be damned with a circular narrative, unlike Rush in Engine Summer, who has no such escape potential. Responds to Alex David Groce's comparison by contrasting Peace's dark, twisted surface with Engine Summer's pastoral surface and its unsettling undercurrent.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Den may be damned with a circular narrative

Yes, I agree: that would be one of the biggest contrasts. Den just =might= be damned, his narrative =might= be circular; Rush has no such escape potential.

PEACE has a weird, dark, twisted surface, with odd moments of beauty and wonder; ENGINE SUMMER has a sweet, lovely pastoral surface, with odd yuckies underneath in the end.

=mantis=
