1998-06-15 Alice Turner #

Clarifies that the poster meant "taluses" without sarcasm, agreeing with SBear that the walking tower machine is not a talus, having forgotten its caterpillar treads. Explains that Vironese names help when names are duplicated, so a single name like Borski is easier to use than Robert, especially in battle.
supports · close-textual-reading

Maybe I should stick to one controversy at a time, but I'm squarely in step with SBear on that walking tower machine. I hadn't meant to be sarcastic (as someone seemed to think) when I called them taluses, btw, but had momentarily forgotten about the caterpillar treads.

As for Vironese names; all I meant is that it can help when there are duplicate names. We have only one Adam, so that isn't a problem. But I felt uncomfortable, at least at first, referring to "Borski," while "Robert" would have been confusing. And two names is too long, especially in the heat of battle.

-alga-
