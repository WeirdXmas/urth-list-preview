1999-10-26 Daniel Fusch #

Responds to William Ansley in a friendly, apologetic way, acknowledging that his earlier posts may have seemed like showing off and explaining that his graduate training in English literature makes him slip easily into debate mode. Quotes Ansley's remark about being overwhelmed by his knowledge and answers with a joke about Bacon's saying that knowledge is power.
chats

Hello, William.

I'm glad you're not offended.

(snip)
"I haven't dared reply to you myself. You have overwhelmed me with your knowledge of literature <g>."

That's all right. You're welcome to debate the point.

I hope it doesn't look like I've been "showing off." I'm a graduate student in English literature, so I slip easily into lecture/debate mode on this sort of topic. Remember Sir Francis Bacon's famous saying: "Knowledge itself is power?" I seem to have an arsenal of knowledge at hand.

(mischievious grin)

To drop another axiom, forewarned is forearmed....

Daniel
