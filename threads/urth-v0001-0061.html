Gene Wolfe Online 5/20/97

Urth list · 1 messages · 1997-05-17 to 1997-05-17

1997urthThe Book of the Long SunRanjit Bhatnagar

1997-05-17 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Informs readers that Gene Wolfe will hold an online chat on PEOPLE Online on May 20, 1997, and that questions can also be left on its message boards. Forwards a notice from a PEOPLE Online representative, with chat access details and a note that the chat uses ichat software and is also reachable through IRC.
informs

Gene Wolfe answers questions online! Remember, though: it's cheating to ask any questions that have already been packed up and delivered on the Whorl Train.

ranjit

From [email] Sat May 17 09:46:52 1997
Subject: Gene Wolfe Online 5/20/97

Hi there,

Don't mean to be intrusive but I came across yr Book of the Long Sun list-serve & thought some participants might like to know that Gene Wolfe is doing an online conference May 20 at 9 PM EDT on PEOPLE Online.

If you're interested, set yr browser to
http://www.people.com/interactive/chat.html for more details. (Note: this chat does use ichat software, but it's also accessible through IRC. Read the documentation for details.)

If you're interested in asking Gene a question, but can't make it to the chat, you can leave questions on the PEOPLE Online Message Boards at http://pathfinder.com/cgi-bin/boards/read/233/3.

Thanks.

Patrizia DiLucchio
PEOPLE Online
