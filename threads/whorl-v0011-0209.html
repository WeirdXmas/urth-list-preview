2000-09-01 Paul C Duggan #

Proposes that Silk's age will reach 365 in the book's future, suggesting the number signals a significant event, comparing it to Enoch who "was not" in Genesis. Grounds this on arithmetic from the 358-year figure and the claim that Silk was an embryo when he joined the whorl.
proposes · source-and-allusion · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk will be 365 years old in the future

358 years is an odd number

seven years from 358 will be 365 years.

Maybe something significant will happen to Silk in the 365th year, like Enoch, who "was not".

Silk will be 365 years old, since he was an embryo when he joined the whorl.
