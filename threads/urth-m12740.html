2010-01-17 aaron #

Informs the list that Audible.com has released audiobook versions of four of the New Sun books, excluding The Urth of the New Sun, narrated by John Davis. Praises the pronunciation and reading quality and recommends the recordings, with no textual interpretation offered.
informs

Just wanted to let everyone know that Audible.com has all four New Sun Books (with the exception of Urth of the New Sun, that is) in audio format. They were just released in early December and are read by a guy named John Davis. The words seem to be pronounced correctly this time and the reading overall is really good. High quality audio as well. Check them out.

2010-01-19 Son of Witz replying to Fred Kiesche #

States that a new audiobook narrator is disappointing and flat compared with the American Library for the Blind's narrator, Roy Avers, despite the appeal of a cleaner recording without background noise. Responds to Fred Kiesche's note that the Audible downloads are large and hard to get on dial-up, sampling the Audible preview before judging.
disputes

I hate to say it, but this narrator pales in comparison with the American Library for the Blind's narrator, Roy Avers. I've been so excited for a version that doesn't have all sorts of background noise, but listening to the sample at Audible, I'm disappointed. This guy makes it all sound so flat and wooden. too bad.
~Witz
