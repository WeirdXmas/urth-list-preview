2004-12-04 Tom Foster #

Points out that the Armada idea in China Miéville's Perdido Street Station-era work may derive from Neal Stephenson's Raft in Snow Crash, with gothic embellishments. Responds to Alice Turner's remark about the Armada as a visual concept, and to earlier comments about Miéville borrowing from other authors, while also saying he enjoyed both books despite Miéville's messiness.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Miéville's Armada derives from Stephenson's Raft

I have read and enjoyed both PSS and The Scar, though I can definitely see where people are coming from with regard to Mi?ville being messy and in need of a better editor. I recently read Neal Stephenson's Snow Crash and was interested to see that the idea of 'Armada' had very likely been lifted from Stephenson's description of the Raft, with some gothic embellishments admittedly. Just thought this was worth poitning out given the comments regarding Mi?ville borrowing ideas from other authors...

Tom
