1998-10-31 Kevin J. Maroney #

Reports that the third AEgypt book, titled Daemonomania, was delivered to the publisher in August 1998 and so is likely due in early to mid 1999. Explains the title's meaning as the belief that a small animating spirit inhabits everything in the world, responding to a fan's earlier query about the book's date and title.
informs

Crowley delivered the manuscript to Bantam in August '98, I believe, which indicates a publication date somewhere in early-mid '99.

_Deamonomania_ is the belief that everything in the world is inhabited by a small spirit (daemon) which animates it and gives it is specific properties. Or at least, that's what Crowley claims it means.
