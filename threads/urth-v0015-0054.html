Star Trek & Alien Stone

1998-07-01 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that Hellen in Wolfe's Alien Stone story is a Bathsheba figure, making Daw a David figure, and adds that several Wolfe starship stories are variations on the Flying Dutchman. Responds to Roborski's thread on Star Trek references, citing "The Adopted Father" and listing "Silhouette," "The Other Dead Man," "All the Hues of Hell" and "Cherry Jubilee" as examples.
proposes · source-and-allusion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Hellen is a Bathsheba figure; Daw is a David figure; Wolfe's starship stories are Flying Dutchman variations

Roborski,

Don't forget the Bath-sheba angle! Hellen is she, and that makes Daw into David.

More direct "Star Trek" references: "The Adopted Father" (1980) has one right at the end.

(Hey, most of the Wolfe starship stories are variations on "the Flying Dutchman," ain't they? "Silhouette," "The Other Dead Man," "All the Hues of Hell" [was it James Jordon who said this one is a sequel to "Silhouette"? On target.] . . . well, "Cherry Jubilee" isn't. Nevermind!)

=mantis=
