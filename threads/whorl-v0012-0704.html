Big Mora

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Ghosts, dreams and visions

2001-04-23 James Jordan #

Proposes that Big Mora may have some godling heritage, genetically altered material that accounts for her dreaming ability and her large size in The Book of the Long Sun. Asks the list's knowledgeable readers for their view, after an earlier question about whether Seawrack is a modified inhuma drew no replies.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Big Mora has godling heritage; Big Mora's size signals genetic alteration

I asked a while back is Seawrack might be a modified inhuma. No takers. So now I'll ask if Big Mora might have a bit of godling in her, and that's a bit of why she can dream as she does? To put it another way, maybe her size is a hint that she has some genetically altered stuff in her, and that's part of her dreaming ability.
Just asking you guys who Know More.
