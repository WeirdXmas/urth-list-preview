New Wolfe Messageboard

Urth list · 1 messages · 2009-03-13 to 2009-03-13

2009-03-13 aaron #

Announces a new online message board dedicated to Gene Wolfe and his works, saying it is meant to complement the mailing list and other Wolfe sites rather than draw people away. Proposes weekly discussions of the short fiction, monthly discussions of the novels, a sub-board for anecdotes and quotes, and gives the link.
informs

Hello all. I just wanted to let everyone here know that I have just started a message board dedicated to Gene Wolfe and his works. I have no intention of taking folks away from here. This is the first and the best. No, my intent is to compliment this list as well as the other Wolfe sites out there. I hope to have weekly discussions of Wolfe's short fiction and perhaps monthly discussions of his novel-length works. I also have a sub-board for you to post your Wolfe encounters, anecdotes, quotes, whatever else we might think of. I am open to suggestions and expect to make some changes after I get some input. Anyway, I hope to see some of you there. Here is the link.

http://usefulphrases.yuku.com/
