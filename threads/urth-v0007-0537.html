A Note for the Lexicon

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-02-08 to 1998-02-08

1998urthThe Book of the New SunDave Lebling

1998-02-08 Dave Lebling #

Explains that the Green Room at the House Absolute refers to the theatrical term for the room where actors wait before going on stage, so Severian's surprise at it not being a room can be excused. Offers this as a note for the Lexicon Urthus, drawing on the theatrical meaning of the term rather than any in-text explanation.
informs · source-and-allusion

mantis,

A note for the _Lexicon_, if you haven't gotten it before: When Severian is told (at the House Absolute) that Dr. Talos and his company are in the Green Room, they are taking part in a tradition that goes back to our time and before. In theatrical parlance, the Green Room is the room (regardless of color) in which the actors wait before going on stage.

Severian's surprise that it isn't a room at all can therefore be excused.
