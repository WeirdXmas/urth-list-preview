Themes: Stories within stories

1997-10-10 Michael Straight #

Proposes that the potato-based orange drink from Weer's company in Peace satirizes processed snacks like Pringles, suggesting a theme that you never know what is really in food. Links the orange logo to the orange Mr. T uses in Mr. Smart's inset story about poisoning fruit, and responds to Tony Ellis's admission of error about the drink.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Weer's orange drink satirizes Pringles and processed snacks; Orange motif links Weer's logo to Mr. Smart's poisoned fruit; Theme that you never know what is in food

This seems so bizarre, I'm inclined to think it has a point. Maybe a satire on Pringles (or potato chips in general) in that they start with a potato and end up with something not very much like a potato? I can't find it now, but I seem to remember that Weer connects in his mind the big orange logo of his company with the orange Mr. T uses in Mr. Smart's story to consider how easy/hard it would be to poison fruit. So maybe it's just a "you never know what's really in there" sort of theme.

-Rostrum
