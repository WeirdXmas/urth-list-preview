Themes: Time loops and causality

2007-12-17 Roy C. Lackey #

Replies to Michael Dale's Sun Cycle timeline questions, arguing that roughly a thousand years passed between Typhon and Severian because the Whorl traveled at relativistic speed. Recalls the interview with Jim Jordan in which Wolfe seems to agree, and notes his own earlier proposal that Typhon drew energy from the sun.

The most accepted figure for the time period between Typhon and Severian is about 1,000 years. The _Whorl_ had been in flight for 300+ years (another 30 or 40 so, but I forget the correct number).

Sidestepping the question of human FTL ships in Briah, but relevant in this context. It is generally accepted that the _Whorl_ was traveling at some percentage of light speed, such that, with deceleration, the ship took about 300 years, ship time, to reach the Blue-Green system. Then a few decades passed. While the ship was traveling at relativistic speed, about a thousand years went by on Urth. That allowed Silkhorn, by way of astral travel to Urth, to meet young Severian.
