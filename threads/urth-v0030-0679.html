Asks whether John Crowley's Aegypt and Love & Sleep will be republished when the fourth book appears, so he can decide whether to buy a hardcover copy of Aegypt now for $35. Requests publication information from list members; the post is a purchasing question with no literary argument.
questions

Does anyone have any information on Aegypt and Love & Sleep being republished when the fourth book comes out? I have an opportunity to buy Aegypt in hardcover, in good condition, for $35. But if it will be republished next year, I would prefer to save my money. If anybody knows, could you please tell me?

Thanks!

Darryl
