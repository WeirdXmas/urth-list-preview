Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

2003-02-11 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Offers several possible explanations for why the aliens in "Alien Stones" keep physical star charts rather than software: hardcopy backup, decorative art objects recalling earlier times, or toys for visiting humans. Responds to Fernando Q. Gouvea's question by suggesting the aliens are robotic rather than pure-thought AI, which makes physical charts less odd.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Alien Stones aliens are robotic rather than pure AI; Star charts are art objects or toys

Hey, that book seminar sounds like a lot of fun!

As for the charts. IIRC, the aliens are kinda sorta robotic rather than "pure thought" A.I. entities.

Possible reasons for physical charts:

1) It is good to have hardcopy!

2) They might not be real tools, they might just be art objects recalling earlier times.

3) They might just be toys for soft lifes to play with when they come visiting, like the amusements in the waiting/screening room at a doctor's office (magazines, toys).

=mantis=
