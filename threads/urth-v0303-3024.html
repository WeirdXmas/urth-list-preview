2003-03-06 Matthew Weber #

Argues that The Tale of the Boy Called Frog paraphrases Kipling's Jungle Book, where Mowgli means Frog, Bagheera stands in for the Black Killer and Baloo for the Naked One. Responds to an earlier post's claim that Spring Wind is Typhon by saying he doesn't accept that exegesis, though he finds it impressive.
