2011-05-08 Rebecca Bushong-Taylor #

Thanks list members for posting on the Wolfe birthday blog, reporting that about 125 posts and many card comments arrived. Reports that Wolfe read them all, made funny remarks about some, and was surprised and very happy.
informs

I wanted to thank everyone who posted on the Wolfe Birthday blog. We ended up with about 125 posts (with some late stragglers still trickling in) and many more comments from folks who wanted to send cards instead. Gene read all of them, and had funny comments and observations about the more interesting ones...:o)

Anyway, he was quite surprised and very, very happy.

Thank you

Rebecca Bushong-Taylor
