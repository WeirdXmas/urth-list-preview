Themes: Slavery and service

1998-05-31 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that Mrs. B's mother could have been a free colonist rather than a prisoner, and that the baby restriction suggests authorities thought the voyage too dangerous for infants. Responds to Robert by suggesting the war on Sainte Anne and Sainte Croix resembles Pearl Harbor, an isolated attack, with Caribbean pirate islands a better model given twenty-year voyages.
proposes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Colonists were prisoners sent as pioneers; Baby restriction reflects danger of travel; Sainte Anne war resembles Pearl Harbor

Robert,

Wait a second--just because Mrs. B's mom =wants= to go doesn't mean that she wasn't a prisoner. A prisoner could be chosing between a cell and a homestead on the frontier. But still, why would there be that baby restriction? Maybe just that the authorities deemed it too dangerous for babies to travel that way.

So the warship clears out the enemy strongpoints and the colony ship delivers a load of undesirables to act as pioneers--win/win!

(Meanwhile, if the ships take twenty years to get to the target, how goes the war on Earth or in the Solar System? Maybe the war on Sainte Anne/Sainte Croix is more like Pearl Harbor [isolated surprise attack] than all of World War Two [a huge war between Great Powers]? The better model would probably be islands in the Carribbean during the Pirate age.)

=mantis=
