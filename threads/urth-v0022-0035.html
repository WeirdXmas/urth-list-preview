Themes: Unreliable narration

1998-12-16 Dave Lebling #

Proposes collecting Severian's lies and misleading statements, since readers have noted him as an unreliable narrator but listed few examples. Gives one case: Severian says in The Shadow of the Torturer that he never returned to the House Azure after paying Roche, yet later mentions hired women in town, implying repeat visits.
proposes · close-textual-reading

It might be interesting to collect Severian's lies. It has been remarked on occasion that he is an unreliable narrator, but we haven't listed more than one or two of his misleading or incorrect statements. Just to get things going I'll mention one I noticed last night (yes, I've been compelled to read it again).

In _Shadow_, after Severian's first visit to the House Azure, he remarks that after that he gave Roche (Drotte?, whomever) the money and never went back. However, later on, when he is with Agia, he remarks that he had been with "hired women in the town," implying multiple visits.

-- Dave Lebling

1998-12-22 Matthew Malthouse #

Argues that nothing in Severian's account shows he returned to the House Azure itself, since it cannot have been the only brothel in the quarter, so his remark may be a misdirection. Responds to a quoted post proposing a list of Severian's lies, offering a textual alternative to calling the statement false.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian's remark to Agia is a misdirection

But nothing in that sugests that he returned to the House Azure itself. It can't have been the only brothel in the quarter.

It may be that the phrase he used to Agia was a misdirection to forstall her asking details?

Matthew
