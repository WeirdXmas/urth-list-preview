terminus est

The thread: Jonathan Laidlow proposed that the Latin title Terminus Est may deliberately echo Jesus' dying words, consumatum est, in another language, grounding this in the book's eclectic religious symbolism while conceding the point was weaker than his original claim. Kevin J. Maroney agreed the link was interesting even if unintended and questioned whether 'it is finished' is a sound translation of terminus est. Mary Whalen then offered an astronomical reading, that the title alludes to a planet's terminator, but gave no textual evidence from the story. The thread ends without resolution, with the religious reading and the astronomical reading left side by side.

1998-12-03 Jonathan Laidlow #

Argues that even if 'Terminus Est' is not a direct echo of the Latin 'Consumatum Est', it may still deliberately echo Jesus' dying words in another language. Grounds this in the eclectic religious symbolism of the book and admits the point is weaker than the original claim, asking whether he has missed the point.
proposes · source-and-allusion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Terminus Est echoes Jesus' dying words in another language

Just because the Biblical and historical evidence does not support our reading of 'Terminus Est' as a deliberate echo of the Latin translations of the Biblical 'Consumatum Est'/It is finished, surely that does not mean that 'Terminus Est' does not deliberately echo Jesus' words, albeit in a different language.

Surely the eclectic religious symbolism used in the Book supports this. Its not as strong a point as the original one that there is a direct correlation, but I believe the general point is sound.

Or am I missing the point somewhat?
Jonathan

1998-12-05 mary whalen #

Proposes that the title Terminus Est alludes to a planet's terminator, the line dividing a round body's lit day side from its dark side that shifts as the planet moves relative to the sun. Offers only the astronomical definition and no textual evidence from the story, and asks whether anyone has raised this link before.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Terminus Est alludes to a planet's terminator

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Has anyone mentioned a possible connection for Terminus Est with a planet's terminator before? A terminator is the line on a planet (or other round body) dividing the lighted day side from the dark side. It's not a permanent line, or anything; it changes as the planet moves in relation to the sun.

prion
