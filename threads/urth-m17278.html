2011-02-15 David Stockhoff replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that Severian is a flawed antihero rather than a clear hero, comparing him to Elric and placing him as Lawful Evil moving toward Lawful Good in Moorcock's alignment scheme. Responds to Lee Berman's joking suggestion that Sir Mordred is a Goth figure in Arthurian legend, noting Mordred was almost bad and that the hero types split into succeeding versus failing and best versus worst.
proposes · genre-and-craft · theories: Severian is Lawful Evil moving to Lawful Good

Possibly, but he was actually almost bad. There was also a fool ...

There are at least two kinds of heroes, after all---those who succeed vs those who fail, of course, but also those who represent the best of their kind vs those who ... represent the worst, and who succeed despite themselves---despite trying to drink themselves to death or tearing apart a kingdom. The untroubled vs the troubled.

Severian is sometimes as plain an antihero as can be. (I recall at some point a few years ago Elric and Stormbringer came up as a rough comparison, and I still think that is a valid pairing. Following the scheme developed by Moorcock, Severian is perhaps best described as Lawful Evil in alignment, moving to Lawful Good.)
