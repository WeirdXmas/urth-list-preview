Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2001-10-16 Michael Straight #

Offers an interpretation of the old couple who shelter Silk near his former home in Return to the Whorl, suggesting the husband fears theft amid society's collapse or resents Silk for past grudges or policies. Responds to Tony West's question about the man's violent reaction, grounding the guesses in the couple's poverty and the failing sun.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: The old husband fears Silk will steal from them; The couple may hold a grudge against Silk

They're pretty poor, and the old man didn't seem too happy that his wife was sharing with Silk. My impression was that he was afraid Silk would try to sneak back and steal from them, that this was a sign of how much society had broken down with the sun failing.

(I take it Silk won't have to go all the way to Yesod to get the Long Sun working again?)

I guess it's also possible they knew Silk and Hyacinth when they were living nearby and have some grudge against Silk. Or that they recognize him as Calde Silk and have some grudge against him because of his policies or whatever.

-Rostrum

2001-10-16 Tony A. West #

Asks what readers think the couple who care for Horn at the start of Return to the Whorl really are, noting the man's sudden violent reaction at the end of their encounter. As a newcomer, Tony West asks for the common ideas of the list rather than offering his own theory.
questions · puzzle-solving

Hello all... I used to subscribe to the BOTNS list years ago and after this short hiatus have subscribed to -this- list.... as a sort of introduction I thought I'd begin with a really sophomoric question that has been bothering me... I'm on my third re-read of BOTLS but have only read Short Snu once.

At the beginning of Return to the Whorl, Horn/Silk is cared for by a couple near Silk's old residence (manse?)... what are the common ideas about who they are? The man's sudden (violent) reaction to Horn/Silk at the end of their encounter is quite odd and implies a lot more than is apparent (surprise there). I'm sure I've overlooked something of critical importance, but I'll go out on a limb and ask for your ideas...

Thanks for your time,

Tony
