The thread: Thread asked whether more Latro books would follow Soldier of Sidon and whether a fan club exists. Rex Lycanthrosaurus and brunians reported Wolfe's earlier plan for a larger series and his promise of Latro IV after a pirate novel, though no schedule confirmed it. Newcomers Theresa Flemming and Powell asked whether the sequel keeps the dreamlike atmosphere of Soldier of the Mist; Dan Rabin and Bob Miller said it does, Miller admitting he wanted more. In February 2007 Rex linked a SciFi Wire item that Latro IV is not scheduled, Nils Göransson said the forthcoming projects are likely novellas, and Mike Myers voiced hope the pirate novel avoids the amnesiac first-person narrator. The thread ends with news confirming no new Latro book is scheduled.
  • Rex Lycanthrosaurus: Latro IV is not on the publisher's schedule; only a pointer to the news item
  • brunians: Wolfe planned many Soldier books and may return to a larger Latro series
  • Dan Rabin: Soldier of Sidon is even better and Latro stays the same character despite amnesia
  • Bob Miller: Soldier of Sidon keeps the dreamlike aware-dream quality, though he wanted more
  • Nils Göransson: the new projects are likely novellas and the list will read anything Wolfe writes
  • Mike Myers: hopes the pirate novel avoids a naive amnesiac first-person narrator

Themes: Memory and forgetting, Ghosts, dreams and visions, Books, libraries and writing

2006-11-20 Josh Geller replying to Rex Lycanthrosaurus #

Reports that Wolfe said in 2002 he originally planned many Soldier books, but his publisher first rejected more after the second, then reversed course and he promised Soldier of Sidon after The Wizard Knight. Speculates that Wolfe will return to his original plan of a larger Latro series.
informs · author-and-biography

Wolfe told me in 2002 that his original plan was to write a whole bunch of 'Soldier' books, but that after the second, his publisher told him there was no market for them. But by 2002 his publisher had changed his mind about that, and he promised 'Sidon' after WK was done (which happened).

I imagine he will go back to his original plan.

.

2006-11-20 Dan Rabin replying to Theresa Flemming #

Argues that the newer Latro volume is even better than the original and that Latro still feels like the same character despite his daily amnesia. Replies to Theresa Flemming's question about the sequel's atmosphere by citing the charismatic gods of Egypt and the south and Latro re-reading his scroll each day.
supports · genre-and-craft

Theresa Flemming asks,

Even better, in my opinion. Egypt and the lands to the south have some _very_ charismatic gods for Latro to see and chat with, but he still has to meet his companions anew every day and re-read the scroll. He still feels like the same character to me after 17 years in storage.

-- Dan Rabin

2006-11-21 Bob Miller replying to Theresa Flemming #

Supports the view that Soldier of Sidon keeps the dreamlike atmosphere of Soldier of the Mist, which the poster calls an aware dream. Answers Theresa Flemming's question about whether the new Latro volume keeps that quality, admitting he wanted more from the book but finds its scenes and comments returning to him.
