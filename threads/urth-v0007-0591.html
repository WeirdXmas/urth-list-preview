1998-03-04 C R Culver #

Lists three outside works that echo The Book of the New Sun: Donaldson's Gap series with a character named Sorus Chatelaine, Robinson's Mars trilogy with its talk of the green and the white, and the Castlevania game's sword items. Offers these as references noticed by the poster, citing the names and phrases themselves rather than arguing an interpretation.
informs · source-and-allusion

Fellow Urthers:

The following BotNS references from other sci-fiction works have got my attention:

1) In Stephen R. Donaldson's "Gap" series, there is a character named Sorus *Chatelaine*.

2) In Kim Stanely Robinson's Mars trilogy, specifically the second book, there is much talk of "the green and the white" in terms of the destiny of Mars. Severian speaks of the "green and the white" during his time with Master Ash.

3) The "Castlevania: Symphony of the Night" video game for the Sony Playstation has some BotNS stuff. There is to be found a falchion, Terminus Est (described as "an executioner's sword" and blunt-tipped), and an estoc.
