Themes: Justice, torture and mercy, Memory and forgetting, Power, rulers and politics, Hidden identities and parentage

2011-10-25 Lee Berman #

Praises David Stockhoff's analysis that Severian visits Ouen as one of his first acts as autarch because he has acquired memories, and credits him for noticing Contessa Carina as a favorite of the Old Autarch. Responds to Stockhoff's argument that Severian learned of his maternity only upon ascension and so is not hiding it in the Inn of Lost Loves scenes. The post offers no new evidence of its own.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian learned of his maternity only upon ascension

Excellent insight in identifying Severian's motivation of going to Ouen as one of his first acts after becoming autarch (and acquiring the memories). And good catch in noting Contessa Carina as one of the autarch's favorites. All in all a great analysis!

2011-10-25 David Stockhoff replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that the Old Autarch would have known of Catherine's execution, since he likely judged her case as he judged all cases brought at court. Grounds this in hints about the Autarch hearing cases, including the discussion in the audience at Talos' play, and responds to Lee Berman's praise of an earlier analysis of Severian's motives at Ouen.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: The Old Autarch judged Catherine's case

I should note (if I didn't) that the Old Autarch would also know of Catherine's execution, assuming she was, as he would probably have been the one to judge her. I base this on hints that the Autarch judged all cases brought at court, including discussion in the audience at Talos' play.
