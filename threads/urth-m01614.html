Recounts the archetypal werewolf legend in which a hunter severs a wolf's paw and finds his sick wife bandaged or a human hand bearing her wedding ring. Replies to mourning's glory's questions on whether injuries carry over between human and wolf forms, noting the legend has many variants and no specific source.
informs · source-and-allusion

The archetypal werewolf legend concerns a man who goes out hunting whilst his wife is ill in bed. Whilst out, he encounters a huge wolf, and cuts its paw off when it attacks him. He either returns home with the paw and finds his wife with a bandaged stump, or the paw, once severed, turns back into a human hand, with his wife's wedding ring on one of the fingers, depending on the version of the story. There are many variations on the story, but no specific source for the myth, unlike with some other werewolf stories.
