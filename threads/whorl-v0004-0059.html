Time Corriders

1997-04-16 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists a few time-corridor references to Silk's post-surgery scenes and the Outsider's healing hands, then remarks on Whorl geography, static daytime shadows, and how dim Nightside would be. Responds to Xury's timing question and to David W.'s suggestion that Typhon had two engineered gods, calling it zany.
informs · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Typhon had two engineered gods

(Q&D count down: 11 remaining)

Xury,

Silk in time corridors, that would be after surgery on Hieraxday, 30 Nemesis. Whoops, I guess not--seems like that's where you were looking before; there's the Outsider with his healing hands. Okay, try post-surgery on Thelxday at Ermine's . . . here we go (III, ch. 8, p. 290).

Talon,

Good puzzlers re: whorl geography. The seasons and artificial lattitude effects I just leave up to the Fliers and their magic; but I hadn't really considered that daytime shadows would be so static that one could literally paint them in, and every day the real shadows would appear within the lines, from shade up to shade low! And before there was talk about "how dark is Nightside, anyway, with the skylands going through their day," which does challenge our common sense of Night.

David W.,

Re: Zany Outsider, so Typhon had two gods engineered for himself: a big daddy ghost in the machine and a bleeding heart vivimancer real god ("real" in the sense of being outside Main Frame) on the side, to be used in case of emergency only? Hmmm. Okay, that's zany! <g>

=mantis=
