Argues that a bowling ball on a tennis racket, rather than a tennis ball, is an image for a super-dense object's effect on gravity, pointing toward a black hole. Responds to Greg's cue-based interpretation, saying he is not fully persuaded but prefers it to a toilet-humour reading.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Bowling ball on racket depicts a black hole

Agreed Greg, furthermore, the image of a bowling ball resting on a tennis racket (as opposed to a tennis ball) is used to describe a super-dense object's effects on gravity. One such object would be a black hole.

I am not fully swung by your interpretation but I am certainly more in favour of it than the toilet humour...
