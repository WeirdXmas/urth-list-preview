Lives and Times of the Master Torturers

2000-08-20 Roy C. Lackey #

Argues that Gurloes and Palaemon both became masters of the torturers' guild in the decade before Severian's birth, using Gurloes' claim of twenty years' mastership. Cites Claw chapter VII, where Gurloes taunts a young Severian about puberty, to date Severian's age and conclude Gurloes was a master before thirty.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Gurloes became master before age thirty; Severian is younger than mantis estimates; Palaemon and Gurloes both masters before Severian's birth

Talarican:

Here is another important datum which must be factored into your chronology, as it relates to Gurloes' age and informs us approximately how long he has been a master. In chapter VII of CLAW, we learn:

"During the period when Drotte was captain of apprentices..." (1-3 years? before Sev became captain), Sev waited on Gurloes one night. While drinking, Gurloes mentioned that he had been "A master of the guild for twenty years...".

I believe Sev to be a couple of years younger than does mantis*, but even using mantis's estimate, that means Sev would have been 16-18 years old on that occasion, and that Gurloes became a master two to four years before Sev was born. If Palaemon was still "...a wanderer, then, perhaps ten tears before I was born. And yet he had returned to the Citadel to become a master of the guild.", as Sev believes, then _both_ Palaemon and Gurloes became masters during the decade before Sev's birth. Unless someone is lying, there's no way around that fact.

And if Palaemon was a master when he taught the apprentice Gurloes, then Gurloes must have been a natural-born torturin' fool, because he had to have made master before the age of thirty.

*One of the reasons I believe Sev to be younger than that comes from that same passage (II, VII), the one where Gurloes is talking about an armiger's daughter who is to be "abused". Sev is young enough then that Gurloes can taunt him about whether he has reached puberty: "Don't tell me you're not hairy yet."

Roy
