Asks whether the Book of the New Sun gives any evidence that Severian is or was the Conciliator, suspecting Wolfe devised the link after finishing the tetralogy. Dear All:

First time reader/poster, so be patient with me. Trying to catch up on the archives, but it will be a while. Long time fan of BOTNS (around 20 years or so).

My Very First Question: Is there any evidence in the BOTNS tetrology that Severian is/was the conciliator?? My suspicion is that Wolfe came up with this after the completion of BOTNS.

(There are clear indications about the Apu-Punchau/Sev connection at the end of Citadel, and I knowing Wolfe he would at least have dropped hints if he originally intended the Con/Sev connection. And what about Typhon's meeting with Severian in BOTNS.... if they had "previously" met in Typhons era wouldn't a clever fellow like typhon have recalled it????)

Thanks in advance,

Ben
