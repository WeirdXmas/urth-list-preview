Asks how inhumi can fly given their weight, materials and lack of bones, and proposes that low gravity makes flight, maglev cars, giant eagles, dirigibles and other Long Sun devices work. Extends a thread about flying leeches by comparing inhumi flight to their space travel and listing Long Sun and Short Sun features that suit low gravity.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Low gravity explains flight and vehicles in Long Sun

Well, this reminds me that I wanted to mention that inhumi flying seems almost as wacky as inhumi space travel. Just how much are they supposed to weigh? What are they made of? How can they fly without bones?

Add that to the list of things in LS and SS that work a lot better if we assume low gravity... maglev cars, giant eagles, Flyers, dirigibles, SSTO landers, godlings, the towers of Green...
