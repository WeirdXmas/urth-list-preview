Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-10-11 Kieran Mullen #

Questions Sean's idea that the aquaestor plays Master Palaemon, asking whether Severian is the first torturer-autarch and whether an RNA transfer to Appian would be needed while Palaemon lives. Also asks for Palaemon's death date, and in reply to the silence-versus-exegesis debate defends open criticism while Borski's theory is at issue.
questions · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Aquaestor takes on the role of Master Palaemon; Severian is the first torturer-autarch; Palaemon's RNA transferred to Appian
