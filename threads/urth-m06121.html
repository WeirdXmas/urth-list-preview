Argues that Silk's enlightenment in the Long Sun books is both a cerebral incident and a work of the Increate, since Silk is one of the stored embryos and the voices he hears are prerecorded messages from Typhon and Mamelta. Grounds this in textual details: Silk's blue eyes, his status as Typhon's lover, and the statement that he would sleep in the tunnels until his mother came.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk is a stored embryo of Typhon and Mamelta; Silk's enlightenment is both cerebral incident and Increate's work; Typhon becomes Pas and Mamelta becomes Kypris
