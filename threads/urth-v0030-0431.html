The Walking Sticks

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-05-20 to 2001-05-20

2001urthNicholas Gevers

2001-05-20 Nicholas Gevers #

Relays Pete Crowther's description of Wolfe's story "The Walking Sticks" as a dark first-person murder mystery of about 6,000 words, echoing Poe, Bloch and some King, and asks list members whether they are tempted to buy the anthology Taps and Sighs. The post offers no interpretation of the story itself, only a recommendation-style solicitation drawn from a third party's summary.
informs

I asked Pete Crowther if he could provide some
information on Wolfe's recent story "The Walking
Sticks", to be found in the (rather expensive)
anthology TAPS AND SIGHS. His response:

"I don't know that there's much info I can provide
about 'The Walking Sticks' save that it's around 6,000
words (as I recall) and is a somewhat dark
first-person murder mystery with undertones of Poe and
Bloch, with maybe an undertone or two reminiscent of
King's short work. I hope that sounds sufficiently
intriguing to persuade people to rush out and buy the
book."

Anyone tempted?

--Nick Gevers.
