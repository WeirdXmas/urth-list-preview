The thread: Ab de Vos asked about Neil Gaiman's relationship with Gene Wolfe after reading the story "Uncaged" and also whether Wolfe had defended George R.R. Martin against fans fearing an unfinished series like Robert Jordan's. Fred Kiesche replied with links to a how-to-read essay, a TLS piece on the Folio Society edition, and a video of Wolfe and Gaiman together. Ab de Vos answered that they own the F&SF Wolfe issue but have not read it and prefer older editions to Folio ones. The thread ends there with no real debate about the story itself.

2019-09-21 Ab de Vos #

Asks about Neil Gaiman's relationship with Gene Wolfe after reading the collected story "Uncaged", about a shapeshifting woman, which the poster finds straightforward but possibly not. Also asks whether Wolfe once defended George Martin against fans who feared the next installment would be a Robert Jordan-style unfinished series.
questions

Hi there,

I just read Uncaged a story by Wolfe collected in 'Rags and Bones' about a shape shifting lady. It seems a rather straightforward story but maybe it isn't. Neil 'American Gods' Gaiman is one of the collectors. What was his relationship with Gene Wolfe? He acted once the great defender of George 'Winter where art thou' Martin after complaints by fans about the next installment who feared Martin would do a 'Robert Jordan' on them.

Says the poster owns the special F&SF issue dedicated to Wolfe but has never read it, and states a preference for older editions over Folio Society ones, using a Kipling comparison. chats

Thanks, the special F & SF issue dedicated tot Wolfe is in my possession. I never read it though. I am not a great fan of Folio editions. For instance the Kipling Short Stories I like better in the old Macmillan edition with the elephant and swastika, the Folio's are too big.
