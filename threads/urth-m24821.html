2013-10-07 Brian Lovely #

Argues that humans, as baser beings than the Neighbors, resist Inhumi infection better, so the Neighbors fled to another plane because they could not act violently against the parasites. Grounds this in the Long Sun scene where Silk and Horn beat Jahlee to death and in other groups burying Inhumi alive, and rejects the idea that kindness made humans stronger.
proposes · close-textual-reading · allegory-and-typology · theories: Humans are baser and resist Inhumi infection; Neighbors fled because they could not kill Inhumi

...it's not that humans, being lower than Neighbors, are more capable of dealing with Inhumi in a "smash and bash" way. If the Neighbors thought humans more capable, it would be through treating them with kindness, ala Silk, creating a generation of kinder, gentler Inhumi.

My interpretation is that humans are baser animals than Neigbhors and are thus more equipped to resist infection by Inhumi. SilkHorn beats Jahlee to death. Other groups bury the Inhumi alive etc. Perhaps The Neighbors were unable to perpetrate such violent atrocities against their parasitic enemies and were forced to escape to another plane of existence. They were unable to deal with the Inhumi here in the material world, as humans are at least partially able to do.

2013-10-09 Marc Aramini replying to Brian #

Proposes that the Inhumi's pretending to be human in their mating ritual is ontogeny recapitulating phylogeny, since Inhumi are a debased but vestigial branch of humanity shaped by divergent evolution. Responds to Brian's quoted passage on Lee Berman's view that humans are baser animals better able to resist Inhumi infection, offering a biological parallel rather than a direct rebuttal.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Inhumi mating mimicry is ontogeny recapitulating phylogeny; Inhumi are vestigial humans from divergent evolution

I still think them pretending to be human in their mating ritual is just ontogeny recapitulating phylogeny. Even though the inhumi are a debased branch (heh heh) that cannot maintain sentience without original humans, they are still a vestigial remnant of people; just divergent evolution.
