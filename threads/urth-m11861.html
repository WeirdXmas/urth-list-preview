2010-12-15 Lee Berman #

Argues that the ending of the Long Sun books on Ushas offers no Christian outcome, with no monotheism or savior, but instead four gods of human origin, a sea demon, and the pagan Green Man as a marker of the future. Responds to Matthew Weber's doubt that the text makes the Green Man the unified god of Briah, and restates the poster's own reading of the final scene.
disputes · theology-and-religion · theories: Green Man marks a pagan future on Ushas; Long Sun ends without a Christian outcome

What I mean is that after the massive plot contortions and planetary upheavals, at the end of the story on Ushas we are not left with much of anything that suggests a Christian outcome. No monotheism, no Savior. Instead we have 4 gods derived from human sources, two male, two female, two light, two dark. Plus a demon in the ocean. And a pagan symbol (Green Man) as the marker for this particular future.
