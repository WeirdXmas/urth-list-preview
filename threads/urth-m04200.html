2007-03-21 John Barach #

Announces two new Wolfe stories not previously discussed on the list: "Six From Atlantis," a Kull story in the Cross Plains Universe tribute volume for Robert E. Howard's centenary, and "The Hour of the Sheep" in Fast Forward 1. Gives publication details and links for each volume, and asks whether others had missed them.
informs

Urthlings --

Have I been asleep? A couple new Wolfe stories I don't recall seeing mentioned on this list:

* "Six From Atlantis" in Cross Plains Universe
(_http://www.amazon.com/Cross-Plains-Universe-Texans-Celebrate/dp/1932265228_ (http://www.amazon.com/Cross-Plains-Universe-Texans-Celebrate/dp/1932265228) and _http://www.revolutionsf.com/article.html?id=3363_ (http://www.revolutionsf.com/article.html?id=3363) ), a tribute volume for Robert E. Howard's hundredth birthday. This is a Kull story.

* "The Hour of the Sheep" in Fast Forward 1
(_http://www.amazon.com/Fast-Forward-Future-Fiction-Cutting/dp/1591024862/_ (http://www.amazon.com/Fast-Forward-Future-Fiction-Cutting/dp/1591024862/) ).
