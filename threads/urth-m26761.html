2019-04-16 Gerry Quinn replying to nono #

Remarks that the story's title echoes a recent list post and the news of Wolfe's death, and notes that element 92 (uranium) is the last naturally occurring element. Admits the poster has not read the story and offers the coincidences as a whimsical connection, asking nothing of others.
chats

Just noticed this was the last story referenced in the title of a post on the list (2nd April).? There was a post about an interview a few days later... then the news of Wolfe's death.

Hope nobody is offended, just noticed the coincidence - a whimsical sort of connection that Wolfe might have used to draw the reader into a story about some other sort of connection.? I haven't read the story so I don't know whether its about the usual colloquial sense of 'checking out'.

92 is also the last 'natural' element in the Periodic Table, in the sense that no elements heavier than Uranium occur in other than trace quantities as part of a decay cascade.? (Once you start with coincidences, you can't stop.)

- Gerry Quinn
