Erebus & Co.

Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Myth and folklore sources

1999-11-30 James Jordan #

Proposes that the four monsters Erebus, Abaia, Scylla and Arioch, each placed by compass direction, parallel the four beasts of Daniel 7 defeated by a Son-of-Man figure in The Urth of the New Sun. Grounds the parallel in the Daniel passage and in the directional meanings of the monsters' names, asking the list for comments.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · source-and-allusion · theories: Erebus, Abaia, Scylla and Arioch parallel Daniel 7's four beasts; Monsters' names mark the four compass directions

Just a thot. Does it work?
Erebus, Abaia, Scylla, Arioch: four monsters. Compare Daniel 7, the four monsters defeated by the "one like a Son of Man" who "ascends to the Ancient of Days" and receives a kingdom. Does this look just a bit like the narrative of Urth of NS?
Also, if Erebus is south (Antarctic) and Abaia is north (tropics), then Scylla is west (being the name of the entrance to the Medit. Sea on the west), and Arioch is east (being the name of one of Nebuchadnezzar's Babylonian courtiers).
Qommints?
