"It is now safe to power off your gods"

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1997-02-25 Dave Lebling #

Disputes alga's claim that the gods have been switched off, arguing that the Trivigauntis still believe Sphigx speaks to them and that other cities have had theophanies from Quetzal since Pas died. Cites a remark by Siyuf or Saba that they ask Sphigx and get answers, and a passage on gods bragging of visits in Wick and Palustria.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Theophanies continue after Pas's death

alga,

I think you're wrong about the gods being turned off. First, the Trivigauntis do talk to Sphigx, or at least they think they do. The relevant quote is when Siyuf or Saba is turning up her nose at the idea of figuring out the will of the gods from the entrails of animals. She says (paraphrased), "We just ask Sphigx and she tells us." Now, maybe she means the priestesses of Sphigx tell them, but...

In addition, we have evidence that other cities (Wick? Palustria?) have had theophanies since the death of Pas, at least from Quetzal. He mentions the other gods bragging about it in other cities.
