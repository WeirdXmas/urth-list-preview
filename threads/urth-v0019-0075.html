Catherine's Crime

Themes: Justice, torture and mercy, Hidden identities and parentage

1998-10-09 C R Culver #

Proposes that Catherine was an exultantess and a concubine of the Autarch who fled the House Absolute to the Pelerines, then was caught at Nessus after a tryst with Ouen and tortured. Grounds this on Severian's exultant features, which must derive from Catherine's side since no exultants appear on Ouen's side, and on the guarded status of the exultantesses.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Catherine was an exultantess concubine of the Autarch; Severian's exultant features come from Catherine's side

<While I don't for a minute suppose that the pelerines would have one of their own sent to the Matachin Tower for any crime, let alone something as mild as breaking a vow of virginity, still, you bring up an interesting point that has vexed essayists for years--what crime, real or suspected, would be serious enough to warrant Catherine's stay with the torturers?>

Keep in mind that Severian has exultant features, and there are no exultants on Ouen's side, so the exultant features must come from Catherine's side. All the exultantesses are concubines of the Autarch, and are kept in the House Absolute under somewhat strict guard. If Catherine ran away, she could find safety in the Pelerines. Having become dissatisfied with the monials, she left, but was caught in Nessus after a tryst with Ouen. The Autarch of the time, whether it was Appian, or his predecessor (the one who closed the roads), had her tortured.

1998-10-10 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks whether the monial Catherine is short for an exultant, suggesting her small stature points to that identity. Replies to Christopher R. Culver by recalling a passage stating that Thecla was Severian's adult height at thirteen and kept growing, using that height detail to test the idea.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Catherine is short for an exultant

Christopher R. Culver,

But isn't the monial Catherine, uh, rather =short= for an exultant? I believe somewhere in the text it says that Thecla was Severian's adult height at the age of thirteen . . . and she kept growing, of course.

=mantis=
