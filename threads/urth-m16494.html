Argues that the Autarch's explanation of how flyers work, involving reversed particle polarity and antimatter gravity, was a known scientific idea in the 1960s and 1970s. Responds to David Duffy's question about whether the notion was circulating then, by reasoning that if electron polarity is reversed in antimatter, other physical properties might reverse too.
answers · genre-and-craft · theories: Wolfe's flyers rely on antimatter gravity

I think so. I guess the idea was that if particle polarity is reversed (antimatter electrons are positively charged) then all physical properties might be. There is probably some mathematical basis for predicting it also.
