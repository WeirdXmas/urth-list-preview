1999-03-16 Alice Turner #

Suggests that a real-world link might be found by checking the wife of missionary Robert Moffat, father-in-law of explorer David Livingstone, relating this to a name in the books. Offers the idea as a research lead, passing along a fact from Britannica and suggesting a university library search.
informs · source-and-allusion

John Bishop, corncrake,

You are certainly onto something! Here's just a thought, taken from the Britannica: the explorer-missionary David Livingston's father-in-law, Robert Moffat, was also a missionary to Africa. Perhaps someone with access to a university library could discover the name of his wife.

-alga
