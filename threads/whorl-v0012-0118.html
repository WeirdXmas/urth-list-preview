Identifies the fern-like roadside plant that folds its leaves when touched as the sensitive plant, Mimosa pudica, and describes its pink blossoms and greenhouse cultivation. Responds to an earlier off-topic question about the plant with no connection to Wolfe's books, so it is a social reply.
answers

Found it! It's a tropical weed called mimosa pudica or sensitive-plant, with little pink blossoms. It can be grown as an annual in the North or in greenhouses, where visitors will be amazed at its ability to fold its leaflets anong the midrib when touched. You find it wild at roadside in some of the Gulf states--that's where you live, I presume.

-alga
