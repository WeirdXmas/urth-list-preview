Themes: Colonization and the native, Gods, angels and the Increate

2001-02-26 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the godlings' shift to telling people to stay on the Whorl reflects the Blue and Green colonies having matured enough to no longer need the starship's help beyond seeds. Offers as triggers Horn's return to the ship and the arrival of the Gaon delegation, answering Robert Borski's question about the timing of the message.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Colonies mature enough to stand alone; Horn's return triggers godlings' change of message

One possibility that I had in mind is that the colonies on Blue (and Green?) are now "grown up enough" that they do not need any further assistance from the starship, beyond the seeds. They do not get a lot of feedback from the colonies on the ship, or so it seems to me, and it may be that Horn's arrival back on the ship is a trigger (alone or one of many different ones: for example, the arrival of the delegation from Gaon at about the same time) that the colonies are ready to stand on their own.
