The thread: The list keeper surveyed subscribers on whether to merge the Urth and Whorl mailing lists, asking about advertising and searching the archives. Endymion9 backed one combined list, said ads in messages would not bother them, and found the browser's find function adequate for searching, though better search would be welcome. mark millman preferred keeping two separate lists because threads are easier to follow, while accepting one list if others preferred it, and called advertising unwelcome but tolerable. Both posted their replies twice, and the thread ended without disagreement or a recorded decision.
  • Endymion9: favors one combined list, ads in messages fine, browser find adequate for search
  • mark millman: prefers two separate lists so threads are easier to follow, accepts one list if others prefer, ads unwelcome but tolerable

2000-10-17 Dennis G. Berdanis #

Supports consolidating the Urth and Whorl mailing lists into one, and answers the list keeper's three questions about advertising and searching. Says ads in other lists don't bother them and that browser find works adequately, though better search would be welcome.
