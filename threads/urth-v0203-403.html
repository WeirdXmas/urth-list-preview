The thread: Michael Andre-Driussi supplied a checklist of Wolfe's uncollected stories with original venues, and when asked for the best uncollected stories he joked that ranking them would be hard work. Michael Straight jokingly named "Tracking Song" and "Forlessen" as stories so good no debate about recommending them would arise. The thread then turned to bibliography resources: John Barach pointed to an online bibliography that stops at 1999, omitting recent stories and the paid-only CD-ROM updates, and extended the complaint about gaps in the Locus bibliography. Fernando Gouvea answered with links to the Locus bibliography sections, noting Strange Travelers appears in the 1999 list. The thread ended in practical pointers rather than any dispute.
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: offers a checklist of uncollected Wolfe stories as a starting point
  • Michael Straight: jokingly recommends "Tracking Song" and "Forlessen" as obviously good picks
  • John Barach: online bibliographies are incomplete after 1999 and the updated CD-ROM is too expensive
  • Fernando Gouvea: points to the Locus bibliography sections, including the 1999 list covering Strange Travelers

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2002-03-21 John Barach #

Points readers to an online Wolfe bibliography and notes that it omits entries after 1999, so recent stories like "The Walking Sticks" and "The Tree Is My Hat" are missing. Reports that current updates are only available on a paid CD-ROM, lamenting the cost.
informs

There's a fairly good Wolfe bibliography starting at
http://www.locusmag.com/index/s820.html#A19065 and continuing on the next few pages. I notice, though, that this bibliography doesn't contain anything after 1999, which means it's missing recent stories such as "The Walking Sticks" and "The Tree Is My Hat." Apparently if you want the more recent updates, you've got to buy their CD-Rom. =(

John

Jokingly replies to Michael Andre-Driussi's request for the best uncollected Wolfe stories, saying the poster would hate to pick one at random and find it forgettable. Cites "Tracking Song" and "Forlessen" as stories so good that no debate about recommending them would be needed.
chats

I'm sorry. I had the impression that you and some of the other folks on the list had read a lot of these stories in magazines or elsewhere and might have a favorite to recommend. I'd hate to go to just pick one at random, work hard at tracking it down, only to find it as forgettable as...that one online about the new president, the name of which I forget.

If you'd read something as wonderful as "Tracking Song" or "Forlessen," you wouldn't need to argue about whether to recommend it!

-Rostrum

2002-03-21 Fernando Q. Gouvea #

Points readers to the online Locus bibliographies of Wolfe's work, listing the pre-1984, 1984-98, 1999, 2000 and 2001 sections, and notes that the stories in Strange Travelers appear in the 1999 list. Offers this as a practical pointer to where material can be found, with the closing remark being social and playful.
informs

There are several bibliographies online at the LOCUS site. They have one for pre-1984 material, one for 1984-98, and then bibliographies for 1999, 2000, and 2001. Putting them together should cover pretty much everything. For example, the stories in Strange Travelers are listed in the 1999 bibliography. [email] and you can get to all of these.

Fernando
