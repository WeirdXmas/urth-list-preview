Argues that the blood on Finch in the story "Trip, Trap" belongs to the traki, since Finch killed him with the sword, and that the troll's slashing wounds without piercing stabs match the broken sword described in the spirit world. Grounds this in the claim that the spirit world is more objective than the material world and is entered through a meeting of minds rather than ego.
proposes · close-textual-reading · allegory-and-typology · theories: Finch killed the traki with the sword, so the blood is the traki's; The troll's wounds lack piercing stabs because the sword tip is broken

The blood is of course the traki's. It is on Finch because Finch used the sword to kill him. There are slashing wounds on the troll but no "piercing" stabs because the tip of the sword does not work, which matches with the description of his broken sword in the spirit world.

The spirit world is more objective than the actual material world, and it is reached by a meeting of the minds instead of ego.
