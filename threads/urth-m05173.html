Themes: Death and resurrection, Time loops and causality

2008-12-12 Son of Witz #

Proposes that The Urth of the New Sun's main theme is a cyclical time loop in which the future and past influence each other, asking which came first, Severian or the Conciliator. Grounds this in Baldanders' castle echoing back through time, the Amphisbaena symbol repeated, and the cyclical view of death and resurrection, while responding to the earlier post's point that Severian's icons and god-self may be echoes of each other.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Severian and the Conciliator form a causal time loop

I think the main theme of UoTNS is this time loop situation. Wolfe seems to be playing a game of chicken and egg.
Which came first? Severian or Conciliator, Apu or Sev? etc. Baldanders castle echos back in time to influence 'old' stories, and the New Sun may echo back in time to give Sev powers. But the myth of the conciliator echos forward to influence Severian and the world that anticipates the New Sun. Was there a world without this Conciliator myth in which 'Severian I' lived and gained the Phoenix Throne? I don't think so.
I think this is why Wolfe's repeats the Amphisbaena symbol. There is a certain intractability of the forwards and backwards waves of time. Thre is NOT just a linear forward path that some folks jump back up stream to alter the course of it's travelers. It's much more fucked up than that. It's Cyclical. There is no beginning or end. It's one more take on Death and Resurrection.
