"Forlesen" exhibition at The Renaissance Society, Chicago

2013-05-07 Bryce Dwyer #

Informs the list that an art exhibition at The Renaissance Society in Chicago by artist William Pope L. takes its title from Wolfe's story "Forlesen" and describes the molded-board architectural construction at its center. Supports the report with details of the show, including reproductions of the story's first and last pages framing the entrance and a wall coated in ketchup and spackle.
informs

An exhibition recently opened at The Renaissance Society on the campus of the University of Chicago. The artist, William Pope L., took the title from Wolfe's short story "Forlsesen" from *Gene Wolfe's Book of Days*.

The show is really great. At its center is an elaborate architectural construction of molded mdf board. Visitors can go in and out of the structure (he described it as something like a spaceship). At the entrance to the space, reproductions of the first and last page of "Forlesen" frame the exhibition. One wall of the construction is plastered with a mixture of ketchup and wall spackle.

More info:
http://www.renaissancesociety.org/site/Exhibitions/Intro.William-PopeL-Forlesen.634.html
