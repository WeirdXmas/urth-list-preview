The thread: A playful thread started by Dan Parmenter pairing Peanuts characters with figures from Wolfe's Book of the New Sun, including Charlie Brown with Severian and Snoopy with Triskele, plus a joke about workstations renamed for Peanuts. William H. 1999-06-22 William H. Ansley #

Playfully proposes a parallel list matching Peanuts characters to Book of the New Sun figures, pairing the little red-haired girl with Dorcas, Rerun with Jolenta and Marcie with Agius. Justifies the pairings by pointing to shared looks, Linus and Rerun differing only in size, and Jonas possibly being Miles, and it answers an earlier post's Charlie Brown list.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Jonas and Miles may be the same person

Woo, hoo, hoo! I nearly fell out of my chair laughing after reading this.

I will propose:

The little red-haired girl: Dorcas
Shirley*: Jolenta
Rerun**: Miles
Marcie***: Agius
Schroeder: ?

*She is an old character who hasn't been seen in quite a while. Her major characteristic was her naturally curly hair and her high opinion of her own appearance.

**Linus and Rerun look exactly alike (except for size) but are not the same person. Jonas and Miles look nothing alike, but may very well be the same person. That's logic!

***Just because of the Peppermint Patty linkage, above.

I will forever more, when I come across another example of Severian missing the point or leaving out some incident we would all really, really like to know about, think, "Severian, you blockhead!"

William Ansley

1999-06-23 Kevin J. Maroney #

Identifies the 'little red-haired girl' from a quoted character list as Frieda, owner of Faron, the world's first boneless cat. Responds to William A.'s quoted list of characters, supplying a name the list does not give and offering no argument beyond that identification.
informs · source-and-allusion
