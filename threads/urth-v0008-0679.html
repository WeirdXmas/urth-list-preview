Key to the Universe

1998urthThe Book of the New SunC R CulverYe Olde MothmanMichael Andre-Driussi
The thread: Newcomer Kevin Taylor asked whether a passage in The Citadel of the Autarch, where Severian recounts Malrubius' cyclical-universe account, repeats its first three paragraphs on the next page, and whether that was a typo or deliberate. C R Culver welcomed him, asked for paragraph numbers, and noted that some readers use the 1994 Orb two-volume reissue. M.driussi answered that it is a large typo in the original paperback, chapter 34, absent from the SFBC hardcover and Simon & Schuster editions, and pointed to the errata list in Lexicon Urthus. The thread ended with the question resolved as a printing error.
  • Kevin Taylor: asks whether the repeated paragraphs are a typo or deliberate inconsistency
  • C R Culver: welcomes Kevin and suggests the 1994 Orb reissue as a reference
  • m.driussi: identifies it as a large typo in the original paperback, chapter 34

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-03-29 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Answers Kevin's question about a passage by identifying it as a large typo found in the original paperback edition of The Citadel of the Autarch, chapter 34, and not in the SFBC hardcover or Simon & Schuster editions. Points to the poster's own errata list in Lexicon Urthus as a further reference.
answers · close-textual-reading

Reply: Item #2403193 from [email]@INET01#

Kevin,

Hello and welcome aboard! To answer your question--I believe it is a large typo that first (and last?) appeared in the original paperback editions of THE CITADEL OF THE AUTARCH in chapter 34 (at least, that is where I earlier found what you are talking about). This typo does not appear in SFBC hardcover editions, nor (I presume) in the Simon & Schuster editions.

(FWIW I covered this in "Lexicon Urthus: Additions, Errata, &cetera--Volume 2.")

=mantis=
