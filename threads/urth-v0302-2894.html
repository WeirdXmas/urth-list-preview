2003-02-07 Joe Cilluffo #

Jokes that a new blackest-ever substance should be named fuligin after the Urth novels' term for the black of the sun's dying, suggesting scientists who read science fiction would pick that nod. Responds to a forwarded news link by Andy Robertson about the new material, playing on the Nigel Tufnel 'none more black' line rather than offering evidence.
chats

To quote the great Nigel Tufnel, "it's like, how much more black can it be= ?
And the answer is none, none more black=2E"

Jokes aside, you just know these scientists are sci-fi readers, it would b= e
a great nod if they called this new substance fuligin! =20

2003-02-07 Josh Geller #

Notes that fuligin already means lamp-black, the soot colour, and observes that lamp-black is intensely black for the same reason as a newly reported ultra-black material. Responds to Andy Robertson's link to a news article about that material, connecting it to Wolfe's word choice.
informs · source-and-allusion

Fuligin is already the color of lamp-black.

Interestingly, if you read this article, lamp-black is so black for the same reason this new stuff is.

J.
