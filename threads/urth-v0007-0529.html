Themes: Light, darkness and colour

1998-02-06 C R Culver #

Notes that the zoanthrops in Dan Maitz's cover painting for The Claw of the Conciliator are blinded by the light of Severian's Claw, linking the image to a remark by Dorcas about the New Sun being too bright to look at. Offers this as an appealing visual and thematic tie-in to the man-apes living in a mutagen-laced environment without sunlight.
