1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunJeffrey Meyers

1997-02-01 Jeffrey Meyers #

Mocks Mantis's reader-response reading of Horn's inhumi story as propaganda, asking whether The Book of the Long Sun hides a secret message for the enlightened and whether Horn is setting up a messianic figure like Silk for a new exodus from Blue. Responds to Mantis's claim that Blue readers would view the inhumi as vampires, through a series of rhetorical questions rather than textual evidence.
disputes

Ah, yes, a marvelous new tack--a reader response analysis of the story! Indeed, what response does Horne wish to evoke among his fellow citizens of Blue? That these inhumi are damn subtle creatures! Don't trust them or before you know it they'll lead you down the primrose path (or across the air bridge to Green!). Could THE BOOK OF THE LONG SUN be propaganda? Does the text show evidence of an embedded secretive message, one that might be understood only by the enlightened? Does Horne hope to tease out a new messianic figure like Silk? One that will lead a new Exodus from Blue?

Hierophant Meyers
