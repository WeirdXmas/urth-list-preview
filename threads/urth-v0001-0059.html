1997-05-16 Richard Horton #

Recommends M. John Harrison as a fine British writer and lists his Viriconium books, noting their publication history. Argues that The Pastel City predates Wolfe's New Sun, so Harrison could not have been influenced by it, though he is still in the same general orbit as Wolfe.
informs · author-and-biography

to recommend:

M. John Harrison is a very fine British writer of SF and mainstream fiction. He's probably best known in the States for his Viriconium books: _The Pastel City_, _A Storm of Wings_, and _The Floating Gods_. There was a story collection, as well, though I think the US title was different than the UK title: moreover, one or more of the novels is an expansion of some of the short stories, and his whole publication history is a bit chaotic. I understand that several of his novels have been published in the UK but not yet in the US. However, the Viriconium books all had paperback editions in the States, the later ones were published by Timescape, IIRC.

He's very good, very evocative, and, I'd say, very British. _The Pastel City_ was published in about 1973, so it, at least, was not influenced by TBotNS. Definitely recommended, though, and certainly of the same general orbit as Wolfe.

-- Tusk

1997-05-16 Jason Voegele #

Asks whether A. A. Attanasio's The Dark Shore, the first book of The Dominions of Irth, is a cheap knockoff of The Book of the New Sun or a more sophisticated metafiction about it. Notes the resemblance to the Wolfe book and says the poster suspects the latter given Attanasio's ambition, though he has not read it.
questions

While we're on the subject of other authors (and before we determine where we're *supposed* to post these things), has anyone read Adam Lee's (AKA A. A. Attanasio's) THE DARK SHORE (Book One of The Dominions of Irth). You can immediately see that this book bears at least a good bit of resemblance to TBOTNS. I haven't read it yet and what I'm wondering is this a cheap knockoff of The Book, or a more sophisticated "metafiction" about the book? Attanasio is a rather ambitious writer so I suspect the latter.

Jason
