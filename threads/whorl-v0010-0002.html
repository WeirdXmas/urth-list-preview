the victims of the inhumi

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-05-24 to 1999-05-24

1999whorlPatrick O'Leary

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1999-05-24 Patrick O'Leary #

Asks whether people bitten by the inhumi in The Book of the Long Sun die or are turned into inhumi like traditional vampire victims, citing Teasel as one such victim. Raises the question as a possibly unbroached topic, without offering an answer or textual argument of its own.
questions

Buds,

This question seems so obvious (and spooky) that I wonder if someone else has already broached it and I missed the entry.

What happens to those bitten by the inhumi?

Teasel was one. I know there were others. Do they all die?

Or, like some victims of the traditional vampires, are they initiated into the fold?

Scary, huh?

Patrick O'leary
