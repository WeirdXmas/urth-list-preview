Themes: Suffering and sacrifice

2001-01-23 Alice Turner #

Notes that Horn repeatedly calls the pit he was rescued from by Krait the most traumatizing event of his life, without ever describing it in detail. Contrasts this with the Shadow Children pit, which the reader sees in detail and finds deeply disturbing, while admitting the observation is unworked.
proposes · close-textual-reading

I can't say that I've thought this through, but it's interesting that Horn says over and over again that being in the pit from which he was rescued by Krait was the most traumatizing even of his life (he never goes into detail), while the Shadow Children pit, which we do see in detail, is pretty traumatizing for the reader.

-alga

2001-01-24 Michael Straight #

Jokingly responds to a remark that Horn calls his time in the pit, where Krait rescued him, the most traumatizing experience of his life, by recalling his account of licking dew from the pit walls. Says the detail was more than they wanted to hear and ends with a shudder, adding no analysis of the passage or its meaning.
chats

Horn telling us that he licked the dew from every inch of the ground and as high on the walls as he could reach was more detail than *I* needed to hear. <shudder>

-Rostrum
