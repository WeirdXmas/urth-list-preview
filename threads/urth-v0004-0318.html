Themes: Beasts and the animal in us, Myth and folklore sources

1997-09-21 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Points out that the Neuri appear in Herodotus' Histories, Book Four, citing the story that they turn into wolves once a year for a few days. Adds that the passage just before it describes the Neuri being driven from their land by an invasion of snakes, giving the source for Wolfe's Neuri and their wolf lore.

alga,

The Neuri are certainly in my Herodotus, the juiciest bit in book four, just before the Amazons: "there is a story current amongst the Scythians and the Greeks in Scythia that once a year every Neuria turns into a wolf for a few days, then turns back into a man again" (around section 105 I guess). And just before that is a neat bit about the Neurians being run out of their own country by an invasion of snakes.
