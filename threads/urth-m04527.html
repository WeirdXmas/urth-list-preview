Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2008-04-23 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that Patera Incus, like Silk and Mucor, is the adult outcome of one of the special embryos in Calde of the Long Sun. Grounds the claim in Chapter 6, where Incus describes his parents' wish for a pious child and his late birth after four older siblings.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Incus is an adult special embryo like Silk and Mucor

Recently looking back through *Calde of the Long Sun*,
I noticed a strong suggestion that Patera Incus is,
like Silk and Mucor, the adult outcome of one of those
very special embryos. Look at Chapter 6, three or four
pages in: Incus describes his parents, their desire
for a pious child, his birth long after the other four
children in the family... I think Wolfe is hinting
fairly openly here.

--Nick Gevers.
