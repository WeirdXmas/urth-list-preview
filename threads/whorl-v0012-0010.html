2000-10-30 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Corrects vizcacha's memory that the family and editorial frame with the line about being read by Hoof & Hide belongs to In Green's Jungles, arguing it is in On Blue's Waters. Notes that its absence from In Green's Jungles implies Horn never got home and his family never saw that manuscript, a deliberate uncertainty in the middle of the trilogy.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Horn did not return home from the jungle

No, vizcacha--you are remembering OBW. That family/editorial frametale is conspicuously missing from IGJ, which has very chilling implications (i.e., Horn didn't make it home; his manuscript of IGJ has not been seen by his family; etc.).

Neat way to introduce uncertainty in the middle of a trilogy!

2000-10-30 Dave Lebling #

Answers Donn Seeley's question about missing Hoof and Hide annotations in In Green's Jungles by recalling a Horn passage saying no one in his family will read it. Cites the reply immediately following it, "We've both read it!" signed Hoof & Hide, and notes the citation is from memory, given no page cite.
answers · close-textual-reading

Posting from work, so I can't give a page cite, but there is an occasion in _Jungles_ where Horn says something like "no one in my family will ever read this passage," or possibly "should read.." In any case, it's immediately followed by "We've both read it! --Hoof & Hide" or words to that effect.
