Argues that the apparent typo or printing error in An Evil Guest is deliberate, citing a phrase from the novel's next-to-last paragraph about Cassie hopping toward a place and Gid failing to help her. Suggests Cassie left the galaxy to find a place where physics would let her bring Wally back, responding to a thread on Cassie's apartment.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Cassie leaves the galaxy to bring Wally back

I've learned my lesson long ago about Gene's "typos" and "printing errors." "And then I entered the room" springs to mind...

I was thinking about this last night. In the next to last paragraph she seems to have this in mind "when the sun was just a sparkle" as she was hopping to Woldercan. Then, either Gid couldn't help her or wouldn't in the way she wanted. She left the galaxy, perhaps trying to find some place where the laws of physics would allow her to bring Wally back.
