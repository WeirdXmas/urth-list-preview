The Floating Islands of Lake Diuturna

Urth list · 2 messages · 2008-09-06 to 2008-09-06

2008urthThe Book of the New SunJosh GellerJohn Smith

2008-09-06 John Smith #

Reports that Lake Diuturna's floating islands in The Sword of the Lictor may derive from Lake Titicaca's real islands, which Darwin describes. Grounds this on Darwin's Voyage of the Beagle and a Wikipedia note that people still live on the islands, then wonders whether other fantastic-seeming things in The Book of the New Sun are real.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Lake Diuturna's floating islands are based on Lake Titicaca's islands

In The Sword of the Lictor, Wolfe populates Lake Diuturna with floating islands which move about with the wind. This, I had thought, was another strange and exotic invention that the author used to separate our world from the future Urth.

Well, I've been reading Charles Darwin's Voyage of the Beagle, a book I had always meant to read. When Darwin gets to Lake Titicaca high in the Andes, he describes the floating islands in the lake.

Looking at Wikipedia, I find that even to this day there are Indians who live on the floating islands.

So what I took to be fantastic was simply fact. I wonder if there are more such fantastic realities in TBOTNS.

Best wishes,

Jack
