2009-03-05 Jeff Wilson replying to David Stockhoff #

Proposes that the banks of oars on a penteconter, seen from ahead or astern, could look like wings, so a ship-based volant emblem fits Wolfe's Commonwealth. Supports this with the claim that oared vessels are familiar in the Commonwealth and that the idea avoids breaking the heraldic rule for wing placement in a volant charge.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Volant emblem may depict oared ships

It has just now occurred to me that banks of oars extended to either side of a penteconter (for example) may have a winglike appearance and movement when viewed from before or abaft and have plenty of textev that vessels equipped with ranks of oars and oarsmen are familiar sights in the Commonwealth. This would even avoid violating the heraldric rule for wing placement in re volant.
