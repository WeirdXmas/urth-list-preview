Proposes that Horn's dying vision in the pit on Blue, of a long-nosed man touching his forehead and the glass chunks littering the ground, echoes Severian and the Claw, suggesting Horn sees a time-traveling Severian. Offers a parallel between Horn's transfer into Silk and Jonas's transfer into Miles as support for the idea.
proposes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Horn sees a time-traveling Severian in his dying vision; Horn's transfer into Silk mirrors Jonas into Miles

when Horn is laying dying in the pit on Blue he remarks that he sees a man with a long nose that looks like a black spider and says that the man touches his forehead with something. Did this strike anyone as similiar to Severian and the Claw? Horn also remarks that the area is littered with large chunks of Blue glass (like the azure crystal housing the Claw). Maybe Horn sees one of Severians time traveling iterations and Horns transferring into Silk is like Jonas transferring into Miles.
