Litany's Looks

2000whorlThe Book of the Long SunRedhaven1

2000-04-05 Redhaven1 #

Reports that the new Orb omnibus paperback of the Long Sun books looks attractive, praising its cover and design and noting the cover art appears to show Silk on the porch. Contrasts it unfavorably with the cover of On Blue's Waters, saying it is a book the poster would not be embarrassed to be seen reading.
informs

My mom just picked up the new Orb omnibus paper-back, Litany of the Long Sun, from Borders and all I can say is wow. This has got to be the best looking book on my bookcase. I think the cover illustration is from first Long Sun book, with Silk on the porch looking down the whorl, but that combined with the rest of the cover design makes for a book I definitely won't be embarrassed to be seen reading. . . Not like, say the cover of On Blue's Waters.

-Steve
