Time Corriders, possibly

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-04-15 to 1997-04-15

1997whorlxury

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Time loops and causality

1997-04-15 xury #

Asks whether anyone remembers the passage where Silk travels through the Corriders back to his boyhood and leaves himself a line, "Don't fear love," and asks for its location. Also notes the Outsider's large, warm, healing hands and suggests Severian, excluded from the Mainframe, may be an Outsider figure, as Typhon treats his god as lesser.
questions · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: Severian is an Outsider excluded from the Mainframe

Does anyone else remember when Silk, dreamed or traveled "The Corriders" to when he was a boy? I can't seem to find it ,however, I do remember him saying that he only had enough time to write one line or phrase on paper to leave the boy (himself) proof that he was there. The verse was something akin to "Don't fear love". I am interested in finding this again, so speak up if you have any idea.
Xury

P.S. The Outsider also has hands that are large, hard, and warm and also "clean and gentle and full of healing." Another point is of if Typhon has the audacity of including his God as a lesser one, where as Severian/Conciliator would be an Outsider since he wasn't included in the Mainframe with the other gods, but was rather excluded for some strange reason.
