The Will Of God

2008urthThe Book of the New SunDavid Stockhoff

2008-12-07 David Stockhoff #

Mocks the idea that the question of divine will needs further discussion, saying the matter is settled and that writers and readers should stop engaging with it. Ironically responds to a quoted post asserting that God is omniscient and omnipotent so whatever occurs is God's will, invoking Boethius as a sign of the topic's depth.
chats

Oh, well, it's all settled then. We can stop discussing it and writers can stop writing about it. Any further questions should be suppressed. Literature is dead. Burn your copies of Boethius.

Thanks, brunians!
