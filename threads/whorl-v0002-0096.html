Rereading and Exodus and a New Sun question

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-01-31 to 1997-01-31

1997whorlMichael Bowman

1997-01-31 Michael Bowman #

Reports that rereading the earlier Long Sun books made the newer Exodus from the Long Sun more enjoyable, after first hoping it would resemble The Book of the New Sun. Then asks for a full list of the New Sun short stories and whether any supplements to the Lexicon Urthus exist beyond the first.
questions

I started reading Exodus when it came out and within half a chapter realized I needed to reread the prior books. I did that last week (it took awhile for me to get to them) and am now about halfway through Exodus. I like this series much better on rereading it. I think initially I was hoping for something like New Sun and was disappointed that it wasn't. Now I can enjoy it for itself.

A New Sun question now that mantis is on the list:

Does anyone know the titles of all the New Sun short stories? The ones I'm aware of are "The Boy Who Hooked the Sun," "Empires of Foliage and Flowers" and "The Map." But I think there's at least one more.

Also mantis: is there anything beyond supplement one for the Lexicon? Which is a great and useful book, BTW.
