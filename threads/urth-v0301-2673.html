Doors: foreshadowing Kypris in LS

Urth list · 1 messages · 2003-01-16 to 2003-01-16

2003urthThe Book of the Long SunMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Mirrors, pictures and art

2003-01-16 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that There Are Doors foreshadows Kypris in The Book of the Long Sun, presenting Silk and Horn as parallel to Green and Silk in the earlier book, with Silk deceived by a mortal woman possessed by the goddess. Supports the case with copper imagery (the lockpick, the brazier, electricity as love), Lara's use of the television, and the postal code "AX" for Marea's area.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Silk is the bait-and-switch mark for Kypris; Copper imagery signals Aphrodite-Kypris throughout Wolfe's work

Exactly. The differences being that Silk is intelligent and highly educated (in contrast to dumb Green), Silk is melancholic (Green seems optimistic), and Silk sees through the mask (a difference: Silk has a mortal woman who was possessed for a time by Kypris, but Green has the genuine goddess . . . meaning that Silk is the mark for a bait and switch number, and got stuck with the Fanny that Green rejected) and gets suicidal (whereas Green happily marches off to Appleland).

(Oh hey, the postal code/state abbreviation for Marea's area is apparantly "AX." Interesting in a few mantic ways, "ax" being the most obvious.)

Wolfe seems to take great joy in working the metal copper into the story, presumably because copper is the metal of Aphrodite. North's lockpick is made from copper wire he pulls from the wall . . . his talk of electricity then can be seen in some way as being about Love. Think I'm kidding? Lara uses the tv to look at Green in his hospital room, then blows the set out (shades of KTV, that is KyprisTV, in LS). She also shows him a playback of his real world apartment at the time when he called and the strange guy answered (this stuff has always reminded me of David Lynch: in fact, TAD and CASTLEVIEW seem like Lynch movies to me).

The cold hotel room is heated with a copper brazier. And so on.

=mantis=
