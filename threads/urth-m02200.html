Reports the arrival of an advance reading copy of Soldier of Sidon, noting its Burton dedication, Herodotus epigraph, mock-scholarly foreword, page count, and illustrations like those in The Wizard Knight. Says the main narrative seems to be a voyage up the Nile, based on a first look at the book.

Just arrived here, via Fedex, an advance reading copy
of SOLDIER OF SIDON. Dedicated to Sir Richard Burton;
epigraph from Herodotus; usual mock-scholarly
Foreword; 319 pages; chapter illos much like those in
THE WIZARD KNIGHT. The main narrative thrust seems to
be a voyage up the Nile.

--Nick Gevers.
