Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2005-02-05 Jim Raylor #

Argues that The Fifth Head of Cerberus has a simple solution that Wolfe repeats throughout, pointing to his earliest story, a 1951 vanishing-ghost tale that opens by referring to detective fiction. Urges readers to set aside who killed whom and ask whether Veil's hypothesis is true, linking to an external site.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Veil's hypothesis about the Fifth Head of Cerberus is the key solution

Ok 5HC may not be a simple proposition, but it has a
simple solution which GW hammers home again and again,
despite his claim that he only likes to give clues
once.
The earliest GW story I have found (the vanishing
ghost 1951) refers to detective fiction in the first
paragraph.
So let's not ask who killed who.
Just ask if Veil's hypothesis is true.
