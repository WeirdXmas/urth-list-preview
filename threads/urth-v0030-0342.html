New Wolfe story online

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-04-26 to 2001-04-26

2001urthNicholas Gevers

2001-04-26 Nicholas Gevers #

Announces that a new Gene Wolfe story, "Copperhead," has been posted online at the SCI FICTION website, noting the poster had earlier urged the editor to publish one. Shares a link to the story; no critical discussion of its content is offered.
informs

A few months ago, I urged Ellen Datlow to run a Gene
Wolfe story on SCI FICTION, and lo--whether because of
my suggestion or without reference to it--she has now
done so. The story is new, it's called "Copperhead",
and it can be found here:

http://www.scifi.com/scifiction/originals/originals_archive/wolfe/wolfe1.html

--Nick Gevers.
