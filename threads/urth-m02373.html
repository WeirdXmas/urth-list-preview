The thread: Jordan Dennis backed the idea of a wiki for Wolfe's work, saying it would have helped with college papers on his fiction. Russell Wodell proposed starting it with a WikiWolfe section on the Latro novels, which are self-contained, and also asked why Wolfe never mentions Thomas Wolfe or Virginia Woolf. maru dubshinki objected that volunteers choose their own tasks, so a project cannot easily be steered toward one section, and noted lacking the Soldier books to write the Latro section. The thread stopped there with no resolution about where the wiki would begin.
  • Jordan Dennis: supports a Wolfe wiki as a study aid for college papers
  • Russell Wodell: proposes starting with the Latro novels as a self-contained section
  • maru dubshinki: volunteers pick their own tasks, so the Latro-first plan won't work

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Memory and forgetting

2006-05-03 Jordan Dennis #

Supports the idea of a wiki for Wolfe's work, saying it would have helped with college papers on his fiction. Jokes that the poster would spend too much time reading it and too little writing the papers.
supports

I think it would be a most helpful tool. I've written several college papers Wolfe's workk, and a wiki would have been a most helpful resource for me at the time.

Of course, I would spend way too much time reading it and not enough time writing the papers, but that wouldn't hurt my feelings too much.

2006-05-09 Russell Wodell replying to Jordan Dennis #

Proposes starting a WikiWolfe section on the Latro novels because they are self-contained and readers will soon revisit the first two books before Soldier of Sidon appears. Also asks whether anyone has wondered why Wolfe never mentions the authors Thomas Wolfe and Virginia Woolf.
proposes

I wonder if it might make sense to begin by building the wiki section on the Latro novels, since (a) they are relatively self-contained and (b) "Soldier of Sidon" is due this fall and many readers will be re-familiarizing themselves with the first two books.

On a totally separate note: Has anyone else wondered why Wolfe has never referred to the two most famous vulpine authors: Thomas Wolfe and Virginia Woolf?

Russell Wodell

2006-05-09 Maru Dubshinki replying to Russell Wodell #

Argues that a wiki project cannot easily be steered toward a particular section, such as the Latro novels, because volunteers choose their own tasks. Responds to Russell Wodell's proposal to start the wiki with the Latro books by noting the poster lacks the Soldier books and would find that section difficult to write.
disputes

It would be a good idea, but as many people have discovered to their rue on many different wikis, it's hard to force volunteers to do a particular subset of the ToDo list. I know I don't have any of the Soldier series, so it would be fairly difficult for me, at least.

~maru
