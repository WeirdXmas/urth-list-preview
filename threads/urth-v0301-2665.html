Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2003-01-16 Dan Rabin #

Proposes that the tattoo "RN" on North in An Evil Guest stands for Richard Nixon rather than North's real name, disputing the earlier suggestion that it reveals "Richard North." Grounds this on North's traits drawn from G. Gordon Liddy and North's questioning of the protagonist about Nixon and loyalty, and argues tattoos signal devotion more than identity.
disputes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: RN tattoo stands for Richard Nixon

Mantis writes;

The tattoo is more likely to stand for "Richard Nixon". The North character takes some traits from G. Gordon Liddy, and I recall him questioning the protagonist about Nixon and loyalty and such. Tattoos more often indicate devotion than the bearer's identity.

-- Dan Rabin
