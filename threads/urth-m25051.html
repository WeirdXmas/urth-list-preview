Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

2014-04-13 Andrew Mason #

Argues that finding a figurative Gene Wolfe inside a story is hard to apply here, since the name Ouen derives from Eugenius but nothing marks him as a wolf. Responds to Jeff Wilson's quoted recollection of Wolfe's GEnie advice and Neil's alzabo reading by noting that wolves appear openly in The Book of the New Sun.
disputes · source-and-allusion · puzzle-solving · theories: the alzabo is a gene wolf

Hm - if it has to be a _Gene_ Wolfe that definitely adds complexity to the issue. (There is a Gene, since 'Ouen' is a form of the name 'Eugenius'. But I don't see any signs of his being a wolf.) Yet there are certainly wolves in _New Sun_, quite openly - so I don't think we need to look for hidden ones there.
