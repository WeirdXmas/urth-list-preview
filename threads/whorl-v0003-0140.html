1997-02-13 Alice Turner #

Proposes that Tartaros, who appears to run Mainframe at the end of the series, is the one who overrides orders and sends the lander to Blue, making him a good character. Reasons from the premise that the landing ship's monitor is connected to Mainframe, so the decision must come from Tartaros.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Tartaros makes the decision to send the lander to Blue; Tartaros controls Mainframe at the end of the series

Another thought on Tartaros. He appears to be in charge of Mainframe at the end of the series. If that is so, and if the monitor on the landing ship is connected to Mainframe, and I would think it surely must be, then it is Tartaros that makes the decision to override orders and send the lander to Blue.

Which makes him a good guy.

-alga-
