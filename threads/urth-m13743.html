Undines

Urth list · 19 messages · 2010-07-19 to 2010-07-21

2010urthThe Book of the New SunLane HaygoodTony EllisLee BermanJosh GellerAdam ThorntonGerry QuinnDavid StockhoffJames WynnJeff WilsonDan'l Danehy-Oakes
The thread: Readers debated whether Severian drowned in the Gyoll and returned as an aquastor or eidolon, and whether Juturna, Dorcas, Idas and Baldanders are undines. Jeff Wilson and Lee Berman proposed linked readings of Juturna's kinship with Severian, the White Fountain connection, and the timing problem with Yesod's craft, while James Wynn offered a drowning-and-revival reading. Gerry Quinn attributed Baldanders' underwater breathing to gill-like scars rather than an undine encounter, and Lane Haygood's claim about Dorcas drew a sharp objection from Wilson. Tony Ellis ended the thread by arguing from Famulimus's 'son your mother bore' line that Severian self-resurrects in the same body, so the aquastor theory is unnecessary; no consensus was reached.
  • Lee Berman: undines may travel between stars; Severian is resurrected as an eidolon, possibly several times
  • Jeff Wilson: Juturna is Severian's twin sister and an undine; Dorcas's husband, not Dorcas, is the candidate
  • James Wynn: Severian drowned at the start of the series and the boy who left the river was an aquastor
  • Gerry Quinn: Baldanders' underwater breathing comes from gill-like scars seen at the inn
  • Tony Ellis: the skull's meaning is symbolic; Severian self-resurrects in the same body without aquastors
  • brunians: becoming an undine would take Baldanders-like deeds, a quip rather than a theory

Themes: Death and resurrection, Doubles, twins and clones, Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies

2010-07-19 Lee Berman #

Speculates that undines may travel between stars by riding on the ship or by mastering time, and links them to hierodules through a kelpie reference. Grounds this in Idas on the ship, Severian's stateroom description of B, F and O, and an undine on Ushas, while also questioning whether swimming through space is literal.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Undines ride on Tzadkiel's ship between stars; Undines travel between stars by mastering time; Undines and hierodules linked through kelpie lore

As Severian's powers of resurrection are an echo of his ability to resurrect the sun? Hm, could be.

Brother Severian, eh? Well, the bizarre sexual attraction between Severian and Jururna might suggest a family relationship between them. Some other connections also. But, as has been mentioned in other threads, we don't have the proper kinship terms for beings related by both sexual and asexual reproduction. Daughter-brides? Half-sisters? Khaibit? I guess these are WOlfe's attempts to translate such terms.

I like this general train of thought. There is something about the ubiquity of Undines on Urth (and maybe the stuff going on in Blue's waters) which suggests they might have a key role in transportation and genesis of megatherians.

I have always questioned if we should take their swimming between the stars literally, though. Island sized Tzadkiel aside, swimming through the void seems like a physics-defying task for a biological creature (can Inhumi realy do it, even for their much shorter distance between planets?)

I think there might be some hints that undines ride on The Ship. We see one of them, Idas, on the ship with Severian. Also, when Severian enters the ship stateroom of B, F and O, he describes:

Severian goes on to waffle, saying it was all a trick of the light. He also goes on to suggest that their chambers suggest to B, F and O (not really O though) that the nature of their chambers suggests they were descended from creatures like kelpies.

A kelpie is a somewhat malevolent Celtic water horse creature which can take the form of a woman to lure people to their death. It seems related to nixies, mermaids, sirens etc., thus establishing a possible link between hierodules and undines. Perhaps swimming between the stars really involves splashing around a liquidy stateroom on Tzadkiel's ship?

On the other hand, Severian runs into an undine on Ushas who is pretty impressive in pointing in the direction of superhuman dimensions. Perhaps they do swim between the stars using mastery of time? Or are they on the ship? I dunno which explanation is best.

2010-07-19 Jeff Wilson replying to Lee Berman #

Proposes that Juturna is Severian's twin sister, given over to Abaia, and that both are undines linked by water breathing and access to higher dimensions. Responds to Lee Berman's remark on their kinship by citing shared traits like merciful acts against masters, fountain associations, and the Green Man's time-skipping ratio.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Juturna is Severian's twin sister; Undines uplifted from the Fish creatures; Juturna and Severian share water-breathing gifts

Juturna doesn't seem to have literal resurrection, though she does save the life of Severian after he has ceased to breathe. But maybe she was preventing him from discovering his water breathing gift too soon?

In short: both breathe water, both have access to the higher dimensions, both show up at times with anachronistic knowledge.
Both appear to be otherwise human beings who have been gifted with these extraodrinary powers. No other people native to Urth in the Old Autarch's time do these things.

They both repeatedly commit acts of mercy against the interests of their masters. And who else but these two, in their time, is associated with fountains?

I think she's just a plain jane sister, likely a twin sister, who went over/was given over to Abaia. She may watch over her "little" brother like Miriam over Moses, or she may not know of their relation, only that they seem drawn together for some reason; perhaps they both like to watch a certain star's reflection on the waters, and feel some kind of connection with it.

If they have access to the fourth dimension and the Brook Madregot, that would seem to make it much easier to transcend the bounds of atmosphere and orbital mechanics, the way the Green Man uses them to transcend the huge gulfs of time between Severian's era and his own; at the very least the Green Man's is covering a day per flash in the few minutes Severian spends watching him run, roughly a factor of 1500:1.

Idas is presumed to be an undine due to his/her gills and complexion. Do the other undines feature gills?

The Fish is the most common creature of the mirrors, perhaps they are uplifted from it in some way? Fish-like or seal-like anatomy would seem both impractical and presumptive for Yesodis, who only get to enjoy the islands of the outer shell after much accomplishment working among the internal machinery, according to Alpheta.

That's Juturna, herself, drawn back to the drowned Citadel.

It's so often the way with Wolfe, isn't it?

2010-07-20 Lee Berman replying to Lee Berman #

Proposes that Severian's superhuman powers, including his healing, resurrection and White Fountain connection, were granted when he was duplicated as an eidolon, possibly during his rescue from drowning by the undine. Builds on Jeff Wilson's remark about aquastors and also speculates on how Baldanders gained water-breathing, linking it to the undine at the gate or to his eidolon formation.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Severian's powers granted during eidolon duplication; Severian's first miracle occurred during undine rescue; Baldanders became water-breathing eidolon at end of Sword; Baldanders met an undine at the gate

Wow, that is a very interesting consideration. Severian has various superhuman powers...could each of them have been endowed during his duplication as an eidolon?

If so, perhaps his first divine manifestation, healing and resurrection, occurred during his "rescue" from drowning by the undine. It was fairly soon after that when his first miracle occurred (Triskele).

We are led to believe Severian gains his connection to the New Sun/White Fountain via intercourse with Apheta. I hope she didn't have to kill him for that to happen (though many female bugs do that sort of thing).

Elsewhere, aren't we told that Severian's Yesod tests were all a sham? And that Tzadkiel had already imbued Severian with his White Fountain connection on the ship. Again we have the possibility that eidolon formation allows this.

(on a side note, how did Baldanders gain the ability to breathe under water? Some have pointed to evidence that he met up with an undine at the end of shadow and that was the mysterious incident at the gate.

If not that then perhaps he became a water-breathing eidolon at the end of Sword. He had B, F and O above him and perhaps Juturna and Abaia in the lake below him. Perhaps either were capable of doing it though one seems more likely than the other.

2010-07-20 Jeff Wilson replying to Lee Berman #

Proposes that Severian's connection to the White Fountain comes through a gene sample taken by the Yesodis, linked to Juturna sharing half his chromosomes, and that the Fountain's power reaches him as a frequency rather than an instant imbuing. Grounds this in the Fountain's power being blocked by the earth's limb and in Juturna coaxing him into water, and asks whether Dorcas's husband was a spy for Abaia.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Yesodis take gene sample to attune Severian to White Fountain; White Fountain power arrives as a frequency, not an imbuing; Undines convert humans to breathe water; Dorcas's husband was a spy for Abaia

I interpret this as the Yesodis collecting a gene sample so that the White Fountain can be attuned to him; this may also explain some of Juturna's transcending of dimension, since she shares roughly half his chromosomes. Despite the name, I don't think the White Fountain gives him underwater breathing, since the its power is blocked by even the limb of the earth, and the stars don't shine deeply under the water.

I got the impression that there's no one time where he is imbued so much as the WF sends out power on a frequency that he receives whenever its light shines upon him, including frequencies that pierce storms and clouds. This doesn't mean that he always has the skill or knowledge to use that power, depending on where along his personal timeline he is.

Juturna tries to coax him into the water and apparently other people are taken below the waves. If it isn't all a murder plot or dinner for the hungry undines, it implies that undines can convert humans to breathe water, and perhaps that the converted humans may even breed water-breathing children who later return to the land, like Idas. A symbiote could be involved, similar to the Green Man's "pond scum".

There remains the question of where the undine in Severian's family tree is. Was Dorcas' husband some kind of spy for Abaia?
