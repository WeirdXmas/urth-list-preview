Buying a sabretache

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-05-07 to 2001-05-07

2001whorlThe Book of the New SunDan Rabin

2001-05-07 Dan Rabin #

Answers the question of where to buy a sabretache by pointing to an online catalog of British ceremonial uniform items that lists one, and suggests a modern fanny pack as the nearest contemporary equivalent. Jokingly adds that he could not find a shop replicating late Commonwealth items like the Terminus Est blade, and threatens a duel to claim one.
answers

Christopher Culver asks:

A quick Google search on "sabretache" turns up
<http://www.users.globalnet.co.uk/~thinred/ceremonial.html>, an entire catalog of British uniform items.

The sabretache goes for 1.45 hectopounds. It doesn't look like something _I'd_ want to wear around town, but that's just me. The insignia part is bigger than the pouch part on this model.

If you don't want to go for replication, the item generally known in the U.S. as a fanny pack sounds like the nearest contemporary approximant.

I couldn't find a site specializing in the late Commonwealth period of Urth. If I could I'd go for the _Terminus Est_ loving reproduced even down to the signature of Jovinian on the tang of the blade. If they won't sell me one, I'll have them killed in a duel and claim it as my victor's right.

-- Dan "Agilus believed in flower power" Rabin
