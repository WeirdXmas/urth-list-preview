Proposes that the Narrator's ability to travel astrally in the Long Sun books comes from the Neighborly process that moved Horn's spirit into Silk's body, giving the resulting person non-human physical laws. Responds to Blattid's suggestion that the Narrator is only loosely attached to Silk's body, offering it as an explanation for the distance effects.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: Neighborly transplant gives Silk non-human physical laws

That's what I've always assumed--that the Neighborly process of transplanting Horn's spirit into Silk's body infused the resultant person with some degree of the Neighbor's unhuman physical laws, including the different relationship with distance.
