2011-02-23 Lee Berman replying to Stuart Hamm #

Argues that Star Trek was so widely watched in the 1960s that it shaped every American science fiction writer, while Doctor Who was not seen in America and so had no influence on Wolfe. Responds to Stuart Hamm's recollection of a dying planet's ruler hollowing out an asteroid, noting that the Daleks do not appear in The Book of the New Sun.
disputes

Hey, back then, when there was only 4-5 channels to watch, everybody watched Star Trek. If you liked science fiction you couldn't help but be influenced by it. On the other hand, nobody (in America) was watching Dr. Who in the late 60's so I agree with those who argue that it wasn't an influence and there are no daleks in BotNS.

2011-02-23 Marc Aramini replying to Son Of Witz #

Recounts the poster's memory of crashing a Star Trek convention in Las Vegas and meeting Spock's wife, reflecting on how that felt different from meeting Gene Wolfe. It is a personal anecdote with a reflection on wanting versus having, prompted by a quip from Son Of Witz.
chats

Which reminds me of the wisest thing I ever took from Star Trek: Having a thing, is not nearly so pleasing a thing, as wanting a thing.

The episode is probably not quite as good as it sounds ...

Hey, when I was a little kid I had a super crush on Spock's Wife (or betrothed or whatever), I thought she was very pretty. One day when I was living in Las Vegas a friend came to visit, and he and my girlfriend talked me into crashing a huge Star Trek convention at one of the casinos. I didn't want to, but they just walked in and I followed (hopefully there is a statute of limitations on breaking into an event like that).

Anyway, EVERYBODY from Star Trek was there, and from just about every science fiction show you could imagine. Then I saw the little old lady who was Spock's wife sitting at a table, and I talked to her for a little and I was almost going to say how beautiful she was but I thought better of it. Would that have made her happy or just sad that the time had already passed? For some reason that was way different than meeting Gene Wolfe or Floyd Mayweather, such a different "prime" between a writer, a fighter, and an actress.
