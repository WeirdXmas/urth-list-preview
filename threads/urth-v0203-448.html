BotNS references in Castlevania

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-03-26 to 2002-03-26

2002urthThe Book of the New SunChristopher Culver

2002-03-26 Christopher Culver #

Recalls that the game Castlevania: Symphony of Night uses the sword name estoc, a rare word that the designers may have taken from The Book of the New Sun, and wonders whether the Japanese version used it too. Responds to Marc Aramini's earlier mention of Terminus Est in the game, offering a memory of another Wolfe-derived weapon name and asking about Japanese translation.
questions · source-and-allusion

It's been about three years since I played that game, but besides Terminus Est I remember that one of the swords was called an estoc, a rather obscure word that the designers could have picked up from the BotNS. I wonder if it was like that in the original Japanese version, or just the product of the whimsy of the English translators. Has the Book of the New Sun been translated into Japanese?

Christopher Culver
