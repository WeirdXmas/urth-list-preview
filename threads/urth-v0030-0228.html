2001-01-04 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes the name "Durra" as a D-initial name for the green men, reasoning that "green" shares an Indo-European root with "grass", the green man smells grassy, and durra is a type of grass. Offers this as a loose fit in reply to Jeff Wilson's request for words meaning green, future or plenty that begin with D.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Durra as a name for the green men

Weeell . . . it ain't exactly what you are looking for, but here's "Durra."

My rationale is that "green" comes from an Indo-European root that also gives rise to "grass." And the green man has a grassy smell. And durra is a type of grass.

2001-01-04 Jeff Wilson #

Asks list members for Wolfean names for several Green Men and one Green Woman from The Book of the New Sun to use in a game, drawing on the GURPS New Sun name lists. Seeks names beginning with D to match a series of mysterious notes signed with that initial, and gives examples built from words for green, the future, and plenty.
questions · source-and-allusion

For gaming purposes, I am trying to name several Green Men from New Sun including one Green Woman. Inspired by Mantis' name lists in GURPS NEW SUN, I would like to give them suitably Wolfean names if anyone would like to contribute some.

Currently, I am compiling various languages' words for the color green, but other possibilities might be words relating to the future, or to plenty. However, I have yet to come across a suitable name beginning with D, and this would be invaluable in tying in with an earlier series of mysterious notes signed with that initial. Any suggestions would be appreciated.

some examples:
Green - Verde, Viridis, Gruen
Future - Skuld, Man~ana
Plenty - Coepia, Panamos
