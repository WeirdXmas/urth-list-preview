Silk for Calde!

Urth list · 9 messages · 2009-01-14 to 2009-01-16

2009urthThe Book of the Long SunThe Book of the New SunStephen CaseDavid StockhoffJames WynnJohn BarachSon of WitzMatthew GrovesStuart Hamm
The thread: Stephen Case jokingly proposed Silk for Calde t-shirts, and the thread quickly turned to casting actors for the Long Sun and New Sun books. Matthew Groves argued Silk should be young, slender and very fair, rejecting Son of Witz's Hugh Laurie pick, and Son of Witz countered that Silk is 23 and too young for Laurie. Stuart Hamm offered to buy a shirt design, while JBarach and David Stockhoff joked about casting Liam Neeson and a young actress as Thecla. Participants repeatedly proposed a separate fantasy-casting thread, so the discussion ended in social drift rather than textual argument.
  • Stephen Case: jokingly proposes Silk for Calde graffiti-style shirts and a matching Severian shirt
  • Matthew Groves: Silk should be young, slender and very fair, not older like Hugh Laurie
  • Son of Witz: Silk is 23, so Hugh Laurie is too old; offers Sigourney Weaver as Thecla and Clint Eastwood as Malrubius
  • David Stockhoff: proposes a separate casting thread and treats Malrubius's weight as an off-the-cuff aside
  • Stuart Hamm: offers to buy a Silk for Calde t-shirt design

Themes: Humour in Wolfe, Names and their meanings, Books, libraries and writing
