1000 to 2000 years from Typhon to Severian

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-02-01 to 1998-02-01

1998urthThe Book of the New SunPaul C Duggan

1998-02-01 Paul C Duggan #

Argues that Gene Wolfe's offhand answer of 1000 to 2000 years applies only to the interval between Typhon and Severian, not to the gap between the New Sun and our own period. Grounds this in the separate questions Duggan put to Wolfe in an online interview, noting that the books never specify the duration.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · author-and-biography · theories: Wolfe's 1000 to 2000 year guess refers to Typhon to Severian

Since I'm the one who asked Wolfe the question on the People Website interview (http://pathfinder.com/people/interactive/wolfetrans.html) I'll state that I'm certain he is giving two seperate answers to two seperate questions.

first, I ask him how long between the New sun series and our own period, and then I ask how long between Typhon and Severian. He answers "That is never specifed in the books" and "My offhand guess is between 1000 and 2000 years".

Chatroom conversations being what they are, and that it is the only way to assume he tried to answer both questions, I believe he is only making an offhand guess at the T-S duration.

Or maybe he's being deliberatly enigmatic :-)
