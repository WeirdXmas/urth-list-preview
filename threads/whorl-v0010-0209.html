Secrets of the inhumi

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-01-11 to 2000-01-11

2000whorlAlice Turner

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2000-01-11 Alice Turner #

Argues that the inhumi's reptilian nature and ability to hibernate cannot be the central secret of the Long Sun books, since readers already suspected reptilian traits. Grounds this in Horn's promise to Krait and in the fact that many helpers and gardeners already know about the hibernation, and rejects a theory of Horn's double vision about limbs as far-fetched, noting Seawrack's limb count.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Horn has double vision about limbs; The inhumi's secret is not hibernation

Plasma is absolutely right about the inhumi being reptilian and being able to hibernate or shut down. But what is new about that---we've pretty much known that all along, almost since we met Quetzal (well, we didn't know about the hibernation). And I certainly don't think that's **the** secret. Mind you, I don't know what the secret is, but I'll lay money that that isn't it. Consider: Horn has sworn up and down to Krait, whom he loves as a son, that he will not reveal the secret. But quite a few helpers, gardeners etc. are in on the hibernation shtick.

I have to say that the theory of Horn's double vision wrt arms and legs seems exceeding far-fetched. Esp. as Seawrack has only one of the former and two of the latter.

-alga
