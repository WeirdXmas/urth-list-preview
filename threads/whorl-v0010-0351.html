Who else has read IGJ?

Whorl list · 5 messages · 2000-06-09 to 2000-06-12

2000whorlThe Book of the New SunNicholas GeversMatthew MalthouseWilliam H. AnsleyAlice TurnerYves Meynard
The thread: Readers asked who had read the advance copy of In Green's Jungles and whether discussion should wait until enough list members had read or ordered the book. Yves Meynard and Nicholas Gevers said they had read it, and Gevers linked the book to the Citadel gate detail in The Shadow of the Torturer. Alice Turner asked how many had read or pre-ordered it. William Ansley argued for starting spoiler-labelled discussion immediately, and matthew.malthouse joked about the gloating over the advance copy. The thread stayed light and ended without any substantive analysis of the novel.
  • Yves Meynard: has an advance copy and has many questions, so will wait for others before discussing
  • Nicholas Gevers: has also read it and asks whether Yves now knows why the Citadel gate had no guard
  • Alice Turner: asks how many have read or pre-ordered, and what readership is needed to begin
  • William Ansley: wants spoiler-labelled discussion to start now rather than after critical mass
  • matthew.malthouse: teases those boasting of the advance copy and jokes about rivals

2000-06-09 Yves Meynard #

Asks whether other readers have an advance copy of In Green's Jungles and says he has many questions after reading it, so he will wait for others to discuss it. Posts a playful, non-analytic message with no specific passage, character or argument cited.
questions

Anybody else out there got hold of an advance copy of _In Green's Jungles_? After reading it, I've still got a lot more questions than answers. I can't wait for people who are smarter than me to get the book and start discussing it. In the meantime, I promise to keep my mouth shut, difficult as that may be.

Yves Meynard
(yes, I'm gloating)

2000-06-09 Nicholas Gevers #

Replies to Yves Meynard's remark about having an advance copy of In Green's Jungles by saying he has also read it and eager to discuss it. Asks Yves whether he now knows why the Citadel gate had no guard in Chapter I of The Shadow of the Torturer, a question that seems to link the new book to the first volume's opening.
questions · close-textual-reading

I'm another of the fortunate (or damnable) few, and
I'm also eager to begin the discussion. In the
dwindling meantime, let me ask you this, Yves: do you
now know why there was no guard at the Citadel gate in
Chapter I of THE SHADOW OF THE TORTURER?

2000-06-11 William H. Ansley #

Says they have pre-ordered the book and want spoiler-labelled discussion to start now, not after critical mass, on condition that all messages carry a label and stay on the list. Responds to David Langford's question about whether enough readers have ordered the book, and notes they read OBW the same way.
chats

I have no desire to see your review (or any) before I read the book, but, as long as you clearly mark it in the subject header I can skip it and save it for later, so post away.

I put in my order about two weeks ago. As far as I am concerned, as long as a suitable label (such as "IGJ Spoilers") is in the subject header of *all* the messages that make up this discussion and it is restricted to the Whorl list, then the discussion can begin right now. I will just save the messages until after I have a chance to read the book (I did this with OBW).

Have fun, you lucky skunks (as Gary Larson of Far Side fame would say).

William Ansley
