Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

2005-06-17 James Wynn #

Argues that Typhon and Ouen are both Severian's fathers, with Severian a clone of Piaton, whom the post identifies as Typhon's son Hieros. Grounds this on Severian leading Scylla and the Narrator to a crypt at the end of The Short Sun, a mirrored failed revolt on Urth, and the claim that the corpse is blind Tartaros.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon is Severian's biological father; Ouen is Severian's father; Severian is a clone of Piaton; Piaton is Hieros, Typhon's son

When Don posted his clone theory a couple years ago, I thought it was interesting and quaint but a little "out there". Now, having read The Short Sun and knowing that Severian's crypt in the necropolis is Typhon's family crypt, I take it more seriously. And having come to the conclusion that Piaton is Typhon's son (whom we have come to know as Hieros), I'm very favorable to the likelihood that Severian is a clone of Piaton.

That doesn't rule out Ouen being Severian's "Dad" just as Maitre was #5's "dad" and Tussah was Silk's. But it *would* make Typhon Severians bio-dad as well. It doesn't rule Ouen out as being Sev's brother or twin as well, for that matter. So my vote is that Typhon and Ouen are Severian's dads.

Evidence? (deep breath) Well first of all, at the end of The Short Sun, The Narrator and little Scylla (whose real name escapes me), Typhon's daughter, tell young Severian they are looking for her crypt and Severian takes them to one...he doesn't help them find one, he takes them to it. Which crypt would that be but the one he hangs out in? There are plenty of literary clues that the crypt actually is Severian's somehow so I say Severian is of the house of Typhon.

I also believe that the failed overthrow of Pas in the Mainframe of the Whorl was duplicated by Typhon's actual family on Urth. After all, at the time of her scanning little Scylla herself was already conspiring with big Urthian tyrant Scylla against her father, so isn't it likely that the originals of the rest of the revolting Mainframe simulations were doing the same thing as well? If I'm right, then on Urth (after the Whorl set sail) the human originals of Hieros, Molpe, Scylla, and Echidna (and maybe others) attempted unsuccessfully to overthrow Typhon and (just as Pas erased those usurpers from the Mainframe) he had them put to death (Hieros excepted but we'll get to that). The blind human original of Tartaros aided his father's victory over them.

The rest of my evidence rests on extremely involved proofs regarding (blonde) Silk's and (dark) Awk's ancestry and the fact that Wolfe treats them purely thematically but undeniably as the same person. I won't flog you with that, except to say that I have become convinced that Piaton=Hieros and that Typhon took his body as punishment for his crimes. Perhaps Typhon's original body was even damaged beyond repair, I can't say.

So who is the male corpse Severian sits around contemplating? Typhon? Nope, his body would be headless. Hieros? Not in my opinion and if I'm right, it is up on that mountian. The only named candidate left in Typhon's family is blind Tartaros.

Now even if you agree that Severian is a clone of a member of Typhon's house, you might still argue that the body in the crypt *has* to be Severian somehow, not his "brother". While that is reasonable, I am convinced that Piaton=Hieros, the Whorl's god of Death, is more like Severian than the god of thieves (and, yes, I know, even of darkness) and I am asthetically inclined to the idea of Hieros overthrowing Pas again through a suicide conspiracy between Severian and Piaton.

~ Crush
