Sabretache

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-09-30 to 1998-09-30

1998urthJon Camfield

1998-09-30 Jon Camfield #

Proposes that Severian's sabretache is really a version of Heward's Handy Haversack, the magic bag from old Dungeons & Dragons material. Lists the items the pouch held, such as the Claw, a whetstone, the letter for the Archon, and flint and steel, while admitting he cannot recall the rest.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Severian's sabretache is Heward's Handy Haversack

You see, it is really Severians Sabretache of Super-Storing. For a description, see "Heward's Handy Haversack" in old D&D material.

What did he keep in it, anyway? The Claw until he found it, his whetstone, the letter for the Archon, flint and steel or a tender box... hmmm... I can't remember anything else...
