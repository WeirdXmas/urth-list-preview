5HC: Sol from Wolf 294

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-06-13 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that Wolf 294 cannot be the star system of Saints Anne and Croix and offers celestial coordinates to place Sol in Sagittarius instead. Works from the constellation Fighting Lizard and the right ascension and declination of Wolf 294, calculating its opposite point on the sky, with a playful tone and an invitation to check the math.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Wolf 294 is not Saints Anne and Croix's star system

I know you're all dying to know, but too shy to ask, "What about the abo Constellation `Fighting Lizard' with regards to this tentative designation of Wolf 294 as the star system of Saints Anne and Croix?" <g>

Well cadets, I'll tell ya hows I sees it.

Wolf 294 is located at Right Ascention 6 hours 51 minutes, Declination North 33 degrees and 20 minutes.

The "opposite" of that (please check my math and/or reasoning) will be around R.A. 18 hours 21 minutes, Dec. South 33 degrees.

This puts Sol in the area of Sagittarius.

=mantis=
