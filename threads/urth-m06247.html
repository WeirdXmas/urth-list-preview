Themes: Stories within stories, Water, the sea and drowning

2008-06-18 Marc Aramini #

Proposes that the retold Theseus story, with its enormous sea ship and novascaputs, indicates the scale of monsters like Abaia in The Book of the New Sun. Argues the student's creation must face that huge sea creature that dyes the ship, and that these inset stories mirror Severian's main narrative both thematically and factually.
