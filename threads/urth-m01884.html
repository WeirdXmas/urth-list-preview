Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

2006-08-21 Roy C. Lackey #

Argues that Severian's destruction of Urth in The Urth of the New Sun was not needed for humanity's survival, since mankind had already spread across the galaxy. Responds to Jack Redelfs, who called the destruction a justifiable redemptive act, and cites Wright's Attending Daedalus as a counterpoint about a slow ice future.
disputes · theology-and-religion · theories: Ushas was not necessary for species survival

Then you won't agree with Wright's ATTENDING DAEDALUS. <g>

The "Ice Future" would take a long time to come about. But no matter what happened to Urth, mankind had already spread, minimally, all across the galaxy. Ushas was not necessary to the survival of the species.

-Roy
