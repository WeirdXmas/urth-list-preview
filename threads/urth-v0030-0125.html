PEACE: genre of chapter one

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-11-17 to 2000-11-17

2000urthAdam Stephanides

Themes: Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both

2000-11-17 Adam Stephanides #

Proposes that chapter one of Peace is modelled on a Western rather than the serious literary fiction he first suggested. He grounds this in frontier talk, the "fake Indians" and the three real Indians in Hannah's recollections, and the one-on-one showdown in which Weer defends his family.
proposes · genre-and-craft · close-textual-reading · theories: Chapter one of Peace is modelled on a Western

In my earlier post in which I pointed out that each chapter of PEACE is modelled on a different genre, I tentatively suggested "serious literary fiction" for chapter one, although I was unsure. Since then I've thought of another possibility, albeit one that seems unlikely at first glance. There is a fair amount of talk about frontier days in chapter one; there are a lot of fake Indians, and three real Indians (in Hannah's recollections). And the principal action of the chapter is a one-on-one showdown (to the death, as it turns out) in which Weer is defending himself and his family, so to speak, against an aggressor. Could chapter one be ... a Western?

--Adam
