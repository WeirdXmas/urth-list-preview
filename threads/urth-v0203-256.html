2002-03-01 Seth Lombardi #

Admits that a pop song's lyrics have little to do with the real meaning of the word "pelagic" and concedes the link to The Book of the New Sun is wishful thinking, while still wondering why a rock song would use such an erudite word. Playfully suggests the lyric "in the wake of his chariot" might point to the new sun, then says he will drop the subject and recommends the musician to Wolfe fans.
chats

I am aware of the meaning of the word, and fully admit the song has little to do with a pelagic argosy sighting land.
Yes, this is wishful thinking on my part. I'm being a goofball. Sorry if it come accross as if I had a strong conviction about this; I was just thrilled to have a pop song that so recalled BOTNS, to me personally.

But "in the wake of his chariot", _could_ be the new sun? Eh? Eh?

Okay, maybe not.

Still, pelagic is one helluva weird word to put in a rock song, where the purpose is not to appear erudite, doncha think?
I will shut up about this now, and only reiterate that just about anyone into GW would probably like this guy's music.

Seth
