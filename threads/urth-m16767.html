Reports that the poster is reading Jay Lake's novel Green and finds it impressive despite looking like generic fantasy, citing its Wolfe-like language, contradictory cosmological myths, and a direct Wolfe reference. Asks whether others have read Green or anything else by Lake.
informs

I'm reading _Green_ by Jay Lake. I would never have read it on my own; it looks like a generic D&D-style fantasy. But 100 pages in I'm very impressed. The language is almost Lupine, there are multiple, contradictory versions of a cosmological myth, and there's even a direct Wolfe reference: the main character at one point reads from "The Seventeen Lives of the Megatherians."

Has anyone else read this, or anything by Lake?
