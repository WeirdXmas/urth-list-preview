The thread: Ab de Vos asked what a list notice about excessive bounces meant after re-enabling his subscription, and what the term 'bounce' means. Marc Aramini and Dave Lebling explained that a bounce means mail was returned undelivered, and Dave suggested the fault may lie with the list's hosting rather than the recipient. Eric Bourland reported that the list's mail to Ab's address had bounced three times because his server rejected it, and asked him to whitelist the list address. The thread stayed entirely on list administration with no Wolfe content, ending with the delivery problem unresolved in the posts.
  • Ab de Vos: asked what the excessive-bounce notice and the term bounce meant
  • Marc Aramini: a bounce means the list's messages are returned undelivered, so Ab may not be receiving them
  • Dave Lebling: bounces are generally undeliverable mail and the fault may be with the list's hosting
  • Eric Bourland: Ab's mail server rejected list mail three times; add the list address to trusted senders
