2000-03-29 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs Inire that the forthcoming Litany volume collects the first two Long Sun books, Nightside the Long Sun and Lake of the Long Sun, rather than all four. Answers Inire's Amazon-based question directly by noting that a later volume will hold the remaining two books.
answers

Inire,

LITANY collects NIGHTSIDE THE LONG SUN and LAKE OF THE
LONG SUN. A later Orb volume, EPIPHANY OF THE LONG
SUN, will contain CALDE and EXODUS.
