The thread: A short off-topic exchange on the Hyperion thread asks whether a film adaptation is coming and whether Wolfe's bookish fiction could ever be filmed. Gryphoncrossing doubts Scorsese would direct and suggests Wolfe's Peace would make a strange film, while maru jokes that the poster would know about the Simmons quartet film if a bigger Simmons fan. Both mock the idea of adapting Simmons's books, citing the poor Ender's Game film. The thread ends without any textual argument about Wolfe, so it simply drifts away.
  • Gryphoncrossing: Wolfe's bookish work resists filming, and a Simmons Hyperion film would be hubristic
  • maru: Mocks the poster for not knowing of the planned film and doubts adaptation given Ender's Game

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both

2005-03-22 Gryphoncrossing #

Riffs on a Hyperion film adaptation, suggesting Scorsese might only executive-produce it, and argues that Wolfe's work is so bookish it resists filming unlike SF written with a movie in mind. Offers no textual evidence; the claim rests on the poster's general sense of which SF translates to screen.
chats

Another Hyperion fan! Excellent...

one three-hour movie, I believe, for Martin Scorsese. I have a feeling Scorsese will end up just exec-producing it, though.

Hyperion exists on that line of great SF books that are literature which cannot reasonably be translated into film, and those that are good stories that can. I bring this up because I find Wolfe perhaps the living SF writer whose work is SO book-ish that I can't imagine it being adapted to film. On the other end are the many SF books which seem to have been written with an ultimate movie adaptation in mind.

John
