Richard Dreyfus as Dr. Talos

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-13 to 1998-05-13

1998urthCraig Christensen

1998-05-13 Craig Christensen #

Suggests that the reader who hears Richard Dreyfus's voice in Dr. Talos may be recalling Dreyfus's role as leader of the traveling actors in Tom Stoppard's Rosencrantz and Guildenstern are Dead. Offers this film-and-stage association as a possible source for the voice impression, answering the earlier poster's remark about Dreyfus.
answers

Robert King,

Have you seen Tom Stoppard's "Rosencrantz and
Guildenstern are Dead"? If you have perhaps that is where you get the association. Dreyfus plays the leader of the traveling actors in this retelling of Hamlet from the minor characters' viewpoint.

Craig
