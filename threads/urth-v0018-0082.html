Lifespan of Hierodules

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-09-02 to 1998-09-02

1998urthThe Book of the New SunC R Culver

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1998-09-02 C R Culver #

Argues that the hierodules truly have short lives, citing Severian's remark in the Resurrection chapter that old Father Inire lived long beyond the span of his short-lived kind. Responds to Peter Westlake's question about whether only Baldanders claims they have short lives, reasoning that Severian's ten years working with Inire and the hierodules give him good grounds for the claim.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Hierodules truly live only about twenty years

<Isn't it only Baldanders who says they have short lives?>

In the tenth year of his autarchy, in the chapter called "Ressurection," Severian writes, "old, twisted Father Inire, alive so long beyond the span of his short-lived kind." After ten years of working with Inire and the noble Hierodules O,B&F, I'm sure Severian knew what he was talking about, and that the Hierodules truly live merely a score of years.
