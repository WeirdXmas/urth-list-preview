2009-01-10 Nigel Price #

Promotes Ultan's Library, a website of critical essays, reviews and features on Wolfe's work, and invites members to submit suitable articles to the co-editors. Mentions Nick Gever's essay on Briah in The Book of the Long Sun as a recent example and gives the site address for newcomers.
informs

Dave Tallman recently mentioned Nick Gever's article "Five steps towards Briah: Gene Wolfe's 'The Book of the Long Sun'", which is published over at the Ultan's Library website.

As there are some new members on the list, can I give a general plug for Ultan's Library, in case anyone hasn't discovered it yet? It's a collection of critical essays about Wolfe's books and stories, plus some reviews and one or two features and news items.

You'll find it at http://www.ultan.org.uk/.

Can I also say that we're always on the look-out for new articles of a suitable standard. Do get in touch with either Jonathan Laidlow or myself if you think you have something that might be suitable.

Nigel Price
Co-Editor, Ultan's Library
