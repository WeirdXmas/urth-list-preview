Silk's perception of time

Urth list · 6 messages · 2002-11-15 to 2002-11-19

2002urthThe Book of the Long SunCharles ReedJosh GellerDon DoggettDan'l Danehy-Oakes
The thread: Don Doggett argued that Silk's enlightenment, when he perceives time stopped, marks the Whorl reaching faster-than-light speed, since Silk stands outside time with the Outsider as God, and that his visions are present perception rather than memory. Charles Reed disputed the timing, noting the Whorl had orbited the short sun for about thirty years, longer than Silk's life. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes disputed the claim that God is always NOW, proposing that God perceives past, present and future as simultaneously present, while agreeing the Outsider makes moments present to Silk. Josh Geller offered a layered-dimensions model with God beyond conception, offered as personal opinion. The thread drifted into a side question on SMERSH, which Danehy-Oakes answered as a real Soviet agency distinct from the fictional SPECTRE.
  • Don Doggett: enlightenment marks the Whorl's FTL speed, Silk stands outside time and sees in present perception
  • Charles Reed: the Whorl had orbited the short sun for about thirty years, so the FTL dropout cannot match Silk's enlightenment
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: God perceives past, present and future as simultaneously present rather than always NOW
  • Josh Geller: humans move through time involuntarily, angels move freely in the time-plane, God lies beyond all conception

Themes: Memory and forgetting, Gods, angels and the Increate, Time loops and causality

2002-11-15 Don Doggett #

Argues that Silk's enlightenment, when he perceives time stopped, marks the point where the Whorl reaches faster-than-light speed, since Silk stands outside time with the Outsider as God. Builds the case from the idea that God experiences past, present and future as one NOW, so Silk's visions are present perception rather than memory. Responds to Crush's claim that Silk's time-stoppage proves nothing about time's speed, turning that objection into support.
proposes · theology-and-religion · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Silk's enlightenment marks the Whorl reaching FTL speed; Silk perceives events as present through the Outsider

Crush says:
"Silk is not only "aware" that Time has stopped. He says he was *outside* Time. The speed in which Time is moving at the "time" of his enlightenment is irrelevant to him since such differences only matters from the perspective of persons *in* Time. So the Time stoppage for Silk doesn't tell us anything either way."

Don smiles broadly and slaps Crush on the back:
Actually your objection only strengthens my theory and makes me certain that Silk's enlightenment is the point where the Whorl achieves and leaves ftl speed. Allow me to explain. When we say God, or the Outsider (I'm assuming that the consensus is that the Outsider is God), is outside of time what we are saying is that God exists in the past, present, and future simultaneously and without differentiation. God isn't in the past, seeing the future, or in the future, seeing the past. God is always NOW. So if Silk is outside of time, with the Outsider (or God) he is seeing the past, present, and future as the Outsider sees them, while they are happening. Of course, he can only describe to Horn (or Nettle, or was it Marble?) what he saw in terms of past present and future, but in his perception it was all now. Outside of time means immersion in reality, without the barriers of time. In essence, when Silk saw Patera Pike pray to the Outsider for help he saw it as it happened (not as a vision of the past), and when he perceived that time had stopped in his present, time really had stopped.

2002-11-18 Charles Reed #

Argues that Silk's enlightenment could not have coincided with the Whorl leaving faster-than-light travel, since the Whorl had been orbiting the short sun for about thirty years, longer than Silk's lifetime. Responds to Don Doggett's theory by pointing to the timeline of Silk's age at the start of Nightside, disputing the claim that his enlightenment marks the FTL dropout.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Silk's enlightenment occurred at FTL dropout

Hello all.

I hate to rain on everyone's parade who seems to think that Silk's enlightenment occurred at the point where the Whorl dropped out of FTL travel (if in fact the Whorl was capable of FTL travel, which I tend to disbelieve), but I'm going to.

At the time Silk was enlightened, the whorl had been in orbit around the "short sun" for about thirty years, and therefore could not have been going faster than light at any time during Silk's life which, at the beginning of NIGHTSIDE, was 23 years long.

Charles

2002-11-18 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues that the quoted claim that God is always NOW misstates the relation between eternity and time, proposing instead that God perceives past, present and future as simultaneously present. Responds to the quoted post's reading of Silk's perception of time, agreeing that the Outsider makes moments present to Silk that he needs to perceive.

Don Dogget, agent of SMERSH, emerged from an alley and dropped the microfilm. When deciphered, it read:

[...]

Actually, I think this slightly reverses the usual understanding of the relationship between God-in-Eternity and Time. I would not say that God "is always NOW"; rather, I would say that God perceives past, present, and future as always-already=20 simultaneously present before God. To say that God is NOW almost=20 seems to restrict God, to place God in the NOW, whereas I posit that we want to say that no moment contains or can contain God.

With this I completely concur -- God/the Outsider makes those moments "present" to Silk which Silk "needs" to perceive.

--Blattid

2002-11-18 Josh Geller #

Proposes a layered model of dimensions in which humans move involuntarily through a fourth dimension called time, while angels move freely in two dimensions of the time-plane and God is beyond all conception. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's quoted point, which Don Dogget made, that God perceives past, present and future simultaneously rather than being always NOW. The poster offers it as personal opinion, relating it to the books' angels and small-g gods rather than citing textual passages.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: Angels move freely in two time-plane dimensions

I greet the Rider of the Mouse!

Here's one (somewhat simplified) way of looking at it:

Human people are creatures which exist in three dimensions. and are in involuntary motion in a fourth dimension. This motion is what we call time. The plenum of parallel Worlds can be conceived as a plane perpendicular to this fourth dimension.

According to this system, the angels (or small-g gods, however you prefer) exist in four dimensions, are free to move in two others, and are in involuntary motion in a seventh. The two dimensions that they are free to move in correspond to the dimensions of the time-plane mentioned above.

Maybe (probably) there are levels beyond this.

Big-G God is always NOW (as Dan'l puts it) to all of this. The angels don't really know all that much more about it than human people do. The angel's angels (assuming there are such) don't know any more about it either and so on.

All of these conceptions are not-God. God is beyond conception.

This is, of course, all my own opinions based mostly on personal research.

.

2002-11-19 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Explains that SMERSH was a real Soviet counterintelligence agency, glossing its name as a Russian phrase meaning death to spies, and that it opposed the CIA and was fought by James Bond in the early novels and films. Answers a poster's question about the term appearing in a quoted Silk-thread passage, distinguishing it from the fictional SPECTRE.
answers · source-and-allusion

SMERSH, short for "Smyert shpionam" ["death to all spies"] --=20 the Soviet spy agency which stood in counterpart to the US's CIA, and against which James Bond fought in the original books and early movies. Yes, it was a real agency, unlike SPECTRE.

--Blattid
