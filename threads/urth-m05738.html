Severian's Family

Urth list · 3 messages · 2008-07-10 to 2008-07-10

2008urthThe Book of the New SunO'Donnell, Tim (BOSI)Roy C. LackeyGreg Bates
The thread: Readers debated whether Dorcas, or others such as Jolenta, Cyriaca, Agia and Agilus, are Severian's mother or relatives in The Book of the New Sun. Greg Bates asked Tim O'Donnell to justify his claim that a single clue in the final story reveals the prisoner in the next room as the prisoner's mother, saying he had never seen that reading. Roy Lackey withdrew his own earlier theory that Dorcas is Severian's mother because the timeline does not permit it, and he judged the rush-basket evidence O'Donnell cited to be weaker. O'Donnell admitted he cannot be certain, calling the support circumstantial and unconfirmed by Wolfe, while being skeptical that Wolfe intended any kinship. The thread ended without agreement, with the mother identification left unresolved.
  • Greg Bates: wants the reasoning behind the single clue for the prisoner-as-mother reading
  • Roy C. Lackey: abandoned the Dorcas-as-mother theory because the timeline rules it out
  • Tim O'Donnell: books invite readers to identify Severian's family, though evidence is circumstantial and unconfirmed by Wolfe

Themes: Fathers, mothers and children, Memory and forgetting, Unreliable narration

To be fair, there is plenty of evidence.  It is all in the archives, Mantis and Agia did a huge amount of work on this and there is some to strong evidence of all these relationships in the books.  There is also the suggestion that Severian may be his own grandfather (the rush basket Dorcas has with her when Severian meets her for the last time) though this is not as strong.

I am not sure what you mean by better and more solid clues.  Wolfe himself stated that he leaves only one clue for the reader.  For instance in 5HOC there is only a single clue that the prisoner in the room next door in the final story is the prisoner's mother.
