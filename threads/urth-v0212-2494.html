Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2002-12-20 Marc Aramini #

Proposes that a figure in Chapter 20 of In Green's Jungles, alongside Rigolio, is the means by which the narrator travels to the Red Sun Whorl, and that the 'witch in the smoke' is linked to this mechanism. Grounds the claim in the narrator's thoughts when first drawn to the Red Sun Whorl and in the recurrence of the witch-in-smoke image elsewhere in Wolfe's work.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Unseen figure in Chapter 20 enables travel to Red Sun Whorl; Witch in the smoke recurs as travel mechanism

If you look at Chapter 20 in In Green's Jungles, you will find that there is someone besides Rigolio there who might have been somewhere/when on the Red Sun Whorl, allowing the narrator to travel to it.
But not everyone can see or hear this individual. Notice also the "witch in the smoke" and what the narrator thinks about when he is drawn for the first time to the Red Sun Whorl. Where else has a witch in smoke appeared, my pretties? Where else will she appear?
This is my own observation.

Marc Aramini
