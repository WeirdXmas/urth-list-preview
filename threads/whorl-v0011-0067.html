"Wijzer warned me" (more Blue mysteries)

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-08-09 to 2000-08-09

2000whorlChris Hall

2000-08-09 Chris Hall #

Proposes that "Wijzer warned me" on page 366 of The Book of the Long Sun refers to Wijzer's remark that Horn will change so much that even his own sons won't recognize him. Answers Fernando Q. Gouvea's question by rereading Horn's conversation with Wijzer, offering the solution tentatively since the poster lacks the book at work.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Wijzer warned Horn he would become unrecognizable to his sons

4) What does "Wijzer warned me" on page 366 refer to?

I don't have the book with me at work, but when I read this line I went back and reread Horn's conversation with Wijzer and figured this out (I think). Wijzer said something to the effect that eventually Horn would change so that even his own sons wouldn't recognize him.
