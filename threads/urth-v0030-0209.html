Since we're marking time....
*Unbreakable* seems also a flicque that many on this list would enjoy. Don't expect it to be as remarkable as *Sixth Sense,* but to say much about it would give away too much. It is fantasy, with fun/interesting allusions to a particular genre of fantasy/SF literature, and also curious symbolism, hints of some kind of theology (like *Sixth Sense*), and would appeal to those who like those aspects of Wolfe.
