2001-06-23 PMorris33 #

Asks when Gene Wolfe will return Latro, the narrator of the Soldier series, home, noting his memory-palace discipline, the chariot races, the Lares spirit's pictures of his farm family, and his last sighting boarding a Punic ship. Written as an eager plea after news of a new Wolfe book, with no argument beyond the request.

Wonderful to hear about a new Wolfe book in any of his many fine narrative schemes. But what about the "Soldier Series"? Will poor Latro ever ever get home? He's beginning to get a handle on his memory, (thanks to Matteo Ricci-style memory palace disciplines), he's made quite a splash during the Chariot races, we know the lovely Lares domestic spirit has shown him pictures of his farming family, but he's still stranded among the Greeks. Well, he's last seen boarding a Punic/Carthogenian ship.

BRING LATRO BACK HOME. NOW !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

Smudgecat.
