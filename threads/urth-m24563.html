Lewis and "Westwind"

Urth list · 1 messages · 2013-03-17 to 2013-03-17

2013urthJesse C. Polhemus

Themes: Catholic faith and sacraments, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

2013-03-17 Jesse C. Polhemus #

Points out a connection between C.S. Lewis's Mere Christianity and Wolfe's story "Westwind", quoting a passage on Christianity as a king landing in disguise and a secret wireless. Offers the quotation alone, apologizing in case the link was already noted, without developing an argument about the story.
informs · source-and-allusion

The connection between Lewis and "Westwind" may have already been pointed out, and I apologize, but MERE CHRISTIANITY (Macmillan paperback, 1977) fell open to page 51 this morning:

"Christianity is the story of how the rightful king has landed, you might say landed in disguise, and is calling us to all to take part in a great campaign of sabotage. ?When you go to church you are really listening-in to the secret wireless from our friends: that is why the enemy is so anxious to prevent us from going."

- Jesse
