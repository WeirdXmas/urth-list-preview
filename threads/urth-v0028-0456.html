Responds to Michael Andre-Driussi's reading of Foila's contest tale in Wolfe's Curses, Foila'd again, agreeing it is reasonable while doubting that Foila knew the other soldiers before her injury. Notes that the poster likes Loyal's tale least and sees Foila's blame of inept soldiers as one possibility among others.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Foila blames inept fellow soldiers for her wound

Salutations....

Whoops. I completely misunderstood your comment, that you felt she had *won* the contest by telling the best story. Quit confusing me with sports metaphors!

This sounds reasonable, if we assume that Foila knew Hallvard and Melito before she was injured. I have the sense that she didn't, that she was in some different formation from the PBI types until they all washed up together in the Pelerines' MASH unit; but either way is possible. Or are you suggesting that she blames the inept infantry in general -- they may be volunteers, but they're not professionals?

This is a reasonable and well-thought out possibility. I don't think it's the only possibility, perhaps because I liked Loyal's tale the least. But it does make sense.
