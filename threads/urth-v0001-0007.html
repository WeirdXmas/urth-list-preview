Witches & eclipse-maker

Urth list · 1 messages · 1997-04-14 to 1997-04-14

1997urthThe Book of the New SunKieran Cleary

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Time loops and causality

1997-04-14 Kieran Cleary #

Asks a set of questions about The Book of the New Sun: what technology the witches' glass-topped table with miniature landscape might correspond to, what the enigmatic object causing the eclipse is, whether the corridors to the atrium of time are literal, why the green man is ignorant of the New Sun, and how Jonas relates to Miles. Says the poster is waiting for the Lexicon and is reasoning from memory of the last read-through.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: Jonas reconstructed from Miles's biological parts

As someone who is eagerly awaiting a copy of the Lexicon, I have a rag-tag bundle of questions which I remember being puzzled about during my last read of New Sun:

- what is the appropriate technological analogue of the device (?) the witches were looking at on Severian's visit to their tower. I remember it being described a a table-like object with a glass surface, through which small hairless animals were visible on some sort of miniature landscape. A holographic remote-viewing device? Were they watching the eclectics? And just what kind of "magic" do the witches practice?

-what was the enigmatic large object teasingly referred to in Wolfe's translator's notes at the end of Citadel which caused the eclipse?

-are the corridors that Severian wandered through en route to the atrium of time just metaphors for the Corridors of Time or is there some type of literal connection?

-why did the green man not know of the New Sun if he came from the future most likely after the New Sun?

-Jonas & Miles: what's the connection? Is Miles the personality from whose biological spare parts Jonas was reconstructed after his ship crashed?

Lasrach
