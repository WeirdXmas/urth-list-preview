Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2008-12-12 David Stockhoff #

Argues that the Severian who finds his healed face in The Urth of the New Sun is not stunned by his identity but marvels at his new likeness to Apu Punchau, and that there is only one Severian consciousness. Questions which body Severian occupies and who placed him there, raising the possibility that he is in an Autarch's tomb built for an earlier Autarch. Responds to Witz's speculation about Severian being his own future self and an aquastor.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Severian has only one consciousness across bodies; Severian lies in an earlier Autarch's tomb

Problems:
---He can't yet know

The quote above, I believe, is accurate speculation by Severian. Good Intuition. He's not "stunned" in tUotNS, he just marvels at it. He encounters his healed face, 10 years older than when he saw Apu Punchau, and he now looks like Apu Punchau whereas he didn't see the likeness before. He's marveling at this 'new face' not shocked to understand his identity.

There is only One Severian Consciousness. Many bodies, but only one consciousness. Consciousness is primary in Being. There are not mulitiple Severians unless there are multiple consciousnesses.

OK, but WHICH body is it? Who placed him there? Did the Sleeper live on as a god on Ushas and then go back to his predecessor's time to a tomb built for him by that Autarch? If so, then he would remember, because he contains that Autarch. And anyway, that would mean he eventually died.

Without the Autarch's knowledge, you can't go building a tomb with Autarchial emblems on it any more than you can build a castle and call yourself a Duke.

Perhaps it was intended to be the old Autarch's tomb. He may lie in it after all. We don't know how many Autarchs went to Yesod and failed since Ymar.
