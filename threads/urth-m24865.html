Is Anybody In Contact With Gene Wolfe?

Urth list · 5 messages · 2013-10-15 to 2013-10-16

2013urthFred KiescheRebecca Bushong-TaylorMarc AraminiRobin B. Smith
The thread: Fred Kiesche asked whether anyone could pass along an invitation to Gene Wolfe for a war stories anthology. Robin B. Smith asked how best to send Wolfe a letter and tributes, proposing the Virginia Kidd Agency. Marc Aramini advised against sharing private contact details and pointed to public routes instead: Tor Books or Wolfe's public Facebook page. The thread is a short practical exchange with no clear disagreement, ending with the public channels suggested as the appropriate way to reach him.
  • Fred Kiesche: asks for a contact to pass on an anthology invitation, thinks Wolfe would suit a war stories anthology
  • Marc Aramini: advises against sharing private contact info and recommends Tor Books or the public Facebook page
  • Robin B. Smith: wants to send a letter and tributes, suggests mailing through the Virginia Kidd Agency

Themes: War and soldiers

2013-10-16 Marc Aramini replying to Robin B. Smith #

Advises against sharing Gene Wolfe's private email or address and points to two public channels for reaching him: Tor Books or his public Facebook page. Responds to Robin B. Smith's question about how to send Wolfe tributes and a letter, offering practical contact routes rather than the Virginia Kidd Agency route Smith suggested.
answers

I would not give out his email or address, but there are two public ways you should be able to contact him- either through tor books or his public facebook page, which shouldn't be too difficult to find.
