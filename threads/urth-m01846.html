Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2006-08-14 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to Jonathon McKitrick #

Argues that Wolfe surely knew the real backstory behind the mysterious events in the Book of the New Sun, including Severian's parents and grandparents, but suspects readers often invent puzzles Wolfe never meant as mysteries. Responds to Jonathon McKitrick's question about whether Wolfe planned Severian's lineage details and whether he will take the final explanation to his grave.
disputes · author-and-biography · puzzle-solving

I'm reasonably sure that Wolfe did not create this complex, beautiful work without a clear idea of what it all meant (I don't mean symbolically, at least not necessarily; I mean what was going on behind the scenes to cause the various mysterious goings-on). I'm fairly certain he knows who Severian's parents and grandparents were.

But ... I suspect that we readers sometimes spend a lot of ingenuity coming up with solutions to mysteries Wolfe never intended to be mysteries at all.
