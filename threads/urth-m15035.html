2010-03-19 Son of Witz replying to David Stockhoff #

Expresses love for the Latro books and says the reader enjoys feeling lost in the plot because Latro is lost too. Wonders how Soldier of Sidon will end, weighing whether Latro regaining his memory would undercut the point, while still hoping he finds peace.
chats

happy to help. it's a hot day.

I really love these Latro books. I don't mind feeling lost in the plot. Latro is lost too.

I haven't heard anything about more of them, though I don't suppose I would. It must take a lot of research to write these. I don't know how Sidon ends. One wants Latro to get his memory, but that might thwart the very point. Still, one wants him to find peace.
