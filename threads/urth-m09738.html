Proposes putting hidden Wolfe and engineering references on a t-shirt for an IEEE student chapter, such as a transistor schematic with traffic lanes from "How I Lost The Second World War" and a radio direction-finding fix over the ocean inspired by Nevil Shute. Asks readers for other electrical engineering touches that would work as Wolfe or engineer-writer references.
proposes

I'm producing a t-shirt design for the local IEEE student chapter, and was thinking about slipping in some sort of reference to "How I Lost The Second World War..." , perhaps showing a schematic symbol for a transistor that has tiny lanes of traffic instead of solid lines.

Would there be anything else apropos to electrical engineering that would serve as a Wolfe reference, or perhaps to another engineer-writer? Maybe a radio direction finder triangulation over the ocean for Schute? ("Many thanks for position fix. Seas rough at Guilford.")
