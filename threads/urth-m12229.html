The thread: Nicholas Gevers asked which embryos besides Silk appear in the Long Sun books, noting that theories about Silk's genetic link to Typhon depend on what the embryos are, and named Mucor and Incus as known cases. James Wynn proposed that Auk is a clone of Tartaros, a strong suspicion grounded in Gene Wolfe's Q&A answer about why Tartaros sends Auk to kidnap and then release Hyacinth. The thread ends with Wynn's distinction standing; no other participant's reply appears in the cards.

2010-12-21 James Wynn replying to James Wynn #

Proposes that Auk is a clone of Tartaros, a view the poster presents as a strong suspicion. Grounds this in Gene Wolfe's Q&A answer on why Tartaros tells Auk to kidnap Hyacinth and release her, and replies to Nicholas Gevers's guess.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Auk is a clone of Tartaros

Oh, yeah. Auk. I strongly suspect he is a clone of Tartaros based on what he said in the Q&A:

Question: "Why did Auk/Tartaros kidnap Hyacinth, and why, subsequently, did he let her go?"

Gene Wolfe: "Because Tartaros had told him to bring a woman.He released her as he recovered from his brain injury, was able to think more clearly, and realized that Hyacinth was not the woman he should bring. Putting it another way, he released her because Tartaros told him to. *These answers are not as disparate as they may appear.*"
