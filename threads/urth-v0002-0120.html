1997-06-28 Alice Turner #

Announces the upcoming trade paperback reissue of The Island of Dr. Death and Other Stories, noting its August publication date and price, and says the poster plans to buy it. Asks other list members whether they will read it and suggests discussing the collection, admitting she barely remembers the stories from the first reading.
questions

I see The Island of Dr. Death & Other Stories & Other Stories will be reissued this summer in trade paperback ($11.95 at amazon). I certainly plan to get and read it. (It never "took" at all the first time around, and I can hardly remember it--in fact I don't think I read all the stories.) What about some of the rest of you? It might be fun for us to discuss. Pub date is supposed to be Aug. 1.

-alga-
