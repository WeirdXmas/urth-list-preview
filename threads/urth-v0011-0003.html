Multiple Severian Disorder

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-12 to 1998-05-12

1998urthThe Book of the New SunDan'l Danehy-Oakes

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Time loops and causality

1998-05-12 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Proposes labeling the resurrected Severians as Sev2.1, Sev2.2 and so on, distinct from an original Sev1 who lived in an erased timeline without Hierodule interference. Mockingly endorses a Severian-as-Archie-Andrews theory, equating Valeria with Sabrina, Triskele with Hot Dog, Dr. Talos with Mr. Wetherbee and the Old Autarch with Pop.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Severian resurrections are Sev2.1, Sev2.2 series; First Severian lived in erased timeline; Severian is Archie Andrews

While we can count the various resurrected Severians (Severiim? Severiani?) as different, they're all part of the same "release" -- I'd call them Sev2.1, Sev2.2, etc., as opposed to Sev1, the un-White-Fountained version. And (I repeat) the most reasonable explanation of _that_ "first Severian" is that he lived in an "erased" timeline, one where the Hierodules et alia did not interfere in his life.

On the other hand, I am in complete awe of the Severian-as-Archie Andrews theory. It explains so much. Valeria, obviously, is Sabrina, the Teen-Aged Witch. Triskele is Hot Dog. Dr. Talos is, obviously, Mr. Wetherbee, while the Old Autarch is Pop of Pop's Soda Shoppe.

I clearly have wasted too much time on the religious aspects of this series; it all seems so clear now...

--Roach
