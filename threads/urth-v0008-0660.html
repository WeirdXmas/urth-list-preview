Argues that the autarch's alzabo-like pharmacon could be far more powerful, letting autarchs hold many personalities the way Severian's perfect memory lets him experience Thecla. Grounds this in the autarch's own statement in The Citadel of the Autarch that his pharmacon is similar to the alzabo but not the same.
