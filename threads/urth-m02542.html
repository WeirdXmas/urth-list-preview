2006-05-20 b sharp #

Speculates about whether Gene Wolfe is secretly posting on the list under a disguised name, proposing how to identify him by his likely style and by the name he might choose. Grounds the guesses in the idea that a technophobic author would arrive late, write factual posts, and pick a name hinting at servanthood or monarchy, ending with a joking signature.
proposes · theories: Gene Wolfe is posting anonymously on the Urth list

Once there was a rumor that Gene Wolfe was disguised in this community. If that were true, how could we identify him?

If he is actually a technophobe then we wouldn't expect he has been here from the begininning. But once he arrived we'd expect him to be intelligent and insightful, yet circumspect. To make regular entries focusing on factual evidence more than grand fanciful theories. His writing would reflect the culture of an older American.

What sort of name would he choose? Since this archive is composed of his loyal fans, he might pick a name which reflects his feeling that he is our humble servant, the artist serving his patrons. On the other hand his name might also reflect that he and his work are the focus, the center of everything here; that we honor and revere him. His name might also reflect that this is a place where an anonymous sovereign might view his subjects, the monarch can see his vassals, lord see peons,
King C. Servant
