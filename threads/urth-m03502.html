2007-12-11 Fred Kiesche #

Asks who painted the cover of Pirate Freedom, suspecting the artist was inspired by Howard Pyle because the cover resembles Pyle's illustration style in a second-hand coffee-table book on him. The question is prompted by the poster's comparison of thumbnail images of the cover with the 1975 Howard Pyle volume.
questions · source-and-allusion

Greetings:

Who did the cover for "Pirate Freedom" (still haven't made it to the bookstore, drat it)?

The reason I ask is because I just picked up a second-hand copy of "Howard Pyle", a "coffee table" book published in 1975. The style looks a lot like the thumbnails of PF I've seen, so I'm wondering if that cover artist used Pyle as a inspiration.

2007-12-12 David Duffy replying to Recursive Loop #

Answers Recursive Loop's question about who painted the cover of Pirate Freedom by naming David Grove, who also illustrated Soldier of Sidon, and noting the cover does not resemble Howard Pyle. Describes the interior illustrations as less finished than classic boys' book art and says some sail plans in Chapter 20 are idealized.
answers · close-textual-reading

David Grove, the same guy who did _SoS_. The cover does not look much like any Howard Pyle I've ever seen. The internal illustrations will remind you, unsurprisingly, of the style he used for _SoS_. They are less "finished" than those in classic illustrated books for boys, but cover all the standard scenes from the life of a pirate ;)

I was going to write that David Grove is an OK nautical artist, but a closer look makes me think that some of the sail plans are a little idealized viz Chapter 20.

Cheers, David Duffy.
