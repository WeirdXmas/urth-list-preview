Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

1997-04-09 Dave Lebling #

Argues that Maytera Rose identifies with Echidna and may be possessed by her, seeing the kneeling figure before a Scylla image as Rose-as-Echidna, and that Urus is a Triv spy rather than an escaped prisoner. Responds to David Lebling's questions about Rose's daughter and the Urus meeting by identifying the Trivigaunti officer as Major Hadale and linking the meeting to the Grand Manteion assassination attempt.
answers · puzzle-solving · myth-and-structure · theories: Rose identifies with Echidna and may be possessed; Urus is a Triv spy rather than escaped prisoner

This is a wonderful find. We come to realize later that Rose identifies herself with Echidna, and possibly has been possessed by Echidna. I suspect we are seeing Rose-as-Echidna kneeling before an image of Scylla, Echidna's daughter. Rose doesn't seem to have a secure hold on her identity or her life. Does anyone else agree that she's in effect an animated corpse, kept alive only by her prosthetics?

We have suspected for a long time that Urus is a spy rather than simply another escaped prisoner in the tunnels. The Triv he meets is Major Hadale, General Saba's Intelligence staff officer. Urus is signing up to spy for the Trivs. The outcome we see soon after is the assassination attempt in the Grand Manteion.

-vizcacha
