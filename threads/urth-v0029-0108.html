Remarks that the novel Little, Big pairs a hopeful tone with a weary one, and that the 'familiar compound ghost' passage is the part the poster likes best, joking that Fred Savage plays it. Responds to a quoted note about borrowing from Little, Big and Eliot's Little Gidding in a graduation speech, reacting by comparing the book's two moods and referring to the ghost phrase.
