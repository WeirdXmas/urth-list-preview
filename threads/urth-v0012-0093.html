Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Stories within stories

1998-06-05 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes a chronological reconstruction of Marsch's journal entries in The Fifth Head of Cerberus, laying out his dated field entries, interviews, and return to Roncevaux against page numbers. It is a reference-style sorting of the 'first revision' pages, offered without argument, so readers can see how the events fit together.
