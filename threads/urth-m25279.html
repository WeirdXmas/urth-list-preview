2014-08-20 Charles Gillingham #

Proposes that the story The Boy Who Hooked the Sun belongs to the Brown Book of the New Sun, and suggests a later story about Empires of Flower and Foliage shows how Severian won the Ascian war in one New Sun while Urth Severian lost. Responds to a write-up of the story, offering a framing for future story posts rather than detailed evidence.
proposes · theories: The Boy Who Hooked the Sun is part of the Brown Book; Severian won the Ascian war in a New Sun

Great write up. This story, I always assumed, is part of the Brown Book from New Sun. I only bring this up, so future stories commented on will be identified as such. Very much looking forward to a write up about Empires of Flower and Foliage. I think that story is about how Severian won the Ascian war. Which New Sun Severian won, while Urth Severian lost.

2014-08-20 Marc Aramini replying to Charles Gillingham #

Asks whether a scene in The Book of the New Sun, where Inire says the mirrors have almost hooked a reflected fish into existence, should be brought into discussion of Wolfe's solar stories. Argues that Severian's link to the sun is largely irrelevant to 'The Boy Who Hooked the Sun' but may connect to a higher power such as God, responding to Charles Gillingham's remarks on the story's place in the Brown Book.
questions · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: Severian's association with the sun is irrelevant to the Boy story

Yeah there is a scene in new sun when thecla's childhood friend (domnina?) is shown the mirrors by Inire and Inire says they have almost hooked the fish reflected into existence. I thought about mentioning that scene.
Sev's association with the sun complicates a lot of these solar stories. To bring it in or not?

At the point of Capricorn and the boy who hooked the sun I feel it is kind of irrelevant to associate the sun with sev. But perhaps not to associate it with something like God.
