2013-09-27 Andrew Mason #

Argues that Oreb was possessed by Scylla after the Rajan's sacrifice in the Grand Manteion, citing the timing before 'The Night Chough', and that Mucor may have possessed him briefly when seen by Silk. Grounds this in the sequence of events and an earlier proposal by Roy Lackey, and holds that Oreb's resurrection is not puzzling.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Oreb possessed by Scylla after the Rajan's sacrifice; Mucor possessed Oreb when seen by Silk

I think we are actually told at some point that Oreb's possession by Scylla began during the Rajan's sacrifice in the Grand Manteion in RTTW. Up to this point Scylla had been hiding in corners of Mainframe, but Oreb's presence before a Window during a sacrifice allowed her to escape. (This comes before 'The Night Chough', which follows on the Rajan's return, with Oreb, to Blue, so that fits all right.)

Oreb may well also be possessed during his mysterious appearance to Silk, but temporarily, by Mucor. (I think Roy Lackey proposed this once.)

Most of the time I think Oreb is just Oreb; his abilities are regular night chough abilities. Severian says at one point that some animals have learned to speak a few words; catachrests are another example.

As for Oreb's resurrection, I have always been a bit puzzled that people find it so puzzling. Why should it be thought a thing incredible with you, that the Outsider should raise the dead?
