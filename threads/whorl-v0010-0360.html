2000-06-16 Alex David Groce #

Asks Alga which character they believe secretly drives the action of The Book of the Long Sun, offering Pike and Quetzal as the two candidates the poster can think of. Responds to Alga's earlier archived suggestion that one character drives Long Sun's action, and asks for the reasoning; closes with a John 8:32 quotation.
questions · puzzle-solving

Alga:

Sometime back (archives taking forever to come up for me) you suggested that the "action" of Long Sun can be attributed largely to one non-divine character. I'm rereading LS again, and I now want to know who your candidate for mastermind is. I can think of two possibilities, one unlikely, the other also hard to defend as the choice: Pike (!?) and Quetzal.

Care to reveal your hypothesis?

"And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free." John 8:32
