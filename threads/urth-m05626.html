2008-07-01 b sharp #

Argues that Father Inire's transport through the Houses may sometimes serve no planned purpose in The Book of the New Sun, citing Severian and Jonas finding the mirror room, Severian's account of years of trysts in the Secret House, and the presence of the Assassin. Responds to Jeff Wilson's claim that Inire manipulates Severian's movements through transcendent doorways, granting the point while pushing back on the idea that everything is planned.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Not all events in Severian's life are planned by Inire

Jeff, you could be right. Perhaps the principle goes so far that there is nothing accidental in the whole of the story of Severian's life. But it seems to me there are unplanned events happening. For me it seems Wolfe specifically tells us that Father Inire's transport may often be used without New Sun purpose, examples being: Severian and Jonas finding and using the mirror room, Severian talking about the many trysts etc. through the years in the Secret House and the presence of The Assassin.

2008-07-02 Jeff Wilson replying to b sharp #

Argues that Severian's and Jonas's use of the mirror room and the many trysts are not accidental but fit the New Sun purpose, since Father Inire's portals could be parked out of the way. Suggests the Assassin could be anyone sent earlier who resumed a mission after resurrection, so nothing special is needed for him.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Father Inire's portals are parked to await a pawn; The Assassin could be anyone sent earlier and resurrected

How are "Sev and Jonas" not a New Sun purpose?

The trysts can use "normal" secret passageways and such; that there are so many makes it easy for Inire to park his portals out of the way where they remain unmolested until a pawn directs Sev toward one of them, or it might even be some "real fake" chicanery like a moving room.

I don't see anything special about The Assassin. They could have been anyone sent at any earlier time who upon gathering most of their wits post-resurrection continues with their mission and attacks the person on the throne from behind.
