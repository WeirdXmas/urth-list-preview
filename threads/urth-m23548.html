2012-11-28 Ryan Dunn replying to Mark Lewin #

Proposes a Gene Wolfe documentary in the style of Errol Morris that would be convincing yet ultimately a hoax and puzzle structured like a Wolfe short story, rather than a conventional biography. Responds to Mark Lewin's question about a Wolfe biography or autobiography, offering a playful alternative with a title and a suggested producer.
proposes

Honestly, I'd rather see a Gene Wolfe documentary in the style of Errol Morris.

The documentary would need to be convincing, and full of useful memoir-style anecdotes, but ultimately a hoax, a lie, and a puzzle, structured like a Wolfe short story.

I would call it:

"DO NOT TRUST THIS FILM AS FAR AS YOU CAN THROW IT (TRUST IT WITH YOUR LIFE)"

Or, alternately:

"UNTRUSTWORTHY"

Neil Gaiman would produce it and appear in it, of course. Maybe help write it along with Wolfe?

...ryan

Asks whether Gene Wolfe has ever hinted at writing an autobiography or whether any scholar has considered writing a biography of him, after reading Jack Vance's autobiography. The post is a plain question with no textual argument, aimed at the list in general rather than at a specific reply.
questions

Hi all,

I figured this was the place to ask this question!

Having just read /This is Me, Jack Vance/, I was thinking how much I'd enjoy reading a Wolfe biography, or better still, an autobiography. Does anyone know if Wolfe has ever hinted at such a work? Or have any of you die-hard Wolfe scholars contemplated writing a biography?
