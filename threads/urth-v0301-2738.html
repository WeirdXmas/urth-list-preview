Proposes that malicapata, a word from Wolfe's text, may derive from 'capon,' a castrated male chicken, with a Latin root meaning 'to cut', so meaning 'badly cut'. Offers this as a tentative guess, hedged with 'I think', without citing a dictionary or other posters.
