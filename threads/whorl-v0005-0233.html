Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1997-04-19 Alice Turner #

Notes that a league is roughly three miles, complimenting a colleague's whorl-analysis, then proposes that the house where Silk is held prisoner is Oosik's house, the room where he lies is Mattak's room, and the woman's scarf he finds belongs to Hyacinth. Offers these as guesses from the house and its furnishings, admitting less certainty about the blue tunic.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk is held in Oosik's house; Hyacinth's scarf is found in Silk's prison

A league is an inexact measure of about three miles. That's very impressive whorl-analysis, vizcacha.

BTW, I'm pretty sure that the house where Silk is held prisoner is Oosik's house, that the room in which he lies is Mattak's room, and I suspect that the woman's scarf he finds will prove to be Hyacinth's scarf. I'm less sure about the blue tunic.

-alga-
