Wooden ships

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-07-03 to 1998-07-03

1998-07-03 Peter Cash #

Disputes the claim that the wooden plane in Free Live Free matches the ship Tzadkiel, arguing instead that it derives from Howard Hughes's wartime plywood aircraft project. Grounds this in the Spruce Goose prototype and Hughes's idea of large plywood planes for the US Air Force, which the poster suggests Wolfe may have endorsed.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Wooden plane in Free Live Free derives from Howard Hughes

Um. The Sergeant is not impressed. You see, he knows that the plane doesn't belong to Tzad--it belongs to Howard Hughes.

During WWII, Hughes wanted to build large multi-engine plywood airplanes for the US Air Force. He even built a prototype called the "Spruce Goose"; as I recall, it made one brief flight. I'm pretty sure that was the genesis of Wolfe's idea. As far as anyone knows, Hughes' idea never got official sanction...but perhaps Wolfe thinks Hughes was right.

Sgt. Rock
