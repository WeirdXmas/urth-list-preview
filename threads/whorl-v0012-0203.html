2001-02-13 Peeter Piegaze #

Proposes that the commotion Horn and his companions cause at the citadel gate on return to Urth in In Green's Jungles matches the disturbance at the gate Severian notes at the start of The Shadow of the Torturer. Asks whether Wolfe planned this link twenty years earlier or left loose ends that he later tied together.
proposes · close-textual-reading · author-and-biography · theories: Horn's arrival at the citadel gate matches Severian's disturbance

Perhaps it has already been discussed (and if so, could someone please point me to relevant part of the archive) but did anyone else notice that when Horn and company go back to the Red Sun Whorl (Urth) in IGJ the commotion they cause at the gate to the citadel is, I think, the same commotion that Severian notices at the beginning of Shadow of the Torturer where he comments briefly about "a disturbance at the gate". I don't have either book with me right now, but perhaps someone can find he exact passages and see if this makes sense.

The next question is: Did Wolfe really plan this whole thing twenty years ago and only now tie it together or did he just leave a few floating loose ends around without knowing exactly what they would eventually be used for?

Peeter
