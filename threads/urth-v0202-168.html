2002-02-17 Alice Turner #

Relays a complaint that the mailing list digest now arrives as separate attachments rather than one simple email, and notes a workaround for Outlook Express users. Responds to Ranjit's wish for the older digest format, sharing that Roy C. Lackey taught a reading method, while admitting non-Outlook users may struggle.
informs

Said Ranjit:

What *I* would like, and others too, is for the digest to work the way it used to, not as separate attachments but as one simple email. Roy C. Lackey taught me how to make reading it much easier, however, and I will pass this arcane skill along (as I'm sure he will) to anyone who uses Outlook Express. Still, it's a nuisance. And of course I haven't a clue as to how non-OE users can cope.

-alga
