Apologizes for dominating the list lately, explaining that after twenty years of rereading the Urth books alone they are now eager to discuss them. Offers the apology as a light, self-aware aside about the community space rather than a substantive reading claim.

2006-05-23 Daniel D Jones replying to b sharp #

Tells b sharp that there is no need to apologize for posting many Wolfe messages on a Wolfe mailing list, since readers can skip or filter posts. Encourages the apologizer to keep posting, says he has found the posts interesting, and explains the old meaning of 'twit' as a filter term.
chats

I'm rather at a loss as to why you'd apologize for discussing Wolfe's works on a mailing list devoted to discussing Wolfe's works. It's email. If someone is offended or bored with your posts, they can easily skip over the posts. If they're really tired of reading your posts, they can twit you. Personally, I've found your posts quite interesting. Post on.

(I'm not sure if the word "twit" is still commonly used in this manner. In the old BBS days and the early days of the Internet, all of a decade and a half or so ago, a "twit filter" was a filter which automatically deleted posts from a specific person. To "twit" someone was to add them to your twit filter.)
