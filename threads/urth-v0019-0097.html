Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Time loops and causality

1998-10-13 Robert Borski #

Argues that Father Inire and the Cumaean are the children of Severian and Apheta, completing a loop of incestuous genealogical inversions in The Book of the New Sun. Grounds this in the Latin meaning of Inire's name and the sense of Apheta's name as "starting place", and lists other incestuous pairings as parallels.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Inire and the Cumaean are Severian and Apheta's children

This is the last of the major genealogical timeloop inversions of New Sun.

Father Inire's name derives from Latin and means "to begin, to enter." Like many of the other incestuously involved couples of Urth (Agia, Agilus; Sev, Dorcas;Sev, Valeria), he too is sleeping with a blood relative--his sister, the Cumaean.

And who are their parents? (Tony Ellis came very close to figuring this out).

They're Severian and Apheta (whose name means "starting place").

Now somebody dare tell me Gene Wolfe does not have one wicked sense of humor!

Robert Borski
