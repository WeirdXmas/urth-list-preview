Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1997-07-03 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that implanting god-prepared embryos, the source of Silk, Mucor, Tick and the Lynxes, would be simpler than extracting usable DNA from non-sex cells, so a biotech group could plausibly exist. Responds to Kieran Mullen's point about a biotech equivalent to the black mechanics, and compares it with the likelihood of inhumi succubi and incubi.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Silk's genesis via god-prepared embryos is simpler than DNA extraction

Kieran Mullen,

Re: your tech solution. Interesting point! It would seem to require a group that is to biotech as the black mechanics are to robotics. The implanting of god prepared embryos (which is the genesis for Silk, Mucor, Tick?, and the Lynxes) must be simple compared to striping useable DNA from non-sex cells. Doctor Crane probably couldn't do it. The brain surgeon from Palustria probably couldn't do it.

But it is possible, and probably more likely than inhumi succubi/incubi.

=mantis=
