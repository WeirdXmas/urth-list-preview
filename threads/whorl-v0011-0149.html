Announces a Gene Wolfe symposium at the University of Birmingham on Saturday 26 August 2000, naming the speakers Peter Wright, Jim Russell, Nigel Price and Nick Gevers. Reminds list members that the event is open to all and repeats the date after an earlier message omitted it.
