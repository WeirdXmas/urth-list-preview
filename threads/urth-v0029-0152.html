2000-08-11 Alex David Groce #

Asks whether readers should start Aleister Crowley's DAEMONOMANIA now, or wait for the series to finish, depending on whether its volumes are self-contained like Wolfe's Sun books or one continuous novel. Frames the question by analogy to Wolfe's New, Long and Short Sun series and admits the comparison may be off-topic.
questions

Speaking of Crowley and DAEMONOMANIA, would it be a good diea to start reading these now? If they are like the volumes of the NEW SUN books, and suffer in isolation, I'd prefer to wait until he (finally) finishes the work--on the other hand, if the books are more like the individual parts of the SUN series (NEW, LONG and SHORT) and thematically related but not really "one big novel" I might as well jump in.

Hope the Wolfe analogy keeps this at least remotely on-topic.
