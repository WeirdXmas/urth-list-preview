BSG Spoiler vs Wolfe

Urth list · 5 messages · 2009-03-23 to 2009-03-27

2009urthThe Book of the New SunJohn WatkinsJames WynnSon of WitzJeff WilsonJohn Smith
The thread: A short thread began with Son of Witz clarifying an earlier comparison of Battlestar Galactica and The Book of the New Sun, and arguing that the TV show lacks Wolfe's subtlety, which he says is not to be expected from television. Jeff Wilson disputed this, offering the TV series Lexx as a counterexample whose farcical surface still carries themes of sex, religion and political power close to Wolfe's. James Wynn then corrected an attribution, noting that a passage about chivalric service and romantic love as an earthly sacrament belongs to Enamel rather than to the earlier poster. The thread ended without a resolution; the debate over whether TV can match Wolfe's subtlety was left open.
  • Son of Witz: BSG's themes and plot can be compared to The Book of the New Sun, but the show lacks Wolfe's subtlety
  • Jeff Wilson: Lexx carries Wolfe-like themes of sex, religion and political power despite its farce, so TV can handle such material
  • James Wynn: the chivalry-as-sacrament passage is from Enamel, not the earlier poster

Themes: Power, rulers and politics, Catholic faith and sacraments
