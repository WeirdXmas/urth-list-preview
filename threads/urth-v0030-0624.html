2001-08-09 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Apologizes to William for any impression that Dan'l thought William believed mice and dinosaurs were contemporaneous, and disparages Ray Bradbury's grasp of scientific accuracy. Responds to William's complaint that Dan'l implied such a belief, with no Wolfe textual argument.
chats

William, If I implied, if what I wrote gave the impression, that I thought you believed mice and dinosaurs to be contemporaneous or was predicating my posting on such a belief on your part, then I apologize. I did not intend to imply such a thing or give such an impression.

(What Mr. Bradbury thinks, on the other hand, is of very little concern. Fabulous writer though he is, he has occasionally shown a rather contemptuous attitude toward matters of scientific accuracy that leads me to believe that he wouldn't have a clue what kinds of mammals did or didn't show up before the dinosaurs died out, unless maybe he got it from Disney's film DINOSAUR.)

--Dan'l
