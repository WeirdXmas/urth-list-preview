More Concerning the BOTNS Manuscript

1998urthThe Book of the New SunC R Culver

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-01-22 C R Culver #

Proposes that the lead coffer thrown into the sea of space and time drifted from the Ship's meta-universe into our universe, which he names Malkuth, so Briah is not the setting. Builds on the first appendix of The Urth of the New Sun, which shows Wolfe saw more than the manuscript, and asks how to resolve that with the coffer-translation story; he jokingly suggests a visit from Tzadkiel.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Severian's lead coffer drifted from the Ship's meta-universe into our universe; Our universe is Malkuth and Gene Wolfe found the coffer there

Friends,

As usual, I discovered an answer to my question 10 minutes after mailing it to the list:
When Severian sets the manuscript upon "the seas of space and time," I assumed he was in Briah. This does not appear to be the case. Severian was aboard the Ship. In its journeys to Yesod, the ship went outside any universe (into a kind of Meta-universe, which contained the others). Thus, he throws the lead coffer into this meta-universe. I suppose it simply floated along until it drifted into Our Universe (which I will in the future deem "Malkuth," after Mantis' appreciated help). Gene Wolfe, a guy in Malkuth, usual in all respects except he had just written a kick-butt novel called _Peace_, one day came upon the coffer. Thus, the story begins.

Said Mantis:
mentions that Gene Wolfe has examined artifacts from Urth, and in fact has been allowed to visit and photograph the era's few extant buildings.<

Yes, this presents an interesting problem. In the last few pages of _Citadel_ and the opening parts of _Urth_, the main attention is on the lead coffer. The reader thinks the coffer is all that Mr. Wolfe got, and that he then translated the BOTNS and its coda using techniques much like those employed to decipher Linear B relics. Then, however, we go back to the first appendix, and learn Mr. Wolfe has indeed seen more than the manuscript. How shall we solve this problem? Is Briah Malkuth? Or did Mr. Wolfe get a visit from a guy(?) named Tzadkiel who had this really cool boat and wanted to take him for a ride? <g>

Christopher "My Best Friend Took My Copy of _Shadow and Claw_ to France, and I Want it Back!" Culver
