Compiles an alphabetical list of unlisted proper names in On Blue's Waters, giving each name's description and page.paragraph location, such as Calf, Dace, Magnesia and Passilk. It offers the list as a reference aid and invites corrections and additions from other readers.
informs

Here is some information that I hope will be useful. Please feel free to post corrections and additions.

UNLISTED PROPER NAMES IN THE TEXT OF ON BLUE'S WATERS

NAME / DESCRIPTION / PAGE.PARAGRAPH

Calf / Horn's brother / 74.-1

Cognizance, His / Honorific title of the Prolocutor Quetzal / 42.2

Dace, Captain / Boat Captain on Lake Limna / 163.3

Daisy / Resident of the village of Lizard. Future co-publisher of the book, with Vadsig, Hoof and Hide / 26.-5, 129.-3

Horn / Name of the Neighbor that talks with Horn, the man / 272.4, 274.3

Hound / Boy at the Sun street Mantion / 379.-2

Incus, Patera / Assistant to the Prolocutor in Old Viron / 97.3

Juganu / Inhumi exhumated by the Rajan of Gaon to fight in the war with Han / 376.3

Magnesia / Name assumed by Matera Rose-Marble (see Molybdenum) / 37.6

Maker, The / Wijzer's name for The Outsider / 115.5

Man, The / The tyrant ruler of the city Han / 370.-1

Marl / Slave in Viron / 100.2

Molpe / Minor goddess on The Whorl / 139.-1, 228.2

Molybdenum / aka Molly. Name assumed by Matera Rose-Marble (see Magnesia) / 37.6

Mura / Town on the Main contenent of Blue / 115.1

Oosik / Generalissimo / 303.-1

Passilk / The entity of Pas merged with Silk / 369.-6

Prolocutor / Title of the leader of the Vironese faith / 29.-3

Shrike / Aquaintance of Marrow / 39.-1

Smoothbone / Horn's father. Ink maker / 79.2

Sphigx / Minor goddess on The Whorl / 289.-3

Tail Bay / Natural bay on the SE end of Lizard Island / 20.4

Teasel / Young girl bitten by the Inhumu Quetzal / 261.2

Tuz / Krait's victim / 373.2

Vadsig / Future co-publisher of the book, with Daisy, Hoof and Hide / 129.-3
