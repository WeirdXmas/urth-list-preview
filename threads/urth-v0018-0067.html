Lake of the Long Sun...?

Urth list · 3 messages · 1998-08-30 to 1998-08-31

1998urthThe Book of the Long SunTony A. WestC R CulverAlex David Groce
The thread: Readers asked why Lake of the Long Sun and other Long Sun volumes went out of print so quickly, and where copies could still be found. CRCulver raised the scarcity of Nightside the Long Sun at Tor and offered money for a copy. Tony A. West asked the same question about the book's status, whether Tor planned a reissue, and how to buy it from rural Japan. Alex David Groce answered by shifting to publishers' habit of reissuing Wolfe later in Orb editions and wished Endangered Species would be reprinted. No one resolved the availability question in the thread, which drifted toward reissue trends.
  • CRCulver: Early out-of-print status is a problem, and Nightside the Long Sun is hard to get
  • Tony A. West: Wants to know why the book went out of print and how to buy it abroad
  • Alex David Groce: Publishers reissue Wolfe later in Orb editions; wants Endangered Species reprinted

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

1998-08-30 C R Culver #

Asks why Lake of the Long Sun has gone out of print so soon after release, and notes that Nightside the Long Sun is out of stock at Tor, making it hard to obtain. Also laments that Peace, Free Live Free and Soldier of the Mist went out of print too quickly, and offers money for a copy of Nightside found in local bookstores.
questions

<Hey everyone/anyone... Does anyone know why, after being released for only like three years, Lake of the Long sun is now out of print??>

I know Tor Books is out-of-stock of _Nightside the Long Sun_, which means it's nearly impossible for me to get it. Incidentally, if anyone can look in their local bookstores for Nightside, I'll send 'em money for it and some extra. Gene Wolfe's books go out of print far too fast: Peace, Free Live Free, and Soldier of the Mist are some examples.

And continued:
<Thanks so much, I hope someone still reads this list...>

There's quite a crowd here, so many people are reading your message(s).

1998-08-31 Alex David Groce #

Remarks that publishers tend to reissue Wolfe's books later in Orb editions and wishes Endangered Species, a collection with favourite stories, would be reprinted. Responds to Tony A. West's question about why Lake of the Long Sun went out of print, shifting to the broader question of reissue trends rather than the book's status.
chats

At least the trend seems to be to eventually put them into Orb. I wish they'd do ENDANGERED SPECIES (which has some of my favorite stories...)
