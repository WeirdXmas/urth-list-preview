A Cabin on the Coast

The thread: Michael Straight asked whether Pirate Freedom expands Wolfe's short story "A Cabin on the Coast" and offered no evidence. Matthew Groves agreed the novel expands the story and credited Robert Graves's The White Goddess as the shared source rather than direct borrowing. Matthew Keeley challenged the Frazer and Graves connection, finding White Goddess influence plausible for The Book of the New Sun but not for Pirate Freedom. Roy C. Lackey asked Marc Aramini to repost Wolfe's supposed statement on Graves and questioned the White Goddess claim. The thread ended unresolved, with the Graves influence left disputed and no decisive evidence offered.

2008-01-16 Matthew Groves replying to Michael Straight #

Argues that the short story "A Cabin on the Coast" and the novel Pirate Freedom resemble each other because Wolfe absorbed Robert Graves's The White Goddess. Agrees with Michael Straight's observation that Pirate Freedom expands the story, and attributes the shared material to that source rather than to direct borrowing.
supports · myth-and-structure · theories: Wolfe's resemblances derive from Graves's The White Goddess

Yes, absolutely. I think one source of their resemblance, and the similarity of other Wolfe stories, is due to Wolfe's absorption of Robert Graves's The White Goddess.

2008-01-16 Matthew Keeley replying to Matthew Groves #

Asks whether there is evidence that Wolfe read Frazer's The Golden Bough and asks Matthew Groves to explain the Frazer-Wolfe connection in Pirate Freedom, which the poster cannot see. Says he finds White Goddess influence plausible in The Book of the New Sun but not in Pirate Freedom, and that he is a layman on Graves.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: Pirate Freedom draws on Frazer's Golden Bough

I saw your "Interpreting Wolfe" list on Amazon; I see you put The White Goddess on that too. It's one of those things I've been meaning to read for a while, and learning it's influenced by Wolfe makes me want to read it more. In a similar vein, is there any evidence that Wolfe has read Frazer's Golden Bough (Or at least some of it - The whole work is 13 volumes or something ridiculous like that).

Not to sound silly, but do you think you could elaborate just a bit on the Frazer-Wolfe connection in Pirate Freedom? I don't know much about Graves, and there wasn't anything in PF that just screamed "White Goddess" to me, a layman. I suppose I could see some influence in New Sun, but PF, not so much. I don't doubt it's there, it's just I don't know enough to see it.

-Matt
