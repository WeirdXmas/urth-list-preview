Proposes that Prometheus is another classic "wound in side" reference and a prefiguration of Christ, since he sacrifices himself for mankind. Notes differences: Prometheus is a non-human Titan whose benefit to humanity is physical and ambiguous rather than spiritual; admits he has not read the story under discussion.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theories: Prometheus prefigures Christ as self-sacrificing benefactor

Prometheus is the other classic "wound in side"
reference; he can be seen as a prefiguration of
Christ, in that he sacrifices himself for the
benfit of mankind.

Prometheus is non-human (he is a Titan), and the
benfit is physical and ambigious, rather than
spiritual and unmixed, so there are differences.
Not having read the story at issue, I can't guess
which makes more sense.

-John
