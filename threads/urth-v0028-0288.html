PKD a modernist?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-10-27 to 1999-10-27

1999urthRon Hale-Evans

1999-10-27 Ron Hale-Evans #

Argues that Philip K. Dick is better described as a postmodernist than a modernist, and defends him as a great artist despite being a hack. Cites Stanislaw Lem's remark that Dick brought gold from the trash, then closes by wondering whether Lem would revise his praise of Dick if he read Wolfe or Egan.
disputes

alga, to label Dick as a modernist writer is barely comprehensible to me. To me he has always seemed a, if not the, quintessential postmodernist writer. As for his being a hack, remember what Stanislaw Lem said of him: that he brought gold from the trash. PKD may have been a hack, but _qua_ hack, he was a great artist.

And I find him also compulsively readable. But I guess you have to have been there...

ObWolfe: I wonder if Lem would today revise his opinion that PKD is the only great contemporary SF writer if he read Wolfe. Or Egan.

Ron H-E
