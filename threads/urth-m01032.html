2005-01-21 Jim Raylor #

Announces a new personal website where Jim Raylor explores his unorthodox speculations about The Fifth Head of Cerberus, moving them off the list to be discussed at leisure. Offers to discuss content related to the site and notes that some sections with a changed style are not his work.
informs

To avoid fouling up this list with my somewhat
unorthodox speculations regarding this book I have
taken the advice given me and created a webpage to
explore these ideas at my own pace. It is very much a
work in progress, but I would be happy to discuss any
issues related to the content. Except for the sections
where the style of the site changes, those bits are
not my work!

http://prisoner143.50megs.com/
