Alzabo Soup starts Shadow of the Torturer this Friday

Urth list · 1 messages · 2017-10-01 to 2017-10-01

2017urthThe Book of the New SunAlzabo Soup

2017-10-01 Alzabo Soup #

Announces that the Alzabo Soup podcast will begin a chapter-by-chapter discussion of The Shadow of the Torturer, with listening options and a plan to cover all of the New Sun books over a year or more. Gives the start date, the 1994 Shadow & Claw printing used, and the schedule of one to two chapters per week, with no argument or interpretation offered.
informs

This is just a quick reminder that this Friday, 10/6 at midnight EST, Alzabo Soup will begin our chapter-by-chapter discussion of The Shadow of the Torturer. If you're interested, here's what you need to know:

--You can subscribe through Itunes, Google Play, or Stitcher by searching for "Alzabo Soup" on the podcasts storefront, or visit
Http://alzabosoup.libsyn.com to listen directly.

--We plan to do 1-2 chapters per week (this first week will cover an introduction to our approach and Chapter 1).

--We're using the 1994 "Shadow & Claw/Sword & Citadel" printing.

--We do plan on going through all four books of BotNS and The Urth of the New Sun in this manner, potentially with short breaks between each book. The whole project will likely take a year or more to complete.

Thanks, and let us know if you listen!
