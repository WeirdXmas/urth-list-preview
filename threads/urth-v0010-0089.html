Gives a way to contact artist Don Maitz, pointing to his editor David Hartwell at Tor and to the NYRSF website maintained by Hartwell's wife, then links Maitz's own website. Responds to William H. Ansley's note that Maitz is the artist in question, offering practical contact information rather than critical discussion.

1998-05-06 Matthew Freestone #

Adds information to an earlier answer about cover art for the Book of the New Sun editions, listing a 1987 Arrow edition with covers by Peter Andrew Jones and an earlier edition with covers by Bruce Pennington. Responds to a question from Mike about which covers he wants, noting that the Don Maitz covers discussed are not the only ones available.
informs

I agree that's probably the set Mike wants, but I should point out that there are others: I have an Arrow edition from around 1987 with (unexciting) covers by one Peter Andrew Jones. There was also an earlier edition (not sure if this was also by Arrow) with rather nice covers by (I think) Bruce Pennington.
