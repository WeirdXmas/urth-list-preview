1998-05-01 C R Culver #

Questions Mantis's claim that Jonas and Hethor speak a foreign language, asking for a citation, and argues that the loss of technology after a supposed First Empire, not the sinking of one port, explains the crash landing. Responds to Mantis's remark that a sunken place explains why they could not find a port, and jokes about a passage on Cyriaca's tale.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Jonas's crash landing caused by general loss of technology

<Fact two. Jonas and Hethor talking some kinda foreign language(s).>

Can you provide citation for Hethor's speaking in a foreign tongue?

< That someplace important sank while they
were gone could also go towards explaining how they couldn't find a port to land (as Jonas says about the crashlanding).>

During the First Empire, if truly an empire it was, one could find a spaceport in any continent on Earth/Urth. I think that Jonas' crashlanding was caused, not by the loss of a single area and its spaceport, but rather the general loss of technology after the end of this Empire.

1998-05-02 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the Xanthic Lands are remnants of a fallen First Empire built by Asian spacefaring people, linking Jonas and Hethor's foreign speech and a sunken Asian-sounding place to Cyriaca's tale. Builds this by assembling three textual facts from the Commonwealth chapters and Jonas's crash account into one speculative chain.
proposes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Xanthic Lands are remnants of the First Empire; First Empire was created by Asians; Jonas and Hethor come from the First Empire era

FunkMonkey,

The terraforming and renaming story is told by Thea (II, ch. 10).

The presumed Xanthic Continent is a bit more dicey. The Xanthic Lands are a group of a couple hundred (or more) islands in the ocean to the west of the Commonwealth (V, ch. 46). The people there speak the Xanthic tongue and are xanthoderms (yellow skinned). The only named island we know is "Blue Flower Island" (V, ch. 46). The people are a bit more primitive than folks in the Commonwealth, such that they have little food security and periodic food riots. Call that fact one.

Fact two. Jonas and Hethor talking some kinda foreign language(s). Jonas talking about an Asian-sounding name being common in a place now sunk under the sea back when he was young. Seems like some Asian spacefaring group was topdog on Urth back when Jonas and Hethor first went up and away.

Fact three. Cyriaca's tale of the Library (III, ch. 6). Rise and long decline of the First Empire.

Speculation puts these three together: Jonas and Hethor come from the time of the First Empire (Cyriaca's tale), which then suggests that the First Empire was created by Asians (since Jonas and Hethor have Asian tongues, as it were), and the Xanthic Lands (populated by what to our eyes appear to be Asians) are the fallen remnants of that particular civilization. That someplace important sank while they were gone could also go towards explaining how they couldn't find a port to land (as Jonas says about the crashlanding).

(Must remember to intercorrelate Cyriaca's tale with Tale of the Boy Called Frog . . . )

=mantis=
