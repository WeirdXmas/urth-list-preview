1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunAlice Turner

1997-02-06 Alice Turner #

Disputes Mantis's claim that Scylla's lake is her screen, noting she enters and leaves Chenille through conventional screens, and argues Silk's near-death vision could be possible without contradiction. Questions Mantis's Kypris-as-Silk's-mother theory since Silk fails to recognize the blonde as Kypris, and argues Chenille is exactly what she seems, Tussah's daughter raised in the gutter who loves Auk.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Kypris is Silk's mother; Pike is not an aquastor; Scylla uses conventional screens to enter Chenille; Chenille is not an uberfrau

First, Dave, I'm so glad you concentrated on Mint---she was going to be next for me, but now I don't have to---and Pas. Especially glad to learn that no Echidna soils little Mint. Agreed that the Pas timeline is puzzling. Maybe mantis, who loves timelines, can help us out.

Mantis, one caveat: Scylla boasts about the lake being her screen, but in fact she uses conventional screens both to enter and to exit Chenille.

I'm of the non-aquastor crowd re Pike (right up there beating the drum) and I think it's perfectly possible, without self-contradiction, for Silk to have a near-death vision. And in Wolfe's world, whether LS or NS, it's possible for that vision to be more or less "true." But, my friend, haven't you undercut your own theory (not shared by moi) that Kypris is Silk's mum, since he does not recognize the blonde as Kypris?

OTOH, I'm impressed with your discovery of Molpe's parentage, and I wish I were near the books so I could check it out at once.

I don't think Chenille is a uberfrau. Chenille, bless her heart, seems to me one of the few people in the book who is exactly what she seems to be (except for her dyed hair). Yes, she's Tussah's natural daughter, but she was brought up in the gutter. And I believe she really loves Auk and he her (well, Kypris *orders* him to love her, but he already does) and I hope for happy ever altering though I'm not entirely optimistic (algae says hastily to avoid seeming sappy).

-algae-
