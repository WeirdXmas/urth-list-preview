Themes: Memory and forgetting, Death and resurrection

1998-04-06 Terence Nickolette #

Argues that Thecla lives on inside Severian and that some of his memories, such as the painful visit to the whorehouse, are really Thecla's, since women feel pain in first sex and men do not. Supports this with the point that Severian had no need to return to the brothel because he had his trysts with Thecla in her cell.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Thecla lives within Severian through the alzabo; Severian's whorehouse memory is Thecla's

I think of his comment after visiting the whorehouse that he never went there again because it was painful. Men don't experience pain in their initiation into sex, but women do, of course. I'll bet that was Thecla's memory.<
