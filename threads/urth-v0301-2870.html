Themes: Mirrors, pictures and art

2003-01-31 Josh Geller #

Notes that Leonardo da Vinci drew and described a set of mirrors resembling the one in The Book of the New Sun, though without its peculiar properties. Offers this as a historical parallel for the mirror device that could be used to view other dimensions.
informs · source-and-allusion

Apparently, Leonardo da Vinci built a set (he certainly drew and described one, though he did not directly mention the peculiar properties found with the BotNS version), presumably modulo the laser in the middle, but it's hard to tell with Leonardo.

If I were trying to see along dimensions other than the standard three, this would be one of the ways I'd go about it.
