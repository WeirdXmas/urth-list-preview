1999-06-14 Clifford Drane #

Asks whether a searchable archive exists and requests immediate reactions to the story The Eyeflash Miracles. Notes that the characters' ending trip to Sugarland matches a real town near Houston where the poster lives, and suggests Wolfe meant that Sugarland, citing its sugar refineries.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: Sugarland in the story is the real Sugarland near Houston

Wasn't there an announcement a while back about a searchable archive? I just read 'The Eyeflash Miracles' and am thirsty for commentary.

I'm certain some/most of you have read the story - any immediate thoughts on it?

I got a kick out of the characters heading off to Sugarland at the end. I live in Houston, and Sugarland is about 15 miles to the southwest. Wolfe having Houston ties, this is certainly the Sugarland he was referring to.

(FYI, there are huge sugar refineries in Sugarland)

Cliff Drane

1999-06-15 William H. Ansley #

Answers a question about a searchable archive by giving the search URL and pointing to his earlier postings on the Oz references in The Eyeflash Miracles. Says the story cannot be fully understood without knowing certain Oz facts, and believes its ending is happy, though some list members disagree.
answers · source-and-allusion · theories: Oz facts are needed to understand The Eyeflash Miracles

Yes, there was. You can find it at:
http://www.urth.net/urth/search/
(All Hail Ranjit!)

<The sound of loud, diabolical laughter> Come into my parlor... Cliff.

I, in the not too distant past, wrote a LONG series of postings about the Oz references in _The Eyeflash Miracles_. I don't think the story can be understood completely if you don't know certain Oz facts. Of course, even if you do know these facts, the story still can't be understood completely (it's by Gene Wolfe, after all) but it can be understood more completely, in my humble opinion.

My postings about this are only in the rough draft stage, if that, but you may find them interesting. (I am going to re-write my Oz stuff up real pretty some day, honest!) If you search for "eyeflash" in the archives at the above URL you will find it all, and a bit more. I was interrupted by an illness while writing my Oz posts and started over after a long pause. If you want to follow my final series of eight postings, search for "ozflash and revised".

I have always liked the end of this story. It seems to be a happy ending, at least to me and at least at first glance. I still prefer to believe it is a happy ending, but there are dissenting opinions on the list.

Thanks for the Sugarland info. I always like to know the origins of names.

If you have any questions to put to me or comments to make after reading my postings, please fell free to do so.

William Ansley
