Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2002-12-11 Adam Stephanides #

Recommends the anthology Leviathan 3, co-edited by Jeff VanderMeer, listing its contributors and noting stories about books and libraries and a Borges-inspired sequence. Offers the link to Wolfe as a justification for discussing it on the list, not any reading of Wolfe's work.
informs

I'm putting in a plug for this recent anthology, which is one of the best original fantasy-sf anthologies I've read. (Most of the stories are original; the few that aren't originally appeared in obscure places.) There are a number of well-known contributors: Moorcock, Stableford and Emshmiller on the sf side and Remy de Goncourt, Theophile Gautier, and Rikki Ducournet on the "mainstream" side. There are also some good pieces by less well-known writers, including a twisted take on Oz by Stepan Chapman (author of The Troika). It's co-edited by Jeff VanderMeer (though he's not a contributor), and published by the Ministry of Whimsy (www.ministryofwhimsy.com), which published the above-mentioned The Troika.

My excuse for discussing Leviathan 3 on this list is that many of the stories, though not all, revolve around books or libraries, and there is a sequence of Borges-inspired short stories running through the anthology (though these latter are actually among the weaker contributions).

--Adam
