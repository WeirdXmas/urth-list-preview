2005-01-19 James Wynn replying to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues that the poster's correspondent has overreached with Google-based associations, suggests Number Five's city may be modeled on Geneva, and proposes that the Cave Canem location is the Saltimbanque theatre rather than the Engineering college. Cites the earlier exchange about the Geneva library in The Fifth Head of Cerberus, where a New York address was posted, as a reason to rein in the posting spree and let the ideas develop.
disputes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Number Five's city is based on Geneva; Cave Canem is the Saltimbanque theatre

I'm almost embarrassed to broach this subject since some people believe I'm as guilty of this as anyone, however...

In my opinion, you have lost your way in Google. It's powers of association are impressive -- so much so that it is a mistake to take results as authoritative in themselves when applying them to Wolfe.

On the other hand, it is also possible that no one else on the list has arrived at Wolfe's works with the proper background knowledge (in say, "Anne of Greene Gables") to appreciate some subtlety that Wolfe has deliberately woven into his works (I've been frustrated by this problem myself at times IMO). In that case, you are casting pearls before swine and should obey Jesus' command regarding this. I recommend writing a series of footnoted articles on these ideas and posting them on a website (here's an example: http://www.urth.org/whorlmap/) so you can attract readers who *are* able to appreciate your annotations.

The best way to tell if only swine are reading your posts is to see whether you are not getting any feedback from other readers on them.

Finally, I am intrigued by your connection with Geneva, Switzerland and Number Five's city. I half suspect that the city is based on Geneva and the cities and streets mentioned there can be found on a map of sites (wouldn't the founding city of Calvinism be a just model for Catholic Wolfe's colony gone wrong?). I don't believe (as you seem to) that the Engineering college is the location of Cave Canem. I think it is quite likely to be the Saltimbanque theatre. Yet, I personally wouldn't mind seeing this theory poked with a few sticks.

But I'm not sure if that will happen because your other posts are unjustly undermining your credibility on any matter. As an example, when I queried for possible candidates in Geneva architecture for the Library in TFHoC, you posted the address for the library in Geneva, NEW YORK. I think this made us both look foolish...as though I had begun an earnest intellectual discussion with a homeless man muttering to himself in the streets, or that I had been taken in by a prankster.

You've planted a lot of seeds in the last two days. Why not sit back and see if anything grows from it?

~ Crush

Responds to Jim Raylor's quoted remark about the young women's play revolving around the loss of power by French-speaking colonists, linking that inset plot to historical colonial conflict.
