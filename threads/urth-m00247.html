The Wizard is shipping

2004-10-23 Brett M. Grace #

Informs readers that The Wizard, scheduled for November publication, is listed as in stock and shipping within 24 hours at major online booksellers, with the ISBN given. Offers the bookstore availability as the basis, and jokes that list silence may mean members are already reading it.
informs

I haven't received any mail from this list for the past few days, so I apologize if this is needlessly redundant. I noticed this morning that The Wizard (ISBN 0765312018), scheduled to be published in November, is shipping today. bn.com and amazon.com both list it as in stock and shipping within 24 hours. Local bookstores may have it also.

Or maybe the reason I haven't received any mail is because everybody already is busy reading it?
