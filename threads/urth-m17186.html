2011-02-11 Roy C. Lackey replying to Andrew Mason #

Quotes In Green's Jungles on the Neighbours leaving for another short sun, answering Andrew Mason's suggestion of a hidden meaning in the phrase Neighbour Whorl. Offers the passage without added commentary, as evidence that the Neighbours went elsewhere and may revisit Blue, which bears on whether the Neighbour Whorl points to Lune.
informs · close-textual-reading

"As for me, I have walked with them, and sat with them around their fire. Thus I know that they exist. They have gone elsewhere -- found a new home circling another short sun. But they have our permission to revisit this one whenever they choose." (IGJ, 72)

-Roy

Thus I know that they exist. They have gone elsewhere -- found a new home
circling another short sun. But they have our permission to revisit this one

2011-02-11 Andrew Mason #

Agrees with Gerry Quinn that a remark about the Neighbours' destination could carry a hidden meaning, while granting the plain literal reading. Notes that 'you' might mean humans generally rather than humans on Blue, and suggests the hidden sense could point to Lune or fairyland, though it may not.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: the Neighbour Whorl hides a reference to Lune or fairyland

To clarify: I'm sure the plain, literal meaning of the remark is that some humans on Blue call the place the Neighbours have gone the Neighbour Whorl, because the Neighbours have gone there. But there _might_ be a secondary, hidden meaning, which might refer to Lune, fairyland, or something else we haven't spotted yet. Then again, there might not.
