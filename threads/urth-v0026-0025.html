Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1999-04-25 Alice Turner #

Argues that Tzadkiel, the figure Roy meets in the Long Sun books, should be treated as an angel rather than a smerp, since that label suits the series' scheme and the author. Quotes Tzadkiel's pawns-and-board remark from Roy as the basis for that choice.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · close-textual-reading · theories: Tzadkiel is an angel, not a smerp

Skipping a great deal of verbiage, here's one Wolfe quote from Roy:

"Yours is a race of pawns," Tzadkiel told me. "You move forward only, unless we move you back to begin the game again. But not all the pieces on the board are pawns."

You can call Tzadkiel a smerp if you like. But it's useful and efficient, considering the scheme of the series and the character of the author, to call him/her an angel, and that's exactly what I'm going to continue doing.

-alga
