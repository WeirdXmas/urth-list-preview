1997-09-12 Alice Turner #

Recounts the poster's first encounter with Smith through the story "A Planet Named Shayol" and recommends starting with his mature work, naming "The Dead Lady of Clown Town" and "Mother Hitton's Littul Kittons" as stories to read. Advises against beginning with the Casher O'Neill novellas, which the poster says are better read once the reader knows Smith's work. Responds to a suggestion from a poster called the Rat.
proposes

My first encounter with Smith was in an old Judy Merril anthology with "A Planet Named Shayol." It knocked me sideways---and I was far from being an infernologist at the time!

Agreed: start with the mature work. Don't forget "The Dead Lady of Clown Town." Or even "Mother Hitton's Littul Kittons." Don't read those Casher O'Neill novellas first as the Rat suggests---they're for after you know the score.

-alga-
