The Neighbourly inhumi

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-08-22 to 2000-08-22

2000whorlThe Book of the Short SunNicholas Gevers

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

2000-08-22 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that the inhumi who once drank Neighbor blood may have survived in altered form, and that the Neighbors Horn meets in The Book of the Long Sun could themselves be inhumi. Grounds this on John Clute's remark and on the inhumi of Horn's time in the City on Green, whose powers seem to come from their human slaves rather than the Neighbors.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Neighbors Horn meets are inhumi; Inhumi who drank Neighbor blood survived

Drawing on a remark John Clute made a few days ago:
are we sure of the fate of those inhumi who, however
long ago, ingested Neighbor blood and thus became
equal to the Neighbors in puissance? The inhumi who in
Horn's time infest the City on Green clearly have a
relation to them, but their qualities seem to derive
from their human slaves, the "inhumans". Did the
original "Neighborly inhumi" survive in some other
guise? Specifically, are the Neighbors Horn encounters
not perhaps inhumi themselves?
