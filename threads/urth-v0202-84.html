league to mile conversion

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-02-05 to 2002-02-05

2002urthMarc Aramini

2002-02-05 Marc Aramini #

Explains how the poster converted 30,000 and 80,000 leagues from The Book of the New Sun to miles, using an online unit-conversion page found by a Google search. Says the page assumes a single standard league and gives no distinction between league types, so the moon-distance figure rests on that one tool.
informs

I was asked about my conversion of 30,000 leagues and 80,000 leagues before the list went down, since I claimed 80,000 leagues was currently the average distance from the earth to the moon. This is how I got my numbers: I did a search on google with the words League to Mile Conversion and clicked on the second page that came up, which is one of those handy conversion pages in which you type in any value and it converts it to everything from picometer to light years. Its address is http://www.speckdesign.com/Tlength.html. It doesn't say anything about different types of leagues, and it seems to assume some kind of absolute, standard value for the league. Check it out; the distance to the moon comes up with that 80,000 league input; I don't know what else to tell you. The page has old russian terms like ell, versht, makhovaya sazhen, etc. It has any measure you could possibly desire. Marc Aramini
