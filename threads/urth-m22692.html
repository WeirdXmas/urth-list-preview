Some Gene Wolfe Covers

Urth list · 8 messages · 2012-03-05 to 2012-03-06

2012urthThe Book of the New SunGreg BatesAntónio Pedro MarquesGerry QuinnMarc AraminiFred KiescheJeff Wilson
The thread: The thread was a casual exchange about cover art for Gene Wolfe's books, prompted by Gerry Quinn sharing Bruce Pennington's covers for the original Sun quartet. Marc Aramini preferred the Maitz covers for the Book of the New Sun volumes and named the limited edition Free Live Free cover as Wolfe's worst, which Gerry Quinn jokingly seconded. Greg Bates said he now wanted a copy of Free Live Free because its cover was striking, and Fred Kiesche asked whether a new edition was planned after noting that a cover detail had been misremembered. The discussion stayed on the art and never reached the books' content, ending in light agreement and drift rather than debate.
  • Marc Aramini: prefers Maitz covers for the Book of the New Sun, finds Pennington's covers landscape-heavy, and calls the Free Live Free limited cover Wolfe's wo
  • Gerry Quinn: admires Pennington's Sun quartet art and says Wolfe's cover artists rarely serve his books as well
  • Greg Bates: disliked Free Live Free but now wants a copy because the cover is striking
  • Fred Kiesche: asks whether a new edition of Free Live Free is planned and corrects a detail about the two-title release

Themes: Books, libraries and writing
