2003-02-06 Nigel Price #

Informs the list that Wolfe will be writer-in-residence at the Odyssey Fantasy Writing Workshop at Southern New Hampshire University, running the fifth week of a six-week course. Points readers to the workshop's website and notes the course is expensive and demanding but a fine opportunity.
informs

Perhaps everyone else round here already knew this, but apparently GW is going to be writer-in-residence at the Odyssey Fantasy Writing Workshop at Southern New Hampshire University this summer. As part of his duties, he will be running the entire workshop for the fifth week of the six week course. For anyone interested, full details may be found at:

http://www.sff.net/odyssey/

It's expensive to go on the course and is clearly going to demand a big commitment of time and energy from those taking part, but what an opportunity for those who get to go!

Nigel
