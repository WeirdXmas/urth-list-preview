2000-02-03 Alice Turner #

Clarifies that the Tiptree and Russ comparison for Wolfe's "The Ziggurat" refers to Tiptree's "Houston, Houston, Do You Read?" and Russ's "When It Changed," not only "The Women Men Don't See." Responds to an earlier post that recommended "The Women Men Don't See" from Her Smoke Rose Up Forever as a possible inspiration for the story.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Tiptree and Russ stories inspired The Ziggurat

When I referred to Tiptree and Russ, I was thinking of this story, but even more centrally of Tiptree's "Houston, Houston, Do You Read?" and Russ's "When It Changed."

-alga
