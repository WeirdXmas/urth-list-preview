Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

1997-05-26 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Notes that the hooded alien vampire creatures in the books may be one of several varieties, some unhooded, so "inhumu" could be a categorical term like cacogen. Also asks Crowley whether he ranks Engine Summer above Little, Big, and lists other Crowley works he thinks were left out.
proposes · close-textual-reading

Reply: Item #4579959 from [email]@INET00#

Lasrach,

Re: the alien vampire critters, as we are told there is a "hooded" variety (cobra-like) there is also the suggestion that there are other types--at least one un-hooded. These types may or may not be related. So, as you say, the term "inhumu" might be as categorical as cacogen.

(Re: Crowley, do you rank ENGINE SUMMER over LITTLE, BIG or was that list unranked? The completist in me wants to write that you left out: THE DEEP and BEASTS and THE GREAT WORK OF TIME and ANTIQUITIES [and "Gone" in F&SF Sep. 1996]; but maybe you don't consider these among his best work. <g>)

=mantis=
