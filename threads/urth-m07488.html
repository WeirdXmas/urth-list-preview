Shares an intentionally awful joke built on Silk from the Long Sun books, a fat opulent man and a bartender on Sun Street, with a pun on 'Blood in my Stool.' The post is an off-topic bit of levity, prompted by a discussion on another board about April Fools pranks with red food coloring.

Someone on another message board was discussing pranks involving feeding someone red food coloring on April Fools to make them think they had Blood in their stool.

That is when I came up with this, an absolutely awful joke:

"Patera Silk walks into a bar on Sun Street, and sees a very fat opulent man sitting in front of the bar tender. He gets a very angry look on his face, crosses his arm and sighs. The bartender looks at him and asks 'What's wrong Patera?' Silk points to the opulent man and says 'What's wrong? There's Blood in my Stool!'"

Better yet--assume Typhon *was the original Neighbor and the founder of their race. *The doubling of limbs is in imitation of his doubling of heads. That's why the Neighbors tell Silk/Horn that Pas is not their god--he's a culture hero/ancestor figure instead.
