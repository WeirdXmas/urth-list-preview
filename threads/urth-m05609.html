2008-01-25 Mo Holkar / UKG #

Announces that Robert Borski's Cave Canem website, containing his essays and pieces on The Fifth Head of Cerberus, has been added to the WolfeWiki and is now readable there. Notes that these pages are read-only except for Borski himself, each has an annotation page for comments, and conversion bugs should be reported.
informs

Hi all,

Just a quick note to say that we've added the contents of Robert Borski's Cave Canem website to the WolfeWiki.

You probably already know that this website, which hasn't been available online for some time, contained a number of Robert's essays and other pieces about The Fifth Head of Cerberus. He's now very kindly agreed to add it to our wiki.

Unlike the other wiki material, these Cave Canem pages can't be edited or revised (except by Robert himself), but each has a discussion / annotation page associated with it, where you can make comments, etc.

(There are though probably all sorts of bugs and oddities that have been introduced during the conversion process of adding it to the wiki, so if you spot any such, do please let me know so I can correct them.)

Anyway, the front page of it is here:
http://www.holkar.net/pmwiki/pmwiki.php?n=CaveCanem.Index

so please go ahead and read, enjoy and muse upon Cave Canem!

best,

Mo
