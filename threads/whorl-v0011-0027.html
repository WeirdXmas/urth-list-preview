2000-08-01 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that the Horn and Babbie transference in On Blue's Waters was a trick by the Neighbors to help Horn escape the inhumi, who have no interest in a human. Notes that if the Neighbors are responsible they act remotely, so Horn is not truly encountering them, responding to a quoted passage and earlier posters' doubts about the dream sequence.
