Seven American Nights explanation wins "award"

Urth list · 5 messages · 2010-08-25 to 2010-08-26

2010urthMo Holkar / UKGJack SmithDavid StockhoffJames WynnDave Tallman
The thread: Dave Tallman reported that the forgery explanation of Seven American Nights he posted on the WolfeWiki had won a mock '2010 Pulitzer Prize for Commentary' from a blog, and the blogger found it the most satisfying. Mo Holkar congratulated him and said he had moved from skeptical to largely convinced, planning to reread the story for remaining problems. James Wynn countered by quoting Wolfe's December 2009 list post, written as Mr Waggish, that the book will never be solved because readers find clues he did not intend. The thread ended without debate on the explanation itself, with the award and the supporter's conversion as the main points.
  • Dave Tallman: announces the forgery explanation's mock award and shares the blog post
  • Mo Holkar: congratulates Tallman and says he is now largely convinced of the forgery explanation
  • James Wynn: quotes Wolfe's statement that the book cannot be definitively solved because of unintended clues

2010-08-26 James Wynn replying to Mo Holkar #

Quotes Wolfe's December 2009 list post, written as Mr Waggish, saying Seven American Nights will never be solved because readers find clues he did not intend. Replies to Mo Holkar by offering this statement as evidence against the idea that the book has a definitive explanation.
informs · author-and-biography

This is what Mr Waggish said about 7AN in Dec. 2009:

"The multiplicity of interpretations of 7 American Nights suggests to me that no, it will never be figured out. There are too many 'clues' people are finding that Wolfe did not intend; or rather, the real clues are too obscure."
