2002urthThe Book of the New SunAdam StephanidesMichael Andre-DriussiMark Millman
The thread: Participants corrected bibliographic details about Gene Wolfe chapbooks and small press editions, including a typo, a title spelling, and the correct title Best New Horror. Michael Andre-Driussi lamented that small press Wolfe books are now nearly impossible to produce, and Mark Millman reported that copies of Cholfin's purchased stock still circulate through secondary sellers. Adam Stephanides turned the point toward Lafferty, noting that several small presses stopped publishing his unpublished works in the early 1990s and hoping they will eventually appear. The thread ended without debate, drifting from bibliographic corrections to a shared wish about small press publishing.
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: corrected a typo and title spelling, and lamented that small press Wolfe books are nearly impossible to produce now
  • Mark Millman: admitted and corrected the Best New Horror title error, and reported that secondary sellers still stock Cholfin's copies
  • Adam Stephanides: small presses once published Lafferty's works before stopping, and many Lafferty novels remain unpublished

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

Corrects a typo cited in a bibliography of Wolfe chapbooks, notes the Ultan's Library title spelling matches another reference, and reports that Bryan Cholfin bought remaining copies of an earlier book before a new edition. Laments that small press Wolfe books are now nearly impossible to produce, citing past publishers and the author's own failed attempts.

2002-03-21 Mark Millman #

Acknowledges a mistake in a bibliographic note, confirming that Best New Horror is the correct title rather than Year's Best Horror, and apologizes to the poster. Also reports that Cheap Street and 20th Century First Editions still sell copies of Empire and Bibliomen, so Cholfin's purchased stock reached the secondary market, and notes no Urth reference in The Monday Man.

2002-03-25 Adam Stephanides #

Reports that four or five small presses once published Lafferty's as-yet-unpublished works in the early 1990s before all of them stopped at about the same time. Replies to Michael Andre-Driussi's point about the difficulty of small press Wolfe books by noting that many Lafferty novels remain unpublished and hoping they will eventually appear.
informs

on 3/21/02 10:03 PM, Michael Andre-Driussi at [email]

At one point there were four or five small presses publishing Lafferty's as-yet-unpublished works. Then, at about the same time (early 90s, I think) they all stopped. There are still quite a few unpublished novels; hopefully they'll see the light of day eventually.

--Adam
