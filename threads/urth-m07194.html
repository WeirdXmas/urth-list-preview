2008-10-22 Stephen Case #

Announces that the poster's first short story, "The Glorious Revolution," has been published in Ray Gun Revival and invites Wolfe readers to read and comment on it. Says the author considers himself influenced by Wolfe but makes no critical argument about Wolfe's books.
informs

This is perhaps a bit off-topic (and perhaps also a bit self-promoting), but in the vein of other writers who Wolfe-readers might possibly enjoy: my first short story has been published in this month's issue of Ray Gun Revival (available for free [email]). I'm certainly no Wolfe, but I do consider myself to have been influenced by his work, and I would be VERY interested to hear what other Wolfe afficionados (and thus by default readers of excellent fiction) think of it. The story is entitled "The Glorious Revolution".

-Steve

2008-10-22 Gwern Branwen replying to Stephen Case #

Replies to Stephen Case's comments on a published short story, agreeing that Wolfe's influence is audible in the justification of kingship. Disagrees that the cyclic elements end in an atheistic place Wolfe would reject, implicitly contesting how the story's cycles relate to the Briah cycle.
