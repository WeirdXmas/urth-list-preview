TALES OF THE NEW SUN?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-10-26 to 1999-10-26

1999urthThe Book of the New SunNicholas Gevers

1999-10-26 Nicholas Gevers #

Expresses regret that Wolfe's uncollected short stories and essays set in the Urth/Whorl universe are scattered across several volumes, and proposes a single companion collection titled Tales of the New Sun. Lists specific pieces by title and the books where they appear, including essays from Castle of Days and stories in Endangered Species, Planet Engineering and Strange Travelers.
proposes · source-and-allusion

Very likely an idle question: do other Lupines regret,
as I do, the lack of a companion volume to the "Sun"
cycles containing Wolfe's short stories and
miscellanea set in the Urth/Whorl universe? Obviously,
we will very likely see the as yet uncollected stories
of this sort included in STRANGE TRAVELERS; but that
will leave them scattered: "These Are the Jokes" and
the sundry CASTLE OF THE OTTER essays in CASTLE OF
DAYS; "The God and His Man", "The Cat" and "The Map"
in ENDANGERED SPECIES; "Books in THE BOOK OF THE NEW
SUN" in PLAN[e}T ENGINEERING; and "The Boy Who Hooked
the Sun", "Empires of Foliage and Flower", "The Old
Woman Whose Rolling Pin is the Sun", and "The Night
Chough" in STRANGE TRAVELERS or other future
collections. What a pity they can't be consolidated
into a single convenient volume, TALES OF THE NEW SUN
perhaps...
