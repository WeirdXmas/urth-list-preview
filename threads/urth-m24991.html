Oreb possessed?

Urth list · 4 messages · 2013-09-13 to 2013-09-13

2013urthThe Book of the Long SunAntónio Pedro MarquesMarc AraminiLarry Miller
The thread: Larry Miller proposed that Oreb was possessed at his apparent death during the failed sacrifice at the Sacred Window, citing the bird Silk sees flying and Oreb's flight despite his injured wing. Marc Aramini suggested Oreb may have possessed Pike, the figure glimpsed in a calotte, linking this to an old woman's story in In Green's Jungles and to a timing oddity in the final book. Miller disputed the link, arguing Pike appears minutes and pages after Oreb's descent so the events are separate. Entonio supported the possession idea in general, voicing affection for Oreb without citing textual evidence, and the thread ended without resolution.
  • Larry Miller: Oreb was possessed at the Sacred Window and the bird Silk sees is Oreb; later disputes that Pike and Oreb's descent are linked
  • Marc Aramini: Oreb may have possessed Pike in the calotte, tied to the old woman's story in In Green's Jungles
  • entonio: possession could start at any moment, but regrets the idea that much of Oreb is not really Oreb; offers no textual evidence

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

2013-09-13 Larry Miller #

Proposes that Oreb was possessed at the moment of his apparent death during the failed sacrifice at the Sacred Window, and that the bird Silk sees flying from the upper window before Pike appears is Oreb. Grounds the idea in Oreb's ability to fly despite his injured wing and in his denial of memory, which the poster reads as lack of control during possession. Asks whether others have raised this before.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Oreb was possessed after apparently dying at the sacrifice; The bird Silk sees fleeing the window is Oreb

I have been rereading Book of the Long Sun, and was struck with the notion that maybe Oreb was possessed from the moment of his apparent death during the failed sacrifice (the sacrificial altar was in front of the Sacred Window). The bird seen by Silk fluttering out the second story window prior to the appearance of Pike was Oreb, able to do so despite his injured wing because of the possession. this would also explain Orebs denial- he don't remember because he wasn't in control at the time. I don't know if this has ever been brought up before. Any thoughts?

2013-09-13 Marc Aramini replying to Larry Miller #

Proposes that Oreb may have possessed Pike, the figure glimpsed in a calotte with Oreb descending, and suggests this links to Silk and Oreb appearing in an old woman's story in the jungle book. Notes a scene in the final book about Silk's tunic colour and suspects time-wise oddities there too.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Oreb possessed Pike before the Night Chough; Silk and Oreb appear backwards in time

I have often wondered about the moment of possession by Oreb, but that glimpse of "Pike" in his calotte with Oreb floating down always seemed to me similar to Silk and Oreb showing up in the old woman's story in In Green's Jungles - those two "guys" headed backwards in time, Silk much older.? There is a curious scene in the final book where they make a big deal about whether Silk was wearing a blue or black tunic, and I think there is something funny going on time wise there as well.? He very well could have been possessed way back then - he definitely was by the time of the story "the Night Chough", but for how long? will pay attention to it when I re-read the sun cycle, some day not too far away.
