The thread: Nigel Price announced a new interview with Michael Andre-Driussi about the revised Lexicon Urthus, which touched on the Suzanne Delage debate and a planned Long Sun/Short Sun volume. Pedro Pereira asked about and welcomed that forthcoming book, and Nigel Price confirmed the working title Gate of Horn, Book of Silk. Jeff Wilson then reported that the interview was missing from the Ultan's Library listing and that a Delage link was broken, and Nigel Price supplied corrected addresses and proposed a new-entries log. The thread ended on site maintenance rather than any dispute over the fiction.

2008-09-25 Nigel Price #

Announces an interview on Ultan's Library with Michael Andre-Driussi about the revised edition of the Lexicon Urthus, covering its origins, Gene Wolfe's foreword and John Brunner's brief involvement. Points readers to the page and notes the interview also touches on the evolution of the Suzanne Delage debate, with a light personal footnote.
informs

The latest addition to Ultan's Library (www.ultan.org.uk) is an interview with Michael Andre-Driussi about the new edition of the "Lexicon Urthus". In the interview, Michael discusses how he first came to write the Lexicon and how the new revised edition came about. He explains how Gene Wolfe came to write the Foreword and how the late John Brunner briefly became involved in the project. In passing, he also casts some interesting light on the evolution of the "Suzanne Delage" debate.

I was fascinated to learn more about the Lexicon and hope that you too will enjoy the interview.

As a footnote, I ought to mention that Michael was keen to make the context of the interview clear and to show that he and I had actually met previously to discuss Wolfe-related matters. He encouraged me to find a novel way of revealing this to the reader and, if you read through to the bottom of the web page, I think you'll find that I failed completely!

Nigel

Replies to Pedro Pereira's question about that project and points readers to the comment facility on Ultan's Library for further questions.
informs

Pedro Pereira asked:

Yes, Michael did say that he had the nucleus of a book about "The Book of the Long Sun" and "The Book of the Short Sun" that he referred to as having the working title "Gate of Horn, Book of Silk". As you say, the contents so far assembled are based around the materials he wrote for the Long Sun chapbooks.

If anyone has any questions about the interview, there is a comment facility on Ultan's Library that allows you to post your responses. If there are queries, we might well be able to get Michael back to answer some of them.
