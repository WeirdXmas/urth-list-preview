2003-03-07 Nicholas Gevers #

Announces and praises Wolfe's new story "Graylord Man's Last Words" in an upcoming magazine issue, calling its telling simple but its elegy profound. Compares it to the earlier story "The Castaway" and notes the trick of narrating in terms the story itself treats as extinct.
informs

GW's story in the May ASIMOV'S, "Graylord Man's Last
Words", is very simple in the telling, but its elegy
is profound. Similar in some ways to "The Castaway".
The trick this time is telling a story in (Dickensian)
terms that are by the story's internal reckoning
extinct...I highly recommend this one.

--Nick Gevers.
