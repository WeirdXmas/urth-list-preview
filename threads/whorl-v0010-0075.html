Expresses amazement at the Long Sun novel On Blue's Waters, saying Wolfe's books grow ever more complex, artful and entrancing. Responds to a quoted enthusiast's praise and ends by relaying the list editor's note that the forum is for discussing The Book of the Long Sun.

1999-10-12 Kevin J. Maroney #

Reports a conversation in which the poster said The Book of the Short Sun's On Blue's Waters might be Wolfe's best since The Shadow of the Torturer, and editor David Hartwell replied that Return to the Whorl might be Wolfe's best book ever. The post is a light, joking aside rather than a critical argument, and it responds to alga's quoted message from Hartwell.
informs

We were talking about _Short Sun_ over the weekend (NYRSF work weekend just finished) and I commented that _On Blue's Waters_ might be Wolfe's best book since _Shadow of the Torturer_. Hartwell responded that _Return to the Whorl_ might be Wolfe's best book ever.

So I beat him up and stole the manuscripts.
