Themes: Stories within stories

1998-04-14 Michael Straight #

Asks why the tale of the Sidhe and the flock of geese closes Peace, saying the story stands alone but its link to Weer's story is unclear. Re-posts an earlier question that drew no replies, without citing any particular reading or passage beyond the placement of the tale at the book's end.
questions · close-textual-reading

I asked this question a while back in the middle of a longer message about Peace and didn't get any takers and I hope y'all don't mind me taking another shot.

I love the story of the Sidhe and the flock of geese at the very end of the book, but I don't think I quite get it. I mean, I enjoy the story all by itself, but I don't see what it has to do with Weer's story. Given the placement of the story there must be some connection I'm missing.

Anyone see something here I'm missing?

-Rostrum
