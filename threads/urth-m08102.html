2009urthThe Book of the New SunAllan AndersonGwern BranwenSon of WitzJeff WilsonDan'l Danehy-Oakes
The thread: Son of Witz announced a one-colour woodcut-style Avern Duel shirt sold through a band's webstore, and Allan Anderson praised it while mentioning a missed concert and an off-topic Juana Molina show. In the follow-up, Son of Witz asked for castle and sun design suggestions, and Jeff Wilson and Gwern Branwen offered ideas such as an afflicted sun face, clouds over the disk, and plague spots, with Gwern noting shading may be impossible on a shirt. The thread stayed friendly and practical, ending with design suggestions rather than any real dispute.
  • Son of Witz: designer of the woodcut-style Avern Duel shirt, pursuing medieval woodcut look and considering bartizan turrets and plague spots for the sun
  • Allan Anderson: admirer of the shirt design who also raised an off-topic concert tangent
  • Jeff Wilson: proposed an afflicted sun face or spooky clouds over the sun as alternatives
  • Gwern Branwen: suggested plague spots but noted that shading may not be possible on a shirt

Themes: War and soldiers, Books, libraries and writing, Light, darkness and colour

2009-02-13 Son of Witz replying to Son of Witz #

Chats about a band's new album and shirt, saying the band is making more music and that a guitarist and composer were pointed toward Wolfe's books. Mentions that Jason Schimmel almost named the band Catafalque, a name from the Long Sun books, and that a composer found a song title, Septentrion, in The Shadow of the Torturer; it replies to Allan Anderson's compliment on the shirt.
chats

Thanks Dan'l, Allan.
I don't want to bore anyone with thanks any time someone makes a compliment, but I don't like to be rude and ignore such, either.

I'm well familiar with baby-related schedule compression and shows. Got a second kid on the way. That show was really good. I figure they'll only get better. They're trying to get another album out this year, the first has been selling better than anyone expected. Still small potatoes compared to, I dunnoh, I suppose Stu's albums, but people are digging it.

I told Jason Schimmel, the guitarist/composer, he should read BotNS, and will probably try to do some more BotNS stuff for him. He ought to like the words. He almost called the band Catafalque, which is in New or Long Sun. The Secret Chiefs composer, Trey, opened up Shadow at random and saw one of his upcoming song titles on the page, Septentrion. Now hopefully he'll read the copies I gave him for Christmas.

~witz

2009-02-14 Son of Witz replying to Jeff Wilson #

Explains the artist's design choices for a one-colour Avern duel shirt, describing the shield wall of Nessus against the sun and the bartizan-style turrets he is considering for the wall sections. Asks for suggestions on castle detail and doubts the sun's radiant lines, adding a link to a sloppy illustration for laughs.
informs

thanks.
during that scene, the shield wall of Nessus is visible, and has climbed almost half across the sun. something like that. It's still a stretch away from the sanquinary field so I didn't make it tower over them. Those little curved raised sections in the silhouette of the wall were my lame first take on a little detail where wall sections met. I realize at each section there should be a bartizan (is that the right word) or turrets. I wasn't sure how "castle" to make it. I'd be open to suggestions about that detail, since I've been sketching Nessus and Citadel landscapes. I'm now more inclined to put a spiky turret on them.

I'm also unsure about the Sun's radiant lines. I didn't think, with my one color limitation, that it would register as a sun without them, but the lines probably make the sun look too healthy and strong.

It's really challenging trying to do something that will adhere to the text. my own inspirations are much more sloppy.
for laughs, here's a very sloppy recent inspiration, BANANA BARBARIAN: http://tinyurl.com/bxyggw

~witz
