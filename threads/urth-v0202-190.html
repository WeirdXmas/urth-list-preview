2002-02-21 Roy C. Lackey #

Remarks that an interview with Wolfe reveals he now has a computer and internet access, and wonders how this will change the list. Contrasts his earlier writing on the list, when Wolfe would never see it, with the prospect that Wolfe might now read posts.
informs

I really enjoyed the Huddleston interview, and there are a lot of things that can be discussed about it, but the one thing that struck me hardest was that Wolfe now has a computer and internet access. I wonder what effect that fact will have upon this list. For myself, when I knew that Wolfe didn't have those things, and at his age didn't seem likely to get them, it didn't matter so much what I wrote on the list, because he wasn't ever going to see it. Now . . .?

-Roy
