Yes. I think we may actually be mostly in agreement but talking past each other. It may boil down to a forest vs. trees approach. I am definitely a forest sort of thinker and I think you are a tree guy.

Surely both approaches are important. And integrating the multiple levels of the text by combining the skills of various types of thinkers is the best way to gain the greatest understanding.

On the other hand, that is a very "forest" way of thinking and keeping it to one level may work best for some.
