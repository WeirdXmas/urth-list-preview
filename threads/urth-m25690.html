2014-05-12 Marc Aramini replying to Tony Ellis #

Concedes that the pronoun 'they' in the story's line about men who liked the rape refers to the women rather than the men, withdrawing a reading that the men were under a possessing spirit. Responds to Tony Ellis's close reading and notes that Wolfe's unclear pronoun references in The Fifth Head of Cerberus and the Long Sun books are deliberate.

2014-05-12 Tony Ellis #

Argues that in the story's line about men saying they liked it, "they" refers to the women victims rather than the men, so the men are repeating the excuse that victims wanted to be raped. Answers a prior post that read the passage as the leader's self-justification, offering a grammatical reading of the antecedent in the preceding sentence.
