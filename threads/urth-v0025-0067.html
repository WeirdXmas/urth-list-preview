Is Agilus a Hierodule? & more

1999-04-13 Roy C. Lackey #

Disputes Nicholas Gevers's claim that Agilus is a hierodule, since Agia would then be one too and hierodules would not want Sev's sword. Then asserts that sexual aberrations recur across Wolfe's work, citing Sev and his grandmother, Orpine, Father Inire, and Sev's sex with women he holds power over, and asks for articles on the subject.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Agilus and Agia are not hierodules; Sexual aberrations recur throughout Wolfe's work

I don't think so. That would make Agia one, too. And Sev had a very good look at his head, after all. <g> If they had been hierodules they wouldn't have wanted Sev's sword, would they? And why would a hierodule want to kill Sev? Agia certainly kept trying. I don't think she would have had any interest in running around in the forest playing Girl-Robin Hood in a pointless game, either.

Which brings me to another observation of a recurrent theme in Wolfe's work; sexual aberrations. Incest with Agilus and Agia; why put that in? Sev and his grandmother. Orpine and her father. Father Inire's inordinate interest in little girls (and the occasional little boy). Weer and Sherry Gold. Baldanders and his bedfellow., Vodalus's leech and Mamas, and many more instances of the like.

Not to mention the many whores like Candy and Hyacinth and a fondness for whorehouses.

Sev goes out of his way to say that torturers don't get off on what they do. Bull.
Sev sure does; many of those he has sex with are women over whom he exercises power of one sort or another (Thecla, Dorcas, Cyriaca, Pia).

Has anyone written an article about these sexual angles in Wolfe's work?

Roy

1999-04-13 Michael Straight #

Suggests that Wolfe, like some Christian authors, may write heavily about sex so as not to be pegged as a Christian writer. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's list of sexual episodes in the books, including Agilus and Agia, Severian and his grandmother, and Orpine and her father, with a joking aside.
proposes · author-and-biography · theories: Wolfe writes about sex to avoid being pegged as a Christian author

I've suspected in other contexts that one way Christian authors avoid getting pegged as "Christian Authors" is to write alot about sex. I wonder if Wolfe might have a little of that bug.

SMTIRCAHIAGEHLT
