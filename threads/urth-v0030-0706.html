"Five" in In Glory Like Their Star [Digest urth.v030.n189]

2001-10-09 John Bishop #

Disputes the claim that the Proto-Indo-European word for five is *pan, arguing it is *penkwe and distinct from *pan, the root for all. Cites the etymological distinction from memory without references, offering it as a correction to the thread on 'Five' in In Glory Like Their Star.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: PIE five is *penkwe, not *pan

The PIE for "five" is *penkwe, not *pan

The PIE for "all" is *pan or *pant.

I don't have my references here, but I believe
the two are distinct.

-John
