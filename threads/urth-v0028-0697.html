The Death of Koshchei the Deathless

Urth list · 3 messages · 2000-01-17 to 2000-01-18

2000urthRon Hale-EvansWilliam H. AnsleyAlex David Groce
The thread: A short thread asked whether the Koshchei in Wolfe's story is the same figure as in James Branch Cabell's works. William H. Ansley pointed readers to Andrew Lang's Red Fairy Book as the source and suggested comparing what Wolfe kept and changed. Ron Hale-Evans asked whether Koshchei is the grand high god in Cabell's Dom Manuel series, admitting he had not read Wolfe's story or Lang. Alex David Groce reported, second-hand from a thesis writer, that Koshchei appears in Cabell's Jurgen and that the two share a source but differ greatly. The thread ended without any reading of the texts; the Cabell claims rested on hearsay and recollection.

  • Alex David Groce: Koshchei appears in Cabell's Jurgen, sharing a source with Wolfe's version but differing greatly, based on hearsay

2000-01-17 William H. Ansley #

Points readers to Andrew Lang's The Red Fairy Book as the source text behind Wolfe's story "The Death of Koshchei the Deathless" and suggests comparing what Wolfe kept and changed. Notes that the Lang version seems cobbled together from pieces that do not fit, and gives online links to a text of the book.
informs · source-and-allusion

I found the story "The Death of Koshchei the Deathless" in _Strange Travelers_ very entertaining. Although I could tell it was based on "fairy tale" material. I didn't recognize that it was from a specific story until I read the note at the end. The title did seem tantalizingly familiar until I got to that point however.

_The Red Fairy Book_ by Andrew Lang is available on line, if anyone is interested in reading the original story Wolfe based his on. It is interesting to see what he kept and what he changed. The Lang version of the story is also interesting in its own right. The story seems to have been cobbled together from bits and pieces and the pieces don't all fit.

This URL should take you to a page that allows you to choose from variously compressed versions of a text file containing _The Red Fairy Book_:

http://sailor.gutenberg.org/by-title/xx1352.html

This URL is a direct link to download an uncomressed text version of the book (its about 663 kB):

ftp://sailor.gutenberg.org/pub/gutenberg/etext96/rdfry10.txt

William Ansley

2000-01-18 Ron Hale-Evans #

Asks whether Koshchei the Deathless, the figure in Wolfe's story, is the grand high god in James Branch Cabell's Biography of Dom Manuel series, possibly also appearing in Jurgen. Admits the poster has not yet read the Wolfe story or the Lang source, so the question rests on recollection of Cabell rather than on the texts.
questions · source-and-allusion

Haven't read either the Wolfe or the Lang yet, but wasn't Koshchei the Deathless the grand-high-god-over-everything in James Branch Cabell's mammoth series _The Biography of Dom Manuel_? I believe he appears in _Jurgen_, among other novels.

2000-01-18 Alex David Groce #

Reports that Koshchei the Deathless appears in James Branch Cabell's Jurgen, passed on second-hand from someone who wrote a thesis on Cabell, and suggests the two Koscheis share a source but differ greatly. Grounds the claim in that hearsay, admitting the poster has not read Jurgen or other Cabell, and closes with a Bible verse, John 8:32.
informs · source-and-allusion

He's definitely in _Jurgen_ (I haven't actually read _Jurgen_ or any other Cabell, but I was told this long ago by someone who did their Master's Thesis on Cabell when they noticed I was reading a Wolfe story with that title). From what I gleaned then, the Koscheis in question are from the same source/root, but radically differ.
