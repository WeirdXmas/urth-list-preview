2001-02-03 james.kellar #

Asks whether the Neighbours sent representatives to the Whorl when it arrived in the Blue/Green system, and whether there are clues to who they might be. Suggests they could be connected to the godling in Return to the Whorl, a question about the book's events rather than a settled reading.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: the Neighbours sent representatives to the Whorl; the godling is connected to the Neighbours

have I understood correctly, do you think, that the Neighbours sent representatives to the Whorl when it arrived in the Blue/Green system?
do we have any clues as to who these might be? I wonder if they could be connected with the (horrible) godling?

2001-02-03 michael braithwaite #

Answers Gerbil's question about whether the Neighbors sent representatives to the Whorl, citing Horn's statement that the Neighbors sent the inhumi before Windcloud and other Neighbors arrived. Argues that the godlings, controlled by Mainframe, likely came from embryos like Silk, Mucor and Pig, grounding this in the text's page reference.
answers · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Godlings came from embryos like Silk, Mucor and Pig
