Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2000-06-23 David Duffy #

Replies to Alex David Groce's question about whether any of Horn's dreams in On Blue's Waters have proved prophetic, offering a brief, playful interpretation that one dream was a favour from a good neighbour. Responds to the quoted post's remark that Horn's dreams seem irrelevant, with only one dream (the pit, possibly Silk's resurrection) noted.
answers · close-textual-reading

Horn says a tall man with a big nose or a spider: therefore that dream was a favour from a good neighbour.
