Themes: Names and their meanings

1999-07-25 Alice Turner #

Reports that a poster's answer on the meanings of character names in the Long Sun books: Rose has positive meanings tied to love and beauty, while Chenille and Orchid are not mentioned, though Orchis means belle. Responds to Mrs. B.'s earlier take on other Whorl characters, continuing Roy's thread.
informs · source-and-allusion

For those wondering about Mrs. B.'s take on other Whorl characters (yes, this probably ought to be in the other forum, but it's a continuation of Roy's thread), Rose has many meanings, all positive and having to do with love and beauty, Chenille is not mentioned, and neither is Orchid, though Orchis means "a belle," which certainly fits.

-alga
