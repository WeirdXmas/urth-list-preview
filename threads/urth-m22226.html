Urth list · 11 messages · 2012-04-17 to 2012-04-18

The thread: A lighthearted thread started by Fred Kiesche, who reported a dream about list members chanting Wolfe titles and fingering rosaries after reading the Best of Gene Wolfe volume before sleep. Marc Aramini and Jerry Friedman traded jokes about searching for each other's names and photos online, with Friedman noting a Severian costume photo would beat any illustration. Antonin Scriabin, António Pedro Marques, and Gerry Quinn added brief playful replies. No one offered a textual argument, and the thread stayed social and ended without any critical discussion.
  • Fred Kiesche: reported a whimsical dream of chanting Wolfe titles with rosaries, no argument
  • Marc Aramini: joked that searching list members' names online is sinister cyberstalking
  • Jerry Friedman: said looking up photos is not cyberstalking and shared his own photo
  • Antonin Scriabin: quipped the dream may just reflect what members really do

Themes: Humour in Wolfe, Ghosts, dreams and visions
