2010-07-02 Nicholas Jost #

Proposes that Abia, the powerful being Severian encounters, is Severian himself, and that Abia has fought Erebus before. Grounds this in Severian's remarks about the Storm God and the priest's correction, his linking of Abia with the God of the Lake, and Abia's single failed attempt to kill him through a child, which drives him toward the woman who later sways his trial.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Abia is Severian; Abia has fought Erebus before; Abia and the God of the Lake are linked; Giant women serve the squid god

Actually I like my Abia=Severiantheory the more I think about it. Erebus is evil, we know he enslaved the Ascians but we don't know about Abia. There's even some indications that he has fought Erebus in the past isn't there? Severian notes that the Storm God is evil, but the priest corrects him. Prior to the fight with Badlanders Severian ties the God of the Lake and Abia together and then notes that he has become like the God of the Lake (who's name I forget).

Tying this into my crack-pot "Guest" theory, the squid God is also served by Giant women, though they don't seem to be as pretty. Maybe it takes time to shape them. Abia only tries to kill Severian once and in doing so sends an incompetent child which drives him further into the arms of the one woman who is able to tip the balance at his trial...which saves him...
