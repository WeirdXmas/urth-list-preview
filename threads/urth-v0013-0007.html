beards and walkers

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-08 to 1998-06-08

1998urthAlice Turner

1998-06-08 Alice Turner #

Explains that a "beard" is slang for an opposite-sex escort who gives the appearance of a heterosexual couple, dating the term to the mid-1970s or possibly the late 1960s in New York. Corrects a slang dictionary's claim that a beard is a male escort for a woman, stating that a beard is usually a female escort for a man and the male equivalent is a walker.
informs

A "beard" in the sense of an opposite-sex escort to give the illusion of a heterosexual couple is a homo (and hetero, actually) sexual slang term that has been around for quite a while. The Dictionary of Contemporary Slang dates it to the mid-70s, but I think that, at least in New York City, it may go back to the late 60s. The DSC, to my surprise, tags it as a male escort (for a woman), but that's wrong. A beard is usually a female escort for a man; the male equivalent (for a woman, not necessarily a lesbian) is a walker.

-alga-
