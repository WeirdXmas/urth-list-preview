Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

1997-11-04 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Connects the fireflies of the thread title to the glowing five-pointed lights Severian first sees in the distance near Saltus, which turn out to be the man-apes. Ties this to a joke about a wand and a 'lightning bug' name for firefly, offering the image as a sudden personal recognition rather than an argued reading.

Nutria,

Glad you like the firefly--something just clicked in my head. I guess we or I (if there are such distinctions anymore) just reached critical mass--listen, this is kinda scarey--'member the mine at Saltus? Sure you do. 'Member the man-apes? Course you do. But at first ol' Severian couldn't tell what they was, just points of light off in the distance . . . five pointed stars . . . glowing . . . with phosphorescence . . . like man-size fireflies . . .

I have to go lie down now.
