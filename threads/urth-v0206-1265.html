2002-06-17 Michael Straight #

Proposes which bodies the gods of the Long Sun occupy, arguing Sphigx likely inhabits an enhanced embryo and questioning whether the godlings are bios or chems. Weighs Sand as an ideal chem host against drawbacks like missing animal pleasures, and suggests Mint is possessed rather than engineered. Responds to Josh Geller's list of host bodies, citing Horn's quotation of Silk about Kypris's influence on Mint.
proposes · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft · theories: Sphigx inhabits an enhanced embryo; Mint is possessed rather than engineered; Councilor Lemur's fate shows bios failing as chems

Sphigx? I see her in an embryo enhanced for physical strength (Chenille, maybe), if not a godling, but, are the godlings bios or chems? I'd think a nuclear-powered, moisture-eating, super-strong, near-immortal chem like Sand would be the ideal host if there weren't some other inherent drawbacks. Maybe they'd miss animal pleasures like sex and eating. Or maybe Councilor Lemur's fate speaks to the problem of bios trying to be chems.

I don't think Mint is an enhanced embryo. Rather, posession gave her an extra boost of bravery, charisma, and leadership skills (Horn quotes Silk as attributing Mint's "warlike nature" to the influence of Kypris).

I think that's probably right -- although I wonder how much of Mucor's spookiness comes from Blood not having the technology to do the embryo right and/or treating her more like an animal than a person.

-Rostrum
