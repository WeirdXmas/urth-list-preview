Another twist

Themes: Time loops and causality

2002-03-25 Jeff Snader #

Proposes that Typhon knew the New Sun was coming and that the Whorl was meant as an ark to save humanity and Urth's creatures, to be returned to Urth later. Grounds the idea in the time travel shown by the Green Man in The Book of the New Sun, and presents it as a new spin on the Urth/Blue discussion.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Typhon knew the New Sun was coming; The Whorl was an ark to return humanity to Urth

Pardon if this has been discussed before, I was away from the list for awhile. There has been quite a bit of discussion on the Urth/Blue topic, I have another spin to put on it.

In tbotns there are several instances of someone traveling the corridors of time (ie: the green man). Doesn't it seem plausible that Typhon new the New Sun was coming, and when. The whorl may have never been intended to take humanity to another galaxy, but to serve as an "Ark" to save humanity and the creatures of Urth, and return them back to Urth when things were safe.

Jeff
