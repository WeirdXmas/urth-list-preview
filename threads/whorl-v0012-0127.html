2001-02-05 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Announces an official list policy allowing discussion of Return to the Whorl, the poster noting he has not yet read it, and asks members to warn of spoilers in subject lines. Also gives reminders about avoiding HTML posts and accidentally quoting whole digests, a moderator announcement rather than a reading.
informs

I shall lay down the law:

Go ahead and discuss Return to the Whorl. I haven't read it yet, but it's only three down in the pile.

Don't put in spoiler space but please do put a warning in the subject of your message if there's anything that I
wouldn't want to see yet.

Some reminders: please don't send HTML messages to the Urth and Whorl lists, and when you reply to a message, check that you didn't accidentally quote the entire digest in your
reply.

And you there -- spit out that gum!

-- ranjit
