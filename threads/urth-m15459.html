2010-11-10 Son of Witz replying to Gwern Branwen #

Recalls seeing the band Lightning Bolt, led by the cartoonist Brian Chippendale, at a Berkeley punk club around 2002, and praises their noisy live set. Responds to a quoted Chippendale remark about Gene Wolfe by mentioning only the poster's musical experience and admitting they have never read his comics. Off-topic social chat with no discussion of Wolfe's fiction.
chats

I've seen this Chippendale guy's band Lightning Bolt once. What a hoot. They played in the audience and a wet futon matress was being tossed around everyones' heads. This was at Berkeley's Gilman, a classic punk dive club, maybe 2002 or so. Killer set. We were rocking out, and this teenager with foot high orange spiked hair said to me and my buddy, "It's good to see old dudes rocking out." which cracked me up cuz I wasn't even thirty yet. I've never read his comix, but I love his cover on Lightning Bolt's Wonderful Rainbow. Super fun lo fi noisy math rock. ~Witz
