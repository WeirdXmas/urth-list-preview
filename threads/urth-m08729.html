Limited edition of *The Best of Gene Wolfe*

Urth list · 1 messages · 2009-03-10 to 2009-03-10

2009urthNicholas Gevers

2009-03-10 Nicholas Gevers #

Announces a limited signed edition of The Best of Gene Wolfe from PS Publishing, titled The Very Best of Gene Wolfe, adding the novelette Christmas Inn and an introduction by Kim Stanley Robinson. Gives the publisher's website for pre-ordering, with no interpretive argument and no reply to anyone.
informs

I thought I should mention that in early June, PS Publishing will be issuing a limited edition of *The Best of Gene Wolfe*. Titled *The Very Best of Gene Wolfe*, it adds to the Tor edition an extra, hard-to-find novelette titled "Christmas Inn", an Introduction by Kim Stanley Robinson, and a rather magnificent cover by J.K Potter. And it's signed! Copies can be pre-ordered from the PS website, www.pspublishing.co.uk .

--Nick Gevers.
