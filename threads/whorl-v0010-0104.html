1999-10-12 Alice Turner #

Supports earlier glosses by backing up the claim that the Long Sun words "Dorp" and "Wijzer" come from Dutch and African sources, and that "Chota" means small in Hindi. Grounds the Hindi claim in the Kipling usage of "chota peg" and the poster's own time in Bombay, while admitting limited knowledge of pidgin.
supports · source-and-allusion

Wow, we're going at warp speed here, aren't we? It's the first time we've ever been able to race along together, and I must say it's a thrill.

Anyway, I want to back up "Dorp" as Dutch/Africans (sp?) and Wijzer probably too. Also, Chota definitely does mean "small" in Hindi--you may remember from your Kipling that a "chota peg" was a small whiskey--and I think we can trust the other terms (I remind you that I lived in Bombay for a while, but I never got beyond pidgin so can't be defiite).

-alga
