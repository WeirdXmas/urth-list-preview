2008-04-13 Gwern Branwen replying to Steven Hall #

Replies to Steven Hall's suggestion that Wolfe might have read Juan Rulfo's Pedro Paramo, noting that Peace appeared in 1975 while an abridged English translation of Rulfo's novel came out in 1959. Argues the influence is still unlikely because Rulfo was obscure for so long and the early translation was undistinguished, citing a New York Times article.

On 2008.04.12 18:24:00 -0500, Steven Hall <[email]> scribbled 2.2K characters:

It's at least chronologically possible. _Peace_ was 1975, and apparently the abridged English translation published by Grove Press appeared in 1959 (<http://query.nytimes.com/gst/fullpage.html?res=990CEFD71030F935A3575BC0A963958260>). But it strikes me as unlikely, because it was obscure for so long (and still isn't as famous as, say, _100 Years of Solitude_), and the 1959 translation is described as undistinguished.
