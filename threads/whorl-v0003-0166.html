1997whorlThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

1997-02-22 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists published critical articles, books and a bibliography on Gene Wolfe's fiction, including Andre-Driussi's essays, Locey's piece on Wolfe's Catholicism, Feeley's on family romance in The Book of the New Sun, and Wright's God-Games articles. Offers no argument itself, only bibliographic references and ordering information for a 1990s reference work.
informs
