Identifies the author Damien Broderick was thinking of as Jean Mark Gawron and names his book Dreams of Glass, then lists Gawron's other novels and suggests Ian Watson's The Embedding as another linguistics-derived book. Offers no Wolfe analysis; the identification rests on the poster's own recollection of his novels and of Watson's book.
answers

I assume you mean Jean Mark Gawron, and the book is, I think, _Dreams of Glass_. (I haven't read it, but I have read his first novel, _An Apology for Rain_, which was OK, and his second, _Algorithm_, which was godawful and practically unfinishable.)

Another linguistics-derived book is _The Embedding_ by Ian Watson, which involved raising kids speaking an invented language in the Amazon. Seems to have been somewhat Whorfian in outlook.

-- Rich Horton (Tusk)
