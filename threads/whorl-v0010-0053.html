Buried Alive--the secret?

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-10-04 to 1999-10-04

1999whorlThe Book of the Short SunPatrick O'Leary

1999-10-04 Patrick O'Leary #

Disputes Alga's claim that Krait's secret is how to fake killing an inhumu, arguing instead that Horn used the burial punishment as a distraction to escape and turn a battle. Grounds this on the idea that the buried-alive episode mirrors Horn's own pit ordeal with Krait, and that OBW lacks enough evidence to settle the secret.
disputes · close-textual-reading · allegory-and-typology · theories: Krait's secret is how to advance without help; Horn used burial punishment as a distraction

Thanks to Alga & Mantis for their input--always a pleasure...but...

<< The secret he has learned from Krait is how *not* to kill an inhumu while giving the
appearance of doing so. >>

I'm going to have to disagree with the learned Alga.

Doesn't Horn say the The Secret was one that could destroy the Inhumi? Pretty sure he does, though I can't find the reference:(

Obviously, burying the suckers alive doesn't destroy them.

Isn't it more likely that Horn simply used the ignorant and ineffective cultural capital punishment of his Kingdom
to his advantage when he felt his "adopted" country threatened? This serves two purposes: provides a distraction while he escapes and turns the tide of the battle.

advance, without
help? That is why I think this knowledge (and possibly advice too) is Krait's secret.<<

Isn't it more likely that this "buried alive and the captor bargaining for an oath of loyalty" perfectly mirrors
Horn's horrible experience in the pit with Krait? It worked then. He just reversed the predicament.
Sweet justice.

I suspect that, after OBW, we don't yet have enough evidence to deduct the Secret.

best,

Patrick O'leary
