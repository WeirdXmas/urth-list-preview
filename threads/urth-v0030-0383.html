Spoilers for copperhead

Urth list · 5 messages · 2001-05-07 to 2001-05-08

2001urthPaul C DugganJohn-Paul WellcoatAlex David Groce
The thread: Paul C Duggan asked for help decoding dialect words "groose" and "grawnt" in Copperhead, reading them as a garbled line about a president, a Cain and Abel allusion, and puzzling over barking trees and winding ram's horns. Alex David Groce answered that the odd details signal a strange cognate of our reality, tied to John 8:32, offering only a brief remark. John-Paul Wellcoat proposed that the dialect speaker is a childlike Eve or serpent scorning the President, with the Cain and Abel lines tied to original sin, and also asked about the trees and horns. The thread ends without resolution, with the barking trees and ram's horns still unexplained.
  • Paul C Duggan: dialect words are garbled grouse/grunt-like lines about a president and Cain and Abel, with uncertain guesses
  • Alex David Groce: barking trees and ram's horns signal a strange cognate of our reality, per John 8:32
  • John-Paul Wellcoat: the speaker is a childlike Eve or serpent, the President is Adam in the other realm, linked to original sin

Themes: Myth and folklore sources, Names and their meanings, Gods, angels and the Increate

2001-05-08 John-Paul Wellcoat #

Offers a reading of the dialect lines in Copperhead, arguing the speaker is a childlike Eve or serpent figure who scorns the President, and suggests the Cain and Abel lines fit after the original sin. Responds to a earlier post's puzzle over the dialect by providing a translation and asking about the barking trees and ram's horns.
proposes · puzzle-solving · allegory-and-typology · theories: Copperhead is an incarnation of Eve and the serpent; President becomes Adam in the other realm; Cain and Abel reference after original sin

I reckon:

i Pretty much agree with this.
She no longer likes him, and is saying he will not get the opportunity to copulate with her again.

The impression is she has a very child like mentality and is trying to hurt the president
before he relegates her back to her previous existence (or another)

Have no idea what this is in reference to. I like the idea of Caine and Abel, but there is nothing to corroberate this idea.

Based on the opinion that she is an incarnation of Eve and the serpent , I was of the opinion that the President became Adam in the other realm.
If Caine and Abel is right then this is after the original sin, and Adam or (God) has just found Eve or the serpent guilty of yet another act of sin.
