Argues that the preacher in Latro's sermon deliberately reinterpreted the camel-and-needle parable to tell wealthy listeners they can still reach heaven, and that the sermon's main aim was political. Grounds this in a fellow poster's report that the sermons urged voting Republican, and quotes a politician's speech about the parable, which the post treats as the target being mocked.
