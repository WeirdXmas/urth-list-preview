Mucor and the Three Fish

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-01-20 to 2000-01-20

2000whorlThe Book of the Long SunE Dorian

2000-01-20 E Dorian #

Proposes that Mucor, the mind-controlling creature in On Blue's Waters, took control of the fish so they leapt onto a flat rock for Horn to catch, then released them. Responds to William H. Ansley's question about how the fish got onto the rock on page 81, offering a reading of the passage rather than citing other evidence.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Mucor took control of the fish to put them on the rock

Hello Everybody,

My name is Darryl and I have never before posted a message to the Whorl list. I am two months behind in reading the Whorl posts, so please bear with me.

When I read this part in OBW I just assumed that Mucor sort of 'took control' of the fish, jumped them onto the rock, and then 'withdrew control' when Horn retrieved them.

Please tell me what you think of this idea.
