Guns ad nauseum

Urth list · 8 messages · 2009-03-18 to 2009-03-19

2009urthThe Book of the New SunJeff WilsonDavid StockhoffJames WynnJosh Geller
The thread: Posters on this off-topic spin-off from a gun politics thread argued whether keeping firearms deters or attracts burglary. David Stockhoff argued that guns are often stolen, even from locked collections, and that gun owners may be likelier targets. Jeff Wilson countered that a gun stolen while the owner is away is a successful deterrent, since it avoids a confrontation at home. Brunians stressed that city residents must protect property and that good safes are affordable, while James Wynn mocked the reasoning with a sarcastic gun-free-zone sign proposal. The exchange ended without agreement, with Stockhoff still insisting that theft of valuables is common regardless of guns and the discussion going nowhere.
  • David Stockhoff: guns are often stolen and gun-keeping homes may attract burglars
  • Jeff Wilson: a gun stolen while the owner is away counts as successful deterrence
  • brunians: city residents must protect property, and a bolted gun safe is affordable
  • James Wynn: sarcastically mocks the claim that guns attract robbers

Argues that guns stolen while an owner is away count as successful deterrence, since that beats a break-in with the owner at home. Responds to David Stockhoff's account of a neighbor's gun stolen through a wall, conceding only if the wish to steal the gun is shown to have prompted the break-in.
