2001-02-27 Alice Turner #

Argues that being a Christian is not required to appreciate Wolfe's Christian references, calling Christianity a favorite hobby, and disputes that Seawrack loved Horn, agreeing with Endy's reading. Responds to Blattid's question whether anyone on the list is Christian, comparing hobbyist readers to Borges and Dante's admirers; also criticizes the hasty, Star Trek-like ending of The Book of the Long Sun.
disputes · theology-and-religion · theories: Seawrack was not in love with Horn

One can be a hobbyist. Like me. I don't believe a word of either testament, but Christianity is my favorite hobby and I'll bet I can out-reference the whole damn barroom. (I think Borges was a hobbyist too, fwiw.)

Oh, on another subject, I don't believe Seawrack was "in love with" Horn either--Endy seems right on the money. I continue to be unpleasantly surprised at the seeming sketchiness and hastiness of an ending that bundles everyone (a bunch of near-strangers) off together on a Star Trek mission.

-alga
