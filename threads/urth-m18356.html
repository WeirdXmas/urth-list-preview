The thread: Posters shared a new Wolfe interview and mostly traded light, joking remarks rather than critical argument. Greg Bates praised the interview and quoted Wolfe's line about Skip and Chelle's father, which he found funny, while also saying he was pondering Home Fires. Craig Brewer joked about misreading the interview through gut reactions and private clues, and Gerry Quinn quipped that the link had been posted several times and asked if the list was repeating itself. The thread ended without substantive disagreement, drifting into self-mocking banter.

2011-01-19 Greg Bates replying to Gerry Quinn #

Says the poster misread a recent Wolfe novel, liking it more than An Evil Guest but less than The Sorcerer's House, and finds its middle bogged down in long repeated conversations and its ending tacked on. Replies to Gerry Quinn's list joke, and plans to reread after clarifying discussion from the board.
chats · genre-and-craft

Oh, i'm absolutely certain i've completely misunderstood it. Partially because I didn't feel there was that much to misunderstand, which is always a sign with Wolfe that I haven't paid enough attention.

For what it's worth, I liked it better than AEG but probably not as much as TSH. I feel like it started out really strongly, but gets completely bogged down halfway through (the action starts to disappear and get replaced with extremely long conversations where it feels like Skip is just explaining the same things to different people). The ending also felt very left-field and tacked on, which of course means I missed something. After I finish a couple other books I got with my Kindle gift card i'll revisit it (hopefully with a bit of clarifying discussion from this board.)
