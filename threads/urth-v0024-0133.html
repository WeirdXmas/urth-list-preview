Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1999-04-02 Roy C. Lackey #

Asks three questions about The Book of the New Sun: why Severian lied or forgot when telling Purn that Hethor never named his ships, what sin made small Tzadkiel exiled from her larger self, and who the weeping woman in the Secret House loggia is. Gives chapter references for each passage so readers can locate the scenes in the text.
questions · close-textual-reading

I just finished re-reading the _New Sun_ quintet and have a few questions I haven't found answers for in the text. Perhaps someone will enlighten me.

1) Why did Sev lie (or did he forget?) to Purn when he told him that Hethor had not mentioned the name of the ship(s) he had sailed on? Ch. IV, _Urth_

2) What was small Tzadkiel's sin (Ch. XL, _Urth_) that caused her to be exiled long ago from her larger self? She doesn't seem to know.

3) Who was the beautiful, weeping woman Sev saw in the loggia of the Secret House, being led away by six Praetorians? Ch. XLI, _Urth_ This was right after resurrecting the dead assassin and right before emerging behind the throne. He can't recall who she is.

Roy
