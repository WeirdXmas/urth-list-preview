Peter Wright's - Shadows of the New Sun

2007-07-06 Nigel Price #

Recommends Peter Wright's book Shadows of the New Sun as a compendium of Wolfe interviews and mentions buying it through Ultan's Library for a commission. Asks whether the site's secondary-source lists could be useful to the wiki team.
informs

I got my copy of Peter's book from Amazon UK a couple of weeks ago.

It's a wonderful compendium of Wolfe interviews, many of which I'd read, but it's great to have them bound into a book.

Essential reading - and if you order it through Ultan's Library at http://www.ultan.org.uk/index.html we get a small commission from Amazon towards the upkeep of the site.

Which reminds me: is there any information in Ultan's Library, such as the lists of secondary sources on Wolfe, which would be of interest to the wiki team?

Nigel
