Typhon & the Whorl (was Prior Race On Blue And Green)

Urth list · 6 messages · 2007-12-17 to 2007-12-20

2007urthThe Book of the New SunThe Book of the Long SunGwern BranwenDan'l Danehy-OakesthalassocratDan
The thread: Dan and gwern0 debated when and why Typhon launched the Whorl and whether he hijacked it. Dan argued Typhon copied himself into the ship's mainframe to hijack it and return to recolonize Earth, placing the hijack late in his decline after the solar cooling disaster, and pointing to the one-headed mountain monument versus two-headed Pas depictions as evidence the Pas pantheon was not in the original design. gwern0 disputed this, holding that a costly generation ship would be a hubristic project of Typhon's prime rather than something begun amid collapse, and argued from the era's fleet that slower-than-light travel made sense. The exchange ended with the two positions still opposed and no sign of agreement.
  • Dan: Typhon hijacked the Whorl late in his decline to return to Earth and restore his empire
  • gwern0: the Whorl was launched in Typhon's prime as a hubristic project, not amid collapse
  • Dan: the one-headed monument versus two-headed Pas depictions shows the cult was not in the original design
  • gwern0: slower-than-light travel makes sense given the era and fleet described in the books

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Power, rulers and politics, Time loops and causality

2007-12-17 Dan #

Argues that Typhon copied himself into the Whorl's mainframe to hijack the ship, planning to return to Earth, recolonize it with worshipping peons, and restore his empire. Grounds this in Typhon's arrogance during his talk with Severian, his drives for survival and power, and the idea that the Whorl was not built as his personal vessel.
proposes · myth-and-structure · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon copied himself into the Whorl mainframe; Typhon planned return to Earth and recolonization; Typhon became godhead of the Whorl religion

I agree that Typhon would never do anything out of altruism. I'm not sure what you mean by "a world he already solidly ruled." Typhon was abandoned and besieged and was awaiting the arrival of the Conciliator to free him of Piaton's stubborn hold on his involuntary functions (which he did and he did, lol). Remember during his brief discussion with Severian, Typhon is absolutely arrogant. This and personal survival are his twin drives for injecting a copy of himself into the Whorl project and hijacking it. Being STL, no one saw it's utility in escaping the global cooling "disaster" that had befallen the Earth. I don't think it was built from the ground up (or shiprock in) as a personal vessel for Typhon's digital self and its amusement. The time frame is too long and his position too tenuous for him to oversee it's completion. Typhon simply copied himself into the mainframe and created it's religion or inserted himself in it as it's Godhead and his treacherous family as pantheon of lesser gods.

Whatever the original design, I think Typhon wanted the Whorl to return to Earth after things calmed down after a few centuries to "recolonize" using technical experts released from suspended animation to start fixing things, millions of peons that worshipped him as a god to provide the labor and armies of chems to reestablish order. Then they could either revive the original Typhon or if he was lost Pas could download into a suitable body and reestablish his empire and glory.

Evidently something went wrong with his plan when he was deposed by his children and the inhumani hijacked the ship and redirected it to Green/Blue.

Dan

2007-12-18 Dan replying to Dan #

Argues that Typhon hijacked the Whorl late in his decline, after its construction was nearly complete, and that the Pas cult and its digital pantheon were not part of the ship's original design. Supports this with the one-headed mountain monument versus the two-headed Pas depictions, the timing of his decline after the solar cooling disaster, and the damage the pantheon's infighting caused aboard ship.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon hijacked the Whorl near completion; Pas pantheon was not in original design; Two-headed Typhon shows post-transplant appearance

Three things: the fact that his mountain monument depicts Typhon with one head ("home" and his seat of power built during his prime) while two headed Pas depicts Typhon as he appears after the life extension transplant. I'd say most rulers are only concerned with their legacy toward the end of their reign. Wanting to extend his life shows that's exactly where Typhon was at. If the Whorl was built or started at the height of his power, I'm sure he would have scanned himself and downloaded into Mainframe immediately in case he was assassinated which would subvert the whole point of the project. Thus he would have been mono-headed.

The solar/global cooling disaster ushered in Typhon's decline by severing him from his interstellar empire when his appointees absconded with his FTL ships (but since his doctors came from other worlds, we know he was grafted before this). Due to this the STL colony ship was his only way out but without the Conciliator there to heal him there was really no point in him going anywhere. So he opted instead just to send a digital copy of himself. The way Typhon recounts the events it seems his decline was rapid (within a few years). This may have been a large enough window to start and complete such a project, but I think it was already complete or nearing completion. Typhon may have had little or no interest in it at all until his decline. That's why I think he hijacked the Whorl when it was nearing completion or perhaps after it was already enroute.

The Pas cult always seemed forcibly kludged over the two distinct cultures we see in BotLS. It was the instigator of a great deal of death, destruction, chaos and waste amongst the cargo and caused a great deal of damage to the ship itself due to the pantheon's digital familial infighting. Hardly things you'd want on a generational colony ship. This indicates to me that this pantheon wasn't part of the Whorls original design and smacks of Typhon's influence after the fact.

Dan

Argues that the Whorl was launched during Typhon's prime rather than during his decline, since a costly interstellar generation ship is a hubristic project, not one begun amid collapse. Responds to Dan's objection that a slower-than-light ship makes no sense when faster-than-light travel existed, by reasoning from Typhon's era and the fleet described in the books.

OK, here's another sticking point for me. We both agree Typhon's decline was swift, and so it's unlikely the Whorl was constructed during or after the period where he was stranded on Urth.

It somewhat follows from that that the whorl was not originally intended to be an escape vessel for some form of Typhon. But the Whorl is a STL ship!
