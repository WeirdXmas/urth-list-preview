1997-10-31 William H. Graham III #

Reports that the poster's planned fanzine reprinting the Wonders of Urth and Sky stories fell through, but he keeps a letter from Wolfe and correspondence from Ms. Kidd as collectibles. Asks others to remind him of Wolfe's upcoming Baltimore engagement and adds a note about Easton Press having copies of Shadow.
informs

I while ago I contacted Ms. Kidd and agreed to buy reprint rights for the <<Wonders of Urth and Sky>> stories ("The Boy Who Hooked the Sun" et al) for the purposes of printing a fanzine.

Now that project has gone belly-up as my would-be-financial-backer went and did something silly like buy a house, but out of all that, I have a very nice letter from Mr Wolfe regarding my request for introductions to the stories.

The fountain pen cranberry-inked signature is striking. If anyone could ever be excited about something linked with a failure, this is it for me!

Incidentally, my brief correspondance with Ms. Kidd gave me to believe that she is one of the classiest people I've met. Her letters, as well as Mr. Wolfe's, are the crown of my collection.

Could someone please tell me again of Mr. Wolfe's engagement in Baltimore next year?

-WHG3

PS Easton Press had 8 copies of "Shadow" as of Thursday. Get em while they're hot!
