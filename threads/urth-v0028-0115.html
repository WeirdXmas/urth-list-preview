new academic piece on Wolfe

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-09-16 to 1999-09-16

1999urthJonathan Laidlow

1999-09-16 Jonathan Laidlow #

Informs the list that John Gerlach's essay on rhetoric, gods and chems in The Book of the Long Sun appeared in Extrapolation and can be bought by pay-per-view download. Notes the essay's footnotes credit the list and says the poster will add it to the Ultan's Library bibliography, while admitting he has not yet read it.
informs

Just noticed that the Summer 1999 issue of Extrapolation (40:2) contains a piece by John Gerlach entitled

'The rhetoric of an impossible object: gods, chems, and science fantasy in Gene Wolfe's 'Book of the Long Sun'.

Not having easy access to this journal I downloaded it from the website of www.northernlight.com. This is a service that provides pay-per-view access to news, business, and scholarly articles. I downloaded the piece for 2.95 dollars. Note that it looked as though Mantis' Posthistory 101 piece is also available for paid download, for those who haven't got access to 'Extrapolation'.

I must admit to being very busy so I haven't read it yet. I noted that the footnotes credit our little community. Are you out there John Gerlach?

Will be adding this piece to the Ultan's Library bibliography as soon as possible.

Jonathan
