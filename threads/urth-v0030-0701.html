Wolfe TV?

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-09-27 to 2001-09-27

2001urthRoy C. Lackey

2001-09-27 Roy C. Lackey #

Asks whether other list members remember a Tomorrow Show TV episode featuring Gene Wolfe alongside Harlan Ellison and Isaac Asimov, and whether anyone has a tape or transcript. Gives vague recollections of the mid-1980s broadcast and its syndication and cable reruns as the basis for the request.
questions

Is there anyone here besides me who remembers Gene Wolfe appearing on Tom Snyder's old TV talk show, the "Tomorrow Show", years ago? My memory of it is hazy, and the older I get the more vague my sense of time becomes. I know Harlan Ellison was on it with Wolfe (it turns out so was Isaac Asimov; I thought there was a third writer, but couldn't remember who). It was probably on during the mid 1980s. The "Tomorrow Show" was in syndication then, I believe, and that episode was shown on cable for some years afterward. Anyone have a videotape of the show, or transcript, or know how to get one?

-Roy
