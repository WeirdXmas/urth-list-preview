Themes: Death and resurrection, Hidden identities and parentage

1998-03-29 adam louis stephanides #

Asks whether a passage in The Book of the New Sun or The Urth of the New Sun says that Thecla is wholly resurrected within Severian, not merely confused fragments of her personality in those who ate her. Responds to mantis's theory that the Claw resurrected Thecla inside Severian, and says the poster could not locate the passage on a quick search.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: The Claw resurrected Thecla within Severian

It has been several years since I read _Book_ and _Urth_, but I seem to recall a passage in one of these in which Severian is told that this is precisely what has happened, that the others who consumed Thecla had only confused fragments of her personality within them. I wasn't able to find the passage again on a quick search, but I'm pretty sure I haven't imagined it. Anybody?

--Adam
