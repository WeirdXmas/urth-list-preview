Themes: Books, libraries and writing

1997-10-31 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Announces Wolfe as guest of honor at Disclave '98 and asks whether a fan's list of Wolfe stories included "The Old Woman Whose Rolling Pin Is the Sun" or left it outside the brown book. Also describes the poster's plan for a faux "real" brown book artifact, with planned collections and new stories, in reply to a prior thread post.

WHG3,

Wolfe to be GoH at DISCLAVE '98, May 22-25, 1998. Membership $20 to 12/31/97; checks to Disclave 1998. Info: Disclave '98, c/o Joe Mayhew, 7-S Research Rd., Greenbelt MD 20770; e-mail: Washington SF Assoc., [email] or [email].

Re: WONDERS OF URTH AND SKY, (another fine dream) did you include among your list of stories "The Old Woman Whose Rolling Pin Is the Sun" or do you consider it outside the brown book? Just curious!

(My plans for doing it: I did include TOWWRPITS on the list; I didn't consider getting Gene Wolfe to write intros since I wanted to create an artifact as close to the "real" brown book as possible . . . including new stories by Wolfe based upon extant fragments . . . and the artwork mentioned, of course. Water stained pages from the Mine at Saltus? "Handwritten" entries by Severian in the back pages, the story-telling competition stories? Sure, why not! But only after the relatively easy task of a hardcover THE ISLAND OF DOCTOR DEATH . . . and then one or two new collections of uncollected short stories: TALK OF MANDRAKES and REMEMBRANCE OF THINGS PROUST . . .)

=mantis=
