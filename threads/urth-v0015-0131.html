Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-07-06 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the 1990s "missing neutrinos" scientific belief, linking a possibly dying Sun to a fading solar fusion source, was consciously used by Wolfe as a science retrofit in the Urth Cycle. Responds to Damien Broderick by citing Clarke's The Songs of Distant Earth as a parallel dying-sun story and noting recent news that neutrinos have mass.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Wolfe's dying sun uses the missing neutrinos science

Damien Broderick,

I'm glad you mentioned the "missing neutrinos" belief! This led to scientific scenarios ranging from a black hole in the Sun (as the ultimate source of the fusion) to a "dying sun"; and the science fiction seems to have moved accordingly--Clarke's SONGS OF DISTANT EARTH has the Solar System wiped out by a dying sun traceable to the missing neutrinos, IIRC. I think it is consciously used by Wolfe in the Urth Cycle--a clever "science retro-fit" of Zothique/The Dying Earth, using off-the-shelf items.

And recent news, "Neutrinos have mass!" (Picture them in their itty bitty little pews.) Science marches on!

=mantis=
