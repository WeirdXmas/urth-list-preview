1998-05-14 Alice Turner #

Casts actors in imagined film roles for characters in The Book of the New Sun, suggesting Alec Guinness as Malrubius, Anthony Quinn as Gurloes, Max von Sydow as Master Ash, and Jeremy Irons as Vodalus. Offers no textual evidence, justifying choices only by appearance and costume, and disagrees with Wolfe's described beard for Ash.
