Neat Little Chart

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-02-21 to 2001-02-21

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunHenry Kaiser

Themes: Unreliable narration

2001-02-21 Henry Kaiser #

Proposes a four-row chart for The Book of the Long Sun, listing a chronology of events, the narrator of each event, whether the protagonist is Horn or Silk, and Silk's location at each time point. Asks whether anything useful is missing and how to place Urth events in sequence, noting he has been mulling over the chart in his imagination.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic

I'd like to see a neat little chart
But I'm not meticulous enough to construct it.
Someone here could certainly do it.

4 rows to the the chart

row 1 = a chronology of the events of the narrative in chronological sequence*
row 2 = nominates the narrator is of each of event
row 3 = status of protagonist: Horn Silk-Horn Silk
row 4 = where Silk is at this time point (multiple places - but specific)

Did I miss anything useful to note?

* = (but when to put Urth events in sequence?)

Anyway - I've been finding it useful to think about lots of folks questions and comments relating to the flawed version of this chart in my imagination.

-Henry Kaiser
