Urth list · 9 messages · 2009-12-23 to 2010-01-23

20092010urthThe Book of the New SunAllan Andersoncarr lawJerry FriedmanGreg BatesChristopher HettingerStuart HammJeff WilsonDave Tallman
The thread: Christopher Hettinger shared a late-1980s or early-1990s music video loosely inspired by The Book of the New Sun and asked whether it made a film adaptation seem possible. Dave Tallman listed six departures from the book, from torturers' shirts to Severian's hair and beard, and Jerry Friedman and Jeff Wilson then debated whether Severian is shown clean-shaven and which actor resembles his unmasked face. Carr Law and Stuart Hamm mostly traded light replies and a fan-favourite video, and Jerry Friedman joked about casting. The thread drifted into casting jokes and a later unrelated band post rather than reaching any conclusion about the books.
  • Christopher Hettinger: video is a funny, loosely inspired oddity that makes a film seem possible
  • Dave Tallman: video departs from the book in six specific ways
  • Jerry Friedman: the video's errors include Severian's appearance and masks should cover torturers and Gurloes
  • Jeff Wilson: asks which actor best resembles Severian's unmasked face
  • carr law: the video is hilarious and not to be read seriously
  • Greg Bates: shares his own Wolfe-inspired music without analysing the books

Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Books, libraries and writing, Humour in Wolfe

2009-12-24 Dave Tallman replying to Christopher Hettinger #

Lists six ways a music video for "Terminus Est" departs from the book, including torturers wearing shirts, a pointed blade, a blue glass ornament standing in for the Claw, Severian's hair colour and beard, Thecla's barred cell, and how Severian begins his exile. Replies to Christopher Hettinger's post about the video, offering a list of errors rather than an argument about deeper meaning.
disputes · close-textual-reading

How many details can one video possibly get wrong?
1) Severian and other torturers wearing shirts.
2) Sharp point at the end of Terminus Est.
3) The Claw represented by some sort of blue glass wind-chime ornament. 4) Severian with brown hair instead of black, and not clean-shaven. 5) Thecla's cell should have a door, not bars.
6) Severian fetched from in front of the tomb to begin his exile, instead of from a cell.
