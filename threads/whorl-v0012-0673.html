Secrets & Denial

2001-04-20 Dave Lebling #

Argues that if the inhumi reach Blue by astral travel, human-inhumi would appear there as humans, and that astral travel makes them vulnerable because their bodies are comatose. Supports Patrick O'Leary's account of Silk's denial as the engine of offscreen events in TBSS, while finding no textual evidence for James Jordan's teleportation claim.
supports · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Inhumi reach Blue by astral travel; Inhumi reach Blue by teleportation; Silk's denial drives offscreen events in Short Sun

If the inhumi reach Blue by astral travel, wouldn't the human-inhumi appear on Blue as humans, as Jahlee does on Urth? However, as a version of the secret that provides an offensive weapon, astral travel is very good, for those who are travelling are comatose. If you find their bodies, you can kill them easily. This isn't a "weapon too heavy to wield," though.

This solves my objection above (i.e., they aren't travelling astrally, they are teleporting), except that I see no evidence whatsoever for it in the book.

Thank you Patrick! A post which for me captures the strengths of the series, and probably gets very close to Wolfe's intent.

So many things in Wolfe's novels happen offscreen, and we learn about them only through allusions elsewhere, or must infer them ourselves. Capturing the importance of Silk's denial as the engine behind this in _Short Sun_ is excellent.

Dave Lebling
aka vizcacha
