Wolfe and Wright book delays

Urth list · 4 messages · 2002-02-07 to 2002-02-12

2002urthNicholas GeversJohn BarachJames Jordan
The thread: A newcomer, John Barach, asked about the forthcoming Liverpool University Press volumes on Wolfe, namely Peter Wright's Attending Daedalus and Shadows of the New Sun. Nicholas Gevers replied that neither has a set publication date, with release expected in late 2002 or 2003, and explained that Shadows is a new essay collection apparently edited by Wright and probably reusing material from earlier books. James Jordan then expressed a hope that the collection would also gather all the New Sun short stories. The thread ended without debate, mostly as an exchange of publication news.

  • James Jordan: hopes Shadows will also collect all New Sun short stories

2002-02-07 Nicholas Gevers #

Explains to newcomer John Barach that Shadows of the New Sun is a new Wolfe essay collection apparently edited by Peter Wright, and that Attending Daedalus is Wright's critical study. Reports that Shadows presumably reuses material from The Castle of the Otter and Castle of Days, and that Wolfe himself is vague about the new book.
informs

John: Briefly, SHADOWS OF THE NEW SUN is a new
collection of Wolfe's essays, apparently edited by
Peter Wright, and ATTENDING DAEDALUS is Wright's own
critical study of Wolfe's work. SHADOWS presumably
includes some of the same material as THE CASTLE OF
THE OTTER/CASTLE OF DAYS; Gene himself is vague about
the new book, so presumably it is Wright's
compilation, Wright's initiative.

--Nick Gevers.
