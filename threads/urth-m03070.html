2006-09-05 Chris Mulder #

Proposes compiling a deeper second glossary or reference work for Wolfe's New Sun, Long Sun and Short Sun books and related stories, going beyond Lexicon Urthus, and offers to help fund and contribute. Asks about annual cost, contributor interest, and copyright concerns, and thanks list members for their varied perspectives.
proposes

Greetings,

I am an avid Wolfe reader, and am just beginning RttW, so no spoilers yet for me! Anyway, I would be interested in helping to compile a glossary/reference work or help in any way on a close read. I recently purchased Lexicon Urthus off ebay and while it is quite good, I was hoping a secord try might run a bit deeper (without turning into the OED) and span New Sun, Long Sun and Short Sun, plus related shorts.

Obviously, it is a big job, and won't be completed anytime soon, but it could be made much easier by numbers of readers, enthusiasm, and the web. I would be willing to help fund it, as well. What I would be interested to know is what an annual cost might be, plus interest level of contributors. Also, I wouldn't want to run afoul of other copyright holders.

By the way, I have been following everyone's comments for months, and have even fought through many of the old posts back to the beginning. Reading different perspectives has really helped me come to enjoy the whole much more deeply. It's just amazing what each individual hits upon as being important, while others (usually me) miss it entirely. Thanks to all of you for that.

cm

2006-09-06 Jon Capps replying to Chris Mulder #

Explains that the poster could host a wiki for free and estimates the only real cost, a domain name at about ten dollars a year, with optional multi-year registration. Responds to Chris Mulder's offer to help fund a web wiki by noting that a free subdomain would avoid any cost, and says he will proceed only if the list wants it.
informs

Well, I could host a wiki at no additional cost to myself, and am online regularly enough to admin it (but would gladly give a few list regulars admin/moderator authority). The only real financial cost would come from getting the domain name, and that would be ~$10/year, give or take, depending on the registrar used. Domains can be registered for more than one year at a time, so it also depends on how long you want to go before having to renew it.

If I had a job and were making real money, then this wouldn't be an issue ;) However, since I'm struggling to return to the workforce after a prolonged temp disability, I'm rather indefinitely strapped for extra cash.

But don't go throwing money around just yet. If it is acceptable to have something like urthwiki.joncapps.net or wikki.urth.net instead of something like www.urthwiki.net, then no one needs to shell out anything.

But I'm not really interested in doing anything unless the list decised it wants it. I just saw the bait and bit the hook. Now it's up to the list to reel me in ;)

jon
