Short Story 194*: The Vampire Kiss

2015urthJeff WilsonMarc Aramini
The thread: Readers debated whether Tommy in 'The Vampire Kiss' could be Tommy Chitling from Oliver Twist, given Fagin's knowledge of a trial or arrest. Marc Aramini argued transportation rules out the match, since Fagin is executed and Tommy was transported after prison, while conceding the word 'transported' is ambiguous and asking whether Fagin would assume it when Tommy simply vanishes. He also floated whether the story's real theme is that society and justice are more monstrous than the monster. Jeffery Wilson countered that 'as we hear' marks the word as hearsay, so the story needs only that Tommy fails to return, traced through a chain of outside tellers. The thread ends with Wilson's hearsay reading standing against Aramini's doubts, with no clear agreement.
  • marcaramini: Tommy Chitling match is doubtful; transportation rules it out, though 'transported' is ambiguous
  • marcaramini: the story may be about society and justice being more monstrous than the monster
  • Jeffery Wilson: 'as we hear' makes 'transported' hearsay, so the story only needs Tommy not returning

Themes: Justice, torture and mercy, Power, rulers and politics, Unreliable narration

2015-04-23 Jeff Wilson replying to marcaramini #

Proposes that the word 'transported' in The Vampire Kiss rests on the phrase 'as we hear', which signals hearsay, so the story needs only that Tommy fails to return. Replies to marcaramini's point that 'transported' is ambiguous by tracing a chain of hearsay: someone outside the story tells someone who passes it to Fagin.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Tommy's transportation is reported through a hearsay chain

"He has been taken now, poor Tommy has, and transported as we hear. His tale has remained with me, however; thus I set it down."

All this requires is that Tommy does not return when expected. "as we hear" is almost certainly meant to imply hearsay, so someone not appearing in the story speaks of Tommy's transportation with conviction to someone who relates it to Fagin.
