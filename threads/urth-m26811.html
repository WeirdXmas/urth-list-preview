Argues that the capitalized "Frostfree" on the fridge in Wolfe's short story should be lowercase, since the freezer plate is an advertising description rather than a name, and that the published Shadows of the New Sun edition wrongly changed it. Supports the case by tracing the name versus description distinction through the story's kitchen scenes, the fridge's own words, and Wolfe's confirmed preference.
