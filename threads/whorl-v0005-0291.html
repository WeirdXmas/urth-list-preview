Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1997-05-16 Robert Borski #

Asks Gene Wolfe two questions: the etymology of the name Mamelta, and who Hyacinth's mother was, given that Exodus reports she has recently died and Nettle seems to have known her. Following an earlier question about how long the inhumu Quetzal has been aboard the Whorl, the post is a lurker's first direct query to the author.
questions · source-and-allusion

Breaking if albeit temporarily my long-held lurker status (and since alga has already posed my initial question, "How long has the inhumu known as Quetzal been aboard the Whorl?"), I'd like to ask GW the following: What is the etymology of "Mamelta"?

Bonus second question that I probably will not be allowed: Who was (since we are told in EXODUS she's recently expired and Nettle seems to have known her) Hyacinth's mother?

Robert Borski
