2000-05-24 Alice Turner #

Announces that the poster will meet novelist John Crowley in New York for an interview for Locus, and invites list members to send questions to be put to him. Mentions a planned follow-up interview after the Wednesday meeting, timed to the magazine's October issue.
informs

To retreat from Pelagianism for a moment (Adam, I'll come back), John Crowley says he'll be in NYC (where I live) next week and may have some free time on Wednesday, so I might start my interview with him (for Locus) then. I won't have much free time before then as I'm going to a college reunion this weekend and I have an exam in tree pruning I have to swot for this Thursday. But if any of you who joined the Crowley debate (or lurkers, too) have questions you'd like answered, I promise to address them. You can e-mail me, or, if you think the group will be interested, post. I will almost certainly continue the interview after Wednesday as (a) it sounds as though it will be a bit hurried, and (b) Locus doesn't need it till their October issue. (WFC is Oct.)

-alga
