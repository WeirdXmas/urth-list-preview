Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2000-04-03 Nil_Ultra #

Argues that The Book of the New Sun is not inferior to the Long Sun books, praising Urth of the New Sun as its best part while calling the New Sun unique for resembling a lost holy book. Responds to a poster who called the Long Sun superior, granting that the Long Sun has clearer plot and good-versus-evil structure but insisting the New Sun's lack of such clarity is its strength.
disputes · genre-and-craft

Ok, that deserves crucifiction, though I will agree that Urth of the new sun is certainly the best part of the book of the new sun, it is heresy to say that the book of the new sun is inferior to the long sun. The long sun series does have more value in the traditional sf/fantasy plotline of being really clear about what the plot is and the good guys vs bad guys theme, but the lack of that was what I think really made Book of The New Sun unique; it is not a fantasy story, it is like reality merged with the text of some lost holy book. I will be the first to agree that the Book of the New Sun is not for everyone, but do not call it inferior until you understand it.

-Rob

2000-04-03 Jack and Melissa Holcomb #

Rebukes Rob for mocking Jeff's opinion that the Long Sun books are better than The Book of the New Sun, arguing that Jeff framed his criticism as personal taste rather than gospel. Says the poster prefers The Book of the New Sun, lists The Urth of the New Sun as optional, and ranks the Soldier books second among Wolfe's works.
disputes

Whoa, cowboy. Look, Rob, you're absolutely entitled to your opinion, but then, so is Jeff. What's more, Jeff was pretty careful about framing every criticism he made of New Sun as his own reaction -- "for me" and so forth, ya see? -- and not as gospel truth (or gospel truth's inverse, heresy). Don't jump all over him, tell him to shut up, and imply that he doesn't understand the book, just because he expressed his opinion about it. That's just damn rude. And so far I've been so impressed with the congeniality of this list.

And for the record, I dig BotNS best, although Urth is optional. And I would agree that the Soldier books are the second-best thing Wolfe's written.

Jack

Responds warmly to a newcomer who started Wolfe with the last two Long Sun books, saying it is surprising they enjoyed entering the series mid-way. Reacts to the newcomer's remark that the books worked despite starting in the middle, admitting it undercuts the poster's earlier defence of tight plotting.
chats

You're certainly entitled to think this way--and it's nice for LS to have a defender! What absolutely floors me, though, is that you were able to come in on the middle of the series and enjoy it thoroughly. So much for my defense of elegant plotting!

-alga
