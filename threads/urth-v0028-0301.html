The thread: The thread asked whether the Apu-Punchau and Pampa names in The Book of the New Sun point to Quechua, Southeast Asian languages, or a South American setting for the Commonwealth. Tony Ellis opened by questioning how an Athens-like stone town fits with a roof built a million years ago in South America, framed around postmodern self-awareness. Carlos Martinho argued firmly for Quechua roots, citing words like p'unchau, apuc, Machu Picchu and Tupac Amaru, and linked the Typhon mountains to the Andes. The thread ends with competing linguistic and geographic readings left unresolved.

1999-10-28 Tony Ellis #

Offers a one-sentence definition of postmodern art as self-aware art that foregrounds its own artificiality, contrasting it with modernist transparency. Illustrates with Ben Marcus's The Age of Wire and String and then questions how Athens-as-stone-town fits with the roof of Apu-Punchau's house built a million years ago in South America, citing Urth of the New Sun chapter L.
questions · close-textual-reading

I suspect that there are as many explanations of
postmodermism as there are postmodernists, but my own pet in-one-sentence definition is to say that postmodern art is self-aware art. It's art with a built-in awareness of its own artificiality.

For modernists art was a way of trying to get as close as possible to the 'Truth' (eg: Joyce's epiphanies): the
characters, the sensations, were all supposed to seem as real as possible, while the art that brought them to you remained transparent. In postmodernism the art itself is the subject of the art. Thus in Ben Marcus's fascinatingly
bonkers 'The Age of Wire and String' you can have a chapter that begins: "Sky Destroys Dog. Air days, in the Western Worship Boxes, traditionally the Wednesday, Friday, and
Half-Man Day following the first Sunday that a dog has
suffocated the weather." You're not supposed to look beyond the words for some higher meaning, you stop at the words themselves and their arrangement.

That is such an ingenious sleight-of-hand! But... but... if the stone town is Athens, how can Severian, the Cumaen and co. be sitting on the roof of Apu-Punchau's house, when that was built a million years ago in South America? "When the building was completed, I recognized the roof upon which Jolenta would die, and I knew I would be buried beneath it." TUOTNS, chapter L.

1999-10-29 Carlos Martinho #

Argues that the Apu Punchau and Pampa names in The Book of the New Sun come from Quechua, not Southeast Asian sources, and that the Typhon mountains resemble the Andes, so the Commonwealth is South American. Supports this with Quechua words like p'unchau and apuc, the name Machu Picchu, and Tupac Amaru, answering Martinho's wife's blank response to the Apu words.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Apu Punchau is related to Incan Quechua; The Commonwealth is South American

Well... I'm Brazilian, also. Amerindians from Brazil
(tupi, guarani, caiapo, txucarramae, etc) are not the
same -- language-wise -- with the Peruvian/ Andean
Incas, who spoke quechua. "Apu-Punchau" sounds a lot
like quechua (see, for instance, the name of the
historical site -- "Macchu-Pichu", "old mountain", or
the native leader Tupac Amaru). And, by the way, the
Typhon mountains seem a lot like the Andes, IMO.
BTW: if I'm not mistaken, "p’unchau" means "day" or
"time" in quechua. And "apuc" means "chief". "Pampa"
is also a quecha word.

My better guess is that Apu Punchau is somewhat
related with the Incans. Ergo, the Commonwealth is
South American.

1999-10-30 JMCAzurin #

Proposes that Wolfe's name Apu-Punchau, glossed as Head of Day, shares a root with a Tagalog word for leader, and builds a geographic theory that Nessus lies south of the poster's region with the Ascians suggesting Asia. Grounds the idea on the translation and on the reasoning that receded oceans would leave Southeast Asia landlocked, and asks whether South American languages hold a similar word.
proposes · source-and-allusion · puzzle-solving · theories: Apu-Punchau derives from a Tagalog word for leader; Nessus lies south of Southeast Asia; Ascians are a satire of Asians

This is just an idle observation. I'm not certain if it's been brought up before but....Wolfe translates 'Apu-Punchau' as 'Head of Day'. In Tagalog, at least, and possibly other languages spoken here in southeast asia, the word 'apo' means leader.

Before I discovered this list, I was trying to find reasons for Nessus being somewhere south of here. Given that Nessus is supposed to be somewhere in a huge land mass, that would mean that the oceans would have receded by the time of the first empire. Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Guam, Borneo - all landlocked, the northern parts of which would be 'the hot lands of the north' (near the equator).

That would put the Ascians in a not-so-hot area north of the equator: China, the former USSR. Both communist countries, which is more or less what the Ascians are. (Ascians=Asians?) I intend no racial slurs here - I'm a Southeast Ascian myself.

(Yes I know this is a crazy theory *g*)

Does anyone speak native south american languages? Those people crossed over into the Americas over the land bridge at the Bering Strait, and are of Asian descent too. Perhaps there is a similar word for 'leader'?

Mikah
