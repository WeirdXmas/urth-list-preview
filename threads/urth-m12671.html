2010-01-04 Matthew Keeley replying to Greg Bates #

Reports that a video game, Killzone 2, contains several Wolfe-linked names: a level called Blood Meridian, a villain ranked autarch, and Sev as a protagonist, while also noting the sword Terminus Est in Castlevania: Symphony of the Night. Responds to Greg Bates's pop-culture question by listing these examples and offering a PSN friend connection; largely social with a recommendation.
informs

I don't think you're reading too much into this at all. I played the game myself and noticed it. Someone on the Killzone 2 team has good taste in literature ? the second level is called Blood Meridian.

Also, the main villain's rank is "autarch".

Terminus Est appears as a sword in Castlevania: Symphony of the Night. Doubtless there are other Wolfe references in games too.

I don't know if your PS3 is online at the moment, but if you want to add a fellow Wolfe fan as a PSN friend, my screentag is keeleymk. Mostly playing Uncharted 2 at the moment ? highly, highly recommended.

-Matt
