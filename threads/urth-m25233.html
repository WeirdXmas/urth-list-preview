Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2014-08-11 Marc Aramini #

Proposes that a line in Doyle's text alludes to a folktale, pointing to a tale titled "twenty years with the good people" in a Knox folk collection. Notes its father-and-son parallels with the cabin-on-the-coast story, where the father is taken for twenty years and will not speak of it on returning to his wife's house.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Doyle alludes to a Knox folktale

Well, on second thought Doyle surely refers to a folktale, though I haven't found it. One folk tale that is almost exactly like cabin on the coast is "twenty years with the good people" in a folk tale collection by d. Knox. It too Involves father and son similarities but this time the father is the one taken for twenty years, and he won't talk about it when he comes back to his wife's house.
