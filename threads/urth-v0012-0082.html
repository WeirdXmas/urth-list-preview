Themes: Unreliable narration

1998-06-04 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists the dated entries of Marsch's journal and the interview and prisoner-note pages in The Fifth Head of Cerberus, with page numbers, to build a chronology of the Marsch expedition and its arrest. Gives the sequence from March interviews through the April events with the cat, the tire-tiger and the boy, to the June arrest, noting that the page citations are for the Ace edition and calling for a conversion table.
