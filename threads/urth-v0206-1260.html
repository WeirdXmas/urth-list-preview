Answers Josh Geller's question about the story titled 'Inside The Bishop's Head' by suggesting it is likely 'The Game in the Pope's Head' or 'How the Bishop Sailed to Inniskeen' and listing where each appeared. Gives anthology and magazine citations and points to online Wolfe bibliographies and Sirius Fiction booklets, with no reading of the text.
answers

Josh Geller asked:

I haven't heard of that one, unless you mean "The Game in the Pope's Head" (or less likely, "How the Bishop Sailed to Inniskeen").

The first is in RIPPER!, an anthology by Casper/Dozois, and also THE YEAR'S BEST FANTASY: SECOND ANNUAL COLLECTION by Datlow/Windling.

The second is in SPIRITS OF CHRISTMAS antho by Cramer/Hartwell, and Asimov's Dec 1989.

There are some good online Wolfe bibliographies, and there is also the one we cobbled together around here.
