Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

2000-04-07 Adam Stephanides #

Asks whether The Book of the Long Sun offers textual evidence that Silk is inhabiting a two-headed Pas-body, and proposes that Silk's consciousness was transferred to another body. Responds to Nacre's remark that such a body would be noticed, reasoning that Horn, in Silk's body, believes Silk is still outside Mainframe.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk's consciousness transferred to another body; Silk is in a two-headed Pas-body

The possibility of Silk being in a two-headed Pas-body hadn't occurred to me. My thoughts had simply been that if Horn is in Silk's body, and Horn thinks Silk is still out there and outside Mainframe, then Silk's consciousness must have been somehow transferred to some other body.

Is there textual evidence from TBOTLS that Silk may be in a two-headed Pas body? (It's been a while since I reread it.)

--Adam
