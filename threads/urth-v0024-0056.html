Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Unreliable narration

1999-03-12 Roy C. Lackey #

Asks whether a contradiction exists between Severian's recollection of Robert and Marie as a brother and sister in The Claw of the Conciliator and a woman's remark calling the man her husband in The Shadow of the Torturer. Points to the Jungle Garden scene and the penultimate page of the later book as the two conflicting passages, and wonders whether the discrepancy is a clue to their identities.
questions · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Robert and Marie are siblings or spouses

Re: The recent traffic about the identities of Robert and Marie:

In the Jungle Garden scene in _Shadow_ the woman refers to the man thusly: "My husband wishes..." (p-168)
However, on the penultimate page of _Claw_, Sev recalls them as "..the woman and her brother..." Does Sev's famous memory fail him? Did Wolfe screw up? Or, are the two passages not mutually contradictory? If not, is that a left-handed clue to their identities?

Roy
