Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Deep time and ruins

1997-04-16 James Jordan #

Argues that Urth is Earth in a future cycle of an oscillating universe, with Urth/Briah preceding our present oscillation, which explains how Severian's book survives and reached Wolfe. Grounds this in the assumption that each oscillation largely repeats the last and in remarks Wolfe made in interviews, including one with the poster.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Urth is Earth in a future oscillation; Urth/Briah precedes our oscillation

Urth/Briah is an oscillating universe preceding our particular oscillation. That is why Severian's book can (somehow) survive and be translated by Wolfe.
Wolfe is assuming that each oscillation pretty much duplicates the one preceding it, with perhaps some advances, as each Briah race becomes the hierodules for the next cosmos. Thus, for all intents and purposes, Urth is Earth, and Severian is in "our" future.
From things Wolfe has said in various interviews, including mine, I think we should take this a given.

Nutria (Jim Jordan)
