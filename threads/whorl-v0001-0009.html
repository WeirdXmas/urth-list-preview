Themes: Unreliable narration

1996-12-06 Peter Cash #

Complains that the revelation at the end of Exodus from the Long Sun, that Horn is the narrator all along, felt like a surprise trick. Argues Wolfe should have planted clues earlier, and asks whether anyone noticed hints about Horn's role in the earlier Long Sun books.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Horn is the narrator of the whole Long Sun

One thing I found really frustrating with the end of Exodus was the revelation that this has been Horn's narrative all along. This revelation came as a complete surprise to me, and I couldn't help but feel that it was a trick Wolfe sprung so that he could end the Book of the Long Sun when he did. After all, if he narrows down the narrator's perspective, then he can't be blamed for not filling us in on details like what became of Silk--Horn just doesn't know.

I feel that writer really ought to drop some clues ahead of time about this sort of thing. Did any of you notice any clues about Horn's role in the earlier books?

Peter
