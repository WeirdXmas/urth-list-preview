2001-07-01 Alice Turner #

Reports that the poster stayed up watching a television miniseries of Gormenghast, praising its character performances and humour, especially the Queen, and says they would revisit it but not the book. Asks whether the work is saturated with Shakespeare, noting references to at least half a dozen plays; the post is social and about another author's work, not Wolfe's.
chats

Last night I had absolutely galloping insomnia and in flipping around the tube landed about 20 minutes into -Gormenghast- again, an all-nighter with both parts. And I take back my Groan. It couldn't have been better company for an all-nighter (it is now 5:15 a.m.), and I obviously quit much too soon before, or wasn't in the mood. Wonderful character parts--I wish the credits had rolled more slowly. My absolute favorite was the Queen, Zoe Wanamaker, I think. (She'd be a good Duchess in Alice, wouldn't she?) It looked great and was very funny at moments. I can't imagine ever wanting to read the book again (I don't think I ever got through all three), but wouldn't mind revisiting the mini-series.

It's just soaked in Shakespeare, isn't it? References from or to at least half a dozen plays.

-alga
