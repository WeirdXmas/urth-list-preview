Kevin Malone

Urth list · 4 messages · 1999-02-25 to 1999-03-01

The thread: Roy Lackey posed a string of questions about the mystery in Wolfe's story "Kevin Malone": who Kevin is if no child existed, where the estate's money came from, why Betty Malone was murdered, and why Marcella stays. Tony Ellis answered that Kevin is neither a ghost nor Betty's son, that the shared surname is coincidence, and that the narrator's final line about possession resolves things. Peter Westlake said rereading made the ending clear and that the puzzle looked solved, pending Ellis's confirmation. William Ansley remained confused, asked Ellis to explain who is possessing Kevin, and pointed out that Ellis's reading undercuts Kevin's childhood memories of the house. The thread ended unresolved, with Ansley's objection left open.
  • Tony Ellis: Kevin is neither a ghost nor Betty's son; the ending's possession explains him
  • Roy C. Lackey: the story's murdered maid and suicide raise unanswered questions about the mystery
  • Peter Westlake: the puzzle is clear-cut once the hints are read
  • William H. Ansley: still confused; Ellis's reading conflicts with Kevin's childhood memories

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage, Memory and forgetting

1999-02-25 Tony Ellis #

Argues that Kevin Malone in the story of that name is neither a ghost nor the son of Betty Malone, since the shared surname is coincidental and no child existed. Answers Roy's questions by pointing to the narrator's final line about possession and asking where Kevin's childhood memories of the big house come from.
answers · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Kevin Malone is not Betty Malone's child; Kevin believes a fantasy of childhood in the house; Kevin Malone is possessed without knowing it

A gem of a story, which I've often re-read purely for the pleasure of doing so. And I have no intention of spoiling it for you or for anyone else, but here are a couple of hints:

He's neither.

There -is- no relationship between Kevin and Betty Malone. Once you understand that it all falls into place: Kevin Malone -thinks- Betty Malone and the elder Mr Malone were married, -thinks- that he was their child in the lost Eden of the house in its heyday. But if the names are just coincidental, and there -was- no child, that cuts his whole fantasy off at the root. In which case, where -do- his childhood memories of the big house come from? And the longing to recreate that lost world which has driven his whole life?

Think about that, and think about the narrator wondering in the very last line of the story, if it might be possible "for a man, even a very rich man, to be posessed and not to know it."

1999-02-26 Roy C. Lackey #

Asks a series of questions about the mystery in Wolfe's story "Kevin Malone": who Kevin is if there was no child, where the estate's money came from, why Betty Malone was murdered, and why Marcella stays. Grounds these in the narrative's testimony about a murdered maid and a suicide, and notes that two narrators are misogynists.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading

I hope these questions were not rhetorical. :-) If there was no child, then who is Kevin Malone? Where did (does) the money come from to buy and maintain the estate? If any of the testimony of the narrative is to be trusted, then a maid named Betty Malone was murdered and someone, who may or may not have been the murderer, committed suicide in consequence thereof. Why was she murdered, and by whom? What bearing does that have on Kevin, or the current "young couple"?
In this story, again, there are two Wolfe characters - the narrator and Kevin - who are misogynists.
And what about the "young couple"? Why does Marcella remain? As far as "spoilers" go, in the archives I believe it is stated that all readers of this list are presumed to be familiar with the corpus of Wolfe's work. Is that not so?

Possessed by whom or what? Spoil it for me, by private e-mail if you wish.

Roy

1999-02-27 Peter Westlake #

Reports that rereading the story Kevin Malone made the ending clear, and thanks Roy and Tony for the hints that revealed the solution. Says the puzzle seems clear-cut, pending confirmation from Tony that the reading is correct.
informs · theories: Kevin Malone thinks Betty and the elder Mr Malone were his parents

Everything is suddenly crystal clear - though I shall check with Tony to see that I've got it right! Roy, Tony - thank you!
