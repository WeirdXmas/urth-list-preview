Proposes that sun references in the Book of the New Sun imply another time loop, or that an earlier autarch of the pre-Typhonian succession knew what was to come. Responds to Andrew Mason's remark that the matter concerns the power of the sun, offering the causal loop as a possible reading of those references.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Sun references imply another time loop; Pre-Typhonian autarch knew what was to come

The sun references are intriguing, implying yet another time-loop, or perhaps that an autarch of the distant pre-Typhonian succession somehow knew something of what was to come.
