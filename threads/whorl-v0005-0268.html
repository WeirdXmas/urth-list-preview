1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunJames Jordan

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Gods, angels and the Increate

1997-05-14 James Jordan #

Asks Gene Wolfe whether the Enlightenments in The Book of the Long Sun are purely miraculous or concurrent with the activities of Mainframe or Pas. Proposes that they may be Outsider actions using Mainframe, comparing them to the Dreammaster's interventions in "The Detective of Dreams" and linking Silk's baptism to the first Enlightenment.
questions · theology-and-religion · close-textual-reading · theories: Enlightenments are concurrent Outsider actions using Mainframe; Silk's first Enlightenment parallels baptism using water

Gene,

Originally I assumed the Enlightenments of Silk were like the invasions of the Dreammaster in "The Detective of Dreams"; i.e., direct actions by God. On the other hand, perhaps the Enlightenments are concurrent actions by the Outsider, utilizing and enhancing something else, like a download from Mainframe. (I don't buy that these are ONLY a natural events, for why then would Silk associate them with the non-mainframe "minor god" Outsider?) The first Enlightenment is Silk's baptism, and since baptism utilizes water, perhaps the Enlightenment utilized mainframe?
At any rate, do you care to enlighten us as to whether the Enlightenments are purely miraculous or are concurrent with activities of Mainframe or Pas? Or would you rather let us keep working on this one!!

Jim Jordan
