Typhon in new Koontz novel

Informs readers that Dean Koontz's new novel THE FACE includes a demi-god-like character named Typhon, and suggests this figure resembles Typhon from The Book of the New Sun. Notes Koontz's style is closer to Stephen King than Wolfe, and calls the novel an excellent, rewarding read.
informs

For anyone interested, Dean Koontz's newest novel, THE FACE, features appearances by a demi-god-like character named Typhon. Though Koontz's storytelling style is closer to Stephen King than Gene Wolfe (I prefer Koontz over King), Koontz's Typhon feels very much like the Typhon from BOTNS. By the way, I found this to be an excellent story and a very rewarding read.

Steve Strickland
