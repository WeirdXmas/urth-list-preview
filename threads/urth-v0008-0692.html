1998-03-30 Tony Ellis #

Disputes David Lebling's claim that the teratoid sign in The Book of the New Sun is a radiation warning, noting the colour and symmetry differ and the mirrors seem unconnected. Cites a Wolfe interview answer that Alden Weer killed no other characters in Peace, and questions the case that Weer is a liar, asking Damien to elaborate on his Peace essay.
disputes · close-textual-reading · author-and-biography · theories: Alden Weer killed no one in Peace; Weer does not lie to himself or the reader; teratoid sign is not a radiation symbol

I thought of this too... but decided no. Our radiation symbol is black on yellow, and this is crimson. Also, its simple rotational symmetry is hardly "teratoid". Finally, as far as we know, the mirrors have nothing to do with radiation. I'm sure that nice Father Inire wouldn't show little girls around the chamber if they did.

In the online People interview, Wolfe was asked if Weer killed many of the other characters in the book and responded "Let me think about that... no. I don't think so, no."

1998-04-02 Michael Straight #

Argues that Wolfe's interview answer about Alden Weer in Peace means Weer did not kill many characters, not that he killed no one, since Wolfe would not need to pause if Weer were entirely innocent. Points to the boy at the top of the stairs as a character Weer plainly, if accidentally, killed.
disputes · close-textual-reading · author-and-biography · theories: Alden Weer accidentally killed the boy at the stairs

I'd always understood that quote to mean that, Weer did not kill "many" of the other characters of the book, not that he didn't kill anyone. I doubt Wolfe would have had to think about it if Weer was entirely innocent, and it seems pretty plain that he at least (accidentally) killed the boy at the top of the stairs.

-Rostrum
