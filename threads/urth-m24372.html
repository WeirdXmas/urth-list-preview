The thread: Thread began with David Stockhoff and Antonin Scriabin debating whether Silk/Horn in the Matachin Tower is a Long Sun figure, whether Typhon launched many Whorls, and whether Short Sun is less mysterious than Long Sun. Marc Aramini countered that Short Sun holds more open puzzles, while Stockhoff and entonio argued the setting and Wolfe's planning differ. Daniel Petersen, Gerry Quinn and Sergei Soloviev doubted the Silk/Horn-as-childhood-client link, and entonio argued Wolfe could not have planned it in advance. The thread then shifted to the alzabo and inhumi, where Scriabin and Stockhoff agreed that the speech is mere animal mimicry, and Jeff Wilson and David Duffy added Yesodis-engineering and Four in One ideas. It ended without consensus, with several threads simply dropped.
  • Antonin Scriabin: Long Sun is more mysterious than Short Sun, and the Matachin Tower Silk/Horn link may be a late staple
  • David Stockhoff: Short Sun's mysteries remain, the Whorl launch need not be mentioned, and the alzabo remains a beast
  • Marc Aramini: Short Sun has more unresolved mysteries, and the Matachin figure echoes maimed Severian
  • Daniel Petersen: Doubts Wolfe planned Silk and Horn as childhood clients, though open to convincing evidence
  • Gerry Quinn: Severian never meets the clients held together, and Typhon would have needed to mention the Whorl launch
  • Jeff Wilson: Inire or the Yesodis genetically engineered the alzabo and the absorption of prey memories

Themes: Stories within stories, Doubles, twins and clones, Beasts and the animal in us
