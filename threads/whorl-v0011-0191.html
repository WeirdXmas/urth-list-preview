Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2000-08-27 Alice Turner #

Proposes that Krait's mother was already pregnant, or about to be, when the first bite imprinted the unborn child, so that the bite rather than sex explains Krait's origin. Grounds this in the fact that Jahlee's troubles rule out sex as the cause, and jokes about winning the Borski prize.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Krait's mother was gravid when first bitten

It couldn't have been sex; we know that from Jahlee's troubles. But what if Krait's mother were already gravid (or about to be, or whatever they do) and the first bite imprinted the foetus (or whatever it is). Maybe it's that first bite.

Now I know I'm up for the Borski prize.

-alga
