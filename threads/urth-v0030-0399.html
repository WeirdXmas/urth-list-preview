Copperhead: The Fall

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-05-10 to 2001-05-10

2001urthMichael Straight

Themes: Catholic faith and sacraments

2001-05-10 Michael Straight #

Argues that Adam and Eden in Copperhead: The Fall can be post-Fall yet still glorious, since humans kept long lifespans for generations after the Fall. Grounds this in Adam's 930 years, the Flood, a creationist theory about a water firmament, and Eden still guarded by the flaming-sword angel, answering Michael Straight's objection that Cain's murder of Abel rules that out.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: Adam and Eden are post-Fall but still glorious

The explanation came to me right after I hit send.

Lilith and Adam and Eden are post fall, as shown by Cain having already killed Abel. It's just that, even after the Fall, it took a while for humans to lose their grandeur -- Adam lived for 930 years, and his children had long lifespans for generations (up until the Flood, leading some Creation Scientists to theorize that the Flood came from a "firmament" of water that had surrounded the Earth, shielding people from UV and other harmful radiation, explaining the longer lifetimes). And Eden after the Fall remains a glorious place that must be guarded by an angel with a flaming sword.

So the Fall has occured, but Adam is still more glorious than those of us who have never talked with God face-to-face in the cool of the morning, and Eden is still the home of barking trees and sounding rams horns.

-Rostrum
