2000-01-25 Jonathan Laidlow #

Suggests that a collection of Wolfe material be housed at Liverpool's SF Foundation, and asks whether Wolfe would deposit his manuscripts in a US or UK library. Responds to an earlier post asking where such a collection could be placed and whether Wolfe would approve, with no textual argument about the fiction.
questions

Living in dear old blighty I'd second the suggestion of Liverpool, but I think its unlikely. There are several US libraries which hold major SF deposits but I don't have the list to hand. Does anyone know what Wolfe does with his manuscripts, and whether he'd be willing to deposit them?

Jonathan
