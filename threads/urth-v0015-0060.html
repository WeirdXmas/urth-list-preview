islands of the Commonwealth

Explains that a previous question about how the Commonwealth can be an archipelago when South America is geographically similar is answered by South America's long chain of southern islands that follow the coast closely. Responds to an earlier list letter asking this and offers the geography as the resolution.
answers · close-textual-reading

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

A previous letter to the list (I'm sorry but I don't remember when) asked how the Commonwealth could be in a South America geographically similar to the current continent when the Commonwealth is described as consisting of an archipelago of islands to the south. The answer is that SA does have a long chain of many islands in the south, but it closely follows the close, so the questioner probably wouldn't remember them or have noticed them just by casually looking in the first place.
