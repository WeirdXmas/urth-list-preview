AEG: Rian's mother

Urth list · 5 messages · 2009-10-04 to 2009-10-08

2009urthDave TallmanRoy C. LackeyJames Wynn
The thread: Roy C. Lackey proposed that Cassie, Rian's mother, is her own older self who returns to Earth after Gideon restores her looks, marries Reis, and later persuades Pavlatos to rescue her younger self from the island, with the loop changing nothing. Dave Tallman disputed this, arguing that Sharon Bench knows both Rian and his mother well and that Cassie would avoid reconnecting with people who knew her. Lackey answered that Sharon was only a small-city celebrity reporter and that Cassie may have used her to watch her younger self, and he playfully teased the Margaret-as-Cassie idea. James Wynn argued that Sharon would have remarked on a resemblance to Pavlatos and that the age gap makes the identification strained. Lackey closed by proposing a time-travel version in which Pavlatos is Bill's second wife, while the thread ended without agreement.
  • Roy C. Lackey: Rian's mother is Cassie's older self, returning via time travel, and the loop changes nothing
  • Dave Tallman: Sharon Bench knows Rian and his mother well, so Cassie would avoid contact with people who knew her
  • James Wynn: Sharon would have noted a resemblance to Pavlatos, and the age gap makes the identification strained

Themes: Time loops and causality, Memory and forgetting, Doubles, twins and clones

2009-10-04 Roy C. Lackey #

Proposes that Cassie, Rian's mother, returns to Earth after Gideon restores her looks, marries Reis, and later persuades Pavlatos to rescue her younger self from the island. Cites the timeline of Reis's career and Gideon's repair of Rian's heart as evidence, and presents the loop as changing nothing, since everything happens as in the book.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Cassie is Rian's mother who rescues her younger self; Cassie's time travel changes nothing in the book; Cassie engineers her own marriage to Reis

One more try. I'll make it brief.

Cassie goes to Woldercan and gets Gideon to make her look good again. She comes back to Earth about 20 years before she left. She is still in her early thirties; Reis should then be in his twenties. She knows his tastes and uses her knowledge and looks to meet and marry him. She gives birth to Rian.

After a number of years, her looks start to fade and Reis strays. He leaves her for another beauty, Pavlatos. Pavlatos goes to Woldercan with him when he is appointed ambassador. Reis dumps Pavlatos after a number of years, but it costs him plenty.

Meanwhile, Cassie knows about Gideon, knows that he can and will fix Rian's heart. He does so. Cassie otherwise stays in the background. She knows the future but cannot change it and dares not try; because otherwise Rian would not exist. When the time is ripe, she contacts Pavlatos and persuades her to rescue her younger self from the island. Younger Cassie goes to Woldercan . . . etc.

Everything happens just as it happened in the book. Cassie changed nothing by going into the past. She went to Woldercan and found her lost love again, if only for a while. She gained a child who is a little bit of the man she loved.

-Roy

2009-10-06 Dave Tallman replying to Roy C. Lackey #

Questions whether Rian's mother is really in the background of An Evil Guest, as the reply suggests, given that Sharon Bench knows both Rian and his mother well. Cites Sharon's remark that Rian is too brave and her request that Cassie protect their privacy (pp. 25-26) to argue that reconnecting with people who knew her would be risky.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Cassie is Rian's mother

I agree this is as plausible as the Pavlatos case. I'm still troubled that Sharon Bench knows both Rian and his mother fairly well. (She knows that Rian is "too brave" for example, and charges Cassie to protect their privacy (pp. 25-26). If Cassie/Rian's Mama is keeping in the background, why reconnect with anyone who knew her in the past? It seems like a risky thing to do.

2009-10-07 Roy C. Lackey replying to Dave Tallman #

Argues that Sharon Bench need not have known Rian's mother well, since she was only a celebrity reporter in a small city, and suggests Cassie may have used Sharon to keep watch on her younger self. Responds to Dave Tallman's point that Sharon seems to know both Rian and his mother closely, and playfully teases the Margaret-as-Cassie theory.
disputes · puzzle-solving · theories: Cassie used Sharon Bench to keep tabs on her younger self

Not to pick on your Margaret-as-Cassie theory <g>, but it's not as risky as Margaret going to work for her younger self. I doubt that Sharon knew Rian's mama well enough to sit down to tea and biscuits. She was a celebrity reporter in a smallish city. Cassie may just have wanted to use Sharon to keep tabs on her younger self.

-Roy

2009-10-08 James Wynn replying to Dave Tallman #

Argues that if Rian's mother were Cassie's older self, Sharon Bench would likely have remarked on the resemblance to Pavlatos, and that the age gap makes the identification strained. Responds to Dave Tallman's point about Sharon Bench's acquaintance with Rian and his mother, adding that Wolfe used a similar device in The Book of the Long Sun.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Ms Pavlatos is Cassie's older self

Furthermore, if Sharon Bench knows Ms Pavlatos, is it believable she would not say "Hey! Cassie looks almost just like Pavlatos"? I mean, Ms Pavlatos cannot be Cassie at more than 20 years in the future. This makes her 55 at the extreme.

I really don't consider this a big deal on it's own, to tell the truth. I mean, it is clear to me that Wolfe did something very similar in "The Book of the Long Sun" (with a slightly greater age differential). But it is one more hard pull at a hypothesis that is already dangerously stretched.

Just sayin'
J.

2009-10-08 Roy C. Lackey replying to James Wynn #

Proposes a time-travel version of the theory that Cassie is Rian's mother and Pavlatos is Bill's second wife. Argues that Sharon Bench seems to know of Rian's mother but that nothing in the text shows she knew Pavlatos, answering James Wynn's doubt about whether Sharon would have failed to mention her.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Cassie is Rian's mother; Pavlatos is Bill's second wife; time-travel version of Cassie-Rian's mother theory

In this version of a time-travel theory, Cassie is Rian's mother and Pavlatos Bill's second wife. Sharon seems to know or know of Rian's mother, but I know of nothing to indicate that Sharon knew Pavlatos.

-Roy
