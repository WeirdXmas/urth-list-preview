Themes: Humour in Wolfe

2001-03-01 Alice Turner #

Parodies the Wizard of Oz song "If I Only Had a Brain" with lyrics about wanting a clue to Wolfe's puzzles, referencing Hyperion and the Shrike. Offered as a playful reply to Ranjit's quoted Shrike verse, with no argument or analysis beyond the joke about Blue and Green convergence and Pig's emergence.
chats

Caroled mantis, responding to Ranjit:

I could while away the hours
Conferring with the flowers
And tightnin' every screw.
While my head I'm mostly scratchin'
While I wish my thoughts were hatchin'
If I only had a clue!

I'd unravel every riddle
For any individdle
In the pink or in the blue
With my musings most errantic
I could wax a trifle mantic
If I only had a clue!

Oh I could tell you why
Blue and Green are in convergence
I could tell you Pig's emergence
And some Christian effervescence.

I would not be just a nothin'
My head all full of stuffin'
My heart all full of pain
I would laugh and I'd be merry
Life would be a dinglederry
If I only had a clue.

-alga

2001-03-01 Alice Turner #

Parodies the song Over the Rainbow as a playful verse about wishing with Horn, Silk or Pas and reaching a land beyond Blue in the Long Sun setting. Offered as light fan humour, with no argument about the books and no reply to anyone, so it is a joke rather than a reading.
chats

Jahlee's Song

Somewhere, over the rainbow, way up high,
there's a land that I heard of once in a lullaby.
Somewhere over the rainbow, far from Blue,
there the dreams that I dare to dream,
really do come true.

Someday, I'll wish with Horn/Silk/Pas
and wake up with the Land of Oz before me.
Where I can dance and strip and ----,
Trust me, with a bit of luck,
that's where you'll find me.

Somewhere over the rainbow, I won't fly.
Why fly over the rainbow,
when I can eat apple pie?
If other little vampires fly beyond the rainbow,
why, oh why, can't I?

-alga
