1999-10-31 David Langford #

Shares a news story about two women killed by lightning that struck their underwire bras, adding a later pathologist's finding that the bras conducted the bolt but would not have saved them. Responds to alga's Nabokov quotation about his mother's lightning death, joking that no modernist could invent such a real event; the post is off-topic.

alga propagated:

Vladimir Nabokov came close: "My very photogenic mother died in a freak accident (picnic, lightning) when I was three ..."

The =Evening Standard= later reported more detailed pathologist's evidence saying that although the underwires acted as a conductor, the women would almost certainly have died if they hadn't been wearing those bras.
