Themes: Unreliable narration

2003-04-21 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Cites the passage in The Urth of the New Sun where Severian admits he might be said to have raped Jolenta, asking whether any earlier instance of the charge can be found. Quotes the text directly in reply to Chris, who argued that Severian makes the charge against himself, and adds only the poster's bookseller signature.
questions · close-textual-reading

"I had been . . . quick and clumsy with Jolenta (whom I might have been said to have raped, though I believed then and believe still that she wished it)" (URTH, ch. XXVII, p. 190).

If anything earlier can be found, please bring it to my attention.
