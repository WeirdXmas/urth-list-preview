Clute Review in NYRSF

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-06-09 to 1997-06-09

1997whorlRichard Horton

1997-06-09 Richard Horton #

Informs the list that a new issue of the New York Review of Science Fiction carries a largely favourable review by John Clute of The Book of the Long Sun, and notes that Clute thanks two list members. Also mentions a May Interzone interview with Gene Wolfe by Elizabeth Counihan.
informs

I just got my the latest New York Review of Science Fiction, which includes a fine (and in my opinion largely correct) review of _Exodus of the Long Sun_ (or actually of _The Book of the Long Sun_) by John Clute.

Incidentally, Clute acknowledges the help he received from two members of the list, Mantis and Alga.

(and the May Interzone includes a sort-of-interview with Gene Wolfe by Elizabeth Counihan.)

-- Tusk
