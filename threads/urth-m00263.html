2004-09-25 Christopher Ashley replying to timothy fletcher #

Gives a practical answer to a newcomer's question about writing to Gene Wolfe, suggesting mailing a letter in care of his publisher with a self-addressed stamped envelope for a reply. Responds to the quoted question from a new reader who could not find Wolfe's address after reading of his correspondence with fans in interviews.
answers

I believe it's customary to write to authors care of their publishers. For example:

Gene Wolfe
c/o Tor Books
175 5th Avenue
New York, NY 10015

If you want an answer, it's best to include a SASE. (Not to insult anyone's intelligence-- I made that mistake with a baseball card once, and have regretted it ever since.)

--Chris
