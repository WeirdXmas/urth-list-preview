Themes: Memory and forgetting

2001-06-25 William H. Ansley #

Argues that eating memories, as in the New Sun books, is scientifically implausible because real memory-transfer experiments were never reproduced. Responds to a post questioning the idea by quoting a neurobiology discussion linked by Joshua Solomon and comparing SF stories built on it to inaccurate ones about Mercury.

>In it, Bernard Agranoff writes, "The claims in the 1960s of memory
>transfer via injection of RNA or of protein have long since
>subsided for want of evidence."
