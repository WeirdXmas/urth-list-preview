Lists John Crowley's works and their status, including Daemonology as a possible third part of AEgypt and the Novelty collection that contains GWOT, and ranks AEgypt, Little, Big and Engine Summer. What is Crowley's latest - on a recent search for Crowley stuff on the Web I came across the title 'Daemonology' as being the third part of AEgypt - do you mean that (I'd assumed it wasn't finished yet)?
