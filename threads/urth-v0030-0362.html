Emil Petaja

2001-04-30 Dave Lebling #

Lists the Kalevala-related novels of Emil Petaja that make up the Otava series, citing an online bibliography, and notes they were published by Ace in the mid-1960s and are long out of print. Responds to Alice Turner's question about Petaja's series and agrees with her criticism of the writing, adding that later segments are excellent.
answers

According to http://www.pandorasbox.com/petaja.html (isn't Google wonderful?) the Kalevala-related books are:

_Saga of Lost Earths_, _The Star Mill_, _The Stolen Sun_, _Tramontane_ which make up the "Otava Series", and peripherally, _The Time Twister_.

These were all published by Ace in the mid-60s (remember Ace Doubles?). I'm sure they are all long out-of-print.

Agree completely. There are some excellent segments further on, though.
