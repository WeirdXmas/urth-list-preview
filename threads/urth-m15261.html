New article on Ultan's Library

Urth list · 1 messages · 2010-05-19 to 2010-05-19

2010urthJonathan Laidlow

2010-05-19 Jonathan Laidlow #

Announces that Ultan's Library has republished an older essay by British SF novelist Stephen Palmer, originally written for the BSFA journal Vector about religion in The Book of the New Sun. Notes that the piece was rediscovered after the author lost his copy, and promises a forthcoming piece by Michael Andre-Driussi.
informs

Hello all,

Just wanted to let you all know that we've updated Ultan's Library with a new essay.

Well I say new, it's actually a piece written by British SF novelist Stephen Palmer twenty years ago for Vector, the BSFA journal, which he used to host on his website, but lost. He's recently come across it again and we're proud to republish it.

http://www.ultan.org.uk/religions-implications-new-sun/

We'll have a new piece by Michael Andre-Driussi soon too!

J
