1998-11-23 Gordon Brain #

Reports that Marble Arch was designed by John Nash and built in 1828 as a monument to Nelson before moving to Hyde Park, and that Wellington Arch by Decimus Burton carries a copy of the Parthenon frieze. Responds to Mantis's search for a Latin inscription by admitting no inscription turned up online and suggesting a Greek one, with a London reader checking in person.
informs

Can't find anything about inscriptions - I'd have to go and look but it's 300 miles away.

All I can find is that Marble Arch was designed by John Nash, built in 1828 as a monument to Nelson, and moved from Buckingham Palace to Hyde Park 20 years later.

The other one was built by Decimus Burton, same year, and has a copy of the Parthenon freize on it. How about a Greek inscription?

Maybe some London based Urthling could go and have a butchers.

Brain
