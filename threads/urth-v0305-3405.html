Latro and Mithra

Urth list · 1 messages · 2003-05-12 to 2003-05-12

2003urthDavid Duffy

Themes: Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies

2003-05-12 David Duffy #

Argues that the Mithra of Latro's world links to the later Roman Mithras, describing his role as a moral god and Lord of the Contract in Zoroastrianism. Supports this by citing web sources on Mithras's December 25 birth, control of the precession of the equinox, and slaying of the bull whose blood was drunk by worshippers.
informs · source-and-allusion

I seem to remember earlier discussion of this. Looking on various web sites, Mithra was incorporated into Zoroastrianism as one of the holy immortals aiding Ahura Mazda: "Mithra was a moral god, ... His primary responsibility was to the rightness of the action", "Lord of the Contract". He of course was popular later in Rome as Mithras, born on December 25th, controls precession of the equinox (Ulansey), kills the bull of the previous age, whose blood is drunk by worshippers (replaced by bread and wine if bull is not on the menu).
