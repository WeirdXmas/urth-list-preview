Themes: Time loops and causality

1999-04-06 Alice Turner #

Thanks the list's founder and argues that the White Fountain's origin at the sexual congress of Apheta and Sev is possible because the ship winks in and out of time and can go backwards. Cites Burgundofara and the hierodules as evidence Wolfe allows this time situation, conceding the physics may be a later fix.
supports · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: White Fountain conceived at Apheta and Sev's congress via time travel

Thank you, Ranjit. It's a wonderful list, and without you we wouldn't have it.

Um, with regard to the New Sun/White Fountain, I bow to you boys in your physics calculations, but I do want to remind you that this was conceived by and at the sexual congress of Apheta and Sev. Wolfe gives us plenty of leeway in the time situation with his explanations that the ship winks in and out of time, and can go backwards--he gives us the situation of Burgundofara, as well as the hierodules. It is possible that this is something he thought up after the fact to make sense of prior loose ends, but, nevertheless, it's done well enough to impress me thoroughly.

-alga
