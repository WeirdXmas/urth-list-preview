1997-06-14 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Compares Wolfe's Silk and Auk to Chesterton's Father Brown, noting that Brown's criminal sidekick Flambeau mirrors the cloth-and-crime pairing. Suggests that Viron's 19th-century technology and society strengthen the Brownish feel, and asks whether Brown is really a 20th-century figure, grounding the date in the Great War and Proust.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Silk and Auk echo Father Brown's cloth-and-crime pairing; Viron's 19th-century society strengthens the Brown influence

If I remember right, Father Brown has a criminal sidekick for a few early stories, named Flammbeaux or something like that (a master jewel thief, or something like that). A team of "cloth & crim" just like Silk and Auk. And the commonfolk of Viron live in a 19th century mode of technology and society; by itself a fact of little consequence, but strengthening the "Brownishness" imho. (Or is Brown solidly 20th century? Well, we might argue that the 20th doesn't really start until the horrors of the Great War have destroyed the European aristocracy . . . works for Proust!)

1997-06-17 Derek Bell #

Supports the earlier observation that Silk and Auk in The Book of the Long Sun echo the Father Brown stories' pairing of a priest with a criminal, Flambeaux. Says the Flambeaux tales may come after the earlier stories, asks whether the Trivigaunti war is the first to break Viron's class-ridden society, and assumes Trivigaunt has fought other statelets.
supports · source-and-allusion · theories: Silk and Auk mirror Father Brown and Flambeaux; Trivigaunti war breaks Viron's class society

Excellent! That's something that eluded me though I read those stories myself! I think that the collection has roughly the first half of the stories predating Flambeaux's alliance (conversion?) with Father Brown. I don't remember looking up the publication order, so I'm not sure whether the Flambeax & Brown tales were first.

Perhaps the Trivigaunti/Ayuntiamento war is the first to break Viron's class-ridden society? (I assume Trivigaunt has fought wars with other statelets or even just skirmished with them.)

Tercel
