Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1997-07-12 Alice Turner #

Agrees with Patrick that readers overcomplicate their reading, and notes she already knew Auk and Tartaros kidnapped Hy. Asks who or what Pike's ghost is, calling it an unanswered mystery from the artful codicil, and thanks mantis for running the thread.
questions · puzzle-solving

Patrick is so right about our bending ourselves into knots---for instance, I also knew the answer to my question (about Auk/Tartaros kidnapping Hy)---I just wanted to make sure, I guess.

But...Pike's ghost? Aaaargh! He never answered the artful codicil...who or what is it?
This is a true mystery.

So, the Wolfe wants no more fleas nipping at his hide! Thank you, mantis, for engineering/conducting the last circus.

-alga-
