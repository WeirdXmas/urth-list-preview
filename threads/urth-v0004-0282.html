Themes: Myth and folklore sources, Stories within stories

1997-08-29 Alice Turner #

Proposes that Squanto is one of Natty Bumppo's aliases in The Last of the Mohegans and that the novel is folded into the story "A Boy Called Frog", while noting a further borrowing to check. Also reports shifting research to Athabaskan myths of Great Raven, a trickster creator, which makes the poster less ready to read the winged figure as a bird.
proposes · source-and-allusion · puzzle-solving · theories: Squanto is an alias of Natty Bumppo; A Boy Called Frog folds in The Last of the Mohegans

I think Squanto is one of Natty Bumppo's many aliases in -Last of the Mohegans- (pc retitle). I think I actually have a copy of that, will check. In any case LotM is definitely one of the many works folded into "Boy Called Frog"---there's something else I recognized from it, will check.

Meanwhile, I have abandoned the Illinois area and am working on the Athab(p)ascans of the Far North. I have learned enough about Great Raven, who made the world, that I am no longer so ready to scorn the winged figure as a bird. Great Raven is something of a trickster, but I guess that could be applied to all gods, eh Ratty?

-alga-
