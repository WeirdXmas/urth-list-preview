2001-01-27 James Jordan #

Proposes that the Neighbor named Horn took that name as an honor, and suggests Neighbors' weird humor hints at later events such as astral projection. Asks whether Neighbors and inhumi are somehow the same, and whether the inhumi are a joke of the Neighbors, citing Horn's remarks about Neighbor humor in The Book of the Long Sun.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Neighbors and inhumi are the same kind; The inhumi are a joke of the Neighbors; Neighbor humor foreshadows astral projection

Two other odds and ends:

1. Someone in the past asked about the Neighbor named Horn. I assumed that the Neighbor was honoring Horn by taking Horn's name as his own.

2. Which brings up another point that I don't recall seeing discussed. Horn says that the Neighbors have a weird sense of humor, as he found out repeatedly. Now, that would seem to be a CLUE about later events, but what events? My first guess is the various astral projections, which is something we know that Neighbors do, and which Horn becomes able to do. But only with an inhumu/a present (?). Which brings up the question of whether Neighbors and Inhumi are the "same" somehow. Are the inhumi, in fact, a Huge Joke of the Neighbors?

Patera Nutria
