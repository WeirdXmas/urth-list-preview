Short Story 82: The God and His Man

Urth list · 5 messages · 2014-08-06 to 2014-08-08

2014urthRichard SimonMarc AraminiJohn BarachYves Meynard
The thread: Readers puzzled over the god's name in 'The God and His Man,' which Yves Meynard proposed reading as 'Isid Iooo Iooo E' so that the doubled Iooo spells a W and the name becomes ICWE, asking what the acronym might stand for. Richard Simon independently backed the garbled-name reading and compared it to the lyrics of Louie Louie. Marc Aramini twice mocked the idea of a decipherable name, first with a playful guess that the god is a sentient household appliance and then by likening the name to indecipherable song lyrics. The thread ended without any solution, with the acronym remaining speculative and the appliance idea admittedly unsupported.
  • Yves Meynard: the name should be read as 'Isid Iooo Iooo E', making ICWE, with possible meanings Integrated Circuit, Weapon, or Wolfe
  • Richard Simon: the name is a garbled form of ICWE, with the doubled Iooo read as W, possibly Weapon or Wolfe
  • Marc Aramini: the god may be a sentient household appliance like a microwave, offered as a tentative playful guess without evidence
  • Marc Aramini: the name may be as indecipherable as Louie Louie lyrics, offering no actual solution

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Names and their meanings, Humour in Wolfe

2014-08-06 Yves Meynard #

Proposes that the god's name in "The God and His Man" should be read as "Isid Iooo Iooo E" rather than with numerals, so that the double Iooo spells a W and the original name becomes ICWE. Asks what ICWE might stand for, floating Integrated Circuit, Weapon, or a Wolf(e) reference as possibilities.
proposes · source-and-allusion · puzzle-solving · theories: God's name is a corrupted acronym ICWE

When it comes to the god's name, I never read it as "Isid 1000 1000 E" but as "Isid Iooo Iooo E". "Iooo Iooo" would be a "double Iooo", or W, which would yield "ICWE" as the original name, whose meaning has been corrupted. I find no obvious candidates on the web for the meaning of this acronym. "Integrated Circuit" was once a common meaning of IC. Does W stand in for Weapon? Or is there a Wolf(e) involved?
