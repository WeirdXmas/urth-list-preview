2001-04-24 Peter Westlake #

Asks whether a longer version of Operation ARES exists and what the story behind its cutting is, prompted by a reply noting its published form is shorter than the author's original. Builds on a remark praising the book's chapter structure of detailed action followed by leaps forward, suggesting that technique links it to Wolfe's usual method.
questions · author-and-biography

At 21:04 2001-04-19, James Jordan wrote (on the Whorl list):

Now that's interesting. One very striking thing about the book is the way it has detailed action within the chapters, and then leaps ahead, leaving you to catch up in a very Wolfe-like way. It's the same kind of technique that we see all the time, but formal and regular. So what's the story behind the longer version? Did some editor ask for it to be cut in length? If so, did the way it was done surprise them?!

Spectacled Bear.
