1998-08-20 Dan Parmenter #

Points out that Wolfe's foreword to Lexicon Urthus restates his reasoning for choosing unusual words, so it partly counts as his writing on The Book of the New Sun, and adds his 1984 Boskone piece on books in the work. Also mentions having read Exodus from the Long Sun and asks whether the new series will get its own list.
