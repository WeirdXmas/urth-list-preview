2003-06-19 Jeff Prucher #

Reports that the McAffery interview with Wolfe, titled On Encompassing the Universe, appeared earlier in Across the Wounded Galaxies, which a Science Fiction Studies review dates to 1986 rather than 1988. Replies to James Wynn's thread quoting Sam Keyes by noting the review omits any earlier publication, and links an excerpt and suggests interlibrary loan.
informs

http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/0252061403/104-4212763-4911130?vi=glance

Jeff Prucher, mysteriously talking in the third person, de-lurks long enough to note that the interview in "Across the Wounded Galaxies" (AWG) was apparently conducted in 1986 (according to a review of AWG in SF Studies: http://www.depauw.edu/sfs/reviews_pages/r55.htm). This doesn't mean that they're not the same interview of course, but the SF Studies review does not mention that they had previously published the AWG, as one might expect. There is also an extremely brief excerpt of the AWG interview here: http://www.themodernword.com/borges/borges_infl_wolfe.html If you don't want to shell out the dough for the book, I recommend getting it through interlibrary loan.
