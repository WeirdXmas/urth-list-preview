The Book of Gold

Themes: Stories within stories

1997-05-17 Jason Voegele #

Proposes that The Book of Gold in Peace is the Chinese Egg, and that this inset book is Peace itself as a whole, just as The Book of Gold in The Book of the New Sun is that book. Grounds the claim in a quoted passage where the painted Egg's scenes run together into a charming but nearly impenetrable confusion, with witnesses disagreeing about them.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: The Book of Gold in Peace is the Chinese Egg; The Book of Gold in Peace is Peace itself

I've got a theory for The Book of Gold in PEACE. It's the Chinese Egg. Consider the following passage:

"[The Egg] was painted with a variety of scenes...No two witnesses agreed precisely about either the number of scenes or their exact content. All coincided in saying that the artist's rendering...and running together the various scenes...resulted in a confusion that, though charming, was nearly impenetrable."

This is a perfect description of Wolfe's definition of The Book of Gold, and also of PEACE itself. It seems reasonable to say that The Book of Gold in PEACE *is* PEACE (just as The Book of Gold in TBOTNS is TBOTNS.)

Jason (do I need a nickname in these parts?)
