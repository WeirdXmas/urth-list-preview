Argues that readability and comprehensibility matter most in list posts, and that posters should be allowed some artistic license to keep ideas flowing rather than strict formatting rules. Gives as grounds the relief at no longer having to wade through long pasted quotations of earlier replies to reach the author's own words.
proposes

FWIW, my 02/100 worth is that readability/comprehensibility is the most important issue. I consider everyone here authors, of a sort, and thus artists. I'd prefer a bit of artistic license be granted promoting the free-flow of ideas over the enforcment of standard forms which might, at times, hinder that free-flow.

(either way, I praise whatver higher powers that be that I no longer have to plow through 40 copied paragraphs of responses to get to the actual words of the post author, which used to be true here.)
