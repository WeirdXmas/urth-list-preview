Reports that the poster has printed records from July 1991 showing they used the labels Sev1 through Sev5 for the Severian figures, and that the term ProtoS appeared only later, around September 1993. Offers this dated evidence to Christopher as a point about the history of the terms in the Five Severians discussion.
