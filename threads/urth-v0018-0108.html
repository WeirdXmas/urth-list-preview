Sev's decendants

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-09-04 to 1998-09-04

1998urthThe Book of the New SunJ. Schultz

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-09-04 J. Schultz #

Argues that Severian's 'descendants' in the Book of the New Sun refers metaphorically to the creatures of Urth whose genesis Severian made possible, rather than to a literal line or to Appian's castration. Grounds this in the passage about the sailors who paid with their pasts and in Severian's remark that what was conceived with Apheta was 'myself', linked to the White Fountain as a second self.
proposes · close-textual-reading · allegory-and-typology · theories: Severian's descendants are the creatures of Urth; Severian's conception with Apheta is the White Fountain; Appian's castration is not Severian's punishment

The quote in question:"Remembering that, it was simple enough to guess who my priest's forbears had been - the sailors routed by my memories had paid for their defeat with their pasts, just as I would have lost the future of my descendants had my own past been defeated." I have always interpreted this as meaning that the sailor's have had their memories wiped and been 'abandoned' on Ushas to begin the human race anew. 'future of my descendents' I had once thought to refer to the ritual castration that Appian had to go through. On a closer look that would not be the punishment for Sev's defeat as the castration was simply a tool by which Sev would be guaranteed ascension to the Pheonix Throne. I now believe that Sev's descendents refers metephorically to all the creatures of Ushas whose genesis has been made possible by Sev.

As to what was conceived between Sev and Apheta: "That something was myself" The White Fountain. Sev always refers to the white fountain coursing through space as a second and sentient self.

FunkMonkey
