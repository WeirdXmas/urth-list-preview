Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2011-02-09 Ashley Crill #

Proposes that Echidna wants Auk because he holds, or will hold, a Piece of Pas, which she seeks to stop Pas regaining full strength by killing Auk or Jerboa. Also argues that Silk is a frozen embryo made from Pas and Kypris genetic material, carried by a friend rather than a Calde mistress, and that spare Silks may exist in storage for a re-bodied Pas.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Echidna wants Auk for a Piece of Pas; Silk is a frozen embryo from Pas and Kypris; Spare Silks stored for re-bodying Pas

Apologies if someone else already gave the same answer to this: I just finished reading through the Long Sun books, and the answer to this question seemed like an obvious plot point to me. Echidna wants Auk because she has learned that he holds a Piece of Pas (or soon will hold one). Echidna wants to stop Pas from returning to full strength, and one way to do this is to kill Auk and/or Jerboa. Also, for the record, I think that Silk was a frozen embryo created from the genetic material of both Pas and Kypris. His actual host mother was a friend, but not a mistress of the Calde. These four are the four parents he sees on the Aureate Path. Also, I think that there are probably other 'Silks' in storage on the Whorl that are potential hosts for a re-bodied Pas. Typhon seems like the kind of guy who hedges his bets.
