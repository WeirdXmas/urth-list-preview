The thread: Fred Kiesche opened a thread asking whether list members had joined Facebook, mentioning a Tor notice about a Solar Cycle reading and sharing library photos. Several members replied with their own Facebook presence, including Lane Haygood, Matthew Keeley, and Jordon Flato, who gave ways to find them. Kiesche also pointed the list to Tor's Facebook page and made joking remarks about Michael Bennewitz and Son of Witz's Facebook use. The thread stayed entirely social and off-topic, with no discussion of Wolfe's fiction, and simply drifted into chatter about social networking.
  • Fred Kiesche: asks who is on Facebook and points members to Tor's page there
  • Lane Haygood: confirms he is on Facebook and invites others to search for him
  • Matthew Keeley: has a Facebook account under his name and distinguishes himself from namesakes
  • Jordon Flato: is on Facebook too and notes his unusual spelling makes him easy to find
