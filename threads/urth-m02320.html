Borski's new book

2006-03-02 Roy C. Lackey #

Informs the list that Robert Borski has a new essay collection on Gene Wolfe's fiction, published by iUniverse and listed at major booksellers. Notes that portions previously appeared in a magazine, on a web site and in list postings, and that the book is dedicated to list members.
informs

I have just learned that Robert Borski has a new book out. The title is _The LONG and the SHORT of IT_, subtitled "More Essays On The Fiction Of Gene Wolfe". It is published by iUniverse, who also brought out _Solar Labyrinth_. I am told by the author (and only after I asked!) that it is currently listed at both Barnes and Noble and Amazon.

I haven't yet seen the actual book (just an e-text version), and I learned of the book's existence only yesterday, so I haven't read it. Portions of it have appeared in the _New York Review of Science Fiction_, on Michael Andre-Driussi's siriusfiction.com web site and scattered postings made to the Urth List. The book is dedicated to the members of this list.

-Roy
