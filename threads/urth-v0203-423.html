Wolfe/Gaiman collaboration; other uncollected stories

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-03-22 to 2002-03-22

2002urthNicholas Gevers

2002-03-22 Nicholas Gevers #

Reports that Wolfe and Neil Gaiman collaborated on the chapbook A Walking Tour of the Shambles for the World Horror Convention, and asks whether it is Wolfe's only collaborative work. Lists other uncollected Wolfe stories with their anthologies: the novella "Viewpoint" in Redshift, "Pocketsful of Diamonds" in Strange Attraction, and "Shields of Mars" in Mars Probes, which the poster has not yet read.
informs

Just heard: Gene and Neil Gaiman have collaborated on
a chapbook, A WALKING TOUR OF THE SHAMBLES, for issue
at the World Horror Convention. GW's only
collaborative work ever?

Other uncollected GW stories:

"Viewpoint". Novella. REDSHIFT, ed Al Sarrantonio, Roc
2001. Fair.
"Pocketsful of Diamonds". Novelette. STRANGE
ATTRACTION, ed Ed Kramer. Bereshith 2000. Brilliant.
"Shields of Mars". Short story. MARS PROBES, ed Pete
Crowther. DAW 2002. Haven't read yet.

--Nick Gevers.
