the Vironese name Shell

2002-12-19 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Points out that the name Shell is genuinely Vironese because the text uses it, noting that a character called Shell first helps Silk after Silk is imprisoned by Oosik and is later revealed as Jerboa's acolyte. Supports the claim with specific chapter references, III and IV, from the Long Sun books.
informs · close-textual-reading

Shell is a fine Vironese name -- in fact, it was used in the text!

Shell first helps Silk when Silk is imprisoned by Oosik after being shot with a needler (III), and he turns out to be Jerboa's acolyte (IV).

=mantis=
