2005-04-05 Iorwerth Thomas #

Lays out the Kabbalistic framework of four worlds (Atzilut, Beriyah, Yetzirah, Asiyah) and their linked sephirot as the book uses them. Relies on an Ars Magica supplement as its source and links Severian to Gevurah and Isaac, while admitting the connections make things no clearer.
informs · source-and-allusion · theology-and-religion · theories: Severian is associated with Gevurah; Severian is associated with Isaac

Ok, referring to my general reference for all thing Jewish and mystical[1], we have 4 universes each corresponding to an aspect of the creative act (the book isn't very detailed on _how_ this works):

Atzilut, Beriyah, Yetzirah, Asiyah.

Each has its own tree of life; Malkhut for the next one up stands above Rachamin (Mercy), also known as Tiferet (Beauty), of the one below.

Further to some discussion months ago linking Severian with Gevurah (Strength, representing strict and unforgiving justice, tempered in the divine by the other sephira), it is also (apparantly) associated with the Old Testament figure Isaac.

I doubt this makes things any clearer.

Iorwerth

[1] The 'Ars Magica' RPG supplement 'Kabbalah: Mythic Judaism'. Not, perhaps, the most authoritative source, but most books in the game line are fairly well researched; best take this with a pinch of salt.
