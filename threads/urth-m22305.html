New edition of Peace

Urth list · 8 messages · 2012-04-25 to 2012-04-26

2012urthDarth EdAntónio Pedro MarquesFred KiescheAntonin ScriabinDaniel Petersen
The thread: Participants shared news about a new print and e-book edition of Peace, including a retailer listing and a new cover with a Neil Gaiman afterword. Fred Kiesche answered Antonin Scriabin's wish for a nice print version by giving the retailer where a copy could be pre-ordered. The thread also turned to Tor Books dropping DRM from its e-book catalogue, which Daniel Petersen flagged and António Pedro Marques supported as welcome for legitimate buyers. Darth Ed and Daniel Petersen praised the new cover and hoped later Wolfe editions would look as good. No one engaged with Wolfe's text; the thread ended in friendly agreement and drifted into cover and DRM talk.
  • Fred Kiesche: print edition is available for pre-order on Amazon, ebook not yet
  • Daniel Petersen: welcomes the Tor DRM news and plans to re-read the new edition
  • António Pedro Marques: dropping DRM is good because copy protection burdens legitimate buyers
  • Darth Ed: the new cover is one of the better Wolfe covers
  • Daniel Petersen: agrees the new cover is welcome and hopes later editions improve

2012-04-25 António Pedro Marques replying to Daniel Petersen #

Agrees with Daniel Petersen that dropping DRM on Tor Books' e-book catalog is welcome news, arguing that anti-copy protections burden legitimate buyers while pirates circumvent them. Responds directly to Petersen's quoted link to a CNET report about Tor dropping DRM across its catalog, with no reference to Wolfe's text.
