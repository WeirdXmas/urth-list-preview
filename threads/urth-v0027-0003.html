NS GRAPHIC Novel controversy

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-05-18 to 1999-05-18

1999urthMichael Andre-Driussi

1999-05-18 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes possible explanations for why a supposed Book of the New Sun graphic novel might exist only as unpublished or undistributed issues, despite a seller's claim. Raises loopholes such as small-press short runs, the Canadian publisher's location, and finished but unreleased layouts, citing a comparison to the small-press title Troll.
questions · source-and-allusion

Wombat,

So your experience matches mine, but is actually more valued because you worked in a comic book store. (Plus, since you are such a completist, I have great faith in your opinions!)

Well, obviously the seller could be mistaken (and probably is). Other possible loopholes: the issues might possibly have been produced in short runs, but never distributed? How likely is that? (I'm fairly sure that it has happened in small press genre publishing . . . wasn't that the fate of "Troll"?) (The eBay seller is in Canada--where was Innovation [or whoever published the Graphic Novel] located? Possible angle?) Final loophole: the issues were ready for publication (layout, lettering, all that technical stuff that I don't know), but never actually published? If this were the case, would there be paper artifacts which could be sold as collector's items? (Granted, such artifacts wouldn't be published issues.)

=mantis=
