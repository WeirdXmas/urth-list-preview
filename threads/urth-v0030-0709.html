New Wolfe in Dec. 2001 REALMS OF FANTASY (mild spoiler)

Urth list · 4 messages · 2001-10-11 to 2001-10-12

2001urthAdam StephanidesJohn Barach
The thread: John Barach, a newcomer to Wolfe and the list, asked whether Adam Stephanides's claim that the Realms of Fantasy story "Queen" is Wolfe's most Catholic work overlooked "The Seraph from Its Sepulchre" from Sacred Visions. Adam announced the story as a minor, short piece, calling it Wolfe's most specifically Catholic, though without textual evidence. In reply, Adam said he only vaguely remembered "Seraph," asked whether its priest was a real recollection, and argued that "Queen" is Catholic in a different sense unless "Seraph" has another Catholic element. The exchange ended unresolved, with the comparison left open.
  • John Barach: challenges the claim that "Queen" is most Catholic by pointing to "The Seraph from Its Sepulchre"
  • Adam Stephanides: calls "Queen" Wolfe's most specifically Catholic story but admits vague memory of "Seraph" and says the sense of Catholicism differs

Themes: Catholic faith and sacraments

Asks whether the priest in the Wolfe story "The Seraph from Its Sepulchre" from the anthology Sacred Visions is a real recollection, and says that "Queen" is Catholic in a different sense unless "Seraph" has some other Catholic element. Responds to John Barach's note about which Wolfe story counts as Catholic, admitting the poster only vaguely remembers the earlier story.
questions

In any case, "Queen" is Catholic in a different sense (unless there's something else about "Seraph" that's Catholic).

--Adam
