2014-04-18 Marc Aramini replying to Gwern Branwen #

Reports that a journalist called for fiction-interview questions and recalls Wolfe answering silly audience questions at a Nebula event with unrelated anecdotes. Responds to Gwern Branwen's forwarded link list, which points to a Pontin tweet and two blog posts on Wolfe's Shadow of the Torturer; the post itself is social and offers no textual argument.

I loved how when the question was asinine Wolfe would just answer with an awesome non sequitur, like telling a story about riding a runaway horse jumping over a chasm or a cow falling off a cliff as he drove by.
