2009-04-06 Bob Miller replying to Ashley Crill #

Argues that a society ruled by an unselfish monarch beats a democracy in which politicians with unsavory motives divide the populace, and that competence does not follow from good faith. Responds to Ashley Crill's point about rulers in the books with general political reasoning and no textual evidence, noting the list's nature makes examples inappropriate.
disputes

To my mind, a society ruled by an unselfish monarch who cares for the interest of his subjects has a lot of advantages over a democracy where politicians with unsavory motives pit one segment of the populace against another. The probable subsumption of competence riding with good faith seems to me to be unwarranted. I truly believe the US's current mess is partly due to ideologues believing the nonsense they enacted/enforced.
