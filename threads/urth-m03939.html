Themes: Myth and folklore sources, Suffering and sacrifice

2007-06-09 Nigel Price #

Argues that Wolfe's descent-into-the-depths episodes draw on Plato's Cave in the Republic and the Harrowing of Hell, citing the mines of Saltus in The Book of the New Sun. Proposes that this material meets Wells' Morlocks in The Time Machine, possibly also in the story Tracking Song, and says the theme of freeing captives fascinates Wolfe.
proposes · myth-and-structure · source-and-allusion · theories: Saltus mines echo Wells' Morlocks; Wolfe draws on Plato's Cave and Harrowing of Hell

Nigel wasn't still here, but Jonathan woke him up by prodding him with a sharp stick and now here he is, back again.

Yes, the Myth of the Cave in Plato's "Republic" seems to be important for Wolfe, but he also seems very interested in the Harrowing of Hell. In TBotNS, in the episode in the mines of Saltus, that interest seems to coalleasce with the story of the Morlocks in Wells' "The Time Machine", and maybe that's going on in "Tracking Song" too. Be that as it may, the theme of the hero's descent into the depths and his setting free of captives seems to fascinate Wolfe.
