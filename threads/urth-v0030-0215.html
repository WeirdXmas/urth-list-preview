2000-12-22 Mr Mnemonic #

Disputes the claim that the director's cut settled whether Deckard is a replicant, arguing that the book shows him to be human. Cites the scene where another blade runner gives Deckard a Voigt-Kampff test that finds him human, contrasting the original film's ambiguity with the cut, which leans toward android.
disputes · source-and-allusion

Well hang on now. If you read the book, Deckard is definitly not an android. At one point in the book, Deckard is wondering if he is an rep or not. He has another blade runner voigt-kampff (known as VK from now on) him, and the results show that Decard is definitly a human. In the original movie, it was rather ambiguous, and it was up to the audience to decide if they thought that Decard was an android or not, and in the cut, he pretty much was, undecided.
