Green's Cover

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-01-31 to 2001-01-31

2001whorlMichael Straight

2001-01-31 Michael Straight #

Criticizes the cover illustration of In Green's Jungles as aesthetically displeasing and factually wrong. Points out that Horn never carries a staff on Green and did not have long white hair when he met the Neighbor there, since he was still in his original body.
disputes · close-textual-reading

Not only is the cover illustration for IGJ aesthetically displeasing, it has several details wrong. Horn never has a staff on Green. And he didn't have long white hair when he met the Neighbor on Green (he was still in his original body then).

-Rostrum
