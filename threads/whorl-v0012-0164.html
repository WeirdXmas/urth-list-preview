A Few RTTW Questions [spoilers]

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunPeter Westlake

2001-02-09 Peter Westlake #

Answers a batch of reader questions about Return to the Whorl: identifies the Flyers as crew-like little people and the black gang as coal-blackened engine stokers, then rejects the idea that Horn/Silk is an inhumu because he eats and drinks. Grounds this in the book's text and a web search for the phrase 'black gang stokers', and treats the Malrubius question as plausible.
answers · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Flyers are crew of the starship Whorl; Horn/Silk is not an inhumu

...

<<<<
The Flyers are "little people", reminiscent of Fairies in their size and secretiveness. And Celticness, I suppose. They are the crew, and the bridge is the bridge of the starship Whorl.

The black gang work near the reactor, so their name is probably an old phrase for coal-blackened engine room stokers. That's just a guess, but searching Google for "black gang stokers" gives a page of results that confirm it. I do love the Web :-)

3. Are we to believe that Horn/Silk is Malrubius?

<<<<
It's an interesting idea, and quite plausible.
4. Who is the man in the cloak on Green? (p. 191)

5. There has previously been a suggestion that maybe Horn/Silk was an inhumi. It has been noted that one feature of inhumi is that they don't eat much. On p. 361, it is said of Horn/Silk: "sometimes it seemed like he was just pretending to eat and drink, and this was one of them." Wow! This is a Gene Wolfe red flare signal of something isn't it?

<<<<
He definitely does eat and drink sometimes, and since he *is* Silk, he can't be an inhumu. So far the idea that Silk just eats a lot less than Horn seems most reasonable to me.

Spectacled Bear.
