Suggests that clues about what Wolfe revealed to Marc, said to lie in the sentient-tree passages of The Book of the New Sun, may be better found in the scenes featuring Vodalus, the Liege of Leaves. Responds to Marc's hints by asking whether anyone has recently reread those Vodalus passages, admitting the poster lacks time to do so.
