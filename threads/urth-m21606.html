The thread: Lee Berman argued Catherine is a time traveler from the Atrium of Time whose death was staged, while larry miller linked the Pelerines and Mannea to the Atrium and the Last House and cited Severian's description of a trick beheading. David Stockhoff disputed that Catherine escaped to the Atrium rather than from it, questioned why Severian would skip his mother, and pressed whether the Cumaean helped her reach the Atrium. Miller revised his claim to focus on Mannea and the Cumaean, then suggested Catherine might already be dead or met Severian offstage. Stockhoff's parentage argument was a separate line, and the thread ends with Catherine's fate unresolved.

  • David Stockhoff: Catherine escaped from the Atrium, not to it; questions Severian's route and Cumaean's motives

Themes: Time loops and causality, Hidden identities and parentage, Names and their meanings

2011-10-24 Lee Berman #

Proposes that Catherine, seen on the Path of Air, is a time traveler from the Atrium of Time towers whose death is staged in the past, linking her to Valeria's later escort by soldiers. Builds the argument from the towers' layered time structure, the Citadel-to-House Absolute passageways, and Severian's ages when he sees Catherine and the sobbing woman.
proposes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Catherine is a time traveler from the Atrium of Time; Path of Air leads to Severian's future; Valeria knows Catherine is escaping a death sentence; Valeria sends soldiers to return Catherine to her fate

Okay. A little preparatory spade work is necessary though. First, I'd say the Atrium of Time itself is not itself a time travel device. HOwever it is surrounded by towers which are. We have The Last House (Master Ash) as a model of static time travel structures. Severian has trouble finding it and Valeria's tower without following the proper path through a labyrinthine entranceway. The lower levels of these time structures open earlier in time. The upper levels open later in time. Valeria lives in the lower levels of her tower. She has an antique quality and thus she likely dwells in the past.

Catherine is apparently a Citadel dweller. The Path of Air is part of the House Absolute (actually part of Father Inire's Secret House, but anyway...). What connections are there from The Citadel to The House Absolute?

We are told Ultan's Library extends from The Citadel to House Absolute. It is below ground level and Severian has to go down some stairs to get there. We are told Rudesind's Art Gallery extends from The Citadel to HOuse Absolute. It seems to be roughly at ground level. Perhaps there is another passageway connecting Citadel and House Absolute which starts at the top of one of the towers off the Atrium of Time. Hence "Path of Air." Since this pathway goes to the top of one of these towers, it exists in Severian's future. About 60 years in his future, I'd say.

Severian sees the appearances of his mother as Holy Katharine and the woman in the coffin when he is about age 20. But he sees the sobbing woman being escorted by soldiers on the day of the Flood, 60 years in his future, when Valeria is about 80. valeria is depicted as an embittered old crone, as Autarchia living in the House Absolute. But she was born in the Citadel and was a resident of an Atrium of Time tower.

I think this implies old Valeria knows about the Path of Air, knows who Catherine is and knows that she is escaping from a death sentence from 60 years in the past. She assigns her soldiers to escort her back to her fate of getting her head chopped off every year and/or being throttled by Allowin's Necklace allowing Vodalus and his followers to dig her up and eat her.

proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Pelerines linked to Atrium obfuscation technology; Mannea once inhabited the Atrium; Catherine's beheading was staged with a trick mechanism and fake head

That makes perfect sense to me. Doesnt Sev search for the Atrium from above in a flyer when Auturch? This fits nicely with The Last House that can only be seen and approached from certain angles. And it was the Pelerines (that Catherine belonged to) that sent Sev to Ash with very specific directions to find the Last House. Perhaps the Pelerines have some connection with the Temporal Obfuscation technology adherrant to these structures? Perhaps even Mannea herself once inhabited the Atrium and thus knows how said structures are to be found. In response to Jeffs question I seem to remember Sev mentioning a trick mechanism and fake severed head being used to create the illusion of Katherines beheading.

2011-10-24 David Stockhoff replying to Jeff Wilson #

Argues that Severian learned or suspected his parentage only upon becoming Autarch, citing the Talos play's line about the Contessa and Severian's interrogation of Ouen about Catherine. Builds the case point by point, then argues the orphan-tale genre explains why Wolfe leaves the parentage unstated, and rejects Andre-Driussi's Atrium of Time idea for Catherine's hiding.
proposes · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft · theories: Severian learned his parentage only upon ascending; Catherine is not hiding in the Atrium of Time

I noticed the following line in Talos' play:

FAMILIAR: The Contessa waits outside, and as she is of exalted blood, and a

favorite of our sovereign's, I beg you see her first.

Enter the CONTESSA.

This suggests Sev's mom was a favorite of the Old Autarch's. If so, this confirms for me that Sev only learned/suspected his maternity upon his ascension.

(1) He only suppresses his knowledge as he writes the Inn of Lost Loves scenes because he didn't know it at the time they occurred. He can't be accused of hiding it, just of not connecting the dots for his reader. He's being delicate in the narrative because of Dorcas as well and has no wish to be cruel to his father. So he leaves the obvious unsaid, even for the reader.

(2) AFAICT, when Sev interrogates Ouen he is only interested in figuring out Dorcas. He already suspects that she is much older than she looks, and that Ouen is her son. He has no idea Ouen is his father. He only inquires about Catherine after he is told he resembles Ouen; he puts 2+2 together because the Old Autarch knows Catherine but not Ouen. Nor, presumably, did the Autarch know about Sev or of twins, or else Sev would know about any Severa. This confirms that the Autarch had no hand in any breeding project, but Inire may have.

(3) Sev's motive for not making his parentage clear at the beginning of his story remains one of needing to set up the orphan tale, and it persists at the end. It's a genre thing: you can't have "Chapter 82: In Which Our Hero Finds His Parents" without changing the tale from "orphan makes it alone in the world" to "orphan finds success and parents." In fairy tales, plenty of orphans discover their parents, which discovery tends to dominate the resolution. BotNS satisfies this technically but not emotionally by passing it by.

Nor does Sev go in search of Catherine, because he is sure, as Old Autarch and young torturer, that she is dead. Andre-Driussi has some great ideas about the Atrium of Time, but Catherine's hiding there doesn't work, because Severian would know she still lives. Further, while Valeria as ancient, frozen child has no real flaws I can see, I don't see the point in a noble family serving the Autarchy for a chiliad by ... not even knowing time has passed. Loyalty must mean more than that.

2011-10-24 David Stockhoff replying to larry miller #

Challenges Larry Miller's suggestion that the Pelerines connect to the Atrium of Time and the Last House, arguing Catherine escaped from them rather than to them. Questions why Severian would go to the Atrium without visiting his mother, and notes that Mannea is another Pelerine named after a beheaded saint.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Pelerines connected to Atrium of Time technology; Catherine was beheaded rather than escaping

Why would the Pelerines have anything to do with the Atrium of Time?

Catherine had escaped from them---not to them---so why would she go there afterward?

What good is a secret that neither we nor the narrator can guess?

Who would have allowed Catherine to escape there when all the signs point to her death by beheading?

Severian knows the Atrium---why does he run there to marry Valeria without once stopping by to see his mother?

Mannea is yet another Pelerine named after a saint who was beheaded.

2011-10-25 David Stockhoff replying to larry miller #

Questions whether the Cumaean helped Catherine reach the Atrium of Time, following Larry Miller's idea that Mannea links to the Atrium and asking why the Cumaean would intervene. Notes that an intervention opposing a theory that the Other Lords tried to kill Dorcas would fit, and asks whether Catherine is dead.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: The Other Lords tried to kill Dorcas at Ouen's birth; The Cumaean hid Catherine in the Atrium of Time

There is a theory that the Other Lords tried to kill Dorcas, putting her in a coma at Ouen's birth. An intervention in the opposite direction makes sense as part of that theory. But why the Cumaean?

The secret I refer to is the Atrium and Catherine as its refugee. Do you think she is dead?
