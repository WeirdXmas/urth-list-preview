Dollo's Law

Urth list · 2 messages · 2011-05-17 to 2011-05-17

Themes: Evolution and the future of humanity, Ghosts, dreams and visions

2011-05-17 Lee Berman #

Notes that Dollo's Law, a principle of irreversible evolution, is mentioned in The Fifth Head of Cerberus and was central to arguments about tool use and whether the Shadow Children on Ste. Anne became human. Observes that the Law has since become a guideline with exceptions, and that the discussion later turned to human racial features.
informs · genre-and-craft

Yep, most Laws of science become more and more guidelines with exceptions as the science advances. And Genetics and Evolutionary science have certainly advanced in the past 100+ years.

Dollo's Law was mentioned by Wolfe in 5HoC. I think its mention was the linchpin of arguments on tool use (handwriting) and whether Shadow Children became humans on Ste. Anne or not. It evolved into some unpleasant real world discussions about human racial features, iirc.
