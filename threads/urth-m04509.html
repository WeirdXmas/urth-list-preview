Fifth Head of Cerberus: Shadowchildren vs. Abos

Urth list · 3 messages · 2008-04-10 to 2008-04-15

2008urthEb sharpJames Crossley
The thread: Readers debated whether the shadowchildren in The Fifth Head of Cerberus are the original inhabitants who became the abos by mimicking human colonists, or degenerate settlers. E proposed that Wolfe's chat remark about the A Story narrator being a shadowchild makes VRT a shadowchild or shadowchild-abo hybrid. James Crossley disputed this and offered his own account of the shadow-children as original natives who took humanoid forms and came to be called abos. b sharp supported E's original-inhabitants reading and extended it, suggesting that natives of both planets may be replaced humans, with Marsch replaced by an abo. The thread ends with the three posts stating their readings, with no clear resolution.
  • E: VRT may be a shadowchild or shadowchild-abo hybrid able to shapeshift, based on Wolfe's chat remark and Marsch's journal
  • James Crossley: shadow-children were the original inhabitants who mimicked colonists to gain humanoid forms and came to be called abos
  • b sharp: shadow children became abos by mimicking first colonists; later colonists replaced by shapeshifting abos, so Marsch may be replaced by an abo

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Beasts and the animal in us

2008-04-10 E #

Argues that Wolfe's chat remark that the 'A Story' narrator was a shadowchild replacing John V. Marsch makes VRT a shadowchild, possibly a shadowchild-abo hybrid able to shapeshift. Grounds this in the quoted interview, Marsch's journal describing VRT as human, and the earlier debate over whether shadowchildren are original abos or degenerate settlers.
proposes · author-and-biography · puzzle-solving · theories: VRT is a shadow-child replacing Marsch; VRT is a shadow-child and abo hybrid

I found a transcript of a chat w/ Gene Wolfe

http://home.austin.rr.com/lperson/wolfe.html

Here is an interesting quote:
LP: "'A Story,' by John V. Marsch."

GW: "'A Story,' by John V. Marsch," yes, which is not actually written by John V. Marsch, but by the shadowchild who has replaced John V. Marsch. (laughs) That's New Wave. But belonging to a literary movement doesn't consist so much in using a certain set of techniques, as it consists in running with a certain set of people, and only to a very small degree did I run with that set of people. So as I said, I would be very peripheral as a New Wave writer.

So if Wolfe is being truthful, Marsch was replaced by a shadow-child, not a humanoid abo. Meaning that VRT is a shadow-child.

I know there was an unresolved debate about whether shadow-children are the original abos or are the result of early space fairing humans degenerating due to addiction to the drug found on the planet.

Anyway, this doesn't really clear that argument up, but if Wolfe is truthful it certainly makes me think differently about the story, because "VRT" was described as a human in Marsch's journal... but then again, maybe VRT edited even the earliest parts of Marsch's journal to simply say that VRT looked human to Marsch?

If what Wolfe says is true this also means the shadow-children themselves (or at least VRT) can shapeshift... which we hadn't seen discussed or mentioned before, it seemed that the shadow children weren't able to do this in "A Story".

Might VRT be a shadow-child + abo hybrid, rather than human + abo hybrid??

Anyway this has left me more confused than ever about the abo vs shadowchild vs settler conundrum but I certainly found Wolfe's quote interesting and surprising.

Maybe you guys have other ideas about this. Then again, Wolfe is quoted as laughing after saying the VRT = shadowchild line...

2008-04-10 James Crossley replying to E #

Argues that the shadow-children of The Fifth Head of Cerberus were the planet's original inhabitants, who gained humanoid forms only by mimicking the human colonizers and came to be called abos. Responds to E's quoted claim that Wolfe's chat shows Marsch was replaced by a shadow-child and that VRT is one, offering the poster's own reading of the shadow-children's origins instead.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Shadow-children were original inhabitants who mimicked colonists; Shadow-children became known as abos

[snip]

My understanding was that the shadow children were the original inhabitants, able to shapeshift but lacking a humanoid model until the humans arrived and colonized the planet. Once that happened, the shadow children mimicked the colonizers (becoming known to them as "abos" along the way) sometimes so successfully that they might forget their true nature.

James

2008-04-15 b sharp replying to E #

Argues that the shadow children of The Fifth Head of Cerberus became the human-like abos by mimicking the first colonists, and that later colonists were replaced by abos who kept their shapeshifting. Supports E's reading of the original inhabitants and extends it, suggesting that all natives of both planets may be replaced humans, with Marsch replaced by an abo.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Abos are shadow children who mimicked colonists; All natives of both planets are replaced humans; Marsch was replaced by an abo

able to shapeshift but lacking a humanoid model until the humans arrived and colonized the planet. Once that happened, the shadow children mimicked the colonizers (becoming known to them as "abos" along the way) sometimes so successfully that they might forget their true nature.<

That's how I see it too. Humans came to the planets in several waves. The first ship crashed and the few survivors became the template for some shadow children to become human-like abos. Later waves brought civilized humans but they were subject to replacement by abos, who despite losing their memory of being shadow children, retained their shape changing ability, perhaps as an unconscious reflex. It might be that all the natives of both planets are abos who have replaced the real humans. Marsch was a true human from earth (not a particularly nice one) but was soon replaced by an abo.
