2010-08-04 Gwern Branwen replying to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Recalls a news item about dark matter in the sun's core and links it to Stephen Baxter's Xeelee novel Ring, where dark matter photino birds cool the Sun. Notes that Baxter's mechanism and purpose differ from the news report's black-hole idea; the post names no Wolfe passage and is an aside about another author's work.
informs

Huh. I remember this from Stephen Baxter's Xeelee universe (specifically _Ring_) - the dark matter 'photino birds' were deliberately cooling the Sun to lengthen its lifespan and thus the timespan in which they could reproduce using it (something about dark matter needing high gravity to reach usable concentrations).

(Which is, of course, an entirely different mechanic and purpose than black holes in the Sun's core.)
