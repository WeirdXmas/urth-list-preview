2012-03-29 Stephen Case #

Praises Marc's summer project of posting summaries of Wolfe's stories and asks whether each story is collected and where, or if it is uncollected and available online. Sent to the list as a thanks and a request for bibliographic information about the stories Marc has outlined so far.
questions

Marc,

I just want to voice my appreciation for this summer project of yours. Normally I scan the list-serve emails pretty quickly for Wolfe news, but I'm stopping to read each of your story posts. They're a great walk through Wolfe, and I hope you keep them up. One question: you mention where the stories are originally published, but can you also mention where they are collected? Perhaps you've already commented on this, but the stories you've outlined so far are unfamiliar to me. Are they uncollected? Are they available online?

-Steve
