1998-05-13 Mark Kelly #

Asks for a complete bibliography of published works set in the Urth universe, listing titles he knows and asking whether any others exist. He also asks whether Empires of Foliage and Flower is still in print in the US and what it is about.
questions

Hi everyone,

I've just joined the list, so forgive me if this is a FAQ, but I've = skimmed through the Urth list archives with no success and there is no = FAQ (maybe there are no frequently asked questions on this list - maybe = questions are only ever asked once?!)

I haven't been able to find a complete bibliography for published works = set in the Urth universe (or about it) (excluding the Long Sun / Short = Sun books). So far I am aware of:
- TBOTNS, TUOTNS (obviously)
- Empires of Foliage and Flower
- The two short stories in Endangered Species ("The Cat" and "The Map")

"Non-fiction":
- Castle of the Otter
- Lexicon Urthus (& supplements)

Is this list complete? Are there any other short stories by Wolfe set in = the Urth universe, and if so where can I find them?

Also, AFAIK "Empires" has never been published here in the UK. Is it = still in print in the US, and if so is it worth trying to order it? What = is it about?

Any information is gratefully received!

Mark.

1998-05-13 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists bibliographic leads for Urth-related Wolfe material, including the Cheap Street chapbook 'The Old Woman Whose Rolling Pin Is the Sun' and the novella 'Empires of Foliage and Flower' in CRANK! and Weird Tales. Gives places to find the works and names critical books on Wolfe, answering Mark's bibliography request.
informs

Mark,

Another Urth story might be/probably is "The Old Woman Whose Rolling Pin Is the Sun," a tiny chapbook published by Cheap Street and likely impossible to find.

Re: "Empires of Foliage and Flower." Best bet is to buy the issue of CRANK! magazine (number 2, 1993) which ran this novella. Then, having read the story, you can better decide whether you want to pay hundreds of dollars for the beautiful yet pricey Cheap Street edition.

The Cheap Street EFF is subtitled, "A Tale From the Book of Wonders of Urth and Sky."

Another Cheap Street book with the same subtitle is THE BOY WHO HOOKED THE SUN. This one can also be found in "Weird Tales No. 290" (Spring 1988).

For non-fiction, there is also:

PLAN[E]T ENGINEERING by Gene Wolfe, NESFA Press.
GENE WOLFE by Joan Gordon, Starmont Press.
STROKES by John Clute, Serconia Press.

and a slew of articles in journals and magazines, a list that I should have around here somewhere but can't find at the moment.

=mantis=
