2009-04-24 James Wynn replying to Stuart Hamm #

Argues that the narrative never explains what happened to Mucor or Babbie, and that Marble would not leave Blue without her, so Mucor probably died. Also finds it unlikely Nettle would abandon her grandchildren on Blue or seek them on Green, and notes she would not tell Daisy if she meant to go to the Whorl; responds to Stuart Hamm's unanswered question.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Mucor has died

No one has ever explained this to my knowledge because I don't think the narrative explains it. Since Marble would presumably not leave Blue without her, a reasonable guess would be that Mucor has died. For that matter, I don't recall any explanation as to what happened to Babbie.

Incidentally, since Nettle was not following *Horn* back to Viron, I've always found it unlikely that Nettle would leave her own (soon to arrive) grandchildren on Blue nor seek to go to Green to find her grandchildren there. If Nettle intended to go to the Whorl in order to get to Green, it is remarkable that she would not tell Daisy.

J.
