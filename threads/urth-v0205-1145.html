2002-05-30 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Replies to Dragonfly's quoted remarks on sin as bondage by noting that Paul teaches all are sinners and that believers remain sinners after salvation. Points out the Catholic and Orthodox view that Mary was exempt from original sin through the immaculate conception, and cites Paul's 'doing what I hate' passage.
supports · theology-and-religion

Oh. Well, you know we're all a bunch of pagan goddess-worshipping slaves of Rome, I presume, so what more would you need to know? 8*)

Of course not -- St Paul is clear on this; all are sinners (tho' Catholics and, I believe, Orthodox believe that Mary is an exception, not through her own doing but through the "immaculate conception," a special miracle which allowed her to be born without the taint of original sin, as a fitting "vessel" for Christ); and even after salvation, we continue to be sinners -- Paul speaks of "doing what I hate," etc.

--Dan'l
