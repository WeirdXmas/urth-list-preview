Greetings:

I had started a re-read of "New Sun", but got completely side-swiped and off track by my grandmother's decline and demise.

During all the hue and cry, I did manage a read of "Build-a-Bear" by Wolfe, which appeared in Jim Baen's Universe, an electronic magazine (from Baen Books), Volume 1, Issue 1. Somewhat creepy, but the best fantasy story in that issue.

I posted a brief review of each story in the magazine on my blog (link below), look for "The Year in Magazine Shorts: 2006" on the left column of links.
