1997-03-26 Kieran Mullen #

Argues that Tick's word "add word" is a euphemism for a swear word, a reading Kieran Mullen first held, then drops in favour of the list's consensus. Reasons from the alternatives "wadd word" and "add erd", which would be the forms if it were a simple spelling shift, and from the surrounding context.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: "add word" is a euphemism for a swear word

I had always thought that that "add word" was Tick's way of saying "Bad word" - a euphemism for swearing. Otherwise it would be either "wadd word" (b -> w) or "add erd" (drop initial b's). But from the context I think was wrong, and the CW on the list is right.

Kieran Mullen
