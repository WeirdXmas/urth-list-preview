Wiki?

Urth list · 4 messages · 2007-06-20 to 2007-06-21

2007urthDaniel D JonesMatthew KingMo Holkar / UKG
The thread: Members asked whether a Wolfe wiki proposed earlier was ever started. Daniel D Jones said interest existed but a project without a leader would only succeed if someone took the initiative, and doubted enough contributors would join. Mo Holkar offered to host and administer a wiki himself, though he could not populate it alone. Matthew King proposed a MediaWiki, asking about open versus member-only editing, starting structure, and who would moderate, citing Lostpedia's separation of speculative theories. Holkar countered with PmWiki because his hosting could not meet MediaWiki's MySQL needs, favored member-only editing, and offered administrative moderation while staying out of academic disputes. The thread ends with the setup debate unresolved and a suggestion that discussion move off-list.
  • Daniel D Jones: a wiki needs a leader and someone to initiate it, with uncertain contributor interest
  • Mo Holkar / UKG: offers to host and administer a wiki, prefers PmWiki and member-only editing
  • Matthew King: proposes a MediaWiki with separate pages for speculation, modeled on Lostpedia

2007-06-20 Daniel D Jones replying to Mo Holkar / UKG #

Argues that a Wolfe wiki project lacks a leader and will only succeed if someone takes the initiative to set it up. Responds to Mo Holkar's question about whether the proposed wiki idea has been started, saying interest exists but it is unclear whether enough contributors will join to make it useful.
answers

As near as I can tell, this is a project in search of a leader. If someone takes the initiative to get things configured and going, then there are a number of interested people who would likely take advantage of it. As to whether there's enough people to create a critical mass and create something useful or not is an open question. I don't think anyone will know until and unless someone is willing to get things going.

2007-06-20 Matthew King replying to Daniel D Jones #

Proposes setting up a MediaWiki for the list, asking whether editing should be open to all or limited to members and what starting structure to use. Points to Lostpedia as a model that keeps speculative theories on separate pages, and asks who would volunteer as moderator. Responds to Daniel D Jones's remark that the wiki project needs a leader.
proposes

Let's pretend for the sake of discussion that we already have MediaWiki installed, and now we're deciding how to configure it.

1) Should the wiki be editable by anyone? Should we reserve editing to list members only?

A halfway option would be to restrict a set of core pages to list members only to prevent vandalism, but to allow the unwashed masses to create and edit pages at the periphery. This would provide for reading journals or the like.

2) What kind of structure, if any, should the wiki start with?

http://lostpedia.com provides a decent example of a full-fledged MediaWiki site. I like that they reserve wild speculation about episodes or characters to "theories" pages.

3) Who wants to be a moderator?

2007-06-21 Mo Holkar / UKG replying to Matthew King #

Proposes using PmWiki instead of MediaWiki because the poster's hosting cannot meet MediaWiki's MySQL requirements, and argues the wiki should be editable only by members who join separately. Responds to Matthew King's configuration questions, offering to moderate administratively while staying out of academic disputes and suggesting the discussion may move off-list.
proposes

Unfortunately I'm not able to install MediaWiki on my hosting, because of the specific MySQL requirements it makes: so if that's a sine qua non, I'll have to withdraw my offer.

My suggestion would be to use PmWiki, which has pretty similar functionality in the important areas:
http://www.wikimatrix.org/compare/MediaWiki+PmWiki
and I think should be fine for a small (numbers of people) project like this

It's not going to be practical, AFAIK, to have membership of the wiki automatically (and securely) respond to changes in the list membership. List users are going to have to join the wiki separately. In which case I would say we should reserve editing to wiki members only.

I would personally tend to allow anyone who goes to the effort of joining the wiki to edit it, just as anyone who goes to the effort of joining this list can post to it. But I don't feel strongly about that: it's easy enough to have areas restricted to certain groups, etc, if that's felt necessary.

I have no strong views about structure.

I'm happy to moderate in the administrative sense, but I would prefer not to be involved in eg. mattes of quasi-academic dispute ;-)

By the way, if people think this discussion should be taken off list, do please say,

best,

Mo
