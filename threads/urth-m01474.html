Jonas, Ship, and ships

2005urthThe Book of the New SunJosh Young

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

2005-05-02 Josh Young #

Argues that the Ship is a singular, all-powerful vessel distinct from ordinary ships, comparing it to God versus gods, and suggests the different names (Fortunate Cloud, Quasar) may be the same Ship called by different names in different areas. Responds to Josh Young's earlier post noting Hethor's ship name and the idea of a single ship in The Urth of the New Sun, offering the naming explanation as a tentative reconciliation.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: The Ship is one vessel known by different names in different areas

I think there's a difference between the Ship and other ships. Sort of like the difference between "God" and "gods." One is all powerful, omnipresent... And the others are imitations. *Shrug* Incidentally, the name of Hethor's ship was "The Quasar."

Although... Now that I think about it, it's possible that the different areas aboard the Ship (Gunnie, or someone, says that they tend to keep the crewmen from the same worlds grouped together.) just refer to the Ship by different names.... Like, On Urth, it's Fortunate Cloud, maybe on some other world it's Quasar... Etc.
