2002-08-15 Craig Brewer #

Asks whether others have worked out how and why Silk is connected to Master Malrubius, a link Wolfe reportedly mentioned in an interview. Wonders why an aquastor like Malrubius would be the one to communicate with Severian and what purpose that would serve.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk is connected to Master Malrubius

I've finally finished reading through the archives,
and I'm left with a question (which I hope I just
haven't overlooked).

In an interview, GW mentions a possible connection
between Silk and Master Malrubius (I can't locate the
interview at the moment). I was wondering if anyone
else had determined how/why this connection would be
made. Why, for example, would an aquastor (as I recall
Malrubius is) Silk be used to communicate with
Severian? I find it an intriguing connection between
the books, but I'm having trouble understanding what
purpose it would serve.

Craig

2002-08-16 Christopher Culver #

Proposes that Severian's visions of Malrubius were really of Silk, and that the machine which rescues Severian from the Ascians made an aquastor of Malrubius because those visions had left a strong impression on him. Responds to Craig's question about why Silk would communicate through an aquastor, offering this as the reason for the connection.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Severian's visions of Malrubius were really Silk; The machine made an aquastor of Malrubius because of Severian's impression

I assume that Severian's incidental visions of Malrubius were actually of Silk, but the machine which saves Severian from the Ascians chose to create an aquastor of Malrubius because Sev's visions of Malrubius-really-Silk had made such an impression on him.

Christopher Culver
