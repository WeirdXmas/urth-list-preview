The thread: Roy C. Lackey and Andy Robertson debated whether Able is the Green Knight of the Arthurian tale. Lackey argued that Abel, not Gawain, is the Green Knight in the story Able tells Leort, and that Able's head was cut off rather than nicked, citing Able's own account of picking up his head. Robertson argued that Able is the Green Knight, beheaded by Gawain, and pointed to the Green Chapel return blow, where Gawain receives only a nick, and linked an online Gawain text. Chris sided with the Gawain story's logic, explaining that the Green Knight was beheaded yet walked away by supernatural power, with a return blow due a year later, and questioned how Lackey's Wizard Knight quotation connects to Leort. The thread ended without resolution.
  • Roy C. Lackey: Abel, not Gawain, is the Green Knight in Able's story, and Able's head was cut off, not nicked
  • Andy Robertson: Able is the Green Knight from the Arthurian tale, beheaded by Gawain, with the return blow a nick
  • Chris: the Green Knight was beheaded but survived by supernatural power, and the link to Leort is unclear

2005-12-01 Roy C. Lackey #

Corrects a earlier claim by noting that Abel, not Gawain, is the Green Knight in the story Able tells Leort, and that Able's head was cut off rather than nicked. Cites Able's own account in the book, where he picks up his head and tucks it under his arm, as evidence.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Abel is the Green Knight

Able's original shield was plain green. He doesn't actually use the designation "Green Knight" when he relates his little story to Leort, but that's clearly the story. And Able doesn't get off with a nick -- according to him, his head was cut off and bounced across the floor. Able stood up, picked up his head, and tucked it under his arm. (W, 98)

-Roy

2005-12-01 Andy Robertson replying to Roy C. Lackey #

Argues that Able is the Green Knight from the Arthurian tale, who challenged Arthur's knights to the beheading game and was beheaded by Gawain. Supports the point by recounting the Green Chapel return blow, where Gawain receives only a nick after the bedchamber episode, and links to an online Gawain text.
supports · myth-and-structure · source-and-allusion · theories: Able is the Green Knight

Abel was THE GREEN KNIGHT - the guy who turned up at Camelot and challenged Arthur's knights to the Beheading Game

Gawain was the knight who took up the challenge and lopped off Abel's head.

Abel picks up his head and requires Gawain to come to THe Green Chapel in a year's time and get a return blow.

But when Gawain does - after some boudoir comedy with what was probably Morcaine - Gawain only gets a nick on the neck

2005-12-02 Chris P replying to Roy C. Lackey #

Explains that Able, the Green Knight, had his head cut off by Gawain yet walked away by supernatural power, with a return blow due a year later. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's quotation from The Wizard Knight about Able's head, supplying the Gawain story context and questioning how it connects to Leort.
answers · source-and-allusion · theories: Able is the Green Knight of Arthurian legend

Yes, the Green Knight (Able) gets his head cut clean off by Gawain - but by supernatural power laughs it off and walks away. The deal was that he would be able to deliver the same blow to Gawain a year later.

I don't know what the connection is with Leort, unless the idea is to remind him, when he had the advantage over Able, that he might not always have that advantage.
