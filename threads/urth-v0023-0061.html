Suggests that the painted bus in the thread recalls Ken Kesey and the Merry Pranksters from The Electric Kool-Aid Acid Test, or other 1960s buses and VW minibuses. Proposes that readers check late Beat or early Hippie books for a similar sequence of vehicles, noting the image had become a cliche.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Painted bus alludes to Kesey and the Merry Pranksters

The painted bus sounds like Ken Kesey and the Merry Pranksters (see _The Electric Kool-Aid Acid Test_), or some other '60s bus (I remeber seeing those painted buses and VW minibuses). It was common enough to become a cliche.

If anyone's read that book, or one of the other late Beat or early Hippie books, there may be such a sequence of vehicles.

-John Bishop
