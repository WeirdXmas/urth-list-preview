Outsider=Pas & Kypris?

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies

2005-06-24 James Wynn #

Relays Nastler's objection to the idea that Pas and Kypris are the voices Silk hears during his enlightenment, arguing Pas could have communicated more directly and that the Outsider distinguishes himself from the Whorl's gods. Offers alternative explanations: Kypris and Pas becoming the Outsider over the 300-year voyage, and Gnostic and alchemical symbolism Wolfe is riffing on.
disputes · theology-and-religion · puzzle-solving · theories: Pas and Kypris are the voices Silk hears; Kypris and Pas become the Outsider over the voyage; Silk's enlightenment is Gnostic and alchemical symbolism

Nastler says:

Marc's argument is intriguing, but if Pas had the mental wherewithall to communicate with Silk so definitively on the ball court and in Hotel garden, then I'm not sure why he needed Awk, Silk, Sand, and Patera Jerboa. Kypris was on the run, but Pas was "killed"....with relics of him
distributed in a few animals like Oreb, in chems, and in the minds of a few people.

Most important, in the Outsider's discussion with Silk at the Hotel pool, he explicitly differentiates himself from the Gods of the Whorl. He says they are *becoming* him by
mimicking him (Remember Mint's argument that Spider's
spies will have to fight and work like the most devoted
citizens so they will be trusted).

There are a lot of potential reasons why Silk hears male and female voices during his enlightenment even if we
could say for a fact that the voices were Pas's and Kypris's. For example: Since Kypris was already *becoming* the
Outsider and during 300 year voyage of the ship, Pas
surely was well along that process, the Outsider talked to them in the voices of his images on the ship.

But there are other reasons for this in the themes and
symbols of Gnosticism and alchemy that Wolfe his riffing on.

~ Crush
