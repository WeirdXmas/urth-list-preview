2002-11-02 Fr Kipling Cooper #

Introduces the poster as a returning reader who first abandoned The Book of the New Sun years ago, then came back through The Fifth Head of Cerberus and has since read Castleview and Soldier of the Mist. Recounts this history to greet the list and says the archives helped with Soldier of the Mist.
chats

Greetings!

How I got here:

I first started TBotNS shortly after all four volumes were released through the SF Book Club, and thought to my horror, that I had mistakenly picked up 'Finnegan's Wake.' (Or something with an equally innovative use of vocabulary) So I rid myself of them at my next move, and read no further Wolfe until earlier this year. While investigating classic SF, and what was accounted as such, 5th HEAD OF CERBERUS and TBotNS frequently appeared, so when I found an anthology with the short story/novella/novellette 5th Head, I had a read and discovered to my astonishment that it was written in English, and written VERY WELL!

Over the last 6 months I have also finished CASTLEVIEW and SOLDIER OF THE MIST and am restarting TBotNS, finding it much less daunting that 10+ years ago. I have greatly appreciated the archives of this mailing list, especially while reading MIST.

I am glad to be here.
--
