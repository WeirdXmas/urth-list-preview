Nasty Germany in 30s without AH [Digest urth.v030.n163]

Argues that 1930s Germany without alternate history would persecute Jews and Gypsies for labour but would not pursue extermination in practice, since it would weaken the state. Cites the Soviet Union's treatment of Jews and Gypsies as its evidence, and is off-topic, not about Wolfe at all.
proposes · theories: Non-AH Germany would not pursue Holocaust extermination

I can easily accept that a non AH Germany would be nationalistic and anti-Semetic; I find it hard to believe that it would be so impractically idealistic as to do more than rob and mistreat worker-units of the low-status groups; that would be a waste of good worker-units. Devoting precious rail-transport bandwidth and army/police time to extermination just weakens the State.

As many have observed, the Soviet Union is the obvious example of how a natioanlistic, totalitarian, anti-Semitic country treats its Jews and Gypsies if the Leader isn't an idealist: it's not nice, but it's not a Holocaust.

-John
