I believe almost all the vocabulary in this series are "real life words" which have meaning in our world. Yes, the Jungle Hut scene surely took place a few decades in our past. Borsky contends that Isangoma is really Father Inire and I tend to agree. Not such a stretch to think Father Inire could fit in as a witch doctor in early 20th centuray Africa. And he is the source of much of the Earth material and legend which can be found on Urth.
