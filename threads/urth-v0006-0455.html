Themes: Books, libraries and writing

1998-01-28 Alice Turner #

Argues that the copy of The Book of the New Sun given to Thecla in her cell is a printed book from a print run, not Severian's manuscript. Answers Christopher's question by citing TNOTNS, which distinguishes manuscripts from books, and speculates on a brown buckram cover to show how Urth books survive.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Thecla's copy of the book is a printed book

Christopher,

There is no reason to think that it is not a printed book, one of a print run, very likely with a buckram cover (I speculate on the brown cover to make the point that on Urth books as they are today survive). TNOTNS differentiates between manuscripts and books, and it is definitely a book.

-alga-
