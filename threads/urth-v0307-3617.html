2003-07-10 jonathan mann #

Proposes that the time loops in The Book of the New Sun might mirror Wolfe's drafting process, with each loop adding another iteration to Severian, as a reply to mantis. Builds on mantis's point that the loops in Free Live Free optimize the looped person, and on the Heinlein and Groundhog Day comparisons in the quoted opening.
proposes · author-and-biography · myth-and-structure · theories: Severian's loops are Wolfe's drafting iterations; Loops optimize the looped person in Free Live Free

There's also FREE LIVE FREE, in which the loops 'optimize' the person being looped. Never let it be said that Wolfe underexplores a theme. Maybe there's more than one Wolfe, and different books with similar themes are by different versions.
