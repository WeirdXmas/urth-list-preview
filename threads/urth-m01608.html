The thread: The thread centered on a mock review attributed to Wolfe in the Argentus zine, shared as a link. Maru Dubshinki found it so bad it was funny and suggested spotting its deliberate references and errors, citing 'Kismet' as an example. mournings glory judged the comedy weak and argued Wolfe should not often attempt humour, while Jeff Wilson countered that nearly everything Wolfe writes has some comic content. Much of the exchange was playful, with Sarah Dorrance-Minch joking about algolagnia and kip tanner offering a coined etymology for 'algolagniaphobia'. The disagreement over Wolfe's humour was not resolved, and the thread drifted into wordplay.

  • Jeff Wilson: nearly everything Wolfe writes has comedic content, however dark

2005-09-20 Jeff Wilson replying to mournings glory #

Replies to a link to a mock review by Wolfe in Argentus, arguing that nearly everything Wolfe writes contains some comedic content, however dark. Responds to mournings glory's dismissal of Wolfe's comic side and glosses the joke word 'algolagniaphobe' as fear of sadists.
disputes · close-textual-reading

My mileage does, but only in that it seems to me that almost everything he writes has some comedic content, however black

alg=pain, lagnia=arousal, phobe=fear; afraid of sadists

2005-09-20 Kip Tanner replying to mournings glory #

Proposes that the word 'algolagniaphobia' is a blend of 'algophobia' (fear of pain) and 'lagniappe' (a small gratuity), reading it as a fear of painful gratuity. Offers this as a playful coinage in reply to mournings glory, who had raised the word.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: algolagniaphobia blends algophobia and lagniappe

'algolagniaphobia' ?
Could be from 'algophobia' - fear of pain, and 'lagnappe' - gratuity. Fear of painful gratuity?

Gotta love Wolfe and his corny coinage.

-KT
