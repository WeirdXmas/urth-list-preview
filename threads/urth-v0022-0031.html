Young Wolfe

Urth list · 4 messages · 1998-12-14 to 1998-12-15

1998urthKevin J. MaroneyMichael Andre-DriussiDerek Bell
The thread: Derek Bell asked whether list members had read Young Wolfe, a collection Amazon attributes to Gene Wolfe, and requested comments. Kevin J. Maroney answered that the book is minor but includes several early Wolfe stories never printed elsewhere, such as "Volksweapon," and said he was glad to have bought it. M.driussi listed the stories collected, noting which were never reprinted and which two were previously unpublished, correcting an earlier remark by Wombat with titles from the AE&2 bibliography. Derek later linked The Dead Man to The Other Dead Man in Endangered Species, recalled a possibly Pohl-bought earlier version, thanked the posters, and said he planned to buy the collection. The thread ends in informational agreement with no real dispute.

1998-12-14 Kevin J. Maroney #

Reports that the book Young Wolfe is minor but contains several early Wolfe stories never printed elsewhere, including "Volksweapon", and says the poster is glad to have bought it. Replies to Derek's Amazon query asking whether anyone has read the collection and what they think of it.
informs

It's minor, but it does contain several early Wolfe stories which have never been printed elsewhere, including the wonderfully titled "Volksweapon". I certainly have never regretted buying it, but I'm a certified Completist.

Wombat, a.k.a. Kevin Maroney [email]

1998-12-14 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists the early Wolfe stories collected in Young Wolfe, noting which were never reprinted elsewhere and which two were previously unpublished, with their original publication venues. Responds to Wombat's earlier remark by correcting it and supplying the titles from the AE&2 bibliography.
informs · source-and-allusion

What Wombat said, except that he meant to say "several early Wolfe stories which have never been REprinted elsewhere and two early stories previously unpublished." Very hard to find most of them in any other form--impossible to find the last two. The stories are (I'm cribbing from "AE&2" page 1 here):

"The Case of the Vanishing Ghost" [1]
"The Grave Secret" [1]
"The Dead Man" [2]
"Mountains Like Mice" [appeared in "If"]
"The Green Wall Said" [appeared in "New Worlds"]
"Screen Test" [3]
"Volksweapon" [3]
"The Largest Luger" [4] (a mystery)
"The Last Casualty of Cambrai" [4] (a mystery)

[1] in a college magazine
[2] appeared in "Sir!" and, much later, "Weird Tales"
[3] appeared in "Mike Shayne's Mystery Magazine"
[4] the unpublished ones

=mantis=

1998-12-15 Derek Bell #

Notes that an early Wolfe story listed in a bibliography, The Dead Man, resembles the later story The Other Dead Man in Endangered Species, and recalls that an editor, possibly Pohl, bought the earlier story with a slightly amended title. Responds to Wombat's and Mantis's bibliographic list of early stories and thanks them while planning to buy the collection.
informs · source-and-allusion

Interesting, given that one of the stories in _Endangered Species_ is called _The Other Dead Man_.

I've heard of this one - Wolfe mentioned it and I think a big name editor of the time (Pohl?) bought it, amending the title slightly.

Many thanks to Mantis and Wombat; I'll look into buying this in the next few months.

Derek - completist, not certified (but not far from being)
