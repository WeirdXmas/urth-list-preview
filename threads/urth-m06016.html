Turnip to T-Bone

Urth list · 7 messages · 2008-07-23 to 2008-07-31

2008urthThe Book of the New SunStuart HammKurt SchwindMo Holkar / UKGPaul B
The thread: Stuart Hamm opened by asking whether a book titled Turnip to T-Bone on eBay, apparently an Oklahoma history, was by Gene Wolfe, and where to buy a Silk for Calde bumper sticker. The thread quickly turned into joking chat about merchandise. Paul B and Mo Holkar pointed to print-on-demand sites selling Wolfe-related shirts and stickers, and Holkar noted one shirt seemed to quote an extensive excerpt from the books. Later Hamm proposed a Killroy-style graffiti campaign for the phrase, and Paul B joked that chalk would be less vandalistic. The thread never engaged the books' content and ended in light social banter.
  • Stuart Hamm: asked about the eBay book and a bumper sticker, then floated a Silk for Calde graffiti campaign
  • Paul B: reported a print-on-demand bumper sticker exists and suggested making one, later joked chalk beats graffiti
  • Mo Holkar: shared a shirt link and noted it appears to quote extensive text from the books

Themes: Books, libraries and writing
