Isid Iooo IoooE ("Hoo-oo-WEE!")

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-12-18 to 1999-12-18

1999urthMitch Bailey

1999-12-18 Mitch Bailey #

Proposes that the name Isid Iooo IoooE is a code, suggesting that capital I's and lower-case o's be read as 1s and 0s, giving ISID 1000 1000E, and possibly a machine model number. Responds to Mr. Ansley's letter about the name's observation, and offers a further reading that treats the s as a 5 and the parts as hexadecimal numbers such as 151D, 1000, 1000E, while admitting their significance is unclear.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Isid Iooo IoooE is a machine or computer model number; I and o letters substitute for 1 and 0 digits

Let me chime in impertinently here to Mr. Ansley's letter of the 16th, = as I haven't read the story yet, but was intrigued by the observation

). I have always suspected that the
answer involves replacing the capital Is in the second and third parts = of
the name with 1s and the lower-case os with 0s. Thus I get ISID 1000 = 1000E,
if I make the first part uppercase at the same time. Unfortunately, this doesn't suggest anything definite to me either, although it does look = like
the identifying number of a machine, a computer model number, for = example.

I suppose you could read that "Isid" as I.S. Identification [number], = whatever I.S. might stand for.

On the other hand, William, since you read some I's as 1's to get = apparent hexadecimal numbers, try reading the "s" as a numeral 5 as = well? I don't know the context in which this name comes to us, but if it = comes of unsophisticated people trying to decipher nearly illegible = labeling, then a "s" for "5" sub seems reasonable. Then you could read = the whole assemblage as two 64-bit hex numbers and one 128-bit hex = number:

151D, 1000, 1000E

Now, what their significance might be, I still couldn't say, but maybe = this is a step in the right direction.

BTW, in honor of my new personal account, I have chosen a list name for = myself which I'm sure all can agree is quite appropriate.

Talarican, a.k.a. Mitchell Bailey
