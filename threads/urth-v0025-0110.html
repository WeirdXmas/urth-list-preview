Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Hidden identities and parentage

1999-04-21 Charles Dye #

Argues that the bent figure who escorts Severian in Chapter XXI of The Claw of the Conciliator may be Father Inire, appearing twice in the book. Responds to Dan Rabin's quoted post citing Robert Borski by pointing out that this episode mentions no mask, so the identification rests on the figure's cowled habit and availability to the Autarch.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: The bent figure in Claw XXI is Father Inire

Father Inire might even appear twice. In Chapter XXI of "Claw", the old Autarch sends Severian away in the custody of "a bent little man" in a "cowled habit like a cenobite's." Severian takes little notice of this servant, though he is instantly available to the Autarch in the Secret House. No mention of a mask in this episode, however.
