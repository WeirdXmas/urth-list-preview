Wolfe article in Chicago Tribune

Informs the list that a Chicago Tribune Sunday magazine article on Wolfe, titled "The Urth Man Cometh", is available online. Notes that the piece quotes Wolfe's wife Rosemary saying he most resembles the character Silk, which the poster finds interesting.
informs · author-and-biography

The Sunday Magazine of the Oct. 5 Chicago Tribune has a long article about Wolfe titled "The Urth Man Cometh." There isn't much that will be news to most lupine types but one comment attributed to Wolfe's wife, Rosemary, that of all Wolfe's characters the one he most resembles is Silk, I find very interesting.

The article is on the Web if you're interested.

http://www.chicago.tribune.com/print/sundaymag/current/sundaymag.html

Ron Crown
