2018-06-01 Marc Aramini replying to Robert Pirkola #

Argues that Wolfe in The Sorcerer's House reuses the Triple Goddess pattern, citing the moon through the windows and on the triannulis in various phases and Lamia's association with Hecate. Suggests Finn and Murrey as light and dark may echo lunar phases, extending a pattern seen in the Latro books; responds to Robert Pirkola's Knecht Ruprecht and Loki clues.
proposes · myth-and-structure · source-and-allusion · theories: Finn and Murrey echo lunar phases; Wolfe uses the Triple Goddess again in The Sorcerer's House

I think he repurposed this use pretty thoroughly but he is definitely using the Triple Goddess again too - the moon through the windows and on the triannulis in various phases and the association of Lamia with Hecate - both listed as mothers of Scylla. Finn as ?light? and Murrey as ?dark? might even resonate with phases of the moon. As he played with the goddess changing in Latro.
