1998-05-07 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Recommends other science fiction writers with literary ambitions similar to Wolfe's, naming Dan Simmons, Samuel R. Delany, Ursula K. Le Guin and Tim Powers, plus Matt Ruff's Sewer, Gas, and Electric. Responds to J. Shultz's request for further reading and points to a recent list discussion about a syllabus for an introductory Book of the New Sun class.
informs

Further than what? 8*)

You might check out the recent discussion of a "syllabus" for an introductory BotNS class. There's been a lot of good stuff mentioned there (and some real trash).

Hoom. Well, there are a few SF writers out there with literary aspirations and executions as high as Wolfe, or nearly so: Dan Simmons comes to mind, as does Samuel R. Delany. Ursula K. Le Guin -- though she seems less likely to appeal to a Wolfe fan. Tim Powers is not quite as good, but packs a lot of layers of meaning into his work anyway.

A few individual books I've read lately that I can recommend: Matt Ruff's "Sewer, Gas, and Electric," which is almost impossible to describe. A computerized ghost of Ayn Rand is a major character; so is a giant flying shark. If that doesn't grab you, forget it, the book's not for you. If it does -- the book is a major hoot.

Say: what ever happened to the idea of "Wolfe" and "Lupine" lists...?

-- Roach
