Scylla -- from Urth to Whorl?

Urth list · 2 messages · 1999-09-29 to 1999-09-30

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Gods, angels and the Increate

1999-09-29 Greene, Carlton #

Asks whether the sea-monster Scylla of The Book of the New Sun might be connected to Typhon's daughter Scyla in the Long Sun books, and whether Typhon's children stayed on Earth and became undersea monsters like Baldanders did. Wonders whether the monsters' appearance beneath Urth's oceans can be dated to Typhon's reign, though he cites no passages.
questions · source-and-allusion · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Typhon's children became undersea monsters on Earth; Scylla of the New Sun is Typhon's daughter Scyla

Is there any connection between the monster Scylla mentioned in the BOTNS and Typhon's daughter Scyla dealt with in the Long Sun books? Is it possible that one or more of Typhon's children remained behind on Earth and took the route that Baldanders did, growing in human form until they became true monstrosities and going to live beneath the sea? Perhaps there are citations (though none occur to me now) that would allow one to place the period at which the "monsters" (Erebus and co.) appear and take their places beneath Urth's oceans -- perhaps these dates are contemporaneous with Typhon's reign.

1999-09-30 Urash, Tom #

Argues that Typhon and his children never boarded the void ship Whorl, and that only software representations of them were planted in Mainframe, so most or all remained on Urth. Responds to Carlton Greene's question about whether Scylla in the Book of the New Sun is Typhon's daughter who stayed behind and became a sea monster, as Baldanders did.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon's children were software copies left in Mainframe; Typhon and his children remained on Urth, not Whorl

My understanding was that neither Typhon/Pas, his wife nor any of his children literally boarded the void ship, Whorl; that only highly functional and powerful software representations of the nine were planted in Mainframe [as were, possibly, some of their friends, lovers (Kypris?), servants and government cronies in the guise of the "minor gods"). So, yes, I think it highly likely most, and likely all, remained behind on Urth.
