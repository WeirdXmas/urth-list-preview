2014-08-04 Marc Aramini replying to David Stockhoff #

Recounts the speech by a writer named Santoro at a Wolfe award ceremony, praising Wolfe as a great man and crediting him with starting Santoro's career. Responds to David Stockhoff's post about a Google Alert obituary, adding a personal memory of the evening rather than any argument about the books.
informs

During the fuller award ceremony, santoro's speech was much my favorite in appreciation of Gene, talking about how Gene is a great man because that's just how he is, regardless of fame, and how Gene recommended him for an appropriate project based on some scant interaction some time before, pretty much crediting Gene with starting his career. I didn't realize he was 70.

He was a very good speaker. That was a very beautiful night.
