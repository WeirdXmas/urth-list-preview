1998-05-28 adam louis stephanides #

Recommends Charles Palliser's later novel Betrayals as Wolfeian because it is built from seemingly unrelated narratives whose connections the reader must deduce. Responds to Mark Kelly's reading-list post suggesting The Quincunx, offering a further related title rather than detailed evidence from Wolfe's own texts.
proposes

Palliser's subsequent book, entitled "Betrayals" IIRC, is also rather Wolfeian, in that it consists of a series of seemingly unrelated narratives, the connections between which must be deduced by the reader.

--Adam
