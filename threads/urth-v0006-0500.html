Typhon, the dying sun, and the Long Sun

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-02-02 to 1998-02-02

1998urthPeter Cash

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Fathers, mothers and children

1998-02-02 Peter Cash #

Speculates that Typhon sent off the colonization ship because he believed the dying sun was imminent, and asks why he did not travel in physical form. Builds on the quoted Feeley claim about a sudden calamity, suggesting Typhon may instead have expected thousands of years and treated the Long Sun ship as an experiment.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Typhon sent the ship expecting the sun's death imminent; Typhon expected thousands of years; Long Sun was an experiment

Hmmm. That would account for Typhon's sending off a colonization ship, wouldn't it? Perhaps he thought the death of the sun was imminent, and wanted to save a fragment of humanity from the cataclysm. But then why didn't he come along in physical form? Hmmm again. Perhaps Typhon thought he had a few thousand years, and the "Long Sun" ship was an experiment. If so, he must be quite peeved by the information his telemetry is giving him...

Sgt. Rock
