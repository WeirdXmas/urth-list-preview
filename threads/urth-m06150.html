2008-06-10 Marc Aramini #

Reports the death of SF writer Algis Budrys and laments the recent passing of several other science fiction writers. Posts a personal note on watching obituaries, naming Clarke, Lafferty, Butler, Knight and Williamson as writers who have died recently.
informs

I am something of an obituary watcher. Algis Budrys appears to have passed away, born in 1931. I get a bit depressed when I think that Clarke, Lafferty, Butler, Knight, Williamson, and others have so recently passed. sigh

2008-06-10 Jeff Wilson replying to Marc Aramini #

Argues that the deaths of Budrys and other SF writers should be seen as the completion of long, productive lives rather than a cause for depression. Responds to Marc Aramini's obituary post, noting that most of the named writers outlived their peers outside the field.
supports

Other than Butler, they'd all outlived the majority of their peers outside the field, and were fortunate to have had long, productive lives beyond what was expected in the times and places they were born. Rather than let their end depress us, let us consider that each one's greater story is now complete, and be cheered that it rose above the common lot as we each fancy our efforts might.
