2001-07-31 Adam Stephanides #

Informs readers that the intended sequel title, The Splendor and Misery of Bodies, of Cities, appeared as an excerpt in a 1996 issue of The Review of Contemporary Fiction, and gives ordering details. Responds to a reply quoting a remark that Delany abandoned the second book after the AIDS crisis changed his outlook.

The subject line above is, or would have been, the title of the sequel.

In the Fall 1996 issue of _The Review of Contemporary Fiction_, along with a short interview with Delany and various articles about Delany, there is a thirteen page excerpt from _The Splendor and Misery_. According to the latest issue of the _Review_ in my library, this back issue is still available. The address is

The Review of Contemporary Fiction
ISU Campus Box 4241
Normal IL 61790-4241

The price is eight dollars, with a ten percent discount for individuals.

--Adam
