Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Beasts and the animal in us, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

2000-08-16 Sheila Herndon #

Proposes that Horn in In Green's Jungles may be an inhumu, citing Horn's warnings to Hide about detecting inhumu, his repeated mention of not eating, and the absence of comment on his appearance. Suggests the apparent suicide scene involves an inhumu that fed on Silk and that Horn is transported into the Silk-inhumu body, responding to Gevers's report of John Clute's review.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Horn is an inhumu; Horn is transported into the Silk-inhumu body

delurking - well, I have been wondering about that.
Horn goes on and on to Hide about how to detect
Inhumu - make-up, scales, lack of eating. And near
to this point, Horn repeatedly talks about his
lack of eating. Also, perhaps the "suicide" scene
with who we presume to be Silk is actually a scene
with an Inhumu who has fed on Silk (blood everywhere)
and has part of Silk's essense, and Horn is
transported into the Silk/Inhumu body.

But no one ever comments on Horn's appearance (wrt
the other warning signs of an inhumu). I also
cannot remmeber temperature conversations - that would
be another telling point.

sh
