This Makes Little Sense to Me

Urth list · 3 messages · 2006-10-04 to 2006-10-08

2006urthThe Book of the Short SunFred KiescheStanisław Bocian
The thread: Fred Kiesche asked why Wolfe's New Sun and related books were missing as e-books while some other titles were available, and said he would rather buy legal e-books than pirate them. Stanislaus replied that Kiesche should buy new paper copies because used sales earn Wolfe nothing, and suggested pirated texts too, admitting it was illegal but arguing the author gets paid anyway. Kiesche rejected that reasoning in his final post, saying he prefers legal eBooks so the author earns more. The thread ended without agreement, with the exchange turning on the ethics of piracy and used sales.
  • Fred Kiesche: wants legal e-book versions of Wolfe's New Sun titles and will not pirate them
  • Stanislaus: buy new paper copies since used sales pay Wolfe nothing; piracy is illegal but matters little because the author is paid anyway
  • Fred Kiesche: prefers buying legal eBooks so the author earns more, rejecting piracy

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2006-10-04 Fred Kiesche #

Complains that Wolfe's New Sun and related books are missing in electronic format, noting that The Knight and Return to the Whorl are available while The Wizard and the first Short Sun volumes are not. Expresses frustration with publishers for releasing scattered titles and says he would rather buy e-books than pirate them.
informs

Greetings:

I have a lot of books in electronic format. Generally,
I'll own both "dead tree" and electronic formats,
especially if they're published by certain authors
(Baen, no snickering please, comes to mind).

So before embarking on a re-read of the "New Sun", I
thought I'd see if they were available in electronic
format. I've found it a good way of making notes,
"dog-earring" the text, etc.

Dang. It ain't so. And the selection that **is**
available puzzles me:

The Knight (but not The Wizard!)
Strange Travelers (O.K., an independent)
Return to the Whorl (volume 3 of the Book of the Short
Sun...but not the first two?)

I'll never understand why publishers do things like
release one book electronically...and not the
first...and never follow it up.

Maybe these wouldn't sell like hotcakes in electronic
format, but I'll bet if I were a dishonest soul who
searched the seamy side of the internet, I could
probably come up with pirate editions of what I'm
looking for. But I won't. I am willing to spend money
to buy electronic versions. But I guess I'll have to
score some more paperbacks (used or new) and mark,
notate and dog-ear them.

Grumble.

Fred Kiesche (FPK3)

2006-10-07 Stanisław Bocian replying to Fred Kiesche #

Suggests that Fred Kiesche buy new paper copies of Wolfe's books, since used sales bring Wolfe no money, and also obtain pirated electronic texts. Acknowledges the piracy is illegal but argues it matters little because the author is paid anyway; the reply responds to Kiesche's request for e-book versions of The Book of the New Sun before a re-read.
proposes

Hello Fred,

I have a simple solution. Buy some paper books
by Wolfe - new, not used, he doesn't get any money from used books - AND get pirated texts. It is NOT legal - but as the author gets the money anyway, it is not so important - unless you want to strictly obey the law.
