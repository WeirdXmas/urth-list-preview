Themes: Fathers, mothers and children

2005-06-24 James Wynn #

Concedes that Piaton cannot be Hieros, accepting Roy's argument that Typhon's children were too young to conspire against their father. Responds to Roy's claim that twelve-year-old Cilinia plotted with an enemy, saying Proto-Echidna was no child, and closes by quoting the lines where Piaton is Typhon's slave and Cilinia's words on the slaves.
