Replies to Tony Ellis that the sailors Adam and Eve will stay in the ring of Ushas for now, and invites him to keep building a case against them. Restates his view that the Test is a winner-take-all contest in which losers lose everything.

Sorry! Sailors Adam and Eve stay in the ring of Ushas (for the moment at least). Continue to build a case against them, if you like.

1998-09-07 Tony Ellis #

Disputes the idea that the sailors Adam and Eve are a case to be built against, arguing the notion comes from readers misreading what Severian says in the final chapter. Responds to mantis's joking remark about the sailors remaining in the ring of Ushas, rejecting the premise that such a case exists.
disputes · close-textual-reading

I don't see any "them" to build a case against. As I understand it the whole idea is just the result of people misreading what Severian says in that final chapter.
