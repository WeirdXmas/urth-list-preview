Themes: Memory and forgetting, Unreliable narration

1997-05-16 Marty Light #

Recommends Damon Knight's novel Humpty Dumpty to Wolfe readers, arguing it resembles Wolfe through an injured narrator, altered memories, shifting time and space, and vague conspiracies. Supports the comparison by pointing to parallels with The Book of the New Sun, Peace and There Are Doors, and says it felt like a homage to Wolfe.
proposes · genre-and-craft · theories: Humpty Dumpty is a homage to Wolfe

I know that Gene Wolfe has mentioned Damon Knight as an influence. I recommend Knight's book "Humpty Dumpty" to fans of Wolfe's work. In this recent work, Knight gives us an injured narrator (bullet injury to head), games with memories real and imagined (caused by head injury), shifts in time and space, vague conspiracies, hints of underlying logic but no clear answers. To me, this book felt like a homage to Gene, and alternatively reminded me of parts of TBOTNS, "Peace" and "There Are Doors". I kept waiting for Severian, the green man and Lara Morgan to show up.
