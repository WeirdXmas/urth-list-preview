JC not of my universe

2000-08-16 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Remarks that a John Crowley interview's description of him as prolific seems at odds with his image, first suspecting irony, then "cloaked irony" like Reynard the Fox. Concludes he is most likely telling the simple truth, and admits the aside is off topic for a Wolfe list.
chats

While I recognize that I really should not even mention it, since clearly I am in a different universe than John Crowley and possibly everybody else, yet still, I keep coming back to this one tiny bit in the baffling interview of John Crowley:

Now, "prolific" is not a word I would ever associate with John Crowley. I don't mean that in a mean-spirited way at all.

Because it seems so wildly at variance with my sense of things, at first snort I took it to be ironic (and the word "prolific" should therefore be in italics); but then I saw that it might be "cloaked irony," that is, Crowley is channeling Reynard the Fox (what, again?!) and telling outrageous tales with a straight face (to see how far he can go, or just to mess with people's minds, who can say?). But then I realized that he is most likely telling the simple truth.

=mantis=
