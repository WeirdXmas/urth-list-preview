2000-01-20 Alice Turner #

Disputes Wm. Ansley's theory about the inhumi and protecting people from their bite, arguing that Wolfe will not reveal the secret of the Long Sun books before Book III. Grounds this in Wolfe's known style, calling the speculations un-Wolfean and saying sweetness and light is not his game.
disputes · genre-and-craft · theories: Wolfe reveals the inhumi secret early

Wm. Ansley posited:

Nicely reasoned, Wm. (and thanks for reprising the Lebling list), but I don't buy it for a moment. (And I don't think you do either.) Look, you guys, you're all familiar with the author--what on earth makes you think that Wolfe is going to give you the secret before Book III (if then)? Would that be in characacter? Of course not! Not that it isn't fun to speculate in advance, but (IMO) the speculations have been spectacularly un-Wolfean to date. Sweetness and Light is hardly his game.

-alga

2000-01-20 Alex David Groce #

Argues that Wolfe plants clues to big secrets early and that the inhumi stealing their nature from humans will matter in the coming story, possibly tied to Horn's Solution. Agrees with the previous poster that full secrets remain unknown but says the Lupine approach of undercutting a starting point is likely at work, citing John 8:32.
supports · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Inhumi steal their nature from humans

True. I doubt we have enough to figure out the whole secret (or even the key). However, Wolfe is also good for sticking clues to Big Secrets early in the text and then making you slap yourself on second reading and say "Why didn't I realize that?" I think it's very likely that the whole "inhumi steal their nature from us" idea is at the very least going to be an important element of the story to come (Krait!) and I suspect it has SOMETHING to do with Horn's Solution. Anyway, it is very Lupine to take Sweetness and Light as his starting point and pull the rug out from under it and us.

"And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free." John 8:32
