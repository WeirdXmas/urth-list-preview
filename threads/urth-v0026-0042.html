Further to my Grand Unified Severain Theory (or G.U.S.T.) outlined in the "Speak, Memory" post, I thought I'd point out a few more hypotheses for your consideration:

1) Tzad as shape-shifter. Consider Tzad's shape-shifting powers (or what I call efficient self-directed Lamarckism) in light of my comments of Sev's abo-modified genes being needed to quickly evolve a species of Green Men who go on to evolve into Hierogrammates.

2) The homonculous in the Citadel. Talk about a smoking gun! Sev finds one of Nerissa's "beans" in the Citadel, and has a telepathic conversation with his fetal clone as the result of a shared brain wave frequencey or brain topography -- a la his shared dream with Baldanders.

Ori
