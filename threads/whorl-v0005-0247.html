Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1997-05-04 Dave Lebling #

Lists textual evidence about the Whorl's shape and gravity: floaters smash into the ground when going north too fast and rise too high going south, and the east pole has little or no gravity. Cites Calde and Exodus page references, including the 'spool-shaped' description, to favour a potato-shaped Whorl over a pencil-shaped one.
informs · close-textual-reading · theories: The Whorl is potato-shaped rather than pencil-shaped

South to North. If a floater tries to go north too fast it smashes into the ground, if south, it floats too high. (_Calde_, p. 318). (I'm not certain I completely buy into the physics here, but this is the explanation given in the text).

The East pole, surrounding the Pylon that creates the Shade, in an area where there is little or no gravity ("everybody flies there").

"Spool-shaped" (_Exodus_, p. 347). Seems to argue more for the "potato" than the "pencil" theory of Whorl shape.
