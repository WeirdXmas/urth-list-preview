Proposes that Able, who claims to be an ancestor, may have become a kind of tutelary Lar like Mani, and reads Mani as Mythgarthr's equivalent of Aelfrice's Aelfs. Grounds the argument in a dictionary definition of Lar as a tutelary deity or deceased household ancestor, and in Able's line "I am an ancestor."
proposes · source-and-allusion · myth-and-structure · theories: Able has become a tutelary Lar; Mani is Mythgarthr's version of Aelfrice's Aelfs

I always vowed to look up Mani's claim of being a tutelary lars, which I assumed was a form of spiritual guide. The definition is below, but I was struck by Able's claim later: "I am an ancestor." Perhaps Able, too, has become a something like a tutelary Lars. It is very clear that Mani is Mythgarthr's version of Aelfrice's Aelfs: a created being artificially conjoined with matter by the original inhabitants of the realm.

Lar \Lar\ (l[aum]r), n.; pl. Lares, sometimes Lars. [L.] (Rom. Myth.)
A tutelary deity; a deceased ancestor regarded as a protector of the family. The domestic Lares were the tutelar deities of a house; household gods. Hence, (Fig.): Hearth or dwelling house.
