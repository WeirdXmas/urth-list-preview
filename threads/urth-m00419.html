2005-04-24 Nicholas Gevers #

Reports that Wolfe is still represented by the Virginia Kidd Literary Agency, the contact point for questions about Japanese translations of his works. Gives no argument; it passes on a practical fact to a list member who asked about translation rights.
informs

--Nick Gevers.

2005-04-27 Abaia replying to Nicholas Gevers #

Reports that a Japanese publisher who kept Wolfe's books out of print for ten years plans to reprint four Book of the New Sun volumes soon. Follows up Nick Gevers's tip about Wolfe's agent by noting the poster contacted the publisher directly and is pleased by the news.
informs

Nick,

Thank you very much for information. I have also contacted the Japanese publisher who has been keeping Wolfe books out of print for ten years. Their said they have intention to reprint four TBOTNS books soon. I am very excited to hear that.

Regards,
