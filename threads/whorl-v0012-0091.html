2001-01-30 Kevin J. Maroney #

Argues that John Clute's SciFi Weekly review of Return to the Whorl casually reveals the novel's central mystery resolution, though the clues are present well before the ending. Responds to sh.'s request for spoiler warnings by advising readers to read the review only after finishing the novel.
informs

My judgment is that Clute casually reveals the resolution of the primary mystery of the novel, but it is not instantly clear that he is doing so. More correctly, I think that if I had read this review before reading the novel, I would have felt that he had spoiled the ending, but actually all of the pieces are there long before the ending.

If you're planning on reading RTW anyway (and the fact that you are posting to this list, or reading it, or, for the greater outside audience, reading its archives leads me to believe that you do so intend), I would recommend reading the review, but only after the novel; Clute's description of the novel is quite haunting.
