The thread: Nicholas Gevers proposed that the entity in the Saltus mine is Urth herself, reacting to the Claw's light as a proxy for the New Sun, citing seismic rumblings and the foundering continents foretold in later books. Che Monro supported the misdirection reading and asked whether all of Urth is hallowed ground. Matthew Malthouse agreed the entity stands in for Urth but argued the guardians' fear implies they had seen such an event before, comparing the drums to the balrog in Moria, and Hubert Rogers backed that comparison while citing Cyriaca's story in The Sword of the Lictor. Malthouse then questioned whether the entity is an intelligence at all and whether Severian, as Autarch and author, is inserting later knowledge into his younger self's conversations. The thread ends with the Urth-as-entity idea still open and the narrator-knowledge question unresolved.

2003-04-26 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that the entity in the Saltus mine is Urth herself, reacting to the Claw's light as a proxy for the New Sun. Grounds the claim in the seismic rumblings during the mine episode and in the foundering continents foretold in Dr. Talos's Play and The Urth of the New Sun.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: The entity in the Saltus mine is Urth herself

The matter of the entity in the mine in Saltus: I
think all the speculation has ignored the obvious. The
entity is Urth herself. When Severian brings the Claw
with him below ground, it shines what is, by proxy,
the light of the New Sun. The bowels of Urth react as
they will when the New Sun comes: with seismic
rumblings, premonitions of earthquake. The whole
episode is an omen of the foundering of continents
prophesied in Dr. Talos's Play and described in THE
URTH OF THE NEW SUN...

--Nick Gevers.

2003-05-06 Matthew Malthouse #

Agrees with the idea that the entity in the Saltus mine is a stand-in for Urth, but suggests the mine's guardians react with fear that implies they have seen such an event before without the Claw or Severian. Compares this to the drums that foreshadow the balrog in the mines of Moria.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: The mine's guardians have seen a similar event before

I like entity much beter than inteligence.

Oh but a small point: the mine's guardians react in a manner to suggest fear based on prior experience; they have some concept of what is coming so they must have seen it before, sans claw, sans Severian.

That bit always reminds me of the drums pre-figuring the balrog in the mines of Moria.

Matthew

2003-05-06 Hubert Rogers #

Argues that Cyriaca's story in The Sword of the Lictor and Severian's remark about knowing something of those machines suggest an entity was in the mine at Saltus. Agrees with Matthew's comparison of the drums to the balrog in the mines of Moria, calling the scene heavily Tolkien-influenced.

I agree this 'scene' does seem to be heavily Tolkien influenced.

2003-05-07 Matthew Malthouse #

Questions whether the entity in the Saltus mine that Severian mentions in The Sword of the Lictor is an intelligence, and asks how the narrator could know about such machines given his limited experience. Argues that Severian, as Autarch and author, may be inserting knowledge he later gained into the conversations of his earlier, more ignorant self, citing his brief flight from Saltus and tourist's view of the Citadel.
questions · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Severian interpolates later knowledge into his earlier narration

I was happy with "entity". I'd have quibled with the ealier used "inteligence".

I can't look right now (me, office: books, home) but I have a feeling that I thought the inhabitants of the Wall of Nessus.

Curious though, at the time of the incident with Cyriaca Severian had some superficial knowledge of the Citadel, had fled whatever approached in the Mines of Saltus and had gained barely more than a tourist's view of the House Absolute.

What knowledge could Severian the lictor of Thrax have of such? I'd have thought very little. So is Severian, Autarch and Author interpolating knowledge that he possesses into the conversations of his earlier, more ignorant self?

Matthew
