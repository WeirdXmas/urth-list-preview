The Second? Best of Gene Wolfe

Urth list · 4 messages · 2014-10-16 to 2014-10-16

2014urthRobert Pirkola
The thread: Robert Pirkola proposed a nomination method for a second best-of-Wolfe anthology, letting members name up to 50 stories instead of 30 so the overlap would show where the picks converge. He and others favored a new uncollected story collection over another best-of volume, and Pirkola backed Marc Aramini's suggestion of "The Changeling" as an obvious inclusion absent from the first volume. The thread stayed a one-sided exchange with Pirkola posting all four messages, and it ended without any resolution or consensus on a final selection.
  • Robert Pirkola: propose a 50-story nomination method to find overlap for a second best-of volume
  • Robert Pirkola: "The Changeling" is an obvious choice for the second collection since it was missing from the first
  • Robert Pirkola: prefers a new uncollected Wolfe story collection over another best-of, citing Wolfe being overdue
  • Robert Pirkola: offers a personal list of thirty favorite Wolfe stories without argument

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2014-10-16 Robert Pirkola #

Proposes a nomination method for a second best-of-Wolfe story anthology: each member names up to 50 stories rather than 30, to see where the overlap lands. Reasons that 30 picks would likely overlap little given how many stories could be nominated, and that the next selection may already be fairly obvious.
proposes

I know that 50 is a lot of stories to nominate but my intention was that if everyone just selected 30 or fewerthere is unlikely to be significant overlap given the amount of stories that could be nominated. Perhaps thisunderestimates the degree to which the next 30 is already somewhat obvious. Why doesn't everyone justpost as many as they would like (up to 50) and we will see where that takes us?

2014-10-16 Robert Pirkola replying to Robert Pirkola #

Agrees with Marc Aramini that Wolfe's story "The Changeling" deserves a place in a second best-of collection, noting it was not in the first volume and so is an obvious choice for the second. Responds to Aramini's remark that it is his favorite Wolfe story and that he had assumed it was already included.
supports

Marc Aramini wrote: "Oh my god how could I leave off the changeling? Probably my favorite Wo[l]festory, I just assumed it was in the first one without thinking about it."

Funnily enough before I wrote the first post on this topic I had to recheck toconfirm that Wolfe hadn't selected "The Changeling" for the first volume. An obvious choice for the second.

Agrees with Antonio Pedro Marques that a new uncollected Gene Wolfe story collection would be preferable to another best-of volume, noting that Wolfe is overdue for a new short story collection. The post gives no textual evidence and only endorses Marques's suggested title and the idea of a fresh collection.
supports
