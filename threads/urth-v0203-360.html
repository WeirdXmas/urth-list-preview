Hierodula

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

2002-03-17 Paul C Duggan #

Points out that the hierodules of The Book of the New Sun share their name with the Latin genus name for the praying mantis, and notes their compound eyes and large mandibles when unmasked. Presents the connection as an interesting find, offering it to a reader known for interest in mantis imagery, without drawing further conclusions.
informs · source-and-allusion

Mantis should be interested in this:

I put this up on my Wolfe News Blog too, but I thought I'd mention it here. The hierodules are the aliens in the New Sun series, but I also discovered that "hierodula" is the latin genus name for the praying mantis. The hierodules are described as having compound eyes and big mandibles when they get unmasked.

Interesting conection hm?

Paul
