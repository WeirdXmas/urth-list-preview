Mucor's fate

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-04-04 to 2001-04-04

2001whorlMarc Aramini

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2001-04-04 Marc Aramini #

Argues that Mucor was actually dead in Return to the Whorl and that her appearance was only a spiritual residue, asking why Marble would leave her behind otherwise. Cites the absence of Mucor from the present-time narrative and the town Pig calls made up but which appears in the proper names list as evidence, and endorses the Pig-as-godling theory.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Mucor was dead and her appearance was spiritual residue; Pig's town is real despite his denial; Pig is a godling

Since I've only read the short sun books once (quickly) I'm sure I missed something, but in RttW it seemed to me that Mucor was quite dead and that her manifestation on the whorl was just a spiritual residue. Why else would Marble leave? (I would re-read the 3rd person narrative sections, but I don't have the book with me). Is Mucor explicitly around when Silk picks Marble up? She doesn't show up at all in the "present" timeline of the text, does she? There's no reason for her to be left behind.
Also, the town that Pig claims he is from, then denounces as made up, is listed as a real town in the proper names of the whorl. Does that name mean anything? Could it be real, a the retractiona mere dissembling on Pig's part? (I like the whole "Pig as godling" theory that Mr. Borski has, and I think it works very nicely)
