Daemon. And on. And on.

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-08-07 to 2001-08-07

2001urthTony Ellis

2001-08-07 Tony Ellis #

Argues that a child reading Pullman's The Golden Compass would still notice when the word 'daemon' is revealed to mean an externalized soul, so the Christian connotation of evil is still given to think about. Replies to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, quoting his point that SF/F readers adapt easily to such word shifts, and insists his original etymology argument still holds.
disputes

And later:

If the word 'daemon' is at some point "revealed to mean something like an externalized soul", right at that point the child -knows- that a word with Christian connotations of evil is being used to mean something as harmless as 'spirit'. If she's spared my lesson in political etymology, fine. She's still being given something to think about, which was my original argument.
