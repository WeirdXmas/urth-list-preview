2000-07-10 Jack Lyons #

Jokes that a repeated sentence in The Claw of the Conciliator has a man-edge and a woman-edge, playfully answering Charles Dye's observation about its two appearances and his question linking Vodalus and Jolenta. It responds to Dan Rabin's explanation by quoting the exchange and praising the pun as a keeper.
chats · close-textual-reading

Dan Rabin explains it all:

:>Charles Dye notes:
:
:>Oddly enough, that second sentence appears twice in _Claw_: near the :>end of the first chapter, and near the end of the last. Vodalus and :>Jolenta --
:>what's the connection?
:
:Perhaps it is one of the mercurial Wolfe's strong, cutting sentences :having both a man-edge and a woman-edge.

ooooh, that's a keeper :)
