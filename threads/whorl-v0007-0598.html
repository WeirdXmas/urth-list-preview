Blue/Green == Urth/Moon???

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-10-06 to 1997-10-06

1997whorlJohn Eric Ivancich

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1997-10-06 John Eric Ivancich #

Asks whether the Blue and Green of the Long Sun books could be Urth and the Moon, recalling that the Moon in The Book of the New Sun was planted with forests, and whether Typhon launched the Whorl to repopulate or retake Urth. Also asks whether the Whorl is a hollowed asteroid and what the term Short Sun signifies, given it names both the prior world and Wolfe's next series.
questions · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Blue and Green are Urth and the Moon; Typhon launched the Whorl to repopulate or retake Urth; The Whorl is a hollowed-out asteroid

Hello All,

I've just completed TBotLS series, and I feel lucky to have stumbled upon WHORL. Having read through the WHORL Archives, (only vols. 4 through 7 so far), I'm amazed at all that I missed.

My first contribution, which I offer with great humility, will be a question/possibility...

In looking through the archives, people have referred to the Whorl as a hollowed out asteroid. My image of it is as a cylinder, perhaps obviously man-made when viewed externally. Did I miss the evidence for this asteroid view?

Also, having also read TBotNS, do I recall correctly that the moon there had been planted with forests, making it green?

And if so, is it possible that Blue/Green is Urth/Moon?

Along those lines, could Typhon have launched the Whorl to repopulate Urth/Moon after a war, or as a hidden, dormant army to retake Urth?

It's obvious where the term Short Sun comes from, but both the world preceding the Whorl is referred to as the Short Sun, as is Wolfe's upcoming series. Does this just refer to generic solar systems, or is this a return.

Furthermore, if the Whorl is a hollowed out asteroid, wouldn't that be great camouflage to hide an army (or a new seed) from a potential enemy. Perhaps this asteroid was sent along some very wide orbit, or just circled for a while in a rather tight orbit.

On the other hand, if Starcrossers' Landfall were an accurate potential title, then this possibility ain't worth much.

Anyway, I'm looking forward to seeing what people say.

Eric
