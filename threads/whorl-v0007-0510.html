1997-07-04 David Langford #

Corrects the attribution of the quoted lines to Alastair Reynolds and argues that the Whorl's double-planet system echoing The Fifth Head of Cerberus is deliberate Wolfe playfulness, a narrative pun. Grounds this in the shapeshifters of Fifth Head being more convincingly human than the inhumi, and points to a cast-list and chapter 6 joke in Exodus from the Long Sun about carp barbels.

Actually it was Alastair Reynolds who wrote the quoted lines. My own feeling was that it's just a bit of Wolfe playfulness, a sort of narrative pun (think of the thematic echoes running through the Wolfe Archipelago stories). For one thing, the shapeshifters in Fifth Head appear to be much better at seeming fully human than the inhumi!

You might be amused by this snippet which I just ran in the July issue of Ansible -- though maybe it's well known here:

(References: cast list and Chapter 6 of Exodus.) Just to make it perfect, carp do of course have these little barbels or feelers on their jaws.

For fairness I should add that Greg Feeley's reviews of my own stuff have been very kind....

Dave
