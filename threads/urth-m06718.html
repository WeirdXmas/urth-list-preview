2008-11-06 Son of Witz #

Describes the poster's own album art for the band Secret Chiefs 3, linking to their website and noting an Obama-as-Antichrist cover design. Responds to a prior post's question about whether Mike Patton is connected to the band and whether the illustrations are online, and mentions a New Sun inspired Amphisbaena and a Monomachy illustration.
informs

sort of started as Bungle without Patton, but has long since eclipsed anything Patton has done in terms quality, but definitely not notoriety. Not too many people know of SC3. It's Trey Spruance's baby. the other bungle guys are not active in SC3 much anymore.

all of them are on my site.
http://www.butcherbaker.org/
it's flash based, takes a bit to load.
once you see the menu, go to Package Art > Secret Chiefs 3:Book of Horizons it's sorta weird. There is a Borges thing going on in SC3 in that it is made up of 7 imaginary bands, and each genre has been alchemically sublimated into individual identities. I had to do logos and illustrations for each band. ALL according to Trey's art direction. (I didn't do the Holy Vehm death metal logo though)

it may be worth a look at SC3:Path of Most Resistance cover. I did it long before Obama was a candidate, and we were going for a Senator as Antichrist thing. So fans of the Obama is Antichrist might like this, cause it actually looks like Obama in silhouette. ha!

'nuff pimping my shit.
*sorry*

that'd be cool as hell
This week, my Monomachy illustration will be printed on a band's tee shirt, and I'm working an New Sun inspired Amphisbaena into another project.

~witz

2008-11-07 Son of Witz replying to Son of Witz #

Recounts how the poster got into album-cover and T-shirt art for the band Estradasphere, including playing their wedding, and mentions starting illustrations for The Book of the New Sun. Shares this as a personal anecdote with no argument about Wolfe's books, replying warmly to a compliment on the artwork.
chats

Thanks.
regarding those bands, I'm very lucky to work with my some of my favorites. It all started when I didn't like the teeshirts Estradasphere had at shows, so I drew one I liked better. I told my wife, "wouldn't that be great if they wanted me to do their album cover? Then maybe I could ask them to play our wedding as payment." They never used that shirt design, but I've done 3 covers, several shirts, and they played our wedding. So I'm a strong believer in just putting yourself out there.

(I've started illustrating Book of the New Sun, though no one is asking me to. Might as well shoot for Lune.)

~witz
