I don't recall whether someone's already posted this to the list, but Willian Smith's 'A Dictionary of Greek and Roman Biography and Mythology' is available on-line at

http://www.ancientlibrary.com/smith-bio/index.html

It's a nice place to look for information because it goes a little bit further than the usual encyclopedia entries that say nothing. Of course, it's old.
