Themes: Names and their meanings, Hidden identities and parentage

Asks how and why Ted left behind a handkerchief with initials during his visit to Doris, why Doris did not identify it, and whether Ted's full name carries meaning. Builds on Branwen's suggestion that the initials T, G and A point to Ambrosius, a usurper, and proposes that Ted is using a similar alias.

I've seen a couple of people wondering what it was Ted left behind when he visited Doris. What would you use while crying that would have your initials on it?

A handkerchief, presumably.

I think the real questions are:

1. How/why did Ted leave it behind?

2. Why did Doris refrain from identifying it?

3. What is Ted's full name, and does it have any significance?

I have nothing to venture regarding 1 or 2, but I think Mr. (or Mrs.? Gwern's not really helping me out re: gender) Branwen is on to something regarding 3: T for Ted, G for Griffin, and A for "Ambrosius. As in, the supposed usurper of Goldwurm. Perhaps Ted pulled a Bax, if you will . . ."
