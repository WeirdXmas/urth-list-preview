Asks list members to stop putting spoiler-space padding before messages, arguing it clutters scrolling and archives that are already hard to load. Suggests readers should assume posters and readers accept spoilers on a list about The Book of the Long Sun.

2000-07-26 Adam Stephanides #

Proposes dropping the spoiler-space padding that list posters have been adding before spoiler content, arguing it is useless for digest readers and unnecessary when subject-line warnings exist. Responds to Alice Turner's request to stop using spoiler space and reduce archive bloat, asking whether any readers who have not read In Green's Jungles need the extra protection.
proposes

I've been including the spoiler space because everybody else has and nobody's objected till now, but I'm dubious of its utility. It's useless for those who get the digest, unless it's in the final message of the digest; and on my email program at least, those who get individual messages are safe as long as there's a spoiler warning in the subject line. Is there anyone out there who hasn't read IGJ and finds the extra layer of protection given by the spoiler space vital?

--Adam
