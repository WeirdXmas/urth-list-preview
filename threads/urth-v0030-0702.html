"Five" in In Glory Like Their Star

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-10-04 to 2001-10-04

2001urthJames Jordan

2001-10-04 James Jordan #

Proposes that the Indo-European root "pan" meaning "all" also means "five," linking the number to the totality of time in the story. Grounds this in cognate words like pentagram and pentagon and in the Pas-Pan name pairing, with the poster citing their own linguistics memory.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Five refers to the totality of time

I should have remembered this from linguistics, but only now at this late date recollected it: The Indo-European word "pan" means "all" and also "five." (Note "pentegram" "pentagon" etc., and of course "pas, pan"). The reference is to the totality of time.
