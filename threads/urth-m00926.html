Why Berthold looks like Ben

2005-01-11 Seth Lombardi #

Proposes that Mythgrather and America in The Wizard Knight share one soul, so their differences are just different viewpoints on the same conflict. Answers the quoted post about Art's magic helm by reading the Ben-for-Berthold reveal at the end as a device to add emotional punch.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Mythgrather and America share one soul; Differences between the worlds are points of view on one conflict

I felt it was something along the lives of: the inhabitants of Mythgrather and America do more than just live prallel lives, but in fact share a soul, and that the differences between the two places were just different points of view on the same conflict, or state of being. Thus being a big reveal at the end of the book, it's an attmpt to give the work an extra bit of relevance or emphasis or emotional punch.
