Themes: Memory and forgetting, Names and their meanings

1999-08-19 Alex David Groce #

Admits a faulty memory about a character's sister-in-law's name and asks whether Barbara Bold is named after anyone in Trollope or Dickens. Previews an unfinished PEACE hypothesis built on the skull motif linking Weer's skull to the cave skull, the river dream walk, and the Sidhe story.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: Barbara Bold named after a Trollope or Dickens figure

Alas, my memory was faulty. Mary, not Barbara, is Eleanor's sister-in-law's name. I wonder if Barbara Bold is named for anyone in Trollope or Dickens? The PEACE hypothesis is under work--since it may be quite a while (moving in and getting used to grad school is taking a lot of time...) for me to polish it, some of the major points are:

-- The motif of the skulls--and the connection between Weer's skull (also a kind of memory palace) and the skull the picnic party sees in the cave--especially in light of Olivia's comments about how mankind will one day vanish...
-- The "dream walk" Weer takes by the river
-- The story of the Sidhe

"And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall set you free." John 8:32
