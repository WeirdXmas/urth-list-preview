Secret of the Inhumi - Please Destroy My Theory

Urth list · 5 messages · 2009-03-26 to 2009-03-27

2009urthThe Book of the Long SunJames WynnSon of WitzJordon Flato
The thread: Jordon Flato proposed that Krait's secret in the Short Sun books is a call to love one's enemy, tied to Silk's departure with the Whorl ending Inhumi breeding and slave supply, using the sewer scene, Green breeding, and landers. Flato then defended the theory against James Wynn, who argued destroying landers would not stop the Inhumi and that Silk's message already ends forced disembarkation. Son of Witz supported the theory and praised its link to the sewer clearing. Flato later retracted the earlier theory as irrelevant to the Long Sun secret, while keeping the Whorl and sewer points, so the thread ended in partial retraction rather than resolution.
  • Jordon Flato: Krait's secret is a call to love one's enemy, ending Inhumi breeding by removing landers, later retracted as irrelevant to the Long Sun secret
  • James Wynn: Destroying landers would not stop the Inhumi, who could cross the Whorl; landers would stop on their own
  • Son of Witz: Supports Flato's theory despite no other landers flying from Blue to Green, praising the sewer link

Themes: War and soldiers, Suffering and sacrifice, Colonization and the native

2009-03-26 Jordon Flato #

Proposes that Krait's secret in The Book of the Short Sun is a call to love one's enemy, and that Silk's departure with the Whorl ends the Inhumi's breeding and slave supply, mirroring Horn clearing the sewers of the past. Grounds this in the Sewer scene's line about the past holding on, the fact that Inhumi breeding happens on Green, and the landers that carry humans there.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Krait's secret is love thy enemy; Silk's departure stops the Inhumi supply cycle; Silk clearing the Whorl mirrors Horn clearing the sewers

Ok, so I'm re-reading SS now, and have just finished Incanto's story of the Sewer's on Green (at dinner with the Inclinto family). I've been really focused on Krait's secret this read through, and some things have come crashing in on me.

Bear with me. I have forgotten much of what happens, detail wise, in the rest of Green's Jungles and RttW, so there is much to destroy my current thinking.

For a long time, I've assumed the secret was some variation on the Golden Rule, as some other do as well. Two big problems with that: That isn't much of a threat to have as a secret, as it's not a very plausible thing for humanity to actualize.

The variation on that is not Love thy neighbor (no pun intended, although now that I think of it....) but Love thy Enemy...enemy being Inhumi. We see that Horn's love for Krait changes Krait. There is something there. But today, the following occured to me.

1) Isn't there something about how breeding for Inhumi has to take place on green? Isn't that at least implied?

2) The 'civilization' of the Inhumi is on green

3) They require human slaves and food on Green to continue

4) Human slaves are only possible by tricking people on a lander (either from the Whorl or by taking them from Blue, which seems to have happened more than just in Pajarocu).

SO: If you destroy all the landers, the breeding/slave cycle would be broken (espcially if you are one of those who think they lie about Conjunction and only get to Blue on Landers). That is something that IS in the power of humans, but would be difficult to accomplish because so many people are still interested in trying to get back to the Whorl. (stuck to the past)

What is Silks last action in the story? GOING BACK TO THE WHORL, and THE WHORL LEAVES. There will be no more landers going to Green. There will be no more landers coming from the whorl to green or from blue to green. This is a major change for the Inhumi. They will shortly have no more human slaves on Green, and no way to get them there at all. They will devolve.

Also, this, to me, ties into the Sewer scene, which, for me at least, is indicated to be a huge clue for the secret. (Neighbor: No, we are trying to show you how to save yourselves") When he clears out the sewers, the Old Cannibal makes a point of saying "It's what's past"..."You see that, don't you? It's the past holding on." This made me think of the Whorl. People get on Landers to go back to the whorl, because that is the past holding on.

Horn clears the sewers. Silk removes the Whorl. Both are reflective symbolic actions, of clearing out the past, which is what the neighbors were trying to show Horn as the way to save humanity.

Well? I haven't seen this discussed ont he board in these terms, but on that I could be wildly wrong. Certainly, to me, it is a big reason why Silk taking the Whorl out of the system (and I now DO think it was Silk, with the bit of Pas in him, that makes the Whorl leave the system) now makes a hell of a lot of sense.
