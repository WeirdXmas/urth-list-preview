Mountains that cast shadows

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-05-01 to 1997-05-01

1997whorlThe Book of the Long Sunxury

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1997-05-01 xury #

Proposes that the Whorl is a cylinder with the sun along its axis and the Mainframe hanging upside down opposite the city floor, asking what lies on the cylinder's sides and how one could walk to the Mainframe. Grounds the idea in the mountains separating the Mainframe from the Ground and casting shadows on both, and openly invites objections to the speculation.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Whorl is a cylinder with the Mainframe hanging opposite the floor

Let us assume that the Whorl is cylindrical. The sun runs along the center. Mainframe, as I have always assumed is dircetly opposite to the "floor" of which the cities have been built on. I've also assumed the the Mainframe is upsidedown, or hanging from the roof (the building roofs are closer to the sun). So then, what could be on the sides of the Whorl? What do you have to go through to get to the Mainframe by foot? As I recall the mountains seperate the Mainframe from the Ground. And so the mountains cast shadows upon the Ground (and the Mainframe, in the other direction). But, there are some problems with this theory, this is just speculation on my part. So tell me what you think.

Xury
