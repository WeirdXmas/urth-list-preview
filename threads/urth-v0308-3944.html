2003-08-18 Marc Aramini #

Argues that Typhon in the Typhon scene does recognize Severian as the Conciliator, not merely wanting the Claw, citing an implication that if Severian bows to Typhon the Conciliator will bow too. Responds to a questioner about the scene, noting the archive's prior debate and urging the reader to re-read and decide.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon recognizes Severian as the Conciliator

One more thing, since you asked about the Typhon scene. There has been a lot of disagreement over that scene. I think that Typhon does in fact recognize Severian, others believe that he doesn't. My primary reason is that somewhere in that scene Typhon implies that if Severian bows to him, the Conciliator will also bow to him. I think there is enough parallelism in that scene to justify "recognition" beyond Typhon's desire for the Claw, but no consensus could be reached on that point.
There should be plenty in the archives on it from a few months ago that presents both sides, so you can re-read the scene and come to your own decision on it.
Marc

2003-08-19 Josh Geller #

Disputes the claim that Typhon fails to recognize Severian in the Typhon scene, stating flatly that Typhon does recognize him and that those who think otherwise are wrong. Responds to maa32's argument that a parallel between Typhon bowing and the Conciliator bowing shows recognition, offering no further grounds beyond the assertion itself.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon recognizes Severian as the Conciliator

There is no doubt that Typhon recognizes Severian.

The people who disagree with this are wrong.

I am sorry, sometimes things are best said very bluntly.
