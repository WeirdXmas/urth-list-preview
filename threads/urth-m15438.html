Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Time loops and causality

2010-11-07 Andrew Mason #

Argues that the Severians seen at the inn in the Urth of the New Sun era were probably not yet working wonders as conciliators, since they are older and lack the Claw. Grounds this on Severian's own statement that he only likes to think those helpers were himself, and on the ages implied by the Apu-Punchau episode.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian's inn helpers were not yet conciliators

Well, yes, he says he _likes_ to think that, so we can't be sure it's true. But grant that Severian made other trips to that time; it's still unclear whether he did any conciliating on those trips. It's not as if when our Severian sees the other Severians at the inn they are all being feted as wonder-workers. Of course, it may be that they started working wonders after our Severian left. (If they had been working wonders before he arrived, wouldn't someone have done something to stop it? And would the arrival of our Severian have caused such a stir?)

Bear in mind, though, that they must have been considerably older than our Severian (since the Apu-Punchau episode, which takes many years, happened in between) and that they would not have had the Claw. (I know the Claw doesn't really matter. But people _thought_ it mattered.)

2010-11-07 António Pedro Marques replying to Andrew Mason #

Argues that Severian, as a conciliator, cannot help conciliating, so the other Severians at the inn may have come in disguise and passed unnoticed there. Responds to Andrew Mason's quoted point that it is unclear whether Severian's other trips involved conciliating, and agrees it is a good point while questioning why readers assumed otherwise.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Other Severians came in disguise at the inn

My view regrding that is that he can't help conciliating, his powers are there.

Knowing the episode, they might have opted to come in disguise and generally walking unnoticed at that particular time and place.

Good point. It's unclear to me why people did think so.
