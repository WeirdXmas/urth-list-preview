Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Hidden identities and parentage

2001-05-09 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Surveys a set of speculations about the new President in a story, including that he is a vice-president who succeeded to office, and about a snake-like Copperhead figure and whether the setting is an Eden. Responds to Mantis's sense that the president is a usurper or doppelganger, and cites details such as the delayed election, the Changer, and the naked man's Adamic role.
proposes · puzzle-solving · allegory-and-typology · theories: the president is the former vice-president who succeeded; Copperhead is the world's Satan or Eve figure; the naked man is the new Adam crushing the serpent
