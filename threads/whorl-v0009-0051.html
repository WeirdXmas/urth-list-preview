1998-11-24 Alice Turner #

Relays David Hartwell's refusal of group discounts for advance orders, because the publisher cannot sell books at a discount. Gives the tentative fall 1999 date for On Blue's Waters and the expected schedule of the next two books and a story collection, based on the agent's word.
informs

Here is a message from David Hartwell concerning the new book. I asked him if we could get a group discount for advance orders. It seems to me strange that he says no--group discounts are not unusual in publishing, and Ranjit tells me that we have about 140 on the list. If even half that number ordered a book, it would be a substantial advance order. Does anyone have a connection with, say, Amazon, who might be willing to offer a group discount?

It's depressing that the first book won't be till fall '99. I wonder if we can cohere till then. We've done well so far, but to hold off a full year longer than first expected will be difficult.

-alga

Oh sigh,I am not able to sell books at a discount. You can get discounts almost everywhere (Costco is probably the highest on books) but from the publisher.

The title of new book number one is On Blue's Waters and I have tentatively scheduled it for next fall. This may change, because the second manuscript, and the third, seem destined to follow quickly--I am told that book two will be turned in perhaps by late January, and the third, Return to the Whorl, perhaps by June, and a new short story collection is coming too. I do not say for certain though what the publication dates or completion dates are, but only what I am given to understand by the agent.

I need to call Gene as soon as I finish On Blue's Waters, which will probably be next week.

David

1998-11-24 Alastair Reynolds #

Speculates that the delayed next Long Sun volume may become a tetralogy like The Book of the New Sun, and reports finding the UK paperbacks of that series in Devon. Cites the Castle of the Otter account of the third volume being split, and describes the Bruce Pennington covers, replying to an earlier post lamenting the wait.
chats

alga:

Yes, that's ages to wait...

What's the betting it'll turn into a tetrology like TBOTNS? (According to the Castle of the Otter, the planned third and final volume got too large and they had to split it).

I'm very happy as while I was in Devon a week or so back (for Milford!), I found all four volumes of TBOTNS in the original UK paperbacks - a bit dog-eared and faded, but with the wonderfully evocative Bruce Pennington cover paintings, which have always defined my mind's eye view of Wolfe's landscapes. Actually I only found 3 of them, cursed my bad luck, but then Ben Jeapes pointed out that the fourth volume had strayed a few books along the shelf...

Al

1998-11-24 Kevin J. Maroney #

Disputes Al's guess that the new book will become a tetralogy, arguing instead that Wolfe drafts whole series before submission, so Return to the Whorl is probably near final form. Grounds this on Wolfe's practice with New Sun and Long Sun and the quick turnaround of the Short Sun books, and adds a note on the Pennington covers.
disputes · author-and-biography

I wouldn't bet on that. Given Wolfe's habit of writing the entire series (in draft) before submitting the manuscript for the first volume--which he did with both _New Sun_ and _Long Sun_, if I recall correctly--and the fast turnaround on the _Short Sun_ books, I'd bet that _Return to the Whorl_ is somewhere close to its final form already.

I believe those are Wolfe's favorite covers, as well. Certainly they're beautiful.
