1998-11-20 Kevin J. Maroney #

Gives ordering and contributor information for the New York Review of Science Fiction issue that printed a list discussion of a peculiar, haunting story, and thanks participants. Responds to a poster who had linked the magazine's web page, and offers contributors copies and others the address for issues priced at $3.50.
informs

Anyone who directly contributed to the piece and is not a regular recipient of the magazine should contact me (off-list) for a contributor's copy. Anyone else who is interested should feel free to contact me, or can just write directly to NYRSF at:

Dragon Press
PO Box 78
Pleasantville, NY 10570

Issues are $3.50 each.

Our thanks, again, to everyone who contributed to the discussion of this peculiar and haunting story.

Wombat, a.k.a. Kevin Maroney [email]
Kitchen Staff Supervisor, New York Review of Science Fiction http://ebbs.english.vt.edu/olp/nyrsf/nyrsf.html

1998-11-21 Damien Broderick #

Announces that a collage piece assembled from URTH-list contributions will appear in the December issue of the New York Review of Science Fiction, and gives a link for obtaining the magazine. Thanks the list members who contributed, and includes the poster's academic and bibliography contact details.
informs

An editor at the New York Review of Science Fiction tells me that the URTH-list collage piece is in the December issue, which is at the printer now. Thanks to everyone who contributed.

The magazine can be obtained most easily (I assume) from

http://ebbs.english.vt.edu/olp/nyrsf/nyrsf.html

Damien
