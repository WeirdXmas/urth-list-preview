Themes: Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both

1998-07-03 Damien Broderick #

Notes that twin-planet stories recur in science fiction and lists examples beyond The Fifth Head of Cerberus and The Dispossessed, including Blish's novella Get Out of My Sky and Forward's Rocheworld books. Responds to mantis's remark on the seventies twin-planet stories, citing Blish, Anderson, Forward and Shaw as a broader trope rather than a Wolfe-specific point.

< There was a rash of "twin planet" stories in the seventies, wasn't there? 5HC, and then THE DISPOSSESSED, and who knows what else. Odd. >

A recurrent trope in sf, neh? Recently re-read a quite impressive long novella by Blish, `Get out of my sky', from Astounding in 1956 (and in an antho with Poul Anderson's `Sister Planet' from 1959). Bob Forward's Rocheworld books (1982-1990), Bob Shaw's 1986-9 wooden spaceship trilogy (where pi = 3.000).

Damien Broderick
