The thread: Michael Andre-Driussi proposed that Silk, not anyone else, killed himself with a knife beside Hyacinth in her coffin, stating the view without textual argument. Tim Boolos partly backed the suicide reading but argued Silk only came close to suicide, that the blood came from a sacrifice like Orpine's, and that Hari Mau then took the body after a transference between two despondent spirits. Fernando Q. Gouvea asked how the suicide reading squares with the book's 'searching for Silk' passages, which he treated as evidence the reading must explain. The thread ends without resolution, with the disagreement left open between the suicide reading and Boolos's alternative.

  • Tim Boolos: Silk nearly killed himself; blood was a sacrifice; Hari Mau took the body after a transference
  • Fernando Q. Gouvea: suicide reading must explain the 'searching for Silk' passages

2000-08-08 Tim Boolos #

Argues that Silk was on the brink of suicide beside Hyacinth's coffin but did not actually kill himself, and that the blood came from a sacrifice like Orpine's. Supports Michael Andre-Driussi's suicide reading while offering an alternative, proposing a single transference between two despondent spirits followed by Silk's body being taken by Hari Mau.
disputes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Silk was on the brink of suicide but did not kill himself; The blood came from a sacrifice like Orpine's; One transference between Silk and Horn, then Hari Mau abducts the body

I pretty much agree with Mantis on this one. Although
I think that Silk was at the point of suicide and had
not actually committed the act. The blood mentioned
was from the final sacrifice ala Orpine.
IMO this is the Occam's razor: one transference, two
despondent spirits attempting to cope, some things
happen (to be revealed to some extent or other in
RTW), then Silk's body containing Horn's and Silk's
spirit is abducted by Hari Mau.
