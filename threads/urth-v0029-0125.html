2000-07-27 Peter Stephenson #

Chats about a radio reading and the poster's own unfinished essay on time themes in The Book of the New Sun, quoting a line from Yeats's 'The Second Coming'. It shares no interpretation of the books, only a personal confession about procrastinating on academic writing.
chats

Missed that, sounds fascinating. I wonder why it's in fashion at the moment?

I've been thinking I really ought to go... For ages I've been planning an essay on time themes in the Book, and I have this inferiority complex about showing my face in polite society until I actually sit down and write it.

`And what rough beast, its hour come round at last,
Slouches towards Bethlehem to be born?'

(Yeats, `The Second Coming', from the collection `Michael Robartes and the Dancer'.) There's another essay I should have written ages ago. Sigh.
