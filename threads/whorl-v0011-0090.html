Themes: Unreliable narration

2000-08-10 Kevin J. Maroney #

Argues that Weer in Peace could have caused several deaths without being a multiple murderer, since the boy and the freezer man might be accidents and the librarian's death might not be murder. Responds to Christopher Culver's quotation of Wolfe's interview, noting Wolfe disowned only the multiple-murderer reading while leaving Weer's guilt for the reader to decide.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Weer killed the librarian but may not be a murderer; Weer's other deaths were accidents

Also, Weir could have killed multiple people without being a "multiple murderer". The young boy and the man who was locked in the freezer could both easily have been accidents, and I don't know if he did kill the librarian; even if he did, that might conceivably not have been murder if she'd had a weapon--I don't remember the details well, and I'm sure many of them are well-hidden.
