1998-02-02 Dragan Milosevic #

Argues that Borges influenced Wolfe beyond the library, pointing to his Encyclopaedia of Imaginary Creatures and its world beyond the mirror that plans invasion. Cites the book's omen of a fish appearing in a mirror and a beast much like the alzabo as parallels to Wolfe's work.
informs · source-and-allusion

Borges influenced Wolfe in another way. In his Encyclopaedia of Imaginary Creatures
Borges speaks of a world beyond the mirror that plans invasion on our world. The first sign of such invasion shall be _a fish that appears in a mirror_. Also, a beast much like an alzabo is described in that book.
