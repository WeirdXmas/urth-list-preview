WorldCon and the Gene Wolfe convention circuit future

Urth list · 2 messages · 2002-08-02 to 2002-08-02

2002-08-02 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Reports that WorldCon is running about 1000 members below expectations and urges readers to meet Gene Wolfe at conventions this year, since he and his wife plan to attend cons only until they can no longer travel. Grounds the appeal in Wolfe's age, his skipped conventions last year, and his planned attendance at WorldCon in San Jose and World Fantasy Con in Minneapolis.
informs

Hello there everybody.

Just got an update on WorldCon today. They are about 1000 members below where they were expecting to be at this point (the event starts August 29). It will likely be a smaller WorldCon than seen in recent times. (Is this due to post 9/11 travel decline or the graying of sf fandom, or both, I wonder?)

I'm going there mainly because Gene Wolfe is going to be there. Of course, there is no absolute guarantee he will be able to make it, but he has membership and all that. (And Vernor Vinge is Guest of Honor, which I find interesting.)

Now then, I don't want to scare people, but only tell the facts as I know them. Gene Wolfe is 70-something years old and he didn't go to any conventions last year. Then they (Gene and his wife) decided to go to conventions this year (Horror Con, World Con, and World Fantasy Con), and they plan to go to conventions every year until they cannot, so when they next stop going to cons, they really will stop for good.

So if you would like to meet Gene Wolfe at a convention, I urge you to do it =this= year. WorldCon is in San Jose, WFC is in Minneapolis (iirc) in a couple of months.

Personally I'd like to see some of the people around here in the flesh at WorldCon, but that's just selfish. Altruistically I want Gene Wolfe's fans to meet him, and the window of opportunity is drawing to a close.

=mantis=
