1999-04-12 Jonathan Laidlow #

Announces an update to the Ultan's Library bibliography resource, adding a list of Wolfe's uncollected short fiction since 1990 and inviting corrections and page references. Asks list members whether Feeley's NYRSF article runs across two issues, since the author has concluded it does while an online index says otherwise.
informs · source-and-allusion

Well thanks to a slow day at the office and some help from various list members I have now added a list of uncollected short fiction since 1990. If you spot anything I've missed please drop me a line. If you have any of these short stories and would like to check the reference or send me the page numbers, that would be great too.

And could someone let me know if Feeley's NYRSF article spans two issues? I've come to the conclusion it does, but not having tracked it down yet, I'm rather mystified by blibliographies that say it doesn't... Such as the NYRSF on-line index. Or am I just having a thick day.

Jonathan

1999-04-12 Kevin J. Maroney #

Informs the list that Gregory Feeley's essay on family romance in The Book of the New Sun ran in two parts in the New York Review of Science Fiction, issues 31 and 32 from 1991. Answers a fellow poster's question about whether the article spans two issues by giving bibliographic details and a link to the magazine's author index.
answers

<http://ebbs.english.vt.edu/olp/nyrsf/F_author.html>:

Feeley, Gregory, The Evidence of Things Not Shown: Family Romance in The Book of the New Sun (pt. 1 of 2), Issue 31, March 1991, Vol. 3, No. 7, p. 1, essay, Wolfe, Gene, The Book of the New Sun.

Feeley, Gregory, The Evidence of Things Not Shown: Family Romance in The Book of the New Sun (pt. 2 of 2), Issue 32, April 1991, Vol. 3, No. 8, p. 12, essay, Wolfe, Gene, The Book of the New Sun.

Wombat, a.k.a. Kevin Maroney [email]
Kitchen Staff Supervisor, New York Review of Science Fiction http://ebbs.english.vt.edu/olp/nyrsf/nyrsf.html
