The thread: A welcome thread for newcomers to the Urth list, with no debate about Wolfe's texts. Jason Cassidy introduced himself as a longtime lurker who has read Wolfe for about twenty years, and several members greeted him and praised the list's archive and theorizing. Marc Aramini mentioned he is working through Wolfe's mid-1970s novellas and short fiction before moving to the 1980s works, and aaron encouraged him. The thread stayed social and ended in friendly banter, with no substantive argument reached.
  • Marc Aramini: welcomes newcomers and notes the list's changed mood, while working through Wolfe's mid-1970s novellas and short fiction
  • aaron: praises the archive's collective analysis and encourages Aramini's coverage of the short fiction

  • Robin B. Smith: gives a general welcome and praises the list's discussions
  • entonio: jokes that readers pretend to know every author mentioned on the list

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

chats

Thanks so much, Aaron!? I appreciate it.? I am getting to the portion of his short fiction in the mid 70s where Wolfe produced most of his superb but?super dense novellas - Forleson, Silhouette, Death of Doctor Island, Seven American Nights, Eyeflash Miracles, Tracking Song, etc. and it may take me a bit to work through those rigorously, (they are denser than most other novels, but there is a lot of existing explication I need to sort through)?but once I get to the 80s his concentration on the longer novels and series will get the short fiction analysis moving more quickly again.? Thanks for the kind words.
