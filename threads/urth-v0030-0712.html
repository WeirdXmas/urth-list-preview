Nero Wolfe on A&E

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-10-14 to 2001-10-14

2001urthWilliam H. Ansley

2001-10-14 William H. Ansley #

Announces that the A&E channel is rebroadcasting the Nero Wolfe television episode The Doorbell Rang, with the rest of the series expected on later Sundays, and gives a link to the schedule. Says the poster thought the series was quite well done. This is a social heads-up about a different mystery series, not a discussion of Wolfe's fiction.
informs

I just wanted to post a heads-up for all the Nero Wolfe fans on this list.

If you have access to the Arts and Entertainment (A&E) cable TV channel, you might want to watch tonight at 8 pm (EST). _The Doorbell Rang_ is being rebroadcast. I assume the rest of the series will follow on subsequent Sundays. You can find out more about the series and its schedule here:

http://www.aande.com/tv/shows/nerowolfe/

I thought the series was quite well done.
