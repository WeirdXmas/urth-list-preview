Inhumi in the Whorl

Urth list · 4 messages · 2011-02-09 to 2011-02-09

2011urthThe Book of the Short SunThe Book of the Long SunGerry QuinnDan'l Danehy-OakesJames WynnLee Berman
The thread: Readers debated what inhumi and Neighbors become when they dream-travel between worlds such as Urth, Blue, Green and the Whorl. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argued inhumi become fully human because they carry stolen human souls, while Gerry Quinn held that travellers take the bodily form matching their soul, so Neighbors stay Neighbors and inhumi take the form of the species they imitate, citing Scylla. Lee Berman pressed the bodily-transformation question, and James Wynn asked what Neighbors and sentient inhumi look like in soul travel and why Wolfe did not explore a wild inhumi feeding on a dream-traveler. The thread ended with the questions open, with no consensus reached.
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: inhumi become fully human in dream travel because they carry stolen human souls
  • Gerry Quinn: dream travellers take the bodily form matching their soul; Neighbors stay Neighbors, inhumi become the species they imitate
  • Lee Berman: asks what inhumi and Neighbors become across worlds, pressing the bodily-transformation question
  • James Wynn: suggests Neighbors are in astral form and asks why Wolfe left feeding on dream-travelers unexplored

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Robots, androids and artificial life, Beasts and the animal in us

2011-02-09 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that inhumi become fully human when dream-travelling because they carry human souls stolen from humans, so the effect follows them to any world. Answers Lee Berman's question about what inhumi become on other worlds by invoking 'the blood is the life' and asks what souls the inhumi had before they began stealing.
answers · theology-and-religion · source-and-allusion · theories: Inhumi become human because they hold stolen human souls

Well, the reason, as I understand it, that inhumi become human in dream-travel is because they have human souls -- souls stolen from humans. (Insert meditation on "the blood is the life" here.) So an inhumX who has a human soul will become human in dream-travel no matter where they wind up.

An interesting question is, before they began stealing human blood/souls, what kind of souls, if any, did these inhumi have?

questions · myth-and-structure · theories: Neighbors encountered by resurrected Horn were in astral form

Yes, what do Neighbors and sentient inhumi look like during soul travel? Honestly, I think the Neighbors that the resurrected HORN encountered were in astral form. There's a spectral feel to that meeting. But, since Wolfe has toyed with every possible metamorphosis of the human soul in these 7 volumes, I'm surprised Wolfe didn't toy with what would happen if a wild inhumi fed on a dream-traveler.

Asks what Inhumi and Neighbors become when they dream travel between worlds such as Blue, Green and the Whorl, building on the claim that Inhumi turn fully human when they dream travel to Urth. Responds to Gerry Quinn's point that dream travel cannot explain how the Inhumi reached Green or infected the Whorl, raising the question of bodily transformation.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Inhumi become fully fleshly human when dream travelling

If I understand James correctly, when Inhumi dream travel to Urth they become human. Not just some ethereal dream-substance but fully, fleshly human, able to have eat, have sex produce gastric juices, seminal fluid, ova, etc.

So, what do inhumi and Neighbors become when they dream travel between other worlds including Blue, Green and the Whorl?

2011-02-09 Gerry Quinn replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that dream travellers take on the bodily form that matches their soul, so Neighbours stay Neighbours and inhumi become whatever species they imitate. Responds to Lee Berman's question about what inhumi and Neighbors become when dream travelling between Urth, Blue, Green and the Whorl, citing Scylla, who appears as a raven but is human in dream travel.
answers · myth-and-structure · theories: Dream travellers take on the shape of their souls

From: "Lee Berman" <[email]>

Neighbours become Neighbours, just as humans become humans Inhumi become whatever species they are imitating.

Essentially, dream travellers take on the shape of their souls. Scylla travels in a raven, but in dream travel she is human. But normally, human bodies house human souls.

Inhumi are an exceptional case.

- Gerry Quinn
