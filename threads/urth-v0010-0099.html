1998-05-06 Tony Ellis #

Announces that the poster has scanned and uploaded Bruce Pennington's cover art for The Book of the New Sun paperbacks to a web page for list members to view. Says the scans were made from worn personal copies, with spines captured imperfectly, and invites opinions on the art.
informs

06/05/98
17:37 Bruce Pennington Covers Uploaded

For Dan Parmenter, and anyone else who hasn't seen them, I've scanned and uploaded the Bruce Pennington TBOTNS covers. They can be found at:

http://easyweb.easynet.co.uk/~ajellis/WolfeArt.htm

Sadly, all I had to scan were my tatty old paperbacks, which like Severian's brown book are much-travelled, water-stained and worn. Scanning the spines was a bit hit-and-miss too, for obvious reasons. Anyway, hopefully there's enough there for people to see what all the fuss is about.

Let's have your opinions, if any.
