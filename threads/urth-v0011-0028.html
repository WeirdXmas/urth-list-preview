Suzanne Delage:Quilts & smallpox

Themes: Colonization and the native

1998-05-13 Michael Doolittle #

Argues that the smallpox-in-quilts idea is historically plausible, citing accounts of European colonists and soldiers giving clothing and bedding from smallpox victims to Native Americans in hopes they would catch the disease. Responds to the thread's question of whether Suzanne Delage's quilts could really spread smallpox, grounding the claim in several historical accounts the poster says he has read.
supports · source-and-allusion

Actually the smallpox is possible. I've read several accounts of European colonists and/or soldiers giving clothes/bedclothes from smallpox victims to Native Americans *specifically* in the hope that they would catch the disease.

Mike
