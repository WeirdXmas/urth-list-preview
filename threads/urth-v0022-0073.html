Asks whether Severian had sexual relations with the Chatelaine Thecla, framed as a follow-up to his acknowledged lies and confused memory. Raises the question directly without offering an interpretation or citing a specific passage as evidence.

1999-01-08 Alex David Groce #

Argues that Severian did have sex with Thecla, citing his statement in The Sword of the Lictor, chapter "The People of the Lake," comparing his entering Pia to his earlier entering of Thecla. Presents this passage as the first definitive admission of their affair, with earlier hints such as the House Azure scene.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian and Thecla had sex

That depends on the meaning of the word "is."
No, seriously, unless Severian is lying, he did have sex with Thecla. Best reference being (in my opinion) chapter XXXI ("The People of the Lake") in SWORD, where Severian says "Pia and I spend the night on one of the floating islands, where I, who had entered Thecla so often when she was unchained but a prisoner, now entered Pia when she was still chained but free." As far as I can recall, this is the first (and only) definitive statement by Severian that he and Thecla were lovers, although it is certainly hinted at much earlier (as in the House Azure).
