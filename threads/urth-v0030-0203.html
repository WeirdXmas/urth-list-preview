Argues that the character Dopey, not Sandwalker, fits the role of the dwarf who challenges and scratches Sandwalker in Fifth Head of Cerberus, correcting the Snow White comparison. Responds to a previous post's list of the seven figures' ages and sexes and its Snow White reading, using the scene of the challenge and scratch as evidence.
