Proposes that Paeon and Appian are the same person, the Autarch playing honey-steward much as Severian's predecessor played whoremaster, with the name a nickname or Severian's mishearing. Grounds the idea in Roy's quotation from Citadel chapter XXV and the sound similarity of the two names, while noting a possible counter-argument.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Paeon is Appian the Autarch; Paeon is Appian's honey-steward nickname; Paeon name is Severian's mishearing

I worry that I've missed some obvious counter-argument, but it seems to me that Appian and Paeon are one and the same.
