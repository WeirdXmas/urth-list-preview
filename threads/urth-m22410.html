Gene Wolfe's literary portrait of Neil Armstrong

Urth list · 1 messages · 2012-08-26 to 2012-08-26

2012urthDaniel Petersen

2012-08-26 Daniel Petersen #

Shares a link to Gene Wolfe's 1980 tribute to Neil Armstrong and asks whether a quoted Armstrong remark about his thumb blotting out Earth sounds Wolfean. Offers the Armstrong quotation itself as the basis, comparing its tone of smallness and perspective to Wolfe's style without developing a detailed argument.
questions

I thought you folks would appreciate being reminded that Wolfe actually composed his own tribute to Neil Armstrong back in 1980:

http://silkandhornheresy.blogspot.co.uk/2012/08/this-warrior-of-dead-world-gene-wolfes.html

While I'm at it, does this reminiscence from Armstrong not sound rather Wolfean?

"It suddenly struck me that that tiny pea, pretty and blue, was the Earth. I put up my thumb and shut one eye, and my thumb blotted out the planet Earth. I didn't feel like a giant. I felt very, very small." (Neil Armstrong)
