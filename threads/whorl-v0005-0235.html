1997-04-20 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Notes that The Claw of the Conciliator's appendix defines a league as about three miles, and argues that Wolfe probably did not play the computer game Starcross, though the Starcrosser name may come from someone who did. Responds to an earlier thread on linear measure and Starcross by reasoning about who coined "Starcrosser's Landfall" and suggesting the editor David Hartwell could be asked.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Starcrosser name may derive from the Starcross game

Re: linear measure, FWIW: "In this book, _league_ designates a distance of about three miles" (THE CLAW OF THE CONCILIATOR, second appendix).

Re: "Starcross" and "Starcrosser"; I doubt that Gene Wolfe has played any computer games since he still doesn't have a computer; OTOH, I know that he wasn't the one who came up with "Starcrosser's Landfall," so it doesn't matter that he probably hasn't played "Starcross," and it becomes quite possible that the "Starcrosser" originator either played it or had second-hand knowledge of it. If we find David Hartwell, Gene Wolfe's editor, at a convention we can employ subtle interrogation.

=mantis=
