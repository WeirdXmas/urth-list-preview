1999-02-01 Patrick O'Leary #

Informs list readers that the Nova Express editor has only about twenty copies left of the Wolfe issue, which includes an interview and a review of the Long Sun books, and gives the mail-order address and price. Reports this as a notice passed along from Lawrence Person, with no argument or reading of the fiction offered.
