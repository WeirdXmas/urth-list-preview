2009-04-08 Dave Lebling replying to Mo Holkar / UKG #

Proposes that Cyby, Ultan's assistant in The Book of the New Sun, is Wolfe's stand-in in the novel rather than Severian. Supports this by quoting Cyby's physical description and noting the book was published in 1980 when Wolfe was about forty.
proposes · author-and-biography · close-textual-reading · theories: Cyby is Wolfe's stand-in in the novel

I thought it was generally agreed that though Severian may be a stand-in for Wolfe, Ultan's assistant Cyby was Wolfe's stand-in in the novel:

"... a stocky, very erect man of forty or so with a flat, pale face. ... Cyby had a high, square forehead, from which his graying hair was in retreat. It made his face seem small and a trifle babyish..."

Remember that Shadow of the Torturer was published in 1980, so it was written when Wolfe was "forty or so."

-- Dave Lebling, aka vizcacha

Responds to Dave Lebling's quoted passage describing Cyby's forehead and face, adding a light joke about one photo resembling Stimpy.
