Inhumi swimming through the stars

Urth list · 1 messages · 2009-06-22 to 2009-06-22

2009urthThe Book of the New SunTim O'Donnell

Themes: Beasts and the animal in us, Water, the sea and drowning

2009-06-22 Tim O'Donnell #

Proposes that Inhumi once swam between stars because they inherited that power by feeding on undines, which originated on Blue/Green, and lost it once no undines remained. Offers this as a plausible explanation for why Inhumi no longer travel between stars and asks whether the reasoning has an obvious flaw.
proposes · myth-and-structure · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Inhumi inherited star-swimming by feeding on undines

Hi,

I was just thinking about whether Inhumi can swim between the stars or not. There is one way I could see them being capable of doing it - assuming that the undines originated on Blue/Green
(which I believe they did) then the offspring of any inhumi that fed on an undine might pick up the power to swim
between the stars.

If Mother is the only undine left in the systems after the others had moved on (possibly to escape the inhumi)
then this may explain why the inhumi no longer have this ability. There are no available victims to inherit the power from.

Anyone else think this is plausible or am I missing an obvious reason this cannot be?

Thanks,
Tim
