Re Typhon's mision

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-07-02 to 1999-07-02

1999whorlAlice Turner

1999-07-02 Alice Turner #

Reports that Wolfe privately told someone that Typhon did not send the sleds out as a food cargo, and recalls his hint that Q boarded the Whorl while in orbit rather than travelling with them. Responds to Josh by dropping her own pizza-delivery theory as a result, noting the plot will supply enough for a next series.
informs · author-and-biography · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Typhon's sleds were not a food cargo; Q boarded the Whorl while in orbit

Josh,

True, I was fond of my idea in a vampiric sort of way. But Wolfe actually vocalized to someone--if it wasn't mantis, or Patrick it might have been Jim Jordan (of whom there has been far too little lately, ou sont les Rats d'antan?)--that it's not true, that Typhon did not send them out as a food Cargo. Perhaps it was at the same time that he intimated that Q had not been with them during the entire trip, but had climbed aboard while the Whorl was in orbit, though how he did it beats me.

There's going to be quite enough plot to fuel the next series even without (thank you, mantis) the takeaway pizza theory.

-alga
