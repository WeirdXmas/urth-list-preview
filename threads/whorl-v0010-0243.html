Themes: Colonization and the native

2000-01-22 Josh Levitan #

Proposes that the people of Pajarocu have a truce with the inhumi, who avoid biting townsfolk because the humans let them load a lander with meat and slaves for their home planet. Offers this as a reply to Alga, grounding it in the staged lander trap and the risk that attacks would scare the inhumi off.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Pajarocu humans have a truce with the inhumi

Allan Lloyd makes some pretty good points, most of which Alga responded to. However:

And Alga replied:

I read this as Pajarocu, because they were staging the trap with the lander, had a truce with the inhumi -- the inhumi wouldn't bite them because the humans allowed them to load up a lander full of meat/slaves for the home planet. If the inhumi started attacking people in Pajarocu, they might get scared off and/or warn other people.
