Themes: Colonization and the native, Names and their meanings

2006-07-26 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to b sharp #

Argues that Wolfe's names in The Book of the New Sun carry symbolic meaning beyond labeling, such as the monster Echidna's husband and the sephiroth-like Briah and Yesod, and that Wolfe offers partial truths rather than lies. Responds to b sharp's quoted claim that Wolfe breaks his own rule that names state what things are, and defends the reading that the shadowchildren in The Fifth Head of Cerberus are real.
