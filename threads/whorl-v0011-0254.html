2000-09-09 Mr Mnemonic #

Argues that the inhuma Fava could have learned composition through long practice, since she is much older than she looks, answering the question of how an inhuma writes. Also argues that the dragon Oreb has his own personality rather than being wholly a god's vessel, citing two occasions in In Green's Jungles where Horn asks Oreb to find something and he does.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Oreb has personality apart from a rider god

As we all know, practice makes perfect. Also, Fava is said to be much older than she looks, even older than Inclito's mother, so it's likely she's been at it for quite a while

I would say that there is much more to Oreb than "fish heads?" and "no cut". Twice in IGJ Horn asks Oreb to find something, and he does (The altar and a way for his horde to pass a swamp). Obviously, there can't be a rider on Oreb _all_ the time, so I believe that much of what we hear is Oreb's personality, rather than a God.

Practice, practice, practive
