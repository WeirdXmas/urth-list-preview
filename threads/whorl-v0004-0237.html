1997whorlThe Book of the New SunThe Book of the Long SunJack LyonsAlice TurnerDerek BellJames Jordan
The thread: Readers debated whether Nessus in The Book of the Long Sun is modeled on Alexandria or Buenos Aires. Alice Turner and Patera Jack argued for Alexandria, citing the library and capital-city parallels and noting Nessus lies south of the equator, while Jim Jordan read it as Buenos Aires, offering reasons such as a possible axis flip and South American details. Derek Bell agreed with the Alexandria resemblance in part but doubted the Buenos Aires link, later clarifying his comparison was metaphorical and that South America is likely. Asked for a Wolfe source, Jordan conceded he lacked confirmation, and the thread ended without resolution. Turner also raised a side point about the young-girl narration and a separate list.
  • Alice Turner: Nessus is in a general way Alexandria, not Buenos Aires; asks whether Wolfe said so
  • Derek Bell: Nessus resembles Alexandria in library and capital, Master Ultan is a Borges figure, Commonwealth probably in South America
  • Patera Jack: Nessus resembles Alexandria; lies south of equator, so Buenos Aires north-jungle reasoning fails
  • Jim Jordan: Reads Nessus as Buenos Aires, admits no Wolfe confirmation, asks for evidence behind Alexandria reading

Themes: Myth and folklore sources, Names and their meanings, Books, libraries and writing

Agrees that Nessus resembles Alexandria, citing its large library and its status as the Commonwealth's principal city, but doubts the Buenos Aires link. Supports the idea that Master Ultan is a Borges figure, recalling Wolfe's remark that any author inventing Borges would deserve a Nobel Prize.
supports · author-and-biography · source-and-allusion · theories: Nessus is modeled on Alexandria; Master Ultan is a Borges figure

I agree - there's a resemblance between both Nessus and
Alexandria. Nessus has a large library and is the principal city of the Commonwealth. I'm not convinced about the "Nos Aires" link, but that may be more convincing in some accent other than mine. I agree that Master Ultan is a Borges figure and Wolfe said once that if Borges didn't exist, any author who created him would deserve a Nobel Prize for literature. 1997-03-17 James Jordan #

Admits that the poster has never read Nessus as Alexandria and instead reads it as Buenos Aires, offering a sequence of reasons including a possible axis flip and South American cultural details. Responds to alga's challenge by conceding he lacks Wolfe's confirmation and asking alga for the evidence behind the Alexandria reading.
questions · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Nessus is Buenos Aires; Nessus is Alexandria; Urth flipped on its axis twice

Concerning Nessus:

No, I don't recall Gene's having told me that. I've assumed it from the beginning, and have never questioned it. We are in the southern hemisphere, and the civilization is undergirded by the Word of the Borgesian library. The only problem I've had is that at one point Vodalus (as I recall) says that Urth has flipped on its axis once. Well, if that be correct, it must have happened twice for the Buenos Aires connection to be correct. I took Nessus as (Bue)Nos Aires in the same way that Cordwainer Smith's Meeya Meefla is Miami Fla. But, that was me, not Wolfe. Smith is not a major influence on Wolfe, despite appearances. (It seems that they both think along the same lines, and that creates the appearance of some dependance.)
What I have never done is try to "solve the crossword puzzle" (to use Mantis's felicitous analogy) by looking for evidence of the direction of flow of the river, whether Urth flipped more than once, etc. If the river flows west instead of east, and if we are in South America, then perhaps Nessus is Santiago.
Now, one other point: Pas has loaded the cargo of Starcrosser with locals. These locals sip mate and are generally (in Viron) a South American culture. This would indicate that Typhon's domain is in the Argentine area, not in an Egyptian area. AHA, but the Trivigauntes speak a kind of Arabic, so maybe they and not the Vironese were the local population around Typhon's throne.
So, okay. I give up. Gene has defeated me. Where's my .357 Magnum....? If Urth flipped only once, then the Alexandria suggestion makes more sense; especially since Alexandria is noted for its library. In that case, the Ultanian nod to Borges would just be a nod, and not a Clew! So then: What do you see as evidence for Alexandria?

JBJordan (Hmmm. What shall I be? How about Nutria!)
