Whole message, with quotes · original
>Last week, New City asked the musical question "Is genre fiction a literary
>blemish or good clean fun?" For the piece, they interviewed some local
"genre"
>authors, including Gene Wolfe, who they introduced by saying, "In the
realms of
>sci-fi, Wolfe is the shit."
>Thus spake El Wolfo:
>"Frankly, I like being discriminated against...I don't think we need a second
>literary fiction category, we have that. It's addressed, frankly, to quite a
>small audience. The audience for literary fiction wants validation for the
lives
>they are leading right now. The audience for genre fiction wants assurance
that
>lives other than the lives they are leading are possible. My objection is
>escapism as a pejorative term. What's wrong with saying, 'You can get out'?
>Nothing, if you ask me."
>
