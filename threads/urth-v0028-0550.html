1999-12-02 Ron Hale-Evans #

Suggests that a rumored Shadow of the Torturer video game is a case of people inventing things they want but cannot have, comparing it to the forger in Peace who fabricates the Necronomicon and other sought-after books. Responds to Fr. Inire's mocking remark about the rumor by offering this psychological explanation, with no textual evidence beyond the Peace parallel.
proposes

Well, Padre, my guess is that the impulse was similar to that shown by the character in _Peace_ who forged the Necronomicon and other much-desired books. Some things we want so much that we create them in fiction if we cannot have them in reality, even if the fiction is both pleasurable and... a torture.

Ron H-E
