1997-10-02 Alice Turner #

Lists a set of place names from Soldier of Arete mapped to their Greek originals, such as Advent to Eleusis, Thought to Athens, and Peace to Salamis. The post offers no argument; it is a reference list that gives the Greek equivalents of Wolfe's renamed locations.
