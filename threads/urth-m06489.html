The thread: Oliver Wakefield asked for steampunk recommendations and criticized China Miéville's Iron Council as a laboured socialist message, and the thread drew several answers about steampunk novels. It soon drifted into a political argument started by brunians, who claimed socialism and Miéville's politics hurt his writing and compared Christian and socialist atrocities. Lane Haygood argued Moorcock is anarchist rather than socialist, while Pedro Pereira repeatedly asked the list to avoid an ideology war. The thread ended in an impasse over politics on the list, with no discussion of Wolfe's work.
  • Oliver Wakefield: Iron Council's socialist message is laboured; asks for steampunk recommendations
  • Tim Walters: Blaylock's The Digging Leviathan is outstanding steampunk, Lord Kelvin's Machine is weak
  • brunians: Socialism and Miéville's politics damage his writing; politics belongs on the list
  • Lane Haygood: Moorcock's fiction is anarchistic rather than socialist
  • Pedro Pereira: Keep ideology out of the list and do not equate Church with socialism
  • David Duffy: Recommends alternate-history and steampunk novels, defends The Difference Engine's ending

Themes: Power, rulers and politics, Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both

Recommends James Blaylock's novel The Digging Leviathan as outstanding steampunk that differs from most of the genre, and calls the more conventional Lord Kelvin's Machine weak. 2008-05-12 Lane Haygood replying to brunians #

Argues that Michael Moorcock's fiction is anarchistic rather than socialist, citing the reluctant heroes who want to be left alone and refuse their duty. Supports the claim with a joking hypothetical about Elric leading a proletarian revolution and an arms race with Melnibone, and responds to brunians' remark that communism has rotted Moorcock's brain.
disputes

I didn't think Moorcock was a socialist/communist. In fact, I've always read his stuff as being much more anarchistic (although it does have a decidedly leftist bent) than socialistic. His heroes (with the notable exception of Hawkmoon) are usually the "just leave me alone I am so tired of everyone expecting me to save the Multiverse just because I'm the Champion" type. Some of them (Erekose) even try to run away from their duty.

If Moorcock were a socialist, Elric would have gone to Elwher, lead the people in a proletarian revolution, and then entered an arms race with Melnibone. "We've got to make more soul-sucking swords before they do. Mutually assured soul-sucking!"

There's a "Dr. Strangelove" joke buried in here somewhere but I can't get to it.

Best,
Lane
