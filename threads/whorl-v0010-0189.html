Some corrections to Talon's list

Corrects Talon's list of proper names in Blue's Waters: notes that Magnesia was Marble's original name and that Molybdenum was assumed to win Hammerstone, that Molpe is actually a major goddess among the Nine, and that a butcher is Scleroderma's husband. Grounds the corrections in the names list at the top of page 9 of the book, where the entries are already present.
disputes · close-textual-reading

Talon has listed some proper names not in the list at the beginning of _Blue_. I would like to offer the following (minor) corrections:

"Magnesia" was her (Marble's) original name; she assumed "Molybdenum" in order to win Hammerstone.

Actually present in the names list, top of p. 9.

Actually a major goddess, one of the Nine.

_Moly_. _Maytera_.

(additionally) A butcher, husband of Scleroderma.

Actually a major goddess, one of the Nine.

-- Dan Rabin
