Winnoc, Beuzec in Lex

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-07-02 to 1998-07-02

1998urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

1998-07-02 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that Beuzec, mentioned in the Lexicon Urthus introduction but lacking an entry, may be the slug Hethor uses to move around without teleporting. Grounds this in Hethor's grayness and ratlike creepiness, and in Beuzec's disappearance into a wall hole where the white wolves lurk like oversized rats.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Beuzec is the slug Hethor carries; Hethor's ratlike traits link to the white wolves

prion,

Beuzec is teasingly mentioned in "the Lexicographer's Introduction" (of "Lexicon Urthus: Additions, Errata, &cetera") but doesn't have an entry in the text ("Additions and Errata") that follows. Winnoc has no mention at all.

Re: Beuzec as the slug. An interesting notion which goes toward explaning how Hethor might transport it around without teleporting all the time.

Hethor has many aspects about him, including for example, aspects of "grayness" and "being a hairless rat." The grayness translates into his great antiquity; the ratliness shows his creepiness, the revulsion he creates in Severian.

But when Beuzec disappears into a hole in the wall (like a rat hole), and Severian supposes that he has escaped into the second house, we have rats again--since the white wolves lurk within the walls like rats writ large.

=mantis=
