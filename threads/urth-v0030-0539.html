Recounts rereading Peake's Gormenghast trilogy backwards and preferring Titus Alone, and recalls a planned 1980s film with Sting as Steerpike. Replies to Dan'l and William, calling the current adaptation visually good but too short and missing Peake's tone; the post is off-topic for Wolfe, with no Wolfe content.
chats

I have to admit, the last time I reread the trilogy, I read it backwards, and enjoyed Titus Alone the best (I was reminded of it last weekend, sitting in deckchairs criticising a sunrise ;)).

Adam said

At one time in the 80's, there was to be a movie version with Sting as Steerpike (a la Brimstone & Treacle I guess) -- I was quite looking forward to it. The current version is visually good and the supporting characters true to Peake's drawings and descriptions (has anyone mentioned the Doctor), but too short and somehow missing the exact tone of Peake's writing (I too recommend reading _Mr Pye_).

Cheers, David.
