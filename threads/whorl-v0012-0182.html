Coincidences

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-02-11 to 2001-02-11

2001whorlDan Rabin

2001-02-11 Dan Rabin #

Argues that several coincidences in Wolfe's sequels are strained, including Jahlee being Krait's mother, the ship and captain in Return reusing the Samru, and Eata's boat rescuing Severian. Raises a continuity nit about Blood's mansion, whose location on the Old Palustria Road in Long Sun seems to conflict with its position on the road from Viron to Endroad in Return.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading

I agree with David Lebling that the coincidence of Jahlee being Krait's mother is a bit of a stretch.

I also find it a bit unsatisfying that Wolfe tends to revisit established locales. Was there any reason why the ship in _Return_ had to be the _Samru_? I didn't notice any particular continuity in the character of the captain, nor any motivation for his later kind treatment of Severian.

In _Urth of the New Sun_, the boat that picks up Severian happens to be Eata's.

I also have a nit concerning Blood's mansion. Wasn't it off the Old Palustria Road in _Long Sun_? In _Return_, it's on the road from Viron that ends in Endroad. Perhaps the road doesn't go all the way to Palustria any more?

-- Dan Rabin
