Pig's eye

Urth list · 6 messages · 2011-11-30 to 2011-11-30

The thread: Marc Aramini and Roy Lackey debated whether Silver Silk stayed in the Mainframe while a copy was trapped in Pig, and whether a fragment or the whole of him was copied, deleted, or flashed back into Silk's body after Hy's death. Lackey argued the reboot did not depend on Pig's eyesight because a Sacred Window had already let the god move into Oreb, citing Return to the Whorl p. 174. James Wynn offered a general account of Mainframe entities reintegrating memories when they return, without citing specific scenes. António Pedro Marques suggested the Long Sun narrator could be a surviving echo of Silk that returned to the Mainframe rather than being fully deleted. The thread stayed circular and ended without resolution.
  • Marc Aramini: asks whether the trapped Pig copy was a fragment or whole, and how copying and deletion allow a flash-back
  • Roy C. Lackey: reboot did not depend on Pig's eye, since a Sacred Window already let the god enter Oreb; a flash-back is impossible if he was deleted or absent
  • James Wynn: Mainframe entities are parts of a larger whole that reintegrate memories and identity when they return
  • António Pedro Marques: the Long Sun narrator may be a surviving echo of Silk or Silver Silk that returned to the Mainframe

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Memory and forgetting, Doubles, twins and clones

2011-11-30 Roy C. Lackey #

Argues that Silkhorn's reboot of Silver Silk did not depend on Pig's eyesight, since a Sacred Window in the Grand Manteion had already let the god move into Oreb. Quotes Silver Silk's reply in Return to the Whorl, page 174, and notes a working glass in Pig's recovery room, answering an earlier post that tied the eye sacrifice to Silk's reboot.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Silver Silk trapped in Pig by blindness; Silkhorn's reboot did not require Pig's sight; Sacred Window let Silk import the god

Marc Aramini write:

Silkhorn didn't need Pig to be able to see in order to "reboot" Silver Silk into Silkhorn. A glass or Sacred Window was needed to import or export a god, and Silkhorn had already been exposed to the Sacred Window in the Grand Manteion before the eye operation. (That is when and how a bit of Scylla got into Oreb, when Silk assisted with the sacrifices at the Grand Manteion on the same day that he was found by Hari Mau.)

Rather, Silver Silk in Pig was trapped there by Pig's blindness. Silkhorn confronted the Silver Silk in Pig in Hy's old room in the ruins of Blood's villa, saying: "You're not with her now, I realize. As long as Pig is blind, you can't go back to her." Silver Silk answered, "Correct. Though I would try to have his sight restored in any case, just as you yourself are making a praiseworthy effort to restore Maytera's." (RTTW, 174) And there was a working glass in Pig's recovery room for Silver Silk to use in order to reintegrate with his greater self in Mainframe.

-Roy

2011-11-30 James Wynn replying to Marc Aramini #

Proposes that Silver Silk, like any other Mainframe entity, is a part of a larger whole that can possess humans, chems and animals, and that when that part returns to Mainframe its memories and identity are reintegrated. Responds to Marc Aramini's question about whether Silver Silk returns wholesale to Mainframe or only reunites with a greater part, offering a general account of how Mainframe entities work without citing specific scenes.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Silver Silk is a part of a Mainframe entity

I presume Silver Silk is like any other Mainframe entity. A part --and aspect-- of him can possess humans, chems, and animals. When the part returns to Mainframe, it's memories and identity are reintegrated into the whole.

Yes, Silver Silk in Pig could only reunite with his greater self in
Mainframe. That is the only way he could reunite with the "her" of the quote
I gave. Not even a human can come close to harboring all that constitutes a
god in the giant computer that is Mainframe. Scylla in Chenille at the lake
also said she must reintegrate with her greater self in Mainframe, when she
left Chenille. There is no reason to suppose that Silver Silk had been wiped
out of Mainframe.

And yes, it would have been possible for the greater Silver Silk in
Mainframe to flash part of himself into Silk's body, whether by way of the
Grand Manteion's Sacred Window or the working glass in Pig's recovery room.

Of course the "her" of the quote most properly refers to Kypris, because Hy
wasn't dead at the time Silver Silk became trapped in Pig. Hy may have "gone
to Mainframe", so to speak, when she died, but it wasn't Hy that Silver Silk
was longing for.

-Roy
