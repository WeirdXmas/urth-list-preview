2002-07-20 David DiGiacomo #

Proposes a possible explanation for how an AI could read a person's thoughts through the eye in Wolfe's Urth books, suggesting evoked potentials triggered by the Holy Hues are detected by an unmentioned sensor in the terminal. Responds to Adam Stephanides's objection that eyes do not broadcast and a computer could not learn enough about neural activity by looking into someone's eye, admitting Wolfe left this unexplained.
proposes · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft · theories: Holy Hues stimulate evoked potentials detected by terminal sensor

This bothered me too, and I think Wolfe could have thrown in a word of exposition, but I have an idea.

In our time, evoked potentials are the most practical way to detect thoughts from EEG signals. Perhaps the Holy Hues stimulate evoked potentials, which are detected by an unseen and unmentioned sensor inside the terminal.

(Have you all heard of the term "fanwank"?)
