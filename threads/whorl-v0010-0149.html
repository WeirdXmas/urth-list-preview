Secret of the inhumi

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-11-05 to 1999-11-05

1999whorlNicholas Gevers

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1999-11-05 Nicholas Gevers #

Extends William Ansley's suggestion that the secret Horn holds is a need for humans to protect one another from inhumi bites, which would stop inhumi from mimicking people. Points out that some human settlers went to Green, so any such defensive strategy would first require freeing them from presumed inhumi captivity.
supports · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Horn's secret is protection against inhumi bites

If William Ansley is correct in his suggestion that
the secret Horn possesses is the need for all humans
to protect each other against being bitten by the
inhumi--thus preventing the inhumi from mimicking
human appearance and intelligence--it should be
remembered that some human settlers went to Green. So
any defensive strategy requires their liberation from
(presumed) inhumi captivity.
