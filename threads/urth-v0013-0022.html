Themes: Colonization and the native, Ghosts, dreams and visions

1998-06-11 Neal Smith #

Argues that the Whorl's natives became spacey and New Agey because the original colonists lost acquired knowledge, and suggests an addictive, possibly hallucinogenic drug shown in "A Story" as another cause. Offers the colonist-inbreeding explanation as a bridge between Alga's and Borski's views, and cites the shadow children's addiction in "A Story" as evidence.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Whorl natives devolved from colonists losing knowledge; Shadow children's drug explains native spaciness

desk? That's why I say colonists rather than explorers, who presumably have been trained in many skills. Or, if that won't wash, say that nearly everyone was killed except for a few folks as dumb as me. And that we then inbred and got kind of spacy, as I would imagine people do get under such circumstances (cf Australia, if you like--hi Damien!). This, it seems to me, would create at least a rope bridge between you and Borski. ...

Exactly. They devolved because of lack of ability to pass on acquired knowledge. Also (this is 1972), they're a bit New Agey to start with.

With respect to the idea that the abos are "spacey" or "New Agey", I think the addictive, (perhaps hallucinogenic?) drug that the shadow children are shown being addicted to in "A Story" would provide this.

-Neal Smith
