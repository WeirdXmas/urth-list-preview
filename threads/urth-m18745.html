Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2011-06-19 Adam Thornton #

Recounts a dream in which the poster convincingly argued that Severian is Baldanders' father and that Frog and Fish stand for Baldanders and Talos. Reports that the supporting evidence vanished on waking, so the theory is offered only as a jokey dream report.
chats · theories: Severian is Baldanders' father; Frog and Fish are Baldanders and Talos

Last night I dreamed that I had convincingly shown that Severian was Baldanders' father. I had a very clever note I had written about it. Further, Frog and Fish were to be understood as Baldanders and Talos. This was in the midst of an otherwise Portal-influenced dream that was taking place in a space rather like an airport (architecturally).

Unfortunately, just what evidence I had to support this hypothesis--which, I reiterate, was convincing--has evaporated with the dream.

Adam
