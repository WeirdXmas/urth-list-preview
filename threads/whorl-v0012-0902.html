Chrasmo Writings

Whorl list · 6 messages · 2001-07-03 to 2001-07-03

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunThe Book of the New SunJerry FriedmanDan'l Danehy-OakesMichael Andre-DriussiJames Jordan
The thread: Participants asked whether the Chrasmological Writings in The Book of the Long Sun draw on real scripture, and whether Silk's Christ-like story reflects a gospel source. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argued the Writings were a source, citing a supposed Jeremiah passage, while James Jordan argued the parallels to the gospels must have been deliberately crafted, since Horn and Nettle wrote the books. Jerry Friedman suggested the Outsider guided the Chrasmologists and proposed Typhon as another source of messianic ideas, and Jordan separately argued Severian was the Conciliator. Michael Andre-Driussi added that the Writings' longest passages come from Marcus Aurelius, then corrected the credit to Charles Dye; the thread ended without resolution.
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Chrasmo Writings likely drew on real scripture, a source for Horn and Nettle's Jesus-like story, or a universal messiah pattern
  • Jerry Friedman: the Outsider may have guided the Chrasmologists and Silk's enlightenment; Typhon may be another messianic source
  • James Jordan: gospel parallels in Silk's story were deliberately crafted, implying gospel versions lie in the Chrasmo Writings; Severian was the Conciliator
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: longest passages of the Writings are taken from Marcus Aurelius, found by Charles Dye; the Writings are a miscellaneous grab bag

Themes: Myth and folklore sources, Books, libraries and writing, Christ figures and messiahs

2001-07-03 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues that the Chrasmological Writings in The Book of the Long Sun likely draw on real scripture, citing a raven-daubing bit said to come from Jeremiah, and that Silk's thought about a crucified enlightened man on the Short Sun Whorl suggests the Writings were a source. Offers this as support for the idea that Horn and Nettle had a Jesus-like story before them, while adding a third option about a universal messiah pattern.
supports · source-and-allusion · theories: Horn and Nettle had a Jesus-like story before them; Chrasmological Writings quote real scripture or variants

Or, (c), the pattern of the Messiah-story is deeply ingrained in the "collective unconciousness" or the nature of the human soul or, uh, whatever.

But in support of (b),
i. The (fairly few) quotes from the Chrasmological Writings actually offered to us are, I believe, either quotes from real Scripture (though not necessary the Hebrew-Christian Scriptures) or variants thereon. I believe the bit
about daubing the raven comes from Jeremiah, for example. ii.Silk actually talks (or maybe thinks) about a man back on the Short Sun Whorl who was both enlightened and possessed by the Outsider and was crucified. Presumably this would come either form the CWs or -- uh, where else?

--Blattid

2001-07-03 Jerry Friedman #

Proposes that the Outsider may have guided the Chrasmologists and Silk's enlightenment, citing the correct prophecies of augury, Gaonese astrology and Chrasmological bibliomancy as evidence. Responds to James Jordan's argument that Silk's parallels to Jesus are either accidental or drawn from an earlier version of the gospel story, suggesting Typhon's contemporaneity with the Conciliator as another source of messianic ideas.
proposes · theology-and-religion · allegory-and-typology · theories: The Outsider guided Chrasmological prophecies and Silk's enlightenment

Or the Outsider has been taking a hand--when he
enlightened Silk, or just in general, the way he makes
sure that auguries and Gaonese astrologers provide
correct prophecies or that Chrasmological
bibliomancies provide good advice. (Unless you
believe all that stuff was put in by Horn
retroactively.)

The Outsider may also have been at work influencing
the "Chrasmologists", incidentally. Dare I say
"inspiring"?

Also, Typhon was apparently a contemporary of the
Conciliator, so messianic messages may have been
familiar to him and his sprats and the early
Chrasmologists.

Jerry Friedman

2001-07-03 James Jordan #

Argues that because Horn and Nettle wrote the Book of the Long Sun, the Silk story's parallels to the gospels must have been deliberately crafted, implying that gospel versions are contained in the Chrasmo Writings. Grounds this on an earlier list of gospel parallels for Silk's story and a dilemma: either the resemblance to Jesus is accidental or the writers had a gospel version before them.
proposes · source-and-allusion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Silk deliberately crafted as a new messiah; Chrasmo Writings contain versions of the gospels

Way back at the beginning of all this I provided a long list of gospel parallels for the story of Silk. Of course, that's Wolfe's doing. But since Horn and Nettle wrote TBLS, and since much of it is pious invention to one degree or another, it follows that either (a) it is an accident that the Silk story parallels the story of Jesus, or (b) that Horn and Nettle had a version of the story of Jesus before them as they wrote TBLS, and deliberately crafted Silk as a kind of new messiah. If the latter, which seems likely, this means that some version/s of the gospel/s are included in the Chrasmo Writings -- which I guess we should expect, but which is nowhere made explicit.
Food for thought, or controversy.

Patera Nutria

2001-07-03 James Jordan #

Proposes that the time-travelling Severian was the Conciliator, and that the theoanthropos mentioned in The Memoirs of Severian predates the Conciliator by many thousands of years. Responds to Jerry Friedman's point that Typhon was a contemporary of the Conciliator, offering a brief view with no detailed evidence.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theology-and-religion · theories: Time-travelling Severian was the Conciliator; The theoanthropos predates the Conciliator by millennia

Well, I've taken it that time-travelling Severian was the Conciliator. We also have reference in the Memoirs of Severian to the "theoanthropos," and I think He predated the Conciliator by many thousands of years. FWIW.
