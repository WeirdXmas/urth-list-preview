2006-06-12 Fred Kiesche #

Laments that the out-of-print Lexicon Urthus is unavailable and notes high used-book prices listed online for copies. Reports asking-prices from Amazon and ABEBooks, wishing the reference book had been around when he first became interested in Wolfe.

2006-06-12 James Crossley replying to Fred Kiesche #

Informs Fred Kiesche that the Lexicon Urthus reference book will be reprinted within a year or two, according to the publisher's website. Also notes that unbound copies can be bought directly from the publisher, offering an alternative to the high secondhand prices quoted in the earlier message.
