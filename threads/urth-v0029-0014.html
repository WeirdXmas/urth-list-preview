2000-05-14 William H. Ansley #

Jokes that Gene Wolfe never openly states the secret of Brother North-wind's Secret, while John Crowley does after a brief delay. Offers a playful riddle comparing Wolfe and Crowley rather than any argument about the story's content, so it is a social aside.
chats

What's the difference between a wolf and a crow? Give up? Well, just look at them. They are pretty different!

How about this one: What's the difference between G. Wolfe and J. Crowley? Well, Wolfe would would never come right out and tell you what Brother North-wind's Secret is, but Crowley does, after pausing for a few pages to keep you in suspense.

William Ansley
