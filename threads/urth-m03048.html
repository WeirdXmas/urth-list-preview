2006-09-04 Eli Goldberg #

Informs list members that a homemade music video inspired by The Book of the New Sun has been posted on YouTube and is also downloadable from a music album page. Gives no analysis; it simply shares the links, naming Per Malm's video for Julia Ecklar's song "Terminus Est".
informs

Hi, everyone --

Just wanted to mention as a courtesy that a homemade music video inspired by Gene Wolfe's "Book of the New Sun" trilogy has been posted to YouTube.

You can find Per Malm's "Terminus Est" music video (to the song by Julia Ecklar) at:

http://youtube.com/watch?v=hqY0dCmAyNM

It can also be freely downloaded in RealMedia format from the Divine Intervention album page at:

http://www.prometheus-music.com/divine.html

Yours,

Eli Goldberg
http://www.prometheus-music.com

2006-09-05 Allan Anderson replying to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Praises a homemade YouTube video of The Book of the New Sun, singling out the moment when Severian's descending feet fade into Thecla's, tracing his footsteps. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's lukewarm review and thanks Eli Goldberg for sharing the video.
supports · close-textual-reading

I love the song. My favorite bit in the video comes when you see Severian's feet descending stairs and they fade into Thecla's, always with him, always tracing his footsteps.

Thanks, Eli!
