Themes: Mirrors, pictures and art, Time loops and causality

1999-12-12 Damien Broderick #

Quotes Stephen Baxter's Picocon talk noting that Wells's Time Traveller was an expert in physical optics with seventeen papers on the subject, then jokes that he never returned because he wanders the House Absolute searching for the right mirror. Offers the joke as a playful tie-in to Wolfe's mirror imagery and the House Absolute, with no argument about the text.
chats

Stephen Baxter, in a talk for Picocon 13, Imperial College, London, 4 February 1996, remarked:

< Wells's Time Traveller was an expert in "physical optics." Wells notes, in passing, the Traveller's "seventeen papers" on the subject in Philosophical Transactions ( The Time Machine , p60.). >

[HG Wells, The Time Machine , Heinemann 1895. Page numbers in the text refer to Everyman's `Centennial' edition, ed. John Lawton, 1995.]

No wonder the Time Traveller never came back. He wanders the House Absolute to this day, searching for the right mirror.

Damien Broderick
