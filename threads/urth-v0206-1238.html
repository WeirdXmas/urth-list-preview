Alex David Groce answered that the Lafferty story is "You Can't Go Back" from Iron Tears and judged that Wolfe's imitation fails, showing that imitating Lafferty does not work. Asks the list for the title of a Lafferty story about a small moon orbiting close to Earth, which the poster recalls Wolfe's story "Has Anybody Seen Junie Moon" referring to. Notes that Wolfe's story is a homage to Lafferty and appears in the Moon Shots anthology edited by Peter Crowther.

2002-06-17 Alex David Groce #

Identifies the Lafferty story about a small moon near Earth as "You Can't Go Back" from Iron Tears, and argues that Wolfe's "Has Anybody Seen Junie Moon" shows that imitating Lafferty does not work. Answers the earlier poster's request for a title by naming the anthology where the story appears and offering a critical judgment of Wolfe's homage.
answers · source-and-allusion · genre-and-craft

It's "You Can't Go Back" in _Iron Tears_. One of Lafferty's better stories, which is saying a lot. While the intention was, I'm sure, all to the good, I think "Junie Moon"'s main virtue is in pointing out that imitation Lafferty just doesn't work.
