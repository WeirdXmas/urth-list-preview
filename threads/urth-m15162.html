2010-05-06 Jerry Friedman replying to brunians #

Clarifies that Jerry Friedman wants candid comments on his Wolfe-themed poem, and that criticism of scansion or meter is not sarcasm while a mocking remark about learning scansion would be. Responds to brunians' birthday post for Gene Wolfe and the link to the poem, setting terms for feedback on the verse.
informs

I wouldn't consider "This or that line doesn't scan" or "You could improve the rhythm of this line this way" or "On the whole, the meter doesn't work" or similar comments to be sarcastic. I'd say an example of sarcasm would be "Why don't you learn the rudiments of scansion before you make your next attempt at poetry?"

Of course if people feel the need to be sarcastic, they might not let my request stand in their way.

Jerry
