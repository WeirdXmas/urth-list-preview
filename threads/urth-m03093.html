The thread: b sharp proposed that the Baldanders-Talos relationship mirrors the humanity-Hierogrammate relationship in The Book of the New Sun and asked what 'Hiero-fools' meant after Dan'l's post spelled it differently. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes agreed the parallel is only loosely drawn, since made things surpassing their makers is a theme that Talos and the Hierogrammates do not share in intent, and explained that the 'Hiero-Foo' joke borrows the computing placeholder 'foo'. Jeff Wilson argued that 'Hiero-fools?' was a deliberate placeholder rather than a typo, because the hierogrammates are too manipulative to be holy fools. The thread ended with the spelling question settled as a joke and the parallel left as a loose analogy.
