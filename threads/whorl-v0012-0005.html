2000-11-03 Kevin J. Maroney #

Reports that Tor's schedule lists Return to the Whorl for February 2001, not the January date a bookseller gave, and that the publisher's dates are usually accurate. Responds to Dachshund's quoted B&N advance-order notice, citing a recent conversation with Patrick Nielsen Hayden and noting the poster has no advance reading copy yet.
informs

The current Tor schedule <http://www.tor.com/scheduletor.html> lists it as a February 2001 release. According to a conversation I recently had with Patrick Nielsen Hayden on rec.arts.sf.written and in e-mail, Tor schedule dates are pretty close to real, so it might show up in stores as by late January but almost certainly not by early January.

I have been mildly pestering David for an advance reading copy; I haven't seen one yet.
