2001-01-04 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Writes a public note to KaguyaHime answering her Amazon review of Lexicon Urthus, saying the book's errors are listed on free errata sheets and chapbooks. Suggests her post-it corrections may duplicate known fixes while welcoming any new ones.
answers

Hello KaguyaHime,

I saw your November 2000 review of LEXICON URTHUS on Amazon.com. Because I have no way of contacting you otherwise, I'm leaving a public note for you here in the hopes that someday you will find it, and the larger community of Wolfe-readers.

Reading over your review, I cannot help but think that you did not receive an errata sheet with your copy of the book (which must be the fault of the seller, since all copies sent out have errata sheets enclosed). Yes, there are errors in the book, and in the several years since I published the Lexicon I've been diligent about listing them all in the short form (updated errata sheets, issued free of charge) and the long form (those pesky chapbooks). That your copy is now dotted with post-its is intriguing: while I would be interested in any new corrections that you may have to offer (as would, I'm sure, all the other people who have sent in additions, corrections, &cetera), I also hope that you won't feel too stupid for reinventing the wheel in those cases where you most probably have.

=mantis=
