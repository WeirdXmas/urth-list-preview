2011-01-19 thalassocrat #

Reports struggling to re-read An Evil Guest and dreading the recurring deli sandwich, noting Wolfe's meals usually add pleasure. Proposes that Cassie, Dr Chase and Reis resonate with Jolenta, Dr Talos and Baldanders, offered as something to ponder rather than a firm argument.
proposes · theories: Cassie, Dr Chase and Reis mirror Jolenta, Dr Talos and Baldanders

& not succeeding very well. Apart from anything, I keep dreading the appearance of the next deli sandwich. Which is a pity, because normally food in Wolfe novels sounds like something I'd want to eat. Meals tend to come around pretty regularly and they usually add a little something to the pleasure of reading.

Anyway, it suddenly occurred to me that Cassie/Dr Chase/Reis resonate with Jolenta/Dr Talos/Baldanders. FWIW, just nice to ponder over.
