1998urthThe Book of the New SunPaul C Duggan

1998-11-24 Paul C Duggan #

Playfully proposes a paper map and counter wargame of the Commonwealth-Ascian war and a line of Urth miniatures, with mounted beasts, Daughters of War, and Ascian suicide rules. Responds to a poster who asked when he would start game design and mentioned the Orthodox church's claim that the suns were fading from sin.
chats

I believe the line was that the Orthodox church claimed the suns were fading as punishment for men's sins. But the rules don't say whether this is the correct interpretation.

I wish. I merely playtested a few SFB scenarios.

But my I keep mulling over a paper map/cardboard counter game of the Commonwealth/Ascian war. Something with the sides being the Heirodules vs. the Others and the Severian unit being a pawn in the middle. maybe.

And if someone would make Urth miniatures I'd really be excited:

Daugters of War on mighty arsinothers!
Mammoth with laser cannon howdah!
Dwarves riding blind giants (THAT makes lots of sense)
Nekkid Anpiels!
Walking Towers (or whatever they are)

GW's mutie riders come close to a Destrier miniature (at least they have toes).

And the Ascian "roll to see how many of your troops commit suicide before the battle" beats GW's Commissar kills Seargent to prevent squad from routing for "dark and gothic" ruleswriting.

But this is my idea an no-one may steal it. :-)
