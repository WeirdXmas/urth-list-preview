1999-04-29 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes a comparative study of generation-starship stories and the colony worlds they produce, treating each as a case study toward a model of likely colony outcomes. Lists examples including Wolfe's Book of the Long Sun, Heinlein, Niven, Stableford and Le Guin, with no close reading of Wolfe's text.
proposes

Long, long ago, we were chatting about generation starship stories and stories about the colonies that result.

Because the subject matters are so big, the two types are not usually combined. The ship stories are usually about: what stage of the journey are we in; "journey, what journey?"; etc.

=GENERATION STARSHIPS=
ORPHANS OF THE SKY by Heinlein
CAPTIVE UNIVERSE by Harry Harrison
NON-STOP by Aldis
THE STAR SEEKERS by Lesser
PHOENIX WITHOUT ASHES by Bryant (based on Ellison)
THE BOOK OF THE LONG SUN by Wolfe
etc.

=COLONIES FROM GENERATION STARSHIPS=
GIFT FROM EARTH by Niven (the caste system, with crew on top) THE PROMISED LAND by Stableford (I don't remember this one) THE EYE OF THE HERON by Le Guin (well, they are isolated . . . ) etc.

Stableford wrote a series of novels (the Daedelus series) about recontacting isolated colony worlds. While there is ftl involved, still, the isolation of the colonies makes them quite similar to what I'm talking about (what =am= I talking about? I'm actually not =talking=, I'm keying . . . well, to tell the truth I am talking, and too loudly. The neighbors might call the police.).

Aside: does anybody know of a book or books similar to the Daedelus series?

Anyway, as an experiment while we wait for the Short Sun books to materialize into our hot little hands, I was toying with the idea of looking at all the generation starship stories that we collectively know, and if consider them all as case studies, come up with a flowchart or likely outcome model for any colony ship setting out today.

Likewise, a study of colonies that have been established by generation starships (and this includes suspended animation or "sleeper" ships--as was the case in GIFT FROM EARTH, iirc).

=mantis=

Suggests adding Samuel R. Delany's The Ballad of Beta-2 to a thread on generation-ship colonies, since its colonists, like those in the Long Sun books, still live inside the ship. Gives no close reading; it is a brief pointer to a related novel and notes the list's Long Sun focus in a closing line.
