The thread: The thread began with a link to a mixed-quality online collection of Wolfe ebooks, which drew copyright concerns and a debate over whether piracy harms authors. It then turned to the uncollected story "Easter Sunday" credited to Wolfe in a Revolutions SF reprint: Rebecca Bushong-Taylor relayed Wolfe's denial, while Gwern Branwen and others argued the style and Borski's essay pointed to his authorship. Rebecca later retracted her claim after Wolfe privately accepted authorship of early pieces, and jokes about unreliable narrators and time loops followed. Jeff reported a close match to Eric Frank Russell's "Displaced Person," and Rebecca ended by citing Wolfe's own remark, so the authorship question was largely settled.

  • James Wynn: argued online piracy does little harm to authors and that scanned ebooks have limited value

  • jeff: said "Easter Sunday" closely matches Eric Frank Russell's "Displaced Person"
