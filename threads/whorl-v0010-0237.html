Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

2000-01-21 Xworders #

Proposes that humans could resist the inhumi by receiving an injection of blood from the Neighbors or Vanished People, citing vague hints of blood brotherhood in On Blue's Waters. Asks the list whether this approach has any chance of success, noting he has received no reply to an earlier version of the idea.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Injection of Neighbors' blood makes humans inhumi-proof

Dear fellow Wolfe fans,

<< To me, the most easily accomplishable and plausible
weapon that humans could have against the inhumi would be the ingestion of some substance. >>

I think no one's ever responded to my suggestion, some time ago, that humans might become inhumi-proof with an injection of blood from the Neighbors or "Vanished People." Maybe this suggestion is so boneheaded that it's deserved no reply. But stubbornly I haven't given up on the notion yet, partly because there's some vague hinting (I'll go back to OBW and quote a couple passages if it would help the discussion) about blood brotherhood, so to speak. Any chance of success in this direction, do you think?

--Henry Rathvon, Wolfe admirer from way back
