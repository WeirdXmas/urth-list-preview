List of Wolfe stories and where to find

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-26 to 1998-05-26

1998urthCraig Christensen

1998-05-26 Craig Christensen #

Reports that the poster found Wolfe's short stories in used magazines after a search turned up a bibliography site, listing the Weird Tales Spring 1988 Gene Wolfe issue and Asimov's Science Fiction October/November 1996. Describes the stories included and notes an interview in the Weird Tales issue, with no literary interpretation offered.
informs

Earlier this month I posted a question- "can anyone provide a list of magazines with Wolfe's short stories in them?" I answered my own question when a search turned up this site.

http://www.sfsite.com/isfdb-bin/exact_author.cgi?Gene_Wolfe

I went out hunting in several local used book and magazine stores and found Weird Tales, Spring 1988 - The Gene Wolfe edition. It includes 'Mary Beatrice Smoot Friarly, SPV', 'At the Point of Capricorn', 'The Boy Who Hooked the Sun', 'The Dead Man', 'John K. (Kinder) Price', and 'The Other Dead Man'. Best of all there is a very good interview here as well. I also found Asimov's Science Fiction, October/November 1996 with 'Try and Kill It'. I'm looking forward to going again next weekend with a more complete list from this site.
