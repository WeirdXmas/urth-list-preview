2006-05-16 Fred Kiesche #

Informs the list that Gene Wolfe wrote the introduction to David Drake's The Complete Hammer's Slammers, Volume One, and calls it a good essay. Says the poster disagrees with some of its claims about who is qualified to write military science fiction, based on the poster's recent purchase of the book.
informs

Greetings:

Not sure if this has been mentioned or not, but Gene
Wolfe (I just found out, having gotten the book
yesterday) wrote the introduction to "The Complete
Hammer's Slammers, Volume One" by David Drake (Night
Shade Books). It's a good essay, even if I disagree
with some of the statements about who is qualified to
write military SF.

Take care!

2006-05-17 David Duffy replying to Fred Kiesche #

Notes that Wolfe's introduction to David Drake's Hammer's Slammers collection was also reprinted in NYRSF. Playfully argues that Wolfe himself has written military SF and so meets the criteria he sets out in the essay, which Fred Kiesche had called good but disputed on who qualifies to write military SF.

The intro was also reprinted in NYRSF.

Of course Wolfe is an author of military SF too, and fits his own criteria ;)
