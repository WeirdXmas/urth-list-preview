What might become of Ouen?

Urth list · 2 messages · 2006-05-06 to 2006-05-07

2006urthThe Book of the New SunAdam Thorntonb sharp

Themes: Free will and choice, Hidden identities and parentage

2006-05-06 b sharp #

Asks what Ouen might become in light of Severian's line that they cannot tell him what he may become, suggesting Severian means Ouen could become a Master Fire-thing like himself, a Mysterious Father-type figure. Grounds the guess in the scene where Severian leaves Ouen at the house where Dorcas is mourning and says they will not tell him who he is.
questions · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Ouen will become a mysterious father figure like Severian

At the risk of cluttering the list with my own issues, I'll toss this small one in the ring. Just before Severian drops off Ouen at the house where Dorcas is mourning he tells him "We will not tell you who you are" "And we cannot tell you what you may become".

What might Ouen become? My unfortunately vulgar and simple mind can only think Severian is saying "you might become an MF as I have been a GMF". Someone please tell me that's not it!

2006-05-07 Adam Thornton replying to b sharp #

Argues that the line Severian speaks to Ouen about what he may become is best read plainly as a statement about free will, not a hint that Ouen will become a genetic-parent figure. Questions whether there is any reason to think Severian can foresee Ouen's future, and calls the sinister reading unnecessarily creepy.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Ouen's future is a matter of free will

I think that's unnecessarily creepy. Do we have any reason to believe that Severian can see Ouen's future?

I always took this to have its obvious meaning, "I will not tell you you are the Autarch's father, and what you may become is a matter of your own free will."

Adam
