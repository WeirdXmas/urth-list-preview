2012-04-07 Tony Ellis #

Corrects Marc Aramini's spelling of the village name, noting it is Stow-on-the-Wold, and suggests the mangled form Stoke-on-Wold may be deliberate. Argues it could be a B-movie script-writer's invented name, reversing his earlier view that Wolfe was simply clumsy at inventing English place names.
disputes · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft · theories: Stoke-on-Wold is a deliberately bad B-movie name

Actually, that's Stow-on-the-Wold, but excellent job on digging up those film references.

Stoke-on-Wold sounds like a combination of Stow-on-the-Wold and Stoke-on-Trent. I used to think it was Wolfe's slightly clumsy attempt to invent an authentic-sounding English village name, but I realise now that it could be *deliberately* bad - the sort of thing a script-writer on a B-movie might have come up with.
