2005-01-16 Jim Raylor #

Proposes that the word saltimbanque and a Geneva street address hide a pun on Gene Wolfe's name and The Fifth Head of Cerberus, suggesting a deliberate hidden signature. Grounds the idea in dictionary senses of the word (street performer, trickster, harlequin-like figure) and the coincidence of 'Gene Ve' from Rue des Grottes, then invites others to look for more such hints.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Wolfe hid his name in a Geneva street address; Saltimbanque hints at Wolfe's signature in the books

I googled Saltimbanque
A street performer, a travelling entertainer, a
tumbler, maybe a trickster, an illusionist or
conjurer.. images remind us of harlequin,
punchinello... the joker in the pack, the fool, the
23rd trump...
Google gave me Picasso, Baudelaire and a few other
artists...
But the biggest surprise was at 26, Rue des Grottes
(Street of the Caves) Geneve.
Gene Ve
Gene 5e
Translating from french... Gene 5th.
Coincidence?
Let's find La Rue D'asticot.
La Rue Delices? anyone... they taste better than they
look.
Then I found the Saltimbanque magic theatre was
established in 1997...
but by then I was lost.

2005-01-17 James Wynn replying to Jim Raylor #

Argues that the Geneva street address Rue des Grottes, read as Gene 5e, is probably no coincidence, and suggests a historic library due west of the Cave Canem site in Geneva. Responds to Jim Raylor's find and mocks his claim that Mr. Million killed Maitre, since the Saltimbanque puppet theatre would make a direct killing unnecessary.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Rue des Grottes address encodes Gene 5e; Cave Canem is the Saltimbanque puppet theatre; Mr. Million did not kill Maitre directly

I don't think so. Nice catch, Jim. I checked the archives and this has never been posted before.

Has anyone been to Switzerland recently? If Cave Canem can be assumed to be located at the site of the Saltimbanque theater in Geneva, then a large historic library might be expected due west of it. Can anyone identify a large monumental library there?

The mountains are north and east of Geneva, right? Checking out a map, the historic city center seems to be east of Rue des Grottes. It seems to fit.

Regarding whether Mr. Million killed Maitre....I'm surprised at you Jim! Having identified Cave Canem with the Saltimbanque PUPPET THEATRE, how could you imagine that Mr. Million would need or want to actually kill him directly?

~ Crush
