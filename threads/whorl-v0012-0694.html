The Neighbors' other friends

Whorl list · 2 messages · 2001-04-22 to 2001-04-22

2001whorlThe Book of the Short SunNicholas GeversMichael Straight

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2001-04-22 Nicholas Gevers #

Argues that humans besides Horn and Silk probably also have the Neighbors' gift of astral travel, and could lead raids on the inhumi birthing pools on Green. Grounds this in Taal's direct dealings with the Neighbors and Windcloud's statement that he has other human friends, plus the Neighbors' direct intervention since Return to the Whorl.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Other humans besides Horn and Silk can travel astrally

Another note on the Secret: does any human other than
Horn/Silk have the ability to travel astrally, surely
a rare gift from the Neighbors? In other words, is
there anyone else who can lead human expeditions to
attack the inhumi birthing pools on Green, once
Horn/Silk has departed? Well, the answer is yes, there
probably is such a person, probably several. Taal has
had direct dealings with the Neighbors. Windcloud
states that he has other human friends besides Horn.
They're around, and their numbers can easily be
augmented, now that (as of RTW) the Neighbors are
intervening directly in human affairs...

--Nick Gevers.

2001-04-22 Michael Straight #

Asks whether Mora's dreamwalking and possessions, which seem linked to Fava's spirit, could be the astral travel that Horn and Silk have, suggesting Mora as a human with this gift. Responds to Nicholas Gevers's question about whether any human besides Horn and Silk can travel astrally, offering Mora as a candidate without developing an argument.
questions · theories: Mora's possessions are linked to Fava's spirit

What about Mora's dreamwalking/possessions, which seem to be linked somehow to Fava's spirit?

-Rostrum
