Six From Atlantis

Urth list · 1 messages · 2006-10-19 to 2006-10-19

2006urthNicholas Gevers

2006-10-19 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs readers that the story "Six from Atlantis" appears in the Howard tribute anthology Cross Plains Universe from Monkey Brain Books. Answers Fred Kiesche's question about where the story can be found, and mentions his comparison with the Robert Silverberg tale that parodies 'Two Gun' Bob.
informs

Fred Kiesche asked:

"Where does "Six from Atlantis" appear? I'll be
interested to see how it compares to the Robert
Silverberg tale that poked fun at 'Two Gun' Bob."

It's in a Howard tribute anthology called Cross Plains
Universe, published by Monkey Brain Books.

--Nick Gevers.
