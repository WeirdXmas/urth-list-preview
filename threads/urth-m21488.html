Father Inire/Paleamon

Urth list · 3 messages · 2011-10-21 to 2011-10-21

2011urthThe Book of the New SunRyan DunnLarry Miller
The thread: Larry Miller opened by proposing that Inire and Paleamon are distinct figures, an exiled human Paleamon and an alien Inire who replaced him to guide Severian, citing the goggles and the saintly name. Ryan Dunn countered that Inire may be one figure playing many roles, including Paleamon, Ossipago, the Shaman and the Boatman, tied to the books' theatrical structure. Miller then backed a Paleamon/Ossipago link, arguing Ossipago is a robot based on the name's meaning and Wolfe's naming habits. The thread ends with the single-identity and two-figure readings still standing side by side, with no resolution.
  • larry miller: Inire and Paleamon are two distinct figures, a human Paleamon exiled and an alien Inire who replaced him
  • Ryan Dunn: Inire is one figure playing many roles, including Paleamon, Ossipago, the Shaman and the Boatman
  • larry miller: Ossipago may be linked to Paleamon and is a robot, based on the name's meaning and Wolfe's naming

Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Doubles, twins and clones, Gods, angels and the Increate

2011-10-21 Larry Miller #

Proposes that Inire and Paleamon are two distinct figures, an original exiled human Paleamon and an alien Inire who replaced him to guide Severian, citing Paleamon's goggles hiding his face and his saintly, mythic name. Lists unresolved mysteries about the old torturer, such as why he was exiled and how he got Terminus Est, and asks whether others have considered the link.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Inire and Paleamon are two separate beings; Inire is an alien who replaced the original Paleamon

Did anybody on here ever consider a connection between Inire and Paleamon? Paleamons face is always obscured by his goggles and his name is one of saintly and mythic origins. I would even go so far as to say that there are two Paleamons. The original human one who himself was exiled and the alien one (Inire?) who replaced him to keep watch over Sev and guide him to his eventual destiny. There are many mysteries regarding the old torturer (why was he exiled? How did he aquire Terminus Est? Etc.) that I think Wolfe wants us to puzzle out. Any thoughts?

2011-10-21 Ryan Dunn replying to larry miller #

Argues that Inire may be the same figure as Paleamon and several other characters, including Ossipago, the Shaman and the Boatman, playing a supervisory escort role in Severian's story. Responds to Larry Miller's suggestion of two Paleamons by offering the idea that Inire plays many parts, linked to the theatrical structure of the books.
proposes · puzzle-solving · allegory-and-typology · theories: Inire is Shaman is Ossipago is Boatman; Palaemon is potentially Ossipago; Inire plays many characters like actors in a play

I was under the impression Palaemon was potentially Ossipago. Some have made a case for:

Inire is Shaman is Ossipago is Boatman is Fechin is Hethor is...

Why I like Inire as playing the part of many characters is that it pairs well with the structure of characters in a play, a theme we see throughout the books.

And I am a sucker for the idea of Inire (father) playing a supervisory, escort sort of role in the Severian experiment, until they get it right and save the planet, universe, et al.

...ryan
