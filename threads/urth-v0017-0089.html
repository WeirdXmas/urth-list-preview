Black hole vs Ice Age?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-08-05 to 1998-08-05

1998urthThe Book of the New SunJon Camfield

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Gods, angels and the Increate

1998-08-05 Jon Camfield #

Proposes that the Urth sun's dimness and daytime stars could be explained by a huge continuous volcanic eruption or comet collision, or alternatively by a black hole placed in the sun for some purpose. Floats several motives for such a placement, such as cooling Earth, making Venus habitable, or a hierodule scheme to help humanity develop, offering these as questions rather than settled answers.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: A black hole was placed in the sun; Urth's dim sun caused by volcanic dust or comet impact

Apart from messing with a lot of the plot (of course, the plot as understood by the characters within the stories as explined to them by various scientists who might have had their own agendas to serve), most of the effects in Urth could be explained by a really really big and continuous volcanic explosion (or comet collision? (The New Sun, beta/untested version?) kicking up a lot of dust in the atmosphere, making the sun appear red and cooling the whole planet, etc. This wouldn't account for stars visible during the day, though.
Maybe a better line of questioning is to assume that there is a black hole in the sun, and ask why we put it there? To make Venus habitable? Because of a greenhouse effect on Earth, and we needed to cool it down? Maybe the sun had begun to expand and this was to halt that? Or, was it a hierodule trick to, in the long run, help the humans to develop? Jon Camfield "GriffJon" |Webmaster, Plan II Honors Program: [email] | www.dla.utexas.edu/~plan2
