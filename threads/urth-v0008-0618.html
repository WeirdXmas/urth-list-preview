1998-03-12 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Doubts that the Commonwealth could really be Delaware-sized, calling the idea a geographical, hydrological and latitudinal paradox while still finding it charming. Also asks Peter which Severian temptation he means, the dream scene or the sandbar scene, and encourages his essay on Wolfe and Yeats.
disputes

Nutria,

The entire Commonwealth as "Delaware-size or smaller" provides a geographical topological hydrological latitudinal paradox. All together, it is a charming idea but I strenuously doubt it.

Peter,

Re: Severian as Faustian when tempted by the undine who quotes Marlow. Please say more--which temptation are you thinking of? The dream scene? The sandbar scene?

Re: Wolfe and Yeats--looks like you've got the germ of a big meaty essay. Go to it!
