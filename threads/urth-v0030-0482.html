PEACE, and timeline alert

2001urthRoy C. Lackey

2001-06-14 Roy C. Lackey #

Argues that the timeline of Wolfe's PEACE cannot be settled by checking the publication dates of Ros's novels, since the Gold Hunt year is only pegged to a five-year range. Responds to Adam's point about DONALD DUDLEY's 1954 date by noting that the author Ros may be a real person and that her books, HELEN and DELINA, were published in different years than the post's details suggest.
