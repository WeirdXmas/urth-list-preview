2007-09-23 Duncan Truter #

Informs the list that the Wikipedia page for Gene Wolfe lists free online works, and names the story The Case of the Vanishing Ghost, which the poster has begun reading and found funny. Notes that few archive posts mention this online availability and that several stories there are new to him, though he owns most of Wolfe's work.
informs

Hi

I was checking the Gene Wolfe wikipedia page
(http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Wolfe) in case it had news about what he's writing now. There was nothing there, can anyone who's more clued-up tell me?

I noticed at the bottom of the page: "Works available online". I've just started reading "The Case of the Vanishing Ghost" and I've laughed out loud several times already. I only saw two mentions of it in the archives so it seems that most people don't know about this online availability. Just a heads-up! There are actually quite a few stories there I haven't read before, and I have most of GW's stuff.

Duncan
