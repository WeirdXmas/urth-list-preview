1998-03-06 Alice Turner #

Invites list members to attend the Disclave 1998 convention and join a planned panel, sharing membership prices, the mailing address, hotel details and dates. Follows up on a promised information request and responds to Ranjit's joking gloss on k.d., mentioning PO'L and John Clute as possible participants.
informs

Ranjit,

Your gloss on k.d. is priceless---maybe *that* should be the subject of our mysterious panel at Disclave...which, btw, John Clute might possibly join.

Speaking of which, I promised info (which I had to get from mantis, but here 'tis). Aside from PO'L, whom I know is coming, a couple of the rest of you have made noises. Please, please come--Ranjit, Rostrum, everyone. If you agree to be on the panel, I think they'll give you a free membership!

Disclave Memberships: $25 until March 30 (or $30 at the door), checks payable to "Disclave 1998" sent to:

Joe Mayhew
7-S Research Rd.
Greenbelt MD 20770-1776

e-mail: [email]
website: http://www.wsfa.org

Hotel reservations by April 10, 1998.
Holiday Inn, College Park, Maryland
(This is right off of I-95)

Group Code: "DIS," Disclave 1998 Convention
Convention date: May 1 to May 3.
Hotel Phone reservation: 1-800-HOLIDAY
(Hotel rooms are $75, and up to 4 can share a room.)

-alga-
