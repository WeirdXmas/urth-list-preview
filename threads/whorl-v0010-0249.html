The Ring

2000-01-30 Alex David Groce #

Argues that the ring Seawrack gives Horn in The Book of the Long Sun echoes the white-gold ring Lena gives Thomas Covenant, and that the parallel is troubling. Grounds this in the shared pattern of a sexual assault followed by a gift of a ring with mysterious powers, and hopes later events will show the resemblance is accidental.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Wolfe's ring in Long Sun echoes Covenant's ring

Actually, the whole ring business is going to bother me until I know what is going on with it, because it reminds me of something very strongly: Thomas Covenant. Yes--Horn rapes Seawrack, and she gives him a white/gold ring with mysterious powers; as Covenant raped Lena, who (did she? I don't really remember) gives him a white-gold ring with mysterious powers. If it was anything else, I'd say the reference was obvious, but I so disliked the Covenant books that I'm really hoping that future events will convince me this resemblance is purely accidental.
