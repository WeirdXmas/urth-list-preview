Tree gods and the ring of glass

2013urthThe Book of the Long SunMarc Aramini

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Myth and folklore sources

2013-10-09 Marc Aramini #

Argues that the vanished gods and trees of the Short Sun recombine with their meals, producing hybrid species through a eucharist-like consumption tied to the Hyacinth myth and the Christian eucharist. Supports this with the eucharist scene watched by the trees, the liana drinking blood, and the corn hybridization in the opening chapter, and asks whether the ring of glass could identify Blue and whether it appears in Yesod.
proposes · myth-and-structure · source-and-allusion · theories: Vanished gods recombine with meals to create hybrids; The ring of glass could identify Blue
