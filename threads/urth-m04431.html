Dying Earth anthology

Urth list · 4 messages · 2007-09-18 to 2007-09-19

2007urthNicholas GeversMatthew KeeleyRon Crown
The thread: Readers asked whether Gene Wolfe would appear in a planned Dying Earth anthology edited by Dozois and Martin, since he was missing from the announced contributor list. Ron Crown raised the absence and suggested the editors might regard Wolfe's Dying Earth work as already done. Matthew Keeley noted that Martin left blog questions about Wolfe unanswered and wondered whether Wolfe had been invited and declined. Nicholas Gevers then relayed secondhand from Dozois that Wolfe declined without giving a reason, and he expressed regret. The thread ended with the decline reported but unexplained.
  • Ron Crown: surprised Wolfe is missing from the list, perhaps because editors think his Dying Earth work is done
  • Matthew Keeley: Wolfe may have been invited and declined, since Martin never answered blog questions about him
  • Nicholas Gevers: Dozois says Wolfe declined without a reason, and he regrets it given Wolfe's Clark Ashton Smith contribution

Themes: Stories within stories, Books, libraries and writing, Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both

2007-09-18 Matthew Keeley replying to Ron Crown #

Reports that George R.R. Martin discussed the Dying Earth anthology on his blog, where readers asked whether Wolfe would be included, and wonders whether Wolfe was invited and declined. Responds to Ron Crown's observation that Wolfe is absent from the contributor list, noting that Martin never answered those questions and that Martin and Wolfe are acquainted.
questions

George R.R. Martin discussed this project on his "Not-a-Blog" ( grrm.livejournal.com), and a few people responded to the news with questions about putting Wolfe in the anthology, but I don't believe Martin ever responded. I gather Wolfe and Martin know each other, and I'm really surprised if no one thought of asking Wolfe. Perhaps he declined an invitation?

-Matt

2007-09-19 Nicholas Gevers replying to Ron Crown #

Reports that Gardner Dozois said Gene Wolfe declined an invitation to contribute to a Dying Earth anthology without giving a reason, and expresses regret given Wolfe's earlier contribution to a Clark Ashton Smith tribute volume. Responds to Ron Crown's prior post, with the news relayed secondhand from Dozois and a Yahoo link attached.
informs

Gardner Dozois told me Gene Wolfe turned down the
invitation to contribute to the Dying Earth
anthology--no reason given. A pity, remembering how
brilliant Wolfe's contribution was to the Clark Ashton
Smith tribute volume some years back.

--Nick Gevers.
