1997-05-16 Alice Turner #

Asks two questions: whether the woman's scarf found in Mattak's room in Oosik's house belongs to Hyacinth, and whether the Outsider is a form of Severian, allowing that Severian may include others. Replies to mantis's earlier greeting and offers the questions as a choice, noting the scarf question is worded to draw more than a yes or no.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: The Outsider is a form of Severian

mantis-

Thanks for warm greeting and second op. Here are two questions, one specific, but a bit sneaky since it tries to get 3 bits of info for the price of one, the second fairly profound. Take your pick.

1) Does the woman's scarf found in Mattak's room in Oosik's house belong to Hyacinth? (Of course he could just say "no" and leave me profoundly frustrated as to homeowning identity.)

2) Is the Outsider a form of Severian (leaving open the possibility that he includes others [as, indeed, does Severian])?

-alga-

1997-05-16 Kieran Mullen #

Asks Gene Wolfe whether Silk, the Long Sun protagonist, has any engineered or extraordinary abilities, citing his gestation from a frozen embryo like Mucor's and a hint that he knew Hyacinth's name before meeting her. Passes along compliments to Wolfe, and the question is directed to him rather than to other list members.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk has engineered extraordinary abilities

Question for Mr. Wolfe:

It's pretty broadly indicated that Silk was gestated from a frozen embroyo as Mucor was. This has some nice symbolic effects ("virgin birth", the hero as having unusual origins, etc.). Does Silk have any human engineered, extraordinary abilities?

(Possible Lupine hint: he knew Hyacinth's name before she was introduced, and felt that they had made a non-physical contact.)

Please pass along my compliments to Mr. Wolfe as one of the most creative, skilled, and theologically fascinating writers I've ever read.

Thanks,

Kieran Mullen
