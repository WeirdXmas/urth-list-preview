Proposes that the male and female voices whispering to Severian during his enlightenment are a single voice, that of the Outsider, a androgynous god linked to Dionysus, Pan and the Green Man. Grounds this in the idea that Severian is an aspect of the Outsider, who is male and female in certain ways, and responds to Marc Aramini's claim that Kypris and Pas speak to him.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theology-and-religion · theories: The voices in Severian's enlightenment are both the Outsider; Severian is an aspect of the Outsider

I didn't find the passage but I wonder if there is only one voice here, from one male/female figure. In other words, The Outsider, who is the God of Wine, like Dionysus, the hermaproditic, gnostic, Green Man, pagan Great God Pan, etc.

Severian is supposed to be an aspect of The Outsider and he is surely male/female in certain ways.
