Nephilim

Urth list · 1 messages · 2010-11-28 to 2010-11-28

2010urthAndrew Mason

2010-11-28 Andrew Mason #

Argues that Genesis 6:4 describes Nephilim produced after the flood rather than survivors of it, which would be odd if the Nephilim caused the flood. Responds to Jerry Friedman's point that the verse implies survival, and notes the fallen-angel reading traces back to the pre-Christian Book of Enoch.
disputes · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion

I would read it as saying that more Nephilim were produced after the flood. - which would be odd if the Nephilim were the cause of the flood. (Thers no doubt, though, that this interpretation - that their fathers were fallen angels, and that they produced the corruption that led to the flood - goes back a long way, being found in the book of Enoch, which at least in its original form is pre-Christian.)
