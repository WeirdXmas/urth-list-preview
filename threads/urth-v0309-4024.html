Typhon and Quetzal - important correction

Urth list · 1 messages · 2003-09-04 to 2003-09-04

2003urthThe Book of the Long SunJames Wynn

Themes: Myth and folklore sources

2003-09-04 James Wynn #

Corrects an earlier claim that Quetzal alone is the Dionysus archetype among the inhumi, arguing instead that the whole inhumi race corresponds to Dionysus. Grounds this in the fact that the Dionysus name is tied to the inhumi race rather than to Quetzal, and extends the parallels into the Short Sun after Quetzal's death.
disputes · myth-and-structure · source-and-allusion · theories: The inhumi race is Dionysus; Dionysus parallels continue into the Short Sun

Oops! My mistake. You said "Quetzal is the only *inhumu* who is...Dionysus." Duh.

Well, all I can say is that I don't believe that (anymore). I think that entire inhumi race is Dionysus. I'm still developing the explanation but note that it is not Quetzal's name that is associated with Dionysus, but the name of the inhumi race.
http://www.urth.org/whorlmap/quetzal.htm

I was confused by this as well once upon a time, but I think the Dionysus parallels continue into the Short Sun after Quetzal is dead, and before the Whorl launches prior to Quetzal boarding the Whorl.

-- Crush
