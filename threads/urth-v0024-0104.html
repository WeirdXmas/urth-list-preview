Concedes a point of letterform: the poster admits they were thinking of lowercase thorn rather than uppercase, noting uppercase thorn is a crossed d, a capital D. Responds to I impetuously's remark that thorn is more an angular P, correcting their own earlier claim with a light, self-deprecating aside about Old English grades.

No wonder I only got C's in Old English...
