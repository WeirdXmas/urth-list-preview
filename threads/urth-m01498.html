2005-05-20 James Wynn replying to Alexander Wallis #

Advises a confused newcomer to re-read, arguing that the Apu-Punchau story holds layers such as prophecy for an Increate outside time. Responds to Alexander Wallis by explaining the Old Sun's black hole and the White Fountain as its reverse, then asks who the Gyol Severian and the UotNS shaft Severian were.
answers · puzzle-solving · theology-and-religion · theories: Two Severians: one drowned in the Gyol, one fell down a shaft; Apu-Punchau is an earlier Conciliator; The White Fountain is a reverse black hole healing the Old Sun

Okay. I'll take this one on. For starters, a re-reading is probably in order. It is often said that Wolfe cannot be read, only re-read.

Hmmm...well, if you only failed to see the significance of Apu-Punchau, then there is probably a lot of which you misunderstood the significance and didn't realize it.

There are possibly many points to the AP story, such as the nature of prophecy to an Increate who exists outside of time. The nature of identity of the Christian Messiah (vis a vis Dionysus, Hari Krishna, and Mithra) in that Conciliator IS, is LIKE, and is NOT the same as, Apu-Punchau who was an earlier Conciliator when Urth's Sun was young and hale. I'm sure there are other significances.

See? You are picking this up fast.

In what way do you mean that? The Old Sun is damaged by having a black hole in it. Severian brings "The White Fountain", a reverse black hole to heal it.

Well, let me ask you: Who was the Severian who "nearly" drowned in the Gyol? Who was the Severian that fell down that shaft in UotNS?

~ Crush
