Disarming Seawrack

Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Hidden identities and parentage

1999-11-14 William H. Ansley #

Asks why Seawrack has lost her right arm and questions why her injury and amputation seem to trouble no one, including her, and whether Horn is being misled about her missing limb. Grounds the question in the text's lack of reaction to her disability, noting she swims and adapts easily and that no one asks how it happened.
questions · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Horn is being misled about Seawrack's arm

OK, all of you people who think Wolfe always has a reason for everything he writes (you know who you are!), what is the reason for Seawrack's missing right arm?

I find it rather odd that having her arm mangled and then amputated, doesn't seem to bother Seawrack very much. Even odder is the way she adapts instantly to having no right arm. She is not unbalanced, even at first, or inconvenienced in any way we are told about. She can swim just as well as ever, apparently. It also doesn't seem to bother anyone else that she has only one arm. It certainly doesn't make her any less desirable to Horn or any other man. Not that it should, but I think it might put some men off. No one even seems to be curious about why she is missing an arm or how it happened, which strikes me as being quite against human nature.

Is Seawrack really missing an arm or is Horn being mislead somehow? ("Prepare for mind control!" -Evil, in the film _Time Bandits_.)

William Ansley

1999-11-15 Alex David Groce #

Proposes that Seawrack's lost arm in Peace may have been arranged by Mother to make Horn less suspicious of her, and suspects Seawrack is less innocent than she seems at the end of On Blue's Waters. Grounds this in the Venus de Milo story told in Peace, the hints dropped about Seawrack, and Krait's evident distrust of her.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Mother arranged Seawrack's arm loss to win Horn's sympathy; Seawrack is less innocent than she seems

One thing it made me think of was the Venus de Milo, especially re: the story about her arms in PEACE. Perhaps Mother arranged for her to lose her arm, knowing this would make Horn less suspicious of her (if beauty doesn't get 'em, go for sympathy). At the end of OBW, I pity Seawrack, and think no ill of her, but I have a sneaking suspicion that the hints dropped and Krait's evident distrust of her may indicate that she's less innocent and good than she seems.
