Off-topic social reply to Jackie welcoming a fellow newcomer and thanking several list members for their wit and insight. Offers to buy a poster's hardcovers, with no discussion of any Wolfe book or idea.

and thanks as always to mr. lackey for his

and ouroboros. Both insight and humor, you guys spoil

as a lowlier woim.-Shannon
