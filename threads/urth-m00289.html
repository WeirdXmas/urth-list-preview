Recommends reading the Latro novels before starting The Book of the Long Sun, suggesting Wolfe was appeasing creative urges left unsatisfied when Tor steered him toward another Urth book. Replies to William Ansley's suggested reading route and notes that the Latro series is unfinished, with Latro still far from home.
proposes · author-and-biography · theories: Wolfe wrote Long Sun to appease creative urges from Latro

However, if you go the route William Ansley suggests, then I recommend reading the Latro novels just before starting "The Book of the Long Sun" since Wolfe seems there to have been appeasing creative demons that were left unsatisfied when Tor gently recommended another Urth book instead of another Latro book.

There should be another Latro book coming out soon. It is essentially an uncompleted series. Latro is still a long way from his home.
