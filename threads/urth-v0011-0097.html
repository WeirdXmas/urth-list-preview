Apology for inadvertent pomposity

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-24 to 1998-05-24

1998urthadam louis stephanides

1998-05-24 adam louis stephanides #

Apologizes for an earlier post that seemed to claim readers would be convinced by the poster's reasoning, clarifying instead a fear that his ideas would look like wild speculation without textual basis. Responds to I.'s quoted remark about conclusions being given without reasoning, and disclaims any suggestion that Mr. Broderick would treat him that way.
chats

Several hours after posting this, I realized it made me sound as if I thought that anyone seeing my reasoning would be convinced by it, which wasn't what I had meant at all. What I had really been afraid of was that my ideas would come off as being wild speculations with no basis in the text; but I hadn't wanted to suggest that Mr. Broderick would treat me in such a way. I really should learn to wait before posting.

--Adam
