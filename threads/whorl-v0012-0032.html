Silk for President!

2000-11-27 Kevin J. Maroney #

Jokingly reports that feverish dreams suggested coded messages in The Book of the Short Sun predicting the 2000 presidential election, with the poster recalling only that Bush is a plant and Gore an animal byproduct. Makes no real argument and offers no textual evidence, presenting the claim as a playful aside tied to the election.
chats

I currently have a cold, possibly with a minor fever, and my troubled dreams kept trying to convince me that there are coded messages in _The Book of the Short Sun_ which will reveal who will, in the long run, win the 2000 presidential election.

Alas, I can't remember any of the codes, but I did come away with one key insight: Bush is a plant, and Gore is an animal byproduct. So is this a battle of the sexes?
