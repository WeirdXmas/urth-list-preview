2009-11-15 Roy C. Lackey #

Reports that The Wizard Knight Companion can be bought at the Other Change of Hobbit and Dark Carnival bookshops in Berkeley. Aimed at Bay Area readers, it gives no review or argument, only the store locations and a sign-off from Roy C. Lackey.
informs

Those of you in the Bay Area may be interested to know that THE WIZARD KNIGHT COMPANION is available at the Other Change of Hobbit and Dark Carnival in Berkeley.

-Roy

2009-11-16 James Wynn replying to Roy C. Lackey #

Adds that a Bay Area bookstore sale of The Wizard Knight Companion could come with an author signature, and notes his own signed copies of Lexicon Urthus and chapbooks. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's report that the Companion is stocked at two Berkeley shops, mentioning a hoped-for meeting with Robert Borski at a convention.
informs

...and if you *can* buy it there, then you can probably be fortunate enough to get it signed by the author if you really want to. I certainly prize my signed copies of Lexicon Urthus ed. 1 & 2, and my chapbooks for Fifth Head and Peace. I'm still awaiting my opportunity to meet Mr. Borski at some con (if you are lurking, Robert).

J.
