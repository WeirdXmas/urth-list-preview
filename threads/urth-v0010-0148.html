Hethor's mirrors

Urth list · 2 messages · 1998-05-12 to 1998-05-12

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Beasts and the animal in us, Mirrors, pictures and art

1998-05-12 Alice Turner #

Notes that Hethor owns a set of mirrors he uses to control his beasts, and points to Father Inire's letter to Severian near the end of The Citadel of the Autarch as the evidence. Declines to address a preceding theory about limbic conjecture and says the poster does not know how Hethor transports the mirrors.
informs · close-textual-reading

Well, I'm not going to touch most of that mantic limbic conjecture, but I will note that Hethor does indeed have a set of mirrors by which he manipulates his beasties--Father Inire alludes to them in his letter to Sev near the end of Citadel. How he transports them, I wouldn't know.

-alga-

1998-05-12 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that Hethor's mirrors are small pocket objects, since the room-sized mirror installation seen in the Presence Chamber cannot be carried, and that they work physically, like Chinese magic mirrors projecting images. Grounds this in Father Inire's remarks about the Fish materializing and in Hethor's lack of baggage, suggesting novices may be limited in what they can summon.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Hethor's mirrors are pocket sized; Mirrors summon creatures through physical action; Only skilled fishermen catch large creatures

alga,

Right, Hethor's mirrors aluded to by Father Inire. But since the only mirrors we see (mainly the "transporter pad" of the Presence Chamber: about which Domnia told Thecla as Severian tells Agia [I, ch. 20]; into which Jonas steps [II, ch. 18]; and into which the cat is thrown into ["The Cat"]) are part of a room sized installation (eight full length mirrors, with "labyrinths" aka teratoid signs on their backs; some sort of laser-like light source at the center of the mirror octagon), and since Hethor does not seem to be carrying as much as a knapsack, then Hethor's mirrors must somehow be, kit and kaboodle, pocket sized.

About summoning mirror critters: it seems that Chinese "magic mirrors" are mirrors that seem normal but they reflect/project a specific image, e.g., you can look at your face, but catch the sunlight and you project the written character for "fire" into the shadows. The mirror silvering is only partial.

Father Inire tells Domnia that the Fish will materialize if they don't darken the lamp or adjust the mirrors. Suggesting that there is a physical action rather than a psychic action: one dials up the critter code on the mirrors rather than thinking strong thoughts of influence. But maybe not--Inire also seems to suggest that the Fish is one of the first shapes to materialize; so there might be limits to what a novice can catch with the mirrors, and only a real fisherman can haul in the wild and very big creatures as Hethor does.

So Hethor, staring into his pocket compact, draws forth a half dozen flying mounts for Vodalus? Yikes!

=mantis=
