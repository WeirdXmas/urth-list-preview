Pirate Freedom: Chris' "Luck"

Urth list · 1 messages · 2008-05-29 to 2008-05-29

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones

2008-05-29 Matthew Keeley #

Proposes that a second, hidden iteration of Chris, one of his other selves, may have fired from the jungle to secure his victory in the pistol duel with a hunter and master gunner. Grounds the idea on the poster's recollection that Chris expected to die, that he stood near jungle cover where a sniper could hide, and on his view that Chris was not merely lucky.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Another iteration of Chris secretly shot from the jungle

In any case, I know we're always looking for other iterations of Chris, and I'm wondering if Chris' other self influenced one of his duels. As I recall, Chris engages in a pistol fight with a hunter and master gunner, in which he expects to die. Of course Chris survives. I believe Chris is standing near a jungle area, so I imagine hiding a "sniper" wouldn't be too difficult. Perhaps Chris was tailing himself to make sure he "won" the battle? I don't think Chris was just lucky.

-Matt
