Themes: Time loops and causality, Unreliable narration

2006-10-01 Josh Young #

Argues that Severian's claim in the closing lines of The Citadel of the Autarch that it is the last year of the old sun may simply be wrong, since narrators are not to be trusted. Also suggests his memoir, left adrift in time, could have reached Urth's past and been read there, so no paradox arises.
