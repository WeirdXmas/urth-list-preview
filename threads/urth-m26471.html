2015-04-30 Charles Gillingham #

Informs the list that three Wolfe short stories, The Tree Is My Hat, Pulp Cover and King Rat, were adapted as radio plays, and shares links to the StarShipsofa audio episodes. Says he has not yet listened and plans to report back on The Tree Is My Hat after a walk with his dog.
informs

This is pretty neat. Three of Wolfe's short stories; The Tree Is My Hat, Pulp Cover, King Rat, were produced as radio plays. I have yet to listen to any of them, so I can't say if they are any good. Here's the links:

http://www.starshipsofa.com/blog/2008/11/06/aural-delights-no-49-gene-wolfe/ http://www.starshipsofa.com/blog/2010/02/09/aural-delights-no-120-gene-wolfe/ http://www.starshipsofa.com/blog/2012/08/15/starshipsofa-no-251-gene-wolfe/

I'll probably go on an epic walk with my dog tomorrow and listen to The Tree is My Hat. Then report back here what I thought of it.
