2008-05-04 johnfrance #

Asks whether Gene Wolfe has read Borges's Harvard lectures This Craft of Verse and suggests them as a gift to thank Wolfe for his books. Gives no textual argument; the post is a personal request about a book gift, with the lectures' remarkable range of material noted in passing.
questions

Hi-

I was wondering if anyone on this list knows if Gene Wolfe has read "This Craft of Verse (The Charles Elliot Norton Lectures)" which is a transcription of 6 lectures that Jorge Luis Borges gave at Harvard back in 1967-1968?

Mr. Wolfe has given me a lot of reading pleasure over the years and I wanted to send him something by way of thanks, (in addition to simply buying his books.)?

The lectures did not come into print until about 2000.? They are remarkable for the range of material Borges referred to in various languages, from memory.

Regards,

John France
