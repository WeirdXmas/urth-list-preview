Argues that Silk may have been absorbed into Pas without consenting, since Horn in In Green's Jungles states Silk is now an aspect of Pas as a definite fact. Answers Alice Turner's question about why Silk agreed by citing Horn's statement and noting Silk's personality could have been uploaded into Mainframe.
answers · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk's personality was uploaded into Mainframe without consent

I can't find the spot now, but iirc somewhere in IGJ Horn says "Silk is now an aspect of Pas," or words to that effect. He doesn't say it as a conjecture, but as something definite.

Even if it happened, though, Silk didn't necessarily agree to it. His personality could have been uploaded into Mainframe without his consent.

--Adam
