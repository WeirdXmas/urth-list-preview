Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1999-04-13 Jonathan Laidlow #

Asks where the text supports the idea that Dorcas's unborn child is Severian's mother Katherine, and whether that would make Severian the product of incest with Dorcas and Ouen. Responds to a suggestion that Dorcas is pregnant, questioning how such interbreeding could produce the saviour, and cites no specific passage.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: Dorcas's unborn child is Katherine, Severian's mother; Severian is the product of incest with Dorcas

Re: Severian as father of his own mother.

I'm in agreement with the suggestion that Dorcas is pregnant. but where is the evidence in the text to suggest that this is in fact Katherine, Severian's mother?

Are we saying that Severian is the result of an incestuous relationship (Dorcas' first child Ouen & second child by Severian)?

And how does this interbreeding produce the saviour? Surely the results would be much less favourable....

Jonathan
