memorial gift in honor of Rosemary Wolfe

Urth list · 5 messages · 2013-12-17 to 2013-12-18

2013urthSte JervisStephen CaseAntónio Pedro MarquesMarc AraminiAntonin Scriabin
The thread: After Rosemary Wolfe's death, Stephen Case proposed that list members pool money for a memorial gift to a charity meaningful to Gene or Rosemary. Antonin Scriabin suggested a crowdfunding page or PayPal account to collect donations, and Ste Jervis offered a JustGiving page, noting it may be UK-only. entonio supported a gift but argued it need not be run by the list, with someone close to the family choosing the beneficiary. Marc Aramini ended the thread by sharing the obituary and noting the Alzheimer's Association as an alternative to flowers. The thread concluded with practical options rather than a single agreed plan.
  • Stephen Case: pool list members' money for a memorial gift to a charity meaningful to Gene or Rosemary
  • Antonin Scriabin: a trusted member should run a crowdfunding campaign or PayPal account for donations
  • Ste Jervis: JustGiving page to collect and forward memorial donations, possibly UK-only
  • entonio: a memorial gift is good but should be chosen by a family-connected person, not coordinated by the list
  • Marc Aramini: obituary link; donations to the Alzheimer's Association in place of flowers

2013-12-17 Stephen Case #

Proposes that list members pool money for a significant memorial gift to a charity in honor of Rosemary Wolfe, and asks for ideas about which organization would have meant something to Gene or Rosemary. Draws on a university colleague's death, where the department gave to World Vision in lieu of cards or flowers.
proposes

Folks on the list have mentioned flowers or letters sent on to Gene in honor of Rosemary. I'm wondering if it wouldn't be appropriate to pool our resources here and make a significant gift or donation in her honor. Does anyone know of any particular organization or charity that was meaningful to Gene or Rosemary? We had a colleague at the university here pass suddenly recently. He was heavily involved in missions work, so in lieu of cards or flowers the department made a gift to World Vision in his honor. I'm not sure how we could coordinate this, but certainly the wonders of the internet and the folks here who know Gene personally could allow us to contribute something meaningful in her honor.

Ideas?
-S
