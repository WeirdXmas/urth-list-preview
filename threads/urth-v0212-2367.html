Pike's ghost

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-12-04 to 2002-12-04

2002urthThe Book of the Long SunMarc Aramini

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2002-12-04 Marc Aramini #

Proposes that the ghost of Patera Pike seen in the Long Sun books was a much older Silk astrally returning to his earlier life, and asks whether Pike's ghost has been explained and whether such ghosts can exist outside the poles. Grounds the idea in the aged augur on the steps and in parallels with Horn visiting Nettle in the Pit and Severian glimpsing his future self as Apu Punchau.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Silk's older self appears as Pike's ghost; Old Silk astrally returns to his earlier life

I was thinking about the whole Patera Pike's ghost thing. Mr. Wolfe denied that it was Quetzal, and I wasn't sure whether Mainframe could project a saved image of Pike outside of the polar region. Anyone up for the idea that a much older Silk returned to his earlier life for a second while astrally travelling, as Horn returned to see Nettle while in the Pit? Could this explain the aged augur on the steps? Silk catching a glimpse of his future old self, as Severian caught a glimpse of his future old self in the past as Apu Punchau?

Has the ghost of Pike been explained? I know that the ghost of Crane shows up at the end of Return to the Whorl, but can these ghosts be formed or maintained outside of the poles? Is there a better explanation?

Marc Aramini
