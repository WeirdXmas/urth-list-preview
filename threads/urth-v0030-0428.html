2001-05-19 Adam Stephanides #

Praises a parody novel, The Eldritch Horror of Oz, as a Lovecraft pastiche that fits both the Oz books and Lovecraft's style, and asks whether the author has written other works worth finding. Replies to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, who first mentioned the book and its revelation about H.M. Wogglebug.
questions

Yes; great fun. The amazing thing about it is how it is simultaneously true to the spirit of the Oz books _and_ of HPL. Has the author (whose name I forget) written anything else worth searching out? I tried to find stuff by her in libraries, but no luck.

--Adam
