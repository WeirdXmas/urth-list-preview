1998-03-21 Alice Turner #

Argues that Fortunate Cloud and Quasar are not the same ship, though Jonas and Hethor knowing each other is unsurprising since ships share ports and sailors change vessels. Floats the idea that Hethor may be KLS, a name the poster has long associated with Chris, and suggests these ships are a different, more mundane kind than Tzadkiel's unique ship, subject to space and time warps.

For what it's worth, I have never thought that Fortunate Cloud and = Quasar were the same ship. But I have never thought it surprising that = Jonas and Hethor know each other. Ships dock at the same ports, sailors = frequent the same taverns, and, too, they jump ship and sign onto = others. OTOH, I've only just begun to seriously entertain the thought = that Hethor may indeed be KLS---a name, I have argued for 2 years, = Chris, undoubtedly Korean.

FC and Q are (or were) a different order of ship than Tzadkiel's, more = mundane, I would say, but also subject to the timewarps of space. (Or do = I mean the spacewarps of time?) Thus, though Tzadkiel's ship really is = the only one of its kind, there are or were others of a different kind. = Hey, there's the Whorl!=20

1998-03-21 C R Culver #

Argues that Quasar and Fortunate Cloud may be different ships, noting Wolfe may not have known what to do with the Ship of Tzadkiel when writing the earlier books, since it appeared years later. Adds that Wolfe is highly self-consistent, and clarifies that others don't dispute KLS's name being Korean, only Mantis's view about the language of Jonas.
disputes · source-and-allusion · author-and-biography · theories: Quasar and Fortunate Cloud are different ships

Oh, I don't think anyone is arguing that KLS's name is not Korean, but Mantis believes the language of *Jonas* is not Korean.

They may have been different, as at this stage of writing the BotNS I'm not sure Wolfe knew what he was going to do with the Ship of Tzadkiel, as it would not appear until about 6 years later in his writing. But, Wolfe is extremely self-consistent.
