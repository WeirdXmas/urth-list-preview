Satirically pitches a Hollywood film version of The Book of the New Sun, mocking casting choices for Severian, Dorcas, the Autarch and others and proposing a blockbuster marketing approach. The joke relies on the characters and the book's violent, sexual material, so it is a comic take on adaptation rather than a reading of the text.
