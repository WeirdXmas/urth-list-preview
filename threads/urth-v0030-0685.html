2001-08-31 Alice Turner #

Answers Darryl Swett's question about whether Aegypt and Love & Sleep will be republished, saying the next book is a stand-alone called The Translator and that rights issues with Bantam and the new publisher Morrow make a reprint uncertain. Gives the publishing background as the grounds, noting Bantam still holds rights to the earlier books.
answers

The fourth book won't be out next year. That book is -The Translator-, a stand-alone. There are publication problems, too, with the series. Bantam, his long-time publisher, ditched him, but they still have rights to those books. Which means they would have to co-ordinate with his new publisher (Morrow). They might put them out in mass-market paper--I wouldn't count on it, though.

-alga
