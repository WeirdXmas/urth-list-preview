2001-10-15 Jacob Corbin #

Chats about a television series, praising the Nero actor and noting low-budget vintage cars, then lists A&E's other shows and cable channels he would buy. Responds to William Ansley's remark that the series was well done, with no reference to Wolfe's books.
chats

Me too. The production was a bit low-rent at times (I think I saw the same two or three vintage cars in nearly every establishing shot) but the actor who played Nero did an excellent job.

A&E has had some very good original series lately - this, the Hornblower movies, and, um, the courtroom show directed by Sidney Lumet. Plus the Equalizer reruns. If I could buy cable a la carte, I'd only get it, Comedy Central, and Food Network (for Iron Chef, naturlich).

Jacob G Corbin
"I'm a man with a mission/In two or three editions." - Elvis Costello
