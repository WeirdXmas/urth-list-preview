Proposes that the name Cut-throat, a character in the Tale of the Boy Called Frog, is a fish name because a cutthroat is a kind of trout, linking it to the frog-and-jump hint. Responds to Sean's suggestion that Mars appears as Spring Wind and that Cutthroat jumps like a frog, offering the trout reading as a playful extension.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Cut-throat is named for the cutthroat trout
