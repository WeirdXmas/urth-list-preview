Wolfe in Science Fiction Hall of Fame -- June 16, Seattle

Urth list · 7 messages · 2007-04-20 to 2007-06-16

2007urthJosh GellerJames WynnStuart HammRoy C. LackeyMichael Straight
The thread: Roy Lackey alerted West Coast readers that Gene Wolfe was being inducted into the Science Fiction Hall of Fame at a June 16 Seattle event and urged them to attend, noting it might be Wolfe's last chance to travel for such an event. Michael Straight asked who from the list would attend and whether to sit together, and Stuart Hamm said he could not go but a friend would. James Wynn announced he would attend the panels and signings and identified himself with a Texas A&M cap. The thread stayed purely logistical and social, with no literary argument, and ended with attendees arranging to find one another.
  • Roy C. Lackey: urges West Coast readers to attend the Seattle Hall of Fame event for Wolfe
  • Michael Straight: asks who from the list is going and proposes sitting together
  • Stuart Hamm: cannot attend himself but has a friend going, jokes about fan seating
  • James Wynn: will attend panels and signings and will wear a Texas A&M cap to be recognized

2007-04-20 Roy C. Lackey #

Passes along two links reporting Gene Wolfe's induction into the Science Fiction Hall of Fame at a June 16 Seattle event and urges West Coast readers to plan to attend. Adds that this may be the last year Wolfe can make such a trip, but cites no quoted text and offers no reading of the books.
