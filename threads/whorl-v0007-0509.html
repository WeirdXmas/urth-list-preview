Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1997-07-04 Derek Bell #

Argues that the Whorl's technology is uneven, with ordinary-looking devices like concrete and cathode-ray screens alongside advanced items such as chems and asteroid spacecraft, and suggests this is an early form of the split-level technology seen in the Book of the Long Sun. Responds to Kieran Mullen's earlier note, and proposes that the chems may have been imported from outside Urth, though who would do so remains unclear.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Chems imported from outside Urth

In message <[email]>, Kieran Mullen
writes [on genetic technology]:

One thing that intruiges me about the tecnology is that some of it seems similar to our own (Shiprock seems to be reinforced concrete, Sacred Windows seem to be cathode-ray tubes-not even a flat display) yet some of it is quite advanced - Chems and their programming; the ability to make a spacecraft out of an
asteroid. Maybe some technology has lasted better than others, but I suspect this is the early stages of the split level in technology seen more vividly in TBoTNS. The Chems could have been imported from outside Urth, though exactly who would do it is still a mystery.

Tercel
