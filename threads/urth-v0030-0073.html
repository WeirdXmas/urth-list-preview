PEACE princess monster

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-10-30 to 2000-10-30

2000urthMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

2000-10-30 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the princess in Peace, who is a monster, resembles the legendary Melusina, a figure the poster recalled from A.S. Byatt's Possession. Offers no textual evidence beyond the poster's own reading, and explicitly says Peace is not based on Melusina.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Peace's princess resembles Melusina

As I was writing before about the princess bride-monster, there was a name on the tip of my tongue, and I couldn't remember what it was until I found my copy of A.S. Byatt's POSSESSION.

"Melusina" is her name. Daughter of the king of Ys.

I'm not saying that "The Princess and Her Four Suitors" is based upon Melusina; I'm just saying that in my view of the Princess as monster, she is something like Melusina.

=mantis=
