Falun Gong

Themes: Power, rulers and politics

1999-10-29 Alice Turner #

Compares the Falun Gong uprising in China with the uprising against the repressive Ayuntiemento in Viron, hoping the movement finds its Mint. Also mentions a news report of two women in Hyde Park killed by lightning that struck their underwire bras, asking which writer could have invented it.
chats

Has anyone else been following the Falun Gong uprising in China? The NY Times has covered it closely and I have been amazed at how familiar it all sounds. Beijing could be Viron, governed by a repressive, corrupt, half-dead Ayuntiemento, and here's this mystical popular uprising that seems to have come out of nowhere--Falun Gong has only been in existence since 1994--and captured the hearts of quite ordinary people, mostly middle-aged, all over China. They have rallied in the most remarkable way, considering the relative lack of modern communication in China. Just like Viron! I hope they find their Mint.

Also yesterday there was a report of two women snoozing under a tree in Hyde Park who were killed by a lightning bolt that stuck their underwire bras. Now what writer, modernist, post-modernist, whatever, could possibly have made that up?

-alga
