2002-04-03 Nicholas Gevers #

Reports a message from Gene Wolfe on the timing of The Wizard Knight's publication process, saying the manuscript is not yet ready to go to his agent and that David Hartwell's reaction is expected by end of June. Passes along Wolfe's own words as the only evidence, with no commentary on the book's content.

--Nick Gevers
