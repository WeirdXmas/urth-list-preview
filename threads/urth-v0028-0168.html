1999-10-04 Dave Lebling #

Proposes that Dr. Talos meant the labels Death and Innocence the other way round, so Severian is Innocence and Dorcas is Death, despite the immediately following text. Supports this by suggesting a tally of occasions when Dorcas corrects Severian's naive opinions, and quotes Roy C. Lackey's passage on the metaphor.
proposes · close-textual-reading · allegory-and-typology · theories: Severian is Innocence and Dorcas is Death

Roy C. Lackey, on Dorcas:
<<She then recalled to Sev Dr. Talos's labeling him as Death, which Sev said was a metaphor. She said it was a bad metaphor and, as they were walking back to the others, Sev wondered to himself if "Dr. Talos's calling Dorcas "Innocence" had not been a metaphor of the same kind.">>

Every time I read _Shadow_, I'm convinced that Dr. Talos meant the attribution the other way, that Severian was Innocence and Dorcas was Death. I know that a close reading of the immediately following text doesn't support that view, but it just feels right to me. As an exercise, tally up the occasions when Dorcas corrects an innocent or naive opinion expressed by Severian: there are quite a few.

Dave Lebling
(aka vizcacha)
