Hey Scase,

I'm not sure if it is any different than what's in Castle of Days (I don't own it.) I can give you the technical specs on the book, though. It's titled "Shadow of the New Sun: Wolfe on Writing/Writers on Wolfe." It's edited by Peter Wright and published by Liverpool University Press. The ISBN is 978-84631-058-4. No price on the cover of my trade paperback copy, but I think I paid around $30 US at amazon.

Looking at the copyrights page, here's the list of where Wolfe's writings originally appeared:

- *Plan[e]t Engineering*
- 10 original (copyrighted 2007) very short works
- *The Writer, *1980

Hope that helps!
