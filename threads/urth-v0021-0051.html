1998-11-07 Alice Turner #

Replies to a question about whether a Gaiman remark was a fact or a delightful oddity, saying the poster has never read Sandman but found Gaiman an interesting short story writer whose troll-bridge tale recurs to them. Responds to the quoted claim that a Sandman character, Gilbert, is modeled on Chesterton, without adding any argument about it.
chats

From Wombat:

Well, hmmm, the second. I've never read a word of -Sandman-; I suppose I should. Though I cottoned to Gaiman right away as an interesting short story writer. That "Troll Bridge" (?) keeps coming back to me at the oddest moments.

=alga

1998-11-08 Kevin J. Maroney #

Praises Neil Gaiman's Sandman comics, especially the scattered short stories, while cautioning against overpraise. Notes that Wolfe has read Sandman and contributed a folk-music story to a Sandman prose collection, and asks whether anyone has a list of those stories.
informs

Well, I'm quite fond of _Sandman_. It's very easy to overpraise it, but there's some really first-rate work in it, especially in the scattered short stories. Wolfe is, by evidence, a reader of it, of course; he had a story in the _Sandman_ prose collection a couple of years back. (One of his folk music stories--does anyone have a list of all of these?)

Wombat, a.k.a. Kevin Maroney [email]
