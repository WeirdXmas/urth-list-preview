1998-11-25 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks readers to identify a Latin inscription the poster believes appears on a Hyde Park building or monument and to translate it, offering a tentative reading of the line about the virgin's return and Saturn's reign. Responds to Matthew Freestone and Gordon Brain's thread about searching the Marble Arch for clues, and proposes calling off that search.
questions · source-and-allusion

Matthew Freestone and Gordon Brain,

Re: Marble Arch(es), inscriptions, Hyde Park. I'm curious what clues might be on the arch(es), but it is probably too much work for too little. So call off the search!

Here's a Latin inscription that seems to be from a building facing roughly south or west, in the general Hyde Park area (or an alternate reality Hyde Park area):

IAM*REDIT*ET*VIRGO*REDEUNT*SATURNIA*REGNA

As you all know, I don't understand Latin. After some study, this line looks to me to be something like "Now [blank] and Virgin they return the reign of Juno."

But "redit" can mean "the matter comes up again," making the line "Now the matter comes up again and the Girl/Maiden they return to the throne of Juno" (Girl Power! <g>).

If this inscription is real, I'd like to know about the
building/monument it adorns. In any event, I encourage those who know Latin to tell me what it says!

=mantis=

1998-11-28 Matthew Freestone #

Reports that he went to Marble Arch in Hyde Park to check the arches himself and found no inscriptions on either one, ending the search for clues. Replies to Mantis's quoted post calling off the search for arch inscriptions, confirming the idea with a physical visit near London.
informs · puzzle-solving

Sad man that I am, I decided I would have to see the arches for myself (don't worry, I only live 20 minutes or so from central London) and having done so, I can confirm there are definitely no inscriptions on either arch.
