Suggestions for podcast episode on "Peace"

Urth list · 4 messages · 2013-11-12 to 2013-11-16

2013urthFred KiescheMarc AraminiDarrell Burgan
The thread: Fred Kiesche asked for suggestions for a podcast episode on Peace, and Marc Aramini offered to write an essay on the novel by month's end using a general metonymic reading strategy while skipping the novellas. Aramini argued Peace is well studied because Wolfe disclosed key details in an interview, citing a critical collection. Kiesche accepted the offer, said a long essay could be shortened if time was short, and confirmed he had the sources, including Shadows and Attending Daedalus. Kiesche then posted a blog review and said the Peace episode might slip later, with C.L. Moore stories taking its slot. The thread ended in scheduling rather than debate about the book.
  • Marc Aramini: will write an Peace essay using a general metonymic reading, skipping the novellas, since Wolfe's interview disclosures already drew much study
  • Fred Kiesche: accepts Aramini's essay offer, can shorten it if needed, and plans a mid-December recording

2013-11-12 Marc Aramini #

Proposes that Marc Aramini will write an essay on Peace by month's end, with a general metonymic reading strategy for Wolfe's work, and skip the novellas. Notes that Peace has been well studied because Wolfe disclosed key details in an interview, and points to a collection of criticism as the source.
proposes · author-and-biography

I am in 1974 on Wolfe's fiction ... If you want I can write a fairly comprehensive essay by the end of November on 1975's Peace along with a general "metonymic reading" strategy for Wolfe's work.

I will have to skip over Forlesen and come back to it, as the novellas take too long to thoroughly explore.

Lots of excellent work has been done on Peace because Wolfe gave away the key details in an interview without obfuscation, so there is less room for disagreement. I believe it is collected in Wright's Shadows of the New Sun (not the tribute anthology)
