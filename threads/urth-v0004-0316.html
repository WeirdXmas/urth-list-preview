Themes: Suffering and sacrifice

1997-09-19 William H. Graham III #

Asks why the old-style humans in Wolfe's Urth would resort to cannibalism, and proposes that the Masters may rarely eat, so food is scarce for them. Lists reasons the old-style humans cannot buy food from Kaminski's Deli, and rejects the money idea as begging the question.
questions · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Masters rarely eat so food is scarce

The rant continues:

Why do the old-style humans (OSH) have to resort to cannibalism? Is there = evidence to support the idea that the Masters don't need to eat?

(so they don't need to raise chickens, so they don't need chicken medicine = or chicken feed, so there's no more chickens et al for the OSH to eat)

If the Masters do feed, perhaps it's very rarely (I could probably go a lon= g way on a cheese sandwich if my body didn't have to fight cancer and heart= disease and stuff). Thus the supply would be very hard to get. Maybe the= food they eat is poisonous to OSH.

If the Masters eat three squares a day of reg'ler ol' stuff, fine. We know= that the OSH can't walk into Kaminski's Deli because :

=09The Masters recognize OSH on sight
=09OSH don't speak the language
=09OSH don't have whatever it is that the Masters use instead of money.

But I don't like the last idea because it begs the question: "Why don't the= OSH just hijack the Oscar-Myer truck while en route to Kaminski's"?

Your thoughts please,

-WHG3
