2000-12-03 Henry Kaiser #

Reports a lurker's spoiler-free reaction to an advance copy of Return to the Whorl, calling it a satisfying end to the Short Sun cycle. Praises the book as emotionally moving and full of repeated surprises, calling it one of Wolfe's best series works and reassuring readers they will not be disappointed.
informs

I'm just a lurker on this list, who stays in the background and reads everything.
A long-time Wolfe fan, since the 60's.
I recently read an advance copy of RETURN TO THE WHORL that I stumbled upon. I wanted to say a few things about my reactions to it without spoiling anything. So that folks will know that they have something great to look forward to.

I was totally satisfied with it as an end to this cycle. I was emotionally moved by it more than any of the other short sun books. I was surprised again and again all the way through it.
I think it's one of Wolfe's best works of a series.
I don't think that anyone here on the list will be disappointed......

H. Kaiser
