1998-06-02 Robert Borski #

Notes that the one-eyed cat in the officer's scene is borrowed from Poe's "The Black Cat," citing the name Pluto and the graveyard detail. Adds that Wolfe attended a school named after Poe, then closes with playful tapping and a list of stray notes on In Green's Jungles colours and characters, ending with NEVERMORE.

The cat is lifted nearly wholecloth from "The Black Cat," from monocularity to its name, Pluto. As the officer tells us he's a *graveyard* cat.

Also a semi-autobiographical note, since Gene Wolfe attended a school named after Eddy Poe.

Re the IGene colors: Green: Victor's mother; Lucius Shepherd.
