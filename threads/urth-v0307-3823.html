Identifies a Borges source for the half-remembered passage Civet mentioned, pointing to the entry "Fauna of Mirrors" in The Book of Imaginary Beings. Adds that the entry "Baldanders" is the source Wolfe himself claims for his Baldanders, citing Wolfe's CASTLE OF THE OTTER/CASTLE OF DAYS.
