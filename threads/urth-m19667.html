The thread: Posters argued over whether the green world in Wolfe's Book of the New Sun-related fiction is literally Urth, with Marc Aramini insisting symbols resolve into literal identities such as Green being Urth and Blue being Ushas. Lee Berman countered that the parallels concern predator-prey dominance and spiritual progression rather than good and evil, and Dan'l Danehy-Oakes and James B. Jordan disputed what Wolfe himself had said about Green as Urth. Aramini claimed Wolfe's private denial was only a request for secrecy, and the thread ended in unresolved dispute over the author's statements.
  • Marc Aramini: symbols resolve into literal identities, so Green is Urth and Latro is Pleistorus
  • Lee Berman: Urth-Green-Blue parallels concern predator-prey dominance and spiritual progression
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Wolfe denied only the planet-pair mapping and left open that Green is Urth
  • James B. Jordan: Wolfe explicitly denied that Green is Urth, so the author's statement settles it
  • Marc Aramini: Wolfe never denied it privately and only asked that it not be shared

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Deep time and ruins, Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies

2011-11-09 Marc Aramini replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that the books' symbols must resolve into literal identities, so Blue is not Ushas and Green really is Urth, and that Latro is Pleistorus, Silk is Typhon's son, and Kypris is Mamelta. Responds to Lee Berman's predator-prey comment and to an earlier correction from Gene Wolfe, citing the mismatch between Blue, Urth and Green and the city under the water as a MacGuffin.
proposes · puzzle-solving · allegory-and-typology · theories: Latro really is Pleistorus; Silk really is Typhon's son; Kypris really is Mamelta; Green really is Urth

My problem with all this is that Blue is colder than Urth is colder than Green - that city under the water is a MacGuffin, we are meant to look at it and try to find a way to map Urth onto it but there is just no way to do it and the mechanism is just not there to explain how they accidentally wind up on Urth when they mean to go to Green. I firmly believed blue was Urth/Ushas until Gene corrected me. I don't see Blue as Ushas. It's the coldest of the three (two) worlds. It's something else.

And I like literal truths to explain things in Wolfe; I feel that is how he utilizes symbols. Thus my opinion Latro really IS Pleistorus, Weer really IS dead, Green really IS Urth, Silk really IS Typhon's son, Kypris really IS Mamelta, the rajan really IS Silk after OBW, etc etc etc. I think that's how Wolfe operates when he creates his identity mysteries: that there is a real tacit solid non-symbolic solution that explains the metonymic resonances that permeate the text (bunch of tales about dead people and sneaky murderers set up in parallel? narrator is really dead and killed at least one person!)

We don't all have to agree though and I really do understand why some don't.

2011-11-09 Lee Berman #

Argues that the Urth-Green-Blue parallels are not about good and evil but predator-prey dominance, and proposes instead that Green and Urth mirror each other through spiritual progression, with Blue and Ushas more advanced, linked by the ruins under their waters. Responds to Marc Aramini's Falling Down quip and David Stockhoff's idea that each world is the future of the one before, offering an alternate explanation to Marc's straight-identity theory.
proposes · theology-and-religion · myth-and-structure · theories: Green is Urth through spiritual progression; Ushas is spiritually advanced beyond Urth; Blue is more advanced than Green

Heh. Good movie! I don't see this in terms of good and evil, though. More like a predator-prey relationship. Those with an abundance of self-confidence will prey on those who are more lacking in it. Has been that way since the early days of this list.

I appreciate Jeff's suggestion too though it also boggles my mind to comprehend what it going on there, not to mention what point Wolfe might have been trying to make with that.

My alternate (to Marc's straight identity idea) explanation for "Green is Urth!" and the resonance of Green structures to Urth structures has to do with spiritual progression.

Ushas is a spiritually advanced progression from Urth. Blue is a more spiritually advanced than Green. Antediluvian Earth is more spirtually advanced than pre-diluvian Earth which was roamed by nephilim and demons and whatever other Biblical horrors Noah's Flood washed away.

Thus "GREEN is Urth!" (I think that is the most accurate representation of the quote).

Green not Blue. Blue is more like Ushas. So much focus is on the similar structures found on Green and Urth in this discussion. Ignored are the similarity of ruins found under the waters of both Blue and Ushas.
