Yesod- what is it?

Urth list · 7 messages · 2010-08-12 to 2010-08-12

2010urthThe Book of the New SunRyan DunnLee BermanAntónio Pedro MarquesDavid StockhoffJeff Wilson
The thread: Readers asked what Yesod, the higher universe in Wolfe's Book of the New Sun, actually is, and whether it should be understood as hyperspace or subspace. Lee Berman argued it is a universe with its own suns, planets and gravity, citing Brook Madregot's escape to Briah and Severian's time travel through it. David Stockhoff first equated Yesod with subspace and hyperspace, then proposed that the Hieromakers colonized it through FTL travel, producing artificial machine planets. Jeff Wilson suggested the answer depends on the physical laws of that realm, offering Stephen Baxter's RAFT as a comparison. The thread ended without agreement, with Marques only reproducing Berman's view that Yesod connects to other universes and has different physical properties.
  • Lee Berman: Yesod is a universe with its own suns, planets and gravity, not hyperspace or subspace
  • David Stockhoff: Yesod is hyperspace/subspace, colonized by the Hieromakers via FTL travel
  • Jeff Wilson: the answer depends on Yesod's physical laws, as with Baxter's RAFT
  • António Pedro Marques: relays Berman's view that Yesod connects to other universes with different physical properties

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Robots, androids and artificial life, Deep time and ruins

2010-08-12 David Stockhoff replying to António Pedro Marques #

Proposes that the Hieromakers reached Yesod by discovering FTL travel, so that Yesod is a colonized hyperspace with artificial machine planets like Apheta's. Builds on António Pedro Marques's question whether hyperspace can hold suns and planets, citing Star Trek's living quarters in ships and the SF tropes Wolfe reworks.
proposes · genre-and-craft · theories: Hieromakers escaped to Yesod via FTL travel; Yesod is colonized hyperspace with machine planets; Yesodi planets must travel at FTL speeds

What I was thinking is that, if the Hieromakers escaped to Yesod, it must have been available to them somehow. Perhaps it was discovered through FTL travel. Given that common SF tropes underly the books---and the older tropes are often deployed in TBotNS as though they were simple things we learned as children, because we DID learn them as children---it seems possible. And we know from Star Trek that it is possible to live and breathe there in a ship.

If they had then learned to colonize hyperspace, it could lead to completely artifical machine planets like Apheta's, there being no native ones to colonize.

Just a thought. For all I know, this would mean that the Yesodi planets must all be travelling at N or FTL to stay in hyperspace.

Responds to Lee Berman's question about whether hyperspace or subspace can contain suns, planets and gravity, and offers the Baxter comparison as a way to think about it.
