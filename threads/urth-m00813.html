2005-01-03 Dave Rolsky #

Asks two questions about the ending of The Wizard Knight: who the "great lord in need of a knight" is that Michael mentions to Able, and how Ben will be able to see the letter Michael has arranged. Notes that Able says Michael has found a way without giving details, and wonders whether this is divine power or something more interesting.
questions · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving

* Who is the "great lord in need of a knight" who Michael tells Able about at the end?

* How will Ben see the letter? Able says "Michael has found a way" but of course doesn't give any details. Is it just supposed to be through an exercise of Michael's divine powers, or is it something more interesting?

I'm sure there I could come up with lots of others, but those were the ones immediately on my mind when I finished the second book.

-dave
