NPR taking suggestions for The Best Science Fiction and Fantasy works of all time

Urth list · 9 messages · 2011-06-22 to 2011-06-23

2011urthLane HaygoodJordon FlatoGreg BatesAntónio Pedro MarquesDan'l Danehy-OakesDaniel Petersen
The thread: Members discussed how to use NPR's online poll for the best science fiction and fantasy works of all time. Jordon Flato urged readers to nominate The Book of the New Sun, noting that only he had nominated it so far. Greg Bates posted a five-book list leaving out Bakker and Vance, then regretted that five picks cannot cover both genres. Daniel Petersen corrected his reading of the rules, saying multiple five-title posts are allowed. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes separately asked whether The Book of the Long Sun holds up without the Short Sun books. The thread ended on the rules clarification rather than any consensus on Wolfe.
  • Jordon Flato: nominate The Book of the New Sun in the NPR poll
  • Greg Bates: five picks cannot fairly cover both SF and fantasy
  • Daniel Petersen: NPR allows multiple five-title posts, not one list
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: asks whether Long Sun is better than remembered without reading Short Sun first

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both
