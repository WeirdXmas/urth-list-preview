Thea/Vodalus -- who were they digging up?

Introduces a newcomer who asks two questions: who Vodalus, Thea and Hildegrin are digging up in the Necropolis, and why Severian never relays a slave's question to Palemon. Says he has read the books and wonders whether the unasked question is meant to make readers ponder its answer.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading

Hello, all! I have recently joined the list, although I have been reading=

the archives and am very impressed with the depth of many list-members' analysis of Wolfe's works=2E Anyway, by way of background, I have read TBONS/UOTNS and recently finnished the first half of BOTLS -- I started re-reading BOTNS again while I waited for Epiphany to arrive and, now that=

it has, am halfway through Shadow and Claw and can't put it down again=2E = =20

Anyway, two things have always puzzled me, and perhaps some listmembers ca= n
shed some insight on them=2E

First, who were Vodalus and Thea (and Hildegrin) digging up when Severian first meets them in the Necropolis? It's a woman, and for some reason I get the feeling that Thea has some interest in or relation to her, but otherwise I can't figure it out=2E

Second, in Sword and Citadel, when Sev is in the lazaret being cared for b= y
the Pelerines, he meets a slave who tells him a story about meeting "Journeyman Palemon," and asks Severian to relay a question to Palemon if ever he meets him again=2E Severian, of course, does subsequently meet Palemon again, but does not (as far as we know) ask Palemon the slave's question=2E Any thoughts as to why? I cannot believe it simply slipped G= W's
mind=2E Is the absence of the question supposed to make us ponder what th= e
real answer is?

Anyway, I hope someone can share their insights with me, and I look forwar= d
to reading everyone's thoughts on other topics as well=2E

Regards,
