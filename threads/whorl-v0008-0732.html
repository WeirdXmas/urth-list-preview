Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-08-07 mary whalen #

Lists dictionary-style definitions of cat, Catamitus, cobra, Crassula and prion, along with references like Orchid's and the Chrasmologic Writings that tie some entries to Wolfe's world. Traces Catamitus to the Latin spelling of Ganymede, recounting his abduction by Zeus as an eagle, his service as cup-bearer, and his placement in Aquarius beside Aquila.
informs · source-and-allusion

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Cat. Any species of family Felidae, especially in the genus Felis, and usually Felis catus (Felis domesticus) the common cat. A man who has visited Orchid's.

Catamitus. Latin spelling of Ganymedes. A character in Greek mythology. Son of Tros or Laomedon. Beautiful boy who was abducted by Zeus in the form of an eagle to be the cup-bearer (and more) of the gods. He sent his father two immortal mares and a golden vine to recompensate him. He was later supposedly put into the sky as the constellation Aquarius (the water-bearer) next to Aquila (the eagle).

Cobra, the. Any species of a group in family Elapidae. Mythological figure in the Chrasmologic Writings who tempts Wo-man.

Crassula. Latin for Orpine. Any species of genus Crassula, which are succulent herbs. One of the girls at Orchid's.
