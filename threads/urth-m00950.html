Time For Latro?

2005-01-12 PMorris33 #

Asks whether a third Latro book is coming now that The Wizard Knight is finished, noting that list members a year earlier had said Wolfe would return to the Latro world after completing it. Reports no answer yet and seeks confirmation from the group about the planned sequel.
questions

Now that the Wizard/Knight has been completed, isn't it time for the third Latro book?
Does anyone in this group know? I asked about a year ago, and several people said that The Master would return to the Latro universe as soon as he had completed Wizard/Knight

Pippen

2005-01-12 James Wynn replying to PMorris33 #

Reports a secondhand rumor that Wolfe planned a fight at a temple and was working out how to bring Latro there, then jokes that Latro's travels would take a long time. Responds to PMorris33's earlier mention of the Latro books' progress, using a comparison to L. Ron Hubbard's sprawling books.
informs

Someone this summer mentioned that Wolfe had decided there was going to be a fight at a temple and he was trying to figure out how to get Latro there. That's the last I have heard of it's progress. If Wolfe were L. Ron Hubbard, no doubt he could get Latro all the way around the world and back to his home in a couple of summers (and they'd be big fat books too). Who knows how long it will take to find out what Latro did in the Levant.
