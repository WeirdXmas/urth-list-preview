Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2000-08-16 Jim Henley #

Jim Henley reposts Nicholas Gevers's quoted exchange with John Clute and objects that Clute turned a tentative guess that Horn or Silk might be an inhumu into a claim that it is reasonably clear. He faults Clute for presenting a private speculation as a publicly proclaimed conclusion, complaining about the shift in tone.

This will sound pissy of me, but why the _fuck_ does Clute turn the privately-acknowledged "shot in the dark" into the publically-proclaimed "it is 'reasonably clear' that Horn/Silk is himself an inhumu?"

By gum, it's more annoying even than people whingeing about the covers!
