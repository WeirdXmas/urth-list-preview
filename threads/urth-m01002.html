Points out that a Belgian translation rendered the hourglass in a window as a "Sand Walker," then links a children's book, Le Marché des Carabas, that the poster believes Wolfe read as a boy. Offers a loose remark about a prehistoric ancestor's semi-opposable fifth toe and cats and dogs, tied to the hourglass image.
informs · source-and-allusion

Le Marche des Carabos

This is an odd one but a belgian translated the
hourglass or eggtimer in windows as a 'Sand Walker'

The prehistoric ancestor of cats and dogs had a fifth
toe that was semi-opposable... before they learnt how
to make these apes hunt for them...by teaching them
how to hunt.

I am sure this book was on GW's library shelf as a boy
