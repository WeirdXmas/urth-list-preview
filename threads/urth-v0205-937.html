Informs Christopher Culver that copies of Operation Ares can be found through the online bookseller AbeBooks, reporting a search that turned up 39 copies priced from $2.89 to $80.00. Responds directly to Culver's question about where to track down an out-of-print early Wolfe novel for his collection.
