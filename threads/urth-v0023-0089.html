1999-02-21 Roy C. Lackey #

Replies to Jack Lyons's complaint about a Gilligan exposition by joking that the theme tune now runs through his head. Also reports that he bought Door Number Three and that he and his wife are reading it aloud and enjoying it.

Bought your _Door_Number_Three_ yesterday. The wife and I are reading it aloud. We like it.

Roy
