Speaking of Art..

Urth list · 4 messages · 2011-01-05 to 2011-01-05

2011urthThe Book of the New SunLane HaygoodDavid StockhoffLee Berman
The thread: Participants discussed whether Severian in The Book of the New Sun resembles Gene Wolfe as a young man, and whether Wolfe writes himself and his family into his fiction. Lee Berman supported the idea, pointing to resemblances between Severian and Number Five in The Fifth Head of Cerberus and asking whether Phaedria resembles Thecla. David Stockhoff added similar observations and asked who Phaedria might be. Lane Haygood and Stockhoff traded jokes about a writer's wife and eating her brain for memories, which drifted the thread away from the textual question. The thread ended in playful banter rather than a settled conclusion.
  • Lee Berman: Severian may be modeled on a young Gene Wolfe, supported by textual resemblances rather than a photo
  • David Stockhoff: Number Five is named Gene Wolfe and looks like Severian; asks who Phaedria is
  • Lane Haygood: Mocks the idea of writing a real spouse into fiction, joking about the brain-eating plot
  • David Stockhoff: Jokes that a writer's wife would know what she can endure

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Books, libraries and writing, Fathers, mothers and children

2011-01-05 David Stockhoff replying to Lane Haygood #

Jokingly argues that a writer's wife would know what she can endure, responding to Lane Haygood's quip about Wolfe putting his wife into a story. Points to Haygood's joke about eating his wife's brain to gain her memories, as a grim parody of Severian's tale and of Wolfe writing himself and his family into fiction.
