Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1998-11-03 Matthew Freestone #

Argues that the chems' reproduction in Wolfe's Long Sun books was probably an idea Wolfe found neat at the time and was not meant to withstand close scrutiny. Responds to a quoted remark about chems running programs they cannot consciously access, using a joke about parents and sex to make the point.
disputes · genre-and-craft

David:
And for all of history, the parents would tell you where to go. I'd especially not like to try such a request on a heavily armed robot soldier.
I think the whole chem reproduction process was just an idea that seemed neat to Wolfe at the time - I'm sure it wasn't intended to stand up to a lot of scrutiny.
