1998-09-02 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Announces the 1998 World Fantasy Convention in Monterey, California, with its dates and web site, and says the poster has booked a hotel and joined. Notes that Gene Wolfe will attend, as Robert Borski reported earlier on the list, and hopes to meet other list members there.
informs

And now for something completely different.

The 1998 World Fantasy Convention (Oct 29 to Nov 1) is to be held in Monterey, California. At the convention center next to the Marriott and Double Tree hotels. Web site: http:www.pensfa.org/wfc98

Gene Wolfe is going (as you know because Robert Borski reported it here). I've made my hotel reservations and sent in my check for membership--I'm going. I trust that Patrick O'Leary is going, and I look forward to seeing him there as well as anybody else from around here.

Just an announcement. That is all.

=mantis=
