New Andre-Driussi book

Urth list · 12 messages · 2012-08-21 to 2012-08-23

The thread: Dan'l Danehy-Oakes announced a forthcoming Michael Andre-Driussi concordance to the Long Sun and Short Sun books, due October 2012, and gave no link since the details were not yet online. Antonin Scriabin and Daniel Petersen welcomed it enthusiastically, Scriabin saying it moved Return to the Whorl up his reading pile. Marc Aramini, who had seen an advance copy, praised its name taxonomy and page-referenced Short Sun timeline and urged support for scholarly Wolfe works. Larry Miller asked whether it includes maps or synopses and what the timeline covers. The thread stayed mostly informational and celebratory, ending without disagreement.
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: announced the concordance's structure and October 2012 date, with no link yet
  • Antonin Scriabin: asked for a link and welcomed the book as a long-hoped-for lexicon
  • Daniel Petersen: delighted that the other two Solar Cycle books finally get a lexicon, praising Andre-Driussi
  • Marc Aramini: recommended the book from an advance copy, praising the name taxonomy and Short Sun timeline
  • Larry Miller: asked whether maps or synopses are included and what the timeline covers

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Names and their meanings

2012-08-21 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Informs the list that Michael Andre-Driussi has a new concordance for the Long Sun and Short Sun books, titled Gate of Horn, Book of Silk, due for publication in October 2012. Describes its structure as two parts, one for each series, with a middle section covering cant and foreign words.
informs

Michael Andre-Driussi has a new concordance coming out for the books of the Long/Short sun. It's called "Gate of Horn, Book of Silk," and it looks pretty good. It's divided into two parts -- what do ya know, one for Long and one for Short -- with a section inbetween that covers cant and foreign words pretty thoroughly.

Official publication date is 10/15/12.

2012-08-23 Marc Aramini replying to Daniel Petersen #

Recommends Andre-Driussi's new companion book, praising its Short Sun timeline with page references and its Long Sun taxonomy of names as useful reference work. Reports that he suggested additions such as a liana entry, and urges support for scholarly works on Wolfe, responding to Daniel Petersen's thread about the book.
supports · source-and-allusion

The timeline in Short Sun with relevant page numbers is the closest thing to "summary" and is his best work on the Short Sun part.?He even?breaks down on which pages the narrator is writing about a certain past event in a multi section?timeline that is more or less chronological.? ?
The Long Sun section was more "helpful" to me, especially taxonomy of the names?(but I spent countless hours thinking about Short Sun) - something I always meant to do but never really got around to, and he has pretty much done all that leg work already.? ? ?
It's worth a buy especially if you haven't gone through the oldest whorl archives.? I made a few "strong" suggestions about entries that?should be included in Short Sun (a liana entry in the short sun half, for example) but I don't know if he will incorporate any changes at all in the final release.? ?
I think we need to support scholarly works on Wolfe if we want to ever see more. ?
