Argues chems lack human-style sex, so only making a child, which takes a year, bars them from seeing the gods. Proposes Lemur rose before Quetzal, having served two Caldes and deceived them and their commissioners. Responds to David Stockhoff's remarks on the Ayuntamiento's humanity, using Lemur's service under two Caldes as chronological evidence.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Lemur rose to power before Quetzal; Lemur deceived the Caldes and commissioners

I don't think chems have anything like what we would call sex. The only thing that disqualifies a chem from seeing the gods is actually making a child, which takes a year.

Lemur has served under two Caldes. That surely puts his rise to power earlier than that of Quetzal. And I take it he deceived them, and the various commissioners through who he governed.
