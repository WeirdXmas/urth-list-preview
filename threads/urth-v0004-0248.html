1997-08-18 Alice Turner #

Proposes merging the two Urth discussion groups into a single general literary reading forum instead of splitting off into separate groups for Simmons and Dan Simmons-style authors. Suggests considering A.S. Byatt's Possession and Peter Hoeg's Smilla's Sense of Snow as shared reading, both puzzle books with strong genre sense published in the mainstream.
proposes

rana,

Here's a suggestion put forth for debate. Consolidation of the two groups seems only sensible, considering that we're not going to get a new book till 1999---at that time you might want to splinter again, especially as a second Short Sun book may follow hard on the heels of the first.

But what if, instead of starting new groups devoted to Simmons, C. Smith, etc., we became a kind of reading forum (if that doesn't sound too bourgeois)? We're a self-selected group, we're obviously working pretty well together, why don't we just throw it open for a while to a variety of literary topics? The people who think they're not interested in Smith or Simmons could either lurk for a while or get and read the books and pitch in. A single book could be considered. It seems to me, for instance, that most Wolfeans would get a thrill from A.S. Byatt's Possession and Peter Hoeg's Smilla's Sense of Snow, puzzle books with a strong sense of genre, but published in the mainstream.

-alga-
