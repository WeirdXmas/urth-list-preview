Lists several words from the aristocratic language of the Trivigauntis, giving each one's apparent meaning and origin, such as 'boraz' as a family name and 'marhaba' as Arabic for hello. Reports these findings as a side note while also asking after the Mamelta question and noting a book update.
informs · source-and-allusion

So, Mamelta continues to elude us? <Sigh.>

In the meantime I've managed to find a few of the words used as the aristocratic language of the Trivigauntis.

Trivigaunte Terms
"boraz" = (in addition to whatever meaning) a family name [Hebrew?]

"karbaj" = (in addition to unknown meaning) a family name [Arabic], used for example as a personal name for a racehorse ("El Karbaj")

"marhaba" = hello [Arabic]
