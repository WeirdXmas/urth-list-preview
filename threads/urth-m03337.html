2007-04-13 Steven Bowen #

Recalls reading Kurt Vonnegut's books as a high school student in the early 1970s, favoring God Bless You, Mr. Rosewater and Cat's Cradle over later work. Says Vonnegut became a bore about the Dresden bombing and disputes that it reflected Allied aims, responding to Bruce Trinque's note about the O'Brian mailing list discussion of his death.
chats

I was 'turned on' to Vonnegut in the early '70s when I was still in high school -- I absorbed a number of his books like they were oxygen. And Player Piano was part of the AP curriculum. I remember most fondly the God Bless You, Mr. R, Cat's Cradle period the best. In later years I thought he'd become a terrible bore, esp. about the Dresden bombing. He's entitled to his opinion, having lived through it, but I don't think it was emblamatic of the Allies aims during that incredible apocalyptic holocaust.

Steven
