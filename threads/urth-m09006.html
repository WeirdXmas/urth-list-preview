2009-03-18 Matthew Keeley #

Asks whether list members have read Bioy Casares's novella The Invention of Morel and suggests it will appeal to Wolfe fans, while declining to discuss its plot. Notes the book is short but satisfying and that Bioy Casares was a close friend and collaborator of Borges, who wrote its introduction.

Has anyone here read this book? I'm not going to discuss the plot here, as I don't want this thread to be immediately spoilery, but I think there's a lot in the book to appeal to Wolfe fans. It's very short, but also quite satisfying. The author, Adolfo Bioy Casares, was a great friend and collaborator of Borges, who wrote an introduction to the book.

-Matt
