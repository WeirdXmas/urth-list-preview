Argues that rigorous logical reasoning is within reach of ordinary readers and that logic can expose contradictions in a flawed premise, though it cannot by itself confirm a premise's truth. Negative, Captain. Rigorously correct logical reasoning is easily within the grasp of a typical person if they wish to seek it out. While logic alone cannot confirm the truth of a premise, it can however reach a contradiction that disproves the truth of a flawed premise, and it can establish relationships between a premise and a proposed truth.
