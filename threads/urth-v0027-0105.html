1999-06-20 Nigel Price #

Chats about naming his computer network drives after characters and places from Wolfe's Urth books, such as Conciliator, Fuligin, Gyoll and Hildegrin, and notes the nodes take names from Spenser's Faerie Queene. Responds warmly to Mitchell's earlier posting and asks whether such naming makes him an Urthling, inviting others to share their own drive names.
chats

Oh, Mitchell, wonderful - I loved this posting!

Do I count as an Urthling if I've got drives on my network named after characters and places in the Urth Cycle? My C: drive is "Conciliator", and somewhere there's an F: drive called "Fuligin", a G: drive called "Gyoll", and an H: drive called Hildegrin.

(The nodes and the other drives, incidentally, are named are characters from Spenser's "The Faerie Queene". Anyone else got a remote CD-ROM drive called "Excesse"?)

Nigel Price
Minety, nr Thrax
The Commonwealth

1999-06-21 Charles Dye #

Jokes that the poster would like a Severian Lives bumper sticker in argent on fuligin colours, and recalls naming six workstations after Urth characters before the IT staff renamed them after Peanuts characters. Responds to Nigel Price's quoted post about drives named after Urth Cycle characters and places, continuing the playful tone without any critical reading of the books.
chats

Where can I get a "Severian Lives" bumper sticker? (Argent on fuligin, if there's a choice of colors!)

I shipped a set of six workstations once, and gave them network names of Thecla, Dorcas, Jolenta, Pia, Daria, and Valeria. The MIS people who bought them immediately renamed 'em after Peanuts characters, of course. Life just is not fair.
