Themes: Books, libraries and writing

1998-11-30 Dan Parmenter #

Proposes reissuing Castle of the Otter under that name with the essay "Books in Books of the New Sun" replacing the long capsule reviews from the original edition, and suggests adding a new Wolfe essay on the whole series. Grounds this in the reviews being tiresome and in the title's origin as a play on a mis-reported Locus coming-attractions title, which predates The Citadel of the Autarch.
proposes · author-and-biography

From: [email]

If you mean me (and it's conceivable that you don't), what I've argued for is slightly more radical: I'd like to see OTTER reissued under that name, with "Books in Books of the New Sun" substituted for what seems like several chapters worth of capsule rave reviews that Wolfe included in the original edition. I don't know if it was vanity or the desire to fill out pages, but it gets a bit tiresome after a while. It might also be nice to have an additional essay by Wolfe on the entire series, since OTTER actually predates CITADEL; indeed, its title is a play on the mis-reported title presented in a LOCUS "coming attractions" feature.

D
