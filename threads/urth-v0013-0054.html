1998-06-14 Alice Turner #

Answers mantis's question about whether Dante's Inferno ties the earthquake to Jesus's Harrowing of Hell, by reporting from a new Pinsky translation. Says cantos V, XII and XXI mention the earthquake but never attribute it to the Harrowing, and that neither Jesus nor God is named in the Inferno.
answers · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Harrowing of Hell caused the earthquake in Dante

mantis,

By coincidence, I'm just reading the new Pinsky tranlation of the Inferno (I'm a junkie; this is my seventh translation). Cantos V, XII, XXI mention the earthquake. It's never said that the Harrowing caused it--never once is Jesus's name mentioned, nor that of God, in the Inferno. But, yes, there was an earthquake, which caused severe fissures in the stone funnel-shaped structure of Dante's Hell.

-alga-
