2003-01-10 Nicholas Gevers #

Argues that the castaway's world in the story "Castaway" is Earth itself, a post-Ragnarok Urth, and that the story treats alienation from Mother Earth with humanity surviving while Earth dies. Responds to Rich Horton's question about whether the castaway is remembering a lost Earth, endorsing his comparison with Tiptree and asking whether the Castaway is the shipwreck victim, the narrator, or the whole human species.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theories: The castaway's world is post-Ragnarok Earth, Urth

SPOILER SPACE

Rich Horton re "Castaway":

"Do you think the world was actually Earth? Or was
the castaway remembering lost Earth somehow?"

The world was Earth, I'm pretty sure--in a sense, Urth
post Ragnarok. I think your comparison with Tiptree is
well founded--the theme here is very much alienation
from Mother Earth, only this time with humanity
surviving, Earth dying. Interesting to consider who
the Castaway really is--the shipwreck victim, or the
narrator, and with the narrator, the entire human
species?

--Nick Gevers.
