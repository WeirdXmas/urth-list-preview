New Sun GRAPHIC Novel

Urth list · 3 messages · 1999-05-18 to 1999-05-19

1999urthThe Book of the New SunKevin J. MaroneyMichael Andre-DriussiDave Lebling
The thread: Collectors compared notes on a New Sun graphic novel adaptation after an eBay listing claimed six issues, though posters had only seen three. Michael Andre-Driussi raised the discrepancy as a bibliographic tidbit. Kevin J. Maroney, citing his experience at the publisher's comics shop, said the Shadow of the Torturer adaptation was never continued past its early issues and praised artist Ted Nafieh's rendering of Urth. David_Lebling added that the listing mentions only the first two issues and suggested the seller was repeating the original publication plan. The thread ended with the claim settled as an unfinished series, with no real disagreement.
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: a seller's six-issue claim is doubtful; he knows of only three issues
  • Kevin J. Maroney: the adaptation was never continued past its early issues, per shop experience
  • David_Lebling: the listing shows only two issues and likely repeats the original plan

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both

1999-05-18 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Informs the list that an eBay seller of issues one and two of the New Sun graphic novel adaptation claims the series ran to six issues, not the three the poster has seen. Offers this as a bibliographic tidbit for collectors, with the poster stating he has never heard of more than three issues.
informs

Fellow Urthlings,

I just saw that a fellow on eBay is selling issues number one and two of the Graphic Novel adaptation of TBOTNS.

That may be of interest to anybody looking for these items.

But what I found of interest: the seller is a comic book expert, and =he= says that the series went on to =six= issues.

I have never seen more than issues one, two, and three. Nor have I even heard of there being more.

So, a biliographic tidbit; more questing beasts for the bibliomaniacs; etc.

=mantis=

1999-05-18 Kevin J. Maroney #

Reports that the Shadow of the Torturer comic adaptation was never continued beyond its early issues, correcting a seller's claim of six issues. Based on the poster's experience working at the publisher's comics shop, and praises artist Ted Nafieh's capture of Urth's look.
informs

I was working in a comics shop for the entirety of the existence of the company which published _Shadow of the Torturer_, and I can assure you that the final issues of the series were not published. I cannot recall without consulting my collection whether the final published issue was #3 or #4, but I will check sometime soon and report.

By the way, these are *very* much worth owning. I don't think any other artist has done as good a job of capturing the "look" of Urth as did Ted Nafieh in this adaptation.

Wombat, a.k.a. Kevin Maroney [email]
