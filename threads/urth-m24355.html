2013-12-24 Marc Aramini #

Argues that Peace draws on Jurgen, the book dedicated to Burton Rascoe, citing Jurgen as a sun symbol and the Nessus shirt. Grounds this in Lois Arbuthnot's remark about Weer's secret sword, a possible Roscoe-McAfee name link, and a centaur Nessus scene where Jurgen talks with his young first love.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Peace draws on Jurgen for its sun symbolism; Roscoe name in Peace points to Burton Rascoe

Tried to send this message a few times but it hasn't gone through.? Still looking up references in Peace, at one point Lois Arbuthnot says that Weer's sword is not like Jurgen's.? (it is a secret sword, a pocket knife) so I started to read Jurgen, which is dedicated to burton rascoe.? Not quite enough to get the name Roscoe?which Blaine uses for James McAfee, but within three pages several things struck me: talk of Jurgen as a symbol of the sun, and then talk of the Nessus shirt. The centaur Nessus allows Jurgen to talk to his earliest love when she is young before her betrayals.? Time shifting of perceptions going on as well there.
