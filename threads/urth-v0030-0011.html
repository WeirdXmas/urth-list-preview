Urth list · 6 messages · 2000-10-17 to 2000-10-19

2000urthWilliam H. AnsleyTalaricanMark Millman
The thread: Ranjit Bhatnagar asked list-administration questions about the Urth and Whorl mailing lists, including whether they should merge and how ads should be handled. Mark Millman preferred keeping two separate lists because threads are easier to follow, and said advertising is acceptable but not preferred. William Ansley supported merging the lists into one, accepted plain-text ads but not HTML ones, and asked that the search feature be advertised with a link in every message or digest. The thread is short and ends with the two posters' views recorded rather than any resolution; the posts are largely duplicated.
  • Mark Millman: keep Urth and Whorl as two separate lists because threads are easier to follow
  • Mark Millman: advertising acceptable but not preferred
  • William Ansley: merge the two lists into one unified list
  • William Ansley: plain-text ads acceptable, HTML ads not; advertise the search feature with a link in each message

2000-10-19 William H. Ansley #

Supports merging the Urth and Whorl mailing lists into one unified list, and says plain-text ads are acceptable while HTML ads are not. Also asks that the list's search feature be advertised, with a link in every message or digest, and endorses an earlier reply on the same point.
supports

I also favor a single list.

I would say an absolute no to any ads that were not plain text. I would prefer no ads, but will put up with plain text ads rather than have no list.

I consider the search capability to be a very good thing. And, if you can include it in the next incarnation of the list, don't keep it such a secret next time, i.e., put a link to the search page in the addendum tacked onto every message or digest.

See previous response.

I heartily second this.
