1999-10-12 Kevin J. Maroney #

Clarifies that he never said Severian's appearance in the Short Sun book was certain, saying only that Severian and his sister appear, and that he does not know who the sister is. Answers Nick's question about publication dates by reporting that the manuscripts are complete and an accelerated schedule was rumoured, and that he will ask Hartwell.

I didn't say that. I said Severian shows up, and so does his sister. I don't know what character that latter is.

I don't know. Obviously, the mss. are complete; that's Wolfe's custom. I had heard that the three were going to be released on an accelerated schedule, say every eight months rather than every 12, but I don't know if that's fact or fancy. I'll ask Hartwell when he gets back from Frankfurt.
