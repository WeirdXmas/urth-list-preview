Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

2003-06-23 Ron Crown #

Shares a link to a Greek translation of Wolfe's story "The Hero as Werwolf" and suggests readers may see where the word "lycanthropy" comes from. Offered as a playful nod to earlier list discussion of Wolfe's Greek-themed novels, with no argument made.
informs · source-and-allusion

Since there's been so much discussion of Wolfe's "Greek" novels, I thought y'all might enjoy seeing some Wolfe translated into Greek at the site below; specifically, Wolfe's story "The Hero as Werwolf." Even if you can't make much sense of the text, perhaps you can figure out (although I'm sure the erudite members of this list already know) where we get the word "lycanthropy." :-)

<http://www.altfactor.gr/cgi-bin/websf.cgi?wolfe>
