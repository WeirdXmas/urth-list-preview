2001-07-18 Dan Parmenter #

Reports finding a used copy of Lake of the Long Sun at an Annie's used bookstore, bought to give a friend reading the posymbol series. Describes the store's romance-heavy genre stock and a small SF section, and complains that paperback editions crop Don Maitz's cover art.

From: [email]

I found a copy of LAKE OF THE LONG SUN at an ANNIE'S, a seemingly once-prominent chain of used bookstores that specializes in "genre" fiction, especially romance. I have the good fortune to have one of the last ones (at least locally) literally across the street from my house and so while I was scaring up used Whitley Strieber paperbacks I scanned the rather small SF section and there was LAKE, which I already owned but bought to give to a friend who was reading my copies of the series.

My only real problem with my paperback NEW SUN books is how on all but the first they crop Don Maitz's artwork to within an inch of its life.

Dan
