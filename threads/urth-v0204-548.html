2002-04-07 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs list readers that Nicholas Gevers has published a short introduction to Wolfe's Sun metaseries, which he calls the Briah Cycle, in a Washington Post special science fiction issue. Gives the article's URL and notes a shorter piece on Wolfe by John Clute appearing in the same issue.
informs

My short introduction to Wolfe's Suns metaseries (the
Briah Cycle, I call it), aimed at general readers but
with some analytical content, appears in today's
WASHINGTON POST BOOK WORLD, a special SF issue. The
URL is
http://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/articles/A62473-2002apr4.html

Some interesting pieces elsewhere in the issue,
including a shorter puff for Wolfe by John Clute.

--Nick Gevers.
