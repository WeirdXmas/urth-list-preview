Urth-Whorl chronology (possible spoiler)

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-02-04 to 2001-02-04

2001whorlNicholas Gevers

2001-02-04 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes an approximate internal chronology of Wolfe's Urth/Whorl cycle, placing the Whorl novels and stories before The Book of the New Sun and listing the order of books and stories. Grounds it in the poster's earlier reading of In Green's Jungles and in what he has heard about Return to the Whorl, noting that the Whorl books may have influenced Severian.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Whorl novels precede The Book of the New Sun chronologically; Whorl books influenced Severian's career

From what I've heard about RETURN TO THE WHORL, I was
quite correct in my earlier, IGJ-based assumption that
the Whorl novels chronologically precede THE BOOK OF
THE NEW SUN in GW's overall scheme. And it seems I was
correct in thinking that the Whorl Books would serve
as some variety of influence or inspiration in the
career of Severian. Herewith an approximate internal
chronology of the Urth/Whorl Cycle (just the order of
books and stories; speculative dates are mantis's
province):

"The Boy Who Hooked the Sun"
"The God and His Man"
"Empires of Foliage and Flower"
NIGHTSIDE THE LONG SUN
LAKE OF THE LONG SUN
CALDE OF THE LONG SUN
EXODUS FROM THE LONG SUN
"The Night Chough"
ON BLUE'S WATERS
IN GREEN'S JUNGLES
RETURN TO THE WHORL
THE SHADOW OF THE TORTURER
THE CLAW OF THE CONCILIATOR
THE SWORD OF THE LICTOR
THE CITADEL OF THE AUTARCH
"These Are The Jokes"
"The Map"
"The Cat"
THE URTH OF THE NEW SUN
"The Old Woman Whose Rolling Pin Is The Sun"

--Nick Gevers.
