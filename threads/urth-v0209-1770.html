Themes: Water, the sea and drowning

2002-09-24 Jeff Veyera #

Asks where in Seawrack's dialogue in The Book of the Short Sun she mentions a great wall under the sea, as part of the Blue equals Ushas debate. Quotes the OBW passages about walls and Seawrack's 'underwater' remark, noting they are the only references found and asking whether anyone has a better one.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Blue is Ushas

First-time poster, long-time lurker.

I've followed the Blue=Ushas debate with great interest, enough so that I went back and combed SS looking for the text where Seawrack mentions the great wall under the sea.

All I could find was the following, from the opening to Chapter 8 of OBW:

"The walls, Horn. Their walls. Don't you see them?"

and

"I know places in the sea where there are walls like those," Seawrack told me. Her voice was hushed. "'Underwater' is what you say."

Does anybody have a better reference?

Teflon93
