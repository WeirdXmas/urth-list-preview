Severian's power loans

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-03 to 1998-05-03

1998urthThe Book of the New SunSara Dann

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Time loops and causality

1998-05-03 Sara Dann #

Proposes that Severian's borrowed power from the elements is repaid when he brings the New Sun, so the future shapes the past. Asks whether the energy he draws from the land is linked to the new energy source he will return, a question raised without citing any particular passage.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Severian repays borrowed elemental power with the New Sun

Assuming that he is 'borrowing' power from the elements. Would it be then that he is returing their power, that was on credit to him, with the bringing of the New Sun? Or is it completely unrelated? I suppose what I am trying to say is this, Is the reason that Severian is able to draw energy from the land (and etc...) because he is going to be able to return it with his new energy source? If this proves true then it is interesting to note that, once again, the future is shaping the past.
Xury
