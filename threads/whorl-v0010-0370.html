2000-06-21 Mark Andersen #

Proposes that Quetzal, not Crane, was behind Silk's rise to calde in the Long Sun books, while suggesting the Outsider may have manipulated both Crane and Quetzal for its own ends. Responds to Michael Straight's quoted argument, which in turn answers Alice Turner, that Trivigaunte's Crane chose Silk as a puppet for a Viron coup.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Quetzal was behind Silk becoming calde; The Outsider manipulates both Crane and Quetzal

I've always felt that it was Quetzal that was behind Silk's move into the position of calde. The arguments for Crane are rather compelling, though. Wouldn't it be intriguing if the Outsider were manipulating both for His own ends?

regards,
