Spading Moon

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-08-14 to 1999-08-14

1999urthAlice Turner

1999-08-14 Alice Turner #

Objects to the placement of the Spading Moon after the Harvest Moon in Wolfe's month sequence, arguing that spading is a spring task. Suggests a Mulching Moon after the harvest instead and notes that a Hunter's Moon falls around that time, drawing on the poster's own gardening experience.
disputes · close-textual-reading

I haven't really been following the dialog on moons and months. But, as a gardener, I object to putting the Spading Moon after the Harvest Moon. Spading is done in the spring. Even pocket gardeners like me do a good deal of spading (in my case, troweling) every spring. After the Harvest, we might do a Mulching Moon <g>. Actually, I seem to remember that a Hunter's Moon comes along about then.

-alga
