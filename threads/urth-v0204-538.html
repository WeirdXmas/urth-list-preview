Disputes the idea that Blue is Ushas and argues that Merryn is not the same kind of creature as the Cumaean, while saying the poster's own theories are plausible but not yet well enough supported by textual citation. Responds to Blattid's speculation about whether Merryn and the Cumaean are inhumi, noting that Wolfe calls inhumi aliens and that Return to the Whorl suggests inhumi live on Urth.

2002-04-03 Marc Aramini #

Argues that the Cummean in the Long Sun books is probably not an inhumu, since Wolfe called her an alien and Merryn is not one either, though Juganu's "We are everywhere!" in Return to the Whorl hints at inhumu on Urth. Grounds this in Wolfe's interview with Nick Gevers and the text, while joking about being cast as the list's villain.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: The Cummean is an inhumu

Well Blattid, I would love to assimilate the Cummean into the status of an inhumu, but (alas) I think Wolfe said she was an "alien". I'm not sure exactly what that means, but he did say for sure that Merryn was NOT an inhumu even though she had difficulty with animals in that recent interview (with Nick Gevers, correct?). Would things from the moon really be aliens ... hmm. Probably not. Juganu does indicate that there are inhumu on Urth with the statement "We are everywhere!" in Return to the Whorl, and I would like to say that the Cummean is one, but I think there might not be enough evidence for that.

Hey, "unfortunately"?! Are you saying I'm like the "bad guy" on the list? If my theories prove true is it like the Dark Side of the Force triumphing or something?! Oh well. "It's better to be feared than loved", I suppose.

Marc Aramini
