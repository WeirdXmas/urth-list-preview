2008-02-12 paul witcover replying to Matthew Groves #

Advises a fan that bringing copies of two or three of Wolfe's novels to a convention signing is fine, while five may be too many. Responds to Matthew Groves, who asked whether bringing his own Pirate Freedom copy or a different book to the signing would be poor etiquette, and notes that Wolfe is always gracious.
answers

As long as you don't go crazy, and wheel in a bag of books (which I have seen done), it's perfectly proper con etiquette to show up with copies of more than one novel for signing. Two or three is no problem. Five may be pushing it. But that's just my sense of what's appropriate. Gene is invariably gracious.

disputes

2008-02-18 Matthew Groves replying to tgoulding05 #

Recounts attending the Capricon convention in Chicago to meet Gene and Rosemary Wolfe and get two recent books signed, and thanks a list member for posting the notice of Wolfe's appearance. Explains that the convention website never listed Wolfe, so list announcements were the only way he learned of it.
informs

I managed to barely make it to Capricon in time to meet Gene and Rosemary Wolfe and have him sign *Pirate Freedom* and *Soldier of Sidon* (I decided to bring only his two most recent books to sign). I wasn't able to do anything else at the con besides browse the vendors (which was interesting); I was only able to drive up Sunday morning, arriving just minutes before his signing session ended, and then returning the same night after a bit of sightseeing in downtown Chicago. I didn't get to talk to Wolfe much, and I'm afraid I was a bit overawed anyway. My friend took some pictures of Gene while he was signing my books, and Rosemary made "rabbit ears" behind his head. I just want to thank Teri for posting the note about his appearance at the con. I never would have known about it otherwise, especially considering his name isn't even listed anywhere on the website for the convention. Since Wolfe has virtually no web presence, I really appreciate it when folks here on the list are able to share information about his appearances, and I hope people will continue to do so. I've still got a lot of books without signatures.
