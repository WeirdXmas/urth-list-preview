Asks why Severian's establishment in the Algedonic Quarter is called the House Azure, noting he says he is beginning to understand the name after the flier crash in The Citadel of the Autarch. Requests further insight or a pointer to an earlier archive discussion, since the poster cannot see the reason in the text.
questions · close-textual-reading

In "Citadel" after Severian and the Old Autarch crash in the flier, Severian recalls the time when he first met the androgyne. He also comments that he is beginning to understand why "his establishment in the Algedonic Quarter" is named the House Azure. Unfortunately, I don't preceive this reason, and would like some further insight (or at least the archive volume where it was discussed before, if this is the case). Thanks,
