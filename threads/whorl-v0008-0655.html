Staves and snakes

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1998-07-09 to 1998-07-09

1998whorlThe Book of the Long Sunmary whalen

Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Myth and folklore sources

1998-07-09 mary whalen #

Proposes that Silk's fight with Blood in Calde of the Long Sun echoes Moses and the Egyptian magicians, since Silk's staff doubles as a sword as Moses' rod became a snake. Notes that earlier list comparisons of Silk to Moses never mentioned this duel, and cites the staff-sword and Blood's Azoth as the parallel.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · source-and-allusion · theories: Silk's staff-sword parallels Moses' rod turning into a snake

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

When I looked back over the archives, I found some comparisons of Silk to Moses, but I don't think the similarity of Silk fighting Blood to Moses and the magician was mentioned. Silk has a staff that is also a sword, and Blood uses an Azoth. Silk wins and kills Blood. This was at the end of _Calde of the Long Sun_.

As the staff is also a sword, so was the stick also a snake.

prion
