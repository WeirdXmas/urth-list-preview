Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2011-02-12 Andrew Mason #

Argues that Silk's sudden prominence right after his enlightenment, and his role as a leader for Viron rather than a figure of Whorl-wide significance, show that the Outsider and the lander embryos matter more than Quetzal or the Trivigauntis. Grounds this in the scene where people kneel for a blessing while Silk talks to Blood at the start, and in the embryos being on the lander for use in the second generation.
proposes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Silk is special but not destined for Whorl-wide significance; Embryos on the lander were meant for second generation
