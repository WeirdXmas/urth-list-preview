Adds a joke about punctuation to a thread on typed marks: notes that the interabang-style combinations "!?" and "?!" are chess annotation symbols for interesting-speculative and bold-but-unsound moves. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's remarks on ASCII punctuation such as the quasi-quote and the interabang, with a playful aside about the list. Not about Wolfe's fiction.
