Proposes that the masked assassin in the Book of the New Sun was a disaffected member of the Order of Seekers for Truth and Penitence who opposed the reforms of Severian's guild. Responds to b sharp's suggestion that the assassin might be Agia or someone else, offering a different reading of the mask as a sign of the Seekers.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Masked assassin is a disaffected Seeker for Truth and Penitence

Actually, I took the mask to suggest that the assassin was a disaffected member of the Order of Seekers for Truth and Penitence who did not approve of the Autarch Severian's reform of his guild.
