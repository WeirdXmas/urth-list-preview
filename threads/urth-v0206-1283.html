2002-06-24 Peter Westlake #

Argues that the gray man in the Pajarocu letter is the monitor or computer inside the lander, citing the colour, the context, and the letter's statement that he will fly again. Responds to a reader's suggestion by agreeing and adding that the pronoun 'he' would have been 'it' if the lander were meant.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: The gray man is the monitor inside the lander

Definitely. He's the right colour, the context is right, and he says that "he" will fly again. He would have said "it" for the lander.

Spectacled Bear.
