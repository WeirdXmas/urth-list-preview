Blood's parents

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-07-13 mary whalen #

Argues that a Long Sun-era reader's question about Blood's parentage is supported by clues in The Book of the Long Sun, noting Blood asks only whether Rose and Pike are still at the manteion. Grounds the point in the text and in the shared initial letters P, R and B of Pike, Rose and Blood, which the poster calls very slight.
supports · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Blood's father was Patera Pike; Rose was Blood's mother

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

When I looked over the Whorl Archives here, I was amazed to learn that Blood's father was Patera Pike. I also read the discussion of clues in the text about this. I don't have much to add, but when I looked over the book, the first thing I noticed was that Blood only asked Silk if Rose and Pike were still at the manteion. I guess if one kept this in mind, as Wolfe would probably want a reader to, it could be a partial clue after we learn that Rose was his mother and that he knew this. Of course, I doubt that anyone could remember this or even connect it without knowledge beforehand.

An even less significant clue, which I wouldn't even mention if the author weren't Wolfe, who loves clues with names, but all three of their names begin with similar letters, each a specific one: P R B. As I said, very slight.

prion
