Lycaeus/Wolf one of zeus’ names

Urth list · 1 messages · 2018-10-01 to 2018-10-01

2018urthMarc Aramini

Themes: Myth and folklore sources, Names and their meanings

2018-10-01 Marc Aramini #

Reports that a mythology textbook note links Lycaon's name to the Greek word for wolf and to a Zeus epithet, Lycaeus, and argues that Wolfe's syncretism in the soldier series fits this, blending Ahura Mazda's light and a Zeus name into Lucius. The post cites the textbook passage on Ovid as its source and plans to use the point in a Latro write-up.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Lucius blends Ahura Mazda light and a Zeus name

I am teaching a 400 level mythology course this semester and as I was preparing to teach the chapter in our book on Ovid this textual side note caught me by surprise: ?the name [ Lycaeon] comes from the Greek word for wolf and is thought to be associated with one of Zeus? names, Lycaeus? .... wolfe?s Syncretism in the soldier series is perfect, blending the light of Ahura Mazda and another name for Zeus into the name Lucius. Damn do I need to stick this in that Latro writeup somewhere ...
