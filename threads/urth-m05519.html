The thread: Thread began with playful chatter about the second edition of the Lexicon Urthus and a Sirius Fiction web address, then collapsed into jokes after Jeff Wilson reported the list and its archives were unreachable. Ranjit Bhatnagar confirmed a hosting move had broken his site and tech support was restoring it, and the rest of the posts were mock verse and song riffs with no literary content. Near the end, stoneox17 and Matthew Keeley raised puzzle questions about Pirate Freedom: who killed Valentin, whether Lesage met Ignacio, whether Ignacio/Chris and Lesage are one person, and what caused Chris's time travel. These were posed as speculation with no textual answers, so the thread ends unresolved.
  • stoneox17: Lesage is the obvious suspect for Valentin's killing and the answers would make the plot chilling
  • Matthew Keeley: Ignacio/Chris and Lesage may be one person, and Valentin may have died by suicide or self-defense

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Doubles, twins and clones, Time loops and causality
