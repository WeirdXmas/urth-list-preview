2003-01-28 Peter Westlake #

Gathers quoted lines from Malicapata about a boy who owned the doll, Attis cutting himself, and a lost goddess, to suggest that Tina was once Lara's doll. Responds to Michael Andre-Driussi's suspicion by joking that 'the goddess at sixteen' could mean sixteen inches and offering the quotations as the evidence.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Tina was Lara's doll

[On first reading I read this as "the goddess at sixteen inches", which is apt, if not what it actually says in the story :-)
]

Anyway:

"The boy who owned her's been dead now for about eight years."

"Attis cut himself ..."
"I wanted him not to die!"

"I used to have a goddess ... And then something happened. I don't know what - something bad."

"Malicapata. Pretty sad, sir, isn't it?"

With apologies for the Subject line,

Enigmatic, though still Spectacled, Bear.
