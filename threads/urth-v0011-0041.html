1998-05-14 Patrick O'Leary #

Jokes in character as a studio executive about pitching a film with Leonardo DiCaprio, complaining about special effects and a July 99 release, and asking who Gene Wolfe is. Responds to Aga's message with no real engagement with Wolfe's fiction, only a mocking question about his track record.
chats

<< I'm sure O'Leary will back me up when I put myself
100% behind Leo, Leo, Leo.>>

Alga, Babe, slow down.

Have you checked his asking price lately? Bottom Line: We get Leo (beefed up, even) and your can kiss any special effects bye-bye.

Imagine BEN HUR without the Chariot race in the third act and you get my drift.

We wan't this sucker before the cameras come August or we can write off a July 99 release.

Besides, Leo's on this Indy quirky thing--going integrity on us. Doubt he'll do it.

By the way--who the hell is Gene Wolfe? Pulper? Pomper? Punner? Gimme the skinny on his track record, Last thing we need in a pitch is a boardroom full of Glazed eyes. Believe me, you say "Tolstoy" to Eisner and he goes smiley on ya. Been there, Done that.

HORNBLOWER
