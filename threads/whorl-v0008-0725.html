1998-08-04 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Announces a new booklet, LS-1: Characters of the Long Sun Whorl, cataloguing all 192 mortal named characters of Wolfe's Book of the Long Sun, along with a companion booklet on Jack Vance's planets. Gives page counts, prices, mailing costs and ordering instructions from Sirius Fiction, with no argument about the books.
informs

=New Products from Sirius Fiction=
(Autumn 1997)

"LS-1: Characters of the Long Sun Whorl" (1997) is the first booklet in the "LS" series, the successor to "Q&D" ["The Quick & Dirty Guide to the Long Sun Whorl" {1997; Out of Print}] in examining Gene Wolfe's THE BOOK OF THE LONG SUN. It measures 5.5" x 8.5", 34 pages. All 192 mortal named characters [including those not found on the character lists!]. Price: $5. (US domestic mail: $1; International Airmail: $3)

"Vance Space" (1997) is a booklet (by Michael Andre-Driussi) surveying a few dozen of the many planets in the science fiction of Jack Vance. From "Aerlith" of THE DRAGON MASTERS to "Zeck" of WYST: ALASTOR 1716, featuring a star map of the Oikumene and a Timeline tracking historical events of thirty novels. It measures 5.5" x 8.5", 52 pages. Price: $5. (US domestic mail: $1; International Airmail: $3)

California residents, please add $0.41 per booklet for Sales Tax and tell us which county you live in.

Send a check or money order (payable to "Sirius Fiction") to

Sirius Fiction
P.O. Box 460430
San Francisco CA 94146
USA

Thank you for your interest in "LS-1"!
