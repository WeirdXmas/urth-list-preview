The thread: Readers debated how the planets in Wolfe's Urth, Green, Blue and Yesod books relate, starting with whether Urth and Green are thematically or physically linked. Marc Aramini argued for physical contiguity based on Silk's travel and the Green city's Severian myths, while James Wynn argued that Quetzal's arrival from Green gives no evidence of traffic with Urth. Stuart Hamm asked whether the Rajan and Horn in Silk's body are the same figure, and Wynn answered yes but more complicated, noting Wolfe's own usage of the name Rajan. Lee Berman proposed that the Flood was caused by Nephilim and fallen angels, and later argued that 'one surviving world' should prompt questions about the lost worlds, which Gerry Quinn rebutted by stressing the stated count of one. The thread ended in a dispute over the plain text versus speculation, with no resolution.
  • Marc Aramini: Urth and Green are physically linked through Silk's intended travel and the Green city's Severian myths
  • James Wynn: Urth and Green are not thematically linked; Quetzal's recent arrival from Green shows no traffic with Urth
  • Stuart Hamm: asks whether the Rajan and Horn are the same figure, and whether the Rajan is something else
  • Lee Berman: the Flood was caused by Nephilim and fallen angels from Genesis, and 'one surviving world' invites asking why others did not survive
  • Gerry Quinn: the text states one surviving world, so speculation must work within that count

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Myth and folklore sources, Catholic faith and sacraments
