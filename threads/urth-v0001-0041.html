Identifies a recent F&SF story, "The Man in the Pepper Mill," as the one Joshua Solomon asked about, and says the poster will check whether God's fingers appear in it. Recalls the story as sweet and cute yet very Wolfe-like, and links its layered reality to an earlier post about Plato's cave.

Re: Joshua A. Solomon's question on recent the S&SF Wolfe story...
It's "The Man in the Pepper Mill" in the Oct/Nov issue of last year. I don't recall a refernce to God's fingers, although the phrase sounds familiar. It's a very sweet, cute story, but also a very Wolfe story. While scanning over it loooking for the Holy Digit reference, I kept thinking about mantis' post about Plato's cave. There's some of onion-layered reality.

I've set it on my bedside table to read tonight, so I'll let you know whether or not Gods fingers are mentioned.

JOEL
