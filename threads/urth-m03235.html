2007-04-09 David DiGiacomo #

Argues that a materialist reading of Wolfe's work, where religion is a pulled-over wool, is one he would be comfortable with but cannot reconcile with Wolfe's other work and beliefs. Answers a poster who said fans dislike this line of interpretation, citing Wolfe's remark that interesting art says the opposite of what it really says.

It is rather rude of you to accuse other fans of being closed minded, even with a smiley.

Personally, I would be very comfortable with such an interpretation, but I can't reconcile it with Wolfe's other work, and what I know of his life and beliefs.

Consider Wolfe's own quote: "Almost any interesting work of art comes close to saying the opposite of what it really says."
