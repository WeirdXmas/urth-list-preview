Vodalus and the Autarch

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-01-31 to 2002-01-31

2002urthThe Book of the New SunDan'l Danehy-Oakes

Themes: Power, rulers and politics

2002-01-31 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Proposes that Vodalus and the Autarch are locked in a symbiotic relationship, each serving the other beneath their public conflict, and that this continues under Severian's reign. Notes that Severian is a sworn Vodalarius and suggests the arrangement gives people an illusion of choosing sides, comparing it to Bokonon and Papa Monzano in Vonnegut's Cat's Cradle.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Vodalus and the Autarch serve each other symbiotically

Thinking about it, the relation between Vodalus and the Autarch is ... interesting. It is implied at some points
that Vodalus serves the Autarch. At others, it's implied that the A. serves V. (he's clearly a member of
V's organization). This peculiar relationship will continue in Severian's reign, as he is a sworn Vodalarius;
even after V. is replaced by Agila, I think it continues. There's this ongoing dynamism - a conflict between
the tyrannical government and the rebels in the forest, on the surface, but underneath they're engaged in an
actively symbiotic relationship. Perhaps this gives the people the illusion of a choice of sides?

Oddly though, the relationship reminds me a great deal of the relationship between Bokonon and
"Papa" Monzano in Vonnegut's CAT'S CRADLE...

--Blattid
