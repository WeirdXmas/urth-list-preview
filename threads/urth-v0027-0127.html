Wolfe piece in Vance book

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-07-06 to 1999-07-06

1999urthJonathan Laidlow

1999-07-06 Jonathan Laidlow #

Informs the list that a new critical book on Jack Vance, edited by A.E. Cunningham, includes an essay by Gene Wolfe alongside pieces by Dan Simmons, Dave Langford and Harlan Ellison. Gives the title, publisher and a web link for ordering details, noting it was spotted in a UK newsgroup discussion.
informs

Okay folks,
I'm making a brief return to posting to tell you about this.

Was scanning thru a small UK newsgroup on SF and noticed a discussion of Jack Vance referring to a new book due any day now about him. It has essays by Dan Simmons, Dave Langford, Harlan Ellison, an Gene Wolfe.

see this website (part of a larger website on Vance)
http://www.massmedia.com/~mikeb/jvm/acunningham2.html

Title: JACK VANCE: Critical Appreciations and a Bibliography Editor: A.E. Cunningham
Publisher: The British Library 1999.

Prices and ordering instructions are on the web-page.

Jonathan
