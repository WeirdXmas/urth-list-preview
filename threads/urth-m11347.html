The thread: Son of Witz opened by arguing that Severian's foes in The Book of the New Sun are masks for classic denials of God, and that overcoming them aligns him with the Increate. Lee Berman countered that the enemies oppose the New Sun rather than the Increate, proposing Abaia, Erebus, Vodalus and his followers, and Agia as foes, and suggested retitling the thread. James Wynn agreed that the Increate's enemies matter only through human followers, comparing them to the Storm King in An Evil Guest. Jeff Wilson pointed to the film The Rapture on how one could harm God, while Matthew Weber and Son of Witz spent much of the thread on social banter about surnames, so the substantive exchange ended without resolution.
  • Son of Witz: foes are masks for denials of God and God's gifts, and overcoming them aligns Severian with the Increate
  • Lee Berman: enemies oppose the New Sun, not the Increate, so the title should change; Vodalus and his followers count as foes
  • James Wynn: the Increate's enemies matter only through human followers, not as powers in themselves
  • Jeff Wilson: The Rapture raises how one could harm God, the only doctrinally accurate way to do so

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Gnosticism, heresy and other theologies, Names and their meanings

2010-12-03 Son of Witz replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that the foes Severian faces in The Book of the New Sun are masks for classic denials of God and God's gifts, and that overcoming them aligns him with the Increate. Cites Severian's return of the claw to the Pelerines after Baldanders, and his attempt to bring Master Ash back to them, as signs he is on the right path.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: Foes of the New Sun are metaphors for denials of God; Pelerines are a remnant of the Conciliator's church; Master Ash winks out because his future is nil

Good points Lee.

That note was originally called Foes of the New Sun.
I changed it because I'm trying to expose the metaphor behind the masks. All of these types of foes represent classic denials of God and God's gifts. By overcoming them, and breaking all alegiences, he aligns himself with the increate. It is significant that after Baldanders, he finally finds the Pelerines and makes good on his promise to return the claw. I'm convinced the Pelerines are a standin for the remnants of a catholic style church of the Conciliator. So then, when Severian tries to bring Master Ash back to them, we get a clear indicator that he is now on the right path. He winks out of existance because the probability of his future is now nil, or next to nil.

2010-12-03 Matthew Weber replying to Son of Witz #

Explains that the suffix "-witz" is a Germanized form of Slavic suffixes such as "-vic" and "-wicz" meaning "son of" or "family of", and jokingly suggests Lee Berman is therefore "Son of son". Responds to Lee Berman's post about his Bennewitz surname and his joke about being "Son of Witz", offering a light etymological aside rather than any reading of Wolfe's texts.
informs

"-witz" is a Germanized version of the Slavic suffix spelled variously as "-vic," "-wicz," "-vich," etc. It means "son of" or "family of" or "clan of".

So I suppose that makes you "Son of son"? :)

Argues that the thread title should be changed to "Foes of the New Sun" rather than "Foes of the Increate", since the enemies in Witz's progeny oppose the New Sun rather than the Increate. Proposes that Abaia, Erebus, Vodalus and his followers, and Agia count as enemies, citing Vodalus's pelagic code words and his ties to the Ascian war effort.

Well, its too late now. ;- ). I'd hoped to put in a plea to change the Subject to "Foes of the New Sun". My image of a foe of the Increate is a guy pulling out a knife and attempting to stab every rock, tree, grain of sand, molecule of air and cell in his own body..and then the stars....Surely the Increate loves all his creations equally, be they friend or foe to the New Sun or even Himself (if that were possible).

I really think all of Witz Progeny's enemies can be more specifically characterized as opposing the New Sun. None of them seem to be around on Ushas anyway (good reason to exclude Juturna).

By mentioning the Ascians, I think Witz sufficiently includes Abaia and Erebus. Those big fat asses don't do anything themselves but only work through their agents. Which should probably include Vodalus and his followers (eventually including Agia) as enemies of the New Sun. Vodalus wants to move humanity back to a former time of glory, not forward. His "pelagic" secret code words suggest their connection to the sea monsters as does their association with the Ascian war effort.
