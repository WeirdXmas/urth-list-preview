Severn and Severian

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-03-17 to 1998-03-17

1998-03-17 C R Culver #

Asks whether the name of painter Joseph Severn, who attended the dying poet Keats in Rome, connects to Severian, noting Severn's comfort of Keats parallels Severian's comforting of the doomed Thecla in the Matachin Tower. Also recommends Dan Simmons's Hyperion books for their treatment of the Severn-Keats relationship.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: Severian's name echoes Joseph Severn

The name of the artist Joseph Severn, who was living with famous English poet John Keats when he died in Rome, looks a little bit like Severian. The only parallel I can see now is that Severn comforted the dying Keats whilst Severian comforted the doomed Thecla during her stay in the Matachin Tower. Does anyone think there is any connection?

Incidentally, I should take this moment to recommend Dan Simmon's Hyperion books. They are most interesting in their spin on the Severn-Keats relationship.
