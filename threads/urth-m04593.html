2008-08-13 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to Kieran Mullen #

Explains that 'anal retentive' takes a hyphen as a compound adjective but not as a noun phrase, jokingly picking a nit in reply to Kieran Mullen's question about correcting others. Illustrates the rule with two example phrases and a playful aside about shouting 'Movie' in a crowded firehouse; it is off-topic banter, not about Wolfe.
chats

Kieran,

Since you asked,

"Anal retentive" should be hyphenated when used as a compound adjective -- "Oh, that's
just my anal-retentive tendencies" -- but not when used as a noun phrase -- "He is SUCH
an anal retentive."

--Dan'l "is it okay to shout Movie in a crowded firehouse?" Danehy-Oakes

2008-08-13 Kieran Mullen #

Jokingly defends the practice of correcting others' spelling and usage, mocking the idea that understanding a message makes correction unnecessary by parodying misspellings. Responds to Mark's question about why he insists on others conforming to his standards, noting that Millman's corrections were brief and taught him unfamiliar words worth using correctly.
chats

On Wednesday night I read:

And I immediately thought:

"Eye suppoze ewer rite. If ewe can sea watt peephole are saying itz knot raleigh kneaded two poynt out tooth em that they are rung."

Mr. Millman may have been a bit exasperated in his corrections, but they were short and to the point. I learned something from them. We should aspire to use these unfamiliar words correctly.

Kieran "Should anal-retentive be hyphenated?" Mullen

2008-08-14 Dave Tallman replying to Kieran Mullen #

Replies to Kieran Mullen that he did not mind being corrected about his use of an unfamiliar word, though he would have preferred feedback on the substance of his post. Says he tries to use words correctly and that using "inhumi" in a gender-neutral sense was a grammatical stretch, offering no argument about the books themselves.
chats

As the corrected poster, I would like to say that I didn't mind too much. I would rather have had feedback on the substance of my post, but it was an interesting response anyway and it showed me that someone was reading and paying attention. I do attempt to use words correctly and the gender-neutral use of "inhumi" was a bit of a grammatical stretch.
