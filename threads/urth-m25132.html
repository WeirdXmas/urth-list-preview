Short Story 71: Silhouette Mephisto/Johann limp

Urth list · 1 messages · 2014-08-03 to 2014-08-03

2014urthMarc Aramini

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2014-08-03 Marc Aramini #

Asks why the lame figure in "Silhouette" is Johann rather than Mephistopheles, given Goethe's Faust, and whether the story's imagery and the diabolists' scrying of Johann's name complicate its moral picture. Raises further questions about whether the silhouette is spreading infection from the planet and whether the apparent good guys are really bad.
questions · source-and-allusion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Johann is Mephistopheles figure in Silhouette; Silhouette infects galaxy from Neuerddraht

Even though Borski loves to assert Goethe's Faust is everywhere in Wolfe's fiction, it really is everywhere in Silhouette. But why is it Johann who has a limp in the story when it is Mephistopholes in Faust? Does this complicate his imagery? Is he taking his silhouette off the planet of neuerddraht to infect the galaxy? Algol has such a bad astronomical reputation, and the images of a civilization which drowned in sand and perished on the planet are a bit apocalyptic. Are the seeming good guys in this bad, in light of the fact that the diabolists keep scrying Johann's name rather than, say, Satan's?
