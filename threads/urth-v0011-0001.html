Themes: Power, rulers and politics

1998-05-12 Charles Dye #

Argues that the carved mountain figures Severian sees are Autarchs rather than mere monarchs, and that Typhon started the fad of carving rulers into mountains. Grounds this in a quoted passage from Urth of the New Sun about unshaped mountains and in Typhon's ego, and replies to Dan'l Danehy Oakes's claim that they are monarchs.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: The carved mountains depict Autarchs, started by Typhon

They're Autarchs. Urth, chapter 38:

"All around us ragged, faceless peaks loomed through pent clouds. We were among mountains, but mountains that had not yet put on the carven likenesses of men and women -- such unshaped mountains, then, as are to be seen in the oldest pictures."

Typhon appears to have started this charming little fad. It's certainly consistent with his colossal (Satanic?) ego. Also note the unstated implication that such mountains are not to be found *outside* of old pictures ....

Given his background, and his role model as First Autarch, it's astonishing that Ymar came within an "Almost" of Just!
