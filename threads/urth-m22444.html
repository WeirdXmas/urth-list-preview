The thread: Stephen Case opened a thread on Charles Williams, mentioning how Williams trained his taste for R. A. Lafferty, and lamented that an earlier discussion of a Gene Wolfe biography had gone nowhere. Gwern Branwen replied that a biography needs funding for years of research and Wolfe's cooperation, so enthusiasm alone is insufficient. Marc Aramini said he has long corresponded with Wolfe but would not write a biography, since biography does not interest him, and noted Wolfe's private, personal exchanges. Larry Miller added that a video documentary would need interviews with Wolfe, which have never been arranged. The thread ended without resolution, with the biography idea stalled on practical obstacles.

  • Gwern Branwen: a Wolfe biography is impractical without funding and Wolfe's cooperation
  • Marc Aramini: has corresponded with Wolfe for years but has no interest in writing a biography

2012-12-17 Marc Aramini replying to Gwern Branwen #

Reports that the poster has roughly fifteen years of correspondence with Wolfe but would not feel comfortable writing a biography of him, since biography does not interest him as a writer. Responds to Gwern Branwen's remark that a biography needs funding and a cooperative subject, adding that Wolfe seems approachable but private and that recent exchanges were personal rather than about his art.
answers · author-and-biography

I have about a a decade and a half worth of correspondence with Wolfe but would not feel comfortable taking on a task like that. Biography doesn't interest me as a writer, and much of the last eight years communication has been personal in nature, less about his art. He strikes me as approachable but private. 2012-12-17 Stephen Case #

Recounts that reading Charles Williams trained the poster to appreciate R. A. Lafferty, names three Williams novels read and says Descent into Hell is favourite. Also laments that a recent thread on a possible Gene Wolfe biography went nowhere and asks for more substantive input.
informs

I've said that reading Williams was what taught/trained me to be able to read and appreciate Lafferty. I've read GREATER TRUMPS, PLACE OF THE LION, and DESCENT INTO HELL, and I think I like the last of these the best.

Also: I'm disappointed the thread a couple weeks ago about the potentiality of a Wolfe biography never went anywhere. Surely someone has something more to say about this than simply how much we'd all love one.

-Steve

2012-12-17 Gwern Branwen replying to Stephen Case #

Argues that a Wolfe biography is hard to produce because it needs funding for years of research and Wolfe's cooperation, and that mailing-list enthusiasm alone is not enough motivation. Replies to Stephen Case's complaint that an earlier thread on a possible Wolfe biography went nowhere, offering practical objections rather than new ideas.
disputes · author-and-biography

A biography is hard work: who will fund the years of work for a really good one? How would one convince Wolfe to cooperate and that one isn't a flake?

Anyone who needs some emails on a mailing list to motivate them probably shouldn't be doing it in the first place.
