Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2000-08-10 Christopher Culver #

Disputes the claim that Wolfe dismissed every suspicion about Weer in Peace, arguing that in an interview Wolfe left it to readers to decide whether Weer killed the librarian. Responds to Pat Nut.'s point that Wolfe rejected the multiple-murderer theory by noting that he did not call suspicion of the single killing nonsense.
disputes · author-and-biography · theories: Weer killed the librarian

<Dire interpretations of Wolfe events are tempting, but recall how often they have been wrong. Weer in *Peace* was supposed to be a multiple murderer, according to some, until Wolfe said that was nonsense.>

Wolfe may have said that about the *multiple* murderer hypothesis, but in one interview he said the reader would have to figure out for himself if Weer killed the librarian, and he didn't say that suspicion of that would be nonsense.

Christopher CULVER <[email]>
