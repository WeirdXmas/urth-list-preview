2000-03-26 Nicholas Gevers #

Lists Millennium's full 2000 Fantasy Masterworks schedule month by month, including Wolfe's Shadow and Claw in March and Sword and Citadel in December. Supplements Jesper's earlier announcement with the rest of the year's titles, giving no commentary or analysis.
informs

Just to fill out Jesper's information about
Millennium's Fantasy Masterworks Series, here's their
full 2000 schedule:

March:
Lord Dunsany, TIME AND THE GODS (584-page collection)
Gene Wolfe, SHADOW AND CLAW
April:
E. R. Eddison, THE WORM OUROBOROS
Jack Vance, TALES OF THE DYING EARTH (all 4 books)
May:
John Crowley, LITTLE, BIG
June:
Robert E. Howard, THE CONAN CHRONICLES
July:
M. John Harrison, VIRICONIUM (complete omnibus)
August:
Roger Zelazny, CHRONICLES OF AMBER
September:
De Camp and Pratt, THE COMPLEAT ENCHANTER
October:
Jonathan Carroll, THE LAND OF LAUGHS
November:
Hope Mirrlees, LUD-IN-THE-MIST
December:
Gene Wolfe, SWORD AND CITADEL
