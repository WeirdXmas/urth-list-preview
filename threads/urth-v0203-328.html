Lune, leagues and miles

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-03-11 to 2002-03-11

2002urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

2002-03-11 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Explains that the "fifty thousand leagues" in Jerry Friedman's quoted passage from Wolfe's text equals 150,000 miles, since a league is three miles. Grounds this in an American Heritage Dictionary definition of league, and closes with an advertisement for Sirius Fiction booklets on Wolfe and John Crowley.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: fifty thousand leagues equals 150,000 miles

Jerry Friedman quoted and queried

". . . the Forests of Lune, fifty thousand leagues away" (III, ch. 32, para. 4).

"LEAGUE, a unit of distance equal to three miles" (AMERICAN HERITAGE DICTIONARY).
