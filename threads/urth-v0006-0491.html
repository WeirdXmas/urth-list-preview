Cumaean's Star

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-02-01 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that the Cumaean's star in The Claw of the Conciliator is really Fomalhaut, the Fish's Mouth, rather than a metaphor for the White Fountain, and that the target of the ftl telephone call is that star. Responds to Christopher and Nutria by noting Nutria raises posthistory points the poster made earlier and challenging Nutria to produce a timeline.
proposes · source-and-allusion · puzzle-solving · theories: The Cumaean's star is Fomalhaut, the Fish's Mouth

Christopher,

The Cumaean might be addressing the White Fountain in a metaphoric manner, but on the surface the real star she is talking about is the Fish's Mouth, aka Fomalhaut (or "Formalhaut" for epopts of the typos--you know who you are). The target of their ftl (faster than light) telephone call attempt; once again, unobstructed line of sight is required.

Nutria,

You are raising most of the points about posthistory that I raised in "Additions, Errata, &cetera: volume 2," which I applaud. Now all that remains is for you to whip up the timeline that you believe strongly enough to take the whipping for! Go to! Look forward to seeing it!

=mantis=
