Short Story 161: The Walking Sticks

2018urthRobert Pirkola

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2018-06-01 Robert Pirkola #

Proposes a link between the Wolfe story "The Walking Sticks" and the witch Thomas Weir, whose walking stick with a carved human head was said to walk ahead of him. Grounds the idea in a Wikipedia account of Weir, which the poster came across while reading Rosemary's Baby.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Thomas Weir's walking stick is a source for The Walking Sticks

I was reading *Rosemary's Baby* recently and bumped into a reference to one Thomas Weir, an infamous witch. There was an interesting tale associated with him that I thought relevant to the GW short story "The Walking Sticks". It is said that he "derived his power from his walking stick, topped by a carved human head, giving rise to later accounts that it had often been seen parading down the street in front of him." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Weir.
