mysteries of the long sun

2011urthThe Book of the Long SunThe Book of the New SunGerry QuinnMarc AraminiLee Berman
The thread: Readers asked what puzzles and mysteries are left in the Long Sun books, especially in Nightside the Long Sun, including a patch of skin, the bird's name Hyrax, the statue of Catamitus, and whether a numeric string is meaningful. Marc Aramini listed the puzzles and argued Lake of the Long Sun is strongest, but later called the number meaningless. Lee Berman pointed to Lake's mysteries and the unsolved numbers on the Seal of Pas as the best puzzle, while Gerry Quinn agreed on Lake and dismissed the number as random noise. The thread ended in lighthearted disagreement, with Berman naming Christ's presence in the series as the main point of dispute with Aramini.
  • Marc Aramini: lists unexplained details in Nightside and calls a numeric string meaningless
  • Lee Berman: Lake holds richer mysteries and the Seal of Pas numbers are the best puzzle
  • Gerry Quinn: agrees Lake is strongest, dismisses the number as random low-entropy string
  • Lee Berman: Jesus Christ's presence is the main disagreement; Green is an analog to Urth

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Christ figures and messiahs, Names and their meanings

2011-10-27 Marc Aramini replying to Lee Berman #

Lists several puzzles in Nightside the Long Sun for a reader who feels he missed things: a patch of skin on the roof, why the bird is called Hyrax, Mint's concern for Auk, and the statue of Catamitus beside Hyacinth in the brothel. Also asks whether Silk's hint at being split in two at the end is symbolic, and whether the catamite association is intended.
questions · source-and-allusion · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk has been split in two at the end

Okay, I think these mysteries are from Nightside: the patch of skin on the roof, why the bird is called Hyrax, why Mint cares about Auk, why the statue of Catamitus is near Hyacinth in the brothel (just because of Musk or because of Hyacinthus man love? hmmm) Why Silk speculates that he has been split in two at the very end, Silk and nightside silk. Is this just his attitude or is it symbolic talk? I think Long Sun is actually harder to get to the bottom of than it first appears. The catamite association with Hy is some disturbing stuff. Unintentional or not? HMMMM.

Replies to Larry Miller's question about overlooked puzzles in Nightside the Long Sun, suggesting the richer mysteries lie in Lake the Long Sun, such as Scylla and the bodies in storage. Points to the unsolved numbers on the Seal of Pas as the best mystery, while also judging the series' arc to fade after Nightside.

2011-10-28 Lee Berman replying to Lee Berman #

Playfully responds to Marc Aramini's joke that Lee Berman and Gerry are one person, and names Jesus Christ's presence in the Sun series as the main point of disagreement with Aramini. Also restates his view that Green is an analog to Urth, not literally Urth, and that Citadel towers may reach Green.
chats · theories: Green is an analog to Urth, not literally Urth; Blue is an analog to Ushas

Heh, funny, since I usually agree with you Marc! I think the only real point of difference between us is the presence or absence of Jesus Christ in the Sun Series. Other disagreements (including on Father Inire) mostly stem from that, I think. (a similar dynamic applies to James Wynn and I). Otherwise, I think we all tend to think alike.

Oh, well, also I guess we disagree on whether "Green is Urth!". I think it is absolutely great that you shared that idea with us. I just tend to take it less literally, i.e. I think Green is that dual planetary system's analog to Urth as Blue is analog to Ushas. (Though it may be true that certain towers in Nessus, notable the Citadel's, extend through space/dream/time all the way to Green.)

Heh, also funny! I suppose it would be a waste of time to deny Gerry and I are the same person since a truth teller and a liar would both do that. But for those who trust me I will assure that Gerry and I are not the same human being.
