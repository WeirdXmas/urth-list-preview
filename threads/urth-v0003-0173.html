Themes: Myth and folklore sources

1997-07-21 Ron Crown #

Admits a mistaken link between the mother and Mariolatry, then argues the story is not a simple allegory of the Catholic Church and proposes Arthurian death legends, especially the island and cave combination, as a source. Grounds this on the name check showing the mother is not called Maria and on the thin but suggestive Arthurian parallel, and asks whether others see Castleview connections.
proposes · source-and-allusion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Arthurian death legends as source for island and cave

Yeah, I guess I took the "mama dominating" part and made a mental leap to Mama=Mary(ia) without checking to see if she is actually called Maria in the story, bu I checked and she isn't. Oh well.

In any case, the story doesn't appear to me to be simply reducible to an allegory of the Catholic church (as nothing Wolfe does is reducible to simple allegory; C.S. Lewis or G.K. Chesterton he ain't).

Let me somewhat hesitantly make another suggestion for "source" material; the Arthur death legends or a conflation thereof in the island/cave combination. Pretty thin, I know, but some have suggested PEACE connections and I was wondering if anyone has noticed any connections with CASTLEVIEW.

Ron Crown
