Themes: Power, rulers and politics

2003-08-21 PMorris33 #

Recalls the scene in The Book of the New Sun where petitioners are stuck in an anteroom living on coffee and pastries, and notes that Severian ends the practice when he becomes Autarch. Expresses how strongly the scene has stayed with the poster, without offering an interpretation or evidence beyond the scene itself.

Who could forget the endless diet of coffee and pastries of those trapped people in the anteroom? And how Severian abolished the practice and freed the generations of petitioners when he became the Ruler?

That scene has haunted me.
