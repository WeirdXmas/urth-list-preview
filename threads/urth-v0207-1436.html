Wolfe, Winchester Mystery House, Worldcon 2002

Urth list · 2 messages · 2002-07-18 to 2002-07-18

2002-07-18 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Reports that the poster asked Gene Wolfe whether he would join a tour of the Winchester Mystery House during Worldcon, and that Wolfe has visited before and recommends it. Gives the logistics: Wolfe is interested but may go alone because his wife cannot manage wheelchair access, so he cannot commit yet.
informs

Update: I saw in the progress report for Con Jose that they are organizing a tour of the fabled Winchester Mystery House. I had not realized that access was so restrictive, I thought people could just drop in any time, pay the money, go inside, which shows how little planning I had done.

Anyway, I decided to let the cat out of the bag and ask Gene Wolfe if he'd ever been, if he would like to go while at Worldcon, and if he would, would he rather go on the organized tour or on a separate one.

He has been there before (D'oh!) and he recommends it. He is interested in going while at Worldcon, but as his wife would not likely be able to go (wheelchair access issues), he would probably have to go without her and cannot commit as yet.

So that's the latest. I'm trying to sign up for the tour, myself.

=mantis=

2002-07-18 Josh Geller #

Confirms that the Winchester Mystery House can be visited without advance booking for small groups, and proposes an Urth list tour of the house during Worldcon. Describes the author's many visits, the accessible garden, and its datura and other plants with interesting reputations, noting Sarah Winchester's learning.
proposes

This is, in fact, the case.

On the other hand, if you have a very large group, it might be advisable to make advance arrangements.

I'm taking (another) group there next week: there are only perhaps ten of us, and we will just be showing up at the gate.

Great news that Wolfe will be at Worldcon.

I've been to Winchester House perhaps a dozen times. I'd have to say that it is one of my favorite places in the World.

Sarah Winchester was a very interesting, very learned woman.

The garden is completely accessible, and of major interest (also free). It may surprise some people to learn (althoughthough it did not surprise me ) that Mrs. Winchester has several varieties of datura growing there, as well as a number of other plants with interesting reputations.

I say we make an Urth list tour, whether or not GW himself can participate.

.
