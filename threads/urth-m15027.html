Found in Central NJ

The thread: Posters in this short thread traded sightings of copies of The Sorcerer's House in central New Jersey stores before its official release. Fred Kiesche reported finding copies on a shelf, noting the count had dropped, and contrasted this with publishers who enforce strict on-sale dates. Matthew Keeley described driving twenty-five miles because no local independent carried the book and big-box stores stock too few good titles. The thread stayed a casual exchange of availability news, with no argument about the novel and no clear resolution.
  • Fred Kiesche: copies were found early on shelves, a lucky break unlike publishers' strict on-sale dates

2010-03-16 Fred Kiesche replying to Gwern Branwen #

Reports that the poster found copies of The Sorcerer's House on shelves at a big-box bookstore before its official release, contrasting this with publishers that enforce strict on-sale dates. Responds to Gwern Branwen's note that a Princeton store had copies of the new book and that his mail-order preorder was delayed, saying the poster got lucky.

With the big box stores you can sometimes luck out. If a publisher has a very firm "do not sell by" policy (for example, the "Harry Potter" books), you can't find it for love or money until everybody can display it. Other publishers are not so firm. So I lucked out!
