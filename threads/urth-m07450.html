WindyCon 35

Urth list · 21 messages · 2008-09-30 to 2008-10-28

2008urthThe Book of the New SunJohn WatkinsMatthew KeeleyJosh GellerNigel PriceJames WynnStuart HammJames Jordan
The thread: Readers on the Urth list mostly used this thread to plan trips to WindyCon 35, a 2008 convention where Gene Wolfe appeared. Nigel Price, who was attending from England, posted about hotels, registration and panel schedules, and others asked about recordings and whether they could make it. The main substantive thread was a panel on post-historic warfare in the Urth books, which Price and Matthew Keeley promoted and which James Wynn and others asked about. The discussion mainly drifted into logistics, personal finances and off-topic chat, ending with Price planning to record Wolfe's panels for Ultan's Library.
  • Nigel Price: promoted WindyCon 35 and the Urth warfare panel, and planned to record Wolfe's sessions
  • Matthew Keeley: wished Wolfe travelled more and asked for a transcript or video of the panel
  • brunians: shared experience of attending Wolfe appearances and riffed on child soldiers and Ender's Game
  • James Wynn: asked for the panel's scheduled time to decide which day to attend

Themes: War and soldiers, Books, libraries and writing

2008-10-20 Nigel Price replying to Nigel Price #

Announces the WindyCon 35 programme, including a Saturday panel on post-historic warfare in the Urth cycle with Wolfe and Michael Andre-Driussi, and a Friday panel on child soldiers where Wolfe appears alongside Ender's Game discussion. Lists panel times and mentions the poster's own Sunday panel on Bujold's Vorkosigan stories.
informs

The WindyCon organisers have now published a detailed programme for the weekend of 14-16 November.

The panel with Gene Wolfe, Michael Andre-Driussi and I in which we will be discussing "Post-Historic Warfare" in the Urth cycle is at 12 noon on Saturday.

Wolfe is also appearing on the following panel on Friday evening at 4 pm:

Child Soldiers ? The award-winning Ender's Game, originally published in 1977, changed SF warfare forever. The story of an unwitting child soldier, it takes place in the far future where children make the best soldiers. There are children serving as soldiers today, but is the future really different? How did Ender's world develop and could ours develop that way too?
R. Karp, R. Neumeier, J. Ringo, F. Ruiz, G. Wolfe

As an aside, and for what it's worth, I'm also on a panel at 10 o'clock on Sunday morning in which we'll be discussing the Miles Vorkosigan stories of Lois McMaster Bujold.

Really looking forward to all this!

Nigel

In reply to [email]'s previous message in this thread...
