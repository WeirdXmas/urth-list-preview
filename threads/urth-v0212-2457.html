2002-12-17 Don Doggett #

Argues that Marc should ask Wolfe's permission before posting what Wolfe privately told him, since Wolfe, a shrewd 70-year-old, probably knew such information could circulate. Says the unresolved 'it is/it isn't' debate blocks thematic discussion, and jokingly suggests that if permission is refused Marc should keep the secret.
proposes

Hi everyone,

I'm no ethicist (no jab intended Crush) but Gene Wolfe is a wiley intelligent 70 year old man and he certainly knew that if he told Marc something there was a chance it would get out. I doubt he cared since he didn't say "don't tell this but . . ." And if he really cared he simply wouldn't have told. He might have even told because he wants the discussion to move on to more subtle aspects of the books. I mean, the simple truth is that little can be resolved thematically as long as this "it is" "it isn't" debate is in the air. I say Marc should ask if he can post the information and if he can great and if not, he should commit seppuku and let the secret die with him.
have a happy day
Don
