1998-10-18 Alice Turner #

Proposes that Palaemon's slip about Thrax's location in Shadow of the Torturer hints he was exiled from the torturers' guild for a youthful offense and later reconciled through a senior advocate. Suggests the same route could explain how Palaemon acquired Terminus Est, a sword suited to a journeyman torturer, answering an earlier question about its origin.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Palaemon was exiled from the torturers' guild in youth; Palaemon obtained Terminus Est the way he regained standing

A megatherium speaks (I believe that is what Mr. Borski invoked, though he spelled it wrong):

I have always taken Palaemon's slip in Shadow XIII--asked where Thrax is, he says "Down Gyoll. Near the sea," when in fact it is in the opposite direction--to mean that he has something of a past, that he may have been exiled from the guild to that place for something similar in his youth, and that he made it up with the help of a senior advocate, as he hopes Sev will. If that is so, perhaps he acquired the sword, which is certainly suitable for a journeyman torturer, in somewhat the same way. I'd like to think so, anyway.

-alga
