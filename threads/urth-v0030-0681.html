Did Wolfe write this short story???

Urth list · 3 messages · 2001-08-30 to 2001-08-31

2001urthNicholas GeversPMorris33Michael Andre-Driussi
The thread: A poster asked whether Gene Wolfe wrote a short story recalled from a Dozois Year's Best anthology, about an embittered young woman in a refugee camp fleeing a future of terror, enslavement and torture, and asked for the author's name if it was not Wolfe. Michael Andre-Driussi said the story did not ring a bell as Wolfe or as a Year's Best piece and asked for the publication date to narrow the search. Nicholas Gevers answered that the story was Michael Swanwick's "Radiant Doors" (1998) and not by Wolfe. The thread ended with the question resolved by a single answer.
  • PMorris33: asked whether Wolfe wrote the remembered refugee-camp story and requested the author's name
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: doubted it was a Wolfe or Year's Best story and asked for the publication date
  • Nicholas Gevers: identified the story as Michael Swanwick's "Radiant Doors" and said it is not by Wolfe

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Memory and forgetting
