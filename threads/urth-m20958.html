2011-10-09 Andrew Mason replying to larry miller #

Argues that the megatherian monsters of Urth and Blue may share a common origin, linking the Mother on Blue with Scylla on Urth as sisters. Supports this with the bean story suggesting the monsters arrived from space, either from Blue or from a third planet.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Mother on Blue and Scylla are sisters; Urth monsters came from space

Some people do find it controversial whether the name 'megatherians' refers to the monsters (Erebus, Abaia and co.), but that's by the by.

As James says, there is definitely a connection between the Mother on Blue and Scylla on Urth - I think they are said to be sisters. There are indications, notably the bean story, that the monsters on Urth came from space; this may mean either that they came from Blue, or that they came to both Urth and Blue from a third planet.
