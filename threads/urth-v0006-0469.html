1998-01-30 Dave Lebling #

Disputes the estimate of about a thousand years between Typhon's reign and Severian's, arguing that the carved rulers' mountains need far more time or that the Sword of the Lictor and Urth of the New Sun are inconsistent. Reasons that if each autarch carved a mountain and reigned ten years, only about a hundred mountains fit into that span, citing Typhon's mountain and the Conciliator meeting in Urth.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Gap between Typhon and Severian is about 10,000 years; Inconsistency between Book of the New Sun and Urth

This is a mantic or even lupine opinion have a lot of trouble with. Primarily, I look at the mountains, all or most of which have been carved in the likenesses of rulers. In _Sword_, we are told (as a guess by Severian) that Mount Typhon must have been the first of them, and (I think) this is confirmed in _Urth_, when Severian-as-Conciliator meets Typhon. So we have a space of 1000 years in which the entire future-Andes have been carved. Every ruler post-Typhon is an autarch: did they each carve a mountain? If each reigned 10 years, we only have time for 100 mountains, assuming that they _all_ did one...

My suspicion is that there is either more time between Typhon and Severian than 1000 years (more like 10,000?), or there's an inconsistency between _TBONS_ and _Urth_.

-vizcacha

1998-01-30 Dave Lebling #

Argues that the time between Typhon and Severian must be far longer than two thousand years, citing geological and archaeological evidence and a proposed era of starflight that resculpted Urth. Responds to Vizcacha's suspicion and to a report of Wolfe's statement about two chiliads, saying the geology and archaeology cannot fit that span.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Urth's geology was resculpted by starflight-era power; Time between Typhon and Severian far exceeds two chiliads

CRCulver opines:

Even Jove nods.

Unless someone (Dr. Talos? mantis?) is faking the geological and archeological evidence, there's a _lot_ more time than that.

The only out I see for the geology is that during the era of starflight (as described in the "Pandora's Box" story -- remarkably like Cordwainer Smith's future, by the way), there was enough power and energy to resculpt the entire face of Urth, and someone did.

The only out I see for the archeology is that a Trantor-like world-city grew and then died before someone started twiddling the geography.

But I still don't see this happening in two chiliads.

-vizcacha
([email])
