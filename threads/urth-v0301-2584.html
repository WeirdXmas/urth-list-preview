2003-01-02 Michael Straight #

Jokingly argues that the colonists on Blue lost the technology for silk underwear, suggesting Horn may have kept some from the Whorl, and that a blurred figure in the lower corner of the picture resembles Babbie more than the cover of On Blue's Waters does. Responds to Andrew Bollen's complaint about the book's cover art and his joke about an old man's head on the fisherman, playing along with the image's details.
chats

I think the colonists on Blue had lost the technology to make silk boxer shorts. I suppose it's possible Horn had some from the Whorl and kept them all these years because they reminded him of You-Know-Who.

On the other hand, that brown blur in the lower right corner looks more like my mental picture of Babbie than the one on the cover of OBW.

-Rostrum

2003-01-03 Andrew Bollen #

Shares a web link to a Victorian painting of a sea-woman luring a fisherman, presented as an image of Seawrack or her sister, along with Goethe's ballad "Der Fischer" that apparently inspired it. Wishes the Overlook edition of On Blue's Waters had used this picture as its cover instead of the artwork it did use, joking about putting an old man's head on the fisherman.
