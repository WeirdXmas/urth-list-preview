2007-06-09 Joshua A. Solomon #

Asks whether a character ends up living in a cave at the close of The Changeling, either the changeling himself or the narrator, noting the island setting and three upside-down pretend swords outside that recall the three crosses at Golgotha in paintings. Offers the image as a fresh item for a list of caves in Wolfe's work, without making an argument.
