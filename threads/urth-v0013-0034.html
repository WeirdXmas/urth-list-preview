1998-06-12 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Replies to Spectacled Bear with a playful jab, recalling that Severian feared he would hit his head in the tunnel from the dripping mouth entrance to the chamber, and mocks the idea that a tower could walk through it. The post is a light, joking exchange about the mine shaft passage in The Book of the New Sun rather than a serious reading.
chats

Spectacled Bear,

That's funny, I thought Severian was afraid he would brain himself in his passage through the tunnel from the dripping mouth entrance to that point where it audibly opens up into a chamber.

Yet you tell me a tower can walk through it--amazing! Just goes to show how wrong you can be.

=mantis=

1998-06-12 Peter Westlake #

Playfully suggests ways a walking tower could emerge from the mine tunnels in The Book of the New Sun, such as going gaseous, a spiral ramp in an open pit, or a hollow Urth. Responds to Spectacled Bear's claim that a tower walks through the passage, conceding he had misread the surface-entrance point and noting the machine is Father Inire's and lives in the mine.
disputes · close-textual-reading

Such a tower from the roots of the continent, how interesting! Perhaps it can go gaseous to get out quickly through such tiny passageways. Or maybe the mine links up with a vast open pit with a long spiral ramp that the tripod can stump up to the surface. Or maybe it walks through an essentially hollow Urth and finds the Tower Access To Surface Tube nearest to the battle front and appears j ust in time.

- and I hadn't realized you meant the actual surface entrance when you were talking about tiny passageways. I thought you meant the deep tunnels (roots of the continent etc). I hadn't thought of the tower coming out of the mine, you see; it clearly couldn't, not the way Severian came in, nor would it need to. This is Father Inire's machine, not one of the Ascians' that fights in the battle, and it lives in the mine.

SBear.
