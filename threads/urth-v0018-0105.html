Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-09-04 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that the thing born between Yesod and Briah is something larger and more obvious than Ushas, namely the white fountain, which has a direct literal link to Severian as another self present across the five books. Responds to Tony Ellis's reading and her earlier quoted question by Ellis's own framing of the 'thing born', also apologising for an earlier joke about Inire.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: The white fountain is the thing born between Yesod and Briah
