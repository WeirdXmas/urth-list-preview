2002-02-05 Marc Aramini #

Recounts the poster's first encounter with The Book of the New Sun in 1989, borrowed from a coworker's book club and found secondhand, and asks Joe how he was introduced to the books. Mentions being upset by the ending of The Citadel of the Autarch and later reading an essay proposing Dorcas is Severian's grandmother.
chats

Hey Joe, you said you first read the book in 1988? I think I read it in '89. My father used to work at an Air Force Base Commisary as the Head Bagger, and his #2 man was a big sci-fi fan who would give me his books when he was through with them. I think he was part of the science fiction book club, because he eventually gave me the hardback of Claw of the Conciliator. I found Shadow at a second hand store. I used to read everything, and I mostly got what was going on, but I remember being immensely upset and disappointed by the ending of Citadel. At the time I was really into Zelazny's books. It was years before I read Urth of the New Sun, and by then I had run across an essay that said Dorcas was Sev's grandmother, probably in Clute's encyclopedia, and I had read the book a couple more times to see what else I had missed.
How were you introduced to the books?
