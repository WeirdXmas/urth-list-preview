Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-10-13 mary whalen #

Notes that Robert Borski's recent genealogical theories are all possible but worries about Merryn, since Wolfe said she is "probably" Severian's sister. Hopes the Short Sun books will settle the question, and corrects the oread to a mountain nymph while listing other Graeco-Roman nymph types.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Merryn is Severian's sister

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

I think that all of Robert Borski's recent genealogical theories are possible, but I'm slightly worried about Merryn, because Wolfe has said that she is "probably" Severian's sister. Hopefully Wolfe will decide this in the Short Sun books. By the way, an oread is a mountain nymph, not a sea nymph. Other types of Graeco-Roman nymphs, in case anyone wants to know, are:

Okeanids/Oceanids: daughters of Okeanos, nymphs of various water features

Nereids: daughters of Nereus, nymphs of the Mediterranean Sea

Naiads: water nymphs

Dryads: (oak) tree nymphs

Meliai: nymphs of ash trees
