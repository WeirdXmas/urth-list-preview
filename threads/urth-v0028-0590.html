1999-12-03 Urash, Tom #

Asks what the GURPS New Sun books are, and relays a question from Ron H-E about whether the Steve Jackson GURPS New Sun release due in December is still on schedule. Quotes the Steve Jackson site's December date as the basis for the question.
questions

"Mantis, you've done an admirable job of not bragging onlist, but I am dying to know when the GURPS New Sun books will be out. I went to the Steve Jackson site, and they said it is due in December. Is it still on course? Can you give us a better sense of the actual date (before Christmas, after Christmas, etc.)?

Ron H-E"

Pardon my ignorance, but would someone please explain what the "GURPS New Sun" books are?

tom urash
