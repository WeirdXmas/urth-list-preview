Themes: Time loops and causality

2002-06-12 Michael Straight #

Michael Straight replies to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, arguing that the claim about the distant future being the distant past is a joke with no real bearing on Severian's story. He offers no textual evidence, only his own sense that the cyclic framing was obviously unserious.
disputes · theories: Cyclic frame is a joke

To me, at that point it became obvious the whole thing was a joke and not meant to have any real bearing on Severian's story.

-Rostrum
