2001-02-08 Dan Schmidt #

Argues that the passage about Latro's writings from his earlier time in the Whorl, including meeting Pig and Hound, was picked up by the editors only after he arrived in Old Viron, based on the wording 'Old Viron and the West Pole' rather than 'the Whorl'. Responds to Adam Stephanides, who read the same text differently, by offering a textual reason for a different interpretation of the editors' role.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Editors inserted Latro's Whorl writings after Old Viron

OK, we're remembering the same text, but interpreting it differently. I took that to mean that he did write about his earlier time in the Whorl (e.g., meeting Pig and Hound) but the editors picked it up after he arrived in Old Viron. Otherwise I would have expected them to write 'the Whorl' rather than 'Old Viron and the West Pole'.
