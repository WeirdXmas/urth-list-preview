Islands in Commonwealth; other Cerberus book

Concedes that the original questioner was himself the author of the earlier question about the Commonwealth's islands, and retracts the worry: he now reads the Severian phrase "a chain of islands like our south" as describing only the southernmost part of the Commonwealth. Grounds the retraction in re-examining the quoted passage and notes the earlier thread for the Cerberus book's title in the archives.
disputes · close-textual-reading

I was the original questioner, and I was aware of the islands in the south of SA. My question was based on my belief that Severian was asserting that the Commonwealth consisted entirely of islands. But reexamining the quote, it now seems clear to me that the relevant phrase--"a chain of islands like our south"--refers to the southern- most portion of the Commonwealth, and not to the Commonwealth as a whole, as I had originally thought. So there's no problem there.
