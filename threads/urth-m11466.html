2010-12-06 Lee Berman #

Argues that the 1970s ancient-astronaut craze, popularized by Von Daniken's Chariots of the Gods, may have influenced Wolfe while he was writing The Book of the New Sun, and recalls the fad's pyramid-power claims. Offers no textual evidence from the books and mainly reminisces about a school science project testing pyramid effects on meat.
proposes · author-and-biography · theories: Von Daniken's Chariots of the Gods influenced Wolfe

Yeah, such ideas were all the rage in the early 70's, just before BotNS was being written. I think Von Daniken's Chariots of the Gods was the peak of it. I've always thought it might have been an influence on Wolfe.

Anyone remember "pyramid power". Heh, I can remember another kid's 7th grade science project, using a cardboard pyramid precisely aligned with coriolis force or star patterns trying to mummify a lump of raw ground beef. Ugh.
