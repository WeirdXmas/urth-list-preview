sidebar PEACE and "IODDAOS"

Urth list · 3 messages · 2000-10-18 to 2000-10-22

The thread: Nutria asked whether Peace and "The Island of Dr. Death" are linked. Michael Andre-Driussi answered twice, in near-duplicate posts, that both feature a shared Dr. Black and several parallels such as a big old house, a woman with suitors, and a bookish boy, while calling the works otherwise very different and not arguing for a deeper connection. James Jordan then asked whether PEACE and IODDAOS share a fictional world as The Changeling does, admitting he was probably wrong and deferring to Mantis. The thread ends without resolution, with the shared-world question left open.

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Stories within stories

2000-10-18 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Answers Nutria's question about a link between Peace and "The Island of Dr. Death" by confirming a shared character named Dr. Black and listing parallels: a big old house, a woman with suitors, a bookish boy, and a dog man. Says the two stories are otherwise very different, offering the parallels as evidence while stopping short of arguing a deeper connection.
answers · source-and-allusion

Yes, there is a Dr. Black in both.

Yes, there are certain points of similarity between the two works: a big old house; a woman and her suitors; a boy who is mostly ignored by the suitors, and who reads a lot, and fictional characters invade his waking life.

And there is the dog man.

But otherwise they are very different stories <g>.

=mantis=
