Daddy Typhon?

Urth list · 3 messages · 2002-07-15 to 2002-07-15

2002urthThe Book of the New SunDan'l Danehy-OakesMichael Andre-DriussiJames Jordan
The thread: Participants debated whether Typhon, the figure Severian later meets as Pas, is dead or merely sleeping in the Book of the New Sun. Michael Andre-Driussi argued the Claw restores Typhon's body with the soul he had a thousand years earlier, while the VR copy Pas-360 lives on separately in the Whorl, and proposed Typhon exists in three places at once. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes suggested suspended animation, reading the passage as dehydration and storage in a jar for later reconstitution, while admitting the text is ambiguous. James Jordan held that Typhon's soul sleeps awaiting Severian's awakening and slaying of him, grounding this in the Mainframe scene and timeline. The thread ended with three competing readings and no resolution.
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: Claw restores Typhon's old soul; Pas-360 is a separate entity; Typhon exists in three places
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Typhon in suspended animation, dehydrated and stored for reconstitution; text ambiguous
  • James Jordan: Typhon is not dead; his soul sleeps until Severian awakens and slays him

Themes: Memory and forgetting, Ghosts, dreams and visions, Doubles, twins and clones

2002-07-15 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that the Claw restores Typhon's body with the soul he had a thousand years earlier, while the VR copy Pas-360 is a separate entity that lived on in the Whorl. Answers the earlier poster's claim that Typhon's soul sleeps, using the Dorcas resurrection parallel and the blond-couple scan idea to suggest Typhon exists in three places at once.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Claw restores Typhon's last soul from a thousand years earlier; Pas-360 is a separate VR entity from Typhon; Typhon exists in three places at once; Blond couple is an original scan of Typhon

The mantis/nutria loop:

That is a somewhat thornier question than the easier one I thought I was addressing. The short answer is that it has to do with what blattid was working on earlier today: how many ways do the non-corporeal parts of a person divide/separate, how are these elements different from each other, and how many places can a person be at one time?

To bear down for a moment on the point you have raised. In TBOTNS we have Typhon dead (absolutely dead -- not in suspended animation, but dead) and then reanimated by the Claw. (If you are sticking to the suspended animation notion then I guess this argument is lost on you and I'm wasting my fingers.)

We understand that the Claw does this trick by bending time back . . . so the "Typhon" essence who is restored to the body is the last one who was there, a thousand years earlier. Just as Dorcas was raised from the dead.

Meanwhile, the VR version of Typhon, made prior to Typhon's death (prior to the rebellion, too) has been living through a few centuries of experience on the Whorl. Pas goes through some pretty rough experiences and may be, as a result, softer and kinder than Typhon: that sense we have noticed in which TBOTLS is like a rehabilitation of the old tyrant.

As it turns out, TBOTSS all takes place a few years before Severian meets Typhon arisen. Until this moment, as I try out different permutations, I had never thought of a system whereby the Claw restores Pas-360 to the body of Typhon . . . I simply thought that Typhon's real soul was being reinstated in the corpse, and Pas is a separate entity with a separate evolution and a separate soul (if such things exist for VR entities), like the chems but without a body. (The Typhon Severian meets sure doesn't seem rehabilitated from the Typhon of URTH.)

On the one hand, we have Typhon in literally two places at the same time: an awakened tyrant on Urth and a benign god on a starship hundreds of light-years away. But as we have seen with Severian, slivering off into Apu-Punchau and all the others (not to forget the enigmatic Claw itself--likely element of the first Severian), things that were once the same become different and independent.

Or, if we go along with my notion that the blond couple is the original scan of Typhon and <Bimbo>, then Typhon is in =three= places at once! (In contrast to Severian, who has separate selves decently scattered across separate centuries.)

=mantis=

Argues that Typhon may be in suspended animation rather than dead, having read the passage as showing him hooked to a device that dehydrated him and stored his fluids in a jar for later reconstitution. Responds to 'My fellow insect', who insisted on the suspended animation notion, while admitting the reading might be wrong and the question is ambiguous.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon is in suspended animation, not dead

I think the question of whether Typhon is dead or in SA might be ambiguious ... I had certainly read him as being in SA up to this posting: in my reading of the passage, he appears to be hooked up to a device which sort of dehydrated him and kept his precious bodily fluids in a jar until it was time to reconstitute him, orange-juice-like. Perhaps I'm wrong...

Grounds this in the Outsider's Mainframe scene, Silk's visit to Urth, and the timeline of Typhon's life, while questioning whether Typhon belongs in the same afterlife as Silk's adoptive parents.

I meant Typhon, not Pas. Sorry.
But, no, I won't agree. You certainly remember more details than I do, so help me out. Sure, Typhon's original body is gone, but his soul is alive, if asleep, waiting to be awakened by Severian and finally slain. So he ain't dead.

Help me out here.
