2002-12-18 Josh Geller #

Says the poster met Wolfe at a WorldCon and had several conversations with him, but declines to share them publicly, even though Wolfe would likely not mind. Replies to Nigel Price's remark about whether Wolfe would be upset by SF fans' writing, and to the discomfort of another poster, Marc, about speaking publicly.
chats

This isn't the point.

Marc feels uncomfortable and doesn't want to say.

You can make a case for saying he shouldn't have opened his [virtual] mouth about it. Whatever.

I met Gene Wolfe at WorldCon and I had a number of conversations with him.

I was invited to tell everyone about them and decided that I didn't want to. Not because I think Gene Wolfe would mind; I am sure he wouldn't care.

But I mind. So I am not going to.
