2002-05-18 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs the list that Wolfe's new story "Mute" appears in the 2002 World Horror Convention Program Book and praises it as a 5,000-word ghost story. Describes its plot of two orphaned children, probably dead, meeting their father's ghost in an old mansion, with Adam and Eve figures entering disturbingly.
