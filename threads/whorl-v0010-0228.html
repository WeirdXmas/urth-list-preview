The thread: Readers speculated about the secret that could destroy the inhumi in The Book of the Long Sun, since the inhumi drink human blood and take personalities from it. Bjjp2 proposed that something humans ingest might be the key, admitting it was pure speculation with no textual support. William H. Ansley playfully countered with a clever-sounding idea involving human antibodies from eating inhumi flesh, while mocking its cleverness and joking Wolfe could change the answer. Jim Henley enthusiastically endorsed the blood-based idea without offering argument or evidence. The thread stayed a light, unresolved guessing game with no consensus reached.
  • Bjjp2: The inhumi may be destroyed by something humans ingest, given they take personalities from human blood; admittedly speculative
  • William H. Ansley: Humans eating inhumi flesh would create antibodies that poison inhumi who drink their blood; playfully mocks the idea as too clever
  • Jim Henley: Enthusiastically supports the eat-or-drink idea as opening interesting lines of inquiry, with no textual argument

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Beasts and the animal in us

2000-01-20 Bjjp2 #

Proposes that the secret that can destroy the inhumi of The Book of the Long Sun is something humans might ingest, since the inhumi drink human blood and take their personalities from it. Admits the idea is pure speculation with no supporting text cited, offering it as a plausible alternative to the common focus on changes in human conduct.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Ingesting a substance could destroy the inhumi

Suggestion for the secret of the inhumi:

Everyone's been focusing on a change in human conduct as the secret that can destroy the inhumi. To me, the most easily accomplishable and plausible weapon that humans could have against the inhumi would be the ingestion of some substance. Since the inhumi drink human blood and appear to derive their personalities from the blood they drink, there may be something that humans can drink or eat that will impact the inhumi negatively. This is pure speculation . . . I have no supporting text to cite.

2000-01-20 William H. Ansley #

Playfully proposes that the secret for destroying the inhumi is humans eating inhumi flesh, so they build antibodies that poison the inhumi who drink their blood. Mocks the idea as too clever, notes alga's doubt that the secret can be guessed yet, and jokes that Wolfe could simply change the answer in the remaining books.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Humans eating inhumi flesh creates antibodies that poison inhumi

Very interesting! And what could this substance be? Hmmm.... let me see. Why inhumi flesh, of course, preferably raw, although cooked might work. Humans eat inhumi, develop anti-inhumi antibodies in their bloodstreams. The inhumi die or get very sick when they try to drink the blood of these humans. Eventually inhumi revert back to beast status, in which state they are too stupid to know that human blood is bad for them, so they continue to remain a nuisance, but a much less dangerous one. (Or they might still be smart enough to let humans alone entirely).

Ingenious, but I don't think so.

alga seems to think we can't guess the secret with what we know now, and that may well be true. But what if we do get it right? Wolfe can just go and change what it is in the other two books! (Of course we'll have to wait a few more years before they come out. <g>)

William Ansley
