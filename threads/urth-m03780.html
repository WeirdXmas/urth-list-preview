A Quick Question

Urth list · 6 messages · 2007-07-18 to 2007-07-19

2007urthThe Book of the New SunPaul BRex LycanthrosaurusDon DoggettStuart HammRoy C. Lackey
The thread: Paul B asked where the text says Severian spent a year as an Ascian slave after becoming Autarch, a detail he could not find in the book. Rex Lycanthrosaurus traced the claim to a 1982 Thrust interview in which Wolfe mentions it as extratextual material he might never print, and Stuart Hamm pointed to a passage on page 55 that he said supports the claim. Paul B then suggested the reference might instead be to a shorter Ascian capture in The Citadel of the Autarch, possibly meaning Severian was captured twice, while calling this speculation. Don Doggett used the interview to argue that the assassins sent to the Secret House were after Severian, supporting his view that Severian and Ymar are essentially the same person. The thread ended without settling whether the year in slavery is in the text.
  • Paul B: asked for the textual source, then suggested a shorter Ascian capture in The Citadel of the Autarch, possibly a second capture
  • Stuart Hamm: says the claim appears in the text on page 55 and cites the 1982 Thrust interview
  • Rex Lycanthrosaurus: sourced the claim to the 1982 Thrust interview, where Wolfe calls it extratextual material
  • don doggett: the Secret House assassins were sent for Severian, so Severian and Ymar are essentially the same person

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage, Memory and forgetting

2007-07-19 Paul B replying to Roy C. Lackey #

Paul B reconsiders his earlier reading of a claimed year as an Ascian slave, suggesting Severian may instead refer to a shorter Ascian capture in The Citadel of the Autarch. He notes the month-long absence mentioned there and proposes that, if Roy Lackey's reading holds, Severian was captured twice, while admitting this is speculation.
answers · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Severian was captured by the Ascians twice

Oh, I see what you mean. I'd thought he referred to his much shorter capture by the Ascians in Citadel, which admittedly seemed much shorter than a year, since he says the Autarch (as Severian and the old autarch) was absent for a month only in XXIV of Citadel. However, I dismissed that as another self-contradiction by Severian.
But, if it's true, then that means he must have been captured twice - one would think that would somehow have been more important than it's been, but I suppose that's solidly in the realm of speculation.

Paul

Points out that the claim about Severian's year as an Ascian slave appears in the text, citing a passage on page 55, and quotes a 1982 Thrust interview in which Wolfe mentions it. Responds to Rex Lycanthrosaurus, who had asked where the text says this and had quoted the interview from the archives.

On Wed, 18 Jul 2007 22:55:43 -0400, "Paul B"

It's extratextual and was mentioned in an interview conducted by Robert Frazier for a fanzine called Thrust in 1982:

Frazier: "Perhaps we could end this by telling something about Severian that doesn't end up in the books."

Wolfe: "There was the time when Severian encountered assassins in the Secret House who had come to kill Ymar, an autarch a chiliad dead. I may write about that sometime. And the year he spent as a slave of the Ascians. But I doudt that either will make it into print."

The interview may be reprinted in the new Wright book, but I'm not sure, as my copy has not arrived yet.
