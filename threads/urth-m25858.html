Themes: Unreliable narration

2014-10-07 Marc Aramini replying to Greg Kurzawa #

Answers Greg Kurzawa's question about where Horn's first-person entrance occurs in Exodus from the Long Sun by pointing to the end of Chapter 13, where Silk is dining and the narrator says he burst into the room. Gives the location from memory and quotes the line, without a text check, to support the claim that the entrance is in first person.
