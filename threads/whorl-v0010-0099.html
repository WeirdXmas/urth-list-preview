the Stoning of Wombat has been called off

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-10-12 to 1999-10-12

1999whorlMichael Andre-Driussi

1999-10-12 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Jokingly reports that the planned punishment of Wombat for posting spoilers about later volumes has been called off, after Nick revealed the spoilers were only in fun. Mixes this with asides about David Hartwell's ribbing and Ori Kowarsky's travel tale about Bute, Montana, a possible convention site.
chats

Yes, that's right.

For posting spoilers on volumes 2 and 3 whilst people are scrambling to get volume 1, wombat was convicted of going too far. After some fiercely competative bidding, the execution contract was awarded to the Amish ninjas.

Then Nick revealed that the spoilers were more in fun than real spoilers (I was not about to pollute my eyes by reading the spoilers--Nick is obviously not so squeamish about maintaining personal purity until the galga sacrament [in this case, OBW] arrives. <g>)

There was plenty of time to call off the assassins. Wombat need no longer fear the dreaded sound "clop-clop, clop-clop" coming after him, slow but relentless.

Besides, David Hartwell already got him back. Like a wet-towel rat-tail whip flicked to snap precisely at his bare fundament.

O, that wascally wombat! How we LOL at his antics!

Meanwhile, Ori Kowarsky's travel tale really is something. If we ever have a Gene Wolfe convention, we really must consider having it there in Bute, Montana. Sounds incredible! Panel attendance would no doubt be unusually high.

=mantis=
