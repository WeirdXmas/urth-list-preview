Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-03-18 Dan Parmenter #

Reports that the poster ran an AltaVista search on the names Hethor and Agia and found a Kabalarian name-analysis page describing their supposed personality traits and health effects. Presents the quoted analyses for the list while stating doubt that they have any merit despite Wolfe's interest in Kabalistic ideas.
informs

Hmmm, just for fun I did an Altavista search on "Hethor" and came up with the following, from a page that gives a "Kabalarian" analysis of the name. "Severian" is not in there, but "Agia" and "Ultan" are. Here are Hethor and Agia. Despite Wolfe's interest in things Kabalistic, I somehow doubt that these analyses have any merit whatsoever. But I present them:

Hethor

Your name of Hethor gives you the ability to understand people and to merge conflicting viewpoints to create harmony in association. You dislike facing issues or witnessing hurt feelings. You make friends easily but must guard against becoming involved or being too easily led. You could do well working with the public giving advice, but find it difficult to make snap decisions and to occupy a leadership position as you lack confidence and you are indecisive. You dislike a great deal of heavy manual work; you are inclined to put plans off until forced to take action. You find it difficult to be systematic in business and you dislike budgeting. When on your own, you lack the energy, confidence, or initiative required to bring an idea to fruition. This name creates weaknesses in the fluids of the body, kidneys, or glandular system.

Agia

Your first name of Agia has given you a very friendly, likeable, artistic, and musical nature, but you lack the incentive and drive necessary to realize your ideals. With this name, you desire the nice things in life but you do not always have the strength and vitality to put forth the effort necessary to fulfil your desires. This name causes you to lack stability and you will always be involved in other people's problems as long as you continue to use it. You have high ideals but lack the practicality, self-confidence, system, and concentration that is necessary to materialize them, and as a result, many times you suffer disappointments in people. Although your name creates a loveable, idealistic nature, it is far too sensitive an influence to allow the stability and practicality of mind you need. In health, this name affects the nervous system and could cause hysteria, fits, or intense nervous conditions. It also affects the fluid functions, kidney or bladder weaknesses.
