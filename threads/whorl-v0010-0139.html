1999-10-20 William H. Ansley #

Argues that the names Blind Pig and Blind Pew, and the peg-legged blinding villain, echo the cartoon supervillain Deadly Bulb from The Tick, so Silk's catacrest Tick in Nightside the Long Sun is no coincidence. Responds to alga by citing the Tick episode Heroes, where the villain was first named Pigleg.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Tick name in Nightside echoes The Tick; Deadly Bulb as Pigleg's image change

alga,

This can't be coincidence!

In the animated tv series _The Tick_, in an episode entitled "Heroes," a supervillian calling himself the Deadly Bulb appeared. He was originally called Pigleg, for the good and sufficient reason that one of his legs terminated in a pig, rather than a foot, but he wanted an image change. As the Deadly Bulb, one of his gimmicks was *blinding* flashes of light.

And of course, you all remember the name of the catacrest Silk is offered before he buys Oreb in _Nightside the Long Sun_: Tick!

"My mind is a scary place"
-The Tick (from "The Tick vs. the Protoclown")

William Ansley
