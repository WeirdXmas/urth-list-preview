2009-03-04 Don Doggett #

Announces a poetry reading by the poster at a Phoenix bookshop and notes his book Carnival of Vulgarities is a partial homage to The Circus of Dr. Lao. Its only link to Wolfe is that Dr. Lao tie-in, and it names no Wolfe passage or character.
informs

If there are any other sinners who live in hell with me (phoenix area) you're invited to my poetry reading at Changing Hands on friday at 7 pm. I'll be reading from my book Carnival of Vulgarities, which (and here is the tangential Wolfe relation) is a partial homage to The Circus of Dr. Lao. No mention of Woldercan however.

Don
