2014-10-22 Mark Lewin replying to Norwood, Frederick Hudson #

Explains that Gyoll, the river running through the city in The Book of the New Sun, is probably named after the Norse river Gjoll, which symbolized death and ran near the gates of Hel, comparable to the Greek Styx. Responds to a quoted suggestion linking the name to Sheol and to Gerry Quinn's question about whether the name Gyoll has been analysed.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Gyoll named after Norse river Gjoll

"Gyoll is a great river which runs through the city, in which the apprentices have just been swimming. Its name is probably a reference to the Norse Gjoll, a river which symbolized death and ran near the gates of Hel ? not unlike the Greek Styx."

Mark
