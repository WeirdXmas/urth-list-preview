Asks where a newcomer might run into Gene Wolfe around Chicago and whether attending conventions there is worthwhile for that purpose. Written as a plain question after mention of proposed symposiums, with no critical argument about the books and no answer offered yet.

2003-07-27 Sheila Herndon #

Reports seeing Wolfe at WindyCon, where he signed books and sat on panels, and points to the con's guest page as not listing him this year. Also qualifies an earlier report that Wolfe said the ghost was real, doubting he specified a real ghost rather than something else, and jokes about the Vironese name Badger.
informs

I've seen him at WindyCon. He signed some books and was on some panels=20=

-- one including mixing genres. He's not listed as a guest at this=20 year's WindyCon, at least on this page:

http://www.windycon.org/guests.asp

Btw, when I first joined the list a long time ago, and people were=20 talking about whether the ghost was real, I reported that at this con,=20=

he said the ghost was real. After being here for more than a year or=20 so, and reading the discussions, I do not think you can take this to=20 mean that he said it was a real *ghost*. :) It could be a real=20 *something* or the other, but I can't actually remember if he=20 explicitly said it was a real *ghost*. Otoh, maybe I'm being silly.

Sign Badger then! :)

mimosa
