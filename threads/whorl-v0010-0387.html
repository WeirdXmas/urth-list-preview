2000-07-16 Adam Stephanides #

Reports that the poster found a copy of In Green's Jungles on a bookstore table, though a Barnes & Noble superstore did not stock it, and asks whether the book has really been released. Says the poster will hold back further non-spoiler comments until the book is confirmed to be out.
informs

So far, aside from the few people with advance copies, nobody but me has mentioned reading IGJ, or even seeing it. I checked out a B&N "superstore" yesterday, and they didn't have it. But I didn't hallucinate my copy, honest. Nor is it marked as an advance copy or anything like that; nor was it gotten out of a back room. It was sitting on a table along with other new sf books that indubitably have been released. Did I manage to stumble into the only store in the world with a copy of IGJ?

Anyway, I do have a couple more non-spoiler comments on the book, but I think I'll wait until I know it's really out there.

--Adam

2000-07-18 William H. Ansley #

Reports that the poster ordered In Green's Jungles from Amazon and that the copy is arriving the next day while on vacation. Replies to a message about buying the book at a Boston-area independent bookstore, joking that rural readers lack such shops.

I'll be able to read it and then finally be able to read Adam's first impressions posting.

Hope all you other Amazon customers get your copies very soon now.

I would love to support my local independent bookstore, but us country folk don't have the good pickins you city slickers do. <g>
