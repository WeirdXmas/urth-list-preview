2000-04-03 Michael Straight #

Disputes Talarican's claim that the hollow-rock generation ship is wholly original to Wolfe, arguing by joke that the idea is no more original than a familiar orphan-hero plot. Lists that plot (a secret-society orphan with an extraordinary sword, a quest, destined greatness, defeating an evil enemy, becoming king) and jokingly suggests Wolfe took it from Star Trek.
disputes · genre-and-craft

Every bit as orginial as the idea of an orphan of unknown parentage, raised by a secret society, who on coming of age is given an extraordinary sword, undertakes a quest, finds that great powers have destined him for greatness, overcomes an evil enemy, and takes his place as king.

Maybe Wolfe stole that one from Star Trek too!

-Rostrum
