2002-05-09 Mark Millman #

Points out a factual error on the 1975 Harper & Row hardcover flap of Peace, which calls it Wolfe's first novel, and guesses that the cover credit rather than the artist's name is wrong on the Orb edition. Responds to Christopher Culver's note about the mismatched cover artist credit, arguing that Gahan Wilson clearly made the art so Tony Roberts is the likely designer.
informs · close-textual-reading

Speaking of errors, there's quite a one on the rear
jacket flap of the 1975 Harper & Row hardcover edition
of _Peace_: it says that _Peace_ is Wolfe's first
novel.

As for the Orb edition, I'd guess that perhaps Tony
Roberts did the cover design, and that the credit,
rather than the name, is what's wrong. I don't think
that there can be any doubt that Gahan Wilson did the
art.

Mark Millman
(Nacre)
