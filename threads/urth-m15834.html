Proposes that the fabled emerald bench in the Autarch's laboratory alludes to the Emerald Tablet of alchemy, which holds alchemical truths such as the formula for gold and the Philosopher's Stone. Probably a reference to the Emerald Tablet aka the Emerald Table, upon which is graven the undoubtable truths of alchemy including the formula for gold, the Philosopher's Stone, the care and feeding of homunculi and mandragoras, etc.
