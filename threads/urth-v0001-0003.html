Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1997-04-12 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Announces a forthcoming volume of additions and errata to Wolfe's books, listing planned entries on terraforming of Mars, Venus and the Moon, stellar engineering of the Old and New Suns, solar neutrino counts, Kardashev levels and the Apollos. Invites list members to help with research, and mentions a paperback chapter page citation key.
informs

Open the Floodgates of Urth!

As I mentioned somewhere else, "Additions, Errata, &cetera: volume III" is taking shape in the magic mirror.

There's a paperback chapter page citation key which should come in handy for some.

Terraforming notes re: Mars/Verthandi, Venus/Skuld, Luna/Lune, and stellar engineering re: Old Sun (as in real engineering answers to "why insert a black hole into a perfectly good star?") and New Sun (as in real engineering answers to . . . well, you get the idea).

Something about the early neutrino count from the sun . . . still researching this one. Feel free to help if you know anything.

An entry on Kardashev Levels of civilization.

An entry on various Apollos.

And more things of that sort.

=mantis=
