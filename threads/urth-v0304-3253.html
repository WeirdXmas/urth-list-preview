Jokingly warns readers away from a novel called Runts of 61 Cygni C, describing it as a source of excruciatingly bad passages and bad sex scenes read aloud at science fiction conventions. It offers no Wolfe analysis; it is a social joke about a non-Wolfe book.

If you ever see a book called RUNTS OF 61 CYGNI C, run!!!.

One of the amusements at SF cons is reading excruciatingly bad passages from books while the audience bid money for you to stop. This book contains THE WORST, including some amazing Bad Sex scenes.
