Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Names and their meanings

1997-07-22 John Bishop #

Proposes that the name Ernie Cotha is a pun on Golgotha, the hill of the crucifixion translated as Place of the Skull, and asks whether Ernie was a colonel. Bases this on the Catholic connection described in Ron's earlier post, and admits not having read the story itself.
proposes · source-and-allusion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Ernie Cotha is a pun on Golgotha

I haven't read that story, but given the Catholic
connection Ron's post describes, I instantly
thought thusly:

Ernie Cotha ->
"Golgotha", the hill where
Christ was crucified ->
translated sometimes as
"Place of the Skull"
Was Ernie a Colonel? Col. Cotha would be a dead
giveaway.

-John
