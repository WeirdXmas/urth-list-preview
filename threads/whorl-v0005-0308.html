Lists the poster's personal ranking of John Crowley's novels (Aegypt, Little Big, Love & Sleep, The Deep, Engine Summer) while noting unread titles. Says they had thought Crowley was more of a coterie writer than Wolfe, and are glad to have been corrected.

oops. Wasn't attempting to give a complete list or ranking, but: I haven't read TGWoT, Antiquities or Gone, so my ranking of the remaining works would have to be (in decending order):

Aegypt
Little, Big
Love & Sleep
The Deep
Engine Summer

I had thought that Crowley was as coterie (more so, perhaps) than Wolfe. Delighted to discover my misapprehension.
