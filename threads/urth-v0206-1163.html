Informs the list that a new book of advice letters from various writers, including a letter by Gene Wolfe, is now stocked in large bookstores, and notes how Borders and Barnes & Noble shelve it. The post reports the poster's own in-store sighting rather than citing a review or reading of Wolfe's contribution.
informs

TAKE MY ADVICE is a book of letters from people, advice to younger generations.

The advice-givers are from a fairly wide spectrum. Gene Wolfe has a letter in there.

The book is new and in the big stores now, places like Borders and Barnes & Noble. (Borders files it under Literary Anthologies; B&N files it under Self Improvement, iirc.)

(I actually learned about it a few weeks ago, but didn't say anything until I'd seen it for myself.)
