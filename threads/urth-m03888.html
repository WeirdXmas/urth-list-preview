2007-06-04 Steven Bowen #

Praises Wolfe's prose for dissolving the gap between author and reader while keeping the sense of wonder and reality that intelligent reading requires. Responds to a quoted line about beautiful events rather than just beautiful language, agreeing that Wolfe's style serves what is described rather than displaying itself.
supports · genre-and-craft

Wonderful panegyric on Wolfe -- thanks. The above words capture what I love best. So many authors woo us with style and pyrotechnics to the detriment of what's being described. Wolfe's style, at his best, dissolves the space between author and reader and yet leaves intact the wonder and grasping after reality that real intelligence is all about.
Steven
