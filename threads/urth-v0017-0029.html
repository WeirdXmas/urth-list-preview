Disputes the theory that the first Autarchs stocked Urth with animals, arguing that Typhon brought the Bear Tower there before any Autarchs existed. Cites the doctor's remark calling it a zoetic transport (UNS 258-259), and questions whether Severian misreads the animal handlers' marriage customs, suggesting that a name like animal husbandry shapes what the practice becomes.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Typhon brought the Bear Tower before the Autarchs; Animal handlers' marriage customs are misunderstood by Severian; Names influence the things they name

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

I don't agree with the theory that the first Autarchs made Urth self-sufficient by seeding the planet with animals. Typhon, according to the doctor who treats Severian, had already brought the Bear Tower to Urth before there were any Autarchs, and he even calls it a zoetic transport (UNS 258-259).

Also, is everyone certain that the marriage customs of the animal handlers has been misunderstood by Severian? Couldn't it just be the name-influences-the-thing-named sort of thing? Since it's called animal husbandry, that's what it becomes. It's not as if the same sort of thing doesn't happen elsewhere on Urth in the books.

prion
