Themes: Humour in Wolfe

2005-07-21 Jim Raylor #

Argues that a breakfast exchange in The Devil in a Forest contains a pun: the pig sits on its hams, which the poster links to ham and eggs. Points to the innkeeper's offer of fried eggs and the pig's haunches as the evidence, closing with a joking aside about the pun.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Wolfe's breakfast dialogue contains a deliberate pun on hams

In Devil in the Forest, Wat orders breakfast and the
innkeeper suggests fried eggs (cooked in grease) and
what the pig sits on.
Like most of us mammals, a pig sits on its haunches ..
or, more archaically, its hams.
ham'n'eggs 4 breakfast? NE1?
Strong men weep, women draw their weapoms...
