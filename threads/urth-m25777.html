2014-11-21 Marc Aramini replying to Robert Pirkola #

Argues that the middle story in The Fifth Head of Cerberus literally and metaphorically treats the shadow children as an infectious group organism, tying this to the life cycle of the Abos. Supports the view with textual points drawn from an article on Ultan's, and reads the bite as the climax of the switching drama that explains why Marsch cannot write.
supports · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Shadow children are an infectious group organism

Definitely read the entire article on Ultan's for textual support which I couldn't get into on the video.

Life cycle of Abos: larva, adult motile stage, tree "carapace" sessile stage. Shadow children: infectious group organism. The middle story is both literal and metaphorical in anthropomorphizing the shadow children.

The bite is the conclusion of the switching drama in a story, and it is the reason Marsch gives for being unable to write ... These misunderstood ironic truths that seem like lies are a hallmark of Wolfe's style.

Yes the vest with a coat is my usual teaching style.
