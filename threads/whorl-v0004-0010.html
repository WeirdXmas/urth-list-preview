Reports Gene Wolfe's comment on the Quick & Dirty Guide to the Long Sun Whorl, noting that his sketch map is right but incomplete and placing the ball court and palaestra east of the manteion. Quotes Wolfe's words directly and adds a light invitation to others to redraw the map.

Hey Gang,

Here's the first comment from Gene Wolfe on "The Quick & Dirty Guide to the Long Sun Whorl"--and lookee, it's about a map!

"The sketch map on the back [cover] is right but incomplete. The ball court's east of the manteion, and the plaestra's east of that, fronting Sun St."

Well all right--get sketching!

=mantis=
