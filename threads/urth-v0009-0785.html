Themes: Books, libraries and writing

1998-04-28 Dan Parmenter #

Corrects another writer's vague claim by giving Borges's actual library career, including his city library post and later directorship of the National Library, and argues this fits Master Ultan. Asks whether Wolfe has confirmed that Father Inire is modeled on Lewis Carroll, which the poster finds vaguer than the Ultan parallel.
questions · author-and-biography · source-and-allusion · theories: Master Ultan is based on Borges; Father Inire is based on Lewis Carroll

Just for the record, Borges didn't just work "at some library in Argentina" as one writer suggested. He worked at a city library from 1937 to 1946, when the Peron regime made him a chicken inspector (no lie!). Post-Peron, he was made Director of the National Library in Buenos Aires.

So the fit with Master Ultan seems a good one and one that almost anyone with the slightest familiarity with Borges' life would recognize immediately.

Just out of curiosity, has Wolfe admitted to the Father Inire as Lewis carroll thing? I can certianly see it, though it seems more vague than the Ultan case.

D
