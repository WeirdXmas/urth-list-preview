1999-07-04 Alice Turner #

Argues that the underwater base and submarines in a Tintin story are not a unique source for Lake of the Long Sun, citing earlier and later works with similar underlake cities and submarines. Responds to Nigel Lupine's suggestion by naming Ozma of Oz, a Bond film, and the new Star Wars film as comparable examples.
disputes · source-and-allusion

Well now, Nigel, let's not get carried away. -Ozma of Oz-, published in the early years of this century (and a book we're pretty sure Wolfe has read, given "Eyeflash"), has an underlake bubble-dome city operated by a sort of hydraulic lift; it had little submarines too. And at least one of the James Bond movies had something of the sort, complete with baddies, also with submarines (well, you'd need 'em , wouldn't you?). And have you seen the new Star Wars movie? A whole underlake city, and drop-dead spectacular.
