1999-04-24 Alice Turner #

Disputes Jack Lyons's claim that Tzadkiel is not a supernatural being, asking what counts as supernatural and whether Zak/Tzadkiel's many manifestations are natural. Points to scholarly works on myth, folklore, the Bible and the Kabbalah, suggesting Wolfe draws on them and that Davidson's dictionary alone is not the final word.
disputes · theology-and-religion · source-and-allusion

And just what is your definition of supernatural? I don't think I need to go into detail with this group, but please consider the many manifestations of Zak/Tzadkiel: are these "natural?"

There are scholarly works on mythology, on folklore, on the Bible, on the Kaballah and (gasp!) even on novels and the plays of Shakespeare. Wolfe is clearly familiar with many of these, and, if you visit the library, one day you may discover them too.

Chacun a son gout.

-alga
