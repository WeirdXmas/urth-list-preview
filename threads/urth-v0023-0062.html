Did Sev1 fail?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-02-11 to 1999-02-11

1999urthMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Time loops and causality

1999-02-11 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that Severian's first self did not fail at the Test, reasoning that Sev2 was improved because both the New Sun and Entropy Gang meddled in his past in response to his success. Builds on the idea that the tampering was two-sided and links it to the inset tale of the Student and his Son, casting Sev1 as the father.
proposes · myth-and-structure · puzzle-solving · theories: Sev1 did not fail; Sev2 is the improved Severian; Entropy Gang and New Sun both tampered with Severian's past; Sev1 is the Student and Sev2 the Son

Tony Ellis,

Right, there is no proof that Sev1 failed. But I think there is a sense of "If it ain't broke, don't fix it"--and since Sev2 has been opitimized, then it stands to reason that Sev1 was flawed.

Then again: the influences on Sev2 come from both camps, New Sun and Entropy Gang. So the tampering/tempting isn't one-sided. Leading us back to the belief that Sev1 didn't fail; that his success at the Test is what triggered the Entropy Gang to start messing with his past; and to counter this the New Sun people began giving little assists in his past. All of which escalation produced a new, improved Severian.

Sev1 is the father of the man; Sev2 is the new son.

(Ooo, which feeds back into that whole "The Tale of the Student and his Son": we can let the Student be Sev1 and assign the Son to Sev2.)

=mantis=
