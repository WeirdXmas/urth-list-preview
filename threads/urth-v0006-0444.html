Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-01-24 C R Culver #

Argues that the Autarch's castration was not a punishment for failing to bring the New Sun but a device by which Tzadkiel gave Severian the circumstances to become autarch. Responds to Vizcacha's quoted line about the far-future someone giving the Autarch a better life, and supports that reading by rejecting the word punishment.
disputes · myth-and-structure · theories: Tzadkiel unmanned the Autarch to give Severian his chance

had lived a life that prepared him better for the test, he might have succeeded. So, they came back and gave him that life, and he did."<

Yes, Tzadkiel gave Severian the circumstances to become autarch by unmanning the previous Autarch. That's why I wince when I hear people use the word "punishment." The Autarch's castration wasn't a punishment for his failure to bring the New Sun back to Urth, it was merely a device to insure Severian, who *could* bring the NS, would get his day.
