1999-08-12 Roy C. Lackey #

Proposes that Wolfe's Spading Moon corresponds to the Planter's Moon, and compiles a list of traditional month-moon names from almanac and web sources to support the idea. Roy C. Lackey reports finding no mention of Spading Moon online and notes names are American Indian in origin and must be adjusted for latitude and hemisphere.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Spading Moon equals Planter's Moon

I could find no mention of Spading Moon, but the following is a compilation of various lists of moon-month names found on the web. The traditonal moon name in the OLD FARMER'S ALMANAC is the first name listed for each month. The names are often American Indian in origin and will need to be adjusted for latitude and hemisphere. I suspect the Spading Moon would be equal to Planter's Moon, aka the Seed Moon, Plow Moon, Planting Moon. FWIW

January - Wolf Moon
February - Snow Moon, Hunger Moon, Opening Buds Moon
March - Worm Moon, Maple Sugar Moon, Plow Moon, Seed Moon April - Pink Moon, Frog Moon, Growing Moon, Seed or Planting Moon, Planter's Moon
May - Flower Moon, Budding Moon, Planting Moon
June - Strawberry Moon
July - Buck Moon, Blood Moon, Hay Moon
August - Sturgeon Moon, Moon of the Green Corn, Barley Moon September - Harvest Moon, Wine Moon
October - Hunter's Moon, Moon of Falling Leaves
November - Beaver Moon, Snow Moon
December - Cold Moon

Roy
