1998-01-29 Dave Lebling #

Argues that the undine who saved Severian was luring him toward evil, since the agents of evil in The Book of the New Sun try to win Severian over rather than kill him. Responds to Xury's reading by raising doubts about the original Severian's success and asking how many Severians there are, how often he dies, and how the timeline runs.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Undine saved Severian to lure him to evil

Xury,

I find it easier to believe that the undine saved Severian as part of an effort to (if you will excuse the expression) lure him to the "dark side of the force." All the exemplars of evil (except for Agia and the Sorcerors) try to win Severian over to their side, not kill him. The undines (or is it the same undine each time?) are particularly persistent in this regard.

I still think (mantis' opinion aside) that the "original" Severian didn't succeed in bringing the New Sun. On the other hand, even after more rereadings than I remember, I still don't have it straight exactly how many Severians there are, and how many times he dies, and what the original timeline looks like. Was Apu-Punchau always Severian? How did Severian end up buried in the tomb below the Citadel? How much time elapsed between Typhon's reign and Severian's? I suppose it is only to be expected that a novel in which time is a major player should be somewhat confusing in this regard. I accept, but I do not understand...

-vizcacha
([email])

1998-01-30 C R Culver #

Argues that no New Sun was brought in the earlier iteration, since Tzadkiel would not need to rework the timeline so Severian lives to bring it. Calculates the gap between Typhon's and Severian's reigns as about a chiliad, citing Mantis's figure of 1000 years between Ymar and Severian, and speculates that Severian's death may be told in the Book of the Short Sun.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Severian's New Sun was not brought in the earlier iteration; about a thousand years between Typhon and Severian

didn't succeed in bringing the New Sun.<

I don't think so either. Why would Tzadkiel need to rework the timeline so that *Severian would live* to bring the New Sun if it had been brought in the past iteration?

Mantis has said that about a chiliad (1000 Urth years) passed between the reign of Ymar and the Reign of Severian. Typhon was the last of the Monarchs, Ymar was one of the first, or the first, of the autarchs. Thus, about 1000 (Urth) years have passed between Typhon's reign and Sev's reign.

I hope this question may be answered in the Book of the Short Sun. However, Gene Wolfe used the word 'cameo' when he mentioned Sev and Merryn are going to be in it, so I wonder if Severian's *death* will be told of in the BotSS.
