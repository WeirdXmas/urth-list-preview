1997-08-08 Alice Turner #

Reports that a web search for the names of the Whorl's native people ruled out Ojibwa, Cherokee, Navaho and Tundra Nenets as sources. Notes that Inuit could not be accessed and suggests trying other Native American languages again later.
informs · source-and-allusion

Wow, there is a ton of Native American stuff on the web! A ton of language stuff too, though clearly this is still in its infancy. Okay, I can now tell you what our names are NOT: Objibwa (Acchanabe), Cherokee, Navaho or Tundra Nenets (that's Siberian---hey, worth a try!). Couldn't access Inuit, which is my first choice or any other NA laguages, though it might be worth another try.

-alga-
