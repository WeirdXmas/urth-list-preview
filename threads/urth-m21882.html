Inn of Lost Loves

Urth list · 9 messages · 2011-09-22 to 2011-09-22

2011urthThe Book of the New SunGwern BranwenGerry QuinnJonathan PostSon of WitzJeff WilsonDan'l Danehy-Oakes
The thread: Readers asked what became of the Inn of Lost Loves, which Severian's early account says no longer stands. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argued it did not die in the floods of the New Sun, since Severian writes the Book of the New Sun before the Urth events, and suggested it simply closed, answering Gwern Branwen's flood theory. Jonathan asked the question twice, noting the book's opening lacks any explanation. Gerry Quinn asked whether the inn links to the Room of Lost Loves in The Wizard Knight, replying to Son of Witz's view that the inn is a simple symbolic device. The thread shows questions and a few guesses but no settled answer.
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: the inn went out of business rather than being destroyed in the floods
  • Jonathan: the books give no explanation of the inn's fate
  • Gerry Quinn: the inn may be linked to the giant wizard's Room of Lost Loves in The Wizard Knight
  • Son of Witz: the inn is probably a simple symbolic device

Themes: Stories within stories, Memory and forgetting
