The thread: Kieran Mullen proposed that Jonas and Hethor arrived on Urth in the same crash, reasoning that Tzad's huge ship would have been noticed and that Jonas's confusion stems from fever. 1999-06-02 Kieran Mullen #

Proposes that Jonas and Hethor may have arrived on Urth in the same crash, while noting Jonas could have been there for years before Hethor appeared. Argues that Tzad's ship, being so large, would have been noticed if it had crashed, so Jonas's confusion is better explained as delirium from his feverish organic parts.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Jonas and Hethor crashed together from Tzad's ship; Jonas's confusion is delirium from organic parts

In one of the postscripts Wolfe speculates that there is only one ship like Tzad's. I always sorta thought that Jonas and Hethor came from the same crashlanding. However, we don't really know how long Jonas has been on Urth - he could have been there for years before Hethor showed up.

It seems unreasonable to think that Tzad's ship crashed on Urth since it is so huge. Surely there would be some notice!

I always put this down to delerium. His organic parts are feverish and that's causing problems for him. He seems more delerious than simply panicky.

Kieran Mullen

Grounds this in both works dealing with the consequences and origins of a race seemingly without sin, and responds to an earlier post inviting comparison with the Urth Cycle.
proposes · theories: Case of Conscience should be read with The Book of the New Sun

1999-06-03 Matthew Malthouse #

Argues that Jonas need not have arrived on Tzad's ship, since many other vessels in the Urth books could have crashed him there, such as Hierodule tenders, ships from the Old Port, and a generation ship from Typhon's era. Responds to Robert Borski's suggestion, quoted by Kieran Mullen, that Jonas and Sidero crashed in the future, by offering alternative vessels for Jonas's arrival.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Jonas arrived on a vessel other than Tzad's ship

I'd offer two reasonable solutions.

First _if_ Jonas sailed Tzad's ship then the vessel in which he crashed might have been some tender or shuttle.

Perhaps more likely; Tzad's ship might be the only one that sails a course between the universes but it most certainly isn't the only one in the skys of Urth of which we know.

There are those vessels of the heirodules - which may themselves be only tenders of The Ship. There are the ships that once came and went from the Old Port (Citadel). There are the ships that made a human empire in space and the same, or others that brough Typhon back. If one want to go slightly outside the bounds of Urth then there is at least one generation ship from Typhon's era.

What is that requires Jonas to have been a sailor on Tzad's ship and no other?

Matthew
