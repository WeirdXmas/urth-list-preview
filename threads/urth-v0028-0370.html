Planarian worms in SF

1999-11-03 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs the list that a recent short story, "Unborn Again" by Chris Lawson, makes much use of planarian worm learning experiments in science fiction. Notes the story appeared in the latest Dozois Best SF of the Year anthology and calls the experimental evidence spurious.
informs

On the subject of the use in SF of the spurious

play of the concept: "Unborn Again" by Chris Lawson,
in the most recent Dozois BEST SF OF THE YEAR
anthology.
