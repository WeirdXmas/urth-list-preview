The fantasy element in "Suzanne Delage"

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-06 to 1998-05-06

1998urthPeter Westlake

1998-05-06 Peter Westlake #

Reports that the poster reread the short story "Suzanne Delage" and found a sentence that seemed to contain a hidden fantastic element, which he withholds as a spoiler. Asks whether other readers know what he means or whether he is imagining it, and notes he is first joining the list after reading the archives.
informs

Reading through the Urth archives before joining this list, I came across someone asking what the fantastic element is in "Suzanne Delage". I had wondered that when I first read it, so I read it again, twice. On the first reading, nothing. But on the second pass I came across a sentence that gave me the most extraordinary sensation I've ever had while
reading: it "caught my gaze" in the most literal sense
imaginable. It's such a major spoiler that I won't mention it right now, especially before being certain about the
conventions for spoilers on the list; but does anyone know what I mean? Or am I imagining the whole thing?

Peter.

Peter Westlake, Harlequin Ltd, Barrington Hall, Barrington, Cambs CB2 5RG.
