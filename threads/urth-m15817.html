Introduces a newly joined reader who announces his blog on Wolfe's Solar Cycle books and says he is catching up on the archive, including earlier discussions of Catholic and religious readings of the Long Sun and Short Sun books. Mainly a greeting and self-promotion, with the poster noting he will join discussion later.
informs

Hi all, I just joined this list and I'm reading through it kind of like Horn's narration in Book of the Short Sun - I'm alternatingly/simultaneously back in the 90s reading the first discussion on the Catholic (or not) nature of Long Sun as well as the up to date recent discussions on the religions of the Whorl (or not), Patera Pike's ghost (or not), and Oreb's 'good fishing' refrain (or not - I think it was Daisy too, for the record).

Anyway, I wanted to let people know of my Gene Wolfe blog ('The Silk & Horn Heresy') where I discuss his works at length - mainly the Solar Cycle so far. The latest is from a few days ago with the same title as this email's subject.

It's great to listen in and I shall join the discussion in due course. Thanks all,

Daniel Otto Jack Petersen
