Typhon/exultants not Of This Urth?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1997-04-21 to 1997-04-21

1997urthThe Book of the New SunDavid Wells

1997-04-21 David Wells #

Asks where The Book of the New Sun implies that Typhon came from off Urth and brought the exultants with him, saying a list correspondent made the claim and he cannot find it. Requests chapter and verse, and also raises unresolved puzzles about Jonas/Miles/Hethor and Valeria and the Atrium of Time, which he has not solved after three readings.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon was of extraterrestrial origin; Typhon brought the exultants from off Urth

Someone (apologies - I forget who) mentioned on the Whorl list that there was evidence that Typhon was of extraterrestrial origin and probably brought the exultants with him. I fairly recently re-read BOTNS and - although I will freely admit to being the most attentive of Wolfe readers - I'm not sure where this is implied. Can someone quote chapter and verse? Thanks,

Dave W/newt

P.S. I'm eagerly awaiting the promised illumination of the Jonas/Miles/Hethor/whoever mystery, which I find both fascinating and baffling. Not to mention the exact nature of "poor" Valeria and the Atrium of Time, which I am still puzzled by after three readings...
