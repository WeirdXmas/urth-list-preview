2001-10-06 Wm F Seabrook #

Asks whether the cover of the first UK edition of Nightside the Long Sun, by Richard Bober, matches the US Tor edition, and reports that the image of Patera Silk on Blood's battlements was taken from an Errol Flynn publicity still from The Sea Hawk. Introduces the poster as new to the list and attaches the cover image, noting the pose seemed contrived yet familiar.

The cover illustration for the first UK edition (NEL 1993) of
-Nightside the Long Sun_ is by Richard Bober (is this the same
as the US Tor edition?). It shows Patera Silk on the
battlements
of Blood's house in a stance that I've always thought a
little
contrived and yet strangely familiar. I've just found out why -- it
was taken from a publicity still of Errol Flynn in the 1940 film
_The Sea Hawk_ ![Image]

Bill Seabrook
England
