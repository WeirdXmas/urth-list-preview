Memorare Hardcover Art and Hugo Award for Wolfe

Urth list · 1 messages · 2008-07-01 to 2008-07-01

2008urthJoe Bushong-Taylor

2008-07-01 Joe Bushong-Taylor #

Reports receiving the hardcover edition of Wolfe's Memorare from Wyrm Publishing, praising its cover art as a movie poster that connects to the story. Urges readers to vote for Memorare for a Hugo Award before the deadline, arguing Wolfe has never won a Hugo and it is overdue.
informs

Just got my hardcover of Wolfe's *Memorare* (Wyrm Publishing). Thanks to whomever on the list noted that it was coming out! The cover art is great (a poster for the movie) and connects to the story on several levels. Getting my copy reminded me that there are still a few days left to vote for a Hugo award for *Memorare* (you can join World Science Fiction Society and vote on the Hugo awards through www.denvention3.org). Wolfe has never won a Hugo. I think it is overdue.
