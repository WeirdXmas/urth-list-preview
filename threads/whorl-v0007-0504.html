1997-07-02 RRHMAH #

Suggests that the Tick-Tock the crest cat reference is a deliberate joke by Wolfe, comparable to the Vernor Vinge books shelved under W in The Fifth Head of Cerberus. Responds to Langford's mention of the reference after noting Ian Watson's Kalevala-based novels and welcoming newcomer Alastair, whose Interzone story he had just read.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Tick-Tock the crest cat reference is a Wolfe joke; Vinge books shelved under W are a joke

I'd forgotten about Petaja's version. Ian Watson wrote two books: Lucky's Harvest and The Fallen Moon, quite recently, based also on the Kalevala.

And welcome to Alastair. A coincidence that he joins this list just as I have read his fine SF thriller "A Spy in Europa", the cover story of the June Interzone. Nice biter-bit (he-he) ending!

I must confess to have missed the Tick-Tock the crest cat reference: and I read _The Universe Against Her_ not 6 months ago. But now Mr. Langford brings it up, I think it must be a little joke of Mr. Wolfe's. (Similar to the Vernor Vinge books shelved under W in _Fifth Head_.)

Tusk (Rich Horton)
