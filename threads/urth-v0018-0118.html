1998-09-07 James Jordan #

Notes that Jorge Luis Borges admired G. K. Chesterton and that Gene Wolfe read Chesterton extensively, suggesting a link among the three writers. Offers no close textual evidence, relying instead on Wolfe's own remark made to the poster about having read all of Chesterton.
informs · author-and-biography

Along Borgesian lines, it is worth remembering that Borges was quite a fan of Chesterton, and Wolfe cut his teeth on Chesterton, claiming (to me) to have read everything of Chesterton's.

Nutria (Jim Jordan)
