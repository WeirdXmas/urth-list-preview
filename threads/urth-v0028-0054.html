Riffs on a cross-posting traffic sign, then tabulates astronomical and seasonal dates for both hemispheres to argue that Hallowe'en can fall in October while the season is early Spring. Well now! Lookee here! We =can= have Hallowe'en in October (religious calendar locked in astronomical sense rather than seasonal) and be early Spring (where other cultures have had ghost holidays).

Naturally, this forces the Feast of Cath. A (if also locked in astronomical sense) into late Spring!

Re: Wolf Moon, here's a tidbit--Wolfe used it as a pseudonym of a genre writer in "From the Desk of Gilmer C. Merton."
