Underrated in Disch's book

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-04-27 to 1999-04-27

1999urthMichael Andre-Driussi

1999-04-27 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Points out that Tom Disch's recent book on science fiction dismisses The Book of the New Sun as Renaissance-fair fare and misspells Wolfe's name, then jokingly suggests blaming editors or Disch's inconsistency. Responds to Disch's praise quoted in the earlier post by noting the apparent contradiction between the two evaluations, with a wry tone.
chats

alga,

Innnteresting. IIRC, in his recent book on sf, THE DREAMS STUFF ARE MADE OF [did I get that right?], Tom Disch gave short shrift to TBOTNS, calling it "Renaissance faire" something or other, and even managed to mangle the author's name in such a way that it looked as though the brave little toaster-cum-copy editor couldn't read a scrawl in the margin, or couldn't comprehend the Master's Voice over the telephoney.

Easy enough to chalk this up to last minute changes--blame it on the editors!

Or, what the hey, Disch contains multitudes, and consistency is the _____ of small minds.

Or, then again, maybe "Renaissance fair" something-or-other is just another way of saying "couth, intelligence . . ." etc.

=mantis=
