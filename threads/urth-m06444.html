Introduces the poster as a 29-year-old science fiction and fantasy reader from Saint Louis who discovered Gene Wolfe through The Knight and has since read about twelve of his books. Shares a link to his own Wolfe fan site that compiles scattered online sources, answering the earlier poster's question about who the list's contributors are.
informs

I'm 29 and from Saint Louis. Always been a Sci Fi fan since I saw Star Wars as a kid. Grew up playing RPGs and got into the fantasy genre a bit as well. It wasn't until about 4 years ago that I picked up my first Gene Wolfe book on a whim "The Knight", and really enjoyed it. I've since downed about 12 of his books and have 5 or 6 more on the shelf waiting to be read. I've been around nerd and geeks my whole life and currently work with a whole lot of them at a computer store and I was the one who discovered and started introducing my friends to Wolfe's work. I'd say I have about 3 converts so far. But it was odd that such a great writer was so barely known. Which is why one has to admit that even though he's good, he's not for everyone.

I hear what you are saying about the lack of Wolfe content online. When I first started typing "Gene Wolfe" into the Google search box it was a little disappointing at first. Truthfully though, there really is a lot of content out there (and a lot that used to be around but is lost) it's just scattered about in little pieces. I ended up starting my own Wolfe Fan Site just so I could compile all the different Gene Wolfe sources I found online over the past few years. Check out http://hoofandhide.wordpress.com . You can search for content via keywords (powered by Magnolia) and I recently put up a gallery of book covers (powered by Flickr). Maybe you'll find it helpful when trying to dig up more info on Gene.

Ryan B
