1999-12-02 Ron Hale-Evans #

Asks when the GURPS New Sun role-playing books will be released, noting the publisher's website said December and asking whether that schedule still holds. Addressed to Mantis, presumably someone involved with the project, and seeks a more precise release date.
questions

Mantis, you've done an admirable job of not bragging onlist, but I am dying to know when the GURPS New Sun books will be out. I went to the Steve Jackson site, and they said it is due in December. Is it still on course? Can you give us a better sense of the actual date (before Christmas, after Christmas, etc.)?

Ron H-E
