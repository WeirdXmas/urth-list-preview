Sun Series---A Newbish Question

Urth list · 3 messages · 2003-06-06 to 2003-06-06

2003urthThe Book of the New SunWilliam H. AnsleySam KeyesTyreal
The thread: Tyreal asked newcomer-style which order the Sun Series books should be read in, naming the Urth, New Sun, Long Sun and Short Sun books. Sam Keyes replied with the order he believes they were published and is reading them, noting he is an undergraduate on a summer project and promising more questions. William Ansley argued the reading order cannot be settled as posed, listing the original novels of each sub-series and related works such as Book of Days, the Endangered Species stories and The Fifth Head of Cerberus, which some say share the Sun universe. The thread stayed a short exchange of information with no real disagreement.
  • Tyreal: asks for the proper reading order of the Sun books as a newcomer
  • Sam Keyes: suggests reading in publication order
  • William Ansley: says there is no single intended reading order and lists the books and related works

2003-06-06 Sam Keyes #

Answers Tyreal's question about reading order by listing the Sun series books in the order he believes they were published and is reading them. Adds that he is an undergraduate doing a summer project on Wolfe's New Sun and promises more questions later, offering no argument beyond the order listed.
answers

Greetings.

I hesitate to toss myself into public, but here goes. I can't really say in what order they're MEANT to be read, but they were, I think, published in this order, and this is the order in which I have and am reading them:

The Book of the New Sun:
Shadow and Claw (Shadow of the Torturer and Claw of the Conciliator) Sword and Citadel (Sword of the Lictor and Citadel of the Autarch)

The Urth of the New Sun

The Book of the Long Sun:
Litany of the Long Sun (Nightside and Lake)
Epiphany of the Long Sun (Cald=E9 and Exodus)

The Book of the Short Sun:
On Blue's Waters
In Green's Jungles
Return to the Whorl

Forgive my ignorance if I have misplaced something or misunderstood.

And, as a brief introduction to the list, I ought to say that I am an undergraduate currently doing a summer project (i.e., my school was kind (or stupid) enough to give me money) on Wolfe--particularly the New Sun.=20 So once I finish wallowing through the stuff I'm doing now, perhaps I'll have a question or two for you people.

Sam

2003-06-06 William H. Ansley #

Argues that the question of the order the Sun books are meant to be read cannot be answered as posed, and lists all the original novels of the Book of the New Sun, Book of the Long Sun and Book of the Short Sun with their combined editions. Adds related books, including Book of Days, Endangered Species stories and The Fifth Head of Cerberus, said by some to share the Sun universe.
informs

"Sam Keyes" <[email]> wrote (in part):

Replying to Tyreal <[email]> who wrote

I am a strong proponent of reading books in the order they were published,= =20
no matter what the order of "internal chronology" is, so I agree with the=20 reading list order given by Sam Keyes, above. Frankly, I am not sure the=20 question, "In what order are Gene Wolfe Sun books meant to be read?" is=20 answerable in that form.

Here is a list of the titles of all the original novels in the Sun series=20 (so far) with some extras:

~The Book of the New Sun~
_The Shadow of the Toturer_
_The Claw of the Conciliator_
_The Sword of the Lictor_
_The Citadel of the Autarch_

(_Shadow and Claw_ combines novels 1 & 2 above.)
(_Sword and Citadel_ combines novels 3 & 4 above.)

_The Urth of the New Sun_

~The Book of the Long Sun~
_Nightside the Long Sun_
_Lake of the Long Sun_
_Calde of the Long Sun_
_Exodus from the Long Sun_

(_Litany of the Long Sun_ combines novels 1 & 2 above.)
(_Epiphany of the Long Sun_ combines novels 3 & 4 above.)

~The Book of the Short Sun~
_On Blue's Oceans_
_In Green's Jungles_
_Return to the Whorl_

Related books:
_Gene Wolfe's Book of Days_
(includes _The Castle of the Otter_, a collection of essays about the Book= =20
of the New Sun)
Endangered Species
(includes the stories, "The Cat" and "The Map," generally considered to be= =20
set in the same universe as the Sun books)

Possibly related books:
_The Fifth Head of Cerberus_
(said by some to be set in the same universe as the Sun books) _Storeys from the Old Hotel_
(includes the story, "A Solar Labyrinth," said by some to be a metafiction= =20
on the Book of the New Sun)

William Ansley
