1997-05-28 Wesley Parsons (7065) #

Proposes a link between the word Orilla in The Book of the New Sun and the Buenos Aires slum where the tango originated, noting that orilla means shore in Spanish. Raises a possible connection between Viron and Nessus, assuming Nessus is Buenos Aires, without citing any passage from the books.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Orilla derives from Buenos Aires slum; Nessus is Buenos Aires

I was watching a documentary on the Tango (the dance) last night, and it was mentioned that the Tango originated in the Orilla, a slum in Buenos Aires.

Orilla means shore or bank in Spanish, and I'd assume the BA Orilla is next to the Rio de la Plata.

Possible link between Viron and Nessus (if one accepts that Nessus is BA)?

Wes
