2006-05-22 Rob Davies replying to Rob Davies #

Recounts the poster's earlier experience meeting Gene Wolfe at Readercon, where he froze while asking for autographs, and says he hopes to do better at Balticon. A personal, social reply to Rob Davies's joke about stammering before an idolized author, with no argument about Wolfe's books.
chats

Hopefully, I will do better this time around.

Rob

2006-05-22 peter heyneman replying to Transentient #

Lists the Balticon convention schedule for Gene Wolfe, naming a Saturday special guest of honor appearance, a Sunday panel with Neil Gaiman, and a Monday kaffeeklatsch. Responds to Transentient's earlier guess about Wolfe's availability, and offers to pass on questions from list members and meet local readers.
informs

SATURDAY:

Neil Gaiman/Gene Wolfe Panel
MONDAY:
