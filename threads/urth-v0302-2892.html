2003-02-07 Fernando Q. Gouvea #

Agrees with Mantis that the narrator of the Castaway story seems female, and suggests this explains why the castaway opens up to the narrator while resisting official debriefers. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's quoted line questioning whether everyone agrees the narrator is female, offering a reader's first impression rather than textual evidence.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Castaway narrator is female

I must say I had the same reaction as Mantis, that the narrator was female. Perhaps this is why the castaway would tend to open up to the narrator while resisting his official debriefers.
