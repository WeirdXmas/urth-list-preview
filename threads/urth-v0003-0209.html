1997-08-08 Alice Turner #

Proposes tracking the names used in Wolfe's books by consulting Native American and Inuit dictionaries, which the poster lacks access to. Reports that a poetry search for a quoted line, suspected to come from Paradise Lost, turned up nothing, and thanks another poster for the search.
proposes · source-and-allusion

Oh, and it really would be fun to track those names. We need a Native American dictionary and an Inuit one to start---anyone have access to a university library? I don't, and the NY Public Library reading room is closed for this year and the next. It seems unlikely that a branch library would have such a thing---I'll check on the website.

Oh, rana, thank you for the poetry search. I thought the middle one might be Milton too, with the "he" being either Adam or Satan in Paradise Lost. No dice (and it took forever because PL hasn't yet been html'd). I'll report if I find it.

-alga-
