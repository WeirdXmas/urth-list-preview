Themes: Death and resurrection

1998-07-22 John Bishop #

Complains about a subscriber who keeps posting mass quotations from the digest and says he has written to the offender about unsubscribing. Then asks whether a prison with vast grey walls, read as shrouds and shells, makes the story another tale of a dead person inside his own corpse.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: The prison in the story is a shroud and the narrator is a dead man inside his own corpse

Fellow sufferers--

I've sent mail to "Internet Megantic", telling
how to unsubscribe. Let's hope that stops the
mass posting of mass quotes.

Given that directions on unsubscribing are posted
at the foot of _every_ digest, arrgghh, words
fail me.

Ok, back to the fun stuff: IoDD, if a prison has
vast grey walls, I think of shrouds and shells.
Is this just another story about a dead person
inside his own corpse?

-John
