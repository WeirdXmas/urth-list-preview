New BotNS cover design

Urth list · 5 messages · 2006-11-23 to 2006-11-24

2006urthThe Book of the New SunMalcolm McGuganJesper SvedbergJames MachinMatthew DeLuca
The thread: Jesper Svedberg announced Irene Gallo's new Tor cover design for the next reprint of Shadow & Claw and Sword & Citadel, which keeps Don Maitz's art but adopts a more restrained layout and typography. James Machin then argued that a serious edition of The Book of the New Sun should avoid pulpy fantasy action art, criticizing the Maitz and Fantasy Masterworks covers and praising the Arrow editions, and proposed abstract or moon-landing imagery instead. Svedberg disputed that the series is pulp, naming the Bruce Pennington and Swedish covers as the best, and called the Masterworks covers awful. The exchange stayed at the level of personal taste, with no resolution beyond the two posters' differing preferences.
  • James Machin: the New Sun deserves non-pulp cover art, such as abstract design or a moon-landing painting, and Arrow editions are the model
  • Jesper Svedberg: the series is not pulp; Bruce Pennington and Swedish covers are the best, and Fantasy Masterworks covers are awful

2006-11-24 James Machin replying to Jesper Svedberg #

Argues that a good edition of The Book of the New Sun should avoid pulpy fantasy action cover art, criticizing the Don Maitz and Fantasy Masterworks covers and praising the Arrow editions. Proposes an abstract design or a moon landing painting hanging on a medieval wall instead. Replies to Jesper Svedberg's thread on the new cover; the post offers personal taste rather than textual evidence.
proposes

I really hope to see a decently design edition of BotNS one day - one that doesn't rely on pulpy fantasy action scenes. I think the Don Maitz art is pretty naf and the Fantasy Masterworks ed is hardly better. The Arrow eds. cover art was much much better IMHO, but what I think would be great would be something along the lines of a good Penguin cover design - my personal choice would be something more abstract, or maybe something involving the moon landing painting hanging jarringly on a mediaeval wall.
