Deaths of Severian

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-01-29 to 1998-01-29

1998urthThe Book of the New SunJames Jordan

Themes: Death and resurrection, Water, the sea and drowning

1998-01-29 James Jordan #

Argues that Severian cannot die and so survives his drowning in the Undine's river without anyone raising him, and reads the scene as one of many baptismal images in the early Book of the New Sun. Responds to newt's proposal that the Undine saves Severian, offering an alternative account of why he lives and pointing to the baptismal and eucharistic scenes.
disputes · allegory-and-typology · theology-and-religion · theories: Severian is unkillable and needs no rescuer; Early Severian scenes are baptismal and eucharistic

No, the old Nutria has not disappeared. He's just been busy finishing a couple of books, and has let things pile up.
My read of this event was that Severian was under water for a long time, long enough to kill anyone else, except that Severian cannot die. I don't think anyone else has to raise him up; he's just unkillable, being so full of life himself that he can even raise others to life again (Triskele, etc.). This event is, btw, in case you didn't notice it (being as how you're an atheist and all), one of numerous baptismal scenes in the early part of the Sev Quintet. There are numerous eucharistic scenes also.

Nutria
