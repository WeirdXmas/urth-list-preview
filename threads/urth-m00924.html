2005-01-11 Alexander, Bryan #

Reports that Bruce Franklin's Vietnam war anthology reprints a 1968 Galaxy page with two opposing petitions by science fiction writers, one urging withdrawal and one urging continued fighting. Proposes that comparing the two roughly equal lists would make a useful teaching tool for science fiction history, responding to a thread on SF political conservatism.
informs

Bruce Franklin's collection of Vietnam war writing has a reprint from a 1968 issue of Galaxy, which includes two petitions on that war. One is a group of writers urging the United States to withdraw, while the other recommends the nation remain in the fighting. It's fascinating to read the two lists (which are roughly equal in size) - in fact, it would make a great teaching tool for science fiction history!
