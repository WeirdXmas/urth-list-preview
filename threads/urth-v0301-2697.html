2003-01-20 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Recommends Tim Powers to readers who like Charles Williams, calling Powers a better prose stylist than Williams and a close heir to the Williams tradition. Offers no Wolfe content; it is a book recommendation drawn from a discussion of Williams's mystical fiction.
chats

For those who do like CW, may I recommend (if you do not already know his work) Tim Powers? The closest living writer to "carrying on the Williams tradition," except that he is, in my humble, a better prose stylist than Williams ever=20 managed to be. Williams was a fine poet, but in the writing of fiction ... I wish I knew who first said it, but I've always agreed with this: "He wrote potboilers, but he=20 always boiled an honest pot."=20

Anywho, Powers is an astonishing writer ...

--Blattid
