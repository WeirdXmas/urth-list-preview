Themes: Death and resurrection

2006-02-21 Duncan Truter #

Asks three questions about The Book of the New Sun: what happened at the Piteous Gate riot and whether a party member caused it, what was awakened in the cave, and how many times Severian dies. Lists possible deaths (drowning as a child, the cart with Agia, the avern) and says he has already searched the list without finding answers.
questions · close-textual-reading

Hi

I just finished reading the botns again, and I have a few questions. I've searched for these in the list already, and couldn't find the answers.

1: what happened at the piteous gate? there was a riot, there were clone soldiers...?

did a member of severian's party cause all of that?

2: what was awakened in the cave?

3: how many times does severian die? all I can think of right now is: -drowned as a child
-possibly died in the cart with agia
-killed by the avern

can't remember any more, but I remember someone mentioning on this list that he was killed by a big gun in the citadel? I haven't re read urth of the new sun yet though, so not asking about that, yet.

Thanks for your help. (Reading island of Doctor Death and other stories and other stories at the moment)

2006-02-21 Maru Dubshinki replying to Duncan Truter #

Answers Duncan Truter's questions about The Book of the New Sun by reporting two theories about the riot at the Piteous Gate, one blaming Hethor with a beast and one claiming the guards were after Jonas. Points to a death in The Urth of the New Sun, Severian's fall down the shaft in the ship, as one answer to how many times he dies.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Hethor caused the Piteous Gate riot with a beast; Guards at Piteous Gate were after Jonas

Some think hethrir caused the riot with a beast. I've also seen suggestions that the guards were trying to get at Jonas.

The UotNS has a couple deaths, notably falling down the shaft in the ship.

~Maru
