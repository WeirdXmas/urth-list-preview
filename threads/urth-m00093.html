Observes that Gaiman's Coraline and Pullman's Dark Materials books remind the poster of C.S. Lewis, and notes this is interesting given reported authors' comments about Lewis. Greetings:

When I read Gaiman's "Coraline" (I think that's how he
spelled it), I was reminded very much of C.S. Lewis.

The same with Pullman and the "Dark Materials" books.
I was reminded of Lewis.

Given some of the comments reported here from those
authors about Lewis, et al, it's kind of interesting
that I got that reaction (I read the books before I
read those comments).
