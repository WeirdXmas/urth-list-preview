Advance word on Green

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2000-01-27 to 2000-01-27

2000whorlThe Book of the Short SunNicholas Gevers

2000-01-27 Nicholas Gevers #

Relays a private acquaintance's advance impressions of In Green's Jungles, praising it as less sad than the earlier Short Sun volumes and promising a strong finish. Notes that the book's chronology is more disarranged than before and full of mysteries, quoting a reader who saw an unpublished typescript.
informs

An acquaintance of mine, who shall remain nameless,
has the following to say about IN GREEN'S JUNGLES:

...I've had the
opportunity to read it in a galley-ized typescript,
and it's great, and in
a strange way less sad than On Blue's Waters or
Strange Travelers, though
full of awful events; all shaping up to a fantastic
finish in the third
volume--but also very disarranged chronologically,
more so than ever, and
full of mysteries and conundrums as you would expect...
