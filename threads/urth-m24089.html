Some possibly old hat Qs from someone who has been offlist for nearly a decade

Urth list · 7 messages · 2013-08-05 to 2013-08-06

The thread: A returning list member asked whether Wolfe had new fiction in progress or had retired, and whether Gate of Horn had been discussed at length on the list. Marc Aramini answered that The Land Across was due in November and that A Borrowed Man was in progress, reporting from his own correspondence with Wolfe that a few hundred pages were drafted but that personal difficulties, including his wife's care and heart surgery, had slowed work. Aramini also praised Gate of Horn as a reference work while critiquing it against Borski's and offering errata privately. Antonin Scriabin then asked for more detail on A Borrowed Man, and the thread ends with that open question rather than a resolved debate.
  • Brian Doherty: asks whether Wolfe has new work or has retired, and whether Gate of Horn was discussed before
  • Marc Aramini: reports Wolfe's upcoming and in-progress novels and his personal difficulties, and praises Gate of Horn with reservations
  • Antonin Scriabin: asks whether A Borrowed Man is far along or still in early stages

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Doubles, twins and clones

Reports that Wolfe's upcoming novel The Land Across is due in November and that he is working on A Borrowed Man, answering Brian Doherty's question about whether new Wolfe work is in progress. Gives the publication timing as the basis, noting the manuscript was turned in around early 2012 and that publishing has slowed except for sure-thing name sales.
informs

Publishing has also slowed down considerably in the last few years save for sure thing name sales. Land across has been turned in since I think before March 2012 or thereabouts.

2013-08-05 Marc Aramini replying to Decanus1284 #

Comments on Gate of Horn, the Lexicon Urthus-style reference book by Andre-druissi, praising its conservative conclusions and useful timelines while critiquing it against Borski's work. Notes Roy's influence on its presentation, faults the rough draft for not identifying Silk's staff as a liana, and offers errata privately.
supports · author-and-biography

Well ... I really like Andre-druissi. His lexicon urthus is great. My issue with gate of horn is pretty simple - while Borski goes way too far and ignores theme in ephemeral conclusions, this one seems very conservative in drawing conclusions but will still posit some strange interpretational stances. The time lines and the long sun commentary are very useful. I can see Roy's influence on its surface presentation. I feel the biggest omission in his rough draft was not acknowledging that Silk's staff was a liana. I offered a whole lot of advice on a very late proof copy but I don't know if any was included. ( have not checked the final version yet). If anyone wants that list of errata-comments I made I will email it off list- I don't want it online anywhere in case Michael decides to do a second edition.

I am just glad there are books published on Wolfe scholarship.

2013-08-06 Marc Aramini replying to Antonin Scriabin #

Reports that Wolfe is a few hundred pages into a draft manuscript, though not finished, and that his personal difficulties, including legal issues over his wife's care and heart surgery, have slowed his writing. Relays the poster's own correspondence with Wolfe and links the new work's theme to The Doctor of Death Island, which concerns simulated authors.
informs · author-and-biography

Not too much information - the last several years have been interfering to some degree with Wolfe's ability to write without distractions.? Legal issues over his wife's care situation, his own heart surgery several years ago, and other issues that come with being in his early 80s probably prevent a rapid pace, but it is at least a few hundred pages into a draft manuscript now, but I don't think a first draft is completed.? I will need to check further correspondence with him to see if he gave any more specifics, but it does seem to resonate with The Doctor of Death Island.? A synthesis of all available texts has allowed simulacrums of authors themselves to be created and interacted with.? I'd say it is in the mid stages.?
