1999-11-08 Michael Straight #

Defends the disputed opening sentence of John Clute's review as a matter of taste, saying it works well. Complains that the review gives away too many spoilers for the other series, despite being pitched to newcomers, and suggests the first sentence may parody Wolfe's style.
disputes · close-textual-reading

Different tastes. I like that sentence. Some of the others are overblown, but I think that one works just fine.

As I said before, my main beef with the review is that it contains way too many spoilers for the other series (or other novels, to use Clute's term) for an interview claiming to be fore a newcomer to Wolfe ("You can start here").

I assumed the first sentence of the review was a parody/pastiche of Wolfe's style.

-Rostrum
