Long, cranky post

Whorl list · 3 messages · 2001-02-14 to 2001-02-16

2001whorlKevin J. MaroneyAlice TurnerPeter Westlake
The thread: Readers debated whether the ending of The Book of the Long Sun is hasty and cheap, especially Jahlee's exit and the fate of the women characters. Akt argued the ending was an authorial shortcut and that Jahlee and Krait were dismissed, which Ansley, the Myopic Bear and vizcacha disputed. Kevin J. Maroney defended Remora getting the emotional climax, reframing her as a character whose role shifts at the end in reply to Alga's view that she went too far. Spectacled Bear defended the resolution of the protagonist's identity against a claim that Silk, Horn and Jahlee are killed off in Return to the Whorl, noting partial agreement and a resemblance to past speculation. The thread ends as a set of separate disagreements rather than a settled view.
  • akt: The Long Sun ending is cheap and hasty, dismissing Jahlee, Dorcas, Jolenta and Merryn
  • Kevin J. Maroney: Remora's emotional climax at the end of the series is a successful choice
  • Alga: Remora was entertaining but her role was pushed too far
  • Spectacled Bear: the identity resolution in Return to the Whorl is convincing and well integrated
  • Ansley, Myopic Bear and vizcacha: Jahlee and Krait are not dismissed and matter to the story

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Women, sex and gender, Suffering and sacrifice

2001-02-14 Kevin J. Maroney #

Argues that Wolfe gives the emotional climax of the series to Remora, a character who had mostly served as comic relief, and calls this a successful choice. Responds to Alga's remark that Remora was entertaining but went too far, reframing the same character as someone whose role shifts at the end.
disputes · genre-and-craft

I think it's amazing that Wolfe put the emotional climax of the entire series into the hands of Remora, who up to that point had basically been comic relief. And pulled it off.
