2011-12-15 Lee Berman #

Argues that Gene Wolfe's Book of the New Sun draws broadly on fairy tales and sword-and-sorcery elements, so demanding a specific Sleeping Beauty parallel like briars is a straw man. Responds to Gerry Quinn by listing the many myths, creatures and fantasy motifs Wolfe invokes, and dismisses Quinn's demand as derision of fantasy that fails against Wolfe.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Book of the New Sun draws on fairy tales and sword-and-sorcery

Gerry you are inventing straw men to knock down again. Nobody has said Doras is "about"

Briar Rose, the Sleeping Beauty. The suggestion was simply and broadly that BotNS has

included elements of fairy tales and S & S stories in it. No "briars" are needed to

make that assertion.

I can understand an atheist's desire to expose and demystify all of society's delusions

regarding faith and myth and superstition. But we are not in the real world here. We are

in Gene Wolfe's world.

This is an author who has invoked vampires, dragons, giants, manbeasts, witches, ghosts,

monsters, Greek myths, Roman myths, Norse myths, S. American myths, Asian myths, sirens,

mermaids, nixies, kelpies, fairies, angels, wizards, knights, ladies in lakes, shape

shifting, miraculous healings and resurrections..and,...whew!

It might be easier to make a list of the fairy tale elements in human history Wolfe has NOT

invoked (werewolves? heh). But against that massive honking pile of massed fantasy elements

we are submitted the argument, "no briars"?

Such derision of fantasy may work in the real world but not against Wolfe. Not in the worlds

he has created. Unless you are speaking strictly for yourself. If so then, no problem.

2011-12-16 Gerry Quinn replying to Lee Berman #

Argues that Lee Berman's claim that Valeria and Typhon have Sleeping Beauty elements is too loose to count as evidence, since criteria that reject nothing make a hypothesis worthless. Responds to Berman's reply to Quinn's earlier challenge about whether Dorcas's correspondence with Sleeping Beauty adds anything to the fairy-tale reading of the Book of the New Sun.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Dorcas corresponds to Sleeping Beauty; Valeria and Typhon have Sleeping Beauty elements

From: Lee Berman

But Valeria and Typhon have elements of Sleeping Beauty too, if you want to define ?elements? so loosely. This is a point I feel the need to make repeatedly: if someone?s criteria for rejecting hypotheses are so flaccid as to be non-existent, that person?s hypotheses will be worth nothing.

- Gerry Quinn
