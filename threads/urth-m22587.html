2012-01-19 James Wynn #

Announces that Gene Wolfe will appear on a panel with Cory Doctorow at Capricon 32 in Wheeling, Illinois, and notes that Wolfe is not mobbed at Chicago conventions, so conversation is possible. Advises fans not to bring him books, citing Wolfe's remark that he rarely reads for pleasure and a book forger in Peace.
informs

On a related note, at Capricon 32 in Wheeling, IL (just outside of Chicago), there's going to be a panel with Gene and Cory Doctorow (excellent!):
http://www.kristine-smith.com/index.php/2012/01/capricon-schedule/

Ironically, Gene has not treated as a superstar at the Chicago cons...which makes the Chicago Literary Hall of Fame event so significant. So it is possible to actually have a normal conversation with him.
(hint) Don't give him a book. He gets these all the time, and he says he doesn't get a chance to read for pure pleasure anymore (ala the book forger in "Peace").
