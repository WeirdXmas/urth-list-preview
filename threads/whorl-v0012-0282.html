RTW and the Plan of Pas (Mora, Fava, Mucor)

2001whorlDennis G. Berdanis

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2001-02-18 Dennis G. Berdanis #

Speculates that Mucor, Mora and Fava were an early stage of Pas's plan to give humans astral travel for the war against the inhumi, arguing that a few possessing Mucors would be too few to matter. Builds the case from the absence of inhumi astral spies on Green and from the question of whether Horn and Jahlee's journey shows inhumi can travel alone, then asks how astral travel differs from possession.
proposes · myth-and-structure · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Mora and Fava were the start of Pas's plan to give humans astral travel

Just chewing over some comments others have made and trying to tie them into Pas plan for conquering Blue/Green.

One person commented on Mucor not being special anymore if Mora/Fava or anyone could possess someone.

My first thought is that one Mucor or even a few Mucors would not be of much advantage in the war against the inhumi. Perhaps Mucor was an advance guard who could be used until the average citizens learned how to astral travel.

I'm assuming the Neighbors could astral travel without any assistance, human or inhumi. My next assumption is that the inhumi have learned to astral travel but only with the assistance of a Neighbor or human. Otherwise the inhumi on Green would be astral traveling to Blue as spies, advance guard, etc. and I saw no evidence of this. This discounts the theory that Horn could astral travel because he was an inhumu. He and Jahlee made a journey by themselves if I remember right and one of them would have to be not inhumi.

So my guess is that Mora/Fava is the beginning of what Pas planned for all humans in their war agianst the inhumi. Not that it did the Neighbors any good, but every advantage is better than no advantage.

And I'm not sure if I'm confusing astral travel with possession. What exactly is the difference. Is possession just astral traveling into someone else?

Dennis/Endy
