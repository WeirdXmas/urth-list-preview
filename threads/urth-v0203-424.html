2002-03-22 Jeff Prucher #

Adds publication details for Wolfe's uncollected 'Joe and Me', noting its Locus issue and a 1999 Carroll & Graf reprint in the anthology Dancing with the Dark. Describes it as a memorial piece about artist Joe Mayhew and asks whether anyone knows more about it, responding to John Barach's list of uncollected stories.
informs

----much snipping ensues----

Locus issue #474, (vol. 45 No. 1). This is a moving remembrance of SF artist Joe Mayhew, and is available online:
http://www.locusmag.com/2000/Issues/07/WolfeOnMayhew.html

Also published in the US by Carroll & Graf, 1999.
The Locus index lists this as biographical. Dancing with the Dark is described as an "anthology of memoirs, subtitled 'TRUE encounters with the paranormal by masters of the macabre'." Does anyone know any more about this piece?

-Jeff Prucher

2002-03-22 Michael Straight #

Shares a passage from Wolfe's obituary remembrance of artist Joe Mayhew describing a walking stick carved with eleven men's faces and an inscription, and jokingly wonders whether Wolfe can make any of the faces talk. Responds to Jeff Prucher's post that linked the Locus obituary, so the post is a light aside rather than an analysis of the fiction.
chats

From that obit:
---
Thousands of fans have seen his cartoons, but I suspect that only a few have seen his wood carvings. Perhaps the most remarkable is a cane decorated with leaves and feathers and stars, and the faces of 11 different men. The inscription at the bottom reads, ''December 1993/This osage orange walking stick was made for Gene R. Wolfe by Joe Mayhew.'' Someday I may let you touch it. But only if you're very, very good. ---

I wonder if Wolfe can make any of the faces talk?

-Rostrum
