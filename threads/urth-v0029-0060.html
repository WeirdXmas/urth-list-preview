Reports that a rumor about Severian appearing in the Short Sun books has no clear source, offering a personal impression that Horn will incorporate aspects of Severian as he did with Silk. Responds to James Russell, who asked where the rumor came from and found no archival support for it.
answers · theories: Horn incorporates aspects of Severian

Yes, this has been around for quite a while, though, as with most rumors, it seems to have no definite source. My impression is that Horn is somehow going to incorporate aspects of Sev, as he has of Silk. Please don't take that as more than an individual impression, however.

-alga
