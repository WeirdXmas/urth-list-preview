2009-04-24 Don Doggett replying to hammstu #

Off-topic chat about the poster's recent reading of noir comics and novels, including 100 Bullets, Criminal, Private Midnight and Sharp Teeth, with no discussion of Wolfe. Replies jokingly to Adam Thornton's question about whether the list is quiet because of a werewolf, mentioning werewolf noir.
chats

I've been on a strange noir kick, re-reading 100 bullets (the series is finally done, sniff), Ed Brubaker's Criminal, and Kris Saknussemm's Private Midnight (which is fantastic until the last thirty pages, but wraps things up too neatly at the end) I thought it was going to be like Sharp Teeth (speaking of werewolves) but they have nothing in common really except the noir angle.
