pdf to epub for eReaders

Urth list · 1 messages · 2010-03-31 to 2010-03-31

2010urthSon of Witz

2010-03-31 Son of Witz #

Asks for a free tool to convert PDF files to ePub for an iPad and reports that a web converter worked on a PDF copy of The Shadow of the Torturer. Also says they plan to buy the iPad mainly as a digital sketchpad and asks readers not to debate the device itself.
informs

so, I'm one of those geeks getting an iPad ASAP.
I have tons of pdfs, and wanted to find a converter for ePub format. There are lots of paid apps, but I found this free one:
http://www.epub2go.com/Web/default.aspx
seems to work. I converted that ShadowOfTheTorture.pdf that's been floating around the internet into a .epub format and it seems to work fine. (no I'm not uploading it for anyone).

this just seems easier than having to use different software to manage the libraries of different format digital books.

I don't want to start an iPad discussion, so spare me the "you don't need a big ipod touch" arguments please. iPad + Brushes.app is a combination I've been wanting for over a decade. I'd consider buying it just for the single use as a digital sketchpad. anything else is gravy.
