Urth's Green

2001-02-12 Dan Rabin #

Argues that the green object in The Book of the New Sun called the Green is the Moon, Lune, which appears green because forests were planted on it in the forgotten past. Answers Matt's question about the phrase "their Green is larger and brighter than ours" by explaining that the speaker uses "Green" for any bright green celestial body, as "moon" is used for planetary satellites.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: The Green is Lune, the Moon, forested in the past

Matt asks,

I recall that what is said is "their Green is larger and brighter than ours". The reference is to the Moon, called "Lune" in _The Book of the New Sun_, and appearing green because forests were planted on it in the forgotten past. The speaker is just calling all bright green celestial objects "Greens", as we call all satellites of planets "moons".

-- Dan Rabin
