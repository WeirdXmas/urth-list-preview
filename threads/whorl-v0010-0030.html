1999-09-24 Dave Lebling #

Asks which large city nicknamed "Second City" Gene Wolfe lives near, linking it to Urbasecundus, the city where a rival version of Silk's story is said to be written in On Blue's Waters. Notes the poster has read two chapters and has several mysteries in mind, including one from the Book of the Long Sun.
questions · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Urbasecundus refers to Wolfe's home city

Horn runs through other versions of Silk's story (Horn's is _The Book of the Long Sun_, commonly known as _The Book of Silk_). One he mentions that he hasn't read was written by a man in the city of Urbasecundus, in English "Second City." Now, what large city with that sobriquet does Wolfe live near?

(I've read two chapters and already have several mysteries to think about, and at least one from tBotLS elucidated -- more on that later.)

--Dave Lebling
(aka vizcacha)

1999-09-25 Alastair Reynolds #

Asks where Gene Wolfe lives, suggesting Chicago as a guess tied to Urbasecundus, the "Second City" in Horn's Book of the Long Sun. Grounds the guess on a shelf detail in The Fifth Head of Cerberus, where a book called Monday or Tuesday sits beside a Trotsky assassination book by Bernard Wolfe, implying an alphabetical link to Wolfe.
questions · puzzle-solving · author-and-biography · theories: Wolfe's own name appears in The Fifth Head of Cerberus

So where does GW live? Is it Chicago?

Funnily enough, (because I only noticed it yesterday) in the Fifth Head of Cerberus, he mentions a book called Monday or Tuesday, leaning against a book on the assassination of Trotsky. Since the Trotsky book must have been written by Trotsky's bodyguard, Bernard "Limbo" Wolfe, I presume we're meant to conclude that the other book, if they're filed alphabetically, is by GW...

Al
