Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

2003-09-10 Don Doggett #

Proposes that the dying sun of Urth may contain a black hole draining energy, and speculates that this energy flows to Yesod to power a white fountain. Offers no textual citation; the idea is raised in a light, self-deprecating tone and ends by asking whether the new sun is simply the old sun returned.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: Urth's sun contains a black hole draining energy; Draining solar energy powers a white fountain in Yesod

Hi everyone

One of the benefits of my soul killing job (it's not quite that bad) is that I have time to think of things like this. If Urth's sun has a black hole in the center of it draining energy (but not sucking in the whole solar system?) all of that energy must be going somewhere. Perhaps to Yesod to form the energy necessary for a white fountain? What a ripoff, I mean thanks for nothing Hiero's. What do you think, Is the new sun just the old sun given back?

Don

2003-09-11 Chris P #

Proposes that the Urth sun's black hole energy flow could imply Severian's white fountain acts as a black hole for the Hieros, causing their sun to decline in a repeating cycle. Responds to a quoted earlier post by Chris speculating that the solar energy drained by the black hole feeds Yesod and the white fountain, reasoning through how the cosmic cycles would connect.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: White fountain acts as black hole for Hieros; Energy drained from Urth sun feeds Yesod

This question actually makes a bit of difference as far as the overall interpretation goes, and it occurred to me as well. This could imply that Severian's white fountain would act as a black hole for the Hieros, bringing about a decline of their sun. Which of course could be replenished later on by a white fountain for them, which would in turn dump another black hole in Ushas, etc.
