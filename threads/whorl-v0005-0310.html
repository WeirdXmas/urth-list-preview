1997-05-27 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks Matthew Freestone to explain how a short story can be an 'infundibular', funnel-shaped, version of a Crowley novel or series, citing AEGYPT and the ANTIQUITIES stories as miniatures. Replies to Freestone's point about the novelty and to Hierodule's remark that the history spread differs if it covers only the Age of the Autarch, and says they will watch for the transcript.

Matthew Freestone,

Ah, you caught the NOVELTY that I missed. I thought of it only after I'd sent, thought of sending an addendum, thought again.

"Infundibular" is "funnel-shaped," isn't it? While I've noticed that the short stories in ANTIQUITIES are often "miniatures" of Crowley novels, I confess I can't quite understand how a short can be a "funnel-shaped" version of either a novel (AEGYPT) or a series of novels (AEGYPT the series). Please elaborate! (I don't have NOVELTY, btw & fwiw.)

Hierodule,

Ah, you were there too! Great! Hmmm--as you suggest, that history spread is quite different if it refers only to the duration of the Age of the Autarch rather than the whole period separating Earth from Severian. <Watching for that transcript!>
