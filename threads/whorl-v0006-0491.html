1997-07-02 Jonathan Coxhead #

Corrects an earlier post's description of the Kalevala, saying it is a modern 1835 compilation but rooted in real Finnish folklore, and names Elias Lönnrot as its compiler. Adds that Ian Watson wrote two books inspired by it, then jokingly asks why the subject came up at all.
informs

The Kalevala is not entirely synthetic, though it is of modern composition (1835). The roots are all in "real" Finnish folklore though, and the author (Elias Lönnrot) is a national figure.

Ian Watson wrote 2 (fat!) books inspired by it: _Lucky's_Harvest_ and _The_ _Fallen_Moon_.

Er ... why were we talking about this?
