2014-11-11 Marc Aramini #

Announces a YouTube video in which Marc Aramini discusses Wolfe in relation to literature and literary history, aimed at a general audience rather than Urth readers. Gives the reason for the video and notes that Aramini cut material tying Aristotle and the early purposes of literature to Wolfe.
informs

I realized that I had been using youtube only for exercise videos and physical feats for years (mostly under a different channel titled "felicibusbrevis") when I could actually be talking about something worthwhile, so I made this little video yesterday. Much of this I have said in various posts, but I thought it might be neat for those who don't have time to read some of the posts to have an audio option. It has a slightly more mainstream target audience than the urth mailing list (people somewhat familiar with literary history and criticism but perhaps those who don't necessarily appreciate Wolfe). I cut some stuff out which tied my discussion of Aristotle and the early purposes of literature to Wolfe, as I felt it made this first video too long.

Hope you enjoy it. (Next time I won't wear a shirt of that color.)

http://youtu.be/VKw_xUI6fDE
