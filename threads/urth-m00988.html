Themes: Stories within stories

2005-01-16 Maru Dubshinki replying to Jim Raylor #

Argues that The Fifth Head of Cerberus has three stories set at different times that share one underlying idea, comparing the book to the three heads of Cerberus and to voices in a fugue. Responds to Jim Raylor's first-reading impression of three stories in a double planetary system, offering a unifying view of the book's structure rather than separate tales.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Fifth Head of Cerberus is three stories with one idea

That's an interesting idea. But my impression of cerberus was that it was three stories, three times, but one idea behind all. Somewhat like how Cerberus has three distinct heads, but each is all about cerberus and doggy-ness. Like math transformations, or the voices in a fugue. I look forward to Jim Raynor's per- prosecution.

~Maru

2005-01-16 Jim Raylor #

Argues that The Fifth Head of Cerberus is not three separate stories set in a double planetary system but one continuous narrative told in three different ways, and promises to lay out evidence. Opens with the admission that the claim cannot be proven, recounting the poster's repeated readings and the sense of hidden depths, and frames the argument as a legal case built on a hypothesis about an aleph concealed in a name.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: The Fifth Head of Cerberus is one story told in three ways

I can't prove this.

I have read t5hoc a few times but I started reading it
with Google. A perilous venture.
On first reading I thought it 3 stories set in a
double planetary system and with a few characters and
themes in common... I liked the first one best. I
hadn't read much WOlfe then.
But I felt I had missed something so I re-read it and
was pleased that it revealed more, though I still felt
there were deeper mysteries hidden.
By the third reading I became convinced that this was
not three stories but part of one continuous
narrative.
Now I am losing count... I got to page 9 and then I
had to re-read what I had missed before and for a
while I became stuck as though I had dropped anchor to
find out why I was not moving.

Levi's hypothesis
The matter under discussion is an aleph.
It is concealed in a name.
This is ONE story told in three dissimilar ways.
I cannot prove this, but I shall supply evidence so
that you, dear reader, can make your own mind up on
the case.

The prosecution enters carrying a battered leather
despatch box.
