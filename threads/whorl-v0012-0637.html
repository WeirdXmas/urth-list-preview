2001-04-18 BraveSaintCroix #

Observes that the yellow crossroad signs with a black plus-like mark in The Book of the Long Sun resemble an homage to Quadrifons, the god of crossroads. Offers this as a casual, non-argued observation, noting that Quadrifons is the name often substituted for his real name, with no textual citation given.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Crossroad signs in Long Sun homage Quadrifons

All,

This isn't really a discussion, just an observation that I've made that I thought was cool (and reflects how I'm probably thinking of SS a little too much). Those yellow crossroad signs with a black "sign of addition" before intersections: I've come to thinking of them as sort of a homage to Quadrifons, god of crossroads. At least, that's what they remind me of. Kinda fitting; that's what his name was supposed to be often substituted with, wasn't it? Yeah, my two bits.

-Steve
