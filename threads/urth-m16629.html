The thread: A short, unlabeled thread in which Lee Berman argued that knowledgeable long-time contributors had been driven off the Urth list by a cadre of enforcers, and that he wanted them back. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes agreed, naming Alice K. Turner, Michael Andre-Driussi and Kevin Maroney as examples of departed members. Daniel Petersen answered with good-natured banter, saying he is a newer poster who might be chased off but will stay. No argument about Wolfe's books was made, and the thread ended in social joking rather than resolution.
  • Lee Berman: knowledgeable contributors were driven off by enforcers and should be brought back
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: agrees that Turner, Andre-Driussi and Maroney were driven away and regrets their absence
  • Daniel Petersen: jokes that he is a newer contributor who will not be chased off

2011-12-16 Lee Berman #

Replies to Daniel Petersen's objection to list members having goals, joking about his own reputation and admitting his aim is selfish: to bring back insightful contributors who he feels were driven off by a cadre of enforcers. Grounds this in the list's history over about fifteen years and in gratitude for those who returned, rather than in any reading of Wolfe's books.
chats

Heh, funny. I am always described as the fair and unflappable peacemaker.

Oh it IS weird and creepy. But sometimes ya gotta do what ya gotta do. "Let's just talk about Wolfe" sounds so simple and easy. But, obviously it isn't and it hasn't been from the beginning, some 15 years ago.

Perhaps I should elaborate that my goals are not really for the group as a whole but more selfish and self-serving. There were a number of very intelligent, insightful, well-read contributors here who seemed chased away by a certain cadre of enforcers. I really wanted to be able to interact with them. Some are gone forever I guess, but I've really really appreciated what I've learned from those guys who came back.
