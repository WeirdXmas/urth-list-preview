Cover of Whorl

Whorl list · 3 messages · 2000-12-14 to 2000-12-14

2000whorlThe Book of the Long SunKevin J. MaroneyDan'l Danehy-OakesPaul C Duggan
The thread: Readers asked who or what the figure on the cover of Whorl depicts, with Paul C Duggan proposing that it is not Wolfe but possibly the Mother, a vaguely feminine figure, based only on the image. Kevin J. Maroney rejected that, saying the figure matches no character from Blue, Green, or other Wolfe works and calling the speculation a dead end. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes also disputed the Mother reading, citing a remark by Maroney, quoted by Blattid, that the figure may be a being of the same sort. The thread is short and ends in disagreement with no resolution, leaving the cover figure unidentified.
  • Paul C Duggan: cover figure is not Wolfe and may be the Mother, a vaguely feminine figure
  • Kevin J. Maroney: figure matches no character in the Wolfe works, so the Mother reading is a dead end
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: figure cannot be the Mother, supported by Maroney's remark that it may be a being of the same sort

2000-12-14 Kevin J. Maroney #

States that the figure on the cover of Whorl does not match any character from Blue or Green or other Wolfe works, so it cannot be the Mother. Responds to Paul D.'s suggestion that the figure could be the Mother by saying the speculation is a dead end.
disputes

It isn't. As I said, the character on the cover of _Whorl_ did not appear in _Blue_ or _Green_, or in any other Wolfe work that I'm aware of. (I believe. There might have been a mention that I've forgotten. But I wouldn't have forgotten the Mother.)

I realize that speculation is fun, but this is a dead end.
