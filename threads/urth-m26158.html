Seven American nights and Nadan's failure to recognize the rubaiyat

2014-09-07 Marc Aramini #

Points out a scene in Seven American Nights where the club-footed actor playing Kreton quotes lines about a potter's mistake from the Rubaiyat after a viewing of Mary Rose, and Nadan shows no sign of recognizing the source. Notes that this comes before the replacement point David Tallman proposed on the Wolfe Wiki, so the poster is adding evidence to that discussion.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Nadan's obliviousness marks him as a replacement of the original Nadan

There is a point well before the replacement point posited by David Tallman extant on the Wolfe Wiki where the club footed American Actor playing Kreton quotes some lines about a potter's mistake from the Rubaiyat of Ommar Kayyam after they watch Mary Rose and Nadan is completely oblivious of its source, though it is a pretty prominent work of Persian literature.

2014-09-08 Jeff Wilson replying to Marc Aramini #

Points out that the Rubaiyat quoted in Seven American Nights differs considerably from the original Omar Khayyam, noting that Wolfe credits Fitzgerald rather than Khayyam. Supports the claim by citing Fitzgerald's own forematter, where he calls his work transmogrified rather than translated, and links a comparison page.
informs · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading

As noted in an earlier discussion of the Rubaiyat appearing in the forematter of URTH, the English translation considerably differs from the original. Wolfe credits translator Fitzgerald rather than Omar Khayyam, and in *his* forematter, Fitzgerald calls it transmogrified rather than translated.
