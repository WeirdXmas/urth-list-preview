The thread: Don Doggett asked whether anyone knew the history of Shadow of the Torturer comic books on eBay, doubting he wanted them if they looked Conan-like, and mentioned his first-edition Book of the New Sun hardcovers. Dave Lebling replied that the comic adaptation is fairly good, not Conan-like, and that he owns the first two issues covering the story up to Thecla's death. Doggett followed up that the planned six-volume adaptation was cancelled after three issues, and said he might try it since the issues are cheap. The exchange ended amicably with the thread drifting to purchase advice rather than debate.

2006-09-22 Don Doggett #

Asks whether anyone knows the history of a set of Shadow of the Torturer comic books offered on eBay, which the poster doubts he wants because they look like a Conan-style Severian. Also mentions restoring his Book of the New Sun set with first-edition hardcovers and admiring their covers.
questions

Hey y'all,

Does anyone know anything about a set of Shadow of the
Torturer comics? There's a set of 3 on ebay for three
bucks plus $5 s&h. I don't think I want them (they
look a little Sev as Conan) but I was curious if
anyone knew the history. Btw, having loaned out my
paperback copies of NS (mistake, mistake, mistake) I
have restored my set with first edition first printing
hardcovers. The covers of the first two are gorgeous.
I was only nine or ten when shadow of the torturer
came out (that was even pre Lord of the Rings for me)
so I'm looking forward to savoring them. Maybe I'll
buy some cheap mass markets as reference copies.

take care

Don

2006-09-23 Dave Lebling replying to don doggett #

Reports that the Shadow of the Torturer comic adaptation is a fairly good version that does not look like Conan, and that the poster owns the first two issues which cover the story up to Thecla's death. Responds to don doggett's eBay query asking about the comics' history and whether they look like Conan-style Severian.
informs

They're actually a pretty decent adaptation, not Conanish at all. I have read (and own) the first two, which take the story up to the death of Thecla.

Dave Lebling, aka vizcacha

2006-09-23 Don Doggett replying to Dave Lebling #

Reports that a comic-book adaptation of Wolfe's Book of the New Sun was planned as six volumes but cancelled after three issues, and that its artist also drew Gloom Cookie. Responds to Dave Lebling's praise of the adaptation as not Conanish, saying he has not yet read it but may try it since the issues are cheap.
informs

Dave Lebling <[email]> wrote: They're actually a pretty decent adaptation, not Conanish at all. I have read (and own) the first two, which take the story up to the death of Thecla.

That's good to know. Maybe someday I'll check them out. They're not very expensive. I did a little looking into it on my own and I guess it was a projected six volume adaptation that was cancelled after issue three. Alas, it was a victim of the 90's comic bust. The guy who does Gloom Cookie drew it.

Don
