Soldier of Sidon

Urth list · 6 messages · 2003-04-26 to 2003-05-09

2003urthNicholas GeversSheila HerndonJames JordanMichael Straight
The thread: Nicholas Gevers reported that Gene Wolfe confirmed work on Soldier of Sidon, a novel set in Persian-ruled Egypt that Wolfe said treats ancient Egyptians sympathetically, with a possible fourth volume. silk tree asked whether it is the long-awaited Latro story given its title and admitted fear of disappointment. Gevers answered that it is the third Latro novel following Soldier of the Mist and Soldier of Arete. The exchange was brief and informational, with no real disagreement and no debate over interpretation.

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Stories within stories

2003-04-26 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs readers that Gene Wolfe confirmed he is working on Soldier of Sidon, a novel set mainly in Egypt during its period as a Persian province. Reports Wolfe's remark that his research presents ancient Egyptians sympathetically, and notes a fourth volume may follow.
informs

Last night, confirmation from GW that he is at work on
SOLDIER OF SIDON, set mainly in Egypt when it was a
province of the Persian Empire. He comments that his
research shows the ancient Egyptians in a warm,
sympathetic light. A fourth volume may well follow.

--Nick Gevers.
