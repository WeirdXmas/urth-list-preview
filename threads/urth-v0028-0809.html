Themes: Fathers, mothers and children

2000-02-22 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists recent periodical items on Wolfe fiction: a NYRSF #138 essay by Robert Borski on paternity in The Book of the New Sun, several book reviews, and Nova Express reviews and chapbooks including Lawrence Person's review of Lexicon Urthus. Gives no argument or evaluation, only bibliographic details and titles of the items.
informs

=Recent items about Wolfe-fiction=

"The New York Review of Science Fiction (#138)" (Feb. 2000) has:

"Masks of the Father: Paternity in Gene Wolfe's BOOK OF THE NEW SUN" by Robert Borski;

A review (by Paul Witcover) of STRANGE TRAVELERS;

A review (by Joan Gordon) of ON BLUE'S WATERS;

A review (by Jenny Blackford) of THE ISLAND OF DOCTOR DEATH AND OTHER STORIES AND OTHER STORIES.

"Nova Express (vol. 5, no. 2)" has:

A review (by David Langford) of ON BLUE'S WATERS;

A review (by Lawrence Person) of (my own) LEXICON URTHUS, and chapbooks "Additions, Errata, &cetera," "Additions, Errata, &cetera (volume 2)," "Additions, Errata, &cetera (volume 3)," "NS-1, or `Synopsis of the Narrative of Severian the Great, Last True Autarch of Urth,'" "LS-1, or `Characters of the Long Sun Whorl,'" and "LS-2, or `Languages of the Long Sun Whorl.'"
