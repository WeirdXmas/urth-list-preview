1997-05-31 Charles Dye #

Relays Matthew Freestone's joke that if Little, Big is infundibular, The Book of the New Sun must be chronosynclastically infundibular, and defines infundibular as funnel-shaped. Traces the word to Vonnegut, not the Crowley allusion in the quoted passage, making a light aside with no interpretive claim about Wolfe's text.
