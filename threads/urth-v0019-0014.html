1998-09-22 Robert Borski #

Informs the list that the story "Four Wolves" appeared in Amazing in May 1983 and lists its four parts, with reprint locations for each. Answers a question about the title by correcting the assumption that it is a collective name for the short-shorts such as "Redwood Coast Roamer" and "In the Old Hotel".
answers · source-and-allusion

"Four Wolves" was published in AMAZING, May, 1983.

It consists of:

1) At The Volcano's Lip (reprinted in STOREYS FROM THE OLD HOTEL)

2) In The Mountains (also in STOREYS)

3) My Book (reprinted in ENDANGERED SPECIES)

4) The River

Robert Borski

Replies to Robert Borski by noting that the final piece, "The River," has not yet been reprinted and its only appearance is hard to locate, which the poster complains about.
