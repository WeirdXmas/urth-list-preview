Themes: Evolution and the future of humanity

2005-04-09 Tony Ellis #

Disputes the claim that the Frozen Urth future is doomed, arguing that its inhabitants are carried to newer worlds by merciful cacogens and so keep access to advanced technology. Responds to I's quotation by asking where the books say this, citing Master Ash's time machine as evidence of a rosy future.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Frozen Urthers are carried to newer worlds by cacogens

Well now I'm intrigued. Where? Where does it say this?

Yes, the future of a Frozen Urth is one of irreversible decline... but so what? The future of the inhabitants of that Urth, taken to newer, fairer worlds by merciful cacogens, is anything but.

We already know that their descendants have access to incredible technology, so why talk as if they never will? Does Master Ash live in a mud hut? No, he lives in a time machine, a technological marvel right off the scale on which I would measure a mere ringworld. Given such a rosy future, anything is possible.
