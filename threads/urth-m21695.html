Words and Such art

Urth list · 18 messages · 2011-10-25 to 2011-10-25

2011urthThe Book of the New SunGerry QuinnMarc AraminiJames WynnDan'l Danehy-OakesAntonin ScriabinNick LeeDaniel Petersen
The thread: Posters on the Urth list traded opinions on cover art for Gene Wolfe's books, with the thread starting from Wolfe's own remarks on how covers worsened with rising sales expectations. James Wynn argued that the short story collections have better covers because Tor expects little from them, and Daniel Petersen agreed. Antonin Scriabin and Gerry Quinn criticized several covers as mismatched to the text, such as a Severian scene and a dog image for Starwater Strains, while Daniel Petersen and Dan'l Danehy-Oakes named personal favourites. The thread stayed chatty and anecdotal, drifting into personal habits and favourite picks without any real argument about the text, ending without resolution.
  • James Wynn: short story collections have better covers because Tor expects few sales, so the art director stays out
  • Daniel Petersen: agrees collections have better covers and lists personal favourite and worst covers
  • Antonin Scriabin: cover art is uneven; criticizes covers that misrepresent Severian scenes and Starwater Strains
  • Gerry Quinn: mocks covers as unreadable on trains and says a Fifth Head of Cerberus cover suffers from washed-out printing
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: likes the Maitz Book of the New Sun covers for nostalgic reasons despite poor depiction of action
  • Marc Aramini: recalls Claw of the Conciliator cover as first Wolfe encounter

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Mirrors, pictures and art

2011-10-25 James Wynn replying to Daniel Petersen #

Argues that Wolfe's short story collections have better book covers than his novels because Tor does not expect them to sell much, so the art director stays out. Supports this by citing Wolfe's own claim that rising sales expectations made the Book of the New Sun covers worse with each volume.
supports · author-and-biography

His short story collections typically are better, but I agree that none are as good as this.
Probably, the collections are not as bad because Tor doesn't expect to make a lot of money off them and so the Art Director did not get involved. Wolfe claimed that expectations of increasing sales is the reason the covers of The Book of the New Sun volumes got worse with each release.

2011-10-25 James Wynn replying to Daniel Petersen #

Judges the original paperback covers of several Wolfe books, calling the Long Sun first editions atrocious and preferring alternate covers for Operation Ares and Endangered Species. Replies to Daniel Petersen's list of favourite and worst covers, praising the original illustrations for The Wizard Knight, Pirate Freedom and An Evil Guest.
chats

The cover on the original Operation Ares paperback pretty good. The cover on the original paperback for tIoDDaOSaOS was better than the one with the purple skull. I preferred the paperback cover of "Endangered Species" with the giant pollen spore to the stick wings one. Usually, however, the original covers to the Long Sun volumes were atrocious IMO. It's the original edition novels that tend to be the worst offenders.

However the original covers and illustrations for The Wizard Knight, Pirate Freedom, and And Evil Guest are very good. Usually, it seems the best his first editions can hope for is to be unoffensive.

J.
