proposed `Delage' collage for NYRSF

Urth list · 2 messages · 1998-05-16 to 1998-05-18

1998urthThe Book of the New SunDamien BroderickPeter Westlake

1998-05-16 Damien Broderick #

Asks list members for permission to compile posts from the thread about Wolfe's story "Suzanne Delage" into a collage article for the New York Review of SF. Proposes crediting posters and seeking their consent before submission to the editor.
questions

Hi team

I've just suggested this to one of the editors at the New York Review of SF, and would like to hear from the relevant list posters if it's cool to snaffle their glittering words, with acknowledgement:

< I've lately been on the URTH email list, devoted to Book of the New Sun and other non Long Sun Wolfeana. Very smart, funny, divergent minds. One minor thread has tried to work out what's going on in Wolfe's quite short story, `Suzanne Delage'. I'd like to create a kind of collage of the posts-to-date as an article. Obviously I'd get permission from the contestants. >

I would guess that they'd want to see it (or samples) before accepting it on my say-so, but I'll take the chance and do the compiling, unless anyone objects.

Regards, Damien
