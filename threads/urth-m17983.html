2011-01-14 David Stockhoff replying to Lee Berman #

Agrees with Lee Berman's reading that the Autarch in Dr. Talos' play is an anti-New Sun figure who wants the world burned rather than renewed, and adds that irrigation cultures passed on more mythic material than slash-and-burn cultures. Responds to Berman's quoted claim that a flood is a better image than fire, noting fertility links in both.
supports · myth-and-structure · theories: Autarch in Dr. Talos' play opposes the New Sun

Interestingly, both are possibly related to fertility---but the irrigation cultures handed down more of our mythical material than did the slash-and-burn cultures. So I agree there: better to drown and cover the world in green than to burn in satanic sterility.

2011-01-14 Lee Berman #

Proposes that the Maimed King is linked to the Arthurian Fisher King and that the Autarch in Dr. Talos' Play acts as an anti-New Sun, aiming to burn the world rather than bathe it in water. Responds to David Stockhoff's note on sacrificial and maimed kings, adding Fomalhaut imagery, a Flood-as-fertility reading, and a Pringles-cloning joke.
proposes · source-and-allusion · myth-and-structure · theories: Maimed King is related to the Arthurian Fisher King; Autarch in Dr. Talos' Play is the anti-New Sun seeking fire over water; Flood as ancient Middle Eastern fertility symbol

Ah, thanks for the extra info! Maimed King is related to Fisher King- Arthurian and related to the Holy Grail, (which certainly has an element of chastity and sexual purity associated with it).

As one who is quite interested in Fomalhaut and the many fish/mouth/caves to be found in BotNS and Long/Short Sun, I am definitely going to perk up a bit when fish and fishing are mentioned.

I'm thinking about the Autarch in Dr. Talos' Play. He hopes to stop the New Sun or at least stop the new Creation which will populate it. Goaded by the demon, he orders his space ships to lay the Urth sere, to burn the "seed" (Meschia/Meschiane) which the demons failed to poison this time, this Creation.

Thus he is the anti-New Sun hoping to bathe the world in fire instead of water. I am thinking that a Flood is much more of a fertility symbol to ancient Middle East types, so influenced by Egyptian and Nile-based culture, than it might first appear to us.

(just some amorphous thoughts...no particular GUT here to unify things)
