Themes: Myth and folklore sources

2000-08-21 Alice Turner #

Adds to the thread on lame figures by noting that Hephaestus (Vulcan), Wayland the Smith (Volund) and Daedalus are all lame craftsmen from myth. Contrasts them with Wolfe's characters, who are adventurers rather than craftsmen, and asks whether the parallel is interesting.
