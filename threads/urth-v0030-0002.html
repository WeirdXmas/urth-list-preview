Wolfe's Next Novel: The Wizard Knight

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-10-17 to 2000-10-17

2000urthRobert Borski

2000-10-17 Robert Borski #

Reports Wolfe's early description of his new novel The Wizard Knight, following a boy stolen by the Aelf through repeated returns and deaths, with adulthood as its theme. Quotes Wolfe's own plot summary, cast list and progress note of over 1,100 draft pages, and notes his hope to write more Latro books.
informs

Wondering what you have to look forward to once the Short Sun trilogy concludes?

Here's the early word on Wolfe's current project, tentatively entitled THE WIZARD KNIGHT, straight from the Bard of Barrington himself:

"The plot in the strict sense is endlessly convoluted. A boy is stolen by the Aelf and released, wants to become a knight, returns to Aelfrice, comes back to his own world (the locale for most of the book--it is the midmost of seven), becomes a knight, sets out to prove himself, does, dies, returns, and so on and so forth. The theme, much less complex, is the meaning of adulthood for an adult who tries to live up to the ideals of youth, refusing to settle for safety and gray comfort.

"Characters include the boy's older brother, the Queen of the Green Aelf, a demon, half-a-dozen giants, a human woman who marries the King of Jotunland, knights and noblemen as needed, a dead witch, her talking cat, a talking dog, a telepathic horse, and a partridge in a pear tree."

To date Wolfe has 1,100+ pages of the first draft done, but cautions it will require "much, much more" to complete. And he's still hoping to write one or two more Latro books, possibly after he finishes THE WIZARD KNIGHT.

O frabjous day! Callooh! Callay! Three more Wolfe works on the way! (Well, eventually.)

Robert Borski
