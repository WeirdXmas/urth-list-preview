"Has Anybody Seen Junie Moon?"

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-08-15 to 2000-08-15

2000urthAlex David Groce

2000-08-15 Alex David Groce #

Argues that Wolfe's Junie Moon works as a tribute to Lafferty but falls well short of his source story You Can't Go Back, because its attempts to catch Lafferty's style fail. Responds to Adam, who pointed out the Lafferty story, and contrasts this with Wolfe's earlier pastiches Suzanne Delage and the Holmes/Nero Wolfe stories, which he found enjoyable.
disputes · genre-and-craft · source-and-allusion · theories: Junie Moon is an inferior imitation of Lafferty

I just read the Lafferty story, "You Can't Go Back," that Adam pointed out. Even Wolfe doesn't seem to be up to the task of imitating Lafferty--while "Junie Moon" works ok as a tribute, I suppose, it really is rather sadly inferior to the source, and the points that seem to attempt to catch Lafferty's style fail badly. Too bad, considering that some of Wolfe's past pastiches have been so enjoyable ("Suzanne Delage" and the Sherlock Holmes/Nero Wolfe stories).
