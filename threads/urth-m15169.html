2010-05-06 Cliff Judge #

Asks whether the lochage's mention of trouble at the river in The Book of the New Sun refers to the later astral journey that Silk and Horn take to Nessus in The Book of the Short Sun. Poses the link as a question, citing the lochage's remark in Severian's meeting with him as the textual basis.

When Severian is brought to meet the lochage, he mentions that there had been trouble at the river.

Could this be a reference to the later astral trip that Silk/Horn took to Nessus in Short Sun??

2010-05-06 Roy C. Lackey replying to Cliff Judge #

Argues that the trouble at the river mentioned by the lochage cannot be the later astral trip of Silk and Horn to Nessus, because the seasons do not match. Answers Cliff Judge's question by comparing the season of Severian's departure from the tower, the cold when he found Triskele, and Jahlee running naked during the Blue party's visit.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading

No. Different seasons. It was early spring when Sev left the tower. (Soon after he left, Talos was whacking the heads off wildflowers in the field near the city gate.)

When Sev found Triskele, it was bitterly cold. (Sev was outside the tower trying to unclog a frozen drain pipe at the time.) Sev had the dog for only about a week. He showed the dog to Silkhorn.

Jahlee was running around naked when the party from Blue came to Nessus, not a cold-weather thing to do. <g>

-Roy
