1999whorlThe Book of the Long SunPaul C Duggan

1999-11-27 Paul C Duggan #

Reports that a new Long Sun book gives a fuller reason for the long technical description of talus production, which the poster earlier found unexplained beyond the author being an engineer. Grounds this in Horn's stated intent that the Book of Silk serve a practical purpose for short-sun readers who may need long-sun know-how, quoting Rosenstock-Huessy as backing.
supports · genre-and-craft · theories: Silk's technical detail serves short-sun readers needing long-sun know-how

If I recall, when we discusses long sun the question of the literary purpose of the long drawn-out description of the mechancial engineering process of talus production came up. Horn's new book at least gives us a fuller explanation of this than "Wolfe is an engineer by trade". Horn alludes to his purposes in making the Book of Silk have a utilitarian function for those on the short sun who may need some of the long sun know-how.
