2007-04-26 Don Doggett replying to brunians #

Reports that Gollancz had planned a one-volume Book of the New Sun for October, quoting a forum post and linking an Amazon listing, and mocks the working title "Severian of the Guild" as confusing. Suggests the "(Abandoned)" tag just means the title was dropped, since publishers often keep titles fluid until printing.

2007-05-13 Matthew Keeley replying to don doggett #

Reports that Amazon UK lists a one-volume paperback of The Book of the New Sun, titled Severian of the Guild, for October 18 with 1008 pages and new cover art. Responds to Don Doggett's question about publication details by quoting a January forum post and noting the poster's own desire for a one-volume edition.
informs

Amazon UK now has some more information on Severian of the Guild, which does seem to be a reprinting of the New Sun books. Amazon says it comes out October 18 in the UK and that it runs to 1008 pages. It's a paperback apparently. They also have the cover art up, though it's far from my favorite New Sun art. Still, I've wanted a one-volume edition of the BotNS for a while, so I may have to pick this one up while studying in Ireland this fall.

-Matt
