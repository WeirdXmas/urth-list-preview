1997-02-05 xury #

Asks whether the planet Blue in the Long Sun books could be Urth, Severian's world, making Green the Lune and the inhumi descendants of Agia, and whether Blue would then be a far-future repopulated Urth. The poster admits doubt and asks the list for feedback on whether the idea makes sense.
questions · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Blue is Urth, Severian's world; Green is the Lune; Inhumi descend from Agia's followers

I have a question. First of all, is the Blue planet Urth of Severian's setting. If so then that would make Green the Lune and the inhumi some sort of followers or desendence of Agia? Maybe? I'm pretty doubtful on this theory. But if Blue is Urth then it is in the future most likely after Severian brings the New Sun and the Urth (Ushas) need repopulation. But I have doubts and would like some feedback to see if this in any way makes sense.Thanks.
Xury

1997-02-26 Kieran Cleary #

Proposes that Typhon copied his consciousness into a Mainframe to possess bodies at will, and that Blue is Ushas, to tie the Long Sun books to The Book of the New Sun. Builds on Xury's Blue-as-Ushas idea by reconstructing a plot in which Typhon sends a near-light-speed generation ship back to a transformed Urth.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Typhon's consciousness copied into a Mainframe; Mainframe is Inhumi technology; Blue is Ushas; Typhon sends a generation starship back to Urth

Perhaps someone can help me out on this one:

In tBotNS, Typhon gets his head grafted onto poor Piatus's (?) body obviously in order to prolong his life and reign. Since he seems to have had the technology to copy/store his consciousness in a computer and thus posess people at will, surely this would have been a better method of achieving the same thing with Piatus or whoever. I do remember that Typhon made a big deal about his face, and that it was important for the leader's face to remain, but he could have gone the Lemur et al. route and fashioned himself a chem.

Which leads me to suggest that, since he didn't, perhaps Mainframe represents Inhumi technology which, for some reason, Typhon himself has access to. I know that, certainly by Severain's time, there is nothing new under the sun, but maybe it's possible?

On another note, I can't help liking Xury's idea about Blue being Ushas, perhaps, like others, because I'd prefer to see a stronger tie in with tBotNS. The current Pas/Typhon is such a weak link on its own. Perhaps it could have happened like this:

Typhon is getting worried: the exultants are fleeing Urth and a miracle worker has arisen telling everyone about the New Sun and spreading foment. So as a gamble on the future he builds a generation starship, fills it with Urthers who, by the end of the journey, will worship him as a god, and sleepers who will have all the technical know-how necessary to rebuild the Empire, while Typhon et famille are copied into the ship's mainframe (problem above still thorny, though), the plan being that Typhon will posess some unlucky individual and resume his activities once they reach Urth (sort of a "Carry On Despot!")
After a subjective near light speed journey of 300 years, the ship returns to Urth, now Ushas. The coming of the New Sun has caused the old continents to sink, and according to Dr. Talos play, a new race of monsters has arisen? Another effect of the New Sun's arrival is gravitational upheaval throughout the system, causing Lune's orbit around Ushas to become eccentric, thus they periodically meet in conjunction.

Kieran
