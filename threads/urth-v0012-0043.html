Human-Annese interbreeding

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-01 to 1998-06-01

1998urthadam louis stephanides

Themes: Evolution and the future of humanity, Hidden identities and parentage

1998-06-01 adam louis stephanides #

Questions whether life that evolved independently on two planets could interbreed, given differences in genetic code, chromosome number and gene arrangement, and asks how this fits V.R.T.'s portrayal as a human-Annese hybrid in The Fifth Head of Cerberus. Offers four possible explanations, including Annese changing their DNA, an earlier human colonization wave, the settler-gone-native alternatives, and R.T. not being V.R.T.'s genetic father. Responds to Robert Borski's remark on human genetic diversity by arguing that cross-world interbreeding is biologically implausible.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Annese can change their DNA; Annese descend from earlier human colonists; Annese are French settlers gone native; R.T. is not V.R.T.'s genetic father

This is something that bothered me when I reread 5HC. I'm no biologist, but given the possible variations in the amino acids used, the genetic code used, the number of chromosomes, the arrangement of genes on the chromosome, and undoubtedly other things, it seems to me that there is basically no chance of life that evolved independently on two different planets being able to interbreed. Of course, the question is how it seems to Mr. Wolfe (the real one).

If he feels the same way as me, this of course raises the problem that V.R.T. is portrayed as a human-Annese hybrid. There are several possibilities:

1) Annese can change their DNA along with their shapes.

2) All Annese, not just the Shadow children, were descendants of an earlier wave of human colonization.

3) Either of the alternatives I presented for "Liev's Postpostulate" (the Annese are French settlers gone native, or projections of the settler's thoughts).

4) R.T. is not V.R.T.'s genetic father.

Any opinions?

--Adam
