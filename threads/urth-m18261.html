2011-01-18 PMorris33 #

Recounts watching the 1970s film Zardoz and suggests it parallels Wolfe's Urth, comparing the advanced and backward civilizations and the stasis of a frozen character. Offered in a light, joking spirit as a recommendation to fans, responding to a thread about quotes from The Citadel of the Autarch.
chats

Has anyone else seen the gloriously bad early 1970's scifi movie Zardoz??? The one with Sean Connery giving a definitively rotten performance wearing bright-red leather hot pants?

Well, I recently sat through the DVD and was struck by how closely the advanced/backward civilizations paralleled the divisions of human society in Wolfe's Urth. And the idea of keeping certain humans in stasis like poor Mamelta to be "unfrozen" or "decanted" at need. (Though properly speaking, that's on the Whorl not on Urth)

I suggest this in a spirit of good humor, but some Lupines might enjoy watching "Zardoz" in the interests of research

Pip
