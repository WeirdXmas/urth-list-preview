Themes: Slavery and service

2001-01-29 Michael Straight #

Guesses that the secret of the Long Sun books involves Horn's kindness to the inhumi, such as giving Jahlee a human soul and winning their devotion. Grounds this in Horn's remark in In Green's Jungles that the Vanished People enslaved the inhumi and fell, and proposes humans could enslave them through love.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Humans can enslave inhumi by showing kindness; Horn gave Jahlee a human soul

I'm guessing that the secret has something to do with how Horn seems to have given Jahlee a human soul. Or that all of the inhumi whom Horn has shown kindness to have found themselves unable to kill him, or even fully devoted to him.

There's also that bit in IGJ where Horn mentions that the Vanished People made the inhumi their slaves and that that led to their downfall.

Maybe the secret is that the humans can enslave the inhumi by loving (showing kindness to?) them. That seems slightly more practical and unsettling than the idea we rejected before that maybe if humans changed their nature and all loved each other, the inhumi would cease to be monsters (why would the inhumi fear that?).

-Rostrum
