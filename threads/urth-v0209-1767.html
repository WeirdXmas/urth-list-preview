2002-09-24 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks whether Maytera Marble's scattered, multiple-personality nature in The Book of the Short Sun comes from her original state or from a critical mass of recycled prosthetic parts, and suggests the 1970s book and film Sibyl may have influenced Wolfe. Compares her to the swordmaster whose leg is made from five others without showing similar weirdness, and raises whether prosthetics move from human to cyborg to robot levels.
questions · genre-and-craft · theories: Marble's scattering comes from recycled prosthetic parts

Now that we're plunging into the question of how many personalities are in/make up Maytera Marble, I'm reminded of the 1970s book and tv movie "Sibyl" that was all about the woman who had multiple personalities. Interesting touchstone, and I wonder if influenced Gene Wolfe's notion of Marble.

Leaving aside the complexities of Short Sun's multiple personalities, I guess my first question would be:

Is Marble's scattered nature due to an inherent quality of her original state, or is it due to a critical mass of recycled parts?

Because the swordmaster, for example, has a leg made from five others, iirc, and he does not seem to show the weirdness. Granted he is a bio, but we are saying that Rose herself may have been influenced by her chem parts.

So is there a level of prosthetic inclusion where the items are purely prosthetic (human level), and then a level beyond that where the prosthetics begin to influence the individual (cyborg), up to a level where the prosthetics either take over the individual or cause it to disintegrate under the babble (robot)? (This does have some bearing on the issue of Horn as a software prosthetic for crippled Silk.)

2002-09-25 James Wynn #

Quotes a Mantis/Crush exchange on levels of prosthetic inclusion, from purely prosthetic human to cyborg to robot, and asks whether chems can take on biological prosthetics. Responds to the earlier thread by pointing to Silk's doubt about whether anything of the real Rose remains beneath her prosthetics.
questions · genre-and-craft

Mantis ponders:
So is there a level of prosthetic inclusion where the items are purely prosthetic (human level), and then a level beyond that where the prosthetics begin to influence the individual (cyborg), up to a level where the prosthetics either take over the individual or cause it to disintegrate under the babble (robot)?

Crush responds:
Well consider that Silk ponders whether there is anything left of the real "Rose" amid all her prosthetics.
As one might expect, I wonder if the process goes the other way. Can chems take on bio prosthetics and is there a point where the prosthetics supercede the prevenient.
