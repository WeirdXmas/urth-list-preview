Hyacinth & Birds

1997-10-01 Marty Light #

Notes a radio broadcast saying the hyacinth macaw is named for its purple-hued feathers and suggests this might connect to Hyacinth, the bird-associated figure in the Long Sun books, as an extension of Silk's bird-related relationships beyond Oreb. Offers the radio fact as a playful link to the Long Sun birds, with no argument beyond the coincidence of the name.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Hyacinth in Long Sun is linked to the hyacinth macaw

Heard this on an National Public Radio broadcast the other day ("The 90-second Naturalist" with Thane Maynord (sp?) from the Cincy Zoo):

One of the most common types of macaws is the hyacinth macaw, named for its purple-hued feathers.

Perhaps Silk's bird-brained relationships extended beyond Oreb.
