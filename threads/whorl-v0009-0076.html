"Nova Express" reviewed

1998-12-24 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Reports that a copy of the Nova Express issue with a Gene Wolfe interview and an essay on The Book of the Long Sun has arrived, praising both as good. Says the essay's author is unfamiliar but that its existence means more people are writing about Wolfe, and invites list members to discuss it once read.
informs

Hey! I got a copy of "Nova Express" vol. 5, no. 1.

The interview is good!

The piece on THE BOOK OF THE LONG SUN is by a person I've never heard of (itself probably a good thing--means more people writing about Wolfe) and is quite good!

Things people can talk about, once they've read it.

Or those of us few illuminated ones can start now.

=mantis=
