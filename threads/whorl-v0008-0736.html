Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-08-09 mary whalen #

Traces the names Oosik and Oreb to Inuit, Latin and Hebrew words, proposing that Oosik's name may reflect his tie to Hyacinth and his role as Silk's staff. Grounds this in dictionary glosses: oosik means walrus baculum, baculum means staff, and oreb is Hebrew for raven, with chough and Silk's sacrifice of a night chough noted.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Oosik's name relates to his relationship with Hyacinth; Oosik's name reflects his role as Silk's staff

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Oosik. Also ooshook. Inuit (Eskimo) for the baculum (penis bone) of a walrus, or of other sea mammals, or just for penis. Baculum or baculus in Latin means staff, walking stick, or augur's staff. Generalissimo of Viron. Father of Mattak (Whale Meat). Name may have to do with his relationship with Hyacinth. Also, since baculum means an augur's staff, this probably has to do with the fact that he supports Silk, or that he is a member of his staff.

Oreb. Hebrew for raven. A chough is any species of genus Pyrrhocorax (red raven). Related to crows. Have black feathers and red legs. Night chough Silk buys to sacrifice, but doesn't. Also the name of a raven refered to in the Chrasmologic Writings
