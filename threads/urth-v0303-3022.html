Themes: Christ figures and messiahs, Hidden identities and parentage

2003-03-06 Lisa Schaffer-Doggett #

Argues that Severian is a hidden king, a return of the monarch in the vein of Richard and Typhon, and that Ouen may not be Severian's father, with Dorcas possibly his mother or sister. Grounds the case in Graves's The White Goddess, the Autarchs as placeholders, Malrubius's remark about the animal and attachment to the Monarch, and parallels to Christ, Arthur and Henry II.
proposes · puzzle-solving · allegory-and-typology · theories: Severian is a hidden king returned; Dorcas is Severian's mother or sister; Ouen is not Severian's father; Typhon is Severian's ancestor through Ymar
