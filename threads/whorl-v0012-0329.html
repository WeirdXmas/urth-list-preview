Another note on all these posts: Tartaros

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-02-21 to 2001-02-21

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunDan'l Danehy-Oakes

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2001-02-21 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Disputes the theory that Tartaros possesses Pig or is one of Pas's sprats, including Severian, arguing Tartaros is incurably blind even when riding Auk and so cannot see through Pig's or Auk's eyes. Grounds the rejection in the blindness of Tartaros and the claim that Pig can see when given an eye, and rejects 'loose reincarnation' as making the notion of sameness meaningless.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Tartaros possesses Pig; Tartaros is Severian reincarnated; Tartaros is one of Pas's sprats

A lot of people (notably Robert Borski) suggest that Tartaros is in possession of Pig, while Michael Andre-Driussi, in discussing the possibility of Sev. as one of Pas's sprats, suggests Tartaros.

No to both, for the same reason. Tartaros is not only blind, was not only born blind, but is utterly and incurably and inalterably blind; even when riding/in possession of Auk, he cannot see through Auk's eyes but must ask Auk to describe things.

So he isn't Sev., unless you want to consider "loose traditional reincarnation," of the sort by which any two persons not alive at the same time might be the same person -- which is to say, by which it would be meaningless to talk about "same."

And he isn't in control of Pig, and Pig certainly isn't him; Pig can see when given an eye.

--Blattid
