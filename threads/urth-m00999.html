Puss-In-Boots in The Wizard

Urth list · 2 messages · 2005-01-17 to 2005-01-18

2005urthAndrew BollenJames Wynn

Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Myth and folklore sources

2005-01-17 James Wynn #

Points out that Wolfe's The Wizard Knight echoes the Puss-in-Boots tale, with a talking cat who acts as ambassador to a giant whose death follows his advice and who gets oversized boots for his sporadic master. Asks whether other readers noticed further parallels to the folk story.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: The Wizard Knight echoes the Puss-in-Boots folktale

Heh heh. A talking cat becomes an ambassador to a giant. His advices leads to said giant's death -- in several senses his masters do take over his castle. The cat procures a over-sized boots for Toug whom he sporadically considers his master. (Yes, I know that's the opposite from the way it occurs in the story.)

There are probably more. I was wondering if anyone else noted them.

~ Crush

2005-01-18 Andrew Bollen replying to James Wynn #

Offers a passage from The Wizard Knight, page 297, where Idnn asks whether the cat could turn into an eagle or bull or become smaller than Mani, and Able replies that someone would catch and eat him. Adds this as another instance of the Puss-in-Boots parallels James Wynn raised, responding to his list of matching details.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: The Wizard Knight echoes the Puss-in-Boots tale

Maybe p297: Idnn says, "Could you do that then? Turn into an eagle or a bull. Or be smaller than Mani." Able says, "Who'd catch and eat me, and serve me right too."
