Urth's Unknown Names

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-03-26 C R Culver #

Asks which names in The Book of the New Sun remain secret, forgotten or unknown, listing ten, including Hethor's real name, the town that forgot Fauna, and Baldanders' undine. Invites readers to offer ideas on Valeria's second husband and the Cumaean's real name, and notes that the Megatherians' names matter little.
questions · puzzle-solving

Just been thinking lately about secret, forgotten, or just plain unknown names in the BotNS:

1) Hethor's real name
2) The real name of the town that forgot Fauna
3) Valeria's second husband (who *was* he? any ideas?)
4) The names of the planets beside Urth, Verthandi, Skuld, and Dis. 5) The names of the 17 Megatherians (doesn't really matter, though), or a single member of the Group of Seventeen
6) The previous Lictor of Thrax.
7) Cyriaca's husband.
8) The name of Dorcas' ("Cas") husband.
9) The Cumaean's real name, if any.
10) The name (and purpose) of Baldanders' undine.

1998-03-27 Peter Stephenson #

Questions whether the Group of Seventeen members in The Book of the New Sun have real names or are only called by a generic title, and jokes that they would not trade witty dinner-party names. Responds to C R Culver's list of unknown names by assuming the members are called 'Member of the Group of Seventeen' and playfully suggesting Ascian names drawn from ASCII control codes like NUL and BEL.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Ascians named after ASCII control codes

I always assumed the latter were referred to simply as `Member of the Group of Seventeen', or something such? It doesn't seem likely they go in for shining dinner party repartee (reminds me of a comedy sketch in a car factor with the punchline `Built by Roberts'). Is there any indication that the leaders, unlike the rest, have real names? I always associated Ascians with ASCII, maybe they're called NUL, BEL, ...

Peter
