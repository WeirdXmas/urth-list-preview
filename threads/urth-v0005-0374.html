1997-11-02 Paul C Duggan #

Suggests Larry Niven's "Inferno" as another modern science fiction story with gory depictions of Hell, answering the earlier poster's question about who else writes such stories. Offers no argument about Wolfe's work, and adds an unrelated epigraph from Eugen Rosenstock-Huessy on the impulse to transmit mental experiences.
