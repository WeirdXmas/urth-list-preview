2000-09-02 David Langford #

Points to Gene Wolfe's earlier answer on this list about the inhumi, quoting him that they come from Green, where they reproduce, and are only tourists on Blue. Responds to Christoph Mnemonic's claim that inhumi must feed and breed on both planets by supplying Wolfe's own statement as the authority.
informs · author-and-biography

This is something that Wolfe briefly discussed when answering questions from this list back in 1997:

"[...] the inhumi. They are fundamentally from Green, which is where they can reproduce ... on Blue they are tourists."

Dave

2000-09-03 Dennis G. Berdanis #

Asks whether the inhumas' eggs can be produced on Blue but fail to hatch there, given a passage in On Blue's Waters. Responds to David Langford's post quoting On Blue's Waters p.126, where the narrator wonders whether the inhumas' eggs would hatch in Blue's climate, and to Wolfe's 1997 remark that the inhumi are tourists on Blue.
questions · close-textual-reading

[email] (David Langford) scribbed:

Further evidence of no Blue breeding.

OBW p.126

"Would it be effective for us to dig up one of the recent inhumations and release him to warn the others? The thought recurs.
If the inhumas' eggs hatched in our climate, would not our human kind become extinct? What tricks Nature plays! If they are natural creatures at all.
But they surely are. Natural creatures native to Green. Why would the Neighbors create something so malign?"

Does this imply the eggs can be produced on Blue but will not hatch?
