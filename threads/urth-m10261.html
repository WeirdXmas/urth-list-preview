Disputes Lee Berman's reading that a mountain passage endorses racism and xenophobia, arguing instead that the idea of common descent from Adam and Eve makes racism and xenophobia senseless. Responds directly to the quoted passage about treating people justly when they share two ancestors, reading the duty owed to all people as filial rather than exclusionary.

I got the opposite impression, that racism and xenophobia are senseless, given the assumption that we are all children of Adam and Eve, and therefore that we owe filial respect and duty to all regardless of clan, nation or ethnicity.

2010-08-02 Lee Berman #

Asks whether the Ascians' plan to copy one individual across their population or the Commonwealth's plan to unite all minds in the Autarch's mind is better. Grounds this in Severian's musings on the mountain about vegetarianism, the green man, and a just society of common ancestry, which the poster reads biblically as a call for human purity.
questions · theology-and-religion · close-textual-reading · theories: Wolfe suggests common ancestry as ideal for justice; Racism reading reversed by biblical interpretation

I think somewhere it is contrasted that the Ascians hope to eventually copy one individual over and over in the millions in their populatce. The Commonwealth hopes to eventually capture the minds of all members of its populace within one mind (The Autarch's).

Is one of these clearly better than the other?

I'll note that Severian's musings on the mountain and in regard to the green man suggest a sympathy with vegetarianism.

Also on the mountain is the suggestion that the ideal society is one in which all people will treat each other justly if they know they are descended from the same two ancestors. On first readings, this seemed like oddly undisguised racism and xenophobia.

But I now take this passage Biblically, in view of Adam and Eve and mating with demons and fallen angels and nephilim and lamia etc. Perhaps Wolfe is suggesting we would all treat each other with respect if we were all fully human (i.e. without evil alien corruption in our genes).
