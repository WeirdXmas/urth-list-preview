2000-11-23 Redhaven1 #

Objects to the cover art of Return to the Whorl showing the Outsider as a frumpy giant in a goofy hat, arguing it clashes with the Outsider's unseen nature. Grounds the objection in the In Green's Jungles scene where Horn on the mountaintop feels he must not turn around and look at the Outsider's face.
disputes · close-textual-reading

The idea of having the Outsider on the cover of RTTW does not sit well with me at all. Wasn't there a point in IGJ where the Outsider came to Horn on the mountain top and Horn specifically felt he shouldn't turn around and see his face? After that, having the Outsider portrayed on the cover of the next book as a frumpy-looking giant with a goofy hat just seems wrong. The idea I get of the Outsider is that one never sees his face, or if one does, it certainly doesn't look like that. At least, I hope not.

-Steve

2000-11-26 Mr Mnemonic #

Lists five candidates for the giant figure on the cover of Return to the Whorl (the Outsider, Silk, Pas/Silk, Gene Wolfe, or a mixture) and rejects each in turn. Grounds the rejections in the appearance of Silk, who is described as thin and handsome, and in the giant's single head, then suggests waiting for the book.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Giant on cover is Silk or Pas

on RETURN TO THE WHORL's cover is either a) the Outsider, b) Silk, c) Silk/Pas, d) >Gene Wolfe, or e) some mixture of any of the above. A) The Outsider? As Steve (Redhaven1) has already said, that seems a bit unlikely
B) Silk? I seriously doubt it. Silk is always described as a thin, handsome young man. That giant guy doesn't look thin or handsome. C) Passilk? Probably not, because Silk doesn't look like the giant, and the giant only has one head
D) Gene Wolfe? Coincidence, nothing more, I'm willing to bet E) Mixture of the above? Seriously doubt it, once again

As James Jordan said, all the covers show things that happen in the book. So I say we just wait and see (and hope Gene Wolfe doesn't decide to make a cameo in his latest book ;o)

-Mnemonic
