Themes: Names and their meanings

2003-07-29 Alice Turner #

Argues that Delaney is hairy and that "Baldy" is a logical contraction of Baldanders rather than a reference to hair. Questions how the nickname relates to Delaney, who is short and rotund, and suggests a Northern European, troll-like flavor fitting the Gothic castle.
disputes · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Baldy is a contraction of Baldanders

One more thing. Delaney is indeed covered in hair, or at least he was two weeks ago. Baldy seems to me a perfectly logical contraction of Baldanders--why should it refer to hair? And how does Baldy relate to Delaney (who is short, and by now quite rotund)? I've always thought of him as Northern European in flavor, what with his troll-like aspects and that Gothic castle.

-alga

2003-07-29 Josh Geller #

Proposes that Baldanders and Baldy hide the name Delany through a qabalistic letter-rearrangement, offering a string of letter fragments as evidence. Responds to Alice K. Turner's objection that Baldy has no link to hair or to Delany, and half-disclaims the puzzle as a wry comment on list nitpicking.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Baldanders and Baldy conceal the name Delany

Excuse me, when you've been doing qabalah for thirty-five years you take some wierd things for granted:

Baldanders Baldy

Ba d rs
ldan e y

Bad SR Delany

Now, like I've said, I am not particularly passionately attached to this little puzzle, and I put the whole thing forward as much as a wry comment on the wrangling over minutiae which, in my opinion, badly infests this list at the possible expense of serious discussion as for any other reason.

But I think there is actually better evidence for it than for most of the similar wrangling that does go on much of the time.

J.
