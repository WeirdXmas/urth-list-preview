Replies to a thread on Mattak by listing Wolfe's tricky Trivigaunte terms and names from The Urth of the New Sun, reviewing them as a set of words to work through. Notes the earlier joke about dried whale flesh and gives Arabic and botanical sources for several terms and names like Hassaan, Sabah and Martagon.
