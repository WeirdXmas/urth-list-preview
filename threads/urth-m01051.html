Themes: Colonization and the native, Justice, torture and mercy

2005-01-27 Jim Raylor #

Argues that the three novellas of The Fifth Head of Cerberus form one story in which the Ascians and aboriginals are people like us, and that Wolfe stands for anyone who suffers unjustly. Replies to Maru with a playful, loosely connected set of claims, offering no specific textual evidence beyond character names and a reference to the prisoner number.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · theories: The Fifth Head of Cerberus novellas form one story; Ascians and aboriginals are people like us

Yes Maru.
It is 3 novellas written as one story.
Of this I am certain.
The ascians/abos are people like us
Even the deer-hunter, the elephant-man and the old
whaler recognised this at last.
There is a method to this madness but the full mystery
(Mr. E is unlike Maitre D'Iffy) is not yet revealed to
me. M'sieur Yves? or did you say Eve?
Gene Wolfe is prisoner 143, but he is a cypher for
everyone who suffers unjustly, whatever their species.
or type.
Bien sur.
We are all related, names only tell half the story.

I hope this helps

Jim
