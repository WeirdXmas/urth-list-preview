2013-05-29 Tony Ellis #

Mourns the death of Jack Vance and notes his large influence on Wolfe, especially through the Dying Earth stories. Quotes Brian Aldiss's description of Vance as a gaudily painted coelacanth washed up on SF's shore, in reply to Craig Brewer's announcement of the death.

Craig Brewer

Wow. Well, he had a good innings, as Dan'l points out, and he wasn't writing anymore, but this still feels like a sad loss. So many wonderful, exotic worlds. And a big influence on Wolfe even before you factor-in the The Dying Earth stories.

Brian Aldiss called him 'a gaudily painted coelacanth, washed up on SF's shore', which I always liked.

RIP.
