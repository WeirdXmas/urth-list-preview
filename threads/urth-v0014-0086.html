Life on Mt. Typhon

1998urthThe Book of the New SunDan Parmenter

1998-06-23 Dan Parmenter #

Asks whether Severian awakened Typhon and why Typhon calls himself Autarch though he is the final Monarch, while stressing that Typhon's reaction to Severian remains unclear. Replies to an earlier post's claim that Typhon thinks Severian is playing coy, noting Severian's imperfect handling of their first meeting and his better condition at the second.

From: Michael Straight <[email]>

Yes, yet another instance of Severian not quite getting the point (we can forgive him - he's only meeting Typhon for the first time) but still more or less doing the right thing.

As for what exactly Typhon makes of him, it's entirely unclear. Perhaps it took an act of supreme will for Typhon not to say "ack! You again!" when first seeing Severian. And of course the Conciliator Severain, at the point of his second meeting with Typhon is in considerably better shape than the first time.

So much mystery and intrigue within the Typhon sequence - was it Severian who awakened Typhon? Why does Typhon call himself Autarch when in fact he is the final Monarch?

D
