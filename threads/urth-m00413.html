Abaia and the Claw

Urth list · 3 messages · 2005-04-20 to 2005-04-27

2005urthThe Book of the New SunStanisław BocianstudioJeffrey Lefstin
The thread: Readers examined the Claw of the Conciliator passage in which Idas admits searching Severian's stateroom. Lefstin supported the reading that she was seeking Inire's letter from the aquastors, and noted from Severian's account that she was also looking for another hidden item. studio then proposed that the healing plant with human blood links to the Neighbor's restoration of Horn, asking whether a thorn was used and whether the Conciliator is a human-plant hybrid, based on the meaning of 'conciliator' as one who joins opposing sides. Bocian pushed back, arguing the Conciliator fits the Paraclete (advocate, intercessor, Holy Spirit) better than a hybrid, citing OED definitions. The thread ends with the two interpretations left side by side rather than resolved.
  • Jeffrey Lefstin: Idas was searching for Inire's letter, and also for another hidden item
  • studio: the Conciliator is a human-plant hybrid linked to the Neighbor's restoration of Horn
  • Stanisław Bocian: the Conciliator is better read as the Paraclete, an advocate or intercessor

2005-04-27 Stanisław Bocian replying to studio #

Proposes that the Conciliator in The Claw of the Conciliator is better linked to the Paraclete than to a human-plant hybrid, as suggested by the previous poster. Grounds this in the dictionary definitions of paraclete as an advocate or intercessor, and the Holy Spirit title, quoted from the OED entry.
