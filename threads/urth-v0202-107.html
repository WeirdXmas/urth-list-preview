Argues that Wolfe in The Book of the Long Sun deconstructs other genres and also critiques profane love as no real answer to existential questions, comparing this to Maugham's Of Human Bondage. Builds on Gevers's earlier suggestion that the novel deconstructs genre, applying the same reading to the Hyacinth and Silk relationship.
