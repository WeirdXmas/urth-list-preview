The thread: A New Year's chat thread in which Dan'l Danehy-Oakes jokingly claims the list's Blushas debate dates back decades, citing a David Bowie lyric on the blue planet. 2002-12-31 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Jokingly argues that the long-running Blushas debate on the list goes back decades, citing a David Bowie lyric about the planet being blue as an early opinion on the question. Responds to no particular poster and offers no evidence beyond the song-title pun on Urth and blue.
chats

Just digging out from under almost two weeks' worth of emails, and=20 fascinated by all that's gone on on this list ... it occurred to me that the Blushas debate has been going on for much longer than anyone realized. Way back in the '70s, David Bowie registered his opinion:

Planet Urth is Blue,
and there's nothing I can do...

--a rapidly scuttling Blattid

2003-01-01 Joe Cilluffo #

Praises a joke about a David Bowie song reworked as a Wolfe-related pun and adds a claimed anecdote that producer Tony Visconti shouted "Planet Urth is Green" during the sessions. Responds to Joe Cilluffo's quoted message, which plays on the same lyric and refers back to an earlier list joke from April 2002. The post is social banter with no reading of Wolfe's text.
