1998-05-23 Damien Broderick #

Recounts Damien Broderick's complaint about being selectively quoted and lampooned in another person's signature file after a Suzanne collage remark, and reproduces the mocking exchange and sig-file quotations. Responds to nothing about Wolfe's books; it is social chatter about list members' fear of misquotation.
chats

i've only been here a brief time, but i assume from the responses to my suzanne collage suggestion that some list members are concerned about being misquoted off the list. quite so. i've just had the mildly entertaining experience of being lampooned by selective quotation, in the sig file of someone with the opposite caps problem to me. my response got the following reply. anyone here responsible, he asked wearily?

damien
