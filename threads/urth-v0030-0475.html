2001-06-13 Michael Straight #

Explains that within Peace the Necronomicon is a fictional book invented by Lovecraft, which the character Gold then wrote in order to make others believe it existed, leaving two imaginary books. Responds to Roy C. Lackey's quoted confusion about whether the book exists and who gets credit for it, drawing on the quoted passages from Gold's account.
answers · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Gold wrote a fake Necronomicon to deceive

In the Peace universe, NECRONOMICON is a fictional book made up by HPL which Gold then "wrote," inspired by HPL's idea, in order to dupe someone into thinking that the book really exists.

So there are two imaginary titles: NECRONOMICON, a book of horrors imagined by HPL and Louis Gold's NECRONOMICON, a fake book of horrors imagined by Gene Wolfe.

-Rostrum
