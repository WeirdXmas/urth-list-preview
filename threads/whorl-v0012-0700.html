The Neighbors' other friends

Whorl list · 3 messages · 2001-04-23 to 2001-04-23

2001whorlThe Book of the Short SunThe Book of the Long SunRobert BorskiNicholas GeversMichael Straight
The thread: Readers asked whether Mora, like Horn and Silk, can travel astrally, and whether her dreamwalking connects to Fava or to the possession of others. Nicholas Gevers argued Mora does not travel like Horn but possesses others as Mucor does, perhaps with Fava's help, and suspected the talent is latent in her. Robert Borski proposed possession too, asking whether it explains who Mora and Inclito remind Horn of (Mucor and Blood), citing Horn's unnamed earlier meetings and objecting that Blood is hardly good people. Michael Straight drew parallels between Mora's dream-travel and Silk|Horn's astral travel and speculated astral travel needs a minimum distance, explaining why Horn never reached Nettle on Blue. The thread ended with three competing proposals and no resolution.
  • Nicholas Gevers: Mora possesses others like Mucor, not astral travel like Horn, perhaps with Fava's help
  • Robert Borski: Mora may possess others like Mucor, so Mora and Inclito may be Mucor and Blood
  • Michael Straight: Mora's dream-travel parallels Horn's astral travel, which may need a minimum distance

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Doubles, twins and clones, Gods, angels and the Increate

2001-04-23 Nicholas Gevers #

Argues that Mora does not travel astrally like Horn but possesses others as Mucor does, perhaps with Fava's help, while suspecting the talent is latent in Mora. Responds to a quoted question asking whether anyone besides Horn and Silk can travel astrally and whether Mora's dreamwalking links to Fava's spirit.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Mora possesses others like Mucor; Fava facilitates Mora's possession talent

Mora isn't travelling astrally in the same manner as
Horn--she's doing, with or without Fava, what Mucor
does, possessing others. Perhaps Fava facilitates
this, but I suspect the talent is latent in Mora;
still, an interesting point.

--Nick Gevers.

2001-04-23 Robert Borski #

Proposes that Mora, like Mucor, may possess others, and asks whether this answers who Mora and Inclito remind Horn of, namely Mucor and Blood. Cites a passage from In Green's Jungles where Horn says he met them before but will not name the names, and notes the problem that Blood is hardly good people.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Mora and Inclito remind Horn of Mucor and Blood

Nick Gevers has written the following:

<Mora isn't travelling astrally in the same manner as Horn--she's doing, with or without Fava, what Mucor does, possessing others. Perhaps Fava facilitates this, but I suspect the talent is latent in Mora; still, an interesting point.>

Hey, does this then perhaps answer the question about who it is that Mora and Inclito remind Horn of --i.e., Mucor and Blood?

Pertinent quote from IGJ: "...after racking my brain for long days I've finally realized who you and your father remind me of. I knew--I felt, at least--that I had met you both before. I won't tell you because the names would mean nothing to you."

The only problem with this is crimeboss Blood--who's hardly what I would call "very good people." Yet maybe...

Robert Borski

2001-04-23 Michael Straight #

Argues that Mora's dream-travel resembles Silk|Horn's astral travel in several respects, and speculates that astral travel may need a minimum distance, so Horn could reach Green but not Nettle on Blue. Grounds the idea in shared traits such as working only during sleep, carrying others along, and links to an inhuma, and in the question of why Horn never visited Nettle.
proposes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Mora's dreamwalking manifests as possession; Astral travel requires distance from one's physical body; Horn's possession of Silk is linked to astral travel

It's not the same, but it has several characteristics in common with Silk|Horn's astral travel that Mucor's posessions don't: only works when Mora is asleep, ability to bring others along, linked in some way with an inhuma. And if Horn's posession of Silk is mediated by the same phenomena that enables astral travel, then we have another similarity.

Does Silk|Horn ever do astral travel to another place on Blue? People have asked why he didn't use it to visit Nettle. Is it possible that there is a minimum distance necessary for it to work, that he could go to Green or somewhere further away, but not anywhere closer? Perhaps one can't have an astral body on the same planet as one's physical body, which would explain why Mora's dreamwalking manefests as posession.

-Rostrum
