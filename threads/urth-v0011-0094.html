Themes: Unreliable narration

1998-05-23 Charles Dye #

Argues that Severian's narration of Dorcas's childlike beauty echoes Nabokov's Lolita, since "Dorcas" resembles Dolores, and that Severian shares Humbert's unreliable, biased narration. Responds to J. Schultz's speculation about Severian's sexual relationship with Little Severian by raising the Lolita parallel as a playful, half-serious link.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Dorcas is a Lolita echo of Dolores; Severian is an unreliable narrator like Humbert

Brain wandering here ....

Doesn't Severian often describe Dorcas's "childlike beauty?" "Dorcas" sounds kind of like "Dolores", Lolita's true name. And "Dolores" in turn suggests pain ... a torturer? Hum, and Humbert Humbert claims (falsely) to have a photographic memory; an unreliable and biased narrator.
