Severian's Sexuality

Urth list · 4 messages · 2008-06-07 to 2008-06-07

2008urthThe Book of the New Sunb sharpJosh Geller
The thread: b sharp argued that Severian hides sexual and family details, proposing that Dorcas is his grandmother, that Jolenta, Cyriaca, Agia, Agilus and an actress are his kin, and that an incest curse and homoerotic hints run through the text. brunians pressed for explicit textual evidence and disputed that the clues were strong enough to show Wolfe intended them, contrasting them with firmer links among Severian, Ouen and Dorcas. b sharp replied by grounding the kinship claims in ages, looks, Pelerine ties and the incest motif, while conceding no explicit statement exists. The thread ended in impasse, with the evidence standard the main point of disagreement.
  • b sharp: Severian's family and sexuality are hinted at through omission and matching traits, including incest and an Oedipal curse
  • brunians: the proposed clues are too weak to show Wolfe intended kinship or sexual lineage
  • brunians: asks for explicit textual evidence of the claimed relationships

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Fathers, mothers and children, Unreliable narration

2008-06-07 b sharp #

Argues that Severian withholds sexual details in his narration, including that Dorcas is his grandmother, and suggests homoerotic and pedophilic hints in his thoughts. Uses Jolenta's "newly hatched chick" pubic-hair simile to support Borski's guess that her real hair is pale gold and her name Severa, and reads the family's incest as an Oedipal curse.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Dorcas is Severian's grandmother; Jolenta's real name is Severa; Jolenta's real hair is pale gold; Severian's family is Oedipally cursed

I'm in the camp which perceives Severian far more guilty of withholding details of his sexuality rather than embellishing them in his story. Heck, it takes him four books of sprinkling hints to finally admit he had sex with Thecla. He doesn't seem too prideful about his other "conquests".

He is most open and graphic in describing his sexual interactions with Dorcas but can never specifically commit to print that she is his grandmother. It also seems to me (sorry I know this is sacriligeous to some) that Wolfe gives Severian occasional homoerotic and even pedophilic thoughts. Naturally these embarassments are also disguised but I think they are there.

In a related vein, I was recently contemplating Wolfe describing Jolenta's pubic hair as a "newly hatched chick". I always thought it seemed an odd, jarring metaphor. I considered that it supports Borski's guess...showing us that Jolenta's real hair color (hidden by Wolfe earlier) is pale gold, like her grandmother, Dorcas, and that her real name might be Severa. The sexual congress between Severian and Dorcas, Dorcas and Jolenta, Agia and Agilus (his cousins I think), and Severian and Cyriaca (his aunt I think) is what leads me to think of this as an Oedipally cursed family.

2008-06-07 b sharp replying to b sharp #

Argues that Severian's family in The Book of the New Sun can be inferred from hints, proposing that Cyriaca, Jolenta, Agia and Agilus, and the St. Katharine actress are his aunt, sister, cousins and mother. Grounds these in matching ages, heights, hair and face traits, Pelerine ties, the incest motif, and the absence of any explicit statement in the text.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Ouen is Severian's father and Dorcas his grandmother; Catherine is Severian's mother; Agia and Agilus are Severian's cousins; Cyriaca is Severian's aunt

Brunian asks:

I don't know!!! I think so. And I'm not alone in that. But maybe that's just because I/ we were looking for evidence. From my first readings of this series I felt we were meant to identify members of Severian's family. For those who don't perceive that challenge the evidence will seem circumstantial or even manufactured. And Wolfe ain't tellin'.

Few debate the conclusion that Ouen is Severian's father and Dorcas his grandmother. But it is never stated openly in the book.

I think maybe it was Clute who deduced that the maid who plays St. Katharine is Severian's mother Catherine, whose face looks like a pool of still water (mirror) to Severian.

As far as I know it was Borski who figured Agia and Agilus to be his cousins. He makes a good case that Dorcas died in childbirth long after Ouen was born, gving Severian an aunt or uncle, so why not cousins. Severian is a twin so perhaps his cousins are also. The incest between Agia and Agilus might connect them to Severian and Dorcas' family. Perhaps the sexual attraction between Agia and Severian also. The golden aureole Wolfe keeps giving A and A could be a connection to Dorcas.

Borski sees the prisoner who nails children's feet to the floor as Severian's aunt. I think Cyriaca is a much better candidate. She is the right age and height. She shares a dark aureole of hair and an oval face with Catherine. They both escaped from the Pelerines. The Oedipal incest theme ties in nicely. Moreover, Cyriaca is comfortable escapingto Nessus, where she can cook and sew (Agia and Agilus ran a used clothing shop in Nessus their mother had owned). Agia possesses knowledge and acoutrements (scarlet cape and misericorde) associated with the Pelerines.

Jolenta might be the best candidate for his sister, Severa because SOMEBODY has to be Severa. She is the right age and height. She has hair color like Dorcas, but that and her real name are suspiciously hidden when Severian first meets her. There is the incest tie-in. Jolenta could have been a witch who left her guild like Severian (though perhaps Merryn and the Cumaean would have mentioned it..or maybe not).

There is evidence for other family members as well, but where do you draw the line of skepticism? Wolfe leaves things in a way which allows a reader the range from dismissing even Dorcas as a family member through accepting the possiblily of even more than I've mentioned.
(I could hint at [grand] Father Inire but perhaps we need not have that can of worms opened ;-).

-bsharp
