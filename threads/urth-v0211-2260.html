Clues: Needles in Haystacks

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-11-26 to 2002-11-26

2002urthJames Jordan

2002-11-26 James Jordan #

Argues that Wolfe's books bury their clues among so many details that readers cannot tell which items matter, so Wolfe's frustration at readers missing things is his own fault. Responds to Jerry Friedman's remark that Wolfe plays fair but may not realize what can be read as a clue, agreeing that the problem is real while saying the reading is still rewarding.
disputes

Yes, that's perhaps THE weakness in Wolfe. Out of the 1,000,000+ items he tosses into every novel, which are the clues? Wolfe gets frustrated that people don't "get" what's going on, but it's his own fault for putting so much stuff into his books and burying the clue-needles in such large haystacks. But even so, the trip is always worth it!

Nutria
