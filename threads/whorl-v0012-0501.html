Themes: Suffering and sacrifice

2001-03-28 Dan Parmenter #

Suggests that Silk, who associates cutting the jugular solely with performing sacrifice, might avoid that method in a suicide attempt, which would explain why he slashed his wrists instead. Agrees with an earlier post that Silk may not have thought clearly, and answers its question about why he did not slash his throat by pointing to his sacrificial background.
supports · close-textual-reading · allegory-and-typology · theories: Silk avoids the jugular because it signifies sacrifice
