2003-03-12 Che Monro #

Asks what deception the girl in a song Silk keeps recalling in Return to the Whorl is practicing, and whether it might be a boy in disguise or Hyacinth's or the inhumi's daughter's deception. Notes the song's gender reversal from typical folk songs and admits the timing of the inhumi daughter theory doesn't quite fit.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: the girl in the song is a boy in disguise; Hyacinth is the deceiver in the song

In Return to the Whorl Silk seems to have a song on his mind a lot it goes= =20
in part (like this):

I wish I knew what the tune was like so that I could try filking this song.= =20
I bet that Gene Wolfe had a tune in mind.

So could this then be loosely related to the theme of Hyacinth as being a=20 "deceiver", if you please?
