Mora as Mucor

2001-04-23 Dave Lebling #

Disputes Robert Borski's suggestion that Horn's unnamed old acquaintances are Mucor and Blood, arguing the remark to Mora in In Green's Jungles points to Chenille instead. Grounds this in Horn's line that Mora will be dead sexy once grown, despite her size and toughness, and floats Auk for Inclito with Blood as a reasonable alternative.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Horn was thinking of Chenille, not Mucor; Inclito resembles Auk rather than Blood

Hmm, given the conversation with Mora in which Horn basically tells her she's going to be dead sexy when she grows up, in spite of being a big, tough woman, my assumption has been that he was thinking of Chenille, not Mucor. I had thought Auk for Inclito, but Blood is certainly a reasonable speculation as well.

Dave Lebling
aka vizcacha
