1998-09-03 John Bishop #

Asks for recommendations of comics similar to Watchmen and for an explanation of the term "pomo" used in discussions of Eco. Adds a personal dislike of endings where everything blows up as a cheap trick, praising Wolfe for avoiding such effects.
questions

I'm another fan of "Watchmen"; are there other such you
can recommend? I know about "Maus".

What is "pomo"? There've been a number of references in the discussion of Eco and "The Name of the Rose". Personally, I tend to hate any story that ends with the set blowing up, no matter how good the lead-in has been--it's a cheap trick, and overused.

One attraction of Wolfe's work for me is that he doesn't go for the cheap effect.

-John

1998-09-04 Michael Straight #

Recommends The Dark Night Returns and V for Vendetta as comics like Watchmen, and explains that "pomo" abbreviates "post-modern" with a wry remark on how the term gets used. Responds to John Bishop's question about other comics and the meaning of the term. The post is social and off Wolfe, so it has no bearing on the books.
answers

The Dark Night Returns and V: For Vendetta are usually mentioned in the same breath as Watchmen (which means you've probably heard of them already too).

Just an abreviation for "post-modern." Which, as near as I can tell from common usage can mean either "I don't understand it, but I like it," or "I don't understand it and I don't like it."

-Rostrum
