When Adam Delved and Eve Span (5HC)

Urth list · 1 messages · 2005-01-27 to 2005-01-27

Themes: Power, rulers and politics, Slavery and service

2005-01-27 Jim Raylor #

Argues that the rhyme 'When Adam Delved and Eve Span' in The Fifth Head of Cerberus also points to the Black Death and the Peasants' Revolt, not only Biblical, Reformation and war references, fitting a recurring Wolfe theme of gradual emancipation through history. Grounds this in the rhyme's origin, found via a linked rhymes.org.uk page, and announces new orders of Wolfe books, Borski's Solar Labyrinth and Stevens's The Heads of Cerberus.
