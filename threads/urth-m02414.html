Forebearance vs. Forbearance

Urth list · 1 messages · 2006-05-08 to 2006-05-08

2006urthDavid F. Driscoll

2006-05-08 David F. Driscoll #

Asks whether the spelling "forebearance" is a real variant of "forbearance", citing a Merriam-Webster law dictionary entry that lists it and noting that the OED omits it. Raises the possibility that it is an Americanism, without reference to any specific Wolfe text.
questions

The Merriam-Webster's Dictionary of Law lists forebearance as an alternative for forbearance (http://dictionary.reference.com/search?q=forebearance). It is very odd though that the OED doesn't have it - is it an Americanism?
