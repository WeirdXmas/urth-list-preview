Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-07-02 mary whalen #

Thanks William Ansley for a compliment and says his idea that the Shadow Children names follow a pattern is not far-fetched. Proposes that if one Shadow Child is named Wolf as a lone wolf, the other levels might be based on fairy tales or puns, while admitting he cannot recall the other names.
supports · source-and-allusion · theories: Shadow Children names based on fairy tales or puns

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

I'd like to thank William Ansley for the compliment. You know the old saying, "He who comes last works hardest."

Actually, your idea about the Shadow Children names isn't really so far-fetched. If one SC is called Wolf (a lone wolf), could the other levels be based on fairy tales or puns? I don't remember what their names are for the other numbers, though.
