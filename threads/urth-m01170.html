Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

2005-06-08 Josh Young #

Argues that Japanese fiction about souls in dolls and robots, tracing back to bunraku puppet theater, offers useful parallels to Maytera Marble, and says Rah Xephon is a better-developed version of Evangelion. Responds to a post that compared Maytera Marble to Kusanagi Motoko, Lang's Metropolis gynoid, and Hadaly, and that called Evangelion gnostic. Mostly an aside that drifts into anime, with a brief apology and return to topic.
supports · genre-and-craft

There really are some interesting things in Japanese fiction with souls, and, specifically, dolls (or robots) with souls. Goes back to bunraku-- puppet theater from the 18th and 19th centuries.

Rah Xephon is like a much better developed Evangelion :)

Forgive me, one of my majors is Japanese. Back to topic :)
