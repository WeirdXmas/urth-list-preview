Passes along a Google Alert about a limited edition of Wolfe's forthcoming novel The Sorcerer's House, told in letters, and a blog link about a Walking Tour of the Shamles written by Neil Gaiman and another author in alternating letters. Notes the first item is already known but that limited editions are interesting, sharing both links as news without further analysis.
informs

Google Alert tells me of a page
http://news.pspublishing.co.uk/2009/08/03/midsummer-madness-60-off-pre-2008-titles/ which apparently says:

"We've been busy as usual with new purchases, including a limited edition of Gene Wolfe's new novel, The Sorcerer's House, due out next March. Told entirely in a series of letters, the result is a bona fide page-turner. ..."

We already knew all that, but limited editions are always fun.

Another fun link is http://kleinletters.com/Blog/?p=4900 on _A Walking Tour of the Shamles_ where Neil Gaiman comments that "One of us wrote four pages and sent them to the other, who wrote four pages and sent them back, and so on until it was done."
