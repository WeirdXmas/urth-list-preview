Nettle - rash?

Proposes three possible reasons Nettle would want to leave the mill and return to the Whorl with Silk and Horn in Return to the Whorl: admiration for Silk as friend and disciple, Silk carrying traces of Horn's personality, and her widowhood freeing her for a new start. Responds to Alan Lewis's remark about the oddness of the book's ending, and adds that the poster also finds many of its details unsettling.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Nettle returns to the Whorl because she is a widow seeking a new start

Greeting, Alan Lewis - welcome to the Urth List.

Here are some possible reasons why Nettle would want to return with Silk/Horn to the Whorl:

1) She has always admired Silk and would want to follow him as both a friend and "disciple".

2) Silk retains in his personality what little remains of her beloved husband Horn.

3) She now knows that her husband Horn is dead. She is a widow, and wants a new start. There's no point in staying on at the mill waiting for Horn to return. Better to let her children take over and run things. Give them a clear run and get out of their way.

But you're right to comment on the oddness of the book's ending. I have to confess that I find many details of the ending of RttW odd and unsettling. I need to raise the business of the killing of the inhumi at the wedding again some time...but not now.

Nigel
