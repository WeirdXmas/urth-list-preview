2005-01-17 Jim Raylor #

Riffs on the myth of Syrinx, a nymph turned into a reed that Pan cut into a pipe, and claims musical themes are hidden so obviously in The Fifth Head of Cerberus that he keeps recognizing them. Offers to find a referent in that book for any well-known piece of music, person, event, place or idea, and says he knows one of Wolfe's tricks.
proposes · source-and-allusion

On a musical note

Syrinx was a nymph who was cut up into pieces to save
her from Pan, probably by Zeus or one of those other
guys who hides children in the skies so that they will
be remembered... No he turned her into a reed or some
other hollow plant which Pan found and cut to
different lengths to make a pipe.... a bit like David.

Musical themes in GW are hidden so obviously that I am
growing numb from kicking myself when I hear a new
theme that I recognise.

Name any important piece of music known in 1967
(possibly 1972) and I will find a referent in 5HOC

Better yet... mention any significant person, event,
place or idea and I will tell you where it is found in
this book.
I DO know one of his tricks at least.
