Another thought about Disclave

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-03-19 to 1998-03-19

1998urthAlice Turner

1998-03-19 Alice Turner #

Proposes moving the planned get-together from Disclave to WorldCon, also held in Baltimore that year, in case Wolfe attends that convention instead. Reports that fellow poster Terry Bisson suggested this, citing Wolfe's close friend in Baltimore, and asks members to share any news and to check on postponing flights.
proposes

I know I'm disappointed about Disclave, but Terry Bisson reminded me that WorldCon (Labor Day weekend) is also in Baltimore this year, and suggested that Wolfe might show for that (he apparently has a close friend in Baltimore), and that we could transfer our whole roadshow to that. If any of us hears anything about that, why don't we post it. And people who have bought plane tix (PO'L?) might inquire about postponing them.

-alga-
