Cilinia's mausoleum

Whorl list · 5 messages · 2001-05-31 to 2001-06-06

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunMatthew MalthouseKevin J. MaroneyMichael Andre-DriussiMichael Straight
The thread: Michael Andre-Driussi questioned whether a claimed consensus, that a mausoleum in the necropolis is not Severian's hideout, had actually been established, saying he could not follow the reasoning, and offered no textual evidence of his own. He then withdrew that challenge and proposed a list-wide voting ballot that would digest long threads into consensus points with percentages, framing it as a summarizing tool rather than a reading of Wolfe. Michael Straight joked about who would pay for the idea and mocked a suggested tallied poll on whether Severian is his own ancestor without engaging its substance. The thread ended in drift rather than resolution, with the consensus question left unsettled and the talk shifting to the ballot idea.

2001-06-01 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Withdraws an earlier challenge and proposes a list-wide voting ballot that digests long threads into broad and fine-detail consensus points with percentages. Offers this as an idea for summarizing discussion rather than a reading of any Wolfe book, and responds to Jerry Friedman's objection to a claimed consensus.
proposes

Nevermind. I withdraw the statement/question.

OTOH, it does lead to an interesting notion: what if, instead of a FAQ about a book/series/story, what if there was a position tabulation, sort of like a voting ballet, showing broad points ("Participants like the piece" xx%; "Respondents dislike the piece" xx%), then medium points, down to fine detail points?

It would be a digested digest, I suppose. A tallying person would read through the thread, picking out the various points of concensus and contention, then use these to create the ballot. Rather like making an index, in a way. The ballot could then be voted on afresh, or the percentages could just be filled in based on how the discussion seemed to go in the original.

At the end of this there would be these handly little one-page summaries of where everybody has been and which sides they were on. Like a busload of tourists posing for a snapshot.

That would be something!

=mantis=
