Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-01-30 Peter Grimes #

Disputes that Wolfe said the Book of the New Sun is only two chiliads from our present, noting the poster does not recall that claim in the People interview. Argues the dying sun places the setting millions of years ahead, valuing the far-future post-apocalyptic frame.
disputes · author-and-biography

I don't remember Wolfe mentioning this in the People interview at all!

Anyway, ignoring all the mountain-carving and such, this book takes place when the sun is starting to lose its energy. I'm not an astrophysicist but I know that such an event is literally millions of years in our future.

This is one of the things about these books that I found so immediately fascinating and I'd hate to give it up. (I love post-apocalypse stories and TBOTNS is a post-post-post-post-etc-apocalypse story)

-Dave
