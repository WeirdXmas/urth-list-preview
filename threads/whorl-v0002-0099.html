answers · source-and-allusion

You've forgotten "The Cat", a short story set in the House Absolute during Severian's Autarchy. It is anthologized in _Endangered Species_, along with "The Map."

I suppose you could include "The Old Woman Who Used The Sun ... " err, something (another Cheap St. chapbook) as another story from _The Wonders of Urth and Sky_ although there's no external evidence, other than the title.
