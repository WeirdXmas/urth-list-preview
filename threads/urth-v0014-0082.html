John V Marsch, polish count

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-23 to 1998-06-23

1998urthTony Ellis

1998-06-23 Tony Ellis #

Relays Sgt. Rock's guess that the interrogator's verse in John V Marsch is a made-up mockery of academic credentials, with each title standing for an abbreviation such as PhD. Admits he cannot work out how the titles map to degrees and asks readers with more knowledge of academic qualifications to solve the puzzle.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Polish Count stands for PhD; The verse is invented, not a quotation

Sgt. Rock quoted:

It's just a little verse that the interrogator recites to show how clever he is. I suppose it _may_ be a quote from somewhere, or a parody of a quote from somewhere, but I've always assumed that he just makes it up.

Since he's ridiculing Marsch's academic qualifications, presumably each title is derived from a recognisable abbrevation. I would imagine that "Polish Count" = "PhD", although I don't pretend to see exactly how, and so forth.

Anybody out there with more time, imagination, and knowledge of academic qualifications than me who'd care to solve this one for us?
