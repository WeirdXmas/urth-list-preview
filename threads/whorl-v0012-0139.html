Silk's upload

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-02-08 to 2001-02-08

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunNicholas Gevers

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Books, libraries and writing

2001-02-08 Nicholas Gevers #

Argues that Horn, as narrator of The Book of the Long Sun, must have known Silk might accept Kypris's offer to upload him into Mainframe, since the offer is recorded in Exodus from the Long Sun. Answers Adam Stephanides's claim that Horn could not have known of Silk's uploading, citing the end of chapter 15 of Exodus as evidence.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Horn knew Silk accepted Kypris's upload offer

"IIRC, at one point in TBOTLS Kypris (or somebody)
offers to upload Silk into Mainframe, where he would
become a god and be linked somehow to Pas (I forget
the details). Evidently Silk either took her up on the
offer, or was uploaded against his will. This happened
after Horn left, which is why he has never heard of
the Divine Silk."

But as Kypris's offer to upload Silk into Mainframe as
a partner for Pas (EXODUS FROM THE LONG SUN, end of
chap 15) is recorded by Horn as author of TBOTLS, he
was certainly aware of the possibility that Silk had
accepted it.

--Nick Gevers.
