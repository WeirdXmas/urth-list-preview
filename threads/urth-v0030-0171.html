2000-12-08 Tony Ellis #

Reports that the poster had not known the word geimhreadh meant winter and says the name of King G fits Winter from context, such as his age, kingship and killing of the Green Man. Responds to Sbear's remark about finding the Gaelic meaning by web search, and says he will reread Castleview before answering the snipped questions.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: King G represents Winter

There now, you see, this is precisely the sort of thing that gives me confidence: I had no idea that the word geimhreadh actually meant ‘winter’. I was under the impression that King G’s name was one of the particulars of the novel we hadn’t been able to solve. But I still knew that he –represented– Winter because of the context. (He’s old, he’s a king, he kills the Green Man, etc.)

< many intriguing questions snipped >

It is years since I read Castleview, and while I feel I could at least answer some of these questions, the sensible thing to do would be to make sure I’ve got my facts straight first. I hereby bump Castleview to the top of my reading list.
