2002-05-21 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists the author's ranking of Jack Vance's mystery novels and gives short verdicts on several titles, including The Four Johns, The Madman Theory and A Room to Die In. Notes that the Ellery Queen ghostwriting attribution is absent from his bibliographies and proposes further investigation, responding to Adam's and Michael's earlier discussion of the attribution.
informs · source-and-allusion

Adam Stephanides asked me to name the titles to my atomic-scale reviews.

THE FOUR JOHNS is the one that is "screamingly good until it completely blows out (alas!)";

THE MADMAN THEORY is the one that is "okay, but still better imho than one of the ones published under his own name";

A ROOM TO DIE IN is very good, one of his best mysteries (of the 6 I've read).

The mysteries published under "John Holbrook Vance" I've read are: THE PLEASANT GROVE MURDERS (Joe Bain #2), THE FOX VALLEY MURDERS (Joe Bain #1), and THE DEADLY ISLES.

Personally I would rank them all this way:

1. the Joe Bain books
2. A ROOM TO DIE IN
3. THE MADMAN THEORY
4. THE FOUR JOHNS
5. THE DEADLY ISLES

Yes, that is correct.

I have not seen that attribution before. It is not given in my Galactic Central working bibliography. Nor do I find it in Cunningham's JACK VANCE: CRITICAL APPRECIATIONS AND A BIBLIOGRAPHY. Further investigation is in order!

=mantis=
