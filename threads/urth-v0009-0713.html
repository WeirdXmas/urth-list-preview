"Patterns of beats" [Digest urth.v009.n003]

1998-04-06 John Bishop #

Proposes that the yes-and-no sequence in the quoted line "Patterns of beats" may encode a binary number, with yes as 1 and no as 0. Works out that the seven answers give 1100011, which is lower-case 'c' in 7-bit ASCII and decimal 99, while conceding this may not help much.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Yes-no sequence encodes binary 1100011

The quote "...Patterns of beats. Yes, yes, no, no, no, yes, yes." could be specifying a binary number. In standard 7-bit ASCII, 1100011 is a lower-case 'c'. It's also the binary version of decimal 99.

Not that that helps much.

-John
