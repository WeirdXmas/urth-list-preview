1998-07-01 Matthew Freestone #

Jokingly adds a quip, attributed to Russell, that one knows oneself to be God because prayer amounts to talking to oneself, supporting Michael Straight's point that Severian's self-identification with Christ is better read as an expression of God's immanence and transcendence than as literal identity. The post is a brief, playful reply with no textual evidence beyond the quoted remark.
