Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2003-09-06 Lisa Schaffer-Doggett #

Asks whether the Egyptian ka, shown in statuary as upraised arms above a figure's head, could connect to the Vanished People in Wolfe's work. Quotes an Egyptology passage describing the ka as a double or individuality, then notes the extra arms distinguish the ka from the living person.
