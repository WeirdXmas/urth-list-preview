Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1998-06-18 Tony Ellis #

Argues that the drug the Shadow Children use in "A Story" is tied to their not using tools, and that the weed is a Shadow Child trait rather than a human one. Grounds this in the Old Wise One's remark that the weed makes them gods, with things made by hands not concerning them, and in Sandwalker's people copying the Shadow Children.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Shadow Children's weed explains their lack of tools

Hmmmm.... depends how you define "PC", I suppose. If, like me, you regard it as deplorable right-wing Newspeak, you're very happy to think of Wolfe as anti-PC. <g>

The Old Wise One says that the weed makes the Shadow Children "...gods, and things made by hands do not concern us." This might be an explanation for why the Shadow Children don't use tools, but the weed seems to be very much a Shadow Child thing, rather than a human thing. (He does go on to say that Sandwalker's people are the same because they copy the Shadow Children, but then the Shadow Children have a very dodgy sense of who they are. Later still, he admits that he's been getting humans and Shadow Children mixed-up all along.)
