Asks whether the list discusses The Book of the New Sun, noting that other Wolfe novels seem to dominate, and says the poster read it and The Urth of the New Sun twice but missed much of the intended meaning. Responds to no one; it is a newcomer's request for help with the books' deeper meaning.

I'm confused by the Urth mailing list. Do you ever discuss "The Book of the New Sun"? The primary focus seems to be other novels by Gene Wolfe.

I've read "The Book of the New Sun" and "The Urth of the New Sun" twice now, and really liked them, but think that I failed to pick up on a lot of the meaning that the reader is intended to understand. Help!

1997-06-16 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Welcomes newcomer Deborah to the Urth list and explains that it covers all of Wolfe's work except The Book of the Long Sun, which has its own list. Invites her to ask questions about The Book of the New Sun, predicts differing answers and friendly arguments, and asks what she feels she missed.
chats

Reply: Item #6089883 from [email]@INET03#

Deborah,

Hello there and welcome aboard!

In truth, the Urth list is really sort of "everything by Wolfe except for THE BOOK OF THE LONG SUN (which has its own list--where we all started)," but yes, there are many people here ready willing and able to talk New Sun until the arrival of Ushas.

So go ahead and ask a question or two and see what happens! (Don't be surprised if there are differing answers and/or friendly arguments arise--happens all the time.) What do you feel you missed--what do you think you don't understand? Everybody's friendly here and we all know that asking a question often makes the asker feel temporarily dumb.

=mantis=
