Themes: Time loops and causality

1999-12-30 shannon wilde #

Proposes that Palaemon, given his peculiar age, may have time-traveled himself like many other characters in The Book of the New Sun, since he was a potential alter-Sev who followed similar paths. Then asks the list whether anyone knows Charles Palliser's real name, noting his Trollope-like name, and closes with social well-wishes.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Palaemon has time-traveled like other characters

As to Palaemon's peculiar age, and maybe other of his,
ah, quirks, i seemed to have the impression that
perhaps he'd done a bit of time-traveling himself,
like so many other characters in BOTNS. After all, he
was a potential alter-Sev, yes? so it didn't quite
work out, but he seems to have traveled many of the
same paths pretty far, so why not? Um, this is way
off-topic, but could anyone fill me in on Charles
Palliser's real name, or does he just happen to really
have a Trollope-oid handle? Reckon this crowd probably
knows, and thank you all for the wealth of fascination
you generously and liberally scatter about. Best
fishes for the next K of years. hopefully we'll all
still be around. Shoot, let's plan on it. May we party
like lizards or otherwise enjoy!
