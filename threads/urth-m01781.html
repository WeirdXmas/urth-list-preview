Hieros vs Hierogrammates

Urth list · 1 messages · 2006-04-27 to 2006-04-27

2006urthThe Book of the New SunRoy C. Lackey

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

2006-04-27 Roy C. Lackey #

Corrects recent list usage by explaining that the Hieros are a distinct group from the Hierogrammates, not interchangeable with them. Grounds the distinction in Urth of the New Sun pages 137-38: the Hieros were humans of a previous universe who created the Hierogrammates, did not survive that universe's death, and the Hierogrammates then created the Hierodules.
informs · close-textual-reading

Not to be pedantic but, several times recently "Hieros" has been used here where the term was clearly not intended. It's an easy mistake to make; I did it at least once myself, years ago, what with "Hierogrammate", "Hierarch", "Hierodule" and such in use in the Urth Cycle.

But the Hieros are a breed apart. They were those bad, bad humans of a previous universe who created the Hierogrammates. The Hieros did not survive the death of that universe, but the Hierogrammates did. The Hierogrammates created the Hierodules. (URTH, 137-38)

-Roy
