1999-09-11 Nicholas Gevers #

Announces that a Kirkus review of On Blue's Waters now appears on Amazon as a preview of The Book of the Short Sun, and asks whether the Whorl list will carry all detailed discussion of the Short Sun books. Its grounds are the Amazon listing and the list-organization question itself, with no argument about the books' content.
informs

After a long absence, a notice and a query:
If anyone would like to get a very modest sneak preview of THE BOOK OF THE SHORT SUN, amazon.com's entry for ON BLUE'S WATERS now carries a brief Kirkus review of that work. Which raises a further matter: what is our plan with the Urth and Whorl lists? Will Whorl carry all detailed discussion of SHORT SUN?
