Nadan's Diary

Urth list · 1 messages · 1997-08-22 to 1997-08-22

1997urthAlice Turner

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1997-08-22 Alice Turner #

Asks how Nadan's diary account can add up to eight eggs when the poster counts only six, and walks through the diary entries sequence by sequence to locate the missing ones. Lists each evening's actions (the Visit to a Small Planet purchase, the Mary Rose performances, the killing of Ardis) and notes where an egg seems to be unaccounted for in the tally.
questions · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic

Here's my count of Nadan's account. I get six---but I'm missing an egg or two!

1. Nadan disembarks, goes to bed quietly.

2. Attends Visit to a Small Planet, purchases eggs, acid, engages prostitutes.

3. Takes egg one. Attends Mary Rose. Kills creature.

4. Takes egg two. Attends Mary Rose. Kreton/Bobby is arrested. Meets Ardis.

5. Takes egg three. Acts in Mary Rose. Makes love to Ardis.

An egg is missing at this point. We'll call it egg four.

6. Presumably takes egg five before going to party. Kills Ardis. Seems to me to take egg six that same night, making two in one night.

I don't see how you get eight. What am I missing?

-alga-
