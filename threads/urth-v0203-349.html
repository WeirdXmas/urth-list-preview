2002-03-14 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Recalls earlier list discussion of the Short Sun books, including a 'limited teleportation' idea, and says a scene in which the narrator questions an inhumu about the transport process suggests it appears in In Green's Jungles rather than Return to the Whorl. Replies to Hartshorn's request for the earlier citations, saying he kept no notes and suggesting an archive search instead.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Limited teleportation explains Short Sun travel

Hartshorn, he Q&Q'ed:

I didn't take any notes in reading the Short Sun books, alas. (Or hooray.) So I don't remember. I remember the discussion we were having around here as each book came out, and I thought that at one thread more than one person was agreeing with a sense of "fly lie" . . . I know I brought up the "limited teleporation" notion at least once . . . the scenes in the text had to do with N questioning an inhumu/a about the whole process, and s/he (probably she; so it was probably in IGJ rather than RTTW) became coy and evasive as he pressed harder.

Maybe this thread would show up in a search of the archives. Sorry I can't be of more help!
