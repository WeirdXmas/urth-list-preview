1998-05-04 William H. Ansley #

Identifies Don Maitz as the cover artist for The Book of the New Sun and says he is not fond of those covers but likes his other work. Cites Maitz's 1988 collection First Maitz, whose introduction by Wolfe praises the covers, and says Wolfe also complimented Maitz's covers in The Castle of the Otter.
answers

Mike,

Ooooh! An easy question! I'll answer it!

Don Maitz is the artist in question. I am not crazy about his covers for TBotNS, but I really like some of his other work. Wolfe does seem to like the covers, though. Maitz's first collection, _First Maitz_ (1988, ISBN 0-942681-01-0) has an introduction by Wolfe, who says some extremely nice things about them. Wolfe also complements Maitz's covers elsewhere, I believe in _The Castle of the Otter_.

William Ansley
