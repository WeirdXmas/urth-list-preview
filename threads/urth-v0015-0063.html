Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

1998-07-02 mary whalen #

Argues that the tribes in Tracking Song are giant Ice Age versions of North American animals, matching dire wolves, giant elk, cave lions and a smilodon-like hunter. Grounds this in the hunter's rib-bone weapon and parallels it to the smilodon in the Tale of the Boy Named Frog, then asks others for further parallels.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Tracking Song tribes are giant Ice Age North American animals; Tracking Song's sabre-toothed hunter parallels the smilodon in the Boy Named Frog tale

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

In Tracking Song, it seems that the tribes don't just represent modern animals, but Ice Age animals. They also are all giant varieties of current animals in North America who also were found in North America.

Wiggikki: dire wolves
Nashhwonk: giant elk
Ketin(sha): cave lion(ness)
Panigaku: (don't know what a giant boar would be)
(Cim's tribe): giant mink
Min: (still don't know anything about these)

I don't remember the name of the big cat guy who attacks the Panigaku, but he is represented by a smilodon (sabre-toothed tiger). This is because his weapon is make of a stick with two rib bones at the end curving like the fangs of this animal did. This also parallels the smilodon in the Tale of the Boy Named Frog in the Book of the New Sun. Does anyone have any more theories on parallels in these two stories?

prion.
