Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2003-07-20 Ken Burstall #

Proposes that Silk is not Malrubius and that the meeting of Silk and Horn with Severian serves as a Chapter Zero explaining Severian's urge to be good. Grounds this in moral symmetry: Severian escapes a terrible upbringing while Silk overcomes a genetic inheritance from Typhon, if Silk is a clone.
