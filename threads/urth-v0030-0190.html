2000-12-10 Roy C. Lackey #

Supports Adam's reading of Peace's visit scene as dreamlike, adding that Weer sees five people in the waiting room, one of whom is himself. Grounds this in the passage itself and in the common dream experience of being both observer and participant, while distancing himself from Adam's end-of-the-world hypothesis.
