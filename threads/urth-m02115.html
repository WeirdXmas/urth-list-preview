Notes that Walter M. Miller Jr. in A Canticle for Leibowitz also contrasts a linear, progressive spiritual history with a cyclical secular one, as a Catholic author. Responds to b sharp's remark that a cyclical view sounds Buddhist, offering a parallel from another Catholic novelist; adds a joking aside about the Protestants in Miller's book.
supports · genre-and-craft

True, but bear in mind that at least one other Catholic author has used the comparison between a linear (and progressive) spirtitual history and a cyclical secular one: Walter M Miller Jr in 'A Canticle for Liebowitz' [1].

[1] A rather wonderful little book, even if one does wonder as to what became of all those Protestants...
