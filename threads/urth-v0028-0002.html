1999-07-16 Dave Lebling #

Shares an Amazon.com list of authors bought by Wolfe readers, noting that it is mostly spy and thriller writers plus William Safire, and remarks on the pre-release sales rank of On Blue's Waters. Makes this observation as a casual aside, praising only John le Carre's prose and plotting.
chats

Apropos of nothing, I find this interesting (from Amazon.com):

"Customers who bought titles by Gene Wolfe also bought titles by these authors:

Frederick Forsyth
John Le Carre
Daniel Silva
Joseph Kanon
William Safire"

What an unexpected list! Except for Safire, it's all spies and thrillers. Only one prose and plot stylist (IMHO), Le Carre.

Also, _On Blue's Waters_ ranks 65,062 in sales several months pre-release. Not exactly _Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban_ (which ranks 8th), but respectable.
