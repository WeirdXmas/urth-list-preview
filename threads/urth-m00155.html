Finished _The Wizard_

Urth list · 5 messages · 2004-11-03 to 2004-11-04

2004urthLisa Schaffer-DoggettDan RabinJames WynnDan'l Danehy-OakesNathan Spears
The thread: Dan Rabin proposed that list members post a signal when they finish a book so the group could tell when discussion would be spoiler-safe, and he announced he had finished The Wizard. Nathan Spears countered with a fixed start date, such as January 1, because he was busy with other books and did not want to miss discussion. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes voted for January 1, citing his own commitments and travel, and James Wynn agreed with that date while planning a reread first. The thread ended in agreement on the January 1 start date, with the finish-signal idea not clearly adopted.
  • Dan Rabin: post a finish signal so spoiler-free discussion can begin when the group is ready
  • Nathan Spears: set a fixed start date such as January 1 instead of waiting for finish posts
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: supports the January 1 start date because of his own commitments and travel
  • James Wynn: agrees with January 1 but wants to reread both volumes before commenting

Proposes a way for list members to signal when they finish a book so the group can judge when it is safe to begin discussing it. Says the poster completed The Wizard on Sunday and suggests posting each finish as a timing cue for spoiler-free discussion.
