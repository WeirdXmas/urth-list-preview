Themes: Memory and forgetting

1998-05-07 Michael Doolittle #

Argues that Palaemon's remark that Severian would forget his advice is not literal, but means the advice would pass unheeded, as young men often ignore their elders. Responds to the quoted observation about Palaemon's odd words, which noted Severian's famously perfect memory, and thanks list members for help with book covers.
disputes · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft · theories: Palaemon's forgetting remark is figurative, not literal

First I want to say thanks everyone for the help on the book covers. It was a great help.

I think what Palaemon meant was not that Sev would literally forget, but that the advice would "go in one ear and out the other", in the way that young men often ignore the advice of their elders.

Mike
