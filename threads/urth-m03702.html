Reports finding the Magazine of Fantasy and Science Fiction available as an ebook online, useful for a reader outside London who cannot easily buy the print issue. Shares a link to the Fictionwise listing and notes that the PDF appears printable and currently available.

I was scouting the net to see if I could find a source for the Magazine of Fantasy and Science Fiction here in the UK (well, outside London, really, as I know I can go to Forbidden Planet there) and found it is available as an ebook:

http://www.fictionwise.com/eBooks/eBook43580.htm

It seems to indicate that the PDF version is printable, and it also appears to be available now...
