Disputes readings that treat every water encounter in Wolfe's novels as a baptism and every dinner as a eucharist, using Freud's cigar as the sensible example. Responds to Alice Turner's post proposing that Reechy becomes Ymar via the serpent and knife letters, mixing this with jokes to several list members about Ratty's return and newts.

1998-01-31 James Jordan #

Argues that Blind Ultan's library in The Book of the New Sun is primarily modeled on Borges's library in Buenos Aires rather than Alexandria, though it may allude to Alexandria too. Grounds the claim in the Borgesian framing of the series, where the narrator says he found and translated a manuscript, and notes Wolfe's own confirmation.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Ultan's library is primarily modeled on Borges's library

Slimy:

Where have I ever made such an assertion? Now, when it comes to Wolfe, I'm pretty safe making the assertion, since it is fairly obvious. Also, since he confirmed it.
Blind Ultan's library may allude to Alexandria (I suspect it doth), but is primarily Blind Borges's library in Buenos Aires. (Note the Borgesian intro to the series: I found this ms and translated it....).

Big Nutria Rat
