Wolfe profile in the New Yorker

Urth list · 5 messages · 2015-04-24 to 2015-04-27

2015urthDavid StockhoffMarc AraminiRichard SimonRobin B. Smith
The thread: Posters on the Urth list shared and discussed a New Yorker profile of Gene Wolfe. Marc Aramini flagged that the piece credits Wolfe with over fifty stories, far below the more than 220 in his own catalogue, and welcomed the coverage. David Stockhoff mostly joked, suggesting that revering Wolfe should be monolatrous and that the list might start a cult, but he also disputed the profile's line that Wolfe's books contain all the nasty genre tropes, asking whether the critic's own view or a supposed general opinion was speaking. Richard Simon repeated the fifty-stories discrepancy and the hope that Wolfe will be revered later. The thread ended without any real back-and-forth, drifting into repetition.
  • Marc Aramini: welcomes the New Yorker profile but notes its story count undercounts Wolfe's catalogue
  • David Stockhoff: jokes about monolatry and a cult, and disputes the profile's genre framing as unclear in whose voice it speaks
  • Richard Simon: repeats the story-count discrepancy and hopes Wolfe will be revered later

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both
