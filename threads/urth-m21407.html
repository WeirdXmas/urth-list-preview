2011-10-19 Matthew Keeley #

Informs the list that Orion's SF Gateway ebook site has launched with many out-of-print titles but no Wolfe rarities yet. Notes that the first three New Sun volumes are sold only to non-US readers because of copyright, and links to the site.
informs

I remember a few months ago there was some discussion here of Orion's SF Gateway ebook site. News of its launch passed me by until just this week; it seems to have an extremely good selection of out-of-print, but unfortunately there aren't any Wolfe rarities available yet. The first three volumes of New Sun are available, it's true, but copyright issues mean they're only for sale to non-US readers. Other books by other authors don't have this restriction, but Wolfe's in print from different publishers in different countries.

http://www.sfgateway.com/

-Matt
