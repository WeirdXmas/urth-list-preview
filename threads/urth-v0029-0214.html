Themes: Unreliable narration

2000-09-11 Alex David Groce #

Asks where in Wolfe's interviews he says that readers who notice two characters describing one event differently should simply conclude one of them is lying, and suspects the McCaffrey interview. Seeks a source citation rather than arguing an interpretation of any passage.
questions · author-and-biography

Does anyone remember where Wolfe says something to the effect of "I'm always stunned when a reader asks me why two people in one of my stories describe an event differently. Don't they think 'One of them must be lying?'"

It's in one of the various interviews, I'm pretty sure, and I wonder if it may be the Larry McCaffrey one that I don't have a copy of.
