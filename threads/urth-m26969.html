2021-10-10 Simon Morris #

Asks whether Gene Wolfe ever visited Cambridge, UK, and suggests that the Garden of Everlasting Sleep in The Shadow of the Torturer resembles the fens around Cambridge. Points to the Botanic Garden's jungle room, with its serpentine path and differing doors, as matching the real Cambridge University Botanic Garden.
questions · source-and-allusion · author-and-biography · theories: Botanic Garden based on Cambridge University Botanic Garden

Did Wolfe ever visit Cambridge (UK)?

The Garden of Everlasting Sleep (I.22) is reminiscent (to me) of the fens around Cambridge (flat-bottomed punts; peat bog). The Botanic Garden is likewise reminiscent of the real Cambridge University Botanic Garden in some specific details: the "jungle room" of the real one does have a serpentine path that, unlike those in other rooms, has different exit and entrance doors. I've never noticed a hut, though.

2022-02-23 Mo Holkar / UKG replying to Peter Westlake #

Asks for someone to take over ownership and administration of the WolfeWiki, a site Mo Holkar set up about fifteen years earlier, offering the domain and files free if the new person can manage hosting and installation. Notes the wiki has been neglected and would benefit from tidying, clearer contribution paths and filling gaps, and mentions it began as a page explaining names in Soldier of the Mist.
informs

Dear all,

I'm looking for someone (or someones) to take over ownership and administation of the WolfeWiki, https://www.wolfewiki.com .

I set this up quite a while back now*, and although there's been some fantastic stuff contributed over the period, it has generally more recently got a bit neglected and under-used. I am too busy these days to do much with it, so I feel like a new pair of hands could make some quick and good progress in tidying it up, streamlining the contribution path, encouraging more contributions, updating what needs updating, filling obvious gaps etc.

The project consists of the domain wolfewiki.com, and some hosting, on which is installed the wiki software and the contributed material. I'm happy to hand over ownership of the domain to the new person, and to send the files once you've set up your own hosting for it. (All free of charge of course, including however long is left on the domain registration.)

But -- I'm limited in the amount of handholding etc that I can do when passing it over. So, you do need to have sufficient technical capability to be able to yourself pretty much manage the process of setting it up. This doesn't need you to be an IT whiz -- but it does need you to be familiar with the ins and outs of how to use web hosting, how to install software via FTP, maybe a bit of wrangling with file permissions, etc.

If you're interested, let me know via email, perhaps with a bit of an outline of your experience with managing this sort of thing.

best wishes,

Mo

* (looking back, it's nearly 15 years... yikes. It started with a simple page explaining the names in Soldier of the Mist.)

2022-03-26 Darth Ed replying to Mo Holkar #

Announces that the poster has taken over ownership and administration of the WolfeWiki from Mo Holkar, closing a loop on Holkar's earlier call for a new administrator. Introduces himself as a long-time wiki contributor and list subscriber, describes his reading history, and invites others to contribute.
informs

Just to close the loop on this in case anyone was wondering, I contacted Mo about this about a month ago and arranged with him to take over ownership and administation of the WolfeWiki. That is now complete.

You might be asking, "Who is this guy?" Well, I've been a long-time contributor of the WolfeWiki (since 2009), and I've been a subscriber to this mailing list for about 11 years. I don't post very often, but I am a passionate fan and have read over 90% of Mr. Wolfe's fiction at least once since my first exposure in 2003.

https://www.wolfewiki.com/pmwiki/pmwiki.php?n=Profiles.DarthEd

I think the WolfeWiki is a valuable repository of Wolfe analysis, knowledge, and theories. If you're interested in contributing to the WolfeWiki, please refer to

https://www.wolfewiki.com/pmwiki/pmwiki.php?n=Admin.BecomingAnEditor

Feel free to email with any questions.

Thanks,
Ed
