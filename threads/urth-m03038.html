Lays out the list owner's official guidelines on what may be discussed, stating that talk of Wolfe's works is good while English usage and email formatting are discouraged. It also addresses interleaving, top-posting and quoting, with no reference to any book or reader's argument.

Here's the official word from the list owner (me!) --

Talking about the works of Gene Wolfe: GOOD
Talking about matters of English usage: DISCOURAGED

"Interleaving": ENCOURAGED
Top-posting: MILDLY DISCOURAGED

EXCESSIVE USE of CAPITAL LETTERS: discouraged. Sorry.
