Jokingly proposes a 'Journeyman Torturer Mary Sue' story, a fanfic parody of Wolfe's Book of the New Sun in which a flawless female torturer outdoes Severian, Barbatus and Baldanders. Responds to a quoted post listing absurd New Sun fanfic ideas, and the joke rests on the fanfic 'Mary Sue' stereotype.
