2001-02-14 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Disputes Peeter Piegaze's claim that the commotion at the citadel gate in In Green's Jungles matches Severian's 'disturbance at the gate' in The Shadow of the Torturer, suggesting the phrase instead recalls the gate of Nessus. Builds on Nick Gevers' earlier idea that Horn's arrival at the citadel explains the guard's absence at the necropolis gate when Severian nearly drowns in chapter one.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Horn's arrival at the citadel explains the guard's absence; The disturbance at the gate refers to the gate of Nessus

Nick Gevers was the first to suggest that the arrival of Horn and co. at the citadel explains the guard's absence at the necropolis gate on the day that Severian nearly drowned (I, ch. 1). (Your phrase "disturbance at the gate" sounds more like whatever it was that happened at the gate of Nessus.)
