2003-07-08 Christopher Culver #

Disputes Marc Aramini's reading that the skull Severian finds in the lake belongs to a former Severian, arguing instead that it is simply an object prompting him to reflect on the many people he has killed to save Urth. The post offers no textual citation in the visible portion, relying on the poster's own interpretation of the ending of The Urth of the New Sun.
