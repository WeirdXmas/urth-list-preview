Argues that the voided cross in Wolfe's work could be read as a gammadion or as a circled, slashed cross, both mockeries of the Christian cross fitting for Typhon's cult. Offers a symbols encyclopedia link and speculates Wolfe may never have been asked about the emblem's appearance.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theology-and-religion · theories: Voided cross is a mockery of the Christian cross; Voided cross suits Typhon's cult with Typhon as Godhead

I agree that the voided cross is a gammadion, composed of 4 gammas arranged in such a way that the empty space between them is cross or plus shaped.

But it is interesting to consider this:
http://www.symbols.com/encyclopedia/13/131.html

Or even a cross with a circle around it and slash through it rendering it "voided".

I don't know if Gene was ever asked about its appearance, but any of the above three would be ideal mockeries to the Christian cross and a fitting symbol for Typhon's cult with him as its Godhead.

D
