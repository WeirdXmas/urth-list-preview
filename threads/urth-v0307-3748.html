2003-07-26 Josh Geller #

Proposes a paid weekend symposium so list members could spend time with Gene Wolfe, with proposed prices, participant limits, and expenses covered for the writer. Makes the plan in a list of bulleted terms and asks whether anything is missing and whether people would pay.
proposes

So would I.

I hope that this implies that you are also willing to pay to hang out with Gene Wolfe, assuming that he goes for it.

The more of you say yes, the easier this is going to be assuming that he agrees.

I have been thinking, and this is the form this thing is taking in my mind currently:

-> It should be for a weekend, possibly a long weekend, not for a whole week.

-> It should cost something like $200 for real-time participants. Real time participants will be limited to 12.

-> It should cost something like $50 for online participants. I am sure if there should be a limit to the number of online participants. Any comments?

-> It is arranged primarily for the writer's convenience. All of the writer's transportation, food and hotel costs shall be paid. It should not cost the writer one dollar to participate in this symposium. If after expenses are paid, there is some cash left over, it will be presented to the writer as a nominal token of gratitude.

-> Discussions will be reduced into articles or books in a way mutually agreeable to the writer and the entity doing the symposium, marketed appropriately, and any proceeds split fifty-fifty between the two.

-> Topics for each day's discussion are at the writer's sole discretion. The writer may do as much or as little as they please by way of work.

-> One fine meal, either a lunch or a dinner, will be served to participants each day. The writer, of course, has all additional meals and those of his spouse paid for.

Any further comments? Anything else I need to say that I am not saying?

J.
