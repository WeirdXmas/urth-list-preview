2001-02-01 Timothy Reilly #

Reports that the poster gave up on Free Live Free after a few pages, finding its folksy prose and weak humour unappealing compared with Wolfe's more literary work. Offers a secondhand account of a friend who read half the novel and skipped to the end, judging it very bad, presented as evidence against recommending it.
informs

I gave up after a few pages, quickly deciding that I didn't like this sort of Wolfe - folksy prose and weak humour, as opposed to his more literary efforts like TBNS. I was tring to introduce a well read friend of mine to Wolfe last year, and her local library only had FLF. She reported to me that she read half of it and then skipped to the end "because it was very bad". So 0/2 from this end.

Tim
