Green Room

Urth list · 3 messages · 1998-02-08 to 1998-02-09

1998urthThe Book of the New SunC R CulverMichael Andre-DriussiJames Jordan
The thread: A poster asked where the term "green room" comes from and whether its theatrical origin explains the convention panel-prep room, while also listing candidate material for a Lexicon addendum and asking whether a proto-Severian article would fit. Jim Jordan then asked where the Green Room appears in The Book of the New Sun and whether other green things recur, and proposed scanning the books into a word processor for thematic searching. CRCulver backed the scanning idea as a scholarly aid while joking that it would violate copyright. The thread ended without any answers about the origin of the term or the location of the room in the text.

  • Jim Jordan: Wants the Green Room located in the text and proposes scanning the books for searching

1998-02-08 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks where the term "green room" comes from, wondering whether its theatrical origin explains its use at conventions for panel prep rooms. Also lists material slated for a Lexicon addendum, including Kardashev civilization types, the dying sun, and terraforming notes, and asks whether an article on the proto-Severian will fit.
questions · source-and-allusion

vizcacha,

Thanks for the note on "Green Room"--I knew of it because the same (no doubt derived from the theatrical tradition) is used at conventions (informal pre-panel prep room for panel participants), but I still wonder as to the etymology or historical reasons as to how and why. (Does anybody know?)

What we have so far certainly deserves mention in "Additions, Errata, &cetera: volume 3," as well as all the other tidbits slated and a huge amount about Kardashev Type I, Type II, and Type III (levels of interstellar civilization); the 20th century science (and subsequent science fiction) regarding a possibly already dying sun; the text points of TBOTNS suggesting a black hole in the Sun (this lack in the LEXICON is shocking); terraforming timelines, stellaforming, and other relevant notes from Fogg's TERRAFORMING, and so on.

Will there be room for the article on "ProtoS" (the proto-Severian)? Probably not, but we shall see.

=mantis=
