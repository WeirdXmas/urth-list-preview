Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

1998-10-07 Robert Borski #

Questions whether the khaibits in The Book of the New Sun are clones, as the Autarch suggests, or women recruited from the poor, as Gurloes and Severian imply. Cites the sleigh-versus-flier arrival of the khaibit Thecla, the trip from the House Absolute, Merryn calling Thecla sister, and the leech's rejuvenation idea, while claiming 'wiggle room' and endorsing Jon Camfield's Healer's Tower sister proposal.
questions · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Khaibits are clones grown from exultant women; Khaibit Thecla is the Healer's Tower sister

In CLAW, Gurloes tells us khaibits are "common girls that look like the chatelaines. [But] I don't know where they get them." Severian, in his first and only visit to the House Azure, believes them to be recruited from the poor. But in CASTLE the Autarch implies they're clones, "grown from the body cells of exultant women so an exchange of blood will prolong the exultant's youth." So which is it?

If clones, as asserted by the Autarch (who is himself a master of deception), why are khaibits somewhat shorter--as both Gurloes and Severian assert?

On the night that Roche and Severian visit the Echopraxia, the Autarch tells them the women at the House Azure have been flown in by flier. But when alone with his khaibit, the "Chatelaine Thecla" tells him that she and Gracia have come by sleigh. Given that it's winter and snowing, is it likely that she and Gracia have come all way from the House Absolute, where the Autarch's seraglio is located? The House Absolute is well outside Nessus, remember, and even by destrier, in winter this would be some trip. And what of the other two hookers present--the Chatelaines Thea and Barbea? How have they arrived? (And why are fake Thecla's clothes so tacky?)

If beloved, alzabo-ingested Thecla has a clone, or has undergone rejuvenation via bloodsharing, Severian does not mention the memory of either. Nor does the leech who attends Severian when he's recaptured by Vodalus. His idea of rejuvenation, because "youth is contagious," is to sleep with a 13 year old boy. And if cloning is practiced in the Commonwealth, are there any other mentions of it in the BotNS?

I claim wiggle room here.

That being said, I do think Jon Camfield's
sis-might-be-in-the-Healer's-Tower proposal to be at least worthy of consideration, especially if he can find any secondary textual evidence.

Robert Borski

ps: I'm surprised no one's mentioned Merryn's calling Thecla "sister" in the fever dream Sev has in the lazaret.

1998-10-07 Peter Westlake #

Proposes that khaibits are younger than their originals and that a growth hormone may be the active agent in the blood exchange that prolongs exultant lives, linking this to the Hierodules' remark about Baldanders. Grounds this in the Autarch's clone remark in Castle and Gurloes's account in Claw, noting Severian's limited telling of the House Azure visit.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Khaibits are younger clones of their exultant originals; A growth hormone drives the blood exchange

The Hierodules tell Severian that Baldanders is so big because on Urth, perpetual growth is the only way to prolong life. The khaibits will be younger than their originals, so maybe a growth hormone or some such thing is the active agent in the exchange of blood; but however it works, the exultants are having their lives prolonged, so they become taller.

It is interesting that Thecla memories don't mention cloning, but then Severian is hardly one to give a complete account of important details.

SBear.
