"Go Get Silk!"

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-10-12 to 1999-10-12

1999whorlThe Book of the Long SunJames Jordan

Themes: Catholic faith and sacraments, Christ figures and messiahs

1999-10-12 James Jordan #

Argues that the search for Silk in The Book of the Long Sun is a cop-out, since the people of Blue already have the Book of Silk, sacraments and their own resources, just as Christians need not wait for Jesus's return. Grounds this in a parallel with Christian faith and in the Blue colonists keeping slave girls, which he says contradicts the Book of Silk's teaching on ruling and serving.
proposes · theology-and-religion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Silk's return is unnecessary, as Christ's return is for Christians; The search for Silk is a cop-out

Look at all the Christians who want Jesus to return soon and bail us/them out of all their problems. I suspect that's part of what Wolfe has in mind when the leaders say they want Silk back.
They don't need Silk back, any more than Christians need Jesus back. Christians have the Bible, which is the Word of Christ and tells them all they need, and Christians have the Spirit, who puts them in touch with Jesus personally, and they have the sacraments. They may look forward to seeing Jesus in the flesh someday, but they (we) have everything we need right now, all we need to do what we are called to do in this world. The same is true on Blue, I suggest. They have the Book of Silk, which if they read it would tell them how to rule and serve wisely. But as long as they keep slave girls for sex, they are not exactly following the Book of Silk, are they? As for sacraments, that seems to be a problem, but is it? They think their seed is bad, and that they have got to go up to heaven to get better seed. Maybe that's what Wolfe intends: that prayer is needed before we get the food we really need. Or maybe they don't need to go back to the Whorl for better seed at all. Maybe they need to learn how to use what they have. We'll have to wait and see.
One thing is for sure: They don't need Silk, and the search for Silk is just a cop-out; even as Christians who keep thinking Jesus is about to come back are frequently coping out on their responsibilities in this world.

Nutria.
