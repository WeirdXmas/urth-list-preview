2003-07-15 Andrew Bollen #

Argues that the time gap between Silk's two visits to Urth in The Return to the Whorl conflicts with Severian's statement in The Shadow of the Torturer that he kept Triskele only a week, and lists four possible explanations, choosing the simple authorial error. Grounds this in the chapter 13 and chapter 19 passages where Severian still has Triskele, set against the Shadow chapter IV quotation about the week.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Wolfe made a continuity mistake about Triskele

Quite a lot more time than a week must separate Silk's visit to Urth = from Dorp (RTTW Ch 13) & his final visit from New Viron (Ch 19). But in = both passages, Sev has Triskele, whereas in Shadow Sev tells us he only = had the dog for a week - Ch IV: "A week after I had carried him down, I = found only Triskele's hobbling footprints in the mud..."

Either:

(1) Wolfe made a little mistake.
(2) Silk can & does astral travel through time as well as space. (3) Silk's first visit somehow changes things, so that Triskele stays = longer (the blankets he has in RTTW aren't mentioned in Shadow ...) (4) Some other thing.

I pick (1).
