Silk & St. Francis

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-01-11 to 1999-01-11

1999urthThe Book of the Long SunMichael Straight

Themes: Christ figures and messiahs

1999-01-11 Michael Straight #

Argues that Silk's divine vision parallels St. Francis's vision of rebuilding a chapel, which Francis later understood as a wider commission to renew the Church. Builds on Alex David Groce's point about Silk's failed burglary and Chesterton's account of Francis repairing a chapel, offering the vision as a further parallel rather than a disagreement.
supports · source-and-allusion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Silk's theophany parallels St. Francis's chapel vision

I think someone mentioned this a while back, but the other parallel between Silk and St. Francis is that St. Francis had a vision from God which he initially interpreted literally as telling him to rebuild a particular chapel, but later came to understand as a commission to help "rebuild" the Church through his missionary order. Silk's theophany is similarly a wider commission than merely saving a few buildings.

-Rostrum
