The Peregrine Palace

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-12-14 to 2000-12-14

2000urthNicholas Gevers

Themes: Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both

2000-12-14 Nicholas Gevers #

Argues that an SF cover for Tales of the Dying Earth is not irrational, since Vance's last Dying Earth story, "Morreion," has the Peregrine Palace, a science fictional vehicle, and Ampridatvir in "Ulan Dhor" is a similar science fictional city. Cites the Peregrine Palace and Ampridatvir as science fictional elements in fantasy, and notes the poster's Vance-derived user name.
informs · theories: SF cover for Tales of the Dying Earth is justified

As I take my user name from the Vance story involved,
I should comment on this Vance DYING EARTH cover
matter. In the very last Dying Earth story, "Morreion"
in RHIALTO THE MARVELLOUS, Rhialto and his fellow
magicians are conveyed across the galaxies by a
so-called Peregrine Palace, owned by Vermoulian the
Dream Walker. This is a kind of science fictional
sortie into Fantasy, as is the city of Ampridatvir in
"Ulan Dhor" (Chapter 5 of THE DYING EARTH), so an SF
cover for TALES OF THE DYING EARTH is not altogether
irrational.

--Nick Gevers.
