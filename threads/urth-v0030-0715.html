New Wolfe novella

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-10-16 to 2001-10-16

2001urthNicholas Gevers

2001-10-16 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs readers that Wolfe has a new novella, "Viewpoint", in the anthology REDSHIFT, and that another new story will appear in MARS PROBES and an older story was reprinted in YEAR'S BEST FANTASY. Gives publication details for each, including the editors and publishers, and calls "The Walking Sticks" a dismal sequel to DR JEKYLL.
informs

A new novella by Gene Wolfe, "Viewpoint", is included
in REDSHIFT, a massive and very impressive-looking
anthology edited by Al Sarrantonio and slated for
November release by Roc Books.

Other GW story notes:

Another new story by Wolfe will appear in MARS PROBES,
a 2002 DAW Books anthology edited by Peter Crowther.

A little-known recent story by Wolfe, "The Walking
Sticks", an unfortunately pretty dismal "sequel" to DR
JEKYLL, first published in a limited edition anthology
edited by Crowther (TAPS AND SIGHS), has been
reprinted in David Hartwell's paperback volume YEAR'S
BEST FANTASY (Eos).

--Nick Gevers.
