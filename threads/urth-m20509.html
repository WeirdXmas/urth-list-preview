2011-11-23 Lee Berman #

Argues that Pig's report of Hierax's death is more puzzling than the death itself, and proposes that the pirate ship and Seawrack are extrusions of The Mother, linked to the off-world naviscaput legend. Grounds this in Horn's shot severing Seawrack's arm, her freshly healed wound, and her swimming speed, and compares Seawrack's sending to the undines sent to Severian.
proposes · puzzle-solving · myth-and-structure · theories: Seawrack is an extrusion of The Mother; Pirate ship is a naviscaput-like extrusion; Horn's shot knocked off Seawrack's arm; Powers in the sea foresee Horn's future

I find it interesting that Hierax is dead, but I find it more interesting to wonder how Pig knows this.

Wow. My interpretation is so different. I think perhaps the "pirate ship" was like the naviscaput in the off-world legend in UotNS. An above-water extrusion of The Mother. I think Horn's shot is what knocked off Seawrack's arm, she also being an extrusion of The Mother. (James do you have a different explanation for Seawrack's arm?). Seawrack's arm seems freshly lost but carefully dressed and healed. I think that occurred via The Mother when she dropped back into the sea. Despite the arm loss she can still swim pretty fast.

As for the purpose in sending Seawrack, I think it is similar to the sending of undines to Severian. There are powers in the sea who have a presentiment of Horn's future and want to be a part of it and/or influence on it.

There is such a personal, wistful note to the description of these characters. I think there is some reflection of the author's personal life which may not directly tie to the story.
