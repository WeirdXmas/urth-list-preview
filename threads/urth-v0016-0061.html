Inire R.I.P.?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-07-12 to 1998-07-12

1998urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

1998-07-12 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the phrase "great days of Father Inire" in Odilo III's speech may mean Odilo II's tenure as steward, not Inire's death. Builds a generational timeline of the three Odilos from their ages and service dates in the text, citing Odilo II's tenure running to 35 S.R.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Great days of Inire mark Odilo II's tenure; Odilo II served until 35 S.R

Add to the rumors of Inire's death mill:

The scene--survivors floating on the Flood waters, circa 50 S.R.

The speaker--Odilo (III), responding to Severian incognito, who asked if his father was steward in the same place before him.

The quote: "He [Odilo II] was indeed, sieur, and gave the most complete satisfaction all his life. =That was in the great days of Father Inire=, sieur, when, if I may say so, sieur, our Hypogeum Apotropaic was famous all across the Commonwealth" (V, ch. 44, italics mine).

The "great days"? Meaning either, "when Inire was still sprightly" or "when Inire was still alive"?

More dredging:

"The Cat" is told by Odilo (II) who heard it from Odilo (I), who was a contemporary of the chatelaine Sancha. Odilo (II), the one who met Severian as torturer wandering in the House Absolute, began serving in 16 P.S. (pre-Severian's Reign) and was still working 20 years later when he wrote "The Cat" in 5 S.R.

Odillo II is a plump man of fifty or so (I, ch. 19) in 1 P.S., which suggests that he was thirty-five when he succeeded his father as steward. (And recalls the long apprenticeship of Cyby.)

If son is like father, then Odillo III is fifty years old in 50 S.R. (and looks just like him), suggesting that he too began serving at the age of thirty-five, which would be 35 S.R. (and making him meet Severian for the first time in the fifteenth year of his service--just like dad did!)

BORN SERVICE
Odilo I 80 P.S. xx P.S. to 16 P.S.
Odilo II 50 P.S. 16 P.S. to 35 S.R.
Odilo III 1 P.S. 35 S.R. to 50 S.R.

So =maybe= Odilo II served until 35 S.R., and for one reason or the other, this period is known as "the great days of Father Inire."

=mantis=
