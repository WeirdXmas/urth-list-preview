Depression in Wolfe

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-11-17 to 2002-11-17

2002urthThe Book of the New SunAdam Stephanides

2002-11-17 Adam Stephanides #

Notes that the depression of Latro in Soldier of Arete resembles Severian's in The Urth of the New Sun, and adds the protagonist of There Are Doors as also depressed. Speculates that these books appearing within a few years might reflect something in Wolfe's own life, while admitting this is only conjecture.
supports · author-and-biography

I don't know if anyone has ever commented on it, but I for one had been very struck by the similarity between Latro's depression and Severian's in UotNS. You can add to the list THERE ARE DOORS, whose protagonist is IMO depressed, though not as severely, throughout the book. That these books all appeared in the space of a few years (apart from the end of EXODUS) led me to wonder if something was going on with Wolfe himself, but that was no more than a speculation.

--Adam
