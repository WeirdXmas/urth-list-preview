1999-02-20 Patrick O'Leary #

Newcomer Patri10629 introduces himself, says a teacher recommended the list for serious Wolfe criticism for his senior thesis on popular-genre exceptions, and complains that the posts are about Paul McCartney's pre-Wings group and Gilligan's Island. Mocks the list by quoting a verse he says is from The Yellow Rose of Texas, asking whether members know anything.
disputes

Hi, I'm new here.

My teacher told me this newsgroup is where to go to delve into serious literary criticism/commentary re: Gene Wolfe--a writer of some merit, I'm told, who transcends the typical SCI-FI. I've not read him yet but I hope to start. And I had hoped to garnish some insight for my senior thesis: "Exceptions To The Rules of Mediocrity in Popular Genres." But what do I find? Pompous summaries of Paul McCartney's old group before Wings. Theological Meditations on Gilligan Island. Heck, I can get that anywhere on the net--Why go here? And another thing:

" Because I could not stop for Death,
He kindly stopped for me;
The carriage held but just ourselves
And immortality.

That's the last verse to "The Yellow Rose of Texas" not a TV themesong. Don't you people know anything?

Newt
