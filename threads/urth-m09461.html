Large Print editions?

Urth list · 3 messages · 2009-05-02 to 2009-05-03

2009urthMatthew KeeleyAdam Thornton
The thread: Adam Thornton asked whether any Gene Wolfe books exist in large print, to give his visually impaired mother a Mother's Day gift. Matthew Keeley replied that a catalog search found no large print Wolfe editions and suggested an eReader instead. Keeley later proposed Library of Congress talking books, while doubting that computer-generated audio would render Wolfe's ornate vocabulary well. The thread ended there, with the practical question answered and no debate about the fiction itself.
  • Adam Thornton: wants a large print Wolfe edition as a gift for his mother
  • Matthew Keeley: no large print Wolfe exists; try an eReader or Library of Congress talking books, though synthesized audio may mangle the prose

2009-05-02 Adam Thornton #

Asks whether any of Gene Wolfe's books have been published in a large print edition, because the poster's mother has failing eyesight and he wants a gift for Mother's Day. I realize this is a somewhat bizarre request, but it's almost Mother's Day, and my mother's eyesight is not what it once was. Has any Wolfe been put out in a large print edition?

2009-05-03 Matthew Keeley replying to Adam Thornton #

Reports that no Wolfe books appear in large print editions, based on a search of Amazon's catalog through a large print search site. Responds to Adam Thornton's request for a large print Wolfe for his mother, suggesting that she might instead read e-book versions on an eReader.
answers

As far as I can tell, Wolfe hasn't appeared in large print editions. I used the Large Print Search on largeprintbooks.com, which searches Amazon for LP books, but apparently no LP Wolfe books are listed in Amazon's catalog. If your mother has a Kindle or other eReader, I believe that some Wolfe books are available in e-form.

-Matt
