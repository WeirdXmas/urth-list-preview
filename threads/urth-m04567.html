The thread: Fred Kiesche recommended Greg Bear's City at the End of Time to list readers, saying its Prolog resembles Wolfe's dense style though less dense, offering no argument beyond the comparison. brunians agreed Bear is a solid writer but not on Wolfe's or Crowley's level, citing Wolfe's own approval of Bear as the only support. Gwern Branwen set the stylistic comparison aside and playfully asked whether the spelling 'Prolog' was a nod to the programming language. The thread ended without any real debate over Wolfe's prose.
