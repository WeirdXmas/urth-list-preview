2005-12-07 Nicholas Gevers #

Announces news from the December Locus: Soldier of Sidon, the third-to-last Latro novel, has been acquired by Tor, and a new Wolfe novelette, Christmas Inn, will be issued as a chapbook by PS Publishing. Notes the poster works for PS Publishing and that the story is a fine one, with subscriber and purchase details given.
informs

A couple of Wolfe announcements, as in the December
LOCUS:

SOLDIER OF SIDON, the third and penultimate Latro
novel, has been acquired by David Hartwell at Tor.

A new novelette by Wolfe, "Christmas Inn" (10,000
words), is being issued as a chapbook by PS Publishing
in January. Subscribers to the PS fiction zine,
POSTSCRIPTS, will get one free copy each; a few copies
will be available for direct purchase. Details at the
website, www.pspublishing.co.uk.

I should acknowledge here that I work for PS
Publishing, so this can be seen as an ad; but
"Christmas Inn" is genuinely a fine story, and
POSTSCRIPTS regularly includes new Wolfe short stories
in the bargain...

--Nicholas Gevers.
