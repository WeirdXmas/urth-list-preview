1997-12-02 Tony Ellis #

Questions Mantis's proposal that the seven scenes of Severian's Book of the New Sun correspond to the days of the week and planets, asking why particular characters are assigned to particular days. Tony Ellis notes the pattern-finding risk, offers alternative schemes such as the seven deadly sins, and says Autarch as Moon would be a sensible reconsideration.

RE>Divine Week

Blimey, so it is! Even down to the sabre-tooth moggy. Most observant of you.

My problem with inventive extrapolations like this: if you look for patterns you will always find them.

Unless I'm missing something (it wouldn't be the first time), the associations you make between characters and days seem
pretty arbitrary. Why not assign the first scene to Nod, or Meschia, or Gabriel, who also appear in it? Why doesn't the Familiar get to be represented by a planet? Or the Contessa? Why is Thursday Jupiter? Why is Friday Jahi's day, and not another?

You say that Meschiane is Moon, making the first scene Monday, but you also say that "Valeria is Moon to Severian's New Sun". I think you have something with this latter point, I like the idea of Valerian's lunar reign being eclipsed by the New Sun, but in that case surely Autarch =3D Moon?

It seems to me that I could just as plausibly prove that the 7 scenes equate to the 7 Deadly Sins (Jahi =3D Lust, Autarch =3D Envy, Nod =3D Sloth etc), or the 7 Dwarfs (Meschiane =3D Bashful, Statue =3D Grumpy etc). Or maybe there are ten scenes and they equate to the Ten Commandments? (Jahi + Meschia =3D adultery, Meschia and Meschiane =3D honour thy father and mother, etc) <g>
