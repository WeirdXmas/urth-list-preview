Defending Wolfe

Urth list · 6 messages · 2008-08-07 to 2008-08-08

2008urthThe Book of the New SunFred KiescheLane HaygoodaaronBlake Hyde
The thread: Fred Kiesche invited list members to join a debate on an outside blog and SF Signal about whether Gene Wolfe is a bad writer, after a commenter there disparaged him. The rest of the thread drifted into mockery rather than engagement with Wolfe's books. aaron jabbed at Terry Goodkind and John Ringo readers, and Lane Haygood mocked Goodkind as a worse Ayn Rand while dismissing people who do not think and read. Nobody discussed the fiction itself, so the thread ended in impasse and banter.
  • Fred Kiesche: invites list members to defend Wolfe against an outside critic on SF Signal
  • aaron: serious readers keep Wolfe while Goodkind and Ringo suit popular tastes
  • Lane Haygood: Goodkind botches Rand's Objectivism and is worse than she is
  • Lane Haygood: some people do not like to think and read, and he will not judge their lifestyles

Themes: Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both, Books, libraries and writing
