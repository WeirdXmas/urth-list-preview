2005urthThe Book of the New SunJosh Young

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Memory and forgetting

2005-06-11 Josh Young #

Proposes that the clone #5 and his father may be bound simulators as well as clones, suggesting the father's experiments installed a bound personality that explains #5's increasing amnesia. Also asks whether the ghoul-bear in the Book of the New Sun resembles the alzabo, recalling the alzabo digging up and eating corpses.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Clone #5 and his father are bound simulators

In regards to the difference between the simulators, I sort of just chalked that conversation up to SF conventions; I pretty much knew the answer before Mr. Million provided it. However, dumb me, now that I think about it, nothing's ever that simple with Wolfe, is it? Interesting train of thought, there; could it be that #5 and father are bound simulators? In addition to clones? Cyborg clone/simulators? Maybe the expirements #5's father performed on him involved installing a bound personality, hence the periods of increasing amnesia?

Also, did anyone notice the similarity between the description of the ghoul-bear and the alzabo? I'm trying to remember if the alzabo had man-like eyes of beast-like eyes; my friend has my copy of the New Sun books. But I also seem to remember the alzabo digging up corpses and eating them.--
~Josh
