Nunnery!

Urth list · 1 messages · 1997-06-25 to 1997-06-25

1997urthMichael Andre-Driussi

1997-06-25 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Supports a reading of the word "nunnery" as slang for a brothel, citing an OED first citation from 1593 that uses it that way. Ties this to Hamlet's wiggy behaviour and to a pun on Nutria's naughty-nun witches, and welcomes newcomer Rory.
supports · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading
