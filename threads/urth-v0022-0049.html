1998-12-17 Kevin J. Maroney #

Points out that the fiction in Planet Engineering was fully reprinted in Endangered Species or Storeys from the Old Hotel, and notes that the Planet Engineering volume uniquely holds the essay on Books in The Book of the New Sun. Explains that Storeys was assembled as a companion to Endangered Species to gather Wolfe's own favourite uncollected pieces, adding that the collections otherwise overlap little.
informs · source-and-allusion

What was not clear from Mantis's list is that the fiction in _Plan[e]t Engineering_ was completely reprinted in either _Endangered Species_ or _Storeys from the Old Hotel_.

The Wolfe Compleatist will want to have _PE_ anyway. Besides the poems and the _Plant Engineering_ article on robots, _PE_ contains the only publication of the brilliant essay, "Books in _The Book of the New Sun_".

_The Wolfe Archipelago_'s introductory story, "The Death of the Island Doctor", was reprinted in _Storeys_.

Other than that, yes, there's not much overlap among the various collections. This is not completely accidental; _Storeys_ was assembled as a small-press companion piece to _Endangered Species_, to collect all of Wolfe's favorites of his own pieces which had not been collected elsewhere.

1998-12-17 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists overlapping story titles across Wolfe's collections (Castle of Days, Planet Engineering, Storeys from the Old Hotel, The Wolfe Archipelago, Endangered Species) and suggests the Urth-Man Extraordinary bibliography as a buying guide. Responds to a question about which stories are repeated versus uncollected, offering a practical shortcut for collectors.
answers

Rostrum,

CASTLE OF DAYS is an omnibus of BOOK OF DAYS (short stories) and CASTLE OF THE OTTER (all about TBOTNS) and a third section of scattered non-fiction, some previously unpublished.

=Overlaps=
"La Befana" in IDDAOSAOS, BOD, COD
"A Criminal Proceeding" in PE, SFTOH
"The Death of Doctor Island" in IDDAOSAOS, WA
"Death of the Island Doctor" in WA, SFTOH
"The Detective of Dreams" in PE, ES
"The Doctor of Death Island" in IDDAOSAOS, WA
"The HORARS of War" in PE, ES
"In Looking Glass Castle" in PE, SFTOH
"The Island of Doctor Death and Other Stories" in IDDAOSAOS, WA "The Marvelous Brass Chessplaying Automaton" in PE, SFTOH "The Rubber Bend" in PE, SFTOH
"When I was Ming the Merciless" PE, ES

(This from a list of 160+ fictions. Not counting poetry.)

BOD = GENE WOLFE'S BOOK OF DAYS
COD = CASTLE OF DAYS
ES = ENDANGERED SPECIES
IDDAOSAOS = ISLAND OF DOCTOR DEATH...
PE = PLAN[E]T ENGINEERING
SFTOH = STOREYS FROM THE OLD HOTEL
WA = THE WOLFE ARCHIPELAGO (collection of the =four= island stories)

Or was the question more about the Uncollected? That is, buying anthologies and magazines that each have one Wolfe story (so-far) uncollected in a book of Wolfe short stories?

The short answer is probably "Buy URTH-MAN EXTRAORDINARY for five bucks and use it to form your blueprint buying-plan."

=mantis=
