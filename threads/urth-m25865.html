Short Story 99: The Boy Who Hooked the Sun

Urth list · 1 messages · 2014-10-07 to 2014-10-07

2014urthRobert Pirkola

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2014-10-07 Robert Pirkola #

Asks whether a complete list exists of the brown book stories, after confirming that "The Boy Who Hooked the Sun" belongs to that group. Supports the point with Wolfe's own introduction to Starwater Strains, which calls the story a brown book piece like those in From the Cradle.
questions · source-and-allusion

On August 20, 2014, Charles Gillingham wrote: ?This
story, I always assumed, is part of the Brown Book from New Sun. I only bring this up, so future stories commented on will be identified as such.?
Your assumption appears to be correct as Wolfe confirmed as much in his "Introduction" to *Starwater Strains*: "'The Boy Who Hooked the Sun' was written for the late Jan O'Nale, who used it in a greeting card. It's a brown book story, like those in 'From the Cradle.'" Is there a list of all the brown book stories out there?
