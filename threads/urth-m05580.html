The thread: Russell Wodell proposed that Señor Guzman in The Fifth Head of Cerberus is a third iteration of Chris, driven to madness and possibly suicide by jealousy of his younger self, and asked whether Guzman's disappearance is a despairing escape into time, without offering textual evidence. Matthew Keeley extended the idea in Pirate Freedom, doubting Chris as wife-beater, suicide and bad priest, proposing a time paradox to explain Jaime's knowledge of the Castillo Blanco, and asking whether Chris's Angel of Death is real, future technology or hallucination, while admitting he lacked the book to check. Paul Witcover asked Wodell to expand and supply the reasoning and textual evidence behind the idea. The thread ends with the proposal unsupported and no resolution reached.
