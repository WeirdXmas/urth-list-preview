The danger of the inhumi

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-04-26 to 2001-04-26

2001whorlNicholas Gevers

Themes: War and soldiers

2001-04-26 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that the danger the inhumi pose to humans on Blue comes mainly from the chance that human aggressors will use inhumi as infiltrators and saboteurs. Grounds this in the analogy with Horn's use of such agents in the Han-Gaon war, as recalled from the Long Sun books.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Inhumi danger lies in use as saboteurs

I think it's reasonable to assume that much of the
danger the inhumi pose to the humans on Blue stems
from the possibility that human aggressors will employ
inhumi as infiltrators and saboteurs--much as Horn
does in the Han-Gaon war.

--Nick Gevers.
