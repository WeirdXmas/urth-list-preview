1999-10-07 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Reports that the third issue of the Shadow graphic novel exists, describing its cover of a green undine holding Severian in cloak and mask and its chapter range from "Lictor of Thrax" to "Destruction of the Altar." Responds to a question about whether there were two or three issues by offering physical evidence, and suggests the fact be added to the FAQ.
informs

vizcacha,

Three! Three! Issue number three, I have it in front of me right now, has big green undine holding buff beefcake Sev in cloak and mask on cover; contents covers chapters "Lictor of Thrax" through to "Destruction of the Altar."

1999-10-07 Michael Straight #

Asks how much Wolfe was involved in the graphic novel adaptation of The Book of the New Sun, suggesting that his choices about what to condense or omit would reveal something about the work. Responds to Michael Andre-Driussi's report of a third issue with cover and chapter contents, building on the question of whether the adaptation was two or three issues.
questions · author-and-biography

How much was Wolfe involved in this project? If he helped condense things, I'd think what he choose to keep and what he choose to leave out might be very enlightening.

-Rostrum
