Urth condemned, the Autarch's memories

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-07-14 to 1998-07-14

1998urthMark Andersen

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1998-07-14 Mark Andersen #

Proposes that Typhon's self-worship as a god caused Urth's condemnation, and asks whether the Long Sun and New Sun parallels between true and false gods are substantive. Responds to adam louis stephanides's quote on the Autarchy's origin, citing the dying autarchs' cry "Find somebody else" and suggesting they want new bodies to live in.
proposes · theology-and-religion · close-textual-reading · theories: Typhon's self-worship caused Urth's condemnation; Dead Autarchs' personalities seek new bodies to live in; Long Sun and New Sun share true-versus-false-god structure

1)

This statement really rang the alarm bells for me. I wonder now if Typhon's
hubris, setting himself and his family up as gods to be worshipped on the
Whorl, was the abomination that led to the condemnation of Urth? Were there other Whorls besides the one we read of in the Long Sun (almost certainly, I would guess)? Is this a substantive link or a coincidental link
between the Long Sun's story of true vs. false gods and the New Sun's story of true vs. false redemption?

My impression has always been that this is because they wish to live on. Thus
the personalities of the dead Autarchs insist :"Find another" (body for us to
live in)!

cheers,
Buteo
