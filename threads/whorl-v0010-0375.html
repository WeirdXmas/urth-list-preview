2000-06-22 Adam Stephanides #

Asks whether any of Horn's dreams in On Blue's Waters have proved prophetic, noting that the dreams seem to have no obvious relevance. Mentions one dream in the pit that may show Silk resurrecting Horn, and admits a single reading may be mistaken.
questions · close-textual-reading

Have any of Horn's dreams in OBW turned out to be prophetic yet? I remember thinking that Horn spent rather a lot of time telling us his dreams, and none of them had any obvious relevance (except for the one in the pit which may have been Silk resurrecting him). But I only read the book once, so I could be wrong.

--Adam

Replies that Horn's dreams in On Blue's Waters have not yet proved prophetic, but argues that many foretold events in the Long Sun books occur only in later volumes. Notes that the prophecies are highly metaphorical and deliberate, so they may only become clear on a second reading.
