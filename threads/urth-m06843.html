2008-11-20 Son of Witz replying to Son of Witz #

Argues that the puzzles and plot curiosities in Wolfe's work are only worth pursuing if they illuminate something important, and that calculating the date of creation is ridiculous and not meant literally. Responds to Mo Holkar's quoted point that Wolfe's plot puzzles, such as Dorcas's identity, get readers drawn step by step into deeper readings.
