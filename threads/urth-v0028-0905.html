Bill Clinton and "Alien Stones"

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-04-16 to 2000-04-16

2000urthMichael Straight

Themes: Humour in Wolfe, Death and resurrection

2000-04-16 Michael Straight #

Argues that a Clinton-era joke about the word "is" helped the poster notice the scripture quotation about husbands and wives in the resurrection at the end of the story "Alien Stones." Explains that the laugh at the political line made the poster reread the closing passage and see its point, which the poster had initially missed.
informs · close-textual-reading

If it weren't for Bill Clinton, I wouldn't have laughed at the line, "What is meant by 'is'?"

And if I hadn't laughed at that line, I would have, as I did the first time I read "Alien Stones," missed the point of the scripture quotation at the end of the story concerning husbands and wives in the resurrection.

One good thing to come from that mess.

-Rostrum
