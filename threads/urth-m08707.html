2009-03-01 Jerry Friedman #

Proposes that the name Hari Mau in the Short Sun books derives from harimau, the Malay word for tiger, and links it to the animal-based male names in Viron and Borges's interest in tigers. Offers a linguistic source and a link to a Malay-language document as evidence, with no reply to any earlier poster.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Hari Mau derives from Malay harimau meaning tiger

Hi, all!

I'm rejoining the list briefly to point out that the name Hari Mau in the Short Sun books may come from "harimau", the Malay word for "tiger". See

http://eyesontheforest.or.id/index.php?option=com_docman&task=doc_download&gid=5

or <http://tinyurl.com/aksr58>.

Maybe interesting that it's an animal name, like male names in Viron, and that the tiger was so important to Borges.

Jerry Friedman
