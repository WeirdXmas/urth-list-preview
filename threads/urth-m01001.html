2005-01-17 Jim Raylor #

Asks whether a large monumental library exists in Geneva, reasoning that if Cave Canem sits at the site of the Saltimbanque theater, a library should lie due west of it. Grounds the search in map positions and proposed matches, including a Geneva Free Library in New York, the white fountain, an outsize chessboard, and the School of Engineers.
questions · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Cave Canem sits at the Saltimbanque theater site

Has anyone been to Switzerland recently? If Cave Canem
can be assumed to be
located at the site of the Saltimbanque theater in
Geneva, then a large
historic library might be expected due west of it. Can
anyone identify a
large monumental library there?
