Themes: Names and their meanings

2003-07-09 Alice Turner #

Corrects the ship name Samru and asks who the original Samru was, the woman Shah Jehan memorialized with the Taj Mahal. Cites Gustav Davidson's A Dictionary of Angels to describe Tzadkiel as archangel of justice, guardian of the East Wind and of Jupiter, and suggests Wolfe drew on it.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Wolfe drew Tzadkiel from Davidson's Dictionary of Angels

Whoops, I did get the ship Samru confused with the starship. Does anyone remember who the original Samru was?--the lovely lady who inspired Shah Jehan to build the Taj Mahal in her memory. (And I have seen the Taj by moonlight....)

As for Tzadkiel (which can be spelled a number of different ways), s/he is not confined to being an angel of biography. According to Gustav Davidson's A Dictionary of Angels, which has essentially been the standard since publication in 1967, and virtually certainly informed Wolfe and almost as certainly Robertson Davies, s/he is the angel of justice, and 4th of the 10 archangels of the Briatic world, as well as a guardian of the East Wind, and of the planet Jupiter.

-alga
