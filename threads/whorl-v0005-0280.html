1997-05-15 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Jokes that the list's game of asking questions is like a wish to a tricky genie, so a literal question would get a useless answer and waste the wish. Also reports that the Whorl and Urth archives are missing because his website was deleted from the mainframe, and says he hopes to revive it within a week.
chats

Um, does it have to be in the form of a question?

I feel like I'm dealing with a tricky gen-ie, and if I say "Can you give me a brief biography of Maytera Mint" the
response will be "Yes. Next question." And I will have wasted my wish.

By the way, you may have noticed that the Whorl and Urth archives are missing. That's because my web site has gone to mainframe -- or, I should say, has been deleted from
mainframe. Luckily, I have shards of the site hidden away here and there; I hope to revive it within a week or so.

ranjit
