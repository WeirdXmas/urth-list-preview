2000-04-10 Ouroboros The Worm #

Supports JJordan's reading that the brothel in The Fifth Head of Cerberus counterfeits the Church and the clone line figures as Antichrist, adding parallels to Revelation chapters 13 and 17. Cites the multi-headed beast, the bottomless pit and the wounded head healed in Revelation 13:3, and proposes that this beast is the mechanical grandfather of the clones.
supports · allegory-and-typology · source-and-allusion · theories: Brothel counterfeits the Church as Great Harlot; Clone line is the Antichrist; Revelation beast is grandfather of the clones

"The brothel is a counterfeit of the Church, the Great Harlot of the Book of Revelation. That's pretty clear in 5HC, where the address is 666 Street of the Counterfeit (Humbug). The line of clones is pretty much the Antichrist..."

Revelations 13:3 "And I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast."

2000-04-11 Damien Broderick #

Corrects the thread's title by noting that the Biblical book is singular, Revelation, not Revelations, and that the plural is a widespread error. Mentions the poster's own co-written novel THE BOOK OF REVELATION as the reason he cares about the correct form, offering no evidence from Wolfe's text.
informs · source-and-allusion

That would be Revelation, singular. Funny how widespread that solecism is.

(I have an interest in knowing this, having recently published a novel in Australia co-written with Rory Barnes, titled THE BOOK OF REVELATION. Steal from the experts, I say.)

Damien
