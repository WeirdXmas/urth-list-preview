Another hint that Horn died in the pit

Urth list · 1 messages · 2011-02-11 to 2011-02-11

2011urthThe Book of the Short SunJames Wynn

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Death and resurrection

2011-02-11 James Wynn #

Argues that Horn may have died in the pit, offering a passage from On Blue's Waters, Chapter 10, in which Krait asks Horn whether he saw a corpse and says being dead makes a difference. Grounds the claim in the exchange itself, where the narrator says he saw the body and the question of death is raised directly.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Horn died in the pit

Krait nodded. "You said you thought he was an old man, and you were angry because he had tricked you. You told me some trooper shot and killed him."
I nodded.
"Did you see his corpse?"
"Yes." Something of the revulsion I had felt must have shown on my face. "What difference does that make?"
"Being dead makes a great deal of difference to some of us. " ~ OBW, Chapter 10, Seawrack's Ring
