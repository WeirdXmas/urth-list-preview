Teleporting inhumi

Whorl list · 7 messages · 2001-04-20 to 2001-04-20

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunKevin J. MaroneyDan'l Danehy-OakesNicholas GeversJames Jordan
The thread: Readers debated whether the inhumi in The Book of the Long Sun teleport between the Green and Blue worlds or possess a passive astral travel capacity. Nicholas Gevers argued for passive astral travel activated by a Neighbour's presence, saying teleportation would leave Blue far more infested and adds nothing to explaining the Secret as a weapon. James Jordan defended a related hypothesis and suggested a ring rather than Neighborliness might enable the travel, citing magic rings in traditional literature. Kevin J. Maroney asked whether Horn's sons ever initiate astral travel on their own and argued the Narrator's ability follows from how deeply the Neighbors reshaped his life. The thread opened with a joke from Dan'l Danehy-Oakes about the list's purpose and ended without resolution.
  • Nicholas Gevers: inhumi have passive astral travel activated by a Neighbour's presence, not teleportation
  • James Jordan: a ring, not Neighborliness, may enable the astral travel; cites magic rings in literature
  • Kevin J. Maroney: the Narrator's ability comes from Neighbors reshaping his life; asks if Horn's sons travel on their own
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: jokes about the inhumi image and reminds readers to stay on Long Sun topics

Themes: Beasts and the animal in us, Robots, androids and artificial life, The dying sun and the cosmos

2001-04-20 James Jordan #

Defends James Jordan's hypothesis that inhumi have passive astral travel activated by a Neighbour's presence, and proposes that a ring rather than Neighborliness may enable it. Responds to Patera Nutria's teleportation objection and to N. Gevers's thesis that Narrator alone travels astrally, asking whether Horn's ring explains the travel and citing magic rings in traditional literature.
proposes · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Inhumi have passive astral travel activated by Neighbour presence; Inhumi plus ring enables astral travel; Narrator alone astral travels due to Neighborliness

N. Gevers objected to my mooted tweak of his proposal:

^^^^ ^^^^ ^^^^^^

Nothing humble about you, Nick! ;-)

Yeah, I don't suppose my thought adds to the Secret, but I'm not sure (yet) that I'm wrong. If inhumi can only teleport (on my supposition) in the presence of others who've been to Blue and returned, perhaps not that many would have made the trip. And after all, there are lots of humans on Green.
I agree with the other objection that there does not seem to be any indication in the text for my hypothesis. Problem is, since flying through space is out of the question, where is there any indication of anything else at all?
But back to your thesis. You're saying only Narrator can do astral travel, not just any human, because of his Neighborliness. Well, is it the ring? I'm not going to search 1000+ pages to find the allusions to the ring, but as I recall Horn left the first ring on Green with his corpse. But then he gets another. Is it the ring? And if so, maybe it's not inhumi + Neighbor that enables the travel, but inhumi + ring.
And how about magic rings in traditional literature? I like your thesis, since it integrates the astral travel business with the nature of the inhumi.

Nutria

2001-04-20 Nicholas Gevers #

Argues that the inhumi do not teleport between Green and Blue but instead have a passive astral travel capacity that activates only when a biological or honorary Neighbour is present. Defends this against a teleportation view by noting that teleportation would leave Blue far more infested and adds nothing to explaining the Secret as a weapon.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Inhumi have passive astral travel activated by Neighbours; Inhumi do not teleport between Green and Blue

Patera Nutria: no, I don't think the inhumi are
teleporting from Green to Blue and then back again. If
that were the case, Blue would be infested with them
to a far greater degree than it is. I stick by my
hypothesis, which is simply that the inhumi have been
given a passive astral travel capacity, activatable
only if a biological or honorary Neighbour is also
present. This makes perfect sense textually; and
teleportation adds nothing to my explanation of the
Secret, the first explanation which is both fully
accountable to the text and a sensible adumbration of
the Secret as an offensive weapon.

--Nick Gevers.
