1999-02-05 Michael Straight #

Agrees with Peter Stephenson that the inn scene in The Book of the New Sun echoes Moby Dick, specifically the bed-sharing with a strange companion, while admitting the poster had forgotten the parallel. Responds to Stephenson's quoted plot outline of the two books' overnight-lodging sequences, adding a brief personal recollection and no further argument.
supports · source-and-allusion · theories: Inn scene echoes Moby Dick bed-sharing

Now that you mention it, this scene did remind me when I read it of Moby Dick, at least as far as sharing a bed with a weirdo, but I'd forgotten about it. I didn't notice all the other parallels.

In other words, "me too."

-Rostrum
