Proposes a comparison between Dickens's Scrooge and the cold house ghost in Wolfe's fiction, noting Scrooge's chill and the Ghost of Christmas Past showing his younger self reading the Arabian Nights. Grounds this in the poster's fresh reading of A Christmas Carol from Project Gutenberg, and the Arabian Nights detail is offered as the key link to the Wolfe story.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Scrooge's coldness parallels the cold house ghost

Okay, I found "A Christmas Carol" on Project Guttenberg.

Getting spooked as I read it . . . the cold chill of Scrooge recalls the cold house ghost . . . the Ghost of Christmas Past shows Scrooge his younger self, reading -- O, I can scarcely write it -- reading THE ARABIAN NIGHTS on one lonely Christmas . . .
