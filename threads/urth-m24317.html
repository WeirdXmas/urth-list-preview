2013-12-19 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to Craig Brewer #

Recommends Connie Willis's collection "Miracle and Other Stories" as a source of science fiction and fantasy Christmas stories for someone who asked for suggestions. Answers Craig Brewer's request for non-Wolfe Christmas sf/f by pointing to a specific Willis collection and saying it suits readers who like her work.
answers

Connie Willis has a collection of sf/f Christmas stories called "Miracle and other stories." If you like Willis's stuff (I do), you'll probably like it.
