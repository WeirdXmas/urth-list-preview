Themes: Deep time and ruins

2002-07-17 Matthew Malthouse #

Argues that the text requires geological ages to have passed since Urth's first civilisations, rejecting a short timescale. Supports David A Baird's objection to the 97 kyear figure, citing the mines of Saltus and Severian's walk down the cliff to Casdoe's house as evidence.
supports · chronology-and-plot-logic · close-textual-reading · theories: Urth's first civilisations lie geological ages in the past

Yay! Exactly!

The mines of Saltus and Severians walk down the cliff to Casdoe's house.

The evidence of the text does not appear to admit to any explanation except that geological ages have passed since there were first civilisations of Urth.
