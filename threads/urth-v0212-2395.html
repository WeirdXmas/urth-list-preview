Two FLF details

2002-12-11 Jerry Friedman #

Points out that Linda Loring is a Chandler character, Marlowe's love interest, absent from the Mitchum films, and that the WROM station in Free Live Free's Unnamed City matches a Rome, Georgia station. Notes IMDB searches only real people, then asks whether the Rome name points to a more important Rome for Wolfe.
informs · source-and-allusion

I carelessly deleted a post that mentioned Linda Loring. She's a character in at least two of Raymond Chandler's novels--Philip Marlowe is in love with her. Robert Mitchum played Marlowe in _The Big Sleep_ and _Farewell, My Lovely_, but Linda Loring isn't in those movies.

The IMDB appears to search only for real people, not characters.

Also, Unnamed City has a TV station called WROM (in Stubbs' questioning of Mrs. Baker). The only TV station by that name that I could find is or more likely was in Rome, Georgia--which can't be the setting of FLF, at least in our universe. Maybe a reference to a Rome that's more important to Wolfe?
