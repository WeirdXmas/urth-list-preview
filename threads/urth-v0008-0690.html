Explication of a few small points

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-03-30 to 1998-03-30

1998urthDave Lebling

1998-03-30 Dave Lebling #

Explains several small points: that the teratoid sign in The Book of the New Sun is a radiation warning symbol, that Aunt Bella's vision shows a downtown street at night in our own time, and that the thousand carriage lamps are car headlights. Also asks whether Wolfe said no one was murdered by Alden Weer in Peace, joking that it may be an evasion.
informs · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Teratoid sign is a radiation warning symbol; Aunt Bella's vision is a modern downtown street; Thousand carriage lamps are automobile headlamps

A couple of quick notes:

mantis,

The "teratoid sign" in TBOTNS is our radiation warning symbol.

Damien Broderick,

Aunt Bella's vision is of a downdown street at night in our own time. The "thousand carriage lamps" are of course auto headlamps.

This is an excellent essay, thought-provoking and complete. Didn't someone report (somewhere in the archives) that Wolfe has said that no one was murdered by Alden Weer in _Peace_? Of course, this could be a Clintonian evasion. (Weer didn't murder anyone, but through negligence, or misadventure, or...)

--viz
