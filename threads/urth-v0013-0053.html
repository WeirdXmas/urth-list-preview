Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-06-14 Alice Turner #

Clarifies that she accepts V.R.T.'s half-abo state and his absorption or takeover of John V. Marsch, and believes the woman in the next cell is likely his mother. Responds to mantis by saying her objection targets only the Aunt Jeanine theory, not the V.R.T. half-abo idea.
disputes · puzzle-solving · theories: V.R.T. is a half-abo who absorbed or took over John V. Marsch; The woman in the next cell is V.R.T.'s mother; Aunt Jeanine theory is preposterous

mantis,

I have not questioned V.R.T.'s half-abo state. Not only do I accept the fact that John V. Marsch has either absorbed in some way or been joined or taken over by V.R. T., I also believe the woman in the next cell to--very likely--be his mother. It's the preposterous Aunt Jeanine theory that I went after.

-alga-
