Short Story 51: How I Lost the Second World War ...

Answers why the exposition in "How I Lost the Second World War" is set in Bath: London is too obvious, and Bath is a historic town with a tradition of leisure and tourism. Cites Wolfe's statement in Castle of Days, from a letter to Nancy Kress, that his mother's family came from Bath and that he had always wanted to see it.
answers · source-and-allusion · author-and-biography · theories: Bath chosen because Wolfe's mother's family was from there

From: Mo Holkar

Wolfe says in Castle of Days that his mother's family was from there, and that he's always wanted to see the place. (From a Letter to Nancy Kress)

Also, once you've dismissed London for being too obvious, it's a very good choice for an exposition. It's an historic town, rather than an antiquated one, in the heart of England, with a longstanding tradition of high culture, leisure and tourism.
