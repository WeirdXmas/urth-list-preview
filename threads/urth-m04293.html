2007-11-17 Cliff Judge #

Asks the list what the quotation placed before the title page of Pirate Freedom is called, since it is neither a preface nor a foreword. Notes Wolfe has used a favorite quote at the start of his books and wonders whether the term is epigram or something more specific.
questions

With Pirate Freedom, Wolfe has again placed a favorite quote of his right at the beginning. After the acknowledgements, before the page with the title on it.

Can anybody tell me what this component of a book is called? Its not a preface, its not a forward...is it an epigram? It's "a quote at the beginning of a book..." and I know there is a more specific definition.

Agrees that the word sought is "epigraph" and notes that they first mixed it up with "epigram" before looking it up. Calls the epigraph in Pirate Freedom very cute, a light aside about the book's front matter rather than an analysis of its content.
