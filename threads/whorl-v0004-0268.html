Oosik and Mattak

1997whorlThe Book of the Short SunAlice Turner

1997-03-21 Alice Turner #

Thanks a previous poster for solving the Oosik and Mattak puzzle, asks whether anyone can identify the Triv language, and argues Mamelta need not be a plant name. Proposes that Mamelta is not a goddess name, rejecting Indian Mamata and Mesopotamian Mamitu as sources, and calls for further searching.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Mamelta is not derived from Mamata or Mamitu

Kudos to Wesley Parsons! Oosik and Mattak were real head-scratchers, and a wonderful find. Now if someone could just pin down the Triv language so we could start in on them. No Arab or Turkik scholars here?

Oh, and I agree that Mamelta needn't be a plant name (pace Olive, which exists as a name now in a less rigid naming system), though it would be neat (literally) if it was.. Mamata was an Indian demi-goddess demi-raped by her brother-in-law (she was already pregnant by her husband and the fetus prevented the rape). Mamitu was an evil Mesopotamian goddess. But I don't buy either of those--and it shouldn't be a goddess name anyway. We'll have to keep looking.

-alga-
