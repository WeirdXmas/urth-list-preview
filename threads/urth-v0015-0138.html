1998-07-06 Michael Straight #

Asks why Ushas needs the White Fountain when her sun seems healthy, and how bringing it from Urth to her universe helps either Urth or Ushas. Says the poster has been confused about the black hole and white fountain discussion and wants the mechanism explained again.
questions · close-textual-reading

Ok, all this discussion of the black hole/white fountain has made me realize that, although I thought I understood, I still don't get it.

Urth's sun is dying for some reason. Severian goes to get a White Fountain, but brings it back to a different universe, to Ushas, whose sun is, as far as I know, doing just fine. How does this action help either Urth or Ushas?

I imagine I'm missing something obvious that re-reading Urth or the archives might answer, but would someone mind explaining it one more time?

-Rostrum
