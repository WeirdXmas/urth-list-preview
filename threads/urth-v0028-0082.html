1999-08-29 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Reports that Gene Wolfe confirmed in writing that the sold but unpublished story "Talk of Mandrakes" will not appear in the forthcoming collection Strange Travelers. Says he asked Wolfe directly, so the poster concludes that no sold-but-unpublished stories will be included in the collection.
informs

. . . so I finally axed him, point blank--"Is the mysterious sold-but-never-published story `Talk of Mandrakes' going to be in STRANGE TRAVELERS, the new collection coming up?" (Or something like that.)

And he wrote back, nope, it won't. (Or words to that effect.)

So scratch that title off your wish list--in fact, there will be no stories that have been sold but not-yet-published.

=mantis=
