Best order to read Sun books?

2004-09-29 Stone Ox replying to Chris #

Recommends reading the Long Sun books first for readers who struggle with The Book of the New Sun, arguing the two series cross-reference little so order barely matters. Advises saving the Short Sun for last because it is harder and refers back to both earlier series.
proposes

I know people who've found it very difficult to get into the New Sun books but really enjoyed the Long Sun books, and I think there's little enough cross-reference between these two series that the reading order doesn't matter.
So if you have difficulty with the New Sun, try starting with the Long Sun. Also, I think it's best to save the Short Sun for last, as it's both the most difficult in certain ways, and it references both the New and Long Suns.

2004-09-29 Chris P #

Argues that the New Sun books should be read before the Long Sun and Short Sun books, though the order matters little. Notes that the chronology of the New Sun series is hard to place and that its connections to the later books are not especially striking on a first reading.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic

For various reasons it's hard to place the New Sun series chronologically. But in any event I think it still works slightly better to read the New Sun books first.

Not that it makes a huge amount of difference. The connections between the New Sun books and the Long/Short Sun books are not especially impressive on first reading. And, of course, the second time through the order won't matter.
