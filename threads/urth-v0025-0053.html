The Roy Trenchard argument again

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1999-04-12 Tony Ellis #

Disputes Robert Borski's claim that Roy Trenchard in The Fifth Head of Cerberus is an aboriginal of the marshmen, arguing that many textual signs point to Trenchard's humanity. Responds to Mitchell A. Bailey's quotation of Borski's essay "Roy Trenchard, Homeboy" and refers back to the poster's own earlier objections in the 1998 archive.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Roy Trenchard is an aboriginal of the marshmen

At the risk of annoying two people at the same time with my obtuseness, Robert Borski has -not- made a solid case that Roy Trenchard is also an abo! There are a dozen different fingerposts pointing to Trenchard's humanity, and simply re-writing Fifth Head to one's own satisfaction is no way of getting rid of any of them. Check out my objections to Robert's arguments in my posts of the 4th and 5th of June 1998, archive vol. 13.
