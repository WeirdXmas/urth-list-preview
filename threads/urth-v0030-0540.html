2001-07-04 Alice Turner #

Chats about a long-ago plan to cast Sting as Steerpike in a film of Peake's books and praises John Sessions as the Doctor in the current adaptation. Replies to an earlier post's remarks on the film's faithful visuals, adding the poster's favourite performances by Sessions and the actor playing Gertrude.
chats

Sting 20 years ago might have been quite good in a lizardy way. The Doctor is John Sessions who has had a rich career in British comedy. He was really splendid, my other favorite together with Gertrude.

-alga
