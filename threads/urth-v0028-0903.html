2000-04-16 Richard Horton #

Argues that the Book of Revelation's author, John of Patmos, is not the apostle John, disputing the attribution of the text to St. John. Responds to an earlier post by someone who listed the book's alternative titles, adding a scholarly caveat about the authorship claim.
disputes · source-and-allusion

Attributing it to "St. John" is controversial as well, as contemporary scholarship seems to agree that John of Patmos, the author of Revelation, is not the apostle John.

2000-04-17 Michael Straight #

Disputes Richard Horton's claim that contemporary scholarship agrees John of Patmos is not the apostle John, arguing the authorship debates rest on too little evidence. Draws an analogy: the same stylistic, vocabulary and theological arguments could be used to claim the Gene Wolfe who wrote the New Sun books is not the author of the Long Sun books.
disputes · author-and-biography

I don't think "contemporary scholarship" is that monolithic. A lot of those authorship debates seem to me to be based on too little evidence. Some of the arguments I've seen for that position could be used to prove that the Gene Wolfe who wrote the New Sun books is not the same person who wrote the Long Sun books (significant stylistic differences, a large chunk of vocabulary unique to one series or the other, different tehological ideas and concerns, etc.)

-Rostrum
