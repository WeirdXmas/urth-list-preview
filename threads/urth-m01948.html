The Old Autarch and Paeon

Urth list · 1 messages · 2006-08-29 to 2006-08-29

2006urthThe Book of the New SunDr. C. Radish Mosswalls

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2006-08-29 Dr. C. Radish Mosswalls #

Proposes that the Autarch's unchanging, androgynous profile on the coinage fits the idea that the Autarch is a whole group of different people sharing one body. Responds to Dan's puzzle that the androgynous counterfeit chrisos from Vodalus may show the Old Autarch, since Severian needs Thecla's memories to recognize the Autarch when he meets him.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: The Autarch is a group of different people in one body; The androgynous coin profile is the Old Autarch

Dan said, *"In case anyone needs another puzzle to speculate about, it has long
annoyed me that Severian describes the Autarch on the counterfeit chrisos from Vodalus as androgynous, which I have (since second reading) always assumed to mean that it's the Old Autarch (the only one since Ymar to take the test in Yesod), yet Severian needs Thecla's memories to recognize the guy as the Autarch when he actually meets him."
*
The general impression I get is that the profile of the Autarch on the coinage never changes. And to me it makes sense for an unchanging, androgynous image since the Autarch isn't just one guy, but a whole group of different people in one body. Just an idea.

-Jessie
