Neighbors in the Whorl

Urth list · 1 messages · 2011-02-09 to 2011-02-09

2011urthLee Berman

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Ghosts, dreams and visions

2011-02-09 Lee Berman #

Argues that the Neighbors on the Whorl are already embedded in the world through possession, not arriving from outer space, and that their symbiosis with Inhumi and humans has existed for a long time. Responds to Andrew Mason and James Wynn by pointing to possession in the Short Sun books, a ring or jewel of the Neighbors, and recurring water and feeding-on-death imagery in The Book of the New Sun.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Neighbors are already present on the Whorl, not invaders from the void; Neighbors possess humans through a ring or jewel

Yes. I think this is getting closer to the heart of the matter. I would ask Andrew about which group, on the Whorl, do we know is able to do "possession", putting their spirits into human bodies. Also, there seems to be some possession going on in the Short Sun stories. It is facilitated by a ring/jewel of the Neighbors. For me the pieces of the puzzle fit together nicely.

I think James also has a sense that Inhumi and Neighbor intrusions into the Whorl were not a product of an outer space invasion through the void. The Whorl likely came with all the pieces we find out about already in place.

I don't think the ecological symbiotic fit of Neighbors, Inhumi and humans that we see on Blue/Green evolved over the course of a few decades. It has been going on for a long, long time.

(this may be what Marc is getting at with his sense of flora and fauna. In BotNS there is a pelagic/blue water theme, a green theme and feeding off the death of humanity theme.)
