Argues that the buyer's stated reason for wanting the house is a lame MacGuffin, and suspects the Roberts family is part-fay without knowing it. Answers SpecBear's question by identifying Morgan's sister as a half-sister through Ygraine, drawing on Arthurian parallels with Morgawse and Mordred, and proposes that the novel is built around a spiral-labyrinth hinge.

think of it? Another day, another reading, and I'm in
