2006urthThe Book of the New SunScott K

2006-09-23 Scott K #

Reports that the poster owns the first three issues of a comic adaptation of Wolfe's New Sun story, judging it a good attempt at a nearly unadaptable work. Mentions the artwork page showing the finding of Triskele and names Ultan's library and the giant undine as favorite sequences.
informs

I have the first three issues of that, and they are a very good attempt at adaptation, for one of those stories that would be so haard to adapt truly as to be almost impossible (like they used to say about a LOTR movie!), but I think it was adapted with Mr. Wolfe's help, or at least approval. Only the covers have that flashy style that looks more Conan. Unfortunately the publisher went out of business before the series could get any farther. I was able to get a colored page of original artwork from one of them from the artist a year or so ago. It details the finding of Triskele. My favorite sequences from the comic was Ultan's library and the giant undine.
-Scott K.
