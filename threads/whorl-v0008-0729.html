Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-08-05 mary whalen #

Explains the name 'Teasel' by identifying the marten, a weasel-like arboreal animal of the genus Martes, and suggests a link to the weasel-Teasel rhyme. Offers the animal's description as the basis for the name's meaning, and the post is a short reply in a thread on animal names.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Teasel's name derives from the marten and weasel

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Marten. Any species of genus Martes. Larger than weasels, somewhat arboreal, long tail, soft fur, light below and grey above. Father of Teasel. Weasel rhymes with Teasel.
