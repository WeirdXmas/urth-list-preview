Supports Matthew Weber's view that the word 'rood' distances the Book of the New Sun's Christian imagery, and argues the setting is a distant past rather than a far future. Points to Wolfe's interview remarks, his archaic vocabulary, archaic saint names, and the word Urth as evidence pushing toward a past universe.
