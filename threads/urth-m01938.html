2006-08-28 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues that Autarchs of the Commonwealth probably do not live much longer than ordinary First World citizens, because Typhon and Baldanders are condemned for artificially extending their lives and the Autarchy was founded in reaction to Typhon. Grounds the claim thematically and cites the shorter lifespans of the cacogens Severian travels with as evidence that they would not be a source of life-extension technology.
proposes · myth-and-structure · close-textual-reading · theories: Autarchs do not live much longer than First World citizens; Autarchy founded in reaction to Typhon; Cacogens are not a source of life-extension technology

The question was asked, how we could tell how long an Autarch might live with alien medicine et al.

The short answer is, we can't...

...but...

...I think that even the highest classes in the Commonwealth don't live much longer than a modern-day First World citizen.

My argument is primarily thematic -- the great sin of both Typhon and Baldanders is artificially extending their lives (by, admittedly, evil and forbidden[?] methods, but if other methods were available ... well, you know). It seems to me that the Autarchs, whose position was created in reaction to Typhon, and whose purpose is to prepare for the coming of the New Sun, would not be likely to commit that particular sin.

One bit of supporting textual evidence ... we know that the cacogens Severian hangs with have _shorter_ lives than humans. Therefore I suggest that they would not be a likely source of life extension technology for humans.

2006-08-29 b sharp replying to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues by analogy that extending life is the gravest sin, comparing it to eating from the Tree of Life and so rejecting God's gift of mortality. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's claim that Typhon and Baldanders sin by artificially prolonging their lives, and adds a side note agreeing that Paeon is an aquastor to Malrubius.
supports · theology-and-religion · allegory-and-typology · theories: Extending life is the greatest sin

And the only sin greater than the Original Sin of eating fruit from the tree of Knowledge (of Good and Evil) would be to eat fruit from the Tree of Life thus throwing away God's gift to us of mortality?

-bsharp

p.s. Me three on Paeon as aquastor to Malrubius
