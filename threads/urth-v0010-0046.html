Typhon, exultants, Miles, and RAW

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-01 to 1998-05-01

1998urthThe Book of the New SunJ. Schultz

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-05-01 J. Schultz #

Clarifies a position that Typhon, leader of the exultants, is a provincial general from a severed galactic-empire world who returned to Urth, explaining the exultants' strange paleness, height and ruthlessness. Also argues Jonas, Hethor and KLS are all lost sailors, and proposes that Severian's claw revived a dead soldier, opening a door for Jonas to return, answering Chris Culver and Mantis.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Typhon is a provincial general from a severed empire world; Miles is a dead soldier revived by the claw, Jonas returning

Just a few clarifications on my position which I wasn't clear on,

Thank you all for responding to my post,
Chris Culver,
I did not mean to say that Typhon and the exultants left Urth. = However, I believe there is a mention that the exultants are invaders = from "another shore" and I think it has been stated that Typhon may have = been their leader. When I say that Typhon returned to Urth, I mean more = in the sense that you or I may return to our "homeland." That is to say = that Typhon may come from a planet that was a part of the old galactic = empire of Urth. In the fall of the galactic empire many areas would = have been cut off from other areas of the galaxy. These areas would = develop their own cultures and genetic traits over centuries. (maybe = hundreds of centuries) I see Typhon as a sort of provincial general who = desires to rebuild the empire to its glory days and decides to set up = the throne on the "most ancient of planets" Urth. In the end it is only = my theory to try to explain why Typhon and the exultants are said to = come from another planet, be so similar to 'stock' humans, and yet be so = very different (as in: snow white complextions, extreme height, = ruthlessness). =20

As to the nature of Jonas, Hethor, and KLS. I agree whole heartedly = that Jonas, Hethor, and KLS are all the same type of sailors lost in the = sea of time, trapped in a future they didn't expect. At least Jonas = didn't come back to Earth to find it overrun by talking apes! : ) = "Get your filthy hands off me, you damn animals!" (or something like = that)

Mantis:
I've read the archives and once again you've blown me away with your = knowledge of Urth factoids. I'm intruiged. Where are the mentions of = Venus and Mars terraforming and their respective names found in the = text? And where is the mention of the Xanthic continent with = posthistorical starfarers? I'd like to reread those sections.

As for the Miles/Jonas enigma, .... I'm stumped. When I first read it, = I always thought it was vague. Then in TUotNS, Gunny (I believe her = name is) uses the same kind of Jonas-speak analogies and Sev. questions = her on it. She says its just the way sailors talk. From this I = gathered that maybe Miles was never really Jonas and maybe just a sailor = as well. HOWEVER, I recently reread the section and its really fishy. = Severian thinks he sees Jonas walking next to him when he and Miles are = walking together. When Sev. asks Miles his real name, Miles responds = that he doesn't know in a very Jonas sort of way.

Theory: Whoever the soldier was, he is long dead. His soul has moved = on and can not be returned. Severian, when he pokes the claw into the = solidiers forehead forces the body to come back to life. Since you = can't have a living body without a soul, a doorway is opened for Jonas = back into the world of Urth.

By the way, If I ever had to get anywhere in the galaxy, you bet your = ass I'd stay far away from Father Inire's mirrors. There about as = predictable as a two year old. They seem to take you to places between = reality, rather than places in reality. Thus everything that goes into = the mirrors enters some sort of 'Alice Throught the Looking Glass' = world.

Finally Mantis:
I'm glad your a reader of RAW. He and Wolf are on the top shelf on = my bookshelf. Strange that I would join one author's group and = instantly find reference to the other. RAW would call in synchronicity, = I'm sure. The story your talking about does not appear in the = ILLUMINATUS! trilogy, to my knowledge, but it is an extremly long book = and I may have missed it. It may be in one of his many other works with = the ILLUMINATI as the theme. Be careful, though, RAW also says that = John Dillinger was actually a quintuplet and one of them was the true = assasin of JFK. Wilson mixes fact and fiction so well its hard to tell = when he's pulling your leg.
Hail Eris,
FunkMonkey

Just a few clarifications on my position which I wasn't clear on,

Thank you all for responding to my post,
Chris Culver,
    I did not mean to say that Typhon and the exultants left Urth.  =
However, I believe there is a mention that the exultants are invaders =
from "another shore" and I think it has been stated that Typhon may have =
been their leader.  When I say that Typhon returned to Urth, I mean more =
in the sense that you or I may return to our "homeland."  That is to say =
that Typhon may come from a planet that was a part of the old galactic =
empire of Urth.  In the fall of the galactic empire many areas would =
have been cut off from other areas of the galaxy.  These areas would =
develop their own cultures and genetic traits over centuries. (maybe =
hundreds of centuries)  I see Typhon as a sort of provincial general who =
desires to rebuild the empire to its glory days and decides to set up =
the throne on the "most ancient of planets" Urth.  In the end it is only =
my theory to try to explain why Typhon and the exultants are said to =
come from another planet, be so similar to 'stock' humans, and yet be so =
very different (as in: snow white complextions, extreme height, =
ruthlessness). =20

As to the nature of Jonas, Hethor, and KLS.  I agree whole heartedly =
that Jonas, Hethor, and KLS are all the same type of sailors lost in the =
sea of time, trapped in a future they didn't expect.  At least Jonas =
didn't come back to Earth to find it overrun by talking apes!  : )   =
"Get your filthy hands off me, you damn animals!"  (or something like =
that)

Mantis:
    I've read the archives and once again you've blown me away with your =
knowledge of Urth factoids.  I'm intruiged.  Where are the mentions of =
Venus and Mars terraforming and their respective names found in the =
text?  And where is the mention of the Xanthic continent with =
posthistorical starfarers?  I'd like to reread those sections.

As for the Miles/Jonas enigma, ....  I'm stumped.  When I first read it, =
I always thought it was vague.  Then in TUotNS, Gunny (I believe her =
name is) uses the same kind of Jonas-speak analogies and Sev. questions =
her on it.  She says its just the way sailors talk.  From this I =
gathered that maybe Miles was never really Jonas and maybe just a sailor =
as well.   HOWEVER, I recently reread the section and its really fishy.  =
Severian thinks he sees Jonas walking next to him when he and Miles are =
walking together.  When Sev. asks Miles his real name, Miles responds =
that he doesn't know in a very Jonas sort of way.

Theory:  Whoever the soldier was, he is long dead.  His soul has moved =
on and can not be returned.  Severian, when he pokes the claw into the =
solidiers forehead forces the body to come back to life.  Since you =
can't have a living body without a soul, a doorway is opened for Jonas =
back into the world of Urth.

By the way, If I ever had to get anywhere in the galaxy, you bet your =
ass I'd stay far away from Father Inire's mirrors.  There about as =
predictable as a two year old.  They seem to take you to places between =
reality, rather than places in reality.  Thus everything that goes into =
the mirrors enters some sort of 'Alice Throught the Looking Glass' =
world.

Finally Mantis:
    I'm glad your a reader of RAW.  He and Wolf are on the top shelf on =
my bookshelf.  Strange that I would join one author's group and =
instantly find reference to the other.  RAW would call in synchronicity, =
I'm sure.  The story your talking about does not appear in the =
ILLUMINATUS! trilogy, to my knowledge, but it is an extremly long book =
and I may have missed it.  It may be in one of his many other works with =
the ILLUMINATI as the theme.  Be careful, though, RAW also says that =
John Dillinger was actually a quintuplet and one of them was the true =
assasin of JFK.  Wilson mixes fact and fiction so well its hard to tell =
when he's pulling your leg.
Hail Eris,
FunkMonkey
