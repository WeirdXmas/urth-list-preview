1999-12-01 Tony Ellis #

Argues that the Erebus entities are alien lifeforms rather than modified humans, citing the ending of The Shadow of the Torturer where Jonas tells a story about a woman who returns from space with magic beans she threatens to throw into the ocean. Answers Daniel Fusch's claim that the Book never says how these entities arrived by pointing to that inset tale as the explanation.
disputes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Erebus entities are alien lifeforms that arrived from space; Jonas's magic bean story explains Erebus's origin

But there is. We've been down this road before. Right at the very end of The Shadow of the Torturer, Jonas tells the
story of the woman who comes back with space with a handful of magic beans, and threatens to throw them into the ocean.

Erebus and friends aren't genetically modified humans like Baldanders, they're alien lifeforms. That grow.
