Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

2001-03-27 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Asks how the inhumi of The Book of the Long Sun can imitate both humans and Neighbors, how many limbs they have, and how much information they take from victims. Wonders whether victims also pass on genetic or somatic material, and jokingly asks if this is another Lysenko-style inheritance trick by Wolfe.
questions · close-textual-reading

Ummmm. A great big "huh?" just hit me.

The inhumi can imitate us.

The inhumi can imitate Neighbors.

How many limbs have the inhumi? And how can they imitate both us and the Neighbors? Or ... can they?

How much "information" do the inhumi get from their victims? We know that they get some kind of intelligence/memory/personality download; do they get some kind of genetic/somatic download also?

Is this yet another case of Wolfe playing Lysenko games?

Aaaargh!

--A very-upset-and-scurrying-under-the-linoleum Blattid
