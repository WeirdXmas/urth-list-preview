Informs the list that Gene Wolfe has finished the third draft of The Wizard Knight and expects a fourth draft to follow, with no clear plans yet for his next project. Relays Wolfe's own status report rather than offering any reading of the text or argument about it.

2003-01-12 Jesper Svedberg #

Asks how long The Wizard Knight will be in final form and how many pages its first and final drafts ran. Relays Nicholas Gevers's recollection that Wolfe reported over 1100 manuscript pages early in drafting, and asks what that suggests about the final book's length.
questions

From: Nicholas Gevers <[email]>

So, do we know approximately how big _The Wizard Knight_ will be in the end? I remember that someone quoted Wolfe a while back, when he was still working on the first draft, as saying that he had done 1100+ pages (manuscript pages I presume) with much more to come. Does anyone know how many pages the first draft finally ended up being, or how many pages the final draft of _The Knight_ is? What would this say about the number of pages in the final book?

// Jesper Svedberg
