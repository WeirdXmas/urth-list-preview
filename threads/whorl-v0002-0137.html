1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunDave Lebling

1997-02-13 Dave Lebling #

Lays out a revised chronology for The Book of the Long Sun, dating births, deaths and the Whorl's arrival from -300 to +10, with Blood's age set at 54. Grounds the dates in stated ages and events, and works through the problem that Silk, aged 23, recalls Calde Tussah's death when he was just learning to walk.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Blood is 54 years old; Silk's first memory comes from his mother

Revised Timeline:

-300+: Maytera Marble "born."
-300: _Whorl_ launched.
-100: (approximately) _Whorl_ arrives in Blue/Green system. -94: Maytera Rose born, based on Blood's estimated age. -56: Crane born.
-54: Blood born. (Stated age (49) plus five years)
-43: Maytera Rose becomes senior sibyl. (Maytera Betal dies?) -36: Maytera Mint born.
-33: Quetzal becomes Prolocutor.
-30: Pas killed.
-23: Silk born. Hyacinth born.
-21: Calde Tussah dies. (Silk "just learning to walk")
-19: Chenille born.
-1: Patera Pike dies. (Dead "less than a year" in TBotLS) 0: most events in _The Book of the Long Sun_.
+10: (approximately) Horn writes _The Book of the Long Sun_.

Notes:

1) Blood's stated age is 49. Silk estimates he's at least five years older. At another point he's referred to as being "nearly 60." I'll take Silk's estimate and call him 54.

2) Chenille is 19, Silk is 23, and remembers Calde Tussah dying when he was "just learning to walk." This presents something of a problem. The earliest Tussah could have died is a bit less than 20 3/4 years previous (i.e., Chenille is about to turn 20, and Tussah dies just after fathering her). If we assume Silk just turned 23, that has him learning to walk after age 2, which is late (especially for a superman...) but not unprecedented.

On the other hand, few people have first hand memories from before the age of 3, so Silk was probably told this by his mother.

3) Most of the action of the book takes place in the winter. I m going to assume, with no evidence yet, that the new year happens when it happens in our calendar, and the winter is January and February. I don t include December and part of March because there is evidence that winters are shorter and milder than they used to be in the _Whorl_. Also, it s very convenient to assume Silk s enlightenment happens on Christmas Day.
