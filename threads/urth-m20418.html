Personal attacks

Urth list · 8 messages · 2011-11-22 to 2011-11-22

2011urthRyan DunnMo Holkar / UKGAntónio Pedro MarquesMarc AraminiDaniel Petersen
The thread: Members debated whether list posts about each other's discourse manners should be stopped, after Mo Holkar asked the list to drop such observations because they breed vitriol. Ryan Dunn backed the request, saying personal attacks had worsened and that the line between passionate and slanderous talk is blurring, while António Pedro Marques argued the line between criticizing ideas and attacking people is clear enough to justify moderation. Marc Aramini apologized for any personal tone and admitted starting an earlier thread, and Daniel Petersen mocked the apology as non-committal. Later António Pedro Marques said Aramini had never written a personal attack and that no apology was needed, so the thread ended without a settled consensus.
  • Mo Holkar: asks the list to stop posting observations about each other's conduct and discourse
  • Ryan Dunn: supports the request; personal attacks have grown worse and the passionate/slanderous line is blurring
  • António Pedro Marques: the line between criticizing ideas and attacking people is clear and moderation is warranted once crossed
  • Marc Aramini: apologizes for personal tone and says his topics get deleted unread
  • Daniel Petersen: mocks Aramini's apparent non-apology and asks for a scholarly digest of the list's debates

2011-11-22 Mo Holkar / UKG #

Asks list members to stop posting observations about one another's conduct and discourse, saying such threads produce vitriol and that the poster has been deleting them unread. Makes no claim about Wolfe's books; it is a moderation request addressed to the whole list.
questions

Hi all,

Guys, some of you are currently making this list a really annoying place to hang out -- to the extent that I'm just deleting threads unread.

It always seems to be observations about one another's distasteful modes of discourse that gets us to this point. I would really like that to be a banned topic, as it seems never to be helpful or informative, but invariably produces a trail of vitriol.

Could you please just lay off each other?

best wishes,

Mo
