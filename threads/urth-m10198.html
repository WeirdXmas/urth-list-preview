Autarchial Succession - How Does It Look From Outside?

Urth list · 1 messages · 2010-08-01 to 2010-08-01

2010urthThe Book of the New SunJeff Wilson

Themes: Power, rulers and politics, Hidden identities and parentage

2010-08-01 Jeff Wilson #

Asks how the succession of Autarchs looks to people outside the palace, given that the brain-eating process is secret. Raises whether the Commonwealth is told a story, and why rivals or vassals would trust a new ruler who might have learned the mastering words by murder.
questions · close-textual-reading

The brain-eating thing seems to be a secret, and as Vodalus says, they may reign a half century or a fortnight. But what story is told to the Commonwealth? What keeps people seeking to corrupt the process in their favor from discovering the secret? What gives the fealty-swearing vasals confidence that the new Autarch isn't just someone who planted a bug on the old Autarch to learn the mastering words and then did him in?
