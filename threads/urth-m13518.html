Typhon's heads

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones

2010-07-12 Lee Berman #

Argues that the talking head of Typhon is the left head, using a passage in The Urth of the New Sun that names the speaking head, which makes Typhon blonde. Builds on a prior reply that Typhon speaks while Piaton only mouths words, and adds that this fits the idea that Silk is a Pas/Typhon clone.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Typhon is blonde because the left head speaks; Silk is a Pas/Typhon clone

Well, not quite so simple to determine. But using these two clues together I found what I never had put together before. Thanks fellas!

I always knew that the left head was blonde and the right head was dark haired. But Wolfe took pains to never say which head was speaking, during that sequence. But in UotNS, Wolfe does say it was the left head which speaks, though not mentioning hair color.

So Typhon is blonde, which jibes nicely if you suspect Silk is a Pas/Typhon clone.
