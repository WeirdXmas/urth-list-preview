Themes: Unreliable narration

1997-06-12 Geoff Alex Cohen #

Reports Wolfe's hesitant answer in a People Online chat about whether the narrator of The Book of the New Sun killed other characters, and suggests that since someone killed them, the real question is who did it. The post recounts the exchange's timing and Wolfe's pauses, leaving the puzzle of the killer open.
informs · puzzle-solving · theories: Someone other than the narrator killed the characters

That was my question, and let me tell you I felt like a dork. The transcript doesn't quite give justice to the timing, though.

Me: Did the narrator kill many of the other characters in the book? Wolfe: Let me think about that... no.
<pause>
Wolfe: I don't think so, no.
<pause>
Wolfe: I'm trying to remember who killed who in that book, and I'd have to go back to it.

At first, I thought he was just dismissing an incredibly stupid question (not the first time in the chat I felt like a dork). But *someone* killed those people.

Now the question is who.

Geoff
