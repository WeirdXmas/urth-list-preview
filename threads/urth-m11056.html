Reports that Wolfe pronounced the name of Reese in An Evil Guest as "rice" and quotes Wolfe calling him a villain who falls just short of being a hero. Responds to Nicholas Jost's question about whether Reese is a good guy by the end of the novel, supplying the author's own description as evidence.

Wolfe pronounced it "rice" with the "i" held as in "rise".

Further, Wolfe described Ries as the perfect villain: "a man who falls just short of being a hero".
