Mucor's fish

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1999-11-09 Joel Priddy #

Asks whether Mucor possessed the fish so they would leap out of the water and wait on a rock, and why Horn did not suspect this given Mucor's known ability. Wonders if Long Sun states that Mucor cannot possess little-brained animals, and adds an unrelated remark about the OBW dust jacket.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Mucor possessed the fish and drove them to leap

When I read the part where Mucor said the fish would be waiting on a rock, I just assumed that she possessed the fish, and drove them to leap out of the water. I was confused that Horn, who clearly knows all about Mucor's ability, didn;t assume this as well. So what am I missing? Is it stated somewhere in Long Sun that Mucor can't possess little-brained animals, or something?

BTW, as soon as I got my copy of _OBW_, I took the paper jacket off and tossed it. I recommend this to everyone because (a) the cover is tacky to the
point of acute embarassment, and (b) the hardcover under the dustwrap is actually a lovely rich blue. This greatly improved the object-quality of the book.

cephalothorax

1999-11-11 William H. Ansley #

Suggests Mucor possessed the fish to make them leap, which Occam's razor favours over a hidden third party. Recalls Mucor possessing only humans in The Book of the Long Sun and her astral projection to Silk in On Blue's Waters. Answers Joel Priddy by citing these passages and noting only Mucor's word vouches for her talk with Silk.
answers · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Mucor possessed the fish to make them leap

I have to admit that when I read the fish story, I thought: telekinesis. Then when Horn thought someone else was responsible, I remembered: Oh, yeah. Mucor doesn't telekines (say tel-u-kin-ees... it's a joke).

I agree that Mucor possessing the fish seems to satisfy Occam's razor better than invoking a mysterious third party but I just don't remember the extent of Mucor's powers. As far as I can remember, the only beings she possessed in _The Book of the Long Sun_ were human. Aside from possession, the only other power she has that I recall is the "astral projection" ability that she uses to talk to Silk, at Horn's request, in _On Blue's Waters_.

Of course, we only have her word for it that she actually "talked" to Silk and for what he "said" to her.

William Ansley
