Boatmen & Exultant Sex

1999urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

1999-04-09 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Jokes about the sexual logistics of exultants and boatmen in The Book of the New Sun, mocking the idea that exultant intercourse must be vertical, and then changes subject to praise the animated show Futurama. Replies to Robert Borski and alga, teasing them about the text's silence on the matter and about boatmen being singular.

Everybody,

No Wolfe content, but I certainly hope everybody (in the US) has been watching "Futurama" (new sf animated comedy show from makers of "The Simpsons"). If not, you've missed three episodes so far.
