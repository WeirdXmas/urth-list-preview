When did the Whorl arrive?

2000whorlThe Book of the Long SunAdam Stephanides

2000-06-19 Adam Stephanides #

Explains how the poster arrived at a minimum of 68 years for the Whorl's stay in the system, questioning whether Horn's 100-year figure limits the Whorl's maximum time there. Argues that Horn's other numbers are mere guesswork, and that the minimum holds only if the Whorl was present when Quetzal came aboard.
disputes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Whorl minimum stay of 68 years

Right--this, and what follows, is where I got the 68 years from. Or am I missing your point?

This I don't see. I'd have to look up the text again (I don't have it with me) but my recollection is that the 100-year figure applied only to the interval between (hypothetical) Whorl-Green conjunctions. I don't recall Horn in this passage considering at all the maximum amount of time the Whorl could have spent in the system.

If we assume that the Whorl was in the system when Quetzal came on board, and that Horn's recollections/extrapolations of Quetzal's career are accurate, then the minimum of 68 years can be approximately relied on. Horn's other numbers are only guesswork, and very shaky at that. (I won't comment on the BoTLS stuff, because I don't remember it at all and I don't have the texts with me.)

--Adam
