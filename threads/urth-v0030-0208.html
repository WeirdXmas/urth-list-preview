Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon

2000-12-17 Alice Turner #

Recommends the film Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon to list members, presenting it as director Ang Lee's artistic treatment of martial-arts genre material. Notes that the poster's companion disliked it and compares it favorably to the earlier recommended film Gladiator, offering no close analysis of Wolfe.
informs

Here's a movie I think would appeal to many on this list. It's an artist's (An Lee)approach to genre material, in this case the chop-sockie Hong Kong flick. God knows it's not everybody's cuppa--the person I went with hated it--but I think it's ours. The last time a movie was touted here was last spring's "Gladiator," and I think that, in a very different way, was also worthy. Check it out.

-alga
