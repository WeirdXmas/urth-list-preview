1997-08-16 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Asks list members whether the Whorl and Urth mailing lists should be merged into one list and suggests possible names for a unified list. Mentions the recent completion of the Long Sun series and asks for opinions to be sent privately, with an aside about a Dan Simmons Hyperion list.
questions

OK, it's been a coupla months now; excitement over the
completion of the Long Sun series has died down; we've all had a chance to settle in. So, the big question: is it
worthwhile keeping the Whorl and Urth lists separate, or is it time to bring 'em together?

And what would the unified list be called? Orth? Whurl? Whulfe?

Send your opinions directly to me ([email]).

-ranjit

(p.s. any takers for a Dan Simmons/Hyperion list?)
