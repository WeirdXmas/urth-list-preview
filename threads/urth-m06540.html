Urth of The New Sun

Urth list · 6 messages · 2008-05-14 to 2008-05-15

2008urthThe Book of the New SunOliver WakefieldBob MillerPedro PereiraSteven BowenRob Thornton
The thread: Readers on the Urth list discussed whether The Urth of the New Sun is enjoyable despite its harder metaphysics. Oliver Wakefield found it harder than The Book of the New Sun, calling it necessary for the first four books yet working as a coda, and asked for the general consensus. oblate777 and Pedro Pereira both said they enjoyed it on a first pass and would save analysis for rereading, with Pereira noting its style and pace differ. Bob Miller gave a more negative verdict, calling it a useful coda but enjoyed less than the first four books. Steven said he really enjoyed it, citing Tzadkiel's ship and Zak's evolution, while admitting incomplete comprehension. The thread ended as a mix of personal verdicts rather than a settled consensus.
  • Oliver Wakefield: harder than Book of the New Sun, necessary for the first four books but works as a coda
  • oblate777: enjoyed it despite hard metaphysics; enjoy first, analyze on rereads
  • Pedro Pereira: enjoyed it, complements Book of the New Sun, first read for enjoyment and analysis later
  • Bob Miller: useful coda but enjoyed less than the first four books, about equal to the first two Latro books
  • Steven: really enjoyed it, a wonder should keep its sense of wonder despite incomplete comprehension

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Gods, angels and the Increate, Books, libraries and writing
