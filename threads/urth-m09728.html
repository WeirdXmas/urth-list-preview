2009-05-21 Gwern Branwen replying to Matthew Groves #

Argues that a signed limited edition of Wolfe's The Very Best of Gene Wolfe is not unreasonably priced, comparing it to Neil Gaiman's Absolute Sandman collections. Responds to Matthew Groves's link offering the book to well-heeled fans by pointing out that the Sandman volumes cost $60-70 new without any signature.

2009-05-22 Nicholas Gevers replying to Matthew Groves #

Praises the introduction to The Very Best of Gene Wolfe, a 7,500-word piece by Kim Stanley Robinson, calling it a brilliant appreciation of Wolfe and perhaps the finest yet written. Responds to Matthew Groves, who had listed the collection's features, by adding this one as a further recommendation.
supports

One other very worthwhile feature of *The Very Best of Gene Wolfe* is the 7,500 word Introduction by Kim Stanley Robinson, a brilliant appreciation of GW, perhaps the finest yet.

--Nick Gevers.
