1999-11-02 Nicholas Gevers #

Reports a online review of On Blue's Waters by John Clute in SF Weekly, noting Clute's claim that Tor will publish In Green's Jungles and Return to the Whorl in 2000. Mentions Clute's speculation that Return to the Whorl covers events after Horn finishes writing On Blue's Waters, without citing any passage from the books.
informs

A very interesting review of ON BLUE'S WATERS by John
Clute appears online in the latest SF WEEKLY. He
states that Tor will release both IN GREEN'S JUNGLES
and RETURN TO THE WHORL in 2000, and speculates
(perhaps on the basis of textual inference, perhaps
from privileged information) that RETURN TO THE WHORL
concerns events that occur after Horn's writing of ON
BLUE'S WATERS.

1999-11-03 William H. Ansley #

Disputes John Clute's speculation that Return to the Whorl takes place after Horn writes On Blue's Waters, arguing instead that Horn has already returned from the Whorl by the time he writes that book. Grounds the claim in hints within On Blue's Waters and jokes that the final title should then be Re-return to the Whorl.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Horn has already returned from the Whorl before writing On Blue's Waters

Interesting is hardly the word for Clute's speculation, quoted above. It don't make no sense! I think it is pretty clear that Horn has visited and returned from The Whorl already by the time he has written OBW from the hints contained within. If that is so, then shouldn't the title of the final book be _Re-return to the Whorl_?

Well, one more reason I just can't wait.

William Ansley
