2002-12-02 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Agrees with Roy's point that the unnamed city in Free Live Free lies on the East Coast, not the Midwest, and argues the High Country hovers in place rather than circling. Builds on the Atlantic-waters passage in chapter 59 and lists candidate coastal cities between DC and New York such as Baltimore, Philadelphia, and Trenton.
supports · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: The unnamed city lies on the East Coast between DC and New York

Good catch! That does narrow things down quite a bit.

I agree, but rather than "circle" I think it more kind of hovers. (For the time travel from High Country 1942 to High Country 1952, I think it really has to be as "in place" as a building on the ground. It is in a "geo-synchronous orbit" while still being within the atmosphere.) There is that talk of the atmospheric winds blowing from west to east, and gliding.

That narrows it down dramatically! There are not that many major cities between DC and NYC, and tending toward the coast: Baltimore, Wilmington (major?), Philadelphia, Atlantic City (major?), and Trenton (major?).

=mantis=

2002-12-02 Jerry Friedman #

Proposes that the unnamed city in Free Live Free may be Newark, or somewhere a little north and east of New York such as New Haven or Providence, and mentions Baltimore as a long-held suspicion. Responds to Michael Andre-Driussi's quoted reasoning, which places the city near New York via the flask, matches and airport clues, by extending the list of candidate cities.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: The unnamed city in Free Live Free is Newark or Providence

...

I think Newark is a possibility too. And why not a little bit north/east of New York? Say New Haven, or more likely Providence, home of Lovecraft. For some reason I always suspected it was B'more, but that may be only a reflection of my ignorance.

Jerry Friedman
