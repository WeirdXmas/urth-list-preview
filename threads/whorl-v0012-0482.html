2001-03-26 Ian Smith #

Disputes Blattid's claim that Tzadkiel's ship only has Urthish speakers because it is Star Trek-like, arguing instead that the ship houses different alien races kept in separate sections. Cites Gunnie's explanation to Sev about similar races being placed near each other and hints of differing atmospheres in the ship's far reaches, and adds a light note on Wolfe having written a Star Trek episode. Also asks where to get a copy of the Clute review.
