Urth list · 6 messages · 2003-02-19 to 2003-02-19

2003urthJeff VeyeraMatt SabAndy RobertsonJosh GellerMichael Andre-Driussi
The thread: A list member asked which Wolfe story involves a hot air balloon, whether any other story uses the premise of inventing something before its time, which collection the list favors, and why a Montreal library held only some New Sun volumes. Jeff Veyera answered the balloon question by identifying the story "Straw" from Storeys from the Old Hotel, which imagines the balloon invented around 500 AD. Josh Geller corrected the title spelling to Lafayette, called it a favorite, and floated a Flash animation idea that he had not acted on. The thread stayed brief and ended with the factual answer and a fan remark, with the library and collection-preference questions left unaddressed.
  • MBS 808: asked for the balloon story and whether other stories share the invention premise
  • Jeff Veyera: identified the balloon story as "Straw" from Storeys from the Old Hotel
  • Josh Geller: corrected the title to Lafayette and praised it as a favorite prose poem

Themes: Books, libraries and writing, Time loops and causality

2003-02-19 Matt Sab #

Asks which Wolfe story is about a hot air balloon, possibly from Endangered Species, and whether any other story uses the introduction's writing-exercise premise of inventing something earlier than it was. Also asks which short-story collection the list favors most and why a Montreal library held the first three New Sun volumes but not the final one.
questions

1) There is a GW short story about a hot air balloon.
What is it called (or even about)? I believe it is in
Endangered Species but I am far from certain.
In the introduction, GW says something about a writing
exercise -- what if something was invented much
earlier than it was? Any other story fit this general
"writing exercise" format?

2) Reading over the posts, I get a general sense of
the novels that are favored, but which collection of
shorter works stands ahead from the pack? This is not
a call for an offlist poll, unless people want one...

3) As a teen in the mid 1980's, I discovered GW in my
local library (in Montreal). I was puzzled because
the library had the first three volumes of the Book of
the New Sun but not the final volume (it had already
been published, at least in hardcover).
Years later in another Montreal library, I discovered
the same thing - volumes 1-3, but not the finale.

Any ideas why or just random circumstances,
coincidence?
