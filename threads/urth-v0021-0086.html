1998-11-23 Matthew Freestone #

Reports that a check of the London Encyclopedia and other guidebooks found no mention of inscriptions on Marble Arch or the Wellington Arch in Hyde Park. Responds to Mantis's search for a Latin inscription, noting a small Marble Arch picture shows none, and asks whether others have better guides or whether Mantis has other arches in mind.
answers · source-and-allusion

Mantis,
A quick check in the (usually excellent) London Encyclopedia and a couple of other guide books reveals no mention of inscriptions on either arch. There's a picture of Marble Arch in the encyclopedia, and no inscription is visible on that either (but it's a small picture, only shows one side etc) but it's hardly conclusive. Anyone have better guide books, or live a bit nearer the centre?

You say probably - do you have other arches in mind too?
