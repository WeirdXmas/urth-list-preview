2009urthThe Book of the New SunDavid Stockhoff

Themes: Buildings, cities and labyrinths, Books, libraries and writing

2009-04-06 David Stockhoff #

Argues that the Severian-Ultan meeting in The Book of the New Sun is a homage to Borges, with Ultan as a labyrinth-dwelling master like the Minotaur, and that Borges-inspired games run through the book. Responds to b sharp's suggestion that Wolfe imagined meeting Borges and may have met him in Buenos Aires, noting the Book of the Wonders of Urth and Sky gift as evidence of Ultan's godlike role.
disputes · source-and-allusion · author-and-biography · theories: Ultan's meeting with Severian is a homage to Borges

Whether Wolfe ever met Borges or not, I'm sure it was a great desire of his to do so. And who wouldn't want to overhear THAT conversation?

Even so, it strikes me more as a homage than anything else: the young book-loving apprentice meeting the grand master lurking like the Minotaur in his labyrinth. Recall Ultan gives Severian the "Book of the Wonders of Urth and Sky." Ultan is like a sorcerer or a god of the underworld.

But I have no doubt there are many Borges-inspired games in BotNS.
