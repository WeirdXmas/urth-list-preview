Themes: Women, sex and gender

2001-08-18 Alice Turner #

Agrees partly with Nigel Price that The Silver Chair is the best Narnia book, but condemns C.S. Lewis for the misogynistic treatment of Susan and wonders whether Lewis's wife Joy challenged him on it. Responds to Nigel Price's praise of The Silver Chair; the post is off-topic and never mentions Wolfe.
chats

I think I agree. Though I am unalterably hostile to Lewis for what he did to Susan. I came across Lewis rather late, maybe in my teens or 20s, and never forgave him for that cool, thoughtless misogyny. I wondered, later on, if Joy, his wife, ever tackled him on that.

-alga
