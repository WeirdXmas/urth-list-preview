SS: Nettle's note

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-05-29 to 2002-05-29

2002urthThe Book of the Long SunRoy C. Lackey

Themes: Unreliable narration

2002-05-29 Roy C. Lackey #

Asks whether Nettle ever wrote the answering note that Oreb was supposed to carry back to Silkhorn in Dorp, since the Short Sun book never mentions one again. Points to the scene where Oreb flies off saying "Girl write. Give bird." and the later jail visit before the trial, and asks whether Wolfe forgot or Silkhorn concealed a reply.
questions · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Nettle replied and Silkhorn concealed the reply

While in Dorp, Silkhorn sent Oreb to Lizard with a note for Nettle. He was dissatisfied with Oreb's bird-brained account of her reaction to his note. Oreb, frustrated with Silkhorn's questioning, decided on his own to return to Lizard, saying "Girl write. Give bird.", and flew away (RTTW, 187). When Oreb next showed up in the story, he was visiting Silkhorn in the latter's cell in the courthouse just before the trial (191). Mention was never made again of Nettle's answering note, if she wrote one.

Did Wolfe simply forget? Or did Nettle reply and Silkhorn neglect to mention the fact for some self-serving reason?

-Roy
