Themes: Unreliable narration

2009-03-19 Stuart Hamm #

Proposes that the opening of Return to the Whorl, where the narrator says "We are four" and then introduces four voices, may reveal an intended structure that complicates an earlier theory about three quills in a pen case. Grounds this in the passages on pages 13 to 20, tracing the narrator, Oreb, Jahlee and Hide and the shifting Horn and Silk voices.
