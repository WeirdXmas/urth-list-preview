1999-05-22 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Announces that the Urth mailing list has moved to its own domain, with archives for volumes 1 to 26 now at a new URL and volume 27 and the Whorl archives still at their earlier locations. Gives no argument about Wolfe; the post is a logistical notice from the list administrator about where the archives can be found.
informs

For almost a year I've been struggling to make room in my web account for the urth and whorl archives -- every two weeks or so I'd have to throw away something else to get the quota alerts to go away. I finally gave in and bought Urth its own home -- welcome to http://www.urth.net/urth !

It'll take me a while to get everything moved over to the new server. For now, everything is where it used to be
except that you will find the Urth archives for volumes
1..26 at http://www.urth.net/urth/archives/
The ongoing volume 27 and all the Whorl archives are still at http://www.urth.net/urth/archives/ and
http://www.moonmilk.com/whorl/archives/ for now.

Ranjit.

1999-05-23 William H. Ansley #

Thanks Ranjit for moving the Urth and Whorl mailing list archives and web site to their own host, and praises the new archive format. Says he had taken the resources for granted and offers to help keep the site running.
chats

Ranjit,

This is wonderful. Thank you.

It is all too easy for me to forget that someone is doing a great deal of work and spending a great deal of time to make the Urth and Whorl mailing lists, web sites and archives exist and function so smoothly. As with so much of the web, I have more or less been taking it for granted that these resources would just always be there, although in the back of my mind I knew this wasn't true.

So I would like to take this opportunity to say that I am very grateful that you are continuing to support this site; it has given me a great deal of pleasure and provided me with a lot of entertainment over the last few years. If there is some way I can help out, please let me know.

By the way, I really like the new format of the web page archive on the new site.

William Ansley
