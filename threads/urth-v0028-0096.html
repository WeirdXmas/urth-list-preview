1999-09-10 Damien Broderick #

Praises a fellow list member for the many articles on John Crowley in the August issue of the New York Review of SF and mentions a cover piece by Michael Andre-Driussi. Announces the recent Melbourne Worldcon and jokingly asks for another drink, without discussing any Wolfe text.
chats

It's a bit late to mention this, but we're slow in Australia - I hope people noticed how that nice clever gel alga had not one, not two, but about fifteen articles (on John Crowley under various species) in the August *New York Review of SF*. What a champion! Not to mention a cover piece by that arcane ferreter Michael Andre-Driussi, on a bestial matter. Yay!

(And all you poor things missed the Worldcon in Melbourne, just wrapped. Ah... I need a[nother] drink.)

Damien Broderick

Reports that a publisher listing gives September 2000 as the release date for John Crowley's Daemonomania, later than the poster had heard. Responds to Kieran's question about news of the book, citing a SciFan forthcoming-books page as the source.
