Themes: Christ figures and messiahs

1999-10-18 James Jordan #

Asks in what ways The Book of the Long Sun follows the events of Exodus through Deuteronomy, noting the poster earlier listed many parallels to the gospels, Acts and Revelation but cannot see Moses in the book. Requests a repost of any older list of Moses parallels, so the discussion can start from existing material.
questions · source-and-allusion

Long ago I posted a score or more of fairly explicit parallels between BLS and the gospels, Acts, and Revelation. I've never been able to see much of Moses here. Of course, Wolfe always adds other things on top of the basic scaffolding, but in what ways does BLS track the events in Exodus-Deuteronomy? Is there an old post that lists parallels that could be reposted here?

1999-10-18 Alex David Groce #

Argues that the Long Sun books parallel the Exodus story, with Silk leading people out of bondage under Typhon toward a promised land, and notes Silk's reluctance to lead like Moses. Supports this with the title Exodus from the Long Sun, the Auk/Aaron comparison another poster raised, and a tentative suggestion that Chemise is Miriam.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · source-and-allusion · theories: Silk's journey is a Moses-style Exodus; Auk parallels Aaron; Chemise is Miriam

Well, I'm not sure there are nearly as many narrative comparisons, but the overall idea of Silk leading the people out of bondage to Typhon & co. in the Whorl to arrival in a new land that (at least once Typhon's storms get going) is "milk and honey" compared to the Whorl is obvious--the last title beginnning with Exodus seems unlikely to be wholly spurious. Silk is rather reluctant to take the role of leader on himself at first, much like Moses. And somebody did comment on Auk/Aaron parallels. Chemise as Miriam?
