1998-10-18 Alex David Groce #

Agrees with alga that Alex Groce's Catholic Encyclopedia entries for Severus and Severian add little beyond the OED, then notes Simon Magus will come later and compares Severian's jungle magic duel with Simon's to show how Severian treats heathens and heretics. Grounds the comparison in Severian's situational rather than principled distinction, which the poster links to his idiot-savant handling of miraculous powers.
supports · theology-and-religion · close-textual-reading · theories: Simon Magus is the type for Cyrix

No problem (I typed both in during office hours as no students showed up.) I agree that the entries don't exactly elucidate anything the OED doesn't. Simon Magus is a little longer and will have to wait until later (and with or without the article it's clear he's the type for Cyrix). It's interesting to examine the differences between that and Severian's jungle "magic duel." Sev. handles "heathens" and "heretics," if you will, differently--although the distinguishing factor is situational, rather than based on any larger principle (which makes sense, Severian being a kind of idiot savant most of the time with respect to his miraculous abilities).
