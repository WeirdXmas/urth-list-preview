Question about "The Cat"

Urth list · 1 messages · 2008-06-15 to 2008-06-15

2008urthDave Tallman

2008-06-15 Dave Tallman #

Asks whether other readers of 'The Cat' noticed echoes of Lewis Carroll's Alice in Wonderland, especially the Cheshire Cat, and lists six parallels. The parallels are the cat's grey striped coat, its vanishing, Father Inire's interest in telling children stories, mirrors opening another world, a shrunken doll-like child, and the cat haunting a dream.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: The cat in The Cat echoes the Cheshire Cat; Father Inire echoes Lewis Carroll

How many people who read this story thought of "Alice in Wonderland"? In particular, the Cheshire Cat?

1) The cat is gray with stripes of darker gray, like Teniel's illustration of the Cheshire Cat in *Alice in Wonderland*.
2) It becomes invisible like that cat.
3) Father Inire's interest in children and telling them stories is like Lewis Carrol.
4) Mirrors leading to an alternate reality was done in "Through the Looking Glass" long before Borges.
5) The doll-like child also seems like a reference to Alice, who shrunk in the story.
6) In the end, the cat haunts someone's dreams. Alice's adventures were dreams.
