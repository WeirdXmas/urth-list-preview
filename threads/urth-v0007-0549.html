Argues that the mysterious woman in the story is only known to be a space traveller with access to alien life forms, comparable to a female Hethor. Supports this by recounting the myth of Nessus the centaur, whose poisoned blood-soaked shirt killed Hercules, in answer to the earlier question about the name Nessus.
