2000-07-03 Nicholas Gevers #

Announces that the Infinity Plus website is featuring a story and long interview with Mary Gentle, whose work the poster says echoes John Crowley's use of Hermetic devices and lore. Suggests her large new novel Ash might appeal to fans of both Crowley and Wolfe, with no textual evidence offered about Wolfe's books.
informs

The site I help edit, INFINITY PLUS
(http://www.infinityplus.co.uk), is currently
featuring a story by and long interview with Mary
Gentle, whose work echoes John Crowley's in many ways
(especially in its use of Hermetic devices and lore),
and whose massive new novel, ASH: A SECRET HISTORY (4
volumes in the USA), might well appeal to admirers of
both Crowley and Wolfe.

-Nick "Rigoglio" Gevers.

2000-07-10 Matthew Malthouse #

Jokingly warns that Mary Gentle's heavy novel Ash: A Secret History could strain wrists and disrupt sleep, reporting that the poster stayed up reading it all night and is eager to resume. Responds to a post praising Gentle's Hermetic lore as appealing to Crowley and Wolfe fans, offering no argument about Wolfe's texts.
chats

Health warnings should be applied: this weighty tome bids fair to break wrists. It may also wreck sleep paterns.

Matthew
(Yeah, foolishly I read the night through and now can hardly wait to get back to it.)
