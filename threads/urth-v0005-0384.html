Cim & Mr. Million go Shopping

Urth list · 1 messages · 1997-11-05 to 1997-11-05

1997urthPeter Cash

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Hidden identities and parentage

1997-11-05 Peter Cash #

Proposes that Mr. Million is a descendant or personality imprint of the devil, reading the cloning experiments as a failed attempt to transcend the devil's inherent mediocrity. Builds on the earlier post's Satan-as-charlatan reading, citing the line about "we never rise any higher" from the young man's father and comparing the idea to C.S. Lewis.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · close-textual-reading · theories: Mr. Million is the devil's ancestor or imprint; Million is a pun on Legion; The devil is naturally mediocre and cannot transcend

I cede the point: lightning bug it is!

Whop! It just hit me what you're talking about...Mr. Million is an ancestor of the line, isn't he? Some kind of personality imprint of great-grandpaw, right? Hmmm....Million=Legion, maybe?

I do remember this line from the young man's father...something about how "we never rise any higher", implying that the devil is stuck in a kind of mediocrity. The whole point of the cloning experiments is for the devil to transcend his limitations, I guess. But it doesn't work--the devil is just naturally mediocre. There's something of C.S. Lewis in that; I think old Clive would like it.

Sgt. Rock
