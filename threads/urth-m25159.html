Severian forgets a few things ...

Urth list · 3 messages · 2014-08-05 to 2014-08-06

2014urthThe Book of the New SunNessun SapràMarc Aramini
The thread: Marc Aramini asked whether Severian's supposed perfect memory in The Shadow of the Torturer really holds, pointing to his failure to recall Thecla's meal and the forgotten coin, and suggesting distraction by an attractive woman might explain the lapse. Nessun Sapra answered by collecting many instances across The Book of the New Sun where Severian claims to forget or cannot recall details, treating them as a running gag rather than one incident. Nessun Sapra also asked whether the lapses are triggered by distraction. The thread stayed between these two posters and ended without a clear resolution, though the second poster's catalogue largely supports reading the forgetting as a deliberate pattern.
  • Marc Aramini: Severian's perfect recall is questionable; the forgotten coin and Thecla's meal suggest lapses, possibly caused by distraction
  • Nessun Sapra: Severian's repeated claims to have forgotten things form a running gag across the tetralogy, not a single incident

Themes: Memory and forgetting, Unreliable narration

2014-08-05 Marc Aramini #

Asks whether Severian's memory in The Shadow of the Torturer is really perfect recall, since he fails to recall Thecla's meal despite trying. Points to the forgotten coin and the leeks, lentils and mutton scene, suggesting distraction by an attractive woman may explain the lapse.
questions · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian's perfect recall fails when distracted by attraction

It's been a long time since I thought about New Sun, but as I am near that point in Wolfe's career where I am going to have to do some of the short fiction related to the books, I have to reread it now, and will probably do a write up sooner or later, but these quotes kind of jumped out at me as I glanced through the opening sections of shadow of the torturer.

?In the remembered terror of the fight I had forgotten [the coin]?

?I tried to recall what her meal had been ? I could not, though I nearly burst my brain with the effort.?

The first one is explainable as kind of a passive oblivion, but the second one he is consciously trying to recollect something and failing to bring Thecla's food to mind, even though it is revealed that his dinner is exactly the same as hers, save for Mutton: "Leeks and lentils, bread and mutton."

"Ah, the torturers get mutton - that's the difference."

So should that be perfect recall until he is distracted by a woman he finds exotic and attractive?

2014-08-06 Nessun Saprà replying to Marc Aramini #

Collects many instances of Severian saying he forgot or could not recall something in The Book of the New Sun, quoting lines about failed recollection. Responds to Marc Aramini's opening quotes, noting the tension with Severian's claimed perfect recall and asking whether his memory lapses are triggered by distraction.
informs · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian's memory lapses are triggered by distraction

Since there are many other instances, the ?I forgot? topic is something like a running in in TBotNS:

?there was one thing I wanted to ask Agia; but I could not recall what it might be?

?beyond the stair I could remember nothing?

?That was Beuzec, of course; I had failed to recall the name until I saw him?

?For you others, as I am told, memory dies; mine scarcely dims?

?I could not at once recall where?

?At that time, I did not recognize ??

?a woman I recalled but could not identify?

?I made several false starts?

?Somehow I missed the little inn?

?I thought I recognized the scent?

?I find I have no memory of such a sound?

?Even now, all I recall?:

?I recalled only?

?for the mind is too full at the time of struggle to do much recording?

?suggesting something I could not quite recall?

?I have the facility of recalling everything. I can?t always lay hands on it?

?While I was ill I had never paid much attention to the people who brought our food, though when I reflected on it I was able to recall them clearly, as I recall everything?

?
