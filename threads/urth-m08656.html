The Sailor the Sailed After the Sun

Urth list · 1 messages · 2009-07-28 to 2009-07-28

2009urthJames Wynn

2009-07-28 James Wynn #

Points out that the closing claim of Wolfe's story, that a five-pointed star appears nowhere else in the Book of Am-Tuat, is false, since the star recurs in other hour illustrations. Supports the point with a link to Egyptian Book of the Dead graphics and says he plans to raise the apparent error with Wolfe at DragonCon.
disputes · source-and-allusion

Final sentences in the story:

"[In the 10th hour of the Book of Am-Tuat] Above the monkey's head, I should add, floats something that you will not find anywhere else in the whole "Book of That Which Is in Tuat". It is a smallish, quite common and ordinary-looking, five-pointed star."

It is true that the monkey and the star are there (see the last graphic here http://www.bibliotecapleyades.net/egipto/egyptian_heavenhell/gate/gate26.htm). However, it is not true that the five pointed star is not found anywhere else in Am-Tuat, as you can see by casually examining the graphics.

I have no explanation for this apparent error. Unless you guys can clarify this, I'll try to bring this up if I get a chance to talk to him casually at DragonCon in Atlanta.

J.
