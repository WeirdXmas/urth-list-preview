2002-03-08 Gage Kerrigan #

Proposes a dating argument that Appian was Autarch sixty-two years before Severian took power, using Odilo's twenty years of service, Sancha's fifty-year absence and the Cat's timeline. Grounds the claim in those intervals and asks readers to check the math before quoting it, then asks for chapter-referenced material on societal decline in the Urth books for a school report.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Appian was Autarch sixty-two years before Severian

Appian was (through info provided in "The Cat")
Autarch 62 years before Severian became Autarch. This
train of thought inspired by recently joining urth.net
and re-reading (atleast beginning to) all posts. I'm
not tremendously far, so this might have already been
proven.
I came to my conclusion based upon: Odilo has
served for 20 years, Currently (relative to "the cat")
5 years after sev. gains power, Sancha leaving for a
50 year time period, Sancha returning in 3rd year of
Odilo's service, and (finally) Appian being Autarch
when Sancha left. Please don't quote this without
checking math first, I hate responsibility.
And Now...
I'm doing a report (for school, if you must know)
based upon the decline of the environment and society
of the Urth universe, that is to say, in TBotNS and
UotNS. Anyone who can provide detailed info on this
topic (along with chapter numbers, if possible) will
earn my undying gratitude and some pocket lint.
Thanks all.

»LORD EL SABAH NUR«

2002-03-08 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Presents a dated timeline reconstructing events from The Cat through The Book of the New Sun, placing Autarch Appian's reign in 66 to 62 PS and linking it to Sancha's departure and Odilo's birth. Replies to G Willickers's claim that Appian ruled sixty-two years before Severian, citing Odilo's service and Sancha's absence as evidence.
supports · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Appian was autarch 62 years before Severian

My most recent numbers are:

80 PS (pre-Severian's Reign) Odilo I born, Sancha born.

66 PS scandal in reign of Autarch Appian: Sancha (age 14) in disgrace, Lomer (age 28) sent to the antechamber (II, ch. 15).

62 PS Chatelaine Sancha leaves the House Absolute.

50 PS Odilo II born (II, ch. 19)

15 PS Odilo II begins work at the House Absolute.

12 PS Chatelaine Sancha returns to the House Absolute.

10 PS Thecla (13 years old) sees Sancha alive (II, ch. 15).

6 PS Chatelaine Sancha dies, age 75.

1 PS Most events of TBOTNS

1 SR The Third Battle of Orithyia. Severian becomes autarch after killing "the Old Autarch" (Appian).

? SR Severian lives among Ascians for a year as a slave (V, ch. 51)

5 SR Odilo II tells tale of "The Cat"

35 SR Odilo III begins work at the House Absolute?

50 SR Severian returns from Yesod . . . . meets Odilo III, Pega, Thais, and Eata (V, ch. 44-46)

150 SR The Sleeper awakens, raises up Pega, Thais, and Odilo.
