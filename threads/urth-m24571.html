Themes: Evolution and the future of humanity, Women, sex and gender

2013-03-24 Marc Aramini #

Argues that the dog house brothel where Number Five is raised is an ironic setting, since its commodified sex is the opposite of the recombination and change that sex serves evolutionarily. Grounds this in evolutionary reasoning about sex and in Marsch's discussion of the appeal of women in V.R.T., which the poster cites without quoting it.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Dog house sex ironically mirrors Number Five's stagnation

I quite forgot to mention that the stagnation prevalent in number five's creation is highly ironic - he is raised in a whore house where sex is a commodity, not an agent of recombination and change. Evolutionarily sex is vital to creating traits that can survive, as " Marsch" examines at length in VRT when he talks about the appeal of women. It has been perverted into entertainment at the dog house.
