Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2000-02-08 Dennis G. Berdanis #

Proposes that the linked stories in Strange Travellers share a single message, namely that life is so hard that delusion is preferable to it. Notes that the delusory worlds in the stories are not paradises, citing the traffic jam, the space suicide mission against evil, and the frozen world of Emery, yet still appeal to the protagonists over their dull jobs and family duties.

Would that message be that life is so hard and unpleasant that delusion is preferable to it?

If that's true, what's interesting to me about Wolfe's worlds of delusion is that none of them are paradises. The traffic jam. The battle of good vs. evil being a suicide mission in space. The frozen world of Emery.
