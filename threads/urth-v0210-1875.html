Wolfe's Knight

2002-10-18 Nicholas Gevers #

Reports that the Conjunctions 39 galley excerpt from Wolfe's forthcoming The Wizard Knight runs six chapters, not the single chapter available online. Relays John Clute's speculation that Wolfe again draws on the Cabala for the multi-world universe of the Knight and Wizard books, and a close parallel with David Lindsay's A Voyage to Arcturus.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Wolfe invokes the Cabala in The Wizard Knight; The Wizard Knight parallels Lindsay's A Voyage to Arcturus

Looking at the galleys of the soon-to-be released
CONJUNCTIONS 39, I see that the excerpt from GW's
forthcoming KNIGHT is much longer than the single
chapter available online--six chapters here. John
Clute speculates elsewhere in the book that Wolfe is
again invokinh the Cabala in constructing the
multi-world universe of KNIGHT and WIZARD; he
additionally suggests a close parallel with David
Lindsay's A VOYAGE TO ARCTURUS.

--Nick Gevers.
