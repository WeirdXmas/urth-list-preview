Typhon, the inhumi & Blue's Waters

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1999-09-23 to 1999-09-23

1999whorlAlex David Groce

1999-09-23 Alex David Groce #

Argues that the Whorl was sent out to spread Typhon's rule, so Pas's glory is the simplest explanation, and doubts that Typhon would sell the Whorl to the inhumi given their low technology. Grounds this in Typhon's character as a monster who is not purely sadistic and in the question of how Q. reached the Whorl with such primitive technology. Also notes the poster has just started On Blue's Waters and will comment later.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Typhon sent the Whorl to spread Pas's glory; Typhon would not sell the Whorl to the inhumi

I think the Occam's Razor explanation for the Whorl is that it was sent out for the greater glory of Pas--i.e. to spread Typhon's rule to new places. I actually think it would be odd for Typhon to sell the Whorl to the inhumi, simply because it's difficult to see what they could offer him. Typhon's certainly a monster, but doesn't seem purely sadistic. The inhumi seem to be on a low enough technological level that Typhon would simply exterminate them if he could. Although how Q. reached the Whorl given this low technology is a good question.

I received my copy of On Blue's Waters yesterday. I've only read two chapters so far. I'll comment later today (with the proper warning).
