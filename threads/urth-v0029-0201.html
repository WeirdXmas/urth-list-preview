2000-09-01 Jonathan Laidlow #

Reports that the Wolfe Symposium papers may be published as a small book by a university press and otherwise offered by subscription. Answers Rostrum's question about how and when the papers read at the symposium will become available to others.
answers

Rostrum asked:

"OK, you've got me all interested. How and when are the papers read at Jonathan Laidlow's Wolfe Symposium going to be published or otherwise made available for the rest of us?"
An interesting question to which I hope to have the answer soon. There is a chance my University's Press will publish the papers as a small book which you will be able to order by mail order.

Failing that, we'll do it the old-fashioned way, by subscription! Jonathan

--
