1999whorlThe Book of the New SunNicholas Gevers

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Time loops and causality

1999-10-11 Nicholas Gevers #

Proposes that Nessus reappears in the Short Sun books with Severian and Merryn, and offers three explanations: Blue is Urth, Green is Urth, or some form of space or time travel. Grounds this in a Nova Express interview saying Short Sun reaches Severian's childhood; also suspects Severian is related to Silk, and asks Kevin about Green and Whorl dates.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Blue is Urth; Green is Urth; Severian is biologically related to Silk; Nessus arrives in Short Sun via travel or dream

So: somehow Nessus arises later in SHORT SUN, with
severian and Merryn in tow; we did indeed have an
inkling of this before, especially when in his NOVA
EXPRESS interview Wolfe said that SHORT SUN takes us
into the world of Sev's childhood. So, the
possibilities:
1) Blue is Urth
2) Green is Urth
3) Some form of space and/or time travel is involved,
or perhaps a wandering in dream.
I have a feeling Severian is biologically related to
Silk somehow.
Kevin: any publication dates for GREEN and WHORL yet?
