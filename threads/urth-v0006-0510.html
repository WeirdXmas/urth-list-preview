The distance between the 20th Century and Severian's Urth

Themes: Deep time and ruins, Beasts and the animal in us

1998-02-03 Derek Bell #

Argues that the gap between our era and Severian's Urth must be long, since rats in the library smear book covers with their own crude writing, which implies either natural rat evolution or genetic tinkering. Reasons that even engineered intelligence in rats would take a long time to develop, so the timescale would be lengthy.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Rats in the library writing implies a very long gap

I imagine that there is quite a long gap between the two eras, because Severian mentions that rats in the library smear the covers of the books in their own crude writing. Either this means that rats have naturally developed this ability or somebody was tinkering with their genes. In the latter case the time might be less, but it take quite a while to develop the ability to increase even a rat's intelligence to the level where they can write.

Derek
