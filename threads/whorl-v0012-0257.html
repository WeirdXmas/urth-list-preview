2001-02-17 Dennis G. Berdanis #

Asks whether the character Mucor died on Blue, since Maytera Marble leaves her granddaughter behind to help finish Olivine and Horn and Silk's scenes with Mucor in the Whorl give an eerie impression. Requests evidence for or against this reading after finishing Return to the Whorl, citing the abandonment and those scenes as grounds.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: Mucor died on Blue before Return to the Whorl

Finally finished all the posts I had avoided while reading RTW. The only further question I have to add is
what happened to Mucor? Maytera Marble decided to return to the Whorl to help finish Olivine, but she just casually abandons her granddaughter Mucor?

During some of Horn/Silk's discussion and interactions with Mucor in the Whorl I got the eerie feeling that Mucor had passed away on Blue. Anyone see any evidence to support this? or not?

Dennis/Endy

2001-02-17 David DiGiacomo #

Rejects the quoted post's suspicion that Mucor died on Blue, answering the question with a flat no. Cites Marble's remark in Return to the Whorl that Mucor is caring for her, so leaving does not hurt Mucor, and adds a reminder that the list is for Long Sun discussion.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Mucor died on Blue

Marble says that Mucor is taking care of her, not vice versa, so she is not hurting Mucor by leaving.

No.
