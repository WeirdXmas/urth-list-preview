Gene Wolfe papers?

Urth list · 4 messages · 2021-02-16 to 2021-02-16

2021urthStephen CaseMarc Aramini

2021-02-16 Stephen Case #

Asks whether anyone is in contact with Gene Wolfe's children about his papers and correspondence, and whether they will be donated to an archive and how scholars could access them. Mentions reported interest from Northern Illinois University in folding them into the SFWA papers in its special collections, and asks for information, preferably off list.
questions

I apologize if this has already been discussed on the list. Is anyone in contact with Gene's children regarding his papers and correspondence? I'm curious whether they'll be donated to an archive and how scholars may be able to access them in the future. I know there has been some interest at Northern Illinois University in making them part of the SFWA papers in the library special collections.

Any information would be appreciated. Please feel free to contact me off list at [email].

Thanks,
-Steve
