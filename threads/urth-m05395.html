Themes: Myth and folklore sources

2008-12-23 Marc Aramini #

Argues that the Apu Punchau discussion in the Book of the New Sun calls to mind Frazer's cycle of murdered solar and vegetative deities and their proxy kings from The Golden Bough. Floats the idea of Vodalus as a vegetative deity, asking whether such an arboreal kingship is a false cycle compared with solar ones.
proposes · myth-and-structure · source-and-allusion · theories: Vodalus is a vegetative deity in a cycle of murdered kings

I will only say these comments put me heavily in mind of the cyclic murder of solar and vegetative deities and their proxy kings by their replacements Frazer studies in The Golden Bough - which began with his study of the King of the Wood at nemi. Vodalus as vegetative deity? A false cycle of arboreal kingship less valid than ones with solar resonance?
