Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-08-03 mary whalen #

Argues that the character Urus, whose name comes from the extinct aurochs (Bos primigenius), is a criminal and that his name echoes the phrase "you are you is." Connects this to Mint telling Urus that some people are naturally bad and cannot change, so you are what you are, supported by the animal's extinction and the character's role as a criminal leader.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Urus's name is a pun on "you are you is"

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Urus. Bos primigenius. Also called the aurochs. Ten foot long bovid. Domesticated by humans. Now extinct. Criminal. Associate of Auk's. Sentenced to the pits. Led group. Gave information to the Trivigauntis. This is the only species used as a name in the books that is extinct. Saying the letters in the name sounds like "you are you is." This reminds me of Mint telling Urus that some people are just naturally bad and can't be changed, you are what you are.
