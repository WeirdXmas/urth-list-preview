Regency

1998-07-09 Charles Dye #

Disputes the Lexicon-style entry claiming Valeria becomes regent during Severian's absence, arguing instead that Father Inire is simply minding the store near the top of chapter 34 of The Urth of the New Sun. Offers a historical comparison, noting the last English regency under George IV after George III was declared insane, to support the point that regency is a loose, not formal, role here.
disputes · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Father Inire is not regent during Severian's absence

I also disagree, though for a different reason. Near the top of chapter 34 of "Urth" Severian indicates that Father Inire is minding the store.

I could be mistaken, but I think the last regency in England was George the Fourth's, after George III was declared insane ...?
