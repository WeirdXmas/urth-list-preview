Adopts a fellow poster's earlier question asking what five very great things the character Mint gave up to become a sibyl in Peace. Cites Mint's own statement that she surrendered "five of them very great" as the basis for the question, and jokes about the thread's train metaphor.

adopted hometown, at least until I can get the hell out of here.

"Mint states that she had to give up many things, 'five of them very great,' to become a sibyl. Can you tell us what those five things were?"

--r.
