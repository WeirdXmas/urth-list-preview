Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2000-02-07 David Langford #

Argues that Wolfe's denial that Pike's ghost is Quetzal may not close the question, since Wolfe said in another chat that Quetzal was not the only one of his kind on the Whorl. Responds to a quoted 1997 Q&A exchange on the list, suggesting the question should have been phrased as whether Pike's ghost belonged to Quetzal's species.
proposes · author-and-biography · puzzle-solving · theories: Pike's ghost is a member of Quetzal's species

Alga bloomed:

[quoting me]

Right on! What does he know? Besides, in an on-line chat elsewhere, Wolfe also remarked that Quetzal was not the only one of his kind on the Whorl. So maybe the answer would have been different if we'd put the question in the form, "Was 'Pike's ghost' a member of Quetzal's species?" Ah, the joys of hindsight.
