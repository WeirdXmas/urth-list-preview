Fifth Head maps--online!

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-06-29 to 2001-06-29

2001urthMichael Andre-Driussi

2001-06-29 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Announces that the map section of the Cave Canum website for The Fifth Head of Cerberus has been restored online, with maps the poster helped assemble from original sketches and new index-page artwork. Shares a link and a playful robot quote, with no argument about the book's content.
informs

Hey look!

Robert Borski has reactivated the map section of "Cave Canum"! (For a long time there was a space there, but no content. I'd given him some photocopies of original sketches years ago, and recently we put it all together.)

He also has new artwork for the index page! Robot say "Danger, Number Five! Danger!"

http://webpages.charter.net/rborski/index.html

Check it out!

=mantis=
