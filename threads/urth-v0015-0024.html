Marsch as spy; fan fiction

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-25 to 1998-06-25

1998urthadam louis stephanides

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-06-25 adam louis stephanides #

Argues that Marsch was first arrested for murdering Maitre and only later judged a spy, so confessing to be an Annese impersonator would deepen suspicion rather than dispel it. Grounds this in the cover letter accompanying Marsch's papers, and then asks whether genuine Wolfe-based fan fiction exists, having been disappointed the thread was not about it.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Marsch was judged a spy before confessing

But my point was that "confessing" to be an Annese impersonating Marsch wouldn't draw suspicion from his spying, but to it.

My reading was that he was first arrested on the grounds of murdering Maitre; later Number Five was arrested for the murder, but by that time they'd already decided "Marsch" was a spy. The cover letter to "Marsch's" papers (p. 267 in the Ace edition) seems to confirm this.

When I saw this subject line in the digest intro, I thought you were referring to genuine Wolfe-based fan fiction, and I confess to being disappointed that you weren't. Does anyone know if such a thing exists?

--Adam
