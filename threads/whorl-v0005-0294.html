1997-05-16 Alice Turner #

Proposes a new question for the thread's list, asking whether the Whorl was ever a legitimate colony ship or always a Middle Passenger cargo sold by Typhon. Suggests merging it with Talon's earlier question, and predicts Wolfe won't answer it because it may become a plot point in the new series.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: The Whorl was always a Middle Passenger livestock cargo

I thought of yet another question, but it may be simply a form of Talon's--if you (mantis) are going to edit these questions, you might merge this with his:

6) Was the Whorl ever a legitimate colony ship, or was it always a Middle Passenger (not slaves for the colonies, but livestock for the inhumi), with a cargo sold by Typhon?

I suspect Wolfe won't answer that question, as I foresee it as a plot point for the new series, but it's worth pondering.

-alga-
