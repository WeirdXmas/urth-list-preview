Napoleon Bonaparte meets the Venus de Milo

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-04-30 to 1998-04-30

1998urthDan Rabin

1998-04-30 Dan Rabin #

Asks about the Peace allusion to Napoleon's hand in his jacket, and what supports Louis Gold's claims that the Venus de Milo's arms are known, were found with the statue, and are a modern forgery. Relays Williams Ansley's question; if Wolfe is not inventing Gold's claims they form an interesting hypothesis, and if he is, he shares Gold's bent.
questions · source-and-allusion

[_Peace_] Williams Ansley wants to know what's behind Weer's allusion to the reason that Napoleon is pictured with his hand thrust into his jacket.

And *I* want to know what's behind Louis Gold's assertions about the Venus de Milo: that the form of the arms is known, that they were found with the statue, and that it's almost certainly a modern forgery.

If Wolfe isn't making this up, it's a very interesting hypothesis.

If he *is* making it up, then he's got a bit of Louis Gold in him.

But we knew that.

-- Dan Rabin
