1999-04-14 Steve Taylor #

Reports that the poster found a Gene Wolfe essay, "Scribbling Giant", in the R.A. Lafferty collection East of Laughter and asks whether anyone has read it. Newcomer to the list, it is a tangential question about a Wolfe essay rather than a reading of the fiction and invites comments.
questions

[Pardon the initial mangled version of this message.]

My first posting to the list and it's a rather tangential one...

I just snapped up two more R.A. Lafferty books to add to my fairly obsessive Lafferty collection, and as I took them back to the office I noticed that one of them - _East of Laughter_ has an essay by Gene Wolfe, _Scribbling Giant_ in the first 260 copies printed.

Needless to say, my copy is not amongst that 260.

Has anyone read the essay in question? Any comments?

Steve
