New Wolfe story online

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-04-26 to 2001-04-26

2001whorlRobert Borski

2001-04-26 Robert Borski #

Informs the list that Gene Wolfe's new story "Copperhead" has been posted on SCI FICTION, and points readers to an autobiographical sketch Wolfe wrote to accompany it. Replies to Dr. Nick's earlier note and asks whether he knows other online editors who might publish Wolfe's work.
informs

Dr. Nick has written:

<A few months ago, I urged Ellen Datlow to run a Gene Wolfe story on SCI FICTION, and lo--whether because of my suggestion or without reference to it--she has now done so. The story is new, it's called "Copperhead", and it can be found here:

http://www.scifi.com/scifiction/originals/originals_archive/wolfe/wolfe1.htm l>

Actually, if you access the site at a somewhat earlier point--

http://www.scifi.com/scifiction/originals/

--you'll be able to access the charming autobiographical sketch Wolfe wrote to accompany the story. It's found in the lower right hand corner at the hypertext link "by Gene Wolfe".

Hey, Nick, do you know any other on-line editors who might be able to run pieces by the man?

Robert Borski
