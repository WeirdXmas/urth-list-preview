Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1999-06-19 Mitchell A. Bailey #

Shares a 1999 BBC news item reporting that American Cell Technology cloned a human cell and destroyed it before 14 days, and quotes the article on the ethics of cloning and what it means to be human. Offers no reading of Wolfe's books; it simply passes the news along to the list with a jokey remark about being on the cutting edge.
informs

Thought this story from the BBC News site of June 18th might interest you (the Drudge Report had a link to it)
An outfit called American Cell Technology has acknowledged that they have succeeded in cloning a human cell, kept it alive, then destroyed it before it was 14 days old. Interesting remark the BBC made in its article: " ...but this development will also [cause] a significant heightening of the debate over the ethics of human cloning, and indeed what it means to be a human."
Are we on the cutting edge, or what?!
