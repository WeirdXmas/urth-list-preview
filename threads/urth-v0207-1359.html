Silkicide is hard to do

Urth list · 1 messages · 2002-07-15 to 2002-07-15

2002urthThe Book of the Long SunMichael Straight

2002-07-15 Michael Straight #

Argues that Silk, an augur who could easily kill himself, is nonetheless very hard to kill, so the two facts cancel out or make a failed suicide attempt more likely. Responds to an unnamed view that Silk's augury should make suicide easy, citing his toughness as a counterweight and proposing failed suicide as the more likely reading.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk's suicide attempt failed

Some have said that Silk, an able augur, ought to have no difficulty killing himself if he wished.

On the other hand, Silk is very hard to kill.

These two probably cancel out. Or if anything, make "failed suicide" a more likely option.

-Rostrum
