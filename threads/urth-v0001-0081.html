1997-05-27 Paul C Duggan #

Reports that the poster was the chat user Heirodule and relays Wolfe's answer that The Book of the New Sun does not explicitly date its future setting, with his guess of 1000-2000 years. Responds to Geoff Alex Cohen's account of the chat by explaining that Wolfe gave two answers to the poster's two-part question about the time between Typhon and Severian, and noting a transcript may clarify it.

Heirodule was me. A bit pretentious, but hey.

That was wolfe's answer to the second part of my question. I asked How far in earth's future were the new sun books set, and how much time is bewteen Typhon aSd severian. Wolfe said that the books don't explicitly say, and that 1000-2000 years was his guess but he'd have to reread to be sure (!)

I think he was answering the two parts of the question with two answers. He made similar "complex" answers to other questions. The transcript might make it more obvious that that was how he answered. (No transcript as of 12:14 PM EDT 5/27/97
