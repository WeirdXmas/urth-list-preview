Blood Pudding (was Neighbors as Faeries)

Urth list · 8 messages · 2009-03-22 to 2009-03-22

2009urthThe Book of the Short SunJohn WatkinsDavid StockhoffJames WynnChris PStuart Hamm
The thread: Posters debated whether the Neighbors and Inhumi of Wolfe's Long Sun books are plants, and whether Jahlee carries Chenille's soul because her mother fed on Chenille. David Stockhoff proposed an Inhumi plant origin, while James Wynn argued for a tree-vine war and a shared-identity reading in which Sinew and Krait, and Jahlee and Chenille, are effectively one person. Stuart Hamm backed the feeding theory with a scene where the Raja mistakes Jahlee's voice for Chenille's. Chris P. disputed the identity claims, allowing only a loose sense in which Sinew is within Krait, and John Watkins offered a twin-like copy-of-soul alternative. The thread ended without agreement, with the identity question left open.
  • David Stockhoff: Inhumi evolved from plants, and the Neighbors may have too
  • James Wynn: Neighbors are trees and Inhumi vines, and Sinew and Krait are one identity
  • Stuart Hamm: Jahlee carries Chenille's soul, supported by the Raja's mistaken recognition
  • John Watkins: Krait holds a copy of Sinew's soul, so they are like identical twins
  • Chris P.: Chenille is not an inhumi, and Krait is not Sinew nor Jahlee Chenille

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting, Evolution and the future of humanity

2009-03-22 David Stockhoff #

Proposes that the Inhumi of the Long Sun books evolved from plants, and speculates the Neighbors may have too, linking this to their survival when buried and to the plant hybridization discussed earlier. Offers no textual citation beyond the quoted post's reference to the Inhumi being usually called reptilian and a general impression from an unnamed source.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Inhumi evolved from plants; Neighbors evolved from plants

The inhumi are usually referred to as reptilian, but I got the impression from somewhere that they did indeed evolve from plants. Wouldn't be surprised if the Neighbors did too.

It fits that they can survive when buried (thus, inhumi), and it fits the discussion of plant hybridization.

2009-03-22 James Wynn replying to David Stockhoff #

Speculates that the Neighbors are trees and the Inhumi are vines on Blue, suggesting a tree-vine war and a link to faerie and Actaeon/Orpheus motifs. Builds the case on shapeshifting and transformation across characters, citing Rose, Chenille, the Calde's girlfriend and Horn and Silk, and notes that plant hybridization may instead concern the Rajan.
proposes · myth-and-structure · source-and-allusion · theories: Neighbors are trees and Inhumi are vines; Inhumi can become vines like Neighbors can become stags; Plant hybridization concerns the Rajan after combination with Horn and Silk

In some way that I haven't got my mind around, the Neighbors are trees on Blue and the Inhumi are vines. The story talks about the vines being the brides of the trees IIRC and the trees and vines being in endless war with each other. This fits with the faerie motif of the stories which Tolkien was also plugged into. It also works in a vague way with the Actaeon/Orpheus connection.

If Rose can become a robot, if Chenille can be come an inhumi and then a woman on Urth, if the Calde's girlfriend can become computer interface and then a goddess of love and then sybil and then a general, if a Neighbor can become a stag and then Horn and then Silk, THEN if wouldn't surprise me if Wolfe has figured out a way for inhumi to become vines.

However, I have presumed all the talk of plant hybridization relates to the Rajan after he is combined with Horn and Silk and whatever.

J.

2009-03-22 James Wynn replying to Chris P #

Argues that in the Long Sun books Sinew and Krait, and Rose and Marble, are one identity, citing resurrected Horn calling Krait his son and the Raja calling Jahlee both sister and daughter. Responds to Chris P.'s view that Krait is only partly Sinew by claiming Wolfe deliberately pushes identity to its limits.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Sinew is Krait; Rose becomes Marble; Resurrected Horn is no longer Horn

I'd say it is exactly the issue of identity in the books and I don't suppose we're going come to a meeting of the minds on this. To the extent that Rose becomes Marble, Sinew becomes Krait.

Resurrected Horn calls Krait his son. Add to that that IMO resurrected Horn is not exactly Horn anymore and you see (as I see it anyway) Wolfe is deliberately pushing the idea of identity to its conceptional limits.

Also, note that the Raja calls Jahlee his sister and his daughter. And he tells Hoof or Hide (during dream travel) that Jahlee could be his sister.

J.

2009-03-22 John Watkins replying to Chris P #

Argues that Krait holds a copy of Sinew's soul taken from Sinew at a very young age, making them more like identical twins than the same person. Extends the idea to Chenille and Jahlee, proposing that Jahlee carries Chenille's adult personality and so effectively is Chenille, in reply to Chris P.'s doubt about the inhumi claim.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: Krait holds a copy of Sinew's soul; Jahlee effectively is Chenille

Krait has a copy of Sinew's soul, but it's a copy of Sinew's Thomistic soul from when Sinew was extremely young--they're more like identical twins then like being "the same person" because Sinew's personality wasn't very individualized at that point.

Chenille/Jahlee ought to be a little different--Jahlee would have the personality of Chenille at an adult age, and so effectively be Chenille.
