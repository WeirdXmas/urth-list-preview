Tracking Song names

Urth list · 1 messages · 1997-08-25 to 1997-08-25

1997urthDave Lebling

1997-08-25 Dave Lebling #

Reports a friend's view that the animal names in the Book of the New Sun look vaguely Algonquian, citing northeastern languages such as Micmac, Abnaki and Massachusett. Passes this on as a lead for readers to research online or in libraries, while noting the friend is not certain.
informs · source-and-allusion

According to a friend of mine who is a anthropologist, the "animal" names look "vaguely Algonkian, the languages which were spoken over much of the northeast (Micma, Abnaki, Massachusett, etc.)"

However, he can't be sure. Perhaps this can send someone in the right direction, either on the net or in a library.

--vizcacha
