re Evan S Barnable

Urth list · 4 messages · 2000-06-26 to 2000-06-27

2000urthAllan LloydWilliam H. AnsleyMichael Andre-DriussiAlex David Groce
The thread: Four posts from June 2000 asked what the S in Evan S. Barnable, the name Smoky reveals in Little, Big, stands for. Allan Lloyd proposed a pun on evanesce, meaning fading like a ghost, and William Ansley agreed, arguing it explains why Letters to Santa gives the first name but withholds the middle initial. Michael Andre-Driussi supported the Evan S. equals Smoky equation and held that the S must be another hidden name. Alex David Groce relayed a claim that the middle initial stands for nothing, comparing it to the S in Harry S. Truman. The thread ended with competing readings and no settled answer.
  • Allan Lloyd: the S is a pun on evanesce, meaning fading like a ghost
  • William Ansley: agrees the S stands for evanesce, which explains the withheld middle initial
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: Evan S. equals Smoky, so the S is itself another hidden name
  • Alex David Groce: relays that the middle initial stands for nothing, like Truman's S

Themes: Names and their meanings, Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2000-06-26 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Agrees with Allan that Evan S. equals Smoky in John Crowley's Little Big, so the S itself must be another hidden name rather than Smoky. Grounds this in the redundancy of the equation and compares it to Crowley's habit of hiding such easter eggs in his fiction.
supports · puzzle-solving · theories: The S in Evan S. is another hidden name

Yowie! Looks right to me . . . and since "Evan S." = "Smoky" (and it was probably the too-clever relatives who cooked up "Smoky" directly from "Evan S.") then it would be redundant for S itself to equal "Smoky." Meaning that the S remains another hidden name, another one of those tough little easter eggs without any real answer, core, yoke, or candy picture inside: John Crowley often hides these in his fictions.

=mantis=

2000-06-26 Allan Lloyd #

Proposes that the S in Evan S. Barnable, the name Smoky reveals in Letters to Santa, is a pun on evanesce, meaning disappearing or fading like a ghost. Offers the pun as an answer to a list question about what the S stands for, and wonders why no one has noticed it.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Evan S. Barnable's middle initial puns on evanesce

I've just been re-reading Little Big (prompted by the discussion on this group) and by some wierd coincidence, just reached the page in Letters to Santa where Smoky reveals his real name.
Some-one asked what the S stands for. Does no-one get the joke?

Evan S. = evanesce = disappearing from sight, fading, or maybe ghost-like or even Smoky

Allan

2000-06-26 William H. Ansley #

Agrees with an earlier poster's reading that the initial S in Evan S. Barnable stands for evanesce, linking the name to Smoky's fading and ghostlike nature in Little, Big. Credits the catch as explaining why the narrator reveals the first name but withholds the middle initial in Letters to Santa.
supports · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Evan S. stands for evanesce

Kudos, Allen!

I re-read _Little, Big_ recently and quite carefully too, I might add, but I missed this completely. An excellent catch.

And it nicely explains why Crowley reveals Smoky's real first name (however briefly) while keeping mum about what his middle initial stands for. I was wondering about that! Thank you.

2000-06-27 Alex David Groce #

Relays William Ansley's proposal that Smoky's middle initial stands for nothing, citing the suit of Truman's he wears at his wedding and the 'S' in Harry S. Truman, which likewise has no meaning. Offered as a solution to the middle-initial mystery raised in a previous post about Smoky's real first name in Crowley's work.
supports · puzzle-solving · theories: Smoky's middle initial stands for nothing
