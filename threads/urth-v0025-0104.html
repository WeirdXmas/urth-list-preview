1999-04-19 Jonathan Laidlow #

Expresses pleasure that a UK publisher is reprinting Wolfe's fiction, noting that most of his works are out of print in Britain apart from The Book of the Long Sun. Asks whether a UK publisher has yet picked up The Book of the Short Sun, and points readers to his Ultan's Library website.
chats

Actually I'm just pleased that a UK publisher is reprinting Wolfe. Apart from the Book of the Long Sun most of Wolfe's works are out of print here. I think Castleview is still available, and possibly Pandora. The Book of the New Sun is readily available 2nd hand, but that's not the point. I wonder if a UK publisher has picked up Short Sun yet?

Jonathan
