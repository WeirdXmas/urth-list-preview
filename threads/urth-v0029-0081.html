The Haunted Boardinghouse vs. the Drinkwater house

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-06-26 to 2000-06-26

2000urthWilliam H. Ansley

Themes: Buildings, cities and labyrinths

2000-06-26 William H. Ansley #

Argues that the haunted boardinghouse in The Haunted Boardinghouse resembles the Drinkwater house in Little, Big only loosely, since the boardinghouse is a normal house with a different style on each side while the Drinkwater house is five interpenetrating houses folded together by design. Responds to Adam Stephanides's post on the house's four faces and architectural styles, offering the differences as a correction to the comparison.
disputes · close-textual-reading · genre-and-craft

Although I agree that the house in THB is supposed to recall the house in LB, there are some differences. The haunted boardinghouse is a more or less normal house (architecturally speaking, although it has had various additions/subtractions over time) except that each side is a front, each in a different architectural/historical style.

The Drinkwater house has five different fronts and is actually five different, interpenetrating houses, somehow folded together. And it was designed that way deliberately, from the beginning.

William Ansley
