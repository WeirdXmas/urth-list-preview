Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2013-12-20 Marc Aramini #

Proposes a five-year shift to the timeline of Peace, arguing from Dr. Van Ness's scene and Sherry Gold's possible pregnancy that Weer's birth falls around 1918 rather than earlier. Supports the idea with Weer's car purchase, the factory job and the cold house prank, and raises the problem that the timing of Dorris's travelling show clashes with her being his daughter.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Weer born around 1918 rather than earlier; Dorris is Weer's daughter

weer gets his first car in college, which could jive with Olivia's death. The women forge the Indian treaty in 1923 or so.
