Marbles hands and Oz magic

Urth list · 3 messages · 2002-11-26 to 2002-11-26

2002urthThe Book of the Long SunJames WynnRoy C. Lackey
The thread: Readers debated how Marble in Book of the Long Sun can have two hands when one was inherited from Rose, a bony blue-veined hand. James Wynn argued the hands could not both come from Rose and suggested the gods' possession might be unlimited, citing Marble's talk with Blood about Rose's visits and Musk's sacrifice. Roy C. Lackey held that Marble received both hands from Rose, added at different times, and cited Marble snapping new fingers to open the attic door. Wynn disputed Roy's claim that the hands came from a dismantled chem and Echidna's involvement, and turned the onus back on Roy to explain the hands' origin and Moly's fate. The thread ends in impasse, with each side still contesting the origin of the hands.
  • James Wynn: the two hands cannot both be Rose's inherited hand; the gods' possession may be unlimited
  • Roy C. Lackey: Marble received both hands from Rose, added at different times, not from a dismantled chem
  • James Wynn: Rose's prosthetics were never made for bios; Marble's snapped fingers and Silk's talk with Horn settle it
  • Roy C. Lackey: Echidna was behind the hands' appearance

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Gods, angels and the Increate, Memory and forgetting

2002-11-26 James Wynn #

Argues that Marble's two hands in Book of the Long Sun cannot both be the bony blue-veined hand inherited from Rose, and asks for other explanations of the discrepancy. Cites Marble's conversation with Blood about Rose's visits and the sacrifice of Musk, suggesting the gods' possession may be unlimited.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Marble inherited only one hand from Rose

Ah. [the quote] from when Marble was killing Musk, LS3: 3. "Her hands, the bony
blue-veined hands of an elderly bio, glowed crimson in the flames."

As for the rest . . . I'll save it for another day. But do you really think that Hammerstone fell in 'love' with a chem "girl" he described as "pretty as a picture", who had hands that looked like the bony blue-veined hands of an elderly bio?

Crush responds:
Whenever is fine. :)

In answer to your question, no, I don't believe Moly had hands like that. I don't believe any chem had hands like that. That's what is troubling, because as you surely remember, Marble only inherited ONE hand (by my reading) from Rose. Remember Marble's conversation with Blood at the end of LS3? Talking about the times Rose would come and visit him as a child, he notes that (quoting from memory) "One time you came with a hand like that one." Then Marble notes "it *was* this one."

So now we're left with the question of how *both* Marble's hands looked that way during the sacrifice of Musk. As you know, I've been inclined to believe that there were limits to what the gods could do with their possessed hosts. The passage you've quoted *seems* to suggest that there is very little they can't do. If Echidna could so dramatically transform Marble's metal flesh to exhibit a more Echidna-like appearance, what can't they do?

At this time (and I don't have the books with me) this sounds Wolfean "Oz Magic" as it seems to be called around here. I'd like to hear ANY other opinion to explain Marble's hands.

--- Crush

2002-11-26 Roy C. Lackey #

Argues that Marble received both of her hands from Rose, not one, and that Rose's prosthetics were added at different times rather than all at once. Answers Crush's claim that Marble had only one hand from Rose and that her hands came from a dismantled chem, citing the Long Sun passage where Marble snaps her new fingers to open the attic door.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Marble inherited both hands from Rose; Marble's hands were not from a dismantled chem; Echidna possession did not produce the hands

Marble did get both her hands from Rose. Rose didn't receive all her prosthetics at the same time, that's all; she would have scared the hell out of that boy if she had. Much of her face was prosthetic, both arms and both legs, her "ears", one eye, and a host of internal organs. Before Marble got the new hands, her old ones had lost most of their synthetic bio veneer; the veneer had worn through from constant work over the course of three-hundred-plus years.

Now that you've acknowledged that you "don't believe any chem had hands like that", that plays hell with your theory that they originally came from a dismantled chem. Either Rose had them made to look that way, or that was the best she could get when she needed them. Echidna had nothing to do with the hands. They were not an illusion produced during possession. Marble "snapped her new fingers" when she was deciding how to get the attic door down, followed by more references to her new fingers (LS3: 5). She couldn't snap her old ones because they were worn down to the bare metal.

-Roy

2002-11-26 James Wynn #

Disputes Roy's theory that Marble's hands came from a dismantled chem, arguing the text shows Rose's prosthetics were never made for bios and that Marble's snapped fingers and Silk's talk with Horn settle the matter. Responds to Roy's claim that Echidna was behind the hands' appearance, and turns the onus back on Roy to explain the hands' origin and Moly's fate.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Marble's hands came from a dismantled chem; Echidna caused the hands' appearance by illusion

Now that you've acknowledged that you "don't believe any chem had hands like that", that plays hell with your theory that they originally came from a dismantled chem. Either Rose had them made to look that way, or that was the best she could get when she needed them. Echidna had nothing to do with the hands. They were not an illusion produced during possession. Marble "snapped her new fingers" when she was deciding how to get the attic door down, followed by more references to her new fingers (LS3: 5). She couldn't snap her old ones because they were worn down to the bare metal.

Crush responds:
Raise one hand and one hand only. Now tell me how you snap your "FINGER" (singular). <g>

There is no doubt that the parts came from a dismantled chem. Silk's conversation with Horn in LS2:1 proves beyond argument that Rose's prosthetics were not made originally for bios. Silk says quite clearly that (1)such technology was left behind on Urth and (2)while Xiphias' leg was such an artifact, Rose's definitely were not. The onus is on you to explain where they came from if not from a chem?

Marble (impersonating Moly) in LS3:10 says she had been at the Sun St. cenoby since before humans came to Viron. The onus is on you to explain what happened to Moly.

Maybe you can show why anyone would create "bony blue-veined" prosthetic hands for ***anyone***, but I'm afraid I find the concept absurd.

I've explained why the hands looked like that during Musk's sacrifice. I don't like the lack of rational explanation FOR the explanation -- I'm not sure "illusion" is the right word -- but I can't come up with any other explanation. If you want to believe Marble inherited TWO hands and that the Chapter had **altered** the hands in order to promote humility in Rose, have at it. I like that explanation a great deal less. But the text places us beyond believing that they were prosthetics originally created for a bio.

--- Crush
