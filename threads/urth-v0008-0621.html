Kimleesoong and the Dating of the Autarchy

Urth list · 3 messages · 1998-03-12 to 1998-03-13

1998urthThe Book of the New SunDan ParmenterC R Culver
The thread: C R Culver proposed that Kimleesoong, the Korean-named prisoner Jonas mentions in the House Absolute antechamber, was a sailor like Jonas who returned to Urth millions of years later, citing Jonas's Korean babbling and the absence of modern-sounding names. Dan Parmenter disputed how far the Autarchy's dating can be taken from that remark, suggesting the seventh-generation descent claim may be Commonwealth lore rather than literal chronology. Culver replied that the claim may come from the prisoners themselves, that earlier generations made it too, and that a name surviving from the present would be staggering and improbable. The posts end with the disagreement unresolved.
  • C R Culver: Kimleesoong was a sailor like Jonas who returned to Urth millions of years later, placing him in the Monarchy or Autarchy era
  • Dan Parmenter: the seventh-generation descent claim may be lore, so the Autarchy's dating cannot be read from it

Themes: Deep time and ruins, Time loops and causality

1998-03-12 C R Culver #

Proposes that Kimleesoong, the Korean-named prisoner Jonas mentions in the House Absolute antechamber, was a sailor like Jonas from a few centuries after our time who returned to Urth millions of years later. Grounds this on Jonas babbling in Korean in the antechamber and on the absence of modern-sounding names in the Urth of the New Sun, while weighing a time-corridor explanation and rejecting a near-future Urth.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Kimleesoong was a sailor from our time; Jonas and Kimleesoong are similarly displaced sailors; Antechamber connected to the Corridors of Time

Fellow Urthers:

There has often been talk on this list of how far in the future the Commonwealth of Severian's time is. I was perplexed, however, at the personage of Kimleesoong (Kim Lee Soong) that Jonas mentions in the antechamber of the House Absolute. Jonas says that he is seven generations removed from the current generation. Unless the prisoners there were extremely long-lived, this would place him after the reign of Ymar and Typhon. We saw in UotNS that the days of Ymar and Typhon are far removed from ours, and there are no names reminescent of modern times like the obviously Korean name of Kim Lee Soong. (Jonas also mentions that Korea has been under the ocean for ages). How do we solve this problem, where a man from our time is present in a future perhaps 1 million years away? Here are my hypotheses:

1) the Antechamber is somehow connected to the Corridors of Time, and our Korean man stumbled upon them. This is possible, but I am not sure it happened.

2) Kim Lee Soong was a sailor just a Jonas was, from a few decades or centuries after our time, who came back to Urth millions of years after his time, and was then imprisoned. Indeed, Jonas appears to be in much the same circumstances, considering that, feverish, he babbled in Korean in the antechamber (Korean is a nasal, monosyllabic language. I am sure it was Korean and not Chinese, because Severian does not speak of tones). Strangely, this is the only place I can find where Jonas is culturally identified. Severian never seems to remark on any Asiatic features with Jonas, however. This is the possibility I most strongly support.

3) The Urth of the Autarchy is not so far in the future as we suspect, a possibility I will, however, reject.

What do you all think?
