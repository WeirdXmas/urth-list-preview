CAVE CANEM now available

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-28 to 1998-05-28

1998urthRobert Borski

1998-05-28 Robert Borski #

Announces the availability of Cave Canem, a guide to characters and placenames in The Fifth Head of Cerberus with 115 entries modeled on Lexicon Urthus, and offers it free to list members as a token of appreciation. Notes the work is incomplete, will add essays, timelines, maps and family trees, and invites feedback and discussion of controversial entries.
informs

Fellow Lycophiles:

I'm happy to announce the availability of CAVE CANEM: A Guide to the Characters and Placenames of Gene Wolfe's THE FIFTH HEAD OF CERBERUS.

It consists of 115 entries organized in much the same way as mantis' LEXICON URTHUS (whose format I shamelessly stole), and while the completed work will contain supplemental essays, timelines, maps, graphics and family trees, I'd like to offer what I now have as a token of my appreciation to the members of this list. Plus I could use the feedback. We could even discuss some of its more controversial entries here.

At any rate you need to be able to receive attachments with your e-mail. CANEM is in .doc format and 71Kb. Sorry, but at the moment, I'm not offering printed, mailable copies, although if you're desperate I might consider a floppy exchange.

Just drop me a line via e-mail and let me know your interested.

Robert Borski
