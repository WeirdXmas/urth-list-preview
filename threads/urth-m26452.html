2015-04-29 Charles Gillingham #

Argues that Vinge's novels promote anarcho-capitalist ideas, calling A Deepness in the Sky the best libertarian novel ever. Responds to an earlier post calling Vinge right-wing by saying private security and military firms recur in his books, a political point with no Wolfe content.
chats

Vinge is anarcho capitalist: the state will dissolve when private companies provide the same services cheaper. Almost all his novels include some sort of private security or private military. A Deepness in the Sky is, in my opinion, the single best libertarian novel ever.

2015-04-29 Norwood, Frederick Hudson replying to Charles Gillingham #

Disputes Charles Gillingham's claim that Vernor Vinge is right-wing, saying Vinge is not right-wing in the sense of wanting government-mandated school prayer. Says the poster enjoyed A Deepness in the Sky for its images and characters and did not notice its politics, so the post is off-topic to Wolfe's fiction.
disputes

Thanks for the info, but I loved A Deepness in the Sky for the images and characters, and didn?t even notice the politics. In any case, Vinge Is not right-wing in the sense of wanting the government to require prayer in the public schools.

Rick
