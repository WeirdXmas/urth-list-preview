Yesod's Scarab Machine

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

2000-01-09 James Jordan #

Answers Christopher R. Culver's question about the scarab machine in Yesod by explaining the Egyptian scarab beetle rolling a dung ball that represents the sun. Argues that in the machine the scarabs move the stars in their courses, as the Great Scarab pushes the sun along.
answers · source-and-allusion · theories: the scarab machine moves the stars in their courses

Did this ever get answered? In ancient Egypt, the scarab or dung-beetle pushes along a ball of dung, which symbolizes the sun. The Great Scarab pushes the sun along. Thus, in the great machine, the scarabs are moving the stars in their courses.
