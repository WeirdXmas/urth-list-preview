Quotes a dictionary definition of "scolex" and notes that capitalization is optional on the list, explaining that the poster and Mantis wrote in lowercase out of loyalty and pretension. Responds to a joke post by scolex and calls the exchange a good-natured squabble.

scolex,

That's pretty funny. For those of you who don't have a dictionary at hand (maybe I shouldn't assume anyone needs one, but I did), here's the def: "The larva or embryo produced directly from the egg in metagenesis; esp. the larva or head of a tapeworm or other parasitic worm."

You don't have to be lowercase, you know. Mantis did it to be pretentious, and I did it out of loyalty to him (because we'd started off together before we got on the list) but lots don't.

And no matter what some of you may have thought, this has been a good-natured squabble.

-alga-
