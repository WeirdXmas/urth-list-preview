Long Sun Laughs

Urth list · 3 messages · 2009-01-21 to 2009-01-21

2009urthThe Book of the Long SunO'Donnell, Tim (BOSI)Dan'l Danehy-OakesSon of Witz
The thread: Readers asked whether Mucor's mind-projection power links to Typhon's mental nets in Long Sun. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argued Mucor's body was meant for one of Typhon's children, calling it obvious without proof. Tim O'Donnell said he had noticed a possible link but could not prove it, and his post was mostly a corporate email disclaimer. Son of Witz offered a flipside theory, with the mainframe beaming gods' consciousness into people and Mucor's power possibly evolved or engineered, while conceding Quetzal could have betrayed the inhumu. The thread ended with three posts and no real resolution.
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Mucor's body was intended for one of Typhon's children to inhabit
  • Tim O'Donnell: a link between Mucor's and Typhon's mental powers is possible but unproven
  • Son of Witz: Mucor's power and the gods' possession of humans are flipsides, possibly evolved or engineered

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Robots, androids and artificial life, The dying sun and the cosmos

2009-01-21 Son of Witz replying to O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI) #

Proposes that Mucor and the gods' possession of humans are flipsides, with the mainframe beaming gods' consciousness into people and Mucor's ability possibly evolved or engineered. Responds to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's suggestion that Mucor's body was meant for one of Typhon's children, and concedes that Quetzal could have betrayed the inhumu.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Mucor and god possession are flipside phenomena; Mainframe engineered consciousness override in Mucor; Quetzal could have betrayed the inhumu

That's an interesting idea, a body for the gods to inhabit.

I can't help but feel that Mucor and the Possessions by Gods are flipsides of the same thing. How does mainframe beam the "Gods" consciousness into the humans? It strikes me that if humans have the potential for this sort of consciousness override, and a machine has the ability to possess them, then that ability could have evolved, or been engineered into someone like Mucor.

and Yeah, Quetzal could have decided to betray the inhumu, fair enough.

~witz
