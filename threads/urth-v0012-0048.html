Proposes that VRT, the supposed human father of RT, may not be human, a view the poster says Mantis has persuaded him of. Replies to Adam Louis Stephanides's summary of the aboriginal-human hybridization problem, adding this point and teasing him with a joke about Superman and Lois Lane.
