1999-11-08 Ouroboros The Worm #

Admits to a slip in two earlier posts where the poster called Nessus a river rather than a centaur ferryman, joking that a perfect memory should have prevented repeating the error. The post is a self-deprecating correction, with the mix-up with Gyoll as its only grounding, and it is responding to nothing specific beyond the poster's own earlier messages.
chats

Re-reading my last two postings, I must admit that I
have a mental fixation with Nessus as a "river". I
can't understand why. Nessus was a centar ferryman,
not a river. Considering my incorruptible memory, it
makes no sense that I would *twice* mis-write "Nessus"
for "Gyoll"....
;-)

-Ouroboros
