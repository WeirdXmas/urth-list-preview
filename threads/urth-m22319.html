2012-04-27 Lee Berman #

Expresses agreement that the preceding comparison of Severian with Marcus Aurelius is a good summary of how Wolfe presents humanity, God and the cosmos in the New Sun series. Offers no new argument, only general approval of the quoted analysis of Severian as a first-person experiment inspired by Roman memoirs.

2012-04-27 David Stockhoff replying to Lee Berman #

Points to several phrases from Marcus Aurelius, including the Criterion, the Test of Truth, and the ideal wise man sufficient unto himself, as echoes in Wolfe's presentation of man, God and the Sun. Supports Lee Berman's comparison by quoting passages about the wise man who trods his predecessors' path and does not expect others to read his writing.
supports · source-and-allusion

I thought so.

That, and notice the Criterion, the Test of Truth, and the "ideal Wise Man, sufficient unto himself." "He trod the path beaten by his predecessors. . . ." "Vulgar vices seem to have no temptation for him. . . ."

And of course that "he does not even contemplate that others should read what he writes. . . . "
