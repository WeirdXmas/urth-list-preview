VRT as half-abo

Urth list · 4 messages · 2011-01-07 to 2011-01-09

2011urthAntónio Pedro MarquesTony EllisGerry QuinnLee Berman
The thread: Tony Ellis argued that VRT is only half aboriginal, reading his mother's limited face-muscle control and voice mimicry as evidence that the Shadow Children amplify thought rather than truly shapechange. Gerry Quinn largely agreed, citing green eyes as a mark of Sainte Anne abos but concluding the evidence cannot settle whether VRT is half-abo or simply educated. António Pedro Marques added only a quoted question about what motivates the abos and inhumi to climb toward human refinement. Lee Berman then contrasted the inhumi, who keep fangs and claws and rely on optical illusion, with the abos, building on that earlier contrast. The thread ended without resolution, mostly as supportive agreement with the original reading.
  • Tony Ellis: VRT is only half aboriginal; his mother's shapechanging is limited, so the Shadow Children amplify thought
  • Gerry Quinn: agrees with Ellis, notes green eyes as a Sainte Anne abo mark, but says evidence is insufficient to decide
  • António Pedro Marques: asks what motivates the abos and inhumi to climb toward human refinement, offering no argument
  • Lee Berman: inhumi keep fangs and claws and use optical illusion, unlike the abos, though both are happy becoming human

Themes: Beasts and the animal in us, Robots, androids and artificial life, Fathers, mothers and children

2011-01-07 Tony Ellis #

Argues that VRT is only half aboriginal, reading his mother's face-muscle control and voice mimicry as limited abilities rather than true shapechanging, which suggests the Shadow Children amplify thought. Grounds this in VRT's cell narration about his mother, the dream of her buttoned into her dress, Trenchard's remarks about her uselessness, and VRT's own statements that he is half Annese and part animal.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: VRT is only half aboriginal; Shapechanging is limited face and voice control; Shadow Children amplify thoughts of the ancient humans

In his cell VRT writes that he remembers his mother making herself unattractive when his father came home drunk, using "the muscles of her face." He adds "I have the same ability, though not to the extent she did."

A page or so later he has a dream memory of her 'buttoned into her dress' by his father. Whereas he can surely dress himself.

Trenchard tells Marsch that his wife was useless and "She could not even cook!" Marsch says VRT is a great camp cook - but adds that he is constantly burning his fingers in the fire.

I don't think shapechanging *is* an ability of the ancient humans (or human-looking aliens if such they be). I don't see any evidence of that, and it creates a lot of problems. I think maybe they can do voices and control the muscles of their faces, something both Trenchards say the mother can do. I'm not even sure it's that. I wonder if maybe they just think themselves different and the Shadow Children send back this thought "greater, greater, greater" than before.

Which again suggests VRT is only half-abo. His mother stole without compunction, "if she had eaten she wanted nothing", and by VRT's own account she "knew nothing" either. She wasn't equipped to integrate with human society beyond a base level.

Hmm, not that I can recall. He certainly thinks he is half Annese. "At least half of me is animal," he says at one point. "The Free People are wonderful... but they are animals." There are lots of other utterances like that.

He doesn't like his father, however, so I think he would have disowned him if he could.

2011-01-09 Lee Berman replying to Tony Ellis #

Argues that the inhumi in V.R.T. differ from the abos in that they keep fangs and claws and rely on optical illusion, yet they are happy when dream-travelling and becoming fully human. Builds on Antonio Pedro Marques's contrast between the inhumi, who imitate humans at their highest refinement, and the abos, who only gradually climb toward humanity.
supports · close-textual-reading

I think this is a good observation. Inhumi have less shape-shifting ability than abos. They retain fangs and claws and must use optical illusion to complete their imitation of humans. But they seem extremely happy when they dream-travel and become fully human. They don't want to go back home and to their original form.
