Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

2000-04-07 David Wells #

Proposes that Silk's body may be one of several clones grown from a special embryo, so Horn might have entered another clone and so not be really Silk. Also suggests Typhon's engineers may have prepared multiple receptacles for Pas, asking whether Silk's embryos were clones of the original Typhon body.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Silk's embryo was one of many clones; Horn may have entered another Silk clone; Typhon prepared receptacles for Pas

From: Adam Stephanides <[email]>

As has been noted before, "Silk's body" may be ambiguous.

It seems very likely to me that the "special embryo" from which Silk was grown was one of many clones(*). Maybe Horn has somehow gotten into one of these. In which case, is he "really" Silk? (or really "Silk"? Or even "really Silk"?).

Strikes me as exactly the kind of thing which Wolfe might be interested in writing about..?

(old) newt

(*) And by the by, it also seems likely to me (as has also been discussed before) that Silk's embryo(s) were custom-designed as a receptacle for Pas/Typhon when the Plan was carried out and Mainframe was being evacuated along with the Whorl.
In fact, if Typhon was sensible, he would have had his genetic engineers cook up a bunch of possible receptacles so that he could choose one "off the peg", according to his divine whim, on arrival at Green/Blue. ...unless Silk's embryo(s) are clones of the _original_ Typhon body..? Has this been discussed?
(Sorry, slightly lengthy footnote there :-)
