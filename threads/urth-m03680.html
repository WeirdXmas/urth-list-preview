Social Bookmarking Group on Ma.gnolia.com

2007-12-28 Ryan Buller #

Announces a new social bookmarking group on Ma.gnolia.com called Hoof & Hide, meant to gather scattered online resources about Gene Wolfe into one searchable collection. Explains that the poster found Wolfe material buried across interviews, reviews and fansites, and describes how links are submitted and approved.
informs

I wanted to take a moment to let everyone know that I started a social bookmarking group on Ma.gnolia.com for collecting and organizing online resources on Gene Wolfe. Basically my goal with this is to create a gateway to all the information out there on the web about Gene. Two years ago when I first started researching him myself I found information to be scattered and buried all over the place. From little interviews and books reviews to fansites full of lost essays and ideas. I was thinking of how great it would be if all of these links were brought together for easy searching and reference and decided to take on the task myself. I call the project "Hoof & Hide" and here is the link to the group.

http://ma.gnolia.com/groups/HoofandHide

Ma.gnolia.com is free to join and it's easy to submit a link. So if you are interested please feel free to contribute. Please note that all submitted links do go through a short approval process but this is to keep spammers and junk links out of the group. Also whether you contribute or join Ma.gnolia.com or not the list of bookmarks is open for everyone to search and use. Please enjoy and thanks for your time!

Ryan B
