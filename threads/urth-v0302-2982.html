Wildside book on John Crowley: publication imminent

Urth list · 3 messages · 2003-02-28 to 2003-03-07

2003urthNicholas GeversAlice TurnerAdam Stephanides
The thread: Nicholas Gevers announced the imminent print-on-demand publication of a critical study of John Crowley's fiction edited by Alice K. Turner and Michael Andre-Driussi from Wildside Press, listing its contents and contributors. Alice K. Turner replied with thanks to the list and to Adam Stephanides for research and copyediting, saying the book would have been pale without the list's contributions. Adam Stephanides answered briefly, congratulating her on publication. The thread is purely informational and social, with no debate about Wolfe's work and no real impasse.
  • Nicholas Gevers: announces the Wildside Press Crowley study and its contents
  • Alice K. Turner: credits the list and Adam Stephanides for the book's help
  • Adam Stephanides: thanks Turner and congratulates her on the book

2003-02-28 Nicholas Gevers #

Announces the imminent publication of a book-length critical study of John Crowley's fiction, edited by Alice K. Turner and Michael Andre-Driussi, from Wildside Press with a listed price and contents. Gives publisher, print-on-demand format, booksellers, and a forthcoming trade paperback, and lists the essays and essayists in the collection.
informs

Knowing the interest people on this list have in John
Crowley, I'd like to make the following announcement
of imminent publication of the first ever book-length
critical study of John Crowley, edited by our very own
Alice "alga" Turner and Michael "mantis"
Andre-Driussi. They have enormously expanded their
earlier chapbook, also called SNAKE'S-HANDS: THE
FICTION OF JOHN CROWLEY.

Full details below. A few notes: the publisher is
Wildside Press, and this is a Print On Demand book.
It's available from amazon.com and
www.wildsidepress.com, as well as other online
booksellers. If the $49.95 pricetag sounds heinous,
there will be a trade paperback next year at around
$20.

--Nick Gevers.

Preface by Harold Bloom

Page Count: 408

April 1st, 2003

2003-02-28 Alice Turner #

Thanks the mailing list and Ranjit for keeping it going and names contributors to a forthcoming book on John Crowley. Credits Adam for research and copyediting, and says the book would have been pale without the list's contributions.
informs

Re Nick's announcement re the Crowley book: I'd like to wax sentimental for three seconds to say how grateful I am to this list and to Ranjit for keeping it going. (And I know I'm speaking for Michael too.) The book would have been pale and wan without contributions from this list. Thanks to William, Matthew, Graham and **especially** Adam, who not only did some astonishing research (nearly worth the *gulp* price on its own) but put in yeoman copyediting time.

-alga
