missing Kipling epigraph

2011-07-03 Jason H #

Asks why the Orb edition of The Book of the New Sun lacks the epigraph for The Citadel of the Autarch, which Wolfe said in Castle of Days he meant to use from a Kipling poem. Notes that the epigraph appears in at least one other edition and praises its fit with the earlier epigraph from Shadow.
questions · source-and-allusion · author-and-biography

My copy of Book of the New Sun (the two-volume Orb edition) contains epigraphs for the first three novels, but nothing for Citadel. In Castle of Days, Wolfe says that he intends to use part of a Kipling poem for the Citadel epigraph, and I've seen this in at least one edition. It works beautifully.

Any idea what happened with the Orb edition? Is this just a bad error? It's a real shame, because the Kipling quote works as well as Shadow's epigraph did ("A thousand ages in thy sight...").

-Jason

2011-07-04 Matthew Keeley replying to Jason H #

Reports that the Kipling epigraph is absent from the poster's Orb edition of the book but appears in the later Gollancz one-volume edition, and calls the omission an error. Responds to Jason H's earlier question about the missing epigraph by supplying an edition comparison as evidence.
informs

I think it's just an error. The epigraph is missing in my Orb edition, but present in the more recent Gollancz "Severian of the Guild" one-volume edition.

-Matt
