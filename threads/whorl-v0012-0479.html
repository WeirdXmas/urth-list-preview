Gene Wolfe's secret identity

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-03-22 to 2001-03-22

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunWilliam H. Ansley

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2001-03-22 William H. Ansley #

Jokes that an Amazon listing garbled Wolfe's name into a secret identity, comparing it to Batman's Bruce Wayne reveal and wondering what the F in his name stands for. The post reacts to a mangled Amazon 'customers also bought' entry that reads 'Gene Calde of the Long Sun Wolfe, Gene Exodus f Wolfe', treating it as a playful disclosure rather than a real finding.
chats

Just now, as I was purchasing a book from Amazon.com, I was presented with the usual "Customers who bought the items in your shopping cart also bought:" list and on it was the entry:

* Epiphany of the Long Sun by Gene Calde of the Long Sun Wolfe, Gene Exodus f Wolfe; Paperback

So, just as the comics revealed who Bruce ("Batman") Wayne was, we now know not only Mr. Wolfe's secret identity but his real name as well.

Now, if only Gene ("Calde of the Long Sun") Exodus F. Wolfe would tell us what the F stands for!

Or, if only Amazon would hire competent data entry personnel! 8->

William Ansley
