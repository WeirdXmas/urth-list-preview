I read Wolfe & Gaiman's _Walking Tour of the Shambles_ last night. I'd say it's fairly entertaining, and worth picking up for completists, but reminds that neither one of these fellows' primary strength is as a humorist. There's a little nod to Kelly Link in it, and the dedication is to Lafferty.
