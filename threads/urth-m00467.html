Proposes that the lander Silk takes at the end of Return to the Whorl is the same lander Horn repaired at the end of In Green's Jungles, with Chenille carrying it to Blue. Builds on the poster's earlier Chenille-on-Blue theory and notes the alternative would require Silk to find another working lander.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Chenille took Horn's repaired lander to Blue

one more thing about the chenille on blue theory: if Horn went back at the end of In Green's Jungles and repaired the lander with his body in it, and Chenille took it to Blue, then the lander that Silk takes at the end of Return to the Whorl could very well be that lander. Otherwise, we have to assume he finds another functional one.

Marc
