Themes: Names and their meanings, Unreliable narration

2000-06-27 Adam Stephanides #

Argues that the S in Evan S. Barnable is a pun: Smoky's "evanescence" in the nickname passage points to Evan-S-ence and possibly Evan-essence. Builds this on the quoted passage where Crowley explains the origin of the nickname, which the poster had missed on first reading. Responds to Allan Lloyd's suggestion that the S stands for evanesce, and admits this undercuts the poster's earlier view of the narrator as straightforward.
supports · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Evan S. Barnable's S stands for evanescence

Wait, it gets better. Crowley actually refers to Smoky's "evanescence" in the passage I quoted explaining the origin of the nickname, and I still didn't get it.

So Smoky's "evanescence" is also his Evan-S-ence. And possibly his Evan-essence as well. (I may have to take back what I just said about the narrator being straightforward.)

--Adam
