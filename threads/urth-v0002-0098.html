The thread: John Bishop asked whether anyone knew the title, author, or publisher of a graphic-novel adaptation of The Book of the New Sun, since he had found a reference but lacked ordering details. m.driussi answered that Innovation published an adaptation of The Shadow of the Torturer in 1991, gave the publisher's contact details, and suggested checking comic shops, while believing only three of six planned issues appeared. Jim Jordan added that Innovation serialized the book as a comic about six years earlier, with three issues produced, calling it faithful but disliking the artwork. The exchange was brief and factual, ending with the ordering question largely answered, though no source for copies was offered.

1997-06-18 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Informs the list that a graphic novel adaptation of The Shadow of the Torturer was published by Innovation in 1991 in six issues, with the poster believing only the first three appeared. Gives the publisher's address and phone number and suggests checking comic book stores for copies.
informs

John,

1997-06-19 James Jordan #

Informs the list that Innovation Comix serialized The Shadow of the Torturer as a comic about six years earlier, with only three issues produced. Responds to a request for graphic-novel ordering information by reporting the publisher, the faithful adaptation, and disliked artwork, and offers no source for copies.
informs

INNOVATION Comix started serializing "The Shadow of the Torturer" about six years ago. I got the first three. I don't think any more were ever done. This comic line also did some of Anne Rice's trash. I have no idea where you can obtain copies, except that I think I have five copies of the first two issues somewhere....
The serialization was faithful to the book, though I did not care for the artwork.
Maybe someone else knows more....

Nutria
