2001-01-04 Nicholas Gevers #

Urges readers who have finished Return to the Whorl to post favorable reviews of it on Amazon and barnesandnoble.com to counter a negative Publishers Weekly review. Notes that the only online review so far is the Publishers Weekly piece, which he calls a foolish denunciation posted on barnesandnoble.com.
proposes

So far, the only review of RTW online is that foolish
PUBLISHER'S WEEKLY denunciation, on
barnesandnoble.com. Can't people who have read RTW
already consider placing positive reviews on amazon
and bn.com, and help give the book a push?
