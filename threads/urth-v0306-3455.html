Seven Lions' Paste

Urth list · 3 messages · 2003-06-08 to 2003-06-09

2003urthMarc AraminiJosh GellerJames Wynn
The thread: A reader relayed a question from Crush about the white paste and vermilion powder carried by Seven Lions in Soldier of the Mist, suggesting a possible allusion to mythic clays used for skin colour. Josh Geller answered that the paste is war-paint Seven Lions applies before battle, citing Herodotus's list of peoples in the Great King's army. James Wynn reported that Crush conceded the point, hoping the paste related to Latro's appearance but accepting it as a Wolfean juxtaposition. The thread ended in agreement, with the war-paint explanation accepted.
  • maa32: Relayed Crush's question and suggested the paste might allude to mythic skin-colour clays
  • Josh Geller: The paste is war-paint Seven Lions applies before battle, per Herodotus
  • James Wynn: Reported Crush accepting the war-paint answer as a Wolfean juxtaposition
  • Crush: Hoped the paste related to Latro's appearance, then conceded to the war-paint reading

Themes: Myth and folklore sources, War and soldiers, Memory and forgetting

2003-06-08 Marc Aramini #

Relays a question from Crush about the white paste and vermilion powder carried in Seven Lions' bags in Soldier of the Mist, asking what the paste is. Suggests a possible allusion to mythic white and red clays used to make skin colours, and compares the bag-carrier to a figure in the film Gladiator.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: Seven Lions' paste alludes to clays used to make white and black skin

Here is a question from Crush about Seven Lions' Paste- perhaps you could help us out - I have only the vaguest conception that it reminds me of the scene in the movie Gladiator in which small household gods and religious artifacts are kept by a black man in a small bag.

Crush asks:

"I pointed to my arm and my hair and asked the black man if he had seen another such as I. He nodded and opened two little bags he carries; there is white paste in one and vermilion in the other."

What is this paste in Seven Lion's bags?

If you don't know, could you repost this question to the Urth list?

Perhaps some allusion to the different white and red clays that went into the creation of white and black skin in different proportions mythologically? I don't know.
Marc

2003-06-08 Josh Geller #

Explains that the paste on Seven Lions' body in Soldier of the Mist is war-paint he applies before battle, citing Herodotus's list of ethnic groups in the Great King's army. Responds to Maa32's relayed question from Crush about a chapter-one passage in which Latro asks the black man about his arm and hair.

2003-06-09 James Wynn #

Reports that a Latro-thread exchange ended with Crush conceding Josh Geller's answer that the paste Seven Lions wears is war-paint, a detail Herodotus mentions among the ethnic groups in the Great King's army. Quotes Crush's reply that he had hoped the paste related to Latro's appearance but accepts it as a Wolfean juxtaposition.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Seven Lions' paste is war-paint from Herodotus

Okay. Fixed my problem.

Crush asked:
What is this paste in Seven Lion's bags?

Josh Geller answered:
These are his war-paint. He puts them on before he goes into battle. Herodotos mentions this, where he is listing the different ethnic groups that were represented in the Great King's army.

Crush concedes:
I was afraid of that. It seemed to me that the paste should have something to do with whether Seven Lions had seen anyone one who looked like Latro. But Josh is probably right. It is only a Wolfean juxtaposition.

--- Crush
