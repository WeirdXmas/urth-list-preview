2000-12-14 shannon wilde #

Chats about receiving a copy of Jack Vance's The Dying Earth, mocking its cover and recalling the flying castle and mummified lady from the book. Responds to a list discussion of Vance and emoticons, with no argument about Wolfe's work.
chats

Hello, all. Yeah, my copy of _Dying Earth_ should be
here tomorrow, and mmhhhh, what a crappy cover it's
got. Almost makes the Wolfe covers look better...no.
No, it doesn't. Anyway, IIRC, no spaceships in there,
and the platform Paul remembers was whatsisname's
flying castle thing, you know, with the FTL incense
and the eons-mummified dame that Rhialto simply must
mack upon. All very deluxe, terrace gardens, and
such. I look forward to returning to this old, old
favorite, and hope my little niece is as geeked-out
and thrilled as I was when she discovers it. Oh, and
Blattid-the emoticons. Yes, apparently we must. XP.
