Inhumi's secret and numbers on Blue (was Re: (no subject))

Questions why inhumi appear so rarely on Blue and suggests Auk's encounters with them on Green may recast them as predators rather than stealthy thieves. Grounds the query in Horn's experiences on Blue, the warlords the Rajan meets, travel to the Whorl without spacecraft, and the inhumi's claimed flight between Green and Blue.
questions · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic

Those humans who prey on other humans generally rely on stealth and deception. </Lee>

The warlords the Rajan encounters following his return to Blue prey on other humans, without necessarily relying on stealth and deception. Horn's experiences on Blue might lead us to think of the inhumi as sneaky ninjas or stealthy pickpockets, but Auk's experiences with them on Green might lead to a different interpretation.

Of course characters in BotSS travel to the Whorl (Horn's walk in) and even as far as Nessus (inhuma included) without spacecraft. They don't take their bodies, though, and I thought the inhumi claimed to fly between Green and Blue, when the time was right, in a very literal sense--like Dr Doolittle's moth flying to the moon.

Are there so few inhumi on Blue that we should be looking for an explanation as to why there are so few inhumi on Blue?

Nate.
