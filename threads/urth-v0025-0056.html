1999-04-13 Peter Westlake #

Responds to Potto's suggestion that Agilus might be a hierodule by praising it as the first explanation that accounts for the ribbon, then objecting that Agia is human and Agilus's sister, so she would know. Questions what motives Agilus would have and notes he seems opposed to the other hierodules.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Agilus is a hierodule

This is a wonderful idea, and the first I've heard that offers any reasonable explanation of that ribbon. But Agia is human, and she is or plays Agilus' sister, so she would surely know about it. What would their motives be? Agilus definitely appears not to be on the same side as the other Hierodules.

SBear.
