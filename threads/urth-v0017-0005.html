Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Names and their meanings

1998-07-14 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that Tin Soldier and Jonas mirror each other as crippled men drawn to artificial, crippled women, with Jonas attracted to the waitress beneath Jolenta's mask. Supports Dan's point and adds that their fake names both begin with J, and recalls Ranjit's joke that Hethor lusts after Agia because she is so Korean.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Tin Soldier and Jonas are parallel crippled figures; Like attracts like: Jolenta's artificiality draws Jonas

Dan,

Right, that's the thing: Tin Soldier is crippled (as is Jonas); Tin Soldier is attracted to ballerina's beauty and her "crippled" nature (as Jonas is attracted to the Jolenta mask and, I agree with you, the crippled nameless waitress beneath that mask). (Then there's the tidbit that their fake names both start with letter J.)

Like attracts like: the artificial nature of Jolenta attracts the artificial nature of Jonas.

Or as Ranjit put it (and I paraphrase): Hethor lusts after Agia because she's so Korean. :)
