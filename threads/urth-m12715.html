Fifth Head of Cerberus surfaces again

Urth list · 3 messages · 2010-01-13 to 2010-01-13

2010urthGwern BranwenLane HaygoodMatthew Keeley
The thread: Three posts on 2010-01-13 passed around a Penny Arcade comic that jokes about giving The Fifth Head of Cerberus to a dog. Gwern Branwen noted the strip's Wikipedia link and said he would track page views to see if it drew new readers to Wolfe. Lane Haygood announced a planned blog column on Wolfe's influence on video games, starting from the comic's reference, and praised Tycho's game writing. Matthew Keeley added that Penny Arcade had mentioned The Book of the New Sun before and said the comic kept Wolfe in mind for him before he had read the books. The thread was brief and ended without disagreement.
  • Gwern Branwen: the comic may bring new readers, and he will check Wikipedia page views to test it
  • Lane Haygood: plans a blog column on Wolfe's influence on video games, starting from the Penny Arcade reference
  • Matthew Keeley: the comic has mentioned Wolfe before and kept him in mind before he read him

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2010-01-13 Gwern Branwen #

Shares a Penny Arcade comic that jokes about calling The Fifth Head of Cerberus a book to give a dog, and notes the strip links to Wikipedia. Estimates the site's traffic and says he will track the Wikipedia article's page views to see whether the strip brings many new readers to Wolfe.
informs

It's funny because the insult is 'dog'.

But it's also worth noting that the link is to Wikipedia. Since penny-arcade.com records a few hundred thousand hits a day ( http://www.statsaholic.com/penny-arcade.com ), odds are good that this is most of the readers' first exposure to Wolfe. It will be interesting to keep an eye on
http://stats.grok.se/en/201001/The_Fifth_Head_of_Cerberus and see just how much the article traffic spikes. If it doesn't go to at least a thousand, I will be disapointed :)
