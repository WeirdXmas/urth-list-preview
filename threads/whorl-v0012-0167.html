Themes: Unreliable narration

2001-02-09 Henry Kaiser #

Lists six questions about the Short Sun ending: who narrates the closing paragraphs and whether it is Daisy, Scylla or Wolfe, how the Whorl was readied so quickly, how many narrators the three books contain, whether the Godling is physically plausible, and whether Amazon censored a review. Raises these as discussion items for the list without offering a single answer, and notes the Oreb material as worth rereading.
