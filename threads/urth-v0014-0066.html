Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1998-06-22 John Moar #

Asks why Little Severian occupies so much of The Sword of the Lictor if he serves only as a plot function, and what the homunculus scene in The Citadel of the Autarch means. Notes the Encyclopaedia of Science Fiction reading that the homunculus is Severian's sibling, and says Merryn seems more likely based on earlier list discussion, as a newcomer's self-introduction.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Homunculus is Severian's sibling; Homunculus is Merryn
