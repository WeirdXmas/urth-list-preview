Themes: Unreliable narration

2002-04-22 Adam Stephanides #

Recommends Bruno Maddox's novel My Little Blue Dress as a non-SF book that plays Wolfe-like games with an unreliable narrator, more accessibly than Wolfe. Argues the book adds a third story the reader must reconstruct about halfway through, beyond the ostensible story and what really happened.
proposes · genre-and-craft

Another plug, for another non-sf book. This one plays Wolfe-like games with a thoroughly unreliable narrator, but more accessibly than Wolfe. In one sense it could be said to go beyond Wolfe: in addition to the ostensible story and the story of what really happened, there's a third "story" that you're cued to reconstruct about halfway through. And it's funny too. (Maddox once edited SPY magazine, but don't hold it against him.)

--Adam
