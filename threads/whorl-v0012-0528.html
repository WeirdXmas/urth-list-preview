Not Pas, but another god of the same name

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2001-04-03 Dave Lebling #

Argues against Michael Straight's view that the Narrator contains no Pas, reading Dan Parmenter's quoted line about presence as meaning that Pas is present because Silk is, since Pas has become partly Silk as Passilk. The post reasons from the quoted passage itself and from the Mainframe identity of Pas and Silk, without citing further scenes.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Pas is present in the Narrator because Silk is; Pas has merged into Passilk

<<From: Michael Straight <[email]>

If it weren't for the passage you quote, I would have said there is no Pas in the Narrator. Pig probably has some Pas because he's been posessed by the Mainframe version of Silk, which is Passilk. But the the human Silk was never posessed by Pas that we know of, so why would the Narrator have any Pas in him?>>

I take the passage quoted by Dan Parmenter oppositely.

It means that because "Pas" on the Whorl is now "Passilk", having Silk present means that Pas is also present, because Pas is at least partly Silk now.

-- Dave Lebling
