2001whorlThe Book of the Short SunAlice Turner

2001-04-07 Alice Turner #

Argues that Return to the Whorl reads as hasty throughout, with an unconvincing Jahlee episode and an editor who should have pushed for revisions, and rejects the list's attempts to excuse it. Responds to Endy's remark about the Jahlee women by saying the whole book irritated the poster, though others may disagree.
disputes · genre-and-craft

Thank you, Endy. I've been away; also I only just got a copy of the book (I read a galley earlier, which I no longer have), and taxes loom. But it wasn't just the hasty and unconvincing Jahlee episode, the whole book irritated me. This list has been struggling to find interpretations or excuses for the author but, frankly, I think his editor should have kicked his ass and made him tow the line. It's one thing when an author deliberately makes an odd choice--to put the climax of a book off-stage, for instance--and another when there is a hasty
let's-get-this-over-with-because-I'm-sick-of-it feeling to a whole book. And that is how RttW reads to me. I know that others don't feel the same way, but I'm sort of sick of it too, and that's why I'm lying low.

-alga
