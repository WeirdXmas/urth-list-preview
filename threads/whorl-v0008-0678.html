1998-07-28 mary whalen #

Continues a list of Wolfe's names from The Book of the New Sun, giving dictionary senses and real-world referents for names such as Bittersweet, Blood, Bluebell, Bongo, Bream, Brocket, Bull and Bustard. Each entry ties the name to a character's role, such as Blood as a Viron crimelord or Bream as a thief who sacrifices to Kypris.
informs · source-and-allusion

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Continuing the name list:

Bittersweet. The poisonous plant in Eurasia (Solanum dulcamara). A weed with purple flowers and red berries. It has a bittersweet taste. This is a member of the black mechanics, and apparently female.

Blood. Blood is a saline fluid in the veins and arteries of animals. It contains red and white blood cells. It is red when oxygenated and purple when not. It allows a person to breathe. It is saltwater, such as what a pike swims through, that is the color of roses (this is a stretch). Blood, the crimelord, has a red face (you can see his blood). Blood runs the criminal network in Viron, like a network of veins and arteries. Blood is an uncommon name, thought to be unlucky because of its sinister connotations. Blood is also fairly sinister and unpleasant. Blood and Musk are the only men named after bodily fluids.

Bluebell. Any flower with a bell-like blue flower, often in genus Campanula. Also the wood or grape Hyacinth. She's a prostitue like Hyacinth and Bellflower.

Bongo. A species of large forest-dwelling antelopes from Africa, either Tragelaphus eurycerus, Tragelaphus angasi, or Tragelaphus isaaci. Reddish brown or chestnut brown in color with white stripes. Also a timber tree (Bombacaceae Cavanillesia platanifolia). Of course, it could also an American canoe (also called a bungo), a Trinidadian dance, or even a kind of drum. This is the name of the baboon that isn't sacrificed.

Bream. The name for many different species of fish, including a cyprinid (Abramis brama). The thief with this name provides a sacrifice for Kypris, whose name is related to cyprinid (they're both from Cyprus).

Brocket. A South American deer of genus Mazama. Small with unbranching horns. Also a male red deer two years old. Mint's cousin.

Bull. The male of many species, usually a male cow. Quetzal's prothonotary.

Bustard. A member of family Otididae. Birds that mainly walk on the ground rather than flying, connecting him with auks. Bustard is Auk's older brother.
