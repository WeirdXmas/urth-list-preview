Isid Iooo IoooE , duh!

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-12-20 to 1999-12-20

1999urthMitch Bailey

1999-12-20 Mitch Bailey #

Corrects a previous post's arithmetic on the hex-number decoding of the Isid Iooo IoooE puzzle, saying the numbers should be two 32-bit and one 48-bit rather than two 64-bit and one 128-bit. Quotes the earlier suggestion and jokingly recalls a Wolfe character who read "M*MILLION" as Monsieur Million, which is a self-deprecating aside rather than a substantive reading.

"Then you could read the whole assemblage as two 64-bit hex numbers and = one 128-bit hex number:
=20
151D, 1000, 1000E
=20
Now, what their significance might be, I still couldn't say, but maybe = this is a step in the right direction."=20

DUH!

I meant, of course, two 32-bit numbers and a 48-bit number. Where do you = ever run across 48 bit numbers in computer science?

This of course comes to us from the man who had a character spend his = entire childhood reading "M*MILLION" as Monsieur Million.
