2014-04-30 Tony Ellis #

Proposes that Jeremy's line about five thousand years of murder refers to the traditional start of recorded history around 3000 BC, when writing appeared. Argues that Jeremy means scribes and scholars have been victims for as long as their class has existed, answering Marc Aramini's puzzle over the quoted statement.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Jeremy's five thousand years refers to recorded history

3000BC is the rough date that is often given to the expression 'the beginning of recorded history', so perhaps that was what he meant. But *why* is that date given? Because it's roughly when writing appeared, hence 'recorded' history. So I think Jeremy means that people like him - the scribes, the scholars - have been the victims for as long as their class has existed.
