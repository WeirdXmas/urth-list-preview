1997-06-26 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Announces a rule for a question-collecting session: no questions accepted until a last-minute deadline set by the poster, with a first question from alga queued up. Jokingly references a train metaphor and a possible late entry from Wesley Parsons' automated reply. Purely administrative and social; no reading of Wolfe's books is offered.

Ah, my mistake--which is why I will henceforth stick to my guns and not allow =any= first train questioneers until the mad dash in real time at the last second as determined by myself and my whim.

What the hey--it is Wednesday already. And I wanna send it out on Friday morning. Okay, we'll throw open the cattle car doors for Thursday, 26 Jun 1997, GMT. And the first one at 00:02:42 is alga, with her question re: what she just said.
