2002-05-08 Adam Stephanides #

Argues that Wolfe's Annese may be mythologized rather than true shapeshifters, drawing a parallel with the Pygmies in Marsch's notes. Responds to Alice K. Turner's remark that Wolfe draws on fairy tradition, citing the explicit reference in Marsch's notes at page 157 of the Ace paperback.
disputes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Annese are mythologized rather than shapeshifters

True; in fact, he does so explicitly, in Marsch's notes (157, Ace pb). But that doesn't necessarily mean that the Annese can actually shapeshift. It could indicate that, just as the "paleolithic, Caucasoid Pygmies" on Earth (157) had been mythologized into shapeshifters, the same mythologization had taken place with the Annese.

--Adam
