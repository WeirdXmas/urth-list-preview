1999-03-16 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Connects the quoted phrase about a madman among tombs howling that the sun would die to the damaged monitor in Mamelta's lander, which howled among tombs until repaired. Offers a joking reading of the monitor's noise as a child asking "are we there yet?" in reply to Peter Grimes's post, with a note that the thread belongs to the Long Sun discussion.

This made me think of the damaged monitor in Moneta's-- I mean Apheta's-- I mean Mamelta's lander. It was certainly howling among tombs until M. repaired it. I thought it was howling "are we there yet? are we there yet?"
