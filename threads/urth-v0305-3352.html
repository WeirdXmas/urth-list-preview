2003-05-06 Joshuafalchion #

Doubts that Wolfe intends a link between the Oracle of Changes (the I Ching) and the Outsider, which he reads as a Christian-Muslim take on the Supreme Being or Increate. Admits he is no expert on the I Ching and so leaves open that a connection could be implied.
disputes · theology-and-religion

I doubt if Wolfe would see much of a connection between the Oracle of Changes (the I Ching, I presume) and the Outsider, who seems a fairly clear Christian/Moslem take on the Supreme Being or Increate. But who knows ... I am no expert on the subject of the I Ching, and a connection could have been implied (although where I am not certain.)

Josh the Falchion

2003-05-06 Che Monro #

Asks whether the Outsider of The Book of the Long Sun might be the same as the outside event in Iain M. Banks's The Excession, after casting an I Ching reading (Hexagram 28, Great Excess) about the spiritual state of the Oracle of Changes. Grounds the question in the hexagram's text on the cycle of life, going down, and something coming from beyond the Great Pass, and invites interpretation.
questions · theories: The Outsider and the Oracle are one and the same

Wise Ones,

In my simple curiosity I sought to enqire of the Oracle of Changes about=20 it's own existance and spirituality. I used this oracle:

http://www.mdani.demon.co.uk/para/iching2.htm

to ask the question:

Are you well? Are you happy? I would like to know about the sprititual=20 state of the Oracle of Changes.

To which I received the reply of Hexagram 28 or Great Excess, with no=20 changing lines and thus no second hexagram.

~*~

Beyond the great pass

Crossing the great pass: the ridgepole is sagging
Harvest: to proceed probing Expansion

The great image says:

The marsh submerges the tree: Great excess
The noble one stands alone without fear, leads a life of recluse without=20 melancholy

Life is a cycle, it is circular. It begins, it grows, and it goes down=20 again. To make life a straight road to a distant future is a desperate=20 attempt of human mind to make it never-ending. It will not help him =96=20 inevitably he will go down some day.

But if man accepts going down as a substantial, important and good part of= =20
life, not as a decline but as a indispensable phase, then he makes his=20 living days twice as many.

Do not live as an old man or an old woman, live as a grand old man, a grand= =20
old lady. Without regret or fear, but with the riches of wisdom and= experience.

This applies to everything, to enterprises, feelings, popularity. In=20 everything all phases are important.

~*~

It seems to me that the oracle is saying that it is beyond the completion=20 of the cycle, that it is old beyond age (and young beyond youth?). It=20 exceeds all expectations, and yet at the same time, it is a broken stick=20 (yarrow stalk?). It is a contradiction, a mystery, an exception to the=20 rules. It is beyond the Great Pass. It comes from the other side.

This description reminded me of two things.

1. The Outsider from The Book of the Long Sun

2. The Excession of Iain M Banks - that paradigmn shifting outside event=20 which changes the entire system and, eventually, reveals new understanding.

Could it be that the Outsider and the Oracle are one and the same? Perhaps= =20
someone with greater insight and understanding could help me to interpret=20 this conundrum.

Thanks,

Che
