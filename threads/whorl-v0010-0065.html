1999whorlThe Book of the Long SunAlex David Groce

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

1999-10-06 Alex David Groce #

Argues that Wolfe intends readers to connect Silk and Horn as somehow combined, with the "caught the ball" line as the key evidence, and that Seawrack is human rather than a Neighbor, rescued by Mother after her mother drowned. Also claims the Neighbors' "other place" is metaphysical rather than another planet, and endorses a Dave Lebling summary of the inhumi's secret; the poster is replying to several points at once.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Silk and Horn are combined into one character; Seawrack is human, not a Neighbor; Mother rescued Seawrack for devious purposes; The Neighbors' other place is metaphysical

I'm replying to a number of points at once--a local mail problem resulted in me getting today in "digest" mode, all arriving in about five seconds.

1. Wolfe clearly DOES want us to have thoughts about Silk and Horn being somehow combined. The "caught the ball" line is too haunting and explicit for any other possibility. What's going on is anybody's guess (Sev./Thecla-ish with Silk's personality mostly buried, perhaps?)

2. Seawrack is a human, not a Neighbor. Her mother drowned and "Mother" rescued her for (probably) devious purposes.

3. Dave Lebling summed up the theory I'm supporting as to what the inhumi's secret is far better than I've managed to thus far.

4. The neighbors "other place" seems to be something metaphysical or Shadow- like, not just another planet they've moved to en masse.
