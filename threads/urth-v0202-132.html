2002-02-11 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Announces that the mailing list archives have been updated through the end of the previous year and that the site now uses Google search, with recent postings to follow. Also asks volunteers for a complete bibliography of Wolfe's books and for annotated recommended-reading lists for a new page.
informs

I finally got the mailing list archives up to date through the end of last year. I'll have recent postings up there too, as soon as I figure out how. Give me a few more
weeks. http://www.urth.net/urth/

The site now uses the google search engine to search the archives. It could take google up to a month to index the newly-posted archives, and it'll lag by about a month in catching up with new postings.

If any volunteers would like to help me spruce up the books page, I could use a list of all of Wolfe's published books. If one of you already has a complete bibliographical
page, let me know and I'll just link to that instead.

I'd also like to set up a page of recommended reading by any authors. Send me your briefly-annotated lists of
recommendations and I'll post them on the site.

-- ranjit
