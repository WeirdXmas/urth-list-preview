2002-02-04 Marc Aramini #

Asks Jerry to revisit Joe's cross-reference of the two-tusked Green Mankiller in In Green's Jungles and Return to the Whorl, the monster the narrator feels lurking at swamps. Recounts earlier guesses that it was Babbie, then links its dripping teeth to Triskele, whose friendly nature seems to undercut the scary association.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Green Mankiller may be linked to Triskele

Jerry, Joe has recently cross-referenced the two appearances of the "Green Mankiller" that appears once towards the end of In Green's Jungles and briefly in Return to the Whorl. His comments are somewhere in the recent posts. When they skirt a swamp, the narrator says he can feel a two tusked green monster lurking for him, with tusks as big as his wrists. It comes up again later. At first I was sure he was talking about Babbie, but that didn't pan out with my later theories. The dripping teeth of Triskele, who shows up at the end of Return to the Whorl made gave me the association, but "friend" does tend to negate its scary qualities. Thanks.
Marc Aramini
