Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1999-12-02 Mitchell A. Bailey #

Proposes that Wolfe signals a non-literal sense in which Cyriaca is Severian's mother, arguing that Cyriaca's extended musings and fantasies about the possibility would otherwise have no purpose. Responds to an earlier post that quoted a claim that Cyriaca could not be Sev's mother, by agreeing that a figurative maternal link is possible.
supports · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Cyriaca is Severian's mother in a non-literal sense

I too had gotten a certain bee in my bonnet to the effect Wolfe was signalling that in some sense (other than literal!) Cyriaca was Sev's mother. Why else would there be all that musing and fantasizing on her part about the hypothetical possibility?
