New Anthology Featuring Wolfe

2009-09-24 Matthew Keeley #

Informs readers of a new flash fiction anthology, Last Drink Bird Head, edited by Jeff and Ann Vandermeer, with proceeds going to ProLiteracy and contributors including Michael Cisco, Jeffrey Ford and Michael Swanwick. Shares the price and a link, noting the stories are loosely inspired by the phrase, and does not discuss Wolfe's own work.
informs

I just read about Last Drink Bird Head, a new anthology of flash fiction edited by Jeff and Ann Vandemeer. From what I gather, all the stories (which, being flash fiction, are VERY SHORT) are somehow inspired by the phrase "Last Drink Bird Head." Proceeds from the book go to the ProLiteracy charity.

http://www.jeffvandermeer.com/2009/09/24/last-drink-bird-head-for-charity-party-pre-orders-awards-and-more/

A few other Urth favorites have stories in it as well - Michael Cisco, Jeffrey Ford, Michael Swanwick.

Book IS thirty bucks for 160 pages, but figured some of you might be interested nonetheless. Not sure if I will be ordering myself.

-Matt
