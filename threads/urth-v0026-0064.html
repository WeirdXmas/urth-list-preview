Wanted: Info on Wraeththu

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-04-29 to 1999-04-29

1999urthC R Culver

1999-04-29 C R Culver #

Asks whether list members have read Storm Constantine's Wraeththu, a far-future Earth novel he compares to The Book of the New Sun and Dying Earth, and seeks advice on whether to buy the Orb reissue. The post does not discuss Wolfe's text or any reading of it, only the poster's interest in a possibly similar book.
questions

I saw at Barnes & Noble the other day the Orb one-volume reissue of _Wraeththu_ by Storm Constantine, and have thought about buying it. I notice it is similar to stories like the BotNS and _Dying Earth_ in that it takes place in a far future Earth different and yet similar to our own. Has anyone on this list read it? Maybe you could inform me as to whether to buy it or not. Orb reissues as supposedly the classics of science fiction, but I hadn't previously heard of this book.

Thanks,

Christopher Culver
