1998-08-03 mary whalen #

Identifies the zoril, the Zorilla of Wolfe's Book of the New Sun, and traces its name to Spanish zorfillo, a diminutive of zorfo meaning fox. Describes the animal as a striped muishond related to skunks, nocturnal, with skunk-like anal ejection, and notes it is a volunteer fighting for Mint.
informs · source-and-allusion

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Zoril. Also zorille, zorilla, and zorillo. From Spanish zorfillo/a, diminutive of zorfo (fox). This is the striped muishond (Ictonyx striatus). Related to and resembles skunks. Has the anal ejection characteristic of skunks as well. Nocturnal. Volunteer who fights for Mint.
