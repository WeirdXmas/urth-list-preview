2005-01-25 Kieran Mullen replying to turin #

Mocks Jim Raylor's apology with jokes about medication and trolling, then notes that Mani, Idunn and Thiassi come from Norse myth but are not simple copies of their originals in The Wizard Knight. Points readers to a mythome.org page on Nordic myth and jokingly concedes that trolls are part of the Norse mythos.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Mani, Idunn and Thiassi are not simple cut-and-paste Norse figures in The Wizard Knight

Hmmm... I'd recommend that you work with your pharmacist until they get the dosages right. Sure, the colors may no longer be as bright, but I think the payoff in ability to focus will make it worthwhile.

Either that, or troll elsewhere.

BTW, Mani, Idunn, Thiassi, are all part of the Norse mythos. However none of them are simple cut-and-paste versions of what in TWK books. Look at:

http://www.mythome.org/nordic.html

Kieran Mullen

P.S. Damn. Trolls *are* part of the Norse mythos. Oh well.

Suggests many more Norse references remain undiscovered, supporting the idea that Wolfe transforms his mythic sources.
