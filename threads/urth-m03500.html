Links a medieval image and notes that St Christopher was sometimes described as dog-headed, connecting this to a character's line in Pirate Freedom about medieval artists seeing beasts and half-human monsters. Offers the tongue-in-cheek suggestion that the character's test-tube may have included dog or wolf DNA, without arguing a firm reading.
informs · source-and-allusion

Chris says: "The artists of the Middle Ages painted allegories, we say. What really happened was that they saw more clearly than we do, and painted what they saw - angels and devils, beasts, and half- human monsters like me."

I wonder if he's thinking of images like this:
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image:L_022.jpg

Apparently St Christopher was sometimes described as cynocephalic.

I doubt that Chris' test-tube included any dog (wolf?) DNA, but you never know ...
