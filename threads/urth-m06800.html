Expresses sympathy for Nigel, a co-editor of Ultan's Library with the poster, after news of his misfortune, and says the whole list is rooting for him. Mentions Nigel's excitement about a plan that came together and says the poster regretted being unable to attend.

Thanks for letting us know about poor Nigel! I'm the co-editor of Ultan's Library with him, and have spent all weekend wondering how the events went - he was very excited about the way the plan came together, and I was very jealous I couldn't go.

Everyone who's met or corresponded with him will tell you what a fantastic guy he is and I'm sure he'll be happy to hear that the entire list is rooting for him.
