Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

1997-01-27 Charles Dye #

Argues that Patera Pike in The Book of the New Sun should be read as an aquastor, linking him to Malrubius's account of hierodule-made eidolons and Famulimus's statement that hierodules make images only of the dead. Supports the idea by citing these passages, and notes Pike dissolves rather than flies, so the poster wonders about another small dark character who flies out windows.
supports · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Patera Pike is an aquastor

More on Pike-as-aquastor. These from [email]:

This fits very nicely with Master Malrubius's explanation to Severian. Another mention of these creations appears near the end of "The Urth of the New Sun": Famulimus states categorically that the Hierodules only make eidolons of the dead. This also fits Patera Pike.

IIRC, Patera Pike doesn't fly; he dissolves. Surely we can find some other small dark character who flies out windows? <grin>
