2002-03-12 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Answers a newcomer's question about the order of the Viriconium books by giving the publication sequence, noting the series is non-chronological and was reissued as an omnibus. Adds a warning that the tone shifts from heroic fantasy in the first book to more artistic, café-style intrigue in later books.
answers

No. The sequence itself is rather non-chronological (as I recall), but it was published in the following order:

THE PASTEL CITY
A STORM OF WINGS
IN VIRICONIUM (also known as THE FLOATING GODS)
VIRICONIUM NIGHTS (short stories)

The whole series was recently published by Millennium as an omnibus VIRICONIUM, iirc.

My favorites would be the first and third, iirc. My only "warning" would be that the tone or flavor of the world changes from book to book: for example the heroic fantasy of the first is not exactly matched in the later books, which are more, what . . . artiste cafe embroglios?

You are welcome! Sorry I misspelled it before.
