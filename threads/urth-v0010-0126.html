Recommends several novels by John Crowley (Engine Summer, Little, Big, Aegypt, Love and Sleep, and the story Great Work of Time) and Richard Grant's Through the Heart to Wolfe fans, with a warning that Aegypt and Love and Sleep form part of a tetralogy. Asks whether anyone has read Grant's later novels Tex and Molly and his latest book.
proposes

I would recommend highly to any Wolfe fan who hasn't already read them _Engine Summer_, _Little, Big_, _Aegypt_, _Love and Sleep_, and "Great Work of Time" (in the collection _Novelty_, and also published separately IIRC) by John Crowley. But be warned that _Aegypt_ and _Love and Sleep_ are the first two parts of a tetralogy (though IMO _Aegypt_ can be read perfectly well as a standalone novel).

I'd also recommend _Through the Heart_ by Richard Grant. (Has anyone read his two more recent novels? I've never seen _Tex and Molly_ in a library, and his latest didn't look that appealing to me.

--Adam
