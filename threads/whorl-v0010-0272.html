2000-03-02 Nicholas Gevers #

Informs readers that the cover of the Tor edition of In Green's Jungles is now viewable on Amazon.com, and judges Jim Burns's artwork more lurid than before. Compares the cover's Neighbor figure to the Pnume or Phung creatures in Jack Vance's fiction.

2000-03-07 Jim Henley #

Argues that the lurid cover of Tor's In Green's Jungles should not make readers hide the book in public, calling fear of being seen with it prissy. Responds to Alastair Reynolds's quoted remark that he would remove the cover in a public place, using the idea that literature helps readers see through false values.
disputes

Since one of the things literature does is help us see through false value systems, why not start with this one? A fear of being seen reading a book with "lurid covers" is prissy. And I mean that in the nicest possible way.

Best,

Jim
