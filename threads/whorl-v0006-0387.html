1997-06-24 Alice Turner #

Proposes that the group pose a collective question about the apparition Silk takes for Pike's ghost, asking whether it is really an aquastor or eidolon of Pike or someone else. Responds to Vance's call to settle the matter and offers two wordings, one of which floats the idea that the figure is Quetzal.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Pike's ghost is an aquastor or eidolon of Pike; Pike's ghost is actually Quetzal

mantis,

Vance is right--we should clear up Pike's ghost. Couldn't it be a "baker's dozen" or "one-to-grow-on" question from all of us as a group on top of our individual questions? I think the wording should be more specific---Weasel should be Wolfe's Vironese name! What about: "Is the apparation at the top of the stairs that Silk takes for Pike's ghost really a form (i.e. aquastor/eidolon) of Pike? If not, who or what is it?"

Alternately, "Is 'Pike's ghost' really Quetzal? If not, who or what is it?"

-alga

1997-06-24 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that Pike's ghost is neither an aquastor nor an eidolon, leaving two candidates: an alien such as an inhumu or Quetzal, or a real miraculous ghost. Responds to alga's post by endorsing the question "Is Pike's ghost really Quetezal? If not, who or what is it?" because the ghost wears the beanie and has physical presence.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Pike's ghost is an alien inhumu or Quetzal; Pike's ghost is a real miraculous ghost

Reply: Item #7269253 from [email]@INET00#

alga,

True, further questions might help. And multiple choice would narrow it down. Since Wolfe wrote "it wasn't machine made," I'm inclined to think that Pike's ghost is neither aquastor nor eidolon--not wanting to drive into the but-an-AI-computer-isn't-a-machine avenue (I think it would be a blind alley).

So it seems to me we are down to two candidates: alien (inhumu, Quetzal or some other one) and miraculous (along the lines of the enlightenments--i.e., it is a real ghost). The thing seems to have physical presence--wears the beanie, after all.

Therefore, your second version: "Is `Pike's ghost' really Quetezal? If not, who or what is it?" appears to fit the bill.

And, if we don't get a whole bunch of questions from new people, then we can let it be yours. (Or vizcacha's, if he wants to use it as his follow-up.)

=mantis=
