Of urthmen and abos

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-04-30 to 1999-04-30

1999urthOri Kowarsky

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Colonization and the native

1999-04-30 Ori Kowarsky #

Proposes that the Hieros may be partly aboriginal, or owe their shape-changing to splicing of human and abo DNA, so that the extermination of Urth's native population counts as the aborigines' revenge on humanity. Builds on the author's own earlier theory of Severian and grounds the reading in the past extermination of the abos by human colonists.
proposes · allegory-and-typology · close-textual-reading · theories: Hieros are part abo; Hieros morphing results from human and abo DNA splicing; Extermination of Urth's natives is abo revenge

Further to my Grand Unified Severain Theory that's captured the hearts and minds of this list (please, no autographs) it has struck me that if the Hieros are part abo, or if their morphing ability is the result of Number Five's splicing of human and abo DNA, then the extermination of Urth's indiginous population and its replacement with abo-spliced human stock would in fact count as their revenge upon the humanity that created them, given the extermination of the abos by human colonists so long ago.

Ori
