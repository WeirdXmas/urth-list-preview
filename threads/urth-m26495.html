2015-01-26 Marc Aramini replying to Michael Thayer #

Identifies Lewis Carroll allusions in The Claw of the Conciliator: Jonas quoting Carroll's knight sliding up a poker in the antechamber, and Inire's mirrors recalling the looking glass. Answers Michael Thayer's question from the Short Story 117 review, pointing to a Cheshire-cat-like cat in the Cat short story write-up as a parallel.
answers · source-and-allusion

Sure - in claw of the conciliator when jonas and sev are taken to the antechamber where people spend their whole lives waiting for sentencing, jonas starts quoting carroll about the knight sliding up the poker or something. I mention it in the Cat short story writeup as well i think ... even alluding to the cat as something like the chesire variety. The mirrors of inire attain a quality like the looking glass in that scene as well, i think. Clearly jonas has read lewis carroll.

2015-01-26 Jeff Wilson replying to Marc Aramini #

Agrees with Marc Aramini that Jonas's quotation of Carroll in The Claw of the Conciliator, about the White Knight sliding down the poker, echoes the Cheshire cat and looking-glass imagery in the Short Story 117 writeup. Cites the passage in which Jonas recalls a history and the White Knight line, linking it to the mirrors of Inire.
supports · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Jonas's Carroll quotation echoes Alice looking-glass imagery

"I used to read, aboard ship. Once I read a history. I don't suppose you know anything about it. So many chiliads have elapsed here." I said, "I suppose not."
"So different from this, but so much like it too. Queer little customs and usages . . . some that weren't so little. Strange institutions. I asked the ship and she gave me another book."
He was still perspiring, and I thought his mind was wandering. I used the square of flannel I carried to wipe my sword blade to dry his forehead. "Hereditary rulers and hereditary subordinates, and all sorts of strange officials. Lancers with long, white mustaches." For an instant the ghost of his old humorous smile appeared. "The White Knight is sliding down the poker. He balances very badly, as the King's notebook told him."
