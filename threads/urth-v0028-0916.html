Recounts Wolfe's remark that The Book of the New Sun grew out of an idea for a costume worn as a fantasy character at a science fiction convention, with the torturer's outfit intriguing him. Responds to Lex Shellac's question about where the image of the bare-chested masked torturer comes from, admitting the poster does not know its source.
answers · author-and-biography

Wolfe himself remarked that the whole book came out of the idea to dress as a character in a fantasy novel for one of those big sci-fi conventions. The outfit intrigued him ( I don't know where he got the image, though), and the novel grew out of that. Sounds a bit coy, but in art, so much can come out of so little. Wolfe was obviously having thoughts in this direction anyway...
DA
