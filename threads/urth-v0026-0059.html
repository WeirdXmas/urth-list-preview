Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life

1999-04-29 Tony Ellis #

Argues that the claim Sidero is hollow because ship's androids are hollow is a faulty syllogism, and that Tony Ellis has taken no position on whether Jonas is hollow. Responds to Roy C Lackey's list of disputes, restating that Sidero is too bulky to be the same android type as Jonas and calling the build question unanswerable from the text.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Sidero is too bulky to be the same android type as Jonas

Actually, I've never said anything either way about (A). Jonas may be hollow, he may not be. I don't see enough evidence on either side for a constructive debate. But the argument that Sidero is a ship's android, Sidero is hollow, therefore all ship's androids are hollow smacks too much of the classic "Plato has a beard, Plato is a man, therefore all men have beards" syllogism for me.

And likewise (B): all I've ever maintained is that Sidero is too bulky to be the same kind of android as Jonas. The issue of whether or not Jonas's class of android is "average" or "slender" in build is another How Many Children Had Lady Macbeth? question.
