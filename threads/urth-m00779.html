2005-02-21 Kieran Mullen #

Asks where a dedicated discussion list for Gene Wolfe's books can be found, complaining that the list has drifted into unrelated topics. Then opens a discussion of The Wizard Knight by asking whether Able merely stumbles into Mythgathr or was fated to arrive there, with a prior special relationship to it.
questions

At the risk of being rude, I pose the above question. Of late I find very little discussion of Wolfe and lots of thoughts on distantly related topics. Perhaps I am the only one, but such random ruminations is the reason why I unsubscribed to this list for a few years. I had hoped people be might discussing the Wizard-Knight books.

So, I'll kick it off. Is Able just some kid who wanders into Mythgathr, or was he fated to do so? By the latter I mean does he have some special relationship to Mythgathr before the start of the first book?

Kieran Mullen

2005-02-21 James Wynn replying to Kieran Mullen #

Argues that Able and Arthur are linked from birth and suggests that all seven worlds are contained within Arthur, going beyond the theory that one of Arthur's parents came from Mythgarthr. Responds to Kieran Mullen's question of whether Able is fated or just a kid who wanders into Mythgarthr, and defends the Wizard Knight discussion against complaints about off-topic chatter.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Able and Arthur are linked from birth; All seven worlds are contained within Arthur; One of Arthur's parents was from Mythgarthr

Questions off the beaten track will persist as long as there are no legitimate interesting topics to discuss. As long as the topics are mildly interesting are they so much worse than nothing? However, I commend you for trying to get everyone back to something that interests you and is on topic.

Since Able and Arthur a in some way the same person, I would say they have to be linked from birth. It has been postulated that Arthur's mother or father was from Mythgarthr. I'm not sure this explains everything. I suspect (although some seem viscerally hostile to the notion, that all the seven worlds are "in" Arthur.
