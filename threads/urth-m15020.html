Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2010-03-15 David Stockhoff #

Proposes that Reis's knowledge of Margaret in An Evil Guest may hide a secret identity: that Margaret is really Cassie. Points out inconsistencies in the dinner scene, such as the gunsel's wrong gender and Reis's claim that Margaret would not steal the bracelet, yet still favors the idea.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Margaret is Cassie

- During the dinner date, Reis seems pretty confident that he knows the gunsel who attacked Chase, but doesn't know who he works for. The gender is wrong, the gunsel is not described as small, and besides, didn't Reis say he looked into Margaret's background and knows she wouldn't steal the bracelet? That's not very consistent with her attempted murder of Chase.

Regardless of how it fits, I like the possibility that Reis checked out Margaret and knows she wouldn't steal the bracelet---because she's Cassie.
