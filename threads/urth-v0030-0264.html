Reports that Dan Rabin, quoting a 1966 letter to Wolfe, sees Wolfe's early concern with words bearing on his surname, and notes that Wolfe mentioned being a Tolkien fan in The Castle of the Otter without mentioning that he had corresponded with Tolkien. Responds to the quoted editor's remark, adding a point about the letter's connection to Tolkien rather than analysing any fiction.
