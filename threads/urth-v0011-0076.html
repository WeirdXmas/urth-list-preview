Asks three questions about Urth: why the small Tzadkiel was banished, whether the thorn Severian gives the chiliarch is Agia's stolen Claw and when it was gemmed, and who the woman led away by Praetorians is. Builds on a quoted earlier post's reading of the Shadow chapter XXXV bean-woman and cites chapters XXXIX, XL and XLI as evidence.

This thought made brought to mind Severian's encounter with the small version of Tzadkiel in Urth, chapter XL, "The Brook Beyond Briah." She indicates that she is a fragment of the original Tzadkiel when she says, "...I'm a part of myself that was banished long ago, long before the first time you met me." Can anyone tell me what she was banished for?

On another subject, is the thorn that Severian gave to the chiliarch in Urth, chapter XXXIX, "The Claw of the Conciliator Again" the Claw that Agia robbed from the Pelerines? If so, when did it become encased in gem-stone?
