Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2000-08-15 Dennis G. Berdanis #

Speculates that Typhon, the human-seeming villain of the Long Sun books, might be a proto-Neighbor or an anomalous Neighbor like Asimov's Mule, given that his parasitic duality clashes with the Outsider and the Neighbors. Responds to Seth Lombardi's suggestion that Typhon's Neighbor ancestry would explain why the Whorl exists, while noting the poster has read only On Blue's Waters.
proposes · myth-and-structure · theories: Typhon is a proto-Neighbor; Typhon is an aberrant Neighbor like the Mule

< Okay, if Typhon was Neighbor-Spawned or related it _would_ help finally < explain the whole Why The Whorl Anyway question, but Typhon was such a < seemingly sinister and human critter that I have a hard time buying it. His
< duality is parasitic and completely antithetical to both the Outsider and (I
< think - I've only read OBW) the semi-enlightened noble savage-ness of the < Neighbors. Then again, the Neighbors ain't exactly what I'd call easy to < relate to, so perhaps Typhon was some sort of proto-neighbor, from back when
< they were a bit more barbaric. On the other hand, I've always liked the way
< Wolfe makes most evil human created - even we seeded Erebus and such.

Or could Typhoon be a similar aberration to Asimov's "Mule"? Not typical of any of the other Neighbors?
