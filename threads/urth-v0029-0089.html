Smoky Barnstable

Urth list · 1 messages · 2000-06-28 to 2000-06-28

2000urthAlice Turner

2000-06-28 Alice Turner #

Argues that Smoky Barnstable is a chosen, essentially decent man who does his duty without belief, not a negative figure, and that the Drinkwaters are not opposed to him but complementary. Grounds this in a Crowley-style ordinary-man-in-extraordinary-circumstances trope and compares Rush, Pierce Moffit and Loren Casaubon, responding to an earlier post's reading of Smoky's childhood anonymity.
disputes · genre-and-craft · close-textual-reading · theories: Smoky is an ordinary decent man chosen for duty

Hastily after a looong weekend:

Said Adam:

I doubt it. It's a nickname for a boy who seems to be "streak of presence surrounded by a dim glow of absence," and whom only cheerful George Mouse clothes in enough presense so that at least the Invisible Man has bandages so that people won't sit on him. It is the Drinkwaters, mainly Alice, who cure him of anonymity. At least, en famille.

Right.

They are not poles; they are complementary. Smoky is terribly important; he is chosen (though he is far to modest to envision himself this way). Without him, the Tale could not be told. Look, this is a trope of Crowley's: The ordinary decent man finds himself in extraordinary circumstances, and, without believing at all in the situation which others seem to think merits action, agrees to do what is asked of him, to do his duty. It is the existential situation in a fairytale setting. Not only Smoky, but Rush who Speaks (from ES) and Pierce Moffit (from the Aegypt series) are heroes of this sort, and I think that Loren Casaubon from Beasts started out this way, though he sort of dribbled off (like the book). But to reiterate, JCs guys do their damn duty. That is their task, their quest, and because they take up that burden, willingly or not (Smoky is willing), the world whirrs.

The Drinkwaters are NOT negative, btw. To my mind that is a complete misreading. Theirs is not a world of good and evil and judgment calls. It just is.

-alga
