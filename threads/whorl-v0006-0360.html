Themes: Women, sex and gender

1997-06-16 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks cephalothorax to assign a list of Wolfe's women characters, including Dorcas, Jolenta, Kypris and Maytera Mint, to three proposed female types, and then to list the types available to Wolfe's men for comparison. Responds to cephalothorax's earlier claim that Wolfe's women follow a limited repertoire of Stand-In, Sex Object and Agent of Destruction.
