5HC a good introduction to Wolfe (and testing 1 2 3)

Themes: Memory and forgetting

2002-04-16 Alex David Groce #

Notes that The Fifth Head of Cerberus compresses Proust's material, citing the Baron Charlus house, the girl in the park and the memory well packed into sixty pages. Also a return to the list after email trouble, the poster adds that each allusion performs several functions at once, and recalls it was his first Wolfe.
informs · source-and-allusion

I've finally managed (I think) to get back onto the list after some struggles due to CMU's bizarre email setup. I've semi-read the archives for my time away.

I'm actually rereading 5HC myself just now, after recommending it to a friend who convinced me to read _Wide Sargasso Sea_. I finished the first novella last night. It was my first Wolfe as well--and the hook was set for life after it.

This time what really struck me was the compressed Proust aspect--the Baron Charlus' house + the girl in the park + the memory well rolled into 60 pages, while every allusion manages to perform six other functions while it's at it.
