2012-06-16 Jerry Friedman replying to Allan Anderson #

Gives a brief positive verdict on Robert Graves's novel Watch the North Wind Rise, saying the author makes his position clear. Replies to Allan Anderson's request for a short opinion before deciding whether to read it, and notes some resemblances to Wolfe's work without elaborating.
answers

I thought it was pretty good.? Graves leaves you in no doubt about where he stands.? What happens has some resemblances to Wolfe, but I won't say any more about it unless you don't mind my spoiling it.

Jerry Friedman
