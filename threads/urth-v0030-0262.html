5HC plot-cloaking device

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-01-24 to 2001-01-24

2001urthTony Ellis

2001-01-24 Tony Ellis #

Argues that the telepathic shield in The Fifth Head of Cerberus covered the whole star system, not only St. Anne, and that the Shadow child's song telepathically summoned the starcrossers. Responds to Rostrum by citing a line from V.R.T. in which Q says both worlds stayed unknown while planets farther out were colonized, and by noting the child's promise to 'sing them in.'
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Telepathic shield covered the whole star system; Shadow child telepathically summoned the starcrossers

Read "V.R.T.". In one of the interrogation dialogues "Q" specifically says that "both our worlds remained unknown when planets more distant from Earth had been colonized for decades." The telepathic shield clearly applied to the whole system.

The fact that the starcrossers arrive so rapidly is kind of convenient for the plot, I agree. To say the least. But notice that the Shadow child doesn't just say he's going to remove the shield, he says of the starcrossers that he is going to "sing them in" - which suggests he's actually telepathically summoning them. This also explains why they happen to land just where they can rescue Sandwalker and co., rather on any other stretch of coastland on the planet. :-)

Well, the song -is- telepathic. It may appear magical to Sandwalker and his friends, but that's because they're ignorant savages. :-)

But surely its effects would appear to "break down immediately" regardless of the point of view of the onlookers?

No, the effect of the song really was to hide the planets. See my answer to Rostrum, just above.
