1997-08-14 Kieran Mullen #

Disputes the earlier claim that Dorcas's name points to Io's myth, arguing instead that it alludes to a New Testament woman. Recalls that the Dorcas of the New Testament, also called Tabitha, was raised from the dead by Peter, and notes the reply is written from memory without a Bible at hand.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Dorcas alludes to the New Testament Dorcas raised by Peter

I think you are off the mark with the Dorcas allusion. Dorcas was the name of a woman brought back from the dead (by Peter, I think) in the New Testament. (I don't have my RSV here, but it something like her name was Dorcas, but she was also called Tabitha).

Kieran Mullen
