Props to Ultan's Library, also Death and the Maiden

Urth list · 1 messages · 2001-04-12 to 2001-04-12

2001urthDan Parmenter

Themes: Justice, torture and mercy, Unreliable narration

2001-04-12 Dan Parmenter #

Praises Ultan's Library for its update and new Jeremy Crampton article, then recommends the film Death and the Maiden as vaguely Wolfean. Compares the torturer-healer ambiguity, unreliable witnesses and ambiguous ending to Gurloes and the torturers' guild in the Book of the New Sun.
informs · genre-and-craft

Great stuff, as always. I haven't fully absorbed Jeremy Crampton's article yet, but it seems fascinating, complete with completely non-gratuitous quotes from Foucault ;-)

Jeremy's topic reminds me that I saw an interesting film recently with vaguely Wolfean overtones: Death and the Maiden, based on a play by the very odd Ariel Dorfman. It features a healer who may also be a torturer with much discussion of the ways that one can become the other, several unreliable witnesses and an unsatisfying, ambiguous ending. Sigourney Weaver is quite good as a woman in an unnamed South American country who was tortured as a political prisoner (for "confessional" reasons, though our tortuer, who takes pleasure in his work, seems to have something of Gurloes in him too). It's rather melodramatic and much of it is prickly and uncomfortable, but I found it quite compelling nonetheless even with its faults. Your mileage may vary.

Shellac
