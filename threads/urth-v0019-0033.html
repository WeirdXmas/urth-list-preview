Eclipse question

Urth list · 3 messages · 1998-10-02 to 1998-10-02

1998urthThe Book of the New SunC R Culvermarcmary whalen
The thread: Marc asked why the angel Tzadkiel would cause Apu-Punchau's eclipse after Severian had already brought the New Sun, suggesting it served no purpose unless tied to a later destiny, and proposed that Pas is an avatar of Typhon/Piaton, contrasting Pas's benevolence with Typhon's cruelty. Mary Whalen answered that Tzadkiel may have wanted Severian to rule as Apu to bring technology such as better building methods and to generate legends for later people. CRCulver disputed the Pas-Typhon identification, noting Scylla's remark that Typhon the First built the Whorl, and argued the ship's crew also helped Severian at the eclipse. The exchange ended with the Pas-Typhon claim disputed but no consensus reached on Tzadkiel's motives.
  • marc: Tzadkiel's eclipse seems purposeless unless tied to a later destiny; Pas is an avatar of Typhon/Piaton
  • Mary Whalen: Tzadkiel wanted Severian as Apu for technology and to seed legends
  • CRCulver: Pas is not Typhon; the ship's crew helped at the eclipse, not only Tzadkiel

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Myth and folklore sources, The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-10-02 marc #

Asks why the angel Tzadkiel would intervene to cause Apu-Punchau's eclipse when Severian has already brought the New Sun, suggesting the act may have no purpose unless tied to a later destiny. Also proposes that Pas is an avatar of Typhon/Piaton, contrasting Pas's benevolence with Typhon's cruelty.
questions · myth-and-structure · theories: Pas is an avatar of Typhon/Piaton; Tzadkiel's eclipse serves a later destiny for Severian

OK, the "obvious" guess for what caused Apu-Punchau's eclipse is the ship. But, why would Tzadkiel bother to help Severian in such a situation? Remember, Severian has already brought the New Sun, and therefore has already insured the continuation of the next cycle... I could understand why Zak, Juturna, the Green Man, etc. would interfere in Severian's past prior to Severian's examination, but it seems pointless to interfere after Severian's role is complete (unless Severian has some additional destiny after UotNS that Gene Wolfe hasn't written about yet... Possibly in a short-sun connection).

In fact, given that Apu-Punchau's people end up killing and entombing him anyway, and given that he's resurrected by the coming of the New Sun, the whole eclipse thing seems kind of pointless. Unless Apu-Punchau's leadership is itself one of the driving factors in his people's development that eventually plays itself out in their future history/evolution to New Sun times... Any thoughts?

On an unrelated not, it seems clear that Pas is the avatar of Typhon/Piaton (unless there are other two-headed all-powerful monarchs floating around capable of building generation ships). But Pas seems to be a mostly benevolent figure, at least compared to Echidna, Scylla, etc... Whereas, Typhon was clearly a bastard... Guess you'd have to chalk that one up to programmer's error, heh.

-Marc

1998-10-02 mary whalen #

Proposes that Tzadkiel may have had reasons for Severian's rule as Apu, such as giving worshippers more advanced technology like better building techniques. Suggests this would also supply material for the legends of later people, which Tzadkiel might want to exist.
proposes · theology-and-religion · theories: Tzadkiel gave worshippers advanced technology through Severian as Apu

Sean Whalen (prion).

One aspect of Severian's rule as Apu that Tzadkiel might have decided was needed was his giving of more advanced technology to his worshippers (such as better building techniques).

Also, it would be material for the legends of later people, and perhaps Tzadkiel wants that to exist, too.

prion

1998-10-02 C R Culver #

Disputes Marc's claim that Pas is Typhon, and suggests the ship's crew, not just Tzadkiel, helped Severian at Apu-Punchau's eclipse. Cites Scylla's remark that Typhon the First built the Whorl, arguing this need not be the Conciliator-era Typhon, and says Pas is not Severian's Typhon.
disputes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Ship crew helped Severian during the eclipse; Pas is not Severian's Typhon; Typhon the First built the Whorl

<OK, the "obvious" guess for what caused Apu-Punchau's eclipse is the ship. But, why would Tzadkiel bother to help Severian in such a situation?>

I don't think it was just Tzadkiel. Severian did have some friends on the ship, especially Gunnie. And perhaps the sailors wanted to repay him for the help in the fight against the muties. And there is always Famulimus, Ossipago, and Barbatus...

<On an unrelated not, it seems clear that Pas is the avatar of Typhon/Piaton (unless there are other two-headed all-powerful monarchs floating around capable of building generation ships). But Pas seems to be a mostly benevolent figure, at least compared to Echidna, Scylla, etc... Whereas, Typhon was clearly a bastard... Guess you'd have to chalk that one up to programmer's error, heh.>

Scylla mentions that the builder of the Whorl was Typhon the First, but not necessarily the Typhon of the Conciliator era. As a matter of fact, I'm uncovering more daily that Pas is not Severian's Typhon, but I shall post those conclusions to the Whorl list when I am ready.
