Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-07-31 mary whalen #

Proposes that the name Murtagon in Wolfe's books may derive from the martagon lily, noting alternative spellings and common names like red-cap and turk's-cap lily. Grounds the idea in dictionary-style spelling variants and a botanical lookup, and suggests a link to the artist's beret through the red cap.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Murtagon is named after the martagon lily

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

Murtagon. All I could find fitting this is martagon, also mortegon, martagan, and martigon. With all those alternative spellings, it's probable that murtagon exists, too. It's also called the red-cap lily, turk's-cap lily, and the turk's cap (Lilium martagon or Lilium superbum). Murtagon is a famous artist. The red cap reminds me of the stereotypical artist's beret.

prion
