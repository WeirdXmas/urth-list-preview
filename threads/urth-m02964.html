Praises Chapter VI of The Shadow of the Torturer as the favorite chapter of a re-read, because it shows the world beyond Severian and its library setting. Compares the chapter's library atmosphere to Mervyn Peake's Gormenghast, with no further argument or evidence offered.

Mervyn Peake's "Gormenghast".
