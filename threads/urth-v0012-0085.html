1998-06-05 Dan Parmenter #

Asks Dan Parmenter to explain why Wolfe defends Lamarckism explicitly and implicitly without ever really explaining why, then moves on to say a re-read of The Fifth Head of Cerberus is pending. Also floats a revised edition of Castle of the Otter, proposing to cut its reviews and add a word list, an essay, and Long Sun chapters.

The COTO of my mind would jettison the reviews, but include most of the actual content of the first, perhaps with an expanded list of words, the essay "Books in Book Of The New Sun" that ran in PLAN[E]T ENGINEERING and perhaps a few chapters on the LONG SUN books, since they're connected.
