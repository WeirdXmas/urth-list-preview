Of these, I've read "Petting Zoo," "The Tree Is My Hat," and "The Walking Sticks."

"Petting Zoo" is a pretty light story, picking up Wolfe's interest in the possibility of genetically re-creating extinct animals (in this case, a dinosaur).

"The Walking Sticks" and "The Tree Is My Hat" are both horror stories. "The Walking Sticks" draws on Dr. Jeckyl and Mr. Hyde. "Tree" is set in the Caribbean, if I remember correctly. The editor of 999, in which it was first published, says something like "The title may sound strange, but by the end of the story, you'll know what it means." Well ... I'm not entirely sure I do.

Hope this helps!

John
