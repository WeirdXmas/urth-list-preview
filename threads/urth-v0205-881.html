2002-05-06 Jerry Friedman #

Proposes two books that exist only inside The Fifth Head of Cerberus for the Invisible Library catalog of fictional books, an introduction to cultural anthropology by a Miller and a field guide to the animals of Sainte Anne. Notes that a real Barbara D. Miller textbook exists from 1998 and asks whether an earlier edition could have predated Wolfe's novel.
proposes · source-and-allusion

I noticed that our contribution to the Invisible Library <http://www.invisiblelibrary.com/ILCatalogf.htm> (for those who joined the list since those discussions, it's a catalog of books that exist only in other books) doesn't include anything from _Fifth Head_. I found these two:

Author: Wolfe, Gene
Title: The Fifth Head of Cerberus
Pseudo-Author: Miller
Pseudo-Title: Introduction to Cultural Anthropology

Pseudo-Author: unknown
Pseudo-Title: Field Guide to the Animals of Sainte Anne, A

Any corrections?

There's a real _Cultural Anthropology_ by Barbara D. Miller (1998). There couldn't possibly have been an earlier edition, with a different title, in Wolfe's day, could there?

Jerry Friedman
