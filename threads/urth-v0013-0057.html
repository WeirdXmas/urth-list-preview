1998-06-14 Charles Dye #

Argues that the water clock stopping at the master's death in The Book of the New Sun is one of Thecla's commonsense explanations of spooky magic, since no slave refilled the water pan. Extends Mantis's remark by linking the anacrisis magician's whoo during Severian's solitary and Thecla's death to Severian's simple explanations of jungle magic.
supports · close-textual-reading

Quoth Mantis:

A bit of spooky magic explained in flat commonsense terms.

Spoken by Thecla. Which makes the magician conducting Severian's anacrisis say "whoo" as Sev's in solitary and Thecla's dead. There's a simple explanation, of course. (And Severian continues to come up with simple explanations for the jungle magicks until it's almost too late.)
