Memories stored in the brain

Urth list · 6 messages · 2013-07-14 to 2013-07-20

2013urthThe Book of the New SunLee BermanAntónio Pedro MarquesGerry QuinnAshley CrillJeff Wilson
The thread: Posters debated where memories are held in Wolfe's Book of the New Sun, specifically how the alzabo transfers them. Ashley Crill argued memories pass by eating the brain, citing Severian's accession to the Autarchy, and Gerry Quinn countered that the alzabo can take memories from either body or brain, with brain-eating more effective given a better elixir. Jeff Wilson held that Vodalus's followers received memories through eaten flesh even without brains, citing Ultan's remark about fingers and hands. Lee Berman backed the view that memory is stored throughout the body, using Ultan's hologram analogy and the feast at Vodalus's house where Thecla's whole body is served. The thread ends without resolution, with the body-wide reading gaining the most textual support.
  • Ashley Crill: memories pass by eating the brain, not stored elsewhere in the body
  • Gerry Quinn: alzabo can take memories from body or brain, brain works better with a better elixir
  • Jeff Wilson: memories come through any eaten flesh, per Ultan's fingers-and-hands remark
  • Lee Berman: memory is stored throughout the body, per Ultan's hologram analogy and the feast

Themes: Memory and forgetting, Beasts and the animal in us, Myth and folklore sources

2013-07-20 Lee Berman replying to Ashley Crill #

Argues that a memory is stored throughout the body rather than only in the brain, using Ultan's hologram analogy from his talk on eating the dead. Supports Entonio's reading by pointing to the feast at Voladulus' house, where Thecla's whole body is roasted and served, so each guest would have eaten far more than a small brain portion.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Memories are stored in every part of the body

I think Entonio is correct. It doesn't have to be the brain. In his conversation about eating the dead, Ultan likens it to a (70'style) hologram plate in which the entire picture is stored in each and every piece, no matter how small. So you would think any body portion would do.

At Voladulus' feast, isn't Thecla's whole body roasted
and served? Her head remains intact enough for Severian
to recognize her. Plus, it seems like a pretty big party and I get the impression they each got more to eat than a 1/50 portion of her brain. Didn't they get legs, thighs, breasts, etc.?
