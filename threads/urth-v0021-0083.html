1998-11-23 Paul C Duggan #

Jokingly remarks on the GURPS New Sun roleplaying project, listing its departures from Wolfe such as a God called Pancreator, an interstellar empire, psi-powers and no time travel. Notes that Games Workshop's Tyranid names like Lictor and Zoanthropes borrow Wolfe's vocabulary, and adds a Thundarr joke and a homunculus reference to Mantis.
