1997-11-01 Alice Turner #

Confirms that the word Saltimbanque is a noun meaning mountebank or quack, citing the OED, and suggests that Wolfe's language might be traced to something snowy. Also proposes Paul Park's Celestis as written almost as an homage to The Fifth Head of Cerberus, and suggests readers ask Wolfe at conventions about his source language.
