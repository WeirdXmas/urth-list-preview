2001-04-03 Michael Straight #

Argues that the corn-genetics passage opening On Blue's Waters lays out the theme of the whole Long Sun series, noting that hybrids are stronger than their original strains but a dead end that forces a return to the originals. Recalls a half-remembered complaint about Horn's corn talk and says the poster rereading the book noticed it again in the first chapter.
proposes · close-textual-reading · myth-and-structure · theories: Hybrid corn stands for the theme of the Long Sun series

I can't find it in the archives, but I could have sworn there was someone complaining about Horn going on and on about corn genetics at the beginning of OBW. I'd forgotten all about it until I started looking at OBW for the second time, and here it is, Wolfe laying out the theme of the whole series in the first chapter.

I note that hybrids are stronger than the original strains, but ultimately a dead end, requiring you to go back to the original.

-Rostrum
