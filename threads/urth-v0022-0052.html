Themes: Unreliable narration

1998-12-17 Dave Lebling #

Argues that Severian's claim about the Tower of Torment is a technically defensible lie, since torturers enter and leave it daily and someone is occasionally released, so Severian escapes blame. Agrees with Greg Feeley's list of Severian's lies, admits he has not read that list, and adds that Severian's memory of the brown book stories suggests he read a different version.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian's Tower of Torment statement is a technical lie

I haven't seen Feeley's list, so I'm making my own mistakes here; however, I agree with him.

In further exculpation, it is stated elsewhere that every now and then (but not often) someone is released from the Tower, so Sev gets a pass there, too. Admittedly, this is sort of a (*cough*) clintonian evasion ("It depends on what you mean by 'all.'")

Hmm. Sev remembers various stories from the brown book, but not _TBotNS_? I guess that confirms that the version he was reading wasn't the same as the one we read!
