2008-07-05 Dave Tallman replying to Marc Aramini #

Proposes that the rows of numbers found underground near the seal of Pas in Long Sun might be a cipher, suggesting the sign of addition Silk makes could be used to sum them. Responds to Marc Aramini's question about the string of numbers and letters, and points to the number 7774 resembling the inverted calculator word for hell.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Numbers under the seal of Pas are a cipher; Silk's sign of addition unlocks the numbers

The two rows of aligned nunbers made me think of the "sign of addition" that Silk was always making. Maybe adding the numbers would get something.

5553 8783 4223 9700 34
2221 0401 1101 7276 56
7774 9184 5324 16976 90

The first number "7774" makes me think of the old inverted-number calculator message "7734" or "hell". This one would be "hlll". The 90 at the end is somewhat interesting, too.
