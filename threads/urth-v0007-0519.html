1998-02-05 Alice Turner #

Proposes organizing a panel of list members at the Disclave convention in Washington D.C., where Gene Wolfe is Guest of Honor, and asks who would be interested in attending. Notes the invitation came via co-guest Terry Bisson and mentions the alternative of the World Fantasy Convention in Baltimore, where Wolfe is also Guest of Honor.
proposes

Disclave is a "bookish" convention in Washington D.C. May1-3. Gene Wolfe is going to be Guest of Honor. I have been asked if I want to participate on a panel (this suggested to them by the other GoH, Terry Bisson, who is a good friend of mine, and who knows about our listserv group).

Well, I'm not much of a convention person, and usually I just do an editors panel on the rare occasions when I attend---but this time I wondered if any of you guys would be interested in coming, and if maybe we could put together a panel from the group. I'm sure they'd be interested. If *you* are, email me. Also email ideas on how to organize it, as I'm kind of a neophyte.

I'm sure most of you know that Wolfe is also GoH at the World Fantasy Convention in Baltimore at Halloween, and maybe you were planing to go to that instead if only one trip east is in the crystal ball. If we can't get it together for D.C., maybe we could do that one. But the advantage here is that I've actually been *asked*! Also, it will be much smaller, which could be a plus or minus.

Anyway, I'm going to wait for feedback before getting back to them.

-alga-
