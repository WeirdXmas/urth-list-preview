1998-10-26 Peter Westlake #

Argues that Pike's ghost is simply the ghost of Pike, lingering from unfinished business and linked to Mucor, since Pike first appears just after Silk sees Mucor and only Mucor sees him the second time. Responds to Greg Neyman's newcomer question about Silk tracing circles on his cheek and Pike's ghost by offering a reading of the Pike-Mucor connection.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Pike's ghost is the ghost of Pike

For my money, Pike's ghost is the ghost of Pike. I think he's hanging around for the traditional reason: unfinished business in this world. It seems to be connected with Mucor; his first appearance is just after Silk has seen Mucor, IIRC, and only Mucor sees him the second time. There's a connection between them, of course, which is in the archives but you may not have seen it yet. Or you may have found it yourself, of course.

Peter.
