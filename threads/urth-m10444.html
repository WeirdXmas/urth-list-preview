2010-08-09 Lane Haygood replying to Jeff Wilson #

Jokes that a commenter on a blog reviewing a Book of the New Sun comic preview could be sent to the list for help, since the list offers much obfuscation. Says the artwork is not liked but the poster would still read the comic to compare it with their own imagined visions of the text.
chats

Is it bad that I want to log in to my little-used LJ account to post a link to the poor beleaugered soul in the comments, directing him/her to this list? I mean, we may not be able to shed any actual light on the reader's confusion with the BOTNS, but at least we can offer up pages upon pages of interesting obfuscation.

That said, not a terrible fan of the artwork, but I'd read it all the same, to see if the artists' visions match up to the one playing through my head when I read it.

LH

2010-08-09 Dave Lebling replying to Lane Haygood #

Argues that a newcomer should read the Book of the New Sun comic adaptation through on their own before receiving the list's many answers, and praises it as good and less damaging to the work than Jackson's Tolkien films. Responds to Lane Haygood's joking suggestion to post a link to the list for a confused reader, offering a mild rebuke and a view of the artwork that differs from Haygood's.
disputes

Yes, it's bad. Let him read it through at least once on his own before getting all the answers (and the different versions of the answers) here.

The comic is pretty good (unlike Lane I rather like the artwork), and does less damage to the work than Peter Jackson did to Tolkien. Only three issues were published, alas.

-- Dave Lebling, aka vizcacha
