Different versions of "Forlesen"

Urth list · 2 messages · 2000-03-06 to 2000-03-07

2000urthMichael Andre-DriussiDan Rabin

2000-03-06 Dan Rabin #

Compares the 1990s Orbit 14 printing of the story "Forlesen" with the longer Book of Days version reprinted in Castle of Days, noting the missing Abraham Beale and Mr. Fleer episodes. Guesses the shorter text was cut from the original submission, citing the Bet-your-life game references that the longer version explains.
proposes · close-textual-reading · author-and-biography · theories: Orbit 14 version was cut from original submission

I just finished reading "Forlesen" in _Orbit 14_, its original appearance. Compared with the version in _Gene Wolfe's Book of Days_ as reprinted in _Castle of Days_, this first published version is missing the encounters with Abraham Beale and with Mr. Fleer.

I would guess that the shorter version was cut down from the original submission, based on the several references to the "Bet-your-life" game in the shorter version, a game that gets a somewhat explanatory mention in the longer version that is absent in the shorter.

I wonder if he got paid for the missing ours...

-- Dan Rabin

2000-03-07 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks whether Dan Rabin will continue comparing textual variants after his work on "Forlesen" and notes that a reference guide records only one variant entry, concerning Free Live Free editions. Responds to Rabin's detective work by sharing interest and pointing to a possible variant in the Fifth Head of Cerberus novella versus its Orbit printing.
questions · close-textual-reading

Dan Rabin,

Wow, great detective work! I don't recall ever hearing about this difference. Are you going to continue in this vein of textual differences? I vaguely remember some talk about possible diffs between "5HC" novella in ORBIT and 5HC, but I can't recall if it was just speculation or somebody had actually gotten down to doing the work of looking.

If you didn't already know, Galactic Central's "Urth-Man Extraordinary" (UMX) only has one entry under Textual Variations, and it deals with the diffs between FREE LIVE FREE published by Ziesing, Tor, and the UK edition.

So in my copy of UMX, I'm writing down your notes on "Forlesen," and looking forward to any further findings.

=mantis=
