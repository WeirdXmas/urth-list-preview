2000-12-27 William H. Ansley #

Recommends a reading order for a newcomer who received Peace, Free Live Free and The Fifth Head of Cerberus, starting with Peace and then the others in publication order. Justifies the order by the poster's preference for reading books as they were written, noting that the short-story collection Strange Travelers fits in roughly.
answers

Definitely start with _Peace_. I would then read _The Fifth Head of Cerberus_, followed by _Free Live Free_ and _Strange Travelers_ (I hope you get it).

I am a strong proponent of reading books in the order they were written, and I think I have given them in this order above, or as close as possible, considering ST is a collection of short stories.

Enjoy!
