Themes: Power, rulers and politics

1996-12-09 Geoff Alex Cohen #

Argues that Wolfe's early novel Operation ARES and Exodus from the Long Sun share a plot pattern: a teacher drawn unwillingly into rebellion, and outside invaders who end up ruling. Points to a near-identical speech on gun rights given by Silk and by John, and suggests this may reveal Wolfe's politics.
proposes · author-and-biography · source-and-allusion · theories: Wolfe's early and late books repeat a rebellion plot

Has anyone noticed a striking similarity between Wolfe's first work, _Operation ARES_ and his most recent, _Exodus from the Long Sun_? (Or, really, the last two books of the Long Sun series).

In both books, the main character is a teacher who is unwillingly drawn into a rebellion against the tyrannical government. He is identified by the government as the leader of the rebellion, despite his denials.

In both books, outside invaders (the Chinese, the Trivigauntis) assist the rebels, but in the process, end up ruling.

The similarity struck me when Silk delivers his Second Amendment speech to one of the Councilors (Loris? Potto?); John from Operation ARES had delivered almost the identical sentiment.

We might draw some conclusions as to Wolfe's politics.

Geoff

1996-12-09 James Jordan #

Reports a private conversation in which Wolfe described himself as formerly a Buckley-style conservative who has grown broader in outlook, citing his remark about freedom. Also states Wolfe declined to rewrite Operation ARES and that its material appears in the Long Sun books, pointing to an interview posted on a fan website.
informs · author-and-biography · theories: Operation ARES material incorporated into Long Sun

Yes, you can. Wolfe told me that he used to be a Buckley-type conservative, but has become broader in his thinking over the years. He said, "I'm big on freedom, and we have lost a lot in recent years." See also Free, Life, Free.
I also asked him if he were going to re-write Operation ARES. He said no, but I think you are right that he has incorporated much of it in Long Sun. My whole interview with Wolfe is on Paul Duggan's Wolfe Page.

JBJordan
