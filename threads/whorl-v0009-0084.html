Themes: Myth and folklore sources

1999-02-20 Alice Turner #

Suggests that Wolfe and Edmund Spenser may have drawn on the same classical sources for the Talos legend, naming Apollonius Rhodius, Apollodorus, Plato and Graves as likely origins. Responds to Robert Patterson's point that Wolfe's Onomastics entry on Talos links to a guard robot and to Dr. Talos in The Sword of the Lictor, treating the shared-reading idea as a playful answer.
answers · source-and-allusion

Well, yeah, I bet they did. Maybe Apollonius Rhodus, Apollodorus or Plato, all of whom tell the story. Or, in Wolfe's case, Graves, who cites them.
