Merryn, and Wijzer

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

1999-10-12 Nicholas Gevers #

Argues that Merryn is widely taken to be Severian's sister, citing Wolfe's hint that she appears in The Short Sun, and suggests that Wijzer is Dutch rather than Scandinavian. Grounds the second point on the Dutch and Afrikaans word 'dorp' meaning town, and the first on a PEOPLE magazine discussion, without quoting the books directly.
disputes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Merryn is Severian's sister; Wijzer is Dutch, not Scandinavian

Two small points:
Merryn as Sev's sister: Wolfe has indicated elsewhere,
maybe in the PEOPLE magazine discussion, that Merryn
appears in SHORT SUN, and she is widely supposed to be
sister to Sev, Witch to his Torturer...
And Wijzer as a Scandinavian: isn't he Dutch? "Dorp"
is Dutch for "town", I think; it means that in
Afrikaans.

1999-10-12 James Jordan #

Suggests that Wijzer's convoluted speech points to Afrikaans rather than Dutch, drawing on the poster's memory of Dutch, and adds that the name may come from a language like Afrikaans. Responds to a message that identified Wijzer as Dutch and noted that 'dorp' means town in Dutch and Afrikaans.
supports · source-and-allusion

Right. Sorry. Given his syntax, I suspect Afrikaans, just because what little Dutch I used to know, I don't recall as so convoluted.
