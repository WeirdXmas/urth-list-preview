Jokingly supports the thread's claim that Father Inire is Jeeves by offering a Wodehouse passage from Joy in the Morning about a man materializing at one's side as a spurious source. Continues the earlier Tintin post's habit of inventing fake sources for Wolfe's ideas, and closes with a John 8:32 quote about truth and freedom.

-P. G. Wodehouse, JOY IN THE MORNING

1999-11-21 William H. Ansley #

Proposes mapping characters from P. G. Wodehouse's Bertie Wooster stories onto Wolfe's New Sun, arguing Father Inire is Jeeves and Severian is Bertie, both missing obvious things. Offers further pairings such as Agia, Baldanders, Abia and Jonas, while admitting the exercise is pointless and hard.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Father Inire is Jeeves; Severian is Bertie Wooster

Aha! Another Wodehouse fan (or, at least, reader).

What other "Bertie Wooster Universe" (or we may need to widen it to the whole "P. G. Wodehouse Universe") to "New Sun Universe" character mappings can we do?

Well, if Father Inre is Jeeves then I suppose Severian is Bertie Wooster. They share a tendency to miss things that seem obvious to the reader, at least.

Agia = Roberta "Bobby" Wickham

Baldanders = D'Arcy "Stilton" Cheesewright

Abia = Aunt Agatha

Jonas = Gussie Fink-Nottle?

This is turning out to be not only one of my more pointless exercises, but quite difficult as well. Wolfe and Wodehouse certainly differ widely (one might say wildly) in style, subject matter and intent in their writing.

William Ansley
