TSH: Rip van Winkle and John Duns Scotus

Urth list · 6 messages · 2010-05-03 to 2010-05-05

2010urthGwern BranwenStephen HoythalassocratRoy C. Lackey
The thread: Stephen Hoy proposed that the TSH character Zwart is concealed behind names tied to John Duns Scotus, including Ieuian, the Dunn brothers and the alias Alexander Skotus, and linked Bax's fox familiar Winker/Winkle to Urashima Taro, Inari and Rip van Winkle. Gwern Branwen rejected the fox and Japanese parallels, finding no kitsune connection or time passage. Roy C. Lackey argued Dunn came through adoption and that Martha's surname Murrey fits the Black line better, so Dunn cannot relate to Zwart. Mr Thalassocrat speculated Ted is Goldwurm, then recast the Scotus name-tracing as a playful easter egg not needed to understand the book, and corrected the spelling of Martha's maiden name to Murray. The thread ended with the objections standing and no consensus.
  • Stephen Hoy: Zwart is hidden behind Duns Scotus-linked names and Winker/Winkle parallels Rip van Winkle and Urashima Taro
  • Gwern Branwen: the fox and Japanese parallels do not hold; no kitsune link or time passage
  • Roy C. Lackey: Dunn came by adoption, so it cannot link to the Zwart Black line; Murrey is closer to Black
  • Mr Thalassocrat: Ted is Goldwurm working in real estate; Scotus is a playful easter egg, not essential

Themes: Names and their meanings, Stories within stories, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

2010-05-03 Stephen Hoy #

Proposes that the TSH character Zwart is hidden behind names linked to John Duns Scotus, by lining up Ieuian, the Dunn brothers and the alias Alexander Skotus. Also links Bax's fox familiar, Winker/Winkle, to Urashima Taro and the Japanese god Inari as a Rip van Winkle parallel.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Zwart is hidden behind the name John Duns Scotus; Bax's fox familiar echoes Urashima Taro

Half-baked thoughts, thought I'd share them in case someone finds something useful thereby.

The philosopher/theologian John Duns Scotus is found by lining up three iterations of the Zwart Black line, - Ieuian, a representation of John in Welsh, which matches his brother Emlyn's Welsh name. - the two Dunns, Baxter and George
- Alexander Skotus (presumably an alias used by Zwart)

'Alexander' heads round to Johannes Duns Scotus via Scotus' treatises elaborating the views of Aristotle. The Greek philosopher Aristotle tutored Alexander the Great. I'm not sure what more to make of this--I can't get past thoughts about Don Defore on the TV show Hazel.

As to Bax's familiar, Winker/Winkle suggests a link to the story of the Urashima Taro, a Japanese Rip van Winkle who went away to fairyland for a few days and returned home only to find years had passed.

Strengthening this
supposition is the second half of the fox's name, Inari, which refers to a Japanese god of success & prosperity, often depicted as an old man riding a white fox.

2010-05-04 Roy C. Lackey replying to Mr Thalassocrat #

Disputes Stephen Hoy's proposal that the surname Dunn links the Zwart Black line to John Duns Scotus, noting that Dunn came to Bax and George by adoption. Points to Martha's original surname Murrey as closer in colour to Black, and says Dunn cannot be related to the Zwart Black line as Hoy suggests.
disputes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Dunn surname links Zwart Black line to Duns Scotus

That may be, but the surname Dunn came to Bax and George by way of adoption (17). I don't see how the surname is or can be related to the Zwart Black line. Martha said she was a Scot, but her original surname of Murrey is closer, in terms of color, to Black than Dunn.

-Roy

2010-05-04 thalassocrat replying to Stephen Hoy #

Speculates that Ted in The Sorcerer's House is really Goldwurm and worked in real estate for Ambrosius at A & I Inc, and asks what Ted did for a living. Builds on Stephen Hoy's Duns Scotus name-tracing by citing Bax's Scots descent and etymologies of Dunn, Ted and Doris, and playfully ends with a sitcom joke.
proposes · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Ted is Goldwurm; Ted worked for Ambrosius in real estate; Ambrosius is the A in A & I Inc

It reminds me that Bax describes himself to Martha as being of Scots descent. He also says that the name "Dunn" comes from a town there (I think). Wikipedia says that Duns Scotus was named after Duns in Scotland (so I guess his name is really "John of Duns in Scotland").

Less interestingly: I assume that "Ted" is short for "Theodore", "gift of God". But presumably "Doris" also comes from the Greek for gift (despite the silly assertion on name sites on the net that its best meaning is "Dorian woman"). No idea what that means; probably nuthin'.

But off on a tangent - do we know what Ted did for a living? My guess is real estate, and that "Ambrosius" when he wasn't in faerie was the "A" in "A & I Inc", and that Ted worked for him. And also, as I said before, that Ted is/was Goldwurm.

I have an image of small town real estate sharks with second lives as sorcerers fighting magical battles with each other. Apart from anything else, it sounds like a good concept for a sit-com ...
