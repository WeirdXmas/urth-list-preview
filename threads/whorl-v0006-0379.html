Financial crisis

1997-06-22 Alice Turner #

Asks what would happen to Silk's already chaotic Whorl if its wealth were diverted to repairing the circuitry or building the lifeboat taluses needed to leave. Raises fiscal bankruptcy as a further crisis awaiting Silk and asks how the taluses could be paid for, treating economics as a plot problem.
questions · chronology-and-plot-logic

Oh, and there's another crisis that awaits Silk---fiscal bankruptcy. What happens to a society, already in chaos, with its infrastructure unmaintainable, when its wealth is suddenly co-opted to another purpose (repairing the circuitry of the Whorl or the lifeboats that make it possible to leave the Whorl?

Those taluses are going to be essential! (But how to pay for them?)

-alga-
