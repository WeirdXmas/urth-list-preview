Blue & Green same as worlds in Fifth Head of Cerberus?

Whorl list · 2 messages · 1997-07-02 to 1997-07-03

1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunPeter CashAlastair Reynolds

Themes: Robots, androids and artificial life, Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

1997-07-02 Peter Cash #

Proposes that the Whorl's destination is the double-planet system of The Fifth Head of Cerberus, explained by a long interval in which shape-shifting inhumi absorbed Blue and Green's human population. Grounds this in Quetzal's skill at mimicking humans, his knowledge of human culture, and the absence of native humans on Blue, and suggests Quetzal may be confused about his own nature.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Inhumi absorbed Blue and Green's humans over time; Quetzal is an inhumi unaware of his nature

Actually, I could suggest a way that the worlds depicted in Fifth Head could be the same as the destination of the Whorl. Assume that the arrival of the Whorl is a considerable time after the events depicted in Fifth Head. Suppose that in the interval, the inhumi (the shape-shifters) absorbed all the humans on Blue and Green. That would account for Quetzal's ability to mimick humans so well, and his knowledge of human culture, and for the absence of indigenous humans on Blue. In fact, Quetzal is probably pretty confused about what he is, if he's one of the shape shifters described in Fifth Head...

This is one of the questions that will be asked of Mr. Wolfe, so we'll see. (Then again, maybe we won't!)

Rock
