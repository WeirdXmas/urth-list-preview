Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2008-06-26 Marc Aramini #

Speculates that the scene between Ultan and Severian about the drachm of sticky fluid that preserves a man's face through generations hints at something hidden, though doubts Ultan belongs in Severian's family tree. Ties the fluid talk to the carrot woman at the grave and the alzabo, resting the hunch on the wording of that encounter.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Ultan belongs in Severian's family tree

I never really put much speculation into the relationship between ultan and the carrot woman at the grave, but the conversation between ultan and sev about the drachm of sticky fluid that insures a man&#39;s face will survive many generations certainly becomes more relevant if there is a potential application within the scene. Alas, I give little credence to putting Ultan in Sev&#39;s family tree as of yet, but something creepy and important is lurking in the subtext of that encounter beyond foreshadowing the alzabo, I am sure.
