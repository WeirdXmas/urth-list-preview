Argues that the Lemony Snicket books are mildly funny but clearly aimed at children, with one joke repeated so often that the poster quit after book five. Replies in a thread moving from Pullman's daemons to Snicket, addressed to Dan'l, and notes that Lemony Snicket is a pseudonym with a faked author history.
informs

Dan'l,

The Lemony Snicket books are mildly funny, but clearly aimed at kids. It's one joke ("Dear reader, this book is not a happy story, maybe you better not read it") repeated many, many times. After a while it gets uninteresting--I quit reading them when he was done after book five.

"Lemony Snicket" is of course a pseudonym, and comes with a faked history for the fake author.

-John
