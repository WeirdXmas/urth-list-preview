Themes: Genre: science fiction, fantasy, both

1999-12-03 David Duffy #

Defends Paul Park's use of old-fashioned words like "horse" and "petrol" in his science fiction, arguing they are translations rather than literal mammals and hydrocarbons. Responds to an earlier criticism of Park's science by saying the archaic terms create a deliberate frisson, and mentions the poster's complaint after rereading Brian Aldiss's Helliconia books.
disputes · genre-and-craft

In defence of Park, it is pretty clear that words like "horse" and "petrol" don't refer to the mammal and hydrocarbon we know and love. Perhaps destrier would have been better, but doesn't give the frisson when you realize halfway through (about the claws IIRC). I decided gunpowder must be another translation. My gripe is after reading Park then rereading the Helliconia set.

David Duffy.
