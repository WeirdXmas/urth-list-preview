1999-06-15 Alice Turner #

Argues that the Sugarland at the end of the story is not the Texas town but a mythical place like the Big Rock Candy Mountain, meaning heaven. Responds to Cliff's remark about Sugarland by urging him to keep looking for material on "Eyeflash" rather than offering evidence for the reading.
disputes · source-and-allusion · theories: Sugarland is the Big Rock Candy Mountain, meaning heaven

Cliff,

There's some very good material on "Eyeflash"---keep looking. But I think Sugarland, in context, is the Big Rock Candy Mountain. You know, er, Heaven.

Alice
