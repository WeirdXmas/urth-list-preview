Supports an earlier list suggestion that banks of oars might evoke wings, citing a passage in Soldier of the Mist where a ship's oars with foam make her look like a bird with four white wings. Uses this quote as evidence for the idea that a ship could be heraldically volant, linking it to the Tzadkiel's masts and oars.
supports · close-textual-reading · theories: Oars and masts on the Tzadkiel suggest wings

Apropos of the question of the necessity of wings on a heraldic representation of anything "volant," I find this quote in Soldier of the Mist (62): "[H]er oars, with the foam on them, made her look like a bird with four white wings." Someone else here suggested that banks of oars might suggest wings (and that the many masts on the Tzadkiel might suggest both banks of oars and wings). This quote lends support to that idea.
