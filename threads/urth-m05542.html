Themes: Unreliable narration, Women, sex and gender

2008-01-05 Dan #

Argues that Novia could avoid rape on the pirate ships because any attempt would cost the attacker his life, and that Chris's killing of Michet would deter others. Responds to Matthew Keeley's question about why Novia is not raped more often, and suggests her journey is more problematic and her story doubtful, perhaps a disguised prostitute situation.
disputes · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Novia's narrated story is doubtful, perhaps a disguised prostitute; Novia avoided rape because attackers would be killed

I'm not sure about the other times she was left alone but if they did rape her they would have to kill her or she would kill them. And after what Chris did to Michet they wouldn't risk it. Lesages pirates probably did, but if they really didn't, that plays into a theory I am developing on him.

I think her journey over proves more problematic. I doubt her story. I envision more of a "Mr. Prostitute" situation, as an example from pirate movie lore.

Dan
