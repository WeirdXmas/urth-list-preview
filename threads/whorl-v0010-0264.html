2000-02-11 Alice Turner #

Relays a long set of agreements and opinions from The Birthday Bear on Long Sun matters: Pike's identity, Babbie's absence, fuligin and the inhumi's breeding and feeding cycles between Green and Blue. Quotes the Bear's replies, adding that Krait's remark about inhumi preferring human blood is the basis for the discussion. Responds to Vizcacha's earlier post and reports the Bear's views rather than advancing its own argument.
informs · theories: Pike is really Pike, not a disguise; Inhumi fade between worlds like astral projection

2000-02-16 Peter Westlake #

Proposes that inhumi take on memories and intelligence from people bitten by their mothers while still in the egg, so Krait's resemblance to Sinew comes from that rather than blood alone. Responds to Alice Turner's earlier idea that Krait models himself on Horn, reasoning that Krait mimics Horn and so comes out like Sinew.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Inhumi absorb memories from people bitten by mothers; Krait mimics Horn and resembles Sinew

Thank you!
[...]

To clarify, I don't mean *really* wrong, just a little different: that inhumi take up the memories/intelligence of people bitten by their mothers while they are in the egg (or whatever). Is that what you think too?

I did wonder how Krait came out quite so much like Sinew if all he had to work with was the genetic material in his blood. I like this explanation; Krait mimics Horn and comes out like Sinew. Thanks!

SBear.
