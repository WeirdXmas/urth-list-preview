2000-11-27 Alice Turner #

Asks what the word "blattid" means, noting it is absent from the OED, in a reply to Blattid's sarcastic remark about cheesy authors who insert themselves into their books. Refers to The Fifth Head of Cerberus and "The Last Thrilling Story" as the examples Blattid cited, but offers no interpretation of them.

2000-11-28 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Explains that the word "blattid" refers to the cockroach family Blattidae, quoting Alga's line from The Book of the Long Sun about the Blattidaea and a folksy phrase for cockroaches. Responds to a question asking what the term means and whether it appears in the OED, supplying the zoological origin and the in-book usage.
answers · source-and-allusion

Alga emerged from her pond and asked:

One of the Blattidaea, of course. Or, as my dear old
grammaw would say, "One of dem cock-a-roaches."

--Blattid
Blatt! Blatt!
