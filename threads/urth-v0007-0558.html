1998urthThe Book of the New SunTony Ellis

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

1998-02-16 Tony Ellis #

Argues that the White Fountain draws its energy from another universe rather than from a black hole in the Old Sun, and that Severian returns to the same old universe he left. Grounds this in the claim that the Fountain is created in Yesod, as shown in The Urth of the New Sun, and in the idea that Yesod is the source universe.
