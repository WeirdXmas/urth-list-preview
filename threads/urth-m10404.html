Sister?

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2010-08-08 Lee Berman #

Argues that Severian may have a sister, citing possibilities that she is the witch Merryn from Return to the Whorl or someone Severian had sex with, and that Wolfe leaves the choice to readers. Responds to Jeff Wilson's point that Severian's lack of mention of a sister suggests he has none, offering alternative explanations that fit the hints about Dorcas and Agilus.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian's sister is the witch Merryn; Severian had sex with his sister

This is a plausible explanation and it surely could be what Gene Wolfe had in mind with regard to "severa".

Among the most popular alternate explanations I've seen are that Severian's sister is the witch, Merryn as hinted at in Return to the Whorl. Another is that he discovered that his sister is someone he had sex with and doesn't want to openly reveal it, managing to drop (conscious or not) hints about it, similar to the hints regarding Dorcas being his grandmother and similar to the hint Agilus drops that he and his sister have a sexual relationship.

I think Wolfe leaves it to each reader's personal preference which explanation to lean toward. There is even latitude for those who are comfortable with the ambiguity of three (or more) parallel explanations and no need for choosing one over the others.
