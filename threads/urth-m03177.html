Corrects an earlier claim that Auk kicked Chenille, pointing out that in Long Sun he confesses during the shriving to having kicked a man to death. Quotes Auk's shriving speech, in which he says he was going to kick Chenille but did not, and that the man's death was part of what he told Patera Pike.
