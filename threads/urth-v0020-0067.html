Other military boondoggles of the New Sun

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-10-19 to 1998-10-19

1998urthPaul C Duggan

Themes: War and soldiers

1998-10-19 Paul C Duggan #

Lists several military oddities in The Book of the New Sun, including dwarves riding on blind giants and a cavalry of women mounted on arsinothers, and asks how the arsinothers avoid being burned by pyrotechnic weapons when the cavalry horses are engineered for speed. Raises the question of whether the arsinothers are fast enough to survive, contrasting them with the fast horses, and jokes about a wargame using these units.
questions · close-textual-reading

Anybody have a problem with:

1. Dwarves on the shoulders of BLIND giants?

2. shock cavalry made up of women riding arsinothers (iirc)?

2a. Cavalry in the New Sun is ostensibly super fast genetically engineered horses that can run 100mph. This is to counteract the high Rate of Fire of the pyrotechnic weaponry. The arsinothers are not genetically engineered to be super fast (are they?) so how do they avoid getting roasted?

Paul "a miniture wargame using new sun units would be visually appealing at least" Duggan
