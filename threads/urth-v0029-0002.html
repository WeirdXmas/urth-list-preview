"The Computer ..." and where to get it

2000-05-09 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Lists the publications where Wolfe's poem "The Computer Iterates the Greater Trumps" appeared, from a 1977 review and a 1978 anthology to several 1980s books and a chapbook, and suggests which might be easiest to obtain. Answers Ori's question about whether the poem is a chapbook or anthologized by giving a bibliographic list drawn from an earlier source.
answers

Hello there Ori,

Happy to see you are still here!

From our old friend "Urth-Man Extraordinary":

The Computer Iterates The Greater Trumps
"Speculative Poetry Review" 1977
THE ANTHOLOGY OF SPECULATIVE POETRY, Frazier, ,1978
PLAN(E)T ENGINEERING (1984)
BURNING WITH A VISION, Frasier, Owlswick Press, 1984
FOR ROSEMARY [United Mythologies,] (1988)

That's the list. Depending on your situation, I'm not sure which might be easier for you to obtain. FOR ROSEMARY is a chapbook all full of Wolfe's poetry, published by UM in Canada; PE has a few other poems besides the one, and a map of the Commonwealth, and an essay on Urthian things.

Happy hunting!

=mantis=
