The thread: Stuart Hamm asked for an old list article on corn-silk genes and plant life on Green, and reminisced about the Whorl's shape and the Rama comparison. Matthew Groves pointed him to Marc Arimini's essay on that theory and shared a compiled list of Short Sun name translations. David Stockhoff argued that the ambiguous term shipstone may hide asteroid matter, so the Whorl was hollowed by blasting or plasma and perhaps built by robots, and asked how builders could orient terrain 'up' from an irregular base. Craig Brewer proposed that the Hiero-folk and Yesod link the Long and Short Suns through human evolution, and wondered whether the Outsider is the Increate. The thread ended without a clear resolution, drifting from the Whorl's construction to the wider series links.
  • Stuart Hamm: asks for the corn-silk and plant-life article and recalls the Whorl's shape

  • Craig Brewer: Hiero-folk and Yesod link the Long and Short Suns through human evolution

Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Evolution and the future of humanity, Gods, angels and the Increate

2009-01-22 Craig Brewer replying to Matthew Groves #

Proposes that the Hiero-folk and Yesod may be present in the Long and Short Sun books through human evolution on Blue and Green, which would tie the series together more closely. Also wonders whether the Outsider is the same God as the Increate, suspecting the Hiero-folk's God is not the real one. Responds to a post about the Green-is-Urth theory and a link to an article.
proposes · theology-and-religion · myth-and-structure · theories: Green is Urth; Hiero-folk evolution continues in Long and Short Sun; The Outsider is not the Increate

Thanks for pointing to this! I can't believe I've never read it before -- I've looked at Wynn's site a number of times and always overlooked that link.

But the potential that Green is Urth starts to address an issue that's been bothering me: Where are the Hiero-folk (or Yesod in general) in Long/Short Sun? We had this huge cosmic conspiracy in New Sun that turned out to be the "real" story behind the surface narrative. But that "real" story apparently even disappears in the subtext of the sequel series.

However...given that the whole point of Yesod/Hiero-folk is to further human evolution for their own purposes, perhaps we actually are seeing evolution happen in what occurs with the Neighbor/inhumi/human evolution interactions on Blue/Green. I haven't worked out the details yet, but this idea at least ties the series together more tightly than I've felt that they were in the past.

I still wonder, however, if the Outsider is the same "God" that the Hiero-folk call the Increate. I know all the general signs would suggest that they are (one true creator god, etc.), but I've always had a sneaking suspicion that the Hiero's "God" wasn't the real deal.
