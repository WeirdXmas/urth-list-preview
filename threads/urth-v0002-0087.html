1997-06-08 James Jordan #

Asks where and when Wolfe's story "Bluesberry Jam" was published and how to obtain a copy, noting Wolfe named it among his favorites along with "Detective of Dreams" and "Westwind". Addresses Mantis directly and also asks who "Michael" is, referring to a signature in Mantis's earlier post.
questions

List (especially Mantis, who can probably answer this in his sleep):

Gene said that his favorite stories are "Detective of Dreams" and "Westwind," which stands to reason since (a) they relate explicitly to his beliefs, and (b) they encapsulate the perspective of all his major fiction. He also mentioned "Bluesberry Jam," which evidently has not been anthologized. I don't recall it. Anybody know where/when it was published, and/or how to get a copy?

BTW, Mantis, who is "Michael"? :-) (That's how you signed your last post on Urth.)

1997-06-09 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Identifies the title and publication details of Wolfe's story "Bluesberry Jam" and says a character called Michael is a mask of Russario, answering a reader's question about the name. Gives the anthology as Space Opera, edited by McCaffrey and Scarborough, published by Daw in 1996, and praises the story briefly.
answers · source-and-allusion · theories: Michael is a mask of Russario

Reply: Item #2076850 from [email]@INET00#

Nutria,

"Michael" is one of the masks that Russario, fiendish imp, dons when I'm not looking.

"Bluesberry Jam" is in SPACE OPERA, the paperback anthology edited by Anne McCaffrey and Elizabeth Ann Scarborough, published 1996 by Daw. Available NOW.

(And that =is= a great story, as I'm sure I've said before!)

=mantis=
