2001-02-14 Johan Akesson #

Argues that Jahlee's attack on Nettle was a crime of passion rather than an act of her inhuman nature, because she had long wanted Horn/Silk. Cites two quotations from page 315 and 316 about Jahlee's wanting him and having too many wives in Gaon, and proposes that Horn's rescue from the grave kindled her love.

My first post,

although I=B4ve lurked for a while.

Greetings all but let=B4s move straight to business.
Spectacled Bear beat me at the finishing line but nevertheless:

Indeed Jahlee=B4s attempt at Nettle was a Crime of Passion and not an = act of her inhuma nature. The quotes that support this on p315:

"I wanted him so long..."

Meaning Horn/Silk

and

p316

"Something like a smile touched Jahlee=B4s lips. "He had so many, Rani, = in Gaon. I couldn=B4t kill them all."=20

With Rani, probably the female form of Rajan (Queen), Jahlee is = adressing Nettle. Jahlee states that the Rajan (Horn/silk) had to many = wifes in Gaon for her to kill them all. Perahaps Rajans rescue (saving = her from the hole/grave) kindled her interest since she according to the = quote presumably was in love with Horn/Silk as early as in Gaon.

Johan
