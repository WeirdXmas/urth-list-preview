Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

1997-02-07 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that the Quetzal-as-Pike ghost theory requires implausible double-dipping and asks how such a charade would help Quetzal, while proposing a family tree for Severian: Ouen father, Catherine mother, Dorcas paternal grandmother, the Lake boatman paternal grandfather. Responds to a reply on Dave and Jahi's Long Sun exchange, citing Shadow of the Torturer and Claw passages for support.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Quetzal is also the ghost of Pike; Severian's father is Ouen; Severian's mother is Catherine; Dorcas is Severian's paternal grandmother

Glad to help out.

Paternal grandfather = the boatman on the Lake of Birds (essentially "Charonus")
