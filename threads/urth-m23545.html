Lee Berman says his own view agrees with Nate Jarvis's objection to Jerry Friedman's claim that the inhumi would die out if the people of Blue and Green stopped being parasitic and dishonest. Berman concedes Friedman's reasoning matches a Wolfe quotation from the Gevers interview, suggesting readers simply accept it at face value even though Berman cannot.
