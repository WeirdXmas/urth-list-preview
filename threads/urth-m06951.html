mother and kypris

Urth list · 1 messages · 2008-11-25 to 2008-11-25

2008urthThe Book of the Long SunMarc Aramini

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Hidden identities and parentage

2008-11-25 Marc Aramini #

Proposes that Kypris and Mamelta, both blue-eyed, may be linked, suggesting Kypris might be Mother herself or love in the sense of motherhood. Cites a passage in Epiphany of the Long Sun where Kypris is called Hyacinth and Mother, a line about the monarch wanting a son, and a tall blue-eyed father, and seeks another dream sequence identifying them together.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Kypris is Mother with blue eyes; Kypris and Mamelta are identified together

well, mamelta and kypris both have blue eyes. I found on page 220 of epiphany of the long sun &quot;he danced with kypris, who was hyacinth and Mother, too.&quot; which can either mean she really is Mother with blue eyes or she is love as mother is love - intentionally ambiguous. on page 208 we know &quot;the monarch wanted a son to succeed him.&quot; and this is probably not one of his existing bad brood but a bright sun with his mistress of love.

on page 226 his father is tall and blue eyed and the woman who does not speak also shows up. the old calde did not have blue eyes.

I am trying to find if kypris and mamelta are identified together in another dream sequence.
