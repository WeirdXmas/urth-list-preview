Quetzal & his body suit

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-04-11 to 1997-04-11

1997whorlKieran Cleary

Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

1997-04-11 Kieran Cleary #

Proposes, half-jokingly, that Quetzal in Calde of the Long Sun is a scarlet-plumed serpent wearing a human body suit. Grounds this in quoted descriptions of his long wrinkled neck, noseless profile, swaying head, and the makeup applied to shape his face and neck, asking whether putty and foundation could really disguise a serpent.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Quetzal is a serpent inside a human body suit

vizcacha:

more out of mischievousness than anything else,

You might expect some stretching and pulling when a human body is used as a piece of apparel

and some wear and tear over the years...

sure, there is a snake inside...

because his form belies his innards...

Is it really feasible that, with deft application of putty and foundation that a scarlet-plumed serpent could masquerade as a human? <g>

Lasrach
