Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2012-04-05 Marc Aramini #

Proposes that Pete Palmer, the character in the story 'Changeling', was born in 1931 like Peter Palmieri, who appears in the year Maria Palmieri was born. Grounds this on a web search turning up an actor named Pete Palmer and Gene Wolfe, both born in 1931, while noting that the character was said to be in fourth grade in 1944.
proposes · puzzle-solving · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Pete Palmer was born in 1931

I was thinking about how the Peter Palmieri showed up in 1931 when Maria Palmieri was born, but Pete Palmer was supposedly in the 4th grade in 1944 but not in the picture.

So i put in google Pete Palmer born 1931. And Pete Palmer, the actor who played "Lil Abner" was born in 1931. As was ... ... ... Gene Wolfe. So I think Pete Palmer was born in 1931.
