VanderMeer's City of Saints / Story Suites

2002-05-12 Jeff Prucher #

Notes that Claude Lalumière's review of VanderMeer's City of Saints & Madmen proposes the term "mosaic" for linked story collections, filling the same gap Le Guin addressed with "story suite." Wonders what it means that two competing terms appeared within weeks of each other for a form that is not new.
informs

In a curious confluence of threads, I'd like to mention that in his review of this book
(http://www.locusmag.com/2002/Reviews/Lalumiere03_VanderMeer.html)Claude Lalumiere posits the word "mosaic" to describe "books made up of previously published stories that form a whole more unified than the standard story collection," attempting to fill in the same perceived gap in the language that Le Guin coined "story suite" to fill. I'm not sure what it means that there have been two alternative suggestions posited within a month or two of each other, since this is not exactly a new type of book.

-Jeff Prucher
