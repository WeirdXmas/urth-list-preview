Granny? Dorcas

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-10-09 to 1998-10-09

1998urthThe Book of the New SunJon Camfield

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Unreliable narration

1998-10-09 Jon Camfield #

Proposes that Dorcas may not be the true mind inhabiting her body, suggesting Severian's imagination filled in her memories and that she could be Severian's sister resembling their mother. Grounds this in the boatkeeper's account of Cas, Dorcas following Severian around dazed as Miles does later, and the Ouen comment about resemblance, while admitting the idea is far-fetched.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Dorcas is not the true mind in her body; Dorcas is Severian's sister; Severian fills in Dorcas's memories

Ugh. a thought occured to me. What if Dorcas isn't the right body for the mind? What is Sev subsconsciously filled in "Cas's" memories as the boatkeeper had related them (note Dorcas' similar action to Miles later on: follow Sev around, dazed)? Dorcas' later memories could be her true memories emerging, or maybe continuing being written by Sev during the journey.

*shudder* Could Dorcas be Severian's sister, who happens to resemble her mother greatly (explaining the Ouen comment)?

OK, that's pretty far out, yes, and again I'm kicking myself for not having my books available to dig through for more references.
