Music, and Kabballah in the BotNS

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-05-28 to 1998-05-28

1998urthThe Book of the New SunC R Culver

1998-05-28 C R Culver #

Responds to Mark Kelly by affirming a liking for Dead Can Dance's songs by Brendan Perry while rejecting the idea that Wolfe's readers must enjoy the group because of gothic mood, arguing the Book of the New Sun is not ordinary sword-and-sorcery. Ends by asking whether anyone owns the Encyclopedia Judaica to define the Hebrew word "Madregot" that a rabbi suggested.
questions · source-and-allusion

<I noticed that Christopher R. Culver <[email]> on this list has a quote from Brendan Perry (half of the group) in his .sig., so I can't be the only person who likes both Gene Wolfe and Dead Can Dance!>

Well, I enjoy the music of Dead Can Dance (at least the songs that Perry writes; I can't stand Lisa's nonsense), but that's not to say everyone here will enjoy it. Saying that BotNS people will like DCD just because of the latter's dark, gothic atmosphere degenerates the BotNS into your usual sword- and-sorcery epic, which it is most certainly not! Incidentally, I've been meaning to change that signature macro for some time, and will prolly do so after I mail this post.

BTW, does anyone have a copy of the Encyclopedia Judaica? A rabbi recommended I consult that for a definition fo "Madregot," which appears to be a Hebrew word (my web searches for it turn up Hebrew pages).
