2014-10-29 Jeff Wilson replying to Norwood, Frederick Hudson #

Quotes a passage in which the chiliarch asks whether the protagonist now has the freedom of the House Absolute, and the Autarch replies that he is a creature of evil, as the speakers are too. Offered as evidence in reply to Norwood's claim that Wolfe likes writing bad protagonists.
supports · close-textual-reading

The chiliarch inquired, "Autarch, has he the freedom of the House Absolute now?"
"He does. He is indeed a creature of evil; but so are you, and so am I."

Argues that Baden, the protagonist of The Tree is My Hat, called Bad, breaks all ten commandments despite acting selflessly at the end and coming to a bad end. Notes that the poster caught only seven commandments on first reading and that Wolfe seems to favor bad protagonists, citing no specific passage.
