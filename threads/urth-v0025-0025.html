1999-04-07 Clifford Drane #

Argues that Dorcas may be dead or in stasis rather than alive, using Charon's remark that the water pickles the bodies, the popping eyes, and the weighted, glued corpses to suggest she was drowned or dumped by Charon. Also notes Severian grasping the sword and Dorcas's hand together as a possible Lady of the Lake parallel, and a friend's observation that Dorcas follows Severian like a dog, paralleling Triskele.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Dorcas was drowned or dumped by Charon; Dorcas parallels the Lady of the Lake

mentioned before? Severian grasps both her hand and the sword at the same time, almost like she gave it to him. Any thoughts about that?

Yikes. I just read the Lexicon Urthus entry about Terminus Est. Guess that idea had been bandied about before.

Also, to continue my ruminations about Dorcas being in stasis or actually dead, 'Charon' tells the group that "the water... pickles them." I guess that could mean it acts like formaldehyde (nothing could survive in that!) and simply preserves bodies. But the eyes popping open remark seems very important. Either she was so ill she was submerged by mistake, and the shock of the water woke her (then she drowned), or she was dumped by 'Charon'. It's also mentioned that bodies are weighed down with lead shot in the stomach, and the eyes glued shut. Dorcas never complained of being unusually heavy, and obviously her eyes weren't cememented shut. So, I guess her being in the lake IS indicative of foul play. How tragic. And 'Charon' seems full of remorse. What a beautiful little tragedy, one of many in TBOTNS.

Interestingly, it was pointed out to me today on the racquetball court by a friend who's reading TBOTNS (he just finished Shadow), that Dorcas, just after being resurrected, follows Severian like a dog (parallel to Triskele).

ccd

1999-04-08 Roy C. Lackey #

Argues that Dorcas was already dead when Severian pulled her from the lake, using the drowned-body details: she was not weighed down with lead shot and her eyes were not glued shut. Builds on Cliff Drane's question about her grasping Severian's hand and sword, and on the idea that Severian's raising of the dead always weakens him.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Dorcas was dead before Severian reached her in the lake

before? Severian grasps both her hand and the sword at the same time, almost like she gave it to him. Any thoughts about that?<<

She not only grasps his hand, she is pulling him _down_. Next thing we know, he is near-drowned at the edge of the water, with barely enough strength to save his sword. Dorcas is already out of the water and helps him to safety. When Sev raises the dead it always weakens him, and the newly-risen dead are always befuddled. So what happened between chapters, when they were both underwater?
