1997-11-28 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Marks the first anniversary of the WHORL mailing list, thanking contributors and noting that activity has slowed since the release of Exodus. Points readers to the newer Urth list and archives and announces an upcoming list for other Wolfe-related discussion.
informs

This week marked the first anniversary of the WHORL mailing list (unless I misplaced a decimal point somewhere). Thanks to all who have contributed over the past year!

Activity on the list has been light of late as discussion after the release of _Exodus_ has died down, but the list will stay open in anticipation of occasional activity.
Meanwhile, if you don't already know about it, there's
another mailing list for discussion of Wolfe's New Sun
series and his other works: information is at the web
address below.

The entire history of the urth and whorl mailing lists is recorded in the archives:

http://www.moonmilk.com/whorl/
http://www.urth.net/urth/

Coming soon: bookofgold, a mailing list for other stuff
Wolfe readers like.

-- ranjit
