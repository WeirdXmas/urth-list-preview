Christianity in New Sun

Urth list · 1 messages · 1999-10-06 to 1999-10-06

1999urthThe Book of the New SunAlex David Groce

Themes: Catholic faith and sacraments, Gods, angels and the Increate

1999-10-06 Alex David Groce #

Argues that Christianity is present metaphysically in The Book of the New Sun because the miracles and plot coincidences favored by the Increate look like the New Testament God's work, and that Long Sun shows clearer historical Christianity. Grounds this on the claim that Long Sun and New Sun share a world and on a quotation from John 8:32, adding to an earlier answer by mantis.
supports · theology-and-religion · theories: The Increate is the New Testament God; Long Sun and New Sun share the same world

Besides the relevant answer mantis gave, it's obvious that Christianity is present in a metaphysical sense. The coincidences involved in the miracles and "plot-lines" favored by the Increate are astounding unless we assume that the Increate is the New Testament's God as well. At the very least, the Increate has read the New Testament and is paying homage.

Also, since Long Sun and New Sun are definitely the same world, we can use the even clearer evidence for a historical Christianity found there.

"And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free." John 8:32
