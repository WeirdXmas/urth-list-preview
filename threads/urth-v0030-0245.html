Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos

2001-01-15 Alice Turner #

Announces and recommends Michael Andre-Driussi's cover essay in The New York Review of Science Fiction, which examines how Jack Vance, Gene Wolfe and Damien Broderick use language to evoke the end of the world as the sun dies. Calls the essay well-researched, strong on parallel and influence, and lively, and urges list members with access to read it.
informs · genre-and-craft

Those of you who have access to The New York Review of Science Fiction should by all means check out the new issue. Michael Andre-Driussi (our mantis) has the cover story, a perfectly fascinating essay on how, by use of language, Jack Vance, Gene Wolfe and Damien Broderick (also ours; hi Damien) evoke the fin du monde as the sun gutters out. It's well-researched, is terrific on parallel and influence, and, as we could only expect, lively as hell.

-alga
