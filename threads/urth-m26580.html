Matt Groening and Wolfe

The thread: Readers asked whether a Gene Wolfe book is visible in Matt Groening's studio in a news image and cartoon self-portrait. David Duffy reported a possible Wolfe title on the bookcase behind a green model spaceship, citing a Hollywood Reporter piece on Groening's attention to detail with book spines. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes said he could not read the titles, and Evan Pickett transcribed the visible shelf titles, noting some spines were blank or obscured. The thread stayed a small identification exercise with no argument about Wolfe's work and ended with the titles only partly legible.
  • David Duffy: a Wolfe title is likely on the bookcase behind the green spaceship
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: cannot make out the titles in the cartoon or the photo
  • Evan Pickett: transcribed the visible shelf titles, several spines obscured
