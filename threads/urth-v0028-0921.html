2000-04-19 Paul C Duggan #

Argues that the Babylon of Revelation may refer to non-Christian Judaism rather than only Rome, and questions how such a reading could be proved. Points to the sevenfold structure shared by Revelation and John's gospel as evidence the two texts are compatible, responding to an earlier post on the late dating and authorship of Revelation.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Babylon in Revelation means non-Christian Judaism

Its also been suggested that the main enemy, and actual identity of Babylon (besides Rome) is non-Christian Judaism ("those who say they are Jews and are not", and all) I would think "modern scholarship" would find that fits right in with the Johnaine "anti-semitism" (as if that term had any contextual utility, which I think it probably doesn't [but that doesn't stop US News and World Report from mentioning it])

I wonder how on earth anyone goes about proving something like that. John's gospel is structurally based abound sevens (seven "I am's", seven miraculous signs, a "seven day creation" in the first chapter) and everyone is well aware of the seven-fold structure of Revelation. Seems that would make them quite compatible.

Paul
