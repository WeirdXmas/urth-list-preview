Evans-Wentz' Fairy Faith

Urth list · 1 messages · 2012-01-19 to 2012-01-19

2012urthBrian Lovely

Themes: Myth and folklore sources

2012-01-19 Brian Lovely #

Reports reading Evans-Wentz's The Fairy-Faith in Celtic Countries and notes several striking parallels to Wolfe, including a female fairy who walks through nettles and fairies in a group that act as one being. Asks Urth readers whether they agree the book is a likely source for Wolfe's research.
informs · source-and-allusion · theories: Evans-Wentz's Fairy-Faith is a source for Wolfe

Since I didn't take notes, I don't have a complete list, but a couple of things stand out. A female fairy who walked through nettles as if they weren't there, and the idea that if one fairy in a group raises his hand, they all do, as if they were actually all the same being. How many darn fairies are in that group, anyway?

On Wed, 18 Jan 2012, Brian wrote: > I've been reading Evan Wentz' "The Fairy-Faith i Celtic Countries," and > I'm fairly convinced it's one of the sources Wolfe uses for his > research. I've noticed many striking similarities, but have just been > reading through rather than stopping to take notes. > > Has anyone else read this book, and do you agree with me as to it's > being a likely source for Wolfe? I've looked through it, and agree it might be a source. But Wolfe always mentions Andrew Lang's books as a childhood love. Lang also edited _The Secret Commonwealth_, and his intro to that covers similar ground to Evans-Wentz. Another source might be Dunsany eg _Curse of the Wise Woman_. Cheers, David Duffy.
