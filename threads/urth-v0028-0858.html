2000-03-25 Joe Leighly #

Proposes that Silk's enlightenment in The Book of the Long Sun, whether real or imagined, is a clear reference to Philip K. Dick. Cites Doctor Crane's explanation of it as a small stroke and notes that Dick made the same kind of arguments about his own experience.
