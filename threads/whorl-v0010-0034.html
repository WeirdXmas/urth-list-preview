Introduces a newcomer who has unlurked, thanks list members for spoilers, and says they are rereading The Fifth Head of Cerberus while reacting to a teasing remark by O'Leary about a forthcoming book. Gives no argument; only social thanks and a promise to ask questions later.

Unlurking neophyte here. Just writing to genuinely
thank you guys for the spoilers-c'mon, Amazon, I want

maintenance mode now, rereading 5HC, appreciating the
taste from you guys, but Mr. O'Leary, so good it
hurts? Don't tease the hardcore so unmercifully-oh,
well, first thing you learn is you always gotta wait.
I hope to contribute meaningfully (?) rsn. I've got a
couple not-too-FAQs, but they'll keep. Rock on,
