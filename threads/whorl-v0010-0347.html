2000-06-07 Jonathan Laidlow #

Recommends the Ultan's Library journal, noting two Nick Gevers pieces on The Book of the Long Sun and a Nigel Price review of On Blue's Waters, and invites proposals for essays on The Book of the Short Sun. Offers these as material to stimulate discussion on the list, with no argument of its own about the books.
informs

Can I just recommend that all those interested in the Long and Short of it, make their merry way to the Ultan's Library journal. We have two pieces by Nick Gevers on the Book of the Long Sun, the first reprinted from *Nova Express*, the second an unpublished companion piece. We also have Nigel Price reviewing 'On Blue's Waters'.

Perhaps these could stimulate discussion on Whorl?

We're looking for proposals for short essays on the Book of the Short Sun for the next edition of UL, coming in December 2000.

http://www.english.bham.ac.uk/ultan

Jonathan
