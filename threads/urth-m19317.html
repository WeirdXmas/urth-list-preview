The thread: Thread on the apparition of Patera Pike and Oreb in Silk's room in Lake of the Long Sun, asking whether it is Silk's older self astral-traveling through time, mistaken identity, or something else. Dave Tallman and Marc Aramini supported the older-Silk time-travel reading, while James Wynn and Jerry Friedman pressed against it and argued over whether Silk and Pike are clones or brothers. Positions kept multiplying: Mucor and Fava as time travellers, Fava as Hyacinth, and Typhon's family tree. The thread ended in drift and impasse, with no consensus and readers conceding the Short Sun material is needed to settle it.
  • Dave Tallman: Silk astral-travels back in time and brings Oreb, citing Short Sun usage
  • James Wynn: Silk mistakes an altered older self or Mucor for Pike; Silk and Pike are brother clones; Fava is Hyacinth
  • Jerry Friedman: Pike resemblance goes unremarked for characters' reasons; Silk is not a copy of Pike; the 'sir' passage is about respect
  • Marc Aramini: Pike could be time-travelling Rajan or mistaken identity; Mucor, Fava and Mora retrieve Hy's spirit
  • Andrew Mason: Oreb's flight undercuts the time-travel reading; the reflective Silk passage need not hide a clue
  • David Stockhoff: Blood as Silk's genetic son is a signal to readers; Typhon's genealogy stays obscure

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Doubles, twins and clones, Time loops and causality

2011-11-02 James Wynn replying to Jerry Friedman #

Argues that Silk and Pike are brother clones rather than Pike being Silk in astral form, and that Wolfe planned Horn's transformation into Silk from the start. Supports this with Silk's two-page struggle over calling Blood "son" despite his training, his awareness of the inhumi and Quezel's aims, and foreshadowing in Silk's talks with Horn.
disputes · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Silk and Pike are brother clones; Wolfe knew Horn would become Silk

For me, it is difficult to get around the explanation that Silk and Pike are brother clones.
The clincher for me is Silk's two-page struggle with why he called Blood "son" even though THAT WAS WHAT HE WAS TRAINED TO DO.

He had to know what the inhumi were there for and why Quezel wanted a human colony on Green. So he had a good idea of the differences between the two planets. There is also the foreshadowing in Silk's conversation with Horn, his ability to mimic Silk and such. So, yeah, I think Wolfe knew that Horn would become Silk. He didn't know if the SS volume would be published. But he knew generally what would happen if it were.

J.
