1997-10-28 John S. Shin #

Asks where to find leather-bound Easton Press editions of The Claw of the Conciliator and The Sword of the Lictor, since they are out of stock, and offers to trade a spare first edition of Soldier of Arete. Mentions preferring the Easton Press editions to first hardcovers, citing Nicholson Baker's dislike of their gilt bindings; the post is a collecting question and trade offer, not literary analysis.

Press editions to the first edition hardcovers, despite what Nicholson Baker says about those ostentatious, gilt, leather-bound tomes.

Also, if anyone is interested in trading any collectible Wolfe, please le= t

John
