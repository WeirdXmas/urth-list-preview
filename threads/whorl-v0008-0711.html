Themes: Names and their meanings

1998-08-03 mary whalen #

Proposes that the Long Sun name Oosik comes from an Inuit word for a walrus baculum, and links it to Latin baculum meaning staff or augur's staff. Offers the name as a hint about Oosik's relationship with Hyacinth and his place among Silk's staff, with the name's meanings as evidence.
proposes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Oosik's name hints at relationship with Hyacinth

This is Sean Whalen (prion) .

Oosik. Also ooshook. Inuit (Eskimo) for the baculum (penis bone) of a walrus, or of other sea animals, or just penis. Baculum or baculus in Latin means, staff, walking stick, or an augur's staff. Generalissimo of Viron. Father of Mattak (Whale Meat). Name may have to do with his relationship with Hyacinth. A baculum is an augur's staff, and Oosik is a member of Silk's staff.
