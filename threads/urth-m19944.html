2011-11-17 James Wynn replying to Larry Miller #

Answers Larry Miller's three questions about the end of Horn and Silk by offering the poster's own theory that a Neighbor possesses Horn's body after his death and that Silver Silk possesses Pig. Cites the greenbuck chase that led Horn into the pit as motive; notes Marc's rival theory.
answers · puzzle-solving · theories: A Neighbor possesses Horn's body after his death; Silver Silk possesses Pig; Silk was scanned into a Mainframe god

1) I don't believe there has been any satisfactory full explanation of this.

2) When Horn dies in the the pit, his spirit goes to the Outsider. Horn's body is possessed by a Neighbor (that greenbuck Horn was chasing when he fell into the pit) who feels culpable for his death. In recompense, he adopts Horn's identity and mission. Horn's body dies on Green. The Neighbor performs the same trick again by possessing and repairing Silk's dying body. Obviously, not everyone agrees with this. Marc has an equally developed theory about what happens in the pit. I don't no of any other strong opinion about what happens when Horn dies the two times.

3) Silk was scanned and has become a Mainframe god. Silver Silk/Silent Silk is possessing Pig.

2011-11-17 Larry Miller replying to James Wynn #

Recalls earlier theories that Blue was Urth and that the island held ruins of the Botanic Gardens, then links Horn's glass description to the claw's gem. Suggests the spider-like figure placing something on Horn's forehead might be Severian resurrecting him with the claw, responding to James Wynn's account of Horn's possession.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Severian resurrects Horn with the claw; Blue is the ruins of the Botanic Gardens

Thanks James. Back when I thought Blue was Urth I thought maybe it was the ruins of the Botanic Gardens and that the island was the floating plantlife that was inside before its destruction. I remember on my reread being struck by Horns description of the glass and the similarities with the gem that housed the claw. I also thought that the spiderlike figure placing something on Horns forehead was Severian resurrecting him with the claw.
