2001-07-17 BraveSaintCroix #

Chats about hunting for Gene Wolfe books in used bookstores, describing finding copies of The Claw of the Conciliator and Nightside the Long Sun while on vacation. Says he buys duplicate used copies so he can lend them out rather than lending his own trade paperbacks, and mentions his sister enjoyed The Shadow of the Torturer.
chats

Hello all,
Just to throw something out there: how many people here find their Gene Wolfe at used book stores? Fishing through shelves of tattered paperbacks in cramped book stores is hobby of mine, but Gene Wolfe always seems scarce. That's why I was so excited to find both Claw of the Conciliator and Nightside the Long Sun at a store on vacation this past weekend. I buy them even though I own them in trade paperback form because, although I try to get others to read Gene Wolfe, I hate lending out my Lupine tomes. This way, I have used doubles to let them borrow. I'll admit though, that the covers on some of them are downright embarrassing.

-Steve

PS: Happy to say, my little sister just finished Shadow of the Torturer and enjoyed it.

2001-07-20 eli+ #

Reports that Wolfe's books, especially Soldier paperbacks and The Book of the New Sun, turn up less often in used bookstores than other authors' books. Recalls a summer SoHo Books stash of nearly new Wolfe titles, probably Tor surplus, and says the poster prefers buying new copies.
chats

For a while I couldn't turn around without tripping over drifts of lonely orphan _Soldier_ paperbacks that nobody would take home. But you're right, Wolfe is mostly not one of the authors who turns up a lot used. A fair bit of _New Sun_. Last summer, SoHo Books (of that ilk) had quite a stash: _Castle_, _Storeys_, _Peace, _Devil_, _Doctor Death_, I forget what else, all like new. Probably Tor surplus. :-( Wolfe is definitely one of the authors whose books I don't wait to find used, both because that's not so easy and because I'd prefer to buy a copy.

2001-07-21 Ranjit Bhatnagar #

Recounts how the poster first encountered The Book of the New Sun in the late 1980s, finding The Shadow of the Torturer used at a bookstore and then the later volumes appearing there one at a time. Shares this as a personal anecdote about used bookstores rather than a reading of the text.
chats

Back in 1987 or so I'd never heard of Wolfe, but I read the review of the Book of the New Sun in a back issue of
Asimov's Magazine, so I went to Moe's (?) and found one lone used copy of Shadow of the Torturer. When I'd finished
that, I went back to the bookstore to find nothing used but the second book. The same with the third and fourth-- they showed up at the store just as I needed them.

- ranjit
