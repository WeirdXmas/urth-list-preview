PMorris33 also noted that the nature of Latro's activity remains unresolved. 2003-01-20 James Wynn #

Asks whether anyone has information on further Latro books, since Arete hints the story continues. Quotes Crush, who reads Apollo's prophecy as a route through South America, India, North America, Scandinavia and Britain, and doubts Wolfe, now in his seventies, will finish it.
questions · chronology-and-plot-logic · author-and-biography · theories: Latro's journey continues through South America and India; Wolfe intended more than one extra Latro novel

Does anyone have any information about the Latro series? It seems that Wolfe left many hints in "Arete" that the story of Latro is to be continued. And we still haven't figured out what he was doing

[Soldier Series spoilers to continue...]

Crush responds:
Well, since Latro hasn't gotten home to Italy (as Apollo suggested he would), the story can be said to not be complete. However, since in Arete, Latro fulfills Artemis' requirements of him and receives his requested reward (to be reunited with his friends), the Soldier novels could not be considered **brutally** unfinished.

I thought it was apparent that Wolfe intended more than **one** extra novel. I interpreted Apollo's prophecy to mean that after Jerusalem Wolfe intended his hero to go to South America before he got back home. I **presumed** he was to go to India on the way and North America, Scandinavia, and Britain on the way back. Wolfe is a septuagenarian; I doubt we're going to see the series completed as it was originally envisioned.

-- Crush

2003-01-20 Josh Geller #

Reports that at a WorldCon conversation the poster asked Gene Wolfe when more Latro books would appear, and Wolfe said he would write more after finishing his current project. The post is a plain informational account of a direct exchange with the author, offering no interpretation of the Latro books themselves.
