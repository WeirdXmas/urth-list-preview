Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate, Myth and folklore sources

1998-03-05 Alice Turner #

Argues that the winged figure on the Great Sleigh in Wolfe's Tracking Song is not a winged angel-like being, suggesting instead a North American Raven figure, the world-maker of Western myth. Grounds this in the fact that only one winged being is seen, possibly a dying person's hallucination, while other witnesses on the sleigh never mention wings. Responds to Dan's suggestion that the winged beings resemble Wolfe's Hierogrammates.
disputes · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Winged figure is Raven, not an angel

Dan,

Since you're open to resonance, remember that "Tracking Song" has a distinct North American flavor, and that a very important figure in Western NA myth is Great Raven (or simply Raven), who made the world. There is only one winged being seen on the Great Sleigh (and it may the hallucination of a dying person), and since the Great Sleigh crew has been seen by many of the people Cutthroat encounters, none of whom mention wings, I think it's fairly safe to say that they don't look like a cartload of cherubim.

-alga-
