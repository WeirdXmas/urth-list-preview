Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2001-06-15 Marc Aramini #

Informs the list that a real Necronomicon has been published and is sold in a bookstore, and asks whether it still belongs in the library of imaginary books. Raises the point that a book once made real cannot become unreal again, comparing this to a line from The Velveteen Rabbit.
informs

With all this talk of fake books, I just wanted to point out that someone has really written a Necronomicon, I saw a copy in Barnes and Nobles, purporting to be the ancient book. You can pick up a copy, too! Since it has come into real existence, does it really qualify for the library of imaginary books? Once you become real, you can't ever become unreal (or something like that bit of philosophy can be found in The Velveteen Rabbit)
