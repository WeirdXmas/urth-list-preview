Severian's skin colour

Urth list · 4 messages · 2005-06-18 to 2005-06-20

2005urthThe Book of the New SunTony EllisDan'l Danehy-Oakesmournings gloryJames Wynn
The thread: Readers asked what Severian's skin colour is and whether it signals a high birth. Tony Ellis quoted the Chatelaine's remark in The Shadow of the Torturer that Severian's skin is very white beside her own. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes took this as support for the theory that Severian is an optimate or possibly an exultant by birth. mournings glory added Gene Wolfe's Thrust interview describing Severian as pale with dark eyes and hair, which was offered as settling the matter. The thread ended in agreement on the textual evidence, with no real disagreement recorded.
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes: Chatelaine's remark supports Severian being an optimate or possibly exultant by birth
  • Tony Ellis: quotes the Chatelaine passage from The Shadow of the Torturer as direct evidence of very white skin
  • mournings glory: Wolfe's Thrust interview describes Severian as pale with dark eyes and hair, settling the question

2005-06-18 Tony Ellis #

Quotes the exchange in The Shadow of the Torturer where the Chatelaine remarks on Severian's very white skin next to her own, to establish his skin colour. Offers the passage itself as direct textual evidence, with no argument added beyond the citation from chapter X.

Severian is white:

"I've never seen such white skin coupled with dark hair... Do you think my own skin white?"
"Very white, Chatelaine."
"So do others, but it is dun next to yours. You must flee the sun when you're a torturer, Severian. You'll burn terribly."
TSOTT, chapter X.

There's also this fascinating description by Mr Wolfe himself from THRUST 19:

Thrust: If Severian were standing in front of us, describe him externally and internally.

Wolfe: You didn't say at what point in his life, so I'll choose him at the time he leaves the Citadel. He is 6' 1" and weighs 175 pounds. He is pale, with dark eyes, dark straight hair, and good teeth. His face is long and rather bony; high, square forehead, moderately high cheekbones, strong chin. He is muscular; his hands are a little larger than average. He wears heavy, black, knee-length leather boots, coarsely but strongly made; soot-black trousers of a fabric like soft wool; and a long, hooded cloak (unlinted) of the same material. He wears no shirt. Mentally, he is an unconscious intellectual--that is to say, he is fascinated by ideas, including problems of ethics and morality, but he is not aware that he is an intellectual and for the most part he lacks the vocabulary. He is a great deal less like most men than he thinks he is. Even so, he knows himself to be alienated by his eidetic memory. Subconsciously, he has been further marked by the loss of his mother in infancy. He has difficulty in forming relationships because of that, although he does sometimes form them; and he tends toward strong erotic attachments to women who subconsciously suggest the mother he lost. On the simplest level, this means toward women who are physically larger than he--Thea, Thecla, the undine--and to Jolenta, who has unusually large breasts. Of course, everyone is attracted to Jolenta, or at least nearly everyone. On a more subtle level he is attracted to women who act as guides, Agia, or counselors, Dorcas.
