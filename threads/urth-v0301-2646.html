Soldier omnibus coming in February

Urth list · 1 messages · 2003-01-10 to 2003-01-10

2003urthDan Schmidt

2003-01-10 Dan Schmidt #

Informs the list that Tor is releasing a trade paperback omnibus of Soldier of the Mist and Soldier of Arete, titled Latro in the Mist, in February. Says the news comes from Amazon and Tor's own website, and admits it may be old news.
informs

I don't remember seeing this news on the list, but I don't read it that carefully, so apologies if this is old news.

Both Amazon and Tor's own website claim that Tor is publishing a trade paperback omnibus of SOLDIER OF THE MIST and SOLDIER OF ARETE, titled LATRO IN THE MIST, in February.

You folks have probably all read them both, but I'm looking forward to finally getting my hands on them.

Dan (currently reading PEACE for the first time)
