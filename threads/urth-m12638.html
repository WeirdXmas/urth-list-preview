Reports that The Book of the New Sun is listed in an online gaming site poll asking which book series should become a video game, and urges list members to vote for it over Harry Potter. Shares the poll's location on the site's page, with no argument about the book's content.

2010-02-04 Lane Haygood replying to Christopher Hettinger #

Chats about a gaming-site poll where Book of the New Sun was nominated for video game adaptation, saying they chose Elric's Stormbringer over Severian's Terminus Est. Adds that the audiobook reading of the series is well done and that they are midway through The Claw of the Conciliator.
chats

I'm sorry, I had to go with Elric. I would rather fight with Stormbringer than Terminus Est.

By the way, the BOTNS audiobooks on Audible are very well done. John Davis' reading of Severian so far is very close to how I "read" them silently to myself. I'm midway into _Claw_ and looking for excuses to listen outside of my commute.

LH
