Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

1998-07-06 mary whalen #

Argues that the animals of the far future can be judged intelligent by pointing to Oreb the night chough from the Long Sun books, since both series share a universe. Supports the point by noting Oreb's speech goes unremarked and by mentioning the catachrest, while observing that Severian met few intelligent animals.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Animals of Urth are intelligent, shown by Oreb

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

I know that a while back it was a fairly big point of contention about the intelligence of the animals of Urth. Severian barely interacted with any animals that might have had intelligence, and clues in the text were hard to decipher. Well, since the New Sun and Long Sun books are in the same universe, can't we use the case of Oreb the night chough? He seems fairly intelligent, and people aren't surprised to hear him talk. There's also the catachrest, of course.

prion
