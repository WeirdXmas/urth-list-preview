What was _Thrust_?

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-09-05 to 2001-09-05

2001whorlThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

2001-09-05 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Explains that Thrust was a fanzine later renamed Quantum, and that the remark Jerry Friedman asked about was Gene Wolfe's own, reported in a Thrust interview. Argues from Talarican's remark and Ymar's reign that the Whorl was launched decades or a century before Ymar's rule, before the Conciliator's arrival.
answers · chronology-and-plot-logic · source-and-allusion · theories: The Whorl was launched decades before Ymar's reign

_Thrust_ was a fanzine, which then was renamed _Quantum_, and then ceased publication. The remark in question was made by Gene Wolfe himself, as reported in the interview published in _Thrust_.

Talarican is saying, I believe, that the Whorl was launched sometime after Typhon got the two heads, but before Typhon actually died (naturally!); and since Ymar (first autarch) died "a chiliad ago," and Ymar was a boy when Severian met him in URTH, in the same era when Typhon had two heads and control of Urth (although the rebellion is going on at that point, isn't it?) . . . so it seems like the Whorl could not have been launched 1000 years ago (when Ymar the Almost Just died), nor even in the decades before then (since it was Ymar's reign), but in the decades before Ymar's reign. Say 100 years, or 50 years if you like. How long did Ymar's reign last, how old was he when he somehow managed to attain the newly minted throne--who knows?

Because the rebellion is going on when the Conciliator appears, this strongly suggests that the Whorl was launched at some point prior to the arrival of the Conciliator, that is, prior to events in that section of URTH (since the rebellion in Mainframe is the same as what happened on the ground, or so it has always seemed).
