Corrects bsharp's claim that the assassin wore a mask, arguing instead that Severian explicitly sees the attacker's face as he revives, and that the attacker carried a batardeau knife. Cites the page in The Urth of the New Sun and rejects any link between the assassin and Agia.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Agia had nothing to do with the assassin

Uh, no, the assassin didn't have a mask of any kind. And Severian specifically mentions seeing the guy's face as it reanimated. The assassin had a batardeau, which is a large knife. (URTH, 291) I can't see that Agia had anything to do with the assassin.

-Roy
