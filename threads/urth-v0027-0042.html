The Commotion at the Piteous Gate is a Mystery?!

Urth list · 3 messages · 1999-06-01 to 1999-06-02

1999urthThe Book of the New SunMitchell A. BaileyTony EllisMichael Andre-Driussi
The thread: Readers debated whether the Piteous Gate incident, in which a burning coach meets uhlans at a closed road, is a real mystery in Wolfe's New Sun books. Michael Andre-Driussi offers the uhlans' literal duty as the explanation and jokes about the Sanguinary Turnstile theory's obvious clues. Mitchell A. Bailey argues the road is simply closed and the coach's occupants ignored Palaemon's warning in The Sword of the Lictor. Tony Ellis redirects readers to an earlier archive post, Urth Vol. 10, making the same case, so the thread ends by pointing back to past discussion rather than new argument.
  • Michael Andre-Driussi: the uhlans' literal duty explains the incident; the Sanguinary Turnstile theory overlooks obvious clues
  • Mitchell A. Bailey: the road is closed and the coach ignored the warning, so there is no real mystery
  • Tony Ellis: the question is settled in the archive (Urth Vol. 10), so it is not a mystery

1999-06-01 Mitchell A. Bailey #

Argues that the Piteous Gate incident in The Book of the New Sun is not a real mystery, since the road is simply closed and the burning coach's occupants ignored the warning. Grounds this in the opening chapter of The Sword of the Lictor and Palaemon's warning about the uhlans, then adds thanks to Ranjit for the new archive site.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Piteous Gate is a closed road, not a mystery

Being a literal-minded, rather than a literary-minded sort, I never considered the Piteous Gate business, as partially explained in the opening chap of "Sword", as any great mystery.

I always figured that, as Palaemon had warned, the uhlans took their duty literally and fanatically. The road begins right there, at the gate of the Wall, and it's CLOSED, see? If you know what's good for you, you'll turn off it just as you leave the Gate, which is what everybody appeared to be doing. The burning coach in Sev's dream/memory had apparently not done so.

"Hey buddy, what part of CLOSED don't you understand?! WHOOMP!"

Let me add my gratitude and kudos to Ranjit for the new archives and site! It loads so much faster, too! Now maybe I can finish reading all the archives before my eyes gutter in their sockets and avoid gaffes like rehashing something Tony had already argued before I ever found the archives.
