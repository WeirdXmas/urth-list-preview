1997-05-13 Patrick O'Leary #

Thanks a previous respondent for tips on the name Oreb, noting its Hebrew meaning of raven and its biblical references, while saying he suspects more to the name than meets the eye and plans to refine his question. He also rejects the nickname talk, asks about making Q & D official, and mentions recent reading by Damon Knight, Patricia Anthony and Lew Shiner.
chats

Dear Mantis,

Thanks for the tips on Oreb. Hebrew for raven, biblical references--I had no idea.

<<In any case, if you'd like to further refine the question, you've got a few days to do it in.)"

Geeze, I was hoping to save myself embarrassment by offering a terse and obscure question--one freighted with import as a haiku. I expect Gene to answer: "He's a Bird. Next." I just sense that there's much more to Oreb than meets the eye--so to speak. Not sure how I can justify it. I'll think on it and get back to you.

I'm delighted that Gene will submit himself to this inquisition.

I just going to introduce you as "That guy, you know, you said `=That= guy,' remember?"?>>

I think I'm past the "that Guy" stage with Gene--I Hope so anyway! And, forgive me, this whole nickname thing seems a bit strange to me. I'm Patrick. Or Pat. Don't mean to be a party pooper, but I've never liked costumes or Halloween. My own persona is deceptive enough, I hope. And I'm terrified of getting the Vironese convention of naming wrong.

Any more plans to make Q & D official?

PS: Damon Knight's THERE ARE BIRDS is magnificent. So is Patricia Anthony's GOD'S FIRES. Am deep into Lew Shiner's GLIMPSES--very fine.

best

Patrick
