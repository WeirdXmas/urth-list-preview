Nicholls on _Fifth Head_: where?

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-02-28 to 1998-02-28

1998urthadam louis stephanides

1998-02-28 adam louis stephanides #

Asks where to find Peter Nicholls's essay on The Fifth Head of Cerberus, which John Clute mentions in his book Strokes as clarifying the novel's plot and themes. Quotes Clute's passage naming the essay for an American compendium and requests a citation or location for it.
questions

In _Strokes_, John Clute wrote "it wasn't until ... Peter Nicholls wrote an essay on [_TFHoC_] for an American compendium that the full plot and thematic integrity of that savage novel began to come clear." Does anyone know where this essay can be found?

Thanks in advance,

Adam
