2008-10-11 Stephen Case #

Asks about WindyCon, where Wolfe might speak and whether one must register for the whole convention to attend a single session. Also questions whether the Lovecraft epigraph on An Evil Guest's dust jacket misquotes the original, and asks for recommendations of contemporary authors Wolfe readers find Wolfe-like.
questions

Just a few responses from a lurker to some things on the list:

a) I'm with James. Does anyone have more specific information regarding WindyCon and when Wolfe would be speaking? I've never been to one of these, so I'd like more info. Do you have to register for the entire thing if you just want to hear a particular session? I'd be coming from Kankakee, so it would be doable . . .

b) I picked up "An Evil Guest" from the library today and started it. The Lovecraft quote at the bottom of the front inside cover of the dust jacket caught my eye. Perhaps my memory is faulty, but is this a misquote? I thought the original Lovecraft quote ended in "die" versus "lie".

c) Like Matt, I'd also like to hear further discussion on books that Wolfe readers may enjoy. Years ago I learned via this listserv that Wolfe was influenced by Borges and Chesterton, and that opened new worlds for me. I've been away for a while so may have missed discussion along these lines, but I'd love to hear about contemporary authors who Wolfe readers here find Wolfe-like. I jotted down the author and title Matt provided.

Thanks,
-Steve

2008-10-11 Matthew Keeley replying to Stephen Case #

Corrects Stephen Case's suspicion that the dust-jacket Lovecraft quote from An Evil Guest is misquoted, saying the full couplet ends in "die" and the jacket simply cites half of it. Supports the point by quoting the complete couplet, "That is not dead which can eternal lie / And with strange aeons even death may die."
answers · close-textual-reading

It ends with "die," but both words are in the full quote. As I recall, the dust jacket only quotes half the couplet:

That is not dead which can eternal lie.
And with strange ?ons even death may die

-Matt
