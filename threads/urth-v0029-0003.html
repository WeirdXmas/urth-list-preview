2000-05-09 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Corrects an earlier bibliographic mix-up, identifying the Wolfe poetry chapbook For Rosemary as the Kerosina edition rather than the United Mythologies one. Reports where copies can be found, noting a bundle with Stories from the Old Hotel and prices for a related title in a dealer catalogue and online search.
informs · close-textual-reading

FOR ROSEMARY [not United Mythologies, of Canada; but rather =Kerosina= of the UK] (1988)

Kerosina. Sold in two forms: by itself (which is how I got it, at a convention I think?), or together with Kerosina's STORIES FROM THE OLD HOTEL.

(I confused it with the chapbook by United Mythologies, "Orbital Thoughts.")

Well there, all better! Sorry for the mix-up. The Kerosina chapbook might be pretty hard to find by itself now. I don't see a copy in the latest relevant Chris Drumm Books Catalog (#126), but there are a lot of other Wolfe titles there; and a quick search on Bookfinder.com showed only a bundle w/ SFTOH, whereas (on the same) PE starts at $30 (ex library of Congress copy) and goes up to around $100 or so, I guess.

=mantis=
