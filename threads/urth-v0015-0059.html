Winnoc, Beuzec in Lexicon Urthus

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-07-02 to 1998-07-02

1998urthThe Book of the New Sunmary whalen

1998-07-02 mary whalen #

Asks whether the characters Winnoc and Beuzec were left out of the Lexicon Urthus and omitted from its errata sheets, and proposes that Beuzec is the slug Hethor carries. Supports the slug idea by noting Beuzec never speaks, vanishes just before the slug is seen in the Antechamber, and that Severian calls him greasy like the slug's secretion.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Beuzec is the slug Hethor uses

This is Sean Whalen (prion).

In the Lexicon Urthus, Winnoc (the slave who was whipped by Palaemon as a young man) and Beuzec (Hethor's companion) do not have entries. Were they put in the errata sheets?

Also, is Beuzec supposed to be the slug that Hethor uses? We never hear him speak, and he disappears right before the time we learn the slug has been looking around in the Antechamber. Also, Severian, upon first seeing Beuzec, describes him as looking greasy, and the slug secretes a greasy substance, if I remember correctly.

prion
