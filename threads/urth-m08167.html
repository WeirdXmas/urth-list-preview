2009-01-04 Gwern Branwen #

Asks why searching the Urth.net site through the site's own searchbox returns no results while a manual Google site search of urth.net does, and reports a workaround using 'site:urth.net'. Gives two example search URLs comparing the Google query with the site's search field to show the discrepancy.

Hash: SHA512

Something seems to be off on the Urth.net site. Take two example searches, one done manually on Google and one done through the little searchbox. The searchbox one turns up no results, but the manual one does.

http://www.google.com/search?q=gwern+site%3Aurth.net turns up a good 66 results, but
http://www.google.com/search?as_sitesearch=www.urth.net&as_q=gwern ? 0. It does seem to work if one modifies it to read 'site:urth.net' or 'site:http://www.urth.net'.

Anyone know what's going on there?

gwern
