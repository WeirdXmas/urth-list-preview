Thecla's Green Book

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2006-10-15 thalassocrat #

Points out an apparent inconsistency: Severian describes the green book from Cyby as thin and small, yet when he delivers the books to Thecla, the green book and the saffian heraldry book are too wide to pass through her door slot. Grounds the claim in the two passages where Severian describes the book's size and the delivery.
proposes · close-textual-reading

Probably been pointed out before, but Wolfe seems to have made a slip juggling his books. When Sev gets the 3 volumes from Cyby, the green book is "no thicker than my index finger" and "hardly larger than my hand" - in other words, unusually small, and presumably the smallest of the lot. But when he delivers the books to Thecla, both it and the saffian (heraldry) book are too wide to fit through the slot in her door.

2006-10-16 Roy C. Lackey replying to thalassocrat #

Argues that Wolfe did not err with the books' sizes but that there are two green books, so the green book Severian delivers to Thecla is a different volume from the small theology book. Grounds this in the scenes at Thecla's cell, the brown book taken before exile, and an essay on books in The Book of the New Sun.
proposes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: There are two green books in the text; The green book Thecla receives is The Book of the New Sun

I'm glad you brought this up. I was going to post about it but didn't. It wasn't until I read "Books in _The Book of the New Sun_" in PE, then looked at the two places in the text you mention, plus the part where Severian visited Thecla's cell and took the brown book just before leaving for exile, that I was able to sort it all out. I don't think Wolfe made a mistake; he was just being particularly devious.

Wolfe was trying to say as little as he could get away with about _The Book of the New Sun_. There is only one oblique mention of it in NEW SUN, and that only by a process of elimination, and even then he muddied the waters. The problem resolves itself when you finally realize there are *two* green books.

One of them is the smallest book of all, the green one about the size of a man's hand, the book of theology with the devotions and enameled pictures. The brown book was "hardly larger" than the little green book. These are the two books that fit through the cell door.

The smaller of the two remaining books that are too wide to fit through the slot is also green. That one is _The Book of the New Sun_. The other is the much larger folio with the arms on the cover.

That essay makes it absolutely clear that the theology book with the pictures is *not* _The Book of the New Sun_. Therefore, there are two green books.

-Roy
