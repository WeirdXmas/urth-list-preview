Peace Puzzler

1999-03-03 William H. Ansley #

Asks who actually drew the cover art for the novel Peace, noting that the Orb edition's back cover credits Gahan Wilson while its copyright page credits Tony Roberts. Jokingly suggests the two may be one person, citing an unrelated Woody Allen film detail as a playful aside.
questions · source-and-allusion · theories: Tony Roberts is a pseudonym for Gahan Wilson

Here is a _Peace_ puzzler. It has to do with the book itself, not its contents, so there may actually be an answer to this question.

Who drew the cover art?

The back of the Orb edition says: Cover art by Gahan Wilson But the copyright page says: Cover art by Tony Roberts

Which one was it? Is Tony Roberts really Gahan Wilson? Then who acted in all those Woody Allen movies? I'm puzzled!

William Ansley
