A Few RTTW Questions

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2001-02-09 BMeyer7 #

Asks five questions about Return to the Whorl, including what happened between Merryn and the dog, who the little men on the bridge and the black gang are, and whether Horn/Silk is Malrubius. Also asks who the man in the cloak on Green is and suggests, from a page 361 remark about pretending to eat, that Horn/Silk may be an inhumi.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Horn/Silk is Malrubius; Horn/Silk was an inhumi

1. What exactly happened with Merryn and the dog? What was that all about?

2. Who are "the little men on the bridge" and "the black gang" (p. 336).

3. Are we to believe that Horn/Silk is Malrubius?

4. Who is the man in the cloak on Green? (p. 191)

5. There has previously been a suggestion that maybe Horn/Silk was an inhumi. It has been noted that one feature of inhumi is that they don't eat much. On p. 361, it is said of Horn/Silk: "sometimes it seemed like he was just pretending to eat and drink, and this was one of them." Wow! This is a Gene Wolfe red flare signal of something isn't it?
