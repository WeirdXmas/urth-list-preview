The thread: The thread began as a debate over Silk and Horn's bodies and eyes in the Long Sun and Short Sun books, with Lee Berman, Marc Aramini, Jordon Flato, James Wynn, Stuart Hamm and António Pedro Marques disputing who occupies the body, whether Pig received one eye or two, and how the chronology runs. Most posters agreed the eye was given before In Green's Jungles began, but they still disagreed about the body's identity and the eyepatch on the cover. Around New Year the discussion shifted to cover art, with Daniel Petersen and Gerry Quinn trading sharp views on whether the covers are faithful or embarrassing, and Matthew Keeley and Son of Witz adding details about reissues. It ended without resolution and drifted into general cover criticism.
  • Marc Aramini: Horn occupies the body and Silk can be recovered from the eye given to Pig
  • Jordon Flato: Silk gave Pig an eye, then became the Rajan on the cover of In Green's Jungles
  • James Wynn: Horn and Silk are the same being across both Short Sun and Long Sun books
  • Lee Berman: Silk's body lost an eye, which explains the single eye and the eyepatch on the cover
  • António Pedro Marques: Only one eye was given to Pig, before In Green's Jungles began
  • Daniel Petersen: Short Sun covers are kitschy, inaccurate and unsuited to Wolfe's subtle prose

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Memory and forgetting, Hidden identities and parentage
