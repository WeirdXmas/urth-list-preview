Identifies the staff-carrying figure Borski hinted at as Ceryx, the zombificator Zamae, and suggests he may be a dark counterpart to Inire. Responds to Robert Borski's clue puzzle, citing the Lexicon Urthus gloss of Ceryx's name as 'a herald' and Severian's sense that Ceryx had travelled similar paths, so the link rests on the text.
answers · puzzle-solving · source-and-allusion · theories: Ceryx is the staff-carrier Borski described; Ceryx as dark side counterpart to Inire

That would be Ceryx, zombificator Zamae. _Lexicon Urthus_ tells us his name means "a herald". But he seems most unlike Inire to me.

On the other hand, Severian gets the definite feeling that Ceryx had traveled by similar paths. Maybe he represents a sort of "Dark side of the Force".

-- Dan Rabin
