1998-04-29 Alice Turner #

Asks Pedro Jorge where Ultan quotes Borges, and reports that Viking will publish a large Borges omnibus in September including Ficciones and Imaginary Beings. Also notes Oliver Sacks suits the Latro books, announces reading choices for Week Two, and asks how many weeks the reading will run.
informs

Pedro Jorge,

For fun, can you tell us where Ultan is quoting Borges?

Good news for Borges fans: In September Viking is putting out a huge omnibus of Borges's shorter works which will includes FICCIONES, IMAGINARY BEINGS and a slew of other stuff, much of which has hardly been seen in English. Viking usually does a nice package, so this should be a must-have. With luck, there will be a nice compact bio in the intro.

Derek,

I agree that Oliver Sacks is really more suited to the Latro books (but he is certainly a fascinating read!). I'm going with Byzantium and Kafka for Week Two and can hardly begin to think of Three! How many weeks will there be? Akkk!

-alga-
