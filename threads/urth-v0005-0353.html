1997urthThe Book of the New SunDoug Eigsti

1997-10-23 Doug Eigsti #

Lists the poster's personal Wolfe collection, including a bound four-volume Book of the New Sun, limited and signed editions, and first editions, and says he seeks Empires of Foliage and Flower. Mentions his wish for an annotated, leather-bound, slipcased New Sun edition, framed as a light dream rather than an argument.
informs

Scott,

I wanted a leather bound copy of THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN so bad that I had all four books bound together in one volume. A local bindery, Rahm Book Binding of Colorado Springs, did the work, and did a great job. Other gems in my Wolfe collection:

Number 10/100 of THE CASTLE OF THE OTTER. Plus the regular hardback. The Ultramarine signed Ltd. ed. of THE URTH OF THE NEW SUN. 1ST eds. of THE BOOK OF THE NEW SUN, THE BOOK OF THE LONG SUN, THE DEVIL IN A FOREST (signed), PEACE (signed), FREE LIVE FREE (Ltd., signed), THE FIFTH HEAD OF CERBERUS, THERE ARE DOORS, PANDORA, BY HOLY HOLANDER, ENDANGERED SPECIES, GENE WOLFE'S BOOK OF DAYS, STORIES FROM AN OLD HOTEL, SOLDIER IN THE MIST, BIBLIOMEN (Ltd., signed), SOLDIER OF ARETE, and the 1st printing of CASTLEVIEW.
I have the first three issues of the SHADOW OF THE TORTURER comic. I would love to get my hands on the rest. I don't think they were ever published.
I am seeking a copy of EMPIRES OF FOLIAGE AND FLOWER.

I told mantis that we need an annotated edition of NEW SUN. Full leather (or maybe black buckram like Master Ultan's Book of Gold), gilt edging (with edge painting), signed, numbered, fully illustrated, and slipcased in a lead coffer. Hey, I can dream, can't I?

=Talon=
