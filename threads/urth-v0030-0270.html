2001-02-01 Jonathan Laidlow #

Invites list members to contribute articles to the revamped Ultan's Library, the e-journal for Gene Wolfe study that Jonathan Laidlow edits, with Book of the Short Sun pieces especially wanted. Notes that Jeremy Crampton's piece is due by end of February and Alex David Groce is writing one, and points readers to the site as a style model.
informs

Hello all,

I just wanted to invite you to contribute to the forthcoming revamp of Ultan's Library - the e-journal dedicated to Gene Wolfe which I edit. We desperately need some new articles, especially considerations of the Book of the Short Sun. By the end of Feb I will have posted Jeremy Crampton's latest piece, and Alex David Groce is writing something for me. What about the rest of you?

Offers gratefully received, you can get an idea of the style and quality we're looking for by examining the existing site.

Jonathan
