1998-11-20 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks whether the second edition of a Tales of Arabian Nights game, which the addressee worked on, is forthcoming or already out of print, and jokes that the game's pyramid is built by slave labor. Also asks if the addressee has seen the game Fading Suns. Responds to a Wombat post about the game, with no discussion of Wolfe's fiction.
chats

Wombat,

Re: "Tales of Arabian Nights" game, coincidentally that is a product I've been looking for (the West End Games version)! (I saw it once, but it slipped through my fingers . . . ) Is the second edition that you worked on soon-to-be-in-print or already out of print (since I'm certain it isn't in print right now)?

But yes, alas, it is true--as of old, the Pyramid is built by slave labor for the benefit of one man! Can't complain about "truth in advertising" here!

And have you seen "Fading Suns"?

=mantis=

1998-11-20 Kevin J. Maroney #

Reports that the English second edition of a Tales of the Arabian Nights game is stalled, with a German edition expected in 1999, and offers a possible fan translation of its rules. Replies to a poster's question about the game's availability and asks whether the first-draft GURPS New Sun material is still online and recommended.
answers

Drifting a bit off-topic, here, but....

The English version of the second edition of _Tales of the Arabian Nights_ is, unfortunately, in limbo right now. A German edition should be forthcoming in 1999. Perhaps I will publish a samizdat translation of the rules and components as an accessory....

The first-draft material for _GURPS New Sun_--is that still available on the SJGames website?

No, I haven't. Do you recommend it, or do you just recommend it as a curiosity?

Wombat, a.k.a. Kevin Maroney [email]
Kitchen Staff Supervisor, New York Review of Science Fiction http://ebbs.english.vt.edu/olp/nyrsf/nyrsf.html
