2000-12-09 Dave Lebling #

Notes that the omnibus trade paperback of Jack Vance's Dying Earth has a spaceship cover, published by Orb, which the poster recalls is Tor under another name. Asks Dan'l to explain the mismatch, following up on a quoted remark about the Short Sun books being treated as planetary adventure; the post is light and not about Wolfe's text.
questions

Dan'l wrote great wisdom on covers, but...

I just recently bought the omnibus Jack Vance _Dying Earth_ trade pb (my Lancer _Dying Earth_ is falling apart, etc.). The cover depicts a genre spaceship hovering over a genre future-abstract planet's surface. This book is published by Orb, which, as I recall, is Tor (Wolfe's publisher) under an assumed name. _Dying Earth_ is, as I hope we all know,
magic&fantasy&manners set in the far, far future. Certainly a Unique, but the people looking for spaceships are going to be very confused.

Explain that one, please, Dan'l.

-- Vizcacha
aka Dave Lebling
