1997-02-25 Kieran Cleary #

Argues that the name Moly, the magic herb in Wolfe's books, is a diminutive of molybdenum, a transition metal, so it works as a chemical-style name. Responds to a correction by Mantis who had confused Moly with Maggie, offering the metal as a possible source of the name while admitting it may already be known.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Moly's name derives from molybdenum

I'm new to the mailing list, so ignore this is someone has already pointed it out. Moly may be a magic herb, but it's the diminutive of Molybdenum which is also a (transition) metal, no. 42 in periodic table, and thus a suitable chem name.
