Violet the Szpy

Whorl list · 3 messages · 1999-03-31 to 1999-03-31

1999whorlThe Book of the Long SunPeter WestlakeMichael Straight
The thread: Peter Westlake asked whether a dress found for Chenille and her companions at Blood's mansion belongs to Violet, the second tallest girl at Orchid's, and what that would imply. Michael Straight answered with a plain reading: since Blood owns the brothel, Violet and other girls regularly entertain guests at his mansion. Westlake then proposed that Violet's offer to entertain the Trivigaunti general might connect to Dr Crane's spy network, suggesting the book's clue-like details could be deliberate. The thread ends with the puzzle still open, as no one resolves whether the dress and the entertaining point to a hidden plot.
  • Michael Straight: the dress means Violet and other brothel girls entertain guests at Blood's mansion, a plain reading
  • Peter Westlake: the dress and Violet's offer may be clues tied to Dr Crane's spy network, possibly deliberate

Themes: Hidden identities and parentage, Power, rulers and politics

1999-03-31 Michael Straight #

Proposes that the phrase about Violet being fitted with a dress means Violet and other girls from Orchid's brothel regularly entertain guests at Blood's mansion, since Blood owns the brothel. Offers this as a plain reading of the situation in The Book of the Long Sun, answering Peter Westlake's question about the dress and his puzzle over Violet's height.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Violet regularly entertains guests at Blood's mansion

I imagine it means that Violet and other girls from Orchid's brothel regularly entertain people at Blood's mansion. Blood owns the brothel.

-Rostrum

1999-03-31 Peter Westlake #

Asks whether a dress found for Chenille and her companions at Blood's mansion belongs to Violet, the second tallest girl at Orchid's, and what that would mean. Grounds the question in the Dramatis Personae description of Violet and the scene where the dress is found, acknowledging the poster is working from memory without references.
questions · close-textual-reading

I intended to read the Book again before asking this question, but you can't read a book that size at the drop of a hat, so I'll ask while I still remember. Hence lack of references, for which I apologize.

Violet is described in one of the Dramatis Personae lists as the second tallest girl at Orchid's. When Chenille et al emerge from the tunnels and fetch up at Blood's mansion, someone finds her a dress that is almost big enough. Does that mean it belongs to Violet? If so, what does that mean?

SBear.

1999-03-31 Peter Westlake #

Suggests that Violet's offer to entertain the Trivigaunti general may tie into Dr Crane's spy network, rather than being only a brothel detail. Builds on Rostrum's point that Blood owns the brothel and Violet's entertaining at his mansion, saying the book's clue-like details may be deliberate.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Violet's entertaining connects to Dr Crane's spy network

True, but we don't need a Wolfeian Clue to tell us that. I wondered if there was a tie-in with Dr Crane's spy network, especially as Violet volunteers to entertain the Trivigaunti general.

SBear.
