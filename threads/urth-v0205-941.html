2002-05-09 Adam Stephanides #

Disputes Christopher Culver's claim that the original PEACE cover art was poor, arguing the plain jacket suits the book's literary-novel presentation. Contrasts it with Gahan Wilson's later paperback cover, which the poster calls out of keeping with the book's spirit and a spoiler.
disputes · genre-and-craft

I don't think the jacket design sucks; I much prefer it to Gahan Wilson's cover art for the tpb, which is completely out of keeping with the book's spirit and is a big fat spoiler, too. The book was originally published as a literary novel, and the jacket design reflects this.

--Adam
