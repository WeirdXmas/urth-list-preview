2000-05-01 Urash, Tom #

Reports an Amazon notice for a new hardcover, The Laughing Magician, credited to Jack Vance as illustrator, and asks whether this is the Jack Vance who wrote The Dying Earth. The post relies only on the listing's details and the poster's puzzlement about the parenthetical.

2000-05-02 Jesper Svedberg #

Confirms that a listed Amazon omnibus credited to Jack Vance is the genuine author's book, identifying it as a collection of Eyes of the Overworld and Cugel's Saga. Recommends a cheaper Millennium Omnibus covering the full Dying Earth sequence for readers who do not collect books; the post is a brief reply to a question about a book listing.
answers

Yup, it's the right Jack.

_The Lauging Magician_ is an omnibus consisting of _Eyes of the Overworld_
and _Cugel's Saga_.

Unless you're into collecting books, i recommend buying the Millennium Omnibus consisting of the whole Dying Earth suite.

// Jesper Svedberg
