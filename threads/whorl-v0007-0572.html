1997-09-09 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Asks whether a copy of Book of Gold No. 1 can be obtained, noting the poster received No. 2 from Jeremy Crampton who said no copies of No. 1 remained. Speculates the publisher may have withheld No. 1 and requests a photocopy from Wombat, who had received copies from Gene Wolfe.
questions

Wombat,

Ah, you got your copies from Gene Wolfe? I got my copy of number 2 from Jeremy Crampton--he said he didn't have any copies left of number 1. Publisher's choice--maybe he doesn't want =me= to have a copy of number 1? <g>

Well, I =would= like a photocopy, so if it is possible please let me know!

=mantis=
