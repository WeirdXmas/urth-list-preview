Themes: Hidden identities and parentage

2000-08-21 Jim Russell #

Proposes that Gurloes and the Autarch are linked, since Severian's descriptions of both share the same mannerisms and bright eyes, and Severian sees the Autarch's mannerisms without placing them. Grounds this in the passage describing Gurloes and the later scene at the house Azure, and asks whether Gurloes or Palaemon uses the prosthetic phallus.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theories: Gurloes and the Autarch are the same person

Several things, one: Thanks for all the input about GW's politics.

Two: This is probably one of the more wacky theories put forward, and I havent got my copy of the book in front of me to check this, but has anyone noticed the similarities between Gurloes and the Autarch? The passage where Gurloes is described, which paints him as one of the most
intriguing minor figures of the book, talks about his
mannerisms and particularly bright eyes. When Sev then goes to the house Azure, his description of the Autarch is
basically the same - and he mentions something about
recognising the mannerisms, but being unable to place them. Is it Gurloes who later has to use the prosthetic phallus, or is that Palaemon ?
Just a thought.

Jim Russell
University of Exeter
