1998-11-26 Patrick O'Leary #

Promotes Michael Mantis's Lexicon Urthus as a Christmas gift for Wolfe readers, urging list members to buy it from Amazon or directly from the author. Responds to Nigel's congratulations to Mantis by praising the reference work's scholarship and joking about converting non-lupinians.
informs

<<
Mantis, are you the author of the wonderful "Lexicon Urthus"?

If you are, then let me congratulate you, and tell you that your work has been a great source of joy and pleasure to me.>>

And Christmas being right around the corner--what better way to convert non- lupinians to the cause, spread good cheer and rigorous scholarship, and warm the cockles of curmudgeons everywhere by purchasing said volume by said Mantis at Amazon Dot Com?

Or directly from the author, should he deem to give ordering to this most eager audience.

There, Michael. Don't say I never did anything for you:)

Patrick O'Leary

1998-11-27 Alice Turner #

Welcomes a new list member named Nigel who is a Virgil scholar with a Vulgate concordance, and points readers to the web archive for faster updates than the digest. Responds to Nigel's arrival as a greeting and offers practical advice about following list posts online.
chats

Nigel,

How very nice to have a Virgilist aboard, and, even better, a Virgilist with a Vulgate concordance on his hard drive! Welcome. If you get too impatient to wait for your daily dose of digest (as I do sometimes), you can bookmark http://www.urth.net/urth/archives/
and point your browser there for minute-to-minute updates. An exaggeration, since the list hasn't been very active lately. But Ranjit has rigged the archive so that it takes posts almost immediately.

-alga
