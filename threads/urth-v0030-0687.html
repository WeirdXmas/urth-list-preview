Themes: Buildings, cities and labyrinths

2001-09-01 David Duffy #

Shares a documentary's facts about the Royal Menagerie at the Tower of London, including the Lion Tower and a white bear kept there, and links these to the prison and torture cells. Asks whether other historical models, perhaps a castle with a nunnery inside its walls, could inform the Tower-like setting in Wolfe's fiction.
questions · source-and-allusion

I just watched a documentary about the Royal Menagerie within the Tower of London. Not only was there a Lion Tower (where the lions and tigers were, demolished in the 19th C), but Henry (III?) kept his white bear from the King of Norway at the ToL (it used to swim in the Thames). There is a Bear Tower at Warwick Castle. Of course, in the same complex, are the various cells and torture chambers for the human prisoners. Are there other historical models (perhaps with a nunnery within the walls)?
