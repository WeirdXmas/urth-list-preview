Ste. Anne/St. Croix

Urth list · 1 messages · 1998-06-01 to 1998-06-01

1998urthAlice Turner

1998-06-01 Alice Turner #

Proposes that the relationship between Ste. Anne and St. Croix in The Book of the New Sun resembles that between the twin American Virgin Islands St. Thomas and St. John, one bustling and one mostly forest. Supports this with a detailed description of the two islands' contrasting economies, resorts, transport and villages as a template for reading the Wolfe islands.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Ste. Anne and St. Croix modeled on St. Thomas and St. John

There is a tiny template for the relationship between St. Croix and Ste. Anne, and that is the one that exists between the twin American Virgin islands St. Thomas and St. John (it's confusing that there is also a St. Croix, but leave that out.) St. Thomas is a relatively bustling island, a cruise-ship stop, shops, resorts, rich people's houses, a real economy mainly fueled by people from the mainland, but with just about anything that you'd want or need (including, I am sure, at least one fancy brothel). St. John is mainly forest. It has one isolated RockResort for zillionaires that seems to be the island's only real source of employment and three tiny native villages with a few guesthouses and some scratch cafes open at odd hours. A ferry goes back and forth irregularly. There are very few cars and they are restricted by the few roads. The two, so close together, are entirely different worlds.

-alga-
