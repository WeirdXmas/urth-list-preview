2015urthThe Book of the Long SunRobert Pirkola

Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2015-04-05 Robert Pirkola #

Retracts an earlier claim that Scleroderma's account was copied into The Book of the Long Sun, arguing that Horn and Nettle relay many events they did not witness. Cites the Hare and eagle scene relayed by the kite maker, admits he could not confirm copying, and asks others to recall, citing On Blue's Waters page 51.
informs · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Parts of Book of the Long Sun copied from Scleroderma's account; Horn and Nettle relate events they did not witness

I may have overstepped in saying that Scelorderma's account had made into *The Book of the Long Sun* and saying that parts of the book were from the pens of others. ?What I more precisely meant to convey was the fact that many parts of the book as written by Horn and Nettle were accounts of events they did not personally witness, such as the scene with Hare and the eagle that was relayed at some remove by the kite maker. ?I had thought there were portions that had to be cribbed from Scleroderma's account as well but I have not been able to confirm that having taken the time to look at it. ?Maybe someone else can? recall better than I. ?Then of course, there is the added problem of determining whether we are reading Horn and Nettle's *The Book of the Long Sun* or that guy from Urbasecundus's. ?(See *On Blue's Waters*, pg. 51).
