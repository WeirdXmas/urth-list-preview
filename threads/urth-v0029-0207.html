2000-09-07 Peter Westlake #

Disputes David Duffy's suggestion that the story is Ship of Fools and his idea that Chang is the Antichrist, noting that there is a Captain and the Parks show no vices. Grounds the rejection in the text: Chang plays too small a part to be the Antichrist, and thanks David for the ideas.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Story is Ship of Fools; Chang is the Antichrist

I don't think so - it has a Captain, for one thing, and the Parks are not shown as having any particular vices. As for Chang being the Anti-Christ, he really doesn't play a big enough part in the story for that, I think. Thanks for the ideas, though!

Peter.
