Long Sun matters

Whorl list · 1 messages · 1997-03-18 to 1997-03-18

1997whorlThe Book of the Long Sunxury

1997-03-18 xury #

Asks whether Silk's "powers of the embryo" could be his enlightenment, whether Chenille is a real color or fabric given her hair's color and the flowers, and whether the bone-handled sacrificial knife and Silk's razor are a meaningful parallel. Also suggests Nessus is in South America, citing the Mate beverage as reasoning; the post is a loose set of questions rather than a worked argument.
questions · source-and-allusion · close-textual-reading · theories: Silk's powers of the embryo are enlightenment; Nessus is in South America

I, for one, feel that two list may be necessary if all of this talk continues like it does now. But, on the other side, it is fun to see the connections between all of Wolfe's books and see how each influence and compliment each other.
In other skies, we have Silk. I think this has been brought up before but, couldn't Silk's "powers of the embryo" be his enlightenment? I think this has been discussed though.
Another question I have is that of, is Chenille a real color? It is a type of material or fabric I know, but Chenille herself refers to it as a color, the color that she died her hair, she also says that there are Chenille flowers, which I am ignorant of also. However, the word in French means caterpillar, which kind of screws of the whole girl names=flowers. One more point is that of both the sacrifical knife and Silk's razor are bone handled. It is probably merely coincidence but I would like some opinions.

Xury

P.S.
My vote is that Nessus is in South America, but then my only reasoning is that Mate is a South American beverage(Peruvian, I believe).
