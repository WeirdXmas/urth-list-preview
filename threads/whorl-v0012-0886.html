Fooey for Fan Fic

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-06-21 to 2001-06-21

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunJosh Levitan

2001-06-21 Josh Levitan #

Argues that Horn's sons writing the missing chunks of The Book of the Short Sun differ from fan fiction, since they did not write from simple affection for Silk and Horn. Compares them to the Gospel writers and the Apocrypha, whose authors filled historical gaps to convey a message they believed was in the spirit of Jesus' teachings.

But there is, at least, a theoretical difference between the two. = Horn's
sons (and their wives) weren't writing the missing chunks of the TBOTSS because they thought Silk/Horn or Horn/Silk was nifty. Just like the apostles didn't write the Gospels simply because Jesus was a swell guy = and
they'd like to continue his adventures. And the writers of the = Apocrypha
aren't fan fic writers either. They were filling in gaps in the "historical" (using the term very loosely) record for reasons of their = own -- to relate a message they believed to be within the spirit of = Jesus' life and/or teachings, for example.

Narwhal
