Themes: Names and their meanings

2011-02-08 Andrew Mason #

Proposes that the place names in Wolfe's Long Sun books, such as Trivigaunte, Palustria and Pajarocu, may have been chosen by other gods rather than by Typhon's planners. Builds on Gerry Quinn's Latin-derived readings of city and tribe names, adding that Sphigx may have named Trivigaunte.
