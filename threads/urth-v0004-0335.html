1997-10-10 James Jordan #

Proposes that the juice ('OJ') in Peace is comic, pointing to Tang, the name Julius, and Pringles as jokes of brand-name substitution. Offers these as examples of humor in the novel's food and drink, with no deeper argument given.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Peace's juice is a comic brand-name joke

The OJ has lots of humor. It's Tang, for one thing. For another, remember the name Julius.... And, from potatoes, it's Pringles. (And vodka?!)
