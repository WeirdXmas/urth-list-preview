Wolfe's Next Book

1998-09-30 Robert Borski #

Informs the list of the publication status of Wolfe's next book, reposting Kieran Mullen's earlier report that the first volume of the Short Sun trilogy is not yet with editor Dave Hartwell. Relays the schedule: a final draft is hoped for in October, with a publication date set only after a contract is issued.
informs

Not long ago Kieran Mullen asked:

It may still be a while, I'm afraid.

GW has yet to submit the final draft for Vol. 1 of the Short Sun trilogy to editor Dave Hartwell, although he hopes to do so in October.

Only if Hartwell accepts the series and a contract is issued will a tentative publication date then be scheduled.

Patience, solarites!

Robert Borski
