1999-10-10 Alice Turner #

Recalls that the eclipse trick appears in the 1931 film A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court with Will Rogers, though not in Twain's novel. Responds to Nicholas Gevers's remark linking Columbus's 1504 eclipse hoax to Herge and Wolfe's neo-Inca gulling scenes, adding a parallel from another source.
informs · source-and-allusion

...

Wasn't there an "eclipse trick" featured in the 1931 movie "A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court" with Will Rogers in the title role? (it wasn't in Samuel Clemens' book)

Yes, it was! I've never seen the movie, and haven't read the book for at least a chiliad, but I remember it vividly.

-alga
