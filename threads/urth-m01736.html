2006-04-16 Maru Dubshinki #

Reports finding a Word-A-Day email signed by Gene Wolfe's address discussing frozen goldfish shipments, and asks whether it is the author or another Wolfe with an interest in obscure words. Sets the post in a playful tone around the word anabiosis, offering no argument about the books.
questions

Like I'm sure many of you do, I subscribe to A Word A Day; in one of the most recent compendiums, I spotted this:

"From: Gene Wolfe (genewlfATaol.com)
Subject: Re: A.Word.A.Day--anabiosis
Refer: http://wordsmith.org/words/anabiosis.html

Goldfish are routinely frozen in blocks of ice for shipment. A few die, but most survive. This has been a common practice for many years."

Is this the Wolfe, or is there another with an equal interest in occult words?

~maru
pondering forbidden knowledge.

2006-04-16 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to Matt DeLuca #

Argues that the Gene Wolfe quoted on a word-a-day list about goldfish frozen in ice is probably a fan who chose the Wolfe name deliberately, not a coincidence. Responds to Matt DeLuca's post, which quotes the anabiosis message and asks whether it is the author or another person with the same name.
proposes

Rather than coincidence, I suspect a Wolfe fan who took that address _because_ s/he is a Wolfe fan, and shares his
interest in rare and unusual words.
