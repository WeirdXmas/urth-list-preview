Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, Gerry Quinn, and António Pedro Marques replied with brief condolences, recalling Vance's long life, his jazz music, and his mastery of the craft. Aaron added a personal tribute, mentioning a three-hour phone call with Vance in 2010. The thread contains no debate about Wolfe's fiction and ends with a simple expression of mourning.

2013-05-30 aaron replying to António Pedro Marques #

Reports the death of Jack Vance, saying the poster has long expected it and feels numb, and recalls a three-hour phone conversation with him in 2010. Responds to António Pedro Marques's post and the news of Vance's passing, which was relayed by Craig Brewer; the post is a personal tribute with no critical argument about Wolfe's work.
informs

Although I have been expecting this for a while in the back of my mind, I hoped it would never happen. I find I am a bit numb. Vance (along with Wolfe) was a real hero of mine. I spoke to him on the phone in 2010 for about three hours. He was truly a beautiful guy, and interested in so many subjects. He had the enthusiasm of a teenager and his curiosity was contagious. Long Live Vance. May he rest in peace.
