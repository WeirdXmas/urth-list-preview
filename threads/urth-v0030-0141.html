Themes: Doubles, twins and clones

2000-11-29 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Argues that a 26.84-hour Annese day fits the characters' ages better than the 30-hour day, using a table that matches ages in Terran years to events such as Number Five's prison term. Grounds this in biological age, Sandwalker's boyish appearance, and a claimed twin link between Sandwalker and Number Five, while accepting that shape-changing may override the calculation.
proposes · chronology-and-plot-logic · puzzle-solving · theories: Annese day is 26.84 hours; Sandwalker and Number Five born same year; VRT is son of Mary Blount

I agree that it gets very close to "too short for JM to feel the diff"; excellent point about the equinox (I hadn't even =thought= about seasonal effects on daylight--duh!) and ugh, that shape-changing has the ability to trump just about anything <g>. And since I've done the work with a 30 hour day, believe me, I'd just asume let it stand.

However, the 26.84 hour day has a certain allure relating to biological age (i.e., Terran years as measured by human growth).
