Themes: The dying sun and the cosmos, Gods, angels and the Increate

1998-07-08 Kieran Cleary #

Recalls an earlier speculation that the monsters in Dr. Talos' play, which foretell Ushas, are inhumu and that Ushas and Lune mirror Blue and Green. Argues that the Hierogrammates' plan to produce their cognates depends on humanity being judged worthy, with Severian as humanity's apotheosis, so the two worlds forge them between New Sun and Ushas.
proposes · close-textual-reading · theology-and-religion · theories: Ushas and Lune mirror Blue and Green; Monsters foretold by Talos are inhumu; Hierogrammates forge cognates between New Sun and Ushas

Rostrum:

The first mention of Ushas is (if I recall correctly) in Dr. Talos' play, where one of the characters asserts that the new sun will cause the old continents to rise with their lost minerals and riches and a new race of monsters to appear. This Urth will then be called Ushas. [a long time ago in a list far away, I speculated that these monsters were inhumu and that Ushas/Lune = Blue/Green, the generation starship returns home to an alien world. *sigh*. Would that it were so]

Adam:

I think there is something to that. But more importantly, there's also the whole issue of the Hierogrammates trying to ensure the appearance of their cognates, those beings which once did or will eventually give rise to the hierogrammates. Severian is referred to as the apotheosis of humanity. Humanity is being judged as to whether it is likely they could be the creators of the heirogrammates. If so, they will be forged between hammer and anvil of New Sun and Ushas.
