Silk v. Hyacinth (celebrity deathmatch?) and Passilk

Whorl list · 1 messages · 2001-02-14 to 2001-02-14

2001whorlThe Book of the Long SunJoshua A. Solomon

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Hidden identities and parentage

2001-02-14 Joshua A. Solomon #

Proposes that Silk's broken soul in Horn's body came from Silk killing Hyacinth after a quarrel, and argues that Silk, merged with Pas as Passilk, now dominates the two-headed being. Grounds this in the quoted bloody scene on page 127, the Pas-Silk genetic background from Exile, and the godlings' changed command, and claims Mainframe's soul never broke.
proposes · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Silk killed Hyacinth, breaking his soul; Silk dominates Pas as Passilk; Mainframe Silk's soul never broke

When Horn's soul was transferred to Silk's body,
"(his) hands and arms and face and neck were all bleeding, and an old, worn knife covered with blood was by (his) hand.
There was no one else in the poor little house in which (he) knelt, and almost nothing in it that was not torn or broken" (p 127). What was it the Neighbor said about Silk's soul?
It was broken or something, right?
My theory is that Silk and Hyacinth had a big row.
Jealousy? Who knows. They spend a few hours throwing plates at each other and then Silk finally snaps. He kills his lover with the sacrificial knife. That oughta be enough to break his soul...

I haven't read Exile since it came out, but let me see if I remember correctly: Silk was genetically-engineered to contain a piece of Pas. Echidna et al. erased Pas from Mainframe. At the end of Exile, Silk got himself scanned into Mainframe. Now that Pas is back, he isn't quite the same. For one thing, he is now referred to as Passilk. Silk only contained a piece of Pas. In some sense, he also contained a piece of the Outsider. Maybe the godlings' about-face (first they say "go," now they say "stay") indicates Pas's losing dominance of the two-headed beast known as Passilk to Silk.

Finally, and someone mentioned this already, the soul of Mainframe's silk never broke. Thus when he possesses his old body he is still a pretty good guy, even if he slays an inhumu or three.

js
