Proposes that the Inhumi are a hybrid branch of humanity, with colonists from the Whorl as the pure strain that breeds into them to create a stronger line. proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: The Inhumi are a hybrid branch of humanity

Could the secret of the Inhumi be that they are a hybrid branch of humanity, and the colonists from the Whorl are the pure strain hybridizing through blood to make a more vigorous strain?

Also, does anyone else see the ruined ring of glass that lets light through in one direction but blocks it in the other as a ruined greenhouse dome?

2011-11-29 Gerry Quinn replying to Brian Lovely #

Disputes the claim that the ruined glass ring on Green blocks light, arguing it amplifies light and might have served as a greenhouse, solar station or observatory glass. Replies to Brian Lovely, citing Horn's greenbuck scene, where he sees motion first through the glass, then in a bush without it, then the greenbuck.
disputes · close-textual-reading · theories: Ruined glass ring amplifies light rather than blocking it; Glass transmits light from adjacent dimensions; Ruined glass ring was a greenhouse or observatory device

From: Brian Lovely

Marc has ideas along somewhat similar lines, I think. But really, I don?t see any strong reason to believe that the creatures of Green and Blue are anything other than alien species that evolved there. [Of course, one wouldn?t expect truly alien planets to have compatible ecosystems, but that?s something SF writers get a free pass on, same as FTL. Planets are like tropical islands during the great age of European sea exploration.]

That?s one reading of it, though I didn?t see anything about it blocking light in any direction. It seems to amplify light: a possibility that occurred to me is that it transmits light from adjacent dimensions. Against that is that when Horn first saw the motion of the bush where the greenbuck was, he thought the glass might have helped him. But he later saw motion in another bush without it, and after that saw the greenbuck. Glass that amplifies light might be used in a greenhouse, or a solar power station, or an observatory, or for other purposes. - Gerry Quinn
