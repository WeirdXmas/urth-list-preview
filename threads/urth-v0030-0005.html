Informs the list that Wolfe's story "The Eleventh City" has appeared in the anthology Graven Images, edited by Nancy Kilpatrick and Thomas S. Roche, with publisher details. Notes the story was originally written for an abandoned anthology titled Grotesques and reattaches the poster's earlier query about the story.
informs

Folks,

"The Eleventh City" has just appeared in the anthology _Graven Images_, edited by Nancy Kilpatrick and Thomas S. Roche
(Ace/Berkley Publishing Group; ISBN: 044100766X; $13.00). The usual disclaimer applies--I have no personal interest in pro- moting the publisher, either of the editors, or anybody else--but since it had been discussed on the list, I thought people might like to know.

The title of the anthology for which "The Eleventh City" was originally written had been _Grotesques_, but either that project folded or the book's title was changed--I don't know which, since I never knew who were to be the editors and publisher of _Grotesques_.

My old query about this story appears below.

Mark Millman
