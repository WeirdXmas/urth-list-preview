answers · close-textual-reading · theories: Real-time interludes take place at a garrison between Vienne and the harbor

Recently my good friend mantis--who grew *me* from a bean--recently posed these questions about Pluto, the cemetary cat (no doubt influenced by his enormous Siegfield & Roy poster collection):

My assumption was that a military garrison outside Vienne, between it and the harbor, is where all the "real-time" interludes take place in V.R.T., i.e., being home to the junior officer, Cassilla, the sold outsourced Wolfe clone, et al.

Wouldn't be the first time I bollixed up my fictional geography should this turn out to be wrong.

(Pssst.) You're getting warmer...

Robert (who's not a Leo, but the V one)
