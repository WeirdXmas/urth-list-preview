The Sorcerer's House Limited Edition from PS Publishing

Urth list · 8 messages · 2010-02-04 to 2010-02-05

2010urthThe Book of the New SunMatthew KeeleyGwern BranwenJames WynnFred KiescheStuart HammJames Jordan
The thread: Members discussed the PS Publishing and Subterranean Press limited edition of The Sorcerer's House after Matthew Keeley shared the listing. Fred Kiesche and Gwern Branwen both felt the price was too high, with Branwen also noting the author's age makes a signed copy uncertain. Kiesche said the signed edition was more appealing than the cheaper expected Tor hardback. Stuart Hamm reported the edition had sold out and that he skipped it over cost. The thread was about buying decisions rather than the novel, and it ended with the edition gone.
  • Fred Kiesche: tempted but deferring the purchase, and finds the signed edition more appealing than the cheaper Tor hardback
  • Gwern Branwen: the signed edition is too expensive, and Wolfe's age makes a signing uncertain
  • Stuart Hamm: skipped the limited edition because of its high price; it has since sold out
