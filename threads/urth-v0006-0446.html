1998-01-27 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Corrects a previous claim about autarchs, arguing that the text says autarchs who fail the Test are unmanned while only Ymar, the Old Autarch and Severian actually took it, and that Typhon was a Monarch. Supports the point by citing the timeline from Ymar's death about a chiliad before Severian's birth and suggests the Old Autarch's name is probably Appian, citing a story in Endangered Species.
informs · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: The Old Autarch's name is Appian

Reply: Item #0147191 from [email]@INET02#

Correction:

The text tells us: (1) autarchs who fail the Test are unmanned; (2) there have been autarchs since Ymar, and he died about a chiliad before Severian's birth; (3) yet only three autarchs have actually taken the Test--Ymar, the Old Autarch, and Severian.

Typhon was a Monarch, the last of the Monarchs.

The Old Autarch's name is most likely "Appian" (see "The Cat" in ENDANGERED SPECIES).
