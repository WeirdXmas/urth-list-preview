The thread: Greg Neyman asked how the chem Prion could draw all its hydrogen from the Long Sun ecocylinder air, how the Whorl powers itself, and why Quetzal worked to stop theophanies in Viron. Michael Straight proposed that the air is humid, supplying water vapour as the hydrogen source, with a joking aside about Hammerstone's weather. Alex David Groce argued that the Calde books do not explain Quetzal's motives and that the Short Sun books will likely address them, citing a Wolfe Q&A remark and John 8:32. The thread ended without resolution on the Whorl or on Quetzal's motives, and the hydrogen question was answered only by Straight's speculation.
  • Greg Neyman: doubts the chem Prion can get hydrogen from the air and asks about the Whorl's power and Quetzal's motives
  • Michael Straight: the Long Sun air is humid, so water vapour supplies the hydrogen
  • Alex David Groce: Quetzal's motives are unexplained in Calde and likely to be covered in Short Sun, citing Wolfe and John 8:32

Themes: Catholic faith and sacraments, Power, rulers and politics

1998-11-01 Greg Neyman #

Asks how the chem Prion could get all its hydrogen from the air of the Long Sun ecocylinder, given how little hydrogen air holds, and asks how the Whorl powers itself. Also asks why Quetzal in Calde worked to stop theophanies in Viron and whether the book explains this, closing a thread on the English problem.
questions

Hey everyone ...
Prion- how the heck can a chem get all its hydrogen needs from AIR? ... it can't be more than a few tenths of a percent in the air (to maintain the ecosphere ... or ecocylinder, as it were, the air composition couldn't deviate too significantly from earth's) ...

and it looks like this English problem has been put pretty much to rest. thanks for all your input, you guys (man I hate to be cowed ... )

last call ... any ideas on how the whorl powers itself?

A New Question: In Calde, Quetzal mentions that he's done his all he could to ensure that theophanies stop occurring in Viron ... I never really understood why ... does it get explained elsewhere in an oblique manner ... or not at all?

Later ...

1998-11-02 Michael Straight #

Proposes that the chem Prion gets its hydrogen from water vapour in the air, reading the Long Sun air as humid rather than dry, in answer to Greg Neyman's doubt about hydrogen from air. Offers a joking aside about humid weather from Hammerstone, with no textual citation beyond the quoted question.
proposes · genre-and-craft · theories: Prion draws hydrogen from water vapour in the air

The classic SF trope is getting hydrogen from electrolysis of water, so I imagine Prion is refering to water vapor in the air.

"Humid weather today. Yum!" -- Hammerstone

-Rostrum
