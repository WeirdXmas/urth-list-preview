1998urthThe Book of the New SunMichael Andre-Driussi

Themes: Stories within stories, Time loops and causality

1998-01-22 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Offers a personal theory that the New Sun freebooter succeeded unaided at first, drawing the Forces of Entropy back in time, and that helpers like the Green Man and Master Ash later appeared in response. Points to the Claw and Citadel books as evidence, and also responds to a poster's question about a lost essay, recommends a bookseller for finding Serconia, and comments on Clute's critical book Catherine the Weal.
proposes · myth-and-structure · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Forces of Entropy tripped back in time to stop the freebooter; Green Man and Master Ash as far-future helpers

Re: Clute's Catherine the Weal tested by the "Mant-O-Meter" . . . well, you can understand my bias--how I might not be the most reliable mantometer technician. But I believe there is a certain amount of mantic antic, with textual evidence (often alluded to in paraphrase; mainly lacking citations, so to trace it all requires a bit of work). Feeley's essay presents some text citations at the end.

Re: your internal archaeology and the lost essay. No, I don't recognize the essay, nor do I have a copy. Yes, I think that TBOTNS is about something like that at one level, but in my version: the freebooter did achieve the New Sun without any super help or hinderence the "first" time; this brought him to the attention of the Forces of Entropy, who tripped back in time to try and stop him; but his success activated the helping circuits (Claw, most obviously) that already existed (at least in potential).

(Your recalled essay: "friends from the far future" would be the Green Man, by himself or acting as an agent, who appeared in CLAW; balanced by "friendly enemy from the far future" Master Ash, who appears in CITADEL, and thus invisible to the pre-CITADEL essayist.)
