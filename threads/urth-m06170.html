2008-06-11 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes replying to James Crossley #

Explains that the poster first disliked The Urth of the New Sun as a dumbing-down that gives away what readers should work out, but after a second reading found it adds much to Severian's story. Responds to James Crossley's post saying he finds the Urth coda unnecessary and uninteresting, and credits other list members for prompting the rereading.
disputes · genre-and-craft

James,

That was my first reaction. I didn't like it at all on first reading, and thought
it was basically a dumbing-down of the series by giving away stuff that we were meant to figure out for ourselves.

But the opinions of some on this list sent me to a second reading, and I realized that there's a great deal more in the book, a great deal that is added to Severian's story by having it there.
