Themes: Beasts and the animal in us

2000-04-10 Timothy Reilly #

Corrects an earlier post by noting that an echidna is a monotreme that lays eggs rather than a marsupial, and points out the platypus as another egg-laying animal. Offers the echidna's ant-eating and ball-rolling defences as a comparison against a fearsome goddess, implicitly against a proposed animal identity in the Long Sun books.
disputes · source-and-allusion

Can I rush to correct a howler in my last post before anyone else does?

An echidna is of course a monotreme, _not_ a marsupial. It accordingly lays eggs, just like the even more adorable platypus (a so far unclaimed potential Vironese name). (Marsupials like kangaroos and wombats have live young which they suckle in pouches). Echidnas also eat ants and roll into a ball when threatened.

Does any of this sound like our fearsome goddess? I think not.

Tim
