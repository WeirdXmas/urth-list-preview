Out of print books

1998urthadam louis stephanides

1998-06-16 adam louis stephanides #

Warns that Amazon's out-of-print listings often carry very high prices and recommends searching instead through mxbf.com and bibliofind.com. Responds to Jason Voegele's suggestion to try Amazon's out-of-print ordering, offering the poster's own experience with mxbf as a search aggregator.
answers

Or a lot extra: amazon.com often charges very high prices for out of print books. Try www.mxbf.com or www.bibliofind.com first. I've used the former myself, and found it very helpful; it automatically connects you to a number of electronic book search services, including amazon.com (when I used it last).

--Adam
