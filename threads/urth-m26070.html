The thread: Participants debated whether Catholic and theological threads on the Urth list should be separated or titled more specifically so uninterested readers could skip them. Jeffery Wilson sarcastically told disinterested members to scroll past, and Gerry Quinn proposed clearer thread titles separating Catholic Wolfe from Catholic Theology. Marc Aramini wryly noted misleading titles and argued Catholic and heretical ideologies are valid interpretive keys for Wolfe. Wilson replied pessimistically that such discussions repeat too often and suggested a periodic non-theological approach. The thread ended without resolution, with the titling dispute left open.

2014-10-26 Jeff Wilson replying to Marc Aramini #

Responds to Marc Aramini's thread on Catholic readings of Wolfe by joking that such discussions have been repeated too often, with original sin deconstructed many times. Argues that a periodic scientific or non-theological approach to Wolfe would be reasonable, mocking the thread's expectations of its own premise.
chats

I've lead mailing list discussions and peer reviews for a gaming publisher, and even a hand-picked panel of referees of games with hundreds of pages of rules and an average of more than one university degree remained iffy on the concept after a decade.

Likewise, and I stayed politely attentive the first seventeen times original sin was deconstructed here, so I think a round of technical science once a year or so isn't out of line for a discussion of the greatest science fiction author living today, or even just the author of "How I Lost the Second World War and Helped Turn Back the German Invasion".
