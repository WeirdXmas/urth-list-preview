Entropy helps?

1998-01-26 xury #

Asks why the undine saved Severian as a child if Severian's predestined life was meant to bring about the New Sun and thereby the destruction of the Forces of Entropy. Frames it as a confusion about whether the rescue serves the Entropy side's interests, and invites suggestions from readers.
questions · myth-and-structure

I cannot help but to wonder that if, supposing, Severian had his fate set up from the beginning, a life predestined by whomever (an earlier Severian or such) and the Forces of Entropy were determined to prevent this catalyst from bringing the New Sun, then why did the undine save him as a child? She saved him so he could gain the Phoenix Throne, but they must know he would bring the New Sun also, thus bringing their own destruction. I am obviously confused on this matter and would welcome any suggestions.

Xury

1998-01-29 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that Juturna, the undine who saved Severian in The Shadow of the Torturer and tried to drown him in The Claw of the Conciliator, switched sides to save the child Severian because she survived the deluge in The Urth of the New Sun. Grounds the switch in her fate under an Entropy victory, given undines swim through time, and asks whether she will replace Abaia as Agia replaced Vodalus.
proposes · myth-and-structure · close-textual-reading · theories: Juturna switched sides to save Severian's child; Juturna will replace Abaia as Agia replaced Vodalus

Reply: Item #1005628 from [email]@INTERNET#

Xury,

Juturna, the undine who saved Severian in SHADOW and tried to drown him in CLAW (probably the first time =she= met him) seems to have gotten the idea of saving Severian the child while talking to Severian the demigod at the deluge scene in URTH . . .

A simple reason why she would switch sides: we see in URTH that she has survived the deluge and is happily munching fish. So perhaps she saw a short and unhappy life in store for herself if the Entropy side won? (The undines swim the corridors of time, as you know.) Will she grow in size and power to take the place of Abaia, as Agia replaced Vodalus?

=mantis=
