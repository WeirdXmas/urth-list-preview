Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

2000-08-10 David Langford #

Proposes that the plural "them" in Horn's altar-scene narration reflects the Outsider speaking with multiple paired voices at the opening of Nightside the Long Sun, possibly echoing the Trinity or two of its persons. Responds to Adam Stephanides's question about whether the word is a typo by offering this reading without quoting further passages.
proposes · theology-and-religion · close-textual-reading · theories: Outsider's paired voices echo the Trinity

When first manifesting at the opening of =Nightside=, the Outsider speaks with multiple -- "paired" -- voices. Wolfe may have the Trinity in mind, or two of its components.

Dave
