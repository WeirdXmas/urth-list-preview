Bye Silk! ... fusion ... and Escape from the Airship ...

Whorl list · 3 messages · 1998-11-02 to 1998-11-03

1998whorlThe Book of the Long SunGreg NeymanMichael StraightAlex David Groce
The thread: Readers debated whether Patera Silk has vanished for good at the end of Exodus from the Long Sun, and whether his escape from the airship cell was too easy. Greg Neyman argued Silk is gone permanently, citing Wolfe's airship-deck foreshadowing, and doubted the Trivis would be so careless. Alex David Groce agreed the disappearance might be the last we see of him but held that Horn's party could find him when they return to the Whorl, and questioned why the black mechanics cannot reach the fusion pack if they control programming. Michael Straight predicted that any Silk in the Short Sun books would be a merged Silk/Pas figure living in Mainframe rather than a returning person. The thread ended as a set of speculative predictions with no resolution.
  • Greg Neyman: Silk has vanished permanently and his airship escape seems too easy
  • Alex David Groce: Silk may still be found when Horn's party returns to the Whorl
  • Michael Straight: any later Silk will be a merged Silk/Pas figure in Mainframe

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions, Robots, androids and artificial life, Time loops and causality

1998-11-02 Greg Neyman #

Speculates that Silk has vanished permanently at the end of Exodus from the Long Sun, citing his earlier remark on the airship deck, and floats ideas about Silk as the positively beautiful man and about childbearing time in Lake. Asks whether Silk's escape from the airship cell was too easy, doubting the Trivis would be so careless.
questions · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Silk disappeared rather than died at Exodus's end

Alga- I think (sniff) that's that last anyone will ever see of Silk -n- Hy ... Wolfe foreshadowed to that much when silk was on the airship deck ... Silk said that if he fell at that moment, he wouldn't really die, not in the eyes of the people on the airship ... he'd just disappear, and who's to say what that means ... anyhoop, that DID happen at the end of exodus ... silk just DISAPPEARED ... an enigmatic ending for an enigmatic hero BTW - silk as the "positively beautiful man?" ... I can see that happening.

Rostrum - Hammerstone said in Lake that making a child takes at least two years, and maybe as long as twenty (as they get the parts, presumably) ... does the couple enter the "love-trance" for twenty years? there's no way they could be productive citizens and produce children simultaneously if that were the case.
And even if it was the case, I'd imagine that black mechanics would be able to find a way to circumvent this.
BTW good call on that water vapor bit ... I'm kicking myself for not having thought of it.

Alex ... thanks for respond RE: Quetzal ... it's nice to know that I didn't miss something that important ...

New question: Anyone else shaking their heads at the way Silk escaped the airship cell ... didn't it seem too easy? I just can't believe that the Trivis would be caught with their skirts down like that. Input would be appreciated, as always.

1998-11-03 Alex David Groce #

Argues that Silk may not be gone for good, since Horn and company might find Patera Silk when they return to the Whorl, while agreeing that his disappearance could be the last we see of him. Responds to Greg Neyman's quoted claim that Silk vanished like Wolfe foreshadowed on the airship deck, and questions why the black mechanics cannot access the fusion pack if they control programming.
disputes · close-textual-reading · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk may return when Horn reaches the Whorl

Yes, it's a nicely evocative connection. I'm not sure Silk is gone--it's possible, and the airship suggests it, but it seems at least possible that when Horn & co. head back to the Whorl, they will indeed find Patera Silk. But I do agree it's at not unlikely we've seen the last of him.

Well, it wouldn't have to be a "trance"--humans don't necessarily go into a trance to make a kid, but don't have conscious access to the workings of it. But it does seem odd the black mechanics can't get at this if they can mess with programming as much as they seem to be able to (maybe Pas had this a lot better guarded, especially the fusion pack part--as that would seem to be the really dangerous part.)

Predicts that if Silk appears in the Short Sun books, he will be a merged Silk/Pas figure residing in Mainframe, rather than returning in person. Responds to Alex David Groce's view that Silk may be gone, though Groce allowed he might be found when Horn's party returns to the Whorl.
proposes · puzzle-solving · theories: Silk and Pas merge into an amalgam in Mainframe

I predict that if we see Silk at all in the Short Sun books, it will be as part of a Silk/Pas amalgram residing in Mainframe.

-Rostrum
