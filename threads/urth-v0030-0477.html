Themes: Books, libraries and writing

2001-06-13 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Proposes that the Necronomicon's status is a fictional-chain puzzle: H.P. Lovecraft invented it through allusions, and Gold merely wrote the book in the 1950s, making it real. Raises a timeline breach in Peace, since O Rare Amanda appears to date from 1954 while Gold Hunt is placed in 1953, and asks for verification.
proposes · source-and-allusion · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Gold wrote the Necronomicon from Lovecraft's fragments; Gold Hunt set in 1953 conflicts with publication

Might be too tricky an entry for the Inivisible Library.

HPL made it up (in the 20s, was it?) through allusions in his fiction.

Gold "just wrote it" in the mid-50s, presumably building up from the fragments HPL scattered about and folding in black magic texts to bulk it up into a book. Gold, being the master forger that he is, seems pretty certain that he has made concrete that which was only a nightmare before (and as I've said before, there is a weird way in which he =has=, seeing the way he recognizes AD Weer as a shade).

Meanwhile, hey Roy! It looked to me as if O RARE AMANDA was published in 1954 . . . you know what that means! Timeline Alert!

<klaxon sounds>

"Breach in PEACE timeline at 1953."

We have a problem, since we place GOLD HUNT (preludes with Amanda Ros stuff at library) at 1953. More research required: was ORA =first= published in 1954? Is "Lusty Lawyer" a documented case of forgery in our world?

=mantis=
