1998-01-22 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Reports that the poster, identified as Wes Parsons, wrote an unpublished essay responding to John Clute's essay on Catherine the Weal and a similar piece by Feeley. Explains the essay will appear in a forthcoming collection, declines to post it because it presupposes familiarity with the Clute essay and The Book of the New Sun, and praises Clute's theory while saying he came to doubt parts of it.
informs · close-textual-reading

Reply: Item #3642999 from [email]@INET03#

Wes Parsons,

Ah-hem, er, why yes, I =did= write an essay called, "The Death of Catherine the Weal and Other Stories," in direct response to Clute's essay and a similar one by Feeley in "NYRSF" #31 and #32 (1991). It is slated for Clute's book of Wolfe related essays by various people, to be published sometime in the future.

Said book has essays by Peter Wright, Jeremy Crampton, Feeley (presumably), and Clute (I hope!), among others.

While it is an interesting thought experiment to consider posting the essay, I won't actually post it.

For one thing, we have had independent analysis that proves beyond the shadow of a doubt that the essay is difficult to follow on its own; that it presupposes familiarity not only with TBOTNS but also Clute's essay. And I am unanimous in agreeing with that verdict.

I really do like Clute's essay. It knocked my socks off and I was under its spell for a while until little cracks began to appear and I determined that I didn't believe it entirely. So I wrote the response.

It is a far better thing to come up with a dazzling theory, however flawed, than it is to pick apart someone else's dazzler. Everyone should read Clute's essay.

=mantis=
