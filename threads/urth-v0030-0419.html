2001-05-14 Gregg Best #

Reports that Wolfe mentioned at Readercon that he was 1100 pages into his next book, and argues that the figure is probably outdated after ten months. Also notes, in passing, that Wolfe's 70th birthday fell the previous week, which bears on the recent timeline talk about Copperhead.
informs · author-and-biography

My attention was caught by the bit about Mr. Wolfe's next book being 1100 pages, and I wanted to point out that at Readercon last year, Mr. Wolfe mentioned he was 1100 pages into his next book. I assumed at the time he meant he was 1100 manuscript pages along on the first draft. That was 10 months ago, and I doubt the 1100 pages is significant any longer--what are the odds that 10 months later, his next book would still be 1100 pages long?

Oh, and thinking about the recent timeline discussions about "Copperhead", I think Mr. Wolfe had his 70th birthday last week. Or not.
