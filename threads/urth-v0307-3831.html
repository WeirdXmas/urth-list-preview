Recounts a past art class in which the poster drew ovals, wavy paper and twigs for weeks while the teacher corrected how they looked at things, and says the frustration paid off in learning to draw. Offers the anecdote without tying it to any Wolfe book, character or reading, so it reads as a general remark about learning craft.
informs

I had an art teacher once. Drawing class. We drew ovals for a week. We drew big peices of paper tacked up on the wall in wavy designs for three weeks. We drew twigs for two weeks.

The teacher would come aroudn the room while we were drawing, and look over our shoulder. He'd say "No, that's not right. You're not *seeing*" "that's not paper, that's cloth"

and then he'd move on.

It was incredibly frustrating, but at the end, I knew how to draw.

Paul
