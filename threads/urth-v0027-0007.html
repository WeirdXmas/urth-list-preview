Proposes that Cyby in The Book of the New Sun may be modeled on Norman Thomas DiGiovanni, the Borges translator, since one photo seemed to fit Wolfe's description. Responds to Dan Parmenter's Borges-apprentice reading, admitting the description is vague and Wolfe was bald by then, but saying that does not disprove it.
proposes · author-and-biography · source-and-allusion · theories: Cyby based on Norman Thomas DiGiovanni

From: Geoff Alex Cohen <[email]>

I've often wondered if Cyby was based on anyone. For a while I was convinced that he was based on Norman Thomas DiGiovanni, the controversial Borges translator. The one photo of him I'd seen seemed to fit Wolfe's description of Cyby, but it's not exactly a detailed description. Wolfe is quite bald and was by then, but that hardly disproves your hypothesis.

Lex Shellac
