1998-06-02 Robert Borski #

Writes a short sample diary entry signed rb, dated 2 June, that imitates a diary's voice to illustrate the thread's diary-as-metafiction idea. Describes a feverish illness, a mirror showing three heads, odd-tasting froglegs and a planned train trip, without stating any link to Wolfe's fiction or responding to another poster.
chats · genre-and-craft

2 June, 19_ _---

Fear I may have picked up a bit of distemper here--so feverish that when I look in the mirror I appear to have three heads. But it might also have been the froglegs--they tasted curiously off, nothing like the last time I had them at Aunt Jeannine's. (Will have to ask Michael where he purchased them--perhaps there's a reason they retailed at 12/$.99).

At any rate, will probably go right to bed after shaving, so I'll be fresh for the train tomorrow.

rb
