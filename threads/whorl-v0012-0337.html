Dorpish Dialect

2001whorlDan'l Danehy-Oakes

2001-02-22 Dan'l Danehy-Oakes #

Argues that the Dorpish dialect in The Book of the Long Sun is not Yodese but is modeled on the older Pennsylvania Dutch speech of Lancaster County. Offers this as a personal observation, with a nod to Mark Twain's essay on the German language.
proposes · source-and-allusion · theories: Dorpish is modeled on Pennsylvania Dutch

Yodese Dorpish dialect is not. Never have any of you Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, visited? The Dorpish in the dialect of the (older) Pennsylvania Dutch speak.

With, perhaps, a nod to Mark Twain's "The Awful German Language."

--Dan'l
