2009-04-09 John Watkins replying to Lane Haygood #

Argues that Severian's use of the word "gnostic" in the Witches' Tower scene is a future-vocabulary usage meaning roughly "occult" or mysterious, not a sign of real Gnostic belief. Responds to Lane Haygood's question about where Severian learned the word, and cites b sharp's quotation of the chalked Gnostic designs in the Witches' Tower as the textual basis.
answers · close-textual-reading · source-and-allusion · theories: Gnostic in BotNS means occult to Severian

Remember, it's Wolfe's future vocabulary. "Gnostic" here, at least to Severian, is probably synonymous with "occult"--the symbols are mysterious, understood or intelligible only to an initiated few.

Of course, *Wolfe *probably wants to evoke Gnosticism even though Severian has no such thought.
