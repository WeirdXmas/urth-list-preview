1999-04-26 Michael Straight #

Recommends Vernor Vinge's new novel A Deepness in the Sky and its predecessor A Fire Upon the Deep, noting that Vinge's Old Sun/New Sun setting is unrelated to Wolfe's New Sun but still evokes mythic resonances. Responds to Peter T. Cash's recommendation of Banks's Inversions by offering a comparison with another author who reminds the poster of Wolfe.
informs

On Fri, 23 Apr 1999, Peter T. Cash recommended Banks's INVERSIONS

Speaking of other authors that remind one of Wolfe, I've been reading Vernor Vinge's new book, _A Deepness in the Sky_, which doesn't have as many really amazing ideas as _A Fire Upon the Deep_, but is still a very good read.

The reason I mention it is that it features an "Old Sun/New Sun" which is not really indebted or related to Wolfe's New Sun tale, but which still produces some interesting mythical resonances in my head.

If you've not read _A Fire Upon the Deep_, I give it one of my highest recommendations in the sensawunda category.

-Rostrum
