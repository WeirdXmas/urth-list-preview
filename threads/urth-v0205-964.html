VanderMeer's City of Saints

Themes: Stories within stories, Unreliable narration

2002-05-11 Nicholas Gevers #

Recommends Jeff VanderMeer's hardcover City of Saints & Madmen as a book Wolfe readers will enjoy, noting its high-quality writing. Cites its unreliable narrators, books within books, disguised storylines, embedded stories and metafictional tricks, which resemble The Fifth Head of Cerberus and The Book of the New Sun.
informs

Just a few words about Jeff VanderMeer's extraordinary
book CITY OF SAINTS & MADMEN, now available in
hardcover (Prime Books). The hardcover, a major
expansion of last year's trade paperback version, is
ideal reading for anyone who likes Wolfe--aside from
the very high quality of the writing, there are enough
unreliable narrators, books within books, disguised
storylines, embedded stories, and metafictional tricks
to satisfy 5HC and NEW SUN admirers. Plus Byzantine
inspiration in common.

--Nick Gevers.
