The thread: Michael Thayer asked for a detailed source on the five Severians theory in The Book of the New Sun, and Larry Miller pointed him to Michael Andre-Driussi's Severian entry in Lexicon Urthus. Thayer replied that the Lexicon entries are opaque about which Severians are the same person and what the five coffins mean. Jeff Wilson argued the Claw and the White Fountain are linked channels of Severian's powers, possibly perceived as separate selves. Lee proposed that the five coffins point to Gene Wolfe himself as the fifth head of The Fifth Head of Cerberus, an autobiographical reading. David Stockhoff reposted a parallels workup mapping characters onto Wolfe's life, and Marc Aramini dismissed that approach as barren, favoring structural patterns and symbols instead. The thread ends in disagreement without resolution.
  • Michael Thayer: asks for a detailed source and finds the Lexicon entries opaque
  • Larry Miller: points to Andre-Driussi's Lexicon Urthus entry on several Severians
  • Jeff Wilson: the Claw and White Fountain are linked channels, possibly separate selves of Severian
  • Lee: the five coffins point to Gene Wolfe himself as the fifth head
  • David Stockhoff: life-parallels workup gives names an origin-level meaning
  • Marc Aramini: life-mapping is barren; structure and symbols like Peace and the Simurgh matter more

Themes: Doubles, twins and clones, Names and their meanings, Books, libraries and writing

2013-12-14 Michael Thayer replying to Michael Thayer #

Asks where Druissi's theory of several Severians is laid out in detail, saying the Lexicon Urthus entries are opaque about which Severians are the same person and what the five coffins mean. Responds to Larry Miller's pointer to the Lexicon Urthus Severian entry, noting the Mausoleum Builder and Dorcas passages do not explain themselves.
questions · puzzle-solving · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian exists as several time-sliver versions; Mausoleum Builder is Severian Eight

Thanks, Larry -- Druissi's eight Severians theory is a bit opaque. It seems that Severians Four, Seven, and Eight are the same Severian, and Severians Three and Five are not Severian at all (I'm not sure why the Claw ("Severian Three") or the White Fountain ("Severian Five") are considered versions of Severian). The theory is especially opaque when it comes to the "Mausoleum Builder" ("Severian Eight"). Druissi says that the narrator of Urth ("Severian Seven: the Sleeper God of Ushas") maintains "the ability to walk the Corridors of Time and will somehow or another end up becoming the mausoleum builder." He then goes on to say that the mausoleum builder "killed Dorcas when she died the first time," which I confess I don't follow. In the "Mausoleum" and "Mausoleum Builder" sections of Lexicon Urthus, Druissi acknowledges but does not explain the meaning of the five coffins, and why two are open and three closed. It is a theory of the five coffins, perhaps, that I am really after.

2013-12-15 Jeff Wilson replying to Michael Thayer #

Argues that the Claw and the White Fountain are intimately connected channels for Severian's powers, and that Severian may perceive them as separate selves. Responds to Michael Thayer's question about why the Claw and the White Fountain count as Severian versions, citing the White Fountain's sense of seeing himself from afar and the genetic tagging by blood and semen from his tryst with Alpheta.
answers · close-textual-reading · theories: The Claw and White Fountain are channels of Severian's powers

They are intimately connected channels for his powers, and at times he may perceive them as separate selves. Particularly with the White Fountain, he has a sense of seeing himself from far away. They are also tagged with his genetic material, The Claw by blood and The White Fountain by semen from his tryst with Alpheta.

2013-12-16 Lee Berman replying to Michael Thayer #

Proposes that the five coffins in the Book of the New Sun mausoleum point to Gene Wolfe himself as the "fifth head" of The Fifth Head of Cerberus, reading the works as autobiographical puzzles. Grounds this in Wolfe's admission that Maitre's name solves to "Gene Wolfe," the title's mismatch of five heads, Severian's father Ouen, and two open coffins standing for Fifth Head and Peace.
proposes · puzzle-solving · author-and-biography · theories: The fifth head of Cerberus is Gene Wolfe; Five coffins represent Wolfe's autobiographical self-insertion; Severian is a clone extension of Wolfe

I agree. Driussi is usually fairly circumspect in injecting his own interpretations onto Wolfe. But now and then, as in this case, he demonstrates an ability to go off on his own tangent with almost Borskian intrepidity.

Personally, I find the five coffins in the mausoleum to be impossible to plausibly understand at just a narrative level. I find it to be most essentially autobiographical in the manner of a puzzle in its referent, The Fifth Head of Cerberus.

I think most here are aware that Wolfe once (remarkably) admitted that the solution to the puzzle of figuring out Maitre's name in Fifth Head of Cerberus is: "Gene Wolfe".

I don't find this to be mere literary cleverness and sleight of hand. I think this is Wolfe's way of saying that much of the mystery of his work can find its solution in autobiography.

I find the title "Fifth Head of Cerberus" itself to be a similar puzzle (Aunt Jeannine suggests that it is). What are the "five" heads? We have Mr. Million, his first clone, Maitre and Number Five. So only four in the sequence. Who is the fifth?

I think the answer is the person who came before Mr. Million and who created him as a character. I think the fifth head is Gene Wolfe (the author) and only by catching that can you understand the title of the book and thus a main intended self-referential auctorial theme of the book.

So for me, the five coffins in the mausoleum are a direct reference to the earlier book title and a way of saying, "once again I, Gene Wolfe as author, am an integral part of this story and my character Severian is a clone/extension of me in some ways". Naming Severian's father "Ouen" (Welsh form of Eugene) may also make a similar point.

I find Driussi's essay on the similarities between Wolfe's and Severian's lives to also be relevant to this general idea.

Explaining why two of the coffins are open is a bit tough, but my best guess is that Wolfe is saying he has "cloned" himself into two of his previous novels, those being Fifth Head of Cerberus and Peace.

Interestingly, Daniel Otto Jack Peterson has recently posted a link to a review of Peace which mentions its autobiographical mysteries and the self-awareness of the author as he creates the story and its characters.

FWIW, I first posted these ideas on this board a number of years ago, so perhaps there is a chance this is what you were looking for Michael?

2013-12-18 David Stockhoff replying to Lee #

Reposts a parallels workup, originally by Mantis, that maps characters and places in The Book of the New Sun onto Wolfe's own life, including the Korean War and his marriage. Proposes that this pattern gives the names some origin-level meaning without explaining the plot, noting Wolfe uses several plotting methods at once.
informs · author-and-biography · source-and-allusion · theories: Characters in BotNS map onto Wolfe's wartime and personal life; Severian's father's name Ouen as Eugene

I did not know that! Sounds almost like "good Gene." Or, maybe, "good /gene/."

I want to repost here Mantis' workup of parallels between BNS and Wolfe's life (perhaps you meant to include it, or you sent it as an attachment?). For the most part it shows that there is more than one way to plot a story (and Wolfe uses all of them at once!), but it does give us a pattern that, while not "explaining" much, does provide a little meaning behind the names (even if just an origin).

The Citadel of the Autarch = Texas A & M.
Valeria = Rosemary Dietsch.
Atrium of Time = the Dietsches' (front/back)yard in Peoria, Illinois A.D. 1935.
Jolenta = Marilyn Monroe (Wolfe saw her when she visited the troops). Triskele = Snooper the dog (one paw crushed by tread).
Pelerines = Red Cross girls.
Pyrotechnic polearms = B.A.R. and the Chinese Forward Observers that Wolfe saw burned to death by flamethrowers on Hill Chinese 200. Agia's ploy at Mine = prostitutes working out of caves.
Erebus = USSR (aiding/directing North Korea).
Abaia = Communist China (aiding/directing North Korea).
Ascia = North Korea.
Nessus = Seoul ("there was a time when they [Ascians] laid waste to Nessus" [IV, ch. 24]; i.e., at beginning of Korean War, Seoul was overrun Vodalus = Howard Hughes-like anarcho-aristocrat.
Baldanders = the Atomic Bomb (Little Boy/Fat Man) [especially check out that mushroom cloud formation when flying saucer is parked over his tower]. Typhon = Gen. MacArthur, "American Caesar" as well as "Arthur awakened from the mountain where he sleeps."
Kim Lee Soong = K-92, one of Wolfe's Korean "katusa" pals. Jonas = Algis Budrys and/or Hawkeye of M.A.S.H. fame.
I, CLAUDIUS = one of the books Wolfe re-read at the front. The 18th Bacele of Irregular Contarii = the 1st Battallion of the 17th [attention 17 watchers!] Infantry (Wolfe's unit).
Orinthyia = Porkchop Hill.
The Samru = troopship Marine Phoenix, which brought Wolfe back to the US (May 18, 1954).
Marriage to Valeria/ascent to the throne = Marriage to Rosemary (A.D. 1956) and conversion to Roman Catholocism.

2013-12-19 Marc Aramini replying to David Stockhoff #

Argues that mapping Wolfe's life onto The Book of the New Sun is interpretively barren, and that structural patterns and symbols matter more for filling in elided closure. Points to Peace details: fairy-tale metamorphosis into birds, the Simurgh, the blue bird and Professor Peacock, and the Sun-sun and Ming-sno pairing of Den and Olivia.
disputes · myth-and-structure · close-textual-reading

I never much cared for this kind of stuff, Jolenta as Marilyn Monroe and whatnot. Of course autobiography is in there in some way shape or form , but this kind of mapping always rubbed me as interpretationally barren.

Patterns and repetitions and structurally created symbols are way more important for filling in elided closure. Personal problems have delayed my Peace write up but I have it framed in my head now - the green fairy book and metamorphosis, mostly into birds - mention of the children of the sidhe that become the flock, olivia as bird boned, the talk of the simurgh which roosts in the tree of life, the story of the blue bird who is king charming from the green book and professor peacock ... The banshee as derived from the word sidhe ... All those fairy stories with their princesses and the princess Indian names the women take when they write on the skin ... Sun sun and Ming sno, fire and ice, den picks up sun-sun while Olivia takes sno. Symbolic associations like that that are only pseudo logical but becoming meaningful as a pattern.
