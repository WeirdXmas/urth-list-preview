DOORS: Tina and other another doll

2003urthJohn Vik

Themes: Ghosts, dreams and visions

2003-01-23 John Vik #

Asks whether the sailor's speech in The Shadow of the Torturer about a lost doll connects to Tina in There Are Doors, and recalls Wolfe's reply 'Keep trying' when asked about Malicapata. Grounds the question in a remembered scene and a book-signing exchange, offering no argument of his own.
questions · close-textual-reading

I hope I'm not pushing through a grown-over and old road but I remember towards the end of TSOTT when the sailor accosts Severian and rails about his (lost?) doll. Many years ago I memorized this speech to use as an audition piece, and no I didn't get the part, but I do remember all the blank looks I got. Is there a connection here with Tina, or am I way, way off base? Also many years ago, at a book-signing at the University Bookstore in Seattle, I asked Mr. Wolfe what Malicapata meant. I told him all the connections/associations that I had found and he nodded and smiled and said, "Keep trying." And it seemed that he said it in a significant way. But, as life goes, I was never able to follow up.
