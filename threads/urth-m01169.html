2005-06-10 Sarah Dorrance-Minch replying to turin #

Jokes that Severian's story would make a good Hamlet spoof, noting Yorick's return after the graveside scene and Dorcas's hydrophobia as Ophelia comparisons. The post is playful social chat with the poster mentioning family life and listing other Shakespearean tragedies, plus quoted verse from Khayyam and Lawrence.
chats

Hamlet didn't have Yorick waking up and tagging along
after him half an hour after the little graveside, er,
tete a tete. Or Ophelia, although I wonder if Ophelia
would share Dorcas' hydrophobia.

I would love to see a spoof of Hamlet, starring
Severian. Now that is a holiday pantomime begging to
be written. Maybe if I can ever sneak some time away
from the children - have one nursing barnacle, one
toddler with full blown autism, which makes long
chunks of time for writing a slight bit problematic.

Of course I'd probably have to also include elements
of Macbeth, Julius Caesar, and Titus Andronicus. Hmm.

Midori/Sarah

Ah, love couldst thou and I conspire/ To grasp this sorry scheme of things entire/ Would we not break it all to bits, and then/ Remold it nearer to the heart's desire - Omar Khayyam

Reach me a gentian, give me a torch
let me guide myself with the blue, forked torch of this flower down the darker and darker stairs, where blue is darkened on blueness. - D.H. Lawrence, "Bavarian Gentians"
