Themes: Time loops and causality

1999-06-25 Paul C Duggan #

Jokingly notes that The Book of the New Sun is a time travel story in which Severian sleeps with his grandmother, though the means is not time travel. Asks which science fiction stories use the grandfather paradox, with the grandmother brought forward and sent back, and closes with an unrelated quotation from Eugen Rosenstock-Huessy.
questions · theories: Severian sleeps with his grandmother

It is somewhat amusing that BOTNS is a time travel story, and Severian sleeps with his grandmother, but the means of so doing is not time travel (at least not by severian).

Any SF stories where the grandfather paradox is a result of the grandmother being brought forward in time (and then sent back?)
