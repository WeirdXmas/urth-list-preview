Severian and the White Fountain (My Ideas)

Themes: Gods, angels and the Increate

1998-07-14 C R Culver #

Argues the Hierogrammates judge whether people deserve a miracle, and guesses Severian was picked to show a wicked torturer could become divine. Answers Adam's questions on why an Autarch must carry the Fountain, citing Severian's Ship of Tzadkiel remark that the Autarch represents Urth to the Hierodules.
answers · theology-and-religion · close-textual-reading · theories: Severian chosen to show a torturer can become divine; Hierogrammates grant miracles only to the deserving

Adam asked:

This is, IMHO, rather simple. The Hierogrammates are theological beings who serve the Pancreator. As such, they judge the sentient beings of their area and decide whether those people are deserving of a miracle.

Also:
<And if someone has to bring it, why must it be an Autarch?>

Severian mentions on the Ship of Tzadkiel that the Autarch is by right the representative of Urth to the Hierodules, even though he rules a small portion of it.

Also:
<And if it has to be an Autarch, why Severian in preference to Ymar or the old Autarch?>

That's an enigma, but my guess is that they picked Severian to show that a person who lives a wicked life (i.e. that of a torturer) could become divine, in agreement with certain Christian doctrine. The Autarch and Ymar were too nice, or something.
