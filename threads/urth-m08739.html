2009-03-16 Matthew Groves replying to Fred Kiesche #

Reports that the poster also saw the new Wolfe story anthology and notes that each story carries a short postscript by Wolfe, giving a reason to buy it even for readers who own the stories. Responds to Fred Kiesche's message announcing his purchase of the same anthology, and says the postscripts reveal no secrets but are often interesting or funny.
supports

I saw it too. I noticed that here are little postscripts by Wolfe for each story, so there is a reason to buy it, even if you have every story contained in one collection or another. The postscripts don't give any secrets, but they're often interesting or humorous.
