Quetzal's Desciption; Pike the Vampire Slayer; Mucor Wakes the Gods

1997whorlThe Book of the Long SunDave Lebling

Themes: Masks, disguise and shapeshifting

1997-04-11 Dave Lebling #

Asks how far an inhumu's shapeshifting goes, whether Quetzal could disguise himself as Pike, and whether Pike ever really met a vampire. Challenges the quoted Quetzal-as-Pike theory by noting Pike only told Silk that devils infiltrated the whorl, and asks how often Mucor woke the gods, given her presence near the Kypris theophany.
questions · close-textual-reading · chronology-and-plot-logic · theories: Quetzal is the vampire Pike fended off; Quetzal-as-Pike theory; Quetzal could disguise himself as Pike

mantis sez:

Actually, I thought _I_ came up with that one. Like Lasrach, I am curious about the limits of inhumu shapeshifting. Could Q disguise himself as Pike? We don't get much of a description of Q's body, other than that it horrified those who went to prepare it for burial (or whatever one does on a lander). I visualize an inhumu in its natural state as having a more-or-less human-looking head, what you would get if you made a cartoon version of a snake's head. (Actually, his description is like nothing so much as E.T.!) The neck in its relaxed state is very wrinkled, in its erect state is like the hood of a cobra. It has wings which can be shaped into arms and a snake-like body which can be shaped into something fairly leg-like.

When did Pike fend off a vampire? He told Silk that devils had infiltrated the whorl, but do we know he had an actual encounter, much less an encounter with Quetzal? (Just curious exactly how long and how sturdy your bridge of inference is...)

How often exactly does Mucor wake the gods/goddesses? She was obviously present fairly close to the Kypris theophany (but Kypris was probably awake already, viz. Silk's close call in Hyacinth's boudoir). When did she awaken a god(dess)? (Not arguing, just curious).

-vizcacha
