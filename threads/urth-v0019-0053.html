1998-10-06 Kieran Mullen #

Asks whether the mailing list should compile a FAQ answering key plot questions about Severian's mother, father and Dorcas, and asks whether the wounded soldier in The Citadel of the Autarch is really Jonas returned. Doubts Severian's reasoning for identifying the soldier as Jonas, finding it unpersuasive.
questions · puzzle-solving · theories: Wounded soldier in CotA is Jonas returned

Is there a FAQ? I think we need one for some important plot points such as "Who is Severian's mother?" and "Who is Dorcas?" and "Who is Severian's Father?"

A completely unrelated point: Do you believe the wounded soldier in CotA is really Jonas returned? Severian thinks so, but that doesn't mean that he's right. I just never found Sev's reasoning very persuasive.

Kieran Mullen

1998-10-07 Peter Westlake #

Supports prion's theory that Severian recreates people from memory, as an aquastor is made, and suggests he rewrites the wounded soldier in The Citadel of the Autarch to match his memory of Jonas. Responds to the question of whether the soldier is Jonas, arguing the theory would explain why his rewriting took so long.
supports · puzzle-solving · theories: Severian recreates people from memory like an aquastor; The wounded soldier in CotA is Jonas rewritten by Severian

I'm very taken with prion's theory that Severian is recreating people from memory, rather like the creation of an aquastor. See his post (from the account "mary whalen") on 1998-08-01. Under this theory Severian would be "rewriting" the soldier's mind to match his (S's) memory of Jonas, which would account for it taking such a long time. Read the original mail; it's very good.

Spectacled Bear.
