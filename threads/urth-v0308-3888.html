2003-08-14 Phoebe Davis #

Suggests that the differing release dates for The Knight on bookseller sites may not conflict, since publishers' January titles often ship in December, so the gap could reflect shipping versus release terminology. Draws on the poster's own bookstore experience to explain the discrepancy, answering Anthony Ferrara's question about the actual release date without giving a definite date.
answers

I don't have a more reliable date but the websites are not necessarily contradicting each other. In my bookstore days, publisher's January releases shipped in December, February in January etc. It could be a difference in terminology rather than ship date.

Phoebe
