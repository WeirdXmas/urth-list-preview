2009-03-16 Jeff Wilson #

Proposes meeting fellow list members at Aggiecon on the Texas A&M campus in late March to tour campus locations that may have inspired parts of The Book of the New Sun and The Fifth Head of Cerberus. Pitches the idea as a social outing tied to the convention rather than a textual argument, offering no evidence beyond the campus setting itself.

If anyone else is going to be there, would we like to meet up, perhaps for a tour of the portions of campus that may have inspired portions of the Book of the New Sun, _the Fifth Head of Cerberus_, and other Wolfean works?

Best,
