LU =still= in print

1999-02-25 Michael Andre-Driussi #

Reports that Lexicon Urthus, the reference book on Wolfe's work, is still in print with hundreds of copies left and that recent Amazon orders have been shipped. Replies to a list member's implied question about availability, offering reassurance that their order is on its way.
informs

Y.,

Yes, LEXICON URTHUS is still in print, hundreds of copies left, I'm sitting on a box of them right now (okay, I made up that last part--the nearest box is in the next room).

Most recently: Amazon.com placed an order on Monday, I shipped the books on Tuesday. Was your order (and thank you for your order) a part of this shipment? Hard to say--it may have been in the last one. But in any event, it is even now winging its way to you!

(icon of a European swallow, volant, bearing a coconut)
