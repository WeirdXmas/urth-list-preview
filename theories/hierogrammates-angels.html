The Hierogrammates are angels

The Hierogrammates and hierodules are angels (or a science-fictional version of them), whether seraphim, archangels or fallen.

23 threads · 38 posts · for 12 / against 27 · first raised 1997-08-07 by Alice Turner
Main voices: Lee Berman (9), b sharp (8), John Watkins (5), Roy C. Lackey (3), Paul B (3), Adam Stephanides (2)

The debate

The earliest post in this record is Alice Turner's 1997 reply [Alice Turner, 1997], which disputes an earlier, unlisted claim that the Great Sleighers are angels or hierodules. The first substantive Hierogrammate-as-angel exchange is C R Culver's 1998 rebuttal, which argues they are seraphim rather than archangels [C R Culver, 1998]. The theory was then revived in 2005, when Roy C. Lackey argued that the Hierogrammates and Hierodules act for the Increate and stopped Typhon [Roy C. Lackey, 2005]. Adam Stephanides replied that they were made by earlier men, can die, and so are not archangels [Adam Stephanides, 2005], and he later argued that they revere the Hieros [Adam Stephanides, 2005].

The theory was most heavily debated in 2008, when thalassocrat proposed that the Hierogrammates are malicious wasp-like parasites rather than angels of the Increate [thalassocrat, 2008]. Stanislaw Bocian defended a Neoplatonic angelic reading [Stanisław Bocian, 2008]. b sharp argued across many posts that the Hierogrammates are intended as angels and morally superior, citing the name Tzadkiel and Wolfe's interviews [b sharp, 2008] [b sharp, 2008] [b sharp, 2008]. Paul B and John Watkins opposed this, citing human creation, mortality, self-interest and Catholic doctrine on angelic morality [Paul B, 2008] [Paul B, 2008] [John Watkins, 2008]. Watkins also argued for a Shrike-style comparison [John Watkins, 2008]. Several 2008 threads, including What's So Great About Ushas, ended without agreement [Paul B, 2008].

Later posts shifted toward the Inire and Hierodule questions. Son of Witz proposed that the Hierogrammates are the angels, djinn and gods of older traditions [Son of Witz, 2008]. Watkins proposed that they are evil aliens who end up serving the Increate [John Watkins, 2010]. Lee Berman argued that the hierodules are lower-tiered Biblical angels [Lee Berman, 2010] and later that Father Inire is a fallen hierodule or Hethor reborn [Lee Berman, 2011] [Lee Berman, 2011]. Gerry Quinn disputed this, arguing that the hierodules are fallen or arms dealers rather than angels [Gerry Quinn, 2011] [Gerry Quinn, 2011]. Berman also cited a James Jordan interview in which Wolfe affirms the hiero-types are angelic [Lee Berman, 2011].

For

  • Roy C. Lackey argued in 2005 that the Hierogrammates and Hierodules act for the Increate and are archangels [Roy C. Lackey, 2005].
  • b sharp argued that the name Tzadkiel is a Judeo-Christian archangel name and that the Hierogrammates are meant as angels [b sharp, 2008].
  • b sharp argued that the Hierogrammates are created directly by the Increate and that Wolfe's interviews suggest an allegorical design [b sharp, 2008].
  • Lee Berman argued that hierodules are lower-tiered angels and cited a Wolfe interview affirming the hiero-types are angelic [Lee Berman, 2010] [Lee Berman, 2011].
  • Son of Witz proposed that the Hierogrammates are the same beings earlier traditions called angels [Son of Witz, 2008].

Against

  • Adam Stephanides argued that the Hierogrammates were made by earlier men and can die, so they are not archangels [Adam Stephanides, 2005].
  • Paul B argued they are human creations, act in self-interest, are mortal and have imperfect foresight, so they are not angels [Paul B, 2008] [Paul B, 2008].
  • John Watkins argued that the Hierogrammates are higher than humans but not literally angels, and rebutted pro-angel points with Catholic doctrine [John Watkins, 2008] [John Watkins, 2008].
  • thalassocrat proposed that they are evolved wasp-like parasites rather than angels of the Increate [thalassocrat, 2008].
  • Gerry Quinn argued that the hierodules are reluctant helpers and arms dealers, not Biblical angels [Gerry Quinn, 2011] [Gerry Quinn, 2011].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the posters never reached agreement, with most of the 2008 and 2011 threads ending in impasse and the angel reading remaining contested.
