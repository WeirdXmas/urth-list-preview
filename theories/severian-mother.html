Severian's mother is Catherine

Severian's mother is Catherine, a Pelerine executed by the torturers, whose story is echoed in the guild's Feast of Holy Katharine.

38 threads · 45 posts · for 33 / against 10 · first raised 1997-02-07 by Michael Andre-Driussi
Main voices: Lee Berman (10), b sharp (6), Jeff Wilson (4), Roy C. Lackey (3), Dave Tallman (3), David Stockhoff (3)

The debate

The theory was first raised on 1997-02-07 by Michael Andre-Driussi, who proposed a family tree with Ouen as father and Catherine as mother [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1997]. Geoff Alex Cohen followed in June 1997, grounding the mother's identity in Ouen's naming of Katharine and proposing Merryn as a sister [Geoff Alex Cohen, 1997]. Terence Nickolette questioned the reasoning in January 1998, citing the face-as-pool description and the long-running Feast of Holy Katherine [Terence Nickolette, 1998], and Andre-Driussi later summarized Clute's view that Catherine is also the Old Autarch [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998].

In 1999 Roy C. Lackey and Jonathan Laidlow took up the idea in new forms, Lackey making Catherine the child in the basket and Laidlow asking for textual support and raising incest worries [Roy C. Lackey, 1999][Jonathan Laidlow, 1999]. Robert Borski's 2002 argument that the arrested contessa Carina is Catherine rested on resemblance and typological foreshadowing [Robert Borski, 2002]. Activity surged in October 2006, when b sharp, Lackey, Chris Mulder, Daniel D Jones and Nathan Spears argued over the nursing memory and the phrase 'borne by Catherine' [Nathan Spears, 2006][Daniel D Jones, 2006][Chris Mulder, 2006]. The Chapter XLIV citation by Rex Lycanthrosaurus was cited for the identification [Rex Lycanthrosaurus, 2006]. Later posters including Dave Tallman, Lee Berman, Jeff Wilson and David Stockhoff proposed variants such as time-warping powers, a khaibit, a clone, or the Bridge of Air woman [Dave Tallman, 2008][Jeff Wilson, 2010][David Stockhoff, 2013][Dave Tallman, 2008].

Supporters pointed to the locket and innkeeper scene [Ryan Dunn, 2010], Ouen's testimony about the stay of execution for pregnancy [Jeff Wilson, 2010], and the Chapter XLIV naming [Rex Lycanthrosaurus, 2006]. Opponents argued that a mother handed to the torturers cannot be the maid playing Katherine [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2006], that trivial matches like hair colour are weak evidence [Lee Berman, 2010], and that cloning or time travel would be needed to link the eidolon to Severian's mother [David Stockhoff, 2013]. The threads ended without agreement, and the evidence standard remained the main point of dispute.

For

  • Andre-Driussi proposed the Ouen-Catherine family tree citing Shadow and Claw passages [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1997].
  • Ryan Dunn built the chain Dorcas-Ouen-Catherine from the locket, the innkeeper's remarks and Severian's questioning of Ouen [Ryan Dunn, 2010].
  • Rex Lycanthrosaurus cited Chapter XLIV of URTH naming Catherine as Severian's mother [Rex Lycanthrosaurus, 2006].
  • Nathan Spears read 'borne by Catherine' as naming the mother rather than a burden [Nathan Spears, 2006].
  • Daniel D Jones and Chris Mulder argued that the suckling memory must predate the taking, so the nursing woman is Catherine [Daniel D Jones, 2006][Chris Mulder, 2006].

Against

  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argued that a mother handed over to the torturers for execution cannot be the maid playing Katherine [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2006].
  • Nickolette argued that the face-as-pool description and the feast observed for over 300 years conflict with the identification [Terence Nickolette, 1998].
  • Lee Berman argued the connection rests on trivial matches like hair colour and cannot be settled by logic alone [Lee Berman, 2010].
  • David Stockhoff argued that the Aquastor-Katherine cannot be Severian's mother without cloning or time travel [David Stockhoff, 2013].
  • Laidlow asked where the text supports the claim and whether it would make Severian the product of incest [Jonathan Laidlow, 1999].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the list ended without consensus, with posters divided on the textual evidence and on whether the maid, a khaibit, an eidolon or a clone is meant.
