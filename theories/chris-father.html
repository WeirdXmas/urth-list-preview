Who Chris's father is

Chris's father is Burt (engineered from the future) or Lesage.

9 threads · 15 posts · for 16 / against 3 · first raised 2007-12-02 by paul witcover
Main voices: Dave Tallman (9), paul witcover (3), thalassocrat (3), Dan (3), Fernando Q. Gouvea (1), Mark Millman (1)

The debate

The theory first appears on the list on 2007-12-02, when Paul Witcover [paul witcover, 2007] proposed that the younger Chris worked near his father in Cuba, with Ignacio as a mentor, and reported Wolfe's denial that Lesage is Chris. The same day Fernando Q. Gouvea [Fernando Q. Gouvea, 2007] mocked such speculation as seeing hidden connections everywhere. thalassocrat [thalassocrat, 2007] then argued for Burt as father, and later [thalassocrat, 2007] floated Lesage following Chris back in time. Dan [Dan, 2008] and later Dan [Dan, 2008] argued Lesage is Chris's son returning through time, citing Lesage's uncanny timing and the visitor who receives the manuscript.

In late 2008 the debate shifted toward Burt. Dave Tallman [Dave Tallman, 2008] retracted an earlier view and proposed that Burt and Lesage are time travelers, citing Burt's anachronistic slur and height, and a possible engineered son named Chris. Mark Millman [Mark Millman, 2008] disputed this, citing the OED's pre-1725 citation of 'dago' and the Golden Age of Piracy dates. Tallman then proposed that Burt masterminds the plot to create his engineered son [Dave Tallman, 2008], and later argued for a multiple-rewrite time loop with Bram Burt as time traveler and Chris as his son [Dave Tallman, 2008].

By 2012 Tallman [Dave Tallman, 2012] argued that Chris cannot be his own father because that would be a bootstrap paradox, favoring Bram Burt as the biological father and offering Lesage as a second candidate. The idea recurred in the threads 'PF - Lesage & Ignacio' and 'Urth Digest, Vol 43, Issue 2', where Ashley Crill and thalassocrat argued Ignacio struck a deal with Lesage, while the posts record no consensus.

For

  • Witcover proposed the younger Chris worked near his father in Cuba, citing Novia's account of Chris calling Ignacio a second father [paul witcover, 2007].
  • thalassocrat argued Burt fits as father given his attachment to Chris, his apparent death at Rio Hato, and his height [thalassocrat, 2007].
  • Dan argued Lesage is a time traveler and Chris's son, citing Lesage's uncanny timing and the buccaneers' laughter [Dan, 2008].
  • Tallman cited Burt's anachronistic slur, the Jersey joke, his height, and a possible engineered son named Chris as textual hints [Dave Tallman, 2008].
  • Tallman argued that a bootstrap paradox rules out Chris as his own father, pointing toward Bram Burt [Dave Tallman, 2012].

Against

  • Gouvea mocked the idea that Lesage is Chris's father as an example of over-reading Wolfe, warning against trying to be smarter than Wolfe [Fernando Q. Gouvea, 2007].
  • Witcover reported that Wolfe denied at a convention that Lesage is Chris [paul witcover, 2007].
  • Millman argued Tallman's time-travel hypothesis was needlessly complicated and rested on factual errors such as the 'dago' citation and the piracy dates [Mark Millman, 2008].
  • Dan's suggestion that a list member ask Wolfe whether Lesage is Chris's son went unanswered on the list [Dan, 2008].
  • thalassocrat conceded the causality problem in the Lesage-as-father scenario [thalassocrat, 2007].

Where it stands: Still disputed and unresolved: the record shows many competing candidates (Burt, Lesage, Lesage from the future, Chris himself) with no consensus reached across the threads.
