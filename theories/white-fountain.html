The White Fountain is a white hole

The New Sun is a white hole (the White Fountain) set in the old sun's place, fed from another universe, and Severian's power comes from its light reaching him across time.

97 threads · 153 posts · for 82 / against 71 · first raised 1997-05-16 by Michael Andre-Driussi
Main voices: Jeff Wilson (29), Gerry Quinn (16), Lee Berman (12), Michael Andre-Driussi (10), Roy C. Lackey (7), Michael Straight (6)

The debate

The white-hole reading of the White Fountain first appears on the list in February 1998, when C R Culver [C R Culver, 1998] proposed a black hole that balances mass flowing in from the White Fountain, and Kieran Cleary [Kieran Cleary, 1998] proposed the Fountain and the black hole as two mouths of one wormhole. In May 1999 Michael Andre-Driussi [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1999] argued that a small white hole could still be visible from Urth, and Jim Henley [Jim Henley, 1999] explicitly called the White Fountain a white hole that adds matter. Michael Straight [Michael Straight, 1999] countered that such a fountain only delays the sun's death, since all stars die.

The idea went quiet for years and returned in 2007 through 2013. Jeff Wilson argued that the White Fountain could be a small white hole with unusual gravity [Jeff Wilson, 2007], then that it could be fed from Yesod and deliver matter that causes the Flood [Jeff Wilson, 2011], and later that it is sub-stellar with negative mass [Jeff Wilson, 2013]. Gerry Quinn [Gerry Quinn, 2010] proposed in 2010 that the Fountain is a white hole paired with the black hole in the Sun, drawing on the maximally extended Schwarzschild solution. Other posters in the same years framed the Fountain as a drain or a counterpart rather than as a white hole in its own right, as with David Stockhoff [David Stockhoff, 2010] and John Watkins [John Watkins, 2010].

Opponents mostly disputed the Fountain's location, timing and effects rather than the white-hole label itself. Roy C. Lackey argued that Malrubius places the Fountain in the sun's heart while the later book has it created far off [Roy C. Lackey, 2004], then later proposed it was created inside the old sun [Roy C. Lackey, 2008]. Adam Stephanides [Adam Stephanides, 2002] objected that a white-hole conduit could not produce temporal leaps. Paul B [Paul B, 2007] argued the Flood is a miracle unconnected to the Fountain. David Stockhoff [David Stockhoff, 2013] summarised a loose consensus that the Fountain restored the sun by adding matter, but the wider threads ended without agreement.

For

  • Jim Henley proposed that the White Fountain is a white hole that adds more matter than the black hole removes [Jim Henley, 1999].
  • Michael Andre-Driussi argued that a white hole with the mass of a black hole would be tiny and could lie within about 40 light years, so it could be visible from Urth [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1999].
  • Gerry Quinn proposed that the Fountain is a white hole paired with the black hole in the Sun, citing the maximally extended Schwarzschild solution [Gerry Quinn, 2010].
  • Jeff Wilson argued that the Fountain could be a small white hole fed from Yesod, delivering matter that drives the Flood [Jeff Wilson, 2011].
  • David Stockhoff summarised a consensus that the White Fountain restored the sun by adding matter [David Stockhoff, 2013].

Against

  • Michael Straight argued the White Fountain cannot be a permanent answer to entropy, since all stars eventually die [Michael Straight, 1999].
  • Roy C. Lackey argued that the Fountain should have been visible to the naked eye across the millennia, so the setting's physics clashes with the fiction [Roy C. Lackey, 1999].
  • Roy C. Lackey argued that Malrubius places the Fountain in the sun's heart, while the later book has it created far off and travelling for thousands of years [Roy C. Lackey, 2004].
  • Adam Stephanides objected that a white-hole conduit from Yesod could not produce temporal leaps, since the energy would incinerate rather than time-shift the ship [Adam Stephanides, 2002].
  • Paul B argued that the Flood is a miracle not tied to the white fountain, so a white hole need not explain it [Paul B, 2007].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the white-hole reading was raised repeatedly from 1998 to 2013 and appears in several posters' views, but the list never settled on it and the thread summaries end without consensus.
