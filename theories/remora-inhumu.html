Patera Remora is an inhumu

Patera Remora is an inhumu.

5 threads · 5 posts · for 1 / against 2 · first raised 1999-12-09 by Joshua A. Solomon
Main voices: Lee Berman (3), Joshua A. Solomon (1), Andrew Mason (1)

The debate

The earliest post on the list is Joshua A. Solomon's 1999 message [Joshua A. Solomon, 1999], which does not claim that Remora is an inhumu. It raises Remora in connection with the inhumu and the lander's destination, and its card words the theory as Remora sending Horn to poison inhumu with kindness. The identity question first appears directly in 2012, when Lee Berman asked whether Patera Remora, whose name suggests a hitchhiker, is inhumi, arguing that inhumi are stowaway parasites [Lee Berman, 2012].

In 2013 Berman proposed the theory outright, reasoning from the name, since remoras hitch rides like stowaway inhumi, and citing the fish theme across the Sun books and the augur's clothing as a convenient disguise [Lee Berman, 2013]. Andrew Mason disputed it the same week, arguing that Quetzal is probably the only inhumi in Viron and that Remora meeting his mother at Mainframe would complicate the claim, and citing Mucor's remark that Remora speaks to a man who isn't there as evidence that Remora is treated as a person [Andrew Mason, 2013]. Berman then shifted to a narrower position: Remora is unlikely to be a straightforward Inhumu like Quetzal, and Neighbors who boarded the Whorl may have arrived already infected from Green [Lee Berman, 2013].

The longer "Short Sun notes: Remora" thread ranged over Remora's Prochain Ami, the origins of the Neighbours and Inhumi, and wider speculation, and it ended without consensus. The record contains no post in which anyone defends the inhumu identity after 2013.

For

  • Remora's name suggests a hitchhiker, like the stowaway inhumi, which Berman took as the basis for the identification [Lee Berman, 2013]
  • Inhumi are parasites who travel space only as stowaways, and Remora's hitchhiker name fits that pattern [Lee Berman, 2012]
  • The fish theme across the Sun books and the augur's clothing as a convenient disguise support the reading [Lee Berman, 2013]

Against

  • Quetzal is probably the only inhumi in Viron, and Remora meeting his mother at Mainframe would complicate the claim [Andrew Mason, 2013]
  • Mucor's remark that Remora talks to the man who isn't there is evidence that Remora is treated as a person [Andrew Mason, 2013]
  • Remora is unlikely to be a straightforward Inhumu like Quetzal, and Windcloud's remark about boarding the Whorl suggests Neighbors came aboard already infected from Green [Lee Berman, 2013]
  • Horn's sighting of multi-limbed Neighbors and the idea that Neighbors rank above humans while Inhumi rank below weigh against an inhumu identity [Lee Berman, 2013]

Where it stands: Still unresolved and largely unsupported: the theory was proposed in 2013 and then disputed by Andrew Mason and by its own proposer Lee Berman, who moved to a narrower reading, and no post on the list settles it.
