The Claw's power comes from Severian

The Claw has no power of its own (it is a thorn, or a gem); the miracles come from Severian, from the future New Sun working through him, or from the Increate.

34 threads · 56 posts · for 27 / against 25 · first raised 1998-02-01 by Michael Straight
Main voices: Lee Berman (9), Jeff Wilson (7), David Stockhoff (4), John Watkins (4), Michael Straight (3), Michael Andre-Driussi (2)

The debate

The theory was first raised on 1998-02-01, when Michael Straight proposed that the Claw's power came from the Increate through thorns, channeled by Severian from the New Sun [Michael Straight, 1998]. Later in 1998 Michael Andre-Driussi argued that Severian's own power was the source [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998], while C R Culver argued for the Pancreator's will [C R Culver, 1998]. Andre-Driussi then proposed that Severian drew life essence from empathetic pledges brokered by the Claw [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998], and Straight backed the idea that Severian could draw on the future white fountain [Michael Straight, 1998]. Mary Whalen later argued that the fountain's light must reach Severian before he regains power [mary whalen, 1998].

From 2002 to 2008 the debate narrowed to whether the Claw was a focus rather than a source. Marc Aramini argued that Severian reverses time and that the Claw is only a focus [Marc Aramini, 2002], and later used Triskele's revival as proof that the power lies in Severian [Marc Aramini, 2007]. Roy C. Lackey disputed time-reversal healing using Herena, Jonas and Jolenta [Roy C. Lackey, 2002] [Roy C. Lackey, 2002]. Pedro Pereira argued the Claw is merely a symbol [Pedro Pereira, 2008], and Pereira then argued that Triskele's revival may show the Claw acting unconsciously [Pedro Pereira, 2008]. Matthew Malthouse replied that the Claw's significance is symbolic and rests on Severian's belief [Matthew Malthouse, 2008].

From 2009 to 2013 John Watkins and David Stockhoff argued for time-reversal, which Jerry Friedman disputed on physics grounds [John Watkins, 2009] [David Stockhoff, 2009] [Jerry Friedman, 2009]. Ryan Dunn, Jeff Wilson and Stockhoff argued Severian's powers were his own or came from the Increate [Ryan Dunn, 2010] [Jeff Wilson, 2010] [David Stockhoff, 2010], while Watkins argued they flowed from the White Fountain's light [John Watkins, 2010]. Wilson, Jane Delawney and Gerry Quinn disputed the fountain-timing reading using the Declan healing and the sickroom sequence [Jeff Wilson, 2012] [Jeff Wilson, 2012] [Jane Delawney, 2011] [Gerry Quinn, 2012]. Gerry Quinn argued for future super-technology, and Lee Berman argued for divine power instead [Gerry Quinn, 2012] [Lee Berman, 2012]. Jeff Wilson ended the period by arguing the Claw and the White Fountain are linked channels for Severian's powers [Jeff Wilson, 2013].

For

  • Severian's power is independent of the Claw, shown by the Triskele revival before he held it [Ryan Dunn, 2010] [David Stockhoff, 2010].
  • Triskele was listed as dead before Severian touched him, so the power to revive seems to come from Severian [Marc Aramini, 2007] [thalassocrat, 2007].
  • The Claw is only a focus or channel, and the Increate or Severian is the real source of miracles [Michael Straight, 1998] [Marc Aramini, 2002] [Jeff Wilson, 2010].
  • Joseph's dream allusion suggests Severian's healing gifts are granted by the Increate [Jeff Wilson, 2010] [Jeff Wilson, 2010].
  • The Hierodules call the Claw a powerless gem, which suggests the power is not in the object [Nathan C. Tresch, 2011].

Against

  • Lackey argued the Claw cannot simply reverse time, since Herena's withered arm was a birth defect [Roy C. Lackey, 2002].
  • Friedman argued that faster-than-light travel makes the time-reversal reading speculative [Jerry Friedman, 2009].
  • Wilson argued that healing draws on power from Urth's daylight, not from Severian alone [Jeff Wilson, 2012] [Jeff Wilson, 2012].
  • Delawney argued that Severian's early miracles in the primitive village show healing that does not depend on fountain light reaching Urth [Jane Delawney, 2011].
  • Malthouse and Pereira argued that the Claw is a symbol with no literal power, so the source would lie elsewhere [Matthew Malthouse, 2008] [Pedro Pereira, 2008].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the list never reached consensus, with posters split among Severian's own power, the Increate, the White Fountain, future technology and the Claw as a symbol.
