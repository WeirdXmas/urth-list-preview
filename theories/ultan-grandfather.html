Ultan is Severian's grandfather

Master Ultan, the blind librarian, is Severian's maternal grandfather and has guided his life.

4 threads · 5 posts · for 5 / against 0 · first raised 2008-06-26 by Dave Tallman
Main voices: Dave Tallman (1), Marc Aramini (1), Son of Witz (1), Ryan Dunn (1), Lee Berman (1)

The debate

Dave Tallman first raised the theory on 2008-06-26, proposing that Ultan could be Severian's maternal grandfather and that the grave-robbery corpse was staged to draw Vodalus to the graveyard [Dave Tallman, 2008]. The same day Marc Aramini offered a looser hunch, speculating about hidden meaning in Ultan's talk of a preserving fluid and doubting that Ultan belongs in Severian's family tree [Marc Aramini, 2008].

The theory came back in 2010 and 2011 with new framings. Son of Witz argued that Ultan has been steering Severian all along, which he tied to a leaning book on a shelf [Son of Witz, 2010]. In October 2011 Ryan Dunn proposed Ultan as maternal grandfather and the boatman as paternal grandfather, building on Lee Berman's reading of the dead woman as a version of Catherine, and asked whether the boatman's identity matters [Ryan Dunn, 2011]. Lee Berman then supported a grandfather link on both sides by treating Ultan as a version of Father Inire and comparing the boatman's interest in young Dorcas with Maxellindis's uncle [Lee Berman, 2011].

The theory was mainly argued within broader threads rather than in a long dedicated debate. The 'Father Inire as Dionysus' thread went deep into Inire's identity without settling whether Ultan is linked to him, and 'The Outsider' thread later turned to Ultan's Book of Gold and Severian's steering, where Jeff Wilson disputed that Ultan could know such things. Those thread summaries do not attach post ids, so the specific arguments there are not cited here.

For

  • Dave Tallman proposed Ultan as Severian's maternal grandfather, arguing the corpse coincidence can be explained without time travel [Dave Tallman, 2008]
  • Son of Witz argued Ultan's apparent guidance and the significance of the book in Severian's life point to a deliberate hidden role [Son of Witz, 2010]
  • Ryan Dunn proposed the maternal and paternal grandfather pair, following from Lee Berman's reading of the dead woman [Ryan Dunn, 2011]
  • Lee Berman argued that if Ultan is a version of Father Inire he would be grandfather on both sides, linking Ultan's remarks to Inire's pagan and gnostic associations [Lee Berman, 2011]

Against

  • Marc Aramini doubted that Ultan belongs in Severian's family tree despite the hints in the fluid conversation [Marc Aramini, 2008]

Where it stands: Raised in 2008 and revived in 2010 to 2011 with only five posts on the list, and it was never resolved: it stayed a minority speculation that was discussed inside wider threads without any settled outcome.
