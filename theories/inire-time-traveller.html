Father Inire travels in time

Father Inire moves through time (with his mirrors or the corridors of time), like Severian, which explains his long life.

35 threads · 58 posts · for 30 / against 22 · first raised 1998-03-24 by Alice Turner
Main voices: Lee Berman (7), Ryan Dunn (7), David Stockhoff (6), Jeff Wilson (4), John Watkins (4), James Wynn (3)

The debate

The theory that Father Inire travels through time first appears on the list in a 1998 post by Alice Turner, who argued that Inire's mirrors are gates to other worlds rather than time machines [Alice Turner, 1998]. The explicit time-travel claim then surfaces mainly in 2005-2006, when Maru Dubshinki argued that Inire's mirrors could carry Jonas through time [Maru Dubshinki, 2005], and in 2006 when b sharp tied the mirrors to Yesod and time travel [b sharp, 2006]. Jeff Wilson disputed the theory in 2008, arguing Inire is an actor hiding nonhuman proportions and that time travel is not needed to explain his service [Jeff Wilson, 2008].

The main burst of discussion came in 2010, when Ryan Dunn argued that Inire is a time traveler disguised among Severian's helpers [Ryan Dunn, 2010] and later that Severian and Inire walk the corridors of time and seed a time loop [Ryan Dunn, 2010][Ryan Dunn, 2010]. Jeff Wilson questioned it, noting that four characters travel in time without artifice while Inire is never shown passing through the devices [Jeff Wilson, 2010]. John Watkins disputed the loop reading, calling it a literal misreading [John Watkins, 2010], and Roy C. Lackey argued against the theory's account of Inire's whereabouts [Roy C. Lackey, 2010]. David Stockhoff supported the idea that Inire cannot travel if he remains human [David Stockhoff, 2010]. Later in 2011, Ryan Dunn proposed Inire taught Severian the mirrors [Ryan Dunn, 2011], and David Stockhoff proposed time dilation as an explanation of Inire's longevity [David Stockhoff, 2011].

The thread stayed divided. Lee Berman and Ryan Dunn pressed hidden-identity and time-travel readings, while Stockhoff, Watkins, Marques, Lackey and Wilson pressed for textual evidence and questioned the coincidences. The discussion ended without consensus, with posters tending to treat the theory as a speculative reading rather than an established one.

For

  • Inire's mirrors were argued to carry Jonas through time, given his time-dilated arrival from the deep past [Maru Dubshinki, 2005]
  • Inire's mirrors were argued to access Yesod and time travel from Urth's vertical time structure [b sharp, 2006]
  • Ryan Dunn argued Inire is a time-traveler disguised among helpers, citing Rudesind's misdirection and Severian's known time travel [Ryan Dunn, 2010]
  • Ryan Dunn argued Severian and Inire seed a time loop, and that Inire, as a time-traveling twin, may have allowed Severian to become the Conciliator [Ryan Dunn, 2010][Ryan Dunn, 2011]
  • David Stockhoff argued that frequent mirror travel could explain Inire's longevity through time dilation [David Stockhoff, 2011]

Against

  • Jeff Wilson argued Inire is not a shapeshifter but an actor hiding nonhuman proportions, and questioned whether one lifespan could fit a thousand years of service [Jeff Wilson, 2008]
  • Jeff Wilson noted that four characters travel in time without artifice while Inire is never shown using the devices [Jeff Wilson, 2010]
  • John Watkins argued that Ryan Dunn's reading of Rudesind's advice as a time loop turns a simple request into an infinite loop [John Watkins, 2010]
  • Roy C. Lackey argued that Inire's known whereabouts as painter, vizier and lake-prober do not fit the supposed deeds of a time traveler [Roy C. Lackey, 2010]
  • David Stockhoff argued that if Inire remains human on Urth he cannot time-travel or space-travel [David Stockhoff, 2010]

Where it stands: Still disputed and unresolved: the theory was raised in 1998 and revived in 2005-2011, but posters including Watkins, Wilson, Lackey and Stockhoff pressed for textual proof and the threads ended without consensus.
