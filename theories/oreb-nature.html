Oreb is more than a bird

Oreb has a human-like soul, travels astrally or in time, or plays the Holy Spirit's part.

22 threads · 40 posts · for 16 / against 21 · first raised 2000-08-09 by Kevin J. Maroney
Main voices: James Wynn (13), Gerry Quinn (6), Marc Aramini (4), António Pedro Marques (4), Andrew Mason (4), Dave Tallman (3)

The debate

The earliest post on the list is from Kevin J. Maroney in August 2000, who argued that Oreb, a talking animal, projects astrally alongside Incanto and Jahlee, and that talking animals may have human-like souls [Kevin J. Maroney, 2000]. Robert Borski then supported a possession reading, that Scylla intermittently rides Oreb [Robert Borski, 2000], and James Jordan proposed that Oreb is Kypris and the Holy Spirit [James Jordan, 2000]. John Clute and Alex David Groce backed a Holy Spirit role for Oreb, with Groce also disputing other mappings of the books [John Clute, 2000] [Alex David Groce, 2000]. Jordan returned to the idea in November 2000 for the cover of Return to the Whorl [James Jordan, 2000]. Phoebe Davis raised a looser Arthurian link through the chough in 2002 [Phoebe Davis, 2002].

From 2008 the debate shifted to astral and time travel. Marc Aramini and James Wynn read Oreb's human-looking form as Scylla's embuing or possession and as a sign of astral travel [Marc Aramini, 2008] [James Wynn, 2008], while Dave Tallman proposed that Silk, projecting astrally, might have been mistaken for Pike [Dave Tallman, 2008]. In late 2010 and 2011 James Wynn argued that the Rajan and Oreb time-travel, citing the grandmother's tale and the second Oreb in Silk's room [James Wynn, 2010] [James Wynn, 2011] [James Wynn, 2011]. António Pedro Marques, Gerry Quinn and Andrew Mason disputed this, offering ghost, night-chough, or out-of-body explanations and arguing that no single item proves time travel [António Pedro Marques, 2010] [Gerry Quinn, 2010] [António Pedro Marques, 2011] [Andrew Mason, 2011]. Roy C. Lackey and Gerry Quinn also argued that Mucor possessed Oreb and linked the apparition to the Rajan [Gerry Quinn, 2011] [Gerry Quinn, 2011].

Later posts remained split. Marc Aramini proposed in 2011 that Pike and Oreb are Silk's older self time-travelling and in 2013 that Oreb possessed Pike [Marc Aramini, 2011] [Marc Aramini, 2013], while Andrew Mason argued in 2013 that Scylla possessed Oreb after the Rajan's sacrifice [Andrew Mason, 2013]. The threads show these positions multiplying without convergence, with posters conceding that none of the explanations were very good [Gerry Quinn, 2011].

For

  • Oreb's talking-animal status and astral projection suggest a human-like soul, as Maroney argued [Kevin J. Maroney, 2000].
  • Borski cited a dream report and an observed arm-like motion as signs that Scylla rides Oreb [Robert Borski, 2000].
  • Jordan and Groce read Oreb in a Holy Spirit role, linking Silk's career and the Trinity-like pattern of the covers [James Jordan, 2000] [James Jordan, 2000].
  • Wynn argued that the second Oreb and the grandmother's tale show the Rajan time-travelling [James Wynn, 2011] [James Wynn, 2011].
  • Aramini proposed that Pike and Oreb are Silk's older self astral-traveling back in time [Marc Aramini, 2011].

Against

  • Marques argued that Oreb's appearance cannot be explained as astral or dream travel by an older Oreb, and that no convincing evidence has been given for time displacement [António Pedro Marques, 2010].
  • Quinn argued that Silk never saw two birds at once, so a sleeping bird explains the sighting without time travel [Gerry Quinn, 2010].
  • Mason argued that out-of-body travel, not time travel, can explain a second Oreb, and that the figure leaving the window is not clearly Oreb [Andrew Mason, 2011] [Andrew Mason, 2011].
  • Wynn's own clone and time-travel readings were challenged by Quinn and Marques as speculative and not compelling [Gerry Quinn, 2011] [António Pedro Marques, 2011].
  • Tallman conceded that Silk could have been mistaken for Pike, weakening the real-ghost and astral readings [Dave Tallman, 2008].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the theory was never settled, with the Holy Spirit reading raised in 2000 and the time-travel and possession readings argued at length in 2008 to 2013 without consensus.
