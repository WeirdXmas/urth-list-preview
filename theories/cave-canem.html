Cave Canem is the Saltimbanques' theatre

The Cave Canem and the Saltimbanques' theatre are the same place.

19 threads · 13 posts · for 9 / against 3 · first raised 1997-10-23 by Tony Ellis
Main voices: James Wynn (3), James Jordan (2), Jim Raylor (2), Tony Ellis (1), Dan'l Danehy-Oakes (1), Joe Cilluffo (1)

The debate

The theory was first raised on the list by Tony Ellis in October 1997 [Tony Ellis, 1997], who read 666 Saltimbanque Street as a pun on the Beast and the Maison du Chien rather than as Christian allegory, and by James Jordan in late 1997, who argued that saltimbanque means counterfeit and that Wolfe likely meant Satan as charlatan [James Jordan, 1997][James Jordan, 1997]. Early discussion focused on hell, Antichrist and false-church imagery and on the 666 number, with Dan'l Danehy-Oakes linking 666 to man making himself a god [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2002]. The question of Cave Canem's identity became concrete in 2003, when James Wynn argued that Mr. Million runs the Cave Canem experiment alongside the Whorl material [James Wynn, 2003], and in January 2005 when Jim Raylor found a 'Gene Ve' hint in the Geneva address Rue des Grottes [Jim Raylor, 2005].

The 2005 exchange was the main turning point for the claim that Cave Canem is the Saltimbanque theatre. James Wynn first asserted it on 17 and 19 January 2005, treating the Geneva address as probably not coincidental and urging the posting spree to slow down [James Wynn, 2005][James Wynn, 2005]. Jim Raylor then searched for a library due west of the site, testing map positions and proposed matches [Jim Raylor, 2005]. Later posts moved to other readings: Lee Berman in 2011 leaned toward performer or trickster for the word and read a possible clone pun [Lee Berman, 2011], David Stockhoff in 2013 paralleled 666 Saltimbanque with the House Absolute [David Stockhoff, 2013], and Richard Simon in 2014 tied Cave Canem's brothel to Annese ancestry [Richard Simon, 2014].

The record contains no post that directly establishes the identity of Cave Canem with the Saltimbanque theatre beyond Wynn's 2005 assertion and Raylor's map-based search. Most of the discussion concerns symbolic, numerological and clone readings that touch the location only indirectly, and threads such as 'Compliant (sic)' drifted from the Joyce question into location and history without resolving it.

For

  • James Wynn argued in January 2005 that the Geneva address Rue des Grottes, read as Gene 5e, is probably no coincidence and that the Cave Canem location is the Saltimbanque theatre [James Wynn, 2005][James Wynn, 2005].
  • Jim Raylor reasoned that if Cave Canem sits at the Saltimbanque theatre site, a large library should lie due west of it and searched Geneva map positions for one [Jim Raylor, 2005].
  • James Wynn in 2003 placed Mr. Million as the figure running the Cave Canem experiment, linking the setting to the wider Whorl material [James Wynn, 2003].
  • Richard Simon in 2014 cited Cave Canem's brothel and Annese ancestry as deliberate clues within the Victor argument [Richard Simon, 2014].

Against

  • Gerry Quinn insisted in the '5HC' thread that the text is consistent and its clues are naturalistic, disputing speculative readings built on them [James Wynn, 2003].
  • Jim Jordan's 1997 reply cautioned that Christian symbol-spotting may be reflexive and that links to hell and false church are not always intended [James Jordan, 1997].
  • Jerry Friedman dismissed a clown-clone pun, and Lee Berman responded by leaning toward a performer or trickster reading of saltimbanque [Lee Berman, 2011].
  • Jeff Wilson argued in the 'Thea's Identity' thread that a many-Severians hypothesis explains successors' foreknowledge better than clone or breeding readings [David Stockhoff, 2013].

Where it stands: Raised in 1997 and revived in 2003 and 2005, the claim that Cave Canem is the Saltimbanque theatre was asserted by James Wynn but never settled and was left unresolved on the list.
