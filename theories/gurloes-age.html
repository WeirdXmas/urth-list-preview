Master Gurloes's past

Master Gurloes was raised to master unusually young, which says something about the guild's past.

18 threads · 18 posts · for 13 / against 7 · first raised 1998-10-07 by Robert Borski
Main voices: Talarican (6), Roy C. Lackey (3), Robert Borski (1), Jim Russell (1), shannon wilde (1), Andrew Bollen (1)

The debate

The earliest post on the list on Gurloes's early mastership is Talarican's 2000 post [Talarican, 2000], which relayed Roy Lackey's chronology of the guild's masters before Severian's time and asked whether Gurloes was young to be made a master. Talarican's follow-up [Talarican, 2000] worked through estimated ages and dates for Palaemon, Malrubius and Gurloes, using Severian's remarks about Malrubius's illness and quotations from The Shadow of the Torturer about Gurloes' age. A separate 1998 post by Robert Borski [Robert Borski, 1998] is filed under this card, but it concerns Odo, Mennas and Eigil rather than Gurloes's own promotion.

Roy C. Lackey gave the main argument for the theory on 2000-08-20 [Roy C. Lackey, 2000]. He held that Gurloes and Palaemon both became masters in the decade before Severian's birth, relying on Gurloes's claim of twenty years as master and on the Claw chapter VII taunt about puberty to conclude he was master before thirty. Talarican then proposed a rival scenario in which Palaemon was already a master before his exile and Gurloes was older when Severian was masked [Talarican, 2000].

Talarican's 2000 dispute post [Talarican, 2000] is the main counter-argument on the list. It holds that Severian's narration cannot fix Palaemon's date of elevation and that Palaemon taught Gurloes before the exile. The posts record no further posts on this question, so the exchange between Lackey and Talarican in 2000 is the whole of the discussion.

For

  • Roy C. Lackey argued Gurloes became a master before age thirty, citing his claim of twenty years of mastership and the Claw chapter VII taunt [Roy C. Lackey, 2000].
  • Talarican relayed Lackey's chronology of the guild's masters and used guild promotion norms to suggest Gurloes was young to be made master [Talarican, 2000].
  • Talarican's chronology estimated ages and dates for Palaemon, Malrubius and Gurloes from Shadow of the Torturer quotations about Gurloes' age [Talarican, 2000].

Against

  • Talarican argued Severian's narration cannot fix Palaemon's date of elevation and that Palaemon taught Gurloes before his exile [Talarican, 2000].
  • Talarican proposed that Palaemon was already a master before exile, which would make Gurloes older at Severian's masking and so would weaken the young-master reading [Talarican, 2000].

Where it stands: Raised in 2000 and argued only by Lackey and Talarican on the list's posts for this card; the question was left open with no consensus recorded, and the other posts filed under this card concern different Gurloes theories.
