Jolenta is Ginger from Gilligan's Island

Jolenta is modelled on Ginger from Gilligan's Island.

6 threads · 4 posts · for 3 / against 0 · first raised 1999-02-20 in the thread "Gilligan's Island"
Main voices: Lee Berman (2), b sharp (1), David Duffy (1)

The debate

The idea was first raised by b sharp on 2006-05-17, who jokingly proposed Jolenta as a Ginger figure while arguing about the giant child and Dorcas [b sharp, 2006]. The next day David Duffy pointed readers to an earlier archive discussion of Gilligan's Island as a possible model for Jolenta and the castaway women, without adding new evidence [David Duffy, 2006]. The idea was not taken up seriously until December 2010, when Lee Berman proposed that Jolenta is modeled more on Tina Louise's Ginger than on Marilyn Monroe, the rival suggestion that Mantis and Borski had made [Lee Berman, 2010].

Berman developed the argument a week later, linking Jolenta's resemblance to Ginger with Hethor's avoidance of Jonas to Dr. Smith's avoidance of the Robot in Lost in Space, citing Hethor's behaviour in the tunnel, on the path and in the antechamber [Lee Berman, 2010]. That 1960s TV parallel was then discussed inside the "Ships" thread, which drifted from empire logistics to Jonas's family and the ancestry of the Ship's occupants, with Andrew Mason arguing the ancestor need not be the first occupant. The posts record no post that rebuts the Ginger identification directly; the main competing view is the Monroe model cited in Berman's first post.

The "golden Severa" thread, where mournings glory had proposed Jolenta as Severian's sister, touched on the Gilligan's Island idea only in passing and did not settle Jolenta's model.

For

  • b sharp first raised the Ginger comparison as a joke, framing it against the Dorcas argument [b sharp, 2006]
  • David Duffy pointed to an earlier 1999 archive discussion linking Gilligan's Island to Jolenta's castaway characters [David Duffy, 2006]
  • Lee Berman proposed that Jolenta resembles Tina Louise's Ginger more than Marilyn Monroe and noted Wolfe may draw on popular culture [Lee Berman, 2010]
  • Berman argued that Book of the New Sun characters echo 1960s TV, with Hethor's avoidance of Jonas paralleling Dr. Smith and the Robot in Lost in Space [Lee Berman, 2010]

Against

  • Mantis and Borski suggested Marilyn Monroe as Jolenta's model instead, which Berman's post treats as the alternative to beat [Lee Berman, 2010]
  • The posts record no post that rebuts the Ginger identification on textual grounds [Lee Berman, 2010]

Where it stands: Raised as a joke in 2006 and revived by only one poster in 2010, so it remains a minority suggestion that the list never widely accepted or fully tested.
