Jolenta is Severian's sister

Jolenta, the waitress remade by Dr. Talos, is Severian's sister.

27 threads · 43 posts · for 24 / against 19 · first raised 1998-10-30 by Peter Westlake
Main voices: Lee Berman (17), b sharp (4), David Stockhoff (4), Gerry Quinn (4), Jeff Wilson (3), Michael Andre-Driussi (2)

The debate

The sister idea, that Jolenta is Severa, Severian's missing twin, was first raised on the list by mournings glory on 2006-05-15, who proposed Jolenta over Morwenna as the missing sister [mournings glory, 2006]. The same day b sharp rejected Robert Borski's candidates Jolenta and Barbea, arguing that golden hair marks Severian's family [b sharp, 2006].

The idea came back in 2008 when b sharp proposed that Jolenta's real name is Severa, citing her pubic-hair simile and an Oedipal family curse [b sharp, 2008]. Josh Geller asked for textual evidence [Josh Geller, 2008], and Tim O'Donnell called the support circumstantial while doubting Wolfe intended it [O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI), 2008]. Jeff Wilson and David Stockhoff then used undine ancestry to argue that Jolenta is Severian's sister [Jeff Wilson, 2008] [David Stockhoff, 2008].

From 2010 Lee Berman became the theory's main advocate, pressing Jolenta as Severa, Severian's twin, against Merryn and Apheta [Lee Berman, 2010] [Lee Berman, 2010] [Lee Berman, 2011]. Gerry Quinn disputed this in December 2011, arguing that Severian's many lovers cannot all be close kin and that a sexual relation does not show kinship [Gerry Quinn, 2011] [Gerry Quinn, 2011]. In the same thread David Stockhoff argued that the witch premise underlying the Jolenta identification was unproven and that Jolenta may not be a witch [David Stockhoff, 2011] [David Stockhoff, 2011]. Berman still defended Jolenta as Severa in September 2014 [Lee Berman, 2014].

For

  • mournings glory argued Jolenta, not Morwenna, is the missing sister Severa, citing the Contessa-first line and the lightning-face echo [mournings glory, 2006]
  • Lee Berman argued Jolenta is Severa, the twin, citing her age, height, veiled name and hair, and the witches' interest in Severian's powers [Lee Berman, 2010] [Lee Berman, 2010]
  • Larry Miller argued Jolenta fits the sister role better than Dorcas, citing her unnamed status and her stage role alongside Dorcas [Larry Miller, 2011]
  • b sharp argued Jolenta's real name is Severa, using her pubic-hair simile and the Borski hair-colour guess [b sharp, 2008]
  • Jeff Wilson and David Stockhoff argued from undine ancestry, Dorcas's awakening underwater and Severian's survival in water, that Jolenta is Severian's sister [Jeff Wilson, 2008] [David Stockhoff, 2008]

Against

  • b sharp rejected Jolenta and Barbea as Severa, arguing the golden-haired family pattern points elsewhere [b sharp, 2006]
  • Gerry Quinn argued that Severian's many lovers cannot all be close kin, so the family pattern fails, and that sex with Dorcas does not show kinship [Gerry Quinn, 2011] [Gerry Quinn, 2011]
  • David Stockhoff argued that Severa is a placeholder rather than a twin's name, and that the witch premise behind the Jolenta identification is unproven [David Stockhoff, 2011] [David Stockhoff, 2011]
  • Josh Geller asked for textual evidence for the kinship claims and found none offered on the list [Josh Geller, 2008]
  • Tim O'Donnell called the support circumstantial and said he doubted Wolfe intended any kinship [O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI), 2008]

Where it stands: Still disputed: the theory was pressed mainly by Lee Berman and a few others from 2006 to 2014, and the threads ended in impasse without consensus.
