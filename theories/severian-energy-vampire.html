Severian is an energy vampire

Severian draws life from those around him (Dorcas, Jonas, others weaken in his company).

4 threads · 4 posts · for 3 / against 0 · first raised 1998-05-14 by Michael Andre-Driussi
Main voices: Michael Andre-Driussi (3), Dan'l Danehy-Oakes (1)

The debate

The energy-vampire theory was first raised on 1998-05-14 by Michael Andre-Driussi, who argued that Severian's miracle-working comes from empathetic life-essence pledges that the Claw draws from local energy sources, citing Apheta's passage in Urth chapter 20 and casting Severian as an energy vampire [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]. The same day Dan'l Danehy-Oakes asked how such a vampire would deal with the inhumi in the Short Sun books and whether the inhumi were misunderstood [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 1998]. The next day Andre-Driussi narrowed the claim, arguing that Severian has no energy link to the white fountain until after leaving Yesod, and that his victims serve as destiny devices [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]. The theory was then widened in 1999, when Andre-Driussi proposed that black and white holes link Abaddon, Briah and Yesod so that Severian and the great monsters share an energy circuit [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1999].

The discussion across the four threads was largely Andre-Driussi's, with others testing it. In the Claw Power thread, Peter Grimes answered that ordinary human endurance with water, plus the New Sun's power, explains Severian's survival in the hills, and Michael Straight supported a time-twisting source from the future fountain. In the Infrared thread, Mark Millman objected on cooling and albedo grounds, and Andre-Driussi conceded that point while keeping the energy circuit as conjecture from analogy [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1999]. Roy C. Lackey argued that the earthquake is an effect of the energy exchange that revives Severian, and Fr.Inire floated Severian as an artificial chem on hints alone.

The for-side rested on Andre-Driussi's reading of Apheta's remarks and his circuit analogy, while the against-side rested on the ordinary-endurance explanation and on the physical objections raised later. The posts on the list do not show the theory gaining other adherents.

For

  • Andre-Driussi argued that Severian's healing and executions are withdrawals from empathetic life-essence banks drawn on by the Claw [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998].
  • Andre-Driussi argued from Apheta's comment that Severian lacks an energy link to the white fountain until after leaving Yesod, and estimated the fountain's distance from its travel speed [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998].
  • Andre-Driussi proposed a shared energy circuit, linked by black and white holes, joining Severian and the great monsters as energy vampires [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1999].

Against

  • Millman objected on cooling and albedo grounds, and Andre-Driussi conceded that point [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1999].
  • Danehy-Oakes questioned whether an energy-vampire Severian could be the figure the inhumi in the Short Sun books require, and suggested the inhumi may be misread [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 1998].
  • Andre-Driussi's own follow-up treated the circuit as conjecture resting on analogy rather than established evidence [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1999].

Where it stands: Raised by one poster and never widely adopted; the threads ended without resolution, with the ordinary-endurance explanation offered as a counterweight, so the theory remained disputed.
