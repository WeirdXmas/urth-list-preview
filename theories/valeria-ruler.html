Valeria rules as Autarch, not regent

In The Urth of the New Sun, Valeria rules in her own right (or has usurped power) rather than as Severian's regent.

8 threads · 8 posts · for 1 / against 7 · first raised 1998-07-08 by adam louis stephanides
Main voices: Michael Andre-Driussi (4), adam louis stephanides (3), Matthew Malthouse (1)

The debate

The theory was first raised on 8 July 1998 by adam louis stephanides, who challenged the Lexicon Urthus claim that Valeria was Severian's regent, citing Urth passages where Severian calls himself Autarch and Eata calls Valeria Autarch [adam louis stephanides, 1998]. Michael Andre-Driussi answered the same day, defending the regency as legitimate once Severian left Urth and defending the Atrium of Time as a time-travelling structure [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]. Matthew Malthouse then argued that Valeria reigns in her own right, since she lacks her predecessors' memories and Severian's long absence ended any expectation of his return [Matthew Malthouse, 1998].

The debate continued over the following days. Andre-Driussi proposed on 11 July that the Commonwealth could run without its ruler, so Valeria's rule might be a regency, pointing to Severian's mention of reclaiming the Phoenix Throne in chapter 23 [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]. In a second post that day he suggested Inire was left in charge and that a usurper like Valeria would invalidate the pact with Yesod [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]. adam louis stephanides disputed this on 12 July, arguing that Severian had abdicated before leaving, so Valeria is an Autarch or usurper rather than a regent [adam louis stephanides, 1998]. On 13 July he added that Valeria can be a legitimate Autarch because the Commonwealth is sovereign, and questioned whether failing the Test always brings unmanning [adam louis stephanides, 1998].

The last post on the list, on 14 July, is Andre-Driussi's, which argues that the autarch is literally legion, so Valeria cannot be a real autarch and pseudo-autarchs will be barred from the Test [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]. The related thread on who ruled while Appian was away, which ends with competing textual readings, is also part of the record, but it did not settle the question.

For

  • Severian calls himself Autarch and Eata calls Valeria Autarch, so she could not have been regent [adam louis stephanides, 1998]
  • Valeria lacks her predecessors' memories and Severian's long absence ended any expectation of his return, so she reigns in her own right [Matthew Malthouse, 1998]
  • Severian abdicated the Phoenix Throne before leaving Urth, making Valeria an Autarch or usurper rather than a regent [adam louis stephanides, 1998]
  • The Commonwealth is fully sovereign, so Valeria can be a legitimate Autarch [adam louis stephanides, 1998]
  • Valeria cannot be a real autarch because the autarch is literally legion, making her a sham [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]

Against

  • Valeria's regency is legitimate because she became regent when Severian left Urth [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]
  • The Commonwealth could run without its ruler during Severian's year as an Ascian slave, so her rule may be a regency [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]
  • Severian's mention of reclaiming the Phoenix Throne at the end of the book suggests Valeria would step down once he appears [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]
  • Inire was left in charge during Severian's absences, which suggests a regency structure tied to the pact with Yesod [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]

Where it stands: Still disputed when the list's record ends: the arguments in July 1998 ran in both directions, and the posters never reached agreement.
