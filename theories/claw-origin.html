The Claw of the Conciliator's origin

The Claw is the thorn from the bush at the stone town (or another thorn Severian himself placed), sent around in a time loop.

10 threads · 16 posts · for 10 / against 5 · first raised 1998-01-31 by C R Culver
Main voices: David Stockhoff (3), Michael Straight (2), C R Culver (1), Peter Stephenson (1), Jerry Friedman (1), Jeffrey Lefstin (1)

The debate

The theory was first raised on the list by C R Culver in January 1998 [C R Culver, 1998], who argued the Claw was a device made by Tzadkiel, a view that is the earliest record post and runs against the thorn-origin idea. Within days Michael Straight proposed the Claw's power came from the Increate through thorns [Michael Straight, 1998], and Peter Stephenson suggested Severian finds a real thorn in the Citadel that returns to Urth as the Claw [Peter Stephenson, 1998]. Michael Straight answered that any thorn Severian carried back would become the Claw, citing the rose bushes on the beach where he removes his boots [Michael Straight, 1998], and Jerry Friedman conceded the thorn was common but insisted the bushes kept something special.

The discussion revived in 2005 and again in 2010, when Jeffrey Lefstin argued the thorn becomes the Claw only when Severian jams it into his finger [Jeffrey Lefstin, 2005]. Jordon Flato argued the corundum claw is the rosebush thorn making a closed loop [Jordon Flato, 2010], while Son of Witz argued it is a real thorn encased in crystal [Son of Witz, 2010]. John Watkins argued the Claw is only a channel for the White Fountain's light, tracing a loop through Yesod and Briah in which a bloodied thorn becomes the Claw [John Watkins, 2010]. Several threads, such as "Corundum of the Claw" and "Abaia and the Claw", drifted from the origin question to Christ imagery and Autarch motives.

From 2012 the thread on fangs turned away from thorn-origin: David Stockhoff proposed the Claw might be a fang [David Stockhoff, 2012], Gerry Quinn disputed this by saying the Claw is a thorn [Gerry Quinn, 2012], and Daniel Petersen argued it is a thorn echoing a serpent's fang [Daniel Petersen, 2012]. Lee Berman tied the plant origin to gendered food symbolism and claimed the Claw was made when a thorn was soaked in Severian's blood [Lee Berman, 2012], a point Stockhoff only tentatively built on [David Stockhoff, 2012]. Marc Aramini later linked the Red Flower and its thorn to the Claw [Marc Aramini, 2014].

For

  • Michael Straight argued any thorn Severian carried back would become the Claw, reading its specialness as the Increate's use of that thorn [Michael Straight, 1998].
  • Peter Stephenson proposed Severian finds a real thorn in the Citadel that returns to Urth as the Claw, grounded in the Proust and Celtic clues [Peter Stephenson, 1998].
  • Jeffrey Lefstin cited the altered dream words, Idas searching the cabin for the thorn, and Apheta's line that it was saturated with blood [Jeffrey Lefstin, 2005].
  • Jordon Flato argued the corundum claw is the rosebush thorn from the end of The Citadel of the Autarch, making a closed loop [Jordon Flato, 2010].
  • John Watkins traced a loop through Yesod and Briah in which a bloodied thorn becomes the Claw, citing Severian cutting himself on a thorn [John Watkins, 2010].

Against

  • C R Culver argued the Claw is a device made by Tzadkiel, citing the nonchalant responses of Famulimus, Barbatus and Ossipago [C R Culver, 1998].
  • Jerry Friedman argued the Claw remains special beyond an ordinary thorn even though the bushes are common.
  • Son of Witz argued the claw is a real thorn encased in corundum and that Severian is a Christ-like conciliator, against Adam Thornton's ordinary-thorn view [Son of Witz, 2010].
  • Gerry Quinn pointed out the Claw is really a thorn, not a serpent's fang, so the fang link does not hold [Gerry Quinn, 2012].
  • Adam Thornton, as reported in the corundum thread, argued the thorns are ordinary and only Severian makes them holy [Son of Witz, 2010].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the thorn-origin idea was raised in 1998, supported by a few posters over the years, and rejected or left open in later threads, with no consensus reached.
