Where Jonas came from

Jonas, the part-mechanical man, came to Urth from a starship (perhaps with Hethor) long ago; theories link him to the Long Sun's chems and read his name from Jonah or Pinocchio.

52 threads · 71 posts · for 38 / against 37 · first raised 1997-08-15 by Michael Andre-Driussi
Main voices: Michael Andre-Driussi (13), Roy C. Lackey (8), Gerry Quinn (8), Tony Ellis (6), C R Culver (4), Dan Parmenter (4)

The debate

The theory that Jonas came from a starship long ago was first raised on the list by Michael Andre-Driussi, who in 1997 proposed that the antechamber prisoners descend from a starship crew linked to Jonas's breakdown and the name Kimleesoong [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1997]. In 1998 the discussion broadened: C R Culver argued Jonas speaks Korean [C R Culver, 1998], Tony Ellis argued Jonas is a robot [Tony Ellis, 1998], and Andre-Driussi argued Jonas and Hethor are aged sailors from one crew [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]. Dan Parmenter proposed the Pinocchio reading and Andre-Driussi proposed Jonas as a name given to a broken android [Dan Parmenter, 1998][Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998].

From 1999 the debate split into several strands. Roy C. Lackey and Tony Ellis argued over whether Jonas is a hollow powered-armor android like Sidero, with Ellis holding that Jonas is slender and Sidero too bulky [Roy C. Lackey, 1999][Tony Ellis, 1999][Tony Ellis, 1999]. Kieran Mullen proposed that Jonas and Hethor crashed together from Tzad's ship, and Matthew Malthouse disputed that the ship had to be Tzad's [Kieran Mullen, 1999][Matthew Malthouse, 1999]. Jim Russell and Andre-Driussi argued for a Tzadkiel link or a starship crew origin [Jim Russell, 2000][Michael Andre-Driussi, 1999].

In 2002 the Long Sun books prompted a chem reading. Andrew Reeves proposed that Jonas is a chem partly repaired with human parts, Roy Lackey and James Wynn disputed this citing his light weight, and Andre-Driussi argued for a hollow-man robot type [Andrew Reeves, 2002][Roy C. Lackey, 2002][Michael Andre-Driussi, 2002][Matthew Malthouse, 2002]. The 2010 Father Inire threads brought the most sustained exchanges: Gerry Quinn argued the antechamber is part of a crashed ship and that Jonas came from a different ship than Hethor, while Andrew Mason argued Jonas had seen Urth and that his ship may not be Tzadkiel's [Gerry Quinn, 2010][Gerry Quinn, 2010][Andrew Mason, 2010][Andrew Mason, 2010].

For

  • Andre-Driussi's 1997 post proposed that the antechamber prisoners descend from a starship crew, linking Jonas to them [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1997].
  • Jim Russell proposed that the single starship in the books has a non-linear history, so Jonas may have served on Tzadkiel's ship [Jim Russell, 2000].
  • Gerry Quinn argued that the room Jonas describes, with its vaulted ceiling, is a repurposed part of his ship and a crater below Urth [Gerry Quinn, 2010][Gerry Quinn, 2010].
  • David Stockhoff argued that the evidence points to a ship that landed intact at the House Absolute [David Stockhoff, 2010].
  • Gerry Quinn argued Jonas knows the mirror transporter because he was a crewman of the Fortunate Cloud [Gerry Quinn, 2010].

Against

  • C R Culver and Tony Ellis argued Jonas is a robot speaking computer-speak or Korean, not a sailor [Tony Ellis, 1998][C R Culver, 1998].
  • Tony Ellis argued Sidero is too bulky to be the same android type as Jonas, so Jonas cannot be the same kind [Tony Ellis, 1999][Tony Ellis, 1999].
  • Roy C. Lackey and James Wynn argued Jonas is light and so not a chem like those on the Whorl [Roy C. Lackey, 2002][James Wynn, 2002].
  • Jerry Friedman rejected the Pinocchio reading, arguing Jonas has not always wanted to be biological [Jerry Friedman, 2002].
  • Andrew Mason argued that Jonas's ship cannot be Tzadkiel's, because Jonas says his ship crashed after failing to find a port [Andrew Mason, 2010].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the list never reached agreement on where Jonas came from, with readings ranging from a starship crew, Tzadkiel's ship, a chem or hollow android, to a braindead man rebuilt with an android body, and the threads ended in impasse.
