The City of the Inhumi is Nessus

The ruined City of the Inhumi on Green is Nessus, or modelled on it.

3 threads · 6 posts · for 2 / against 3 · first raised 2000-08-22 by Nicholas Gevers
Main voices: António Pedro Marques (3), Roy C. Lackey (2), Nicholas Gevers (1), Marc Aramini (1)

The debate

The theory was first raised on the list by Nicholas Gevers in August 2000, who asked whether Wolfe meant readers to equate the City of the Inhumi with Nessus because both cities are accursed [Nicholas Gevers, 2000]. The idea lay dormant until late 2010, when António Pedro Marques proposed that the City of the Inhumi links The Book of the Short Sun and The Book of the New Sun, and noted that the name Nessus resembles "nexus" [António Pedro Marques, 2010]. In November 2011 the matter became a sustained exchange: Marc Aramini argued that the City of the Inhumi in On Blue's Waters is Nessus from The Book of the Long Sun, and that the narrator is a rebooted Silk [Marc Aramini, 2011]. Marques pressed him to name concrete correspondences and warned that a link would need evidence beyond the stripped rocket-ship tower [António Pedro Marques, 2011][António Pedro Marques, 2011].

The strongest counterargument came from Roy C. Lackey, who reconstructed Horn's repeated returns to the City and argued that the canted tower quoted from In Green's Jungles is a ruined lander where Horn died, far from the City [Roy C. Lackey, 2011]. The thread "vanished people=Hieros" carried the dispute much further, with Aramini and Marques in a long fight joined by Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, David Stockhoff and Lackey, who pressed textual objections, while the positions stayed entrenched [Marc Aramini, 2011][António Pedro Marques, 2011]. The separate thread "planetary problems" also touched the Nessus question, with Lee Berman and Thomas Bitterman proposing ideas about the city of Nessus, while James Wynn disputed the Green-is-Urth premise that the argument relied on.

The posts record no Wolfe remark cited by posters and no decisive new clue; the discussion turned on whether the textual parallels between the two cities are sufficient. Overall the posts show the theory being argued by Aramini and Marques with little resolution.

For

  • Gevers suggested the two cities seem linked because both are accursed [Nicholas Gevers, 2000].
  • Marques proposed that the City of the Inhumi is a hub joining the two series and pointed to the resemblance of Nessus to "nexus" [António Pedro Marques, 2010].
  • Aramini argued that the narrator's memories of Green before the inhumi and comparisons between the two cities support the identification [Marc Aramini, 2011].

Against

  • Marques asked Aramini for specific features of resemblance and said that a link would need Nessus-specific evidence beyond the tower [António Pedro Marques, 2011][António Pedro Marques, 2011].
  • Lackey argued that the canted tower is Horn's ruined lander, far from the City, so it cannot show the City is Nessus [Roy C. Lackey, 2011].
  • Wynn disputed the Green-is-Urth premise on which the claimed equivalence depended in the "planetary problems" thread.

Where it stands: Still disputed: the theory was raised in 2000, argued at length in late 2011 with Aramini in favour and Marques and Lackey against, and the threads ended without agreement.
