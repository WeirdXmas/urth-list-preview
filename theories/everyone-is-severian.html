Everyone on Urth is genetically Severian

Because Severian's line spreads through all later humanity (or every character is a version of him), everyone on Urth carries his genes.

6 threads · 8 posts · for 4 / against 1 · first raised 2005-06-24 by Chris P
Main voices: Chris P (2), Adam Thornton (2), Dan'l Danehy-Oakes (1), Maru Dubshinki (1), David Wells (1), James Wynn (1)

The debate

The theory was first raised on the list by Chris P on 2005-06-24, who proposed that everyone on Urth is genetically Severian, including Typhon, with the Hierogrammates producing Severian-based embryos to avoid inbreeding [Chris P, 2005]. Responses that day were mostly playful: Dan'l Danehy-Oakes joked that it resolves the Problem of the Spanish Barber [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2005], and Maru Dubshinki extended it with a Platonist account of shared minds [Maru Dubshinki, 2005]. Chris later said the joking reply was meant to lighten the mood rather than answer a serious argument [Chris P, 2005].

In 2008 Adam Thornton broadened the claim to say every character in the whole Book of the New Sun is a version of Severian, while saying they did not intend to defend it [Adam Thornton, 2008]. David Wells responded with confusion about the Sev1/2/N divergence and a Feynman analogy rather than endorsement [David Wells, 2008]. In 2010 Thornton again proposed, jokingly, that every character in The Urth of the New Sun is a reincarnation of Severian, building on Son of Witz's point that Severian could be Typhon's son without cloning [Adam Thornton, 2010].

James Wynn disputed the inference, calling it a misreading of what anyone had argued and mocking it with a reductio [James Wynn, 2010]. The posts record no post that supports the everyone-is-Severian claim with textual evidence beyond the time-weaving and resurrection points Chris offered in the original post [Chris P, 2005]. The thread summaries describe the idea as surfacing mostly in joking exchanges and in side debates about clones rather than as a sustained argument.

For

  • Chris P proposed the theory, citing Severian's time-weaving lives such as Apu Punchau, resurrection technology, and clone proliferation as support [Chris P, 2005].
  • Maru Dubshinki argued that a shared common origin would explain how minds could be shared and scanned in the New Sun and Long Sun books [Maru Dubshinki, 2005].
  • Adam Thornton argued that time travel and reincarnation elements make every character in Urth an incarnation of Severian [Adam Thornton, 2010].

Against

  • James Wynn disputed the inference that time travel and reincarnation make Severian the single source of every character, calling it a misreading [James Wynn, 2010].
  • David Wells questioned how the Sev1/2/N divergence works and did not accept the repetition reading in the thread summary [David Wells, 2008].
  • Chris P later framed the original post as a joke meant to lighten the mood, not a serious retort, which weakens its standing as a sustained argument [Chris P, 2005].

Where it stands: Raised by Chris P in 2005 and mostly kept alive as a joke or a side claim, with no poster on the list adopting it as a serious position and the list leaving it unresolved and largely dismissed.
