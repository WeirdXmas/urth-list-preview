Catherine reappears in disguise

Catherine, Severian's mother, survives or recurs in other guises (the maid playing Holy Katharine, the woman on the Path of Air, the woman in the loggia, the Contessa Carina).

54 threads · 82 posts · for 75 / against 21 · first raised 1998-01-06 by Michael Andre-Driussi
Main voices: Lee Berman (31), b sharp (15), Roy C. Lackey (10), Robert Borski (8), Dave Tallman (5), Son of Witz (5)

The debate

The theory was first raised on the list in January 1998 by Michael Andre-Driussi, who proposed that Severian's mother Catherine was an escaped khaibit of the Thea and Thecla family [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998], and in May 1998 he argued the maid who plays Holy K is Catherine [Michael Andre-Driussi, 1998]. The idea of a Carina/Katharine link was pressed by Robert Borski in 1998 and 1999, with Borski proposing that the arrested woman is the original Katherine [Robert Borski, 1998] and that the Contessa is Katharine or Catherine [Robert Borski, 1999]. Roy C. Lackey extended this in April 1999 by equating Catherine and Katharine [Roy C. Lackey, 1999], and Tony Ellis disputed the chain, arguing Severian's statement that he did not know her face rules out the match [Tony Ellis, 1999].

From 2006 the discussion became much busier, with b sharp proposing kaibit and doppelganger readings of Carina and Catherine [b sharp, 2006][b sharp, 2006], and Roy C. Lackey and Dan'l Danehy-Oakes disputing the maid's identity, with Lackey arguing for an eidolon [Roy C. Lackey, 2006] and Danehy-Oakes for an artificial chem [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2006]. Jeff Wilson proposed serial khaibit clones for the Holy Katharine role [Jeff Wilson, 2006]. In 2008 Dave Tallman argued the woman on the Bridge of Air is Catherine, citing the Contessa's three maids named for beheaded saints [Dave Tallman, 2008], and Roy C. Lackey questioned the loggia identification [Roy C. Lackey, 2008].

The 2010 and 2011 posts were dominated by Lee Berman, who repeatedly proposed that the Path of Air woman, the coffin woman and the maid are Catherine, sometimes with a time-tower mechanism [Lee Berman, 2010][Lee Berman, 2011]; Lee Berman's view was met with disputes from Jeff Wilson and Ryan Dunn [Ryan Dunn, 2010][Jeff Wilson, 2011]. David Stockhoff and Larry Miller then argued about whether the Cumaean hid Catherine in the Atrium of Time [Larry Miller, 2011][David Stockhoff, 2011]. The theory was never settled, and the thread-level debates ended in impasse.

For

  • Catherine as a time traveler or escapee from the Atrium of Time is argued by Lee Berman and Robert Borski [Lee Berman, 2011][Lee Berman, 2010][Lee Berman, 2011]
  • The Contessa Carina and the Path of Air woman are argued to be Catherine via matching physical description and maids named for beheaded saints [Dave Tallman, 2008][Lee Berman, 2011][Lee Berman, 2010]
  • Robert Borski's Karina-Katherine name link is cited as evidence that Carina derives from Catherine [Robert Borski, 1999][Robert Borski, 2002]
  • Thalassocrat and Dave Tallman read the loggia woman as Catherine, with Tallman citing close quotation of the Contessa and Meschia dialogue [Dave Tallman, 2008][Dave Tallman, 2008]
  • Lee Berman argues that the coffin woman and the Path of Air woman match in face and in the strangling method of Allowin's necklace [Lee Berman, 2010][Lee Berman, 2011]

Against

  • Tony Ellis argues Severian's statement that he did not know the Contessa's face contradicts the Carina-Katharine identification [Tony Ellis, 1999]
  • Roy C. Lackey argues that the text calls the elevation-feast woman a maid, and a maid is a virgin, so she cannot be Catherine [Roy C. Lackey, 2006]
  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argues that if the mother was handed to the torturers for execution, the Katherine maid cannot be her [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2006]
  • Jeff Wilson argues the Katharine maid cannot be strangled because she is alive or a robot, so the coffin-woman match fails [Jeff Wilson, 2011]
  • Robert Borski argues on timing grounds that Dorcas and Ouen cannot be the parents Lackey proposed [Robert Borski, 1999]

Where it stands: Still disputed and unresolved: the Catherine/Katharine/Carina identification was raised repeatedly from 1998 to 2013 with many supporters and critics, but no consensus emerged and several threads ended in impasse.
