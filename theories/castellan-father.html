The castellan is Severian's father

Severian's father is the castellan of the Citadel (biologically or as the cuckolded husband of Severian's mother).

4 threads · 10 posts · for 5 / against 5 · first raised 2006-08-16 by Roy C. Lackey
Main voices: Roy C. Lackey (4), b sharp (3), Marc Aramini (1), Lee Berman (1), David Stockhoff (1)

The debate

The earliest post on the list that ties the castellan to Severian's parentage is b sharp's 2006 family-tree proposal, which casts the Castellan as an exultant stepfather [b sharp, 2006]. Roy C. Lackey's first post, which came before it, objected to the proposed tree on caste grounds, arguing that Thea and Thecla cannot be children of Catherine and the Castellan [Roy C. Lackey, 2006]. Lackey then argued that Thecla's primary estate was a northern chateau, so the Castellan could not be her father [Roy C. Lackey, 2006].

The idea returned in 2008. b sharp relayed Lackey's view that Severian knows his family ties but hides them, with the lame Castellan as the likely father, citing Zama's pale skin and non-gushing wound and Severian never seeing the Castellan again [b sharp, 2008]. Lackey then proposed that the Citadel was the family's primary residence, which would make the Castellan a cuckolded non-biological father, but in the same post rebutted the idea by noting that Thecla was an exultant and the Castellan was not [Roy C. Lackey, 2008]. Marc Aramini and Lackey also argued about the castellan and Valeria, with Aramini citing time travel to make lineage claims unreliable and Lackey insisting the castellans of Valeria's family ended generations ago [Marc Aramini, 2008] [Roy C. Lackey, 2008].

In 2011 Lee Berman proposed that the Castellan is Catherine's lamed, cuckolded husband, linking Severian's lameness to kingship myth and explaining how the Castellan learned of the affair [Lee Berman, 2011]. David Stockhoff supported the cuckold angle by floating that Severian is Catherine's son, but offered no evidence [David Stockhoff, 2011]. The thread's other summaries show the theory was debated in threads on Sev's silence about Catherine and on lameness, where it was mixed with clone speculation and mythology, and no consensus emerged [Lee Berman, 2011].

For

  • Stepfather reading: b sharp proposed the Castellan as an exultant stepfather to Severian in the first post on the theory [b sharp, 2006].
  • Lame castellan as father: b sharp relayed Lackey's view that the lame Castellan is the likely father and that Severian never saw him again [b sharp, 2008].
  • Cuckold residence argument: Lackey proposed that the Citadel as primary residence would make the Castellan Severian's cuckolded non-biological father [Roy C. Lackey, 2008].
  • Lameness as marker: Berman argued Severian's lameness marks a lamed, castrated husband of Catherine who learned of her infidelity through his lameness [Lee Berman, 2011].
  • Catherine's son: Stockhoff floated that Severian is the son of Catherine, the Castellan's wife, without offering evidence [David Stockhoff, 2011].

Against

  • Caste rules: Lackey cited the caste rules in the appendix, arguing exultant status is hereditary and the Castellan is a commander Appian would not trust with an exultant [Roy C. Lackey, 2006].
  • Thecla's estate: Lackey argued that Thecla's primary estate is a northern chateau, citing her dialogue and Cyriaca's remark, so the Castellan cannot be her father [Roy C. Lackey, 2006].
  • Exultant closed caste: Lackey rebutted the cuckold idea by noting Thecla was an exultant, a closed caste, and the Castellan was not one [Roy C. Lackey, 2008].
  • Castellans ended: Lackey argued from the text that Valeria's family's castellans ended generations ago, so the lame Castellan cannot be her father [Roy C. Lackey, 2008].
  • Unreliable generations: Aramini argued that time travel makes statements about the castellan's lineage both true and false, which weakens any firm lineage claim [Marc Aramini, 2008].

Where it stands: Still disputed and never settled: the theory was argued repeatedly from 2006 to 2011 with most of the contributions disputing it and no consensus reached.
