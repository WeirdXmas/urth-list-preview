The Hierogrammates are manipulators

The Hierogrammates manipulate humanity and Severian for their own benefit, and are not simply benevolent.

23 threads · 29 posts · for 10 / against 16 · first raised 1999-04-23 by Jonathan Laidlow
Main voices: Paul B (5), Dave Tallman (3), Chris P (2), Roy C. Lackey (2), b sharp (2), Jeff Wilson (2)

The debate

The theory was first raised on the list by Jonathan Laidlow in April 1999, who laid out Peter Wright's reading that hierogrammates use human mythology for their own ends [Jonathan Laidlow, 1999]. It returned most visibly in 2006, when Roy C. Lackey reported Wright's book Attending Daedalus as arguing the Hierogrammates manipulate humanity and that Severian is a puppet [Roy C. Lackey, 2006], and Chris P disputed this reading as implausible given Severian's role as narrator [Chris P, 2006]. The 'ATTENDING DAEDALUS' and 'academic commentary' threads then split between defenders of Wright's deception-based method and critics who said it ignored the divine and the Increate [Roy C. Lackey, 2006] [Craig Brewer, 2010].

From 2008 the debate was mostly carried by the 'What's So Great About Ushas?' threads. Dave Tallman argued that engineering Severian as a genetic puppet makes the New Sun epic pointless and that the Hierogrammates were humanized beings pursuing goals rather than manipulators [Dave Tallman, 2008] [Dave Tallman, 2008]. Paul B. and Roy C. Lackey pressed the self-serving-manipulator side, with Lackey citing Apheta's dialogue [Roy C. Lackey, 2008] [Paul B, 2008], while Lane Haygood and b sharp defended judging them by other moral standards [Lane Haygood, 2008] [b sharp, 2008]. Jeff Wilson and John Watkins offered further readings of them as false gods or evil creations [Jeff Wilson, 2008].

Later posts added new clues and theories. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes and Ryan Dunn read the manipulation as a puppet show with the hierodules having a stake in Severian's success [Ryan Dunn, 2011], and Jerry Friedman asked for specific textual examples beyond the obvious ones [Jerry Friedman, 2011]. The threads on the flood and the black hole show posters such as Paul B. and thalassocrat arguing for engineered events, while Bruce Hayles and Jeff Wilson gave physical or non-engineered explanations [Paul B, 2007] [thalassocrat, 2008]. Jeff Wilson's 2018 post still shaped history through Briah rather than settling the question [Jeff Wilson, 2018].

For

  • Hierogrammates exploit human myths for their own ends, per Laidlow's reading of Wright [Jonathan Laidlow, 1999]
  • Wright's book argues the Hierogrammates manipulate humanity and that Severian is a puppet, as Lackey reported [Roy C. Lackey, 2006]
  • Roy Lackey argued the Hierogrammates are self-serving manipulators who can be judged morally, citing Apheta's dialogue [Roy C. Lackey, 2008]
  • Paul B. argued the flood was an engineered event, doubting that melting ice could produce it [Paul B, 2007]
  • Ryan Dunn compared the manipulation to The Truman Show and suggested the hierodules have a stake in Severian's success [Ryan Dunn, 2011]

Against

  • Chris P. argued Severian as author and narrator cannot be ignored, so total control is implausible [Chris P, 2006]
  • Dave Tallman argued the genetic-puppet reading makes the New Sun epic pointless and that Wolfe meant a mix of hope and sadness [Dave Tallman, 2008]
  • Dave Tallman argued the Hierogrammates were humanized beings pursuing goals and that bringing the New Sun was a good act [Dave Tallman, 2008]
  • Craig Brewer argued that the textual case for manipulation is easier to make than one for a divine Increate behind it [Craig Brewer, 2010]
  • Bruce Hayles answered that the text gives a stated physical cause for the flood, the New Sun's gravity [Paul B, 2007]

Where it stands: Still disputed: the manipulation reading was argued by Wright and several posters but never settled, with critics and defenders trading meta-arguments and the threads ending in impasse.
