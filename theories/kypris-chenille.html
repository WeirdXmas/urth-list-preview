Kypris possesses Chenille

Kypris speaks through Chenille (and others) by possession.

16 threads · 22 posts · for 13 / against 8 · first raised 1997-02-04 by Dave Lebling
Main voices: Marc Aramini (8), Dave Lebling (3), Roy C. Lackey (3), CoxRathvon (1), Marcos B. Gouvea (1), stilskin (1)

The debate

The theory that Kypris speaks through Chenille by possession first appears on the list in 2000, when stilskin proposed that the bird Oreb in Lake of the Long Sun carries a shard of Pas and that Chenille speaks as Kypris, citing dialogue on pp. 91-92 [stilskin, 2000]. Earlier 1997 posts by Marcos B. Gouvea and Dave Lebling had already built possession theories on Chenille-Kypris's remark that possession leaves part of a god in a person, but they concerned Hyacinth, Silk and Mint rather than Chenille [Marcos B. Gouvea, 1997] [Dave Lebling, 1997] [Dave Lebling, 1997]. The Chenille-specific claim was taken up mainly in January 2011, when Marc Aramini argued that Chenille, Hyacinth and Mamelta are partly Kypris, citing Chenille's possession and Hyacinth's hair [Marc Aramini, 2011]. Gerry Quinn disputed this, arguing that Chenille's memories of an ordinary Earth childhood and sleeper training do not suggest the concubine, and that Silk's uneasy sense of Chenille's smile comes from Mucor's possession [Gerry Quinn, 2011].

In November 2011 Larry Miller asked whether Chenille is possessed by Kypris and said he was confused about when she is herself, while Lee Berman argued the blurring is deliberate because Chenille is a prostitute and Kypris a sex goddess, citing a Wolfe interview [Larry Miller, 2011] [Lee Berman, 2011]. Roy C. Lackey offered a narrower version in January 2011, reading Chenille's remark about Mint as a sister as figurative possession by Kypris, drawing on a passage from Lake of the Long Sun [Roy C. Lackey, 2011]. Dave Tallman disputed the knife-skill link, arguing that Kypris possession alone would not explain Chenille's skill with blades, citing General Mint's confidence without new physical ability [Dave Tallman, 2010].

The discussion was mostly carried in threads about Mamelta and the family relations of Silk, and Chenille's possession got little separate attention. Aramini repeatedly restated Kypris's possession of Chenille in later posts, including his 2013 claim that Wolfe plainly states Kypris is Hyacinth, Mamelta, Chenille and Silk's mother [Marc Aramini, 2013].

For

  • Oreb is a shard of Pas carrying Pas's consciousness, supported by Chenille speaking as Kypris and the 'One does. Two.' line [stilskin, 2000]
  • Chenille's possessed speech, Hyacinth's hair and Mamelta's blue eyes are Kypris traits, so the three women are partly Kypris [Marc Aramini, 2011]
  • Wolfe's Long Sun text plainly states Kypris is Hyacinth, Mamelta, Chenille and Silk's mother, according to Aramini [Marc Aramini, 2013]
  • Confusion between Chenille and Kypris may be deliberate, given Chenille is a prostitute and Kypris a sex goddess, per Berman [Lee Berman, 2011]
  • Mint's remark that Chenille is her older sister reflects possession by Kypris, drawing on Chenille's own account of another presence [Roy C. Lackey, 2011]

Against

  • Chenille's memories of an ordinary Earth childhood and sleeper training do not suggest the concubine Kypris [Gerry Quinn, 2011]
  • Silk's uneasy sense of Chenille's smile is better explained by Mucor's possession than by Kypris [Gerry Quinn, 2011]
  • Kypris possession alone would not explain Chenille's knife-throwing skill, as General Mint's confidence arose without new physical ability [Dave Tallman, 2010]
  • Kypris's hair colour is never stated in the text, so the hair-based links to Chenille and Mamelta are weak [Marc Aramini, 2011]

Where it stands: Still disputed and never settled: the Chenille-possession claim was proposed by a few posters, disputed by Quinn, Tallman and others, and left unresolved in the threads that carried it.
