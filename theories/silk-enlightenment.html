Where Silk's enlightenment came from

The Outsider, not Mainframe or a natural cause, enlightened Silk; rival readings credit Typhon, Pas, Kypris or a stroke.

60 threads · 82 posts · for 42 / against 34 · first raised 1997-02-11 by Dave Lebling
Main voices: Marc Aramini (19), Dan'l Danehy-Oakes (9), James Wynn (6), Gerry Quinn (5), James Jordan (4), Roy C. Lackey (4)

The debate

The theory was first raised on the list by Dave Lebling in 1997, who argued that Silk's readings of his enlightenment are self-serving [Dave Lebling, 1997], while James Jordan the same year argued that the Outsider, not Mainframe, was the primary cause [James Jordan, 1997]. Early posts also floated Typhon as a backup form of the god [David Wells, 1997] and a Christ-figure reading linked to the Triune God [James Jordan, 1997]. The debate widened in 1999 and 2001 as Alex David Groce and others defended materialist and theological readings [Alex David Groce, 1999], and Dan'l Danehy-Oakes and Michael Straight argued over whether Horn's narration can be trusted about the enlightenment [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2001] [Michael Straight, 2001].

From 2002 the discussion centred on time: Don Doggett linked the enlightenment to the Whorl reaching faster-than-light speed [Don Doggett, 2002], and Charles Reed and Chris P disputed that timing and proposed mystical or sequential explanations [Charles Reed, 2002] [Chris P, 2002]. Jerry Friedman argued the Outsider directly stopped time [Jerry Friedman, 2002], while Dan'l Danehy-Oakes proposed a non-temporal supernatural experience [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2002]. From 2008 to 2011 Marc Aramini repeatedly argued for preprogrammed or biological causes, including Typhon and Mamelta or Pas and Kypris as the voices [Marc Aramini, 2008] [Marc Aramini, 2011], while Gerry Quinn, Roy C. Lackey and James Wynn insisted on a genuine Outsider communication [Gerry Quinn, 2011] [Roy C. Lackey, 2010] [James Wynn, 2010]. Dave Lebling and Gerry Quinn disputed Crane's aneurysm and mini-stroke explanations [Dave Lebling, 2011] [Gerry Quinn, 2011].

Later threads kept returning to the same split: Jordon Flato rejected the preprogrammed reading outright [Jordon Flato, 2008], and Aramini in 2013 and 2018 argued that the physical cause and Silk's artificial enlightenment serve the Outsider or Pas [Marc Aramini, 2013] [Marc Aramini, 2018]. Threads such as "secular interpretations" and "Wolfe and Materialism" ended in impasse rather than agreement.

For

  • The Outsider is the cause because Silk's enlightenment came as a genuine communication from the Outsider rather than a natural event [Roy C. Lackey, 2010].
  • Silk's enlightenment is miraculous yet physically manifested, so a physical cause does not exclude divine origin [António Pedro Marques, 2011].
  • Gerry Quinn argued the text gives no evidence the Outsider always works through neurological catastrophes, so Crane's explanation is wrong [Gerry Quinn, 2011].
  • Don Doggett cited Marble's internal clock being about 2,700 years off as circumstantial evidence that time stopped during the enlightenment [David Stockhoff, 2010] [Marc Aramini, 2010].
  • James Wynn cited the text's own statement of enlightenment as the basis for belief in the ball-court enlightenment [James Wynn, 2011].

Against

  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes argued that the Long Sun account is unreliable because it was written years later from hearsay [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2011].
  • Marc Aramini argued that Pas and Kypris or Typhon and Mamelta preprogrammed the voices, so the enlightenment has a biological or programmed cause [Marc Aramini, 2013] [Marc Aramini, 2008].
  • Dave Lebling argued that Silk's readings are self-serving [Dave Lebling, 1997], and Adam Stephanides argued the enlightenment is an aesthetic choice [Adam Stephanides, 2001].
  • Roy C. Lackey and Jordon Flato rejected the preprogrammed reading as far off the mark [Jordon Flato, 2008].
  • Charles Reed noted the Whorl had orbited the short sun for about thirty years, longer than Silk's life, against the FTL-dropout timing [Gerry Quinn, 2011].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the list never settled whether the Outsider, a physical cause or a preprogrammed plan caused Silk's enlightenment, and the threads closed in impasse.
