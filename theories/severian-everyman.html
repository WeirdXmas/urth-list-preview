Severian is an everyman of many selves

Severian contains many persons (Thecla, the Autarchs, his other selves) and stands for humanity as a whole.

12 threads · 13 posts · for 8 / against 5 · first raised 1998-03-26 by Michael Straight
Main voices: Michael Straight (1), Peter Westlake (1), adam louis stephanides (1), Dan Parmenter (1), Alex David Groce (1), Roy C. Lackey (1)

The debate

The earliest post on the list is Michael Straight's [Michael Straight, 1998] (1998), which argues that Severian's perfect memory makes Thecla's presence feel like continuity rather than stored files. It does not yet call Severian an everyman. The everyman idea first appears in Dan Parmenter's [Dan Parmenter, 1998] (1998), which reads the hairless, crippled animals in the Witches' glass as a sign that Severian embodies many inherited personalities. Peter Westlake [Peter Westlake, 1998] and adam louis stephanides [adam louis stephanides, 1998] also argued in 1998 that Thecla's personality is preserved in Severian and that his personality is the essential matter of the book.

The 1999 posts turned to whether Severian is an everyman at all. Alex David Groce [Alex David Groce, 1999] called him a flawed 'dark everyman' with real moral growth. Roy C. Lackey [Roy C. Lackey, 1999] answered that he is an anti-hero rather than a sympathetic Everyman. Jonathan Laidlow [Jonathan Laidlow, 1999] argued that Severian keeps asserting a particular identity despite some everyman symbolism, and Peter Stephenson [Peter Stephenson, 1999] read him as Everyman while conceding that his entanglement in causality undercuts that reading.

From 2006 to 2011 the discussion shifted to whether the many selves are really present. Mark Millman [Mark Millman, 2006] argued that Thecla's personality intrudes into Severian's memory. Dan'l Danehy-Oakes [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2006] read the shifting personalities as a sign of growing memory confusion, with the hope that they would merge. John Watkins [John Watkins, 2008] argued that Thecla's personality dominates the absorbed Autarch memories, and Jeff Wilson [Jeff Wilson, 2010] argued that the Autarchs' minds do not survive in Severian at all. Antonin Scriabin [Antonin Scriabin, 2011] took the multiple selves as a reason the narration is unreliable. In the 'Myfather's mountedguard' thread Wilson supported Watkins, citing the ten years of adjustment shown by The Urth of the New Sun, but the thread drifted into side questions without consensus.

For

  • Parmenter argued that the crippled animals in the Witches' glass signal inherited personalities, a folk-process everyman [Dan Parmenter, 1998].
  • Groce held that Severian is a flawed dark everyman whose moral growth is real [Alex David Groce, 1999].
  • Stephenson read Severian as Everyman, with his lack of prior claim to being chosen strengthening the religious resonances [Peter Stephenson, 1999].
  • Danehy-Oakes read his 'childhoods' as many lives from the Autarchial line of memories [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2006].
  • Scriabin argued that the multiple selves and absorbed minds make the narrating self a problematic narrator [Antonin Scriabin, 2011].

Against

  • Laidlow argued that Severian keeps asserting a particular identity and is a torturer trying to be good, not an everyman [Jonathan Laidlow, 1999].
  • Lackey argued that Severian is an anti-hero who lacks the relatability of Everyman figures [Roy C. Lackey, 1999].
  • Watkins argued that Thecla's personality dominates Severian's absorbed memories, since the older rulers lacked the Claw and eidetic memory [John Watkins, 2008].
  • Wilson argued that the Autarchs' personalities do not survive in Severian through the alzabo analeptic, leaving Thecla as a single will with confused memories [Jeff Wilson, 2010].
  • Millman argued that the memory of pebbles and dodging hooves is Thecla's, with her personality briefly overtaking Severian's narration [Mark Millman, 2006].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the everyman reading was raised in 1998 and argued over for years, but the list reached no consensus on whether Severian contains many persons or is dominated by Thecla.
