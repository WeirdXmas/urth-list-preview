Horn's spirit is in Babbie

Horn's spirit (or part of it) goes into the hus Babbie when Horn's body dies or is left.

47 threads · 105 posts · for 57 / against 42 · first raised 1999-10-06 by Dave Lebling
Main voices: Marc Aramini (29), David Stockhoff (15), James Wynn (10), António Pedro Marques (8), Andrew Bollen (5), Jerry Friedman (4)

The debate

The earliest post on the list that touches this idea is Dave Lebling's 1999 post [Dave Lebling, 1999], which says the identities of Babbie, Horn and Silk blur at the end of Blue. Later in 1999, William Ansley [William H. Ansley, 1999] and Tom Urash [Urash, Tom, 1999] tied the blurring to Horn's pit vision and a hallucinated Babbie. Nicholas Gevers [Nicholas Gevers, 2000] and Dennis Berdanis [Dennis G. Berdanis, 2000] first raised an actual switch of Horn and Babbie in 2000. The explicit claim that Babbie is Horn came from Marc Aramini in 2002 [Marc Aramini, 2002] [Marc Aramini, 2002], and he built a plant-to-animal theory around it [Marc Aramini, 2002] [Marc Aramini, 2002].

The 2002 discussion was mostly a fight over textual support. Stephen Case [Stephen Case, 2002] said Horn's flight into Babbie fit well, while Michael Straight [Michael Straight, 2002] disputed it without citing text. Aramini's narrative-shift argument [Marc Aramini, 2002] was met by Dan'l Danehy-Oakes [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2002], who proposed that Horn moved into Babbie. Jerry Friedman questioned why a humanoid Babbie should carry Horn's spirit [Jerry Friedman, 2002] and asked whether Babbie has two horns [Jerry Friedman, 2002]. Roy Lackey [Roy C. Lackey, 2002] said the passages Aramini quoted were misassembled. In 2003 Andrew Bollen [Andrew Bollen, 2003] proposed the reverse, Babbie into Horn, and Aramini [Marc Aramini, 2003] and Danehy-Oakes [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2003] disputed the details of who moves where.

The idea came back in 2008-2011. Aramini [Marc Aramini, 2008] and [Marc Aramini, 2008] and Pedro Pereira [Pedro Pereira, 2008] supported a partial or essential transfer, and Dave Tallman [Dave Tallman, 2008] proposed one after the Rajan's near-death encounter. John Watkins [John Watkins, 2009] and Son of Witz [Son of Witz, 2009] discussed the idea through the Babbie-Pig parallel, and Witz rejected a partial transfer for lack of a plot reason. In December 2011 a long burst of posts split the list: Roy Lackey [Roy C. Lackey, 2011] listed problems with the premise, António Pedro Marques [António Pedro Marques, 2011] said Horn remained in the Rajan, and Gerry Quinn [Gerry Quinn, 2011] argued Horn was still alive when Babbie saw him. Against them, David Stockhoff [David Stockhoff, 2011] called Horn a spiritless corpse, and Aramini [Marc Aramini, 2011] and [Marc Aramini, 2011] argued for the transfer. Sergei Soloviev [Sergei SOLOVIEV, 2011] read Babbie's claim as metaphorical. In 2013 Aramini [Marc Aramini, 2013] proposed that Horn ended up entirely in Babbie, and in 2014 Dan Harris [Dan Harris, 2014] argued for transfer on Odyssey parallels. The latest post [Unknown, 2023], from 2023, argues Babbie is Horn's spirit in a beast.

For

  • Aramini argued from the narrative voice change after the farewell and Silk's awakening that Horn fled into Babbie [Marc Aramini, 2002].
  • The tree vision under the great tree at the end of On Blue's Waters was cited as the sending point of Horn's spirit [Marc Aramini, 2002] and [Marc Aramini, 2010].
  • Babbie's embrace of Horn's son in Return to the Whorl and the three-horned beast prophecy were offered as evidence of Horn's presence [Marc Aramini, 2011] and [Marc Aramini, 2008].
  • Tallman proposed that part of Horn entered Babbie after the Rajan's near-death encounter, a partial-transfer version [Dave Tallman, 2008].
  • Harris cited Odyssey parallels and the change in voice to argue Horn transfers into Babbie [Dan Harris, 2014].

Against

  • Lackey listed problems with the premise, including Babbie's two-fingered hands, Silk's ignorance of Babbie before Dorp, and Babbie's departure after the exorcism [Roy C. Lackey, 2011].
  • Friedman questioned why humanoid Babbie should carry Horn's spirit and suggested moved memories kept their original timestamps [Jerry Friedman, 2002].
  • Quinn argued Horn was still alive when Babbie saw him in the pit, so Babbie's diagnosis of death is wrong [Gerry Quinn, 2011].
  • Marques argued the Rajan's later dream-travel appearance as Horn shows Horn remained in the Rajan, not in Babbie [António Pedro Marques, 2011].
  • Witz found no symbolic or plot reason for a partial transfer and noted Horn stays very present on Green [Son of Witz, 2009].

Where it stands: Still disputed and never settled: the theory was raised in 1999-2000, argued heavily in 2002-2003 and again in 2011, and the list reported no consensus, with Ansley saying in 2002 that most Short Sun issues were unresolved [William H. Ansley, 2002], while a 2023 post still argues for it [Unknown, 2023].
