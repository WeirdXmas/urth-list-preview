Tzadkiel is modelled on Melek Taus

Tzadkiel is modelled on the Yazidi Peacock Angel, Melek Taus.

1 threads · 5 posts · for 5 / against 0 · first raised 2010-07-08 by John Watkins
Main voices: thalassocrat (2), John Watkins (1), Lee Berman (1), Gerry Quinn (1)

The debate

The theory was first raised on 2010-07-08 by John Watkins, who proposed that the Yazidi Peacock Angel Melek Taus may underlie Tzadkiel's peacock-like eye-wings and villainy, and suggested a fuller study of Melek Taus across Wolfe's other demons [John Watkins, 2010]. The next day Mr Thalassocrat proposed the same link, citing the eye-wings and the parallel of an angel refusing to honor Man, and noted that the Yazidi tale reverses Satan's story [thalassocrat, 2010]. Lee Berman then argued that Tzadkiel is a combined figure drawn from Melek Taus and from Cherubim with eye-spangled wings, grounding this in the mirror-book scene where Severian sees a winged being as big as an island [Lee Berman, 2010].

The discussion stayed within a single thread, "Tzadkiel/Melek Taus", and moved toward wider readings. Thalassocrat argued that the Melek Taus use is specific to The Book of the New Sun and Urth, where the young Severian is manipulated into serving a Lucifer-like figure, and that the reader can be drawn into accepting a false enlightenment reading [thalassocrat, 2010]. Gerry Quinn offered only a brief supporting remark, suggesting the Green Man could fill the structural role that John Watkins had described for the text [Gerry Quinn, 2010]. The thread summary also records debate over whether Tzadkiel is truly evil, along with drift into unrelated topics such as the cause of the flood, Father Inire and Typhon's sled, but none of those points is tied to a post in this record.

The record contains no post that directly disputes the Melek Taus link, so the against side is empty. The thread summary says that some posters pushed back, arguing that the flood reads as renewal, but no post in this record carries that argument.

For

Where it stands: Raised by John Watkins in 2010 and supported by Mr Thalassocrat and Lee Berman, with no post in this record disputing it directly; the thread ended in unresolved disagreement and drifted to other topics.
