Ted is Goldwurm (or Ambrosius)

In The Sorcerer's House, Ted is Goldwurm the griffin, or Ambrosius.

10 threads · 23 posts · for 20 / against 7 · first raised 2010-03-19 by Gwern Branwen
Main voices: Roy C. Lackey (8), Craig Brewer (5), thalassocrat (4), James Wynn (3), Gwern Branwen (3), Marc Aramini (3)

The debate

The idea that Ted is Ambrosius (or Goldwurm) was first raised on 2010-03-19, when James Crossley proposed that Ambrosius is Ted [James Crossley, 2010], and Gwern Branwen proposed the same identity that day, speculating about hidden twins and Baxter's role in George's death [Gwern Branwen, 2010]. Within days nsowards16 built on Branwen's initials T, G and A pointing to Ambrosius, suggesting Ted used a similar alias [nsowards16, 2010]. John Watkins then offered a different variant, that Ted Griffin/Ambrosius is Kate Finn's father, and tested it against the ages and dates [John Watkins, 2010].

In April 2010 the theory was pressed hardest on the ring and the T.A.G. initials. James Wynn argued Ted is Ambrosius, using the initials and the opal's colour change as clues [James Wynn, 2010]. Dave Tallman proposed instead that Mr. Isaacs is Goldwurm and that Doris may have betrayed her husband [Dave Tallman, 2010]. Craig Brewer and Roy C. Lackey traded readings over whether Ted is Ambrosius as a ghost or disguised figure [Craig Brewer, 2010] [Roy C. Lackey, 2010] [Craig Brewer, 2010]. Lackey then argued the gold ring was Ambrosius's, not Ted's, and that Ted was buried rather than lost in the river [Roy C. Lackey, 2010]. Wynn questioned that reading and conceded Ambrosius need not be Ted [James Wynn, 2010]. Gwern Branwen disputed Lackey's reading of the Emlyn passage, arguing Emlyn knows only that Goldwurm was killed by someone who may have been Ambrosius [Gwern Branwen, 2010], and Lackey answered that Emlyn is the only source for the events [Roy C. Lackey, 2010].

The Goldwurm-as-griffin variant came from thalassocrat, who proposed that Ted is Goldwurm the griffin, a guardian of gold, linked to Ambrosius's ring [thalassocrat, 2010]. thalassocrat later argued Ted worked in real estate for Ambrosius at A & I Inc, building on Stephen Hoy's name-tracing [thalassocrat, 2010]. Lackey proposed Ted may be Goldwurm and that Doris was Ted's accomplice, citing her knowledge of the ring before Bax showed it to her [Roy C. Lackey, 2010] [Roy C. Lackey, 2010]. In 2018 Marc Aramini shifted the theory, arguing Goldwurm is the Lamia of Corinth and that Ambrosius is strangled as Goldwurm [Marc Aramini, 2018] [Marc Aramini, 2018]. The discussion ended without consensus, and the 2018 Lamia reading drew no recorded rebuttal.

For

  • Crossley proposed Ambrosius is Ted in the first post raising the idea [James Crossley, 2010].
  • Branwen proposed the same identity and linked Ambrosius to the twins and Baxter's motives [Gwern Branwen, 2010].
  • Wynn grounded Ted-as-Ambrosius in the T.A.G. initials and Doris spelling them out [James Wynn, 2010].
  • Brewer grounded the identity in the TAG initials, the wet object and the fish's three shades of black [Craig Brewer, 2010].
  • thalassocrat grounded Ted-as-Goldwurm in the ghost's avoidance by Ieulan and the ghost's ineffectiveness against werewolves [thalassocrat, 2010].

Against

  • Lackey argued the gold ring belonged to Ambrosius and that Ted's body was buried in a casket rather than thrown in the river [Roy C. Lackey, 2010].
  • Branwen argued Emlyn knows only that Goldwurm was killed by someone who may have been Ambrosius, not the whole scenario [Gwern Branwen, 2010].
  • Lackey argued Emlyn is the only source for Ambrosius and Goldwurm, so the supernatural elements must be accepted [Roy C. Lackey, 2010].
  • Wynn questioned whether the character list is authoritative and conceded Ambrosius need not be Ted [James Wynn, 2010].
  • Lackey argued Ted's having been killed by a werewolf is unlikely given the timeline, and that the ring is a magic object [Roy C. Lackey, 2010].

Where it stands: Raised in March 2010 and never settled, with the Ted-as-Ambrosius and Ted-as-Goldwurm variants left disputed, and the 2018 Lamia reading by Aramini a single-poster case with no recorded agreement.
