The Neighbors are trees

The Neighbors are (or live on as) trees, or the trees spawn them.

35 threads · 63 posts · for 32 / against 28 · first raised 2000-08-02 by Tim Boolos
Main voices: James Wynn (23), Marc Aramini (9), David Stockhoff (6), Dan'l Danehy-Oakes (5), Brian Lovely (5), Gerry Quinn (4)

The debate

The theory first appears on the list on 2000-08-02, when Tim Boolos proposed that a forest god of the neighbors comforts Horn in The Book of the Long Sun and Short Sun [Tim Boolos, 2000]. The first explicit claim that Horn is a tree hybrid came from Joe Eull in 2002 [Joe Eull, 2002]. Over 2002 and 2005 the idea was mostly disputed by Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, who floated a Narrator-as-tree idea but doubted that trees spawn Neighbors [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2002][Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2002], and by Marc Aramini, who in 2008 proposed a tree hybrid Horn [Marc Aramini, 2008]. The idea grew in 2005 when James Wynn argued that the Neighbors are the trees, citing their sleepy, Huorn-like feel [James Wynn, 2005][James Wynn, 2005].

The 2009 posts were the most active period. Brian Lovely, James Wynn, David Stockhoff and Tim O'Donnell argued over whether trees and vines could explain the inhumi, with objections centred on how inhumi turn into vines or back into reptiles [Brian Lovely, 2009][James Wynn, 2009][James Wynn, 2009][O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI), 2009]. Stockhoff suggested soul-trees and Wynn answered that a Neighbor's soul form is not a tree [David Stockhoff, 2009][James Wynn, 2009]. In late 2010 and 2011 Lee Berman, Aramini and Wynn kept building the case, with Aramini arguing that trees fed on human blood to produce Neighbors [Lee Berman, 2010][Marc Aramini, 2011][Marc Aramini, 2011]. Wynn and Gerry Quinn pushed back, with Quinn treating the trees as metaphor rather than literal plants [James Wynn, 2011][Gerry Quinn, 2011][Gerry Quinn, 2011].

Supporters pointed to Wynn's endorsement of Aramini's essay, which he called the only comprehensive theory anyone had assembled [James Wynn, 2011], and to Dan'l Danehy-Oakes's view that metaphors in Wolfe's SF can become literal [Dan'l Danehy-Oakes, 2011]. Opponents cited the Rajan's soul form, the lack of any farmer describing animals becoming plants, and Sergei Soloviev's objection that trees should not like fire [Marc Aramini, 2011][Sergei SOLOVIEV, 2011], which Stockhoff answered with forest-management analogies [David Stockhoff, 2011].

For

  • Tim Boolos's early forest-god reading gave the first Neighbor-tree link on the list [Tim Boolos, 2000].
  • Brian Lovely argued that blood transfusion passes part of a donor's being, so Neighbors absorbed Horn through the pit tree [Brian Lovely, 2009].
  • Marc Aramini argued that trees fed on human blood to produce Neighbors and inhumi, a eucharist-like process [Marc Aramini, 2011][Marc Aramini, 2011].
  • James Wynn cited the Rajan's description of the trees as sleepy and Huorn-like to argue that the Neighbors are the trees [James Wynn, 2005][James Wynn, 2010].
  • Lee Berman proposed that shapeshifting beings of Green began as simple trees and vines that imitated more advanced creatures [Lee Berman, 2010][Lee Berman, 2010].

Against

  • Dan'l Danehy-Oakes doubted that trees spawn Neighbors and questioned the copying mechanism.
  • James Wynn argued that dead Neighbors would have become ordinary trees, so trees on Blue prove nothing about Neighbor survival [James Wynn, 2010].
  • Gerry Quinn argued that the trees and vines are metaphors, and that Wolfe would have stated any real plant-animal evolution outright [Gerry Quinn, 2011][Gerry Quinn, 2011].
  • Sergei Soloviev argued that Neighbors who like fires cannot be trees [Sergei SOLOVIEV, 2011], and Marc Aramini argued Jugano's report that baby inhumi swim in water tells against the literal reading [Marc Aramini, 2011].
  • James Wynn argued that the Rajan's soul form is not simply a tree and that the inhumi transformations remain unexplained [James Wynn, 2009][O'Donnell, Tim (BOSI), 2009].

Where it stands: Still disputed: the idea was pressed repeatedly by a small group, notably Wynn, Aramini and Lovely, but the threads ended without agreement and critics such as Quinn and Sergei Soloviev kept rejecting the literal reading.
