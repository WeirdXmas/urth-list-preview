Little Severian is Severian's kin

The boy Little Severian, whom Severian adopts, is related to him.

14 threads · 15 posts · for 11 / against 2 · first raised 1998-05-21 by J. Schultz
Main voices: Lee Berman (4), adam louis stephanides (2), J. Schultz (1), John Moar (1), Alice Turner (1), Lisa Schaffer-Doggett (1)

The debate

The earliest kinship claim on the list is from b sharp in 2006, who argued that Severian, Dorcas, Jolenta and Little Severian are family members through a family-curse theory [b sharp, 2006]. This built on Dan Rabin's paired revelations in the Jolenta and Dorcas discussion. Earlier 1998 posts treat Little Severian differently: J. Schultz asked about a sexual relationship with the younger self [J. Schultz, 1998], and Alice Turner called him a spare replacement rather than a relative [Alice Turner, 1998].

The theory was developed in 2010 by Lee Berman, who argued that Little Severian is Severian's cousin by way of a family tree that includes Father Inire in disguise, grounding this in resemblances that others had not acknowledged [Lee Berman, 2010]. Berman later placed him as a third or second cousin once removed, admitting that this required a couple of major stretches [Lee Berman, 2010]. Andrew Mason then linked the family to Casdoe, proposing she is Dorcas's niece on the basis of the name Cas and an aunt called Cas [Andrew Mason, 2010].

The main opposition came from Gerry Quinn, who argued that Little Severian is just a boy with that name, that any family tie would be distant, and that the name might hint at Severa without the text confirming it [Gerry Quinn, 2010]. Son of Witz read Casdoe's household as a symbolic family rather than a literal one, making Little Severian stand for the humanity Severian saves [Son of Witz, 2011]. The longer 'Sev's family tree' thread broadened into other lineage readings and ended without consensus.

No post on the list establishes the kinship as accepted, and the proposals rest on name similarity, resemblance and family-tree reasoning.

For

  • b sharp proposed a family-curse link tying Severian, Dorcas, Jolenta and Little Severian together [b sharp, 2006]
  • Lee Berman argued Little Severian is a cousin, citing unacknowledged resemblances and the absence of anyone else addressing the old person [Lee Berman, 2010]
  • Lee Berman's family tree placed Little Severian as a third or second cousin once removed, while admitting stretches [Lee Berman, 2010]
  • Andrew Mason proposed that Casdoe is Dorcas's niece, so the shared name points to a family link [Andrew Mason, 2010]

Against

  • Gerry Quinn argued Little Severian is simply a boy with that name, with no evident link to Severian in his Urth [Gerry Quinn, 2010]
  • Gerry Quinn suggested any family tie would be distant and that the name may hint at Severa, which the text never clearly confirms [Gerry Quinn, 2010]
  • Son of Witz read Casdoe's household as a symbolic family, making Little Severian humanity rather than a literal relative [Son of Witz, 2011]

Where it stands: Raised by b sharp in 2006 and developed by Lee Berman in 2010, but still disputed, with no post on the list accepting the kinship and the main objections from Gerry Quinn and Son of Witz left unanswered.
