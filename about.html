About

The Urth mailing list (from 1997) and its sister list Whorl (1996-2002) were where readers worked through Gene Wolfe's fiction together. This index gathers 38,304 messages from two sources: Moonmilk's archive at urth.net (1996-2003) and the copy of the list's later archive on archive.org (2004-2023). September 2003 to August 2004 is in neither.

Email addresses are removed throughout. Each message's summary and labels (what it does, the kind of reading, themes, theories) are written by a language model and can be wrong; the message itself is always shown with it.

This is a temporary preview of the private build, with the full text of every message, shared with a few people for a short time. The posts belong to their authors; follow the "original" links to read them in the list's own archives.
